Auditor General uncovers Rs23.95bn irregularities in Sindh Irrigation Department

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Auditor General of Pakistan has uncovered serious financial irregularities and regulatory violations amounting to over Rs23.95 billion in the Sindh Irrigation Department during the 2024-25 fiscal year. The AG Sindh handed a detailed 54-point audit report to the Secretary Irrigation for final action, raising grave questions about the provincial department's financial transparency. The report flagged the illegal splitting of contracts, payments made in violation of approved laws and the flouting of constitutional regulations in the passing of bills.

It found that all legal procedures were bypassed in the approval and execution of development projects, while suspicious steps taken under the cover of emergency works inflicted heavy losses on the national exchequer. The audit team strongly objected to the acceptance of unlawful tenders and procurements made against the rules. Crores of rupees were shown as spent on filling flood breaches, yet the department could provide the audit team with no legal or solid justification for the heavy expenditure.

The audit authorities recommended a transparent investigation into the matter and immediate legal action against those found responsible.
