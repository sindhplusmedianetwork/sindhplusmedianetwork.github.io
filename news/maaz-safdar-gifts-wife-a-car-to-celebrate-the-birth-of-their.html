Maaz Safdar Gifts Wife a Car to Celebrate the Birth of Their Daughter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani YouTuber and family vlogger Maaz Safdar gifted his wife Saba Maaz a car to celebrate the birth of their third child, a daughter. He bought a grey Suzuki Wagon R, which was delivered to their home. Saba shared photos of the car on social media, thanking her husband for the gift.

Fans congratulated the couple and praised the gesture. The couple had welcomed their baby daughter earlier this month, and the gift marked the happy occasion for the family.
