Government, JI Committees to Meet Friday Over Petroleum Levy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-i-Islami and the federal government will hold committee-level talks on Friday over the party's demand to abolish the petroleum levy. JI Vice Emir Liaqat Baloch announced the meeting after a telephonic conversation with Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal on Thursday. The talks will take place at Punjab House in Islamabad.

According to Baloch, the two sides will also work out a schedule for a proposed meeting between Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and JI Emir Hafiz Naeemur Rehman. He said the party would continue its nationwide protests alongside the negotiations. The petroleum levy has been a central point of contention in the party's ongoing protest campaign.
