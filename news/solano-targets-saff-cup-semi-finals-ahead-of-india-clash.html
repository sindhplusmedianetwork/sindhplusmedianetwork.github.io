Solano targets SAFF Cup semi-finals ahead of India clash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan football team head coach Nolberto Solano has urged his side to rise to the occasion at the SAFF Championship 2026, declaring that every match will be treated as a final. Speaking at a press conference in Islamabad on Monday evening, Solano identified qualification for the semi-finals as the team's primary objective. Pakistan will face arch-rivals India on Thursday and the Maldives in their second group match, with both results crucial to reaching the last four.

Solano compared the Pakistan-India rivalry to the football rivalry between his home country Peru and Chile, saying he understood how important such matches were for the fans. The Green-shirts will be without their overseas-based players in the initial stage, as the championship is not being held during a FIFA international window, but Solano insisted their absence would be no excuse. He called for unity, discipline and defensive solidity, stressing that football was a collective game in which every player must contribute.

Solano also welcomed the announcement of a professional football league in Pakistan next year, describing it as an important development for the players' fitness and the standard of the game.
