Peshawar High Court Seeks Replies on Detention of Sohail Afridi, Other PTI Leaders

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Peshawar High Court on Tuesday sought replies from the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government and the inspector general of police over the detention of 'non-functional' provincial chief executive Sohail Afridi, who has remained unseen since governor's rule was imposed in the province on October 11. The court fixed the next hearing for November 3. Petitions were filed by Afridi's brother and relatives of other detained PTI leaders, including KP Assembly Speaker Babar Saleem Swati and several lawmakers.

The petitioners alleged that the leaders were taken from the Chief Minister's House and the KP Assembly by masked armed individuals. They asked the court to trace and recover the detained leaders, preserve CCTV footage and official records, and order their release if the custody is found unlawful.
