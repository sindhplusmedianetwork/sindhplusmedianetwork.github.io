Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum Ends With 13 Agreements and MoUs Signed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The two-day Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 has concluded in Islamabad with 13 agreements and memorandums of understanding signed between Pakistani and international companies. The Frontier Works Organisation estimated copper reserves of 350 million tonnes at Dasht-e-Kain in Chagai, Balochistan, underlining the scale of the country's mineral potential. Mari Minerals announced its first gold and copper discovery at the forum.

National Resources Limited reported copper-gold mineralisation across at least eight porphyry deposits and confirmed a $100 million commitment to mineral exploration in Pakistan. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif assured international investors of their security in the country. He said Pakistan aims to move beyond exporting raw minerals toward local processing, refining and value-added manufacturing.
