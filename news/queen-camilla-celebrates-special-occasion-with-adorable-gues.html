Queen Camilla celebrates special occasion with adorable guest at Clarence House

Queen Camilla has welcomed the holiday season with a festive outfit as she celebrated a major milestone with an adorable guest. On Thursday, November 19, the official Instagram account of the Royal Family shared a video of the Queen, dressed in a red dress with a blue shawl around her shoulders, as she marked twenty years as President of the Brooke Charity. In the video, Camilla was seen feeding a carrot to a pony as she exchanged laughs and small talk with its caretaker.

The caption said Teddy the Shetland visited the Queen at Clarence House to celebrate her twenty years as President of the Brooke Charity, the international working animal charity. With a lifelong passion for horses, the Queen became the charity's President in 2006 and has visited Brooke's work in India, Pakistan, Jordan, Kenya and Egypt, as well as hosting supporter events in the UK. The milestone came after it was confirmed Camilla is set to attend her Royal Variety Performance as Queen, after being forced to skip the gala in 2024 due to illness, with the performance set to take place at the Royal Albert Hall in London on Friday, November 20.
