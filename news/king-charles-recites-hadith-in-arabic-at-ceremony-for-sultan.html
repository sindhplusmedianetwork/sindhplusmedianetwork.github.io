King Charles Recites Hadith in Arabic at Ceremony for Sultan of Oman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Britain's King Charles III recited a hadith from Sahih Muslim in Arabic during an official ceremony at Windsor Castle held in honour of the Sultan of Oman, Haitham bin Tariq. The hadith, quoted by the King, carries the meaning that if one were to go to the people of Oman, they would neither abuse nor harm him. The ceremony was organised as part of the Sultan's three-day official visit to Britain.

Sultan Haitham bin Tariq arrived in the United Kingdom for a state visit — the first official visit by an Omani sultan in nearly 44 years. The King recited the narration in Arabic before the distinguished guests at the castle. The gesture was noted as a mark of respect for Omani culture and the long-standing ties between the two countries.

The visit is expected to include discussions on bilateral relations and regional matters.
