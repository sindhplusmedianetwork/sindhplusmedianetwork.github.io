PML-N's Rashid Bashir Wark wins NA-77 Gujranwala by-election

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The results of the NA-77 Gujranwala by-election have been announced. PML-N candidate Rashid Bashir Wark won with 43,365 votes. Pakistan Markazi Muslim League's Muhammad Ibrahim Gujjar came second with 30,218 votes, followed by independent candidate Iftikhar Rasool Waraich with 5,373 votes.

Returning Officer Samira Anbreen issued Form 47. Turnout was 15.65 percent, with 80,016 of 518,620 registered voters casting their ballots, and 1,135 votes were rejected. The seat fell vacant after the death of PML-N's Mahmood Bashir Wark.
