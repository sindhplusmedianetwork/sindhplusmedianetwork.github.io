Saudi Civil Defence Gives All-Clear After Alerts in Several Areas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's civil defence said on Sunday that the danger had passed, shortly after issuing alerts warning of potential threats in several parts of the kingdom, including Dhahran, home to oil giant Saudi Aramco's headquarters. The danger had also passed in Dammam, Abqaiq and Al-Ahsa governorate, the civil defence said. The alerts came as Yemen's Houthi rebels claimed they had launched multiple missile and drone attacks against Saudi troops and infrastructure both in the Gulf kingdom and Yemen, targeting Aramco oil facilities in Khurais as well as Riyadh airport, Saudi troops in Najran and military facilities on both sides of the border.

The Saudi civil defence had also earlier lifted a warning issued in the kingdom's Najran city. The developments came as fighting in Yemen intensified, with the Houthis' health ministry saying the death toll from airstrikes by the Saudi-led coalition on the village of Al Shariyah, east of Sanaa, had risen to 33, with at least 39 others wounded. The United Nations says more than 1,600 people have been killed and more than 300,000 displaced since fighting resumed in Yemen.

Meanwhile, Iran's navy said two tankers had suffered severe damage and stopped after hitting sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz, and US Central Command said an American military aircraft had fired on and disabled a commercial vessel that tried to break the US blockade of Iran.
