Gas coming from Qatar, ample supply to continue in December and January: minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Petroleum Ali Pervez Malik has assured that gas is coming from Qatar and that ample gas supply will continue during December and January. According to Geo News, he was speaking at the convocation of a private university in Lahore when he said the situation in the Strait of Hormuz remains uncertain, which has affected oil and gas prices. He added that Pakistan has coped with the situation successfully over the past six months.

The minister said oil prices in the country are fixed in a transparent manner. He further said the government is providing subsidized petrol for poor people, and reaffirmed that gas shipments from Qatar are continuing as normal.
