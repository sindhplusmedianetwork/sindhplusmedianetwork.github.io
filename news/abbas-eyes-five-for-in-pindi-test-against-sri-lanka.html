Abbas Eyes Five-For in Pindi Test Against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's experienced Test fast bowler Mohammad Abbas is hoping to carry his good form into the upcoming two-match Test series against Sri Lanka, saying he is targeting a five-wicket haul at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. Abbas told reporters on the sidelines of Pakistan's training session at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Saturday that taking five wickets in an innings was due for him, and he wanted to get his name on the honour board. Abbas has not claimed any five-wicket haul in Pakistan in his career, during which he has mostly played cricket overseas.

He credited his continued involvement in domestic cricket for helping him maintain his performance in the longest format, saying regular four-day cricket had played a significant role in his development. Abbas said he played 20 to 25 four-day games in a year, whether regional or departmental cricket in Pakistan or county cricket when there was no cricket at home. He said preparations for the series were progressing well, with players benefiting from regular match practice, and he identified young fast bowler Razaullah as a promising prospect.

Abbas said the team had yet to inspect the pitch for the opening Test, which begins on November 9, and would make its assessment closer to the match. He also backed the younger players in the squad, saying the opportunity to represent Pakistan would allow them to demonstrate their abilities and establish themselves at international level.
