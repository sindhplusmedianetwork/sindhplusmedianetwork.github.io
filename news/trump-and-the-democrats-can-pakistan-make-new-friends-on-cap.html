Trump and the Democrats: can Pakistan make new friends on Capitol Hill?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

With the Democrats now controlling Congress after the midterm elections, analysts say Pakistan's close ties with President Donald Trump may face new scrutiny, though the impact on Islamabad could be limited. BBC Urdu reports that Pakistan built its recent closeness with Washington around Trump personally, with Field Marshal Asim Munir visiting the White House twice, and through its role mediating between the US and Iran. Experts quoted in the report say Democrats back ending the Iran war, so Pakistan could win new friends on Capitol Hill if it proves useful to that agenda.

They warn, however, that the relationship rests largely on personalities rather than institutions. The report says Pakistan's challenge is to build ties with the new Democratic majority while keeping its channel to Trump open. Analysts add that much will depend on how the Iran situation develops.
