Fire breaks out on hotel lawn from fireworks during Diwali celebration in Sukkur, goods burnt to ashes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fire broke out at the Pakistan Club Hotel on Sukkur's City Bypass during a Diwali celebration by the Hindu community, when fireworks set the lawn's furniture, marquees and other items ablaze. The fire spread quickly and engulfed the furniture, marquees and other goods placed on the hotel lawn, reducing them to ashes. As soon as information about the incident was received, Edhi ambulances and Rescue 1122 teams were called to the scene.

Rescue workers began operations to bring the fire under control. No final details about any loss of life or the extent of the financial loss had emerged yet at the time of reporting. The incident caused alarm among those attending the celebration, and rescue teams remained engaged in controlling the blaze.
