SME Financing Doubles to Rs1.1 Trillion, Says SBP Governor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Outstanding financing to small and medium enterprises has risen to approximately Rs1,110 billion in June 2026 from Rs491 billion in June 2024, State Bank Governor Jameel Ahmad said at the Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry. The number of SME borrowers has climbed to 325,000 over the same period. The governor announced that the collateral-free limit for SME loans has been raised from Rs10 million to Rs50 million.

He said the SME Connect Portal would become operational on November 1, allowing entrepreneurs to apply for financing online from home. The central bank also raised the Export Refinance Scheme limit from Rs1 trillion to Rs1.5 trillion, earmarking Rs300 billion for SME exporters. Business leaders welcomed the measures, saying easier credit is critical for job creation and industrial growth.
