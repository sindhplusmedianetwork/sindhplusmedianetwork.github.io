Initial Outline of New Provinces and Referendum Emerges for First Time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

TV anchor and journalist Shahzad Iqbal, citing informed sources, said new provinces will be created on an administrative basis and not on a linguistic basis, each province will have its own provincial assembly, chief minister and cabinet, and there is no proposal to give administrative powers to any powerful mayor or administrator in place of an elected government. Giving the news in his programme on Geo News, he said that even after PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto's opposition to the referendum, matters appear to be moving rapidly towards a referendum, as Bilawal Bhutto had given the government a message to refrain from holding a referendum and had also claimed to ring danger bells in case of a referendum. He said the news he was giving today makes it clear that despite Bilawal Bhutto's warning, the state and government have decided to hold a referendum in any case, which will happen very soon.

Informed sources on the matter presented the initial outline of the referendum and the creation of new provinces for the first time in an exclusive conversation, saying the referendum for new provinces will be held across the country very soon, the counting of referendum results will be separate at the provincial level while the decision will be by majority opinion, and the referendum will reveal in which province which districts or cities are in favour of new administrative provinces. The sources said new provinces will be created on an administrative basis and not a linguistic basis, these will not be mere administrative units but complete administrative provinces, every province will have its own provincial assembly, chief minister and cabinet, the chief minister will remain the chief executive of the province, and there is no proposal to give administrative powers to any powerful mayor or administrator in place of an elected government. They said the demand for new provinces is a popular demand in all four provinces and if voters of any province ratify the creation of new provinces, their mandate will gain political and moral weight.
