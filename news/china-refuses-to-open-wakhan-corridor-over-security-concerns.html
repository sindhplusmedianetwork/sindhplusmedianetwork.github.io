China Refuses to Open Wakhan Corridor Over Security Concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China has refused to open the Wakhan Corridor citing security concerns, dealing a blow to the Taliban regime, according to an Afghan journal. The report says terrorism threats emanating from Afghan soil under what it calls the illegitimate Taliban regime have affected the security of neighbouring countries. China refused to open its border with Afghanistan until security concerns are addressed, and did not attend the Wakhan road's opening ceremony nor formally recognise it at the diplomatic level.

Beijing remains concerned about border security and views the East Turkestan Islamic Movement as a threat to Xinjiang. Chinese international affairs expert Zhu Yongbiao was quoted as saying the chances of the Wakhan border crossing opening are extremely low, because for China the trade benefits with Afghanistan are small while security risks and costs are high.
