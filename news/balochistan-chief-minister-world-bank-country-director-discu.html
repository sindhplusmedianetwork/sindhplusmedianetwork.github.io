Balochistan Chief Minister, World Bank Country Director Discuss Wider Development Cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan Chief Minister Mir Sarfraz Bugti and World Bank Country Director for Pakistan Bolormaa Amgaabazar held a meeting in Islamabad to discuss expanding development cooperation, promoting economic stability and strengthening partnerships in key sectors across the province. The meeting focused on opportunities for enhanced collaboration in education, healthcare, water supply and infrastructure development, while also reviewing prospects for joint support of ongoing and future development projects in Balochistan. Balochistan Chief Secretary Shakeel Qadir Khan was also present during the discussions.

Amgaabazar expressed interest in deepening development cooperation and exploring greater collaboration across various sectors, and both sides exchanged views on strategies to accelerate socio-economic progress and improve public service delivery. Bugti underscored the provincial government's commitment to sustainable development, economic stability and the provision of essential services to citizens. He stressed that the development of Balochistan is not only critical for the province itself but also holds significant importance for Pakistan's broader economic growth and prosperity.

The chief minister said the provincial administration is pursuing a results-oriented development strategy based on transparency, effective monitoring and efficient utilisation of public resources, so that the benefits of development reach communities directly. He added that Balochistan welcomes meaningful partnerships with global institutions and is implementing policies designed to stimulate economic activity, generate employment and raise living standards.
