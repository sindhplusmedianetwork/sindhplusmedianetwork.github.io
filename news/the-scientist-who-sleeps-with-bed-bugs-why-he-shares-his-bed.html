The Scientist Who Sleeps With Bed Bugs: Why He Shares His Bed With 60,000 of Them

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A British biologist, Dr Richard Naylor from the Forest of Dean in Gloucestershire, sleeps with thousands of bed bugs for scientific research. He keeps around 60,000 bed bugs to test the effectiveness of products designed to protect against them around the world. Dr Naylor says he has been bitten millions of times over more than a decade.

Testing products requires a human presence because the insects are activated by human breath and body heat. His unusual experiment helps researchers judge how well protective products work against the insects. The work puts him in close contact with the bed bugs night after night.
