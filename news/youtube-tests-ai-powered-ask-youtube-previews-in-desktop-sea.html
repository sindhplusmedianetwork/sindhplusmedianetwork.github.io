YouTube Tests AI-Powered 'Ask YouTube' Previews in Desktop Search

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

YouTube is testing previews of its conversational "Ask YouTube" feature in desktop search results, showing AI-generated summaries before users click on a video. The limited trial is running in the United States among signed-in desktop users who search in English. For how-to and learning queries, YouTube may display an AI-generated overview at the top of the page with written guidance, key video segments and timestamps.

Users can click "Show more" for the full AI answer or scroll down for traditional video results. The experiment is part of YouTube's broader push into AI-assisted discovery. There is no word yet on a wider rollout to other countries.
