Manchester United appoint Pochettino as new manager

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Manchester United have named Mauricio Pochettino as their new manager following the dismissal of Michael Carrick. The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss will take charge on November 18. The club said it reached an agreement with the US Soccer Federation for Pochettino and his staff to be released after the United States' two matches against Haiti.

Pochettino led the US to the World Cup last 16 on home soil earlier this year. He inherits a side sitting in the relegation zone after their worst-ever start to a Premier League campaign. The appointment ends the search for a successor to Carrick as United look to turn their season around.
