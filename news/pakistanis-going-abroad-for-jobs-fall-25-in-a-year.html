Pakistanis going abroad for jobs fall 25% in a year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The number of Pakistanis going abroad for employment has fallen by 25 per cent in a year, according to data from the Bureau of Emigration received by Samaa. From January to October this year, 476,240 Pakistanis went abroad for employment, while 636,270 people had taken up employment abroad in the same period last year. The data also shows a continuous decline in the number of Pakistanis going abroad for work over the past three years.

Stakeholders fear that the decline in overseas employment opportunities could affect Pakistan's expected remittance target of $45 billion. According to overseas employment promoters, the UAE, Qatar and Oman are already closed to Pakistani workers, while the number of Pakistani workers in Saudi Arabia is also falling because of government policies and a complicated system. The stakeholders have urged the government to resolve the problems facing Pakistani workers through diplomatic contacts with Middle Eastern and European countries, while the Overseas Employment Promoters Association says its business is shrinking day by day as overseas job opportunities fall.
