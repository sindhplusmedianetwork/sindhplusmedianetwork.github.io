Turkiye Moves Saudi-Pakistan Defence Pact Toward Ratification

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkiye has submitted the Saudi-Pakistan defence pact, the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement signed in August, to its parliament for ratification. The move brings NATO's second-largest army closer to a formal mutual defence pledge with Riyadh amid Houthi attacks. The pact treats an armed attack on one member as an attack on all, similar to NATO's Article 5.

Parliament's foreign affairs commission could debate the matter as early as Friday. Erdogan's communications director described the step as a milestone towards institutionalising strategic cooperation.
