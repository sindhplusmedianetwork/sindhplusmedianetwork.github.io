Trump's Board of Peace faults Israel and Hamas for failure to end Gaza war

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Board of Peace overseeing US President Donald Trump's plan to end the Gaza war has accused both Israel and Hamas of failing to meet their commitments. In a 12-page report to the UN Security Council, the board warned that a ceasefire delivering nothing visible cannot be sustained indefinitely. The wider plan, under which Hamas would disarm, Israeli troops would withdraw and reconstruction would begin, has effectively stalled, the report says.

Near-daily Israeli strikes have continued, alongside continued Hamas attacks, and neither side has taken the steps the plan requires. Senior Hamas official Basem Naim dismissed the report as pure disinformation, rejecting its account of the situation. Israel's defence minister, meanwhile, said Israel would not withdraw until Hamas is dismantled.

The report underlines how little progress has been made since the ceasefire was agreed, and how far apart the two sides remain on the steps needed to end the war.
