Pakistan Squash Team Members Receive Rs4 Million Cash Awards for Asian Championship Gold

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Inter-Provincial Coordination and President of the Pakistan Sports Board Rana Sanaullah Khan distributed cash awards of Rs1 million each among four members of Pakistan's gold medal-winning men's squash team on Wednesday. The players honoured were Noor Zaman, Muhammad Asim Khan, Nasir Iqbal and Ashab Irfan, taking the total cash awards to Rs4 million. The award distribution ceremony was held at the Pakistan Sports Board in Islamabad to honour the national players for their outstanding performance in the 23rd Asian Team Squash Championships 2026, held at the Mushaf Squash Complex from November 3 to 7.

Pakistan secured the men's team gold medal after defeating South Korea 2-0 in the final, with Noor Zaman defeating Jeongmin Ryu 11-5, 11-7, 13-11 and Muhammad Asim Khan overcoming Seojin Oh 11-6, 11-5, 11-4 to seal the championship. On the occasion, Rana Sanaullah congratulated the players, coaches and management of the national team, describing their achievement as a matter of immense national pride, and said the government's recognition of outstanding athletes reflected its commitment to promote sports and support national talent. In the women's event, Hong Kong China defeated Malaysia 2-1 to win the gold medal, while South Korea claimed silver in the men's category.
