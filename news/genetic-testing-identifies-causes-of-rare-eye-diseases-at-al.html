Genetic testing identifies causes of rare eye diseases at Al-Shifa hospital

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Families who have spent years searching for answers to unexplained illnesses are finding renewed hope at Al-Shifa Trust Eye Hospital, where genetic testing has helped identify more than 50 genetic conditions in just 18 months. The Department of Ophthalmic Genetics has analysed 297 individuals from 225 families using specialised tests that examine DNA to identify the causes of inherited and other genetic conditions, many of which remain undiagnosed despite repeated medical investigations. Led by head of the department Dr Rutaba Gul, the facility has established a fully functional genetic testing laboratory supported by a specialist team.

It has received referrals from more than 30 doctors across multiple hospitals and cities, as well as different departments within Al-Shifa Trust Eye Hospital, reflecting growing demand for genetic diagnosis in complicated cases. The department is also developing a genetic database of patients and families to improve understanding of inherited conditions affecting the local population, including rare and unusual genetic findings that could help improve diagnoses and clinical decisions for future patients. Genetic testing can help doctors identify diseases when routine investigations fail, assess the risk of complications and guide treatment, and where appropriate it can support targeted therapies or personalised medication while reducing unnecessary investigations and costs.

These tests are particularly valuable for families affected by incurable genetic conditions, because even when no cure is available, identifying the underlying cause can help doctors explain the disease, assess potential complications and guide patients towards appropriate care.
