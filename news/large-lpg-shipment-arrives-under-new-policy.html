Large LPG shipment arrives under new policy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first major LPG shipment since the government's deregulation efforts, carrying 15,000 metric tonnes of fuel, berthed at Port Qasim, the government announced on Wednesday. SSGC LPG Limited imported the record cargo, the largest of its kind, which berthed at Engro Vopak Terminal. The shipment is the first major import under new LPG guidelines aimed at ensuring availability at affordable rates.

More shipments are expected in the coming weeks to ease shortages ahead of winter. The arrival is being seen as a step towards steadier LPG supply for consumers in the colder months.
