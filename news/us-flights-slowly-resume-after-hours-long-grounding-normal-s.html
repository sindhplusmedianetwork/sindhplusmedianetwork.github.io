US Flights Slowly Resume After Hours-Long Grounding, Normal Service to Take Time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Flights in the United States were resuming on Saturday after being grounded for several hours due to a major communications outage, though authorities said returning to normal operations would take time. Nearly 6,000 flights were cancelled, including some international routes, according to FlightAware. The outage disrupted travel across the country as aircraft were held on the ground for hours.

Officials said the failure was caused by decades-old technology. They stressed there was no evidence of hostile action behind the outage. Airlines and airports were working to clear the backlog as services gradually returned.
