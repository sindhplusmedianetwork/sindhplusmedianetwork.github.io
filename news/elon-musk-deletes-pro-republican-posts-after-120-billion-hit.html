Elon Musk deletes pro-Republican posts after $120 billion hit from midterm results

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Billionaire Elon Musk, who spent heavily backing Republican candidates in the US midterm elections, suffered a major blow as Democrats won. According to US media reports, the value of his companies Tesla and SpaceX fell by more than $120 billion after the results, while his personal wealth dropped by an estimated $30 billion. Tesla shares fell 3.3 percent and SpaceX shares 3.8 percent in the aftermath of the outcome.

The losses followed a campaign in which Musk had thrown his weight and money behind Republican candidates. After the results, Musk deleted some of his posts on X supporting the Republican Party, including one urging people to 'vote red today'. He also removed a pinned post appealing for votes for Republican candidates.

The deletions marked a quiet retreat from the public backing he had given the party during the campaign.
