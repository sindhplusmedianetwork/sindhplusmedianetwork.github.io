EU Pauses National Social Media Bans for Children Ahead of Bloc-Wide Law

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The European Union on Thursday moved to pause country-level social media bans for children until a bloc-wide law is in place. The European Commission said it does not want any more national bans before the pan-European legislation is approved, blocking draft rules put forward by Austria that sought to ban children under 14 from social media. The Commission decided to postpone for 12 months any draft national laws overlapping with future harmonised rules under the EU Kids Act.

Under that proposed law, the Commission has suggested banning under-13s from social media, with limited supervised access allowed until age 15. Exceptions apply to France and Greece, which notified Brussels before the bloc-wide legislation was put forward. The move signals Brussels' push for a single, consistent set of child-safety rules across the union.

Debate over children's online safety continues to grow across member states.
