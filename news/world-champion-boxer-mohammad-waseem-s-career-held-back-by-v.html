World Champion Boxer Mohammad Waseem's Career Held Back by Visa Delays

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two-time WBA bantamweight world gold champion Mohammad Waseem says persistent visa problems are holding back his career. The Quetta-born boxer told The Express Tribune he submitted his passport to the Spanish Embassy in July and is still waiting for a visa. Waseem has won two WBA gold titles in the past year, with both bouts held in Pakistan.

He says he has missed fight offers in Europe because of visa issues. He also missed a world title fight in Dubai earlier for the same reason. Waseem, a 2014 Commonwealth Games silver medallist, has again asked the federal government for help with visas and a diplomatic passport.

He is now aiming for a ranking fight in Europe before pursuing another world title.
