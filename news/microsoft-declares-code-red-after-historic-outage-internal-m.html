Microsoft declares 'Code Red' after historic outage, internal memos show

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Microsoft has declared a "Code Red" drive to overhaul how it prevents and responds to major service failures, after a 24-hour outage exposed shortcomings in its engineering systems and crisis response, according to internal memos reviewed by Reuters. The outage on November 12-13 hit governments from Australia and Japan to the United States, delayed trading firms and disrupted airline communications. The internal memos show that the company is now pushing a sweeping effort to fix the weaknesses the outage revealed in both its technology and its handling of major incidents.

The "Code Red" label signals that the work is being treated as a top priority inside the company. Microsoft's services are used by governments and businesses around the world, so the disruption affected a wide range of users. The company is now working to strengthen its systems so that a similar failure can be prevented or handled more quickly in future.
