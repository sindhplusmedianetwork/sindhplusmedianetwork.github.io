Pakistan Beat Oman to Win Hong Kong Sixes Plate Final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan beat Oman by 18 runs to win the Plate Final of the Hong Kong International Sixes tournament. The match was played at the Mission Road Ground in Mong Kok, where captain Abbas Afridi played a blistering unbeaten innings of 73 runs off just 16 balls to play a decisive role in the team's success. Batting first, Pakistan scored 154 runs for the loss of four wickets in the allotted six overs.

Afridi struck 73 with the help of 11 sixes and one four, while Abdul Samad made 32 off seven balls including four sixes and two fours, and Ahmed Daniyal scored an unbeaten 25 off five balls. In reply, Oman could only manage 136 for four in their six overs. Zohaib Amanat played an unbeaten 61 off 18 balls and Usman Ali made a fighting unbeaten 67 off 17, but the team fell 18 runs short of the target.

Abbas Afridi was named player of the match for his match-winning innings for Pakistan.
