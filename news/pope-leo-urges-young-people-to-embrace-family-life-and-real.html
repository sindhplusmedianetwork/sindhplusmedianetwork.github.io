Pope Leo Urges Young People to Embrace Family Life and Real Human Connection During Uruguay Visit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pope Leo XIV urged young people on Saturday to face the uncertainties of the modern world with courage, embrace real human connections and consider marriage and family life during the second day of his visit to Uruguay. Addressing 70,000 people at a prayer vigil at Montevideo's historic Estadio Centenario, the pontiff warned that virtual interactions cannot replace genuine human relationships. 'Nothing can replace real human contact,' he said, urging young people not to isolate themselves in the superficial world offered by screens and algorithms.

He encouraged them to look into one another's eyes, hold hands and share their struggles and joys face to face. The pope also expressed concern about growing secularisation and declining interest in religion, and encouraged young Uruguayans to have the courage to marry and start families rather than view children as a burden. During a meeting with academics and students at the Catholic University of Uruguay, he underlined the need for the responsible and wise use of artificial intelligence, putting the human person before algorithms.
