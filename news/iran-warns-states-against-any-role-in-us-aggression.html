Iran warns states against any role in US aggression

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's foreign ministry has warned that any direct or indirect involvement in aggression or 'economic terrorism' against Iran would be treated as a hostile and unlawful act amounting to participation in US aggression. The warning was issued as regional tensions continued to rise around the conflict between the United States, Israel and Iran. It came as Yemen's Houthis claimed drone and missile attacks on Saudi oil facilities, while denying that they had targeted Makkah.

Saudi civil defence had earlier said debris from two intercepted ballistic missiles fell in the Makkah region on Wednesday. Two oil tankers also hit sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz, adding to concerns over shipping in the waterway. The warning underlines how the conflict is drawing in states across the region.
