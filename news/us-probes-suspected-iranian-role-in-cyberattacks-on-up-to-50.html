US Probes Suspected Iranian Role in Cyberattacks on Up to 50 Ships

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has begun investigating Iran's possible role in cyberattacks on dozens of energy-sector ships, with as many as 50 vessels believed targeted. The affected ships carried oil, liquefied petroleum gas, chemicals and other explosive cargo. The attackers exploited a vulnerability in Fortinet routers widely used in the maritime industry.

The US Coast Guard has sent cyber protection teams aboard at least four ships. A Trump administration official said no evidence of Iranian involvement has been found yet. Russia is also being considered as a possible culprit.
