Envoys Briefed on Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 Ahead of Event

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ISLAMABAD: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs on Friday held a pre-event briefing for ambassadors and diplomats based here to familiarise them with the upcoming Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 (PMIF26). The session provided an overview of PMIF26, including its concept, major thematic sessions, programme outline, venue, registration process and participation opportunities for ministers, diplomats, international investors and industry leaders. The participants were also briefed on the planned exhibition and opportunities for government-to-government (G2G) and government-to-business (G2B) engagements.

The briefing aimed to build diplomatic and investor interest in the country's minerals sector ahead of the forum.
