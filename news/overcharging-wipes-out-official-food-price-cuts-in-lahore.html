Overcharging wipes out official food price cuts in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Consumers in Lahore continued to pay far more than government-notified prices for food last week, as a market survey found that official price cuts had failed to reach the markets. Traders charged above notified rates for vegetables, fruit and poultry, while some commodities were unavailable at official prices or missing from markets altogether. Tomatoes, whose official rate rose by Rs40 to Rs180-215 per kg, retailed at Rs300-350 per kg, and chicken meat sold for Rs600-680 per kg despite its official rate being cut by Rs29 to Rs570.

Garlic and ginger showed the same pattern, with local garlic officially fixed at Rs230-240 but selling at Rs300-320, and both Thai and Chinese ginger retailing at Rs400-500 per kg against official rates of around Rs315-355. Bitter gourd, pumpkin, green chillies and capsicum were also sold well above their notified rates, while prices of several vegetables and fruit rose during the week. The survey showed that even items whose official rates had come down continued to sell at substantially higher prices, leaving households with no relief.
