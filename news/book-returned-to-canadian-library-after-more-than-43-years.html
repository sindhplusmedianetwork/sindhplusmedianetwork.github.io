Book Returned to Canadian Library After More Than 43 Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A book has been returned to a Canadian library more than 43 years after it was due, leaving the staff at the Greater Victoria Public Library in British Columbia surprised. The returned book is Herman Wouk's long novel 'War and Remembrance', which also inspired a television series in the 1980s. Its old paper checkout card showed that the book was due back in 1983.

The library had abolished late-return fines in 2024, so no penalty was involved. However, the identity of the borrower and the reason behind the decades-long delay remain a mystery.
