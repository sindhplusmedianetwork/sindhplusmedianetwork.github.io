CM Maryam Gives Sanitation Contractors Seven Days to Shape Up

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has given the relevant authorities a seven-day deadline to implement the Suthra Punjab beat system across the province. Chairing a meeting to review the programme, she sought a comprehensive micro-plan for cleanliness and a province-wide progress report within a week. She ordered that sanitation companies delivering less than 80 per cent performance in any tehsil be issued warnings, while those scoring below 50 per cent should be removed.

The chief minister also ordered the termination of non-performing private companies and the activation of government-run sanitation services within a week. Emphasising the rights of sanitation workers, she directed officials to ensure payment of their outstanding dues, agreeing that the arrears should be recovered from the defaulting companies. She said workers should not be made to suffer for contractors' unpaid liabilities and called for a legal mechanism to ensure timely payment of salaries.

She further directed that rural areas be cleaned at least every alternate day and accumulated garbage removed from villages.
