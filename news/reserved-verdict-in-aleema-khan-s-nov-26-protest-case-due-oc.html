Reserved Verdict in Aleema Khan's Nov 26 Protest Case Due October 26, Says Muneeb Farooq

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Television anchor and analyst Muneeb Farooq has claimed that the reserved verdict in the November 26 protest case against Aleema Khan is scheduled to be announced on October 26. Speaking in his vlog, Farooq said that, as things stand, the situation does not look favourable for Aleema Khan, the sister of PTI founder Imran Khan. He suggested that an adverse verdict could add to her legal difficulties in the coming days.

The case was registered over the PTI's protest at Islamabad's D-Chowk, during which demonstrators were accused of vandalism, attacking security personnel and damaging state property. Cases were filed under anti-terrorism and other stringent provisions, and Aleema Khan was detained along with other leaders at the Kohsar and Secretariat police stations. She was later released after courts granted bail, while hearings in the November 26 case have now concluded and the judgment was reserved.

Legal experts quoted in the report said a verdict against her on October 26 would bring fresh legal complications.
