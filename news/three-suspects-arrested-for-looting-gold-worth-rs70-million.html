Three Suspects Arrested for Looting Gold Worth Rs70 Million in Sambrial

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Police has arrested three suspects wanted in the case of looting gold worth millions of rupees from a jeweller's house in Sambrial. According to the Punjab Police spokesperson, on November 6 armed persons looted more than 2 kilograms of gold worth Rs70 million from a jeweller's house in Sambrial. On receiving the report on 15, police set up a cordon, upon which the suspects, fearing arrest, threw the stolen gold in the fields and fled; police recovered all the gold the same day and handed it over to the jeweller.

DPO Captain (retd) Dost Muhammad had formed special teams under DSP Sambrial Sajjad Ahmed Bajwa for the arrests. Punjab Police said all three suspects were arrested with the help of modern technology and professional expertise while further investigation is under way, and IG Punjab Abdul Karim appreciated the Sialkot police for the arrests.
