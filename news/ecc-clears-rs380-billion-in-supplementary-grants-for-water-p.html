ECC Clears Rs380 Billion in Supplementary Grants for Water Projects and Armed Forces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Economic Coordination Committee has approved supplementary grants worth about Rs380 billion, including Rs275 billion for five major water projects facing financial constraints and around Rs84 billion for the armed forces. The water funds will go to the Diamer-Bhasha Dam, Mohmand Dam, the Greater Karachi Water Supply project known as K-IV, the Chashma Right Bank Canal and the Kacchi Canal. The committee also approved Rs3.71 billion for the Frontier Corps Balochistan (North).

In addition, the ECC approved the transfer of Rs15.625 billion in austerity savings to the Prime Minister's Austerity Fund. The grants are meant to keep the water projects moving despite their funding shortfalls.
