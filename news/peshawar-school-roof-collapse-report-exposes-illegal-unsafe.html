Peshawar School Roof Collapse: Report Exposes Illegal, Unsafe Building Construction

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A detailed inquiry report on the Oct 15 roof collapse at a private school in Peshawar's Dera Colony, which killed dozens of children, has exposed shocking lapses in the building's construction. The Capital Metropolitan Government Eastern Zone prepared the report and sent it to the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government and the governor, stating that the rear portion of a classroom had collapsed. According to the report, the building's plan was not even present in the Capital Metropolitan Government's records, and the structure was built in 2011 under the jurisdiction of the Tehsil Municipal Administration Town-4.

The building was not originally constructed as a school at all — it was a residential building with a ground and first floor. The report says the ground floor walls were made of cement bricks with an iron-girder tile roof, while the first floor walls had no plaster and its roof was made of bricks laid on wooden planks — a temporary, crude construction wholly unfit for a school. The crude first-floor roof could not bear the weight of the children and furniture, causing the tragedy; because the building looked safe from the outside, the planning control inspector never reported it, and all responsibility for regulating, supervising and monitoring schools rests with the Private Schools Regulatory Authority.
