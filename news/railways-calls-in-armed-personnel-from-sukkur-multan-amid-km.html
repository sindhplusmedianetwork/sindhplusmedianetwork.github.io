Railways Calls In Armed Personnel From Sukkur, Multan Amid KMC Standoff

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Railways has called in additional armed personnel from Sukkur and Multan to protect its assets in Karachi, in a sharp escalation of its standoff with the Karachi Metropolitan Corporation. Letters sent to senior railways police officials in the two cities asked them to release personnel 'along with arms and ammunition' for deployment in Karachi, where the department already has more than 500 personnel. SP Railways Police Karachi Khurram Khursheed confirmed the move, calling it a 'precautionary measure' after 'recent incidents', including an attack on a railway police station and the dumping of garbage outside railway offices.

The dispute centres on the KMC's Rs780 million road project linking Natha Khan Bridge with Pehlwan Goth, with both sides claiming ownership of the land. The two institutions have already lodged cases against each other, accusing one another of offences including terrorism and kidnapping. The railways stopped construction of the dual carriageway on Sunday, but the KMC says work resumed the next day and aims to complete it by the end of the month.

Talks between senior officials of both sides are continuing, but the issue remains unresolved.
