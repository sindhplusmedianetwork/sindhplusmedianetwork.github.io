Seven Killed, Including Two Children, as Jeep Falls Into Ravine in Muzaffarabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Seven people, including two children, were killed when a passenger jeep fell into a deep ravine in Muzaffarabad. According to rescue officials, the jeep was travelling from the Kundar area to a nearby village when it fell into the ravine at Shakarpattian. Rescue workers and local residents recovered four dead bodies from the site and shifted three injured people to hospital.

The three injured people also died during treatment at the hospital. Rescue officials said the jeep was also loaded with grass at the time of the accident. They added that the accident may have been caused by a defective road and the vehicle not being in proper working condition.
