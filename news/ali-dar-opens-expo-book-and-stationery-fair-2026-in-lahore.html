Ali Dar Opens Expo Book and Stationery Fair 2026 in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Advisor to the Punjab Chief Minister on Artificial Intelligence, International Cooperation and Special Initiatives Ali Dar attended the Expo Book and Stationery Fair 2026 at Lahore Expo Centre on Friday as chief guest and formally inaugurated the exhibition. Speaking on the occasion, Dar said the educational initiatives and reforms introduced under the leadership of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif were unprecedented, adding that historic measures were being taken to promote education and provide better opportunities to youth across the province. He highlighted the CM Punjab Honhaar Scholarship Program, Laptop Scheme, Khud Mukhtar Program and Hunarmand Naujawan, describing them as significant investments in the future of the younger generation.

Dar said empowering youth through education, modern skills and better opportunities was among the key priorities of the Punjab government. He emphasised that no nation could achieve sustainable progress and prosperity without promoting education and knowledge. Describing books as a person's best friend, he said they were among the most effective sources of knowledge, awareness and intellectual development.

He observed that nations embracing books, knowledge and research were better positioned to achieve progress and leadership at the global level, and said reading books helped develop critical thinking, understanding and creativity among young people. The advisor appreciated initiatives aimed at promoting books and reading habits, stressing their importance in developing an informed, educated and progressive society.
