Jinnah Institute of Cardiology Begins Cardiac Surgery With First Successful Complex Heart Operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jinnah Institute of Cardiology in Lahore has crossed another important milestone by formally beginning complex heart surgery, successfully completing its first complex aortic valve replacement operation, with which a regular series of cardiac surgeries has started at the institute. Renowned cardiac surgeon and Dean of Jinnah Institute of Cardiology Professor Hasnat performed the important operation along with his expert team, and the first successful surgery is being described as a major advancement in the institute's history. On this important achievement a celebration was held at Jinnah Institute of Cardiology where doctors and medical staff cut a cake to celebrate the success, attended by Chairman Board of Management Professor Farqad Alamgir, Dean Professor Hasnat, surgeons and other medical staff.

Chairman Board of Management Professor Farqad Alamgir congratulated Professor Hasnat and his entire team on the first successful cardiac surgery and said it is a major achievement and hoped the team would take the series forward with the same hard work and spirit. Speaking specially to Roznama Pakistan, Dean Professor Hasnat said cardiac surgery has formally begun at the institute and the complex aortic valve replacement operation was successfully completed as the first surgery, which will pave the way for more complex heart operations in future. He said our goal is not limited to traditional surgery only but to also introduce the latest treatment facilities at Jinnah Institute.

Professor Hasnat revealed that the institute is now moving towards artificial heart implantation and, in accordance with the directions and vision of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, a target has been set to start the artificial heart programme in the next two to three months, for which contacts are continuing with America, China and other countries for the supply of artificial hearts and acquisition of modern technology. He said work on this project is in its final stages and, God willing, a new and modern facility will be provided to the people of Punjab in the treatment of heart diseases, while the institute's echocardiography team has also completed more than one thousand echocardiography and TEE tests and the electrophysiology team has also shown notable performance.
