Saudi-Pakistan Business Council delegation visits Karachi Port

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A delegation of the Saudi-Pakistan Joint Business Council has visited Karachi Port to explore investment in the maritime sector. The delegation was led by the council's chairman, Prince Mansour bin Mohammed Al Saud. The visitors went to the headquarters of the Karachi Port Trust and to the South Asia Pakistan Terminals.

They showed keen interest in the Green Port project and in the expansion of terminal infrastructure. The proposed 140-acre Maritime Business District and the development plans for the Manora shipyard were also discussed..
