Light snowfall brings winter chill to Kaghan Valley

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The weather has turned cold again in the mountainous areas of the upper Kaghan Valley following light snowfall, intensifying the winter chill across the region. The snowfall has covered the mountain peaks with a white blanket, further enhancing the beauty of the natural landscape. Temperatures have dropped following the snowfall, accompanied by cold winds.

The picturesque views of lakes, snow-covered mountains and green valleys have added to the area's natural beauty, once again drawing the attention of tourists. Babusar Top has received around three inches of snowfall, while up to two inches have been recorded at Lulusar Lake and other locations. The sudden change in weather has prompted local residents to start wearing warm clothing, while temperatures in the upper areas have also dropped for tourists.

The arrival of winter has become increasingly evident in the naturally beautiful Kaghan Valley.
