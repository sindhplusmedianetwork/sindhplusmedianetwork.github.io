Keenjhar Lake Pollution Reports Are Unverified, Says Sindh Irrigation Dept After Lab Tests

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Irrigation Department has dismissed reports about pollution in Keenjhar Lake's water as unverified and contrary to facts, releasing laboratory test reports to back its claim. Executive Engineer Kalri Baghar Division Thatta, Muzammil Memon, said the water quality of the lake — a key source of drinking water for Karachi — is monitored regularly, and that the water of the KJ canal feeding Karachi is tested daily. He added that the lake's main inflow point, Lake Regulator RD 2.4, is fully open.

According to the laboratory report, the water at the lake's central point recorded a pH of 8.3 and total dissolved solids (TDS) of 350 milligrams per litre, while the downstream point of the KJ canal recorded a pH of 8.4. Memon said the chemical ingredients tested in the water were all within the limits set by the World Health Organization, so the lab reports do not confirm the pollution claims circulating online. The department urged the public to avoid unverified news and rumours and to rely on authentic official sources for information on water quality, adding that continuous monitoring of the lake and Karachi's water supply system would go on.
