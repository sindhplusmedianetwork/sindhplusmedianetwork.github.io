Two-Year-Old Stabbed to Death in Faisalabad Over Marriage Grudge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A two-year-old boy was killed in Sahianwala, Faisalabad, after an accused attacked him with a knife. According to police, the accused took two young brothers from their neighbourhood the previous day on the pretext of buying them treats, and later took them to a canal, where he stabbed two-year-old Zohan. The severely injured child died at the Tehsil Headquarters Hospital in Chak Jhumra, while five-year-old Rohan managed to escape and save his life.

Police said the accused wanted to marry the victim's mother and held a grudge after she married someone else. Raids are being carried out to arrest the accused, who remains at large.
