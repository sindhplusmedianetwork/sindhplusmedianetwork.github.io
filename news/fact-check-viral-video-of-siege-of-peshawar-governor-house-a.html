Fact-Check: Viral Video of 'Siege' of Peshawar Governor House Actually From Nepal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A video doing the rounds on social media claims that supporters of Imran Khan have besieged the Peshawar Governor House after governor's rule and emergency were imposed in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Fact-checking has established that the video is not from Pakistan at all — it is from the protests that took place in Nepal in September 2025. A reverse image search showed the same video was posted on YouTube on September 9, 2025.

The footage shows protesters outside the Birendra International Convention Centre in Kathmandu, not any building in Peshawar. The spokesperson for the KP Governor House confirmed that the building visible in the video is not Peshawar's Governor House. The AFP news agency had also clarified on October 14, 2026, that the video came from Nepal's old protests and had nothing to do with Pakistan.
