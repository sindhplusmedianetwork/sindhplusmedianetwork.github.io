Anthropic to begin trading after record-breaking market debut tops SpaceX record

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Anthropic will begin trading on Thursday on the Nasdaq exchange after pricing its initial public offering at $83 a share, above its expected range and toppling SpaceX's IPO to set a new record. Hot demand for the AI giant brushed aside market jitters over the spy scandal that toppled Nvidia's chief executive, despite lingering concerns of an investment bubble. The company said on Wednesday that it offered 956 million shares for sale, which gives the maker of the Claude chatbot a valuation of $1.5 trillion, including employee stock options and restricted shares.

Its listing will give ordinary investors their first chance to bet directly on a stand-alone AI giant when it begins trading under the ticker ANT. The offering breaks the record $75 billion that Elon Musk's SpaceX raised in June at a valuation of $1.77 trillion. Anthropic raised $79.4 billion, which could increase to as much as $89.6 billion if its underwriters likewise buy additional shares.

The strong demand comes even as AI stocks have swung sharply, with investors questioning whether heavy spending on the technology will pay off. Earlier estimates had put Anthropic's valuation as high as $2 trillion, but they fell as AI stocks slid on Wall Street in recent weeks. The five-year-old company's revenue has surged, reaching $17 billion in the third quarter, up from $1.4 billion a year earlier, with much of it coming from customers paying to use its AI models for computer coding.
