Chevening and Allied Bank Launch Fully Funded UK Master's Scholarships for Pakistanis

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The UK government's Chevening programme and Allied Bank Limited have announced a partnership to provide fully funded master's scholarships to Pakistani students at leading British universities. Two scholars will be funded each year over three academic years, beginning in 2027-28. The scholarships will cover subjects including artificial intelligence, technology, cybersecurity, digital transformation, economics and financial inclusion.

The first two scholars will be selected from the existing 2027-28 applicant pool, officials said. Applications for the 2028-29 cycle will open with the Chevening cycle in August 2027. The partnership was announced at a ceremony attended by British High Commission and Allied Bank officials.
