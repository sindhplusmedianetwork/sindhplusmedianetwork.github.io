Russell Brand's Wife Calls Him 'Best Father' in Testimony at Rape Trial

Laura Brand has testified at London's Southwark Crown Court in defence of her husband Russell Brand, calling him caring, kind and the best father to their three children. She told the court there is love and trust between them, saying she met him at the 2006 Edinburgh festival, that they separated and reunited in 2015, and married in 2017. She acknowledged his past infidelities.

Brand denies all seven sex-offence charges dating from 1999 to 2009, and statements from two of his former girlfriends were also presented in court. The trial continues, with jury deliberations expected to begin on Tuesday.
