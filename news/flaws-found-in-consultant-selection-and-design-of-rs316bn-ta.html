Flaws found in consultant selection and design of Rs316bn Tarbela expansion project, documents reveal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Official documents have shown that flaws were found in the way WAPDA selected consultants for the Tarbela fifth extension project and in the project's design. The Central Development Working Party and the Executive Committee of the National Economic Council had directed a review of those flaws in the Rs316 billion project. The two bodies had also ordered the formation of a committee headed by the federal minister for water resources, but the committee has still not been formed.

The disclosure came after the safety barrier of the Tarbela T5 hydropower project was breached on Monday, allowing a large quantity of water to enter tunnel five. Construction work at the project has been suspended indefinitely following the incident. WAPDA is carrying out the project to convert the fifth irrigation tunnel of Tarbela Dam for power generation.

Once completed, the project is expected to add 1,410 megawatts of electricity generation capacity.
