Microsoft Exits UK Data Center Deal With Nscale Over Power Delays

Microsoft has pulled out of a data center deal with British cloud provider Nscale, Britain's Telegraph newspaper reported on Sunday. Microsoft, one of Nscale's two largest customers, recently terminated its agreement for the data center in Loughton, Essex. The Telegraph did not specify why Microsoft withdrew, and Microsoft declined a Reuters request for comment.

The report said Nscale had faced repeated delays with the project, and national power grid officials told Nscale they did not expect a power supply for the data center until the 2030s. An Nscale spokesperson told Reuters: "Our relationship with Microsoft remains strong and long-term, and this unfortunate London-wide grid issue has no impact on our other contracts." Nscale said it remains committed to the Loughton project and the UK, and is exploring alternative power sources plus other expansion sites in the country. Nscale filed for a US IPO last month, with Microsoft and Anthropic expected to be its major customers, according to its prospectus.
