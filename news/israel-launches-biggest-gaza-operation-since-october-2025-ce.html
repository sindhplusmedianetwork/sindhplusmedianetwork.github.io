Israel launches biggest Gaza operation since October 2025 ceasefire

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli forces have launched a major military operation in the central Gaza Strip, the largest since the October 2025 ceasefire, according to Arab media. Dozens of Israeli tanks and drones are patrolling the central areas of Gaza as part of the operation. The Israeli army has destroyed several buildings and homes in central Gaza with explosions.

Israeli tanks and drones have shelled densely populated areas, according to the reports. Residents of three blocks in the Al-Bureij and Al-Nuseirat areas have been told to evacuate the area. The operation marks the biggest Israeli military action in Gaza since the ceasefire came into effect in October 2025.
