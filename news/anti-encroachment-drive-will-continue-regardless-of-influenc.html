Anti-encroachment drive will continue regardless of influence: Talal Chaudhry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry on Saturday said the capital's anti-encroachment campaign would continue regardless of political influence, questioning the legality of a security guard cabin and generator at the residence of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman. The remarks came a day after the Capital Development Authority issued an encroachment notice at Fazl's residence, directing the removal of illegal constructions within 15 days. Speaking in a televised address, Chaudhry said there would be no encroachments of any kind and zero tolerance, adding that the campaign had been running for more than a year and a half and that notices had gone out in every other street of Islamabad.

He described the four-by-four guard cabin as a cage-like structure in which a guard sits in summer and winter, outside a house large enough to park some ten cars worth millions, and said it was highly inappropriate to expect such illegal construction to be protected. He rejected suggestions of oppression, saying action had been taken against everyone in Islamabad, rich or poor, powerful or not, and cited Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's demolition of a police station on government land a day earlier, the FC's clearance from a park it had occupied for decades, and the removal of encroaching walls of five-star hotels. Chaudhry also said illegal structures belonging to diplomats, bureaucrats and businessmen had been removed from green areas with fines imposed.

He called on Fazl to answer his questions directly, reiterating whether keeping a generator on the road and guard rooms on the green belt was legal.
