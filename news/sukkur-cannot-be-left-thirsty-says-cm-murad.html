'Sukkur Cannot Be Left Thirsty,' Says CM Murad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has declared that a city on the banks of the Indus cannot be left thirsty. He directed officials to fast-track plans for a modern water supply system for Sukkur, with a treatment capacity of 40 to 50 million gallons a day. The chief minister issued the direction while chairing a meeting at the Chief Minister's House on Sunday.

He noted that Sukkur's population had increased by about 68 per cent since 1998. The planned system is aimed at meeting the water needs of the growing city. The chief minister said the supply of clean drinking water to Sukkur was a priority.
