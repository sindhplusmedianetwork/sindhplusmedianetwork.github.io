Man sentenced to 48 years for attack on Chinese nationals' vehicle in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-terrorism court in Karachi has sentenced alleged banned BLA member Dad Bakhsh to a total of 48 years in prison and fined him Rs1.35 million for the attack on a vehicle carrying Chinese nationals. Judge Aminuddin Khaskheli of Anti-Terrorism Court No. 4 announced the verdict at the Central Jail's anti-terrorism complex.

The prosecution said the attackers had opened fire on the Chinese nationals' vehicle in the Gulbai area, injuring two Chinese citizens. The accused also faced involvement in a university bombing, according to the prosecution. Three co-accused in the case were declared absconders.
