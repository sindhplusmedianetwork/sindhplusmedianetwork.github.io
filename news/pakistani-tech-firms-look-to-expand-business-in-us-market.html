Pakistani Tech Firms Look to Expand Business in US Market

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani technology companies are seeking to expand their business with American clients by offering specialised services and products, as changing United States immigration policies complicate the movement of foreign professionals. The opportunities were discussed at Pakistan-US Tech Connect 2026, a day-long business networking event hosted by the Pakistani embassy in Washington on Wednesday. Sixteen Pakistani technology companies met American business representatives and industry experts to explore partnerships and build lasting commercial relationships.

Organised with the Ministry of Information Technology and Telecommunication, the Pakistan Software Export Board and OPEN Global, the event featured companies working in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, data analytics, financial technology and healthcare technology. Participants stressed that Pakistani firms need to move beyond routine IT services and cost-based competition, and instead develop proprietary intellectual property, scalable products and specialised expertise. Senior vice-president at Oracle Sofyan Yusufi emphasised understanding American business culture, strengthening technical skills and building diverse teams, noting that an exclusively male team could signal a limited understanding of the US market.

PSEB Chief Executive Faisal Jeddy said North America is critical to Pakistan's IT industry, with 80 percent of the $4.6 billion in IT exports cited at the event coming from the region.
