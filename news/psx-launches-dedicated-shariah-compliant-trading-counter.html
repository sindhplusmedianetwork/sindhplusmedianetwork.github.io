PSX Launches Dedicated Shariah-Compliant Trading Counter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange has launched a dedicated Shariah-Compliant Trading Counter after the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan approved the Shariah Trading Counter Regulations. Thirty-nine brokers have been onboarded, and a gong ceremony was attended by SECP, State Bank, CDC and Shariah scholars. Trading on the counter is limited to Shariah-compliant equities, Government Ijarah Sukuk and Corporate Sukuk, while interest-based leverage, short selling and derivatives are blocked, with Murabaha Share Financing offered as the alternative.

MD and CEO Farrukh Sabzwari said investor accounts have crossed 650,000, driven largely by Millennials and Gen Z.
