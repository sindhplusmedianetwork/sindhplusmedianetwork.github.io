Government Allocates Rs300 Billion for SME Exporters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has decided to provide Rs300 billion in financing facilities for small and medium-sized enterprises in financial year 2026-27. The facilities will be provided under the Export Finance Scheme, administered by Pakistan EXIM Bank. The move aims to support SMEs and promote export-led growth.

Access to affordable working capital remains a major challenge for small exporters. The financing is meant to give small exporters access to affordable working capital. The scheme is part of the government's effort to strengthen the export sector.
