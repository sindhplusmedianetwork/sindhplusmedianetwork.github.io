Mystery Deaths of Four Youths Raise Water Safety Fears in Islamabad's Outskirts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four young people have died within a month in villages on the outskirts of Islamabad, raising fears of contaminated drinking water. Some of the deaths have been linked to liver failure, alarming local residents. Villagers have urged the federal health minister to order an immediate investigation into the deaths.

Residents are demanding laboratory testing of water samples from the affected areas. Health officials have not yet released an official statement on the cause. The cluster of deaths has deepened anxiety about water safety in the capital's rural fringe.
