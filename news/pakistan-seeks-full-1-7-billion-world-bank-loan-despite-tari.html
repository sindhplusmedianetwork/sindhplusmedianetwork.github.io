Pakistan seeks full $1.7 billion World Bank loan despite tariff breaches

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan plans to draw the full $1.7 billion multi-tranche World Bank loan to widen the tax base and align expenditure with the Constitution. The move comes even though the lender has twice rated implementation on the approved $600 million tranche as moderately satisfactory. The government breached the National Tariff Policy condition twice, in June and again last month when the ECC approved additional customs duty to protect a tyre manufacturer.

The $1.7 billion package, under the Public Resource for Inclusive Development programme, will benefit the federal and all four provincial governments. The loan is aimed at broadening the tax base and bringing public spending in line with constitutional requirements.
