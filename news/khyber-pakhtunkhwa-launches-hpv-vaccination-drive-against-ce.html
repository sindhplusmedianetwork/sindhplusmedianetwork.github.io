Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Launches HPV Vaccination Drive Against Cervical Cancer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A human papillomavirus immunisation drive begins across Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa on Wednesday and will run until November 24. The drive aims to protect adolescent girls against HPV, the main cause of cervical cancer. Health authorities urged parents to ensure that daughters aged 9 to 14 receive the vaccine.

They described the vaccine as safe and effective. Parents seeking information can contact the Sehat Tahaffuz helpline at 1166. The drive is part of efforts to protect young girls from a preventable cause of cervical cancer.
