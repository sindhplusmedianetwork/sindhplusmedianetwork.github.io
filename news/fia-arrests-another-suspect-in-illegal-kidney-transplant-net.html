FIA arrests another suspect in illegal kidney transplant network

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency's Islamabad Zone has arrested another suspect allegedly involved in an illegal kidney transplantation network. During a raid on Sunday, an FIA team arrested Noorul Wahab, who had worked as a phlebotomist at eShifa Labs in Takht Bhai. Officials said a case (60/2026) had already been registered in connection with the network, and several suspects, including its alleged ringleaders, had been arrested.

The suspect allegedly prepared fake medical prescriptions in the names of various doctors for patients seeking kidney transplants, in connivance with other nominated accused. Officials said further investigations were under way to identify and apprehend other members of the network.
