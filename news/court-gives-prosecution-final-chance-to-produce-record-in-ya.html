Court Gives Prosecution Final Chance to Produce Record in Yasmin Rashid Bail Plea

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-terrorism court in Lahore hearing Dr Yasmin Rashid's post-arrest bail plea gave the prosecution a final opportunity to produce the case record. The court sought the case record from the prosecution. It said the bail application would be decided on merit if the record was not produced.

The court kept the bail plea pending. It also sought arguments from Dr Yasmin Rashid's lawyer Rana Mudassar Umar. Anti-Terrorism Court Judge Manzar Ali Gul heard the case.
