Karachi Suspect Beaten by Women With Slippers During Court Appearance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man accused of stealing more than 50 mobile phones from women after luring them to a farmhouse in Gadap under the pretext of job interviews was beaten by some of the affected women during his court appearance in Karachi on Tuesday. The suspect had been arrested by police on Monday for his alleged involvement in stealing the mobile phones of 59 women, who were brought to the farmhouse in October under the pretext of a job interview. He was presented before the Malir judicial magistrate on Tuesday, where the investigating officer said mobile phones had been recovered from his possession, more phones still needed to be recovered, and his accomplices were at large.

The court granted police one-day physical remand of the suspect. A number of the affected women were present at the premises, and in a video seen by Dawn they could be seen asking police to show the suspect's face, gathering around him, and then beating him with slippers and slapping him. As the suspect tried to move away, the group of women could be seen chasing him until police took him inside a room.

A day earlier, Malir Senior Superintendent of Police Dr Abdul Khaliq Pirzada said police had recovered 40 mobile phones from the suspect's possession, and that he was arrested while on his way to sell 40 mobile phones in an attempt to destroy evidence. The SSP said police had obtained clues suggesting the remaining 19 mobile phones were in the possession of another absconding suspect, and that the main suspect, allegedly with a female suspect's help, had created a WhatsApp group and placed job advertisements to lure the women before hiring a bus to take them to the farmhouse.
