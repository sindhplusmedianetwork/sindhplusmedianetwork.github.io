Marriage ceremony of 13-year-old girl stopped in Lahore, groom among six arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Liaquatabad police in Lahore acted on information received on the emergency helpline 15 and stopped the marriage ceremony of a 13-year-old girl, arresting six people including the groom. Police said the rukhsati ceremony of the underage girl was under way at Madina Marriage Hall in Kashmiri Mohalla. During the operation, the groom, Sabt-ul-Hassan, and the girl's father, Sabtain, were arrested, along with four other people who were present at the ceremony.

Initial investigation found that the girl's nikah had been solemnised on 4 October, while the rukhsati ceremony was being held on 8 November. Police registered case number 2533/26 at Liaquatabad police station and started further investigation. DIG Operations Faisal Kamran said child marriage is a crime under the law and no compromise can be made on the protection of children, adding that indiscriminate legal action will be ensured against those involved and that protecting children's rights is the top priority of Lahore Police.
