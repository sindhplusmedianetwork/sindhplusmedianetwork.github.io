LPG Price in Karachi Jumps Rs150 per kg in Two Days to Rs600

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The price of liquefied petroleum gas in Karachi has shot up to Rs600 per kg from Rs550 within just two days. The market price is now far above OGRA's notified October rate of Rs279.34 per kg. Rawalpindi and Islamabad are also seeing LPG at Rs600 per kg, with an 11kg cylinder crossing Rs6,500.

LPG Distributors Association chairman Irfan Khokhar accused OGRA of being a silent spectator to what he called billions of rupees of profiteering. He said 70 per cent of the country's LPG is imported while 30 per cent is produced locally. With winter approaching, dealers warn that rising demand could push prices even higher.
