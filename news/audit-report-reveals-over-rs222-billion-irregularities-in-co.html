Audit Report Reveals Over Rs222 Billion Irregularities in Communications Ministry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An audit of the Communications Ministry for fiscal year 2022-23 has revealed financial irregularities exceeding Rs222 billion. The report documented 40 cases of fraud, theft and dacoity involving Rs48.8 million. Recruitment-related cases showed irregularities of Rs2.73 billion, including 4,252 appointments in grades 1 to 15 made in Pakistan Postal Services during the caretaker period.

Of these, 314 were hired on political grounds in violation of the rules, the audit found. The report also pointed to losses of over Rs3.26 billion in five procurement cases. It further flagged Rs20.85 billion in unauthorised administrative dealings with a commercial bank.

The findings raise fresh questions about financial management in the ministry.
