Diwali Lamp Kindles the Flame of Love, Says CM Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has congratulated the Hindu community on the festival of Diwali. In her message, she paid tribute to the Hindu community for its active role in the country's progress and prosperity. She reaffirmed the commitment to follow the vision of Quaid-e-Azam on the freedom and rights of minorities.

'The lamp of Diwali kindles the flame of love,' she said, adding that the government is not only a protector of minorities' rights but is determined to give them their due share in every field. She said Pakistan is a beautiful bouquet in which people of different religions live together in brotherhood, and Punjab is moving fast towards a society where love and tolerance become its identity. She said minorities are Pakistan's beautiful colours and their colours will never be allowed to fade.

She also noted that financial assistance is being provided to minorities through the Special Minority Card in Punjab, and Minority Virtual Police Stations have been set up under the Safe Punjab vision. Punjab also has the distinction of approving the first Sikh Marriage Act, she said.
