Why Do People Talk in Their Sleep? Experts Explain the Causes

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read

Many people talk or utter incoherent words during sleep without realising what they are saying. Sometimes this mumbling can even sound like clear sentences, which may be difficult for those nearby to understand. Medically, talking during sleep is called somniloquy, and it is among the sleep-related conditions known as parasomnia — a category that can include talking, walking, or unusual movements during sleep.

During sleep, a person passes through different stages, including deep sleep and REM sleep, and brain activity keeps changing; some people can talk in any stage. The words spoken in sleep do not necessarily mean the person is thinking about a particular subject or experiencing that topic in a dream. According to experts, the tendency to talk in sleep can run in some families, and conditions such as mental stress, traumatic experiences, anxiety, or depression can also be linked to it.

However, the cause of the habit is not the same for every person, and occasionally talking in sleep is not a sign of any serious problem. Sometimes the words spoken at night may seem to resemble the day's activities or thoughts, and a person may suddenly utter a sentence while waking up — but it cannot be concluded that they have necessarily found an answer to some problem. Experts say that if sleep talking happens repeatedly, starts affecting sleep, occurs with severe shouting or unusual movements, or is accompanied by unusual daytime drowsiness, seeking medical advice is appropriate.
