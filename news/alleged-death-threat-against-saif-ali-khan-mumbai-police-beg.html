Alleged death threat against Saif Ali Khan, Mumbai Police begin investigation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian actor Saif Ali Khan has allegedly received a death threat. Mumbai Police have begun investigating the matter after the alleged threat. Police suspect the threat may have come from Zeeshan Akhtar, a wanted accused in the Baba Siddique murder case.

Investigators are still determining whether he is actually behind it. The Mumbai Crime Branch arrested a 30-year-old man from Bengaluru during the investigation into threats to actors. Police said no evidence of his link to the Lawrence Bishnoi gang has been found so far.
