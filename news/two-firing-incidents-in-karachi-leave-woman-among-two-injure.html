Two Firing Incidents in Karachi Leave Woman Among Two Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two firing incidents in different areas of Karachi left two people, including a woman, injured on Tuesday; both were shifted to hospital, according to Chhipa officials. In Gulistan-e-Johar Block 11 near Manawar Chowrangi, 17-year-old Sufiyan was injured and taken to Jinnah Hospital. In Gulshan-e-Iqbal Block 4's Quaid-e-Azam Colony, 40-year-old Rubina was injured in a mysterious firing incident inside a house.

Further details of both incidents were not immediately available, and police said inquiries were underway to determine the circumstances of each shooting. Officials said both victims were reported to be in stable condition.
