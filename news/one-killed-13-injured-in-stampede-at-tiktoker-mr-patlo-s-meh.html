One killed, 13 injured in stampede at TikToker Mr Patlo's mehndi event

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

One person was killed and 13 injured in a stampede at TikToker Mr Patlo's mehndi ceremony at Sialkot Sports Stadium. According to Aaj News, the gates were opened early during a prize event, causing panic among the crowd. Police registered a case naming Mr Patlo and eight others, along with 40 to 45 unidentified persons.

Six suspects have been arrested in connection with the incident. The stampede broke out when a large crowd rushed in after the gates were opened ahead of schedule.
