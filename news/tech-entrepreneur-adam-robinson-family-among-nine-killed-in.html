Tech entrepreneur Adam Robinson, family among nine killed in Florida jet crash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tech entrepreneur Adam Robinson, 44, was among nine people killed when a Learjet 60 crashed into a roadway in Florida's Palm Beach County on Tuesday. Robinson, the founder and CEO of Retention.com and RB2B, died along with his wife Helen Sharp and their two young children, Emma, 4, and Charlie, 2. Also killed in the crash were the family's caretaker, two friends and the two pilots aboard the aircraft.

According to reports, the jet issued a mayday call after the crew reported turbulence and icing at an altitude of 35,000 feet. The National Transportation Safety Board and the Federal Aviation Administration are investigating the crash, with weather conditions a key focus of the inquiry.
