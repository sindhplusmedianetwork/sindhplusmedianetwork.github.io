Meghan Markle's Father Re-Arrested for Cutting Off Ankle Monitoring Device

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thomas Markle, the father of Meghan Markle, wife of Britain's Prince Harry, has been re-arrested and jailed for violating court orders. According to Reuters, the 82-year-old cut off the electronic monitoring device attached to his ankle during house arrest. A United States court then ordered him held without bail.

He had been arrested last week on charges of possessing images of child sexual abuse. The FBI said objectionable images and other material were recovered from storage units he used in Los Angeles. Markle denies the charges.
