Nepal PM in India for economic, energy talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah is in New Delhi for talks with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi. It is Shah's first official visit to India since his election eight months ago. The talks are focused on bilateral trade, connectivity and energy cooperation.

Modi said the discussions would add new momentum to India-Nepal relations. India is Nepal's biggest trade partner, accounting for more than 60 percent of its trade. India also supplies Nepal's petroleum products.
