PML-N has agreed to new Punjab provinces after 2029 elections, claims Sohail Warraich

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior journalist Sohail Warraich has claimed that the PML-N has agreed to the creation of new provinces in Punjab and will fully support the required legislation. Writing in his column, Warraich said the party has made its agreement conditional on the process taking place after the 2029 general elections. He wrote that the next five months will be decisive for future politics, the 2029 elections and the country's future rulers.

Warraich added that dividing Punjab into four provinces carries the most weight in top circles. The claim has revived the long-running national debate over creating new administrative units, with leaders across parties holding sharply differing positions on the issue.
