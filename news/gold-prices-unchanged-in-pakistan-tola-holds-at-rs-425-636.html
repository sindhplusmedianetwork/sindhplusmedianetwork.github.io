Gold Prices Unchanged in Pakistan, Tola Holds at Rs 425,636

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices stayed flat in Pakistan today, with one tola of gold holding at Rs 425,636 according to the All Pakistan Gems and Jewellers Association. Ten grams of gold also remained unchanged at Rs 364,914. International bullion held steady at $4,031 per ounce.

The rates show no change from yesterday's closing prices.
