Hamilton and Leclerc Say Ferrari Row Is Settled After Brazil Talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ferrari drivers Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc say they have settled their row over the Mexico City Grand Prix after direct talks at Interlagos ahead of the Brazilian Grand Prix. Speaking to Sky Sports after qualifying, Hamilton said the two drivers had sat down together and resolved the matter themselves, adding that there had been no demands from either side. Leclerc likewise said the issue was a closed chapter and that talking face to face had helped, while noting that both drivers still wanted to finish ahead of each other.

The row began on the opening lap in Mexico, when Leclerc's attempted overtake on Hamilton at Turn 4 saw neither Ferrari make the corner, allowing Max Verstappen to slip through and win the race. Leclerc received a five-second penalty, Hamilton finished second and Leclerc fourth, and Hamilton publicly demanded serious talks inside the team. Team principal Fred Vasseur confirmed the discussions took place on Thursday night at Interlagos.

The immediate problem for both drivers in Brazil is grid penalties: Leclerc qualified fourth but drops ten places for an engine change, while Hamilton qualified 11th and starts from the back after further power-unit changes.
