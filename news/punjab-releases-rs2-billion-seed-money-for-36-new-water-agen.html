Punjab Releases Rs2 Billion Seed Money for 36 New Water Agencies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has approved Rs2 billion in seed money for the establishment and initial operations of 36 newly created Water and Sanitation Agencies across the province, providing temporary financial relief to the new agencies. Each agency was supposed to receive Rs200 million immediately, but the required funds are not currently available, and officials said all 36 agencies would have faced the risk of closure soon after their establishment had the immediate release not been approved. The Punjab Finance Department has issued a formal notification approving the provision of funds to the newly established agencies during the financial year 2026-27, with the money to be provided through a technical supplementary grant.

An amount of Rs56.71 million has been allocated for each agency, while Rs42.857 million, Rs42.857 million and Rs42.856 million have been earmarked for the agencies in Talagang, Taunsa and Kot Addu respectively. The agencies receiving the funds include those in Bahawalpur, Dera Ghazi Khan, Hafizabad, Jhang, Jhelum, Okara, Rahim Yar Khan, Sahiwal, Sargodha, Sialkot, Gujrat, Sheikhupura, Murree, Nankana Sahib, Attock, Bahawalnagar, Bhakkar, Chakwal, Chiniot, Kasur, Khanewal, Khushab, Wazirabad, Layyah, Lodhran, Mandi Bahauddin, Mianwali, Muzaffargarh, Narowal, Pakpattan, Toba Tek Singh, Vehari, Rajanpur, Talagang, Taunsa and Kot Addu. However, officials warned that the establishment of the agencies may become difficult if the entire required funding is not released at the beginning of the new year in January.
