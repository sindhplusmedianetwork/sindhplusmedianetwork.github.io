Rashmika Mandanna Opens Up About Discomfort With Bold Outfits

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian actress Rashmika Mandanna has spoken openly about her discomfort with wearing overly revealing outfits. She said she does not feel comfortable in clothes that expose too much of the body. The actress believes performers should have the right to express their preferences about wardrobe choices or scenes required for a role.

She was speaking on the ninth season of Karan Johar's show 'Koffee with Karan', where she appeared alongside her husband Vijay Deverakonda. Rashmika said a director's demand for bikinis or short clothes does not mean an actor must accept every demand without question. She added that when she struggles to voice her discomfort directly to a director, she discusses it with her husband, who encourages her to speak clearly.
