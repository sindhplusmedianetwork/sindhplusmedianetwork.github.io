Snowfall continues in Chitral and Swat, cold intensifies as mountains wear white blanket

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rain and snowfall continue in Chitral, Swat and Peshawar, including various areas of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and the upper areas have worn a white blanket of snow, while the weather has turned cold and citizens have started wearing warm clothes. Continuous snowfall is continuing in various areas of Upper Chitral, including Trich, Khot, Yarkhun valley, Laspoor and Shandur, while snow is also falling in Madaklasht, Garam Chashma, Bogusht, Gaboor, Kiar and Sosom, and relatively heavier snowfall has been recorded in Broghil valley. The upper places of the Kalash valleys of Lower Chitral have also been covered in a white blanket of snow, while rain and snowfall continue in the upper areas of Lowari Tunnel and Karimabad.

In Swat, light rain fell in the main city of Mingora, while snowfall occurred in the upper areas of Matiltan and Mahodand, after which the weather turned colder and citizens started wearing warm clothes, and light drizzle in Peshawar made the weather pleasant. On the other hand, the increased cold due to rain and snowfall in Chitral has created difficulties for citizens in obtaining firewood, as firewood prices have risen sharply, while the prices of gas cylinders have also gone beyond the reach of citizens.
