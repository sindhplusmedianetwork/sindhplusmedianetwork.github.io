Trump holds talks with 'friend' Putin ahead of trilateral meeting with Xi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump and Russia's Vladimir Putin began a bilateral meeting in China on Thursday ahead of a three-way discussion with Xi Jinping. Trump said in Shenzhen, according to a recording of their meeting released by the Kremlin, that they had great relationships for many years and that a lot of wonderful things could come out of the meeting, calling Putin his friend. Prior to the bilateral meeting, the presidents of China, the United States and Russia posed for a photo with other Asia-Pacific leaders ahead of their first trilateral summit, billed by Beijing as talks about world peace.

The three-way meeting at the Kylin Villa in Shenzhen comes with Russia's offensive in Ukraine approaching the five-year mark. Trump is bogged down by his war with Iran, which has choked oil supplies and pushed up consumer prices worldwide, while China is looking to strengthen its hand in East Asia. The conclave has stoked fears that the three powerful men may seek to divide the globe into spheres of influence, for example by deciding the fate of Ukraine over the heads of Kyiv or Europe.

Beijing, Washington and Moscow have given different accounts of the agenda for the meeting on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum. Also up for discussion are the Iran war and nuclear disarmament, according to Washington, after a US-Russia bilateral treaty on atomic weapons expired in February.
