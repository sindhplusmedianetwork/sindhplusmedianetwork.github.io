Ice and Cocaine Recovered from Bungalow Linked to PPP Lawmaker's Brother; Aijaz Jakhrani Demands Probe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi police have registered a narcotics case against Azad Jakhrani, the brother of PPP lawmaker Mir Aijaz Hussain Jakhrani, over the alleged recovery of crystal methamphetamine and cocaine from a bungalow linked to him. The case was lodged at Darakhshan police station on behalf of the state under four provisions of the anti-narcotics laws, after officers on routine patrol near the Clock Tower stopped a suspicious motorcyclist and allegedly recovered 18 boxes of cocaine weighing 62 grammes and 41 packets of crystal methamphetamine weighing 106 grammes from a plastic bag. During questioning, the arrested suspect allegedly named Azad Jakhrani as the owner of the drugs and claimed more narcotics were stored at his bungalow.

Acting on that statement, police raided a property in Khayaban-e-Seher, where they said a wooden cupboard held shopping bags containing over one kilogramme of ice and another bag with 32 boxes of cocaine weighing 22 grammes, along with a digital weighing scale. The bungalow's watchman told investigators that Jakhrani was out of the country at the time. MNA Aijaz Jakhrani responded that he had already disassociated himself from his brother but maintained the property was their family home, accusing police of raiding it in the dead of night and violating its sanctity.

He said he had informed Sindh Home Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar and IG Javed Alam Odho and demanded an inquiry and action against officials responsible for any misconduct. Faryal Talpur, chairperson of the Sindh Assembly's Standing Committee on Home Affairs, has sought a report from the Sindh police chief on the raid.
