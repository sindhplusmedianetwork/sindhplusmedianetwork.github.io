Balochistan 'Money Heist' bank robbery solved after 18 months, three arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Balochistan solved the 'Money Heist' bank robbery case after 18 months, arresting three of the five suspects. The case concerns the robbery of over Rs2 billion from a bank in Hub. Those arrested were identified as Zohaib, Murtaza and Bilal.

Police said Zohaib considered himself the 'Professor' of Money Heist. According to police, the suspects were university students who turned to robbery after watching films in a bid to get rich. Two vehicles, a motorcycle, a rickshaw and mobile phones bought with the stolen money were recovered.

Two fugitive suspects are still being sought.
