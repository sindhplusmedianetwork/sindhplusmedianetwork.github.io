MQM-P launches street movement for new administrative units, announces Nov 29 rally

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

KARACHI: The Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan has launched a major mobilisation campaign for the creation of new administrative units, with party chairman Dr Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui announcing a 'historic rally' on Sharea Faisal on November 29. Addressing a workers' convention at KMC Park in PIB Colony on Sunday, attended by a large number of activists including women, Siddiqui said the time had come for the oppressed to unite against 'an entrenched system of feudal domination and family-based politics'. The federal education minister called for referendums under the Constitution to determine the will of the people on the creation of new administrative units in Sindh and the three other provinces, arguing that the existing provincial structure had failed to deliver inclusive governance and equitable distribution of resources.

'The era of feudal democracy has run its course. The establishment of new administrative units has become the country's destiny,' he said, adding that the November 29 rally would mark a significant step in the campaign. Senior leader Dr Farooq Sattar said the four-province arrangement had become a mechanism for sustaining the dominance of a handful of political families, and criticised the Pakistan Peoples Party for its mismanagement of Sindh over the past 18 years, while insisting the proposed units would not undermine Sindh's cultural identity.

Former Karachi mayor Wasim Akhtar urged workers to remain united under Siddiqui's leadership. Party leaders credited the show's success to former Karachi Tanzeemi Committee in-charge Hammad Siddiqui, while senior leader Mustafa Kamal and his close aides were conspicuously absent.
