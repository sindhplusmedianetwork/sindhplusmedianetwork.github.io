Fatima Asghar Murder Case: Court Orders Submission of Incident Videos

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A hearing in the murder case of 13-year-old domestic worker Fatima Asghar was held at the District and Sessions Court Islamabad, where the accused, Afshan and Qari Ali, were produced before Judicial Magistrate Naima Iftikhar. Prosecutor Wajid Munir informed the court that an electric iron had been recovered from accused Afshan, while a stick had been recovered from accused Ali. He further told the court that the Digital Video Recorder (DVR) had been sent for forensic examination, and Investigation Officer Nadeem Mughal informed the court that the device contained seven days of recorded data.

The prosecutor also informed the court that videos related to the incident were available, prompting the judicial magistrate to ask whether they had been made part of the case record. The court directed the prosecution to submit the videos before the court on the same day and instructed the concerned officials to ensure that the videos were submitted without delay. The hearing focused on the recovery of items allegedly connected to the incident, the forensic examination of the DVR and the availability of video evidence.
