PTI leader Mian Akram Usman sent on 10-day remand for Askari Tower identification parade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-terrorism court in Lahore on Friday sent PTI leader Mian Akram Usman to jail on a 10-day judicial remand for an identification parade in the Askari Tower attack case. The investigating officer told the court that Usman was allegedly involved in the attack and arson at Askari Tower. The court accepted the police request and ordered the remand for the identification parade.

Usman had earlier been convicted by a military court in the Jinnah House attack case. He was released after completing his sentence in that case. The latest remand places him back in custody while the Askari Tower case proceeds through the identification process.
