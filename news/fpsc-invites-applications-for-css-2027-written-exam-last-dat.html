FPSC Invites Applications for CSS 2027 Written Exam; Last Date November 25

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has invited applications for the written examination of the Central Superior Services (CSS) 2027. According to ARY News, the last date for submitting online applications is November 25. Only candidates who have passed the MPT 2027 screening test are eligible to apply for the written examination.

The written exam will begin on January 27 and will be held in 19 cities across the country. The FPSC has made it mandatory for candidates to submit the signed hard copy of the online application along with the required documents by December 4. Candidates must hold at least a second-division bachelor's degree, and the age limit has been set at 21 to 30 years.

Age will be determined as of December 31, 2026, while eligible candidates will also get a two-year age relaxation.
