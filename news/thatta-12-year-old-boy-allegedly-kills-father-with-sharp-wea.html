Thatta: 12-Year-Old Boy Allegedly Kills Father With Sharp Weapon After Being Denied a Mobile Phone

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 12-year-old boy in the Gharo area of Thatta allegedly attacked his father with a sharp-edged weapon after the father refused to give him a mobile phone. The father was seriously injured in the attack and was being shifted to Karachi when he died, according to police, as reported by Jang. The incident has sent shockwaves through the local community, where residents expressed grief over the tragic turn of events.

Police reached the scene after being informed and took the boy into custody for further legal proceedings. Authorities said an investigation is under way to establish the full circumstances of the case.
