Glenn Maxwell Announces Retirement From International Cricket

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Australia all-rounder Glenn Maxwell has announced his retirement from international cricket, bringing down the curtain on a glittering career. Maxwell represented Australia in 7 Tests, 149 one-day internationals and 130 T20 internationals. He last played for Australia against Oman at the T20 World Cup 2026.

Maxwell was a key member of Australia's World Cup-winning squads in 2015 and 2023, as well as the triumphant 2022 T20 World Cup team. The highlight of his career came at the 2023 World Cup in India, where his double century against Afghanistan is counted among the greatest ODI innings ever played and single-handedly carried his team to victory.
