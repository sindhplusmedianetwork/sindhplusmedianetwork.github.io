Body of missing daughter of former naval officer recovered four months after Swat boat tragedy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa say the body of a woman who went missing when a boat capsized in Saifullah Lake in Upper Swat about four months ago has been handed over to her family. The boat overturned on July 1, killing retired Pakistan Navy Lieutenant Commander Aamir, his 20-year-old son Abdullah, his daughters Parwa, 27, and Rawail, 23, and Parwa's two young children. Aamir's third daughter, Bushra, who had come from the United States for a holiday, went missing in the accident, and a rescue operation lasting several days failed to find her.

On November 4, a woman's body was found in Mahodand Lake, which is connected to Saifullah Lake, and police said it was believed to be Bushra's. District Police Officer Upper Swat Jamil Akhtar said the family identified the body on the spot from visible signs, including a broken front tooth, and from the remains of a sandal and jeans. He said DNA samples have also been sent to a laboratory for further confirmation.

The family had arranged the trip to Swat when Bushra arrived from the United States, and the accident turned the holiday into a tragedy.
