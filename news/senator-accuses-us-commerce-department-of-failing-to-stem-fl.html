Senator Accuses US Commerce Department of Failing to Stem Flow of AI Chips to China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senator Maria Cantwell, the top Democrat on the Senate Commerce Committee, has accused the US Commerce Department of failing to stop advanced AI chips reaching China. She said Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's inaction undermined national security. In a letter dated 15 November and seen by Reuters, she said the department delayed action despite repeated warnings.

Cantwell noted Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's remarks that US agencies had backed blacklisting Singapore's Megaspeed for months over the Nvidia chip scandal. A spokesperson for Lutnick declined to comment on the accusations. The dispute centres on controls meant to stop advanced American chips from reaching China.
