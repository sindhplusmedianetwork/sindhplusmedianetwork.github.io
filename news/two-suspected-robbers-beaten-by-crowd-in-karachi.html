Two suspected robbers beaten by crowd in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two suspected robbers were injured after being caught and beaten by a crowd near Lucky One Mall in Karachi. The men were allegedly involved in the robbery of a family riding a motorcycle on Rashid Minhas Road. Yousuf Plaza police reached the scene between 2:30am and 3am on the night of October 20-21, after receiving reports that two men were being assaulted by the public.

The suspects were taken into custody and shifted to the police station. A case has been registered against them.
