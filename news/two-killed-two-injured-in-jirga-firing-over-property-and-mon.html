Two killed, two injured in jirga firing over property and money dispute on Peshawar Ring Road

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A jirga convened over a property and money dispute turned into a bloody clash near Kabootar Chowk on Peshawar's Ring Road. Two people were killed and two others were injured in the exchange of fire. Police said the incident took place at Musafir Fish Centre, which falls within the limits of Shahpur police station.

The jirga had been called to settle the dispute over property and money, but the meeting ended in gunfire. Police said evidence was collected from the scene.
