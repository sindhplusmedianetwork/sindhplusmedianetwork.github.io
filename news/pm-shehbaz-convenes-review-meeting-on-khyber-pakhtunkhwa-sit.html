PM Shehbaz Convenes Review Meeting on Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Situation Today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has convened an important review meeting today to assess the situation in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. According to sources, federal ministers and senior military officials will participate in the meeting, which will also be attended by the KP Governor. The session will review the overall situation prevailing in the province.

The prime minister is expected to be briefed on the decisions taken so far regarding Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the outcomes of those decisions. Officials say the meeting reflects the federal government's focus on the province's affairs. No further details of the agenda have been disclosed.
