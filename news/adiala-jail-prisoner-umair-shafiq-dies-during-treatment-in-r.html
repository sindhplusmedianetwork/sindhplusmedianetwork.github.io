Adiala Jail Prisoner Umair Shafiq Dies During Treatment in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A prisoner held at Adiala Jail has died during treatment at a hospital in Rawalpindi. The prisoner, Umair Shafiq, was brought from Adiala Jail to Rawalpindi Teaching Hospital. He died while under treatment there.

He had been shifted from Adiala Jail to the hospital after his health deteriorated. According to police sources, the deceased prisoner was nominated in three theft cases registered at Waris Khan police station.
