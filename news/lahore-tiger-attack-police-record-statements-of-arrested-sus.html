Lahore tiger attack: police record statements of arrested suspects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Lahore are investigating the death of a young man, Taha, who was killed in a tigress attack in the Kahna area, and have recorded the statements of the arrested suspects. Police say the incident happened when the TikToker tried an adventure stunt, and because of the farmhouse owner's alleged negligence. Investigators found 28 lions at the farmhouse, which was run under a breeding farmhouse licence.

Taha's father and brother also worked at the farmhouse. Police are continuing their investigation into how the incident happened and who was responsible.
