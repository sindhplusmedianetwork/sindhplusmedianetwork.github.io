Sindh drawing up master plans for 31 cities, says Chief Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah announced on Saturday that the provincial government is working on master plans for 31 cities across the province, including Karachi and Hyderabad, to ensure planned, sustainable and inclusive urban development. Addressing the Sindh Cities Forum 2026, organised by the Sindh Master Plan Authority with NED University and UN-Habitat on World Cities Day, he said master planning was vital to address unplanned construction, traffic congestion, water shortages, inadequate drainage and the effects of climate change. According to the local government minister, master plans for 17 cities have been completed while work on the remaining 14 is in its final stages, and notifications have been issued for nine cities including Sukkur, Larkana, Mirpurkhas and Nawabshah.

The chief minister said work on the Karachi and Hyderabad master plans was progressing rapidly, with international consultants engaged for Karachi, which he admitted would be a particularly challenging exercise. Recalling the 2020 monsoon floods in Yousuf Goth, he said roads built with proper carriageways, medians and storm-water drains had been encroached upon by shops, damaging the original infrastructure. He acknowledged that successive governments, including his own, had made mistakes that contributed to Karachi's present condition.

He stressed that urban challenges could not be addressed without the cooperation of citizens, planners, builders and utility companies.
