Two High-Speed Trains Collide in Spain, Killing at Least 10 People

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two high-speed trains have collided in Spain on the line connecting Madrid to the southern Andalusia region, killing at least 10 people, rail operator Adif said. A Renfe train and an Iryo train crashed just after 9am, causing two Iryo wagons to derail, according to a government spokesperson for the Castilla-La Mancha region. Traffic on the Madrid-Andalusia high-speed line was suspended after what Adif described as a rear-end collision between the two trains, and the cause and the number of injuries were still being determined.

The incident took place on the line from Madrid to Seville, near the town of Urda, where the mayor, Manuel Galan, said emergency services had asked the council to prepare emergency facilities, including a sports hall, for passengers and the injured. Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez said he was shocked by the serious train accident in Toledo, sending love, solidarity and support to the victims and their families, while his agenda for the day was cleared and the opposition People's Party cancelled all campaign events. The collision comes nine months after an accident between high-speed trains belonging to the same operators on the same line that killed 46 people near Adamuz, in Cordoba province, in January.
