When all flights in the US were stopped for the first time since 9/11

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US authorities have lifted a ground stop that affected millions of Americans across the country. The disruption is counted among the biggest nationwide interruptions to the US air system since the 11 September 2001 attacks. Flight tracking site FlightRadar24 reported nearly 10,000 flights cancelled by Saturday.

Delta and American Airlines cancelled more than 2,200 flights each. United cancelled 1,800 flights, while Southwest cancelled around 1,400. The stop brought air travel to a halt across the United States for the first time since 9/11.

Authorities have now lifted the ground stop and flights are resuming.
