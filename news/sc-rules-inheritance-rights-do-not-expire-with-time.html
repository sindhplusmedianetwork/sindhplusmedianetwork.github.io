SC rules inheritance rights do not expire with time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Supreme Court ruled on Friday that an heir's right to inheritance arises immediately upon the property owner's death. The court held that this right cannot be extinguished merely by the passage of time. A five-member bench led by Justice Shahid Waheed decided the matter.

The bench held that the limitation period begins only when an heir is informed of a clear denial of their ownership rights. The court also issued directions to revenue and registration authorities. Those directions include requiring NADRA family registration certificates and written notice to all heirs, including women, before inheritance mutations are attested.
