Online Classes Extended in Madina's Yanbu Until November 12 Amid Attack Fears

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Authorities in Madina have extended the closure of all educational institutions in Yanbu governorate. Online-only classes will continue until November 12. The Madina emirate said students and school staff will study and work from home.

Lessons will be delivered through the Madrasati platform and other approved digital systems. Officials did not specify the exact circumstances behind the extension. They described it as a continuation of precautionary measures.

The extension comes amid fears of Houthi attacks in the region.
