Pakistan seeks climate finance for household solar power

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Climate Change Minister Dr Musadik Malik has called for international climate finance to be directed towards households adopting renewable energy. Speaking at a ministerial panel of the COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkiye, he said Pakistan's rooftop solar expansion is being driven by ordinary households investing their own money. He cited the example of a villager who sold 12 buffaloes and cows to buy solar panels for his home.

Malik said Pakistan has saved around 12 billion dollars in liquefied natural gas and gas imports through the adoption of rooftop solar power. He urged international partners to support battery storage and grid connectivity so that households can make full use of solar energy. The minister said shifting climate finance towards direct household support would speed up the clean energy transition in developing countries.
