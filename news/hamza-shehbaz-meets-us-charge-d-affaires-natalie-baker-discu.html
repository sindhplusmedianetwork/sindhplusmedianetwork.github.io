Hamza Shehbaz meets US charge d'affaires Natalie Baker, discusses ties and economic cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hamza Shehbaz met US Embassy Charge d'Affaires Natalie A. Baker in Islamabad. The meeting discussed Pakistan-US bilateral relations and the regional situation.

Pakistan's economy and expanding cooperation between the two countries also came under discussion. Hamza Shehbaz congratulated Baker on her nomination by President Donald Trump as the new US ambassador to Pakistan. Agriculture cooperation and investment opportunities were discussed during the meeting.

Local governance was also among the topics covered.
