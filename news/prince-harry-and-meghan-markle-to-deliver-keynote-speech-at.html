Prince Harry and Meghan Markle to Deliver Keynote Speech at European Parliament in Brussels

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle announced on November 4 that they will make their first joint trip in Europe since returning to the UK. They will travel to Brussels on Tuesday, November 10. On the day of their arrival they are set to deliver a keynote speech at the Lost Screen Memorial in the European Parliament.

In the speech they will stress the importance of meaningful action to protect children's digital safety. Prince Harry will also take part in a panel discussion in Belgium. The panel is titled 'Beyond Promises: Building a Digital Future That Works for Children'.
