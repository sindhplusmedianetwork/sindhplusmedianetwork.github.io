Tense wait for Mississippi Senate vote result

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mississippi voters faced a nerve-wracking wait as counting dragged on after the US midterm elections. With 97 per cent of the vote counted, Democrat Scott Colom was leading Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith by 2,574 votes. If Colom wins, he would become Mississippi's first Black senator in nearly 150 years.

He would also be the state's first Democratic senator since 1982. The long wait reflects Mississippi's procedures, which allow officials several days to process mail-in and provisional ballots. The result remained undecided as counting continued.
