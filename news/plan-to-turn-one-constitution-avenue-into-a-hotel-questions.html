Plan to turn One Constitution Avenue into a hotel, questions over owners' future and JIT report revelations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has decided to give a new shape to Islamabad's disputed One Constitution Avenue project and to re-tender it, while protecting the interests of genuine buyers and investors. Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi said the government has taken possession of the project and it will be converted into a five-star hotel in line with its original concept. A joint investigation team report found that the CDA had approved 120 apartments in the project's two towers for the builder BNP and Abdul Hafeez Sheikh, but 254 apartments were built after official documents were altered.

The report says Sheikh collected a total of Rs9.5 billion from the owners of those apartments. The JIT has recommended blacklisting Sheikh and BNP and barring them from future government contracts. The Supreme Court had in 2019 ordered the company to pay Rs17 billion to the CDA, and after the payment was not made, the CDA cancelled the lease in 2023, a decision the Supreme Court upheld in 2026.

Information Minister Attaullah Tarar said NAB's investigation is expected to be completed within three months, after which court proceedings will begin. Economist Nadeem ul Haque criticised the government's compensation offer, saying owners should be paid at current market value rather than only their original investment and profit.
