Dry days ahead for Karachi as weak western system eyes upper Sindh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A weak western weather system has started affecting the western parts of the country, bringing the possibility of light rain to some areas of upper Sindh. According to the Met department's early warning centre, light rain is expected tomorrow in the western parts of Qambar Shahdadkot and Dadu districts. Karachi, however, is forecast to remain completely dry over the next three days.

The city's minimum temperature is expected to range between 21 and 23 degrees Celsius from Tuesday to Thursday. Daytime temperatures will stay warm, with maximums likely between 33 and 35 degrees. Residents of Karachi can expect dry and warm conditions through the week while the system passes to the west.
