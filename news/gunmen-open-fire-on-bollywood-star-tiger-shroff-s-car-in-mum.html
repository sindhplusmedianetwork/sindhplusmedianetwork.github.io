Gunmen Open Fire on Bollywood Star Tiger Shroff's Car in Mumbai; Actor Escapes Unhurt

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gunmen opened fire on Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff's silver BMW in Mumbai on Sunday evening as he was leaving a personal engagement at around 7pm. The actor escaped unhurt, but his driver, Firdous, was shot in the left hand during the attack. Mumbai police have formed around 15 special teams to investigate, with forensic experts examining the scene and scanning CCTV footage from the area.

Police Deputy Commissioner Mohit Garg said the probe is pursuing all angles. Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Eknath Shinde met the actor after the attack and promised a review of security arrangements for film personalities. Opposition Congress and NCP leaders attacked the state government over law and order, pointing to recent attacks on Salman Khan's residence and threats against other film-industry figures.
