Bachchan Family Row Over Ownership of Amitabh's Jalsa Bungalow

A surprising family dispute has emerged over the residence of Bollywood legend Amitabh Bachchan, his bungalow Jalsa. According to Indian media reports, his sister-in-law Ramola Bachchan has challenged the 1993 gift deed under which ownership of the bungalow was transferred to Jaya Bachchan. Ramola, the wife of Amitabh's brother Ajitab Bachchan, has filed several complaints with the registration authorities over the past seven months.

Her position is that Jalsa was originally registered in her name, and the transfer to Jaya Bachchan took place without her knowledge or consent. The matter resurfaced when her name was allegedly sought to be removed from the official property card and replaced with Jaya Bachchan's name. Interestingly, housing society records show Jaya Bachchan as the owner, while the official property card still carries Ramola's name.

The complaint also points out that the 1993 gift deed was prepared on plain paper without stamp duty or witnesses, making its later registration questionable.
