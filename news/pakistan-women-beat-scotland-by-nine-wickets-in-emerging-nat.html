Pakistan Women Beat Scotland by Nine Wickets in Emerging Nations Trophy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan have defeated Scotland by nine wickets in the Super 6 stage of the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy. Scotland scored 124 runs for the loss of five wickets, with captain Kathryn top-scoring with 53. Umm-e-Hani took two wickets for Pakistan.

Pakistan chased the target of 125 runs with 17 balls to spare. Gul Feroza scored 54 runs in the chase. Captain Muneeba Ali remained unbeaten on 59 as Pakistan completed a comfortable nine-wicket victory.
