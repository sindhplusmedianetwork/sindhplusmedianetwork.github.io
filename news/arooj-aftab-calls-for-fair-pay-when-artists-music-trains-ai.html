Arooj Aftab Calls for Fair Pay When Artists' Music Trains AI

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Grammy-winning Pakistani singer Arooj Aftab has called for fair pay for musicians whose work is used to train artificial intelligence. She shared campaign messages from United Musicians and Allied Workers on Instagram demanding clear labelling of AI-generated music and fair compensation when artists' recordings are used to train AI systems or generate derivative music. "Even if you don't use AI to make music — it is going to use your music to make AI music!!" she wrote.

The campaign backs the proposed US "Living Wage for Musicians Act."
