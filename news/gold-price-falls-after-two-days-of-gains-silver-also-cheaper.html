Gold Price Falls After Two Days of Gains; Silver Also Cheaper

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices in Pakistan fell on Friday after rising for two consecutive days. The price of gold dropped by Rs6,500 per tola to Rs429,136, while 10 grams of gold became cheaper by Rs5,573 to Rs367,914. Silver also fell, with the price per tola dropping by Rs85 to Rs5,678.

The decline followed a fall in the international market, where gold dropped by $65 to $4,066 per ounce. The local fall came as the international market also moved lower. Friday's fall ended a two-day run of gains in the local market, a day after gold had climbed sharply to Rs435,636 per tola.
