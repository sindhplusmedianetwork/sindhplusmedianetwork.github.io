WhatsApp tests sharing Instagram reels directly inside chats

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WhatsApp is testing a new feature that will let users share Instagram reels directly inside their WhatsApp chats. Under the feature, a separate Reels shortcut will be added to WhatsApp's media sharing menu, allowing users to easily share videos they have seen on Instagram. Users will need to link their WhatsApp account to Meta's Accounts Centre to use the facility.

With the new shortcut, users will no longer need to open their phone gallery or switch to another app to share a video, as they will be able to access Instagram reels from within the WhatsApp chat itself. However, not all Instagram reels will be available through the feature; it will mainly include videos users have recently watched or liked. The aim is to make sharing Instagram videos with friends, family and groups faster and easier.

The feature is currently being rolled out for WhatsApp Android beta version 2.26.42.9, and the company has not announced any date for its final release.
