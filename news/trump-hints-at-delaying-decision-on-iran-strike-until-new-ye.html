Trump hints at delaying decision on Iran strike until New Year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has hinted at delaying a decision on a possible military strike on Iran until the start of the New Year, saying he does not want to rush the matter. In interviews with Axios and Israeli TV Channel 12, Trump said he was in no hurry to decide on Iran and would see what the situation looks like after the New Year begins. When asked whether a new attack on Iran would come at the same time as the war that began in early February this year, the president said that was also possible.

Axios noted, however, that just a day before the current war began in late February, Trump had also said he had not yet decided what to do, although in reality he had already decided on the attack. According to Israeli Channel 12, US officials are sceptical about the alleged pressure from Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to restart the war against Iran. A US official considered very close to Trump said the pressure was being applied for political purposes, as Netanyahu was trying to stay in power despite losing his parliamentary majority in the October 27 elections.

A separate Channel 12 report claimed Israel had prepared several times in recent weeks for a US attack on Iran, but Trump held back from ordering action at the last moment each time, with Israeli military systems ready for the attack before the US president changed his mind.
