Tikka shop owner shot dead in Rawalpindi over rickshaw parking dispute

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A tikka shop owner was killed in Rawalpindi after a dispute over parking a rickshaw turned into a fatal shooting in the Scheme Three Ghareebabad locality under Chaklala police jurisdiction. Police said the worker, Sain Hafeez, was busy at his shop when a rickshaw driver parked his rickshaw in front of it, and an argument broke out after Hafeez objected. The rickshaw driver then pulled out a pistol and opened fire, hitting Hafeez, who died of his wounds after being badly injured.

Mobile footage of the killing has emerged in which the shop owner can be seen lying on the ground after the firing while the driver tries to flee. As the accused, Shahid Nazeer, tried to escape, local people caught him, pinned him to the ground and assaulted him. The incident took place last evening, and a murder case has been registered at Chaklala police station on the complaint of the victim's brother.

Police said the FIR states the driver shot the tikka seller in the chest at a private plaza square for refusing to let him park the rickshaw, and Chaklala police moved quickly to arrest the suspect.
