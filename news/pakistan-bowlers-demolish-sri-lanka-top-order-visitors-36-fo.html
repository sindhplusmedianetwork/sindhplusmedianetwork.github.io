Pakistan bowlers demolish Sri Lanka top order, visitors 36 for 5 in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's bowlers demolished Sri Lanka's top order on the first day of the second Test in Rawalpindi, reducing the visitors to 36 for 5. Lahiru Udara (4), Pasindu Sooriyabandara (2), Dinesh Chandimal (7), Kamindu Mendis (8) and Dhananjaya de Silva (1) were all dismissed. Mohammad Abbas and Razaullah took two wickets each, while Saqib Khan took one.

Pakistan won the toss and chose to bowl first. The match was moved from Lahore to Rawalpindi because of smog. The first Test ended in a draw after bad light and rain wiped out about 140 overs.
