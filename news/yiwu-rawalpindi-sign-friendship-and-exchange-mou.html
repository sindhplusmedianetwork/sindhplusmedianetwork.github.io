Yiwu, Rawalpindi sign friendship and exchange MOU

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yiwu, a major trade hub in China's Zhejiang province, signed a memorandum of understanding on friendly relations and exchanges with Rawalpindi. The signing took place at the opening ceremony of the 7th Zhejiang International Friendship Cities Exchange Exhibition. Yiwu also signed similar agreements with Bandar Lampung in Indonesia, Addu City in the Maldives and Guanajuato in Mexico.

The agreements brought Yiwu's friendly exchange network to 68 cities and institutions in 42 countries. Pakistani jewellery is on display at the Rawalpindi City Pavilion at the exhibition. The event marked a new step in people-to-people and city-level exchanges between the two sides.
