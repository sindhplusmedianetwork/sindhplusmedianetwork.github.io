Gold prices fall, one tola down Rs700 to Rs410,936

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices in Pakistan recorded a slight fall on Saturday. According to the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association, the price of one tola of gold dropped by Rs700. After the fall, one tola of gold stood at Rs410,936.

The price of 10 grams of gold fell by Rs600 to Rs352,311. In the international market, gold also moved down, falling by 7 dollars. The international price stood at 3,884 dollars per ounce.
