Trump threatens to double tariffs on Mexico over migrant caravan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has threatened to immediately double tariffs on Mexico unless it stops migrant caravans heading towards the United States. In a post on Truth Social on Thursday, Trump said the caravans would not be accepted and warned there would be no leniency. An estimated 1,500 migrants, mostly from Central America, set off on Tuesday from Tapachula, a city near Mexico's border with Guatemala, and began walking north towards the United States.

The group set out days after last week's US election. Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum responded to the threat by saying that no migrant caravan has reached the US border since Trump returned to office in January 2025. The tariff threat adds fresh pressure to the relationship between the two countries, with trade and border security once again at the centre of the dispute.

It was not immediately clear when any new tariff would take effect.
