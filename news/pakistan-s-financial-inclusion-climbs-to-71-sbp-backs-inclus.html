Pakistan's financial inclusion climbs to 71%, SBP backs inclusive climate finance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's financial inclusion rate has climbed to 71 per cent, with the gender gap falling to 27 per cent, State Bank of Pakistan Governor Jameel Ahmad said on Tuesday. Addressing the inaugural session of a four-day joint learning programme organised by the SBP and the Alliance for Financial Inclusion (AFI) in Islamabad, he called for climate financing to be directed towards women, smallholder farmers and small businesses. The programme has brought together 38 representatives of central banks and financial regulators from 24 countries across four continents, and is being held in Pakistan for the first time.

Ahmad said climate change and financial exclusion were closely linked, with communities most exposed to climate risks often having the least access to insurance, savings and formal financial services. Citing the devastating 2022 floods, he noted they caused nearly $15 billion in physical damage and another $15 billion in economic losses, affected more than 30 million people and damaged 2.2 million homes. He outlined the SBP's measures to integrate environmental considerations into finance, including the Green Banking Guidelines of 2017, the Environmental and Social Risk Management Framework of 2022, the Pakistan Green Taxonomy and the Climate Stress Testing Guidelines issued in December 2025.

He also highlighted the Pakistan Women Inclusive Finance Programme supported by the Asian Development Bank, and noted that Pakistan joined the Women Entrepreneurs Finance Code in 2025.
