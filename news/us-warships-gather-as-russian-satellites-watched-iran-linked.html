US warships gather as Russian satellites watched Iran-linked attacks, report says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A third US aircraft carrier, USS Theodore Roosevelt, is arriving in the Middle East alongside USS George H.W. Bush and USS George Washington, and fears of renewed military action against Iran have grown. An AP investigation found Russian spy satellites moving over Saudi oil facilities and military bases before and after missile and drone attacks by Iran-aligned groups between July and October.

Researchers say the satellite data could help Iran track US movements in the region. However, there is no direct proof that Russia passed the information to Iran. Moscow has rejected the claims made in the investigation.

The arrival of the third carrier comes as the concentration of American naval power in the region continues to rise.
