Tech Firms Soar as Huang's Return Reinvigorates AI Trade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Technology firms rocketed as Asia joined a global stock surge following the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as Nvidia boss. Huang had stepped down less than two weeks earlier over extramarital affairs including with an alleged Chinese spy. News of his return sent Nvidia shares soaring 31.5 per cent.

The rise added more than $900 billion to the firm's market value, and pulled the Nasdaq up more than 6pc. Taipei was the standout in Asia, rising more than 9pc as chip titans TSMC and MediaTek added 10pc each. Seoul also gained more than 6pc.
