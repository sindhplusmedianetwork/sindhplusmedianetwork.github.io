Fuel Prices Cut Again: Petrol Down Rs3, Diesel Down Rs7 Per Litre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government on Wednesday cut the price of petrol by Rs3 per litre and high-speed diesel by Rs7 per litre in another reduction of fuel prices. Following the revision, petrol will retail at Rs335 per litre, while high-speed diesel will cost Rs364 from Thursday, November 12, according to a notification by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority. The government continues to levy Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel.

Oil refineries will bear a cumulative impact of Rs15 billion over 30 days from November 2, contributing to the petroleum levy, which allows a Rs14.71 reduction in the petrol price for consumers. The government has been revising fuel prices on a near-daily basis because of fluctuations in international market prices following renewed hostilities between Iran and the United States. The petroleum minister said the cabinet and the prime minister had decided to give Ogra the responsibility of setting fuel prices daily on the basis of international market trends.
