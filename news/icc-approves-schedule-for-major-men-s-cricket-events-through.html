ICC approves schedule for major men's cricket events through 2035

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Cricket Council has approved the calendar for future editions of the Cricket World Cup, T20 World Cup, Champions Trophy and World Test Championship final, setting out a roadmap for international cricket up to 2035. The decisions were taken at a meeting of the ICC board in Dubai. Australia and New Zealand will jointly host the 2028 ICC Men's T20 World Cup from 12 October to 12 November, and the next edition will follow in 2030 with England, Scotland and Ireland as joint hosts.

The ICC also approved the 2032 and 2034 T20 World Cups, with their host countries to be announced later. After next year's ODI World Cup in South Africa, the 2031 edition will be played in the subcontinent, with India and Bangladesh jointly hosting from 9 October to 23 November, while the host of the 2035 edition is yet to be decided. The World Test Championship final will continue to be held every two years in June through 2035, with England hosting the 2029 and 2031 finals and the hosts for 2033 and 2035 to be announced later.

Future editions of the Champions Trophy will also form part of the global calendar, with the 2033 edition scheduled for October. ICC chief executive Sanjog Gupta said global events play a key role in promoting the game and creating new opportunities for players, and that staging them helps spread the sport more widely and gives new talent a chance to emerge.
