Punjab Women Chambers Discuss Facilities and Support for Women Entrepreneurs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President of the Women Chamber of Commerce and Industry Sialkot (WCCIS), Shabnam Asif, on Friday participated in a meeting of the leadership of Women Chambers of Commerce and Industry from across Punjab, held at the Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FPCCI) in Lahore. The meeting was attended by Zaki Aijaz, Regional Chairman and Vice President of FPCCI, Dr Sheila Javed Akram, Founding President of the Women Chamber of Commerce and Industry Lahore (WCCIL), and other office-bearers representing women chambers across the province. The participants discussed ways to strengthen institutional capacity, address common challenges faced by women chambers and explore practical measures to improve their effectiveness and sustainability.

Key issues included the provision of permanent office buildings for women chambers and the establishment of essential facilities, including offices, display centres, training centres, exhibition centres, daycare facilities and transportation services to facilitate women entrepreneurs and support their business activities. The meeting also provided an opportunity for participants to exchange experiences, discuss common concerns and explore collaborative strategies to strengthen women chambers across Punjab. The participants aimed to promote a supportive business environment for women entrepreneurs in the province.
