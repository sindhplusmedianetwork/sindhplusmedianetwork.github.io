China's Xi says Taiwan 'reunification' must happen as history cannot be stopped

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese President Xi Jinping said on Thursday that the tide of history cannot be stopped, while urging peaceful reunification with Taiwan and making no mention of using force, though he still drew a firm rebuff from Taipei. China views democratically governed Taiwan as its own territory and has repeatedly offered a 'one country, two systems' model of autonomy, which no major Taiwanese political party supports. Resolving the Taiwan question is an unwavering historical mission of China's Communist Party, Xi said in a speech in Beijing's Great Hall of the People marking the 160th birthday of revolutionary hero Sun Yat-sen.

Xi said China adheres to peaceful reunification and the policy of 'one country, two systems', and is willing to talk to Taiwan while upholding the one-China principle. Taiwan's Mainland Affairs Council said neither the government nor Taiwan's people could accept 'one country, two systems', which would ultimately mean submitting to Communist Party rule, and its spokesperson said the wording on unification was not much different from the past. Taiwan's defence ministry said it had detected 29 Chinese military aircraft, including J-16 fighter jets and drones, carrying out a joint combat readiness patrol around Taiwan in conjunction with Chinese warships, starting on Thursday.

Xi's speech comes a week ahead of the APEC summit in China's southern tech hub of Shenzhen, where he is due to meet US President Donald Trump again, and a little more than two weeks before important local elections in Taiwan.
