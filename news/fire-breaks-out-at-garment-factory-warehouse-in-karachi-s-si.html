Fire Breaks Out at Garment Factory Warehouse in Karachi's SITE Area

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fire broke out at the warehouse of a garment factory near Metroville in Karachi's SITE area on Monday, prompting an immediate response from fire and rescue teams, according to a spokesperson for Sindh Rescue 1122. The spokesperson said the Central Command and Control Centre received information about the fire and dispatched a fire and rescue team, along with a fire brigade truck and an ambulance, to the site. More than five fire tenders from Rescue 1122 and the Karachi Metropolitan Corporation Fire Brigade took part in the firefighting operation, while two water bowsers and a snorkel vehicle were also deployed as a precautionary measure.

Officials said the fire broke out at the warehouse located on the upper floor of the factory at Plot No. A-14, adding that a fire had also broken out at the same location a few days earlier. Firefighters were battling the blaze to prevent further loss of assets.

Further details about the cause of the fire, the extent of the damage and any possible casualties were not immediately available, and officials described it as a developing story.
