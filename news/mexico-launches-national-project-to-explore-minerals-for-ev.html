Mexico launches national project to explore minerals for EV batteries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mexico's government has formally launched a major national project to explore and extract precious minerals in the country's northern regions to meet growing global demand for electric vehicles and modern batteries. According to the minerals ministry, modern surveys and scientific research had identified large reserves of lithium and other rare metals in mountainous areas, which are the backbone of the future clean energy industry. Officials said the project would use the latest environment-friendly technology, which has been made compulsory because traditional mining methods had badly damaged the local environment and water resources.

The minerals sought under the project are those needed for electric vehicle batteries and modern energy storage. Experts said the move could earn Mexico billions of dollars in foreign exchange and pave the way for new trade agreements with foreign technology companies.
