PSX Falls Ahead of SBP Policy Decision as Selling Grips Major Sectors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Selling pressure persisted at the Pakistan Stock Exchange on Monday as investors remained cautious ahead of the State Bank of Pakistan's monetary policy decision, while continued attacks on key energy routes in the Middle East added to market uncertainty. According to Pakistan Stock Exchange data, the benchmark KSE-100 Index opened on a bearish note, shedding more than 1,000 points in early trading before touching an intraday low of 161,250.06 points. As of 12:45 pm, the index was trading at 161,744.83 points, down 630.01 points, or 0.39 per cent, from its previous close of 162,374.84 points.

Selling pressure was observed across major sectors, including automobile assemblers, cement, commercial banks, oil and gas exploration companies, oil marketing companies, power generation and refineries. Investors are awaiting the State Bank's Monetary Policy Committee meeting scheduled for Monday, with analysts expecting a 50-basis-point increase in the benchmark policy rate amid concerns over prolonged inflationary pressures and external economic shocks. The market's decline follows five consecutive weeks of losses, with the KSE-100 Index shedding 4,044.84 points, or 2.4 per cent, during the previous week to close at 162,374.84 points.

The weekly decline came amid surging Brent crude prices, a widening trade deficit and persistent geopolitical tensions, which compounded uncertainty over Pakistan's economic outlook, while oil prices eased on Monday from six-month highs ahead of a week packed with central bank meetings and corporate earnings.
