Best Breakfast Habits That Help Reduce Body Weight

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Breakfast is described as the most important meal of the day, and nutrition experts say the choice made at breakfast matters for anyone trying to lose weight. Experts say starting the day with a protein-rich breakfast is better, because protein keeps the feeling of fullness for longer and the body spends more energy digesting it than it does on fats or carbohydrates. Eggs and yoghurt are named as good examples of a protein-rich breakfast.

Many people think it is better to exercise after breakfast, but experts say this is not correct: when exercise is done on an empty stomach, the body begins to burn fat for fuel, though they advise only light exercise rather than intense workout, followed by breakfast within 30 minutes. Experts also note that around 60 percent of the human body is water, and drinking one or two glasses of water before breakfast can help healthy digestion and support weight loss and the burning of belly fat. They further advise keeping at least a 12-hour gap between dinner and breakfast so the body can burn more fat, and eating the first meal of the day slowly and chewing it well.

Green tea can help burn fat, adding ginger to tea can activate the metabolism, and a pinch of cinnamon in tea or coffee can help keep blood sugar levels balanced, though experts stress that none of this brings magical weight loss but can help reach the goal gradually.
