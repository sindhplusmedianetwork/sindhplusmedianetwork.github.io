PIA Announces Direct Islamabad–Birmingham Flights From December 1, Three Times a Week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan International Airlines has announced direct flights between Islamabad and Birmingham starting December 1, expanding its flight operations to the United Kingdom. According to the airline's spokesperson, three flights a week will initially operate on the route, running on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays. The launch of the direct service is expected to ease travel for the large number of Pakistanis living in northern and central parts of the UK.

The airline said the United Kingdom is an extremely important air route for the national carrier, and that steps are being taken to further expand air links between Pakistan and Britain.
