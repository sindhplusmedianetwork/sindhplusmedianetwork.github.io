Jamal Pasha attempted suicide to marry me: Nisho Begum

Senior Pakistani film industry actress Nisho Begum, mother of actress Sahiba, has revealed that she persuaded her husband Jamal Pasha to restore relations with his first wife and children. Nisho Begum said in a recent vlog on her YouTube channel that Jamal Pasha was already married, however he had separated from his first wife. She said Jamal Pasha made various excuses to marry her and even said that if she did not marry him, he would commit suicide.

According to Nisho Begum, she was quite innocent at that time, her first husband had passed away and she also had small children, and she stopped Jamal Pasha from suicide and explained that life is very precious. The actress said that later she helped bring Jamal Pasha's first wife and children back home, because the first wife had no place to stay in her village, and assured the first wife that she would get all rights and would be fully taken care of. Nisho Begum said she initially refused Jamal Pasha's proposal, however after his suicide attempt she gave another chance, and Jamal Pasha had taken pills, after which he was taken to Services Hospital where his stomach was washed.
