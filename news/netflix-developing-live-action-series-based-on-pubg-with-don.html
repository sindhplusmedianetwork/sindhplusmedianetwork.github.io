Netflix developing live-action series based on PUBG with Donald Glover

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Netflix is working on a live-action series based on PUBG, the hit online multiplayer battle royale game, with Donald Glover reported as the star of the show. Glover, the actor known for his work on Community, 30 Rock and the satirical 2016 rap song This Is America, would lead the series, while his brother Stephen will write and executive produce. PUBG, short for PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds, was first released in 2017.

Each match consists of 100 players dropped into an arena who must fight each other, with the last player standing declared the winner. The game enjoys widespread popularity in Pakistan and has been under the microscope more than once over its overtly violent gameplay and the negative effects that may have on its mostly young audience. The game made headlines again last month after Pakistan's five-man national team won a bronze medal in e-sports at the Asian Games in Japan.

Should the project go ahead, it would be part of a line of video game adaptations in the works at Netflix, including a show based on the Assassin's Creed franchise and a movie based on Gears of War. The streaming platform is also said to be working on animated series based on Minecraft and Clash of Clans.
