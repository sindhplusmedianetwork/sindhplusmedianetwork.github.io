Hania Aamir's new look reminds social media users of Aamir Khan's former wife Kiran Rao

Pakistani actress Hania Aamir has become a talking point on social media once again because of her distinctive outfit and new hairstyle. She attended a press event for her upcoming drama series 'Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo', where her unusual fashion style drew the attention of users. At the event, Hania wore knee-length camouflage-print shorts, a white top and a short brown cardigan, while her short curly hair made her look even more distinctive.

When her photos appeared, many users said her style resembled that of Bollywood actor Aamir Khan's former wife and filmmaker Kiran Rao. Some users criticised Hania's outfit and advised her to change her fashion stylist, while others suggested she pay more attention to her choice of clothing herself.
