Scammers Withdraw Crores From Bank Accounts After Blocking Mobile SIMs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fraudsters siphoned millions of rupees from bank accounts in Pakistan by obtaining duplicate mobile SIMs and allegedly misusing victims' biometric data. Among the victims are two members of the National Assembly and a Karachi businessman. Rs1.5 million was withdrawn from the account of MQM MNA Farhan Chishti, while Rs8.5 million was taken from the account of Karachi trader Sunny Kumar.

During one investigation, a bank employee admitted to providing customers' confidential data to another person. The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority imposed total fines of Rs155.6 million on the telecom company involved in two of the cases. The cases have raised serious questions about the security of biometric verification systems and the safeguards around SIM issuance.

Authorities have urged citizens to remain vigilant and report suspicious activity on their accounts immediately.
