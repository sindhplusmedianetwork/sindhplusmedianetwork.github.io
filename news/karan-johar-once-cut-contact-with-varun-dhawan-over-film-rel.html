Karan Johar Once Cut Contact With Varun Dhawan Over Film Release Clash

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Bollywood actor Varun Dhawan has revealed that filmmaker Karan Johar once cut off contact with him over a professional disagreement. Speaking on the show 'Koffee with Karan', Varun said Johar stopped answering his calls and messages after a clash over a film's release date. He said that when both their films were scheduled to release on the same day, Johar stopped responding entirely.

The rift deepened when Varun texted him to stop behaving childishly. Johar gave his side of the story on the show as well. Both stars confirmed that all differences have now been resolved and their relationship is back to normal.
