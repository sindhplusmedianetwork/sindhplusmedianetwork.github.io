Islamabad Gears Up for Key ECO Ministers' Meeting Next Week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The regional security situation and simmering geopolitical tensions, which are hampering greater economic cooperation, are expected to feature prominently at the upcoming Economic Cooperation Organisation Council of Ministers meeting in Islamabad on November 15-16. The gathering will be closely watched as Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi is expected to travel to Pakistan for the meeting, at a time when Islamabad is navigating its diplomatic engagement with Tehran and Washington amid continuing tensions in the Middle East. Araghchi is also expected to hold bilateral meetings with Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar.

The meeting comes against the backdrop of heightened regional tensions that have already affected the organisation's planned activities in Pakistan, including the postponement of an ECO interior ministers' meeting scheduled for late October in Islamabad. Pakistan is chairing the ECO for 2026-27 and is expected to use the opportunity to push its regional economic and connectivity agenda. The ECO was established in 1985 by Pakistan, Iran and Turkiye and expanded in 1992 to include Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan.
