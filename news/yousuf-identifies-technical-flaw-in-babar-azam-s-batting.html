Yousuf Identifies Technical Flaw in Babar Azam's Batting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Pakistan captain Mohammad Yousuf has identified a technical flaw in Babar Azam's batting, saying his open right shoulder is affecting his ability to judge the line and movement of deliveries. Yousuf explained that the flaw in Babar's stance is making it harder for him to read the ball early. Babar has scored 45, 34 and 10 in his three innings of the ongoing ODI tri-series involving Pakistan, England and Sri Lanka.

The former captain's assessment has added to the debate around Babar's recent batting form in the series.
