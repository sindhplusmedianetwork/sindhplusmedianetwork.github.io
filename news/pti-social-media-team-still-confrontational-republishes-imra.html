PTI social media team still confrontational, republishes Imran's 2025 statement

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf, already fighting to regain political ground after losing its Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government, is facing a major challenge from its own social media accounts, writes Ansar Abbasi. On October 26, the party's verified X account republished a statement attributed to founder chairman Imran Khan that used extremely harsh language about the top military leadership. The statement was not new, however — it dated back to 2025 and was republished nearly a year later, at a time when the party had suffered a series of political setbacks.

A senior party leader described the situation as regrettable, noting that such posts are shared without the knowledge of chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan or secretary general Salman Akram Raja. One party leader questioned what purpose republishing the old statement could serve, and whether the party's social media strategy is helping it or further shrinking its room for political engagement. The post surfaced while Imran Khan remains in continuous detention, his three sisters are under arrest, and the party lost its KP government after governor's rule was imposed on October 11.

The article recalls that since the fall of its government in April 2022, the party has faced crackdowns, legal challenges, loss of its electoral symbol and the imprisonment of its founder. It also notes that Barrister Gohar told the Islamabad High Court on October 15 that the PTI does not claim ownership or control of Imran Khan's X account.
