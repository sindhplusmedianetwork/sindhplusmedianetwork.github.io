Islamabad court sentences man to death for raping stepdaughter

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Islamabad court has sentenced Iftikhar Ahmed Khan to death for repeatedly raping his stepdaughter over about a year. The assaults resulted in the birth of a child. The court imposed a fine of Rs0.5 million on the convict.

It also ordered Rs1 million in compensation for the victim and Rs2 million for the child. Additional District and Sessions Judge Muhammad Sohail convicted him under Section 376(1) of the Pakistan Penal Code. A DNA report played a key role in the conviction, establishing a 99.9999996 per cent probability that the convict had fathered the child.
