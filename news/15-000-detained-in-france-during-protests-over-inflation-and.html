15,000 detained in France during protests over inflation and education, minister tells parliament

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 15,000 people have been detained in France in recent weeks during protests over inflation, wages and conditions in educational institutions, the authorities have said. Justice Minister Gerald Darmanin told parliament that 7,184 people were detained since 17 October during protests over inflation and wages, some of which turned into violent clashes. About 8,000 students were also detained since September over protests in schools.

Legal proceedings were initiated against 3,053 of those detained in the cost-of-living protests, sentences have been handed down in 666 cases, and 195 people were sent directly to prison. The figures were presented as the government faces continuing demonstrations over the cost of living and the state of education. The minister said the detentions followed weeks of protests across the country.

The protests have included marches by workers and students demanding action on wages and school conditions.
