Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Universities Face Rs5.6bn Deficit, Seek Rs18bn Federal Grant

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's Higher Education Department has written to the Higher Education Commission chairman about the financial crisis facing the province's public universities. The letter says the universities, which teach about 200,000 students, face a total deficit of Rs5.6 billion. It says grants fell short by Rs18 billion over the past 10 years.

Expenses rose by more than 18 percent during the same period, according to the letter. The department has asked the federation for an Rs18 billion grant. It says the grant should come instead of putting more fee burden on students.
