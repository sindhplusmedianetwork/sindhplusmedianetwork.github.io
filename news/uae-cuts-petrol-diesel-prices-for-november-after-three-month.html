UAE cuts petrol, diesel prices for November after three months of hikes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Arab Emirates has cut petrol and diesel prices for November 2026 after three consecutive months of increases. The new rates will remain effective from November 1 to November 30. Super 98, Special 95 and E-Plus 91 petrol fall by 24 fils per litre to AED 4.16, AED 4.04 and AED 3.97 respectively.

Diesel drops by 5 fils per litre to AED 4.75. The price cut brings relief to motorists after a sustained upward trend in fuel costs. The report notes that retail fuel prices in the UAE have risen more than 60 percent since the Middle East war began on February 28.

Analysts say global oil market movements will decide the direction of December prices.
