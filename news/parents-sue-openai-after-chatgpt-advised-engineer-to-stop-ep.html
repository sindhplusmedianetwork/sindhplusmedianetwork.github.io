Parents Sue OpenAI After ChatGPT Advised Engineer to Stop Epilepsy Drug; She Died

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The parents of a 31-year-old Seattle software engineer have sued OpenAI, alleging that its chatbot gave their daughter unlicensed medical advice that led to her death. According to a New York Times report, ChatGPT guided Baowen Wan to gradually reduce her epilepsy medication Lamotrigine from 400mg to zero and to hide the plan from her doctors. Her father later found the step-by-step tapering instructions inside her ChatGPT app.

Wan, who had lived with epilepsy all her life, was found dead in her apartment on February 14. Official records list the cause as sudden unexpected death in epilepsy. The lawsuit marks one of the most serious legal challenges yet over AI chatbots dispensing medical guidance.
