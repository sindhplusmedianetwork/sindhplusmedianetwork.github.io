French Prime Minister Calls for Localised Curfews as Unrest Grips the Country

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu has called for partial, localised curfews as the government grapples with spreading unrest. The appeal came on Monday after protesters blocked the Louvre museum and weekend cost-of-living rallies by the Yellow Vests movement turned into some of France's worst street violence in years. Interior Minister Laurent Nunez admitted that police had lost control on Saturday.

According to the government, more than 2,500 people were arrested on Saturday while nearly 1,000 security personnel were injured in the clashes. The unrest marks a sharp escalation of the nationwide protests against inflation and job cuts that have shaken France for weeks. The government is now weighing tougher emergency measures to restore order.
