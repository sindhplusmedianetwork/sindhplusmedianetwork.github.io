FO rejects India's remarks on OIC Kashmir resolutions, flags Delhi mosque eviction notice

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read

ISLAMABAD: Pakistan's Foreign Office on Thursday rejected what it called India's 'baseless and unwarranted assertions' regarding the Kashmir resolutions adopted by the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation. FO spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan said the resolutions were adopted at the 52nd session of the OIC Council of Foreign Ministers in Baghdad and reflected the Muslim world's consistent position on the Kashmir dispute. He also drew attention to an eviction notice served on the historic Race Course Mosque in New Delhi, describing it as another sign of 'worsening Islamophobia' in India.

The remarks came during the weekly media briefing at the Foreign Office.
