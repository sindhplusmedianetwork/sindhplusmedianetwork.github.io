Dia Mirza's post about Nawazuddin Siddiqui stirs debate online

Indian actress Dia Mirza has surprised fans with a social media post about actor Nawazuddin Siddiqui. She wrote that working with him was like a nightmare, adding that he lacked manners and had a very inappropriate attitude. Nawazuddin responded that finding fault in someone's behaviour is easy, but they should try finding fault in their work.

The exchange has divided social media users. Some called it a real dispute, while others saw it as a publicity stunt for a new project. Indian media report the two actors may appear together in the upcoming medical drama Doctor Hasan, in which Nawazuddin plays a strict doctor.
