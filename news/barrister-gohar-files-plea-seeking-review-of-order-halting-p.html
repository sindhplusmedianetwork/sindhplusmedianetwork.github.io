Barrister Gohar files plea seeking review of order halting Peshawar High Court constitutional petitions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan has filed an important plea in the Federal Constitutional Court over the matter of halting the hearing of constitutional petitions in the Peshawar High Court. The constitutional plea has been filed through advocates Shah Faisal Ilyas and Ali Zaman, and it has been filed to become a party to the case pending in the Peshawar High Court. The plea takes the position that Barrister Gohar Ali Khan and the other petitioners should be made parties to the case and their stance should also be heard.

It says the court order of 5 November affected the proceedings of constitutional petitions pending in the Peshawar High Court. The plea requests the Federal Constitutional Court to review its 5 November order and allow the Peshawar High Court to hear and decide the pending constitutional petitions in accordance with the Constitution and the law. It further requests that the protection of citizens' fundamental rights and access to justice be ensured.
