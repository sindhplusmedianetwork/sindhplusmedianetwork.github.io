Trump says US still has to 'take care' of one or two targets in Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said the United States has destroyed much of Iran's military and nuclear programme but still has to 'take care of' one or two remaining targets, naming a suspected underground nuclear complex he referred to as Pickaxe Mountain. Speaking to reporters at his Florida residence, he said the action 'can do that in 10 minutes', while Iran has denied any nuclear work takes place there. The president maintained that talks with Tehran were 'going fine' but added that the Iranians were 'not very reliable'.

His remarks came as Iran's national security chief Mohsen Rezaei said the country's seven conditions must be fulfilled for diplomacy to proceed, warning of 'devastating blows' with new weaponry if Iran is attacked. Rezaei also claimed, in comments carried by state media, that Saudi Arabia's defence minister had privately warned that Israel would 'target Turkiye and then Saudi Arabia after Iran'. Separately, Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Trump had not yet decided on fresh strikes, while Tehran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi said Iranian and Qatari mediators had agreed to shorten the implementation of a proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz to two days.
