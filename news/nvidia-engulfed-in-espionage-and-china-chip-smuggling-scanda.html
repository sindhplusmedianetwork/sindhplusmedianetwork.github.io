Nvidia Engulfed in Espionage and China Chip-Smuggling Scandal After Huang's Resignation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nvidia is facing a deepening scandal over alleged espionage and the illegal supply of advanced chips to China after its founder Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive on November 12. Colette Kress has been appointed interim chief executive as the controversy around the company intensifies. US officials suspect that advanced American chips reached China through the Singapore-based firm MegaSpeed despite export controls, though the company denies any illegal activity.

The affair has also exposed divisions inside the Trump administration, with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent favouring sanctions on MegaSpeed while the Commerce Department has delayed action. The scandal centres on claims of espionage, personal relationships and the unlawful movement of sensitive technology. Nvidia has rejected the allegations of wrongdoing as the investigation and political debate around the case continue.
