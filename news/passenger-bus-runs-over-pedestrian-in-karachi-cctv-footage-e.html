Passenger Bus Runs Over Pedestrian in Karachi, CCTV Footage Emerges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man lost his life on Saturday night after a passenger bus ran over him on Daudpota Road in Karachi's Saddar area. Express News obtained the CCTV footage of the accident, which clearly shows the bus crushing the pedestrian who was standing at the roadside. The deceased was identified as Hassan Ali, a resident of Lucky Star, Saddar, and a father of five children.

A large crowd of citizens gathered at the scene following the incident. Police took the bus involved in the accident and its driver into custody, and further legal proceedings are under way.
