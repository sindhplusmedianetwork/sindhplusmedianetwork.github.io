Vessel carrying 51,000 tonnes of imported wheat arrives at Karachi Port

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 51,000 metric tonnes of imported wheat arrived at Karachi Port on Wednesday, according to the Karachi Port Trust. The geared vessel MV Gokova M, carrying a declared manifest of 51,000 metric tonnes of wheat, was allocated berths 16 and 17 and arrived at the port at 12:25 pm on 28 October. Another geared vessel is expected to arrive on 3 November, and together the two vessels represent a declared wheat cargo volume of over 110,000 metric tonnes, the port trust said.

The trust said it is facilitating the handling of the bulk wheat cargo at Karachi Gateway Terminal Multipurpose Limited, reinforcing the port's role in supporting essential commodity imports. Separately, Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar chaired a meeting of the Steering Committee on Wheat on Wednesday, which evaluated overall grain availability and examined import plans aimed at keeping local supplies steady. Dar stressed that maintaining sufficient stocks and preventing any disruption in grain supply remain a top priority, and directed the authorities to monitor market trends closely and ensure that the supply of wheat is adequate and flour prices move downward.
