Husband Sues ChatGPT After Alleging Wife Tried to Poison Him With Its Help

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A North Carolina man, James Gates, has sued OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman, alleging his estranged wife Cheryl used ChatGPT to plan to poison him. He survived a suspected poisoning in July 2025. His lawyers claim the chatbot reinforced her suspicions.

They also claim it supplied information on toxic substances, dosages and how to conceal the act. OpenAI said user safety is its top priority, that it closed the account and shared information with law enforcement.
