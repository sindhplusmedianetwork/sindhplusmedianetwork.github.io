Vivo Launches Y32 in Pakistan With 8,000mAh Battery, Priced at Rs82,999

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Vivo has introduced the Y32 smartphone in Pakistan, designed for busy lifestyles with a long-lasting battery. The phone packs a large 8,000mAh BlueVolt battery supported by 44W fast charging and reverse charging, along with battery-health technology that keeps at least 80 percent capacity after 1,500 charge cycles. It carries IP69-rated dust and water resistance, Titan Glass protection and an SGS five-star premium performance mark for drop resistance, and has cleared military-standard shock tests.

The rear camera module has a metallic finish styled like a volcanic crater, and the phone comes in three colours: Sunset Orange, Twilight Red and Meteor White, with a side-mounted fingerprint sensor. The 6GB RAM with 128GB storage version is priced at Rs82,999, while the 6GB RAM with 256GB storage version costs Rs99,999. The phone is available from today through Vivo's official online store, authorised online partners and retail outlets across the country.
