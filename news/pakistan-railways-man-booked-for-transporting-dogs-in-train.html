Pakistan Railways Man Booked for Transporting Dogs in Train's Power Van in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Railway police have booked a Pakistan Railways employee after two dogs were found being transported illegally, without booking, in the power van of the Allama Iqbal Express (9-Up) during checking at Raiwind Railway Station on Friday. Officials said the train's power plant operator, Mohammad Khan, allegedly charged Rs5,000 to transport the dogs from Karachi. The animals were to be handed over to a person at Kot Lakhpat Railway Station.

Police registered a case against the operator for violating railway regulations. The dogs were subsequently booked, with a fine of Rs7,690 deposited into the railway's account.
