Petition filed in Federal Constitutional Court for recovery of Sohail Afridi, Babar Saleem Swati and Junaid Akbar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A constitutional petition has been filed in the Federal Constitutional Court seeking the recovery of Sohail Afridi, Babar Saleem Swati and MNA Junaid Akbar Khan. According to Dunya News, the petition was filed by MNA Shandana Gulzar under Article 175E(3) of the Constitution, making the interior ministry, the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief secretary and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa inspector general of police respondents. The petition raises important questions about the constitutional limits of the powers under Articles 232, 234 and 245 of the Constitution, with the petitioner contending that these provisions do not authorise the unlawful detention of any person.

It asks the court to order that Sohail Afridi, Babar Saleem Swati and Junaid Akbar Khan be produced before the court and to direct the authorities concerned to disclose at once the whereabouts of the three leaders and the reasons for their detention. The petition further seeks orders that the three leaders be given access to lawyers and their families, medical facilities and protection from torture. The petitioner contends that action taken for political purposes is a misuse of powers and a violation of fundamental rights, and asks the court to take notice of the matter and bind the authorities to act in accordance with the law.
