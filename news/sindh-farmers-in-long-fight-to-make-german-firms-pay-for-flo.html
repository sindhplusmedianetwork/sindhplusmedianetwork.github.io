Sindh Farmers in Long Fight to Make German Firms Pay for Flood Damage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khoso and 38 other farmers from Pakistan's southern Sindh province, who were devastated by the 2022 flooding, are suing German energy firm RWE and cement maker Heidelberg Materials in a rare bid to make big polluters pay damages for climate impacts. The litigation argues that the companies' emissions contributed to climate change and turbo-charged the rain that wiped out their crops, livestock and homes. The floods hit a third of the country, killed 1,700 people and affected 30 million Pakistanis.

The plaintiffs estimate that the two firms contributed 0.68 percent and 0.12 percent to global industrial emissions respectively. Both firms deny wrongdoing and call the claims unfounded. The case is a long-running fight by the farmers to hold major polluters responsible for the damage caused by the floods.
