PSX jumps 5,449 points in spectacular rally after holiday break

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange staged a powerful rally on Tuesday, with the KSE-100 index surging 5,448.56 points, or 3.13 percent, to close at 179,335.24 after reopening following the Iqbal Day holiday. Strong remittance inflows, falling international oil prices and easing Middle East tensions drove across-the-board buying. UBL, Lucky Cement and Meezan Bank were among the top contributors to the rise.

Trading volumes fell to 972.4 million shares worth Rs50.7 billion. Foreign investors bought shares worth Rs1.68 billion during the session.
