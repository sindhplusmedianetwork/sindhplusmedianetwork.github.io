Online gambling network busted, five arrested, Rs240 billion transactions uncovered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The NCCIA exposed a major online gambling and betting app network in Karachi. Investigators uncovered Rs240 billion in transactions and arrested five suspects. Additional Director Shahzad Akbar said 38 bank accounts and 38 companies are under investigation.

Total financial activity is feared to reach Rs1,000 billion. Assets worth Rs1 billion were frozen in the operation. Cash worth Rs7.3 million was recovered from an arrested suspect.
