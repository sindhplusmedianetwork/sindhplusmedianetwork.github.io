NADRA Assistant Director Murder Case: Major Progress as Probe Reveals Shooter Trailed Victim

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Investigators have made major progress in the murder case of NADRA Assistant Director Fahad Khan in Peshawar. The investigation team said the shooter followed the assistant director from his home to the NADRA office, riding as a passenger on a motorcycle. The main accused followed Fahad Khan's car to the NADRA office with his companion, while the victim was followed from University Town.

Fahad Khan travelled in a bulletproof vehicle to Hayatabad, where the attackers allegedly killed him over enmity. Police said the crime scene had been visited for reconnaissance several times, and the suspects' identity cards have been blocked. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has issued instructions for the arrest of the suspects and to stop them from fleeing abroad.
