Israel Deports Pro-Palestine Activists, Frees Arab Journalist

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israel said on Sunday it had deported 14 activists who were detained while visiting the occupied West Bank for solidarity missions with Palestinians facing violence from Israeli settlers. The activists had come from the United States, Canada, Italy and Germany with hopes of protecting Palestinians during their annual olive harvest, which has been hit by a surge of attacks by Israeli settlers. Israeli authorities arrested them near Ramallah on Friday after an altercation with settlers.

Meanwhile, Israeli authorities on Sunday released without charge an Arab-Israeli journalist who had been detained for five days over an interview he conducted in Qatar. The deportations and the release both took place on Sunday, according to the report.
