Imran Khan Likely to Be Shifted to Private Hospital in Coming Days, Says Geo News

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI founder Imran Khan is likely to be shifted to a private hospital in the coming days, according to a Geo News report. The private hospital administration has already been informed about the possible transfer, though it is too early to say for how many days he would be admitted. The report follows a meeting on November 9 between Barrister Gohar, Senator Ali Zafar and Salman Safdar and an important judicial figure, during which Khan's health and access to him were discussed.

After that meeting, orders were issued for two lawyers to meet Khan, and his late-night transfer to PIMS hospital was also said to be a result of the same meeting. Sources say Barrister Gohar Khan was aware of the PIMS transfer, and the possibility of moving Khan to a private hospital is now under consideration. Sources also say the release of his sisters is under consideration, with their release possible in the coming days, while Bushra Bibi may also be shifted to hospital within a few days for a complete check-up after Professor Dr Nadeem Qureshi returns from abroad.

According to sources, a meeting between Barrister Salman Safdar and Latif Khosa and Imran Khan on the day covered only legal and political matters.
