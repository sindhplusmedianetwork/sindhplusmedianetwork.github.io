Patrick Stewart's cause of death confirmed in official certificate weeks after death

Patrick Stewart's official cause of death has been confirmed. The veteran actor, known for his roles in the Star Trek franchise and Marvel's X-Men films, died at the age of 86 on Thursday, October 28. At the time, his long-time spokesperson Kelly Bush Novak announced that the actor had died following a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia.

On Thursday, November 19, details of his death certificate were shared, confirming Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia as the reason for his death. The certificate also lists cardiopulmonary arrest and a neurocognitive disorder with Lewy body, a progressive form of dementia that affects memory, thinking and movement. In the October 29 statement, Bush Novak said Stewart had died the previous day at his home in Los Angeles in the loving care of his wife Sunny.

The update came days after Stewart's wife Sunny Ozell penned an emotional tribute to her late husband expressing grief and gratitude. Stewart's final screen appearance will be reprising the role of Charles Xavier in the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday, set for release on December 18.
