Maryam Nawaz Announces 10 Measures for Farmers, Own Kisan Bank

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has announced 10 major measures for the province's farmers, including the establishment of the province's first own Kisan Bank for their financial support. Under the package, loans of up to Rs10 million will carry zero markup, loans of up to Rs30 million will carry 2 per cent interest, and loans of up to Rs50 million will carry 5 per cent interest. The per-acre loan limit under the Kisan Card has been raised from Rs30,000 to Rs33,500 in view of rising fertiliser prices, and Rs17 billion in subsidy loans will also be given to farmers.

The chief minister said the wheat support price of Rs4,500 per maund decided under the prime minister will be implemented, and a campaign against water theft will continue along with canal cleaning measures. She announced a special package and subsidy to promote olive cultivation, agro-processing parks in Sargodha, Bhakkar, Khanewal and Okara, and 400 mini dams in Potohar with modern irrigation systems for farmers. Under the Green Tractor Scheme, 25,000 subsidised tractors have already been provided and the programme will be expanded to 50,000 tractors in total, while Rs70 billion has been allocated for a high-tech mechanisation programme.

The chief minister also said 1,500 agriculture interns are working in the field at a monthly stipend of Rs70,000, and appealed to farmers to avoid burning crop residue to help end smog.
