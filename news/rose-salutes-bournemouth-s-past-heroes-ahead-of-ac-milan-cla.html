Rose Salutes Bournemouth's Past Heroes Ahead of AC Milan Clash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bournemouth manager Marco Rose has saluted the heroes who preserved the club's Football League status in the 2008/09 season, as his side prepares to face AC Milan in the Europa League. Rose spoke warmly about the team that kept Bournemouth in the league during a difficult chapter of its history. Milan, their opponents, are coached by former Manchester United boss Ruben Amorim.

Bournemouth are playing in the Europa League for the first time and began their campaign with victories over Real Sociedad and Sturm Graz. On Sunday, they beat Sunderland to hand Rose his first Premier League win in charge. The Milan clash is set to be one of the biggest nights in the club's history.
