FIA arrests two in Karachi hawala-hundi network raid, recovers Saudi and UAE currency

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The FIA Commercial Banks Circle Karachi raided an office of the HMS Institute of Technical Skills on the ground floor of Arab Tower in Musa Lane, Lyari. Two men were arrested in the raid, Yasir son of Abdul Ghani and Abdul Ghani son of Abdul Razzaq. The raid recovered Rs600,000 in cash, 10,000 UAE dirhams and 16,500 Saudi riyals.

Officials also seized cheque books, three mobile phones and a laptop. The laptop contained digital evidence of alleged illegal hawala-hundi transactions. A case has been registered against the arrested men and they have been moved to the Commercial Banks Circle for further legal proceedings.

The FIA said investigations to trace other members of the network are under way.
