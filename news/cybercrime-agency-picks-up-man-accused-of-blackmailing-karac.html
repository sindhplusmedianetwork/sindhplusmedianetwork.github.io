Cybercrime agency picks up man accused of blackmailing Karachi woman with private photos

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency's Karachi zone has arrested a man accused of befriending a woman on the pretext of marriage and then blackmailing her with obscene photos and videos. The accused, Shoaib Nawaz Afridi, was picked up near Millennium Mall in Gulistan-e-Johar, Karachi. According to the victim's statement to the agency, she had rented a car from the accused, who gained her trust and befriended her before creating the private images and videos.

She alleged that he later used the material to harass and blackmail her. Investigators have registered case number 111/2026 against the accused under sections 20, 21 and 24 of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, along with section 109 of the Pakistan Penal Code. Officials said further investigation into the case is underway.
