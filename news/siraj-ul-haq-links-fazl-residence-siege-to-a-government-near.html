Siraj-ul-Haq links Fazl residence siege to a government nearing its end

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami leader Siraj-ul-Haq has said that when a government's end draws near, it starts suppressing opposing political voices and stripping the opposition of constitutional and legal rights. Writing on X, he said the siege of Maulana Fazlur Rehman's residence was an example of this style of governance. He linked the episode to a wider pattern in which governments under pressure turn against their critics.

Siraj-ul-Haq also pointed to the Sharif family's own political history, noting that it had been in opposition and spent a long time outside the country. His remarks framed the siege as a political act rather than an isolated incident. The statement adds to the continuing debate over the treatment of opposition leaders and the space available for dissent.
