Chelsea Beaten 3-2 at Nottingham Forest as Pressure Grows on Alonso

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelsea suffered a 3-2 defeat at Nottingham Forest on Friday, piling more pressure on manager Xabi Alonso. Igor Jesus opened the scoring for Forest before Morgan Gibbs-White struck twice, including a penalty, to put the hosts 3-1 ahead. Emmanuel Emegha scored twice for Chelsea, but his late goals were not enough to rescue a point.

It was Chelsea's fourth defeat in five Premier League matches and their third in a row. Chelsea now sit eighth in the table after a sixth defeat in 12 league games under Alonso, a run that is likely to fuel speculation about the manager's future.
