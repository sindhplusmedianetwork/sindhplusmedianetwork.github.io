Messages being exchanged between US and Iran via Pakistan and Qatar, says Iranian ambassador

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's ambassador to Pakistan, Reza Amiri Moghadam, has said that messages are being exchanged between the United States and Iran through Pakistan and Qatar. In an interview with American media, he said various proposals and views were being shared with the foreign ministers of Pakistan and Qatar. The ambassador said Iran was ready for negotiations, but would respond if it was attacked.

He also welcomed the efforts of China and Russia to bring the war in Yemen to an end. He added that Iran would welcome efforts by China or any other country to end the civil war by recognising the Houthis as a party to the conflict. The remarks come as tensions between Washington and Tehran remain high over the naval blockade of Iranian ports and the wider conflict in the region.
