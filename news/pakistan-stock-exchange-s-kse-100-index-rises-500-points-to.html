Pakistan Stock Exchange's KSE-100 index rises 500 points to close at 177,873.88

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange witnessed a bullish trend on Friday, with the benchmark KSE-100 Index gaining 500.11 points, or 0.28 percent, to close at 177,873.88 points. The index closed higher against 177,373.76 points recorded in the previous session. Market capitalisation rose to Rs19.336 trillion during the session.

Of the 497 companies traded in the ready market, 284 posted gains. Trading volume in the ready market stood at 1,042.810 million shares worth Rs31.001 billion. The session reflected broad buying interest across the market as the benchmark index ended the day in positive territory.
