France Faces Economic Strain and Political Tensions as Protests Enter Third Straight Week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

France has entered its third consecutive week of protests over inflation, the cost of living, the education system, public services and government policies. Around 150 demonstrations are expected across the country. Authorities have deployed nearly 70,000 police and gendarmes nationwide to manage the rallies.

Interior Minister Laurent Nunez has warned that vandalism, looting or violence will not be tolerated. Metro closures and shut railway stations forced protesters to travel long distances to reach the rallies in Paris.
