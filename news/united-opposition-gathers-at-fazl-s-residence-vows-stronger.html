'United Opposition' Gathers at Fazl's Residence, Vows Stronger Alliance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Opposition leaders gathered at the Islamabad residence of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, pledging to build a stronger alliance. The leaders agreed to improve parliamentary coordination among opposition parties. They formed committees to resolve mutual differences and to consult on convening a multi-party conference.

The meeting also discussed the imposition of governor's rule and emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. PTI leaders raised alarm over Imran Khan's reported blood pressure spike in prison. The huddle marked a renewed show of unity among opposition parties.

Leaders announced plans for a countrywide movement against what they called an undemocratic government.
