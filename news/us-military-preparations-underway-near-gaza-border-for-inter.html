US Military Preparations Underway Near Gaza Border for International Force

US military preparations are underway near the Gaza border ahead of the planned deployment of an international stabilisation force inside the enclave, Israeli media reported on Monday. Israeli news site Walla said Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's government had given the US approval for an initial deployment of around 250 foreign troops, with Indonesia, Morocco, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Uganda and Albania among countries willing to take part. US troops and foreign officers have been seen near the border.

An Israeli base in the south has been converted into a secured US facility. Work is also advancing on a UAE-backed project to build a city on part of Rafah for tens of thousands of screened Palestinians.
