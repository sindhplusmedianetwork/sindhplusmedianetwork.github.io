Sindh forms JIT to probe Mir Raza murder case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has formed a joint investigation team to probe the murder case of Mir Raza. The move follows the recommendations of a judicial commission on the case, and the government has begun implementing them. The JIT will be headed by DIG Irfan Ali Baloch.

Senior representatives of the ISI, the Intelligence Bureau, Sindh Rangers and the NCCIA will also be part of the team. Two more investigation committees have been formed to determine the role of police officers and personnel in the case. Officials said the team would conduct a thorough probe and submit its findings to the government.
