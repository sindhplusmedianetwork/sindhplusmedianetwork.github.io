Brazilian Club Santos Appoints Neymar's Father as New CEO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brazilian football club Santos has appointed star footballer Neymar's father, Neymar Sr, as the club's new chief executive officer (CEO). The club confirmed the appointment on Friday, while president Marcelo Teixeira expressed hope that his arrival would improve the club's administrative capacity. Neymar Sr manages his son's career and is the founder of the NR Sports agency, which holds the commercial rights to Neymar's name and images.

According to Brazilian media, his contract will run until December, but he may stay in the post if Marcelo Teixeira is re-elected in the presidential elections next month. The club president said steps are being taken to solve the problems facing Santos and improve its administrative structure, and Neymar Sr's appointment is part of that effort. He added that he hopes Neymar Sr's experience will help make the club stronger.
