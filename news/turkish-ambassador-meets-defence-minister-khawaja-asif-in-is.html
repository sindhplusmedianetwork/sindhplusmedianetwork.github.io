Turkish Ambassador Meets Defence Minister Khawaja Asif in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkiye's Ambassador to Pakistan, Irfan Neziroglu, has met Defence Minister Khawaja Muhammad Asif at his office in the federal capital. The meeting discussed matters of mutual interest and further strengthening bilateral defence cooperation between the two countries. According to 24News, both sides discussed further promoting the longstanding brotherly relations between Pakistan and Turkiye and the possibilities of increasing cooperation in different sectors.

On the occasion, Defence Minister Khawaja Muhammad Asif said Pakistan was committed to further deepening and strengthening its relations with Turkiye in all areas of mutual interest. The meeting also stressed the importance of further strengthening friendly relations between the two countries and promoting cooperation in the defence sector.
