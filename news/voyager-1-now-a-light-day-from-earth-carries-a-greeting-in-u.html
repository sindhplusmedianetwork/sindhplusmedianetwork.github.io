Voyager 1, now a light-day from Earth, carries a greeting in Urdu for any alien life

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft has now travelled more than one light-day away from Earth, continuing its long journey through interstellar space. The spacecraft, which has been travelling for decades, has now crossed a distance that light itself takes a full day to cover. On board, Voyager 1 carries a 12-inch gold-plated disc known as the Golden Record, prepared as a message from humanity to any extraterrestrial life that might one day find it.

The disc contains greetings and messages from the people of Earth in 55 languages, including Urdu, recorded so that whoever finds the spacecraft can hear the voices of our planet. Alongside the greetings, the Golden Record also carries music and sounds from Earth, offering a portrait of life on our world. The Urdu greeting means that the language of millions of people in Pakistan and around the world is travelling with the spacecraft into the depths of space.

As Voyager 1 moves ever farther from home, it remains a symbol of human curiosity and of the hope that our message may one day be heard beyond the stars.
