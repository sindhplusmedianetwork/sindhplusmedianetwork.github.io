GB CM Presses Islamabad for Interim Provincial Status and NFC Share

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Minister Amjad Hussain Advocate is pressing Islamabad to grant the region interim provincial status and a due share in the National Finance Commission Award. The demand reflects the Gilgit-Baltistan Assembly's unanimous resolution seeking a constitutional amendment declaring the region an interim province without prejudicing Pakistan's position on the Kashmir dispute or UN resolutions. The resolution calls for GB residents to get the same rights as citizens of other provinces, including representation in the National Assembly, the Senate and other federal constitutional institutions.

It also asks that until provisional status is granted, GB be included in the NFC Award on the pattern of Azad Jammu and Kashmir so the region receives a fair share of national resources and development funding. CM Amjad Hussain has said the region faces a financial shortfall of around Rs17 billion against an estimated requirement of Rs320 billion, and under the proposed arrangement the federal government would provide Rs250 billion. Meanwhile a high-level committee formed by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and chaired by Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal is examining GB's financial, governance and development issues, including a resource-sharing mechanism based on NFC principles.
