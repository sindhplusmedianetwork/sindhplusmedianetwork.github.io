Climate finance must go beyond green investment, says SBP governor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

State Bank Governor Jameel Ahmad has said that climate finance must go beyond green investments. He said it should help communities anticipate climate risks and build resilience. He noted that climate shocks hit small farmers and low-income households the hardest.

He was speaking at the inauguration of a four-day joint learning programme of the State Bank and AFI in Islamabad. He recalled that the 2022 floods caused $15 billion in physical damage and another $15 billion in economic losses. He said financial inclusion in Pakistan had reached 71 percent.
