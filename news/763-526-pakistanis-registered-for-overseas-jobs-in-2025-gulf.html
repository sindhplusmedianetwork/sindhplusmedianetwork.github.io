763,526 Pakistanis Registered for Overseas Jobs in 2025, Gulf Remains Top Destination

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A total of 763,526 Pakistanis registered for overseas employment in 2025, compared with 476,238 during January-October 2026, according to the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment. Average monthly registrations declined from around 63,627 in 2025 to 47,624 in the first 10 months of 2026, a fall of 25.2 percent. Labourers accounted for the largest share with 465,724 registrations in 2025 and 293,401 in January-October 2026, followed by drivers with 163,718 and 94,479 respectively.

The figures also included 5,947 engineers, 3,883 doctors and 5,659 accountants in 2025, alongside nurses, teachers, technicians and skilled trades. Saudi Arabia remained the leading destination with 530,437 registrations in 2025 and 294,297 in the first 10 months of 2026, while the United Arab Emirates recorded 52,664 and 53,018 respectively. Together, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Bahrain and Kuwait accounted for about 86 percent of registrations in January-October 2026, compared with around 91 percent in 2025.

Registrations for Türkiye rose from 5,288 in 2025 to 10,284 in the first 10 months of 2026, while of 220,170 foreign job vacancies reported, 106,978 remained available and 113,192 had been filled.
