Lampard Says Hair Pull Warranted Coventry Penalty

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Coventry City manager Frank Lampard said a hair pull on Caleb Yirenkyi warranted a penalty as he questioned three refereeing decisions in Friday's 3-1 Premier League defeat at Everton. Thierno Barry scored twice and Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall added a late third as the hosts recovered after Yann Gboho had put Coventry ahead early in the second half. Lampard was left frustrated as he felt Coventry should have been awarded a first-half penalty when James Tarkowski pulled Yirenkyi's hair while challenging for the ball, but referee Stuart Attwell rejected their appeals.

The rules on hair-pulling have been relaxed this season, with officials also considering intent and the degree of force when deciding whether an offence has been committed. Lampard told reporters that officials were saying it was not enough of a pull, adding that it was a hard area to judge. The defeat left Coventry reflecting on a match they had led, while Everton took all three points at home.
