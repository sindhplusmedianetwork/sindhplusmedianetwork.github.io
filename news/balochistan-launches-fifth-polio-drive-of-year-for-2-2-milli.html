Balochistan launches fifth polio drive of year for 2.2 million children as Afghan spillover feared

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

QUETTA: Balochistan on Monday launched its fifth seven-day anti-polio campaign of the year, targeting more than 2.2 million children across 31 districts amid fears of virus spillover from neighbouring Afghanistan. Health Secretary Mujeebur Rehman announced the launch, warning that more than 50 polio cases reported in Afghanistan this year had increased the risk of cross-border transmission, particularly in Chaman and Qila Abdullah. Speaking at the inauguration in Quetta, he said over 8,880 vaccination teams had been deployed to give polio drops to children under five.

He stressed that frequent movement of people across the border could facilitate the spread, making stronger surveillance and targeted vaccination essential. The secretary said Balochistan had reported no polio cases in the past 24 months and environmental samples collected last month tested negative, but he cautioned against complacency. He warned that malnourished children could be especially vulnerable if the virus re-entered the province.

He urged parents, teachers, religious scholars and community leaders to support the campaign and ensure no eligible child is missed.
