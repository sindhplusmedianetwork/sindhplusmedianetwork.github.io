PTI Moves Peshawar High Court Against KP Emergency, Governor's Rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf has approached the Peshawar High Court to challenge the imposition of emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Party chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan filed the petition on Wednesday, seeking restoration of the province's elected government and annulment of the presidential proclamation issued on October 11. The 86-page constitutional petition argues the measures are unconstitutional and violate several articles of the Constitution, including Articles 232 and 234.

It contends that no prior resolution seeking an emergency was moved, debated or passed by the provincial assembly, as the Constitution requires. The petition also challenges the deployment of army troops, the formation of an 11-member technocratic advisory council and the removal of Chief Minister Sohail Afridi. PTI leader Salman Akram Raja said the party was also pursuing the courts to establish the chief minister's whereabouts.

Separately, National Democratic Movement leaders Mohsin Dawar and Bushra Gohar filed a similar petition in the court.
