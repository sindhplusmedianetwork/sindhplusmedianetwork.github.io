Two Americans Jailed for Life by Caribbean Court Over Murder of Canadian Entrepreneur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two American men were sentenced to life in prison on the Caribbean island of Dominica for the 2023 murder of Canadian tech entrepreneur Daniel Langlois and his partner Dominique Marchand, local media reported. Langlois and Marchand were killed on December 1, 2023, in what the judge described as 'an unprovoked attack'. The judge said the case was 'the rarest of the rare'.

The court in the capital, Roseau, gave Jonathan Lehrer and Robert Snyder Jr two consecutive life sentences each. The pair had been convicted on July 28 this year. Their lawyer has said they will appeal.
