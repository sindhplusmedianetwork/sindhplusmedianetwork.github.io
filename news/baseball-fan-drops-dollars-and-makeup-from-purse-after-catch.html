Baseball fan drops dollars and makeup from purse after catching home run ball

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A baseball fan in Chicago caught a home run ball and lost items from her purse while celebrating. Emma Rodriguez, 24, caught the ball from Jake Rogers' first career walk-off home run for the Chicago White Sox against the New York Mets. While jumping in celebration, money, lip gloss and other items fell from her purse.

She later received a signed bat, ball and jersey from Rogers. The items were given to her in exchange for the home run ball.
