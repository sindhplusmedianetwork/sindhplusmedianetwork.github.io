Punjab Assembly Speaker Malik Muhammad Ahmad Khan Calls on CM Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Assembly Speaker Malik Muhammad Ahmad Khan called on Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz in Lahore. The two discussed development and public welfare projects, and the Speaker briefed the Chief Minister on assembly affairs. He congratulated her on the success of the Apna Chhat Apna Ghar programme and praised the Honehar Scholarship and laptop schemes, as well as the beautification plan in Kasur.

Maryam Nawaz said uniform development of every city and village is her resolve. She noted that over 45,000 km of roads have been built or rehabilitated in the province. She added that cath labs, cancer centres and children's hospitals are being brought to the districts.
