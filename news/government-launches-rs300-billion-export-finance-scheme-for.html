Government Launches Rs300 Billion Export Finance Scheme for Small Businesses

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has launched a Rs300 billion export finance scheme exclusively for small and medium enterprises. Agricultural SMEs will also be eligible for financing under the scheme. Under the new policy, only businesses that meet the official definition of a small or medium enterprise can obtain loans.

The separate category for new borrowers has been abolished. The Finance Ministry said the Export-Import Bank of Pakistan has directed partner commercial banks to implement the new policy strictly. The scheme aims to increase exports from small businesses and make loans easier for them to obtain.
