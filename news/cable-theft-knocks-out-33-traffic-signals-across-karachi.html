Cable Theft Knocks Out 33 Traffic Signals Across Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The theft of cables and other equipment has rendered 33 traffic signals dysfunctional across Karachi, worsening traffic congestion on major roads. After targeting iron railings, suspected drug addicts have now turned to traffic signals, damaging critical urban infrastructure. The Karachi Development Authority's Traffic Engineering Bureau says 94 signals are installed across the city, but repeated thefts have left many out of service with no effective preventive measures in place.

Road cameras have largely been used to issue fines rather than to identify the culprits. Bureau sources said repairs had been suspended for over a year due to funding shortages, with more than Rs10 million in contractor payments still outstanding. The Sindh government later allocated Rs100 million to the Karachi commissioner's office, enabling the repair of 20 signals — though cables were stolen again from seven of them, including Ziauddin Road near Lily Bridge, Boat Basin Chowrangi and Sir Syed Khalid bin Walid Road.
