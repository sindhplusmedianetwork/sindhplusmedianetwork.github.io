Ukraine, US and Europe discuss ways to tempt Russia into talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ukrainian, US and European negotiators have held their first talks together on future negotiations with Russia, which is receiving more ballistic missiles from North Korea, President Volodymyr Zelensky said on Monday. Zelensky said US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff joined the online talks and that for the first time, Europe was represented by those who will represent it in negotiations with the Russians. France, Britain, Germany, Poland, Italy and European Union negotiators have been chosen to represent the continent at future talks with Russia, and the five countries, the EU and a Nato representative were involved in the latest talks with the US envoys.

Zelensky said the conversation was not easy, but there is a result, without giving details, while Kushner confirmed the talks, which included national security advisers from the European nations, and insisted that progress was being made to end the war. Kushner said much of the focus of the talks had been on ways to facilitate direct engagement between Ukraine and Russia, adding that the exchanges were helping explore potential solutions and narrow the remaining gaps, though important challenges remain and a final agreement will require difficult decisions. Zelensky warned that Russia was receiving dozens of missiles per month from North Korea, calling on European allies to send more weapons so that Ukraine can respond on equal terms, and he noted that Europe has missiles that can deliver truly effective strikes.

The Ukrainian leader also warned his countrymen of possible incoming Russian strikes, urging them to pay attention to missile danger alerts, and said that for every evil from Russia there will be a just response from Ukraine.
