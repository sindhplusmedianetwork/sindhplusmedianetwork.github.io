Farming leads PM's youth loan drive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Farming leads the prime minister's youth loan drive, the report said. The maximum loan size under the scheme has been raised to Rs25 million. A target of Rs292 billion has been set for disbursement under the drive.

Officials said agriculture-related businesses are the biggest beneficiaries of the scheme. The figures place farming at the front of the youth loan programme. The scheme's focus on agriculture means farm-related businesses account for the largest share of its benefits, officials said.
