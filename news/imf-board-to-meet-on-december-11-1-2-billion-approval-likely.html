IMF board to meet on December 11; $1.2 billion approval likely for Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Executive Board of the International Monetary Fund will meet on December 11 to consider the release of the next loan tranche for Pakistan. Finance ministry sources say there is a strong likelihood of overall approval of $1.2 billion for Pakistan at the meeting. Pakistan completed talks with the IMF mission in October, during which matters under the standby arrangement were settled.

The fourth review under the country's Extended Fund Facility programme was also held in October, and its completion is expected to bring the fifth tranche of $1 billion. Talks were also held for the third review under the Resilience and Sustainability Facility, which deals with climate change, under which Pakistan is likely to receive a fourth tranche of $210 million. The expected approvals would provide fresh support to the country's economy as it moves from stability towards sustainable growth.
