Real Madrid's Record Signing Diomande Starting to Shine Ahead of Leipzig Return

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yan Diomande, Real Madrid's 19-year-old club-record 140 million euro signing from RB Leipzig, is beginning to justify the fee. The Ivory Coast winger scored against Villarreal and Sevilla and provided an assist for Arda Guler in Sunday's 4-1 rout of Sevilla. He has two goals and three assists in 11 appearances so far.

Diomande has earned more starts after Vinicius Junior's four-match ban. He now hopes to face his former club Leipzig in the Champions League on Wednesday. Real Madrid are seeking a third win from three in the competition.
