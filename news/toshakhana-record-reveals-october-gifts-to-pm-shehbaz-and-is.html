Toshakhana Record Reveals October Gifts to PM Shehbaz and Ishaq Dar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Cabinet Division has released the record of gifts received in Toshakhana during October 2026, showing presents received by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar and other government officials. According to the documents, the prime minister received a tea set, prayer beads, a crockery set, a model of a Maldivian boat, a table clock and other gifts. Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar was given a crystal tea cup set, a Holy See medal, a model of a traditional Bahraini ship and a watch.

Pakistan's ambassador to China, Khalid Hashmi, received two expensive mobile phones, while the prime minister's daughter-in-law was given a traditional Maldivian dress. The documents state that all gifts received during October 2026 have been deposited in Toshakhana. The government has already made public the Toshakhana record from 1997 to September 2026.
