Elon Musk Plans World's Largest Building in Texas With Investment of Up to $119 Billion

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

US billionaire Elon Musk plans to build a huge industrial complex in Texas named Terafab, jointly by SpaceX and Tesla, to manufacture advanced parts for artificial intelligence, computer chips, self-driving vehicles, humanoid robots and space technology. According to Architectural Digest, the proposed complex would cover about 100 million square feet in Grimes County, around 70 miles northwest of Houston. It could become the world's largest building if completed at the announced size.

The Texas governor's office announced in August that the first phase involves more than $16.8 billion in investment and about 3,000 jobs. Local channel KBTX estimates total investment across phases at about $119 billion. Construction could continue until 2036 under the plan.
