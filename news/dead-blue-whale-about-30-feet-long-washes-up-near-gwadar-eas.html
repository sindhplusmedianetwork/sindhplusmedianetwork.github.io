Dead Blue Whale, About 30 Feet Long, Washes Up Near Gwadar East Bay

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A dead blue whale has washed up on the shore near Gwadar East Bay. The whale, measuring about 30 feet, had been seen floating in the sea off Gwadar at different spots for two days before Wednesday's waves carried it close to the shore. WWF-Pakistan technical adviser Muhammad Moazzam Khan said the death of a large whale is an alarming event for marine biodiversity.

He said an initial theory is that the whale strayed into shallow water, which affected its navigation and left it lost near the coast. The exact cause of death is still unknown, and WWF-Pakistan has started a further investigation. He added that WWF-Pakistan keeps a record of whale stranding incidents along the country's coast and works for public awareness on marine biodiversity.

Similar incidents have been reported in past years, including a 34-foot whale at Gwadar in April 2019 and a 42-foot whale at Jiwani in May 2023.
