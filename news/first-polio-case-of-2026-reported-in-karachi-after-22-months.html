First Polio Case of 2026 Reported in Karachi After 22 Months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi reported its first polio case of the year on Monday after a gap of about 22 months. Wild poliovirus type 1 was confirmed in a six-month-old child from UC Jamali Colony in District East. The National Emergency Operations Centre confirmed the case, bringing Pakistan's total number of polio cases in 2026 to seven.

The last polio case in Karachi had been reported in December 2024. Health officials said environmental samples from the Karachi block have continuously shown the presence of the poliovirus, which they described as a concerning sign. Of the seven cases reported this year, Sindh now has two and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has five.

The authorities have urged parents to ensure their children receive polio drops in every campaign.
