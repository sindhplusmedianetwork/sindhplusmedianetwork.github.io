Fazlur Rehman Hits Out at Government and Establishment in Education Conference Speech

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam (F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman has criticised both the government and the establishment in an address to a national education conference in Islamabad. He said loyalty is now measured by following unknown powerful circles rather than by love for the country, and those who hold a different view are called traitors. He said a gathering of students in Islamabad a few days ago and a gathering of teachers were both stopped, which shows that the rulers attach no importance to education, students or teachers.

He said such actions breed hatred against the government and can damage the state system, adding that everyone knows who makes the policies and takes the decisions. Fazlur Rehman said he has been in politics for 34 years but has never appeared before a court in a corruption case, and he accused the accountability institutions of being used against political opponents. He also questioned the governor's rule and emergency measures in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, asking whether their purpose is to end terrorism or to take control of mineral resources.

He said decisions about provincial resources, minerals, coastal areas and islands are being made without taking the nation into confidence, and his party will continue its constitutional, democratic and peaceful struggle.
