Shan Masood dropped as Pakistan turn to youth for Sri Lanka Tests

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan have dropped former Test captain Shan Masood as selectors turn to youth and domestic performers for the two-match ICC World Test Championship series against Sri Lanka, announced by the country's cricket board on Saturday. Babar Azam retains the captaincy of a revamped 14-member squad, with three batters, Mohammad Taha, Mohammad Suleman and Sameer Minhas, earning maiden Test call-ups. Shan's omission marks a significant change in Pakistan's top order as the selectors look beyond established players amid the team's struggles in the current championship cycle.

The left-handed opener scored 109 against the West Indies in the opening Test in Trinidad in July before missing the second match with a finger injury, and later managed 161 runs in six innings across three Tests against England. He has scored 2,926 runs in 50 Tests at an average of 30.80, including seven centuries and 15 half-centuries. Taha, 26, has been the leading run-scorer in the ongoing President's Trophy Grade-I competition with 730 runs in seven matches since September, while Suleman has 652 runs and Sameer 405 runs in the same tournament.

Pakistan will host the first Test at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium from November 9 to 13, with the second scheduled at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore from November 17 to 21.
