Nvidia halts shipments of US-approved AI chips to China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nvidia has stopped shipping powerful artificial intelligence chips to China, its interim CEO Colette Kress said. The halt raises doubts about a special licensing agreement negotiated by Washington and Beijing. Kress replaced Jensen Huang after his resignation last week.

She said the company sold $2.4 billion of licensed Hopper-generation data centre products to China in the quarter ended October 25. She added that the company has no current plans for further shipments. She did not explain why shipments stopped.

She also did not say whether the halt would be permanent.
