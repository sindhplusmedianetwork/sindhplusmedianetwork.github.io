Punjab CM Rejects Registration of Orphanage After Boy's Death

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has rejected the registration application of the unregistered orphanage in Multan after 15-year-old orphan Waseem Abbas was crushed to death by a car. The orphanage had sent the boy to work with an electrician, which officials described as extreme negligence. Child Protection Bureau chairperson Sarah Ahmed said the facility, which houses 122 children, had applied for registration.

Multiple CCTV cameras proved the death was a deliberate killing rather than an accident. The victim's brother, cousin and sister were also residing at the orphanage. Authorities say the case is being pursued to ensure justice for the young victim.
