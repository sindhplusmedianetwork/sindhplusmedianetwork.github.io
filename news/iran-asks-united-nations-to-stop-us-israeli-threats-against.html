Iran Asks United Nations to Stop US, Israeli Threats Against Tehran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has written to the United Nations demanding that threats against Tehran by the United States and Israel be stopped. The letter was signed by Iran's ambassador to the UN headquarters in New York, Gholam Hossein Darzi. It was addressed to the UN Secretary-General and the current president of the Security Council.

The letter condemned a reported US threat to use the 'Mother of All Bombs' against Iran. It demanded immediate compliance with the UN Charter and international law, warning against any military aggression.
