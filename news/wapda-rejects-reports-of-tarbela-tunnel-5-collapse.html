WAPDA Rejects Reports of Tarbela Tunnel 5 Collapse

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WAPDA has rejected circulating reports that Tunnel 5 of Tarbela Dam has collapsed. Its spokesman said a weak section of rock fell during excavation of the second bench of a connecting tunnel for the fifth extension hydropower project. The rockfall allowed water to enter the tunnel and the powerhouse from the intake pit.

All workers were safely evacuated from the site. Water has since been drained from the intake pit. The spokesman said the main tunnel, powerhouse, tailrace culvert and switchyard are undamaged.

He confirmed that there was no loss of life in the incident.
