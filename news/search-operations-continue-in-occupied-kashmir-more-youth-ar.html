Search operations continue in occupied Kashmir, more youth arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian forces are continuing cordon-and-search operations across Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir, arresting more young men during raids. The operations are under way in Srinagar, Kulgam, Islamabad, Pulwama, Shopian, Kishtwar and other districts, according to Kashmir Media Service. Hundreds of youth have been detained for questioning in recent days, most of them from Kulgam district.

The crackdown follows an attack at Mir Bazar in Kulgam on Wednesday, in which three CRPF personnel were injured. Search operations are continuing in several districts, with more arrests reported as the crackdown goes on.
