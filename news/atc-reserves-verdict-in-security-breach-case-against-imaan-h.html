ATC Reserves Verdict in Security Breach Case Against Imaan, Hadi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The trial of human rights lawyer Imaan Zainab Mazari-Hazir and her husband, Hadi Ali Chattha, in the security breach case concluded at Adiala Jail on Tuesday, with the anti-terrorism court reserving its verdict. Imaan and Hadi are facing a jail trial in a 2024 case pertaining to the alleged disruption of an international cricket team's security route and incitement to public unrest, and the couple, accused of removing barriers from the route, was indicted on October 7. On Tuesday, both accused recorded their statements under Section 342 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, while final arguments by the state counsel also concluded during the proceedings.

Following the completion of the trial and final arguments, the court reserved its verdict and said it would be pronounced on October 29. A day earlier, the couple approached the Supreme Court to challenge the Islamabad High Court's October 19 order refusing to stay the trial in the case. The high court had heard their appeals last week against an October 10 anti-terrorism court order dismissing petitions challenging the framing of charges and the inclusion of terrorism-related provisions.

The couple has been in jail since their arrest on January 23 in a case registered against them for protesting outside the Islamabad High Court and allegedly manhandling the high court Bar Association president, an arrest that prompted criticism by rights bodies, politicians and journalists who stressed the couple's right to a fair trial.
