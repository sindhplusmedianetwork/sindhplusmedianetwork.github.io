Italian military helicopter crashes in Bosnia, 4 killed, 11 injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four people were killed and eleven injured when an Italian military helicopter crashed in Bosnia. The CH-47 transport helicopter was serving with the European Union's peacekeeping mission in the country at the time of the accident. The crash was reported with photographs from the scene showing the wreckage of the aircraft.

No further details were immediately available about what caused the helicopter to go down. Italian authorities had not yet issued a full statement on the casualties or the circumstances of the crash. The incident marks one of the deadliest accidents involving the EU peacekeeping mission in the region.
