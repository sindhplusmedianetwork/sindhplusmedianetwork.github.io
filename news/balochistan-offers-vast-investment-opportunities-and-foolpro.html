Balochistan Offers Vast Investment Opportunities and Foolproof Security, Says CM Bugti

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti has said that Balochistan offers vast investment opportunities and that providing investors with every possible facility is among the government's top priorities. Addressing a ceremony, he said a fertiliser city is being planned in Sui, keeping in view the availability of gas there. He said the government will guarantee investors foolproof security, all necessary facilities and one-window operation, and there will be no room for red tape in the province.

Bugti said the consensus passage of the Mines and Minerals Act by the provincial assembly was an important step towards a favourable and stable environment for investment. He said Balochistan holds attractive opportunities for investment and welcomes both domestic and foreign investors.
