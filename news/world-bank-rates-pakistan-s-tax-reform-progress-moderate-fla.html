World Bank Rates Pakistan's Tax-Reform Progress 'Moderate', Flags Risks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Bank has rated Pakistan's progress on tax reform as 'moderate' in a review of two loan programs worth about $670 million. The review found implementation 'moderately satisfactory' but flagged the overall risk to the programs as 'substantial'. One of the programs, the $700 million PRID-MPA, aims to lift Pakistan's tax-to-GDP ratio from 12.3 per cent to 15 per cent by June 2030.

The Bank also raised concern that the post of chief statistician had been vacant since September 2024, affecting data quality. An advance of $164.52 million was released on June 29 under the program. A separate $70 million Connected Punjab project was also flagged in the review.
