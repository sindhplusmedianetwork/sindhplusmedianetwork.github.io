UAE and Timor-Leste presidents discuss stronger ties in trade, energy and water

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United Arab Emirates President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan has received Timor-Leste President José Ramos-Horta, who is on a working visit to the UAE. The two leaders met in Abu Dhabi and discussed bilateral cooperation, particularly in the economy, trade, investment and renewable energy, and the opportunities to strengthen ties further to support lasting growth and prosperity. They reaffirmed their commitment to advancing cooperation between the two countries.

The meeting also addressed the importance of the 2026 UN Water Conference, which the UAE will host in December, in finding innovative solutions to water challenges, improving water management and ensuring the sustainability of water resources. The two leaders also highlighted cooperation through the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) as an important framework for strengthening bilateral relations, noting that the UAE is a Sectoral Dialogue Partner of ASEAN and places great importance on its economic and investment ties with the association. They also discussed issues of mutual interest, underscoring the importance of promoting coexistence, cooperation and peace in building strong societies.

The meeting was attended by senior UAE officials, including Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Deputy Chairman of the Presidential Court for Special Affairs, and the delegation accompanying the President of Timor-Leste.
