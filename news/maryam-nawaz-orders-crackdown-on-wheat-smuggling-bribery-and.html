Maryam Nawaz Orders Crackdown on Wheat Smuggling, Bribery and Spy Devices in Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has issued a set of strict directives to the provincial police. She ordered that wheat smuggling be stopped in every district of the province. She also directed the police to intensify the campaign against bribery within the force.

The chief minister ordered action against people keeping illegal weapons. In a notable move, she ordered a ban on camera-equipped glasses and other spying devices. The orders came during a review of the province's law and order and administrative performance.

She stressed that no negligence would be tolerated in enforcing the directives.
