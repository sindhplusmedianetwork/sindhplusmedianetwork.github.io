DR Congo court sentences 66 soldiers to death for cowardice and mutiny

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 66 soldiers have been sentenced to death in the Democratic Republic of Congo for cowardice, mutiny and refusing to obey orders. The sentences followed a military court trial in Walikale, North Kivu province. The trial covered 71 soldiers of the 3415th regiment, who were arrested on October 20 after allegedly abandoning their positions during clashes with the Front for the Refoundation of the Nation rebel coalition, which includes M23.

Four soldiers were acquitted, while one received a 10-year prison sentence. The army said the trial aimed to improve discipline among troops. The verdict is among the largest mass death sentences handed down by a Congolese military court in recent years.
