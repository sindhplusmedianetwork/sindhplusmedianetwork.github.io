Vinicius hat-trick inspires Brazil rout

Vinicius Jr continued his return to form with a hat-trick in Brazil's 7-0 friendly rout of Singapore on Tuesday as the five-times world champions bounced back from their dramatic 3-2 defeat by Japan. He scored both of Brazil's goals in the defeat by Japan. He completed his hat-trick inside 21 minutes.

His Real Madrid teammate Endrick also scored, along with Estevao Willian and Rayan. Singapore right back Raoul Suhaimi added to the hosts' misery with an own goal. The 26-year-old said on Monday that a reduced training schedule and transfer interest may have contributed to his slow start to the season for Real.

He has scored only one goal and provided three assists in 12 appearances since signing a long-term contract extension in August.
