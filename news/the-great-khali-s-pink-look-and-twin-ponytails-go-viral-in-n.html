The Great Khali's Pink Look and Twin Ponytails Go Viral in Netflix Campaign

Former WWE wrestler The Great Khali has set social media abuzz with a striking new look. Dalip Singh Rana, as the wrestler is known outside the ring, appeared in viral photographs wearing a pink outfit with his hair tied in two ponytails. The transformation is part of Netflix India's new promotional campaign 'K-Paglu' for upcoming Korean dramas in the country.

In the campaign photos, the towering former wrestler is shown eating noodles and watching dramas in a room decorated in K-drama style. The unexpected makeover delighted fans, and the pictures quickly went viral. Social media users flooded the posts with amusing comments about the wrestler's new avatar.
