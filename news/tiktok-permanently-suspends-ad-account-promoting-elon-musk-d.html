TikTok Permanently Suspends Ad Account Promoting Elon Musk Documentary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

TikTok has permanently suspended the advertising account used to promote the documentary film 'Musk', based on the life of billionaire entrepreneur Elon Musk. The suspension was imposed over what the platform described as repeated violations of its policies related to political content. The film's distribution company was informed of the decision on Friday afternoon.

The distributor expressed disappointment over the move and has filed an appeal against the suspension. The documentary's regular, unpaid TikTok account remains active and unaffected by the decision. However, the company will no longer be able to purchase advertisements on the platform.
