Oil prices dip as Asian energy markets open for the week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Oil prices fell slightly as the business week began in East Asia, with Brent crude trading at 97 dollars and 69 cents a barrel on Monday morning. US President Donald Trump recently told a broadcaster in an interview that he did not want to escalate tensions with Iran, and that statement was linked to the fall in prices. The softer tone could keep the downward trend in oil prices going through the day.

At the same time, reports of at least three maritime incidents involving merchant ships and oil tankers in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday could revive concern in energy markets. Traders will be watching whether the incidents affect shipping through the strait, a key route for global oil supplies. For now, the market appears to be weighing the easing of tensions against the fresh risks to shipping in the region.
