US Senate Banking Committee chair calls on Jensen Huang, Lutnick to testify

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Senate Banking Committee Chairman Tim Scott has called on Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to testify over recent developments at Nvidia. Scott sent letters to both men on Thursday asking them to appear before the committee. Huang stepped down from Nvidia on Thursday after reports of a relationship with a businesswoman whom US authorities suspect of spying for China.

Scott said he would consider subpoenaing Huang if he does not appear voluntarily. House committee chairs have separately demanded closed-door interviews in connection with the matter. The committee is seeking answers about the circumstances surrounding Huang's departure and the developments at the company.
