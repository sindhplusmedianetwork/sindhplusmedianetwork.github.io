Punjab Withdraws Security from PML-Q Leadership After Salik Hussain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Police's Elite Force and security personnel have been abruptly withdrawn from the central leadership of PML-Q and their residences in Gujrat. After Federal Minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain, police security has now also been withdrawn from his cousin and Gujrat MPA Chaudhry Musa Elahi. All security guards and Elite Force vehicles were also removed from Salik Hussain's main residence, Salik House, in Gujrat.

Police sources said strict orders were issued for Elite Force personnel in Gujrat and Lahore to report back to their lines. Federal ministers will now get only temporary protocol during visits. Musa Elahi condemned the move and said the Chaudhry family would not retreat from its demand for new provinces.
