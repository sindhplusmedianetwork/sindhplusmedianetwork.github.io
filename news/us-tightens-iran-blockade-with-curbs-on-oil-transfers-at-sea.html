US tightens Iran blockade with curbs on oil transfers at sea

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The US military said it will treat ships that receive cargo at sea from vessels loaded in Iran as attempting to evade its blockade of Iranian ports. According to a US Central Command advisory, action could be taken against such ships. The naval blockade on Iran-related oil exports was first imposed on April 13.

It was lifted in June after a draft understanding with Tehran, and reimposed on July 14 after the agreement collapsed. The update came amid a day of fast-moving developments in the US-Iran war. These included Iran striking US drone and missile sites in Kuwait.

US intelligence also warned that threats from Iran to Americans have increased substantially.
