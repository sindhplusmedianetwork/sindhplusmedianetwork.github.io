Pakistan Bars Taiwanese Airlines From Its Airspace Under One-China Policy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has barred Taiwanese airlines from using its airspace in support of Beijing's One-China policy, becoming the first country to take such a step, Bloomberg reported. Defence Minister Khawaja Asif told Bloomberg News that the aviation division follows the One China policy and that only mainland China is allowed, adding that Pakistan has permitted only Chinese airlines to fly over its airspace. Taiwanese carriers began rerouting flights over the weekend, with China Airlines and Eva Airways no longer flying over Pakistan since the morning of October 25, according to flight-tracking data cited in the report.

At least a dozen flights operated by the two airlines between Asia and Europe have flown over Russia since Sunday, according to Flightradar24 data, while the two airlines declined to comment and Taiwan's Civil Aviation Administration did not comment on the rerouting. The report added that Pakistan's restrictions are stricter than those imposed by mainland China, which allows Taiwanese airlines to fly over its territory, and that it remains unclear what prompted Pakistan's decision. Pakistan severed official relations with Taiwan in 1951 in favour of closer ties with China, and the rerouting has added hours to flights between Asia and Europe and increased fuel costs.
