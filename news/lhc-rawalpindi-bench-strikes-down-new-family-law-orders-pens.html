LHC Rawalpindi Bench Strikes Down New Family Law, Orders Pension for Divorced, Widowed and Unmarried Daughters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court's Rawalpindi bench has declared the new family law on pensions void and ordered that divorced, widowed and unmarried daughters be paid family pension immediately. Justice Ahsan Raza Kazmi announced the reserved verdict, with the court holding that depriving widowed, divorced and unmarried daughters of family pension violated Article 25 of the Constitution. Under the new provincial family law, family pension had been limited to widows only, after the Punjab government changed the law in 2024 and deprived divorced, widowed and unmarried daughters of family pension following the death of their parents.

The law had been challenged by Falak Shah and advocate Tanveer Suhail Shah, and the court ordered family pension to be paid to all women who had been deprived of it. The court observed that thousands of women who lost out on family pension under the new law would benefit from the decision.
