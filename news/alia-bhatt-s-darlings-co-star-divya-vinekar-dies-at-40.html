Alia Bhatt's 'Darlings' Co-Star Divya Vinekar Dies at 40

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian actress Divya Vinekar, known for her role as a police constable in the 2022 dark comedy 'Darlings' alongside Alia Bhatt, died suddenly on Tuesday, October 20 at the age of 40. Her close friend and radio jockey Rohini Ramnathan announced the death, describing it as sudden and unexpected and asking for privacy for the family. Vinekar also appeared in 'Jalsa' with Vidya Balan and Shefali Shah.

Further details about her death have not been shared publicly.
