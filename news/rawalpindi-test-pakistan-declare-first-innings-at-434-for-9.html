Rawalpindi Test: Pakistan declare first innings at 434 for 9 against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan declared their first innings at 434-9 against Sri Lanka in the Rawalpindi Test. Saud Shakeel made 93, Arafat Minhas 52 and Ghazi Ghori 48; debutants Tayyab Arif scored 57, Mohammad Taha 51, Babar Azam 50, Azan Awais 30 and Salman Ali Agha 16. For Sri Lanka, Dilshan Madushanka and Asitha Fernando took 3 wickets each.

Sri Lanka had won the toss and elected to field. Four Pakistan players made their Test debuts in the match: Tayyab Arif, Mohammad Taha, Arafat Minhas and Saqib Khan. Pakistan resumed the second day at 281-5 and added 153 runs before the declaration.

The second and final Test of the series will be played in Lahore from November 17.
