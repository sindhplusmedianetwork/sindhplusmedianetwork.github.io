'Heated Rivalry' Reportedly Filming Seasons 2 and 3 Back-to-Back Alongside Christmas Movie

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

'Heated Rivalry' is reportedly expanding its television and theatrical plans, with the hit hockey romance set to film its second and third seasons back-to-back before moving on to a standalone Christmas film. According to Deadline, the Crave series, starring Connor Storrie and Hudson Williams, will have two six-episode seasons filmed consecutively. Production is expected to wrap towards the end of November or early December, with filming on the standalone movie scheduled to begin in December.

The upcoming film is reportedly being developed as a Christmas special or holiday-themed instalment, with a theatrical release planned for December 2027. However, it is not expected to feature Storrie and Williams as Ilya Rozanov and Shane Hollander. Instead, the story is said to centre on the other couple introduced in the first season, Scott Hunter, played by François Arnaud, and Kip Grady, played by Robbie G.K., alongside additional characters from the series.

The reported storyline has a connection to Rachel Reid's bestselling Game Changers book series, which inspired the television adaptation. Crave has not confirmed the reported projects, with a representative for the Canadian streaming service telling Deadline that there were no projects to announce.
