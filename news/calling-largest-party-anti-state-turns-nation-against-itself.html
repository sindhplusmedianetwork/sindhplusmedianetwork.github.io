Calling Largest Party Anti-State Turns Nation Against Itself: Ali Zafar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Barrister Ali Zafar, PTI's parliamentary leader in the Senate, has said that branding the country's largest political party as anti-state turns the entire nation against itself. Speaking on a television programme, he said the prime minister should take back his statement. Zafar claimed that the government had decided to impose governor's rule a month and a half ago.

He said that from now on, PTI's parliamentary party would make the party's decisions. According to him, there is no chance of negotiations with the government at the moment.
