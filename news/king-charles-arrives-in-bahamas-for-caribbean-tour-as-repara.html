King Charles Arrives in Bahamas for Caribbean Tour as Reparations Calls Grow

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

King Charles III arrived in the Bahamas on Tuesday as part of a nine-day trip to the Caribbean, where he faces calls for an apology and slavery reparations. It is his first official visit to the Bahamas as king, with the archipelago's government closing all public schools on the island of New Providence to welcome him. Charles stepped out in a light grey suit and sunglasses as he greeted Governor-General Dame Cynthia A.

Pratt and Prime Minister Philip Davis at Lynden Pindling Airport in Nassau. He then travelled to Clifford Park, which he last officially visited as Prince of Wales in 1973 to grant the Bahamas independence after 300 years of British rule. Members of the Bahamas' Rastafarian community and others lined a road near Clifford Park in protest, demanding reparations, while a cheering crowd awaited him at the park, with dozens of young students waving small Bahamian flags ahead of a cultural event.

In his first public remarks since arriving, Charles said it was only right to acknowledge the great suffering and injustices of the past in these islands and beyond, even as people rejoice in the successes of the present. He also acknowledged the climate challenges facing the island nation, noting that 90 per cent of its land sits below two metres. Charles is scheduled to spend three days in the Bahamas and then fly to Guyana on October 29 for two days, before travelling to Antigua and Barbuda on October 30 for the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting, where Queen Camilla will join him.
