PM Launches 500,000 Chromebooks for Students, Says Poverty Must Not Block Education

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Sunday launched a nationwide initiative to distribute 500,000 Chromebooks equipped with internet connectivity among talented and deserving students over the next two years. Addressing the launch ceremony of the Prime Minister's Youth Chromebook Scheme, he said financial hardship must not prevent young people from pursuing education and developing their abilities. He said the devices would be distributed strictly on merit, with students from mainstream educational institutions and religious seminaries eligible, and that beneficiaries would be able to attend classes remotely and access electronic libraries.

The prime minister said the connectivity offered through the initiative could benefit an estimated 2.5 million people, including family members of the recipients, and that selected students were also being provided with bags and devices to facilitate internet access. Recalling earlier laptop programmes, he said 600,000 laptops had been distributed on merit over approximately a decade, and that many beneficiaries had gone on to become doctors, engineers and other professionals. He also highlighted the Daanish Schools programme, saying he had recently inaugurated a Daanish School in Islamabad while another 27 campuses were under construction across the country, offering modern facilities without charging students.
