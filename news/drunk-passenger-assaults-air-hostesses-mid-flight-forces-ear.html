Drunk Passenger Assaults Air Hostesses Mid-Flight, Forces Early Landing in Spain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 36-year-old passenger, intoxicated with alcohol on a flight from Amsterdam to Malaga in Spain, created a disturbance and allegedly sexually assaulted four air hostesses. The man tried to touch the crew members inappropriately during the flight, and his behaviour forced the pilots to land the plane early. When the aircraft reached Malaga airport, Spain's Civil Guards arrested the man.

During the arrest he also attacked a police officer. Because he was in a state of severe agitation he was first taken to hospital and later released after questioning. A court investigation continues against him for sexual assault and attacking a public official.
