Faisalabad Deploys Over 1,100 Personnel to Secure Churches for Sunday Services

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Faisalabad Police deployed more than 1,100 officers and personnel across the district on Sunday to ensure peaceful Christian religious services. All churches and surrounding areas were placed under heightened security. Police spokesperson Tariq Jutt said screening at church entrances, CCTV monitoring and patrols were in place.

Walk-through gates and metal detectors were installed at places of worship. City Police Officer Tanvir Hussain said protecting the lives, property and places of worship of the Christian community was among the top priorities of Faisalabad Police.
