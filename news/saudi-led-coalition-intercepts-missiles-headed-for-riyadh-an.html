Saudi-Led Coalition Intercepts Missiles Headed for Riyadh and Drones in Eastern Province

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Saudi-led coalition said on Sunday that it had intercepted two Houthi ballistic missiles launched towards Riyadh and three drones in the kingdom's Eastern Province. The announcement came amid escalating hostilities between the Houthis and Saudi Arabia. Separately, Yemeni government forces said a senior military commander and several other personnel were killed in clashes with the Houthis in the northern province of Al-Jawf.

The Southern Armed Forces said they had repositioned their forces from Zuqar Airport and the Bab al-Mandeb area in western Taiz province, citing efforts to secure the interim capital, Aden, and neighbouring provinces. The developments came hours after the Yemeni army said it had intercepted Houthi drones targeting the city of Marib in central Yemen. According to the Saba news agency, six people, including two children and two women, were injured in Houthi artillery shelling and sniper attacks in Taiz province.

Houthi-run Al-Masirah TV also reported airstrikes in the capital, Sanaa, accusing Saudi Arabia of being behind the attacks, while Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree claimed the group had shot down a Saudi CH-4 armed reconnaissance drone in Al-Jawf, a claim on which there was no immediate comment from Saudi officials or the Yemeni government.
