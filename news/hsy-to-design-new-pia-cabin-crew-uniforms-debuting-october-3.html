HSY to Design New PIA Cabin Crew Uniforms, Debuting October 31

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan International Airlines has selected renowned couturier Hassan Sheheryar Yasin, popularly known as HSY, to design the new uniforms for its cabin crew. The uniforms will make their debut on October 31 on the airline's inaugural Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner flight from London to Islamabad. According to the airline, the collection blends Pakistan's cultural heritage with contemporary fashion, giving the crew a refreshed and modern look.

The final design was chosen after ten leading designers presented their concepts at a competitive showcase held in Karachi. The unveiling is part of PIA's broader effort to modernize its brand and passenger experience. Aviation watchers say the refreshed uniforms will coincide with a milestone moment for the national carrier.
