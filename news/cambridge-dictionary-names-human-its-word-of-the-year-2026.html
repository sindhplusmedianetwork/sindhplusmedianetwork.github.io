Cambridge Dictionary names 'human' its Word of the Year 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cambridge Dictionary has chosen the word 'human' as its Word of the Year for 2026. The choice highlights the growing importance of humans in the age of artificial intelligence. The dictionary said the year saw a prominent global debate over what it really means to be human in the AI era.

Its linguist Colin McIntosh said AI has changed how people talk about being human. He pointed to the growing use of terms such as 'human-made', which shows the distinction between human creation and AI-generated content. The dictionary also added new terms this year, including 'aura farming', 'lux maxing' and 'air gel'.
