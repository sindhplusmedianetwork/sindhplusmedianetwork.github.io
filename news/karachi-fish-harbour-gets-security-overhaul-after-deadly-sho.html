Karachi Fish Harbour gets security overhaul after deadly shooting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah on Wednesday ordered a sweeping security overhaul at Karachi Fish Harbour, where about 20,000 people enter daily, after a deadly shooting exposed gaps in law and order. He directed Home Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar to pursue the establishment of a dedicated police station for the harbour. He also ordered mandatory registration of all harbour users with RFID-based identity cards.

Officials briefed the chief minister on a firing incident on September 8, which was triggered by a dispute over placing fish on a road. One person was killed and six were injured in that shooting. The new security measures are aimed at preventing such violence and tightening control over access to the harbour.
