Putin Lets Europe Join Ukraine Peace Talks as Kyiv Reports New Disruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Vladimir Putin agreed on Thursday to let European representatives join the next round of Ukraine peace talks, a US envoy said, though the Russian leader said Washington must work with its European allies to understand if they want peace or war in Ukraine. US envoy Steve Witkoff, who met Putin in the Kremlin alongside Jared Kushner, told Russian state television that the Europeans would join at the next meeting, which he called a very important moment. But Putin also warned that the European idea of sending Western troops into Ukraine once a peace deal is concluded would trigger a resumption of the war, because Russia would see it as a threat to its own security.

In a sign of renewed tensions, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said new restrictions were affecting Starshield, the US-made satellite communications system Kyiv uses on the battlefield, and that the disruption was significant across the entire front line. Zelensky said that because of systemic limits on the speed at which Starshield could be used, Ukrainian interceptors could not reach sufficient speed to shoot down Russian jet-powered drones. The main outcome of trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi earlier this week, the first direct US-mediated negotiations between Russia and Ukraine in nine months, was agreement on a major exchange of prisoners of war likely to take place in the coming weeks.

Serious unresolved issues remain, including differences over territory, with Russia holding about one-fifth of Ukraine and Kyiv refusing to give up the rest of the Donbas region in its east.
