GTA 6 fans question possible regional censorship after smaller download files emerge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

GTA 6 fans have raised concerns about possible regional censorship after players found the game's preload files are smaller in Japan and Australia than in other regions. Rockstar Games opened preloads on November 10, nine days before the scheduled launch. Most players are downloading about 210GB, but the PlayStation 5 version measures 208.725GB in Japan against 209.913GB elsewhere.

The difference in file sizes has led fans to question whether content has been changed for some regions. The exact reason for the differences remains unconfirmed. Publisher Take-Two has not publicly explained them.
