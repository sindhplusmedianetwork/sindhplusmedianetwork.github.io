Man Arrested in Multan for Allegedly Killing Mother and Two Sisters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police have arrested a man for allegedly murdering his mother and two sisters and setting their bodies on fire to make the killings appear an accidental blaze at their home in Multan on October 28. The suspect, identified as Aman Abbas, allegedly killed the three women in the name of honour, according to a police spokesperson. The incident initially appeared to be an accidental fire at a house in the DHA police station's jurisdiction, but investigators later found that the deaths were murders.

Police said the suspect poured petrol on the victims and set them on fire in an attempt to conceal the crime. Investigators also obtained CCTV footage from the petrol pump where the suspect had allegedly purchased the petrol used in the crime. A case has been registered against Aman on behalf of the DHA police, and he has been arrested.

Police have also launched action against the officials of the petrol pump who allegedly supplied petrol to him in violation of the relevant rules.
