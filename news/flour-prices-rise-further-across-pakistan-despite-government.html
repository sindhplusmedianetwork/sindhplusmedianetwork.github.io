Flour prices rise further across Pakistan despite government efforts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Flour has moved further out of the public's reach across Pakistan despite the government's efforts. The series of increases in flour prices continues in the country, and people have not received relief from inflation even though wheat has been imported alongside government measures. According to fresh figures, the price of a 20-kilogram bag of flour has risen to Rs3,800 in different cities.

A document of the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics states that the 20-kilogram bag is being sold at the highest price in Hyderabad, where citizens have to pay Rs3,800 for it. The same bag is available for Rs3,750 in Peshawar and for Rs3,733 in Rawalpindi and Islamabad. According to the document, the price of a 20-kilogram bag of flour has been recorded at Rs3,600 in Karachi, while consumers in Bannu and Quetta also have to buy the same weight for Rs3,600.

The price has reached Rs3,560 in Larkana. In Gujranwala, a 20-kilogram bag is being sold for Rs3,533, while in Lahore and Sialkot its price has also risen to Rs3,533.
