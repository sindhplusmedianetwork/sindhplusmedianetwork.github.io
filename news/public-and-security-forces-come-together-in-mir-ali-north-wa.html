Public and security forces come together in Mir Ali, North Waziristan, in show of solidarity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Local elders and residents of Hassu Khel and Musaki in Mir Ali, North Waziristan, held a dialogue with security forces and expressed support and trust in the Pakistan Army, according to the report. Young participants raised their hands to pledge defense of the homeland during the gathering. Security personnel urged youth and children toward education.

The event ended with slogans of Pakistan Zindabad and Pak Fauj Zindabad.
