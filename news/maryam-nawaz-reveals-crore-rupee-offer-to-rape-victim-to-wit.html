Maryam Nawaz Reveals Crore-Rupee Offer to Rape Victim to Withdraw Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has revealed that the wealthy family of an accused in a recent rape case allegedly offered the victim between 15 and 20 million rupees to withdraw her allegations. She shared the disclosure in a post on the social media platform X, noting that the offer was made even though the victim's statement had already been recorded under Section 164 of the Criminal Procedure Code. Maryam Nawaz said it was further alleged that the judge who granted bail to the accused had been bribed.

She described these allegations as deeply disturbing questions about the integrity of the justice system and the vulnerability of rape survivors to financial pressure and the misuse of power. The chief minister said she would keep a close eye on such cases so that no rapist or perpetrator escapes justice because of money, influence or connections. Justice must never be for sale and no one should be above the law, she added.
