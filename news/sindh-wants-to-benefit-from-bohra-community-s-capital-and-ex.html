Sindh wants to benefit from Bohra community's capital and experience, says Murad Ali Shah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah has said that the Sindh government wants to benefit from the capital and experience of the Bohra community. Addressing a ceremony in Karachi, he said the Bohra community is an important partner in the economic progress of Sindh. Murad Ali Shah said the community has played a role in trade, industry and welfare work for generations.

He said Karachi is the country's commercial and financial centre and that there are wide investment opportunities in Sindh. The chief minister also said that special economic zones offer important opportunities for industrial investors, and that every new investment creates jobs. He said the provincial government was keen to work with the community for the province's economic development.
