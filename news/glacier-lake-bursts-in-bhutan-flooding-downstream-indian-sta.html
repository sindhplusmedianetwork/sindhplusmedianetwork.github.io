Glacier lake bursts in Bhutan, flooding downstream; Indian states on high alert

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A glacier lake burst in Bhutan's Gasa district on Thursday evening, triggering a sudden flood downstream. The Shintafu glacier lake gave way on October 29, sending floodwater through the valley below. Installations at the Gasa Tsho hot springs were damaged in the flooding.

Authorities said there was no loss of life because residents were evacuated in time. Bhutan issued a warning to India after the incident, as the floodwater flows across the border. The Indian states of West Bengal and Assam have been placed on high alert following the warning.
