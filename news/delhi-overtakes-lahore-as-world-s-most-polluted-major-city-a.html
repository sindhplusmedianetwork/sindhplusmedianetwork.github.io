Delhi overtakes Lahore as world's most polluted major city as air quality deteriorates

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's capital Delhi overtook Lahore to become the world's most polluted major city on Friday, with air quality reaching hazardous levels and visibility dropping as smog blanketed the city of 20 million people. Delhi's air quality index stood at 334, categorised as hazardous and the worst among major cities globally, according to the Swiss air quality monitoring group IQAir. India's official Central Pollution Control Board recorded a higher reading of 403 in Delhi and classified the air quality as severe, with the difference put down to the different scales and methods used to calculate the index.

According to IQAir's hourly readings, Delhi's index had dropped to 208 by 11am Pakistan time, while Lahore was ranked seventh among the world's most polluted major cities, with an index of 155. Friday's readings marked a drop in Lahore's pollution levels after the city had ranked as the most polluted major city in the world for several days in a row. Earlier this week, Lahore's average PM2.5 concentration was eight times the World Health Organisation's guideline value, according to a report by the Pakistan Air Quality Initiative, which said the city's average concentration stood at 126 micrograms per cubic metre.

Pollution is a seasonal problem in many parts of northern Pakistan and India, as the cold, heavy air of winter traps vehicle emissions, construction dust and smoke from agricultural burning.
