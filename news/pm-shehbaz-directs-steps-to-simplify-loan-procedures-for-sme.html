PM Shehbaz directs steps to simplify loan procedures for SMEs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has directed steps to simplify loan procedures for small and medium enterprises seeking financial support. He called for effective guidance and simplified procedures for SMEs applying for loans. Small and medium enterprises are the backbone of Pakistan's economy, the prime minister said.

He said SMEs are key to expanding economic activity in the country. He also said they are key to creating jobs. The prime minister's directions aim to make it easier for small businesses to obtain loans.
