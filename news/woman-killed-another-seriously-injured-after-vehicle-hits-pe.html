Woman killed, another seriously injured after vehicle hits pedestrians on Rawalpindi's Murree Road

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman was killed and another was seriously injured after a speeding vehicle struck them on Murree Road in Rawalpindi on Friday. The two women were crossing the road when the vehicle hit them, police said. The woman who died was identified as Sana Saleem.

The injured woman was taken to Benazir Bhutto Hospital for treatment. Police said the driver fled the scene after the accident. A search has been launched to trace the driver, and police are investigating the incident.
