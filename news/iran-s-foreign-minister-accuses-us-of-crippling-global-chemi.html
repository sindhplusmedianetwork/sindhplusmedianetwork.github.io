Iran's foreign minister accuses US of crippling global chemical weapons watchdog

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has accused the United States of crippling the global organisation for the prevention of chemical weapons by pushing it into a financial crisis and limiting its powers. In a statement on the social media platform X, Araghchi said the United States expresses concern over chemical weapons while at the same time paralysing the global body. He warned that financial difficulties could force the organisation to suspend its work and scale back important programmes.

Araghchi said the United States was deliberately obstructing the organisation's powers and putting world peace and security at risk, which he described as shameful. The accusation comes after the US administration last week accused Iran of attempting to develop chemical weapons, a charge Tehran rejected. A news agency reported that the organisation faces a shortfall of nearly 20 million euros in its 83-million-euro budget this year.

Its head said the financial difficulties could force its operational work to be suspended from December and its plans for next year to be reviewed, while the United States, its largest financial contributor, had paid only 20 percent of its annual dues by October.
