Rs5 billion damages were demanded for 'Madam', not the treasury, says Salik Hussain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain, reacting on ARY News to the Punjab government's defamation notice against him, said the Rs5 billion demanded in the notice was not for the national exchequer but was demanded by Madam for herself. He said the Additional Advocate General's office was wrongly used for a personal matter. He said he has replied to the notice in detail on 15 points and received no answer, adding that his focus was only on the Auditor General's report.

He also criticised Punjab's management, saying Rs30 to 40 billion was given in advance while projects were stalled, and said that if not satisfied, the other side should take the matter to court.
