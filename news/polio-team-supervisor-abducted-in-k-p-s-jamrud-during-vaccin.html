Polio team supervisor abducted in K-P's Jamrud during vaccination drive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A polio campaign team supervisor was abducted by unidentified armed men in Jamrud, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, during the ongoing seven-day polio vaccination campaign. DPO Waqar Ahmad said Jalil Khan was taken from the Chapri area of Jamrud on Wednesday. Police have set up blockades across the area and dispatched a heavy contingent for recovery efforts.

The abduction comes days after two policemen were killed in Mohmand and four polio workers were abducted in the Tank district earlier in the week. Security for polio teams has been tightened as the campaign continues.
