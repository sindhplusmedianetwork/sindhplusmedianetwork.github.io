CCTV Video Emerges Showing Beating of Fatima at Police Officer's Home in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

CCTV footage has emerged in the Fatima torture case at Islamabad Police Lines Headquarters. The footage shows the girl being beaten at a police officer's home. It shows a woman beating Fatima with a stick.

Images of burn wounds on her body have also surfaced. Video from 40 minutes before her death also emerged, and the footage and post-mortem report confirmed the torture took place an hour and a half earlier.
