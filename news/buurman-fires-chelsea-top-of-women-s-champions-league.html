Buurman fires Chelsea top of Women's Champions League

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Netherlands defender Veerle Buurman struck late as Chelsea edged Paris Saint-Germain 1-0 on Wednesday to move top of the Women's Champions League's league phase. Buurman's 87th-minute intervention took the English club a point above last year's runners-up Lyon, who play Arsenal on Thursday, and champions Barcelona, who face Servette. Chelsea midfielder Keira Walsh told UEFA that the team had spoken before the game about wanting to finish the evening on top, and that they were in a good spot going into the last game.

Earlier, English champions Manchester City and Spain's Real Madrid earned vital wins to keep up with the early pacesetters after five rounds of the season. Second-half substitutes Lauren Hemp and Khadija Shaw gave City a 2-0 win at Swedish club Hacken. Danish forward Signe Bruun made the difference off the bench for Real Madrid, scoring twice in a 3-1 win over 10-woman Inter Milan.

The win took City up to fourth in the Champions League table, equal on nine points with Real but ahead of the Spaniards on goal difference. In Wednesday's other game, Juventus also needed late goals to overcome Belgians OH Leuven 2-0, with efforts from Annamaria Serturini and Matilde Pavan in the final six minutes sending them to sixth.
