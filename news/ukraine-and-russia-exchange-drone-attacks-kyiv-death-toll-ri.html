Ukraine and Russia exchange drone attacks, Kyiv death toll rises to 11

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Deaths from a Russian attack in Kyiv have risen to 11 after rescuers recovered another body from the rubble. The body was found in a five-storey residential building that partly collapsed a day earlier. A Ukrainian drone attack damaged an industrial facility in Russia's Perm province.

Russian strikes also targeted energy and port infrastructure in Izmail. A ship near Odesa was also targeted.
