Additional SHO Meera Khan Martyred in Gun Attack Near Miranshah Bypass

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Additional Station House Officer Meera Khan of Miranshah police station was martyred in a gun attack in North Waziristan on Tuesday evening. Unidentified armed men opened fire on the officer near the Miranshah Bypass, and he sustained multiple bullet wounds. District Police Officer Sajjad Hussain confirmed that Khan succumbed to his injuries.

His body was shifted to Miranshah hospital for medico-legal formalities. Police launched a search operation in the surrounding area after the attack. The identities and motives of the attackers were not immediately known.

The attack drew condemnation from police officials, who paid tribute to the martyred officer.
