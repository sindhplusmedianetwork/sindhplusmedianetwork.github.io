12 killed, 22 injured as bus and van collide near Uthal in Lasbela

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 12 people were killed and 22 others injured when a passenger bus collided with a passenger van near Zero Point in Uthal on the Karachi-Quetta Highway in Balochistan's Lasbela district on Friday. Officials said seven people died on the spot while five others succumbed to their injuries in hospital. Seven of the injured were in critical condition and were shifted to a trauma centre in Karachi for treatment.

Women and children were among the dead and injured, and officials said the passengers of the van bore the brunt of the crash. Police, rescue teams, district administration officials and local residents rushed to the scene and moved the dead and injured to hospitals. An investigation has been launched to determine the cause of the collision, which disrupted traffic on the busy national highway for some time.

Rescue officials noted that Balochistan has seen several fatal road accidents in recent weeks, including one in Bolan district on October 19 that killed four people.
