Pakistan needs 'middle-power alliances' to navigate emerging world order, US-China rivalry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan needs to forge alliances and partnerships with 'like-minded' middle powers to navigate the emerging world order and resist pressure to pick sides in the US-China rivalry. Professor Carlos Masala of the University of the Federal Armed Forces in Munich, Germany, made the argument in a seminar at IBA City Campus in Karachi on Friday. Masala said that in an increasingly bipolar international system, most countries will 'hedge' their bets rather than becoming dependent on one superpower.

He argued that countries will instead look for the 'best offer' from either Beijing or Washington. The seminar examined how Pakistan and other middle powers can position themselves in the changing global order. Masala's remarks pointed to a strategy of flexible partnerships rather than fixed alignment with one side.
