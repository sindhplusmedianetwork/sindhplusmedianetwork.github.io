Sindh plans special relief package for drought-hit Thar farmers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government is preparing a special agricultural relief package for farmers in Tharparkar after drought devastated nearly 90 per cent of cultivated crops across the district. Provincial Agriculture Minister Sardar Muhammad Bux Khan Mahar has directed the provincial agriculture secretary to prepare proposals for the package at once. Mahar said a detailed report by the Provincial Agriculture Department on the drought and crop losses in all seven talukas of Tharparkar had been submitted to Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah, and on the basis of the report Tharparkar had been declared a calamity-hit district.

According to the report, around 85 to 90 per cent of cultivated crops had been destroyed or rendered unproductive, with only 10 to 15 per cent surviving. The minister blamed uneven and insufficient monsoon rainfall, particularly a severe shortage of rain in September, and said crops including guar, millet, sesame and sorghum had suffered extensive damage while natural grasses dried up before reaching maturity. The proposed package may include per-acre financial assistance, support through the Benazir Hari Card, special subsidies on fertilisers and certified seeds, and help with cultivation in the coming Rabi season.

The government will also consider facilitating the repayment of agricultural loans, concessions in agricultural taxes and reducing other financial burdens on affected growers.
