Iran Blames US Blockade for Maritime Insecurity in Gulf, Strait of Hormuz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has blamed the United States and Israel for the growing maritime insecurity in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz, telling the UN Security Council that Washington's actions lie at the root of the crisis. According to Iran's Press TV, Tehran's representative told the Council that continued American and Israeli military aggression, coupled with the US naval blockade, had endangered maritime security across the region. The envoy argued that the causes of insecurity in the Gulf and the strait could not be understood without looking at American actions, calling Washington's policy the fundamental reason for the situation.

Iran presented its position at a time of mounting concern over threats to shipping traffic and global oil supplies amid heightened tensions in the Gulf and around the Strait of Hormuz.
