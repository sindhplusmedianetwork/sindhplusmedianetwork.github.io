Flour Prices Climb Further; Karachi Pays the Most in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Flour prices have climbed further across Pakistan, with Karachi's residents forced to buy the most expensive flour in the country. According to Pakistan Bureau of Statistics data, a 20kg bag in Karachi rose by Rs140 to Rs3,540. Faisalabad saw a sharp Rs800 jump to Rs3,000, while Khuzdar rose Rs400 to Rs3,400 and Hyderabad Rs280 to Rs3,480.

In Larkana the bag climbed Rs300 to Rs3,200, in Quetta Rs190 to Rs3,220, and in Sukkur Rs140 to Rs3,200. Multan and Sargodha both stand at Rs2,933, Bahawalpur at Rs3,000, Gujranwala at Rs3,000, Sialkot at Rs3,133, Peshawar at Rs3,300, Bannu at Rs3,200, Islamabad at Rs3,373 and Rawalpindi at Rs3,333. Lahore remains the cheapest city in the country at Rs2,300 for a 20kg bag.
