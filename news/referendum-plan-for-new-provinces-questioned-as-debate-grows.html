Referendum plan for new provinces questioned as debate grows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government is talking about a referendum to create new provinces, while opposition parties and some constitutional experts are questioning whether new provinces can be created through a referendum at all. PPP chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari has opposed the move, warning of bloodshed if Sindh is divided. Former attorney general Ishtar Ausaf said a referendum is neither parallel to nor a substitute for the constitutional procedure.

The debate turns on Articles 239(4) and 48(6) of the Constitution and the constitutional route that must be followed for new provinces. The discussion has therefore moved beyond the idea of a referendum itself to the question of what the Constitution actually requires before a new province can come into being.
