Pakistan Seeks World Bank Group Funds for ML-1 Railway, National AI Programme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Economic Affairs and Pakistan's Governor to the World Bank Group (WBG) Ahad Khan Cheema sought continued WBG financing for the ML-1 railway project and the National Artificial Intelligence and Economic Development Programme during a meeting with WBG President Ajay Banga in Bangkok. The meeting took place on the sidelines of the 2026 WBG-IMF Annual Meetings, according to a statement issued on Friday. Both sides discussed translating macroeconomic stabilisation into sustainable, investment-led growth, productive employment and improved livelihoods, building on Banga's visit to Pakistan in February 2026.

Cheema acknowledged the WBG's operational efficiency gains, including a reduction in average project preparation time from 19 months to nine months and record $112 billion in private capital mobilisation in FY2026. He said Country Partnership Framework implementation and policy reforms must align with large-scale, high-impact investments in digital transformation and AI, minerals and value addition, primary healthcare, skills development, agricultural value chains, energy and transport infrastructure. Separately, Cheema also met World Bank Managing Director of Operations Anna Bjerde to discuss accelerating implementation of Pakistan's Country Partnership Framework 2026–2035, and sought enhanced support for the ML-1 Railway Upgradation Project — the 1,800-kilometre Karachi-Peshawar line now estimated at $6.68–6.80 billion after Chinese financing was withdrawn — along with a proposed Minerals Compact for the minerals sector.
