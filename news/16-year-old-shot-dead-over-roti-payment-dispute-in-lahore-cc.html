16-Year-Old Shot Dead Over Roti Payment Dispute in Lahore; CCTV Footage Emerges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 16-year-old boy was shot dead following a dispute over payment for roti at a naan shop in Tufail Colony on Band Road, Lahore. The customer had come to buy roti but was short of money, leading to a heated exchange during which he pulled out a pistol and shot the boy. CCTV footage of the incident has now surfaced, showing several people beating the boy as the argument escalated, the armed suspect drawing his pistol and firing directly at the victim, and the accused fleeing with his companions afterwards.

An FIR has been registered on the complaint of the victim's father, Nasir Ali, nominating four persons: Hasnain Shahbaz, Irfan alias Bhola, and Bhola son of Ashfaq. Police officials said the CCTV video provides documentary evidence of the serious crime. The main accused, Irfan alias Bhola, is in custody while police are searching for the remaining nominated suspects.
