Russell Brand Jailed for Ten and a Half Years Over Sexual Offences

British comedian and actor Russell Brand has been sentenced to ten years and six months in prison. A court in London handed down the sentence after Brand was convicted of raping a fan in 2009. He was also found guilty of sexually assaulting three other women during his showbusiness career.

The judge said Brand had become a sexual predator while rising to fame. Brand showed no reaction as the sentence was read out in court. The case related to offences committed over a number of years during his career in comedy and entertainment.

The sentence brings to a close one of the most high-profile sexual offence cases in Britain in recent years.
