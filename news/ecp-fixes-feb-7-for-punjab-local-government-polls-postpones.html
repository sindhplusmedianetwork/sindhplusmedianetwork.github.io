ECP Fixes Feb 7 for Punjab Local Government Polls, Postpones KP Elections

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Election Commission of Pakistan has issued a short order announcing the first phase of local government elections in Punjab on February 7, while postponing local government elections in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The commission accepted a request from the Punjab chief secretary, saying equal opportunities should be given to all candidates and voters in the constituency. The decision was taken by a four-member bench headed by Chief Election Commissioner Sikandar Sultan Raja, which had initially proposed holding the elections on December 14.

The Punjab government had asked for polling to be held after January 20 in view of fog and weather conditions. The commission also considered a federal government order issued on November 4 suspending Article 140-A of the Constitution, which makes local government elections the commission's responsibility, and noted that the order's application has been extended beyond the districts named in earlier orders. The last local government elections in Punjab were held in 2015, and the polls have been repeatedly delayed since then.
