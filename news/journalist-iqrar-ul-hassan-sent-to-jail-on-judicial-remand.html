Journalist Iqrar ul Hassan Sent to Jail on Judicial Remand

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A court in Karachi has ordered journalist and Awam Raj Party chief Iqrar ul Hassan to jail on judicial remand. The order came over allegations that he posted anti-state content on social media. The case relates to his criticism of the imposition of governor's rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa.

Iqrar ul Hassan is a well-known television journalist who heads the Awam Raj Party. The remand order was issued on Monday. Further proceedings in the case are expected to follow.
