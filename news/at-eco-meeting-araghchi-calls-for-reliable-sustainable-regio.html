At ECO Meeting, Araghchi Calls for Reliable, Sustainable Regional Energy Mechanism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has stressed the need for a reliable and sustainable regional energy mechanism, as he addressed the 30th meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation Council of Ministers in Islamabad. He made the remarks while speaking about the US-Israeli war on Iran, which has also choked key fuel supply routes, the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab. 'Our region needs not only an independent security framework but also a reliable and sustainable regional energy mechanism to safeguard energy flows, ensure stable supplies, reduce external vulnerabilities and enhance collective resilience against geopolitical shocks and disruptions,' Araghchi said, according to a transcript of his speech provided by the Iranian embassy in Pakistan.

His remarks followed the opening speech by Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar, who is chairing the meeting. Dar said Pakistan desired a peaceful, prosperous and stable Afghanistan, but meaningful progress towards regional cooperation required the Afghan authorities to address the legitimate security concerns of neighbouring countries, particularly the threat of terrorism. He added that a stable Afghanistan, at peace with itself and all its neighbours and fully integrated into the ECO framework, would benefit the Afghan people as well as the wider region by opening up avenues for trade, transit and economic cooperation.

Pakistan, which holds the ECO chairmanship for 2026-27, is hosting the meeting under the theme 'Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO'.
