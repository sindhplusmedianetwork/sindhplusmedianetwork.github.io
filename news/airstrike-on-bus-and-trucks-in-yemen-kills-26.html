Airstrike on bus and trucks in Yemen kills 26

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An airstrike on vehicles in Yemen's Lahj province has killed 26 people. The strike hit buses and trucks in the southwestern province, and a passenger bus was also caught in the attack. The Houthi rebels and Yemeni government forces have blamed each other for the attack.

Both sides have accused the other of carrying out the strike on the vehicles. The attack is among the deadliest recent incidents in Yemen's long-running conflict. The conflicting claims leave responsibility for the strike disputed.
