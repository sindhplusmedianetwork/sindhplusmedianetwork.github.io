Pak-US Ties Need Shift From Counterterrorism to IT: Ambassador

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's ambassador to the United States, Rizwan Saeed Sheikh, has said that Pakistan-US relations need to shift from counterterrorism to information technology. He was speaking at a TechConnect event in Washington. The ambassador said that in the rapidly changing global landscape, Pakistan must be presented as an effective trading partner in the American market.

He stressed that technology and digital cooperation could open a new chapter in bilateral ties. The event was attended by American tech professionals and business leaders.
