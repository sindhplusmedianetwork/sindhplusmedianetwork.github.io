Ayaz Sadiq says Naqvi only carries out assigned tasks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq has said that Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, like other federal ministers in Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif's cabinet, only performs the tasks and assignments given to him and does not act on his own. Speaking to journalists in Lahore, he said the impression that the interior minister works according to his own wishes is not correct, adding that every minister has his own role and responsibility. When a journalist asked who assigns the tasks to Naqvi, the Speaker did not give a clear answer and stayed silent.

Sadiq said there was much noise about the 28th Constitutional Amendment, but its draft had not yet been tabled in the National Assembly, and that it would be examined under the Constitution when it comes before the House. He added that talk of new provinces and a referendum was premature. The Speaker was speaking after inaugurating a four-day world neurosurgical conference in Lahore, organised by the Society of Neurosurgery Pakistan, which is attended by neurosurgeons from more than 50 countries and over 700 neurosurgeons, doctors and medical students from across Pakistan and will continue until November 15.

On a question about a statement by Chaudhry Salik Hussain on corruption in Punjab, he said the statement should not be given undue importance and that corruption must be ended jointly, adding that the government has a zero-tolerance policy against corruption.
