Pakistan Virtual Assets Alliance Expands Global Leadership, Appoints Korean Web3 Executive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Virtual Assets Alliance of Pakistan has expanded its global leadership. In a statement, Virtual Assets Alliance of Pakistan Chairperson Hunza Asif said South Korea's D'CENT Wallet executive Wan Kyu Kim has joined the advisory board. She said Wan Kyu Kim has been appointed as an advisory board member of the Virtual Assets Alliance.

Hunza Asif said the purpose of the appointment is to connect startups with global Web3 companies, which will give new strength to blockchain cooperation between Pakistan and South Korea. She said D'CENT Wallet supports more than 10,000 digital assets on more than 100 blockchain networks. She said leading Web3 companies of the world will be brought to Pakistan, adding that cryptocurrency adoption in Pakistan has increased significantly but regulatory challenges remain.

The chairperson said the Virtual Assets Alliance of Pakistan is committed to strengthening the digital economy through education, cooperation and responsible innovation.
