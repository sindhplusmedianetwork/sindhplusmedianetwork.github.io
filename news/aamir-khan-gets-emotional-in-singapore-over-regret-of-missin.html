Aamir Khan Gets Emotional in Singapore Over Regret of Missing Time With Family

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Aamir Khan became emotional at the Singapore International Film Festival while speaking about a major regret of his life. He said that being excessively busy with work, he could not spend precious time with his children and loved ones. The actor's voice broke as he described how his work commitments kept him away from family moments.

His remarks drew a warm response from the audience. On the occasion, Hong Kong director Peter Chan presented Aamir Khan with the Screen Icon Award. The award honoured his long contribution to cinema.
