Punjab announces grand Diwali celebrations for Hindu community

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government will celebrate the Hindu festival of Diwali with colourful festivities across the province, officials announced on Monday. Provincial Minorities Minister Ramesh Singh Arora said in a video message that the Human Rights and Minority Affairs Department was arranging special events to share the joy of the festival with the Hindu community. A grand Diwali fair will be held in Rahim Yar Khan on November 4, followed by another colourful celebration in Lahore on November 6.

Arora said the events aimed to promote love, tolerance, brotherhood and interfaith harmony, and invited Hindu families from Sindh, Balochistan and adjoining areas to take part. He said Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's vision was that minorities were an important part of society and that protecting their religious and civic rights was a basic duty of the government, quoting her as saying minorities "are the crown of my head." He added that the practical steps taken over the past two and a half years for the welfare and protection of minorities reflected that vision. Arora said Diwali was not merely a festival of lights but also a message of happiness, love, hope and peace, and that the celebrations would project Pakistan's image of religious freedom and mutual respect to the world.
