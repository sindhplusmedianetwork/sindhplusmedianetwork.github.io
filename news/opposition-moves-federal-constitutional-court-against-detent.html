Opposition moves Federal Constitutional Court against detention of PTI lawmakers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Opposition leaders in the National Assembly and Senate have approached the Federal Constitutional Court against the arrest and detention of PTI lawmakers in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Mahmood Khan Achakzai and Allama Raja Nasir Abbas filed the petition after governor's rule and emergency were imposed in the province. The petition questions whether state institutions can stop elected representatives from carrying out political and parliamentary activities.

It argues that keeping elected lawmakers in custody undermines the mandate given to them by the people. The petitioners have asked the court to order the immediate release of all detained elected representatives. The move comes as the opposition continues to challenge the emergency measures in the province through the courts.
