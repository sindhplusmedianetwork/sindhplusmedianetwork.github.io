British town crier becomes world's loudest-screaming woman at 105.9 decibels

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman who has served as a town crier in Britain since 1987 has set a Guinness World Record as the loudest-screaming woman in the world. Brenda Wilson made the official attempt on September 9, shouting the word 'Oyez' at 105.9 decibels. The achievement earned her two world records: the loudest female town crier and the loudest scream by a person in the women's category.

'Oyez' is a word town criers traditionally use during public announcements, meaning 'hear ye' or 'pay attention', and it comes from Anglo-Norman or Old French. Wilson said she felt her voice had not reached its fullest possible level during the official attempt. She took up her duties as the town crier of Peterborough in 1987 and has been associated with the role ever since.
