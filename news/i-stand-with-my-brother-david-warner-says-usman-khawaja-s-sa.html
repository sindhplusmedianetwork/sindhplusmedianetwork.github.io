'I stand with my brother': David Warner says Usman Khawaja's 'sandpapergate' claims '100pc accurate'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

David Warner has backed former teammate Usman Khawaja's claim that the entire Australian team was involved in the 2018 ball-tampering scandal known as Sandpapergate, describing the claim as 100 per cent accurate. Khawaja reignited the long-running controversy ahead of the release of his autobiography, saying that the whole team was involved. Cricket Australia, however, said it remained comfortable with its original investigation, which found that only Steve Smith, Warner and Cameron Bancroft knew about the plan.

Warner criticised that investigation, calling it rushed and botched. He said it took courage to speak the truth about what happened.
