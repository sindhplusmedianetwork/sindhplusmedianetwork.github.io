Six Suspects Arrested in Karachi Police Encounters, Five Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Karachi held four alleged encounters in different areas of the city on Wednesday, arresting six suspects, five of them injured. The encounters took place in Ferozabad near Hill Park, in Awami Colony near Jalebi Chowk in Korangi No. 6, in North Nazimabad near Asghar Ali Shah Stadium, and in Gulbahar.

Police said the suspects were wanted in street crime and other cases. Weapons, five mobile phones, cash, motorcycles and other items were recovered from the arrested men. The injured suspects were taken to Jinnah Hospital and Abbasi Shaheed Hospital for treatment.

Police said further investigations were under way to establish the suspects' involvement in other crimes.
