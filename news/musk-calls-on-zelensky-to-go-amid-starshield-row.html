Musk Calls on Zelensky to 'Go' Amid Starshield Row

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Elon Musk has called on Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky to step down, writing on X: 'And so the slaughter continues. Time for Zelensky to go.' The post was a reply to former Ukrainian defence minister Mykhailo Fedorov amid a row over the Starshield satellite system. Musk said decisions on Starshield limits were made by the US military and the president, distinguishing it from Starlink, which he said was civilian and not used for offensive operations.

Kyiv has accused Washington of helping Russia, while Zelensky said he felt 'betrayed' over Trump's diesel deal with Moscow. Trump, meanwhile, blamed Ukrainian refinery strikes for rising fuel prices, with the dispute unfolding against the backdrop of the US midterm elections.
