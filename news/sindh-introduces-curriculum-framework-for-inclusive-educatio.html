Sindh introduces curriculum framework for inclusive education

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has introduced a comprehensive curriculum framework for inclusive education to ensure equal educational opportunities for children with different abilities, children from minority communities and disadvantaged groups. Sindh Minister for Education Syed Sardar Ali Shah inaugurated the Inclusive Education Curriculum Framework for Sindh from early childhood education to Grade VIII at a ceremony in Karachi. The framework is aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goal 4 on quality education.

It was reviewed by the Directorate of Curriculum, Assessment and Research, Jamshoro, and formally notified by the School Education and Literacy Department. The minister was speaking at an event on November 12, 2026. The framework aims to bring children with different needs into the mainstream education system.
