Raids on two Islamabad call centres expose obscene content made from private data

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Raids on two call centres in Islamabad revealed that private data of people was being used to create obscene content. Additional Director NCCIA Islamabad Shoaib Khan told a press conference that 143 people were arrested in raids over the past three days, and that they were committing fraud in South Asian countries. He said 11 people were arrested from a call centre in Rawalpindi, and that the Islamabad call centre worked in four phases.

In the first phase, the gang took information from victims to access their mobile galleries, and then gave them small loans. The victims' data was then sent to another floor, where in the second phase AI was used to create obscene videos or pictures from their data, which were then used to blackmail them. He said 37 accused involved in blackmailing women were arrested, and he urged parents to ask their children where they work.

He added that online gaming and gambling apps were being widely used and were controlled, deliberately giving users profits at first, and that the NCCIA was planning a major crackdown on these applications. He said the local facilitators of every call centre were being arrested, and that those involved from abroad would also be brought to justice through the forces of those countries.
