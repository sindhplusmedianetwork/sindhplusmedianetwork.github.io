De Silva and Mendis centuries lift Sri Lanka to 404-4 against Pakistan on day three

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Play on the third day of the first Test between Pakistan and Sri Lanka at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium was brought to an early close because of poor light. At stumps, Sri Lanka had scored 404 runs for the loss of four wickets, with Dhananjaya de Silva unbeaten on 167 and captain Kusal Mendis unbeaten on 117. The pair shared an unbroken 249-run stand, bringing Sri Lanka within 30 runs of Pakistan's first-innings total of 434 for nine declared.

Mendis became the first Sri Lankan to score a century in his maiden Test as captain. Kamindu Mendis made 58 and Lahiru Udara scored 42 for the visitors, while for Pakistan, debutants Saqib Khan and Muhammad Abbas took two wickets each. On day two, Pakistan had declared their first innings at 434 for nine, with Saud Shakeel making 93, debutant Tayyab Arif scoring 57, Irfan Minhas 52, Muhammad Taha 51, Ghazi Ghori 48 and Babar Azam 50.

Four Pakistan players are making their debut in the match: Tayyab Arif, Muhammad Taha, Irfan Minhas and Saqib Khan.
