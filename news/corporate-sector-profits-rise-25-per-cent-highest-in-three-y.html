Corporate sector profits rise 25 per cent, highest in three years, says Finance Ministry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Finance Ministry has released figures for corporate sector profits, according to which profits of Pakistan's corporate sector rose by 25 per cent, and the corporate sector earned its highest profit in three years. In the first nine months of 2026, profits rose by 25 per cent on a year-on-year basis, according to a statement from the ministry issued from Islamabad. According to the statement, profits in the financial year 2026 rose by 18 per cent on a year-on-year basis across all sectors, with the engineering sector earning 304 per cent more profit and oil marketing companies earning more than 144 per cent.

According to the Finance Ministry, profits in the oil and gas exploration and production sector rose by more than 43 per cent, while the technology sector earned more than 31 per cent profit. The statement said the automobiles sector earned more than 19 per cent profit and the pharmaceuticals sector more than 14 per cent, while the cement sector earned over 13 per cent and the fertiliser sector more than 11 per cent profit. According to the ministry, corporate earnings reflect an improvement in the economic fundamentals.
