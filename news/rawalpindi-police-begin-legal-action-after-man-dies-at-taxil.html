Rawalpindi Police Begin Legal Action After Man Dies at Taxila Checkpost

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rawalpindi police have initiated legal proceedings after a 30-year-old man died at the Gangu checkpost in Taxila following what his family describes as a brutal beating by police personnel. The family alleges that the man was beaten to death during a vehicle search at the checkpost, despite repeated pleas to the personnel to stop. The death angered local residents, who took to the streets in protest and blocked the Taxila-Khanpur Road, demanding justice.

Police spokesperson Inspector Sajjadul Hassan said legal action was underway over the incident. He added that the matter would be investigated strictly on merit. Those found guilty would face homicide charges, he said.
