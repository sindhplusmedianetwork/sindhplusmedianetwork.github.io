Eight bar council members urge judiciary to act on Imran Khan's plea for medical treatment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Eight members of the Pakistan Bar Council have called on the judiciary to ensure implementation of the Supreme Court's August 18 order. The order directed that incarcerated former premier Imran Khan be transferred to Shifa International Hospital for medical treatment. The government instead took him to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences for a check-up and returned him to prison.

The bar members raised the matter in an open letter to the chief justices of the Federal Constitutional Court and the Supreme Court. Their letter asks the judiciary to take note of the plea for medical treatment and to ensure the court's order is carried out.
