Punjab Assembly Sitting Cut Short Again as Quorum Fails

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government faced embarrassment for the second consecutive day on Tuesday after it failed to ensure the required number of members in the provincial assembly. The session began three hours and seven minutes behind schedule and lasted only one hour and 22 minutes because ministers, lawmakers and parliamentary secretaries were absent. After quorum was pointed out in the house, the proceedings were adjourned until 11:30am on Wednesday.

During the sitting, treasury member Tahir Pervaiz raised the deaths of workers in boiler explosions in Faisalabad and questioned the lack of an effective safety policy. Parliamentary Secretary for Industries Hassan Askari Sheikh said inspectors were authorised to check registered boilers, and added that some accidents happened because of unregistered boilers. Speaker Malik Muhammad Ahmad Khan directed the parliamentary secretary to sit with Tahir Pervaiz and resolve the matter.

Opposition members also raised factory closures, unemployment and the issue of sugarcane growers whose crop was ready but mills were not buying it.
