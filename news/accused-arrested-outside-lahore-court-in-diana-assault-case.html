Accused arrested outside Lahore court in Diana assault case despite bail extension

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

LAHORE: In a dramatic turn in the alleged assault case of foreign national Diana, a sessions court on Thursday extended the interim bail of accused Tanveer till October 27, but police arrested him outside the court premises moments later. Police also took Tanveer's brother and a few others into custody. According to Express News, Tanveer's lawyer Advocate Safdar Shaheen Pirzada appeared before the court and argued that police had been deployed around the sessions court to arrest his client, expressing fear that he was being arrested in a new case.

Diana's lawyer, on the other hand, filed an application seeking permission to record the complainant's statement through a video link, contending that she was not being granted a Pakistan visa. During the hearing, the court summoned the sessions court's security in-charge and ordered that the accused be safely escorted to his vehicle, remarking that security could be provided up to the vehicle and no further. A separate petition against the arrest claims that the accused, who was on interim bail till October 27, was picked up by CCD and plainclothes personnel and taken to an unknown location, raising fears that he could be harmed in custody.

The petition has asked the court to recover the accused and order his production in court. It also emerged that Tanveer had secured protective bail from the Sindh High Court, while two other accused, Mohsin and Zain, have already been arrested; the case was registered by Defence-C police on Diana's complaint. In a video statement, Diana herself declared accused Ahmed Tanveer innocent, saying he had committed no assault against her and had returned her mobile phone and belongings, appealing for the case to be closed.
