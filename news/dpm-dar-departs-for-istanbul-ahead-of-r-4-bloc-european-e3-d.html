DPM Dar Departs for Istanbul Ahead of R-4 Bloc, European E3 Diplomats' Meetings

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar has departed for Istanbul to attend a meeting of R-4 bloc foreign ministers and an R-4+E-3 meeting on Friday, the Foreign Office said. The R-4 group, comprising Pakistan, Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye, emerged in March and has been focusing on regional security and mediation efforts between the United States and Iran. The E-3 group comprises France, the United Kingdom and Germany, set up more than 20 years ago to advance diplomatic coordination over Iran's nuclear programme.

Dar will attend the meeting at the invitation of Turkiye's Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan, and it will be the first meeting of the seven countries in this format. The meetings will focus on key regional developments and explore coordinated diplomatic approaches to advance regional peace and stability, the FO said, adding that Dar will also hold bilateral meetings with his counterparts. A Turkish diplomatic source told Reuters the seven countries would meet at Turkiye's initiative as wars involving Iran, Yemen and Ukraine threaten shipping routes and regional security.

The ministers are expected to discuss the Israeli-Palestinian issue, the Strait of Hormuz situation, Yemen, efforts to end the Russia-Ukraine war, and closer regional-European cooperation.
