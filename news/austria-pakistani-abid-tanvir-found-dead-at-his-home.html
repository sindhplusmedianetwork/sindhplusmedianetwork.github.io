Austria: Pakistani Abid Tanvir Found Dead at His Home

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Pakistani, Abid Tanvir, was found dead at his home in Gleisdorf village near the city of Graz in Austria. According to police details, when the door of Abid Tanvir's home was broken on November 12, he was found dead. According to the police report, Abid Tanvir's death occurred on November 7 or 8, however the possible cause of death could not be known yet.

Police say they are investigating the cause of death. Sources say Abid Tanvir's neighbours informed police that a smell was coming from inside. Abid Tanvir, who belonged to Mandi Bahauddin in Pakistan, had been residing in Austria for the past 17 to 18 years, however a few days before his death, on November 5, he received an Austrian visa, after which he was preparing to go to Mandi Bahauddin, Pakistan.

Haji Malik Muhammad Amin Awan, chief servant of the Pak Tadafeen Committee, has appealed to the Pakistani community for funds to send Abid Tanvir's body to Pakistan.
