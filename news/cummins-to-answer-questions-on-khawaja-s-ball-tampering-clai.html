Cummins to answer questions on Khawaja's ball-tampering claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Australian Test captain Pat Cummins has said he will break his silence on Usman Khawaja's ball-tampering claims, announcing that he will answer questions from the media at Cricket Central on Friday at 9:30am. Khawaja claimed in his new autobiography, 'Sabr', that all Australian players knew about the plan to tamper with the ball during the 2018 Cape Town Test against South Africa. Former captain Michael Clarke has urged Cummins to tell the truth about the episode, while Khawaja's former opening partner David Warner has backed the claims, saying what is written in the book is correct.

Mitchell Starc, meanwhile, has said his position has not changed and that he did not know about the plan to use sandpaper on the ball. Separately, a video said to show Cummins in a heated exchange with his wife Becky on a street in Sydney has gone viral, although what was actually said in the exchange is not clear. Cummins has not commented publicly on the video.
