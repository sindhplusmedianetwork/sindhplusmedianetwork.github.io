Iran Accuses US of Choosing War, Destroying Islamabad Memorandum

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's foreign ministry has sharply criticised the United States, accusing Washington of choosing war and then trying to dodge responsibility for it. In a post on X on Wednesday, ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei said the US could not launch a war of choice, nor could it evade accountability by turning the aggressor into the accuser. He charged that Washington had attacked Iran twice in less than nine months while talks were still ongoing between the two sides.

Baqaei said the US had also destroyed the very memorandum of understanding it signed in Islamabad on June 17, blaming Washington for wrecking the agreement. Separately, Ebrahim Azizi, head of the Iranian parliament's National Security Commission, issued a stern warning to Washington. He said Iran was fully prepared to rid the region of evil 'once and for all' in response to the slightest operational error.

The remarks underline the sharp escalation in rhetoric between Tehran and Washington amid the ongoing standoff.
