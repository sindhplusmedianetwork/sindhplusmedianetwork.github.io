Manchester City Stumble Again in Fulham Draw, Arsenal Held by Newcastle in Premier League

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Manchester City's winless Premier League run extended to four games after a 1-1 draw against Fulham. Leaders Arsenal were held to a 2-2 draw at Newcastle United on Saturday. Third-placed City trail six points behind Arsenal.

City's title challenge wilts in the wake of a guilty verdict on widespread breaches of financial rules, which the club deny. Elliot Anderson's strike saved City from a fourth consecutive defeat. Noni Madueke's 78th-minute goal salvaged a point for Arsenal at St James' Park.
