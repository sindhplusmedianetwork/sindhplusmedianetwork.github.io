KP honours 17 sports personalities at Sports Heroes Awards 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Seventeen outstanding sports personalities from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa were honoured on Friday at the Sports Heroes Awards 2026 ceremony held at Nishtar Hall in Peshawar. The awards recognised their remarkable services, national and international achievements and distinguished performances in the field of sports. The awards were distributed in three categories: Legends, Champions and Emerging Talent.

Former world squash champion Jahangir Khan was among the recipients in the Legends category. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Sports Adviser Dr Syed Sarfraz Ali Shah congratulated the winners at the ceremony. He said the awards recognise the players' services and encourage the younger generation towards sports.
