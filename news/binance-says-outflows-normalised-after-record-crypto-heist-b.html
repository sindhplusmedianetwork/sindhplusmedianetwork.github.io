Binance Says Outflows Normalised After Record Crypto Heist Blamed on North Korea

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cryptocurrency exchange Binance says asset outflows have normalised after customers withdrew a net $4.72 billion in the wake of what could be the largest financial heist in history. More than $6 billion in crypto assets were stolen in the breach, which the FBI has blamed on North Korea. Binance co-CEO Richard Teng said hourly deposits now exceed withdrawals and that 95 per cent of the affected tokens had been replenished.

North Korea rejected the US allegation, with Kim Yo Jong calling it 'absurd rhetoric and nothing new'. Pyongyang said it had 'issues to reconsider' over US hostility toward resuming summit diplomacy. The exchange said customer funds were being restored as the investigation continues.
