AFC Postpones Asian Cup 2027 Over Security Concerns in Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Asian Football Confederation announced on Tuesday that the 2027 Asian Cup in Saudi Arabia has been postponed, pushing the 24-team tournament from its original January 7 to February 5, 2027 window to December 23, 2027 to January 21, 2028. The AFC said the decision was taken in view of exceptional circumstances in the region, citing recent regional developments, substantial logistical complexities and travel disruptions affecting participating teams, officials and spectators. The confederation will now submit the revised dates to FIFA for approval.

The tournament was scheduled to be hosted in Riyadh, Jeddah and Al Khobar. The decision follows a Houthi missile attack on Riyadh airport in October, in which 13 people were killed. Other events in Saudi Arabia have also been affected, including the Six Kings Slam tennis tournament, which has already been cancelled.
