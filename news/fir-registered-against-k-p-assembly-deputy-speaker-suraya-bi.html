FIR Registered Against K-P Assembly Deputy Speaker Suraya Bibi Over Official Vehicle

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An FIR has been registered against Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Assembly Deputy Speaker Suraya Bibi over the alleged failure to return an official vehicle. The case was filed by the Deputy Commissioner of Chitral, and its text states that Suraya Bibi did not return the government vehicle. Family sources offered a different account, saying the vehicle had been given to her by the district administration after she became a member of the assembly.

According to them, the office of the Deputy Commissioner Chitral was contacted several times by phone with a request to come and take the vehicle back, but no one arrived to collect it. In a separate development, the Peshawar High Court has fixed for hearing the petitions seeking the recovery of former Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa chief minister Sohail Afridi and provincial assembly Speaker Babar Saleem Swati. The petitions for their recovery will now be taken up for hearing.
