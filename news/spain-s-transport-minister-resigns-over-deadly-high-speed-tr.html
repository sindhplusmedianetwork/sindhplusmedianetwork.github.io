Spain's transport minister resigns over deadly high-speed train crash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Spain's transport minister Oscar Puente has resigned over the high-speed train crash that killed 69 people near Urda on October 28. It was the country's second deadly rail crash in less than a year — a January crash on the same line had killed 46 people. 'A transport minister in a country like Spain cannot continue in office with two accidents of this severity in less than a year,' Puente told lawmakers on Tuesday.

Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez praised Puente's service on social media. Earlier on Tuesday, before announcing his resignation, Puente had unveiled an emergency aid package for victims' families. The package includes doubled insurance payouts, a 40 kph speed limit when signals fail, and what officials called a world-first AI system to monitor driver-controller conversations.
