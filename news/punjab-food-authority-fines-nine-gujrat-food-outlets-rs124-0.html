Punjab Food Authority fines nine Gujrat food outlets Rs124,000

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Food Authority has fined nine food outlets in Gujrat district a total of 124,000 rupees for violating food safety regulations. Inspection teams led by Deputy District Officer Sadia Rafat carried out 54 inspections across the district on Friday. During the checks, teams issued corrective notices to 12 outlets and issued an Emergency Prohibition Order.

Inspectors discarded 15 litres of expired beverages, 10 litres of unsafe used cooking oil, two kilograms of spoiled meat, 2.5 kilograms of expired bakery items and 300 grammes of China salt. The authority said the action was taken to protect public health and ensure that food sold to consumers meets safety standards.
