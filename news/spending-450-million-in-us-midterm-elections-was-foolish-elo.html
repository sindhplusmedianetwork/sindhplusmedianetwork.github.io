Spending $450 million in US midterm elections was foolish: Elon Musk admits

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Elon Musk, head of American technology companies Tesla and SpaceX, has admitted that despite spending a record approximately 450 million dollars on the 2026 US midterm elections, he regrets this investment after not achieving the desired results. Elon Musk, addressing the New York Times DealBook Summit online, admitted that electoral success cannot be bought with heavy financial support. Musk was the largest individual donor in the 2026 election cycle and provided millions of dollars to various political super PACs to maintain the Republican Party's majority in the Senate, while his major focus was the state of Texas, where his companies' headquarters are located.

He also spent millions of dollars on Republican candidate Ken Paxton for the Texas Senate seat, however he lost to Democratic candidate James Talarico. Overall, Democrats succeeded in gaining control of both houses of Congress. Musk said that if he got a chance to go back in time, he would spend less money, and that he was so busy building SpaceX and Tesla that he could not personally pay attention to election campaigns.
