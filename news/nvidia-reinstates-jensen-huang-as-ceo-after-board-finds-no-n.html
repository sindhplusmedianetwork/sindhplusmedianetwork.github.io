Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after board finds no national security risk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday. The board acted after input from senior US government officials left it confident he posed no national security risk. Huang had resigned less than two weeks earlier over an alleged affair and spy scandal involving Alice Huang of Megaspeed.

The board voted unanimously to restore him as president, CEO and board member effective immediately. Shares jumped more than 25 percent in premarket trading. President Trump had publicly urged his return.
