Trump Hints US May Attack Iran Again After Midterms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump, speaking to journalists outside the White House, has hinted that the United States may soon attack Iran again. 'We hit Iran very hard and we may take some more action,' he said, adding that Iran cannot be allowed to acquire nuclear weapons. Asked about the war's effect on voters during the US midterms, he said in one sense he likes it because he is doing what other presidents should have done, while also noting 'there is a price to pay'.

He denied negotiating with Iran and said the US is performing very well against Iran militarily and financially. Trump alleged Iran is raising oil prices before the midterms and dismissed reported attacks and mine incidents near the Strait of Hormuz as false. The report also cites a US newspaper claiming the US and Israel have planned new post-midterm strikes on Iran, with possible targets including energy installations, nuclear centres, and missile and drone stockpiles.
