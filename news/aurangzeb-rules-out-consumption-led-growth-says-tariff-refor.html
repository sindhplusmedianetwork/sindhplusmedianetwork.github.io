Aurangzeb rules out consumption-led growth, says tariff reforms will continue

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb has ruled out pursuing liquidity-fuelled, consumption-led economic growth, saying Pakistan must prioritise sustainable, export-led expansion and continue with tariff reforms. Speaking at the Lahore University of Management Sciences on Saturday at an event organised by the American Business Forum, he said Pakistan could not afford to repeat the cycle of import-dependent growth that had previously led to balance-of-payments problems. He said the government was committed to continuing tariff reforms, primarily to reduce the cost of raw materials and intermediate goods for exporters, despite concerns over timing.

“We are in year two of the tariff reform, and we are going to continue with that; there is no turning back,” he said. He pointed to measures in the latest budget, including the elimination of super taxes, reductions in tax slabs and financing for exporters at 4.5 per cent. He also said the combined twin deficits had fallen from 12.5 per cent of GDP to 2.6 per cent by the end of the last fiscal year, the fiscal deficit had dropped to a 22-year low, and the current account deficit had narrowed to around 0.1 per cent of GDP.

Energy supplies had been secured through November amid the Middle East conflict, with winter arrangements underway, he added.
