Pakistan's debt pile climbs to Rs98.881 trillion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's total debt and liabilities rose by 6.46% to Rs98.881 trillion by the end of September 2026, according to data released by the State Bank on Friday. A year earlier, the figure stood at Rs92.879 trillion. Total debt alone reached Rs97.038 trillion, driven by higher domestic and external government borrowing.

Debt servicing in the first quarter of the current fiscal year jumped by 33% to Rs2.9 trillion. The sharp rise was caused by higher principal repayments and interest payments. The figures underline the growing weight of debt servicing on the country's public finances.
