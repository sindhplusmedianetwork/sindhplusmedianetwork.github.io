Ahsan Iqbal calls for permanent system to end pay gaps for TTS university teachers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Planning, Development and Special Initiatives Ahsan Iqbal has said a permanent, transparent and sustainable system should be devised to remove disparities in the salaries of university teachers under the Tenure Track System, so that meetings are not needed again and again to fix pay. He expressed these views while chairing a high-level meeting called to review the salary structure of TTS teachers and the existing disparities. Minister of State for Finance Bilal Azhar Kayani, senior officials of the finance ministry, the Higher Education Commission, the Planning Ministry's Member Science and Technology and other officials, along with nominated representatives of universities, attended the meeting.

Ahsan Iqbal said a clear and fair formula should be made keeping a proper difference between the salaries of professors, associate professors and assistant professors, and that a lasting system working on a set principle was needed both to remove the current pay gap and to govern future increases. He said it was a matter of serious concern that many capable and highly educated professors in Pakistan had left the country in search of better opportunities, and that since the government had invested in their education and training, they must be kept in the country by providing proper pay and a better professional environment. The minister directed that a workable system be prepared on the basis of the current pay gap, the inflation rate and future increases linked to the Consumer Price Index, so that the financial impact and the resources needed for each teaching post could be properly determined.

He instructed the HEC that the data and information presented at the meeting must be complete and authentic, and made clear that if it were proved that the HEC had provided misleading or wrong information to the forum, action would be taken against the commission.
