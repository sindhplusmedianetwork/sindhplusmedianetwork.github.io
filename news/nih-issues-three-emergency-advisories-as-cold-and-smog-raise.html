NIH Issues Three Emergency Advisories as Cold and Smog Raise Seasonal Flu Risk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Institute of Health has issued three emergency advisories over the rising risk of seasonal flu in winter. The advisories warn that falling temperatures and smog have increased the risk of seasonal flu. The institute also warned of the possible spread of measles, diphtheria, whooping cough and tetanus.

Federal and provincial health departments, hospitals and laboratories have been directed to prioritise prevention. The advisories also call for stronger surveillance and timely diagnosis.
