Painter creates Statue of Liberty painting in under a minute, sets Guinness record

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Performance painter David Garibaldi set a new Guinness World Record by painting the Statue of Liberty in under a minute. He completed the painting in just 43.5 seconds. The attempt took place at a New York City coastal location with a clear view of the monument.

It was held during the 22nd annual Guinness World Records Day. Garibaldi said the attempt focused entirely on speed. Official judge Michael Empric praised the choice of painting the Statue of Liberty on the New York coast.
