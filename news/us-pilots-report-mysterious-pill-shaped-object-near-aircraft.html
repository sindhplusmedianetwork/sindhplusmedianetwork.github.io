US Pilots Report Mysterious Pill-Shaped Object Near Aircraft, Radar Shows Nothing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pilots flying near Ohio in the United States have claimed to see a mysterious shiny, silver object near their aircraft. One of the pilots photographed the pill- or capsule-shaped object, according to a report filed with the National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC). However, air traffic control radar showed nothing at the location.

The report was filed on October 26 about a flight that took place on August 16. Investigators who examined the case said they found no wrinkles, seams or markings on the object. Controllers at Cleveland Center also reported no radar contact with anything unusual.

The object remains unidentified. NUFORC continues to collect such reports from pilots and the public.
