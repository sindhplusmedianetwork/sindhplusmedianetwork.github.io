Challan in Hina Javed murder case presented to sessions judge in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Rawalpindi District Prosecutor has presented the challan in the Hina Javed murder case to the sessions judge. The District and Sessions Judge will hold the initial hearing in the case tomorrow. The case was registered at Civil Lines police station after 45-year-old Hina Javed was allegedly strangled to death by her husband on August 20.

The Punjab Prosecution declared the case high-profile. Rawalpindi police completed the investigation in three months.
