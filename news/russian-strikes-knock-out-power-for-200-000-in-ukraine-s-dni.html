Russian Strikes Knock Out Power for 200,000+ in Ukraine's Dnipro Region

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian missile and drone strikes on Ukraine's Dnipropetrovsk region have knocked out electricity for more than 200,000 customers in Dnipro city and surrounding districts. The grid operator ordered emergency shutdowns in Dnipro, its suburbs and parts of Pavlohrad after the attacks severely damaged power infrastructure. Energy workers are trying to restore key installations, officials said.

The blackout came a day after a Russian drone strike on a busy Dnipro railway platform killed at least 14 people and wounded more than a dozen. President Volodymyr Zelensky called that attack horrific. Ukraine says Russia is systematically targeting energy infrastructure ahead of winter to weaken the country's resistance.

Russia's defence ministry separately claimed it shot down more than 1,000 Ukrainian drones overnight.
