At least 21 people killed as migrant boat sinks off Senegal coast

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 21 people were killed when a boat carrying migrants trying to reach Europe sank off the coast of Senegal, while one person was rescued alive. Senegal's interior ministry said in a statement issued on Sunday that the accident happened near the coast about 180 kilometres north of the capital, Dakar. Rescue teams had recovered 21 bodies from the sea by Saturday evening, the ministry said.

Officials said the people on board were trying to travel illegally to other countries, but the total number of people on the boat is still unknown. Search and rescue operations are continuing in the sea to trace any more possible survivors. Every year, large numbers of migrants from West Africa take the dangerous sea route towards Spain's Canary Islands, and boats have sunk on the journey many times before, claiming thousands of lives.
