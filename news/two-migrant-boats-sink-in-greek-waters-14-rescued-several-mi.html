Two Migrant Boats Sink in Greek Waters; 14 Rescued, Several Missing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two boats carrying illegal migrants sank in Greek waters near the islands of Samos and Lesbos, with several people missing, Arab media reported from Athens. The Greek coast guard rescued 14 people from the sea, and the search for others is ongoing. Greek authorities detained some people on human-smuggling charges.

The eastern Aegean route from Turkiye to the Greek islands remains one of the most used and dangerous crossings for migrants heading to Europe.
