Supreme Court Upholds Death Sentence for Killer of Woman Who Rejected Proposal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A three-member Supreme Court bench headed by Justice Muhammad Hashim Khan Kakar, with Justices Salahuddin Panhwar and Ishtiaq Ibrahim, has dismissed the review petition of convict Muhammad Kamran and upheld his death sentence. Kamran was convicted of murdering Mst Memoona Shaheen after she and her family rejected his marriage proposal. In a nine-page judgment authored by Justice Kakar, the court held that a woman's refusal of a marriage proposal aggravates rather than mitigates the offence.

The bench observed that Kamran had already spent around 15 to 16 years in custody but did not qualify for the life-expectancy relief available to prisoners who have served 25 years. The judgment strongly condemned customary practices such as ghag, swara, vani and watta-satta, terming them grave violations of women's dignity. It reaffirmed that a valid marriage under Islamic injunctions requires the free and willing consent of both parties.

The court ruled that killing a woman for rejecting a proposal was a grave offence that warranted no leniency.
