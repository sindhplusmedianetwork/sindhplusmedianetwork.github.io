Muhammad Abbas Becomes Leading Wicket-Taker in Test Cricket This Year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan medium-fast bowler Muhammad Abbas has become the leading wicket-taker in Test cricket this year. He took 6 wickets in the second Test against Sri Lanka and played an important role in the team's win, earning the player of the match and player of the series awards. He claimed 8 wickets in the two-match series, taking his tally for the year to 39 wickets in 14 Test innings.

England's Josh Tongue, with 33 wickets in 14 innings, and Australia's Mitchell Starc, with 32 wickets in the same number of innings, stand second and third respectively. Abbas's performance in the second Test was central to Pakistan's victory in the match.
