Bannu: Two Civilians Wounded as Remote-Controlled Bomb Explodes After Attack on Police

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two civilians were wounded in a remote-controlled bomb blast in Bannu on Thursday night. According to Express News, unknown militants opened fire on a police patrol party in the jurisdiction of Maryan police station during a routine patrol. Police retaliated with return fire, after which a remote-controlled bomb planted in a motorcycle parked by the roadside exploded.

Police said the police party remained safe during the exchange of fire, while the two wounded civilians were immediately shifted to the District Headquarters Hospital. Police have launched further investigations into the incident.
