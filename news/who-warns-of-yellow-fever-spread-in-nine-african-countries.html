WHO warns of yellow fever spread in nine African countries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Health Organization has warned that yellow fever is spreading in nine African countries, with 67 confirmed cases reported so far. The affected countries are Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Cote d'Ivoire, Ghana, Guinea, Liberia, Togo and Nigeria. The WHO said the risk of further spread is rising because of cross-border movement of people, the presence of mosquitoes that carry the disease, and differences in vaccination rates between countries.

It has rated the public health risk as moderate at the regional level and low at the global level. Health authorities in the affected region are being urged to strengthen surveillance and vaccination efforts to contain the outbreak and prevent it from reaching new areas.
