Punjab orders stronger garbage-tax collection as only Rs25 million recovered against Rs18 billion target

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Director General of Excise in Punjab has ordered improved collection of the garbage tax after recovery so far this fiscal year reached only Rs25 million against a six-month target of Rs18 billion set by the Punjab government. According to 24News, billing for the garbage tax began on 1 September, and 2.2 million bills have been sent in 70 days. The Excise Department spokesperson said the department has ordered the issuance of bills across Punjab to be completed this month, and the officers concerned will themselves remain active in the field for garbage-tax recovery.

The officers will improve recovery by the end of the first quarter and send a report, and no property owner will receive an NOC without paying the garbage tax. The department has also directed timely steps for the recovery of Rs36 billion in the next quarter.
