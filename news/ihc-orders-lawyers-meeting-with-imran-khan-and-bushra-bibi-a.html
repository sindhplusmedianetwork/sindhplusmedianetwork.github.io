IHC Orders Lawyers' Meeting with Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has directed authorities to let lead counsels meet Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail. Sardar Latif Khosa and Salman Safdar will meet them today at 3pm for one hour. The order came while the court was hearing their appeals against the January 2026 conviction in the 190 million pound case.

Safdar told the court that Imran Khan's eye condition had improved but his blood pressure remained uncontrolled. He also told the court that Bushra Bibi's eyesight was in very poor condition. The court directed the jail authorities to arrange the meeting as ordered.
