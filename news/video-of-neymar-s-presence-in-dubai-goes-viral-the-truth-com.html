Video of Neymar's presence in Dubai goes viral, the truth comes out

A video circulating on social media showing a man resembling Brazilian footballer Neymar Jr near Dubai Mall left users confused, but the man in the clip is actually the footballer's lookalike, not Neymar himself. The video was viewed more than 340,000 times in under 24 hours and showed a man strolling near Dubai Mall whose face and appearance closely resemble the star, with several people gathering around him to take photographs. The words 'Neymar in Dubai' written on the video added to the impression that the Brazilian star had suddenly arrived in the city.

In reality, the 34-year-old Neymar was in his home country Brazil, where on Monday 16 November he attended a Kings League event in Guarulhos, Sao Paulo. He was invited onto the pitch as a special guest for a Kings Cup Brazil match involving his co-owned team Furia FC, and took a special 'president penalty' with his hand covering his eyes, scoring without looking at the goal. Despite his successful penalty, Furia FC lost the match 5-4 to Podpah Funk Bol Club.
