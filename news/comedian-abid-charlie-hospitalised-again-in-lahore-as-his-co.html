Comedian Abid Charlie hospitalised again in Lahore as his condition worsens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Well-known Pakistani comedian and actor Abid Charlie has been shifted to Lahore General Hospital after his health deteriorated again. According to the report, he was moved to the hospital in Lahore when his condition worsened once more. The hospital's medical superintendent, Professor Dr Faryad Hussain, said the actor was brought to the hospital in a critical condition.

He said the best possible medical facilities are being provided to him. The medical superintendent added that, under the Punjab government's policy, Abid Charlie's treatment is being carried out free of charge. He said the medical staff have been instructed to ensure the actor's special care and the best treatment.

The hospital administration is continuously monitoring his health, the report added.
