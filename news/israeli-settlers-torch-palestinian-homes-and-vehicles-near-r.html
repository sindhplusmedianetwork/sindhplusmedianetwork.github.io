Israeli settlers torch Palestinian homes and vehicles near Ramallah, opposition leader calls it Jewish terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Extremist Israeli settlers torched several Palestinian homes and about a dozen vehicles in the villages of Deir al-Sudan, Deir Abu Mashal and Kafr Malik near Ramallah in the occupied West Bank on the night between Saturday and Sunday. The settlers also wrote provocative and racist slogans on walls during the attacks. No injuries were reported in the incidents.

Israeli police began investigating the arson and vandalism. Israeli opposition leader and Yashar Party head Gadi Eisenkot condemned the attacks as Jewish terrorism in every sense. He blamed the policies of National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir and Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich for the attacks.
