Senate Committee Seeks Report on Minor Girl's Alleged Torture Death at FIA Officer's Residence

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

On the matter of the alleged torture death of 13-year-old Fatima Asghar at an FIA officer's official residence, the Senate standing committee has sought a detailed report from the IG Islamabad on the girl's murder and police action. The report was directed to include details of the post-mortem, medico-legal findings and marks of torture. Facts regarding the role of the nominated accused, including the concerned FIA officer, and their presence abroad were also sought.

According to the notice, details of steps to ensure the availability of the alleged dual-national accused for investigation and trial were also sought. The notice also sought an explanation of the girl's employment as a domestic worker, her education at a madrassa and her residence at Police Lines Headquarters.
