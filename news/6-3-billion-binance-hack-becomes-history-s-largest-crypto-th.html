$6.3 Billion Binance Hack Becomes History's Largest Crypto Theft

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read

The largest cryptocurrency theft ever recorded has hit the exchange Binance, with hackers draining 6.3 billion dollars, worth 17 trillion 64 billion rupees. The North Korean hacking group TraderTraitor has been blamed for the heist. According to a Guardian report, the hackers are using new and never-before-seen techniques to hide the stolen money.

Stablecoins worth 140 billion rupees (50 crore dollars) were converted into other cryptocurrencies. The stolen amount was scattered across different digital wallets through thousands of transactions. Investigators say the laundering methods are unusual and extremely hard to trace.

The incident is a heavy blow to confidence in the world's biggest crypto trading platforms.
