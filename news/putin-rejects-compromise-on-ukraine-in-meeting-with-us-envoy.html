Putin rejects compromise on Ukraine in meeting with US envoys

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian President Vladimir Putin indicated during a Kremlin meeting with US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff that Russia will not compromise on its hardline demands to end the Ukraine war, while offering a short-term ceasefire in a key frontline city. Putin ruled out foreign peacekeeping troops in Ukraine and rejected demilitarised 'grey' zones, saying the only line of demarcation is the state border. Witkoff said Putin had agreed to let European representatives join the next round of talks.

The envoys then travelled to Dublin for talks with President Zelensky. The meeting was the latest round of US-led efforts to end the war. No breakthrough was reported from the talks.
