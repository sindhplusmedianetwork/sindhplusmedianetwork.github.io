Ali Asgar Reveals Why He Left The Kapil Sharma Show

Indian comedian and actor Ali Asgar has finally revealed why he walked away from The Kapil Sharma Show after several years. Speaking in an interview with Maniesh Paul, he said that playing women characters continuously had become a source of monotony for him. The actor said he wanted to explore his abilities in new directions instead of repeating the same kind of roles.

He admitted that the decision to leave the show was not an easy one for him to make. The revelation answers a question that fans had been asking for years. Ali Asgar became widely known for his comic female characters on the show, which made him a household name.

His comments shed light on the personal reasons behind his exit.
