Development Comes From Education and Jobs, Not Guns: Aimal Wali Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Awami National Party central president Aimal Wali Khan has said that true development is achieved through education, employment and basic facilities, not through guns and gunpowder. Addressing supporters, he stressed that every citizen has the right to live in peace. He expressed regret that Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, despite being rich in natural resources, sees its people forced to seek work abroad.

He pointed out the irony that gas from Karak fuels factories in Punjab while local communities in the province remain deprived of the same resource. Aimal Wali further noted that the province generates nearly 6,000 megawatts of electricity while its own requirement is only about 2,500 megawatts. He argued that if the province's own resources were used for its own people, its fortunes would change.
