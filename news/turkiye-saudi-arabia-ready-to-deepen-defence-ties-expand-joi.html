Türkiye, Saudi Arabia ready to deepen defence ties, expand joint production

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Türkiye is ready to expand defence industry cooperation with Saudi Arabia, backed by over 1,400 projects and a portfolio of around $120 billion, the country's top defence official said on Thursday. The head of the Secretariat of Defence Industries, Haluk Gorgun, was speaking at the Türkiye-Saudi Arabia Defence Industry Days in Istanbul, organised by Türkiye's Foreign Economic Relations Board, the defence industry cluster SAHA Istanbul and Saudi Arabian Military Industries. Saudi Minister of State Bandar Al-Khorayef, Türkiye's Deputy Minister of Industry and Technology Cetin Ali Donmez and Istanbul Governor Davut Gul also attended the talks.

Gorgun said Türkiye had built a strong defence ecosystem over the past 20 years and aimed to co-develop its model with friendly nations, noting that Turkish officials had held 75 meetings with Saudi counterparts over the past three and a half years. He added that Turkish companies were conducting joint work with Saudi partners to support Riyadh's Vision 2030 goal of localising at least 50 per cent of defence production. Al-Khorayef said Saudi Arabia's defence localisation rate had risen from 4 per cent to 27 per cent, with a target of reaching 50 per cent through technology transfer and long-term partnerships.

Donmez noted that Türkiye's defence and aerospace exports had surged from $248 million in 2002 to over $10 billion today, employing more than 100,000 skilled personnel across over 4,000 entities.
