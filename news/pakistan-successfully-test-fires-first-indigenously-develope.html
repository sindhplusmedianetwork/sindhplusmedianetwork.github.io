Pakistan successfully test-fires first indigenously developed air-to-air missile 'Fa'az'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan on Tuesday performed a successful flight test of Fa'az, the country's first indigenously developed air-to-air missile, the military's media wing ISPR announced. According to the ISPR statement, the missile is equipped with a state-of-the-art terminal guidance system and can engage enemy targets at longer ranges. The statement said Fa'az will significantly enhance the operational flexibility of the Pakistan Air Force and further strengthen the country's overall defence posture.

It added that the successful test underscores the technical maturity, innovation and self-reliance achieved by Pakistan's defence industry. The launch was witnessed by senior military officers, scientists and engineers involved in developing the system. President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Chief of Defence Forces and Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir, the service chiefs and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi congratulated the scientists and the nation.

The test follows last month's successful launches of the Fatah-2 extended-range hypersonic missile and the extended-range Fatah-IV cruise missile.
