Raiwind Ijtima Concludes With Emotional Final Prayer; Over 600,000 Attended

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first phase of the annual Raiwind World Tablighi Ijtima came to an end with an emotional final prayer attended by more than 600,000 people. The concluding supplication prayed for the dignity of the Muslim world, the security of Pakistan and unity among Muslims, and many in the gathering were moved to tears. Scholars addressing the congregation, including Maulana Zia-ul-Haq and Maulana Zubair-ul-Hassan of India, said the gathering brings change to people's lives and urged Muslims to fill mosques, promote brotherhood and spend in the way of Allah.

They said that spending in Allah's way does not reduce sustenance, and that Muslims should live by Islamic principles and learn to live together as a community. Thousands of participants announced they would dedicate their lives to preaching Islam, and the prayer asked Allah to illuminate hearts, forgive sins and accept everyone for the work of faith. After the gathering ended, the departure of participants was completed and all roads leading to the venue were reopened to normal traffic.
