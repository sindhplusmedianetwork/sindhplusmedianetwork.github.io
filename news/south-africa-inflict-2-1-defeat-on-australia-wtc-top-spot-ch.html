South Africa inflict 2-1 defeat on Australia; WTC top spot changes hands

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Australia lost the top spot in the World Test Championship table and the ICC Test rankings after a 2-1 Test series defeat to South Africa. New Zealand moved to first in the WTC table, with South Africa rising to second. South Africa also took first place in the ICC Test team rankings.

The result marks a significant shift in the race for the championship final. Australia now faces a crucial home series against New Zealand, starting December 9 in Perth. The four-Test series will be a key test of Australia's bid to reclaim the top position.
