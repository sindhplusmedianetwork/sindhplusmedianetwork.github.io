US Judge Rejects BBC Bid to Dismiss Trump's Defamation Suit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A federal judge has allowed US President Donald Trump's multi-billion dollar defamation suit against the BBC to proceed. The judge rejected the British broadcaster's bid to dismiss the case on Tuesday. Trump has accused the BBC of deceptively editing a documentary to make it seem as if he was inciting violence when he told supporters to march on the US Capitol on Jan 6, 2021.

He is seeking at least $5 billion for defamation and has also raised a separate Florida consumer protection claim. US District Judge Jeffrey T. Kuntz rejected the bid to dismiss the defamation suit but dismissed the Florida protection law claim.

In a 40-page ruling, he wrote that whether Trump can prove the claims and establish damages is left for another day.
