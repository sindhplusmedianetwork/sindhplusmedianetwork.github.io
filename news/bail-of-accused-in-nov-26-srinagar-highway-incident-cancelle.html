Bail of accused in Nov 26 Srinagar Highway incident cancelled

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A division bench of the Islamabad High Court comprising Chief Justice Sardar Mohammad Sarfraz Dogar and Justice Shahrukh Arjumand allowed the prosecution's petition seeking cancellation of the accused's bail, observing that the trial court had failed to provide reasons for treating the case as one requiring further inquiry. The case relates to the November 26, 2024 incident on Srinagar Highway during the PTI protest, when Rangers personnel were run over by a vehicle. During the hearing, Islamabad Prosecutor General Manzoor Ahmed Jajja, representing the prosecution, argued that the trial court had granted bail to the accused on technical grounds.

Counsel for the accused submitted that bail had been granted around one-and-a-half years ago and that his client had regularly appeared before the trial court on every hearing date since then. In its written order, the high court observed that it was an admitted position that the trial court, while granting post-arrest bail, had not recorded any reasoning to establish the necessity for further inquiry, and it consequently accepted the prosecution's petition and cancelled the bail. The court remanded the bail matter to the trial court for a fresh decision and directed the prosecution and defence to appear before it on November 25, which will rehear the bail application and pass a fresh order in accordance with law.

According to police, the accused, Hashim Abbasi, is already in custody in connection with another case.
