Messi Makes History as First Player to Win MLS Golden Boot in Two Straight Seasons

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lionel Messi has made history in Major League Soccer by becoming the first player to win the Golden Boot in two consecutive seasons. The Inter Miami captain scored 26 goals and provided 17 assists in 31 matches in the 2026 season. His contributions added up to a direct involvement in 43 goals over the campaign.

Messi had also won the award in 2025, when he scored 29 goals. The back-to-back Golden Boots underline his continued dominance in the American league. At 38, Messi remains the central figure in Inter Miami's attack.

The record sets a new benchmark for goalscoring consistency in the league's history.
