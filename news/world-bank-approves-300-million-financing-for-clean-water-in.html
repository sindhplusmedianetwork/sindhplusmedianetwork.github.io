World Bank approves $300 million financing for clean water in rural Sindh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Bank has approved $300 million in financing for Sindh province. The funding is for the STAR WASH One project, which will bring clean water, sanitation and hygiene facilities to 1.5 million people in rural Sindh. The Bank said unsafe water and poor sanitation are a major cause of stunting among children in Pakistan.

The project is aimed at tackling a root cause of stunting in rural Sindh by improving the water and sanitation services available to families. Its country director pledged continued cooperation with the province on rural water supply. The approval marks a major investment in the health and wellbeing of rural communities across Sindh.
