Pakistan and Kuwait Agree to Expand Economic Cooperation and Bilateral Partnership

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Kuwait have agreed to further expand economic cooperation and bilateral partnership. In a meeting between Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and his Kuwaiti counterpart, the two countries reaffirmed their resolve to further strengthen their longstanding relations. The longstanding relations between Pakistan and Kuwait are moving towards new heights through growing investment and economic cooperation.

The two leaders discussed matters of mutual interest, including trade, investment, energy projects and people-to-people contacts. Separately, Pakistan State Oil and Kuwait Petroleum Corporation agreed to further expand their 50-year partnership in the energy sector. Under the agreement, new opportunities will be explored in fuel supply, refining and investment to develop Pakistan's energy sector.

The strategic partnership for investment in Pakistan's energy, storage and infrastructure sectors is being described as an important development.
