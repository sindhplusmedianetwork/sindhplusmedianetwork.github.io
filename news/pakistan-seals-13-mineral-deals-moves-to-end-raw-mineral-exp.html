Pakistan Seals 13 Mineral Deals, Moves to End Raw Mineral Exports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan on Friday announced over a dozen mineral cooperation deals with foreign companies and nations on the concluding day of the Pakistan Mineral Investment Forum, declaring it would no longer encourage export of raw minerals. Pakistani companies finalised 13 joint ventures, memorandums of understanding and agreements during the forum. The deals include projects in Chagai, Balochistan with Turkish, UK and US firms.

Both the prime minister and the Chief of Defence Forces stressed building complete value chains from exploration to finished products. The forum marked a major step in Pakistan's push to develop its mineral sector with foreign partners. Officials said the focus would now shift from exporting raw material to processing minerals within the country.
