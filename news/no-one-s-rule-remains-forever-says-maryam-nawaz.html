No one's rule remains forever, says Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has said that no one's rule remains forever and that authority slips through one's fingers. She was speaking at a ceremony at the Paragliding Championship 2026 in Chakwal. Addressing female students at a ceremony for distributing scholarships and free laptops on Friday, the country's first female chief minister noted that nothing is more transient than a government.

Her remarks came as she addressed the gathering of students and participants at the event. The chief minister's comments reflected on the temporary nature of power and authority.
