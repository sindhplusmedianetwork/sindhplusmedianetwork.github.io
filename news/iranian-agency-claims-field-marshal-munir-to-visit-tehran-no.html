Iranian Agency Claims Field Marshal Munir to Visit Tehran; No Confirmation Yet

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A claim has surfaced that Chief of Army Staff and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir is travelling to Iran for meetings with senior Iranian leadership. Iran's Tasnim news agency, considered close to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, made the claim, which was shared by journalist Waseem Abbasi on social media citing sources. According to the report, the visit would come amid heightened regional tensions and Pakistan's continuing diplomatic efforts over the US-Iran standoff.

In August 2026, Munir had visited Tehran and met Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and other senior officials, according to the ISPR. Those talks covered reducing regional tensions, reopening the Strait of Hormuz and a negotiated settlement, the ISPR had said. However, neither the ISPR, the Foreign Office nor Iranian authorities have confirmed the new reported visit.

The report itself cautions that it is premature to link any possible visit to a new agreement or ceasefire.
