PM tears into PTI's 13-year 'misrule' in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has strongly criticised the PTI's 13-year rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, accusing it of developmental neglect. Addressing the PML-N's K-P chapter on Saturday, he said the party's only motto during its long rule was sit-ins and protests. The prime minister questioned transparency in the spending of Rs800 billion that the province accrued from its one percent NFC share.

He defended the imposition of governor's rule in K-P, calling it an unavoidable step to protect the state, the economy and provincial welfare. He said negotiations with the PTI had failed to produce a workable arrangement. The remarks are his sharpest attack yet on the PTI's provincial governance record.
