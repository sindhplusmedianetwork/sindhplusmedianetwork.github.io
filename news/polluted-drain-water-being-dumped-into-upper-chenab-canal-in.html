Polluted Drain Water Being Dumped Into Upper Chenab Canal in Gujranwala

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Polluted water from a drain near More Emnabad in Gujranwala is being channelled through a pipe into the Upper Chenab Canal, it has emerged. The drain carries sewage from a private housing society as well as untreated, chemical-laden water from various factories. According to reports, the drain's water previously flowed along a set route, but work on the metro project in Gujranwala disrupted its drainage.

It is alleged that, to avoid obstructing the project work, a route along the canal was dug up, a pipe was laid, and the drain's water was released into the Upper Chenab Canal. The practice threatens not only the canal's water but also human health, aquatic life, the environment and groundwater, with chemicals in industrial waste posing a serious risk to water quality. The irrigation department has so far taken no effective action in the matter, raising the question of who allowed polluted water into the canal and who is responsible.

The authorities concerned have been urged to stop the practice immediately, take water samples for laboratory testing and act against those responsible.
