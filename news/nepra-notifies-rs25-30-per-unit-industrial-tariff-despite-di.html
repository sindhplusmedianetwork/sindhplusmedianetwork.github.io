Nepra notifies Rs25.30 per unit industrial tariff despite dissent within regulator

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Electric Power Regulatory Authority has notified a revised special industrial tariff of Rs25.30 per unit, despite dissenting notes from two of its four members. The revised incremental consumption package replaces the Rs22.98 per unit rate introduced in December last year to encourage industrial and agricultural consumers to use more electricity at a marginal cost. In a statutory regulatory order published in the Gazette of Pakistan, Nepra said it had notified its decision of November 6, along with dissent notes from members Amina Ahmed and Maqsood Anwar Khan and a casting vote by chairman Waseem Mukhtar.

The two dissenting members called the revised rate a 'random number' unsupported by evidence, noting that Nepra's own calculations put the marginal cost at Rs31.83 or Rs29.65 per unit. Industrial consumers complained that the higher rate would push up monthly fuel cost adjustments and burden all categories of consumers, including those served by K-Electric and other distribution companies. The authority was divided two-two on the matter, and the chairman, who endorsed the dissenting members' arguments, used his special casting vote to approve the rate.

In its determination, all four members held the Power Division and its subordinate entities responsible for failing to meet their legal obligations within the prescribed timelines, which they said had pushed the regulator into an unprecedented deadlock.
