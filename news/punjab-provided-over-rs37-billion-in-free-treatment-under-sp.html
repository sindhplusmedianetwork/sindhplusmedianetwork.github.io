Punjab Provided Over Rs37 Billion in Free Treatment Under Special Health Programmes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has provided over Rs37 billion for free medical treatment of patients under its specialised health programmes at selected hospitals across the province. Provincial Health Minister Khawaja Salman Rafique, while chairing a meeting, said historic and foundational measures were being taken in the health sector, and credited the Chief Minister's special health programmes with a significant reduction in patient waiting lists for adult cardiac care, cancer, dialysis, stroke, paediatric heart surgery and transplants. Under the Chief Minister's Adult Cardiac Surgery Programme, free treatment worth Rs3 billion a year is being provided to heart patients, while the Special Initiative for Cancer Patients provides free diagnosis, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and surgical and medical therapies at an annual cost of Rs10 billion.

The Special Initiative for Stroke Management provides free treatment worth about Rs1.25 billion a year to stroke patients. The minister noted that under the Children's Heart Surgery Programme, more than 16,857 children across the province have received completely free heart surgeries. He also reviewed the administrative affairs and performance of the Nawaz Sharif Cardiology Centre in Murree and the Sahiwal Institute of Cardiology, where medical equipment and human resources were assessed; the Murree centre has for the first time procured modern machinery worth millions of rupees and now offers care through a fully functional cath lab.

Health Secretary Azmat Mahmood said equipment procurement, deployment of human resources and other foundational steps were being taken to ensure quality cardiac care in line with Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif's vision.
