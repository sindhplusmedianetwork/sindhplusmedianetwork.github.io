Nvidia approaches Arm chief Rene Haas as possible successor to Huang

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nvidia's board has approached Arm Holdings chief executive Rene Haas as a potential successor to Jensen Huang, according to reports citing people familiar with the matter. Huang stepped down on Thursday after revelations of extramarital affairs, including one involving a suspected Chinese spy, plunging the chipmaker into crisis and sending shockwaves across the artificial intelligence industry he helped bankroll. Haas' name has been making the rounds among analysts since Huang's exit, and he is among several candidates being considered, though there is no guarantee he will be offered the job or accept it.

Haas spent seven formative years at Nvidia, rising to vice president and general manager of its computing products business, before joining Arm in 2013, and he became Arm's chief executive in 2022, overseeing the company's Nasdaq listing in 2023. Nvidia's finance chief Colette Kress is serving as interim chief executive while the search continues. Arm declined to comment on the report, while Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment, and the report could not be independently verified.

Arm designs processor architectures and licenses them to other companies that develop chip designs, with its historic strength in mobile phones and a growing expansion into computers. Arm's US-listed shares pared losses after the report and were down more than 5 per cent, while Nvidia's shares were down over 3 per cent.
