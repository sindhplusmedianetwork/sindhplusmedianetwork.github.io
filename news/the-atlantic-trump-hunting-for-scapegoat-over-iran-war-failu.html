The Atlantic: Trump hunting for scapegoat over Iran war failure, new operation may follow midterms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Washington: US President Donald Trump is reportedly looking for a scapegoat to blame for the failure to end the Iran war, according to a report in the American magazine The Atlantic cited by UrduPoint on Monday. The report, quoting two sources close to Trump who are not part of the administration, claims a new US military operation against Iran could begin soon after Tuesday's midterm elections. It says Trump has started asking his close associates for their opinion on the performance of Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth and Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff General Dan Caine.

The report claims Trump is consulting allies about a large and decisive strike before possible congressional intervention after the elections. It notes that before the war began in February, advisers had assured Trump the conflict would end well before the midterms, an assessment that proved wrong. The report also claims Hegseth recently removed Pentagon war planner Lt General Brett Sylvia from his post.

The Pentagon rejected the allegation that Hegseth gives overly optimistic assessments, saying he keeps the president fully informed of the operational situation.
