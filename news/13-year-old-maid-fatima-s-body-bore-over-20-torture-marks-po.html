13-year-old maid Fatima's body bore over 20 torture marks, post-mortem finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The post-mortem report on 13-year-old domestic worker Fatima in Islamabad found at least 20 marks of torture on her body. The medico-legal officer concluded that she died from severe injuries caused by repeated physical abuse. Police have recovered CCTV footage and sticks from the house where she worked, which are now part of the investigation.

A court has approved the physical remand of two people in the case, including the wife of a police officer. The case has drawn attention to the treatment of child domestic workers and the violence they can face inside employers' homes. Investigators are examining the footage and the recovered sticks as they build the case against the accused.
