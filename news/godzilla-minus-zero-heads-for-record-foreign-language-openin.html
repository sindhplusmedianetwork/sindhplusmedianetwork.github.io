Godzilla Minus Zero Heads for Record Foreign-Language Opening in North America

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Godzilla Minus Zero has made a strong start in North America, taking $13.5 million on its opening Friday, including $6.7 million from Thursday preview screenings, across 3,254 theatres. The film is now tracking for a $30 million to $33 million opening weekend, which would make it the biggest domestic opening ever by a live-action foreign-language film, passing Zhang Yimou's Hero, which opened with $17.8 million in 2004. Opening-night audiences gave the film an A CinemaScore, the same grade its predecessor earned.

The sequel to the Oscar-winning Godzilla Minus One opened in Japan on November 3, Godzilla Day, earning 910 million yen from more than 590,000 admissions, the biggest opening day ever for a live-action film in Japan. Its three-day total in Japan reached about 1.38 billion yen. Directed by Takashi Yamazaki, the 135-minute film is the first Japanese production ever filmed for IMAX, and it arrives in India on November 20 through PVR INOX Pictures.

The figures are trade estimates and final totals will be confirmed after the weekend.
