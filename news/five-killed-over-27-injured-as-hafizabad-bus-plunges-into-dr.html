Five killed, over 27 injured as Hafizabad bus plunges into drain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five people were killed and more than 27 injured when a bus carrying passengers to a tablighi ijtema gathering fell into a drainage nullah in Hafizabad on Thursday. The bus fell into the sim nullah near the Fatki flyover, with reports saying speeding caused the vehicle to plunge off the flyover. Rescue officials said the injured were shifted to the Tehsil Headquarters Hospital in Pindi Bhattian for treatment.

The accident happened as the passengers were travelling to attend the religious gathering. Rescue teams responded at the scene and moved the injured to hospital, while the deaths were confirmed at the site. Further details about the condition of the injured were not immediately available.
