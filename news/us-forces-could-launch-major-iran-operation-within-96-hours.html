US Forces Could Launch Major Iran Operation Within 96 Hours of Trump Order, CNN Reports

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States forces in the Middle East could launch a new and possibly large-scale military operation against Iran within 96 hours of an order from President Donald Trump, CNN has reported, citing two American officials. The report says the operation would go beyond limited strikes on radar systems, missile launchers and rebuildable military targets, with US Central Command and the Pentagon preparing a broad target list that includes Iranian energy infrastructure, military installations and nuclear sites. Officials said President Trump has not yet made a final decision on whether to order action, and that action is seen as more likely after the American midterm elections than before the end of the year.

The report also notes that Iranian forces are now more familiar with American military tactics than before, which officials say would make it harder to destroy every intended target. It adds that Trump has in the past appeared to favour certain operations but pulled back at the last moment, so a major strike on Iran cannot yet be treated as a final decision.
