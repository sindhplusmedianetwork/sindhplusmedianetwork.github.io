Mohammad Rizwan Gets PCB Nod to Play Domestic Cricket, to Lead SNGPL From October 19

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Pakistan Test captain and wicketkeeper Mohammad Rizwan has been granted permission to play domestic cricket, Daily Pakistan reported, citing Geo News. The 34-year-old will lead Sui Northern Gas against Khan Research Laboratories in a four-day President's Trophy match starting October 19 at Rawalpindi's Shoaib Akhtar Cricket Stadium. SNGPL's director cricket and head coach Mohammad Hafeez confirmed the development, saying PCB director domestic Usama Shiron Niazi had conveyed the approval in a formal email.

Rizwan was among seven players recalled from the England tour in August 2026 after Pakistan's heavy defeat at Lord's. He was later summoned by the NCCIA, where an inquiry is underway against him. Reports suggest efforts are under way to improve relations between Rizwan and the board, with the wicketkeeper keen to focus on his cricket rather than further confrontation.
