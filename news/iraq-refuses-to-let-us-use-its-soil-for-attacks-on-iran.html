Iraq Refuses to Let US Use Its Soil for Attacks on Iran

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iraq has refused to allow the United States to use its territory for attacks on Iran. In a phone call with US President Trump, Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zidi said Iraqi soil should not be used as a base for attacks on neighbouring countries. Iraq's foreign minister said Iraq is not ready for war and an attack on Iran could be a disaster for Iraq.

Iran has meanwhile warned it could extend the range of its retaliation to Europe if hit again by fresh US and Israeli attacks. Iranian parliament member Hossein Ali Haji Deligani said that if new attacks take place, Iran could use missiles whose range reaches Europe.
