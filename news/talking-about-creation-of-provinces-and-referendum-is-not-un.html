Talking About Creation of Provinces and Referendum Is Not Unconstitutional: Ali Khurshidi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Assembly Opposition Leader and MQM-Pakistan leader Ali Khurshidi has said that talking about the creation of provinces and a referendum is not an unconstitutional act. Speaking at a press conference at the Sindh Assembly media corner in Karachi, he condemned Bilawal Bhutto Zardari's statement and behaviour and said the PPP interprets the constitution and democracy according to its own interests. He said giving threats of bloodshed over the demand for new administrative units was condemnable, noting that no such threats were made when new provinces were discussed in Punjab.

He pointed out that the Punjab Assembly passed a resolution for a new province in 2012 and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Assembly did so in 2014. Khurshidi said people in Hyderabad and Sukkur lack clean drinking water and that MQM-Pakistan had boycotted local government elections over such basic issues. He said his party will continue its political struggle with a policy of non-violence and will not let peace in the city be disturbed.
