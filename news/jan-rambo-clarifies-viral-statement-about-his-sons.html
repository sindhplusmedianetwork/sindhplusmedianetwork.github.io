Jan Rambo clarifies viral statement about his sons

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Afzal Khan, known as Jan Rambo, has clarified a viral old interview in which he was seen remembering his late father. In the clip, he advised young people to spend more time with their parents. Some social media pages presented the clip as claiming that his sons do not give him time.

Rambo said his sons Ahsan and Zain are obedient and excellent children and he is proud of them. He said he was saddened by the false reports about his family. Fans have said he had no need to explain, as people already know his children are well brought up.
