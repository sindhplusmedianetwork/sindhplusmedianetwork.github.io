'Water has passed over the head, now is the time for decision,' PM tells cabinet on KP terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Wednesday delivered a strongly worded address to the federal cabinet on the terrorism situation in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He said terrorists are targeting ordinary citizens and security forces in the province, yet the KP government does not utter a single word against the militants. 'Water has passed over the head; now is the time for decision,' the prime minister declared.

He revealed that Rs800 billion was given to the KP government over the last 10 years for the war against terrorism, but the province's Counter Terrorism Department was never strengthened. Shehbaz Sharif made it clear that the law will be enforced under all circumstances. He added that no one would be allowed to harm the country's progress and its recent diplomatic achievements.
