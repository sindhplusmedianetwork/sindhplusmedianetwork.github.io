Balochistan Sacks 72 More Doctors Over Absenteeism, Total Reaches 200

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Balochistan government has sacked 72 more doctors for chronic absenteeism following the introduction of an artificial intelligence-based attendance system in the health department. With this latest action, the total number of dismissed employees has reached 200. Officials also disclosed that Rs410 million has so far been deducted from the salaries of government employees for violating attendance and discipline rules, while 11,000 employees have been asked to explain their conduct and disciplinary proceedings are underway against 800 more.

In a statement posted on X, Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti said the provincial government had introduced the AI-based attendance system in the health department for the first time, and the results were now becoming visible. He made it clear that government employees were servants of the people and that no one would be allowed to draw a salary while sitting at home.
