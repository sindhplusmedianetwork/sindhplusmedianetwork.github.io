Amir Muqam says peace, welfare and development top PML-N agenda in KP

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister Amir Muqam told a PML-N rally in Swabi that peace, public welfare and development are the party's sole agenda in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He was addressing the rally in Swabi when he set out the party's priorities for the province. Muqam called for accountability over alleged financial irregularities during PTI's 13 years in power.

He said those in power during that period should answer for the alleged irregularities. He also praised police efforts to maintain peace in the province. Peace, welfare and development remain the party's agenda in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, he said.
