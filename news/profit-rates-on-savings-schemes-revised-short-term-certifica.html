Profit rates on savings schemes revised, short-term certificates raised

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Directorate of National Savings has revised the profit rates on some savings schemes, according to a notification. The profit rate on the first five profits of Special Savings Certificates has been set at 11.8 percent, while the sixth profit remains at 12.8 percent, and the rate on Defence Savings Certificates is unchanged. The profit on Regular Income Certificates is 12.12 percent, while Behbood, pensioners and martyrs' family accounts stay at 12.96 percent.

The profit on short-term savings certificates has been raised, with three-month certificates now paying 11.56 percent, six-month certificates 11.90 percent and one-year certificates 12.14 percent. The ordinary savings account rate has been set at 10.50 percent, the one-year Sarwa Islamic Term Account pays 12.04 percent and the three-year Sarwa Islamic Term Certificate pays 11.98 percent, while the five-year Sarwa Islamic Term Certificate stays at 12.12 percent and the expected rate on the Sarwa Islamic Savings Account is now 12.04 percent.
