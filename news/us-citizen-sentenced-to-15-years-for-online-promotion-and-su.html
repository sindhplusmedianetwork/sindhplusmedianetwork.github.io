US Citizen Sentenced to 15 Years for Online Promotion and Support of ISIS

By Sindh Plus Media Network

A 30-year-old American citizen, Anas Saad, has been sentenced to 15 years in prison for promoting the banned group ISIS online and supporting its activities. According to the US Department of Justice, Saad admitted to making videos and posters in support of ISIS and spreading the group's propaganda on social media. Officials said he was in contact with ISIS supporters on Telegram and consulted them, and he also sent his videos to a media outlet linked to ISIS.

US officials said Saad also admitted to hiding information about weapons from the FBI and lying to investigators. The court sentenced him to 15 years in prison for supporting ISIS and misleading the federal investigation agency. The court also ordered that his movements and activities remain under lifetime supervision after his release.
