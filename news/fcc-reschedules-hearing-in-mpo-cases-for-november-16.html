FCC reschedules hearing in MPO cases for November 16

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Constitutional Court has rescheduled the hearing of the Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa government’s petition for November 16. The petition challenges rulings of the Peshawar High Court in cases filed under the Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance. At the previous hearing, the court had suspended the high court’s orders directing the release of people detained under the ordinance.

The court had also restrained the Peshawar High Court from exercising its writ jurisdiction under Article 199 of the Constitution in such cases. The matter will now come up before the court on November 16.
