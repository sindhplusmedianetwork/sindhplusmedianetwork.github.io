Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon, says Trump; Vance vows no forever war

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump said on Tuesday during meetings in Manila with Japan, India and Australia that Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon. Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesperson denied an earlier report that it planned to detonate a dirty bomb in Washington, calling the allegations baseless. Separately, Iran's Revolutionary Guard said it carried out a drone attack targeting a US military radar system at Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain, in retaliation for what it described as repeated American violations of Iranian airspace.

US Central Command denied the claim, saying multiple Iranian one-way attack drones launched towards Bahrain were intercepted without any damage to US equipment or injury to American personnel. US Vice President JD Vance vowed in an interview that President Trump will not allow the ongoing conflict with Iran to turn into a prolonged military commitment. He said the administration would not allow the conflict to become a ten or fifteen year commitment, or even a two year commitment.

The United States and Iran signed a memorandum of understanding in June, but negotiations later stalled over disagreements on ensuring safe navigation through the Strait of Hormuz.
