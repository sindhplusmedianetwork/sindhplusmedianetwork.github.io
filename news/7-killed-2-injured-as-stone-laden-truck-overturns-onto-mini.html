7 killed, 2 injured as stone-laden truck overturns onto mini-truck in Bahawalpur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Seven people were killed in a truck accident in Bahawalpur's Uch Sharif early on Monday morning. Two others were injured in the accident, according to an official and Rescue 1122. A stone-laden truck hit a mini-truck and overturned onto it.

Bahawalpur District Police Officer (DPO) Rana Abdul Wahab said the mini-truck was travelling from Dera Ghazi Khan to Khairpur Tamewali with 16 people on board. The mini-truck had stopped along a highway due to fog while the driver cleaned the windscreen when the stone-laden truck hit it and overturned onto it.
