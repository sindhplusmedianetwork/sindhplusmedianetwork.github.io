Pakistan's foreign reserves slip to $26.84 billion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's total liquid foreign reserves fell by $25.8 million to $26.84 billion in the week ended November 6, 2026, the State Bank of Pakistan reported. The central bank's own reserves rose by $11 million to $21.57 billion during the week, while commercial banks' holdings dropped by $37 million to $5.27 billion. The previous week's total had stood at $26.86 billion, the central bank said.

Foreign reserves are closely watched because they show how much foreign currency the country holds to pay for imports and service its external debt. The central bank's reserves have been supported in recent months by inflows from the International Monetary Fund programme and other official sources. Analysts say the weekly figures move up and down with debt payments and inflows, and the overall level remains a key measure of the external position.
