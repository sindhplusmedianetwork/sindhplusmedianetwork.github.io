CID Creator B. P. Singh Dies at 77

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brijendra Pal Singh, the creator of India's long-running crime series CID and horror show Aahat, died on Tuesday at the age of 77 following a massive heart attack. Dayanand Shetty, who played Inspector Daya in CID, said Singh had not been well for some time and that on Tuesday morning he simply did not wake up. An alumnus of the Film and Television Institute of India, Singh began his career at Doordarshan before creating CID, which premiered in 1998 and became one of the longest-running shows in Indian television history.

The series featured Shivaji Satam as ACP Pradyuman, Aditya Srivastava as Inspector Abhijeet and Shetty as Inspector Daya, and its original run ended in 2018 before the show returned in 2024. In 2004, Singh filmed a CID episode in a single continuous shot lasting 111 minutes, a feat that earned a place in the Limca Book of Records. He also created Aahat, which first aired in 1995 and became one of Indian television's best-known horror shows, and he himself played DCP Shamsher Singh Chitrole in CID.

Filmmaker Ashoke Pandit paid tribute to Singh as a brilliant filmmaker and dear friend, and said the last rites were scheduled for October 28.
