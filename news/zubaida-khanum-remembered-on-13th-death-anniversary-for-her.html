Zubaida Khanum Remembered on 13th Death Anniversary for Her Musical Legacy

Playback singer Zubaida Khanum is being remembered on her 13th death anniversary for her contribution to Pakistani film music. She died in Lahore on October 19, 2013, at the age of 78. Born in Amritsar in 1935, she rose to become one of the prominent female voices of the 1950s film industry.

She recorded around 250 songs in Urdu and Punjabi during her career. She stepped away from films in the 1960s after marrying film cameraman Riaz Bokhari. Film personalities have paid tribute to her musical legacy, recalling her melodious voice that defined an era of Pakistani cinema.
