MQM-P Puts New Provinces and Local Poll Victory at Top of Agenda

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan has made the creation of new administrative units in Sindh and victory in the upcoming local government elections its top priorities, as the party seeks to regain its political foothold in urban centres. The decision was taken at a high-level meeting chaired by party chairman Dr Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui at the party's Bahadurabad headquarters in Karachi. Senior leaders Dr Farooq Sattar, Syed Aminul Haque and Waseem Akhtar attended, along with federal and provincial lawmakers and the central committee.

The leadership finalised a strategy to address what it called the deprivation of urban Sindh, speed up Karachi's reconstruction and prepare for upcoming political and local government contests. Siddiqui directed all lawmakers and organisational wings to mobilise at the grassroots level, going door to door to strengthen the party's presence ahead of the local government elections. The meeting also reviewed development projects being carried out across Karachi through special funds allocated to the party's lawmakers, and directed that public money be used transparently and effectively, warning that bureaucratic delays and administrative hurdles in public welfare projects would not be tolerated.

The meeting stressed that new administrative units in Sindh were essential to empower people at the grassroots level and ensure fair access to basic municipal services. Siddiqui said the restoration of Karachi and other major urban centres of Sindh, transparent use of public funds and protection of citizens' fundamental rights remained central to the party's agenda, and he described the meeting as a milestone in shaping its future political and local government strategy.
