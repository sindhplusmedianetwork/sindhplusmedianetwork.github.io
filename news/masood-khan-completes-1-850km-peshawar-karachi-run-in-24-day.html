Masood Khan completes 1,850km Peshawar-Karachi run in 24 days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Record-holding ultra-endurance athlete Masood Khan completed a 1,850km journey from Peshawar to Karachi on foot in just 24 days. He received a warm welcome at the Peshawar Sports Complex on Saturday, where DG Sports Wasal Khan announced a Rs 500,000 cash award for him. Masood ran 70 to 85 kilometres daily after 90 days of intensive training, and dedicated the feat to his late father.

He has won 49 medals in full and half-marathons across Pakistan, including 24 golds.
