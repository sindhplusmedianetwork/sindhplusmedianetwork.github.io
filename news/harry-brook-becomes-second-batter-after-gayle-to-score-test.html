Harry Brook Becomes Second Batter After Gayle to Score Test Triple, ODI Double, T20I Hundred

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

England captain Harry Brook became only the second batter in cricket history, after West Indies' Chris Gayle, to score a Test triple century, an ODI double century and a T20I century. He reached the milestone with the fastest ODI double century by an English batter, against Sri Lanka in the third match of the tri-series in Rawalpindi. Brook struck 202 off 116 balls, including 21 fours and 10 sixes.

Notably, his Test triple century was also scored at Pindi Stadium. Despite his record knock, Sri Lanka chased down the second-highest target in ODI history to win the match. The feat cements Brook's place among the most explosive batters of the modern era.
