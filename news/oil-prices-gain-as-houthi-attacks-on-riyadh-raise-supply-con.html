Oil prices gain as Houthi attacks on Riyadh raise supply concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Oil prices rose on Friday but were set for a weekly decline as concerns over supply disruptions grew following Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia's capital Riyadh and reports of tanker disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz. Brent crude futures rose 79 cents, or 0.9 percent, to 92.77 dollars a barrel by 0404 GMT. US West Texas Intermediate futures for December climbed 25 cents, or 0.3 percent, to 83.46 dollars a barrel, while the more-active January contract gained 71 cents, or 0.9 percent, to 83.15 dollars.

Both benchmarks settled little changed on Thursday, but Brent was set for a 3.5 percent decline for the week. The December WTI contract was set for an almost 6 percent weekly drop as expectations of a supply surplus grew because crude shipments through the Strait of Hormuz have returned to roughly pre-war levels. Brent and WTI were set to fall for the third week in a row.
