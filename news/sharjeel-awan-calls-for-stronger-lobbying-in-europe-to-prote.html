Sharjeel Awan Calls for Stronger Lobbying in Europe to Protect Pakistan's GSP+ Status

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Businessman and youth leader Sharjeel Awan has said the need to defend Pakistan's national interests and maintain the country's positive image at the global level has grown greater than ever before. Speaking in Brussels, where he visited the European Parliament and held meetings with several of its members, Awan said the efforts of Field Marshal Asim Munir and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi had raised Pakistan's profile around the world. He said practical and effective lobbying was now indispensable to protect Pakistan's GSP+ status in the European Union and to strengthen the country's position.

Awan said the government and the prime minister's team were active, but the results Pakistan needed in Europe and Britain were not being achieved, while elements opposed to Pakistan remained active on various platforms. He called for more organised diplomatic coordination, parliamentary lobbying and positive publicity in Pakistan's favour, and said hostile propaganda should be answered with argument, facts and effective diplomacy rather than emotion. Awan also said young people drifting away from the national mainstream over political differences or misinformation should be brought into positive activities through counselling and confidence-building, because national unity could not be achieved without engaging the youth.

He described GSP+ as extremely important for Pakistan's economy, exports and employment, and pledged to continue his activities in Europe for Pakistan's positive image, the protection of GSP+ status, the Kashmir cause and national interests.
