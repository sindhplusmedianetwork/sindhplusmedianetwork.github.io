Robbers open fire on resistance during Karachi robbery, 3 injured including 2 brothers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In Karachi's Gulshan-e-Maymar area of Gabol Goth, armed robbers opened fire during a robbery at a general store when the people present resisted, injuring three people including two real brothers, Express News reported. According to police, the armed robbers attempted to loot the general store in Gabol Goth, Gulshan-e-Maymar, and when the people at the shop resisted, the suspects opened fire, leaving three people injured. The injured were immediately shifted to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital.

Rescue sources identified the injured as Inayat, Jafar and Naveed. Police said the injured Inayat and Jafar are real brothers. The robbers fled from the scene after the firing.

Police have started further investigation into the incident.
