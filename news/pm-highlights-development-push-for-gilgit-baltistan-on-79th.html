PM Highlights Development Push for Gilgit-Baltistan on 79th National Day

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has said the federal government remains steadfastly committed to the development and prosperity of Gilgit-Baltistan and to ensuring a bright future for its youth. In a message marking the region's 79th National Day, he extended congratulations to the people of Gilgit-Baltistan on the anniversary of its liberation and accession to Pakistan on November 1, 1947. He listed federal initiatives across energy, education, healthcare, employment and public safety, noting that the 16-megawatt Naltar Hydropower Project has been completed and a 100-megawatt solar project launched.

Around 100,000 solar panels, with a total capacity of 58 megawatts, have been distributed free among the public through a transparent digital lottery, he said. Foundation stones have been laid for four Danish Schools in Sultanabad, Bunji, Ghanche and Skardu at a cost of Rs12.46 billion, and 4,245 laptops distributed under the Prime Minister's Laptop Scheme. To boost investment, Makpon Das has been approved as a Special Economic Zone spanning 250 acres, expected to host 155 enterprises and create about 5,000 jobs.

The second phase of the Safe City project in Gilgit-Baltistan has been inaugurated, with another project being launched in Skardu.
