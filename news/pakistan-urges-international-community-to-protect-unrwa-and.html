Pakistan urges international community to protect UNRWA and sustain its operations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has urged the international community to protect the UN agency for Palestine refugees, UNRWA, and to sustain its operations despite a severe financial crisis. Addressing the UN General Assembly's Fourth Committee, Pakistan's delegate Ansar Shah praised the agency for continuing to deliver life-saving aid to Palestinians in Gaza, the West Bank, Lebanon, Syria and Jordan under extremely dangerous conditions. He said nearly 400 UNRWA workers have been killed in Gaza since October 2023 while serving Palestinians under bombardment.

Shah rejected Israeli legislation seeking to ban UNRWA's operations, revoke its immunities and designate it a terrorist organisation, calling the move a travesty of international law and a violation of the UN Charter. He said the agency faces a 2026 budget deficit of 250 million dollars, including a cash-flow deficit of about 70 million dollars, and called for sustained, predictable financial and political support. UNRWA chief Christian Saunders told the committee that more than 117,000 children have been taking part in learning activities in 85 temporary learning spaces since September 2026, while some 280,000 children are registered on the agency's digital learning platform.
