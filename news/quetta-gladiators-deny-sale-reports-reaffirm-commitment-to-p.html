Quetta Gladiators deny sale reports, reaffirm commitment to PSL

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Reports before the 12th edition of the Pakistan Super League suggested Quetta Gladiators could be sold and might part ways with the league, stirring debate in cricket circles. The franchise management has now rejected all such speculation and announced that it remains committed to the PSL. In a clarification issued on Friday, the management said reports about a possible sale, financial difficulties and the owners' health do not reflect the facts, and that the team's financial position is stable.

The statement added that the franchise has been among the league's successful teams since the PSL began and has made a profit every year since the fourth season. Owner Nadeem Omar said his association with Quetta Gladiators is the result of years of hard work and affection, and his resolve to continue the journey is stronger than before. The owners did not completely rule out considering an offer in the future if one arrived that could not be refused.

The franchise also described the direct signing of New Zealand fast bowler Lockie Ferguson as a positive development for the team's future and for the PSL's growing importance. Quetta Gladiators are among the PSL's founding franchises, won their first title in 2019, reached the final in 2025, and renewed their agreement with the PCB for a further ten years last year.
