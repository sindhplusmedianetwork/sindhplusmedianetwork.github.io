LHC Orders Clubbing All Petitions Against Expulsion of Afghan Students From Medical Colleges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has ordered that all petitions filed against the expulsion of Afghan students from medical colleges be clubbed together. Justice Asim Hafeez heard the case of Afghan male and female students of medical colleges on Friday, where the government lawyer requested the court that other petitions of Afghan students were also pending and the court should hear all cases together. The court directed the Registrar Office to fix all petitions of this nature for hearing together and adjourned further hearing of the case until November 26.

The court has extended the stay order barring all medical colleges of Punjab from expelling Afghan students, while the PMDC lawyer has also been given time to submit a reply. More than 30 Afghan male and female students have filed petitions in the court, contending they had taken admissions in medical colleges on the Afghan refugee quota and merit. According to the petitioners, the PMDC had issued a final notification on September 1 to expel them from the colleges.
