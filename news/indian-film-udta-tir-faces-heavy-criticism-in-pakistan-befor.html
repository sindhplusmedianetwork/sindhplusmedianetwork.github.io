Indian Film 'Udta Tir' Faces Heavy Criticism in Pakistan Before Release

By Sindh Plus Media Network

The Bollywood film 'Udta Tir' is facing a wave of criticism from Pakistani social media users even before its release on October 23. The film, starring Sara Ali Khan and Ayushmann Khurrana and produced by Karan Johar and others according to Indian media, is being criticised over its alleged anti-Pakistan theme. Pakistani users flooded social platforms with satirical remarks about the film.

One user joked that since Bollywood earns from their stories, Pakistan should receive royalties. Another asked why Bollywood remains so obsessed with Pakistan. The online backlash shows the film's subject matter has already struck a nerve across the border.
