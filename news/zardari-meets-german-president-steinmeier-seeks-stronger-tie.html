Zardari Meets German President Steinmeier, Seeks Stronger Ties on 75th Anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari on Friday met German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier in Berlin and called for a holistic expansion of bilateral cooperation to build a robust and constructive partnership. Zardari, who is visiting Germany from October 22 to 24, thanked Steinmeier for the invitation and warm hospitality, noting the visit comes as the two countries mark 75 years of diplomatic relations. He underscored the need to expand political, economic and defence cooperation with greater emphasis on trade, emerging technologies, climate resilience, skills development and cultural exchanges.

Zardari said Pakistan valued Germany as a major European partner and appreciated its support for women's empowerment, social protection and human development. He also thanked Germany for acknowledging Pakistan's mediation role in the US-Iran conflict, saying conflicts should be resolved through dialogue and constructive negotiations. Steinmeier welcomed the visit, conveyed condolences over the deaths of children in a recent school roof collapse in Peshawar, and expressed hope that both countries would intensify business and trade engagement.

He praised Pakistan's growing diplomatic profile in resolving regional and global disputes and appreciated Pakistan for facilitating the verification of 500 Afghans set to be repatriated to Germany. The two leaders also discussed Afghanistan, with Zardari saying Pakistan had no conflict with the Afghan people but would not allow terrorists to use Afghan soil against its citizens. Earlier, Zardari was accorded a guard of honour at the German Ministry of Defence.
