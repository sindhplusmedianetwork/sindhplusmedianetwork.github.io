Imran Khan's Blood Pressure Hit 192, 'Extremely Alarming': Salman Akram Raja

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja has raised the alarm over the health of party founder Imran Khan. Raja said Imran Khan told party leaders that his blood pressure had risen to 192, which he described as extremely alarming. He said the party would take the matter of the health of Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi very seriously.

According to Raja, the party will consult the family and the parliamentary party before deciding its course of action. He announced that the party would raise a strong voice for their health protection both inside the country and abroad. The statement comes amid continuing concern among PTI supporters about the well-being of their jailed leaders.
