Gohar, Safdar hold six-hour meeting with Naqvi on Bushra Bibi's release

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Barrister Gohar and Barrister Salman Safdar held a nearly six-hour meeting with Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi in Islamabad, where matters related to Bushra Bibi's release were discussed. The meeting came after Imran Khan conveyed a message through Safdar that talks should be held on Bushra Bibi's release. Bushra Bibi herself supported negotiations and gave Gohar the mandate to hold talks.

Gohar then contacted Naqvi, which led to the lengthy meeting between the two sides. The discussions covered the matters linked to Bushra Bibi's release. It remains to be seen whether the talks will produce any practical progress.
