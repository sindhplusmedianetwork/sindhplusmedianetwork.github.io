Kam Air to run daily Kabul–Islamabad flights from November 1

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Afghan airline Kam Air has announced daily flights between Kabul and Islamabad from 1 November 2026, increasing the service from four flights a week. The airline said the move responds to growing demand on the route. It said the daily service will help business travellers and families travelling between the two capitals.

The airline also pointed to onward connections to Gulf destinations through Islamabad. The expansion follows an interline agreement signed with Pakistan International Airlines in September. Under that arrangement, passengers can connect more easily between the two carriers' networks.

Kam Air said the added frequency reflects the steady rise in travel on the Kabul–Islamabad route.
