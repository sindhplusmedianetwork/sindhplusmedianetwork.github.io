US VP JD Vance goes viral as Dumbledore in Halloween costume

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States Vice President JD Vance has gone viral after appearing in a Harry Potter-themed Halloween costume. Vance dressed up as Albus Dumbledore from the Harry Potter films, complete with a long wig, a pointed hat and a shimmering cloak. His infant son was dressed as the house-elf Dobby.

In a video shared on X, Vance introduced himself as 'Vice President Dumbledore' and his son as 'little Dobby'. He also shared a family photograph showing the children dressed in Harry Potter and spider costumes. The post drew a large reaction online, with the costume becoming one of the most talked-about political Halloween looks of the season.
