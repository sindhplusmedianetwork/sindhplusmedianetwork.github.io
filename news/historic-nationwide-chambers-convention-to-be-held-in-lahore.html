Historic nationwide chambers convention to be held in Lahore on December 13; finance minister to attend

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A historic and important convention of chambers of commerce from across the country will be held in Lahore on December 13, organised by the Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry at a private hotel in the city. The convention, on the theme of the role of chambers in policymaking, will be chaired by Lahore Chamber President Fahim-ur-Rehman Sehgal, and Federal Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, who was invited to the nationwide convention, has accepted the invitation. The gathering will discuss the private sector's participation in policymaking, the method of implementing policies, the need for a clear mechanism for consultation, and the hurdles faced by industrialists, traders and exporters, including the taxation system and high production costs, and recommendations will be compiled on these matters.

Sehgal said the private sector's participation in policymaking is indispensable because it is the business community that knows the ground realities and is fighting for the survival of businesses, and their views and proposals will form the basis of sustainable and successful economic policies. He added that the private sector wants to remain fully active and play its role in the country's progress and prosperity, but for that its problems must be resolved. A large number of chamber presidents, representatives, economists and people from different walks of life are expected to attend, and it is hoped that comprehensive recommendations will be compiled after the convention and sent to government institutions.

The convention has gained further importance at a time when the country is facing economic pressure, and organisers say that if it builds trust between the government and the private sector and brings the private sector into policymaking, it could prove a decisive turning point for Pakistan's economy.
