HRCP warns Punjab theatre bill could put government beyond criticism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Human Rights Commission of Pakistan has raised alarm over the recently passed Punjab Dramatic and Theatrical Performances Bill 2026. In a statement issued on the social media platform X on Thursday, the commission said it was deeply concerned that the bill effectively places the government beyond satire and criticism. It warned that prohibiting performances deemed likely to create disaffection towards the government is a gross violation of artistic freedom and the right to freedom of expression.

The commission also raised concerns over the bill's mandatory licensing requirements, government-controlled script reviews and censorship mechanisms, arguing these provisions institutionalised state control over artistic and cultural activities through threats of fines and imprisonment. The HRCP announced it would convene a consultation with artistes and other relevant stakeholders to examine the legislation's implications and discuss possible measures to safeguard artistic and cultural freedoms.
