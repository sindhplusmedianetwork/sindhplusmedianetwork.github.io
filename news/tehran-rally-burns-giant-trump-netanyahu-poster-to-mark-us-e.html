Tehran rally burns giant Trump-Netanyahu poster to mark US embassy takeover anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Large crowds gathered at Tehran's Revolution Square to mark the 47th anniversary of the takeover of the US embassy, burning a giant poster showing Donald Trump, JD Vance, Steve Witkoff and Benjamin Netanyahu, along with effigies of the two leaders. According to AFP, Iranians were celebrating the defeat of Trump's party in the US midterm elections and Netanyahu's failure to win a simple majority in Israel, vowing revenge for the killing of Ali Khamenei. Netanyahu is facing difficulties forming a government in Israel, while Trump faces a tough period after the midterm results, which he himself has blamed on the Iran war.

The rally marked the anniversary of the embassy takeover with crowds burning the poster and effigies in the square.
