Five Killed in Fresh Ethnic Violence in India's Manipur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five people were killed in two separate incidents in India's northeastern state of Manipur, police said on Sunday, as fresh ethnic violence erupted. Three men from the Kuki tribal group were killed in an exchange of gunfire in Noney district on Saturday. The bodies of two other Kuki men who went missing earlier this week were recovered in Kangpokpi district.

A top Kuki tribal body blamed Naga separatist outfits for the killings. Mass protests were reported in Imphal demanding abrogation of a ceasefire agreement. Security has been tightened in the affected areas.
