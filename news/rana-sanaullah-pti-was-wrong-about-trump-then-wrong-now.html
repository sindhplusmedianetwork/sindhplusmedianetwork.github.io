Rana Sanaullah: PTI Was Wrong About Trump Then, Wrong Now

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Adviser to the Prime Minister on Political Affairs Senator Rana Sanaullah has criticised the PTI over its shifting narrative on US President Donald Trump. Speaking on November 4, 2026, Sanaullah said PTI members had claimed that Trump's arrival would change everything. He said the same people were now arguing that Trump's defeat would harm the government.

Sanaullah said PTI was wrong then and remained wrong today. He added that Trump's defeat was neither good nor bad news for Pakistan. He said such matters had very little bearing on diplomatic relations between countries.

The remarks come as the midterm fallout continues to shape Pakistani political debate.
