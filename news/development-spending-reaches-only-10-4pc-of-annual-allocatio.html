Development Spending Reaches Only 10.4pc of Annual Allocation in Four Months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Public Sector Development Programme spending in July-October 2026-27 reached only Rs103.87bn, just 10.4pc of the Rs1 trillion annual allocation, according to the Ministry of Planning and Development. The slow spending came despite a 0.8pc budget surplus in the first quarter. Ministries and divisions spent Rs74.65bn, about 11pc of their Rs682bn allocation.

Corporations, chiefly the power sector and the National Highways Authority, spent Rs29bn, or 9.3pc of their share. Six federal ministries, including commerce, petroleum and privatisation, spent nothing on development in the four months. The figures point to a sharp lag in development spending in the opening months of the fiscal year.
