Priyanka Chopra channels Kylie Jenner for Halloween, celebrates 'Diwaloween' with Nick Jonas and daughter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

On Sunday, November 1, Priyanka Chopra shared a set of photos on her official Instagram account showing her little family celebrating Halloween 2026. The actress chose to dress as Kylie Jenner, reviving the businesswoman's famous King Kylie era with pink locks, a black tank top, matching pants and a hat, while Nick Jonas dressed as a cowboy and their daughter Malti Marie appeared as an adorable little ghost. In her caption, she wrote: 'Quite an unlikely alliance.

A Cowboy, a Ghost princess and King Kylie went trick-or-treating. Happy Halloween to everyone celebrating #diwaloween season is here.' Chopra blended her desi festival, Diwali, with the Western Halloween celebration. Lisa Rinna also channelled Kylie Jenner for Halloween 2026, recreating her orange Chrome Hearts dress look from the Marty Supreme premiere, to which Kylie reacted in the comments: 'Thought this was me.' Chopra also hosted a Diwali bash at her Los Angeles residence, where she wore a deep burgundy off-the-shoulder embellished gown with zardozi and gold-thread embroidery while Jonas wore a cream-and-white kurta; guests included Lilly Singh, Winnie Harlow, Sanya Malhotra, Rhea Raj and Radhi Devlukia-Shetty.

On the work front, Chopra is filming her return to Indian cinema in S. S. Rajamouli's 'Varanasi', co-starring Mahesh Babu and Prithviraj Sukumaran, scheduled for release on April 7, 2027.
