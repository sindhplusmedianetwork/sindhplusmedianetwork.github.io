Pakistan Army holds free veterinary camp in Bagh; over 1,000 animals examined

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Army held a free veterinary camp in Bagh district, where more than 1,000 animals were examined. The camp was set up to provide veterinary care to animals belonging to local residents free of charge. Veterinary staff examined the animals brought to the camp and checked their health.

More than 1,000 animals were examined during the camp. No further details about the camp were published on the video page.
