Mega dams' price tag soars to Rs3.5tr as ECC okays Rs275b for water projects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Wednesday approved Rs275 billion in supplementary grants to fund six priority water projects, including three mega dams whose cumulative cost has escalated 176% and which are now being funded through cash grants taken from the provinces. The Economic Coordination Committee of the Cabinet (ECC), which took the decisions, also approved Rs84.2 billion in three supplementary grants to finance armed forces projects, including the Istaqlal project and the induction of an aircraft, the finance ministry announced. The cumulative cost of the three mega dams has escalated from Rs1.28 trillion to Rs3.5 trillion, a surge of Rs2.3 trillion, according to details presented by the Ministry of Water Resources.

The cost of the Diamer-Bhasha Dam increased by Rs642 billion to Rs1.12 trillion, an increase of 134% attributed to changes in foreign exchange rates, while the Dasu Hydropower Project's estimated cost surged from Rs486 billion to Rs1.74 trillion, a rise of around 260%. The Water and Power Development Authority has also reassessed the cost of the Mohmand dam at Rs666 billion, against the Rs310 billion approved in 2018 with a deadline of September 2025. Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb chaired the meeting, where the committee also approved Rs31.32 billion for the Special Security Division North and South, Rs29.1 billion for the Istaqlal project and Rs1.1 billion for the induction of a Lineage L-1000 aircraft.

The committee further approved restrictions on the import of camera-enabled smart glasses and a Rs1 billion grant for the gasification of villages within five kilometres of gas production fields.
