Punjab Assembly speaker takes on Fazl, asks him hard questions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Assembly Speaker Malik Muhammad Ahmad Khan says JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman had supported jihad during the Afghan war though the parliament had not approved it. Addressing the opening ceremony of phase two of the Jashn-i-Khudian Sports Gala at Khudian Khas Sports Stadium in Kasur on Tuesday, he said the nation later faced terrorism and other horrible consequences of the war, and that past political decisions and Pakistan's involvement in the Afghan war must be debated. He said terrorism was the consequence of misguided decisions spanning over several decades, and that decisions had been made to involve Pakistan in wars without obtaining parliamentary approval, with their consequences continuing to haunt successive generations in the form of fear, terrorism and insecurity.

He asked which parliament had authorised Pakistan's involvement in the Afghan war following the Soviet invasion, and in which parliamentary forum decisions regarding the Mujahideen and the then US administration had been taken. He suggested that Pakistan's involvement in the Afghan war, subsequent policies and political decisions taken during different periods required accountability and explanation through reasoned debate. He also asked Fazlur Rehman to explain, through reasoned argument, past political decisions concerning the Legal Framework Order, the election of a uniformed president, the Hasba Bill, the formation of the Muttahida Majlis-i-Amal and the 2002 general elections.

He stressed that difference of opinion must be countered with arguments rather than abuse, and that respecting the dignity and honour of Pakistan's armed forces is a national responsibility.
