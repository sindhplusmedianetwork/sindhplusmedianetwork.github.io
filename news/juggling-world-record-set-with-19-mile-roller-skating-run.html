Juggling world record set with 19-mile roller-skating run

By Sindh Plus Media Network

A man from the US state of Idaho has set a world record by juggling while covering 19 miles on inline skates. David Rush already holds several Guinness World Records for juggling, and his earlier records include juggling while balancing on a unicycle, a roller board and a Swiss ball. He said setting the record for the longest distance covered on roller or inline skates while juggling three objects in the air was his toughest challenge so far.

Rush said that when juggling and skating at the same time, even a pebble as small as a pea can jam a skate wheel, affecting speed and balance, and months of preparation can be wasted in just half a second. He said he began preparing for this record three years ago, when the world record stood at 5,000 metres, which he broke by covering a distance of one mile. However, his record was broken again soon after, and he then decided he would have to cover a far longer distance.

Rush said endurance records are not set only on the day of the attempt, but need months of physical preparation, and that the route also had to be cleaned with machinery before the record attempt.
