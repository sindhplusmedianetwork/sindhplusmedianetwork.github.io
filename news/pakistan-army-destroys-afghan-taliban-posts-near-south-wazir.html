Pakistan Army destroys Afghan Taliban posts near South Waziristan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Army has targeted and destroyed several Afghan Taliban posts and military positions in areas adjacent to South Waziristan and Parachinar, Radio Pakistan reported on Friday, citing security sources. The action was in retaliation for unprovoked Taliban firing along the border in Angoor Adda on Thursday. The army used heavy weaponry in the operation, forcing Taliban personnel to abandon their posts, with several Taliban personnel reportedly killed.

The escalation followed an October 27 attack by Afghan Taliban forces on Pakistani border posts and civilian areas. Tensions along the Pakistan-Afghanistan border have remained high amid the ongoing standoff between the two sides.
