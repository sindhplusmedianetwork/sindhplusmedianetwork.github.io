Petrol Price Cut by Rs2.71 to Rs392.66 a Litre From October 14

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has cut the price of petrol by Rs2.71 per litre, bringing it down to Rs392.66 for the fortnight starting October 14. High-speed diesel will cost three paisas less at Rs396.82 per litre, according to a notification issued by the Petroleum Division. The revision follows a mixed adjustment a day earlier, when petrol was reduced by Rs2.93 per litre while diesel became 61 paisas dearer.

Fuel prices in the country have been revised daily since July 17 under the government's new pricing mechanism. Officials said the latest cut would pass on relief to consumers amid fluctuating international oil prices. The move comes weeks after the government announced a targeted fuel relief scheme on September 13, offering Rs100 per litre off for motorcycles, rickshaws and cars up to 800cc.

That scheme is expected to benefit around 11.8 million consumers across the country.
