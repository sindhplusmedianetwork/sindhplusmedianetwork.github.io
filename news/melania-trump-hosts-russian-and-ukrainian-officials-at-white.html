Melania Trump hosts Russian and Ukrainian officials at White House as ceasefire talks progress

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US First Lady Melania Trump hosted Russian and Ukrainian officials at the White House, where a six-day ceasefire in the Russian-held Ukrainian city of Oleshky was reviewed. Russian President Vladimir Putin's special envoy Kirill Dmitriev attended the meeting and praised Melania Trump's humanitarian efforts. Dmitriev described her role as commendable, saying that President Putin had himself issued instructions in support of the effort.

Ukrainian officials David Arakhamia and human rights ombudsman Dmytro Lubinets also took part in the meeting, while President Donald Trump was not present. Under the agreement reached on October 23, aid supplies were delivered to Oleshky and civilians were allowed to leave. The temporary ceasefire ran from October 27 to November 1 and has been described as the most important pause in fighting since Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

About 2,000 people were in the village when the agreement began, and more than 750 were moved to safe places as a result of the effort. Addressing the meeting, Melania Trump expressed hope that the temporary ceasefire could pave the way for lasting peace, saying the important thing was not that the beginning was limited but the resolve to turn it into an extraordinary success.
