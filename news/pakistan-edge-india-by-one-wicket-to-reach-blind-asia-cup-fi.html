Pakistan edge India by one wicket to reach Blind Asia Cup final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan reached the final of the Blind Asia Cup with a dramatic one-wicket win over India in Dhaka. India batted first and scored 179 for six in their 20 overs, with captain Durga Rao top-scoring with 62. In reply, Pakistan chased the target of 180 and reached it on the final ball with nine wickets down.

The match turned in the last over, when Naimatullah hit a six off the fifth ball to level the scores. He then struck a four off the last ball to complete the victory and spark celebrations among the Pakistan camp. The final of the tournament will be played on 20 November.
