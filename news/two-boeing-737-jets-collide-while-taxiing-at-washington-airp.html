Two Boeing 737 Jets Collide While Taxiing at Washington Airport

Two American Airlines Boeing 737 aircraft collided while taxiing at Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport in the US capital, Washington, though no one was injured in the incident. According to US officials, both aircraft were in a queue on the runway to take off when one of them moved forward beyond the hold line and its wing struck the other aircraft standing beside it. Officials said the incident took place at Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport.

After the incident, the US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) began an investigation to determine the causes of the collision. The report noted that a major accident was averted, as the collision happened at low speed while the aircraft were taxiing rather than during take-off or landing.
