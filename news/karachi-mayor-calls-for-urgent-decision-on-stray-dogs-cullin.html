Karachi Mayor Calls for Urgent Decision on Stray Dogs' Culling and Sterilisation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Mayor Barrister Murtaza Wahab has called for an urgent decision on how the city should deal with its growing stray dog population. He said authorities need to decide immediately between culling and sterilisation of the animals. The mayor clarified that he personally has no objection to sparing the dogs' lives, but pointed out that the sheer size of the stray population means relying on sterilisation alone would take far too long to bring the problem under control.

His remarks come amid recurring public complaints about dog-bite incidents and the rising number of strays across Karachi's neighbourhoods.
