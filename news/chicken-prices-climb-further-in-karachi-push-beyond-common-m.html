Chicken Prices Climb Further in Karachi, Push Beyond Common Man's Reach

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chicken has become costlier once again in Karachi, moving further beyond the reach of ordinary citizens. During October, the price of live chicken rose by Rs110 per kg, climbing from Rs358 to Rs468 per kg. Chicken meat saw an even sharper increase of Rs170 per kg, going from Rs570 to Rs740 per kg.

The official price list issued by the commissioner sets live chicken at Rs456 per kg and chicken meat at Rs685 per kg. Market prices, however, continue to run above the official rates, adding to the burden on household budgets.
