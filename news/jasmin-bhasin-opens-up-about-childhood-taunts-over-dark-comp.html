Jasmin Bhasin Opens Up About Childhood Taunts Over Dark Complexion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian actress Jasmin Bhasin has spoken candidly about the taunts she faced as a child over her appearance. Appearing on Jai Madan's podcast, she said she was mocked for her dark complexion, acne and thin frame, which gave her an inferiority complex. She recalled that her father once took her to the Bijasan Mata temple in Rajasthan, where she climbed many stairs and prayed to be made beautiful enough that people would one day want to meet her.

Bhasin said the experience stayed with her as she grew up and worked her way into the entertainment industry. She also said that she believes in and respects all religions.
