Seven Bangladeshis, Including Women and Children, Killed in Saudi Road Accident

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least seven Bangladeshi nationals, including women and children, were killed in a traffic accident in Saudi Arabia on Saturday. According to officials of the Bangladeshi consulate, the accident took place on a highway while travelling from Medina to Jeddah, where the driver lost control of the vehicle. All seven people in the vehicle died on the spot.

The victims were employed at a company in Saudi Arabia and belonged to Chakaria tehsil of Cox's Bazar district in Bangladesh. The company's marketing manager, Nooruzzaman, said the dead included the company's storekeeper, Ziaur Rahman, his two sons and a daughter. The consulate said it was in contact with the families and the company to complete the formalities for repatriating the bodies.
