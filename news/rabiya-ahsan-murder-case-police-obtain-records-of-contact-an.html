Rabiya Ahsan murder case: police obtain records of contact and chats with a federal minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police investigating the murder of anchorperson Rabiya Ahsan have obtained information about her contact and chats with a federal minister. The investigation into the killing is continuing, and police have taken the suspect, her husband Ahsan, to Lahore for a polygraph test, while a forensic examination of the victim's three mobile phones has also been decided. According to sources, a Rawalpindi police team left for Lahore with Ahsan for the polygraph test, carrying samples collected from the crime scene and the post-mortem.

The samples will be submitted to the Punjab Forensic Science Laboratory, and a DNA test of Ahsan will also be carried out in Lahore. Police sources said a forensic examination of Rabiya's three mobile phones has been decided; the phones had no password or code, and their initial analysis has been completed. The chats between Rabiya and her husband have also been obtained, and the initial analysis of her phones produced no evidence of any dispute.

Jail sources said Rabiya, as a journalist, remained in contact with important personalities, and sources said her contact and chat with a federal minister have also been obtained. A detailed report after the forensic examination of all three of her mobile phones will be made part of the challan.
