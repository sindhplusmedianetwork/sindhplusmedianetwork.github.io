Karachi Board Announces First-Year Intermediate Results Under New Grading System

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Board of Intermediate Education Karachi has announced the results of the 2026 annual examinations for first-year intermediate students. The results cover the pre-medical, general science, commerce, arts, special candidates and home economics groups. Under the new grading system introduced for these examinations, candidates must secure at least 40 percent marks in each paper to pass.

A candidate scoring less than 40 percent in any paper will be classified as ungraded and considered unsuccessful in that subject. In the pre-medical group, 25,157 candidates registered and 24,544 appeared, with 11,966 passing all six papers. In the general science group, 15,036 of the 30,386 candidates who appeared passed all six papers.

The board said electronic assessment of all optional subjects in the science groups was carried out using modern Optical Mark Recognition and electronic marking systems.
