LPG Reaches Rs600 Per Kg in Hilly Areas Against OGRA Price of Rs279: Distributors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

LPG prices have soared far beyond the official rate, with the fuel selling for up to Rs600 per kg in hilly areas of Pakistan, the LPG Distributors Association said. Chairman Irfan Khokhar stated that OGRA had fixed October's LPG price at Rs279 per kg, but it was being sold at Rs500 per kg in Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and at even higher rates in the mountainous regions. He accused OGRA of being a silent spectator to what he described as looting worth billions of rupees through the overcharging.

Khokhar urged the government to bring the SSGC terminal into use to rein in prices and provide relief to consumers. The association warned that unchecked price hikes ahead of the winter season would hit ordinary households the hardest.
