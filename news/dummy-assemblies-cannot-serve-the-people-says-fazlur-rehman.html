Dummy Assemblies Cannot Serve the People, Says Fazlur Rehman at Peshawar High Court Bar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman has said that dummy assemblies cannot serve the people and that transparent elections are the solution to the country's problems. Addressing the Peshawar High Court Bar, he said politics of contradictions is being done for personal interests. He said his party is not against the army, calling the army the country's defensive strength, and said they want to see the army strong against the enemy, not against themselves.

He criticised the imposition of governor's rule and emergency measures in his province and questioned why similar conditions in Balochistan had not led to governor's rule there. He said their security had been withdrawn. He also said that if they want to send him to jail at this age, he would rest, adding that such assemblies can only serve the powerful.
