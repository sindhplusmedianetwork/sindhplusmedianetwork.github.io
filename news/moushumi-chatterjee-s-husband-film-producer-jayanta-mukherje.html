Moushumi Chatterjee's husband, film producer Jayanta Mukherjee, dies in Mumbai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Veteran film producer Jayanta Mukherjee, husband of actress Moushumi Chatterjee, has died in Mumbai at over 70 years of age. Federation of Western India Cine Employees president B.N. Tiwari confirmed that he died on Thursday evening at his residence in Khar, Mumbai.

Mukherjee had been suffering from prolonged age-related health problems. He was the son of legendary singer-composer Hemant Kumar. As a producer, his films included Mazaaq (1975) and Do Ladke Dono Kadke (1979).

He is survived by his wife and his daughter Megha.
