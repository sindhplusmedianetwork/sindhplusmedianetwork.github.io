PPP May Eventually Back New-Provinces Referendum, Officials Say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Despite public opposition to a proposed referendum on creating new provinces, the Pakistan Peoples Party may eventually take part in the process, senior government officials say. Officials are confident the party could ultimately back both the referendum idea and a broader proposal to restructure the administrative setup. That stands in contrast to the public position of PPP chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari, who has opposed the plan.

The proposed restructuring, also discussed in connection with a possible 28th constitutional amendment, envisages 12 or more provinces across the country. The plan faces major political, constitutional and financial challenges, and its future remains uncertain.
