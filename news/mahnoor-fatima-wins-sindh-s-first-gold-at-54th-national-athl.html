Mahnoor Fatima Wins Sindh's First Gold at 54th National Athletics Championship

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mahnoor Fatima has won Sindh's first gold medal at the 54th National Athletics Championship, claiming the women's 5,000m race walk title. She completed the race in 32 minutes and 32.40 seconds at NED University in Karachi. WAPDA's Ammara Manawwar secured the silver medal, while Punjab's Sawera took bronze.

The championship is being organized by the Sindh Athletics Association with Rs9 million in prize money. The event was formally inaugurated on Thursday evening by Sindh Home Minister Zia Hussain Lanjar. The victory is a proud moment for Sindh, which had been waiting for its first gold of the games.
