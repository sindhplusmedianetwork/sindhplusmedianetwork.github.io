Cotton arrivals rise 6% to 5.13 million bales, but quality badly hit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's cotton arrivals reached 5.131 million bales by 15 November, a rise of 6 percent on the same period last year, according to Pakistan Cotton Ginners Association figures. Cotton Ginners Forum chairman Ehsan-ul-Haq said the increase was recorded despite adverse weather and pollution from sugarcane cultivation, which have badly affected quality. The poor quality has caused concern across the cotton sector, with fears of greater demand for imported cotton.

Punjab's ginning factories received 2.252 million bales, up 4 percent, while Sindh received 2.879 million bales, up 7 percent. Textile mills bought 4.442 million bales during the period, and exporters purchased 94,400 bales, leaving 591,000 bales of saleable stock at ginning factories. A total of 203 ginning factories are active in Punjab and 207 in Sindh.

Sanghar in Sindh received the most cotton, at 1.122 million bales, followed by Bahawalnagar with 603,000 bales.
