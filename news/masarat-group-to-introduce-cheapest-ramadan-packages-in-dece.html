Masarat Group to Introduce Cheapest Ramadan Packages in December, Says CEO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Masarat Group chief executive Muhammad Afzal Chaudhry has said the group will introduce the cheapest Ramadan packages in December. He congratulated the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah on the record participation of more than 150 countries at the four-day annual Hajj conference and exhibition in Jeddah. He also praised Saudi Hajj Minister Dr Tawfiq Al-Rabiah for the arrangements.

Chaudhry welcomed the Pakistan-Saudi agreement for Hajj 2027 and said the Ministry of Religious Affairs should ensure a fair distribution of the 40 percent private-scheme quota among clusters, as Hajj organisers are concerned about how the quota is shared. He demanded the immediate return of Hajj 2026 guarantees and an extension of the private-scheme booking date.
