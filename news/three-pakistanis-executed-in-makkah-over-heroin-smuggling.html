Three Pakistanis executed in Makkah over heroin smuggling

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three Pakistani nationals have been executed in the Makkah region after being convicted of smuggling heroin into the Kingdom. Saudi security agencies arrested the three men, and the charges were established during investigation, after which the case was presented before the competent court. The court sentenced them to death after all the evidence and the legal responsibility and capacity of the accused were established.

The verdict was upheld by the appeal court and the Supreme Court, and a royal decree was then issued for the sentences to be carried out. The executions were carried out in the Makkah region on 24 November 2026. The Saudi Interior Ministry reiterated its resolve to protect citizens and residents of the Kingdom from the curse of drugs.
