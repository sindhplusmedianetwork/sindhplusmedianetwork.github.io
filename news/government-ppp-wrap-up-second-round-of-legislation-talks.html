Government, PPP wrap up second round of legislation talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The second round of negotiations between the government and the Pakistan Peoples Party on proposed legislation concluded on Wednesday, with both sides agreeing to continue discussions. Sources said the PPP maintained its firm stance on the proposed Right of Way Bill, questioning how a mobile phone tower could be installed forcibly on privately owned land. The bill is the Pakistan Telecommunication (Re-organization) (Amendment) Bill, 2026, which aims to streamline the deployment of telecom infrastructure such as optical fibre cables, towers and 5G networks.

The PPP's objection centres on the rights of private landowners when telecom infrastructure is placed on their property. Both sides ended the round without a final agreement but with a shared commitment to keep talking. Further rounds are expected as the government seeks to move the legislation forward while addressing the PPP's concerns.
