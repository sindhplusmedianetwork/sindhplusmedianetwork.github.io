Fired US General Confesses to Leaking Classified Iran, Ukraine Secrets

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A high-ranking US Army general dismissed by Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth has confessed on video to leaking classified military information to journalists. Lt Gen Brett Sylvia, who headed the Joint Chiefs of Staff's Strategy, Plans and Policy Directorate, admitted leaking sensitive intelligence about US military operations in Iran and Ukraine over roughly one year. According to an internal Pentagon report, Sylvia failed multiple polygraph examinations during the official probe before cooperating with investigators and making his recorded confession.

Investigators found the unauthorised disclosures began as early as June 2025 and continued even after the Pentagon imposed strict media access limitations. Sylvia used the encrypted messaging service Signal to communicate with journalists, and held secret in-person meetings at an Arlington diner near the Pentagon, where he shared confidential details about US aid to Ukraine from handwritten notes with a reporter writing a book. Hegseth terminated Sylvia for severe misconduct, stressing the need to preserve national security.

The Pentagon has handed its forensic evidence and findings to the Department of Justice, where a federal criminal investigation remains ongoing.
