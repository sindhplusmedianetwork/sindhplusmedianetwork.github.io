Musadik Malik Calmly Asks Shouting Indian Journalist to Lower His Tone

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

During his recent visit to India, federal climate change minister Musadik Malik asked an Indian journalist to keep his tone down after the journalist raised his voice while asking a question about terrorism. Malik told the journalist that he could ask whatever he liked, but there was no need to shout. He reiterated Pakistan's position that discussion on the matter must be evidence-based.

The minister also expressed hope that the whole region works together on the issue. The exchange drew wide attention on social media in India. Many Indian social media users criticised the journalist's conduct and praised the Pakistani minister's patience.
