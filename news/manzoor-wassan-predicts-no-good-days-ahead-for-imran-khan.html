Manzoor Wassan predicts no good days ahead for Imran Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior Pakistan Peoples Party leader in Sindh, Manzoor Wassan, has predicted that in the current political situation he sees no good days ahead for Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf founder Imran Khan. Speaking during a meeting with party workers and others in Karachi, he said he also sees no protest movement by Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam, arguing that if it had intended to protest, it would have done so by now. He said he had not expected Imran Khan and his party to announce support for new provinces so quickly, adding that Sindh has its own history and identity and cannot be viewed as merged with other provinces.

He said the people of Sindh and the Sindh Assembly have already given their decision on the proposal for new provinces and rejected it, and that changing the names of provinces would not benefit the country. He also said that although the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa governor belongs to the Peoples Party, the decision to impose governor's rule was taken by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. On petroleum prices, he said a rise in petrol prices pushes up the prices of essential goods and other items, and the government should abolish the levy on petrol to provide relief to the people.
