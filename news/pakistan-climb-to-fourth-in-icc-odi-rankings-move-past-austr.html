Pakistan climb to fourth in ICC ODI rankings, move past Australia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan have moved up to fourth place in the ICC ODI team rankings after overtaking Australia on the back of their strong performances in the tri-series under way in Rawalpindi. Pakistan's rating points climbed to 103, leaving Australia fifth, while third-placed South Africa sit one point ahead on 104. India continue to lead the table with 115 points, followed by New Zealand on 109.

The turnaround was powered by an 87-run win over England, in which Arafat Minhas starred with 59 runs off 58 balls and six wickets for just 40 runs. Pakistan had slipped to 81 for four before recovering to post 302, thanks to Hasan Nawaz's 79, Minhas's half-century and Faheem Ashraf's 39, while England were dismissed for 215. The result underlines Pakistan's improving form in the 50-over format ahead of a busy international season.
