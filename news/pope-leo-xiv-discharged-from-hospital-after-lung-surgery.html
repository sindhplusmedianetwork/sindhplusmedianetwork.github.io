Pope Leo XIV Discharged From Hospital After Lung Surgery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pope Leo XIV returned to the Vatican on Saturday after spending four days at Rome's Gemelli Hospital. Doctors there removed part of his right lung to extract a cancerous tumour, with an intraoperative tissue examination confirming lung adenocarcinoma. Physicians Luigi Carbone and Stefano Margaritora declared the 71-year-old, Chicago-born pontiff fit for discharge after blood tests and a chest X-ray.

The pope had continued celebrating Mass in hospital and phoned Russian President Vladimir Putin on Friday, urging a ceasefire in Oleshky, Ukraine. Audiences at the Vatican are cancelled until October 20, and his planned November 6-17 trip to South America remains uncertain.
