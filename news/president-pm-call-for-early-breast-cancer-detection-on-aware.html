President, PM Call for Early Breast Cancer Detection on Awareness Day

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif issued separate messages on Sunday marking Breast Cancer Awareness Day, observed on October 19. Both leaders called for greater public awareness of breast cancer, early detection and timely treatment. President Zardari emphasised the role of family and community support, healthy lifestyles and compassionate care for patients.

He also commended the services of healthcare workers, volunteers and researchers working in the field. Prime Minister Sharif said the federal government was working together with the provinces to ensure the provision of essential healthcare services across the country. He added that specialised cancer treatment facilities were being expanded so that patients could access quality care closer to home.
