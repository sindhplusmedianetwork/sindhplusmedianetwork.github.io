'Jumanji: Open World' Trailer Out — Game Characters Invade Real World

Sony Pictures has released the trailer for 'Jumanji: Open World', the fifth and final instalment of the Jumanji film franchise. The movie is a sequel to 2019's 'Jumanji: The Next Level'. This time the story takes a new turn as the game's characters break out of the digital world and enter the real one.

Dwayne Johnson, Kevin Hart, Jack Black and Karen Gillan are all returning in their roles. The cast also includes Nick Jonas, Awkwafina and Danny DeVito. Fans of the franchise have been eagerly awaiting the finale after the previous film's success.

The film is set to release in cinemas on Christmas, December 25.
