Rs25m recovered after Rs37.5m snatched from Chinese firm's accountant in DHA

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police have arrested one suspect and detained an accountant employed by a Chinese organic meat company for questioning after over Rs37 million was reportedly snatched from him in Karachi's Defence Housing Authority on Thursday. South DIG Syed Asad Raza said Rs25 million had been recovered from the held suspect later in the evening. Mohammed Hanif Metlo had reported to Gizri police that he was travelling along Khayaban-i-Bahria, near Sultan Masjid, when two armed men riding a motorcycle intercepted him and snatched a bag containing Rs37.5 million.

The accountant, who works for a Chinese organic meat company based in Korangi, had withdrawn cheques worth the amount from I.I. Chundrigar Road and was on his way to DHA Phase VII when he was reportedly intercepted. Preliminary investigations suggest that he himself is involved and that the alleged robbers are purportedly his henchmen, the DIG said, adding that the employee had been detained for questioning.

The DIG pointed out that it was the second reported incident of a robbery involving Chinese nationals, with the first also reported within the jurisdiction of Gizri police about a month earlier.
