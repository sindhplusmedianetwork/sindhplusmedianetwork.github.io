Electric Bus Shoving Case Takes New Turn as Videos of Girl Surface

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The incident in which a girl was shoved on an electric bus has taken a new turn, with videos of her unusual behaviour on buses surfacing. The footage shows her hanging upside down inside a bus and lying on the floor. Sources cited in the report say the girl faces mental health problems and spends most of the day travelling on buses.

Passengers have demanded that staff remove her from the buses. Sources say a similar incident of violence over her behaviour occurred a few months ago. In April, a government employee was dismissed after an incident on a Speedo bus.

Citizens have demanded that her parents be traced and that she be handed over to a hospital or a government institution for treatment.
