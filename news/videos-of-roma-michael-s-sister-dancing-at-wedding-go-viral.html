Videos of Roma Michael's Sister Dancing at Wedding Go Viral

Videos of actress and supermodel Roma Michael's sister Maryam Michael dancing at Roma's Shendi ceremony have gone viral on social media as the wedding celebrations of Roma Michael and actor Shaan Baig continue. After the colourful Mayoun celebration, the couple also held the Shendi ceremony, videos of which are circulating on social media. Roma Michael appeared as a bride in a beautiful red lehenga at the ceremony, while Shaan Baig wore black attire.

Both were seen in a cheerful mood together, and their close friends and family made the ceremony memorable with dance and music. Roma Michael and Shaan Baig were both working in their respective fields when they met and later grew close. Roma began her career with Miss Veet Pakistan, remained prominent on fashion ramps as a supermodel and later turned to acting, working in various dramas including Iqtidar.

The two have already solemnised their nikah, while the series of other wedding ceremonies continues.
