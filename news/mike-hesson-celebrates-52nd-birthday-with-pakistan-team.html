Mike Hesson Celebrates 52nd Birthday With Pakistan Team

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan men's head coach Mike Hesson celebrated his 52nd birthday a day earlier, cutting the cake with the national team players. He also celebrated with his family, who are currently in Pakistan. Hesson thanked well-wishers on social media for their messages.

He said his family's presence, two cakes and a beautiful bouquet made the birthday memorable. He added that he hoped the team would beat Sri Lanka in the match that night.
