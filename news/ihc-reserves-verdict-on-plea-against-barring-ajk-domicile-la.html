IHC Reserves Verdict on Plea Against Barring AJK-Domicile Lawyer from Civil Judges Exam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court on Thursday reserved its judgment on a petition filed against barring a lawyer holding an Azad Kashmir domicile from appearing in the Islamabad civil judges recruitment examination. Justice Ayaz Shaukat heard the petition filed by advocate Zofshan Siddiqui. The petitioner's counsel requested the court to allow her to appear in the examination.

However, Justice Shaukat observed that the petition had become ineffective, noting that the examinations began on October 23, the final paper is scheduled for October 30, and the department's reply had already been submitted on the 20th. He remarked that the petitioner should have filed a miscellaneous application before the examinations began. The counsel then requested that the court order that she not be barred from any future examination.

The judge assured that he would pass an order in that regard, after which the court reserved its verdict.
