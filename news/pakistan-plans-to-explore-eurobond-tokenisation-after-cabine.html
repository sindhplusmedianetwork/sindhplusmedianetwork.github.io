Pakistan plans to explore Eurobond tokenisation after cabinet approval: finance minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb said on Monday that the government is reviewing the possibility of tokenising Pakistan's existing Eurobond debt and will seek cabinet approval before issuing a request for proposals, The Express Tribune reported. Speaking virtually at the "Dialogue on Digital Assets and the Future of Banking", organised by the Pakistan Banks' Association in collaboration with LUMS, he said the government was in discussions and looked forward to cabinet approval before moving ahead with the RFP. Aurangzeb identified remittances as another potential use of tokenisation, noting Pakistan received $41.6 billion in remittances during the previous fiscal year, with first-quarter inflows suggesting the figure could exceed $44 billion this year.

He said Pakistan needed financial innovation that reduced the cost of doing business and brought more people into the formal economy. The minister cited JPMorgan's use of deposit tokens and the Bank for International Settlements' Project Agora as international examples. He also warned of growing cybersecurity threats, saying the country was under attack round the clock by state and non-state actors.

In December 2025, the government signed a memorandum of understanding with Binance to explore tokenising up to $2 billion worth of government assets.
