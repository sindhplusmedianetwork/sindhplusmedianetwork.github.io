PTI’s shrinking political space blamed on hardline elements, party insiders say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sober and relatively realistic political leaders inside Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf believe the party could have prevented its rapidly shrinking political space had it adopted a more flexible political strategy instead of a confrontational path, according to party sources. The sources say social media campaigners, sympathetic YouTubers and vloggers operating from abroad, and leaders who left the country played a key role in pushing the party toward an increasingly confrontational narrative. On several occasions, they contend, the party had real opportunities to reduce tensions, reopen channels of political contact, and move toward normalization.

Those opportunities were squandered because leaders favoring patience or a more realistic approach faced criticism from the party’s own social media circles. The criticism is directed at the party’s hardline elements, whose influence continues to grow and makes it harder for PTI to revisit its political strategy. The loss of its Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government — the party’s only provincial government — has deepened these concerns, raising questions inside the party about whether the hardline path was effective.

The fundamental question now, for the party’s realistic camp, is not only how PTI responds to its recent political setbacks, but whether it can regain the political space it has gradually lost.
