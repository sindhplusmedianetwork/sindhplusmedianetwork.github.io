Four Karachi fishermen die mysteriously within a week of returning from sea; cause unknown

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four Karachi fishermen have died mysteriously within a week of returning after spending about a month at sea, according to Sukkan police. Two of the men died five days ago, the third died a day earlier and the fourth died on Saturday. After returning from the sea, all four had stopped passing stool and urine and had complained of kidney pain.

They received treatment at different hospitals, where doctors declared their deaths natural. Their families initially described their condition as a spiritual ailment and have said they do not want legal proceedings.
