Pakistan Receives Second October LNG Cargo From Qatar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has received another liquefied natural gas cargo from Qatar, the second Qatari shipment to arrive in October, according to energy ministry sources. The delivery comes as the country prepares for higher winter gas demand and continues to rely on long-term contracts to keep costs down. Officials noted that gas bought under the Qatar contract costs around 14 to 15 dollars per MMBTU, roughly half of what spot-market purchases can reach, with spot prices climbing as high as 30 dollars.

The cheaper contracted supply helps shield consumers and the national budget from volatile international prices. Pakistan has been arranging multiple cargoes to bridge the gap between domestic production and winter consumption. Energy planners say contracted shipments from Qatar remain the backbone of the country's LNG import strategy.
