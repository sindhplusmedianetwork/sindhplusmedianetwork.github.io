Pakistan, Turkiye to sign judicial cooperation MoU on November 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Supreme Court will host a high-level delegation from the Court of Cassation of the Republic of Turkiye. The visit will take place from November 23 to 27, 2026. The delegation will be led by its president Omer Kerkez, along with judges and senior officials.

A Memorandum of Understanding on judicial cooperation will be signed at the Supreme Court on November 23. The MoU will focus on judicial exchanges, capacity building and the sharing of best practices in adjudication. During their stay, the delegation will also visit Taxila, Faisal Mosque and the Walled City of Lahore.
