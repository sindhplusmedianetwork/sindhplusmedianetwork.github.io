Freiburg third after beating Union

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Freiburg beat Union Berlin 2-0 at home to return to third place in the Bundesliga. An own goal from Union captain Rani Khedira and a strike from Yuito Suzuki secured the win for the home side. The victory moved Freiburg back into the top three of the German league.

In the day's other matches, Bayer Leverkusen beat Cologne 2-1 in the derby to move up to fifth. Aleix Garcia and Patrik Schick scored the goals for Leverkusen. Hoffenheim also won 2-1 away at Schalke in Sunday's late game.
