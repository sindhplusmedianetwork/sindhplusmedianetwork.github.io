One in Three Adults in Pakistan Lives with Mental Disorder: Study

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nearly one in three adults in Pakistan is living with a diagnosable mental and behavioural disorder, a landmark national survey has found. The study found that 32 percent of adults are currently living with such a disorder, while 38 percent have experienced one in their lifetime. It is Pakistan's first nationally representative mental health survey, conducted over five years between 2018 and 2022.

The research was led by Professor Raza-ur-Rahman of Hamdard University and has been published in the British Medical Journal. It surveyed nearly 18,000 adults across all four provinces of the country. Experts have described the findings as the most reliable national data on mental health in Pakistan to date.

They urged greater investment in mental health services and renewed efforts to reduce the stigma around mental illness.
