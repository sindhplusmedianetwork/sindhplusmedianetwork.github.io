Pakistan U-15 End FIFA World Cup Campaign With 4-2 Win Over Djibouti

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's under-15 football team has ended its FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival campaign on a winning note, defeating Djibouti 4-2 in their final match. Captain Muhammad Mustafa scored from a free kick to bring Pakistan level, before Kaleemullah and Danish Masih added goals to seal the victory. Pakistan played 10 matches in the tournament, winning four and drawing two.

The young Green-shirts showed steady improvement through the competition, with the coaching staff praising the squad's discipline and fighting spirit. Officials say the exposure at this level will strengthen the country's youth football pipeline. The team's spirited finish has drawn praise from fans following the campaign back home.
