Spain's Former Labour Minister Fátima Báñez Killed in Train Crash; Death Toll Rises to 68

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Spanish labour minister Fátima Báñez was among those killed in the devastating collision between two high-speed trains near Toledo, Spain. Báñez was travelling on an Iryo train bound for Seville to attend a CEOE event when it was hit from behind by a Renfe high-speed train. According to the newspaper La Razón, she was in the last carriage of the Iryo train at the time of the crash.

The death toll from the accident has risen to at least 68, with rescue operations still ongoing. Báñez served as Spain's minister of labour and social security from 2011 to 2018 during the governments of former prime minister Mariano Rajoy. She hailed from the province of Huelva and had been associated with the Popular Party in Andalusia for most of her political career.

Since 2019, she had headed the foundation of the Spanish employers' confederation CEOE.
