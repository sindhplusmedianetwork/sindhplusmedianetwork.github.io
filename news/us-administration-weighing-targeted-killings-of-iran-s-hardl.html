US administration weighing targeted killings of Iran's hardline leaders to end war, report says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The administration of US President Donald Trump has begun reconsidering targeted killings of Iran's senior leaders, according to a US media report. The report, citing an American official and another person familiar with the matter who spoke on condition of anonymity, said the option of targeting Iran's hardline leadership had been under discussion before, but debate over it has intensified in recent weeks. Supporters of the proposal within the administration argue that removing hardline leaders would send a message to the remaining leadership that the United States can reach them at any time, which could push Tehran towards ending the war.

Opponents, however, fear that targeting Iranian leaders could make the remaining leadership adopt an even harder line and make future negotiations with the United States more difficult. Some American military officials have also warned that such operations could increase the risk of retaliatory attacks by Iran against US officials. The report said President Trump has not yet taken a final decision on targeting hardline Iranian leaders, but options including large-scale military operations in Iran remain before him.

It added that neither the United States nor Iran has reacted to the report so far. The report also noted that a joint strike in late February had targeted senior leadership, including Ayatollah Khamenei, yet its objectives had still not been achieved.
