Six Robbers Snatch Rs6m From Petrol Pump Cashier in Karachi; Passerby Shot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Six robbers intercepted a petrol pump cashier and his security guard near Mosamiyat in Gulistan-i-Jauhar, Karachi, on Monday. The cashier was on his way to deposit the day's cash at a nearby private bank when the robbers held the two men at gunpoint and snatched Rs6 million. As the robbers were fleeing the scene, the security guard opened fire, but the bullet hit a passer-by, Mohammed Afzal, 48.

The suspects managed to escape, leaving behind their motorcycle and a pistol. Police said the abandoned motorcycle had no number plate but that investigators had obtained significant clues. Concerted efforts are underway to arrest the suspects, according to police.
