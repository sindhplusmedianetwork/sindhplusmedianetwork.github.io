WFP Slashes Gaza Food Aid as 1.4 Million Face Severe Food Shortage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Food Programme has sharply reduced food assistance for Gaza, cutting the number of monthly recipients by 500,000 — from 1.6 million to 1.1 million — because of funding shortfalls. The agency has also cut food rations by 50 percent. Daily distributions of ready-to-eat meals were reduced from 250,000 to 150,000.

Some families in hard-to-reach areas now receive food only once every two days. The WFP says 1.4 million people in Gaza are facing severe food shortage. The cuts have raised fears of worsening hunger in the besieged territory.

Aid agencies have repeatedly warned that funding gaps are crippling relief operations. The WFP has appealed for urgent donor support to restore full assistance.
