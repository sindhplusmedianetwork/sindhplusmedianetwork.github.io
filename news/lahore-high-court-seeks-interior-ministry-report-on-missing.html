Lahore High Court Seeks Interior Ministry Report on 'Missing' TLP Leaders

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court on Tuesday asked the federal interior ministry to report on the whereabouts of Saad Rizvi and Ansar Rizvi, leaders of the proscribed Tehreek-i-Labbaik Pakistan. The court's direction followed statements by 30 station house officers of the Punjab police, all of whom denied having arrested the two leaders. Justice Muhammad Jawad Zafar was hearing a petition filed by TLP leader Sarwat Fatima, who has challenged the alleged detention of the Rizvi brothers.

The judge directed the interior ministry to submit its reply by October 27. The case centres on the party leadership's absence from public view, which the petitioner claims amounts to an undisclosed detention.
