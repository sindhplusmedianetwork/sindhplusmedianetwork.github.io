Australia Bushfires Out of Control as Hundreds Move to Safety

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Bushfires in New South Wales, Australia, are out of control, forcing hundreds of residents to move to safety. Mass evacuations have been ordered on the Central Coast as more than 50 fires remain active across the state. A 42-degree heatwave, combined with strong winds and dry weather, is hampering efforts to control the blazes.

Dozens of homes have been destroyed by the fires. Rail services between Gosford and Hornsby have been suspended because of the danger. More than 250 firefighters are battling the blazes, and evacuation centres have been set up at Gosford for residents forced to leave their homes.

Authorities have advised residents in the affected areas to stay alert.
