Five-Year-Old Girl Dies, Boy Missing After Falling into Swat River

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A five-year-old girl died and a 13-year-old boy went missing after the two children fell into the Swat River in the Aryan area of Bahrain, in Swat. The children were crossing a temporary wooden bridge when they fell into the river. The girl, Kulsoom, died and her body was recovered from the water.

The boy, 13-year-old Syed Rehman, remained missing after the incident. Rescue 1122 teams launched a search operation to locate the missing boy. The local administration confirmed the incident and said the search was continuing.
