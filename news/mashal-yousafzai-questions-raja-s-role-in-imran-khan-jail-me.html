Mashal Yousafzai questions Raja's role in Imran Khan jail meetings

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Mashal Yousafzai questioned who was troubled that Imran Khan's solitary confinement was ending and his jail meetings were resuming. She said that when her case about meetings with Khan was fixed before a larger bench headed by Justice Dogar, Zaheer Abbas was Khan's lawyer and Sohail Swati was her lawyer. She said Salman Akram Raja suddenly arrived in court and allegedly filed an affidavit without Khan's consultation or permission, pledging no media talk after meetings at Adiala Jail.

Raja was then appointed coordinator for Khan's jail meetings, she said. She added that the same condition later blocked Khan's sisters' meetings. She asked how lawyers Latif Khosa and Salman Safdar, who have signed vakalatnamas for Khan and represent him in the Al-Qadir case, could be called facilitators if a court allowed them to meet him.
