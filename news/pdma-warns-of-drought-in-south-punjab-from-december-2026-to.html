PDMA warns of drought in south Punjab from December 2026 to February 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Provincial Disaster Management Authority Punjab has warned of a possible drought in south Punjab from December 2026 to February 2027, as rainfall is expected to remain below normal. The authority issued an alert and directed the departments concerned and district administrations to ensure advance arrangements to deal with the situation. Letters were sent to the departments of local government, agriculture, irrigation, environment, health, livestock, forests, education, Rescue 1122 and transport, while the commissioners of Bahawalpur, Sargodha and Dera Ghazi Khan and the deputy commissioners of the districts concerned were also told to complete preparations.

PDMA Director General Muhammad Khizer Afzal said the dry spell could reduce moisture in farmland and increase pressure on water resources. He said that on the instructions of the Punjab chief minister, all departments concerned had been told to ensure advance measures, with staff present round the clock at district emergency operation centres and close coordination maintained between the authority and the departments. He added that a complete database of areas and populations likely to be affected should be prepared so that relief can be provided in time, alongside public awareness campaigns on saving water and measures to protect livestock, including the supply of medicines and fodder.

District administrations were also directed to deploy additional staff and resources in the more sensitive areas and to ensure the availability of medicines in Rescue 1122 and hospitals.
