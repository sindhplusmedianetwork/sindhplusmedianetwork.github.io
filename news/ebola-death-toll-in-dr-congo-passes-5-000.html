Ebola death toll in DR Congo passes 5,000

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 5,000 people have died in the Democratic Republic of Congo's Ebola outbreak, making it the country's deadliest on record, according to government data. The health ministry reported 5,026 deaths among 10,351 cases, a fatality rate of 48.6 percent, with eight of the country's 26 provinces affected. The outbreak, which began in May and is caused by the Bundibugyo virus, became the deadliest in the country's history in August, public health officials confirmed.

The epicentre remains the north-eastern province of Ituri, where conflict conditions are complicating efforts to bring the virus under control. Cases have also been confirmed in Kenya, Nigeria and Uganda. The United Nations says this is the 17th Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo's history and the second-largest ever, after the 2014-2016 West Africa epidemic.

The World Health Organization's African office said containment efforts need to be stepped up, with better access to affected areas and wider awareness campaigns among local populations.
