Home disappointment as all nine Pakistanis exit Karachi squash championship in first round

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

It was a disappointing day for home fans as all nine Pakistani players, including three women, lost their first-round matches at the Venus ABC Open International Squash Championship in Karachi on Wednesday. World No. 92 Nasir Iqbal suffered an upset, going down 3-0 to Canada's Saleh Eltorgman in the opening round.

Abdul Basit Khan, Abdul Saeed, Azan Ali Khan, Anas Ali Shah and Abdullah Nawaz were also knocked out at the first hurdle. The only Pakistani still in the draw is Muhammad Asim Khan, who received a bye into the pre-quarterfinals. He will face Malaysia's Muhammad Shafiq Kamal in the second round on Thursday.
