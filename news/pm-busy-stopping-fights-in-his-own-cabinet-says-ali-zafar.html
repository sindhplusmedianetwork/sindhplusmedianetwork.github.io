PM busy stopping fights in his own cabinet, says Ali Zafar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Senator Barrister Ali Zafar has said that the prime minister himself is busy stopping fights within his own cabinet. He made the remark about Shehbaz Sharif's preoccupation with infighting among his ministers. The comment comes amid recent statements by federal ministers against each other, which the prime minister has already taken notice of.

Ali Zafar suggested that the prime minister's attention is consumed by managing divisions inside the cabinet. His remarks reflect the PTI's criticism of the government's internal differences. The exchange of remarks between ministers has become a visible issue in national politics.
