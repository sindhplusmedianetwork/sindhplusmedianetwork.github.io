South Asian Games postponed again, likely to be held in November 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 14th South Asian Games, scheduled to begin in Pakistan on April 3 next year, have been postponed for the sixth time, with the regional multi-sport event now likely to be held in November 2027, sources familiar with the matter said. The decision to delay the Games was taken for unspecified reasons, according to informed sources. Pakistan was originally awarded hosting rights for the event in 2021, but the Games have repeatedly been postponed because of administrative delays, funding problems, the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic and clashes with the regional sporting calendar.

The Games were scheduled to take place in Islamabad, Lahore and Peshawar, featuring 28 men's and 25 women's sports disciplines, with 346 gold medals on offer, and more than 4,200 male and female athletes and officials from South Asian member countries were expected to take part. Pakistan Olympic Association President Arif Saeed had previously said the country was fully prepared to host the Games and that the government was providing full support, adding that the required funding had been approved. The latest postponement, however, raises further questions about Pakistan's ability to stage the regional sporting event after years of delays.

A well-placed source told the correspondent that full effort will be made to conduct the Games at the end of next year, but if that is not possible they will be shifted to 2028, adding that the postponement of the Asian Indoor and Martial Games had created a big issue for the organisers, as both events were on the same timeline, and that winter Games cannot be held because of smog in Lahore.
