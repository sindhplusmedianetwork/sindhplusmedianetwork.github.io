Gas Circular Debt Up Rs344 Billion in a Year to Rs3,631 Billion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The gas sector's circular debt grew by Rs344 billion in a single year to reach Rs3,631 billion by June 30, 2026, the Petroleum Division revealed in the National Assembly. A year earlier, on June 30, 2025, the debt stood at Rs3,288 billion. The mounting debt includes Rs1,783 billion in surcharges on late payments.

The division also told the house that Pakistan's total recoverable gas reserves stand at 68,803 billion cubic feet. Of this, 48,140 billion cubic feet are currently being produced. The figures underline the financial pressure weighing on the country's gas sector.
