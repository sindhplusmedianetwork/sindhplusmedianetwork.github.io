Rs1,500 Prize Bond 108th Draw: First Prize of Rs3 Million Won by Bond Number 290479

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Savings has announced the initial results of the 108th draw of the Rs1,500 prize bond, which was held in Hyderabad. The first prize of Rs3 million went to bond number 290479. Three second prizes of Rs1 million each went to bond numbers 699105, 780676 and 839522.

A total of 1,696 third prizes of Rs18,500 each were also announced. The draw was the 108th in the series for the Rs1,500 bond.
