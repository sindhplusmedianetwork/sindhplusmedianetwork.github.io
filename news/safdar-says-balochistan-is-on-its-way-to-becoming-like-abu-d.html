Safdar says Balochistan is on its way to becoming like Abu Dhabi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Captain (retd) Safdar, husband of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz, has said in Chiniot that Balochistan is on its way to becoming like Abu Dhabi and that Punjab has seen more development in two years than in 80 years. He defended the Punjab government, dismissed talk of a no-confidence move against Maryam Nawaz as based on lies, and said land grabs in Gwadar would be reversed so that business and visitors come to Balochistan under Nawaz Sharif's vision. Safdar spoke in support of the provincial government's performance, presenting the recent pace of development in Punjab as unprecedented.

He also addressed concerns over land in Gwadar, saying that illegal occupation would be undone to open the way for business activity and visitors. The remarks place Balochistan's development and Gwadar's future at the centre of his message, tying both to Nawaz Sharif's vision for the province.
