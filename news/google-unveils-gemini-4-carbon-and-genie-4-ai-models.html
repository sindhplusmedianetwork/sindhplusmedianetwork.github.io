Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon and Genie 4 AI models

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Technology company Google has announced two new artificial intelligence models, 'Gemini 4 Carbon' and 'Genie 4', just two weeks after it had presented new AI models earlier. In the latest development, one model is built for cybersecurity tasks while the other focuses on the ability to create and run digital worlds. According to Google, Gemini 4 Carbon is its most advanced AI model yet, and the company claims it outperformed some other models, including Gemini 4 Argon and OpenAI's GPT 6 Astra, in benchmark tests.

The model is designed specifically to help cybersecurity specialists identify and fix vulnerabilities in computer systems. Genie 4, on the other hand, is a 'world model' aimed at creating digital worlds based on real or imagined environments. While ordinary chatbots predict the next words in response to a user's question, world models attempt to model an environment, its elements and their interactions, a technology useful in robotics, virtual environments and video game development.

Genie 4 also includes built-in audio so developers can add background sounds and dialogue to the digital worlds they create, making those environments feel more real and dynamic. For now, neither model is available to the general public, and access to Gemini 4 Carbon is being given to selected cybersecurity experts.
