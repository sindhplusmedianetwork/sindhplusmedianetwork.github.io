Pakistani youth delegate Abeerah Zia represents Pakistan at Global Youth Festival 2026 in Paris

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Pakistani youth delegate has represented the country at a major international youth gathering in Paris. Abeerah Zia, a member of the Prime Minister Youth Council's Overseas Chapter Germany, took part in the four-day Global Youth Festival 2026 in the French capital, which was organised by HISA, the Public Policy Research Institute, in collaboration with the International Youth Institute and attended by 230 young delegates from different countries. The festival's sessions focused on youth leadership, diplomacy, sustainable development, global cooperation and the challenges facing the current generation.

During the event, Zia spoke on a topic close to her heart, girls' education, stressing that education is every girl's basic right and that no girl's dreams should remain unfulfilled for lack of resources or opportunities. She added that education enables girls to make informed decisions, use their potential and play an effective role in society's progress. The visit also included a tour of France's National Assembly, where she learned about French democratic history, the evolution of equal rights and the parliamentary decision-making process.

Participants also heard from speakers and representatives of UNICEF France and the OECD, including UNICEF France Youth Ambassador Amadou Lee, whose remarks highlighted meaningful youth participation and children's rights. Zia described representing Pakistan as an honour, saying her meetings with young people from diverse cultures proved a valuable opportunity for learning and mutual cooperation. She expressed confidence that young people can play a key role in building a more just, inclusive and sustainable future through dialogue, joint efforts and practical action, and thanked both organising institutes, hoping the friendships and professional contacts made in Paris would lead to meaningful future cooperation.
