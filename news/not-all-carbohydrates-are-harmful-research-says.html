Not all carbohydrates are harmful, research says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nutrition experts say it is common to link carbohydrates with weight gain, type 2 diabetes and other health problems. However, they say not all carbohydrates are the same. Processed foods made from sugar and refined grains may be high in carbohydrates but low in vitamins and minerals.

Many natural foods, by contrast, provide useful nutrients along with carbohydrates. Oats, bananas, sweet potatoes, oranges, beans and chickpeas are among the foods cited as providing fibre, vitamins and minerals. Experts say attention should be paid to the sources and quantity of carbohydrates rather than abandoning them altogether.
