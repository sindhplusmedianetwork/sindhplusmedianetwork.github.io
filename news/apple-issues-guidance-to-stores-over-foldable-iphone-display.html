Apple issues guidance to stores over foldable iPhone display cracking issue

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apple has circulated formal guidance to its retail stores and authorised service providers on how to handle reports of the iPhone Duo's inner display cracking. According to a memo shared internally, technicians have been told that if a customer brings in the device with this complaint, they should inspect the iPhone Duo and repair or replace it following their usual procedures. While the instructions themselves are routine, the very act of issuing an internal memo signals that the problem has surfaced often enough to require official direction.

The memo notes that the issue has appeared in only a very small number of units, consistent with a statement the company released on Monday. In that statement to MacRumors, Apple acknowledged awareness of a problem affecting the inner screen of a few iPhone Duo units and advised affected users to contact AppleCare to obtain a replacement device. Customers in the United States and Canada can seek help through Apple's website or by calling 1-800-APL-CARE.
