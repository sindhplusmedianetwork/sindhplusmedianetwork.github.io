NASA launches $1 million Moon Base student challenge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

NASA has launched a $1 million "Moon Base Student Challenge" for teams of high-school and university students across the United States. The challenge asks students to design a permanent human base on the Moon. Participants will also have to solve practical problems of living there.

The competition was announced at the "Max Power" event at the Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex in Florida. It is part of NASA's "Golden Age of Science" initiative. NASA says the aim is to encourage young people's scientific and creative skills and generate workable ideas for a sustained human presence on the Moon.
