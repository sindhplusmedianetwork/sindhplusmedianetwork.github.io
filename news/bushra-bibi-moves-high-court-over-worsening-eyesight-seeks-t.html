Bushra Bibi moves high court over worsening eyesight, seeks treatment from doctors of her choice

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bushra Bibi, the wife of PTI founder Imran Khan, has approached the Islamabad High Court over concerns about her deteriorating eyesight. She has sought an immediate medical examination by an ophthalmologist and permission to receive treatment from doctors of her choice at her own expense. Her counsel's affidavit says doctors diagnosed a mature cataract in her eye.

The affidavit further says doctors advised that another eye surgery would be required around November 15. Bushra Bibi is serving a seven-year sentence in the 190 million pound corruption case. The court has been asked to allow her to get the recommended treatment without delay.
