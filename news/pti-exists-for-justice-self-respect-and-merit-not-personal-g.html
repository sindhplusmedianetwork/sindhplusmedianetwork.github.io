PTI Exists for Justice, Self-Respect and Merit, Not Personal Gain, Says Ali Muhammad Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Ali Muhammad Khan has said that Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf is not a movement for any personal gain but a struggle to establish justice, self-respect and merit in Pakistan. Speaking in a statement in Islamabad, he said the party is a trust of a person who spent his entire life for Pakistan and who now watches his nation from inside jail after sacrificing his life and his happiness for it. He said the party is another name for the nation's love for its country, and that this name must be kept alive and radiant.

He urged every worker and leader to reflect on why they are part of the movement, stressing that they joined it for no personal motive but only for Pakistan, so that a system of justice is established here. For him, he added, PTI is the name of a political struggle to make Jinnah's Pakistan, where there is justice, self-respect and merit, and where the dreams the elders carried in their eyes become reality. He said the movement must be kept clean of hatred, accusation, abuse, malice, greed, conspiracy, backbiting, oppression and corruption, and appealed to all to protect this movement for justice at least until Imran Khan comes out of jail.

Difference of opinion, he said, is not enmity, and after faith, unity is the greatest strength. He expressed confidence that the plant of justice planted by Imran Khan would flourish and that the nation would reap the fruit of the 30-year struggle in the form of a strong, powerful and Islamic welfare state.
