Pakistan Railways hands Awam Express to private operator

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Railways has handed over the commercial management of the Awam Express to a private operator as part of its efforts to improve passenger services and expand public-private partnerships, Railways Minister Hanif Abbasi said on Monday. He was speaking at a ceremony to inaugurate the outsourcing of the commercial management of the Peshawar-Karachi Awam Express to AA Enterprises at Rawalpindi Railway Station. Abbasi said Pakistan Railways' revenue had increased from Rs88 billion to Rs115 billion, crossing the Rs100 billion mark for the first time.

The outsourcing is part of the railways' wider effort to improve passenger services through public-private partnerships. The ceremony at Rawalpindi Railway Station formally marked the transfer of the train's commercial management to the private operator. The Awam Express runs between Peshawar and Karachi, and its commercial operations will now be handled by AA Enterprises.
