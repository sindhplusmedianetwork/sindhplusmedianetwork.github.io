Sindh Assembly passes bill naming in-charge judge for areas with multiple anti-terrorism courts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Assembly has passed the Anti-Terrorism Act amendment bill, under which an in-charge judge will be named in areas with more than one anti-terrorism court. All cases in the area will first be filed in the in-charge judge's court. That court may hear them or transfer them to another ATC judge before charges are framed.

The bill also changes the procedure for appointing ATC judges. Under the new procedure, the Home Department will refer the panel to the government if no agreement is reached within ten working days.
