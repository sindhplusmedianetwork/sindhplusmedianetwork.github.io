Iran War Shadows Republican Future as Vance Distances Himself From Trump

US Vice President JD Vance is in a difficult position as the war with Iran casts a shadow over the Republican Party's future. Vance is part of the administration that started the war with Iran, while also being counted among the leading contenders for the Republican presidential nomination in 2028. After the Republicans' losses in the midterm elections, Vance publicly blamed the Iran war for the defeat.

His remarks distanced him from President Donald Trump, who has continued to defend the decision to attack Iran. The disagreement has raised questions about the direction of the party and Vance's own political future. The Iran war has become a central issue in the debate over the Republicans' path ahead.
