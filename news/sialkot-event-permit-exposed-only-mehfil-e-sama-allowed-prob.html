Sialkot Event Permit Exposed: Only Mehfil-e-Sama Allowed, Probe Widens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The original permit for TikToker Mr. Patlu's event at Sialkot's Faraz Ground allowed only a traditional mehfil-e-sama gathering. Instead, the organizers drew in thousands of people by announcing prizes, far beyond the permitted limits.

A stampede broke out days earlier in which people were killed. The Deputy Commissioner and District Police Officer questioned how organizers failed to provide internal security and emergency exits. Officials said the negligence of the administration and organizers is now under scrutiny.

Police have launched raids to arrest Anser Jutt, known as Mr. Patlu. The investigation into the event's violations continues.
