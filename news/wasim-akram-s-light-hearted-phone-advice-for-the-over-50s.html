Wasim Akram's Light-Hearted Phone Advice for the Over-50s

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read

Former Pakistan cricket captain Wasim Akram shared a humorous video on Instagram advising people over 50 on how to use mobile phones, WhatsApp and social media without overwhelming others. He wrote: "Viewer discretion: THIS VIDEO IS AVAILABLE FOR PEOPLE OVER 50 ON HOW AND WHEN TO USE A MOBILE PHONE/WHATSAPP. AND YOU ARE WELCOME..." His first advice was to "stop sending random stuff to people every day" — daily good morning messages can annoy recipients: "I get you mean well, but it annoys people." On videos, he said attention spans are short these days — nobody will watch a 30-minute video, while around two minutes is suitable for many viewers.

On constant availability, he said there is nothing wrong with replying five minutes later, and constantly checking notifications and responding immediately can create unnecessary stress. He recalled friends who never put their phones down even when spending time together, urging people to enjoy life and manage their messages. He said he still checks and forwards messages but keeps things under control instead of letting his phone dominate his time.

The video sparked discussion online: one user wrote, "never make someone feel like a burden because they are trying to stay connected," while another agreed, particularly about daily good morning messages.
