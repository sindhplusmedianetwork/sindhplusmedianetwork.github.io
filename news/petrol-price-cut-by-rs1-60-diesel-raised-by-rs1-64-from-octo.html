Petrol Price Cut by Rs1.60, Diesel Raised by Rs1.64 From October 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has cut the price of petrol by Rs1.60 per litre, bringing it down to Rs377.71, while increasing the price of high-speed diesel by Rs1.64 per litre to Rs391.75. The revised prices take effect on Friday, October 23, under the government's daily fuel-price revision regime. Officials said taxes and duties currently stand at Rs114 per litre on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel.

Dawn reported that diesel has come down considerably from its April 3 peak of Rs520.35 per litre, while petrol has eased from Rs458.41. The adjustment follows September's austerity measures, which included shutting markets by 9pm and cutting official-vehicle fuel use by 50 percent.
