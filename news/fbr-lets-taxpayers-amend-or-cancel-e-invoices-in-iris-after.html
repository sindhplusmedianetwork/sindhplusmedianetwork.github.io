FBR lets taxpayers amend or cancel e-invoices in IRIS after 72 hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue has introduced a new facility in its IRIS system for taxpayers, allowing an electronic sales tax invoice to be amended even after 72 hours have passed. Taxpayers will also be able to get a sales tax invoice cancelled after the deadline has passed. Amendment or cancellation of an invoice will require prior approval from the relevant Commissioner Inland Revenue, and PRAL has issued a user guide on using the new facility.

Taxpayers can submit an amendment or cancellation request through the IRIS portal, attaching supporting documents in PDF format for correction of the invoice. A single application cannot cover both amendment and cancellation for the selected invoices. Until a decision is made on the application, filing Annex-C of the sales tax return will remain barred.

After the Commissioner Inland Revenue approves, the amended invoices can be included in Annex-C, while if the application is rejected, the original invoices will be included in Annex-C. The new facility does not apply to invoices already attached to Annex-C of a filed return, and invoices included in rejected or pending applications cannot be part of a new application.
