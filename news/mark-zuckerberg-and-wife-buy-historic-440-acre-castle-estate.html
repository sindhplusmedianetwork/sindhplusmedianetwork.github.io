Mark Zuckerberg and Wife Buy Historic 440-Acre Castle Estate in Ireland

Meta chief executive Mark Zuckerberg and his wife Priscilla Chan have bought a historic castle estate in Ireland. The estate, Strancally Castle in County Waterford on the River Blackwater, spans about 440 acres. The deal for the main house and about an acre of land was reported at around 51.17 million euros, about 57 million dollars.

The Gothic Revival mansion was built around 1830 and covers about 16,000 square feet. The purchase was registered in Ireland's Property Price Register on August 17, 2026. The full 440-acre estate was not part of that registered deal, so the total cost of the purchase is likely to be higher.
