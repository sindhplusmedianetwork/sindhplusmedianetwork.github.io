WhatsApp Testing Audio Stickers That Let Users Add Voice to Pictures

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WhatsApp is working on a new 'sound stickers' feature that will allow users to attach short audio clips to images and animated stickers. The feature was spotted in beta versions 2.26.41.3 and 2.26.41.11 of the messaging app. A sound-wave icon in the sticker creation toolbar will give users the option to record a voice note or select an existing audio file to pair with a sticker.

The audio clips will be limited to 10 seconds in length. Importantly, the audio will not play automatically when a sticker is received — recipients will need to tap a speaker icon to hear it. WhatsApp says the audio stickers will remain protected by the app's end-to-end encryption.

The feature is still in testing and there is no confirmed date for its public rollout, but it is expected to arrive in a future update of the app.
