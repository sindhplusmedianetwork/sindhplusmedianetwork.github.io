New polio case in K-P takes Pakistan's tally to eight this year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Emergency Operations Centre for Polio Eradication has confirmed a fresh polio case from Khyber district of Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa. The affected child is a 13-month-old girl from Jan Baz Union Council in Bara tehsil. This is the second polio case reported from Khyber district this year and the sixth from K-P, taking Pakistan's national tally to eight cases.

The centre reminded parents that polio is incurable but preventable through vaccination, and urged them to ensure children receive polio drops in every campaign. A special sub-national immunisation campaign will begin on November 2 and continue until November 8, covering 97 selected districts including all districts of Karachi. More than 24 million children under the age of five will be administered polio drops during the drive.

According to the centre, Pakistan has reduced polio cases by 99.8 percent, from around 20,000 in the early 1990s to 31 in 2025, with four large-scale campaigns already conducted this year.
