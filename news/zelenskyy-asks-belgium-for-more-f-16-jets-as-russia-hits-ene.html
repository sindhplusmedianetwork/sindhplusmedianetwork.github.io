Zelenskyy Asks Belgium for More F-16 Jets as Russia Hits Energy Grid

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has called for more F-16 fighter jets and ammunition during meetings with Belgium's King Philippe and Prime Minister Bart De Wever, as Russian attacks continued to pound Ukraine's energy infrastructure. Zelenskyy said Russian strikes on energy facilities had left nearly 15,000 people without electricity in the Volyn region, while Kyiv and its surrounding areas suffered partial power outages. He warned that the threat of repeated Russian strikes was preventing repair crews from starting restoration work in some communities.

The president said F-16 jets were playing a significant role in intercepting Russian jet-powered Shahed drones, and that Ukraine was working with Belgium to secure additional aircraft as quickly as possible. The two leaders also discussed further ammunition supplies for the fighter jets. During his audience with King Philippe, Zelenskyy thanked Belgium for its continued support and stressed the urgent need to strengthen air defences against missile and drone attacks.

He also called for frozen Russian assets to remain immobilised for the duration of the war, arguing Moscow should not regain access to funds that could finance further attacks.
