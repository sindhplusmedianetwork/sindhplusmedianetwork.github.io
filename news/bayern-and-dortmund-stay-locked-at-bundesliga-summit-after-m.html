Bayern and Dortmund Stay Locked at Bundesliga Summit After Matching 2-1 Wins

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bayern Munich and Borussia Dortmund remain neck and neck at the top of the German Bundesliga after 2-1 wins over Mainz and Elversberg respectively on Saturday. The two sides, who drew 2-2 in Munich last Saturday, are locked on 21 points after nine rounds, with the defending champions ahead on goal difference. Harry Kane, goalless in three games since winning the Ballon d'Or in late October, set up Joshua Kimmich's opener after 14 minutes and laid on a second-half goal for Lennart Karl, taking his season tally to 10 goals and 10 assists in all competitions.

Bayern won for just the third time in their past seven league matches at Mainz, and goalkeeper Manuel Neuer became the oldest player in the club's history at 40 years and 225 days before being substituted before half-time with a foot injury. In Dortmund, Felix Nmecha scored a second-half brace from outside the box, and although Noel Futkeu pulled one back with nine minutes left, the hosts held on. Elsewhere, RB Leipzig came from behind to beat Augsburg 5-2 and climb to third, VfB Stuttgart won 1-0 at Werder Bremen, and Eintracht Frankfurt drew 2-2 at Paderborn.
