NAB Chief Seeks Authority Over Corruption Cases Below Rs 500 Crore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

NAB Chairman Lt-Gen Nazir Ahmad has asked the government to grant the accountability watchdog authority over corruption cases involving amounts below Rs 500 crore. He told the government that no case under that threshold currently falls within NAB's jurisdiction. Nazir Ahmad said NAB had recovered state land worth Rs 14 trillion over the past nine months.

He said the bureau was now focused on institutional corruption and reclaiming state land from the qabza mafia. Giving a breakdown of those facing corruption cases, he said more than 60 percent are private businessmen, 18 percent bureaucrats, 8 percent bank defaulters and 4.5 percent politicians. The chairman's remarks came as the government considers changes to the accountability framework.
