Azam Khan calls Babar Azam's fans 'ignorant' over abusive messages

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan wicketkeeper-batter Azam Khan has criticised the behaviour of some of Babar Azam's fans, calling them ignorant. Speaking on a podcast, Azam said that during an international match in Ireland, he was batting with Babar when Babar was nearing 75 runs. He said a fan then left an abusive comment on his Instagram picture, writing that if he did not let Babar complete his century, his mother would die and his father would get cancer.

Azam said he had no problem with Babar Azam or his batting, but the behaviour of some fans was highly inappropriate. He asked what players would think of fans after receiving such comments.
