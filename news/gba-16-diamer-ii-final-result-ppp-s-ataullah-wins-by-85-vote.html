GBA-16 Diamer-II final result: PPP's Ataullah wins by 85 votes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The final result of Gilgit-Baltistan Assembly constituency GBA-16 Diamer-II has been announced, and Form 49 has also been issued. According to the result, Pakistan Peoples Party candidate Ataullah was declared successful with 6,758 votes. Independent candidate Syed Imam Malik stood second with 6,673 votes, leaving a margin of only 85 votes between the two.

PML-N candidate Engineer M. Anwar secured 6,025 votes, while Istehkam-e-Pakistan Party candidate Atiqullah finished fourth with 5,475 votes. A total of 25,327 valid votes were cast in the constituency, and 306 votes were rejected.

The returning officer issued the final result on 16 November 2026. The election tribunal had ordered re-polling on Malik's petition, in which he had challenged alleged irregularities at three polling stations. After the counting of postal ballots, Ataullah had earlier secured 6,618 votes, with a lead of only 13 votes over Malik.
