Apple shares dip as rising memory costs cloud profit outlook

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apple shares slid about 4 percent in premarket trading on Tuesday after the company warned investors that rising memory chip costs would weigh on its gross margins. Chief Executive John Ternus, delivering his first earnings report since taking the helm, forecast gross margins of 45 to 46 percent for the December quarter. The guidance includes the cost of upgrading the AI-powered Siri assistant.

The margin warning overshadowed an otherwise strong quarter, as Apple reported record iPhone revenue of $57.58 billion for the September quarter. The record sales came despite recent price increases on the company's flagship devices.
