Petrol and high-speed diesel drop by Rs3 per litre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Wednesday reduced the prices of petrol and high-speed diesel by Rs3 per litre for November 19, according to the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority. The new petrol price is Rs322 per litre, while high-speed diesel now costs Rs355 per litre. The cut follows the government's earlier relief measures for fuel consumers.

These include a daily price-review mechanism introduced in July. They also include a Rs100-per-litre relief scheme for small vehicles announced in September.
