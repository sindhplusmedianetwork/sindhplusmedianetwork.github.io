Newborn 'bitten by animals' dies in Sukkur's Pano Aqil hospital incubator

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A newborn died at a private hospital in Pano Aqil after allegedly being bitten by cats or other animals in an incubator room, prompting allegations of negligence against the facility. The district administration has ordered an inquiry and the hospital has reportedly been sealed. The hospital is located on the National Highway, and according to the family, a woman from the Dadloi area was admitted there four days ago for delivery.

The newborn, who had breathing difficulties, was placed in an incubator on the staff's advice, in a room to which animals allegedly had access. The father initially told reporters that staff locked the room and left for the night, and when it was opened the next morning, the baby allegedly had bite marks on the neck and cheeks and had lost a significant amount of blood, which he said caused the death. The body was handed over to the family with cotton placed over the wounds, and when relatives removed it while preparing the body for burial, they allegedly saw blood dripping from the wounds.

Later reports suggested that the hospital administration persuaded the father to withdraw his account, and he subsequently changed his statement and denied that any such incident had occurred, while there were also allegations that the family was offered money to stay silent.
