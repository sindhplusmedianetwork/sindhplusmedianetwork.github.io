India's opposition stages mock impeachment of election chief over voter-list revision

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's political opposition on Saturday staged a symbolic impeachment of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar to protest a nationwide revision of voter lists that they say could unfairly benefit the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party. The opposition parties gathered at the residence of Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge in New Delhi and re-enacted a parliamentary session, holding mock impeachment proceedings that carry no constitutional or legal force. Kharge said the motion was aimed at safeguarding the dignity of a constitutional institution and ensuring the election commission functions impartially.

Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi said the opposition had resorted to the symbolic exercise as they 'have nowhere else to go', adding that 'every single institution ... is closed for us'. The election commission says the voter-roll revision is necessary to ensure accurate lists, and both the commission and the BJP deny the manipulation allegations.

Home Minister Amit Shah said the BJP was ready to hold a special parliament session on the revision, accusing the opposition of refusing to let parliament function.
