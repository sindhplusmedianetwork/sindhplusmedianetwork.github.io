US Indicts 28 People, Including Two Indians, for Election Fraud

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States Department of Justice has indicted 28 green-card holders in California for election fraud. Two Indian nationals, Amandeep Kaur and Kamlesh Shah, are among the indicted. They are accused of illegally registering as voters and voting in the November 2024 United States elections.

Non-citizen green-card holders cannot vote in federal elections, officials said. Eleven people were arrested during the operation, though the arrests of the two Indians have not been confirmed. The accused face charges including misrepresentation on citizenship applications, which carry up to five years in prison.
