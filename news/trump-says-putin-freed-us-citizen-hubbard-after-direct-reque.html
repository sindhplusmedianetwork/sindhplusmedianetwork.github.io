Trump Says Putin Freed US Citizen Hubbard After Direct Request, With No Prisoner Swap

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russia has released US citizen Stephen Hubbard after holding him in detention for more than four years. US President Donald Trump claimed he directly contacted Russian President Vladimir Putin and requested Hubbard's release, after which Moscow decided to free him. According to an international news agency, Hubbard's release was announced on October 21 during a meeting between Putin and US special envoy Steve Witkoff in St.

Petersburg. US officials said no Russian prisoner was handed over and no other concession was announced in exchange for Hubbard's release. Trump said on social media that he raised the matter during a telephone conversation with Putin the previous week and that the Russian president accepted his request.

Russian authorities had accused Hubbard of joining Ukraine's Territorial Defense Forces and agreeing to receive payment to fight against Russian forces, with prosecutors saying he was promised $1,000 per month. In October 2024, a Moscow court sentenced him to six years and 10 months in prison for mercenary-related activities. However, Hubbard's family and lawyer rejected the allegations, maintaining he was not a trained soldier and had been detained during the war in Ukraine.

Hubbard is from Big Rapids, Michigan; a retired schoolteacher who previously served in the US Air Force and National Guard, he taught English in Japan for several years before moving to Ukraine. Russian officials said Hubbard was arrested in April 2022 after Russian forces captured the eastern Ukrainian city of Izium. The US government declared his detention unlawful in January 2025 and continued diplomatic efforts to secure his release.

Hubbard's release comes as diplomatic contacts between the United States and Russia continue over prospects of negotiations to end the war in Ukraine, and is being viewed as a significant development.
