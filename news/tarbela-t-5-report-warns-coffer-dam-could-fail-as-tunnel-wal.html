Tarbela T-5: Report warns coffer dam could fail as tunnel walls keep eroding

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Work on the Tarbela Dam's T-5 hydropower project was suspended indefinitely after a protective bund was breached and a large volume of water flooded Tunnel Five. All Pakistani and foreign workers were safely evacuated, and one worker who was swept into the water was rescued. A new report by the Haripur deputy commissioner says the wall towards the dam and the upper part of the tunnel are continuously eroding.

The report warns that the coffer dam built at the intake area could fail completely. If that happens, water from the dam could flow directly into the tunnel. The report has raised fresh concern about the safety of the project site.
