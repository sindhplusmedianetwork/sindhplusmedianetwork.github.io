G7 ministers to discuss recent cyber attacks, Italy says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani told a briefing at his ministry that his own personal data had been published on the dark web by what he said were pro-Russian hackers, adding that the move was linked to Italy's support for Ukraine. He noted there had been hybrid warfare incidents across Europe and also in South America in recent days, and said the G7 countries would discuss possible joint initiatives to counter them. Tajani stopped short of directly blaming the Russian government for the cyber attacks.

'I said the hackers are pro-Russian. I did not say that the Russian government is responsible. If I had proof of Russian government involvement, I would say so,' he said.
