Technical Bids Opened for Demolishing Gul Plaza in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Technical bids for demolishing Gul Plaza in Karachi have been opened. Seventeen companies have submitted applications for demolishing Gul Plaza, scrutiny of the applications is under way, and the names of successful companies will come on November 24. According to SBCA's Farhan Qaiser, financial bids for the demolition contract will also be opened on November 24 and the contract will be awarded to an eligible company.

The work of demolishing Gul Plaza will have to begin within 7 days of receiving the contract, and the building will have to be demolished within 90 days. Within 90 days the building must be demolished and the debris fully cleared, and levelling the Gul Plaza site after clearing the debris will also be the contractor's responsibility.
