Four Bogies of Freight Train Derail Near Lodhran, Track Closed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four bogies of a Yusufwala freight coal train derailed near Rukanpur in Lodhran district. The derailment shut the main up track, which could not be restored even after seven hours. A railway spokesman said relief trains had reached the site and restoration work was under way.

The closure of the track disrupted rail traffic on the route. Tezgam, Allama Iqbal Express and several other trains were delayed as a result. Railway authorities said the cause of the derailment was being investigated.

No casualties were reported in the incident.
