Export Facilitation Scheme Review Committee hears exporters' concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior Vice President of the Sialkot Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Asad Naseer Malik, on Friday participated in an online meeting of the Committee to Review the Export Facilitation Scheme. The meeting was chaired by Minister of State for Finance and Railways Bilal Azhar Kiani. It reviewed various aspects of the scheme and discussed implementation issues.

Business representatives were given an opportunity to highlight their concerns during the meeting. They were also able to share feedback and propose measures to improve the scheme. The committee session formed part of the ongoing review of the Export Facilitation Scheme.
