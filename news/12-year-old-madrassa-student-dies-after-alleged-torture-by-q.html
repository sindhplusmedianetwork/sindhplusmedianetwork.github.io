12-Year-Old Madrassa Student Dies After Alleged Torture by Qari in Bajaur; Suspect Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 12-year-old student of a madrassa in the Qazafi Bandagai area of Bajaur has died after allegedly being subjected to torture by the qari. Police took timely action, arresting the suspect as he attempted to flee, and registered a case against him. Further investigation and legal proceedings are underway.

District Police Officer Bajaur Khan Zeb Mohmand described the innocent child's death as a deeply tragic incident. He said the police stood with the grieving family in this hour of sorrow and would fulfil all legal requirements to deliver justice to the family. The DPO added that the case would be investigated strictly on merit and that no one would be considered above the law.
