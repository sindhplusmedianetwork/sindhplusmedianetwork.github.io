KSE-100 Index Slips 454 Points Amid Weak Investor Sentiment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange ended Monday's session lower as investors struggled to find direction amid subdued sentiment. The benchmark KSE-100 Index shed 453.63 points, or 0.27 per cent, to close at 165,966.09. During the session, the index touched an intraday high of 166,698.46 before slipping to a low of 165,918.35.

Selling pressure was seen in key sectors including automobile assemblers, cement, commercial banks and oil and gas exploration companies. Trading volume declined to 370.9 million shares worth Rs19.56 billion. Of the 499 traded stocks, 302 closed in the red.

Analysts said cautious trading reflected uncertainty over near-term market direction.
