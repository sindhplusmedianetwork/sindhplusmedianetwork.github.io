Pakistani drama 'Dar-e-Nijat' draws crowds at special Delhi screening

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani drama 'Dar-e-Nijat' and Shehryar Munawar's character Abubakar are winning fans across the border. A cafe in New Delhi hosted a special screening of the drama's 33rd episode, which drew hundreds of interested viewers. Only 45 people were allowed inside, with tickets priced at 899 Indian rupees, and the audience was made up of 60 percent men and 40 percent women.

Fans also got the chance to speak with director Saqib Khan on a video call for about 40 minutes. Organisers say a special screening of the final episode is now being considered after the overwhelming response. The event shows how Pakistani dramas continue to find a large and enthusiastic audience in India.
