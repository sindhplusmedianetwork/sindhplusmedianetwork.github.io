Madinah Braces for Flash Floods as Saudi Weather Centre Warns of Widespread Rain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's National Center for Meteorology has warned of moderate to heavy thunderstorms, flash floods, hail and strong winds across several regions of the Kingdom on Saturday. The centre said the weather system could trigger flash floods and bring hail and winds that stir up dust and sand, reducing visibility to near zero in some areas. The forecast covers parts of the Makkah, Madinah, Hail, Qassim and Northern Borders regions, as well as northern parts of the Eastern Province, with similar conditions expected in Jazan, Asir and Al-Baha.

In a separate warning, the centre forecast heavy rainfall across Madinah region from 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. on Saturday, accompanied by strong winds, hail, thunderstorms and flash floods.

The Saudi Red Crescent Authority in Madinah has deployed 70 ambulance teams and support vehicles in anticipation of the weather, including volunteer teams in the central area of the city and around the Prophet's Mosque. The centre said the rainy spell was expected to expand and intensify across much of the country on Monday and Tuesday, and urged residents to follow official forecasts and Civil Defense guidelines.
