Rs202 million bank robbery in Balochistan: university students ordered security forces' uniforms online

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Lasbela, Balochistan, say university students planned and carried out a bank robbery in Uthal on 28 February 2025, looting Rs 202 million. The suspects were dressed in security forces' uniforms and posed as investigators during the robbery. Five of the suspects are current or former students of Lasbela University of Marine Sciences, police said.

The uniforms were ordered online in December 2024, and the suspects burned the getaway vehicle to destroy evidence. Three suspects have been arrested after investigators traced the crime through the main suspect's bank account, which showed transactions of Rs 170 million over 18 months. The SHO who chased the suspects lost an eye in an exchange of fire.

Police are continuing their investigation into the case.
