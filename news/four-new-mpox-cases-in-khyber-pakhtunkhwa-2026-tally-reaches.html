Four New Mpox Cases in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; 2026 Tally Reaches 18

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa health department has reported four new mpox cases in the past 72 hours, taking the province's active cases to four and its total confirmed cases in 2026 to 18. Four patients are currently under treatment in hospitals, while 13 of the 18 confirmed patients have recovered. One death has been reported in the province, and no new death was reported in the past 24 hours.

The first KP case of 2026 was reported on 9 January 2026, according to the health department.
