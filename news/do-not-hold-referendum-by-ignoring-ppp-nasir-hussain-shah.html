Do Not Hold Referendum by Ignoring PPP: Nasir Hussain Shah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Local Government Minister Nasir Hussain Shah said they are not afraid of a referendum. He said if a referendum is held by bypassing the Pakistan Peoples Party, they will stand with the people of Sindh. Shah was speaking to journalists in Hyderabad.

He said Sindh is one and will remain one. He questioned where businessmen had brought the story of provinces from. He said all stakeholders should sit together on the new provinces issue to decide what would lead to improvement.
