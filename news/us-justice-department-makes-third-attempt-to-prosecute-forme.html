US Justice Department Makes Third Attempt to Prosecute Former FBI Chief Comey

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 2 min read

The United States Justice Department is attempting to prosecute former FBI Director James Comey for a third time, with the latest case accusing him of sharing sensitive national security information in 2016, according to court documents made public on Monday. The case was revealed in a court document known as a criminal information, which the Justice Department said it filed to stop the 10-year statute of limitations on the offence from expiring earlier this month. The document filed in Washington federal court charges Comey with transmitting and conspiring to transmit national defence information, alleging he shared classified information with a reporter from the New York Times in October 2016.

Comey said in a video on Instagram that they are back again and referenced the Justice Department, but did not provide further details, adding that he is still innocent, while his lawyers have already asked a judge to dismiss the case, arguing it is legally improper. President Donald Trump told reporters at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland that Comey is a dirty cop, adding that he does not know much about it. The new case adds to the legal fights facing Comey, whom the Justice Department has already charged twice, accusing him of lying to Congress about media leaks and threatening Trump in a social media post, while a judge dismissed the false statements case and the threats case remains pending.

Comey's lawyer, Patrick Fitzgerald, said in a statement that the document purports to charge Comey with crimes that he did not commit. The criminal information was filed on October 5, but was unsealed on Monday at the request of Comey's lawyers, and a Justice Department spokesperson described the manoeuvre to halt the statute of limitations as a widely recognised practice in federal court, adding that it is an ongoing criminal investigation and they will not comment further.
