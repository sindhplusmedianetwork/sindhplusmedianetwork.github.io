Governor Kundi Calls for Improved Rail Services in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has called for improving the railway system in the province and emphasised reviving the Landikotal Tourist Train to promote tourism and boost economic activity. He made the remarks during a meeting with Minister for Railways Hanif Abbasi in Islamabad. They discussed making the railway network in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa more efficient, modern and responsive to public needs.

The meeting reviewed proposals to upgrade railway facilities in the province. It also reviewed proposals to improve passenger services and the overall quality of railway operations.
