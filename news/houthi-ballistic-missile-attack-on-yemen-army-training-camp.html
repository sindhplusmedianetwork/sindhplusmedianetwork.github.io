Houthi Ballistic-Missile Attack on Yemen Army Training Camp Kills 18

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 18 people were killed on Monday when the Iran-aligned Houthi movement fired ballistic missiles at a Yemeni government military training camp in Shabwa province. Government and medical sources told Reuters that the attack targeted soldiers during a morning training session and was carried out using three ballistic missiles. Fighting between the Saudi-led coalition backing Yemen's internationally recognised government and the Houthis, who control the capital Sanaa and much of the north, has escalated in recent weeks.

The Yemen strike came amid a wider regional conflict that began with US and Israeli strikes on Iran in late February. Earlier on Monday, several US embassies across the Middle East issued identical security alerts for American citizens in 12 countries, citing potential threats from Iran or the Houthis. Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov was also due to arrive in Tehran on his first visit since the outbreak of the war, as Moscow seeks a mediating role.

In a separate development, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said an oil tanker caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting a mine.
