Fact Check: Viral 'Siege of Peshawar Governor House' Video Is Actually From Nepal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A viral video claiming that Imran Khan's supporters besieged the Peshawar Governor House after governor rule and emergency were imposed in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has been proven false. Fact-checking has established that the footage is from Nepal and relates to the protests of September 2025. A reverse image search traced the video to a YouTube post dated September 9, 2025, showing protesters outside Kathmandu's Birendra International Convention Centre.

The spokesperson for the KP Governor House confirmed that the building seen in the video is not Peshawar's Governor House. The news agency AFP also debunked the video on October 14, 2026.
