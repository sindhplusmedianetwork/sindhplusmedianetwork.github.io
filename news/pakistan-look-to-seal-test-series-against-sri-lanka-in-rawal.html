Pakistan Look to Seal Test Series Against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi Decider

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan will look to seal the two-match Test series when they take on Sri Lanka in the second and final Test at the Pindi Stadium in Rawalpindi on Tuesday. The first Test at the same venue ended in a draw, leaving the series to be decided in this match. Bowling coach Shaun Tait said he had seen some grass on the pitch and expected it to assist the pace bowlers, while defending his attack after the drawn opener.

Pakistan handed Test debuts to four players in the first match, and debutant pacer Saqib Khan took seven wickets for 130 runs in Sri Lanka's first innings. The home side will hope their bowlers can make better use of the conditions this time. Sri Lanka, meanwhile, will aim to avoid defeat and share the series.
