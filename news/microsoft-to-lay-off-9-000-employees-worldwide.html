Microsoft to Lay Off 9,000 Employees Worldwide

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

American technology company Microsoft has announced it will lay off around 9,000 employees worldwide, nearly four percent of its global workforce. The company confirmed the job cuts as part of a restructuring of its organisational structure. According to AFP, the biggest share of the reductions will fall in the Xbox gaming division.

More than 3,200 jobs in the gaming unit will be affected, and four game studios will also be impacted. The company said the move reflects changing global business conditions and the need to reshape its operations. The announcement marks one of the largest rounds of layoffs in the technology sector this year.

Employees across multiple regions are expected to be notified in the coming days.
