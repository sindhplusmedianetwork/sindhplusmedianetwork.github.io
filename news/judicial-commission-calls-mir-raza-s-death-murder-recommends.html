Judicial Commission Calls Mir Raza's Death Murder, Recommends JIT

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The single-member Judicial Commission of the Sindh High Court, headed by Justice Omar Sial, has concluded that the death of 25-year-old Mir Raza Ali, owner of the dessert shop Wafflix whose body was found in Gulistan-e-Jauhar, was murder and not suicide. The commission termed the police investigation an 'epic failure'. It recommended the formation of a joint investigation team led by DIG Irfan Baloch, with representatives from the ISI, Intelligence Bureau, Pakistan Rangers (Sindh) and the NCCIA.

It also called for an FIA-led inquiry into the conduct of the police officials who handled the case. The commission's report was submitted to the chief minister on October 15.
