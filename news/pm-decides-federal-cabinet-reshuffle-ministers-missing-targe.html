PM decides federal cabinet reshuffle, ministers missing targets may be removed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has decided to reshuffle the federal cabinet, under which ministers who failed to achieve their assigned targets are likely to be removed from the cabinet. According to the report, the prime minister has taken a principled decision to add new faces to the cabinet to improve government performance and to hand responsibilities to other members in place of those who did not deliver satisfactory results. A private TV channel claimed, citing sources, that the prime minister had earlier assigned different targets to all federal ministers and had also sought performance reports from the relevant institutions to review the completion of those targets.

In light of these reports, it has been decided that members who failed to deliver the required results in their sectors will no longer remain part of the cabinet, while ministers of state who showed good performance are also being considered for promotion to federal minister. Sources said the prime minister has taken PML-N leader Nawaz Sharif into confidence on the proposed cabinet changes and reshuffle. According to political circles, formal announcements and notifications regarding the cabinet changes are likely to be issued in the coming days once the consultation process is complete.
