Zardari Tells IMF Delegation Pakistan Committed to Fiscal Reforms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari on Tuesday reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to fiscal sustainability, energy sector reforms, privatisation and social protection, saying the government was pursuing inclusive and green economic growth. He was speaking in a meeting with International Monetary Fund Middle East and Central Asia Department Director Jihad Azour at Aiwan-e-Sadr in Islamabad, according to a press release issued by the President's Secretariat. The IMF delegation also included mission chief for Pakistan Iva Petrova and resident representative Mahir Binici.

The president cited an increase in the size of the economy to $452.1 billion in the fiscal year 2025-26, while real GDP growth accelerated to 3.7 per cent from 3.2 per cent in the preceding fiscal year. He highlighted improvements in fiscal management, saying the fiscal deficit had declined to 2.6 per cent of GDP in FY2025-26 and the country had recorded a primary surplus for the third consecutive year. Zardari appreciated the IMF's continued engagement with Pakistan under the Extended Fund Facility and the Resilience and Sustainability Facility, noting the successful conclusion of the fourth review of the EFF and the third review of the RSF along with the staff-level agreement.

Pakistan remains engaged with the IMF under a $7 billion EFF and a $1.4 billion RSF; on board approval, Pakistan will have access to about $1 billion under the EFF and about $210 million under the RSF. Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, Senator Sherry Rehman, Senator Saleem Mandviwalla and former finance minister Naveed Qamar also attended the meeting. Pakistan will chair the Boards of Governors of the IMF and the World Bank Group in 2027.
