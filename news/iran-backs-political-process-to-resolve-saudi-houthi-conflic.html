Iran Backs 'Political Process' to Resolve Saudi-Houthi Conflict, Araghchi Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said on Saturday that Tehran supports an 'inclusive and comprehensive political process as the only practical and sustainable way to resolve the issue' between Saudi Arabia and Yemen's Houthi rebels. Speaking at the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation meeting in Baghdad, he repeated Iran's offer to mediate between the two sides. The offer came after Deputy Foreign Minister Saeed Khatibzadeh said Riyadh had not responded to Tehran's earlier mediation overture.

On Friday, Iran denied any role in Houthi attacks, after Saudi Arabia accused Tehran of encouraging the group's escalation. Araghchi's remarks underline Iran's public push for a negotiated settlement even as regional tensions remain high.
