The Simple Sitting Test That Predicts Your Risk of Dying

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Scientists at the Cleveland Clinic in Ohio studied around 2,400 adults in 1999 to find a simple physical test linked to longevity. Over a six-year review period, they examined which participants faced a risk of death nearly four times higher than others. The test in question measures the ability to sit down on the floor and rise again without support.

Researchers found that people who struggled most with the movement had significantly higher mortality risk. The findings suggest that basic strength, balance and flexibility reflect overall health. Experts caution that the test is an indicator, not a diagnosis, and recommend regular physical activity.
