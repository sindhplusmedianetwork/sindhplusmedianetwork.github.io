India-West Indies T20I Series Smashes Records With 98 Sixes in 918 Balls

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India and West Indies have turned their ongoing T20I series into the fastest-scoring bilateral contest in men's T20 international history, clearing 98 sixes in just 918 balls across the first four matches. In Ranchi, the West Indies produced the chase of the series, hunting down India's 249 to reach 252 for four in 18.3 overs with six wickets in hand. India hit back in Indore, posting 223 before restricting the visitors to 209, but the West Indies answered in Hyderabad with another batting onslaught, captain Shai Hope carrying his bat for an unbeaten 102 off 50 balls as his side piled up 247 for three.

India were bowled out for 223 in reply, Keemo Paul taking five wickets, as the West Indies squared the series at 2-2. The numbers are extraordinary: the West Indies have scored 838 runs with 57 sixes in the series to India's 823 with 41, and Roston Chase's six wickets in four matches mark him as the only bowler to keep his economy rate in check. With the series level, attention now turns to Bengaluru, where a decisive fifth T20I will settle one of the most remarkable boundary festivals the format has ever seen.
