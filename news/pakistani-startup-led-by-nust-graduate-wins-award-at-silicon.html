Pakistani Startup Led by NUST Graduate Wins Award at Silicon Valley Summit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani startup RLabs has won the best startup award at the Silicon Valley November Summit in California. The government hailed the achievement as showcasing the growing strength of the country's startup ecosystem. RLabs is incubated at the National Incubation Centre for Aerospace Technologies.

The startup is led by Harris Ahmed, a 2018 graduate of the National University of Sciences and Technology. It won the GOAL Startup Award at the summit, which was held from November 2 to 5. IT Minister Shaza Fatima Khawaja called the win a moment of immense pride for the nation.
