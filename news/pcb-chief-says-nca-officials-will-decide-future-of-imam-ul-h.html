PCB chief says NCA officials will decide future of Imam-ul-Haq and Mohammad Rizwan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Cricket Board chairman Mohsin Naqvi has said that the future of Imam-ul-Haq and Mohammad Rizwan will be decided by the National Cricket Academy officials, who will summon both players within a few days. Speaking to the media during a visit to the National Cricket Academy in Lahore, the PCB chairman said he was satisfied with all the decisions taken for the betterment of cricket, that his team was doing good work, that he trusted them, and that the strategy which had been made would continue. He said he was not making any change in the selection committee and would accept whatever decisions were taken.

He made it clear that the credit for designing the academy's master plan goes to Aqib Javed, who prepared it together with the designing team. The PCB chairman said the deadline for upgrading the academy is January 31 and it will be a complete state-of-the-art academy where every kind of training facility will be available. He said the most modern machinery in the world was being imported for a modern biomechanics laboratory, which would provide scientific data on the technique, movement and flaws of not only national players but also under-15, under-17 and under-19 players so that corrections could be made in time.

He said special training camps of three to six months were being held for young players at the National Cricket Academy, whose purpose was not only fitness but also to remove the young players' basic technical flaws so that when they joined the national squad they could perform directly instead of learning the basics. He added that work on the central contracts was under way and an announcement would be made soon, and he endorsed the policy of continuing to give young players opportunities at every level, including Test matches.
