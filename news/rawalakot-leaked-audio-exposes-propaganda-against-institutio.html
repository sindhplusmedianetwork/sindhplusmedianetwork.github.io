Rawalakot: leaked audio exposes propaganda against institutions over girl's death

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A leaked audio has exposed a plot to build a false narrative against state institutions over the death of a girl in Rawalakot. In the audio, miscreants allegedly admit that the firing on girls was carried out by their own armed workers, while planning to blame security forces for the incident. The audio also allegedly reveals a conspiracy to pressure the bereaved family of Javeria Saqib into giving a dictated statement.

It also allegedly reveals plans to defame institutions through false videos and posts on social media. The leak has exposed what Express described as a plot by anti-peace elements in Azad Kashmir. The audio is being cited as evidence of the propaganda campaign.
