Pakistan Stock Exchange ends trading week on a positive note

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange ended the trading week on a positive note, with the benchmark KSE-100 index closing at 173,886. The index gained 7,477 points over the week, reflecting sustained buying interest from investors. During the week, the index moved within a wide 9,614-point band, touching a high of 176,604 and a low of 166,989.

Trading activity remained strong, as 4.28 billion shares changed hands for a total value of Rs142 billion. Market capitalisation also increased, rising by Rs520 billion to reach Rs18,909 billion by the end of the week. The week's performance pointed to an overall firm trend in the market, with the index holding well above its weekly low.
