De Villiers Says Ball-Tampering Began in First Durban Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former South African captain AB de Villiers has claimed that ball-tampering began in the first Test in Durban during Australia's 2018 tour of South Africa. Speaking on his podcast, he said the ball was reversing unusually early at Kingsmead. He said Australia's players, including the bowlers, knew it was not happening naturally.

De Villiers also criticised Cricket Australia's response at the time. He said the organisation left the players on their own. He further said the punishments handed out were extremely inappropriate.
