Autopsy reveals multiple injuries on teenage domestic worker found dead at FIA official's residence

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The autopsy report of a 13-year-old domestic worker, who was found dead at the official residence of a senior Federal Investigation Agency official in Islamabad on Sunday night, suggests she had been subjected to repeated physical assaults. The girl was identified as Fatima Asghar, and the report stated she died from repeated physical assaults that caused grievous injuries and severe inflammation, leading to a blood clot blocking a blood vessel and the cessation of heart and breathing functions. The autopsy documented 20 injuries on the girl's body, all suspected to have been caused by physical assault, including swelling and bleeding in the head, a bald patch on the scalp, scars on the cheek, bruises on the hands and arms, and an old, unhealed burn mark on the back.

The report said all injuries were inflicted before death and were sufficient to cause death in the ordinary course of life. The suspects are the FIA official's dual-national wife and a religious teacher who worked as a servant at the residence, and both were arrested on Sunday after a murder case was registered at Shams Colony police station. Fatima's father, a resident of Rahim Yar Khan, said his daughter was a seminary student and that he was told in the morning that she had suddenly fallen ill and died at a hospital.

First Lady Bibi Asifa Bhutto Zardari expressed sorrow over the death, saying no child should be subjected to violence, abuse or exploitation, and called for a thorough, impartial and transparent investigation.
