Dacoity Attempt Foiled in Karachi's Ibrahim Haideri; One Suspect Caught by Citizens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An attempted dacoity at a shop in Karachi's Ibrahim Haideri area was foiled when citizens caught one of the suspects. Three suspects had entered a shop in Charan Mohalla, Ibrahim Haideri, but citizens intervened and caught one of them while the other two fled the scene. Angry citizens beat the arrested suspect before handing him over to police.

Police identified the arrested suspect as Shahzad Yaseen. Police recovered a weapon and two mobile phones from him. His previous criminal record is being checked, and efforts are under way to arrest his accomplices.
