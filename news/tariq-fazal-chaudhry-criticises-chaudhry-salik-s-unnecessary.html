Tariq Fazal Chaudhry criticises Chaudhry Salik's 'unnecessary and inappropriate' statement

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PML-N leader Tariq Fazal Chaudhry said the PML-Q is an allied party and Chaudhry Shujaat practised politics with great grace. He said that had Chaudhry Salik followed his father's style of politics with mutual respect, matters would not have reached this point. Speaking to a private TV channel, he said the government only reacted to Chaudhry Salik's statement and did not initiate the exchange of statements.

He called Salik's remarks unnecessary and inappropriate. He added that if Salik had any grievance it could have been discussed in private. He said that a government working sincerely for better governance and against corruption will respond when it is accused.
