Sixth World Hajj Conference and Exhibition Opens in Jeddah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sixth World Hajj Conference and Exhibition 2026 has opened in Jeddah under the patronage of King Salman bin Abdulaziz, hosted by Hajj and Umrah Minister Dr Tawfiq bin Fawzan Al-Rabiah. The opening ceremony was held at the Jeddah Superdome, with Hajj ministers, senior government officials, heads of Hajj missions and representatives of Hajj service providers from more than 150 countries in attendance. Pakistan was represented by Federal Minister for Religious Affairs Sardar Muhammad Yousuf, accompanied by Religious Affairs Secretary Abrar Ahmed Mirza, Joint Secretary Syed Waheed Shah and other members of the Pakistani delegation.

More than 520 government, private and non-profit institutions are taking part in the exhibition, presenting modern projects and services covering Hajj arrangements, technology, transport, accommodation, food, health, security and facilities for pilgrims. During the conference, Hajj ministers and officials from different countries will hold meetings on arrangements for Hajj 1448 Hijri in 2027, better facilities for pilgrims and stronger international cooperation. The Pakistani delegation aims to secure better facilities for Pakistani pilgrims, timely completion of arrangements and closer coordination with the Saudi authorities for the next Hajj season.

The exhibition runs from November 8 to 11 and aims to bring Hajj institutions from around the world onto one platform to make the system of serving pilgrims more modern, effective and easy.
