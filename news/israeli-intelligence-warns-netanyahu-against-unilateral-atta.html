Israeli intelligence warns Netanyahu against unilateral attack on Iran

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli military intelligence warned Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu that a unilateral attack on Iran could trigger war on several fronts, including Lebanon, Syria and Gaza. According to Israeli TV Channel 12, senior military intelligence officials briefed Netanyahu and senior defence officials in a closed meeting last week. They assessed that an attack would not remain a limited war between the two countries.

The warning differed from an alleged assessment by Netanyahu and Mossad chief Roman Gofman that a military operation against Iran could be completed in three to five days. Opposition leader Avigdor Lieberman also opposed an attack on Iran, fearing Netanyahu could decide on unilateral military action without US support and permission, while the Israeli army declined to comment to Channel 12 on the closed meeting.
