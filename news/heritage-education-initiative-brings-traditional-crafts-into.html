Heritage Education Initiative brings traditional crafts into classrooms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training, in collaboration with the National Heritage and Culture Division, has launched the Heritage Education Initiative in Federal Directorate of Education schools to promote cultural awareness and connect students with Pakistan's heritage. The programme aims to integrate heritage-based learning into the experience of students in Classes VI to VIII through practical engagement, creative exploration and experiential learning. In the pilot phase, 10 public schools will each set up a Heritage Club of 20 to 30 students.

The initiative will not add new subjects, examinations or graded assignments; instead, students will explore cultural traditions through hands-on activities, research, observation and creative expression. It will focus on six traditional and contemporary craft areas: truck art, patchwork, jewellery making, clay pottery, rabab and digital art. The six-month pilot follows a structured journey of discovery, experience, creation and showcase, and will conclude with a student-curated exhibition of their work.
