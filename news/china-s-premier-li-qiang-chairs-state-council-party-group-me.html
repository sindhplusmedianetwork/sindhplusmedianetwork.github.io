China's Premier Li Qiang chairs State Council party group meeting in Beijing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A meeting of the leading group of party members of China's State Council was held in Beijing under the chairmanship of Premier Li Qiang, who also serves as secretary of the group. According to Xinhua, the meeting reviewed and discussed plans to implement the guiding principles of the fifth plenary session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China. The meeting noted that the resolution adopted at the plenary session on strict and full self-governance of the party analyzes the challenges and responsibilities facing the party in the current and coming period, and sets out overall requirements and key priorities.

It was stressed that the leading group and all departments of the State Council must make studying and implementing the plenary session's guiding principles a key political priority for the present and coming period. The meeting also emphasised that the requirements of strict and full self-governance must be applied to every sector and every stage of government work, and that the State Council and its departments will remain steadfast in implementing the decisions and plans of the CPC Central Committee.
