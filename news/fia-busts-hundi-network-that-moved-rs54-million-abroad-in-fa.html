FIA Busts Hundi Network That Moved Rs54 Million Abroad in Faisalabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has busted a network in Faisalabad that moved Rs54 million abroad through hundi/hawala. A case has been registered against four people. Those named in the case include a private bank manager.

The main suspect was arrested from Canal Road, and his mobile phone and vehicle were seized. Officials said messages found on the suspect's phone showed bank transactions worth hundreds of thousands of rupees during October.
