Bilawal asks Achakzai to bring opposition back into parliamentary committees

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari addressed the National Assembly on Thursday and appealed to Leader of the Opposition Mahmood Khan Achakzai to use the authority given to him by PTI founder Imran Khan to bring opposition lawmakers back into key parliamentary committees, especially the one on electoral reforms. He argued that the absence of the opposition from committees and official briefings would weaken any consensus on electoral reforms, counterterrorism, defence and economic matters. Bilawal pointed out that the Assembly had already passed a resolution for an Electoral Reforms Committee before the opposition withdrew from the committees last August, and urged Achakzai to nominate opposition members and send their names to the speaker's office.

He said opposition participation was the way to address complaints of election rigging in the next general and local government elections. The PPP chairman also called for the finance, foreign, interior and defence ministers to give Parliament a detailed briefing on the Iran war, the disruption of maritime trade routes like the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea, and Pakistan's recent defence agreements. In a related development, a PPP delegation comprising Raja Pervez Ashraf and Syed Naveed Qamar met Achakzai in the National Assembly and formally invited him to join the parliamentary committee on electoral reforms, with Senate Opposition Leader Raja Nasir Abbas also present at the meeting.
