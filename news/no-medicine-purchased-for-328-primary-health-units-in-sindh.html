No medicine purchased for 328 primary health units in Sindh this year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

No medicine was purchased for 328 primary health care units across Sindh during the current fiscal year, official disclosures have revealed. The government's budget for medicine purchases and repairs at these centres has also been stopped. The Sindh government had decided to hand over all such units to the People's Primary Healthcare Initiative from July 1.

The PPHI says it has taken over 426 units, while 328 units, including 33 in Karachi, are still awaiting transfer. No budget was released to district health officers for the remaining centres. The provincial health department did not respond to queries on the matter.
