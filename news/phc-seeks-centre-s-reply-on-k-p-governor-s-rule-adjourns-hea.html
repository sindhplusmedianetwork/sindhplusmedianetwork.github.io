PHC Seeks Centre's Reply on K-P Governor's Rule, Adjourns Hearing to Nov 10

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Peshawar High Court on Tuesday issued notices to the federal government, the attorney general, the advocate general and other authorities on petitions challenging the imposition of governor's rule and emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The measures were imposed on October 11. A bench comprising Justice Ijaz Anwar and Justice Muhammad Faheem Wali heard petitions filed by PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan and NDM leader Mohsin Dawar.

Counsel argued that the measures were unconstitutional and lacked the required approval of the provincial assembly, adding that Chief Minister Sohail Afridi and provincial assembly speaker Babar Saleem Swati were missing. Gohar alleged that 12 PTI MNAs and 14 MPAs had been picked up. The court adjourned the hearing to November 10.
