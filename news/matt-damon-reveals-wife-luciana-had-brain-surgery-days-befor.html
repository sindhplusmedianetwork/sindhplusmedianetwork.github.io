Matt Damon Reveals Wife Luciana Had Brain Surgery Days Before 2024 Oscars

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Matt Damon has revealed that his wife, Luciana Barroso, suffered a brain aneurysm and underwent brain surgery just one week before the 2024 Academy Awards. Speaking in a GQ Men of the Year cover interview published on Thursday, November 5, the actor said his wife is totally fine now, but described the episode as a frightening experience for the family. The couple missed the Oscars ceremony itself, where Damon's film Oppenheimer won seven awards including Best Picture, but Barroso surprised him by getting dressed and joining him at an after-party on the first night she felt well enough to go out.

Damon called her decision pretty heroic, saying she wanted to be there because he had missed the 2007 Oscars, when The Departed won Best Picture, while he was filming The Bourne Ultimatum. Damon, 56, and Barroso, 50, have been married since 2005 and share four daughters. He also spoke in the interview about his new film The Odyssey, describing it as the biggest production he has worked on.
