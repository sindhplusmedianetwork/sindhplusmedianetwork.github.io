Section 144 imposed in Upper, Lower South Waziristan over security concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The district administrations of Lower and Upper South Waziristan imposed Section 144 restrictions for Saturday, restricting movement in several areas and closing markets and major routes. Lower South Waziristan Deputy Commissioner Musarrat Zaman and Upper South Waziristan Deputy Commissioner Irfan Ali issued separate notifications on Friday evening. In Lower South Waziristan, Section 144 was enforced in Wana Bazaar, the Wana Bypass and several other localities, with movement prohibited on routes passing through Wana, Tiarza Gate, Karab Kot, Tanai, Azizabad Chowk and Dargai Bridge.

In Upper South Waziristan, the restrictions covered Servekai and Sararogha tehsils and adjoining areas, with markets and commercial centres ordered shut. The administrations said travel on affected routes would be allowed only in emergencies and with prior authorisation from the police or security forces. Motorists were directed to stop their vehicles at least 50 metres away upon seeing security forces.

Special provision was made for polio workers and vaccination teams, who may move through the areas after establishing their identities.
