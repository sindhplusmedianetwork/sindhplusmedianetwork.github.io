Shah Rukh Khan gets emotional recalling childhood without a home of his own

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood star Shah Rukh Khan became emotional at the Dubai launch of Shahrukhz Residences by Danube on November 11 as he spoke about his childhood. He said his longing for a home comes from not having one of his own while growing up. Wherever he travels, he still thinks of buying a house there, he told the audience.

His children tease him that he cannot own a home in every city, he added. Shah Rukh said he had no home of his own until he married Gauri Khan. He added that buying both his first house and Mannat in Mumbai stretched beyond his means.
