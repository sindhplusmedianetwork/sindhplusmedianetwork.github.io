Security forces kill 74 terrorists in operations in K-P and Balochistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces killed 74 terrorists in a series of intelligence-based operations in Balochistan and Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa during the past 48 hours, the Inter-Services Public Relations said in two separate statements on Friday. According to the military's media wing, 43 terrorists belonging to Fitna al-Khawarij, a term used for the outlawed Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan, were killed in Mir Ali and Orakzai areas of K-P, while 31 terrorists were eliminated in Balochistan. The ISPR said 35 terrorists were killed in Mir Ali and eight in Orakzai following fierce exchanges of fire, and a large cache of weapons, ammunition and explosives was recovered during sanitisation operations, including homemade explosives and improvised explosive devices.

The military said the recovery had thwarted the terrorists' plans to use the material in terrorist activities. Separately, the ISPR said 31 terrorists belonging to Fitna al-Khawarij and Fitna al-Hindustan were killed during an area sanitisation operation in the Quetta district, with the operation continuing in the Karam area since November 3. President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif praised the security forces for the successful operations, reaffirming the government's resolve to eliminate terrorism and thwart attempts to destabilise the country.
