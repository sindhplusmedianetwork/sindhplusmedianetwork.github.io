Government Decides to Phase Out State-Set Petroleum Prices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has decided to withdraw from the business of fixing petroleum product prices, sources said, with Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif directing that the government's authority to determine fuel prices be ended. Under the plan, oil marketing companies will be granted the power to set prices in a phased manner, and the final framework for deregulation will be presented to the prime minister. The petroleum minister has summoned a key meeting, while the chairman of the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority has been instructed to prepare an analysis and roadmap of the deregulation's impact.

Petroleum dealers have opposed handing pricing power to the marketing companies, warning that unchecked authority could lead to profiteering. Sources said the move is aimed at shielding the government from public criticism over rising fuel prices. Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik separately stated that the country's energy challenges can only end with a ceasefire between Iran and the United States.
