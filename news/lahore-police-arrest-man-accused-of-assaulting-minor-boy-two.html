Lahore police arrest man accused of assaulting minor boy; two held for assaulting woman in Kahna

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islam­pura police in Lahore arrested a man accused of assaulting a minor boy, while Kahna police arrested two men accused of assaulting a woman over a money dispute. According to police, the accused, Nadeem, lured the boy to his home in Islam­pura, where he allegedly assaulted him and then sent him home after threatening him. After receiving the report, Islam­pura police acted promptly and arrested Nadeem, registered a case against him and started an investigation.

SP City Arshad Hayat Kanjo said prompt action is being ensured on complaints of violence and sexual exploitation of children. In the second incident, in Kahna, a woman named Khadija went to the home of the accused, Aqeel, to recover money she had lent him, and was allegedly beaten by Aqeel along with his uncle Muzammil. SP Model Town Asad Ali said the woman and the accused worked together in a private company, and the accused have been handed over to the investigation wing for further inquiry.

He added that those who exploit women deserve no concession.
