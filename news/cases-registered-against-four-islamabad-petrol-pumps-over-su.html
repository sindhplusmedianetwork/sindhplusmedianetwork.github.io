Cases Registered Against Four Islamabad Petrol Pumps Over Suspicious Devices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad district administration has registered cases against four petrol pumps after suspicious electronic devices were found during inspections. The administration has also decided to cancel the no-objection certificates of the pumps involved. Officials inspected 43 fuel pumps in total during the drive.

They confirmed tampering in 42 nozzles at 14 pumps, which were sealed on the spot. Fuel measurement at 29 pumps was found correct. The action follows an inspection campaign to check fuel measurement and detect tampering at filling stations in the capital.
