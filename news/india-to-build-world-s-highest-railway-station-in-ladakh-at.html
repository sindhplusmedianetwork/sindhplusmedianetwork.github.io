India to build world's highest railway station in Ladakh at 5,256 metres

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India has finalised plans to build the world's highest railway station in Ladakh, despite China's opposition. The station will be built near Tanglang La at an altitude of about 5,256 metres above sea level. Once completed, it would break the record of China's Tanggula railway station and become the world's highest railway station.

The plan was reported from Lahore on November 6, 2026. The new station would stand higher than any other railway station in the world.
