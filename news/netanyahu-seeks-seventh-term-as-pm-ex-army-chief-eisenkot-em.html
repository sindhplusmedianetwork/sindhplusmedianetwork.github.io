Netanyahu Seeks Seventh Term as PM; Ex-Army Chief Eisenkot Emerges as Key Rival

Benjamin Netanyahu, Israel's longest-serving prime minister, is seeking power for a seventh time in parliamentary elections scheduled for 27 October. The veteran leader faces a crowded field of challengers and a deeply divided electorate. His most significant electoral rival is former army chief Gadi Eisenkot, who has emerged as a prominent opposition figure.

Eisenkot's military credentials and centrist appeal present a direct challenge to Netanyahu's security-focused campaign. Analysts say the election will test whether voters want continuity or change after years of political turbulence. Campaigning has focused on security, the economy and the country's direction.

The outcome could reshape Israel's political landscape for years to come.
