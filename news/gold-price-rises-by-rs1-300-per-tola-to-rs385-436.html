Gold price rises by Rs1,300 per tola to Rs385,436

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices rose in Pakistan on Tuesday, with the rate for one tola climbing by Rs1,300 to Rs385,436. The All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association said the price of 10 grams of 24-karat gold increased by Rs1,115 to Rs330,449, while 10 grams of 22-karat gold rose by Rs1,022 to Rs302,922. The association reported the figures for 24 November.

In the international market, gold also gained, rising by 13 dollars per ounce to 3,629 dollars. Silver, in contrast, became cheaper in the country, with one tola falling by Rs49 to Rs4,687 and 10 grams dropping by Rs41 to Rs4,018. The international silver price also fell, slipping by 48 cents to 42.09 dollars per ounce.
