'Sunrise on the Reaping' opens with $225 million worldwide

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lionsgate's Hunger Games prequel "Sunrise on the Reaping" earned an estimated $225 million in its opening weekend. The total includes $120 million from North America and $105 million from international markets. Directed by Francis Lawrence, the film explores the early life of Haymitch Abernathy, played by Joseph Zada.

The story is set 24 years before the original films. The strong debut makes it one of the biggest theatrical openings of the year. The prequel revisits the world of Panem through Haymitch's story, decades before the events of the original films.
