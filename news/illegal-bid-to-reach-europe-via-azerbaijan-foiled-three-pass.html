Illegal Bid to Reach Europe via Azerbaijan Foiled; Three Passengers Offloaded

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency's Immigration wing at Multan airport has foiled an illegal bid to reach Europe via Azerbaijan. Three passengers — Hafiz Asif, Tariq Bashir and Muhammad Ilyas — were offloaded from flydubai flight FZ-326, which was bound for Azerbaijan from Karachi, after their travel profiles appeared suspicious during immigration checks. Examination of their mobile phones revealed a plan to travel illegally from Azerbaijan to Serbia and onward to other European countries.

The men told investigators they had paid a Vehari-based travel agent Rs 600,000 for visas and tickets, with the remaining amount to be paid on arrival. All three passengers were handed over to the FIA's Anti-Human Trafficking Circle Multan for further legal proceedings. The FIA said the case would also be pursued against the agent involved.
