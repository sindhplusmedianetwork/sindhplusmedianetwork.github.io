ISPR releases 'Hum Shuhada Hum Ghazi Hain' anthem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) has released a special commemorative anthem, 'Hum Shuhada Hum Ghazi Hain', dedicated to the courage, resilience and sacrifices of the men and women of the Pakistan Police. The tribute honours both the martyrs who laid down their lives in the line of duty and the veterans who continue to serve. The anthem is performed by Head Constable Raja Azam Javed of the Punjab Police.

The full video of the anthem is available on the official ISPR YouTube channel. The release pays tribute to the police force's service and sacrifice for the country.
