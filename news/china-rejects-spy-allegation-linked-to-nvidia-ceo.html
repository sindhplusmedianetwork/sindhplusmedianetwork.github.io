China rejects spy allegation linked to Nvidia CEO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read

Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun on Monday rejected an allegation of Chinese spying following media reports that Nvidia had opened a probe into its CEO Jensen Huang. Huang had acknowledged an affair with a woman US officials say was working for Chinese intelligence. "The so-called Chinese spy allegation is groundless.

It is nothing but a smear against China," Guo said at a regular news briefing when asked to comment on the reports. The CEO's relationship with Alice Huang helped facilitate shipments of at least $7.5 billion worth of Nvidia chips to Chinese firms, The Information reported on Tuesday, citing two people who know her. Alice Huang and Jensen Huang are not related.
