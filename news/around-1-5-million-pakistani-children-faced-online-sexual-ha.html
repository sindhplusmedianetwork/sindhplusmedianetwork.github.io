Around 1.5 Million Pakistani Children Faced Online Sexual Harassment in a Year, Says UNICEF

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 1.5 million Pakistani children faced sexual harassment online over the course of one year, according to a UNICEF report cited by Geo News. The report revealed that 57 percent of the affected children told no one about their ordeal, citing shame, fear of family reaction and social stigma. It found that social media accounted for 44 percent of online harassment incidents, while in 85 percent of the reported cases the alleged offender was someone the child already knew.

UNICEF also flagged AI-generated fake images and videos of children as an emerging threat facing young internet users. The agency urged Pakistan to fix gaps in its legal system, strengthen investigations and provide safe, accessible channels for children to report abuse. Child protection advocates say the findings underline the urgent need for digital safety education in schools and homes.
