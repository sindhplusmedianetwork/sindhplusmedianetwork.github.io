Chinese Researchers Develop Aluminium Catalyst for Longer-Life Batteries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Researchers at the Chinese Academy of Sciences have developed a new aluminium-based catalyst that could extend the life of sodium-sulfur batteries. By replacing one oxygen atom with nitrogen in aluminium's atomic structure, the team created a novel Al-O3N structure. The new structure strengthened the aluminium-sulfur interaction and accelerated chemical reactions inside the battery.

In laboratory tests, the battery retained 1,196 milliamp-hours per gram of capacity after 100 charge-discharge cycles. It went on to complete 2,500 cycles with just 0.017 percent capacity loss per cycle. The advance could make sodium-sulfur batteries, a cheaper alternative to lithium-based cells, more viable for large-scale energy storage.
