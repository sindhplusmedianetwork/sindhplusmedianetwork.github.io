SpaceX Starship returns to landing pad from orbit in reusable rocket milestone

SpaceX's Starship spacecraft has returned from a 22-hour voyage in orbit and descended for the first time into the grasp of giant mechanical arms at the Texas tower it launched from a day earlier. The 171-foot-tall Starship fell out of thick clouds over SpaceX's Starbase launch site, reignited its engines to flip vertical and slowly descended into the tower's Mechazilla arms after orbiting Earth 14 times. The catch is essential to the company's effort to develop a fully reusable rocket.

The Super Heavy booster had failed to make a similar return shortly after liftoff, so SpaceX has yet to recover both stages in a single mission. The successful return of the upper stage marks a key engineering milestone for the Starship programme.
