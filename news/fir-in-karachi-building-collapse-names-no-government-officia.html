FIR in Karachi building collapse names no government official despite action against building authority

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The building in Karachi's Baloch Colony area collapsed on Wednesday, November 25, killing eight people and injuring 14 others, while around 30 labourers were reportedly inside when the structure gave way. Initial findings indicated that the building had been approved for a basement, ground floor and one additional storey, but six extra floors had allegedly been constructed. Police detained six people, including the contractor and the structural engineer, as suspects in the case.

The first information report, filed at the Tipu Sultan Police Station, invokes sections 34, 109, 119, 302, 337-H(1) and 427 of the Pakistan Penal Code, raising allegations relating to illegal construction, negligence, damage to property and criminal responsibility. The absence of any government official among the nominated accused raises questions about the scope of the investigation, particularly the role of regulatory authorities responsible for overseeing construction and enforcing building regulations. Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah held the building control authority responsible for the tragedy, and the provincial government removed its chief from his post and suspended 16 other officials, while also sealing 12 other under-construction buildings in the area.

Seven of the deceased labourers have been identified, while the identity of the eighth victim, whose body was recovered on Friday afternoon, could not immediately be established, and more than 10 people were still feared trapped beneath the rubble as rescue operations continued.
