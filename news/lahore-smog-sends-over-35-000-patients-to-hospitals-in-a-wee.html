Lahore smog sends over 35,000 patients to hospitals in a week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The worsening smog in Lahore has triggered a health emergency, with residents suffering from flu, cold, cough, fever and breathing difficulties. More than 35,000 patients visited six major government hospitals of the city over the past seven days. Mayo Hospital alone reported over 11,000 patients, while Services Hospital received more than 9,800 patients in the same period.

Health officials say the surge in patients is directly linked to the deteriorating air quality in the provincial capital. Environmental experts have recommended immediate measures to control the smog, including artificial rain, noting that conditions during October had been highly suitable for it. Citizens have been advised to take precautionary measures and limit outdoor activity.
