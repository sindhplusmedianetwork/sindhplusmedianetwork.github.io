Crude Oil Prices Fall Over 3% as US-Iran Talks Raise Hopes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Crude oil prices fell more than 3 percent on Monday as reports of talks between the United States and Iran raised hopes of easing supply disruptions. Brent crude dropped to about $110 a barrel, US crude slipped to around $101, and the UAE's Murban grade traded near $115. The fall came as markets reacted to signs of diplomacy after weeks of tension in the Middle East.

Abu Dhabi's state oil company warned that the closure of the Strait of Hormuz had cut global oil reserves by nearly one billion barrels. It also said diesel stocks had fallen to a 35-year low. Traders are watching the talks closely, as any progress could bring more oil back to global markets.

Further developments are expected in the coming days.
