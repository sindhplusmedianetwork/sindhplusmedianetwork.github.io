Salman Ali Agha Hopes Pakistan Top Order Turns Up in Tri-Series Final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan batter Salman Ali Agha has admitted that the team has not yet played its best cricket in the ODI tri-nation series, with flaws visible even in the matches they won. Speaking at a press conference in Lahore ahead of Saturday's final against Sri Lanka, he said the side had suffered batting collapses, underwhelming bowling spells and fielding lapses. He expressed hope that the top order, including Babar Azam and Fakhar Zaman, would deliver in the final.

Agha revealed that head coach Mike Hesson is demanding a 'perfect game' from the players. He also praised young cricketers Arafat Minhas, Hassan Nawaz and Saad Baig, describing them as the future of Pakistan cricket.
