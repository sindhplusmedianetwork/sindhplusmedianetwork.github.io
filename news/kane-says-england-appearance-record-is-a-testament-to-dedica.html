Kane says England appearance record is a 'testament to dedication'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

England captain Harry Kane said breaking the Three Lions' appearance record in Thursday's Nations League clash with Croatia will be a 'testament to all the dedication' he has put into his career. When Kane leads England out at Wembley to win his 126th cap, he will surpass the previous record held since 1990 by former goalkeeper Peter Shilton. It will be the latest landmark in an incredible 12 months for the Bayern Munich striker, who in October became the first English player in 25 years to win the Ballon d'Or.

The 33-year-old Kane is also England's record scorer with 91 goals. 'It's definitely up there as the biggest [achievement],' Kane told reporters on Wednesday. 'I think it showcases consistency at the highest level.

It's a testament to all the dedication and the time people don't see that keeps me fit and healthy. This is one of the biggest milestones in my career.' Since leaving Tottenham Hotspur to join Bayern in 2023, Kane has taken his game to another level, scoring 156 goals for the German club and finally winning the first silverware of his career, including Bundesliga titles in 2025 and 2026. However, England have not won a major tournament since winning the 1966 World Cup on home soil, and Kane suffered fresh frustration in July as he lost in a World Cup semi-final for the second time when Argentina came from behind to beat England.

Kane said he hoped there was enough left in his legs for another tilt at the World Cup in 2030, adding that reaching 100 goals for England was a milestone to aim for as soon as possible. England manager Thomas Tuchel, speaking earlier on Wednesday, was coy when asked if Kane was England's greatest footballer, saying he was 'up there' and that it would be a beautiful occasion to present the Ballon d'Or trophy to the fans on the same day Kane sets the all-time appearance record.
