Kasur murder mystery solved: victim's brother and brother-in-law arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Kasur say they have solved the murder of a woman whose body was found in a drain on October 21, arresting her own brother and brother-in-law. The victim, Mehak Bibi, had left home to see a marriage proposal and never returned, and her body was later recovered from the Bahadur Pura Rohi drain. Her brother, Syed Waqar Hussain, had registered a murder case at Sadar Kasur police station against unknown persons.

District Police Officer Aftab Ahmed Phulrwan told a press conference that investigators used geo-fencing and CCTV footage and traced a suspicious auto-rickshaw seen near the scene back towards the victim's home. He said contradictions in the complainant's statements led police to show him the footage, after which he disclosed the facts. Police say the investigation revealed that Waqar Hussain and his brother-in-law Basharat were involved in the killing.
