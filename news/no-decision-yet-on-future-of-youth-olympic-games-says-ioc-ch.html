No decision yet on future of Youth Olympic Games, says IOC chief Coventry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

International Olympic Committee president Kirsty Coventry has said no final decision has been taken on the future of the Youth Olympic Games. Speaking at the closing press conference of the Dakar 2026 Games in Diamniadio, Senegal, she said the IOC is reviewing the event's format and purpose in light of the Dakar 2026 experience and data. Coventry said the IOC must decide how best to keep young people connected to the Olympic movement, and no deadline has been set for the decision.

The Dakar 2026 Games were the first Olympic event held on the African continent. Many athletes called the Olympic Village their favourite part of Dakar 2026, where they had the chance to meet, eat together and learn about different cultures. The IOC will weigh the experience of Dakar before deciding the future shape of the Youth Games.
