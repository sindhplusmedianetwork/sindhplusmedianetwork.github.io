Mariyam Nafees Hits Back at Intrusive Questions About Having Another Child

Actor Mariyam Nafees hit back at repeated questions about having another child during an Instagram question-and-answer session on November 4, 2026. Responding to a follower who asked when the next baby would come, she answered sarcastically. Nafees said people's interference in celebrities' family planning was intrusive.

She asked what difference her having another child would make to anyone's life. Nafees made her debut in the drama serial Diyar-e-Dil in 2015. She married filmmaker Amaan Ahmed in March 2022 and welcomed her first child in March 2025.
