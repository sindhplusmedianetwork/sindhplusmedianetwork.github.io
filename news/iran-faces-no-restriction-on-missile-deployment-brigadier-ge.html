Iran Faces No Restriction on Missile Deployment: Brigadier General Ali Balali

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brigadier General Ali Balali, senior advisor to the commander of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) Aerospace Force, has said Iran faces no restriction on missile deployment. In an interview with Iranian media, he said that if ordered, missiles can also be used in new geographic areas. Brigadier General Ali Balali said the Iranian armed forces are fully ready to respond to any kind of aggressive action.

He said the forces' fingers are on the trigger and they are waiting for any miscalculation or folly by the enemy. Ali Balali said if any aggressive action is taken, the enemy will have to face missile attacks wherever it is present. He said Iran faces no restriction regarding the number of its missiles, their installation locations or capabilities, while opponents have faced numerous difficulties in confronting Iranian missiles.

He said Iran's enemies have had to develop defence systems like THAAD, worth billions of dollars, at heavy cost and over long periods to counter Iranian missiles. He also accused Iran's enemies of waging psychological warfare and said the countries of the region have understood the consequences and price of cooperating with the United States.
