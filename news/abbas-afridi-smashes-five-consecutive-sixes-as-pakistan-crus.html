Abbas Afridi Smashes Five Consecutive Sixes as Pakistan Crush India in Hong Kong Sixes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan have stormed into the next round of the Hong Kong Sixes with a commanding win over India, thanks to a breathtaking display of power-hitting from captain Abbas Afridi. Playing at the Mission Road Ground in Mong Kok in Group C, Pakistan won the toss and chose to bowl first. India posted 88 runs for five wickets in their six overs, with Shashank Singh contributing 40, while Indian captain Krunal Pandya was dismissed without scoring.

The toss itself made headlines after both captains did not exchange the customary handshake. Pakistan's chase turned into a one-man show when Pandya came on to bowl and Afridi launched five consecutive sixes off him, collecting 31 runs from the over. Pakistan reached the 89-run target in just 3.5 overs to seal victory by four wickets.

Afridi finished with an unbeaten-style blitz of 35 runs from only eight balls and was named player of the match for his stunning all-round show.
