Steve Smith Examined by Specialists in New York After Neck Injury

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Australian batter Steve Smith has been examined by specialist doctors in New York after suffering a neck injury. Smith picked up the injury during the second Test against South Africa. Chief selector George Bailey said Smith has met the specialists and the injury is progressing well.

The doctors have assessed the neck problem and are monitoring his recovery. Smith is hopeful of returning to action in the fifth round of the Sheffield Shield. His availability for the domestic fixture will depend on how the injury responds in the coming days.

The Australian team management is keeping a close watch on his fitness.
