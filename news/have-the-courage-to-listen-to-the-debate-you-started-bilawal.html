'Have the courage to listen to the debate you started': Bilawal sticks to his guns after speaking his 'truth'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has stood by his recent remarks on the country's political situation, describing them as the truth and urging government representatives to have the courage to listen to his point of view. Speaking on Hum News on Wednesday, he said the debate now under way was started by others, and those who began it should be willing to hear his side as well. He repeated his call for a political reset and cautioned against the creation of new provinces, saying such a step would carry serious consequences.

Bilawal said that as a major stakeholder in the ruling coalition, the government should pay attention to what he is saying rather than treating him as an opponent. He questioned how many fronts the government wanted to fight on at the same time, pointing to India, a near-war situation with Afghanistan and terrorism, and adding that it now seemed to be taking on the PTI, the JUI-F and himself as well. He also said that neither he nor the President had ever been consulted about provinces, and warned that any forced division of Sindh would not have his support.

The remarks come amid a widening public disagreement between the PPP and the government over the new-provinces debate.
