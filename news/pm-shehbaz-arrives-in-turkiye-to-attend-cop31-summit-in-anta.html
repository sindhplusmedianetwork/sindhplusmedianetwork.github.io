PM Shehbaz arrives in Turkiye to attend COP31 summit in Antalya

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has arrived in Turkiye to attend the Leaders' Summit of the 31st Conference of the Parties (COP31) to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change. He was received at Antalya airport by Turkiye's Deputy Minister for Culture and Tourism, Gokhan Yazgi. The prime minister will address the summit on November 11, urging the global community to take urgent and effective measures against the adverse effects of climate change.

He will also stress stronger international cooperation under agreed principles and address a high-level dialogue on the transition to renewable energy. During the visit, he is expected to meet Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan and other world leaders, and a Pakistan Pavilion has been set up at COP31.
