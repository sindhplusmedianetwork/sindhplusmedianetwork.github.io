Riyadh Air Moves 72 Aircraft Out of Riyadh Amid Fears of Further Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Riyadh Air and other airlines have moved 72 high-value aircraft out of Riyadh's King Khalid Airport to safer airports in Medina, Al-Ula and elsewhere, flight data shows. The relocation follows fears of further attacks that have shaken the region's aviation sector. October 12 was Riyadh's worst day of the crisis, with no flights operating at all.

Aircraft departures from the Saudi capital were 16 on October 13, 36 on October 14, 11 on October 15, one on October 16 and seven on October 17. Billions of dollars' worth of parked aircraft have been shifted to protect them from further losses. The emergency repositioning reflects the severity of the security concerns facing Saudi aviation.

Airlines are expected to keep the aircraft at the alternative airports until the threat subsides.
