Barrister Gohar and Mohsin Naqvi have been in contact, confirms PTI's Ali Muhammad Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Ali Muhammad Khan has confirmed that PTI chairman Barrister Gohar and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi have remained in contact. Speaking on Geo News, he said the contacts should continue. He said meetings with the PTI founder signal an improvement in the situation.

Khan also said the prime minister has offered negotiations five times in 18 months. His remarks confirm that communication between the two sides has been maintained.
