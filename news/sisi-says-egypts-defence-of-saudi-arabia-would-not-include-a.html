Sisi Says Egypt’s Defence of Saudi Arabia Would Not Include Attack on Yemen

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Egyptian President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi said on Wednesday that Egypt’s military effort to defend Saudi Arabia would not include an attack on Yemen. According to a statement from the Egyptian presidency spokesperson, the effort would be limited to defending the kingdom’s territory and borders. Egypt’s parliament on Monday delayed until October 26 a vote on authorising the armed forces to support Saudi Arabia if requested.

The vote follows recent Houthi attacks on the kingdom. Sisi added that Egypt would defend any other Arab country if asked. He said Egypt would act in accordance with the directives of Saudi King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman.
