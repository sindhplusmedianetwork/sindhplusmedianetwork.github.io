Magnitude 4.6 Earthquake Jolts Swat and Surrounding Areas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Earthquake tremors were felt in Swat and surrounding areas on Thursday morning, sending residents out of their homes and buildings in fear. The seismological centre recorded the quake at magnitude 4.6, with a depth of 182 kilometres. The centre said the epicentre was in the Hindu Kush mountain range.

People in the affected areas came out into the open as the tremors were felt, but no loss of life or property was reported. Authorities said they were monitoring the situation after the quake.
