France braces as Yellow Vests plan major Paris march on Saturday

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

France's Yellow Vest movement has filed plans for a major march through Paris on Saturday, setting a fresh test for the government after riots and looting last weekend. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu was scheduled to meet union leaders on Wednesday in an effort to defuse one of France's worst crises in years and avoid a repeat of the violence that swept cities on October 17. Yellow Vest activists are reviving their 2018 campaign against rising living costs and declining public services.

French government borrowing costs rose sharply, pushing ten-year bond yields to five percent, the highest since July 2002. Last weekend's demonstrations degenerated into violence in Paris, Marseille, Lyon and Limoges, overwhelming police. President Emmanuel Macron said he would meet intelligence services on Thursday ahead of the new protests, with troops expected to be deployed alongside additional police.

Marine Le Pen said the government had 'lost control of everything'.
