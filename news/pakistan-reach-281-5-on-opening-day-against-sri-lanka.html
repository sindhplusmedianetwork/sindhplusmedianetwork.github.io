Pakistan Reach 281-5 on Opening Day Against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan reached 281 for five on the opening day of the first Test against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi on Monday, with captain Babar Azam and two debutants among four batters to score half-centuries. Opener Tayyab Arif made an impressive 57 and Mohammad Taha a patient 51, both playing their first Test, while Azam scored 50. Sri Lanka's decision to bowl on a green pitch appeared to backfire.

Saud Shakeel was unbeaten on 57 when bad light stopped play 15 minutes before schedule, with Ghazi Ghori seven not out. Azam has now gone 41 innings without a Test century, his last coming against New Zealand in Karachi in December 2022. Asitha Fernando was the pick of the bowlers with two wickets for 64 runs.

The second and final Test will be played in Lahore from November 17.
