Germany beat Serbia to set up decisive Netherlands clash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Germany cruised to a 3-1 win over Serbia in Belgrade in the UEFA Nations League on Friday, setting up a decisive clash with the Netherlands in Berlin on Monday. In teeming rain in an almost empty stadium, the Germans forced Serbia defender Srdan Babic into an early own goal, before Florian Wirtz and debutant Brajan Gruda added further goals. Babic made up for his error with a second-half consolation strike, but Serbia finished with nine men after substitutes Vanja Dragojevic and Andrija Zivkovic were sent off late on.

Serbia's supporters were banned by UEFA over racist and discriminatory behaviour earlier in the competition, leaving only 5,000 schoolchildren and 200 travelling Germany fans in the ground. The Netherlands' 7-0 thrashing of Greece in Amsterdam, inspired by Tijjani Reijnders' first-half hat-trick, means Germany will probably need to win on Monday to reach the quarter-finals, unless Serbia beat Greece away from home. The Dutch lead the group with 11 points, three clear of both Greece and Germany, going into the final round of matches.

Germany coach Jurgen Klopp, whose side have now won twice, drawn twice and lost once in five games under him, said his team had played a truly fantastic game and were looking forward to the final in Berlin.
