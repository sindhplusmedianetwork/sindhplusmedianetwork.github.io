Cybersecurity central to Digital Pakistan vision, says IT minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

IT Minister Shaza Fatima Khawaja has said cybersecurity is fundamental to the Digital Pakistan vision, national security and protection of the digital economy. She was addressing the Pak Crypt International Symposium on Cybersecurity in Islamabad. The minister pointed to a 90-day action plan and warned that advances in quantum computing pose challenges to existing encryption.

She said Pakistan recorded 3.7 billion retail payments between January and March 2026, 92 per cent of them digital. She urged preparation for post-quantum cryptography. She said the country must prepare for a future in which quantum computing changes the encryption landscape.
