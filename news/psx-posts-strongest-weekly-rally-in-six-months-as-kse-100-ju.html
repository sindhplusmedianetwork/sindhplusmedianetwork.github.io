PSX Posts Strongest Weekly Rally in Six Months as KSE-100 Jumps 7,477 Points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange staged its strongest weekly rally in nearly six months, with the benchmark KSE-100 index surging 7,477 points, or 4.5%, to close at 173,887. The week began quietly as profit-booking dragged the index below 167,000 on Monday, but buying returned on Tuesday with a gain of 1,455 points, followed by a surge of 3,995 points on Wednesday and 3,366 points on Thursday, which briefly carried the market past 175,000 before profit-taking pulled it back on Friday. Arif Habib Limited said in its weekly report that sentiment improved after the Democrats' victory in the US midterm elections, which pushed international oil prices sharply lower on November 4.

Brent crude closed the week at $98.5 a barrel, down from an average of $115.5 the previous week, while petrol and diesel prices at home were cut to Rs352.59 and Rs382.25 a litre. October inflation eased to 9.6% from 10.3% in September, and the government raised Rs385.6 billion in an auction of its papers against a target of Rs350 billion. Pakistan's trade deficit stood at $3.9 billion in October, with exports of $2.9 billion and imports of $6.8 billion, while average daily trading volumes rose 43% to 856 million shares.
