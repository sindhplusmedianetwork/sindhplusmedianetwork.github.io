Debt Trap Drives Nawabshah Mother to Offer Four Children for Sale

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nawabshah — A woman in Qazi Ahmed tehsil of Sindh's Nawabshah district has announced that she will sell her four children, driven to desperation by loan demands and financial pressure. Shamsad Bibi told the media at a local press club that an alleged moneylender, Anwar Shah Lakhiyari, was demanding two million rupees from her. She said the moneylender had been issuing threats and exerting constant pressure, pushing her to the verge of losing her mental balance.

She further alleged that her husband, Allah Rakhio Khokhar, had been abducted with the assistance of police and made to disappear. She said her family had reached the point of starvation due to her husband's disappearance and the moneylender's pressure. She appealed to the Sindh government, the Sindh Home Minister, the provincial police chief and the DIG Shaheed Benazirabad to recover her husband and take strict action against the moneylender.

She also urged the authorities to protect her impoverished family and ensure justice.
