Street Politics Costs Pakistan Rs120 Billion Per Day, New Analysis Estimates

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A newspaper analysis published on Monday has put a price tag on street politics in Pakistan, estimating that a single day of disruption from long marches and blockades drains about Rs120 billion from national output. The breakdown puts losses at Rs86 billion in services, Rs25 billion in industry, Rs9 billion in agricultural wastage and Rs17 billion in lost fiscal revenue. According to the analysis, a week-long protest or a volatile quarter shaves 0.2 to 0.5 percent off annual real GDP.

It further notes that digital kill-switches during protest cycles cut software-house billings and digital-services exports by up to 80 percent during outage windows. The piece recommends designating specific protest zones, ending what it calls the 'Container Doctrine', and adopting a cross-party Charter of the Economy to shield growth from political turmoil.
