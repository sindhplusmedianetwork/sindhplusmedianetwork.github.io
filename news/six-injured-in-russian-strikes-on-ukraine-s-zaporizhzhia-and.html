Six Injured in Russian Strikes on Ukraine's Zaporizhzhia and Chernihiv

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Russian strike on Ukraine's southeastern city of Zaporizhzhia injured four people overnight, regional governor Mykhailo Semikin said on Telegram on Monday, while two more people were injured in Russian strikes on the Chernihiv region, Ukraine's emergency service said on the same messaging app. Also on Telegram, the Russian defence ministry said it hit a power station in Ukraine's second-largest city of Kharkiv, a warehouse in the surrounding region and another power station in the Khmelnitskiy region. It also said it had targeted port infrastructure in Chornomorsk and a cargo vessel en route to Odesa used for military purposes, the ministry said.

The United States has pressed Ukraine to suspend strikes on Russian oil refineries, arguing that they have added to high energy prices, a sensitive issue for the Trump administration amid its war on Iran as midterm elections approach next month. In an interview aired on Sunday, President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said that in order for Kyiv to halt the strikes, Moscow must stop hitting power infrastructure. Alternatively, the United States should allow the use of Starshield over Russia to detect missile launch sites, or supply Ukraine with Patriot missiles, he added.
