Netanyahu Says He and Trump Will 'Eliminate the Iranian Threat'

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has said his close relationship with US President Donald Trump will help the two of them 'eliminate the Iranian threat'. Netanyahu made the remarks in a video released by his Likud party, linking the goal to a possible victory in what is expected to be a tight election race. The statement follows Trump's public endorsement of Netanyahu ahead of Israel's October 27 election.

The update came as part of Dawn's live coverage of the Iran-Israel-US war. The live blog also reported that Saudi civil defence said six people were injured after a projectile fell in Najran. It further reported that Iran's Revolutionary Guards declared readiness for 'strategic synergy' with the country's defence ministry.
