Cabinet committee approves Foreign Exchange Regulation Bill

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Cabinet Committee for Disposal of Legislative Cases (CCLC) has approved several legislative proposals, including the Foreign Exchange Regulation (Amendment) Bill 2026. The committee, chaired by Federal Minister for Law and Justice Senator Azam Nazeer Tarar, met in Islamabad on Friday and was attended by senior officers of the relevant ministries and departments. Alongside the forex bill, the committee approved a legal and administrative package concerning plant protection functions.

It also approved a proposal regarding leave prior to retirement (LPR) in the Armed Forces, including payment in lieu of the unavailed portion of such leave. The approvals clear the way for the proposals to move forward through the legislative process.
