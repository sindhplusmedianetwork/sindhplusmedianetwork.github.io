BISP audit exposes Rs25.7 billion in irregular payments

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An audit of the Benazir Income Support Programme for the financial year 2024-25 has found irregularities worth Rs25.7 billion, the Auditor General of Pakistan's report says. The report found that 12,078 government employees, pensioners and their spouses received Rs515.712 million even though a federal cabinet decision of December 2019 excludes them from the programme. It also recorded Rs13.041 million paid in 261 cases in which beneficiaries or their spouses had travelled abroad, and payments to vehicle owners, ineligible students and people with incorrect marital-status records.

The audit said BISP had 10.02 million active beneficiary households, with an inclusion error rate of 6.8 per cent against a 5 per cent benchmark and an exclusion error rate of 13.27 per cent against a permissible 10 per cent. The largest problem involved identification records: in 596,252 cases, records lacked spouses' CNIC details, involving Rs25.46 billion, while in 5,598 cases the same spouse's CNIC was linked to as many as seven women, involving Rs239.035 million. The report also found 97,179 cases of multiple Proxy Means Test scores against the same household head, leading to the wrongful enrolment of 18,964 beneficiaries and possible misallocation of Rs533.536 million.

In the Benazir Taleemi Wazaif programme, duplicate stipends of Rs17.69 million were paid where 165 schools already receiving Pakistan Baitul Mal assistance were also enrolled, and 278 government employees were registered as students, resulting in payments of Rs202.546 million. Auditors blamed weak data verification, poor information-sharing with government departments and weak enforcement of eligibility rules, and sought explanations from NADRA and field verification of the flagged cases.
