Weekly inflation rises another 0.65pc; annual rate reaches 11.85pc

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Weekly inflation rose by another 0.65 percent, taking the annual inflation rate to 11.85 percent, according to the Bureau of Statistics weekly report. Nineteen essential items became more expensive, 12 became cheaper, and 20 remained stable. LPG recorded the sharpest weekly rise of 22.44 percent, followed by chicken at 12.20 percent and onions at 4.19 percent.

Tomatoes fell 21.99 percent, while petrol and diesel prices declined 3.63 and 1.68 percent respectively. On an annual basis, LPG prices are up 107.12 percent, onions 72.81 percent, and electricity rates 58.59 percent.
