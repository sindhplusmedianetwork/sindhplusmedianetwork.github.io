Five of a Family Killed as Coach and Car Collide on Tando Adam–Jhol Road

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five members of a family, including two five-year-old boys, were killed and three women injured when a passenger coach and a car collided head-on on Tando Adam–Jhol Road in Sanghar district in the early hours of Sunday. The crash left the car trapped beneath the coach, with the dead and injured pinned inside for hours, while the coach driver fled the scene. The passenger coach was travelling from Karachi to Sanghar when it collided head-on with the car.

The deceased were identified as 48-year-old Mano Kolhi, his 40-year-old wife Neelan, their 30-year-old son Ramoon, his five-year-old son Deval, and five-year-old Kanyo, identified in the police account as Ramoon's nephew. Three women, Roshni, Heeri, wife of Ramoon, and Gulabi, sustained injuries and were shifted to the taluka hospital before being referred to Hyderabad for further treatment. According to police, the victims belonged to the village of Bilawal Khoso in the New Saeedabad taluka of Matiari district and were returning home after attending a charity feast in the village of Piro Faqir Shoro.

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah expressed deep sorrow over the loss of lives, extended condolences to the bereaved families, and directed the authorities to ensure the best possible medical treatment for the injured.
