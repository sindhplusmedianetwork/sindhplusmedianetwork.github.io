Body found in marshland during search for missing bride-to-be Erin Wright in UK

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A body has been found in a coastal marshland area between Lytham and Warton in Lancashire, England, during the search for 29-year-old Erin Wright. Wright went missing from her home on October 27, two weeks before her wedding. Police said the body was found on Thursday afternoon at around 3:30pm.

Although the body has not yet been formally identified, police believe it is Erin Wright and have informed her family. Police said her death is not being treated as suspicious. The matter will be referred to the coroner.
