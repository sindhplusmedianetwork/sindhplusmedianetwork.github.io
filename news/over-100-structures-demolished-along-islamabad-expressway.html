Over 100 Structures Demolished Along Islamabad Expressway

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Capital Development Authority has sealed 58 buildings and demolished 124 structures between Sohan and Khanna Bridge along Islamabad Expressway Service Road (East). The action is part of development work on a major road-widening project. The authority said on Saturday that more than 28 per cent of the project had been completed.

The project stretches about 9.3 kilometres from Faizabad to Gulberg. It envisages a dual carriageway with three lanes on each side.
