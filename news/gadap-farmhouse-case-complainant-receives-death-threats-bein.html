Gadap Farmhouse Case Complainant Receives Death Threats, Being Followed; Seeks Police Protection

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read

The complainant in the Gadap farmhouse case says her life is in danger after receiving death threats over the case. The woman, identified as Khushnuma Wasif, a resident of Gulistan-e-Johar Block 18 in Karachi, is the complainant in the case registered at Gadap City police station regarding the theft of mobile phones from more than 50 women who were brought to a Gadap farmhouse on the pretext of jobs and training, with Sahil as the main accused. She told police that on October 20 she received threats on WhatsApp of serious consequences and murder if she did not withdraw the case, and provided screenshots.

She said that on October 21, while she was on her way to work, two unidentified men on a motorcycle gestured that they would kill her; one of them came close and tried to grab her, his hand touching her shoulder, before both fled toward Johar Mor. She has asked the police to investigate the people sending threats on WhatsApp, the two motorcyclists, and the role of an accused named Babar, and to take immediate legal action. She has requested that she and her family be given life and property protection so she can continue her daily life and work without fear.
