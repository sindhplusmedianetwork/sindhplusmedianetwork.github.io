Jamaat-e-Islami's Islamabad 'Super Long March' to Begin on October 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami has announced that the third phase of its Islamabad super long march will begin on October 23. Naib Amir Liaqat Baloch said peaceful protests would be held at district headquarters across the country on Friday. He said Rana Sanaullah and Ahsan Iqbal had proposed that the negotiating committees of both sides meet before the meeting with the prime minister.

However, he added, the government's committee had not reported any progress on the talks so far. The announcement marks the next stage of the party's protest movement against the government.
