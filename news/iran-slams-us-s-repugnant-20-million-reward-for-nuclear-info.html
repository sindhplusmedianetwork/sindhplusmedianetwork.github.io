Iran Slams US's 'Repugnant' $20 Million Reward for Nuclear Information

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has hit out at the United States' offer of up to 20 million dollars for information on its nuclear programme, calling it 'repugnant and regrettable'. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi urged the United Nations' atomic energy agency and the Security Council to condemn the reward. In a letter dated Tuesday, Araghchi called on the UN Secretary-General, the Security Council and the Director General of the IAEA to condemn the announcement explicitly and unequivocally.

The reward was announced last week by the US military, which said up to 20 million dollars and 'possible relocation' were available for information about the programme's facilities, equipment and associated personnel. Araghchi said the announcement encouraged the collection of secret information about nuclear installations and personnel. He repeated Iran's insistence that its nuclear programme is peaceful.
