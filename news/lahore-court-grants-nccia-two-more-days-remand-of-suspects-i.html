Lahore Court Grants NCCIA Two More Days' Remand of Suspects in US Postage Stamp Fraud

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Lahore court has granted the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency a further two-day physical remand of the suspects arrested in the fake US postage stamp fraud case. The agency told the court it needed more time to complete its investigation into the multi-million-dollar scam. According to the case record, a grand jury in Florida has charged a suspect from Khanewal in connection with the $126 million fraud.

Investigators say more than five million counterfeit US postage stamps were sold through a website run by the accused. The NCCIA had earlier arrested the suspects after tracing the digital trail of the online operation. The court directed the agency to produce the suspects again after the remand period ends.
