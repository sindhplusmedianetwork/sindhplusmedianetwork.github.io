PSX extends rally, reclaims 175,000 mark

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange extended its bullish rally for a fourth consecutive session on Thursday, with the benchmark KSE-100 index surging 3,366 points, almost 2 percent, to close at 175,380. The index touched an intra-day high of 176,605 points before some profit-taking trimmed gains, with UBL, Lucky Cement and Meezan Bank among the stocks contributing most to the rise. Trading volumes were recorded at 999.8 million shares, with the value of shares traded at 46 billion rupees.

The rally took the market back above the 175,000 mark, marking another strong day for investors after three earlier sessions of gains. Buying interest remained broad-based through the session, although late profit-taking kept the close below the day's high. The performance underlined continued confidence in the market as the index set fresh ground above the key psychological level.
