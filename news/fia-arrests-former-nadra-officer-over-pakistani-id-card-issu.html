FIA Arrests Former NADRA Officer Over Pakistani ID Card Issued to Iranian Citizen

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has arrested a former National Database and Registration Authority officer for his alleged role in issuing a Pakistani identity card to an Iranian citizen. The FIA’s Anti-Human Trafficking Circle in Karachi arrested former assistant director (technical) Zia-ul-Haq Araeen in connection with the case, registered as FIR 273/2026. The case began when two passengers travelling to Qatar on Pakistani passports were stopped at Karachi’s Jinnah International Airport, where immigration checks raised doubts about their citizenship.

The two men admitted they were Iranian-born and had obtained Pakistani documents illegally in exchange for money. The FIA says it is verifying the arrested officer’s role and the relevant NADRA records. The agency has widened the investigation to identify other officers, agents and facilitators involved in the network.
