Sindh Cabinet Raises SPSC Exam Age Limit to 33, Lowers Passing Marks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The cabinet decided that computer-based testing will be introduced for one-paper examinations, with results printed immediately after the tests, and interview marks will be revised in line with the ratio followed by the Federal Public Service Commission. A Cabinet Reforms Committee will be constituted to develop IT-enabled and AI-based reforms for the SPSC. The package includes safeguards against retaliatory action against candidates who took part in recent protests and a mechanism to review allegations of candidates being targeted during interviews.

The cabinet also approved the World Bank-supported Sindh Property Revenue Enhancement Programme, with $150 million in financing, aimed at raising property-tax collection across Karachi's 25 town municipal corporations from Rs2.3 billion to Rs25.5 billion. It approved a 115-million-euro European Investment Bank package for the third phase of the Sindh Flood Emergency Housing Reconstruction Project. The chief minister also briefed the cabinet on his recent China visit, noting 36 memorandums of understanding signed at the Shanghai investment conference with over 400 delegates.
