Anmol Baloch Questions Depiction of Forced Marriages in Dramas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actress and model Anmol Baloch has questioned the trend of depicting forced marriages in dramas. She said she personally does not agree with depicting forced marriages and forced nikah scenes, although the topic often becomes part of dramas. Speaking on Maliha Rehman's show, she said Pakistani dramas often show the hero forcibly marrying the heroine.

She added that sometimes both lead characters are shown married against their will. She said one reason may be that romantic relationships between unmarried couples are avoided on TV. Because of this, she said, nikah or marriage is made the basis of the relationship in the story.
