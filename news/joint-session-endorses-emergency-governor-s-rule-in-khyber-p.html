Joint Session Endorses Emergency, Governor's Rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A joint sitting of parliament on Thursday ratified the presidential proclamations imposing a two-month emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, formalising the October 11 measures that suspended the PTI-led provincial administration. The sitting, chaired by National Assembly Speaker Ayaz Sadiq, adopted two separate resolutions — one under Article 232 approving the emergency and the other under Article 234 approving governor's rule — after a procedural motion moved by Federal Minister Dr Tariq Fazal Chaudhry passed by 214 votes to 56. Opposition members registered their dissent by tearing up copies of the resolutions, and several wore black armbands during the proceedings.

Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar defended the move as a last resort taken through constitutional procedure, saying the federal cabinet had debated the matter for two and a half hours after the K-P governor wrote a detailed letter warning that the terrorism conflict was intensifying. Leader of the Opposition Mahmood Khan Achakzai questioned how terrorists kept entering Pakistan despite the border wall with Afghanistan and alleged that the opposition's chief minister and assembly members had been abducted. PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan categorically rejected allegations that his party supported terrorism and declared that PTI rejected the emergency, governor's rule and the calling in of the army.

Under the Constitution, the measures can be extended in two-month increments up to a maximum of six months, and after the proceedings the joint sitting was adjourned indefinitely.
