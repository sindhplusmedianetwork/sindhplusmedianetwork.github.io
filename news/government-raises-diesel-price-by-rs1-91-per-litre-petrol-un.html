Government Raises Diesel Price by Rs1.91 Per Litre, Petrol Unchanged at Rs377.71

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government announced on Friday an increase of Rs1.91 per litre in the price of high-speed diesel, while keeping the petrol price unchanged at Rs377.71 per litre. With the revision, high-speed diesel will now be sold at Rs393.66 per litre. The new fuel prices will remain in effect from October 24 to October 26.

Alongside the price notification, the government has reintroduced austerity measures as part of efforts to conserve energy and manage resources. Under these measures, markets across the country must close by 9pm. The diesel hike is expected to affect transport costs and the prices of goods, as diesel powers most freight and agricultural machinery.
