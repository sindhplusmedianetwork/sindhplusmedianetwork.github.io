KP Governor Invites Chinese Investment in Minerals and Economic Zones

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi on Saturday said the province was rich in natural resources and mineral reserves, offering vast investment opportunities for Chinese investors, particularly in the minerals sector and economic zones. He expressed these views while talking to a delegation of Chinese investors, which called on him at the Governor House, Peshawar. Member of the Provincial Assembly from the Pakistan Peoples Party, Arbab Zarak, was also present during the meeting.

The meeting discussed investment opportunities in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with special focus on the minerals sector and the investment potential of the province's economic zones. Welcoming the delegation, the Governor said Chinese investors could take advantage of the immense business opportunities available in the province, and assured them that the provincial administration would extend all possible facilities and support to facilitate investment and create a business-friendly environment. Governor Kundi said the investors should hold consultations with the relevant advisers to the Governor and government officials to identify viable investment projects and prepare practical proposals for further consideration.
