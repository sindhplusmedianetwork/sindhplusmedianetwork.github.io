Kundi asks Fazlur Rehman whom he appointed governor in PDM government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has strongly criticised JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman at a ceremony in Peshawar. Kundi said Fazlur Rehman sends other people's children for jihad while his own children and brothers travel to Europe and America for leisure. He said Fazlur Rehman now remembered rejected people, and asked whom he had appointed governor when he was part of the PDM government.

The governor made clear that no leniency would be shown to those waging war against the state. He said the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa police currently has 17,000 vacant positions that will soon be filled transparently.
