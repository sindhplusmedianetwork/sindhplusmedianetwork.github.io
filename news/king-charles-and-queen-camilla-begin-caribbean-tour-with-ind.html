King Charles and Queen Camilla begin Caribbean tour with Independence celebrations

By Sindh Plus Media Network

King Charles III and Queen Camilla have kicked off the Caribbean leg of their royal tour with a special celebration in Antigua and Barbuda. Queen Camilla joined the King on Saturday, October 31, for the nine-day state visit, with programme highlights shared on the Royal Family's official Instagram account. Buckingham Palace said the royal couple will take part in celebrations marking the nation's 45th anniversary of Independence and attend the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) 2026.

As Head of the Commonwealth, the King will bring together leaders, young people and business and civil society representatives from across the member nations. The royal couple will also appear at Government House to celebrate Antiguan and Barbudan culture, heritage and artistic talent, and will observe nature and conservation projects involving historic sites, marine ecosystems and coral reefs. The tour began on Tuesday, October 27, in Nassau, The Bahamas, where the King met Governor-General Dame Cynthia Pratt and Prime Minister Philip Davis and addressed a youth rally at Clifford Park.

It then continued to Guyana for two days before the final four-day stop in Antigua and Barbuda.
