Ombudsman Punjab secures Rs9 million scholarships for 65 students

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Office of the Ombudsman Punjab has secured the release of educational scholarships worth Rs9 million for 65 students across the province. The scholarships were released after the ombudsman resolved complaints regarding delays in payments. Students had complained that their scholarship money was being held up, leaving many struggling to continue their studies.

The ombudsman's intervention led to the release of the funds to the affected students. The office said such complaints are taken up promptly so that students are not deprived of their rightful support. The resolution brings relief to 65 students and their families across Punjab.
