Indonesia says 24 killed in factory blaze as rescuers search for dozens missing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indonesia says 24 workers died in a fire at the PT Eterno Plastic Industry factory in Tangerang, Banten. Rescuers are searching for 53 missing people after the blaze. Eleven people were hospitalised.

The fire spread from a warehouse to the production area on Friday. A presidential adviser said the implementation of safety protocols at the factory was being assessed. The factory's chief executive will be summoned, and the cause of the fire is under investigation.
