Punjab launches 'PRIME' platform for digital monitoring of health education

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The University of Health Sciences has introduced a digital platform called 'PRIME' to improve the quality of medical and health professionals' education and training in Punjab. The platform will also monitor the practical skills of health professionals. Provincial minister Khawaja Salman Rafique inaugurated the portal at UHS.

The system will link medicine, dentistry, nursing, pharmacy and allied health sciences in one digital framework. It will replace manual logbooks with electronic records of training activities, clinical experience and practical skills. The platform is intended to bring health education and training in the province under a single digital monitoring system.
