Islamabad Local Government Election Schedule Challenged in High Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The election schedule for local government polls in Islamabad and the Election Commission's order of October 21 have been challenged in the Islamabad High Court. The Election Commission had on October 21 ordered local government elections in Islamabad under the Local Government Act 2015 and issued the election schedule, under which polling is to be held on December 20. Citizen Chaudhry Amjad Ali has challenged the schedule, and his lawyer Adil Aziz Qazi has filed a petition in the high court.

The petition argues that the Election Commission wants to hold elections on old delimitations, whose legal status has not been determined, and that elections cannot be held without settling their legal standing. It further states that the legal term of Islamabad's local bodies ended in February 2021, and that under the Elections Act 2017 elections were legally required within 120 days of the expiry of the term. The petitioner has asked for the Election Commission's October 21, 2026 order and the election schedule to be declared illegal and annulled, for the election process to be suspended until the court's decision, and for fresh and transparent delimitations to be ordered.

The Election Commission, the interior ministry, the law ministry and the Capital Development Authority have been made respondents in the petition. The last local government elections in the federal capital were held in 2015, and the elected local government's term ended on February 14, 2021, after which the election process has remained stalled for five years because of repeated changes in the law and administrative issues.
