Tiger Shroff's Family Breaks Silence After Firing Attack on Actor's Car

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The family of Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff has given its first reaction after unidentified persons opened fire on his car in Mumbai a day earlier. His mother Ayesha Shroff told the media that Tiger is completely fine, safe and out of danger. She thanked fans for their prayers and good wishes for the actor.

The actor escaped unharmed in the attack, while his driver and a fan present at the scene were injured. Mumbai Police and the Crime Branch have taken the incident seriously and started an investigation.
