Bilawal Wants PM Shehbaz to Become First Premier to Complete Five-Year Term

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari said on Friday that he wanted Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to become the first premier to complete a five-year term in office. Since the country came into being in 1947, no elected prime minister has ever completed their five-year term. Addressing an event in Karachi, Bilawal, citing the economic crisis resulting from the US-Israeli war on Iran, called for empowering PM Shehbaz so he could present a way forward to tackle the crisis and said his objective was to strengthen the hand of the prime minister.

Bilawal noted the prime minister had not been given the chance to focus on economic issues so far and said whenever the prime minister makes a plan, some federal minister objects and presents their own agenda to divert the topic at hand. He insisted he wanted the premier to complete his term as he foresaw difficulties ahead for the country and said whether one province is created or a thousand, the only worry the public has is having access to meals twice a day, recalling his party's slogan of roti, kapra, makan. Bilawal reiterated the premier's hand should be strengthened so he can draw attention to economic issues and underscored the need for a national effort, saying one or two political parties alone cannot face the real issues of the country.

He called on the government to engage the opposition, particularly the PTI, in dealing with the country's issues and said the PTI still has a majority representation in the opposition and should be engaged to play its role in parliamentary committees.
