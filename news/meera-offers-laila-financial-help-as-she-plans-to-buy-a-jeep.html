Meera Offers Laila Financial Help as She Plans to Buy a Jeep

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Reality-show actress Laila has said she plans to buy a white jeep as soon as her payment from the show reaches her account. She also wants to build a new house from her earnings. Speaking on a podcast, she said she was paid for the 63 days she spent on the reality show.

She added that her earnings from the show exceed the competition's prize money. She also revealed that actress Meera has offered her Rs1 to 1.5 million if she falls short of the money needed for the jeep. Her remarks have drawn attention to the earnings of reality-show contestants.
