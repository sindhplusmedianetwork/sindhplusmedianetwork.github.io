Abandoned Boeing aircraft parked at Sialkot Airport for 9 years auctioned for over $400,000

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An abandoned Boeing aircraft parked at Sialkot Airport for nine years has finally been auctioned for more than US$400,000. According to Geo News, the Fly Jet Asia Airways Boeing 767 had been parked at the airport since 29 August 2017. Dues worth lakhs were recovered through the auction.

The aircraft manufacturer has gone bankrupt and the unusable aircraft was auctioned on court orders. The reserve bid in the auction advertisement was set at $400,000. Its engines, seats and landing gear were in working condition.

Sialkot International Airport spokesperson Muhammad Umair Khan confirmed the auction.
