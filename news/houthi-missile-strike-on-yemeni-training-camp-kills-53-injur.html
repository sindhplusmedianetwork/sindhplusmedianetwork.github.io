Houthi Missile Strike on Yemeni Training Camp Kills 53, Injures 200

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A missile strike by the Houthi group Ansarullah on a security forces training camp in Yemen's Shabwa province killed 53 people and injured 200, according to reports from Sanaa. The Houthis also claimed to have targeted a Saudi Aramco oil installation, an oil field, and the Riyadh and Abha airports. In response to the attacks, authorities in Riyadh directed government employees to work from home, and Saudi Arabia postponed its Future Investment Initiative investment conference to next year.

The reports also said Iranian naval officials claimed US forces targeted the Konarak naval base, injuring two personnel. US citizens in the Middle East were advised to remain alert. Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov visited Tehran, meeting Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi and President Pezeshkian; Pezeshkian said US military bases in the region increase tension while Iran is ready to resolve issues through dialogue.

Separately, Qatar announced a new regional defence partnership, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards warned that any new US attack would trigger a serious global energy crisis and draw a much tougher response.
