PM Shehbaz Calls for Collective Efforts to Tackle Regional Security Threats at ECO Meeting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Monday called for concerted efforts to address the challenges and threats to regional security, in a meeting with foreign ministers and delegation heads of the Economic Cooperation Organization member states in Islamabad. He reiterated Pakistan's principled stance on resolving disputes through dialogue and diplomacy. The meeting was held as the foreign ministers and delegation heads are in Islamabad for the 30th session of the ECO Council of Ministers.

The prime minister said collective efforts are essential to addressing the challenges and threats to regional security. He welcomed the adoption of the Islamabad Declaration and the strategic objectives for economic cooperation for 2026–2035, describing them as important steps towards strengthening the organisation. He underscored the importance of enhancing cooperation in trade and investment, connectivity through transport and corridors, energy, information and communication technology, and sustainable development.

He said Pakistan was committed to strengthening regional connectivity through road, rail and multimodal transport corridors, including access to the ports of Karachi, Port Qasim and Gwadar, which would particularly benefit landlocked ECO member states.
