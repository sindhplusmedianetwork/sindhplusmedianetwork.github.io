Haaland Double Keeps Manchester City Perfect as Neto Brace Sinks Everton for Chelsea

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Manchester City bounced back from their midweek setback in Europe to maintain their perfect Premier League start, with Erling Haaland's double propelling them to a 3-2 home win over Ipswich Town on Saturday. Paris Saint-Germain had inflicted a first meaningful defeat on Pep Guardiola's side in the Champions League on Wednesday, but City shrugged off a gallant Ipswich to make it seven wins from seven and cement their place at the top of the table. Phil Foden gave City a short-lived lead that Daizen Maeda cancelled out, before Haaland restored the advantage before half-time with a header from Mateo Kovacic's cross.

The Norwegian then gave his side breathing space with his second in the 69th minute, powering past Abdoul Ouattara before a low finish into the corner, and was denied a hat-trick by an offside decision before Zian Flemming tucked away a stoppage-time penalty for Ipswich. In Saturday's early action, Chelsea beat Everton 2-0 thanks to Pedro Neto's brace, though the win was overshadowed by an injury to goalkeeper Emi Martinez, who was stretchered off after a clash of heads with Jarrad Branthwaite and taken to hospital for precautionary tests. Neto struck 43 seconds after the restart from Jorrel Hato's cutback and again in the 90th minute as Everton suffered their first defeat of the season.

Liverpool came from a goal down to beat 10-man Brentford 2-1, with Yehor Yarmolyuk sent off for a head butt, while Fulham moved off the foot of the table with a 1-0 home win over Hull City — their first league victory of the season.
