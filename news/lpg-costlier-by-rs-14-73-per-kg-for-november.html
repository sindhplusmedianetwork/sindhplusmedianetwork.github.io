LPG Costlier by Rs 14.73 per Kg for November

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority has raised the price of liquefied petroleum gas by 14 rupees 73 paisas per kilogram for the month of November. According to the official notification, the new LPG price has been fixed at 294 rupees per kilogram, effective from November 1. The domestic 11.8-kilogram cylinder will now cost 173 rupees 86 paisas more, bringing its price to 3,470 rupees and 9 paisas.

Sources say LPG is being sold in the market at nearly double the rate fixed by OGRA. Consumers, already burdened by rising energy costs ahead of winter, face an additional squeeze on household budgets. The increase follows the regulator's monthly price review, which factors in international market trends and the rupee's value.
