If Punjab Awakens, Revolution Can Sweep the Country, Says Hafiz Naeem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman has said that Punjab's population is more than half of Pakistan's population, and if Punjab awakens, a revolution can sweep the entire country. Addressing the 'Kull Punjab Ijtema-e-Zimmedaran' in Lahore, he said the current political parties have no solution to the people's problems, and there is no option other than Jamaat-e-Islami for changing the system. He said Jamaat-e-Islami must come forward as a major political party in Punjab and as a major parliamentary force across the country.

Whoever establishes himself in Punjab, he said, is the one whose politics runs across the country. The time has come, he added, for Jamaat-e-Islami to emerge as a major political party in Punjab. He expressed confidence that very soon Jamaat-e-Islami would become a major parliamentary force throughout the country.
