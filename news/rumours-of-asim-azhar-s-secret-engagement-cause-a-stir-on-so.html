Rumours of Asim Azhar's Secret Engagement Cause a Stir on Social Media

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Rumours of singer Asim Azhar's engagement have started circulating on social media after new photos of him with an unidentified woman went viral. In the photos, apparently taken at a family event, Asim is wearing a cream-coloured traditional outfit while the woman is dressed in light green. The images prompted fans to ask whether the singer had got engaged.

Neither Asim Azhar nor his team has confirmed any engagement. The identity of the woman and the nature of the event remain unclear. His earlier engagement to actress Meerub Ali ended by mutual consent in June 2025.
