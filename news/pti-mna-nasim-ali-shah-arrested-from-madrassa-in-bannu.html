PTI MNA Nasim Ali Shah Arrested From Madrassa in Bannu

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police on Monday arrested PTI Member of the National Assembly Maulana Nasim Ali Shah from his madrassa, Al-Markaz-ul-Islami, in Ghoriwala, Bannu. A video circulating on social media purportedly showed police officers taking the lawmaker away, and the footage was also shared from the detained MNA's own X account. The circumstances of the arrest, the cases registered against him, and his whereabouts afterward had not been officially confirmed.

The arrest came amid a wider crackdown on PTI leaders and workers in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa following the imposition of governor's rule and a state of emergency in the province on October 11. PTI has rejected the measures as unconstitutional and challenged them in the Peshawar High Court. Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry has confirmed that former chief minister Sohail Afridi and some cabinet members were placed in preventive custody.
