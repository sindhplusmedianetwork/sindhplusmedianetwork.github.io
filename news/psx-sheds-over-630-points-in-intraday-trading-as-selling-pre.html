PSX sheds over 630 points in intraday trading as selling pressure persists

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi: The Pakistan Stock Exchange began the week on a subdued note on Monday, as selling pressure emerged shortly after the opening bell, The Express Tribune reported. The benchmark KSE-100 index fell to 166,169.41 points at 9:34am, down 239.84 points, reflecting weak buying interest. The downward momentum persisted through the morning session, with the index trading at 165,776.85 points at 12:29pm, a decline of 632.40 points or 0.38 per cent against the previous closing level of 166,409.25.

The index touched an intraday high of 166,581.95 and a low of 165,601.22, while trading volume stood at 101.08 million shares with a traded value of Rs6.31 billion. The weakness came despite softer international oil prices and a third consecutive day of talks between Iranian and Qatari officials. The latest decline followed a weak close to the previous trading week, when the index shed 784.19 points on Friday.

Market participants remain cautious amid geopolitical uncertainty and the State Bank of Pakistan's decision on October 26 to raise the benchmark policy rate by 50 basis points to 12pc.
