State Bank Triples Gemstone Export Retention Limit to 30 Percent

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The State Bank of Pakistan has tripled the permissible limit for commission, brokerage and discount payments and the retention of export proceeds by the gemstone sector to 30 percent from 10 percent. The change was made through an Export Policy Department circular issued on Monday. The move aims to support gemstone exporters as the government seeks to unlock the country's largely untapped mineral wealth.

The National Gemstone Policy 2026-2030 targets $1 billion in gemstone exports within five years, against current formal exports of just $5.8 million. Gemstones now enjoy the second-highest retention ceiling of any sector, after books, journals and magazines at 33.33 percent.
