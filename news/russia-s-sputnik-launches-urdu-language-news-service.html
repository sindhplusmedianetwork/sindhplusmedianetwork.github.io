Russia's Sputnik Launches Urdu-Language News Service

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian state news agency Sputnik has launched an Urdu-language news service, marking a new development in media cooperation between Pakistan and Russia. The service will carry news in Urdu for audiences in Pakistan and Urdu-speaking communities elsewhere. Its first interview was conducted with Pakistan's Ambassador to Russia, Faisal Niaz Tirmizi, at the agency's studios in Moscow.

The launch reflects growing engagement between the two countries in the field of media. Urdu is one of the most widely spoken languages in South Asia, and the new service is aimed at reaching that audience directly. The move is expected to broaden the exchange of news and views between Pakistan and Russia.

Further programming in Urdu is expected to follow as the service develops.
