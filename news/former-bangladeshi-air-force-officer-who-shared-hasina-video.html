Former Bangladeshi air force officer who shared Hasina video found dead in Dhaka river

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Bangladesh Air Force officer KM Tahsif Suri was found dead on Wednesday under mysterious circumstances. According to foreign media reports, the 28-year-old had been accused of uploading a controversial video showing deposed prime minister Sheikh Hasina leaving the country. His body was recovered from the Turag River on October 28, a day after he went missing.

He was wearing a black shirt and white trousers. Police retrieved the body near Rajbari Ghat in Dhaka's Uttarkhan area at around 4:30 pm after receiving a call on the national emergency helpline. His family has alleged that he was continuously harassed after the video surfaced and that he was killed because of it.

His cousin, Mushfika Jahan, also blamed the video for his death. Police have not yet confirmed the cause of death. His death has revived questions that arose when he was dismissed from service.

An earlier newspaper report had claimed that Suri spied for India's intelligence agency RAW, after which the government seized his passport for two years. A few months ago he appeared on a podcast where he spoke openly against the newspaper, though he avoided detailing some sensitive matters. Sheikh Hasina fled to India by military aircraft after violent protests toppled her government in 2024, and she has been living in exile there since.
