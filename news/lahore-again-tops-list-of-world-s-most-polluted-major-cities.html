Lahore again tops list of world's most polluted major cities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore remained at the top of the list of the world's most polluted major cities this morning. According to reports, air pollution in Lahore was recorded at 291 particulate matter. Air quality in several other cities of Punjab was also extremely hazardous to health.

According to the global environmental website, Nankana Sahib recorded the highest air pollution at 685 particulate matter a short while ago, while Dera Ghazi Khan recorded 611. According to the global environmental body, Jhang recorded 498, Multan 301, Gujranwala 296 and Faisalabad 294 particulate matter of air pollution.
