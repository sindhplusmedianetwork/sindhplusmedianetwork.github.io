FIR alone cannot bar anyone from public employment, rules Federal Constitutional Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Constitutional Court has ruled that the registration of an FIR alone cannot disqualify a person from public employment. The court stressed that an accusation is neither proof nor presumption of guilt. A bench led by Chief Justice Amin-ud-Din Khan issued a 10-page judgment on the matter.

The judgment lays down guidelines for character verification of candidates seeking public employment. It states that candidates who have been acquitted must not face adverse character verification. It further holds that pending cases may only be disclosed without presuming guilt on the part of the candidate.

The ruling is expected to affect how public departments assess the background of job applicants facing criminal accusations.
