Hundreds of Thousands March Across France Against Inflation and Job Cuts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

People from various sectors took to the streets across France to protest the rising cost of living and issues around wages and pensions, with students' ongoing protests over education conditions adding momentum. Students had been demonstrating since mid-September over education conditions and school problems, and complaints about inflation and public services later joined the movement. Following a nationwide protest call, students, farmers, environmental workers, former Yellow Vest movement members and other groups joined demonstrations across the country.

The CGT, one of France's largest labour unions, backed the protest, demanding wage and pension hikes, price cuts and protection of public services. An official source estimated about 200,000 participants nationwide with around 40,000 expected in Paris, though these were advance estimates and final attendance was not confirmed. The protests come as France faces political and economic challenges before its next presidential elections, and their spread may pressure the government on wages, inflation and public facilities.
