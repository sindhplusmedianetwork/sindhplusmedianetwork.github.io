Punjab Governor says he knows nothing of any move to replace CM Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Governor Sardar Saleem Haider Khan has said he has no knowledge of any change of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz and will not comment on assumptions. Speaking to the media, he made clear that he was not aware of any move to replace the chief minister. He said Faryal Talpur's visit to Punjab would have positive effects, and added that President Asif Ali Zardari is arriving in Lahore today and he will receive him.

The governor said the People's Party believes new provinces should be created where there is demand, and that the Potohar province resolution should be tried. He also said the FATA merger with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa happened without consultation, and demands for its reversal are now being made. His remarks came amid speculation about political changes in Punjab, which he declined to discuss.
