Court Disposes of Petitions After Detention Orders Against Imran Khan's Sisters Withdrawn

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has disposed of petitions against the detention of Imran Khan's sisters Noreen Niazi and Uzma Khan. The Lahore deputy commissioner told the court their detention notifications had been withdrawn. The two sisters were detained in September under the Punjab Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance.

They were released from Kot Lakhpat Jail on 12 November, while their sister Aleema Khan remains in jail. Justice Abher Gul Khan disposed of both petitions in light of the DC's statement. The court's order brings the legal challenge against the two detentions to a close.
