Petrol Price Unchanged, Diesel Up Rs 2.29 Per Litre for Three Days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has announced the petroleum prices for the next three days. The price of petrol remains unchanged at Rs 377.91 per litre. High-speed diesel has been increased by Rs 2.29 per litre, taking its new price to Rs 402.96 per litre.

The revised prices take effect immediately and will remain in force for the next three days. The fortnightly price review was conducted on the basis of international oil market trends and the exchange rate. Fuel prices in Pakistan are reviewed every 15 days by the government on the recommendation of OGRA.

The unchanged petrol price brings some relief to motorists, while the diesel hike is expected to affect transport costs and freight charges across the country.
