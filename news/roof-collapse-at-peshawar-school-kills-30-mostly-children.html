Roof Collapse at Peshawar School Kills 30, Mostly Children

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The roof of a private school in Peshawar's Dir Colony collapsed during morning classes on Thursday, killing 29 to 30 people, including 28 children and at least one female teacher. More than 50 others were injured in the collapse at the Al-Khair school, which had around 110 enrolled students. Police took the school's owner, Khan Muhammad, into custody as authorities began probing the use of substandard construction material.

An FIR was registered under sections including 322 (qatl-bis-sabab), 148 and 149 of the Pakistan Penal Code. Rescue teams and local residents dug through the rubble for hours after the collapse, which followed heavy overnight rain in the area, according to rescue officials. Deputy Commissioner Afaq Wazir ordered an inquiry into the incident.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif took notice of the tragedy, while President Asif Ali Zardari expressed grief over the deaths.
