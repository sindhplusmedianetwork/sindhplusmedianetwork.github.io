Mashal Yousafzai Using Imran Khan's Health for Personal Gain: Shaukat Basra

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fresh internal rift has surfaced within the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf, with leader Shaukat Basra publicly accusing fellow party member Mashal Yousafzai of exploiting sensitive issues for personal ambition. In a post on X, Basra claimed that Mashal was using Imran Khan's health and the matter of his release to advance her own political interests. He alleged that her real objective is to secure re-election as a senator when the term ends next March.

Basra further claimed that she was attempting to gain access to the PTI founder through conspiracies and a fabricated narrative, adding that Imran Khan himself has sought her expulsion after learning the truth. According to him, her conduct is deepening divisions inside the party at a critical time.
