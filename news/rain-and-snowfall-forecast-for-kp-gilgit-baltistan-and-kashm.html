Rain and Snowfall Forecast for KP, Gilgit-Baltistan and Kashmir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Meteorological Department has forecast dry weather in most parts of the country. However, rain and snowfall are expected on high mountains in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan, Kashmir, Potohar and Islamabad during the evening and night. Islamabad may see rain accompanied by strong winds and thunder.

Fog or smog is expected in parts of Punjab in the morning. The forecast covers the coming evening and night, with dry conditions continuing elsewhere in the country.
