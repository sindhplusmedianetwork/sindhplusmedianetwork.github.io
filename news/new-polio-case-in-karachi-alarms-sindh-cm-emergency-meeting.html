New Polio Case in Karachi Alarms Sindh CM; Emergency Meeting Called

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah has taken strict notice of the confirmation of a new polio case in Karachi, describing it as an alarming development. The case has surfaced despite continuous efforts by the provincial government to eradicate the virus. The chief minister summoned an emergency meeting to review the situation and the response so far.

He directed the health department to carry out a comprehensive investigation into the case and trace the possible source of the infection. Officials were also ordered to take immediate measures to prevent the virus from spreading further in the affected area. The chief minister emphasised that the prescribed protocols for polio eradication must be implemented strictly and without delay.

Health authorities in Sindh have been asked to submit a detailed report on the circumstances of the case.
