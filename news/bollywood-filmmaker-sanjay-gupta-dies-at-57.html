Bollywood Filmmaker Sanjay Gupta Dies at 57

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood director, producer and screenwriter Sanjay Gupta has died at the age of 57, Indian media reported. The news was first carried by Filmfare and Bollywood Hungama, though the cause of death has not been disclosed. Gupta was among the filmmakers who built a distinct identity through action, crime and thriller cinema across a career spanning more than three decades.

He made his directorial debut in 1994 with 'Aatish', starring Sanjay Dutt and Aditya Pancholi. His well-known films include 'Kaante', 'Musafir', 'Zinda', 'Shootout at Lokhandwala' and 'Shootout at Wadala'. The 2002 film 'Kaante' is counted among the standout successes of his career.

He also directed the 2015 film 'Jazbaa' starring Aishwarya Rai and Irrfan Khan, and the 2017 film 'Kaabil' starring Hrithik Roshan. The family has not yet released details about the cause of death or funeral arrangements.
