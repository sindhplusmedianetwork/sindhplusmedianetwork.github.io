USA Joint Bid and UK Confirmed as 2031 and 2035 FIFA Women's World Cup Hosts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

FIFA on Monday confirmed that a joint bid led by the United States will host the expanded, 48-team Women's World Cup in 2031, while the United Kingdom will host the following edition in 2035. The winning bids were approved by acclamation after FIFA president Gianni Infantino confirmed the USA, in partnership with Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica, was the sole candidacy to host in 2031, when the tournament will feature 48 teams for the first time. The joint English, Scottish, Northern Irish and Welsh bid was also the only candidacy for 2035.

Infantino said the 2031 tournament would have 48 teams and four hosts, calling it incredible growth in just a few years. The next Women's World Cup will take place next year in Brazil, running from June 24 to July 25, featuring 32 teams, with the final at the Maracana Stadium in Rio de Janeiro, and Spain go into that tournament as the holders. The USA has hosted the Women's World Cup twice before, in 1999 and 2003, and is the most successful nation in the tournament's history, having lifted the trophy four times.

No senior men's or women's World Cup has been played in the UK since England hosted and won the men's edition in 1966, and the UK bid proposed 22 stadiums in total, with 16 in England, three in Wales, two in Scotland and one in Northern Ireland.
