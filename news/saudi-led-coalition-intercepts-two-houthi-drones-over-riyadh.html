Saudi-Led Coalition Intercepts Two Houthi Drones Over Riyadh, Southern Regions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Saudi-led coalition fighting Yemen's Houthi group said on Friday that it intercepted and destroyed two drones launched toward Saudi Arabia's Riyadh and its southern regions. Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki announced the interceptions on X. The Saudi-led coalition also asked civilians to evacuate areas in the strategic Red Sea port of Hodeidah for their safety.

Earlier, Saudi air defences intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile launched by Yemen's Houthi movement towards the kingdom's southern region, the coalition said, as cross-border attacks between the two sides showed no signs of abating. Saudi Civil Defence also reported that drones had been intercepted in the southern region and issued early-warning alerts for several areas, including Jazan, Abha and Khamis Mushait, before announcing that the danger had passed. In Yemen, Houthi-run Al-Masirah TV reported Saudi airstrikes on the capital Sanaa on Friday, while there was no immediate comment from Saudi Arabia on the reported strikes.

The latest attacks came less than a day after Riyadh's tallest skyscraper, the headquarters of the Saudi sovereign wealth fund, was struck in a Houthi attack on Thursday. Attacks between Saudi Arabia and the Houthis resumed in July, following a period of relative calm after a truce in 2022.
