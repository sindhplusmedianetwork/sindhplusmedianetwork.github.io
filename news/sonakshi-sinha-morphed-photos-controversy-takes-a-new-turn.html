Sonakshi Sinha Morphed-Photos Controversy Takes a New Turn

Bollywood actress Sonakshi Sinha's complaint over her morphed photos led Bandra police to arrest Aryan Deepak Jain from Delhi. A Mumbai court later rejected the police request for three-day custody of the accused. The court cited procedural flaws in the arrest.

It ordered his release. The court's decision marks a new turn in the controversy. Police investigations continue despite the release.

The case has drawn attention to the misuse of morphed images of public figures.
