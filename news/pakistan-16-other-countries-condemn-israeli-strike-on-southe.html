Pakistan, 16 other countries condemn Israeli strike on southern Gaza hospital

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and 16 other countries have condemned in the strongest terms the Israeli airstrike on the Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis, in the southern Gaza Strip. The strike killed at least six Palestinians, including a 13-year-old boy, and wounded nearly 30 others. In a joint statement issued on Saturday, the foreign ministers of the 17 countries said the loss of patients and medical staff violated international humanitarian law, which gives special protection to medical facilities and healthcare workers.

The countries include Pakistan, Egypt, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Turkiye, the United Arab Emirates, Jordan, Indonesia, Palestine, Algeria, Mauritania, Sudan, Libya, Djibouti, Somalia, Lebanon and Oman. The ministers also condemned continued Israeli attacks on Palestinian civilians, including strikes on tents sheltering displaced people. They said these attacks undermine the ceasefire and efforts to restore security and stability in Gaza.

They also called for the sustained and unimpeded delivery of humanitarian aid, shelter materials and medical supplies to Gaza as winter approaches.
