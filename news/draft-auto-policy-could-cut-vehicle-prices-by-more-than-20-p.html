Draft auto policy could cut vehicle prices by more than 20 per cent

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's draft Auto Policy 2026-31 has raised hopes that vehicles could become significantly cheaper in the coming years. Under the proposed GST changes, petrol vehicles are expected to become 8 to 10 per cent cheaper, while plug-in hybrid vehicles could become more than 20 per cent cheaper. The draft maintains a 1 per cent GST rate on battery electric vehicles, while setting 9 per cent on plug-in hybrids and 6 per cent on range-extended electric vehicles.

Industry observers say the policy could reshape buying choices if the tax relief is fully passed on to consumers. Auto parts manufacturers, however, warned that rising completely built-up imports and lower tariffs would hurt the local industry. They pointed to a Rs7.3 billion revenue loss from imported vehicles in September as a sign of the pressure on domestic makers.
