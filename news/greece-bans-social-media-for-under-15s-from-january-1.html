Greece Bans Social Media for Under-15s From January 1

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Greece's parliament passed a law on Friday banning children under 15 from social media, joining a growing number of countries imposing curbs on young people's use of the apps. Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis's conservative New Democracy party used its majority to pass the bill, which no other party backed. The opposition Pasok abstained after seeking an under-13 ban instead.

The bill takes effect on January 1 and will require age verification on social networks. Companies that fail to comply will face harsh penalties under the new law. Athens says the measure aims to protect children from the harms of excessive social media use.
