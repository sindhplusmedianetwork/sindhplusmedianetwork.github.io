Anthropic set for $1.8 trillion valuation in Nasdaq debut

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

AI company Anthropic was on course to secure a $1.8 trillion valuation in its Nasdaq debut on Thursday. Its stock was set to open at $99.82, according to premarket indications. That price is about 20 percent higher than its $83 IPO price.

Anthropic, the maker of the Claude chatbot, sold 980 million shares at $83 each. The IPO was priced above its initial range of $70 to $78, with proceeds potentially reaching $93.5 billion if underwriters fully exercise their option. Anthropic is the first frontier AI company to go public, and CEO Dario Amodei and his six co-founders together own about 10 percent of the company.
