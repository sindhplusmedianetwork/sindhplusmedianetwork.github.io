Book Leaks Details of Queen Mette-Marit's Lung Transplant, Drawing Outrage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new Norwegian book has published previously unknown details of Queen Mette-Marit's lung transplant, prompting condemnation from her doctor and an admission of error from its author. The book, Eventyrnettene, about the Norwegian national football team's World Cup run, includes a passage claiming that during Norway's match against Iraq, Mette-Marit received new lungs from a donor who had died suddenly in an accident in the Nordic region. Until the book appeared, the exact timing of the operation and the details of the donation had not been made public.

Her primary physician at Oslo's Rikshospitalet, Are Martin Holm, condemned the publication as a breach of medical confidentiality that could risk identifying the donor. Author Alfred Fidjestol has since admitted the timing claim was wrong, telling the newspaper Dagbladet that he should have written that she had recently undergone surgery, not that it happened on the day of the match. The Norwegian palace announced on June 17 that Mette-Marit, 53, had undergone a successful lung transplant.

She was diagnosed with the incurable condition pulmonary fibrosis in 2018, and her father-in-law, King Harald V, died on August 28.
