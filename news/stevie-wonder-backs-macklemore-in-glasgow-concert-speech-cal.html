Stevie Wonder backs Macklemore in Glasgow concert speech calling for an end to wars

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Stevie Wonder publicly defended rapper Macklemore during his concert at Glasgow's OVO Hydro on November 11, delivering a speech calling for an end to wars and greater protection for people around the world. The performance marked the conclusion of the European leg of Wonder's Songs in the Key of Life 50th anniversary tour and his first visit to Scotland since 1989, and the concert also coincided with Remembrance Day in the UK. According to footage filmed by Far Out Magazine, Wonder paused during the show to discuss a singer who had faced a ban after speaking out against wars, and after discussing the matter with his daughter, backing vocalist Aisha Morris, he identified the artist as Macklemore.

"We want wars to stop everywhere. We want every person in life to be protected from the destruction of war," Wonder told the audience. Addressing the crowd's enthusiastic response, he added: "Ban me as you want.

I'm only speaking the words that are given to me by the most high, our God. Let's bring peace to the world." The comments mark Wonder's first known public show of support for Macklemore following the rapper's removal from Ed Sheeran's The Loop Tour in September after declaring "Free Palestine" on stage. Macklemore subsequently launched a Free Palestine tour, visiting Dublin, Paris and London, but recently revealed that nine major US arenas, including New York's Madison Square Garden, had blocked him from bringing the show to the country.

Wonder's tour continues with another Glasgow performance scheduled for November 19.
