Trump Vows to Secure Permanent Deal With Iran on Nuclear Issue

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said he intends to secure a permanent deal with Iran over its nuclear programme. He insisted that no future American president should be able to allow Tehran to obtain a nuclear weapon. Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Tehran was seeking an agreement that would ensure there was no further war.

The remarks came as two tankers caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz. The exchange underlines the continuing standoff between Washington and Tehran over the nuclear issue.
