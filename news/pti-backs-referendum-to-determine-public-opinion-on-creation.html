PTI Backs Referendum to Determine Public Opinion on Creation of New Provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) senior leader and Opposition Chief Whip in the National Assembly Aamir Dogar on Friday backed a referendum to determine public opinion on the creation of new provinces, saying any decision on the matter should reflect the people's will and be made in accordance with the law. Speaking to reporters outside Parliament House, Dogar described a referendum as an effective way to establish public opinion on the issue. He said PTI founder Imran Khan had consistently advocated the creation of smaller provinces, adding that a separate South Punjab province had been included in the party's 2018 election manifesto.

Dogar said any move to create new provinces should have public approval and follow constitutional and legal procedures, and that PTI's final position would be determined by Imran Khan. He said discussions on the proposed 28th Constitutional Amendment remained confined to the media and social media, and PTI would not take a position until a draft was formally presented in parliament. Asked whether support for a South Punjab province also extended to a separate province for Karachi, he said the matter would first be discussed within the party's internal forums.

He added that the date of the proposed All Parties Conference in Islamabad would be finalised through consensus within the next one or two days.
