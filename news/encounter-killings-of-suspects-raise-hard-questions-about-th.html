Encounter killings of suspects raise hard questions about the justice system

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A recent report has raised uncomfortable questions about Pakistan's justice system over the killing of accused persons in alleged police encounters. It asks why those accused were not tried and sentenced under the Pakistan Penal Code, and what stands in the way of fair trials. The Human Rights Commission of Pakistan had already demanded a judicial inquiry into what it calls CCD encounters back in February.

According to the commission, 924 suspects were killed in 670 CCD encounters in 2025 alone. Rights advocates argue that such numbers point to a pattern of extrajudicial killings rather than genuine shootouts. The findings have renewed calls for independent oversight of police actions and for the courts to take up the matter.
