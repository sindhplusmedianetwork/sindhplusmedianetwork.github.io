VXR Festival Brings Immersive Art by More Than 70 Artists to Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Virtual and Extended Reality (VXR) Festival opened on Friday at the Pakistan National Council of the Arts in Islamabad, bringing Pakistani and international artists together with art lovers, students and the wider public. The Islamabad edition, titled 'Between the Prompts', features work by more than 70 artists exploring the creative possibilities of virtual and extended reality. The festival was formally inaugurated in the evening by the Ambassador of France to Pakistan, Jean-Noël Poirier, in the presence of diplomats, cultural representatives, artists and members of the public.

Director General of PNCA Ayoub Jamali welcomed the guests as host of the venue, alongside French Cultural Attaché Zakaria Haffar, and visitors used VR headsets to explore digital environments created by artists from Pakistan and abroad. The edition is curated by festival director Dr Najam Ul Assar and co-curator Sarah Rajper, with Zainab Amir as assistant curator, and brings together artists from Rawalpindi, Lahore, Paris, Madrid and Berkeley. The festival is organised by the Lahore Digital Arts Festival team, with support from its international cultural partners, including the Embassy of France, the Embassy of Spain, Institut Français and the Spanish Agency for International Development Cooperation.

VXR is a travelling festival without a permanent venue; after a week-long programme in Lahore and the Islamabad edition, it will move on to Karachi.
