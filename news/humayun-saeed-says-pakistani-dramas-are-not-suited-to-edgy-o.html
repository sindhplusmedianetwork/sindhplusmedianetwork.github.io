Humayun Saeed Says Pakistani Dramas Are Not Suited to Edgy OTT Content

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor and producer Humayun Saeed has offered an explanation for the absence of Pakistani dramas from global streaming platforms, arguing that the industry's distinctive style does not align with how most over-the-top (OTT) platforms work. The gap, he believes, is one of compatibility rather than quality. Saeed made his remarks during an interview with Ali Salman published on Monday, saying that Pakistani dramas have a unique style of their own, with their own style of writing and of delivering lines because they are spoken in Urdu.

He said that OTT content is edgy and different, and that the Pakistani industry is not really good at that kind of content, although there are some people in the industry who can tell OTT stories but no suitable platforms for them. Saeed acknowledged that political reasons may play a role in Pakistan's absence from Netflix, though he did not elaborate on that point. He said the local industry should prioritise creating its own OTT platform, which could help attract the attention of streaming giants like Netflix, noting that in India domestic OTT platforms came first and have been developing for the past 12 to 13 years.

He added that the Pakistani government should take steps to talk to Netflix for a resolution. Saeed is one of many celebrities who have weighed in on the subject, after Urwa Hocane said last week that Pakistan does not need to obsess over Netflix, and Federal Minister for Planning and Development Ahsan Iqbal announced in July that the government was in talks with Netflix and other global streaming giants to adjust the regional framework.
