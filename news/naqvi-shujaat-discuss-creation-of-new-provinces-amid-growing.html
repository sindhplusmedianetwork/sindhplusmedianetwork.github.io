Naqvi, Shujaat discuss creation of new provinces amid growing political debate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi met PML-Q chief Chaudhry Shujaat Hussain at his residence in Lahore. The two leaders discussed the creation of new provinces and the current national political situation. Shujaat said the creation of new provinces was linked to public convenience and better governance.

He said governance suffers because of the country's massive population. Federal Minister Salik Hussain was also present at the meeting. The meeting comes amid a growing political debate over new provinces.
