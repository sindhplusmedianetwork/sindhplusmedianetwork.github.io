Using mosques for terrorism is desecration of sacred places, says PM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has praised the security forces' successful intelligence-based operation in Mir Ali, North Waziristan. He said terrorists' use of mosques to store explosives and as centres of violence is a clear desecration of Islam and sacred places. The prime minister said using mosques for terrorism amounts to desecrating sacred places.

He also condemned Israel's measures depriving the people of Gaza of humanitarian aid. The statement came after the security operation in Mir Ali, which the prime minister described as successful.
