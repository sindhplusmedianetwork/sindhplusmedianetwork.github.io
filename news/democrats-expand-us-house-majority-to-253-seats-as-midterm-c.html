Democrats Expand US House Majority to 253 Seats as Midterm Count Continues

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Democrats expanded their majority in the US House of Representatives as more results emerged from Tuesday's midterm elections, securing 253 seats against 157 for Republicans with 25 races still uncalled. The party also secured control of the Senate with a combined 54 seats alongside two independents. Democrats led governorships 28-20, with Alaska and Kansas still too close to call.

Democrats flipped several Republican-held House seats, including in New Jersey, New York, Ohio, North Carolina, Colorado and Arizona.
