Ticket sales begin for first Pakistan-Sri Lanka Test in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ticket sales for the first Test between Pakistan and Sri Lanka begin today, Friday, from 2pm at designated TCS Express centres. The PCB has fixed ticket prices for all five days of the Rawalpindi Test. General, first-class, premium and VIP enclosure tickets cost Rs200, the PCB gallery costs Rs800 a day and the platinum box costs Rs8,000 a day.

The first Test will be played from November 9 to 13. Both Tests in the series are part of the ICC World Test Championship 2025-27.
