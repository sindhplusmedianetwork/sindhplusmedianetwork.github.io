Search Continues for 36 Missing Workers After Indonesia Candy Factory Fire Kills Three

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rescue teams in Indonesia are searching for 36 missing workers after a fire at a candy factory killed three people. The fire broke out on Saturday afternoon at the factory in Cikande, Serang, in Banten province. Fifteen people were rescued alive from the building, including four who were injured.

Parts of the roof of the three-storey building collapsed during the fire. Rescue teams continued their search for the missing workers after the blaze. Three people were confirmed dead in the incident, while the search for the 36 missing workers remained under way.
