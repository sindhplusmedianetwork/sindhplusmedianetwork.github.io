PHC Summons IG Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in Person Over Non-Recovery of Missing Child

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Peshawar High Court Chief Justice S.M. Attique Shah has ordered the Inspector General of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to appear before the court in person. The order relates to the non-recovery of a missing child from Faqirabad, Peshawar.

The child was abducted five months ago and has not been recovered since. According to the case record, a ransom call made in connection with the abduction has been traced. The court expressed dissatisfaction over the lack of progress in recovering the child.

The chief justice directed the provincial police chief to personally explain the situation to the court.
