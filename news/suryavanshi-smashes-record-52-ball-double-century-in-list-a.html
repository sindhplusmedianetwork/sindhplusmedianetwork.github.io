Suryavanshi smashes record 52-ball double century in List A cricket

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's 15-year-old batting sensation Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has rewritten the record books, smashing the fastest double-century in List A cricket with a remarkable innings of 204 off just 53 balls against the United Arab Emirates in the Asia Cup Rising Stars event in Doha. Sooryavanshi reached his hundred in only 28 deliveries, breaking the previous record of 29 balls held by Australia's Jake Fraser-McGurk. The left-handed batter accelerated further after reaching three figures, scoring his second hundred in just 24 balls to bring up his record double-century off 52 deliveries.

His effort comfortably surpassed the previous List A record for the fastest double-century, which belonged to South Africa-born New Zealand cricketer Chad Bowes, who had reached the milestone in 103 balls. The innings also saw him set a new benchmark for the fastest 150 in the format, reaching the landmark in 40 balls, breaking his own previous record of 59 deliveries set while representing Bihar against Arunachal Pradesh in domestic cricket last year. The teenager now holds four major List A batting records at the same time, having earlier this year registered the fastest fifty in the format, reaching the milestone in just 11 balls against Sri Lanka A.

His innings helped India chase a 250-run target in just 14.2 overs with seven wickets in hand, with Rinku Singh contributing an unbeaten 27 off 19 balls.
