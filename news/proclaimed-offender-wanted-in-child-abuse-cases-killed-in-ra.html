Proclaimed Offender Wanted in Child Abuse Cases Killed in Rawalpindi Firing

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A proclaimed offender wanted in serious cases, including the sexual abuse of minors, has been killed in an exchange of fire with a CCD team in Rawalpindi. The encounter took place on Daulta Main Road within the jurisdiction of the Jatli police station. The deceased was identified as Hammad, also known as Madu.

He was wanted in cases of robbery-cum-murder, attempted murder and the sexual abuse of young children. Police said he also shared videos of aerial firing on social media. Other suspects managed to flee from the scene.

The body was shifted to the tehsil hospital for a post-mortem examination.
