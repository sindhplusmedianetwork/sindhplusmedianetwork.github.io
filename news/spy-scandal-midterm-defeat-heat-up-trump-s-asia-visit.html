Spy scandal, midterm defeat heat up Trump's Asia visit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump headed to Asia on Monday after a midterm election defeat for his Republicans and allegations involving a suspected Chinese spy raised the stakes for his meeting with China's Xi Jinping. As he left the White House, Trump said he might also meet North Korean leader Kim Jong Un in China, and that he would discuss denuclearisation, Iran and Ukraine in three-way talks with Xi and Russia's Vladimir Putin. Trump will first visit Manila for meetings with Southeast Asian leaders before travelling to Shenzhen for the APEC forum.

The visit comes at a sensitive moment for the White House, with the spy allegations and the election defeat both hanging over the trip.
