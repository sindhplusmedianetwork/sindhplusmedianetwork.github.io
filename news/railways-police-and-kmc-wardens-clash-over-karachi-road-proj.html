Railways Police and KMC Wardens Clash Over Karachi Road Project

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A clash broke out in Karachi between Pakistan Railways police and KMC City Wardens over a road project, with railway police taking the chief of the KMC City Wardens to a police station after a physical scuffle. The dispute centres on a dual carriageway linking Natha Khan Bridge with Pehlwan Goth and Habib University, running parallel to Chakor Nullah as an alternative route for Gulistan-i-Jauhar commuters. Valued at about Rs780 million, the project is meant to ease pressure on Sharea Faisal, Rashid Minhas Road, Dalmia Road and Gulistan-i-Jauhar, with an October 30 completion deadline looming.

Railways claims the road encroaches on Karachi Circular Railway land and was built without authorisation, while KMC says railways never raised the objection before and all legal requirements were fulfilled before the launch more than two months ago. KMC alleged that railway police severely beat City Wardens Director Saim Imran before taking him to a Pakistan Railways police station. The confrontation began on Sunday afternoon when railways police intervened at the construction site and stopped the work; the KMC City Wardens chief arrived with his contingent, leading to a heated exchange that escalated.

The dispute threatens a wider political dimension between the PML-N-led federal government and the PPP, which governs Sindh and Karachi's municipal administration.
