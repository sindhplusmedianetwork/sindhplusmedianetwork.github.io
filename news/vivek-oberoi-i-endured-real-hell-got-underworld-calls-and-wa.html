Vivek Oberoi: I Endured Real Hell, Got Underworld Calls and Was Blacklisted

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Vivek Oberoi has said he endured real hell, received calls from the underworld and was blacklisted by an influential lobby in the film industry. The 50-year-old actor spoke on a podcast about the severe crisis and the worst period in his film career after his famous press conference in 2003. He said he was punished for raising his voice against an injustice and for the sake of respect for women.

Director Apoorva Lakhia said in a recent interview that senior actors and producers supported casting Vivek in 'Shootout at Lokhandwala' despite the criticism. Vivek said the period tested him deeply, but he continued to work in films. His remarks have drawn attention on social media, where fans have praised his honesty about the difficult years.
