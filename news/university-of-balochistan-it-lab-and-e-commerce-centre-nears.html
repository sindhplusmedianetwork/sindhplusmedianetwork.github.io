University of Balochistan IT Lab and E-Commerce Centre Nears Completion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A review meeting on the establishment of a state-of-the-art Information Technology Lab and E-Commerce Centre at the University of Balochistan was held under the chairmanship of Deputy Commissioner Quetta, Mehrullah Badini, to assess progress and discuss administrative and operational matters. During the meeting, officials said work on the modern facility has entered its final phase. Upon completion, the Deputy Commissioner is expected to visit the facility, while a formal inauguration ceremony is also likely to take place in the near future.

The project aims to equip young people with practical skills in emerging technologies, information technology and e-commerce. It is designed to help them benefit from new employment and entrepreneurial opportunities in the digital economy. Participants reviewed the implementation progress and discussed the remaining requirements needed for the facility's timely completion and operational launch.

Deputy Commissioner Badini stressed the importance of expediting the remaining work and ensuring that all administrative and logistical arrangements are completed without delay. He directed the relevant authorities to maintain momentum so that the facility can be made operational as soon as possible.
