Fact Check: Eiffel Tower Palestinian Flag Video Is AI-Generated, Declared False

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A viral video claiming to show a Palestinian flag hoisted at the top of the Eiffel Tower during protests in Paris has been declared false by a fact check. The Express Tribune's iVerify desk examined the clip, which has been shared on X since October 19 and viewed millions of times. The verification found the video to be AI-generated rather than real footage.

AI-detection tools flagged the video as between roughly 50 and 57 per cent machine-made, pointing to visual inconsistencies in the flags and the tower's structure. The fact check also noted that no credible French or international media outlets have reported any incident of protesters hoisting a Palestinian flag atop the tower. The verdict is clear: the viral claim is false, and the video circulating online is an AI creation, not genuine footage from Paris.
