ECC asks for placement status of 885 agri professionals trained in China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Economic Coordination Committee has asked the food and education ministries to disclose where the 885 agricultural professionals trained in China have been placed. The professionals were trained under a Rs3,072 million programme that was launched after the prime minister's visit to China in 2024. In the same meeting, the ECC approved a technical supplementary grant of Rs1,666.176 million for the prime minister's initiative to train 1,000 agricultural professionals in China.

The committee wants a clear account of how the trained experts are being utilised. The decisions were taken during a meeting of the Economic Coordination Committee.
