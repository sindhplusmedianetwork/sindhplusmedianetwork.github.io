Pakistan's trade deficit rises 16 percent to over 14 billion dollars in four months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's trade deficit increased by approximately 16 percent during the first four months of the financial year. According to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, the deficit reached 14.628 billion dollars from July to October, marking an annual increase of more than 2 billion dollars. The trade deficit stood at 12.614 billion dollars during the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

During the four months, exports totalled 11.33 billion dollars, an increase of 8.52 percent, while imports exceeded 25.96 billion dollars, an increase of 12.59 percent. The monthly trade deficit increased by 10.30 percent to 3.87 billion dollars in October, and compared with October 2025, the trade deficit rose by 19.70 percent. The figures show imports continuing to grow faster than exports, widening the gap between what Pakistan buys from abroad and what it sells.
