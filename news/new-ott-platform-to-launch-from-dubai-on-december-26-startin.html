New OTT platform to launch from Dubai on December 26, starting with UAE and India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new entertainment platform is preparing to launch its over-the-top (OTT) service from Dubai on December 26, 2026. The United Arab Emirates and India will be its first two markets. The platform aims to offer digital entertainment at low cost.

It aims to create new opportunities for filmmakers and production companies. An introductory monthly subscription has been proposed at 10 Indian rupees in India and 5 dirhams in the UAE. The project is led by Emirati businessman Hameed Mohammed Al Kalbani.

It has an initial budget of about 52.9 million US dollars. Final packages and the full content line-up are yet to be announced.
