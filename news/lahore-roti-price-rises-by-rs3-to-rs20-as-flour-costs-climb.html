Lahore Roti Price Rises by Rs3 to Rs20 as Flour Costs Climb

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The price of roti in Lahore has risen by Rs3, with a roti earlier sold at Rs17 now being sold for Rs20, residents said on Sunday. Citizens complain that along with the price hike, the dough weight of the roti has also been reduced. Tandoor owners say flour prices rise every week, maida bags have become costlier, and gas shortages force them to run their tandoors on gas cylinders, pushing up costs.

The hike has added to the burden on households already struggling with rising food prices. Residents have demanded that the authorities take notice of the price increase and ensure that the official weight of roti is maintained.
