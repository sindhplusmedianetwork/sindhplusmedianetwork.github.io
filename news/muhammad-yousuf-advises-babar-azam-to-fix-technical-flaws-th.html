Muhammad Yousuf Advises Babar Azam to Fix Technical Flaws Through Specialized Coaching

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former national captain and renowned batsman Muhammad Yousuf has advised Babar Azam not to play first-class cricket until he overcomes his batting technical flaws, and instead obtain specialized coaching at a cricket academy in England or Australia. In a post on social media platform X, Yousuf wrote that struggling in first-class cricket could further harm Babar Azam's confidence and career, so he should pay special attention to his batting. He said Babar should be sent to an academy in England or Australia so he can rectify his technical issues under the supervision of expert coaches.

Yousuf added that Babar is still Pakistan's best player and can return to his best form with the right guidance and a little effort. He further wrote that he had said the same thing four years ago but nobody listened, and asked people to listen now. It may be recalled that Babar Azam is scheduled to represent Lahore in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy from November 3, while the two-Test series between Pakistan and Sri Lanka is set to begin on November 9.
