NCCIA Karachi Raids Apartment, Arrests 42 Over Online Pornographic Animation Network

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency's Karachi wing has raided an apartment and arrested 42 people in a major operation. The suspects belonged to three companies — Pixel Craft, Art Office and Art Design — allegedly involved in preparing, promoting and selling pornographic cartoon animations and digital content to foreign clients. Officials said the firms ran organised teams of online agents who contacted foreign customers on Instagram and X using fake identities and women's names.

Investigators seized 41 mobile phones for forensic analysis. The agency said the network operated as a structured business targeting overseas buyers. Further arrests are expected as the investigation widens.
