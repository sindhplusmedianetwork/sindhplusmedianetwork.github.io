Three Terrorists Killed in Swat Intelligence-Based Operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three wanted militants linked to the Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan were killed on Thursday in a joint intelligence-based operation by the Swat Counter-Terrorism Department and district police in the Kanju area. Acting on confirmed intelligence that fighters from TTP commander Muzaffar alias Khalid's group were hiding in the mountainous Segram and Lalo Banda stretches to plan sabotage attacks, security personnel launched a well-prepared raid. At Sakhadoob, the militants opened fire as soon as they spotted the forces, then took positions inside a nearby house and kept up indiscriminate firing until the exchange ended.

A search of the house afterwards yielded the bodies of three terrorists, while two of their companions managed to escape and are being actively tracked. From the dead militants' possession, forces recovered three AK-47 Kalashnikov rifles, six magazines, a large quantity of ammunition, a cooking gas cylinder and utensils, along with the men's national identity cards, a map of Shakardara police station and a diary. The slain men were identified as Abdullah son of Syed Bashir and Mansoor Ali son of Abdul Muttalib, both residents of Neela Gram in Matta, and Ghufranullah son of Afzal Khan of Shidala in Shakardara.

Preliminary information indicates the three were involved in multiple terrorist incidents this year, including a firing attack on Afzal Khan Gujjar, firing on the house of Nemat Ali Khan, and the targeted killing of Saleemullah alias Sajid of Neela Gram.
