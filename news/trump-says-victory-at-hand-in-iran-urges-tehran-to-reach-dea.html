Trump says 'victory at hand' in Iran, urges Tehran to reach deal

US President Donald Trump said victory was at hand in Iran and urged Tehran to reach a deal as tensions flared in the region. Iran's Revolutionary Guard said a tanker hit a mine and exploded in the Strait of Hormuz. The remarks came amid heightened tensions between the United States and Iran over a maritime blockade.

The tanker incident in the strait followed the flare-up in tensions around the blockade. Trump's call for a deal came as the confrontation between Washington and Tehran continued.
