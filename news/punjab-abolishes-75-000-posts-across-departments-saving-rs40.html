Punjab Abolishes 75,000 Posts Across Departments, Saving Rs40 Billion a Year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has abolished 75,000 posts in 44 departments and subordinate institutions under its right-sizing policy, which aims to remove vacant posts, reorganise unnecessary positions and run office work through digital systems. According to finance department sources, the move saves between Rs35 billion and Rs40 billion every year. The abolished posts include 30,391 in school education from grades one to 16, 5,199 in agriculture, 753 in forests, 1,041 in irrigation, 727 in housing, 244 in special education and 221 in the prosecution department and its subordinate bodies.

The government said these posts had been vacant for a long time, and in August it approved the abolition of 101 more posts, including in the South Punjab Secretariat. At the Civil Secretariat, 128 posts of grade-17 section officers were abolished and replaced with a policy of hiring experts in law, finance, information technology, artificial intelligence and data analysis. In May 2025, the school education department approved 26,898 new posts for 1,961 new and upgraded schools, but instead of fresh hiring it decided to adjust existing teaching and support staff to schools according to need.
