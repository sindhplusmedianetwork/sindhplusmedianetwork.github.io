Salman Khan Says He Has No Issue With People Earning From Criticising Him

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood superstar Salman Khan has spoken about criticism against him on social media. During promotion of his new film, he said he has no issue with people who earn money by criticising him. He also said he has no issue with people who increase their followers in this way.

Salman Khan referred to rumours that he no longer has the same energy as before. He said that if people are earning money by doing all this, there is no harm in it. The trailer of the action film Matrubhoomi, starring Salman Khan and Chitrangada Singh, was recently released.
