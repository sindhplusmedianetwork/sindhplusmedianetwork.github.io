SNGPL limits gas supply in Punjab, KP and Islamabad to cooking hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited has restricted gas supply to domestic consumers in Islamabad, Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to cooking hours only, effective Wednesday. According to Geo News, the decision was taken in view of reduced LNG availability and rising demand during the winter season. Under the new schedule, consumers will receive gas from 6:30am to 9am, 12pm to 2:30pm, and 6pm to 9pm daily.

Given the limited reserves, the government has decided to prioritise domestic consumers over other sectors. The company has also reduced gas supply to the CNG sector from 50 million cubic feet per day to 35 million cubic feet, and plans to shut it off completely in the coming days.
