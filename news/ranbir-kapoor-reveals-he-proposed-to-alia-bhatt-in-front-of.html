Ranbir Kapoor reveals he proposed to Alia Bhatt in front of three lions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Ranbir Kapoor has shared the story of how he proposed to Alia Bhatt, revealing it happened in Kenya's Maasai Mara jungle with three lions nearby. He told the story on the program 'Kaun Banega Crorepati 18,' where he appeared with the team of his film 'Ramayan.' Ranbir said he had instructed his guide, who was also the driver, to stop the vehicle at a spot in the middle of the jungle. He had brought a ring along and planned to go down on one knee to propose to Alia.

Host Amitabh Bachchan jokingly remarked that there should have been proof of the moment. Ranbir said that on one side, about 100 meters away, were three lions, while his 'lioness' Alia stood in front of him as he knelt to ask her to marry him. He described it as one of the most beautiful moments of his life, one he will always remember.

Ranbir and Alia's friendship turned into love during the making of 'Brahmastra' in 2018. He proposed to her in 2022, and the couple married on April 14, 2022, at their home in Mumbai.
