183 Pakistani personnel sacrificed their lives in UN peace missions, says Khawaja Asif

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Muhammad Asif has said Pakistan has sent 237,000 personnel to 48 United Nations peace missions over seven decades, with 183 of them losing their lives in the line of duty. He was addressing the Schuman Security and Defence Forum at the European Parliament in Brussels, where he highlighted Pakistan's long-standing contribution to international peacekeeping. He said cooperative security policy and shared responsibility are indispensable for global peace, and no country can secure lasting stability alone.

The minister warned that sudden cuts to peace missions endanger human security in the regions where they operate. He said lasting peace requires human protection, economic improvement and social development together. Pakistan, he added, remains committed to supporting peace efforts through its armed forces and its cooperation with international partners.
