Viral APC parade in Karachi's DHA lands two in police net

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A viral video showing a convoy of armoured personnel carriers parading through Defence Housing Authority in Karachi has prompted a police crackdown, with South Zone police arresting two suspects and registering a case against six people. According to a South Zone police spokesperson, the video surfaced on social media a few weeks ago and showed a parade of armoured vehicles in DHA, creating panic among residents. Police said a case, FIR No 315/2026, had been registered at Sahil police station, nominating six people including Noreen Sheikh, owner of 4x4 Extreme, and Sufyan, manager of S&T Tech Armoured.

Sections 420, 170, 171, 268 and 290 of the Pakistan Penal Code have been included in the case. During the operation, police seized a Land Cruiser and secured video evidence related to the incident. Legal proceedings are under way to arrest the remaining nominated suspects and vehicle owners, and police said they are strictly opposed to the display of weapons and VIP vehicle culture, urging citizens to abide by the law.
