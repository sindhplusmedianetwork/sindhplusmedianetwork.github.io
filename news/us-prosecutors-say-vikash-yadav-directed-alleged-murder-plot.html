US Prosecutors Say Vikash Yadav Directed Alleged Murder Plot in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US prosecutors have said Indian government official Vikash Yadav directed an alleged assassination conspiracy in Pakistan, according to a sentencing document in the Gurpatwant Singh Pannun murder-plot case. Prosecutors recommended a sentence of 24 years and five months for Nikhil Gupta, who pleaded guilty in the Pannun plot. The filing says Gupta messaged Yadav in May 2023 that operatives sent to kill a target in Pakistan had arrived.

It says Yadav sought updates and urged extra payment to the hitmen. Gupta is due to be sentenced on November 20.
