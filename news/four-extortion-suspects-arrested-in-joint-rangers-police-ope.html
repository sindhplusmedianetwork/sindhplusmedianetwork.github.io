Four Extortion Suspects Arrested in Joint Rangers-Police Operation in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Rangers Sindh and the Special Investigation Unit (SIU) of Karachi Police arrested four suspected members of the extortion mafia in a joint operation in Paposh Nagar, Karachi. Three suspects were injured in an exchange of fire during the operation, while the fourth was arrested without resistance. The arrested men are said to belong to the Samad Kathiawari and Jamil Chhanga groups.

The injured suspects were identified as Zameer alias Prince, Muhammad Shakir and Barkat Ali alias Braha, while the fourth suspect was identified as Muhammad Zaheem. Weapons and ammunition were recovered from the arrested men. According to Rangers, this was the fourth major joint operation against the extortion mafia in Karachi since October 24, 2026.
