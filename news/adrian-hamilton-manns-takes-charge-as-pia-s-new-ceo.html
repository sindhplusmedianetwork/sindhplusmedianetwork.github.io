Adrian Hamilton Manns Takes Charge as PIA's New CEO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Adrian Hamilton Manns has formally assumed charge as the new chief executive officer of Pakistan International Airlines, the PIA Consortium and board announced. He brings 30 years of commercial aviation experience to the role, having previously served as CEO of three airlines in Oman, Kazakhstan and Africa. He is regarded as a specialist in bringing reforms to the aviation industry.

The board also thanked outgoing CEO Air Vice Marshal Amir Hayat for his services. It credited his tenure with a marked improvement in PIA's flight safety standards, the end of European and British restrictions, and preparing the airline for privatisation. The board expressed hope that Hamilton Manns will use his extensive experience to raise service quality and place the airline among the leading carriers.
