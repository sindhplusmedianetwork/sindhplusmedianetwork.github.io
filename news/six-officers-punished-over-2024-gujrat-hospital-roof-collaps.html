Six Officers Punished Over 2024 Gujrat Hospital Roof Collapse

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Six officers have faced punishment over the collapse of a surgical-ward roof at Aziz Bhatti Shaheed Teaching Hospital in Gujrat in April 2024. The incident had killed two patients and injured several others. Three officers of the Buildings Department had three years of their service confiscated following the inquiry.

Three doctors involved were stopped from promotion and denied their annual increments for two years. The action follows findings that linked the tragedy to negligence. The roof collapse had drawn widespread attention to maintenance standards at the teaching hospital.

Families of the victims had long demanded accountability for the loss of life.
