Petrol drops by Rs2, diesel remains unchanged till Nov 16

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Friday reduced the price of petrol by Rs2 per litre. The new price of petrol is Rs330 per litre. High-speed diesel remains unchanged at Rs360 per litre for November 14 to 16, according to a Petroleum Division notification.

The revision comes a day after petrol and diesel were cut by Rs3 and Rs4 per litre respectively for November 13. The latest change sets the fuel prices for the three-day period beginning November 14.
