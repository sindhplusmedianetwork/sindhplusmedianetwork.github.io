Man arrested in Lahore for allegedly assaulting woman on marriage pretext

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore police have arrested a suspect, Shehroz, accused of allegedly sexually assaulting and torturing a young woman for eight months on the pretext of marriage. The suspect is also accused of assaulting her when she insisted on marriage. SP Model Town Asad Ali said a formal case has been registered and the matter referred to the Gender Cell for investigation.

In a separate incident, police took a woman found in a distressed condition on a roadside into protective custody. She was shifted to a safe location.
