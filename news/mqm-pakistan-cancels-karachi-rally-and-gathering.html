MQM-Pakistan Cancels Karachi Rally and Gathering

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan has decided to cancel its rally and public gathering in Karachi scheduled for tomorrow, November 29. According to MQM sources, the final decision will be announced today at a press conference to be held at the Bahadurabad centre. Sources said the gathering was cancelled keeping the law and order situation in view.

Meanwhile, the Commissioner Karachi has refused permission to MQM to hold a rally on Shahrah-e-Faisal. According to the Commissioner's office, police authorities had recommended not granting permission for a rally on Shahrah-e-Faisal, including at Mazar-e-Quaid, in light of which permission could not be granted. The Commissioner's office has directed MQM-Pakistan to submit a new proposal regarding the rally.

It is worth noting that earlier, the Sindh government's decision not to grant MQM permission to hold a rally on Shahrah-e-Faisal on November 29 had also come to light. According to sources in the Sindh Home Department, the request for a gathering on November 29 had first been submitted by the Sindh Taraqqi Pasand Party, and permission for the rally could not be granted in view of a possible clash.
