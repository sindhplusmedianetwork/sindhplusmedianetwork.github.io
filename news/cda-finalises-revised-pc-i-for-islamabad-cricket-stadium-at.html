CDA Finalises Revised PC-I for Islamabad Cricket Stadium at Rs16.7 Billion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Capital Development Authority has finalised the modified PC-I of the Islamabad Cricket Stadium, increasing the estimated cost of the project from Rs11.9 billion to Rs16.7 billion for both packages, covering the grey structure, finishing and electrical works. A CDA officer told Dawn that the modified PC-Is for both phases have been completed and will soon be presented before the upcoming CDA Development Working Party for approval. He said the cost had increased after the inclusion of additional components, including the grey structure, finishing, lighting, heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems, preparation of the ground and pitches, and a security camera system.

The officer said the earlier PC-I envisaged a seating capacity of 25,000, but when the CDA invited requests for proposals earlier this year, it was decided to increase the capacity. The stadium will be developed in two phases, and the CDA aims to complete the entire project within a year. The officer said the bidding process for the grey structure and road network has already been completed, and groundbreaking of Phase-I, the grey structure, will be held soon and completed within six months.

A Pakistan Cricket Board official indicated that Pakistan was likely to host some matches of the ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2028 at the proposed Islamabad stadium. According to the CDA, the stadium will have a seating capacity of 35,000, making it the largest cricket stadium in Pakistan, and it will be a replica of the Dubai Cricket Stadium.
