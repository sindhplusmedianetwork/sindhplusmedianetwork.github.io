Over 1,500 youngsters join youth run race at Karachi's Seaview

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 'Dining Youth Run Race' was held at Karachi's Seaview beach, drawing a large number of young boys and girls. The five-kilometre race had separate categories for men and women. According to the organisers, more than 1,000 young boys and over 500 girls took part in the race.

Participants who win the competition will be given certificates and cash prizes. The organisers said the aim of holding the race is to draw young people towards healthy activities, promote the trend of sports, and make them aware of the importance of physical fitness.
