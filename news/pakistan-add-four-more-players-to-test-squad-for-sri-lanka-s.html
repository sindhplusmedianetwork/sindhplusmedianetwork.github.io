Pakistan Add Four More Players to Test Squad for Sri Lanka Series, Release Two

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has added four more players to the Test squad for the series against Sri Lanka, with all-rounder Aamer Jamal, fast bowlers Ali Shabeer and Saqib Khan, and batter Mohammad Tayyab joining the team. In the announcement issued on Saturday, the board said spinner Ali Usman and batter Abdullah Fazal had been released from the squad, while Ali Shabeer, Saqib Khan and Mohammad Tayyab were included in a Pakistan Test squad for the first time. The four players also took part in the Patrons XI versus Sri Lanka practice match played from November 4 to 6 at the Islamabad Club.

The first Test of the two-match series begins in Rawalpindi on November 9, while the second Test is scheduled in Lahore from November 17 to 21. The board had announced a 14-member squad at the end of last month and said two fast bowlers would be added after the three-day warm-up match. Among the newcomers, 29-year-old Mohammad Tayyab of Lahore scored 727 runs at an average of 66.09 in seven matches, including three centuries in his first season, while 19-year-old Ali Shabeer of Rahim Yar Khan has taken 22 wickets in eight first-class matches and 31-year-old Saqib Khan of Karachi took 47 wickets at an average of 20.60 in nine matches during Karachi Blues' Quaid-e-Azam Trophy title run.
