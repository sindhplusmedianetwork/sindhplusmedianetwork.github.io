LPG Hits Rs580/kg in Hyderabad as Gas Shortages Bite

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cooking fuel has become painfully expensive in Hyderabad, where LPG retailers are charging between 550 and 580 rupees per kilogram. The price is nearly double the official rate of 279.34 rupees per kilogram notified by OGRA, the oil and gas regulator. Residents have been turning to LPG in large numbers as persistent natural gas outages continue, pushing the city's daily demand above 50 tonnes.

The Sui Southern Gas Company is supplying domestic gas for only three hours at a time, three times a day. Frustration boiled over when women residents of Latifabad's Amani Shah Colony blocked traffic in protest over the prolonged shortages. Retailers say supply constraints and rising wholesale costs are behind the spike.
