PM Shehbaz hopes Pakistan will become world's 6th largest economy 'earlier than 2050'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said on Friday that Pakistan could become the world's sixth-largest economy earlier than 2050. He was addressing the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum in Islamabad, where the country's mineral wealth and its potential for investment were under discussion. The prime minister said the country had the resources and the ability to move ahead, and that the right policies could bring the goal closer.

He noted that Pakistan's mineral sector had attracted growing interest from investors at home and abroad. The forum brought together government officials, business leaders and international delegates to explore partnerships in mining and minerals. Shehbaz Sharif said the government was working to create an environment in which investment in the sector could grow.

He expressed hope that, with sustained effort, Pakistan could reach the ranking well before the middle of the century.
