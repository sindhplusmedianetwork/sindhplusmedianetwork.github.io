Durefishan opens up about love, heartbreak, healing and Pakistani TV

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Durefishan Saleem has spoken about the emotional complexities behind her character Zaryab in the drama Dar-e-Nijaat. She addressed criticism of the character's decisions and what the story raises about love, self-worth and healing. In an interview with Khaleej Times, she said an actor's job is to portray a character honestly rather than tell audiences what to think.

She said love should allow a person to evolve rather than disappear. She added that healing is about learning to look back without wanting to return.
