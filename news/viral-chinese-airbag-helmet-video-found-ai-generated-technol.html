Viral Chinese airbag helmet video found AI-generated; technology unconfirmed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A viral video claims a Chinese helmet deploys an airbag around a motorcycle rider's body in a crash. The two Chinese companies named in connection with the video have not confirmed such technology. The helmet in the video shows an Italian brand logo.

Fact-checking found inconsistencies in the footage, including clothing colour changes and a disappearing arm. Experts say airbag jackets already exist. The helmet's existence remains unconfirmed, and the video is concluded to be AI-generated.
