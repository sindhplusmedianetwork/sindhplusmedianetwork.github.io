NA panel calls for easing rules for pilgrimage organisers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Assembly Standing Committee on Religious Affairs has called for easing the rules for pilgrimage organisers and directed the religious affairs ministry to submit the Zaireen Management Policy to the panel. The committee, which met under acting chairman Muhammad Ijazul Haq, also directed the ministry to expedite security clearance and the issuance of registration certificates to organisers. It recommended reducing the Rs1 million security deposit required from organisers, particularly small-scale operators, to ease the financial burden on pilgrims.

The ministry informed the panel that 922 companies had obtained security clearance from the interior ministry, 282 had been processed for registration, and 261 registration certificates had been issued. The committee also asked the ministry to coordinate with the foreign affairs ministry to facilitate visas for members of minority communities wishing to visit their holy sites. It decided to examine the Zaireen Management Policy to identify gaps in its implementation and assess the consistency of its regulatory framework.

The committee further stressed that all financial transactions and contractual arrangements in Haj operations must ensure transparency and accountability and comply strictly with prescribed rules and competitive procurement procedures.
