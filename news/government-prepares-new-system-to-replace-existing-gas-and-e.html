Government prepares new system to replace existing gas and electricity slabs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has prepared a new method for giving subsidy to electricity and gas consumers, under which the existing slab system will be ended. According to sources, the Power Division and the Petroleum Ministry have prepared a new economic social registry for providing subsidy to electricity and gas consumers. The IMF wants the new system to be implemented from 1 January for electricity consumers and from February 2027 for gas consumers.

The decision on implementing the new subsidy system will be taken by a steering committee headed by Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, and a test run will be carried out before the new system is implemented. The government, however, wants to keep the current subsidy method in place until 30 June 2027. According to sources, on the direction of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, the matter of continuing relief for a further six months will be raised with the IMF, and the government is working to extend the subsidy for consumers using up to 200 units of electricity by six months.
