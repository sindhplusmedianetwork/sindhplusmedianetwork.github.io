Pakistani imports from India rise 69% despite trade curbs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani imports from India rose 69 percent despite trade restrictions, with goods worth 61.7 million dollars imported from India in three months, against 36.6 million dollars in the same period last year, according to central bank figures. Medicines and chemical products make up more than 96 percent of the imports, with vaccines, blood products and related items forming the largest category. Traders say Indian goods continue to reach Pakistan through third countries including Dubai and Malaysia, even though the precise routes and volumes are not publicly established.

Formal trade between the two countries has been restricted since 2019, and the curbs tightened further after the April 2025 attack in Pahalgam and the military confrontation that followed in May 2025. Despite the restrictions, the return of fishermen and a handshake between football players are seen as signs of a thaw in relations between the two countries. Overall, India accounted for only about 0.3 percent of Pakistan's total import bill of 64.5 billion dollars in 2025-26.
