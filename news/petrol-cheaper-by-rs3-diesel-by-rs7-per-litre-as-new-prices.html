Petrol cheaper by Rs3, diesel by Rs7 per litre as new prices notified

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has announced a reduction in petroleum prices, cutting the prices of petrol and diesel. According to the notification issued by the government, the price of petrol has been reduced by Rs3 per litre, taking the new price of petrol to Rs335 per litre. Similarly, the price of diesel has been reduced by Rs7 per litre, after which the new price of diesel stands at Rs364 per litre.

The announcement was made in Islamabad on 11 November 2026. The notification sets out the revised prices for both fuels, which are the latest in a series of adjustments to petroleum product prices. The government issued the notification after reviewing the prices, and the new rates apply under the official notification.
