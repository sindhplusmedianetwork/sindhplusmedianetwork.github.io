Government meets IMF fiscal conditions for first quarter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan met three major fiscal conditions set by the International Monetary Fund for the first quarter of the current fiscal year, keeping budget consolidation on track. The federal government reported a primary surplus of Rs2.97 trillion, well above the target of Rs1.95 trillion. The Federal Board of Revenue collected Rs3.089 trillion in taxes against a target of Rs3.03 trillion.

However, the Centre received only Rs57.5 billion, or 6% of the annual Rs1.035 trillion provincial cash grant, for national projects. The four provinces missed the IMF's tax-collection condition but met the cash-surplus target, generating a combined surplus of Rs599 billion. The performance keeps the programme on course, though the provinces' tax shortfall remains a concern.
