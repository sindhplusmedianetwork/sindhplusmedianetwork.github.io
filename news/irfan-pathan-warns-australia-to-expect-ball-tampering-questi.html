Irfan Pathan Warns Australia to Expect Ball-Tampering Questions Ahead of India Tour

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Indian all-rounder Irfan Pathan has warned Australia's cricket team to expect tough questions about 'Sandpapergate' when they tour India for the Border-Gavaskar Trophy in 2027. Speaking on the programme 'Sandpaper Gate: The Unsettled Debate', he said even young Australian players not involved in the 2018 incident may face questions, which would be unfair. He advised the team to stay silent on the matter and focus on cricket.

He added that even a fresh Cricket Australia inquiry would not end the debate, which could continue for 10, 20 or 30 years. The 2018 Cape Town scandal led to one-year bans for Steve Smith and David Warner and nine months for Cameron Bancroft.
