Robbers Strike Three Times in 40 Minutes in Islamabad's D-12

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A gang of robbers struck three times within 40 minutes in Islamabad's D-12 area, targeting three food delivery riders, police said. The victims lost a combined Rs23,000 in cash and three mobile phones worth about Rs183,000 near the service road in D-12/4. Police registered three separate robbery cases against the gang.

A few hours later, another robbery took place at Shamshad Market, where Rs51,000 in cash and a mobile phone were looted from a motorcyclist. Police said efforts were under way to trace and arrest the suspects.
