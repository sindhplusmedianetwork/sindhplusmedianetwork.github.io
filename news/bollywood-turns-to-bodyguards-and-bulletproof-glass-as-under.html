Bollywood turns to bodyguards and bulletproof glass as underworld anxiety rises

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A spate of shootings and extortion threats targeting Mumbai film stars has sent Bollywood scrambling for bodyguards and bulletproof protection, reviving memories of the underworld's grip on cinema in the 1990s. Police have linked the latest violence to the gang of jailed gangster Lawrence Bishnoi, including gunfire targeting actor Tiger Shroff's car in October and an alleged $600,000 extortion demand against actor Tusshar Kapoor. Police have arrested 13 people under organised crime laws in connection with the October shooting, alleging it was intended to extort money, establish gang dominance and spread fear.

Stars including Amitabh Bachchan, who recently greeted fans from behind a bulletproof enclosure, and Shah Rukh Khan have stepped up security. Actor and security agency owner Ronit Roy said demand for bodyguards had risen sharply, now covering family members and children as well. Producer Boney Kapoor said the latest wave of violence would be short-lived but added that the shootings could not be ignored.
