Indian creator trolled for copying a scene from Pakistani drama 'Mitti De Baway'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The popularity of the Pakistani drama 'Mitti De Baway' has crossed the border, with the show starring Mahira Khan, Wahaj Ali, Amar Khan and Junaid Khan drawing fans in India too. Indian content creator Milan Thakur recently recreated a famous scene from the drama showing Bala's first meeting with a widow named Qamar-un-Nisa, and shared the video online. Pakistani and Indian social media users quickly pointed out that the video was a copy of the original scene.

One user joked that the Pakistani drama had become so popular that the neighbouring country was now imitating it. Another user suggested watching the original drama instead of the copy, while others remarked that the chemistry of Bala and Qamar-un-Nisa's original characters was unmatched. The creator's video has since become a talking point on social media.
