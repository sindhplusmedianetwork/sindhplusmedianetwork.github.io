Khawaja Asif Raises Alarm Over Bureaucrats' 'Exodus' to Portugal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif said on Sunday that a significant number of senior government officers had planned to settle abroad while still serving Pakistan. In a statement on X, he alleged that officials had made Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and European countries their permanent homes. He said Portugal had recently emerged as a new destination for what he described as a bureaucratic exodus.

Asif further claimed that a person who arranged permanent residency deals for influential bureaucrats had fled after collecting large sums of money from them. These allegations have not been independently verified. His remarks have stirred debate about accountability and loyalty within the civil service.
