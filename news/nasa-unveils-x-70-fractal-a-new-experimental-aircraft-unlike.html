NASA unveils X-70 Fractal, a new experimental aircraft unlike any before

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

NASA has announced a new experimental aircraft, the X-70 Fractal, which the agency says will be able to fly in ways no aircraft has before. The crewed, modular aircraft will let NASA test flight control systems and swap internal and external payloads quickly, carrying up to 910 kg of research equipment. NASA administrator Jared Isaacman said the aircraft is expected to make its first flight within the next two years.

The X-70 is designed as a flying testbed, allowing researchers to try out new flight technologies in the air rather than only in simulations. NASA said the modular design means different research instruments can be fitted and removed without long rebuilds. The announcement adds a new aircraft to NASA’s long-running X-plane programme of experimental flight research.
