China Successfully Launches CERES-2 Rocket With Four Satellites

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China successfully launched a CERES-2 Y3 carrier rocket with four satellites on board on Saturday, state media reported. The rocket blasted off at 7:37 am Beijing Time from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China and successfully sent the satellites into a 535-kilometre sun-synchronous orbit. The four satellites belong to different fields, ranging from ocean remote sensing and commercial meteorology to university-led scientific research and space situational awareness.

They include the Guanlan experimental satellite, the Yunyao-1 28 commercial meteorology satellite, the North University of China-1 satellite and the Juntian-1 06A satellite. The rocket development team said the successful flight test and the precise orbital insertion of the four satellites mark the official entry into service of a new commercial launch vehicle. The CERES-2 is a medium-lift launch vehicle developed by Galactic Energy for the commercial launch market, and the mission was its second flight.

The company said the satellites will be used for collecting meteorological data, ocean observation and demonstrating new technologies.
