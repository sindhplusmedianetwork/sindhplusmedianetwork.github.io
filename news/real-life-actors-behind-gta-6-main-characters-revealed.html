Real-life actors behind GTA 6 main characters revealed

Details have emerged about the real-life actors behind the main characters of GTA 6 following the game's release. Foreign media reported that the character Lucia Caminos was played by American actress Manni L. Perez.

The character Jason Duval was reported to have been played by American actor Dylan Rourke. The reports surfaced after the game's release drew worldwide attention from fans. Game maker Rockstar Games has not yet officially announced the full cast.

The identities of the performers therefore remain based on media reporting rather than an official announcement.
