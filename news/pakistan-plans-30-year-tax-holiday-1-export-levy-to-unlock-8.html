Pakistan plans 30-year tax holiday, 1% export levy to unlock $8tr mineral reserves

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government is set to announce an incentive package to tap the full potential of copper and gold reserves, estimated at $8 trillion, in the National Mineral Policy 2026. A model of the package, drawn up by consultancy Wood Mackenzie, incorporates reduced withholding tax rates of 4% on local goods and 8% on local services, with payment deferred until mine production begins. The package had been submitted to the Finance Division during the current year's budget, but a comprehensive programme could not be introduced then; the government now plans to offer the incentives to foreign investors at the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum scheduled for November 12-13.

The prime minister had directed the Petroleum Division to develop and finalise the National Mineral Policy, and sources said the policy document was ready but needed a fiscal package to achieve its objectives — the Finance Division being the authority to approve it. Sources told The Express Tribune the proposals include a 30-year exemption from general sales tax on the import and local purchase and supply of goods and services for mining projects during the exploration and development phase, possibly continuing after production begins given the export orientation of mineral output. Other proposals include exempting import duties on capital goods and project equipment during exploration, development and construction; replacing corporate income tax with a 1% presumptive tax on export proceeds plus a 0.5% development surcharge; and fiscal stability clauses protecting terms agreed at the time of award, along the lines of the oil and gas framework.

The model also proposes exempting mining projects from super tax for at least 30 years, a 15-year exemption on withholding tax on dividends after commercial production, perpetual carry-forward of exploration losses, and accelerated depreciation of up to 100%.
