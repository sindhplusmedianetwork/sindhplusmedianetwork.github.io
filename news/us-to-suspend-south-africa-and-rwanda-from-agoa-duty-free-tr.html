US to suspend South Africa and Rwanda from AGOA duty-free trade from January 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said on Monday that Washington will suspend South Africa and Rwanda from the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA) duty-free trade programme from 1 January 2027, after notifying Congress and both governments. Greer said President Donald Trump found both governments were engaging in practices inconsistent with AGOA eligibility, citing human rights, corruption, trade barriers and threats to US national security interests. South Africa, the programme's largest beneficiary with about $1.7 billion of AGOA exports in 2025, said it would support affected sectors and work to diversify its export markets.

Rwanda, which has already been under a partial AGOA suspension on apparel since 2018, had no immediate reaction. The move marks one of the most significant curtailments of the flagship US-Africa trade programme.
