Tuchel confident England can end Spain's unbeaten run

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

England manager Thomas Tuchel believes his team is ready to end Spain's 43-match unbeaten run when the world and European champions meet in Madrid on Sunday. A victory would give England a more favourable Nations League quarter-final draw in March, giving the tie added importance beyond the result itself. England arrive after beating Croatia 2-1 at Wembley on Thursday, although they lost the reverse fixture 3-2 in September.

Tuchel spoke with confidence about his side's ability to challenge a team that has not lost in 43 matches. He also said England would never accept racism and that protocols were in place, after England players previously faced racist abuse in Spain. The match is now set to be a stern test of England's progress under Tuchel against the strongest side in world football.
