Professor Mahmood-ul-Haq Haqqani Passes Away at 87

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Professor Mahmood-ul-Haq Haqqani has passed away at the age of 87. He was the younger brother of Maulana Sami-ul-Haq and the son of Sheikh-ul-Hadith Maulana Abdul Haq, the founder of Jamia Darul Uloom Haqqania in Akora Khattak. According to JUI-S chief Maulana Abdul Haq Sani, the funeral prayer will be held at 11am on Saturday at Jamia Darul Uloom Haqqania, Akora Khattak.

Professor Haqqani spent his life in education and was a renowned chemistry teacher at Islamia College Peshawar. He also served as educational counsellor at the Pakistani embassy in Saudi Arabia. His death has been widely mourned in religious and academic circles.
