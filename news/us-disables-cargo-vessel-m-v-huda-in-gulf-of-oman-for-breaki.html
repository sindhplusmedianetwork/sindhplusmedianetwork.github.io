US Disables Cargo Vessel M/V Huda in Gulf of Oman for Breaking Iran Blockade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An American military aircraft fired on and disabled a commercial vessel that tried to break the United States blockade of Iran on Saturday, US Central Command said. The St Kitts-flagged cargo ship, M/V Huda, tried to depart regional waters after visiting an Iranian port, and a US plane fired precision munitions at its stern after US forces directed the crew to abandon the targeted spaces. Central Command said the ship's civilian crew ignored repeated warnings and attempted to violate the naval blockade against Iran multiple times.

The command said it has enforced a maritime blockade on Iranian ports in the Strait of Hormuz since July, and that its enforcement actions have redirected 147 commercial vessels that complied with warnings and disabled five merchant ships that refused to comply. The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations centre said it had received a report of an incident involving a bulk carrier and military forces in the Gulf of Oman, about 18 nautical miles east of Khor Fakkan in the United Arab Emirates. The US military also said on Saturday it was launching a task force to crack down on Tehran's movement of advanced weapons and other sanctioned material over its land borders and through the Caspian Sea.

Central Command estimated that its naval blockade has prevented Iran from exporting more than $25 billion in crude oil. US President Donald Trump has been threatening to carry out a new offensive against Iran, while Tehran has threatened broad retaliation against Gulf infrastructure.
