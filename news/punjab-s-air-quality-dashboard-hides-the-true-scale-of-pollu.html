Punjab's air quality dashboard hides the true scale of pollution, experts warn

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's official air quality dashboard caps readings at 500, so whether the actual air quality index climbs to 600, 700 or beyond, citizens see the same number. That leaves people unable to judge just how toxic the air around them has become, raising questions over transparency. Experts say the 500 ceiling is the limit of the index, not of pollution itself.

They argue the public needs real pollutant concentrations and PM2.5 readings instead of a single compressed figure. Senior Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb said the government does not intend to hide data. WWF Pakistan's Hammad Naqi, however, insisted that citizens have a fundamental right to accurate air quality information.
