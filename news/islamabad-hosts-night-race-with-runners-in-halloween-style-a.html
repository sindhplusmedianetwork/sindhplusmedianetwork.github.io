Islamabad hosts night race with runners in Halloween-style attire

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The second edition of 'After Glow Dash', organized under the banner of Run With Us, began with a children's fun run followed by a five-kilometre race. With October 31 as the occasion, some runners appeared in Halloween-style makeup while others had glittering colours and glitter on their faces. Although the real theme of the race was a night run, the participants turned it into a colourful celebration with their creativity.

Colourful faces, sparkling lights and fireworks made the night even more memorable. Fireworks, lights and the enthusiasm of the participants created a festive atmosphere at the event.
