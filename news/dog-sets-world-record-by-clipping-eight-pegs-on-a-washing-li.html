Java is owned by 46-year-old Lisa Rickards from Missouri. She first came into Lisa's life as a service dog in training with Canine Companions, an organisation that trains dogs to help people with disabilities. Lisa was responsible for raising Java and familiarising her with different social environments for the first 18 months of her life so she could become a service dog.

Java was later sent for advanced training, but it was eventually decided that she was not suited to the programme. Lisa was then fortunate to be able to adopt her again as a pet.
