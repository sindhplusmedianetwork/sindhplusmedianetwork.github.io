EY-led consortium signs pact for Karachi, Lahore airports outsourcing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Privatisation Commission and a consortium led by EY Middle East signed a financial advisory services agreement on Thursday. The agreement is for the outsourcing of Jinnah International Airport, Karachi. It also covers Allama Iqbal International Airport, Lahore.

The commission termed the signing an important milestone in the government's privatisation programme.
