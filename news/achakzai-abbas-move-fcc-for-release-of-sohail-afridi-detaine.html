Achakzai, Abbas move FCC for release of Sohail Afridi, detained lawmakers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The leaders of the opposition in both Houses of Parliament approached the Federal Constitutional Court on Saturday, seeking the immediate release of Sohail Afridi, the non-functional Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister, along with eight other members of the provincial assembly and six members of the National Assembly. Afridi has not been seen since the Centre imposed governor's rule and emergency in KP on Oct 11, and a federal minister confirmed the next day that he and some of his cabinet members were in preventive custody. National Assembly Opposition Leader Mehmood Khan Achakzai and Senate Opposition Leader Raja Nasir Abbas jointly filed the petition, which sought the enforcement of fundamental rights and constitutional protections against the alleged unlawful arrest, detention and confinement of the lawmakers.

The petition also sought disclosure of the places of detention, production of the detainees before the court, and a declaration that their custody was unlawful and without lawful authority. The federal secretaries of interior and defence, the KP chief secretary, the secretary of the KP Home and Tribal Affairs Department, the provincial police chief, the Peshawar capital city police officer, the Peshawar deputy commissioner and the FIA director general were named as respondents. Separately, Abbas and Balochistan National Party Chairman Sardar Akhtar Jan Mengal also approached the court on Saturday, seeking to be made parties in the case about the restraint imposed on the Peshawar High Court from hearing petitions about the detention of PTI activists.

The PTI maintains that Afridi remains the KP chief minister with the confidence and support of party founder Imran Khan.
