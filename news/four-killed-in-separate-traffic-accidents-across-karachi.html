Four Killed in Separate Traffic Accidents Across Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four people, including motorcyclists, were killed in separate traffic accidents in Karachi. In Landhi's Mansehra Colony, 25-year-old Tariq was killed when a speeding passenger coach hit his motorcycle. In a second incident, 25-year-old Hamza died in a car-motorcycle collision near Karsaz on Shahrah-e-Faisal.

In a third incident, 32-year-old Wajahat Baig was killed by an unidentified vehicle near Ghani Chowrangi in SITE. In a fourth incident, an unidentified pedestrian was killed near Dua Hotel on the Northern Bypass. Three of the deceased were identified, while one remains unidentified.

Police are investigating each incident.
