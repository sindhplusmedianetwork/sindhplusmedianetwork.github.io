Brent Crude Slips Below $100 as Global Oil Prices Tumble Nearly 5%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global crude oil prices fell sharply on Wednesday. Brent futures dropped 4.7 percent to settle at $98.76 a barrel, slipping below the $100 mark for the first time since early October. The US benchmark, West Texas Intermediate, slid 4.5 percent to close at $91.73 a barrel.

According to the outlet, investors are linking the decline to the Republicans' heavy defeat in the US midterm elections, with markets expecting a possible shift in Washington's policy toward Iran. It noted that Iran has kept the Strait of Hormuz closed in response to US and Israeli strikes, a move that had severely disrupted global crude shipments and pushed fuel prices sharply higher. The price slide will be closely watched in the coming trading sessions.
