K-P's people will have 'first right' to province's resources, says Governor Kundi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi said on Sunday that the province's people would have the 'first right' to its natural resources, including gas, minerals, electricity and oil. Speaking at the annual Urs of Darbar-e-Aalia Murshidabad Sharif in Peshawar, he said: 'As long as I hold this office, the first right over our resources will belong to the people of our province.' Kundi said restoring peace was his first priority, calling on the province's people to stand united against terrorism, and noted that more than half of the national cricket team's players belonged to Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa. Governor's rule and emergency were imposed in the province on October 11, transferring executive authority from the PTI-led provincial government to the governor, with an 11-member advisory council formed.

On October 17, Kundi had said the province's minerals would be 'extracted and sold', inviting domestic and foreign companies to invest through joint ventures. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Tariq Fazal Chaudhry rejected any link between the emergency and mineral extraction, saying it was imposed for security reasons and that Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan would have first right to their mineral resources.
