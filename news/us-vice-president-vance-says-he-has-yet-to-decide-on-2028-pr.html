US Vice President Vance Says He Has Yet to Decide on 2028 Presidential Run

US Vice President JD Vance said he had not yet decided whether to run for president in 2028, admitting that "of course there's a chance" he might opt out. He made the remarks in a CBS town hall in Michigan released on Saturday night. Vance said he would "have to think about it," pointing to his four young children and his wife Usha, who he said had "never loved politics." President Trump has previously called Vance his "most likely" Republican successor.

Vance also defended the administration's Iran strategy during the town hall. He said petrol prices would fall once the conflict ended.
