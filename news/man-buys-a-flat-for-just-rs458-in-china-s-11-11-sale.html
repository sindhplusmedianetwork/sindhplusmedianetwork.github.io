Man buys a flat for just Rs458 in China's 11/11 sale

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In China's famous Double Eleven online shopping festival, the discounts took an extraordinary turn this time, as a complete flat was sold for just 11 yuan, worth about Rs458 in Pakistani currency. The flat is in Hegang city in Heilongjiang province, and the 68-square-metre residential unit has two bedrooms and sits on the seventh floor of a building. It was put up for auction through an online e-commerce company on the occasion of the Double Eleven festival.

The auction began at just one yuan and the bidding rose to 11 yuan, with 1,667 people taking part, according to the platform's record, while the bidding process was completed on 5 November. The successful bidder belongs to a province in eastern China, though his identity has not been revealed, and he said during a live stream that he had no intention of moving to Hegang but would keep the flat to spend holidays there. Despite the flat's price of 11 yuan, the buyer will still have to pay another 2,000 yuan to transfer the ownership into his name.

Property apps estimate that the general market price of used flats in the city is about 40,000 yuan. A manager of the organisation that arranged the auction said the deal had been made with an online e-commerce company under the Double Eleven promotional campaign, and the company would pay the difference between the flat's actual price and the amount fetched at auction.
