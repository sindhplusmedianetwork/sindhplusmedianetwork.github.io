150 Afghans deported from US to Kabul via Pakistan, says Foreign Office

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Foreign Office said Pakistan is in contact with its allies and partners over the deportation of Afghan citizens from the United States to Kabul via Pakistan. The spokesperson said at a briefing that discussions are continuing on all aspects of the matter. He said a route acceptable to all parties will be found.

He also said preparations are under way for the Economic Cooperation Organization Council of Foreign Ministers meeting in Islamabad on November 15 and 16. The spokesperson added that Pakistan condemned the terrorist attack on a church in Jordan on October 24 and the Houthi attacks on Makkah and Madinah.
