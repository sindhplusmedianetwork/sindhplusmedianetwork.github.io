Another Life Lost to Banned Heavy Traffic: Trailer Kills Motorcyclist in Karachi's Orangi Town

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A trailer running through Karachi in defiance of the ban on heavy traffic has claimed yet another life, this time near Orangi Town's Bangla Bazaar. On Monday evening, the trailer struck a motorcyclist in the Sector 15 area, killing 30-year-old Talha on the spot; his body was shifted to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital for legal formalities while the driver abandoned the vehicle and fled. Witnesses say the crash happened at an hour when heavy vehicles are banned from entering the city — they are only permitted between 10 at night and 7 in the morning.

The incident is part of a deadly stretch from Friday night to Monday evening in which trailers and water tankers have killed three people, including a pregnant woman, and seriously injured four. On Friday night, a trailer took the life of 40-year-old pregnant Jameela near the Sindh Government Hospital in Liaquatabad, leaving her husband injured, and angry residents set the trailer ablaze. Saturday morning saw 40-year-old Adeel killed by a water tanker near the Jamali bridge on the Super Highway, while three motorcyclists were hurt when a tanker hit them near the Khurshid Pura graveyard on Hub River Road.

On Sunday night, 50-year-old Ghulam Abbas was badly injured at the Karimabad flyover, with one leg severely damaged, and residents torched the front of that trailer too. Traffic officials admit the trailer in Monday's fatal crash crossed the Orangi section into the Manghopir section and was heading toward Gulshan-e-Bahar when the tragedy struck.
