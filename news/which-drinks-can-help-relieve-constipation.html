Which drinks can help relieve constipation?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Some drinks can help keep the body hydrated and support digestion, but no drink is a guaranteed cure for constipation. The piece notes that constipation has many causes, from low fibre intake to medication and hormonal changes. It looks at popular options including ginger tea, lukewarm lemon water, peppermint tea, turmeric milk, aloe vera and apple cider vinegar, while noting that the evidence for each is limited.

It also carries warnings: aloe vera latex can cause cramps and dehydration, while undiluted vinegar can damage teeth and the food pipe. Getting fibre from vegetables, fruit, pulses and whole grains, drinking enough water and staying active matter more. Persistent constipation, severe pain, blood in the stool or unexplained weight loss are signs to see a doctor.
