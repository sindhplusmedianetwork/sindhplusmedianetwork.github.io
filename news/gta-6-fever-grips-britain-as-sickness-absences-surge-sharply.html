GTA 6 Fever Grips Britain as Sickness Absences Surge Sharply

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The fever of the recently released video game Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6) has gripped Britain, as a result of which one million people took leave from work and school to play the game. According to British media reports, around 2.8 million people had planned to play the game for a long time on its release, and on the release day of the 70-pound action game the British economy was estimated to lose up to 400 million pounds due to reduced productivity. Interestingly, some schools also offered prizes to students for coming to school so that they would not stay absent because of the game.

However, the game's extraordinary popularity could also become a source of about 1.3 billion pounds in revenue for the British treasury, including 20 per cent value-added tax on sales of games, consoles, accessories and other products. According to British human resources company Bright HR, GTA was mentioned in 4.6 per cent of employees' leave requests, while about 83,000 working days of leave were taken in November and December specifically to play the game. The company said sickness absences rose 56 per cent compared with the previous Thursday, and employees arriving late at work were on average 30 minutes late.
