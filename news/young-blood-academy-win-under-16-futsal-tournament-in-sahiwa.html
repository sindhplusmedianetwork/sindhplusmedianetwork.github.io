Young Blood Academy win Under-16 futsal tournament in Sahiwal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Under-16 boys futsal tournament marking Allama Iqbal Day was held at the Maryam Nawaz Sports Arena in Sahiwal. Eight teams took part in the tournament. Young Blood Academy beat Ishfaq Academy 3-2 in the final to lift the trophy.

The winning team received a trophy and a cash prize of Rs15,000. The runners-up received a trophy and Rs10,000.
