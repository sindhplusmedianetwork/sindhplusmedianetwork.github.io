17th management research conference held in Istanbul with Pakistani universities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 17th edition of the International Conference on Management Research (ICMR 2026) was held at Istanbul Aydin University, jointly organised by Superior University Lahore and Istanbul Aydin University. A 64-member delegation from 32 Pakistani universities attended the conference, led by Superior University Vice President Chaudhry Hamza Rahman, with Prof Dr Mustafa Aydin as chief guest. The conference theme was economic transformation through tourism, covering tourism policy, technology, destination management and sustainable tourism.

Distinguished academics, researchers, policymakers, business figures and international delegates took part in the gathering. Prof Dr Mustafa Aydin, founder and president of Istanbul Aydin University, spoke warmly of Pakistan and its people on different occasions, describing Pakistan as a brotherly country. The conference highlighted the close ties between Pakistan and Turkiye and the shared commitment to strengthening friendship, brotherhood and mutual respect through academic cooperation.
