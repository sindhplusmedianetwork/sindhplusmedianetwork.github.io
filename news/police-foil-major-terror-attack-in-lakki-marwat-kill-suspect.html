Police foil major terror attack in Lakki Marwat, kill suspected suicide bomber

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Lakki Marwat thwarted a major terrorist attack on Friday in the jurisdiction of Thana Bragi, acting on intelligence. Members of the Police Aman Committee sealed internal and external routes to the area, and a heavy police contingent led by District Police Officer Sajjad Ahmad Sahibzada was rushed to the scene. During the operation, security forces recovered a Mazda vehicle packed with explosive material, and in an exchange of fire one alleged suicide bomber was killed.

Preliminary examination showed drums inside the vehicle contained a substantial quantity of explosives, and police said intelligence had indicated terrorists had prepared an improvised explosive device in a Mazda vehicle for a mass-casualty attack. DPO Sahibzada, along with Counter Terrorism Department personnel, local police and the Quick Response Force, launched a search operation while bomb disposal squads inspected the vehicle to neutralise the explosives. The slain attacker is believed to be an Afghan national, with his identity being verified, and IGP Zulfiqar Hameed praised the Lakki Marwat police for foiling the plot.
