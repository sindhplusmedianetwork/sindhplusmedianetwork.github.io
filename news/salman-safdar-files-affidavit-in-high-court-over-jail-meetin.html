Salman Safdar files affidavit in high court over jail meeting with Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In the 190 million pound case, Barrister Salman Safdar has submitted an affidavit in the Islamabad High Court regarding his jail meeting with PTI founder Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi. According to the affidavit, the Adiala Jail authorities informed him on 31 October about the meeting that was scheduled for 2 November. The affidavit gives an account of the meeting and of the condition in which Bushra Bibi was seen during it.

Safdar stated that her eyes appeared to be in a delicate condition and that she was wearing dark glasses. He further stated that she could not bear sunlight or artificial light, and that her eyesight seemed to be severely affected.
