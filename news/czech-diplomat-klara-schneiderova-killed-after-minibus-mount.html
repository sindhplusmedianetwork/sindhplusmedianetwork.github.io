Czech Diplomat Klara Schneiderova Killed After Minibus Mounts Sidewalk in Jerusalem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Klara Schneiderova, the 30-year-old head of the Czech Embassy's branch office in Jerusalem, was killed on Thursday when a minibus mounted a sidewalk near the Mahane Yehuda market and struck her. Rescue officials said she suffered multiple serious injuries and was pronounced dead after being taken to hospital. The Czech Embassy announced her death on Friday, expressing condolences to her family.

Israeli police detained the driver at the scene and opened an investigation into the circumstances of the crash. Schneiderova had arrived in Israel only a month earlier to take up her post in Jerusalem. Her father served as the Czech ambassador to Israel in the late 1990s, making her death an especially heavy blow to the Czech diplomatic community.

Authorities have not yet said whether the driver will face criminal charges.
