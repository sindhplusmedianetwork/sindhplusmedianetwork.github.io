Dhruv Rathee calls Modi 'anti-national' after student leaders' arrests

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian YouTuber Dhruv Rathee has launched a strong criticism of Prime Minister Narendra Modi following the arrests of student leaders Abhijit Deepke and Neha Bora. Rathee said Modi has become anti-national in his reaction to the police action. He claimed that a woman was detained by nearly 50 policemen merely for waving the Indian tricolor.

The YouTuber drew a parallel with the British colonial period, saying people were once arrested for hoisting flags. His comments come amid growing debate in India over the police action against the student leaders. Rathee's remarks have drawn wide attention on social media.
