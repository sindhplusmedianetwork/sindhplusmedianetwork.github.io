Owono to decide on return after De Miguel tragedy: FC Andorra coach

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Goalkeeper Jesus Owono will decide for himself if and when he returns to football following the death of Tenerife striker Jesus de Miguel, FC Andorra coach Carles Manso said on Thursday. De Miguel, 30, died last week after a collision with Owono during a Spanish second division match on November 2. Owono has not trained with his team-mates since the incident.

Spanish police have opened an investigation into the incident. A crowdfunding campaign for De Miguel's family has raised over 200,000 euros. The coach's remarks underline that the goalkeeper's return will be on his own terms, in his own time.
