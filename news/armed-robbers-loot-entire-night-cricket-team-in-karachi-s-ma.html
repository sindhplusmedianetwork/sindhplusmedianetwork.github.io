Armed Robbers Loot Entire Night-Cricket Team in Karachi's Manzoor Colony

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three armed robbers raided a night cricket match in Manzoor Colony's Sector F, Karachi, late Monday night, holding the young players at gunpoint. The robbers snatched mobile phones and cash from the players and assaulted some of them before escaping with ease. CCTV footage of the incident has surfaced, showing the armed men looting the entire team on the ground.

Police said a hunt for the suspects is underway using the footage. SHO Baloch Colony Shehzada Saleem said legal proceedings are ongoing and arrests are expected soon. The brazen robbery at a public sports ground has raised fresh questions about street crime in the city.
