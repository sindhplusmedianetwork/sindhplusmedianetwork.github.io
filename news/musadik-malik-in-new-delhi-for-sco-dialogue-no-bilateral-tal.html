Musadik Malik in New Delhi for SCO Dialogue, No Bilateral Talks Planned

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik arrived in New Delhi on Friday for the SCO's first civilisational dialogue. It was the first visit by a Pakistani federal minister to India since Bilawal Bhutto Zardari's May 2023 trip and the four-day May 2025 conflict. Malik said the visit was purely about Pakistan's SCO commitment with no bilateral talks.

He said terrorism must be discussed on evidence, offering to present Pakistan's evidence at bilateral or multilateral forums. Former ambassadors Aizaz Chaudhry and Imran Ali said the visit was limited to the SCO dialogue. They said it should not be read as bilateral progress.
