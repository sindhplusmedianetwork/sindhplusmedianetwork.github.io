Pakistan's New Trader Tax Registration Scheme Fails, Only 1,750 Shopkeepers Join

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government's scheme to bring traders into the tax net has been revealed as a failure, with only 1,750 shopkeepers taking advantage of the new easy registration scheme so far. According to sources, only 220 new shopkeepers joined the tax net through the retailers' scheme. The Federal Board of Revenue has decided to act against traders who failed to join the scheme, and penalties can be imposed on shopkeepers who stay outside the tax net.

Sources said the government had set a target of collecting Rs50 billion from the retailers' tax scheme, but it is facing difficulties in bringing retailers into the tax net. The FBR has identified 3.6 million potential non-filers in the retail sector and plans to geo-tag markets to track them. Non-filers face fines of Rs10,000 in the first month, Rs25,000 in the second month, and Rs50,000 in the third month, and the authorities also have the power to seal the shops of non-compliant traders.

Meanwhile, the deadline for filing income tax returns was extended twice, and the FBR had received 7.6 million income tax returns by October.
