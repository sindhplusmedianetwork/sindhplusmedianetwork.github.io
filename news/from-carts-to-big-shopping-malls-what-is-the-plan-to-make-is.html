From carts to big shopping malls: What is the plan to make Islamabad the 'world's first cashless capital'?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistani government has announced making Islamabad the country's first fully cashless city and the world's first cashless capital. According to the interior ministry, the target is to connect all business centres, shops and traders to the digital payment system by 31 December. The plan covers everything from street carts to big shopping malls, bringing all traders into the digital payment network.

However, trader leader Ajmal Baloch and cybersecurity expert Mirza Burhan Baig expressed concerns about the plan. Their concerns relate to digital literacy, cyber fraud and technical infrastructure. The announcement has opened a debate over how quickly the capital can move to a fully cashless system.
