Shehbaz Sharif and Field Marshal Asim Munir to Visit Peshawar Next Week: Official Sources

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir will visit Peshawar next week, official sources have said. The two key central figures will visit the Governor House Peshawar, where they will meet Governor Faisal Karim Kundi and also sit with members of the consultative council. The sources said a briefing will be given on the system in the wake of the situation following the imposition of governor's rule and the emergency.

The prime minister will also give instructions regarding the imposition of the emergency in 12 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the area development system. Both leaders are described as the two key central personalities heading to the provincial capital for these consultations.
