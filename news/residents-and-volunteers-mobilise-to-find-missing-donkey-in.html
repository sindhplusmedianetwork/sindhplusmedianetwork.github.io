Residents and Volunteers Mobilise to Find Missing Donkey in US Town

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Residents and volunteers in Glenwood, Iowa, have mobilised to search for a donkey named Hope, who has been missing for several weeks. The donkey, aged between one and a half and two years, escaped around Halloween from the home of a woman who was caring for her. The animal had previously suffered abuse before being taken into care.

The animal protection organisation Hoofs and Paws Rescue has put up posters across the area and appealed to the public for information. Thermal drones, all-terrain vehicles and horses are being used in the search effort. The donkey was last seen near Paddock Avenue and Moon Avenue, and rescuers say they remain hopeful of finding her safe.
