Gohar Rejects Reports, Says Imran Knew of Governor's Rule in K-P

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan said on Tuesday that party founder Imran Khan was fully aware of the imposition of governor's rule and emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, rejecting reports that he had welcomed the fall of the provincial government. Speaking to the media near Adiala Jail in Rawalpindi, Gohar said Imran had expressed no satisfaction over the removal of the K-P government. He said the recent meeting between Imran's lawyer Salman Safdar and the PTI founder was the first in 380 days, since April 8, and called it a positive development.

Gohar said the prolonged suspension of meetings had deepened political tension, adding that regular meetings could have kept the situation from reaching the point of governor's rule. He also rejected reports that his own name had been finalised for a meeting with Imran. Separately, PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja said Safdar had found Imran in high spirits, and that the lawyer had told the court he would not argue cases until he was allowed to meet his client.

Raja ruled out any immediate political breakthrough from the meeting and said he did not see a forward bloc emerging within the party, though he would not rule out any possibility entirely.
