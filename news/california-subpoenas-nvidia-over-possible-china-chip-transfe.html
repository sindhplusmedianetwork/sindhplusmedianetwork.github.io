California Subpoenas Nvidia Over Possible China Chip Transfers, Investor Claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

California has served an investigative subpoena on US chipmaker Nvidia to determine whether China obtained advanced semiconductor technology or confidential information through the actions of the company or its former CEO, Jensen Huang, the state's top law enforcement officer said on Thursday. The investigation also seeks to establish whether Nvidia misled investors and the public about potential risks stemming from Huang's reported relationship with a woman suspected of working for Chinese intelligence, according to a statement from California Attorney General Rob Bonta's office. Bonta said his office is investigating whether Nvidia or Jensen Huang's actions resulted in China obtaining advanced chips or confidential information, and if Nvidia misled investors and the public about this risk in violation of the law.

Bonta said the information obtained through the subpoena would help the California Justice Department determine whether the company's conduct violated state law. The investigation follows Huang's resignation last week after reports emerged that he had an extramarital relationship with a woman suspected by US intelligence officials of being a Chinese spy. The woman, identified in US media reports as Alice Huang, was previously associated with Singapore-based data centre company Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer that has faced scrutiny over suspected transfers of advanced AI chips to China.

Beijing has denied allegations that she was involved in espionage. Following Huang's departure, Nvidia appointed Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim CEO while its board searches for a permanent successor. The investigative subpoena does not establish that Nvidia or Huang violated the law or that sensitive technology was transferred to China.
