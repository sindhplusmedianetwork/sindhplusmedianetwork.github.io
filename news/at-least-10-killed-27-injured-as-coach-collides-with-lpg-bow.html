At least 10 killed, 27 injured as coach collides with LPG bowser in Gwadar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 10 people were killed and 27 others injured when a passenger coach collided with an LPG bowser in Balochistan on Thursday. Those killed included a woman and three children. The collision took place on the Makran Coastal Highway near Taji checkpost.

The coach was travelling from Turbat to Karachi when it collided with the bowser near Basol in the Ormara area of Gwadar district. Both vehicles burst into flames after the collision. At least 27 passengers suffered burn injuries and were shifted to hospitals in Ormara and Pasni, where their condition was reported critical.
