Judicial Commission's Transfer-Only Policy for IHC Judges Challenged

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A writ petition filed in the Islamabad High Court has challenged the Judicial Commission of Pakistan's April 28 decision that vacancies arising from the transfer of judges 'shall be filled through transfer only'. The petition, filed by Advocate Nayyab Hassan Gardezi, argues that the IHC's three vacant seats should be filled through fresh appointments under the Constitution rather than transfers. It cites procedural defects, including the Rule 11(2) agenda notice, and questions the composition of the commission that took the decision.

The IHC is currently functioning with 9 of its 12 sanctioned judges. The petition asks the court to strike down the transfer-only policy.
