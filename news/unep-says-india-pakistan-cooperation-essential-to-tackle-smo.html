UNEP Says India-Pakistan Cooperation Essential to Tackle Smog

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Nations Environment Programme's chief in India, Dr Balakrishna Pisupati, has stressed that India and Pakistan must work together for a lasting solution to smog and air pollution. He described the problem as a health, economic and development crisis. Pisupati noted that Delhi's air quality index averaged 348 last week and crossed 403 on Friday morning.

He was speaking against the backdrop of the COP31 climate conference in Turkiye. He urged both countries to learn from London and Beijing, which have beaten smog in the past. His remarks underlined that air pollution crosses borders and needs a joint response.
