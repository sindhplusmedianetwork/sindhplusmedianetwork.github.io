Trump, Xi Did Not Discuss Nvidia's Huang, Says US Treasury Secretary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States President Donald Trump did not discuss former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang with Chinese President Xi Jinping during their meeting this week, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Friday. Huang resigned as chief of the chip giant at the centre of the artificial intelligence revolution after revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy who may have funnelled restricted chips to that country. Responding to a question, Bessent told US broadcaster CNBC that Huang had not come up in Trump's talks with Xi, held earlier this week in Shenzhen.

Speaking about the Trump administration's support for the domestic AI sector, Bessent stressed that the US must stay ahead of China in that realm and said the best protection against foreign exploits is for US labs to maintain their substantial lead, which in effect is their shield. He said there is no turning back and labs must maintain their leads through continued innovation, access to the best chips and smart policy. Bessent said Trump and Xi had agreed on certain guardrails on the use of AI in the military domain, including that humans should always be involved in any military decisions that the technology makes and it should not be allowed to be autonomous for major military actions.

On the threat of AI-enabled cyberattacks, Bessent again stressed that the best defence was to allow US AI companies to continue at the frontier and provide the best defence against other models.
