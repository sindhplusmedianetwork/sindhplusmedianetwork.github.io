Analysts Say PlayStation 6 Could Launch at $1,000, Push Gamers Toward PCs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Industry analysts believe Sony's next-generation PlayStation 6 could launch at $1,000, according to an October 17 ScreenRant report. Sony has not officially announced the console's price. Researcher Joost van Dreunen told GamesRadar the next generation could start about 50 per cent higher than the current one, making a $1,000 console the norm, while Circana analyst Mat Piscatella also described $1,000 as possible.

The context for the speculation is rising hardware costs: the PS5 Pro now sells for $899.99 and the standard PS5 for $599.99 after recent price increases. The prospect has sparked debate among gamers, with some saying they would keep their existing consoles rather than pay that much.
