ANF seizes drugs worth over Rs28 million in 10 operations, arrests 14 suspects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Anti-Narcotics Force carried out 10 operations in different cities and seized 158 kilograms of drugs worth Rs28.2 million. Fourteen suspects, including a woman, were arrested during the operations. Recoveries included 60 kilograms of hashish near the Kuchlak bypass in Quetta and 22.8 kilograms of opium in Peshawar.

Ice and heroin hidden in parcels and luggage were recovered at the Karachi and Lahore airports, while weed was recovered from a vehicle in Karachi. Cases have been registered against the arrested suspects under the Anti-Narcotics Act 1997.
