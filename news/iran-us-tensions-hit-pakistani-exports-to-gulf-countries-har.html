Iran-US Tensions Hit Pakistani Exports to Gulf Countries Hard

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani exports to Gulf countries fell sharply during the Iran-US conflict. Exports dropped by 32.3 per cent from March to June 2026, falling to 663.7 million dollars from 980.6 million dollars a year earlier. Exports to the United Arab Emirates fell by 42.7 per cent in the same period.

Exports to Saudi Arabia declined by 1.7 per cent, while exports to Oman fell by 25.1 per cent. Exports also remained weak in the following months, with July to October exports down 6.4 per cent compared with the previous year. The figures show Pakistan's trade with Gulf countries remained under pressure during the period of tension.
