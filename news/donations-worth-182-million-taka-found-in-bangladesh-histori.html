Donations Worth 182 Million Taka Found in Bangladesh Historic Mosque Boxes, Counting Takes 15 Hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Donations worth 182,169,517 taka were recovered from the donation boxes of the historic Pagla Mosque in Bangladesh's Kishoreganj district, which took about 15 hours to count. According to media reports, the Pagla Mosque's 15 donation boxes were opened after an interval of four months and 24 days, from which 51 sacks full of cash were recovered. The mosque's president, confirming this at around 10:15 at night, said that besides local currency, foreign currency and gold jewellery were also recovered from the donation boxes in large quantities.

Kishoreganj's Pagla Mosque is famous across Bangladesh for its unique architecture and people's devoted donations, where donation boxes are opened every few months and crores of rupees are collected. Earlier this year on June 27, when the mosque's donation boxes were opened, cash worth 159,080,146 taka besides large quantities of foreign currency and gold and silver jewellery were recovered.
