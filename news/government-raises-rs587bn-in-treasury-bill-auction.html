Government Raises Rs587bn in Treasury Bill Auction

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government raised Rs586.7bn through Wednesday's auction of Market Treasury Bills, according to the State Bank of Pakistan. The amount was well above its Rs400bn target and the Rs435bn of maturing bills. The government raised Rs232.46bn through the auction itself and a further Rs354.25bn via non-competitive bids.

Banks and corporates had placed cumulative bids of Rs2.192 trillion. The highest amount accepted for any tenor was Rs201bn for 12-month papers. In contrast, only Rs19.46bn was raised from the three-month tenor despite bids of Rs666.3bn.
