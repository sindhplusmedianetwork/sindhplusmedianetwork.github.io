NDU National Security Workshop Delegation Meets Punjab CM Maryam Nawaz in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A delegation of the National Defence University's 28th National Security Workshop met Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz in Lahore. The chief minister briefed the delegation in detail on the Punjab government's good governance, development projects and public welfare programmes. Chief Secretary Punjab Zahid Akhtar Zaman informed the delegation about the provincial government's administrative affairs and measures.

Maryam Nawaz also answered the participants' questions, and the workshop participants praised Punjab's governance and public welfare projects. The delegation was also invited on study visits to different cities of Punjab. The participants presented a commemorative souvenir to the chief minister, a group photograph was taken, and the participants described their meeting with her as useful and informative.
