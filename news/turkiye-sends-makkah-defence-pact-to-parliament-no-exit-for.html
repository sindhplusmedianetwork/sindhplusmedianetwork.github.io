Turkiye Sends Makkah Defence Pact to Parliament; No Exit for First 10 Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkiye has sent the Makkah defence pact to its parliament for ratification, moving the agreement closer to a formal mutual-defence commitment between Turkiye, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan. Under the pact, which was signed in August, an armed attack against any one member will be treated as an attack against all. The agreement remains valid indefinitely, with a clause barring any party from withdrawing during the first 10 years.

Turkish officials have clarified that Ankara's support under the pact will be limited to the supply of weapons and equipment and the sharing of intelligence. The move comes as Saudi Arabia faces Houthi attacks and seeks to deepen its security partnerships. Turkiye's army is the second-largest in NATO, giving the pact significant military weight in the region.
