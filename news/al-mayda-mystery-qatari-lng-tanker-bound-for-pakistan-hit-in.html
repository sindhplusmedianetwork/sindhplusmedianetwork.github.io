Al-Mayda mystery: Qatari LNG tanker bound for Pakistan hit in the Gulf; 25 crew safe, six of them Indians

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Qatar's gas transport company Nakilat has confirmed that its LNG tanker Al-Mayda was struck by unidentified projectiles on Friday evening in the Persian Gulf. The Marshall Islands-flagged vessel was carrying liquefied natural gas from Qatar toward Pakistan when it was hit near the Strait of Hormuz. All 25 crew members on board are safe, including six Indian nationals, a fact confirmed through India's shipping ministry.

Qatar and Saudi Arabia strongly condemned the attack on the tanker. Following the incident, the vessel changed course and headed back toward the Gulf, but it remains operational, according to Nakilat. The identity of those behind the attack is still unknown.
