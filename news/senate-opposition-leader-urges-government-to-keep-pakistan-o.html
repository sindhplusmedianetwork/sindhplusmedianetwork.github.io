Senate Opposition Leader Urges Government to Keep Pakistan Out of Yemen Conflict

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senate Opposition Leader Allama Raja Nasir Abbas on Sunday urged the government to keep Pakistan out of the conflict in Yemen and instead pursue a mediatory role. Addressing the Shuhada-i-Muqawamat and Tajdeed-i-Ahd Conference organised by the Majlis Wahdat-i-Muslimeen, he warned that violating the unanimous parliamentary resolution of 2015 would damage national interests. He reminded attendees that parliament had unanimously decided in 2015 that Pakistan would not become part of the Yemen conflict or send troops there.

'On whose authority is Pakistan being made a party to this conflict?' he asked, calling on the government to pull the country out of what he described as a dangerous situation. He said Yemen had offered Pakistan a mediatory role and that he had written to the prime minister about the matter. He also condemned US and Israeli aggression against Iran and criticised the government's excessive praise of US President Donald Trump, including proposals for a Nobel Peace Prize nomination, as contrary to national dignity.
