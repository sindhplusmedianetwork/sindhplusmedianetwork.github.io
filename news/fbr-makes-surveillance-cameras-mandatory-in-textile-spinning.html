FBR Makes Surveillance Cameras Mandatory in Textile Spinning Units

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue has made video surveillance cameras mandatory in textile spinning units to electronically monitor the processing of natural, synthetic and blended fibres. Under Sales Tax General Order No. 30, all registered spinning units must install cameras at specified points by November 15, 2026.

The cameras will cover hopper bale openers, hopper feeders, weighing hoppers and blending feeders where fibres are mixed, specific blow-room lines, waste openers and recycling lines, and places where fibres are stored or mixed before entering the blow room. The system must provide real-time video recording, identify and count fibres, and send data instantly to the FBR's central control unit, while also flagging unexpected stoppages and supporting production analysis and legal action. The FBR said the new order is in addition to Sales Tax General Order No.

08 of 2025, which already covers the monitoring of cotton bales, and that the new instructions take effect immediately.
