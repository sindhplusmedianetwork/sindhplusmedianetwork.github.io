Shadab Khan Ruled Out of Tri-Nation Final on Medical Grounds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan suffered a blow ahead of the tri-nation series final as Shadab Khan was ruled out on medical grounds. The Pakistan Cricket Board confirmed that the all-rounder will miss the final against Sri Lanka at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium. Sources said Shadab was struck by a bouncer during the match against Sri Lanka and could not recover in time.

Fahim Ashraf has been named as his replacement in the playing XI. The final is a major occasion for the hosts, and the team management will hope the change does not disturb their momentum.
