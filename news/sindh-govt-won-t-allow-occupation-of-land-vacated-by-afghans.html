Sindh Govt Won't Allow Occupation of Land Vacated by Afghans, Assembly Told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Assembly was informed on Monday that the provincial government would not allow anyone to occupy land vacated after the repatriation of a large number of Afghan nationals who had been living on Karachi's outskirts for decades. The issue was raised by the lone member of the Jamaat-i-Islami, Muhammad Farooq, during the Sindh Assembly's Question Hour that pertained to katchi abadis. Mr Farooq said an operation had been carried out a few days ago, Afghan settlements were vacated and people had gone back, and some mafias were present to occupy the land when the settlements were being vacated.

Replying to his query, Chief Minister's Adviser Najmi Alam said the vacated lands had been handed over to the Malir Development Authority and the government would never allow any forcible occupation of the land. Mr Alam also said no lease would be granted to settlements located within 50 feet of any drain, on or near protective embankments, or in areas at risk of flooding. He said the Katchi Abadis Department was providing land leases to leaseable settlements, and planning was underway for the relocation of those which could not be leased.

The session also took up gas load-shedding, with the speaker fixing Friday for a debate on the issue, and the annual audit report of the Sindh government accounts for 2025-26 was presented and referred to the Public Accounts Committee.
