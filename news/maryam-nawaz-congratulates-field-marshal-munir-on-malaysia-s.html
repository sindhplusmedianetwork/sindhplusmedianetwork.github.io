Maryam Nawaz congratulates Field Marshal Munir on Malaysia's Hall of Fame honour

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has congratulated Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir on being inducted into the Hall of Fame at Malaysia's National Defence Studies Centre. She expressed her gratitude and good wishes on the honour being conferred on the Field Marshal. The Chief Minister said the recognition is a matter of pride for the country and its armed forces.

She also extended her congratulations to the Pakistan Armed Forces on the occasion. The Hall of Fame honour at Malaysia's National Defence Studies Centre marks a notable recognition for the Field Marshal. The message from the Punjab Chief Minister was shared from Lahore, where she praised the achievement.
