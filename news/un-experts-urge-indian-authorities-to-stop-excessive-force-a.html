UN Experts Urge Indian Authorities to Stop 'Excessive Force' Against Protesters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United Nations human rights experts on Tuesday urged Indian authorities to stop using excessive force against protesters and to order an independent investigation into a recent crackdown in which demonstrators were injured. The experts said opposition parties and student groups had been protesting the Election Commission's revision of voter lists, a process that removed about 130 million names from the rolls. They complained of lathi charges against unarmed students, bans on public assemblies and digital surveillance of protesters.

India has rejected the criticism, saying its law enforcement acted "strictly in accordance with the law." The appeal adds to international scrutiny of the authorities' handling of the demonstrations.
