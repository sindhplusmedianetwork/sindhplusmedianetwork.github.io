Hong Kong Democracy Activist Joshua Wong Sentenced to Another 10 Years in Prison

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hong Kong's High Court has sentenced pro-democracy activist Joshua Wong to another 10 years in prison. The 30-year-old was convicted of conspiracy to collude with foreign forces under the national security law. He had pleaded guilty in September to conspiring with self-exiled activist Nathan Law and others to seek foreign sanctions against Hong Kong and China in 2020.

Judge William Tam set a starting point of 13 years and reduced the sentence to 10 years for the guilty plea. Only two months and two days of the new sentence will run concurrently with his existing sentence. Wong has already spent almost six years in prison and had been due for release in January.

He is now not expected to be freed until late 2036.
