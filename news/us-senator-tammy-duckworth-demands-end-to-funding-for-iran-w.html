US Senator Tammy Duckworth demands end to funding for Iran war

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

United States Senator Tammy Duckworth has demanded that Congress cut off funding for the war against Iran and force President Donald Trump to bring the conflict to an end. In a statement issued on social media, the Democrat senator from Illinois said the time had come for Congress to stop funding the war and compel the president to end the confrontation. Duckworth, a member of the Senate Armed Services Committee, claimed that since Trump began the war against Iran, American citizens have spent more than $1,200 extra per household on petrol and diesel.

She also appealed for greater pressure on the Trump administration to end the war. Her call comes as the conflict between the United States and Iran continues, with Washington maintaining a naval blockade of Iranian ports and Tehran vowing to resist it.
