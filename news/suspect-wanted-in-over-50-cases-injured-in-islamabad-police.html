Suspect Wanted in Over 50 Cases Injured in Islamabad Police Operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A suspected member of an inter-provincial car and motorcycle theft gang, Junaid Ashraf, was injured during a police operation in Islamabad's Sangjani area. Ashraf was wanted by the Islamabad and Rawalpindi police in more than 50 cases. Police had taken him to under-construction rooms in a forest area to recover stolen motorcycles.

During the recovery, four armed accomplices opened fire in an attempt to free him. Ashraf was injured by shots allegedly fired by his own accomplices, while the police remained safe behind bulletproof jackets. Police recovered two stolen motorcycles and parts from the site, and a search for the accomplices is under way.
