Two Killed in Stampede at TikToker Mr Patlu's Event; Case Registered, Six Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Sialkot have registered a case against TikToker Ansar Mahmood, known as Mr Patlu, and others after a stampede at his mehndi ceremony left two people dead and several injured, and six people have been arrested. The FIR names nine accused including Mr Patlu, besides 45 unidentified persons, on charges including causing death by negligence, violating official orders, vandalism, and provocative and unlawful assembly. Police said a second injured man, Rehan, who had been referred to Lahore in critical condition, succumbed to his injuries on Sunday, while Aqeel had died on the spot on Saturday evening.

According to the FIR, the ceremony was underway at Faraz Cricket Ground on Daska Road on Saturday evening, where Mr Patlu had drawn huge crowds through social media by offering motorcycles, a car and other prizes. When he announced from the stage that everyone should enter the ground at once, the crowd surged forward and a stampede broke out. Initial investigation found the organisers had advertised a cash distribution of Rs20 million along with a car, 50 motorcycles and mobile phones, drawing people from Sialkot, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Wazirabad, Mandi Bahauddin and Narowal.

The district administration said an NOC had been issued for the event but was cancelled by the deputy commissioner after violations surfaced, as the organisers began arrangements around 4pm though the permit allowed the programme only after Isha prayers. Rescue 1122 teams reached the spot promptly and shifted the injured to hospitals, while police say Mr Patlu and the other accused are absconding and efforts are underway to arrest them.
