India Sets Conditions for Sheikh Hasina's Return to Bangladesh in December

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India has set conditions for the return of ousted Bangladeshi prime minister Sheikh Hasina to Bangladesh in December. According to British media reports, India wants the Bangladesh army to oversee her return and Dhaka to guarantee her a fresh trial, so that her life and safety are not at risk when she returns home. Hasina has announced she will return to Bangladesh in December, and New Delhi agrees with the plan but wants guarantees about her safety and a fair trial.

She fled to India about two years ago aboard a Bangladeshi military aircraft after a mob stormed her Dhaka residence. The conditions now set out how India expects her return to be handled.
