PSX Crosses 172,000 as KSE-100 Surges Nearly 4,000 Points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange closed sharply higher on Wednesday, November 4, 2026. The benchmark KSE-100 index gained 3,994.62 points to finish at a record 172,013.48. A total of 568 companies' shares changed hands during the session.

Of these, 368 advanced, 103 declined and 97 remained unchanged. More than 841.61 million shares worth Rs35.49 billion were traded. TPL Properties, Bank of Punjab and K-Electric led the session by volume.

Pervez Ahmed Consultancy, Kohinoor Mills and First ANOR Modaraba topped the list of advancers.
