Essa's double inspires Pakistan victory over Laos in U-17 Asian Cup qualifier

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Under-17 side defeated hosts Laos 3-0 in their Group D AFC Under-17 Asian Cup 2027 qualifier at the Laos National Stadium on Saturday. Muhammad Essa scored twice in the match, while Hazrat Abbas added the third goal to complete the victory. The result gave Pakistan their second win in three matches in the qualification campaign.

The win strengthened Pakistan's position in the group as the campaign moves towards its closing stage. Pakistan will now play their final group fixture against Jordan on November 20. The team will look to carry the momentum from the Laos victory into that decisive match.
