AJK Assembly Speaker Calls for Consensus on Future of Refugee Seats

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Azad Jammu and Kashmir Legislative Assembly Speaker Chaudhry Tariq Farooq has called for a broad-based consensus on the future of refugee seats in the assembly, urging political leaders to help resolve the current crisis through dialogue and reforms. Speaking to journalists at the Speaker House in Muzaffarabad on Thursday, he said leaders should align with national objectives and find a way forward rather than dwelling on the past, adding that learning from mistakes is wisdom and he supported reforms in all institutions. He said AJK could not progress with its large administrative structure and high expenditure, and that the newly elected young government should be given an opportunity to move in the right direction.

He also said subsidies on electricity and flour would not be withdrawn, though relief and concessions should be targeted on the basis of data. Distinguishing constitutional offices from the executive, he said the assembly speaker was not part of the government, just as the state president was not, and noted the government had been in office only a few weeks with the second phase of cabinet formation pending Nawaz Sharif's return to Pakistan. The cabinet had not been expanded because elections in seven Poonch constituencies were still pending, without which the government could not be considered complete and fully functional.

On the Poonch situation, he said informal negotiations were under way through the AJK president to improve law and order, expressing hope that backchannel contacts would produce positive results while urging the media to avoid speculation. He accused the PPP of exploiting the Poonch situation for political gain during its brief stint in power, and recalled that he and some PML-N leaders had called for a fact-finding commission a year ago but no party had supported the proposal.
