FIA Arrests Woman for Taking Rs5.2 Million With False Promise of UK Visa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) has arrested a woman in Rawalpindi accused of taking Rs5.2 million from a citizen by promising him a work visa for the United Kingdom. According to the FIA, the woman took Rs1 million from the victim and gave him a fake IELTS certificate in return. She then promised to arrange the visa through marriage and handed over a fake nikah registration certificate.

The agency said the woman had no lawful means of arranging the visa and had deceived the victim at every step. A case has been registered against her and further investigation is under way. The FIA has advised citizens to verify visa agents and documents through official channels before paying any money.
