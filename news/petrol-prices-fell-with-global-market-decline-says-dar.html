Petrol prices fell with global market decline, says Dar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar has said petroleum prices in Pakistan have fallen in line with the decline in global oil prices. He said petrol, which peaked at Rs458 per litre on 3 April 2026, now costs Rs325 per litre. Diesel has fallen to Rs358 per litre, a drop of Rs162, while petrol is down Rs133 from its peak.

He said the fall in prices reflects the decline in international oil markets. He added that the government's fuel subsidy scheme has so far reached 16 million beneficiaries. The beneficiaries include motorcycle owners, rickshaw owners and owners of 800cc cars.
