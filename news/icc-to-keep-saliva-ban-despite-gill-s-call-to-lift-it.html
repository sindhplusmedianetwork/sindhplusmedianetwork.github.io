ICC to Keep Saliva Ban Despite Gill's Call to Lift It

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Cricket Council has no plans to reverse the ban on applying saliva to shine the ball, despite India skipper Shubman Gill's call to allow the practice again. Gill said on Monday the ban should be revoked to help bowlers get reverse swing. His remarks came after India's high-scoring opening Test against New Zealand ended in a draw.

The ICC says saliva can materially alter the ball when mixed with sweets, chewing gum and mints. The governing body also says the practice is extremely hard to monitor. The ban therefore looks set to stay in place.
