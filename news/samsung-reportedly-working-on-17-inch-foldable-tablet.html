Samsung Reportedly Working on 17-Inch Foldable Tablet

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Samsung is reportedly working on a large foldable tablet whose screen could reach up to 17 inches when unfolded, according to early reports. Folded, the device's screen may measure around 8 inches. Possible designs include an outward-folding 17-inch screen or an inward-folding 17-inch display with a separate 8-inch cover display like the Galaxy Z Fold phones.

The company has not officially confirmed the device. No launch date or price has surfaced so far.
