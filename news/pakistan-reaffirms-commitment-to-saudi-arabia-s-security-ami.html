Pakistan reaffirms commitment to Saudi Arabia's security amid regional tensions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has reiterated its firm commitment to safeguarding Saudi Arabia's sovereignty, security and territorial integrity, describing the Kingdom's defence as a fundamental and non-negotiable priority. Pakistani military spokesman Lieutenant General Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry, in an interview with the Saudi newspaper Asharq Al-Awsat, said Islamabad remained prepared to extend all necessary defensive assistance to Saudi Arabia in view of the prevailing regional situation. He said Pakistan considers the security of Saudi Arabia and the Two Holy Mosques a matter of utmost importance, and was willing to extend support in various forms, including the deployment of military personnel, provision of defence capabilities, training programmes and logistical assistance, depending on the Kingdom's requirements.

The remarks come amid continued attacks on Saudi territory attributed to Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthi movement, with reported strikes involving drones and missiles targeting critical infrastructure, including energy facilities, airports and civilian locations, and Saudi Civil Defense reported the interception of two ballistic missiles over Makkah on Thursday. Chaudhry confirmed that Pakistani military personnel are already stationed in Saudi Arabia and are engaged in defensive assistance, joint training activities, military exercises and manoeuvres. Pakistan and Saudi Arabia strengthened their defence cooperation through a bilateral defence agreement signed in September last year, and Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye later signed the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement on August 7, establishing a collective security framework under which aggression against one participating country is treated as a threat to the others.

Commenting on diplomatic engagement with Iran, Chaudhry said the October 26 meeting in Tehran between representatives of the Makkah Joint Defence Alliance and Iranian officials was intended to develop a mechanism for reducing tensions and promoting regional cooperation.
