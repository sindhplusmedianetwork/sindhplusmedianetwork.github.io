Hajj, Umrah Meningitis Vaccine Sold at Nearly Double Price; Airport Looting Group Exposed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The meningitis vaccine meant for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims is being sold at nearly double its approved price. The DRAP-approved vaccine, priced at Rs1,356, was supplied to the Ministry of Religious Affairs at Rs2,555. The federal health ministry has formed a high-level committee headed by the Additional Secretary Health to review vaccine procurement and pricing from 2022 to 2026, with a report due by October 29.

Three Border Health Services officers are already in FIA custody over alleged irregularities in Umrah vaccine procurement. In a related development, a group collecting Rs4,500 from every Umrah pilgrim in the name of vaccination, under the guise of the federal health ministry, has been exposed at airports. The group's plan to set up temporary vaccination centres at airports nationwide and continue the practice has been uncovered, and the prime minister has taken strict notice, forming an inquiry committee immediately.

The same group was reportedly running a social media campaign pressuring Umrah pilgrims to get vaccinated and not to travel without a card, even though Saudi Arabia has imposed no such mandatory condition and polio and other vaccines are available free of charge at hospitals.
