Asad Umar demands announcement of new elections

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former federal minister Asad Umar called for the announcement of new elections. He said new elections are needed to pull the country out of its political quagmire. In a post on X, he said PTI, PPP and JUI-F all call the 2024 election results fake.

He said even by Form 47 figures these parties hold half the votes. He said the government has been dragged like a vehicle without an engine for four and a half years. The economy has collapsed in this period, he added.
