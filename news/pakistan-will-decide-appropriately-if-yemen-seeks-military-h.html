Pakistan Will Decide Appropriately if Yemen Seeks Military Help: DG ISPR

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan will take an appropriate decision if Yemen requests military assistance, DG ISPR Lt Gen Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry has said. In an interview with Independent Arabia, he said the protection of the Haramain Sharifain remains the top priority and there will be no compromise on it. He said that if negotiations fail to settle the matter, the collective defence system will necessarily have to be activated.

The military spokesperson stressed that Pakistan's commitment to the security of Saudi Arabia is unwavering. He added that expanding the military conflict in the Middle East serves no one's interest. The Makkah alliance, he said, has no aggressive intent, and Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye all believe in regional peace and prosperity.
