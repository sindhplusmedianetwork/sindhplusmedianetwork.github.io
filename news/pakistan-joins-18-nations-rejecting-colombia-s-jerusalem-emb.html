Pakistan Joins 18 Nations Rejecting Colombia's Jerusalem Embassy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has joined 18 other countries in condemning Colombia's inauguration of an embassy in Jerusalem, declaring that any move to recognise the city as Israel's capital is null and void and has no legal effect. The joint statement was issued by the foreign ministers of Pakistan, Algeria, Djibouti, Egypt, Indonesia, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Libya, Malaysia, Oman, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, Syria, Sudan, Turkiye and Yemen, according to a Foreign Office statement released on Friday. The ministers expressed their unequivocal rejection of the move, saying it contravened relevant United Nations Security Council resolutions, particularly resolutions 476 and 478 adopted in 1980, and undermined efforts for a just and lasting peace based on the two-state solution.

They called on the Colombian government to reconsider and reverse the decision and to uphold its obligations under international law. Colombia opened its embassy in Jerusalem on October 14, becoming the 10th country to establish a diplomatic mission in the city, according to Reuters. The move marked a shift in Colombia's relations with Israel under President Abelardo de la Espriella, who reversed his predecessor Gustavo Petro's decision to sever diplomatic ties with Israel in May 2024.

Most countries maintain their embassies to Israel in Tel Aviv, while Israel regards Jerusalem as its capital and Palestinians seek East Jerusalem as the capital of a future state.
