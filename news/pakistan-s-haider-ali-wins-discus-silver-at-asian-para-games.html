Pakistan's Haider Ali wins discus silver at Asian Para Games in Nagoya

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Paralympic star Haider Ali has won the silver medal in the men's F37 discus throw at the Asian Para Games in Nagoya. The Tokyo 2020 gold medallist recorded a best throw of 57.20 metres on Wednesday. Gold went to Uzbekistan's Marufkhujaev with 57.39 metres, a new Asian Para Games record, leaving Haider just 19 centimetres short of the top spot.

India's Haney claimed the bronze medal. It was another podium finish for Haider Ali, one of Pakistan's most decorated para athletes, who continues to represent the country at the highest level of para athletics.
