Lahore High Court sets aside life sentence of husband in wife murder case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has set aside the life sentence awarded to a husband in a wife murder case. The court accepted the appeal of the accused, Muhammad Riaz Umar, and acquitted him on the grounds of contradictions in the statements of the witnesses. The court observed that the eight witnesses in the case could not state the correct facts, while the case was registered eight hours late even though the police station was only two kilometres away.

Justice Waheed Khan heard the appeal of the accused Muhammad Riaz Umar and issued the order acquitting him.
