Chelsea sink Roma 2-0 to keep pace at Women's Champions League summit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelsea claimed their second win of the Women's Champions League season with a 2-0 victory over Roma at Plough Lane on Wednesday. Substitute Ellie Carpenter, on her 50th Champions League appearance, broke the deadlock in the 51st minute from Wieke Kaptein's cutback, before Kadeisha Buchanan headed home Kaptein's cross a minute from time. The result moved Chelsea to seven points, joining Arsenal and Lyon at the top of the table, though they sit behind both on goal difference.

Coach Sonia Bompastor said she was pleased with the early second-half goal but felt her side could have killed the game off earlier. Arsenal also won, beating Benfica 3-0 as Alessia Russo's hat-trick made her the highest-scoring English player in European football with 23 goals, while Lyon thrashed Inter Milan 5-0 with a hat-trick from Vicki Becho off the bench. In the night's other big tie, Bayern Munich came from two goals down to draw 2-2 with Real Madrid, for whom Athenea and Lineth Beerensteyn had scored before Edna Imade and Momoko Tanikawa levelled.

There was a moment's silence before kick-off in Munich in memory of the at least 68 people killed in a train crash near Madrid earlier on Wednesday, with Real Madrid's players wearing black armbands.
