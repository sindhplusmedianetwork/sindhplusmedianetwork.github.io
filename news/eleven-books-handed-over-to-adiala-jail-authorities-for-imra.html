Eleven Books Handed Over to Adiala Jail Authorities for Imran Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Eleven books have been handed over to Adiala jail authorities for PTI founder and former prime minister Imran Khan to read. Khalid Yousaf Chaudhry confirmed the handover of the books. The books cover a diverse range of subjects, including world and ancient history.

They also cover Islamic history, law, philosophy, Sufism and international relations. The collection includes works on the rise and fall of civilisations. It also includes books on global political developments.
