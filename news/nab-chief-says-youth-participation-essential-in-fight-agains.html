NAB chief says youth participation essential in fight against corruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Accountability Bureau Chairman Lt Gen (retd) Nazir Ahmed Butt has said that the participation of young people is indispensable in the struggle against corruption. He described the recovery and protection of national wealth as among NAB's key priorities. He stressed that educational institutions can play an important role in promoting awareness against corruption and the values of transparency and honesty.

The NAB chairman was speaking during a meeting with the president of a private university, Ibrahim Hassan Murad, in Lahore. Murad praised NAB's efforts for depositing Rs6.2 trillion back into the national treasury, calling it important progress in protecting national wealth and fighting corruption. The meeting also discussed promoting educational and awareness activities and the possibilities of mutual cooperation to raise awareness against corruption at the national level.

Murad said that ending corruption is not possible through legal action alone, and that awareness of honesty, transparency and the rule of law must also be raised in society.
