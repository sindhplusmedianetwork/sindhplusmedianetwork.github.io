15 Terrorists Killed in Mastung Operations Under Radd-ul-Fitna-3, Say Security Sources

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces killed 15 terrorists in Balochistan during operations under Operation Radd-ul-Fitna-3, according to security sources. The sources said security forces killed 15 terrorists in two operations in Mastung district. Five terrorists were killed in one operation in Mastung, while ten terrorists were killed in the second operation.

Security sources said multiple terrorist hideouts were destroyed during the operations and a large quantity of weapons and ammunition was recovered. The areas of Killi Pasand Khan, Musa Khan, Khorasani and Kaftari were completely cleared of terrorists during the operation. Security sources said the security forces are determined to eliminate terrorism from Balochistan and operations will continue until the last terrorist is eliminated.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif paid tribute to the security forces for killing 15 terrorists in Mastung, saying the sacrifices and determination of the security forces for the complete elimination of terrorism are commendable and the whole nation stands with its brave security forces in the war against terrorism.
