Pakistan edge South Korea 3-2 to keep Azlan Shah Cup final hopes alive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan kept their Sultan Azlan Shah Cup final hopes alive with a hard-fought 3-2 victory over South Korea in Ipoh, Malaysia, on Tuesday. Hannan Shahid struck twice and Rana Waheed Ashraf was also on target as Pakistan registered their third win in four matches at the 32nd edition of the invitational tournament. Pakistan made the ideal start when Rana Waheed scored a field goal in the fifth minute, and Hannan doubled the lead in the 23rd minute to give his side a 2-0 advantage at half-time.

South Korea pulled one back five minutes into the third quarter through Chun Semin, but Hannan restored the two-goal cushion with his second field goal in the 53rd minute. Pakistan suffered a setback a minute later when Sufyan Khan received a yellow card, leaving them a player short during the closing stages, and Lee Minhyeok converted a penalty corner in the 60th minute, but the side held on for three crucial points. All three of Pakistan's goals came from field play, while South Korea scored once from open play and once from a penalty corner.

The win took Pakistan's tally to nine points from four matches, and they will face hosts Malaysia in their final round-robin fixture on Thursday with a place in Saturday's final at stake.
