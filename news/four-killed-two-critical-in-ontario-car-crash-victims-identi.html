Four Killed, Two Critical in Ontario Car Crash; Victims' Identities Withheld

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A horrific car crash near the city of Guelph in Ontario has left four people dead and two others fighting for their lives. According to Ontario Provincial Police, the crash took place at around 1:35 on Saturday morning on Watson Road South in the Puslinch area of Wellington County. Six people were travelling in the vehicle; four died at the scene while two were rushed to hospital with life-threatening injuries.

Police said their specialized collision investigation teams are examining the scene, but the cause of the crash has not yet been determined. Watson Road South remains closed between Wellington Road 34 and Maltby Road East, and motorists have been asked to use alternate routes. Investigators have appealed to residents and drivers who may have dashcam or home surveillance footage from the time of the crash to come forward.

While some media reports claimed Pakistani nationals were among the victims, police have not confirmed the identity or nationality of anyone involved.
