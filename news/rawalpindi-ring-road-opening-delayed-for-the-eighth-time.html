Rawalpindi Ring Road Opening Delayed for the Eighth Time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The opening of the Rawalpindi Ring Road has been delayed for the eighth time, after the Punjab Ring Road Authority postponed the inauguration ceremony that was scheduled for November 6. Officials said the road will not be opened to the public until its toll booth system and weigh stations are fully completed, so that no vehicle can pass without paying the toll tax. A new inauguration date will be announced once the remaining work is finished, and Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz will inaugurate the project at the Banth Interchange on GT Road.

The 38.3-kilometre road runs from the Banth Interchange on GT Road to the Thalian Motorway link, and its construction continued through three successive governments. Four interchanges — Banth, Chak Beli Khan, Adiala and Chakri — have been completed, while the fifth and final Thalian Interchange remains unfinished because of its broad-based design. The Provincial Development Working Party has meanwhile approved the third revised PC-I of the project, worth Rs50.4 billion, which also covers the construction cost of the Thalian Interchange.

Officials added that installation of the toll booth system is still underway, although the finishing work on the road itself has been completed.
