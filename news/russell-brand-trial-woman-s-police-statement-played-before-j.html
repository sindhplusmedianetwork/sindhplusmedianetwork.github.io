Russell Brand Trial: Woman's Police Statement Played Before Jury in London Court

During the ongoing trial of British actor and comedian Russell Brand, a woman's police statement was played before a jury in a London court. The woman alleged that in 2004, Brand dragged her into a nearby toilet at a London bar party and sexually assaulted her. She said he grabbed her arm and forcibly held her by the head and hair while she was frozen with fear and shock.

The trial covers charges relating to six women from alleged incidents between 1999 and 2009: three counts of rape, three of sexual assault and one of indecent assault. Brand denies all the charges against him.
