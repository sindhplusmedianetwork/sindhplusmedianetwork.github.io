Mumbai woman loses arm after being pushed from train for protesting harassment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A young woman in Mumbai lost her left arm after a male passenger pushed her from a local train at Naigaon station when she protested against him touching her inappropriately. The incident took place on November 4, and the woman also received 16 stitches to her head after falling from the moving train. Police arrested the 53-year-old suspect on November 9, five days after the incident, following an investigation into the attack.

The woman's family says attempted murder charges have not been included in the case against the accused, despite the severity of her injuries. The case has drawn attention to the safety of women travelling on Mumbai's crowded local trains, where harassment complaints are frequently reported. Police say the investigation is continuing, while the woman is recovering from her injuries.
