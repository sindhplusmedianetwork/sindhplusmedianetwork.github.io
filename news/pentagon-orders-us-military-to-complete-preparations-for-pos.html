Pentagon Orders US Military to Complete Preparations for Possible Major Operation Against Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pentagon has instructed the US military to complete preparations for a possible resumption of major operations against Iran, according to US officials cited by the news website Axios. The preparations include deploying additional military assets to the Middle East. Any renewed operation could involve large-scale air strikes on targets including Iran's nuclear facilities, energy centres and key infrastructure.

The report stressed that President Donald Trump has not yet made a final decision to restart military action. The possible involvement of Israeli forces was also mentioned in the report.
