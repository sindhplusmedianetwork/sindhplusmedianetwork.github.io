PMS Officers Question 'Two Standards' in Punjab Bureaucracy Postings

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The PMS Officers Association has raised questions over what it calls an imbalance in Punjab's bureaucracy postings, alleging that relatively junior PAS women officers are being appointed to key posts while senior PMS women officers are sidelined. The association claimed that junior officers are being posted as Deputy Commissioner and to other key field assignments, while senior PMS women officers are denied equal opportunities in the field. Its comparative poster asks whether there are 'two standards of justice' for women officers in the province.

The association has demanded that the Chief Secretary Punjab take notice of the alleged disparity. Administration experts cited in the report said postings should follow rules, merit, seniority and transparent criteria, without discrimination between service groups.
