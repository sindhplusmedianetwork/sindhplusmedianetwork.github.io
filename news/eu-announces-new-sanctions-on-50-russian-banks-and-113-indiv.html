EU Announces New Sanctions on 50 Russian Banks and 113 Individuals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The European Union has announced a new package of sanctions against Russia, adding 50 Russian banks and financial institutions to its sanctions list. The measures also cover 113 Russian citizens linked to sectors that fund the war effort. The sanctions target people and entities connected to Russia's banking, oil and energy sectors, as well as those supporting its shadow oil fleet.

The European Union said the steps are aimed at cutting off revenue that finances the war in Ukraine. It also reiterated that there will be no compromise on Ukraine's sovereignty and territorial integrity.
