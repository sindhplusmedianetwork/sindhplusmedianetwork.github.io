BISP to include unmarried deserving women aged 45 and above

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Benazir Income Support Programme board has approved including never-married deserving women aged 45 or older in the programme. BISP Chairperson Senator Rubina Khalid said these women will receive financial assistance if they meet the eligibility criteria. The decision extends BISP coverage to a group that was previously excluded from the programme.

Under the approval, women in this category who qualify under the programme's rules will be able to receive support. The move brings a new group of deserving women within the scope of the programme. It marks a widening of BISP's coverage to include women who had remained outside it because of their marital status.
