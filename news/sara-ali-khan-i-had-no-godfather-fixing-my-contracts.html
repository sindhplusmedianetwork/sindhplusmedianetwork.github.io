Sara Ali Khan: 'I Had No Godfather Fixing My Contracts'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Sara Ali Khan has spoken candidly about the nepotism debate in the film industry in a recent interview. She said that being born into a film family opened doors for her, but the constant debate ends up overshadowing the hard work of artists. Sara revealed that she lives with her mother and had no father or godfather in the industry who could arrange contracts or request roles for her.

She named Janhvi Kapoor and Ananya Panday as fellow artists who, like her, never chose which family to be born into. The actress argued that it is unfair to judge an artist's efforts only by their family background. She maintained that while her lineage gave her access, her work and talent had to speak for themselves.

The remarks come amid an ongoing public conversation about privilege in the entertainment world.
