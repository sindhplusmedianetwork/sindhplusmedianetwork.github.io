Monks visit ancient Buddhist sites

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An international delegation of senior Buddhist monastics and religious scholars from across Asia visited the Islamabad Museum and the historic Shah Allah Ditta Caves. The visit is part of the three-day Gandhara Strategic Dialogue 2026, being held under the theme 'Towards a Shared Future'. The delegation represents Thailand, Laos, Vietnam, Japan, Cambodia, Sri Lanka, South Korea and Bangladesh.

The dialogue is aimed at strengthening cooperation for the preservation and global recognition of Gandhara's shared Buddhist heritage. The delegates toured the museum's Gandhara artefacts and the ancient caves, which are among the region's most important Buddhist sites.
