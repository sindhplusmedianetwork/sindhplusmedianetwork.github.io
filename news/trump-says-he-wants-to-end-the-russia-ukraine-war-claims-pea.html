Trump Says He Wants to End the Russia-Ukraine War, Claims Peace Talks Progressing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said he wants to bring an end to the ongoing war between Russia and Ukraine. In a statement posted on Truth Social, he said he did not want to take any step that would push Russia away from the negotiating table. Trump claimed significant progress was being made in peace talks, including negotiations over the release of hostages.

He said signing a relevant document at this stage could affect the negotiation process, though he could sign it at any time later. The president accused Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy of striking Russian diesel refineries, saying the attacks were hurting the global diesel market, including American farmers, ranchers and businesses. He also accused Zelenskyy of lacking interest in ending the war, saying Ukraine was receiving heavy financial aid from Europe while its military had suffered heavy losses.

Trump closed by saying he wants the war ended, though his allegations and claims were presented without independent verification.
