Qatari LNG Ship Attacked Near Strait of Hormuz While Bound for Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Qatari liquefied natural gas carrier Al Mayda, carrying gas from Qatar to Pakistan, was attacked near the Strait of Hormuz on October 30. Pakistan's authorities had contacted Iran in advance to secure safe passage for the vessel through the strait, and Tehran granted permission just hours before the attack, according to a Bloomberg report. The vessel, sailing under the flag of the Marshall Islands, was hit by unknown objects despite the clearance.

Operator Nakilat confirmed the strike, saying no crew member was injured and the ship remains safe. Following the attack, the Al Mayda turned around and headed back toward the Gulf. The development has made the delivery of the gas cargo to Pakistan uncertain.

The incident adds to growing security concerns over energy shipments passing through the strategic waterway.
