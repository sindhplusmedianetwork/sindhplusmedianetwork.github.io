Press bodies, UN condemn settler assault on American photographer in West Bank

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An American photojournalist covering violence in the occupied West Bank was attacked by Israeli settlers on Friday and taken to a hospital in Ramallah. Heidi Levine, a photographer for the Washington Post, was documenting Palestinian olive farmers when she was assaulted. She sustained injuries to her leg and her hand and was receiving treatment at a Ramallah hospital.

The Washington Post said it was 'outraged' by the attack, adding that Levine was wearing clearly visible press markings at the time. The United Nations and press freedom organisations joined the condemnation and demanded a thorough investigation into the incident. Israeli police have opened an investigation, while the military said the circumstances surrounding the attack would be examined.
