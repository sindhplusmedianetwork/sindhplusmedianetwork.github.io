Rabia Ahsan's brother rejects reports of withdrawing murder case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khurram, the brother of slain anchor Rabia Ahsan, has rejected reports that the family had decided to withdraw the murder case. He said the news attributed to him was incorrect and maintained that his sister was murdered. He said his sister leaves behind two daughters and that he has not met his mother since yesterday because she is in trauma.

He asked how he could forgive the unjust murder of his sister. According to him, the family servant told police that a shot was fired in the bedroom, after which police first took the servant away and later released him. He added that the family still does not properly know what happened.
