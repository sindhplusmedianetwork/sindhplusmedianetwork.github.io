Winter Power Bills May Rise by Over Rs 6,000 as Fuel Price Adjustment Set to Climb

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Electricity consumers in Pakistan are bracing for a difficult winter as the fuel price adjustment for December, January and February is feared to increase two to three times, which could push monthly bills higher by more than Rs 6,000. According to official figures, the Power Division has estimated September's fuel price adjustment at approximately Rs 2.50 per unit, on which NEPRA's decision is still pending. Power Division sources say that if this adjustment forms the baseline, the winter months could see the fuel price adjustment climb two to three times because of lower hydel power generation and costlier electricity produced from expensive fuels.

Consumers are therefore expected to face one of the most expensive billing quarters in the coming months.
