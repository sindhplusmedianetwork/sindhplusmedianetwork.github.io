'How long will people bear inflation and heavy taxes?' asks Miftah Ismail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former finance minister Miftah Ismail has said that people need relief, not more economic burden. In a statement, he asked how long the public will keep bearing the burden of inflation, heavy taxes and rising expenses. He said the prices of essential commodities in Pakistan are higher than in other countries of the region, and the direct burden of rising prices falls on the pockets of ordinary citizens.

Miftah noted that a comparative review of electricity, gas, petrol, diesel, LPG, flour, rice, ghee, chicken and pulses prices in Pakistan, India and Bangladesh reveals an alarming reality. The question, he said, is how long people will continue to shoulder inflation, heavy taxes and growing costs. The public wants relief, he added, not further economic burden.
