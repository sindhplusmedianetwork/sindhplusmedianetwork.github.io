World Bank Sees Pakistan Growth at 3.8%, Rising to 4.2%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Bank has projected Pakistan's economic growth at 3.8 percent for the current fiscal year, improving to 4.2 percent in fiscal years 2027-28. In its Pakistan Development Outlook, the bank warned that the pace of growth is too low to create jobs for the 3.6 million people entering the workforce each year. The report noted that poverty rose from 22 percent in 2019 to 29 percent in 2025.

It said the rupee's managed parity and elevated energy prices remain drags on the country's competitiveness. The bank urged faster structural reforms to lift growth and reduce poverty.
