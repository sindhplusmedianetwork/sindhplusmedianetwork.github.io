Chinese Researchers Say 'Electric Comb' Could Treat Hereditary Baldness

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Researchers at Beihang University in China have developed a comb-shaped device called Detangle that combines electrical stimulation with the delivery of genetic material deep into hair follicles to treat hereditary hair loss. In tests on mice with testosterone-induced hair loss, the device restored nearly 100 percent of hair over 28 days. That result outperformed the standard treatment minoxidil, which achieved about 70 percent recovery.

The researchers say the device works by combining electrical stimulation with genetic material delivered deep into the hair follicles. The device has not yet been tested in humans. It is therefore not a proven treatment for people, and further testing would be needed before it could be used on humans.
