Trump Openly Endorses Netanyahu in Israel's Upcoming General Elections

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Trump has openly declared his support for Benjamin Netanyahu in Israel's upcoming general elections. Addressing Israeli voters, Trump described Netanyahu as a strong, confident and courageous leader. He said that without Netanyahu it would be difficult for him to complete the 'job' on Iran.

Trump added that replacing Netanyahu would be a bad decision. Netanyahu thanked Trump for his open endorsement and said the two leaders would together complete the 'job' on Iran. The remarks mark an unusually direct intervention by a US president in another country's election campaign.
