Woman badly burned in acid attack by brother-in-law in Kasur

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

KASUR: A woman was seriously injured after her brother-in-law allegedly threw acid on her in the Purani Mandi area within the limits of the Pattoki City police station on Saturday. According to the police, Shahbaz allegedly threw acid on his sister-in-law, leaving her in a serious condition with burns on her face and other parts of the body. The victim was shifted for medical treatment.

Police have registered the incident and begun proceedings against the suspect. Acid attacks on women remain a grave concern in the region, with rights activists repeatedly calling for stricter enforcement of laws against such violence.
