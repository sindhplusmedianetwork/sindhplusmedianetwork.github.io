Balochistan's First Women's Job Fair Brings 365 Skilled Participants, 61 Job Offers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

For Zara Bibi, a young woman from Noshki, Balochistan's first-ever job fair for skilled women was more than just an event, as it offered her an opportunity to turn the skills she had acquired through technical training into a source of income and take a step towards financial independence. The Balochistan Technical Education and Vocational Training Authority (B-TEVTA) organised the fair, bringing together trained women, businesses, potential employers and financial institutions under one roof. A total of 365 skilled women participated in the event, of whom 61 received job offers, while 110 were selected for paid internships designed to prepare them for professional employment, and 25 business organisations conducted interviews with women trained in various vocational fields.

Zara, who had completed an eight-week technical training programme, said she was excited about an upcoming three-day visit to Lahore that would provide further opportunities to learn and develop professionally, adding that organising such job fairs was the need of the hour. For Sabira Sultana, provincial coordinator of ECI, the job fair represented the culmination of 10 months of sustained efforts supported by the German development organisation GIZ, and she said the broader objective was to enable women to use their training by securing jobs or establishing their own businesses. Fatima Nazir, who runs a beauty studio in Quetta, interviewed around 38 women during the fair, of whom 11 met the standards required by her business, and welcomed the event as a positive step towards expanding women's access to employment.

Saleh Muhammad Nasar, secretary of Labour and Manpower in Balochistan, said the provincial government was taking steps to promote the economic empowerment of young people and women, noting that the province has 135 vocational training institutions that require further improvement. B-TEVTA Managing Director Abdul Kabir Zarkoon expressed hope that the job fair would help trained women secure employment after completing their courses, saying the authority was focusing on vocational training aligned with market demand and intended to invite more national and international companies to future job fairs.
