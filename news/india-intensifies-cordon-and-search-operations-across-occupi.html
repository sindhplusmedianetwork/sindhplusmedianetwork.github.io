India intensifies cordon-and-search operations across occupied Kashmir, says KMS

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian occupation forces have intensified cordon-and-search operations (CASOs) across Indian-occupied Jammu and Kashmir to target freedom activists and their sympathisers, according to Kashmir Media Service. Indian police, in close coordination with the army, Central Armed Police Forces and intelligence agencies, have stepped up operations to dismantle logistical and communication networks supporting the ongoing freedom struggle. The renewed crackdown follows directions from the New Delhi-installed Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha during a high-level meeting in Srinagar to maintain sustained pressure on freedom activists.

Director General of Police Nalin Prabhat has underlined the need to go beyond encounters and systematically dismantle support structures, warning that individuals supporting freedom activities would be identified. Officials said the strategy targets local networks suspected of facilitating movement, providing shelter and maintaining communication links, with sympathisers under scrutiny for alleged roles in communication, transportation and supply movement.
