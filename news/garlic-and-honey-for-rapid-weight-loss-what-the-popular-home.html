Garlic and honey for rapid weight loss: what the popular home remedy really does

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A common home remedy suggests using garlic with honey for weight loss, but experts say no single food can deliver rapid weight loss on its own. The traditional method involves filling a medium-sized jar with pure honey, adding peeled garlic cloves, and keeping the jar in an airy place for about a month so the two can blend. The remedy is usually taken in the morning on an empty stomach, by eating one crushed garlic clove from the jar and then drinking water.

Garlic contains nutrients including vitamin B6, vitamin C, fibre, manganese and calcium, and it is traditionally believed to support digestion and protect against stomach ailments. However, the report notes that rapid weight loss depends on overall eating habits and physical activity rather than any single remedy. It also cautions that raw garlic can cause stomach irritation in some people and that honey is high in sugar.
