Rising global interest in minerals forum shows confidence in Pakistan, says Dar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar has praised the success of the third edition of the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026. He was speaking as chief guest at the forum's closing gala dinner in Islamabad. Dar said the marked increase in international participants reflects growing global confidence in Pakistan's mineral sector and its investment potential.

He congratulated the organisers of the forum on its success. The deputy prime minister expressed hope that the contacts and discussions during the forum would lead to meaningful partnerships and mutually beneficial cooperation.
