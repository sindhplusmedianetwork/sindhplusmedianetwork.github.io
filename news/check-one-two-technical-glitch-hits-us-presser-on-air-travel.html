'Check, one two': Technical glitch hits US presser on air travel chaos

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Trump administration officials were left red-faced on Saturday when their public effort to address the massive tech disruption that grounded thousands of US flights was itself hit with embarrassing technical glitches. Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy had just begun a televised press conference, a day after a nationwide ground stop blamed on ageing technology, when a deep echoing hum came from the microphones and speakers. Duffy paused and apologised as technicians replaced the microphones, and resumed speaking only to be hit by brittle feedback again seconds later.

The press conference had been called to explain the disruption that stranded thousands of travellers across the United States. The repeated glitches during the live briefing drew attention on the day, turning an attempt to reassure the public into an awkward moment for the administration.
