Report Alleges Fraud in Punjab's Rs7 Billion 'Parwaz Card' Overseas-Jobs Scheme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A special report by journalist Ausaf Ali Khan has alleged large-scale fraud in the Punjab government's Rs7 billion 'Parwaz Card' overseas-employment scheme. The report claims that more than 145 selected candidates were charged up to Rs4 lakh each for visa, airfare and travel costs that the government had promised to bear, with the money supposedly to be refunded. Youth selected for field-relevant jobs in Saudi Arabia with salaries of 11,000 to 13,000 riyals were instead given low-paid municipal and cleaning work, the report says.

When the workers refused the jobs, the company allegedly demanded a 5,000-riyal penalty to terminate their contracts. It further alleges that participants at the scheme's launch ceremony were instructed to tell Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz that they had received all promised funds and facilities.
