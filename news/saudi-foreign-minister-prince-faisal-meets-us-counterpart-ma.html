Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal Meets US Counterpart Marco Rubio

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan has met United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio in Atlanta. The meeting took place on the sidelines of the G20 foreign ministers' meeting. The two sides discussed the challenges facing the region, according to Saudi media reports from Riyadh.

They also signed an agreement on American participation in Expo 2030 Riyadh. The meeting marks a fresh round of high-level contact between the two countries on regional issues.
