Ruling PML-N continues to dither on new provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The ruling PML-N continues to show an ambiguous position on the creation of new provinces. Several leaders, including cabinet members, have supported a referendum on new administrative units. Other party leaders have opposed the move.

Senior PML-N leader Khawaja Saad Rafique called for fundamental governance reforms before any move to reduce the size of provinces. Adviser to the Prime Minister on Political Affairs Rana Sanaullah said positions expressed by federal ministers did not represent an agreed party policy.
