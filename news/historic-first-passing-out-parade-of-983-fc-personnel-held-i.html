Historic first passing out parade of 983 FC personnel held in Shabqadar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first passing out parade of the first batch of the Federal Constabulary's Special Protection Wing and Anti-Riot Wing was held at the Shabqadar Training Centre, with Federal Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi attending as the chief guest. For the first time, 983 personnel from all four provinces, Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan passed out after completing their training, including 35 women. The Interior Minister inspected the parade, and a smartly turned-out contingent presented him a salute.

He also watched demonstrations of the professional training of the personnel of the Special Protection Wing and Anti-Riot Wing, and distributed prizes among those who showed outstanding performance. Addressing the ceremony, Mohsin Naqvi said the splendid parade had proved that all the personnel are united, organised and fully prepared for their mission, and that their performance reflects their practical training as well as their determination, courage and spirit of serving the country. He said the passing-out personnel are the practical representatives of a major change coming to the Federal Constabulary and the first soldiers of a new era, founded on responsibility, clear direction, modern requirements and the spirit of national service.

He said the Federal Constabulary, formerly the Frontier Constabulary, is a force with a history of more than a century, which has played an important role in operations against terrorism, the protection of government installations and national projects, and the establishment of law and order. He said the Interior Ministry has taken practical steps to improve the training and facilities of the personnel, including ensuring 24-hour electricity supply at the FC Training Academy in Shabqadar, approval of a modern swimming pool, and Arabic and English language courses for the personnel.
