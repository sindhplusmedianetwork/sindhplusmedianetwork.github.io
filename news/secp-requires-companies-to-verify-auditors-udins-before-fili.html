SECP Requires Companies to Verify Auditors' UDINs Before Filing Accounts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) has advised companies to verify the Unique Document Identification Number (UDIN) on their auditor's report before filing audited financial statements. This applies to companies required by law to file audited financial statements with SECP, said a news release. A UDIN is a unique number that helps users confirm who issued an audit report, and it also helps prevent the use of forged reports.

Auditors generate the number through the portal of their respective institute, either the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan or the Institute of Cost and Management Accountants of Pakistan. Regulation 6A of the Auditors (Reporting Obligations) Regulations, 2018 requires auditors to include a UDIN on every report they issue under the regulations, and companies can check the number through the relevant institute's portal before filing. SECP will accept filings only if the accompanying auditor's report carries a valid UDIN, and use of an invalid UDIN may lead to regulatory action or civil or criminal proceedings under applicable law.
