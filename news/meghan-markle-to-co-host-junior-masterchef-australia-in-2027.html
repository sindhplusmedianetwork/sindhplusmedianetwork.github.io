Meghan Markle to Co-Host Junior MasterChef Australia in 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, will co-host the 2027 season of Junior MasterChef Australia alongside presenter and chef Poh Ling Yeow, Australian broadcaster Channel 10 has announced. The children's cooking competition, a spinoff of MasterChef Australia with young contestants, is returning after being off air since the end of 2020, with filming expected in Melbourne. Meghan appeared as a guest judge on the flagship Australian show during her four-day visit to the country with Prince Harry in April, and her episode drew the programme's largest Sunday audience since 2023.

In a statement, Meghan said she had an incredible time with the MasterChef team and that working with young talent was an easy yes, adding that she loves cooking with her own children. Poh, who was runner-up in the first season of MasterChef Australia before becoming a judge, said reuniting with Meghan would be a real treat and that she expected plenty of delicious surprises and a little mischief. It remains unclear whether Meghan will be paid for the role, and whether Prince Harry and their children will join her in Australia for filming.

Meghan and Harry moved back to Britain from the United States in August after six years in California.
