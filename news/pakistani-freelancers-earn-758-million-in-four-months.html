Pakistani freelancers earn $758 million in four months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani freelancers earned $758 million in four months, the Pakistan Freelancers Association said, marking a 47 percent rise in their earnings. IT freelancers accounted for $463 million of the total, while non-IT services brought in $295.5 million. The association said young professionals are increasingly turning to freelancing platforms in search of better employment.

Association chairman Ibrahim Amin said the growth showed the promise of the country's freelance workforce. He urged government and private institutions to pay attention to training freelancers so the momentum can continue. The association's figures underline how freelancing has become a major source of foreign exchange for Pakistan.
