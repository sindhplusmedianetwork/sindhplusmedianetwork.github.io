December showdown looms over demand for new provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

MQM-P has stepped up its campaign for new provinces, calling administrative restructuring a constitutional necessity. The party has warned that December 2026 could prove decisive. Party chairman Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui said Karachi generates 66 percent of Pakistan's revenue yet is deprived of basic facilities.

He demanded the resignation of Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah. Senior leader Mustafa Kamal said a referendum on new provinces would take place. He added that major decisions were expected in the coming days.
