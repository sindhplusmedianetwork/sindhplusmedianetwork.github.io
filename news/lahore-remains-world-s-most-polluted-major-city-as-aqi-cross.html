Lahore remains world's most polluted major city as AQI crosses 600

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore was ranked the world's most polluted major city on Monday as its Air Quality Index reached 590, with an hourly reading of 647 placing it in the hazardous category, according to IQAir. Within the city, several areas recorded far worse levels, with Allama Iqbal Town at 1,033, while other neighbourhoods also reported hazardous air quality. The crisis was not confined to Lahore, as other parts of Punjab also recorded hazardous air quality, with Raiwind topping the Pakistan city ranking at 688, followed by Multan at 655 and Faisalabad at 600.

The deterioration came a day after the Punjab government warned that smoke from Diwali fireworks in Indian Punjab, crop-residue burning and easterly winds could worsen air pollution in Lahore and adjoining districts. The warning had forecast that smoke from the fireworks could reach Lahore and raise pollution levels in the city, Kasur and border areas on the morning of November 9. With pollution levels already in the hazardous category, residents faced dense smog that forced many to remain indoors during the early morning and late-night hours.
