World Bank rates progress on KP hydropower, renewable energy project 'moderately unsatisfactory'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Bank has rated the progress towards the development objectives of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Hydropower and Renewable Energy Development project as moderately unsatisfactory, with substantial risk to its 727 million dollar loan. Various segments of the loan were approved in 2020 with a targeted completion date of November 30, 2027, while the bank rated overall implementation progress as moderately satisfactory and the overall risk rating as substantial. The rating reflected moderate risk in political and governance and technical design categories, but substantial risks from macroeconomic, institutional capacity, fiduciary and environmental and social factors.

The bank said it processed a restructuring of the project at the government's request to align its scope and financing with implementation priorities, and noted that implementation had gained momentum, with key milestones achieved under the Gabral-Kalam Hydropower Project, including the start of civil works, progress on land acquisition and advancement of electro-mechanical procurement. The original package, approved in September 2020, included 594 million dollars for hydropower and renewable energy, 59 million for environmental and social management and 55 million for institutional strengthening. Of the 200 million dollar market-based loan, only 3.95 million dollars has been disbursed so far, while about 34 million dollars of the 240 million dollar concessional loan has been disbursed.
