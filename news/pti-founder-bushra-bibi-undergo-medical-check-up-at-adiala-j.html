PTI founder, Bushra Bibi undergo medical check-up at Adiala Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI's founder and his wife Bushra Bibi underwent a medical check-up at Rawalpindi's Adiala Jail. According to jail sources, a medical team from PIMS Hospital visited the jail with paramedical staff. The team included Dr Arif Khan and Dr Muhammad Ali.

The two were examined separately during the visit. Sources said the couple also met during the check-up and enquired after each other's health. Necessary medical tests were carried out as part of the follow-up examination.

Jail officials did not release details of the test results.
