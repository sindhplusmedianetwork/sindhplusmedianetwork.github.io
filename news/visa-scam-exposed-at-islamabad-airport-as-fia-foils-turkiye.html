Visa scam exposed at Islamabad airport as FIA foils Turkiye travel on fake NGO visa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) Immigration at Islamabad International Airport foiled a passenger's attempt to travel to Turkiye on a suspected fraudulent NGO/COP31 visa obtained through an agent for Rs1.15 million. Officials also uncovered an alleged plan to bribe immigration officials with Rs150,000 to secure travel clearance. The passenger was scheduled to travel to Turkiye on flight QR-615 but was offloaded after an immigration officer detected discrepancies in his travel documents and stated purpose of visit during routine immigration clearance.

The officer, using professional profiling techniques, identified inconsistencies in the passenger's documents and travel plans, and the passenger failed to provide a satisfactory explanation for his intended visit, after which he was referred for secondary profiling. During further examination, a second-line immigration officer scrutinised the passenger's travel documents and mobile phones, revealing that he had allegedly obtained an NGO/COP31 visa through an agent after paying Rs1.15 million. The agent had reportedly assured the passenger that he could undertake employment in Turkiye using the visa, despite such employment being contrary to the visa conditions, and further investigation revealed that the passenger was not a member of the HOPE NGO.
