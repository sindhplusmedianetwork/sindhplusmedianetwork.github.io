Shah Rukh Khan's 'King' does Rs665 crore pre-release business ahead of December launch

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Shah Rukh Khan's upcoming action film 'King', directed by Siddharth Anand and releasing on December 24, has reportedly done record pre-release business worth Rs665 crore. The deals include Rs175 crore for overseas rights bought by Phars Film, Rs250 crore for India distribution through Pen Marudhar, Rs150 crore for Netflix digital rights, Rs25 crore for satellite rights and Rs65 crore for music. Media reports say the film's budget has risen to about Rs400 crore, with marketing costs of around Rs50 crore.

Given these figures, the film would need to earn at least Rs800 crore at the global box office for producers and distributors to meet their targets. The trade is watching the release closely as one of the biggest Bollywood events of the year.
