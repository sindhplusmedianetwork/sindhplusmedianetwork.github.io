PSX sheds nearly 1,500 points as investors book profits

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange ended Friday's session lower as investors booked profits ahead of the long weekend, with the benchmark KSE-100 index falling 1,493.18 points, or 0.85 per cent, to close at 173,886.69. The decline trimmed the market's gains for the week to 4.5 per cent. Trading remained robust, with 1.48 billion shares worth Rs22.57 billion changing hands during the session.

Of the companies traded, 190 closed higher, 277 declined and 33 remained unchanged. The selling came as investors locked in gains after a strong run for the market earlier in the week.
