Alberta Voters Reject Separation From Canada, 76% Back Unity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Voters in Alberta, Canada's oil-rich province, have decisively rejected separation from the country in a first-of-its-kind referendum. According to the results, 76 percent of voters opposed the provincial government starting further legal action toward a binding final referendum on separation. The referendum, held on Monday, October 19, asked voters whether the province should begin the legal process for a decisive vote on leaving Canada.

The outcome represents a significant setback for the separation movement in the western province. Prime Minister Mark Carney welcomed the results, calling them a strong endorsement of national unity.
