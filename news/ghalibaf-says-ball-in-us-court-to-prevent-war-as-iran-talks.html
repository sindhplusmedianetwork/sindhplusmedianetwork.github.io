Ghalibaf Says Ball in US Court to 'Prevent War' as Iran Talks Show Progress

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's parliament speaker and chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has said the ball is in Washington's court to 'prevent war'. In a televised speech on Tuesday, Ghalibaf said Iran stood ready for battle with its finger on the trigger, but had also given the American side a powerful and rational response. His remarks came as indirect talks between the United States and Iran continued in Doha.

US Vice President JD Vance said Washington had made progress in the negotiations aimed at averting a military flare-up. President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social that a deal with Tehran 'could happen' and that Iran wanted a settlement as soon as possible. Tehran, however, insisted the United States must first lift its naval blockade before any agreement can be reached.

The diplomacy is unfolding alongside continued military pressure in the region.
