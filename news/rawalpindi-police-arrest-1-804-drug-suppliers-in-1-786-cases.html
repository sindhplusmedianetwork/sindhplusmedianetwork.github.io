Rawalpindi Police Arrest 1,804 Drug Suppliers in 1,786 Cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rawalpindi police have registered 1,786 cases and arrested 1,804 drug suppliers in operations against narcotics. A police spokesperson said drugs worth crores of rupees were recovered from the arrested suppliers. The recovered drugs include more than 1,288.613 kilograms of hashish, over six and a half kilograms of heroin, more than 17 kilograms of crystal meth, known as ice, and 10,744 litres of liquor.

The spokesperson said all possible measures are being taken to end the menace of drugs. Rawalpindi police said they remain engaged around the clock in providing citizens with a peaceful and safe environment.
