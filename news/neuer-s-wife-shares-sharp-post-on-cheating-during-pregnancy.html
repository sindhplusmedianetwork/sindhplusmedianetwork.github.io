Neuer's Wife Shares Sharp Post on Cheating During Pregnancy Amid Separation Reports

Annika Bissell, the wife of former German goalkeeper Manuel Neuer, has shared a sharply worded social media post about men who cheat on pregnant women. In a re-shared Instagram story, she wrote that cheating on a pregnant woman is the lowest form of evil. The post is being widely interpreted as an indirect jibe at her husband, though she did not name him in it.

According to German media, Annika and the 40-year-old Neuer separated shortly after the birth of their second child in August. Some readers have taken the post as a suggestion that Neuer may have begun another relationship while she was pregnant. No direct allegation has been confirmed, and the reports describe the message only as an apparent indirect reference to the separation.
