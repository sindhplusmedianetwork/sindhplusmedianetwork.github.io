Portuguese Foreign Minister Paulo Rangel Arrives in Pakistan for Two-Day Official Visit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Portugal's Minister of State and Foreign Affairs Paulo Rangel arrived in Pakistan on Sunday for an official visit. The visit runs from November 22 to 23 and comes at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar, the Foreign Office said. Rangel will hold delegation-level talks with Dar during the visit.

He will also hold meetings with the Pakistani leadership. The Portuguese foreign minister is accompanied by a business delegation. The delegation is in Pakistan for the inaugural Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum.
