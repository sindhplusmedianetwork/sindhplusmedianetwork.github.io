US Army sergeant pleads guilty to selling defence secrets to China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An active-duty US Army sergeant stationed in Washington state has pleaded guilty to conspiring to gather and transmit national defence information to buyers linked to China, the US Justice Department announced on Tuesday. Sergeant Jian Zhao admitted that he betrayed the country he swore an oath to defend by providing the Chinese government with sensitive US military information, according to Roman Rozhavsky, assistant director of the FBI's Counterintelligence and Espionage Division. The Justice Department said Zhao conspired to pass on the defence material to buyers connected to China.

The case was brought after an investigation by US counterintelligence authorities into the handling of the sensitive military information. Zhao now faces sentencing after entering his guilty plea in court. The case has drawn attention to the risks posed when serving members of the armed forces share classified defence material with foreign buyers.
