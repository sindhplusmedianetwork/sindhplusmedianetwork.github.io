Islamabad May Get Special Administrative Status Under Proposed New Model, Report Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Journalist Waqar Satti has reported on social media that preparations have been completed to give the federal capital special administrative status under a proposed new model. Under the plan, a mayor would serve as the administrative head of Islamabad. Between 25 and 30 elected members would manage the capital's municipal and administrative affairs through a modern system.

The proposal also considers expanding Islamabad's boundaries to include Haripur district in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, parts of Rawalpindi, and areas of Murree district's NA-51 constituency. At the same time, a proposal to bring Gwadar or other ports under Islamabad has been abandoned, according to the report. The plan remains a proposal reported by the journalist and has not been presented as confirmed government policy.
