British-Pakistani businessman Asif Khaliq opens fourth UK Arshad Chaiwala cafe in Leyton

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

British-Pakistani businessman Asif Khaliq has opened the fourth UK branch of Cafe Arshad Khan Chaiwala in Leyton, east London, joining outlets in Tooting, Sutton and New Malden. Khaliq, the brand's UK master franchise holder, said the expansion reflected growing demand for Pakistani tea and food among customers from different communities. The cafe serves kadak chai, freshly prepared parathas, Pakistani-style breakfasts and savoury snacks.

"Our aim is to make Cafe Arshad Khan Chaiwala a household name across Britain," Khaliq said, adding that the business planned to open more outlets in the UK. Khan welcomed the opening and thanked Khaliq for expanding the brand, saying it makes him very proud to see a Pakistani brand growing internationally and that he looks forward to many more branches opening across the UK. Khan rose to fame in 2016 after a photograph of him serving tea at a roadside stall in Islamabad went viral, and he went on to model and appear on television before launching his cafe business in Pakistan in 2020.

The brand's original UK franchisees, Nadir Durrani and Yawar Akbar Durrani, opened an outlet in Ilford in 2023, but the arrangement later broke down amid disputes over royalties, contractual obligations and intellectual property rights. Khaliq subsequently took over as the UK master franchise holder and opened branches in Sutton, New Malden and Leyton alongside the existing Tooting outlet.
