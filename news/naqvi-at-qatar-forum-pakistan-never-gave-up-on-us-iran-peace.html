Naqvi at Qatar Forum: Pakistan Never Gave Up on US-Iran Peace Efforts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi has said Pakistan never gave up on its efforts to bring peace between the United States and Iran. Speaking at the 8th Global Security Forum in Doha, Qatar, which drew officials from more than 100 nations, he recalled the difficult months since the war began with US-Israeli strikes on Iran on February 28. He said Pakistan chose the path of peace and made every possible effort to reduce tensions, even when the situation was extremely tense.

Naqvi highlighted the remarkable role of Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, who visited Iran in August as part of the peace push. He recalled the June 18 signing of the 14-point Islamabad memorandum of understanding between Iran and the US, which Pakistan signed as a mediator. He also described the Lake Lucerne summit in Switzerland, organised by Pakistan and Qatar, where he said Qatar's prime minister and Munir worked for more than 16 hours without a break to keep the talks alive.

Naqvi praised Qatar for choosing dialogue when others chose sides, saying the two mediating countries had one aim: to stop further conflict.
