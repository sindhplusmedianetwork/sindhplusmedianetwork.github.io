World Khatoon-e-Jannat Conference to be held in Rawalpindi today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 57th annual World Khatoon-e-Jannat Conference will be held at 2pm on Friday, November 13, at the Pearl Continental Hotel in Rawalpindi. The programme was finalised at a meeting of the Markazi Imam Hussain Council's executive committee, chaired by Dr Ghazanfar Mehdi. Scholars, religious leaders and academics from all schools of thought from across the country will attend and speak on the life of Bibi Fatima.

A resolution presented by Syeda Alvi, chairperson of the Azadari Council Pakistan, demanding that the death anniversary of Bibi Fatima be observed officially was approved at the meeting. A separate conference on the death anniversary of Bibi Fatima will also be held the same afternoon in Multan, at Jagir Ali Akbar in Abbas Pura, street number one, and will be chaired by Syeda Alvi. The organisers said the gatherings will focus on the life and teachings of Bibi Fatima.
