Cyclone Gezani devastates Madagascar, thousands left homeless

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cyclone Gezani has devastated Madagascar after making landfall on Tuesday, November 10, at Toamasina, with winds reaching 250 kilometres per hour. At least 38 people have been killed, six are missing and 374 have been injured. More than 12,000 people have been displaced from their homes, and over 18,000 homes have been destroyed.

More than 250,000 people have been affected by the cyclone across the island. President Randrianirina has declared a national disaster in response to the scale of the destruction. Relief efforts are under way to reach the displaced families and the areas worst hit by the storm.
