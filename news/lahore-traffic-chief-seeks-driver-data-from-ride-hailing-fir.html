Lahore traffic chief seeks driver data from ride-hailing firms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief Traffic Officer Lahore Syed Abdul Rahim Sherazi has asked all online ride-hailing companies to provide data on their drivers, saying no driver with a criminal record will be allowed to drive online cabs. He gave the direction during a meeting with representatives of online taxi companies, held as part of practical steps for smart traffic management and modern security features in Lahore. He said the data of drivers of all online cabs was needed for the safe travel of passengers, especially women, and that more than 100,000 rickshaws, motorcycles and cars in Lahore alone are linked with online services.

The companies were also directed to install dash cams and trackers in their vehicles and to complete installation of the Chief Minister Punjab QR panic button in all ride-hailing vehicles as soon as possible. It was also decided in the meeting that action would be taken against online cab services that do not adopt the safety and security features. The CTO said Lahore traffic police had checked the criminal records of 10,000 commercial vehicle drivers and found 2,050 drivers with criminal records, against whom 3,700 cases were registered.

He said citizens' lives and property could not be left in the hands of drivers with criminal records, and that modern camera systems would help protect citizens, prevent crime and assist in accident investigations. He added that modern tracking systems in online cabs would make monitoring of passengers and drivers more effective and that the use of technology would provide citizens with safe, reliable and quality travel facilities.
