US air system failure was not a cyber attack, says Director of National Intelligence

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US authorities have begun investigating the technical fault that disrupted flights, but have confirmed there is no evidence that it was caused by a cyber attack. The Director of National Intelligence said the computer systems of various agencies are being upgraded, and that technical faults are not being labelled as cyber attacks. The US transport secretary also said Friday's flight disruption was not a cyber attack.

The exact cause of the air traffic control fault is still unknown, and the FBI has joined the investigation. Officials are examining the systems involved to find what went wrong and to prevent a repeat of the disruption.
