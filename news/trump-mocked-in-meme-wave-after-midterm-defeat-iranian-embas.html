Trump Mocked in Meme Wave After Midterm Defeat; Iranian Embassy's AI Video Goes Viral

A wave of memes mocking US President Donald Trump swept social media after the Republican Party's defeat in the midterm elections. Users flooded platforms with sarcastic posts and AI-generated videos targeting the president and his policies. Many described the Democrats' victory as a 'blue tsunami' rather than a 'blue wave'.

One viral meme joked that America had decided to deport Trump's majority itself. Iran's embassy in India joined the mockery, sharing an AI-made video showing a Trump lookalike being thrown into a bin labelled 'hazardous waste'. The post was captioned 'US midterm elections'.

The video was widely read as a taunt over Trump's election defeat.
