Corruption Allegations Refuse to Die Down as PML-N Pushes Clean-Punjab Narrative

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz has intensified its efforts to portray Punjab as a model of transparency and accountability, seeking to counter allegations of corruption levelled against the Maryam Nawaz-led government by a coalition partner. An adjournment motion was submitted in the Punjab Assembly on Saturday by opposition member Waqas Mahmood, demanding full details of the travel and accommodation expenses from Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's recent visit to London. During a television talk show a few days ago, PML-Q leader Chaudhry Salik Hussain criticised the Punjab government, claimed he regularly heard complaints of corruption, alleged that development funds were disproportionately channelled toward Lahore, and questioned lavish government spending like the use of official aircraft.

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bukhari said on Saturday that Punjab was the only province taking practical action against corruption rather than merely making statements. In a statement, Bukhari said the Anti-Corruption Establishment Punjab was functioning as a transparent institution, while strict departmental action was being taken against corrupt officials across provincial departments. She claimed there was neither favouritism nor bribery in Punjab, and said the chief minister herself was holding corrupt elements accountable while demonstrating zero tolerance towards corruption.

Meanwhile, Sindh Governor Nehal Hashmi, addressing journalists at the Lahore Press Club's Meet the Press programme, said those responsible for delays in Karachi's Bus Rapid Transit projects should learn from Punjab, and maintained that no government was more transparent than the Punjab government.
