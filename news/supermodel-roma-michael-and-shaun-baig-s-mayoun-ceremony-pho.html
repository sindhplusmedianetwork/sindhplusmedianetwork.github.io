The wedding ceremonies of Pakistani supermodel and actress Roma Michael and actor Shaun Baig have begun, with both celebrating their mayoun ceremony among close friends and family, whose photos and videos are gaining attention on social media. At the ceremony Roma Michael and Shaun Baig were seen in traditional yellow and green outfits, and their pleasant moods and happy moments with each other made the ceremony more memorable, while glimpses of the mayoun decoration and colourful atmosphere are also being liked by fans. Roma Michael started her showbiz career from the reality show Miss Veet Pakistan and gained attention at the very beginning due to her height, attractive features and confident style, and later also participated in the Miss Universe contest.

After making her place on fashion ramps and covers of famous magazines, she is now also active in the field of acting and is appearing in dramas. Roma Michael has recently married actor Shaun Baig, and this is Shaun Baig's second marriage. After the nikah, other wedding-related programmes of both have now formally begun.
