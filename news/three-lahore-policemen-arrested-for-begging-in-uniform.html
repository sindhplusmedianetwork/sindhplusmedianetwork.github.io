Three Lahore policemen arrested for begging in uniform

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three policemen in Lahore have been arrested and a case has been registered against them for begging while in uniform. A police spokesperson said the officers stopped a citizen at Barkat Market and demanded money for food while on duty. The arrested policemen were identified as Fayyaz, Farhan and Ahmed Mustafa, and a case was registered against them under the Vagrancy Act.

DIG Operations Faisal Kamran said there would be no compromise on the dignity of the police force. He said officers involved in corruption would face the law.
