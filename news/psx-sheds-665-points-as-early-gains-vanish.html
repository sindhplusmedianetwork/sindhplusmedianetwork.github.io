PSX sheds 665 points as early gains vanish

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Volatility returned to the Pakistan Stock Exchange on Tuesday as the benchmark KSE-100 index failed to sustain early gains and closed down 665 points at 177,517.74. The market had climbed as high as 179,450.96 in early trading before selling pressure pushed it into negative territory. Investors resorted to profit-taking after the early rise, wiping out the day's gains.

The decline came despite Pakistan recording a current account surplus of 39 million dollars in October 2026, according to the State Bank of Pakistan. That compared with a deficit of 371 million dollars in September. The contrast between the strong external account figures and the market's fall underlined the cautious mood among investors.
