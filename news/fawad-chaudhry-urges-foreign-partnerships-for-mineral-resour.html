Fawad Chaudhry Urges Foreign Partnerships for Mineral Resources, Backs PTI Unity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former federal minister Fawad Chaudhry has said that Pakistan must work with companies from China, the United States, Canada, Australia and South Korea to extract and market the mineral resources of Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He warned that without such partnerships, the claims about these mineral reserves would remain mere stories. Commenting on PTI's internal situation, Chaudhry said Salman Akram Raja rightly took the stance that this is not the time for differences within the party.

He added that branding half of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's parliamentary party as traitors is beyond comprehension.
