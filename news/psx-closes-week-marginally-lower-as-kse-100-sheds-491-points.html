PSX Closes Week Marginally Lower as KSE-100 Sheds 491 Points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) closed the outgoing week marginally lower. The benchmark KSE-100 index shed 491 points, or 0.28%, to settle at 177,383 as cautious investor sentiment and profit-taking offset support from easing international oil prices. During the week, the index gained 309 points on the first trading day, then declined 665 points the next day, shed a further 1,223 points midweek, gained 478 points on Thursday and recovered 610 points on the final trading day to close above 177,000.

Pakistan recorded a current account surplus of $39 million in October 2026, compared with a deficit of $307 million in October 2025. Net foreign direct investment inflows were $216 million in October 2026. Brent crude settled at $91.2 per barrel, while local petrol prices fell to Rs323 per litre and high-speed diesel to Rs355 per litre.
