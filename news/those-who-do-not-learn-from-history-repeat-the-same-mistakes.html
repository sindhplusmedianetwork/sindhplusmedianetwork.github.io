Those who do not learn from history repeat the same mistakes, Araghchi tells Rubio

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has responded to US Secretary of State Marco Rubio's statement by citing the history of ancient Rome. He said those who ignore the law by force of arms should learn from history. Araghchi wrote on X that the phrase about not citing the law to those who hold swords is attributed to the Roman general Pompey.

Pompey was defeated despite having a larger army on his last military campaign. His remarks came after Rubio criticised the International Criminal Court in Washington last Friday. Rubio said the court uses international law for political purposes and has no authority to impose its laws on America.

Araghchi's reply drew on the ancient example to answer Rubio's criticism of the court.
