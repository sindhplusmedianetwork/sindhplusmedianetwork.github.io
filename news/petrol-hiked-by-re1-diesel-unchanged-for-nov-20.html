Petrol Hiked by Re1, Diesel Unchanged for Nov 20

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government hiked the petrol price on Thursday by Re1 for Nov 20. According to a press release from the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority, the new petrol price is now Rs323 per litre. The price of high-speed diesel is unchanged at Rs355 per litre.

On November 2, the government announced that oil refineries would bear a burden of Rs15 billion per month, which would allow petrol to be sold Rs15 cheaper than it was in the global market amid the US-Israeli war on Iran. On July 17, the government announced a new pricing mechanism under which petroleum product prices would be reviewed and notified daily, replacing the weekly pricing mechanism, as renewed tensions between the United States and Iran continued to drive volatility in global oil markets. On September 13, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif announced a special relief scheme offering Rs100 per litre off petrol for motorcycles, three-wheeler rickshaws and cars with engines up to 800cc, in an attempt to shield lower-income consumers from the impact of rising petroleum prices, with an estimated 11.8 million beneficiaries to be covered under the proposal.
