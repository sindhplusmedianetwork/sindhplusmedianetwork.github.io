Nowhere are 75-year-old sisters kept in jail; they must be released immediately, says Barrister Gohar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan has said that the party founder's sisters are being kept in jail unjustly and must be released immediately. Speaking to the media in Islamabad, he said that nowhere in the world are 75-year-old sisters kept imprisoned. Barrister Gohar said the party considers every day the founder's sisters spend in jail to be a sin.

He described their detention as completely wrong and unjust, and stressed that their immediate release was necessary. The PTI chairman made the remarks while talking to journalists in the federal capital.
