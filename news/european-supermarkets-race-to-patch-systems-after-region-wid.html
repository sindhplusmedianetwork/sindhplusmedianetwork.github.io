European Supermarkets Race to Patch Systems After Region-Wide Fridge Hack

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

European supermarkets were racing on November 3-4, 2026, to patch vulnerabilities after a second wave of cyberattacks that forced up the temperatures of fridges and freezers. Stores in eight European countries had to discard vast quantities of spoiled food. The attacks mirrored one that hit dozens of supermarkets in France three weeks earlier.

Software provider Danfoss was again among those targeted. Retailers scrambled to upgrade systems and restore safe refrigeration. The EU cybersecurity agency ENISA said the scale of the attacks underlined the need for European cooperation and rapid information-sharing.
