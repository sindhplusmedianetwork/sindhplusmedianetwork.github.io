Noor Zaman Stuns Defending Champion at Silicon Valley Squash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Noor Zaman pulled off one of the biggest upsets of the squash season in the United States. The Pakistani player defeated defending champion and world No. 4 Karim Gawad of Egypt at the PSA Gold Silicon Valley tournament, winning the 52-minute match 3-1.

Tournament observers are calling it the biggest shock of the event so far. In another match at the same tournament, Pakistan's Ashab Irfan lost 3-0 to Egypt's Mohamed ElShorbagy. The tournament carries total prize money of 143,500 dollars.

Zaman's victory over a top-four player marks a career-defining moment for the young Pakistani.
