Karachi board announces Class 9 science group results with 69.56 percent pass rate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Board of Secondary Education Karachi has announced the results of the annual examinations 2026 for Class 9 science group (regular). Board Chairman Professor Musharraf Ali Rajput said transparency in the results was among the board's top priorities, and congratulated the students who passed all papers. Controller of Examinations Ahmed Khan Chhutto said 180,054 students had registered for the science group, of whom 177,923 appeared in the examinations.

The number of absent students was 2,131. A total of 123,757 students passed all papers, while 21,999 passed in six papers, 11,006 in five papers, 7,431 in four papers, 4,409 in three papers, 3,101 in two papers and 2,439 in one paper. The number of students who failed was 53,849, and the overall pass rate stood at 69.56 percent.
