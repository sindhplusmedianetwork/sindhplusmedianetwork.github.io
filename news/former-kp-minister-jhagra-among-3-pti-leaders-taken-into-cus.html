Former KP minister Jhagra among 3 PTI leaders taken into custody despite bail from PHC

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ministers Taimoor Saleem Jhagra and Kamran Bangash were among three PTI leaders taken into preventative custody in Peshawar shortly after the high court granted them interim bail. The third leader was PTI Peshawar president Irfan Saleem. Earlier in the day, an anti-terrorism court had denied them bail in the case concerning vandalism and arson at the Radio Pakistan building during the May 9, 2023 riots, after which they evaded arrest and reached the Peshawar High Court.

Chief Justice S. M. Attique Shah, heading a single-member bench, granted them bail till November 23 on the condition of furnishing two sureties of one million rupees each or depositing an equivalent cash amount.

The judge directed them to join the investigation and appear before the investigating officer whenever required. Following the order, an hours-long standoff unfolded between a large police force around the high court and the three petitioners, who had taken refuge in the court's barroom with members of the Insaf Lawyers Forum. Led by Senior Superintendent of Police Operations Farhan Khan, police sought to take them into custody, saying the district administration had issued detention orders under the Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance.

Finally, at around 6pm, the three leaders left the barroom and were taken into custody by police at the high court's entrance.
