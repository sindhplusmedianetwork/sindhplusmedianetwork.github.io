PM pitches mineral wealth to fuel economic boom

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Friday called for Pakistan's mineral resources to be developed alongside environmental protection. Addressing the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 in Islamabad, he said mining projects must follow international standards and use modern technology to safeguard water resources and workers' health and safety. He urged foreign investors to mine, process and manufacture minerals in Pakistan rather than export them in raw form.

The prime minister highlighted the Reko Diq project, describing it as one of the world's largest untapped copper and gold deposits, with production expected to begin in 2028. He cited a Goldman Sachs assessment that Pakistan could become the world's sixth-largest economy by 2075, and said the target could be achieved earlier than 2050. The forum brought together investors and officials to discuss the development of the country's mineral sector.
