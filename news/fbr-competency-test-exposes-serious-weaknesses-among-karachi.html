FBR competency test exposes serious weaknesses among Karachi tax officers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A competency assessment of Federal Board of Revenue Inland Revenue officers posted in Karachi found serious professional weaknesses. No sales tax officer reached the 60 percent pass mark, and only four income tax officers did so. Average scores were 26.4 percent in sales tax and 33.3 percent in income tax among the 150 sales tax and 143 income tax officers tested.

Only 15 sales tax and 14 income tax officers could independently issue legally valid tax orders. The training review report recommends training officers in decision-making and assigning duties on the basis of tested ability rather than grade.
