Peshawar Traders Accuse Police of Shielding Suspects in Rs10 Million Robbery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two spare parts dealers from the Naak Market in Peshawar's Shoba Bazaar have accused a local deputy superintendent of police (DSP) and some police personnel of allegedly protecting suspects involved in a daylight robbery worth over 10 million rupees. Addressing a news conference at the Peshawar Press Club on Monday, Qasim Subhan and his brother Fazal Subhan appealed to the capital city police officer (CCPO) to take notice of the case and ensure the arrest of the suspects and recovery of their stolen property. Fazal Subhan claimed that 13 armed men had stormed their shop at around 2pm on Friday, held him and his two sons at gunpoint, and loaded spare parts worth over 10 million rupees into a wagon in broad daylight.

He alleged that the armed men also took away 600,000 rupees in cash and two mobile phones from his son Hamza. He identified Israr Ahmed and Bakht Mohammad as the alleged leaders of the group, and claimed he had called Police Emergency 15 and contacted the Khan Raziq Shaheed police station, but the police arrived after a considerable delay, inspecting the shop at around 8pm, several hours after the incident. Fazal Subhan claimed that they had provided the police with CCTV footage and the names of the alleged suspects, but the investigation had not resulted in their arrest, and that the name of one suspect, Habib, had been omitted from the FIR despite being identified by them.

He accused DSP Shahenshah Khan of attempting to undermine the investigation and provide protection to the suspects, while the allegations could not be independently verified. Qasim Subhan alleged that they were being summoned to the police station and made to sit with people who had no connection with the case, and that they had been asked to put their thumb impressions on documents and were filmed in connection with the release of certain individuals who had nothing to do with the robbery. The complainants urged the CCPO to ensure that all nominated suspects were included in the case in accordance with the evidence, arrested and brought to justice.
