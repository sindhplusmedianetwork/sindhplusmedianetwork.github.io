PAF to get control of Punjab government's jet

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has approved handing over the operational control and maintenance of its Gulfstream G-500 aircraft to the Pakistan Air Force. The decision was taken after the provincial government's VIP Flight Wing faced technical difficulties in operating the sophisticated jet. According to official documents, the provincial cabinet formally approved the proposal.

The approval also covers the aircraft's deregistration from the Civil Aviation Authority and the allocation of a Pakistan Air Force tail number. A memorandum of understanding will also be signed between the provincial government and the air force. The documents state that the VIP Flight Wing has limited technical capacity to maintain and operate the modern aircraft on its own.
