Maryam Nawaz welcomes participants of Pakistan's first paragliding championship in Chakwal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif on Friday welcomed paragliders at Dharabi Resort for Pakistan's first-ever paragliding championship. She asked the pilots whether they had seen anything more beautiful than Dharabi Lake, adding that the skies of Pakistan belonged to the pilots, that Chakwal was an ancient land, and that Pakistan was sending a message to the world that its skies represented progress, peace and friendship. The pilots were testing their skills in precision landing and cross-country racing, and the chief minister said she had come to the resort for the first time and had been unaware of the bewitching natural beauty of Chakwal, with its centuries-old mountains, the Khewra Salt Mine and Katas Raj.

She said Pakistan had been portrayed wrongly before the world, but the world was now witnessing its beauty and the talent of its youth, and discussions about Pakistan would no longer be based on hearsay and assumptions. She pointed out that the tourism sector had remained neglected despite the country's archaeological sites, heritage and wildlife corridors, and said she had brought all departments together and created the Tourism Authority, with between 1,100 and 1,200 tourist sites, of which 170 had been identified and were being developed on a priority basis and would be ready by December. Earlier, at the University of Chakwal, she addressed students, noting that 500 students from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa had come to Punjab and were being provided scholarships, and that a share of laptops had also been allocated for them.

She said talent had no province or border and that every child was her child, adding that her aim was that no son or daughter of Punjab should leave education incomplete only because of a lack of resources, and that a cardiac catheterisation laboratory had been established in Chakwal, the Apna Ghar Apni Chhat scheme would be expanded with a target of constructing 250,000 houses in two years, and separate compartments and CCTV cameras had been installed in Green Buses for women. In Talagang, she inaugurated the Green Bus Service and made Talagang district functional, promising that a deputy commissioner and district police officer would be appointed soon.
