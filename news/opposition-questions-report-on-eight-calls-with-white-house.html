Opposition questions report on eight calls with White House over Trump's praise for Modi

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Opposition parties in India have raised questions over a media report about eight phone calls held this year between India and senior officials at the White House. The report has prompted criticism of the government and questions about whether payments were made in connection with praise for Prime Minister Narendra Modi by United States President Donald Trump. The opposition has demanded answers from the government about the nature of the calls and the reported payments.

The controversy centres on whether the praise for Modi was linked in any way to money changing hands. The government has faced calls to clarify what was discussed during the eight calls and whether any payment was involved. The row has added to political tensions over India's dealings with the Trump administration.
