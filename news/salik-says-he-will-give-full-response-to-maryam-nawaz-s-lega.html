Salik Says He Will Give Full Response to Maryam Nawaz's Legal Notices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain has responded to the defamation notices sent by Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz, saying he saw the legal notice on social media and will give it a full response. Speaking to journalists at Parliament House, Salik said Maryam Nawaz herself had admitted to corruption in Punjab in her own statement two days earlier. The notices accuse Salik of claiming, in a TV interview, that corruption files in Punjab had grown into cupboards and that Punjab's entire budget was being spent on Lahore.

They allege the claims were made without evidence and were broadcast by the channel without verification. The exchange follows a string of public accusations between the federal minister and the Punjab chief minister.
