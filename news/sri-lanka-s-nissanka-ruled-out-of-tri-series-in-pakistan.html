Sri Lanka's Nissanka Ruled Out of Tri-Series in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sri Lanka opener Pathum Nissanka has been ruled out of the upcoming ODI tri-series against Pakistan and England after failing to recover from a hamstring injury, dealing a significant blow to the visitors. The right-hander sustained the injury to his left thigh during Sri Lanka's recent tour of England, which had already forced him to miss the three-match T20I series against Pakistan earlier this month. Sri Lanka Cricket confirmed on Thursday that his rehabilitation had not progressed enough for medical clearance to resume competitive action.

The selectors have named Lahiru Udara as his replacement in the 16-member squad for the tournament. Udara is yet to make his ODI debut but has played five Tests and seven T20 Internationals, including the recently concluded T20I series against Pakistan, and owns a solid List A record of 3,017 runs in 102 matches. The tri-series begins on October 18 with Sri Lanka facing hosts Pakistan in the opening match at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium.

Kusal Mendis will captain the side and also take the wicketkeeping duties in the absence of one of their key opening batters.
