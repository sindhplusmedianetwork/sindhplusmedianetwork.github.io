Those Objecting to Makkah Defence Pact Are the Ones Criticising It: DG ISPR

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Director General ISPR Lieutenant General Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry has said that those who have an issue with the Makkah defence agreement are the ones levelling criticism against it. In an interview with Independent Arabia, reported by a private TV channel, he said the real worth of anything is judged by its results. He described the Makkah joint defence agreement as the combined thinking of three countries and a manifestation of their alliance, noting the historic ties between them.

The DG ISPR said the agreement had moved forward in a very short time. He stressed that Saudi Arabia's sovereignty and the protection of the Harmain Sharifain are non-negotiable, adding that Makkah and Madinah hold the utmost importance for the Muslim Ummah. He said Pakistan is ready to take all necessary measures in support of Saudi Arabia.
