Hundreds of thousands take to streets in France as nationwide protests heat up

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hundreds of thousands of citizens gathered across France in widespread demonstrations against economic conditions and proposed government spending cuts. Most rallies were peaceful. Student unions, trade groups and public service workers joined the protests to demand action on inflation, stagnant wages, high living expenses, rising fuel prices and cutbacks to social budgets.

Security forces were visibly deployed in Paris, Marseille and other major cities. They managed traffic and safeguarded public buildings while marches proceeded along authorized routes. Government officials acknowledged the scale of the rallies and urged union and student leaders to enter formal dialogue.
