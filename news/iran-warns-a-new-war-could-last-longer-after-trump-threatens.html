Iran Warns a New War Could Last Longer After Trump Threatens More Strikes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has warned that any renewed war with the United States could last longer than the previous round of fighting, after President Donald Trump said he still had to deal with more targets in the country. Iranian army spokesman Mohammad Akraminia said a potential future war could outlast the previous conflict, and the American president would not be in a position to dictate the timing or the conditions of its conclusion. The remarks followed Trump's comments to reporters at his Florida residence that one or two Iranian sites remained, and that dealing with them would take '10 minutes', including a suspected underground nuclear complex he called Pickaxe Mountain.

The most intense phase of the war began on Feb 28 with joint US-Israeli strikes on Iran, to which Tehran responded with missile and drone attacks across the region. Revolutionary Guards spokesman Hossein Mohebbi echoed an earlier warning from Iran's new supreme leader that in a conflict with America there would be 'no geographical limitations'. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Iran hoped the other side would choose diplomacy and that Tehran remained prepared for both defence and diplomacy.

Tehran has signalled that a deal is possible only if its conditions are met, including unfreezing Iranian assets, ending the US naval blockade of Iranian ports and lifting sanctions.
