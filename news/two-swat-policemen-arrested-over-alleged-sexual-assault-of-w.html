Two Swat Policemen Arrested Over Alleged Sexual Assault of Woman Visiting From Peshawar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two police officers in Swat have been arrested after a married couple visiting from Peshawar alleged the officers stopped them at Engro Dherai Chowk in Mingora on the pretext of checking, took them to a hotel on Bypass Road, and sexually assaulted the woman. The couple had travelled from Peshawar to Swat for tourism and were returning to Mingora after sightseeing in the tourist area of Bahrain when police officers stopped them. The suspects allegedly incapacitated the husband in the hotel room and forcibly subjected the woman to sexual assault, and the couple were also allegedly threatened after the incident.

A case was registered at Rahimabad Police Station on the woman's complaint, and police arrested both accused officers and launched an investigation. Members of the public expressed outrage over the incident and demanded the harshest punishment under the law. Provincial authorities and the Inspector General of Police took notice of the incident and directed immediate and transparent legal action against the officers involved.
