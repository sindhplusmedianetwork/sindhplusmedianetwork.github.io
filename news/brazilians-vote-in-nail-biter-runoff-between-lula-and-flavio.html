Brazilians vote in nail-biter runoff between Lula and Flavio Bolsonaro

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brazilians voted on Sunday in a presidential runoff to choose between leftist leader Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and his right-wing challenger Flavio Bolsonaro. Final polls showed the two candidates neck-and-neck in what is the final electoral contest for Lula, who turns 81 on Tuesday and is seeking a fourth non-consecutive term. Lula finished second in the first round of voting to the son of former president Jair Bolsonaro, who is serving a 27-year sentence for attempting a coup after his 2022 election loss.

Lula has presented himself as a bulwark against US interference and democratic backsliding and has promised to expand social protections, while Flavio Bolsonaro has vowed to crack down on crime and cut government spending. The runoff will decide whether Brazil remains one of Latin America's few leftist nations or joins a regional swing to the right. Heavy rain fell across Brazil on Sunday, which may affect turnout after a record abstention rate in the first round earlier this month, and polling stations were due to close at 5pm local time, with results expected within hours.
