Punjab Moves to Rewrite Building Laws, Seeks Proposals From Development Authorities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab has begun preparing a major change to the province's construction laws and regulations, with development authorities across the province, including Lahore, asked to send suggestions on building rules, map approvals, commercialisation and other regulations. The Housing and Urban Development Department has started a consultation process to bring uniformity to construction laws, circulating the draft Punjab Building Regulations 2025 and seeking a comparative review from the relevant institutions. Letters were sent to the development authorities on October 14 and 20, and a second reminder had to be issued on October 30 after some institutions failed to respond within the deadline.

The draft was also received by the Lahore Development Authority's director general and chief town planner's office on November 2. The proposed changes aim to create a single regulatory framework for map approvals, commercialisation and construction standards across different authorities. Experts say that while uniformity is important, master plans, local needs, road capacity, parking, drainage, water supply and building safety must also be built into the new system.

No final decision on approval or implementation of the proposed regulations has been made yet.
