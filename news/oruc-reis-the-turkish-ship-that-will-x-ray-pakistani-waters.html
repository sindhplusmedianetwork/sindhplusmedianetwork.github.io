Oruc Reis: The Turkish Ship That Will 'X-ray' Pakistani Waters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkey's seismic research vessel Oruc Reis has arrived in Pakistan. The vessel will survey for possible oil and gas reserves in Pakistan's maritime limits. The survey will cover areas including the Indus and Makran basins.

Turkish Petroleum will collect 3D seismic data over 670 square kilometres in the Indus-C block. The data collection is expected to take about 52 days. Drilling is targeted for next year based on the data collected.
