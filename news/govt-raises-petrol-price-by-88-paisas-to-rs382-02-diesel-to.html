Govt Raises Petrol Price by 88 Paisas to Rs382.02, Diesel to Rs389.80 for Oct 21

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Tuesday raised petroleum prices, announcing a hike of 88 paisas per litre in petrol and 53 paisas in high-speed diesel effective October 21. According to a notification issued by the Petroleum Division, petrol will now retail at Rs382.02 per litre while HSD will cost Rs389.80. The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority revised the ex-depot prices in line with the federal government's daily pricing mechanism.

The Petroleum Division said the increase was necessitated by global developments, including movements in Platts rates, premiums and incidentals. The latest revision comes a day after the government reduced petrol by Rs5.16 to Rs381.14 and diesel by Rs7.69 to Rs389.27 for October 20. Under the daily pricing framework, fuel rates are reviewed from Monday to Friday based on international oil markets and announced by OGRA.

Oil prices slipped from a five-month high on Tuesday as traders weighed Houthi attacks on Saudi infrastructure and vessel attacks in the Gulf.
