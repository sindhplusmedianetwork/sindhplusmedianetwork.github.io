Government Cuts Petrol by Rs5.16, Diesel by Rs7.69 Per Litre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Monday reduced the prices of petroleum products for October 20, cutting petrol by Rs5.16 and high-speed diesel by Rs7.69 per litre. Under the daily price mechanism, petrol now stands at Rs381.14 per litre and HSD at Rs389.27 per litre. In the previous revision covering October 17 to 19, the government had cut petrol by Rs3.93 while keeping diesel prices unchanged.

Officials said the reduction reflects movements in the international oil market. The government also noted that its Rs100-per-litre relief scheme for motorcycles, rickshaws and cars up to 800cc, announced on September 13, covers around 11.8 million beneficiaries.
