India's Supreme Court rejects Anita Advani's plea to be declared Rajesh Khanna's widow

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's Supreme Court has refused to hear former actress Anita Advani's plea seeking legal recognition of her relationship with late superstar Rajesh Khanna as a marriage and to be declared his widow. A bench of Justices M.M. Sundresh and Prasanna B.

Varale said the plea appeared to be litigation pursued for luxury and found no basis to entertain it. The plea followed the Bombay High Court's April 1 ruling, which upheld the dismissal of her 2015 claim. The High Court had held that her claim was legally unacceptable because Khanna's marriage to Dimple Kapadia had subsisted.

The Supreme Court's decision brings an end to the long-running legal battle over her status.
