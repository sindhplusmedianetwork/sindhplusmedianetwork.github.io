flydubai captain blocked axe blows from co-pilot to save Tel Aviv flight

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Captain Smit Machchhar of flydubai flight FZ1073 has described how he fought off his co-pilot to stop the aircraft from being crashed into Ben Gurion Airport in Tel Aviv last month. Speaking to The National from a hospital bed in Abu Dhabi with his head and left arm bandaged, Machchhar said the first blow made him think something had gone horribly wrong with the aircraft structure before he realised he was under attack. He said he freed himself from his seat belts, blocked the crash-axe blows to his head with his arms, and screamed for his colleagues while reaching for the cockpit door knob.

The plane was in Saudi airspace when the co-pilot launched the attack and plunged 16,000 feet in 30 seconds before the attacker was overwhelmed. Two other pilots who happened to be on board then landed the aircraft safely in Saudi Arabia. The suspect, an Omani national, was later transferred to the UAE, where prosecutors say the plan was inspired by the September 11, 2001 attacks.

Machchhar said the scariest moment was feeling the aircraft's nose going steeply down. "I was not very sure that I'll make it out alive from this. Did I give up?

No," he said.
