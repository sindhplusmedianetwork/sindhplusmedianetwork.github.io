Rickelton, Mulder Tons Swing Series-Deciding Test South Africa's Way at Newlands

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former schoolmates Ryan Rickelton and Wiaan Mulder hit centuries and swung the series-deciding third Test in South Africa's favour at Newlands on Friday. Rickelton made 101 and Mulder 115 before South Africa were bowled out for 387 in the last over of the fourth day, setting Australia 288 runs to win on the final day. The series between the two leading Test nations is tied at 1-1.

South Africa started the day on 140 for four — with a 100-run first-innings deficit effectively making it 40 for four — before the pair shared a sixth-wicket stand of 154. Both attended the elite St Stithians College north of Johannesburg, and Rickelton said the longer they batted, the more frustrated the Australians became. Just when South Africa seemed to be forging an impregnable position, Rickelton was run out and four wickets fell for 16 runs, before Kagiso Rabada helped Mulder complete his century in a last-wicket stand of 36.

Australian bowling coach James Franklin said the pitch had not deteriorated markedly and the stage was set for a great final day of the series.
