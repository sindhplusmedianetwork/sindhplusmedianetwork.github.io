Frequent Petroleum Price Revisions Challenged in Lahore High Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The frequent changes in petroleum product prices have been challenged in the Lahore High Court. The Judicial Activism Panel filed a miscellaneous petition against the repeated increases and decreases in fuel prices. The petitioner stated that prices were revised 15 times between October 9 and October 29 — 11 increases and 3 decreases.

The petition contends that the repeated hikes have worsened inflation and placed a heavy burden on citizens. It further asks the court to summon the complete record of how petroleum prices are determined. The court will now take up the petition for hearing.
