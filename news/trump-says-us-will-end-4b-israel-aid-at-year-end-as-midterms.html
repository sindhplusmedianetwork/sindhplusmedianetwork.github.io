Trump Says US Will End $4B Israel Aid at Year-End as Midterms Begin

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said Washington will cut off its aid to Israel at the end of this year, telling Fox News on Election Day that the country 'doesn't need it'. The US currently provides about $4 billion in aid, including $3.8 billion a year in military assistance under a ten-year memorandum of understanding signed in 2018 and running through 2028. Trump said the aid had become a political talking point for Democrats and that he had 'reached an agreement' with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on the change.

The announcement came as American voters were casting ballots in the midterm elections. No detailed timeline or official mechanism for ending the aid has been disclosed so far. The 2018 deal was negotiated under the previous Trump administration's predecessor and covers military support through 2028.

Observers say ending it early would mark a sharp shift in the long-standing US-Israel aid relationship.
