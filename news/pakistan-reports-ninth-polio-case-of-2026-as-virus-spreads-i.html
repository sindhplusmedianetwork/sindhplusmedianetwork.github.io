Pakistan reports ninth polio case of 2026 as virus spreads in K-P

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has reported its ninth wild poliovirus case of 2026 after a seven-month-old boy from Mastak Union Council in Bara Tehsil, Khyber District, tested positive for the virus. It is the third case from Khyber District this year and the seventh from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Genetic analysis has linked all three Khyber cases to poliovirus strains circulating in Afghanistan, which has reported 50 cases this year.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has directed federal health officials and the national polio team to visit all four provinces and prepare a coordinated eradication plan within a week. A nationwide door-to-door vaccination campaign is under way in 97 districts, targeting nearly 24 million children under the age of five. The campaign began on November 2 and will continue until November 8.
