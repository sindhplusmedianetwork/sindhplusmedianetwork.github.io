Emirati Diplomat Lana Nusseibeh Joins Race for UN Secretary-General

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Emirati diplomat Lana Nusseibeh has joined the race to become the next Secretary-General of the United Nations. Nusseibeh served for more than a decade as the United Arab Emirates' ambassador to the UN in New York. She has taken leave from her Emirati post to run the campaign for the world body's top office.

If elected, she would become the first woman to serve as UN Secretary-General. The vote to choose the successor to António Guterres, whose second term ends on December 31, 2026, is expected to take place on Friday, October 23. Costa Rica's former vice president Rebeca Grynspan is also seen as a prominent contender; reports say two of the five permanent Security Council members voted against her in earlier rounds.

The selection requires the support of at least nine of the 15 Security Council members, with no veto from a permanent member.
