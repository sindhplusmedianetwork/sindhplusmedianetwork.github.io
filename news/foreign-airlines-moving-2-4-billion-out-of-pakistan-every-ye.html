Foreign airlines moving $2.4 billion out of Pakistan every year, operators allege

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Aircraft Owners and Operators Association has accused foreign airlines of transferring $2-2.4 billion abroad annually through ticket sales processed via offshore payment gateways. In a letter to Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, the association said annual ticket sales of $3.2-3.6 billion were largely invisible to local tax authorities, with only about $1.226 billion in documented streams. It called for a five-year forensic tax audit of all airlines operating in Pakistan.

The association proposed SBP-regulated payment gateways and FBR-integrated collection to bring ticket sales into the tax net. It also proposed audits by three international firms to verify the airlines' financial flows.
