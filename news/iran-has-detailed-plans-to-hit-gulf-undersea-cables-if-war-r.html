Iran Has Detailed Plans to Hit Gulf Undersea Cables If War Resumes, Officials Tell Reuters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tehran has prepared detailed contingency plans to strike undersea communications cables in the Gulf if the United States and Israel launch another attack on Iran, two senior Iranian security officials have said. Speaking to Reuters, the officials described the cable-targeting plans as part of Iran's response options should war resume. On Wednesday, IRGC official Yadollah Javani publicly warned that Iran could target seven major internet cables running beneath the Strait of Hormuz if Iranian infrastructure came under attack.

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical maritime chokepoints, carrying a significant share of global internet and energy traffic. The remarks come amid continued tensions in the Gulf following the recent US-Israeli conflict with Iran. Analysts say any attack on undersea cables could disrupt global communications and trade.

Neither Washington nor Tel Aviv has issued an immediate response to the reported Iranian plans. The development adds a new dimension to the already volatile security situation in the region.
