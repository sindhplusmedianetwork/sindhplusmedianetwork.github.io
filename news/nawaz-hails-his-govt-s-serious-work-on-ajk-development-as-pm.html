Nawaz hails his govt's 'serious' work on AJK development as PML-N charts 100-day plan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PML-N supremo Nawaz Sharif praised his party government's development work in Azad Jammu and Kashmir. The top leadership, including Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, discussed development and reconstruction in the region with its president and premier. Nawaz highlighted a 100-day programme for the region.

He praised Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz for providing green buses, clinics on wheels and laptops. He asked the prime minister to give top priority to a planned Muzaffarabad-Mirpur motorway. AJK Prime Minister Faisal Mumtaz Rathore outlined 100-day plans including Suthra Kashmir, Apni Chhat Apna Ghar, green bus services and scholarships.

The premier later inaugurated the reconstructed Rathoa Haryam Bridge.
