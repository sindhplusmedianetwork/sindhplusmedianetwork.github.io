Tarbela Dam accident: Water Resources Ministry seeks report from WAPDA

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Ministry of Water Resources has sought a comprehensive report from WAPDA on the Tarbela Dam accident, and the report has been sent to the ministry. WAPDA Chairman Lt Gen (retd) Muhammad Saeed has ordered a departmental inquiry into the incident in the tunnel of the Tarbela 5th Extension hydropower project. The chairman visited Tarbela and inspected the intake structure, the tunnel, the powerhouse and other construction sites.

Federal Water Resources Minister Mian Moeen Wattoo is also reviewing the incident.
