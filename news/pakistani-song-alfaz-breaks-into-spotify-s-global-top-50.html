Pakistani song 'Alfaz' breaks into Spotify's Global Top 50

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani song 'Alfaz' has made it to Spotify's prestigious Top 50 Global chart. The track by Zain Ali, Zohaib Ali and Hamza Malik was ranked 39th on the global chart on Wednesday. Sung in Urdu and Punjabi, the song has been a streaming phenomenon across South Asia, topping the Top 50 charts in both Pakistan and India.

It also reached number two on the UK's Official Asian Music Chart. The achievement marks another milestone for Pakistan's independent music scene on international streaming platforms. Fans celebrated the chart entry widely on social media, calling it a proud moment for the country's music industry.
