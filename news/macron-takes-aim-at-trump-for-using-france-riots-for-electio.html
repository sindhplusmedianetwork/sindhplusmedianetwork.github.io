Macron takes aim at Trump for using France riots for election gain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French President Emmanuel Macron on Wednesday appeared to criticise Donald Trump for exploiting France's street unrest to gain advantage in the battle for the US midterm elections. Speaking in Doha, Macron said there were 'people who campaign at France's expense', without naming anyone directly. Trump had described the turmoil in France as a 'revolution' driven by 'migrants, jihadists and communists' and praised far-right leader Marine Le Pen, who is polling as the frontrunner in France's presidential race.

The French government admitted it had 'lost control' during last weekend's violence by masked youths, which followed four weeks of student protests. Paris and other cities responded with night curfews, and Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu vowed a 'massive' police deployment ahead of Saturday's planned Yellow Vest march starting from Bastille Square. Several countries, including the United States, China and Germany, have issued travel advisories for France.
