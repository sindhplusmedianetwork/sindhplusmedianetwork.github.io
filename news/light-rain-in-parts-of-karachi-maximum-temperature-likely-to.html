Light rain in parts of Karachi, maximum temperature likely to stay at 34 degrees over next three days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Light rain and drizzle fell in various parts of Karachi, including Motorway M-9, DHA City and Surjani Town, the Met Office said. The department had said two days earlier that a westerly wave was affecting the western and upper parts of the country. Under the system, light rain was expected on Friday at a few places in Karachi, Thatta, Qambar-Shahdadkot, Jamshoro and Naushahro Feroze.

The Met Office said most of Sindh is expected to remain dry and cool, with light fog in the plains in the morning, while light fog was also expected in Karachi on Thursday. Karachi's daytime maximum temperature may stay at up to 34 degrees Celsius over the next three days.
