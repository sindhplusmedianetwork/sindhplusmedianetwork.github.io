Vanessa Trump says she is cancer-free after breast cancer battle

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Vanessa Trump, the former wife of Donald Trump Jr., has announced that she is cancer-free after a six-month battle with breast cancer. The 48-year-old made the announcement in a video posted on the Instagram account of her 19-year-old daughter Kai Trump, saying her recent medical tests showed no signs of cancer. She said she was diagnosed with breast cancer in April this year, after which she had surgery and worked out a further treatment plan with her doctors.

Kai Trump said she would release a conversation-based video with her mother and Dr Nicole Saphier discussing breast cancer awareness, early diagnosis and prevention. The announcement came in October, which is marked as breast cancer awareness month in many countries around the world, when medical experts urge women to get regular check-ups and learn the symptoms. According to the American Cancer Society, women in the United States face an average lifetime risk of about one in eight of developing breast cancer.

Vanessa Trump has five children with Donald Trump Jr., from whom she separated in 2018, and she publicly shared her illness in May. Social media users welcomed the announcement with messages of joy and best wishes.
