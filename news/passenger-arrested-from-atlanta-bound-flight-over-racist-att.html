Passenger Arrested From Atlanta-Bound Flight Over Racist Attack at JFK

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 60-year-old passenger from Brooklyn was arrested and removed from a Delta Air Lines flight to Atlanta at New York's John F. Kennedy airport for alleged intoxicated, lewd and racist behaviour. According to aviation sources, the flight was carrying 160 passengers and six crew members.

As the flight prepared to depart, the passenger suddenly began hurling racial slurs and abusive shouting, creating unrest among travellers. The commotion delayed the flight by about 45 minutes. When the crew tried to stop him, he turned aggressive toward them.

The airport authority called the police after the disruption to the flight's departure. Officers offloaded the passenger and took him into custody. He faces charges including allegedly assaulting a fellow passenger, disorderly conduct, disrupting a flight, resisting arrest and obstructing officials in the performance of their duties.
