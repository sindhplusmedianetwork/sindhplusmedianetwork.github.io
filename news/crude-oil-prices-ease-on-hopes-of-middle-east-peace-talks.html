Crude Oil Prices Ease on Hopes of Middle East Peace Talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Crude oil prices declined in global markets on Thursday as optimism over possible peace talks in the Middle East eased fears that escalating tensions could disrupt oil supplies. According to Reuters, benchmark Brent crude futures fell by $1.22, or 1.1%, to $114.10 a barrel, giving up part of the previous day's gains. US West Texas Intermediate crude also dropped $1.33, or 1.3%, to $101.45 a barrel.

On Wednesday, Brent had risen 1.3% and WTI had climbed 3% as markets reacted to regional tensions. Reuters noted that the prospect of diplomatic progress is a key reason for the latest pullback, although concerns over security in the region and potential supply disruptions continue to influence the market. Analysts say oil prices remain tied to the security situation in the region, supply levels, and diplomatic developments.

Any renewed escalation or interruption in shipments could push prices higher again, while progress in talks could further calm supply concerns.
