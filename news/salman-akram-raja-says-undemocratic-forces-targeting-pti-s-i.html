Salman Akram Raja Says Undemocratic Forces Targeting PTI's Internal Unity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Salman Akram Raja has said that undemocratic decision-makers are attacking PTI's internal unity, in a statement posted on the social media platform X. He said the narrative being run against the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa leadership should not be believed until investigations are complete. Raja clarified that Junaid Akbar remains the party president in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, appointed by Imran Khan himself, while Ali Asghar Khan is acting president in his absence.

He added that Sohail Afridi remains the chief minister under the constitution. Raja also expressed regret over the defamation verdict against opposition leader Moeen Qureshi in the Punjab Assembly.
