The Simple Test That May Predict Your Risk of Death: Heart Rate Recovery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A simple test could offer a clue about a person's risk of dying, according to health experts. The test measures how quickly the heart rate returns to normal after exercise. This is known as heart rate recovery.

Doctors consider it an important indicator of the health of the heart and blood vessels. A fast recovery generally suggests a healthy cardiovascular system. A slow recovery, on the other hand, may signal a higher risk of heart problems.

Experts say the test is easy to perform and does not require expensive equipment.
