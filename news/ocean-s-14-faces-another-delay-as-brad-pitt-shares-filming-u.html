Ocean's 14 Faces Another Delay as Brad Pitt Shares Filming Update

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Brad Pitt has offered a fresh update on Ocean's 14, revealing that the long anticipated sequel is now expected to begin filming in late 2027. The actor shared the revised timeline during the Hollywood premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth on November 16, telling Variety that the production was being discussed for sometime late next year. The fifth instalment of the Ocean's franchise had reportedly been scheduled to begin shooting in June before being postponed to the following month, and the latest update suggests audiences will have to wait considerably longer to see the original cast reunite.

Pitt is expected to return alongside George Clooney, Julia Roberts, Matt Damon and Don Cheadle, while David Leitch is attached to direct the film and Pitt praised the work behind the screenplay. Clooney previously discussed the project in 2023, describing the script as great, and Roberts also confirmed in January that she had read it and was impressed by the story. Pitt made the remarks while promoting The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, a follow-up to Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, which is directed by David Fincher with Tarantino writing the screenplay.

For now, the biggest update for Ocean's fans is the revised production window, with the reunion still planned but its return to the screen appearing to take longer than initially expected, and no confirmed release date announced.
