Faster Reforms Could Triple Pakistan's Incomes by 2050, Says World Bank

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's incomes could triple by 2050 if the country ensures faster implementation of structural reforms, according to the World Bank's latest Pakistan Development Update titled 'Attaining Escape Velocity'. The report says Pakistan has stabilised its economy, with real GDP growth rising to 3.7 percent in FY26 and primary fiscal surpluses hitting a record 2.9 percent of GDP. International agencies S&P and Moody's have both upgraded the country's credit ratings.

However, real per capita growth remains stuck at around 1.6 percent, the report notes. If Pakistan upgrades its policies and institutions to the average standard of middle-income countries, sustained annual growth of 6.2 to 6.9 percent is possible. That pace would take Pakistan to Vietnam's current income level by 2050, the Bank says.
