More police officers named in Karachi kidnapping case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More police officers and personnel have been named in a kidnapping case involving a citizen in Karachi. An accused in the case said that DSP AVLC Amjad was also involved in the young man's abduction. The naming of further officers has widened the case, which centres on the alleged abduction of the young man.

The accused's statement has brought the Anti-Vehicle Lifting Cell officer into the investigation. Police officials named in the case are now part of the inquiry into the abduction. Further developments are expected as the investigation continues.
