Rain Forces No Result as India and West Indies Draw T20 Series 2-2

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India and West Indies settled for a 2-2 draw in their five-match T20 series after the deciding game in Bengaluru ended in a no result on Saturday. Persistent showers delayed the start and reduced the contest to nine overs per side before rain returned and officials called off the match. India had reached 118 for 5 in 7.4 overs, with Ishan Kishan smashing 30 off 10 balls.

It was the first international fixture at Chinnaswamy Stadium in two years. Veteran seamer Bhuvneshwar Kumar, recalled to India's T20 squad for the first time since November 2022, was denied a chance to bowl.
