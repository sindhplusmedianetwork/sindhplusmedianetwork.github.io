AB de Villiers Makes New Claim About Australian 'Sandpapergate' Ball-Tampering Scandal

Former South African batsman AB de Villiers alleged Australia engaged in ball-tampering from the first match of their 2018 tour of South Africa in Durban, not only in later Tests. He said the Australian bowlers must have known about the tampering. He urged them to admit wrongdoing.

The claim revived debate after Usman Khawaja claimed the whole team knew about the plot.
