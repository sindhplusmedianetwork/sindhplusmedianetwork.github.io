Trade gap widens 16% to $14.6b as imports hit $26b

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's trade deficit widened 16 per cent to $14.6 billion during the first four months of this fiscal year, from July to October, the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics reported on Friday. Imports hit $26 billion over the period amid continued sluggish export growth. Imports averaged $6.5 billion a month against exports of $2.8 billion a month, a gap that may keep Islamabad dependent on foreign creditors longer than planned.

In October alone, the monthly trade deficit rose 10.3 per cent to $3.9 billion. Imports grew 5.7 per cent to over $6.8 billion in October, while exports stayed flat at $2.9 billion.
