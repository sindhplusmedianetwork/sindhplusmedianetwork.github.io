Karachi: SBCA chief orders demolition of all illegal floors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Building Control Authority Director General Abid Saleem Qureshi has directed that all illegally built additional floors in buildings across Karachi be demolished. He said the aim of such measures was to prevent building-related accidents and to ensure protection against such incidents in the future. The newly appointed SBCA chief visited the collapsed building in Karachi's Admin Society along with Additional Director General Farhan Qaiser, where he inspected the rescue operations and the ongoing rehabilitation work.

During the visit, the relevant officers briefed him on the possible causes of the accident and the work under way to remove debris from the site. The SBCA director general directed that a complete investigation into the incident be carried out and that strict action be taken under the law against those responsible if any negligence came to light. He further directed that all illegal floors built in buildings be demolished so that the way to such accidents in the future could be blocked.
