Omar Ayub: US Voices Heard Because of Independent ECP; Pakistan's Election Commission Dysfunctional, Illegal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Omar Ayub Khan said the American people's voice was heard because the United States has an independent election commission, while Pakistan's election commission is dysfunctional and illegal. In a statement posted on X, he accused Pakistan's election commission of not only stealing the PTI's mandate but also using it as a weapon to keep Imran Khan and the party out of power. Ayub noted that four years had passed since the assassination attempt on Imran Khan in Wazirabad, adding that after the attack he had taken the injured leader from Wazirabad to Shaukat Khanum Hospital in Lahore.

He said that despite being hit by four bullets, Imran Khan had shown extraordinary courage and steadfastness. According to Ayub, Pakistan needs free and transparent elections to move past its wounds, progress, and develop.
