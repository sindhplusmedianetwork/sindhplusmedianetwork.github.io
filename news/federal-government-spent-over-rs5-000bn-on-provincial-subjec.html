Federal government spent over Rs5,000bn on provincial-subject departments in 16 years, Senate panel told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A sub-committee of the Senate Functional Committee on Devolution has revealed that the federal government spent more than Rs5,000 billion over the past 16 years on departments and ministries that fall under provincial jurisdiction after the 18th Constitutional Amendment. The committee described the spending as against the Constitution and directed that no further funds be released to these ministries. The Auditor General's office told the meeting that between 2011 and 2026, more than Rs5,000 billion, about 18 billion dollars, was spent on these ministries.

The office noted that the amount was more than the 15 billion dollar loan obtained from the International Monetary Fund during the same period. The committee has already given the government a two-week deadline to abolish 17 ministries. The ministries named for abolition include health, education, food security, water resources and railways.
