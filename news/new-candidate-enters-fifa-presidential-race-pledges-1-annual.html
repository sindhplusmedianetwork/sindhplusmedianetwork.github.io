New Candidate Enters FIFA Presidential Race, Pledges $1 Annual Salary

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

British university lecturer Peter O'Der Hyde has begun preparations to challenge FIFA president Gianni Infantino in the election in March 2027. Hyde, a South African citizen who teaches journalism at Southampton Solent University, has pledged to take an annual salary of only one dollar if he wins. He told BBC Radio Solent that every election needs a contest so accountability and transparency can be ensured.

To become a formal candidate, he needs nominations from at least five national football associations. He called Infantino's reported annual income of about $6 million unreasonable, saying $450,000 would be a suitable salary for the FIFA president.
