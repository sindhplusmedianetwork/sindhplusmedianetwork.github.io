Karachi's National Bank Stadium to Get Rs5.062 Billion Makeover

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has allocated Rs5.062 billion for phase two of the renovation of Karachi's National Bank Stadium, aiming to transform it into a world-class venue. The plan includes removing the obstructive railings around the playing area and replacing them with a dug-out moat and protective nets. Spectator stands in all enclosures will also be demolished and rebuilt as part of the upgrade.

The first phase of the renovation was completed ahead of the ICC Champions Trophy 2025. That phase added a new five-storey pavilion, 24 hospitality boxes and upgraded dressing rooms. The fresh investment is expected to further improve the experience for players and fans at one of the country's leading cricket grounds.
