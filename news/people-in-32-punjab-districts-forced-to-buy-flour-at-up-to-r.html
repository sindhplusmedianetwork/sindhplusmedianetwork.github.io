People in 32 Punjab Districts Forced to Buy Flour at Up to Rs2,500 per 15kg

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

People in 32 districts of Punjab are being forced to buy a 15kg bag of flour for Rs2,400 to Rs2,500, Jang reported. The picture is very different in the 10 divisional headquarters, where flour mills receive a PASSCO wheat quota at Rs3,800 per 40kg. There, a 10kg green bag costs Rs1,100 and a 20kg bag costs Rs2,200.

In the other 32 districts, consumers have to buy wheat from the open market at Rs5,500 per 40kg. This is despite the fact that the Punjab government bought wheat from farmers at Rs3,500 per 40kg and stored it in its warehouses. The report highlights the wide gap between the price at which the government bought wheat and the price consumers are paying in the open market.
