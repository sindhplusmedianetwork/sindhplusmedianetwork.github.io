Section 144 in Lahore extended for three more days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The deputy commissioner of Lahore, Captain (retired) Muhammad Ali Aijaz, has extended the enforcement of Section 144 in the city for three more days. According to the notification issued by the district administration, the restrictions will remain in force from November 26 to November 29. The order has been imposed in view of public order and security concerns, and gatherings of five or more people as well as processions are banned across the district.

Inciting unlawful gatherings is also completely prohibited under the notification. Weddings, funeral prayers, burials and prayers in places of worship are exempt from the ban, as are official and semi-official duties and court proceedings. The deputy commissioner said the protection of citizens' lives and property was the top priority, and that Section 144 had been imposed in light of sensitive security information.

He added that the three-day restriction was aimed at safeguarding citizens, and appealed to the public to cooperate with the administration in maintaining peace in the district.
