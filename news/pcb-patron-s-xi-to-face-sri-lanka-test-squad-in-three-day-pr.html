PCB Patron's XI to Face Sri Lanka Test Squad in Three-Day Practice Match

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The three-day practice match between PCB Patron's XI and Sri Lanka's Test squad begins on Wednesday at the Islamabad Club Ground, serving as an important warm-up for the upcoming two-Test series. Both sides held full training sessions at the club on Tuesday ahead of the fixture. Patron's XI head coach Ijaz Ahmed Junior and captain Saad Baig told PCB Digital that the match is an important opportunity to prepare for the series, which opens at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on November 9.

The Patron's XI squad includes seven members of Pakistan's Test team, giving the home players valuable match practice before the series. According to the team management, two more fast bowlers from the Patron's XI will be added to the Test squad after the practice match concludes. The two-Test series marks an important home assignment for Pakistan against the visiting Sri Lankan side.
