Father Killed, Son Injured in Sharp-Weapon Attack During Dispute in Karachi's Machhar Colony

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man was killed and his son injured in a violent dispute in Machhar Colony, Maripur, in Karachi's Docks area on Tuesday. Police said 63-year-old Muhammad Hussain died after being struck with a sharp-edged weapon, while his 23-year-old son Muhammad Yasin was wounded in the same incident. Edhi volunteers shifted the deceased and the injured youth to Civil Hospital, where the father was identified and the son received medical treatment.

Docks SHO Waqar Qaisar said the initial investigation indicates the incident occurred during a dispute between the two sides. He added that police are recording the injured young man's statement, on the basis of which further legal action will be taken. Police said they are also collecting more information from the area about the incident.
