Trump to Meet Qatar's Prime Minister as Iran Diplomacy Faces Hurdles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump will meet Qatar's prime minister at the White House on Monday, according to the White House schedule, as the conflict with Iran drags on and diplomatic efforts to end it face significant obstacles. Trump is due to meet Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al-Thani, who also serves as Qatar's foreign minister, in a closed-door meeting. US Vice President JD Vance is also scheduled to meet the Qatari leader, according to a US official.

The meeting comes as mediation efforts have stalled, with Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi saying mediators striving for a peace deal between the US and Iran had not travelled at all since the shock of the US midterm elections. Araghchi said it would take some time before the atmosphere was favourable for the resumption of mediator travel, and he called for patience. Trump has said he is highly unlikely to order fresh attacks on Iran as long as talks to end the conflict progress, adding that it depends on whether a very strong agreement is signed.
