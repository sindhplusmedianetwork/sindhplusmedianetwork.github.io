Pakistan's Growth Slows to 2% as Industry Contracts 4.7%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's GDP growth slowed to 2 percent in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025-26, covering April to June, the National Accounts Committee reported at its 118th meeting. The slowdown was driven by a 4.7 percent contraction in the industrial sector. Agriculture grew 3.7 percent during the quarter, while the services sector expanded 3.62 percent.

Electricity, gas and water supply fell sharply by 33.2 percent. The committee also revised the full-year growth for FY 2024-25 slightly downward to 3.65 percent. Separately, the government approved a 14.8 billion dollar upward revision of import data across five fiscal years from 2021 to 2025, following the earlier trade-data discrepancy.
