UEFA Backs France's Diallo to Stand Against Infantino in FIFA Election

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

European football's governing body UEFA has given its unanimous backing to French Football Federation chief Philippe Diallo to stand against incumbent Gianni Infantino in March's FIFA presidential election, it said in a statement on Monday. The body said Diallo had been invited to stand as a candidate in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027 and that European national associations had unanimously pledged their full support. German FA president Bernd Neuendorf called Diallo an excellent candidate, with a different way of conducting himself and a different understanding of how to hold such an office.

The deadline for rival candidates to declare themselves is this Wednesday, November 18, and UEFA, which boasts 55 member associations, said Diallo had accepted the invitation to stand and that his candidature was set to be confirmed at a meeting of the French federation on Tuesday. Under its Slovenian president Aleksander Ceferin, UEFA has led the opposition to Infantino since his announcement in late July of a project to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors. Infantino quickly abandoned the proposal in the face of widespread uproar, but UEFA has since gone to a court in Florida in an attempt to gather evidence for a potential complaint of criminal mismanagement against the FIFA president in Switzerland.

The election will take place at the FIFA Congress on March 18 next year in the Moroccan capital, Rabat, and the Asian Football Confederation, which consists of 47 member associations, announced last month that it was backing Infantino for re-election.
