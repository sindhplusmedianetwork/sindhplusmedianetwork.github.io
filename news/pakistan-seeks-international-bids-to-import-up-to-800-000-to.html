Pakistan Seeks International Bids to Import Up to 800,000 Tonnes of Wheat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Trading Corporation of Pakistan on Monday invited international bids to import up to 800,000 metric tonnes of wheat in bulk for delivery to Karachi or Gwadar ports. Bids below 50,000 tonnes will not be accepted. Sealed technical and financial bids must be submitted by 2pm on November 30 at the corporation's Karachi office.

The move comes as flour in Hyderabad has risen to Rs185 per kg amid delayed release of government stocks. Pakistan faces a possible shortfall of three to four million tonnes, with production of 29.61 million tonnes in FY26 against consumption of 31 to 32 million tonnes.
