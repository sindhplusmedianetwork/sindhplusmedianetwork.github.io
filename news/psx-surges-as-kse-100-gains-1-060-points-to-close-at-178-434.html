PSX surges as KSE-100 gains 1,060 points to close at 178,434

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange rose sharply on Friday, the last trading day of the week. The benchmark KSE-100 index gained 1,060 points during the session. The index closed at 178,434, marking a strong finish to the trading week.

The rise reflected a positive day for the market, with the benchmark moving firmly higher. Friday's session brought the week's trading to a close on an upward note. The market will resume trading at the start of the next week.
