Doctors from PIMS Examine Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A team of doctors from Islamabad's Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences visited former prime minister Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail on Sunday evening. The team, led by Dr Arif Khan, conducted a medical check-up on the couple and inquired about their health. The doctors measured their blood pressure and cholesterol and reviewed the medicines they are currently taking.

The medical team was facilitated by the doctors at Adiala Jail. The visit came a day after lawyer Salman Safdar said on Saturday that Bushra Bibi was suffering from an eye infection and that her health condition was deteriorating.
