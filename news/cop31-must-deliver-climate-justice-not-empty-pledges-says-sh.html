COP31 must deliver climate justice, not empty pledges, says Sherry Rehman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senator Sherry Rehman on Friday called for the COP31 climate conference in Antalya to move beyond declarations and deliver a credible, accessible and equitable climate financing framework. In a statement, she warned that Pakistan and other climate-vulnerable countries were being pushed into a dangerous cycle of disasters, debt and declining development opportunities. She appreciated Turkiye's hosting of the conference and said its presidency offered an opportunity to bridge divisions between developed and developing countries and restore confidence in multilateral climate action.

Referring to the UN Secretary-General's warning that fossil fuels must go the way of the dinosaurs, she said governments could not keep subsidising the industries driving the climate crisis while pleading scarcity when vulnerable countries sought climate finance. She noted that global fossil fuel subsidies were estimated at about $7 trillion, calling it an extraordinary misallocation of resources. She warned that the 1.5 degrees centigrade goal was slipping out of reach, with current policies putting the world on course for about 2.7 degrees centigrade of warming by the end of the century.

Recalling that the 2022 floods affected 33 million people in Pakistan and caused about $30 billion in damages, she said Pakistan was not asking for charity but demanding climate justice, financial accountability and a fair international system.
