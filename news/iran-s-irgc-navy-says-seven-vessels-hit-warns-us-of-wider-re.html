Iran's IRGC Navy Says Seven Vessels Hit, Warns US of Wider Response

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy said on Thursday that seven vessels were hit near the Strait of Hormuz in two separate actions. It stated that four vessels involved in illegal activities were struck, while three more collided with mines west of the strait. The IRGC Navy warned the United States of a wider response if hostilities continue.

The claim comes amid a sharp escalation in the region's standoff between Tehran and Washington. US President Donald Trump said on Wednesday that Washington is 'not negotiating' with Iran and that something will happen 'fairly soon'. Separately, the New York Times reported that the US and Israeli militaries have developed plans for renewed strikes on Iran after the American midterm elections.

Tensions around the Strait of Hormuz, through which a large share of the world's oil trade passes, have rattled shipping and energy markets. Gulf states are watching the situation closely as diplomatic channels appear to have narrowed.
