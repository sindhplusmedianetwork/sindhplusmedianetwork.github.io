Brother Shoots Two Sisters Dead at Home in Karachi's Shah Faisal Colony

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two sisters were killed after their brother opened fire inside their home in Karachi's Shah Faisal Colony. According to SSP Korangi, the accused, Raheem alias Daku, shot and killed his sisters, with initial investigation indicating that the incident took place due to personal enmity and a dispute. The SSP said the accused has a criminal record and has been to jail multiple times, adding that the accused's father had died five days earlier.

The bodies of the two sisters were shifted to Jinnah Hospital. Police are investigating the circumstances of the firing, which has left a family grieving the loss of two daughters in a single incident of domestic violence.
