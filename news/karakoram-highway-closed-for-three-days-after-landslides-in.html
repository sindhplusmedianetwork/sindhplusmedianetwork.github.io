Karakoram Highway closed for three days after landslides in Upper Kohistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Karakoram Highway, the key road linking Gilgit-Baltistan with the rest of Pakistan, has been completely closed to all traffic for three days. Heavy landslides struck at Chochang in Upper Kohistan. Hundreds of vehicles carrying thousands of passengers, tourists and freight are stranded on both sides.

The land link between Gilgit-Baltistan and the rest of the country is cut off. Heavy machinery is clearing mud, stones and debris from the road. The risk of further landslides is hampering restoration work, and traffic has still not been restored.
