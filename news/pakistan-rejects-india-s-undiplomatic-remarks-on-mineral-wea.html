Pakistan Rejects India's Undiplomatic Remarks on Mineral Wealth at UN

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Foreign Office on Friday categorically rejected India's undiplomatic and irresponsible remarks about Pakistan's mineral wealth made at the United Nations. The exchange took place at a UN Security Council Arria-formula meeting on Thursday on the role of natural resources in fuelling conflict, where India's Permanent Representative Parvathaneni Harish accused Pakistan's military of looting the country's resources for personal gain and triggering turmoil, according to Indian media reports. The remarks came after Pakistan raised India's holding of the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance following the Pahalgam attack, in which 26 people were killed.

Pakistan's First Secretary Ansar Shah had already rejected India's frivolous remarks during the same meeting. In its weekly press briefing on Friday, the Foreign Office spokesperson rejected the remarks and said they were so baseless that they merited no attention, calling them unacceptable interference in Pakistan's internal affairs and antithetical to the UN Charter. In a detailed statement, the Foreign Office said India's diatribe could only be rubbished, accused New Delhi of sponsoring terrorism in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and urged India to implement UN Security Council resolutions on Jammu and Kashmir and introspect rather than lecture others.
