Singapore Pushes Deeper Trade Ties With Pakistan on FM's First Visit in Two Decades

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Singapore's Foreign Minister Dr Vivian Balakrishnan has said trade and investment between Singapore and Pakistan lag far behind the two countries' six decades of diplomatic ties, as he visited Islamabad on his first trip to Pakistan since 2005. In talks with Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, the two sides discussed cooperation in small and medium enterprises, the digital economy, fintech, carbon markets and direct air links. An MoU on disaster management cooperation was signed between Pakistan's NDMA and the Singapore Civil Defence Force.

Balakrishnan also met Chief of Defence Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir during the visit. Both sides agreed the relationship had strong potential in technology, logistics and green energy, and pledged to turn the political goodwill into concrete business deals.
