Post-mortem documents 20 wounds in death of 13-year-old domestic worker Fatima Asghar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The post-mortem of 13-year-old domestic worker Fatima Asghar, who died at a senior police officer's residence in Islamabad, documented around 20 major wounds and signs of prolonged torture. The report also noted burns allegedly caused by a heated iron. Two suspects, the officer's wife Afshan and tutor Qari Ali, gave contradictory statements.

Both were remanded for four days. The findings have documented visible marks of violence in the child's death.
