AI-Designed Bird Flu Vaccine Succeeds in Early Trials

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new bird flu vaccine designed with the help of artificial intelligence has achieved remarkable success in early trials, opening new possibilities for vaccine development against pandemics. Research by the University of Cambridge and its subsidiary DIOSynVax, published in the journal Cell Reports Medicine, found that all trial animals given the new vaccine were protected against genetically different strains of bird flu. Only about half of those given a conventional vaccine survived.

The vaccine fully protected mice and ferrets against strains that have already infected humans. Researchers say artificial intelligence could pave the way for vaccines that protect against multiple virus strains at once. They added that further research and clinical trials are needed before the vaccine can be used in humans.
