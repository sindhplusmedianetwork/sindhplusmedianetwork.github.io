Karachi: Fire breaks out at wood warehouse near Liaquatabad Sindhi Hotel

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fire broke out at a wood warehouse near Sindhi Hotel in Karachi's Liaquatabad area. Five fire brigade vehicles reached the scene to extinguish the fire. The cause of the fire was not immediately known.

Fire officer Ahsan said the fire started in a shop and reached the first floor and roof of the building. The fire in the wood warehouse was stopped from spreading further.
