Khawaja alleges racial abuse by MCC member at Lord's during 2023 Ashes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Australian Test cricketer Usman Khawaja has revealed that an MCC member used racist remarks against him after Jonny Bairstow's controversial run-out in the Lord's Test during the 2023 Ashes. In his autobiography 'Sabr', he writes that as the Australian players returned to the pavilion at lunch, some MCC members in the Long Room used abusive language against him and his team-mates. Khawaja says he was at one point told to keep quiet, though he could not say with certainty which member had spoken those words.

Footage broadcast by Sky Sports showed Khawaja stopping to speak to one member and later pointing out another member to the stewards. He writes that the members showed their true colours, behaving like a mob of privileged drunks while hurling abuse, and that at first he could only laugh in shock. The MCC suspended three members for inappropriate behaviour immediately after the Lord's Test, and after an investigation one member was expelled from the club four months later while the other two were banned for four and a half years and two and a half years respectively.
