Tarbela incident: fears the Rs82 billion expansion project could reach Rs200 billion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Water entering the Tarbela fifth extension project has affected construction, and the water resources minister and the Prime Minister's Office have taken notice. Sources say investigations are considering whether the construction was substandard or the incident was natural, and WAPDA says a full investigation will be carried out, with an inquiry committee likely after a complete assessment today. Documents show the project's originally approved cost was Rs82.36 billion, but Rs153.95 billion had already been spent by January 2026.

WAPDA says a weak part of rock collapsed during excavation of the connecting tunnel, letting water in. There was no loss of life and key installations are safe. After the incident the PC-I is likely to change and the T5 project's cost is feared to exceed Rs200 billion.
