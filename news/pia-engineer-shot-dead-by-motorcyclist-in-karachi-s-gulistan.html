PIA engineer shot dead by motorcyclist in Karachi's Gulistan-e-Johar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 54-year-old PIA engineer, Imtiaz Ahmed, was shot dead by an unidentified motorcyclist in Gulistan-e-Johar Block 13, Karachi. The incident occurred while the victim was out of his home to buy bread. Police said his mobile phone and wallet were untouched, ruling out resistance during a robbery as the motive.

Investigators recovered four 9mm pistol shells from the crime scene. CCTV footage showed a motorcyclist speeding away after the shots were fired. Police have launched an investigation into the killing.
