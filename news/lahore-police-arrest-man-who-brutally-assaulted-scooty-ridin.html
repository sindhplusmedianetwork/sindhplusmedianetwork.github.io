Lahore Police Arrest Man Who Brutally Assaulted Scooty-Riding Girl in Mozang

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore police have arrested the motorcyclist accused of brutally assaulting a scooty-riding girl in the Mozang area, after CCTV footage of the attack went viral on social media. According to police, the call to the 15 emergency helpline brought Mozang police to the scene, and a case was initially registered on the complaint of the girl's father against an unknown suspect. The CCTV footage shows the motorcyclist overtaking the girl's scooty and forcing her to stop, then getting off his bike, grabbing her and throwing her to the ground.

The footage further shows him pulling the girl's hair, hurling abuse and harassing her before people present at the scene intervened and rescued her. According to the FIR, the suspect snatched the girl's bag and smashed it on the ground, breaking her laptop worth around Rs85,000. The assault left the girl with a fractured nasal bone and wounds on her face, and the suspect fled after abandoning his motorcycle, which bystanders handed over to the police.

Police officials said a special team formed under the supervision of SP Civil Lines Manzah Karamat used modern technology to raid and arrest the accused, and further investigation is underway. DIG Operations Lahore said those who assault women deserve no leniency and the accused would be given strict punishment in accordance with the law.
