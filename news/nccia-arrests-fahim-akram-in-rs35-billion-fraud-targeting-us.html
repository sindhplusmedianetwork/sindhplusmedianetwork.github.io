NCCIA Arrests Fahim Akram in Rs35 Billion Fraud Targeting US Citizens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has arrested the alleged mastermind of a Rs35 billion fraud targeting American citizens. Fahim Akram was arrested in Lahore on Tuesday in a major operation by the agency. Additional Director Lahore Zone Atif Amir said the suspect operated a website, LabelsBank.com, from August 2023 to September 2026, through which he sold more than five million counterfeit US postage stamps at $2 each.

Sixteen bank accounts were used to channel the proceeds of the fraud. US authorities had announced a reward of $100,000 for information leading to his arrest. Eight special teams took part in the operation, raiding 35 locations and questioning 47 people.

Officials said the case was linked to an online fraud investigation conducted jointly with the US Postal Department. Further arrests are expected as the investigation continues.
