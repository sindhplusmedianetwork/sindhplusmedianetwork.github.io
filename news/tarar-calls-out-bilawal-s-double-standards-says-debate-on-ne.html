Tarar Calls Out Bilawal's Double Standards, Says Debate on New Provinces Cannot Be Stopped

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Information Minister Attaullah Tarar has accused PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari of double standards, saying the debate on new provinces and good governance cannot be stopped. Tarar said it was a contradiction to benefit from government privileges, protocol and ministries while also claiming the moral high ground as the opposition. He said the debate over new provinces was a democratic and constitutional requirement, and that a referendum was part of the Constitution rather than a backdoor route.

Tarar added that the people of Pakistan, including Sindh, would decide whether they wanted new provinces, and that talk of shedding blood over the issue was beyond comprehension. He argued that all political parties had been part of the existing system for decades and could not distance themselves from its shortcomings. The debate began on 30 July, when Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi called for additional administrative units, and it widened after the Sindh Assembly unanimously passed a resolution on 3 September rejecting any division of Sindh.

Tarar said smaller administrative units should be seen in a constructive manner, and that the debate would continue inside parliament and outside it.
