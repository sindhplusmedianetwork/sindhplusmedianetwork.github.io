US midterms to become history's most expensive with $11.93bn in spending

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States midterm elections being held today are set to become the most expensive midterms in history, with total spending projected at $11.93 billion. The figure, compiled by the watchdog Open Secrets, breaks all previous records for midterm elections. All 435 seats of the House of Representatives and 35 Senate seats are up for grabs in today's vote.

Democrats are expected to spend $5.52 billion while Republicans are projected to spend $5.12 billion, with independent candidates and groups contributing about $1.3 billion. Only the 2020 and 2024 presidential elections cost more, at around $15 billion each, reflecting the high stakes of the contests that will decide control of Congress.
