CDF Munir, Malaysian PM reaffirm commitment to stronger defence ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir met Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim during an official visit to Malaysia. The meeting took place at Perdana Putra in Putrajaya, where the two sides reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening bilateral defence ties and enhancing military cooperation, the military's media wing said on Monday. They also agreed to maintain dialogue on regional and international security issues, according to the Inter-Services Public Relations.

Anwar warmly welcomed the field marshal and acknowledged the longstanding bonds of friendship, mutual trust and cooperation between Pakistan and Malaysia. Field Marshal Munir appreciated Malaysia's constructive role in promoting peace and stability in Southeast Asia and reiterated Pakistan's resolve to deepen defence cooperation and institutional links between the two armed forces. On his arrival at Malaysia's Ministry of Defence in Kuala Lumpur, the field marshal was accorded a ceremonial guard of honour, and he also met Defence Minister Mohamed Khaled bin Nordin to discuss expanding defence cooperation.

Anwar said the meeting provided an opportunity to exchange views on regional and international security, including easing tensions in West Asia, ensuring maritime security and addressing border tensions through dialogue and restraint. He added that the two countries were exploring collaboration in maritime security and counterterrorism, including trilateral cooperation with Turkiye.
