Cricket Chief Backs Selectors and Sets Deadline for Academy Revamp

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The cricket board chairman said on Thursday that the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency would contact wicketkeeper-batter Mohammad Rizwan for some clarification before submitting its report. Speaking to reporters at the National Cricket Academy, Mohsin Naqvi said he had been briefed on Rizwan's case a few days earlier and expected the relevant officials to get in touch with the player soon. Turning to the academy's redevelopment, Naqvi announced January 31 as the deadline for completing the upgraded facility, which he described as a state-of-the-art centre equipped with world-class training machinery.

He credited Aqib Javed with playing a key role in the master plan and design, adding that the facility was already being used to conduct extended training camps for emerging cricketers. The chairman also backed the existing selection committee, saying he was satisfied with its work and expected the current approach to produce positive results. On the Pakistan Super League, Naqvi defended the franchise model amid questions about the financial challenges facing some teams, saying owners had been aware that profitability could take time.

He reiterated his confidence in the board's current direction, saying he would continue to support the decisions made by his team.
