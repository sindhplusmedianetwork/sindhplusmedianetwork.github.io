Late Double Sinks Pakistan as Malaysia Win Johor Junior Hockey Thriller

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hosts Malaysia defeated Pakistan 3-2 in the Sultan of Johor Junior Hockey Tournament in Johor Bahru. Pakistan were on course for victory and led 2-1 by the 54th minute of the match. The game turned after Pakistan's Rana Waleed was shown a red card in the 51st minute.

Malaysia took advantage of the situation and scored twice in the last six minutes to snatch the win. The defeat is a setback for the young Pakistan side in the tournament. Pakistan will look to bounce back when they next play Japan on Friday.
