Rawalpindi Test: Sri Lanka bowled out for 446 as debutant Saqib Khan takes seven wickets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sri Lanka were bowled out for 446 in their first innings on the fourth day of the first Test against Pakistan at Pindi Cricket Stadium, taking a 12-run lead over the hosts. Dhananjaya de Silva and Kusal Mendis resumed the innings for the visitors on the fourth day, but wickets continued to fall from the start and the whole team was dismissed for 446. Dhananjaya de Silva scored 167 and Kusal Mendis 119 for Sri Lanka, the two standout batters of the innings.

Debutant Saqib Khan took seven wickets for Pakistan, while Mohammad Abbas took two and Razaullah took one. Pakistan had declared their first innings at 434 for nine on the second day of the match. Saud Shakeel top-scored for Pakistan with 93, while debutant Tayyab Arif made 57, Arafat Minhas 52, Muhammad Taha 51, Babar Azam 50 and Ghazi Ghori 48.

Four players are making their debut for Pakistan in this first Test against Sri Lanka: Tayyab Arif, Muhammad Taha, Arafat Minhas and Saqib Khan.
