US and Iran very close to a de-escalation deal, says US ambassador to Türkiye

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States ambassador to Türkiye, Tom Barak, who is also Donald Trump's special envoy for Syria and Iraq, has said that Washington and Tehran are very close to reaching an agreement on reducing military tensions. Speaking to CNN in Doha on Thursday, according to BBC Urdu, Barak expressed hope that important steps would be taken over the next two weeks to de-escalate and prevent military clashes. He said the two sides had come so close that they could say: let us take a pause and reduce the intensity of the tensions.

Barak credited the diplomatic efforts of Qatar, Pakistan and other American allies for building consensus on a political process between Tehran and Washington. He stressed that an immediate comprehensive agreement was not possible, but a pause in hostilities could pave the way for further negotiations and new regional arrangements. He added that the use of military force is something the US president and other officials take extremely seriously, but every possible alternative is being explored to avoid such action.
