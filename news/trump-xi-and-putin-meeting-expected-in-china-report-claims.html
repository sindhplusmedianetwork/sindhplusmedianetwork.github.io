Trump, Xi and Putin meeting expected in China, report claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin are expected to meet in Shenzhen, China, on Thursday, according to a report. The three leaders are likely to discuss nuclear arms control, North Korea, Ukraine and Iran. The possible meeting is being compared to the 1945 Yalta Conference, when the leaders of the United States, Britain and the Soviet Union met to shape the post-war world order.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has voiced reservations about the reported meeting. He said a Shenzhen meeting could amount to a new division of global spheres of influence for the 21st century. No official confirmation of the meeting has been reported so far.
