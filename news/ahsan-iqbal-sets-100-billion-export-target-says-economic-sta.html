Ahsan Iqbal Sets $100 Billion Export Target, Says Economic Stability Coming Soon

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Planning Ahsan Iqbal has said economic stability will soon arrive in Pakistan and that the country must achieve a $100 billion export target. Speaking to the media at the EGA TEX Pakistan textile and garments machinery exhibition at the Expo Centre in Lahore, attended by participants from 30 countries, he said seeing the enthusiasm at the exhibition felt like the industry's wheel had started turning. He said the government had travelled a difficult path over the past four years and that Pakistan's positive economic indicators were being acknowledged by the world.

Iqbal said increasing export capacity was the biggest challenge of the current era and that adding value to the supply chain was essential. He called industry the backbone of Pakistan, saying growth was now visible in it. He added that Pakistani products should be improved in quality and presented to the world, and that exports were indispensable for economic sovereignty — only then could Pakistan free itself from the IMF and foreign aid.
