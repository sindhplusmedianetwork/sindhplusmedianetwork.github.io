Saudi-led coalition says two Houthi drones intercepted over Riyadh and southern regions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Saudi-led coalition said on Saturday it had intercepted and destroyed two drones launched by Yemen's Houthis towards the Saudi capital Riyadh and the kingdom's southern region. Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al-Malki announced the interception, saying the drones were launched by the Iran-aligned group. The announcement came amid continued Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, which Pakistan condemned at the United Nations Security Council this week while urging a Yemeni-led political settlement.

Yemen's government-aligned forces also said they had repelled a Houthi attack in the southwestern province of Lahj. Cross-border fighting between the coalition, which backs Yemen's internationally recognised government, and the Houthis has intensified amid the US-Israeli war with Iran.
