Musadik Malik Denied Permission to Visit Taj Mahal and Nizamuddin Shrine in India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik could not visit the Taj Mahal and the shrine of Hazrat Nizamuddin Aulia during his visit to India. According to Indian media, the federal minister had expressed the desire to visit the Taj Mahal and the shrine during his India tour and had formally sought permission from Indian officials, but he was not allowed to go to these places. Malik had requested visits to both places, but Indian authorities did not approve them due to security concerns.

Malik's visit to India is significant in connection with representing Pakistan at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Civilisational Dialogue Forum. Issues relating to civilisational links, cultural heritage and mutual dialogue among member countries are discussed at the forum. Musadik Malik has reached India to participate in the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum being held in New Delhi from November 20 to 22, 2026, where he is leading the Pakistani delegation.
