Israel's expansionist efforts behind region's crises, says Foreign Office

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign Office spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan said Israel's expansionist efforts are the major cause of the crises facing the region. He said the effects of these efforts are being felt in Palestine, Syria, Lebanon and other neighbouring countries. Speaking at the weekly briefing, he said the Makkah alliance is not aimed against any country and its purpose is to promote peace and stability in the region.

He added that Pakistan will continue cooperating with the United States government on trade, investment, security and counter-terrorism, regardless of which party is in power there. The spokesperson clarified that Pakistan has not stopped issuing visas to Indian citizens. He noted that more than 9,000 Indian Sikh pilgrims received visas last year and more than 4,000 have received them this year.

He also said Pakistan has received an invitation to the SCO meeting in New Delhi on November 20-22, and participation will be decided after institutional consultation.
