Tiger Shroff Was Not the Real Target of Attackers, Investigation Reveals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A significant new revelation has emerged in the investigation into the alleged firing incident involving Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff in Mumbai's Juhu area. According to Indian media reports, investigators now believe that Shroff was not the attackers' original target and that another famous film personality was in their sights. Mumbai Crime Branch officials say the accused had first planned to target a different Bollywood star but could not reach him because of his tight security.

The attackers then allegedly considered Shroff a relatively easier target, according to the reports. In the incident, the actor escaped unhurt while his driver sustained injuries. The Lawrence Bishnoi gang has claimed responsibility for the attack, and police say the investigation is continuing.
