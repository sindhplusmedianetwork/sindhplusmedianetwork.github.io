Pakistan's First Giraffe Cafe Opens at Lahore Safari Park

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's first giraffe cafe has been set up at Lahore Safari Park, where visitors will be able to hand-feed giraffes and sit close to them while enjoying tea and coffee. The cafe has been designed on a jungle theme and is part of the Lahore Safari Zoo's revamping project. The park currently houses 10 giraffes — two males and eight females — that were brought from South Africa in September 2025.

Special rooms and enclosures have been built for the animals, where they are monitored around the clock and the temperature is kept at 25 degrees because they cannot tolerate excessive heat. The park's veterinary officer, Dr Warda Gul, said giraffes are social animals that live in groups and can become accustomed to humans; they are given a balanced diet of green fodder, minerals and other essentials. The administration has invited applications to operate the cafe on a three-year lease and hopes to partner with a well-known coffee brand.

The final opening date and operator details are yet to be announced.
