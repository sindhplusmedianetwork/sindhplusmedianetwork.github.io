Bangladesh adviser presents five-point development paradigm for South Asia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bangladesh's finance adviser Dr Rashid Al Mehmood Titumir has proposed a five-point development paradigm for South Asia at a sustainable development conference in Islamabad. He stressed that the region should prioritise resilience over efficiency in its economic approach. Pakistan's Climate Change Minister Dr Musadiq Malik said 48% of Pakistan's Rs16 trillion revenue goes to debt servicing.

Malik declared the country 'closed for debt but open for business in equity'. He also criticised the finance ministry for taking fresh foreign loans despite that stance. The conference brought together policymakers and experts on sustainable development.

The remarks underlined growing concern over the region's debt burden.
