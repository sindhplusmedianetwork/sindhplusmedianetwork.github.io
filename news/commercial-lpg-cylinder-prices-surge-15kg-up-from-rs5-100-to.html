Commercial LPG Cylinder Prices Surge: 15kg Up from Rs5,100 to Rs8,200 in a Week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prices of commercial LPG cylinders have risen sharply across Pakistan within a week. Shopkeepers raised the price of the 15-kilogramme commercial LPG cylinder from Rs5,100 on 30 October to Rs8,200 by 7 November. The 35-kilogramme commercial cylinder, which cost up to Rs25,200 in the last week of October, was selling for Rs38,200 by the evening of 7 November.

The price of the 11.8-kilogramme cylinder used by households also jumped, from Rs3,469 to Rs7,316, after a rise of Rs620 per kilogramme on Saturday, 7 November. The increases were reported in the twin cities of Islamabad and Rawalpindi, all four provinces and their capitals, as well as Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan. The report notes that only two days earlier, Federal Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik had warned in a media conference that gas dealers and the mafia would not be allowed to sell LPG above the OGRA rate of Rs294 per kilogramme.

It says the minister should order a countrywide survey of LPG sales and present a report to Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif within 48 hours.
