IHC Grants Protective Bail to TikToker 'Mr Pattlo' in Wedding Stampede Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has granted protective bail to TikToker Muhammad Ansar Jatt, popularly known as 'Mr Pattlo', in the case registered over a deadly stampede at his wedding event. The court's order prevents his arrest until November 10 while he moves the relevant forum for relief. Jatt appeared before the court in a wheelchair during the hearing.

An FIR was registered on October 31 at Motra Police Station after the stampede broke out at his mehndi event in Sialkot, killing two people and injuring 18. The case names Jatt, his brother, the ground owner and others, after hundreds of people surged through the stadium gates as they were opened. His lawyer argued that permission had been obtained for the event and that maintaining order was the administration's responsibility.
