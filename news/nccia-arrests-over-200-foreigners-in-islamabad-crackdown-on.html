NCCIA Arrests Over 200 Foreigners in Islamabad Crackdown on Digital Fraud

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has arrested more than 200 foreign nationals in a crackdown against digital fraudsters in Islamabad. According to sources, the NCCIA conducted the operation in the federal capital, beginning with raids in Sector I-9. On the identification of the arrested suspects, further raids were carried out in other areas of the city.

More than 200 foreign nationals were taken into custody from sectors I-9, F-8, F-10 and E-11 in total. During the operation, officials recovered multiple devices, laptops, advanced servers and computer hard disks from the possession of the accused. The NCCIA is examining the seized equipment as the investigation continues.
