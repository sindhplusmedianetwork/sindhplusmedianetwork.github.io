Gas Connection Dispute Turns Deadly in Peshawar: One Brother Killed, Another Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A dispute over the cutting of a gas connection turned deadly in the Pashtakhara area of Peshawar, leaving one brother dead and another injured. According to Express News, the complainant Shamsuddin Khan told police that he was with his 30-year-old brother Salahuddin when their neighbours confronted them. The accused first started a verbal altercation and then beat both brothers with pistol butts before opening fire on them.

Neighbours rushed the two injured brothers to hospital, where one of them succumbed to his injuries. Police have registered a case on the complainant's statement and started searching for the accused, who fled the scene after the shooting.
