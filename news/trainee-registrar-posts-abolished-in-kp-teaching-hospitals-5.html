Trainee Registrar Posts Abolished in KP Teaching Hospitals, 500 Doctors at Risk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The policy board of the medical teaching institutions in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has decided to abolish trainee registrar posts, a decision that could leave around 500 trainee medical officers working in teaching hospitals across the province without jobs. The board has directed all boards of governors to immediately withdraw recruitment advertisements for trainee registrar posts. In a letter issued on September 1, 2026, the policy board said the trainee registrar posts provided a route to extend the training of trainee medical officers who had completed their training but failed to pass the FCPS examination.

The letter said it was not appropriate to deny the same facility to doctors who passed the FCPS examination in the same period, and therefore there was no justification for retaining the posts. The board pointed out that experiential registrar posts already exist for young doctors who have passed the FCPS examination, to gain two years of experience for eligibility for faculty posts, with a maximum duration of two years. The letter directed that doctors already appointed to trainee registrar posts be informed of the decision, though they may be allowed to continue working for up to three months in line with legal and contractual requirements, provided at least three months remain in their initial contract.

Trainee doctors said doctors had been appointed to trainee registrar posts in major hospitals, calling the abolition of the posts sheer injustice, and demanded that the government review the decision.
