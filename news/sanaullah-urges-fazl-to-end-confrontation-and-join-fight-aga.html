Sanaullah Urges Fazl to End Confrontation and Join Fight Against Terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Adviser to the Prime Minister on Political Affairs Rana Sanaullah on Wednesday urged JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman to end his confrontational approach towards the establishment. He urged Fazl to align with the government in the fight against terrorism. Sanaullah called for a 'ceasefire' between the government and the JUI-F chief, saying Fazl should not find himself on the opposite side in the fight against terrorism, which he described as a threat to Pakistan's existence.

He said he would discuss the matter with the prime minister and reach out to Fazl, stressing that both sides needed to make efforts to de-escalate tensions. He described terrorism as a threat to Pakistan's existence and said Fazl should not find himself on the opposite side in the fight against it.
