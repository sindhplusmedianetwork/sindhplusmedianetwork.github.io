Haze Triggers Highest-Level Alert in Parts of Southeast Asia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indonesia's forest fires have pushed toxic smoke across Southeast Asia, triggering the region's highest-level haze alert. The meteorological monitor activated its top alert for the southern ASEAN region as smoke spread from Indonesian fires. Schools have closed in affected areas and cases of respiratory illness have surged.

Singapore and Kuala Lumpur ranked among the world's most polluted cities on Saturday. Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and East Timor are all reporting hazardous air quality. Authorities have urged residents to stay indoors and wear masks.
