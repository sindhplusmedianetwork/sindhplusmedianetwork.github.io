Pakistan, Saudi Arabia discuss early signing of GCC free trade deal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Saudi Arabia have discussed the early signing and implementation of the Pakistan-Gulf Cooperation Council free trade agreement. Federal Minister for Commerce Jam Kamal Khan raised the agreement at a meeting with the newly appointed Saudi Ambassador to Pakistan, Dr Rajeh Albugamy, in Islamabad, according to a statement issued on Saturday. The minister said the agreement would enhance market access, expand bilateral trade and strengthen economic engagement.

He said Saudi Arabia's economic diversification under Vision 2030 and Pakistan's production and industrial capabilities offered considerable opportunities for greater economic integration. The minister also underlined the importance of expanding Pakistan's export base and strengthening private-sector partnerships to convert the growing engagement into tangible trade outcomes. The Saudi ambassador reaffirmed the bonds of brotherhood between the two countries and assured continued support for stronger bilateral economic ties.

Both sides agreed to maintain regular coordination and follow-up to advance bilateral trade, investment and agricultural cooperation.
