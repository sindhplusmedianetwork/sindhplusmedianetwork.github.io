PTI-Government Talks Could Resume in Coming Days, Says Aamir Dogar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI chief whip Aamir Dogar has indicated that negotiations between PTI and the government could restart within the next few days. Speaking to Express News, Dogar dismissed reports of internal rifts within the party, insisting that the ranks remain united. He said Sohail Afridi remains the constitutional and legal chief minister of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, holding a two-thirds majority in the provincial assembly.

Dogar added that no chief minister can come to the province without the consent of the party's founding chairman. He said the party believes in dialogue as the way forward and expressed hope that the negotiation process would begin in the coming days.
