NCCIA Arrests Man for Negative Propaganda Against State Institutions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has arrested a man in Lahore for spreading negative propaganda against state institutions. According to the agency's spokesperson, the suspect used social media to circulate fake and defamatory material targeting state institutions and government figures. A mobile phone was recovered from the suspect's possession, which yielded evidence of the dissemination of objectionable content.

During initial questioning, the suspect confessed to sharing derogatory material on social media platforms. The agency said the recovered device contained clear traces of the propaganda campaign. NCCIA Director General Ali Nasir Rizvi stated that there is zero tolerance for those engaged in fake and negative propaganda against state institutions.

The arrest is part of the agency's ongoing crackdown on organised online misinformation networks.
