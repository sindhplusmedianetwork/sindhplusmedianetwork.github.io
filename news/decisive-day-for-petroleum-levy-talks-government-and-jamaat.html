Decisive Day for Petroleum Levy Talks: Government and Jamaat-e-Islami Sit Down at the Foreign Office

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The decisive round of talks between the government and Jamaat-e-Islami over the petroleum levy will take place today at the Foreign Office in Islamabad. Acting Jamaat chief Liaquat Baloch announced that the meeting begins at 11 in the morning in the office of Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, with the focus on broad public relief and a gradual end to the petroleum levy. He said he had spoken by phone to Jamaat chief Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman, currently in Turkey, to brief him and seek his guidance ahead of the sitting.

The government team will comprise Ishaq Dar, federal minister Ahsan Iqbal and Interior Minister Rana Sanaullah, while the Jamaat side will be represented by Baloch himself, Syed Farasat Shah and Engineer Nasrullah Randhawa. Baloch stressed that the party's first goal is relief for ordinary people — even if the Jamaat does not get the credit, that would still count as a major success for it. He urged the government to decide on wide-ranging public relief and the phased removal of the levy during these talks.

If the negotiations fail, he warned, the Jamaat chief will return from Turkey, address workers online, and announce the date for a long march beginning from Multan.
