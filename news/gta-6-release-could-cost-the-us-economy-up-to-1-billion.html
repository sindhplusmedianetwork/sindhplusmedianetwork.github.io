GTA 6 Release Could Cost the US Economy Up to $1 Billion

By Sindh Plus Media Network

The release of Grand Theft Auto 6, the long-awaited video game, could deal a heavy blow to the American economy, with losses estimated at up to one billion dollars. A report says that on the day of the game launch, around 1.5 million people may take leave from work to play it. The wave of expected absences reflects the enormous anticipation among gamers who have waited years for the new title.

The release of the game is expected on November 19. Analysts cited in the report warn that mass time off could dent productivity across workplaces nationwide. The scale of the expected leave shows just how popular the franchise has become among players.

The projection covers a single day of release-day absences, yet the number is striking.
