400+ Deepfake AI Apps Found on Apple and Google Stores, Wired Reveals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A major investigation by technology publication Wired has exposed more than 400 apps on Apple and Google's app stores capable of generating sexual and violent deepfake content. The apps appeared harmless on the surface, but concealed features that could transform an ordinary photo of any person into obscene or violent fabricated images and videos. The findings highlight how easily AI-powered abuse tools have slipped past the review systems of the world's biggest app marketplaces.

After Wired raised the alarm, Apple removed 19 of the offending apps from its store. Meta also acted, taking down more than 10,000 advertisements promoting such apps. The revelations are expected to intensify pressure on tech giants to tighten oversight of AI applications.
