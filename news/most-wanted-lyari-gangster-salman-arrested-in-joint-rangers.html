Most-wanted Lyari gangster Salman arrested in joint Rangers-police operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Rangers (Sindh) and District City Police carried out a joint operation against Lyari gangsters in Afshani Street, Kalakot, Lyari, Karachi. During the operation, they arrested Salman alias Salim alias Salam, described as a most-wanted member of the Baba Ladla and Ismail Afshani groups. Arms and ammunition were recovered from the arrested man.

He was most wanted in several cases of target killing, kidnapping for ransom, extortion and encounters with police, and his name is also listed in the CTD Red Book. According to the report, the accused joined the Baba Ladla group of the Lyari gang war in 2011, and in the beginning was involved in drug peddling and picket duty. He was later involved, along with his associates, in grenade attacks on a rival group and firing on police, in which opponents and several passers-by were injured and killed.

Several FIRs are registered against him in Karachi and interior Sindh, including cases of police encounters. He had fled to Balochistan and later abroad to avoid arrest during the Karachi operation.
