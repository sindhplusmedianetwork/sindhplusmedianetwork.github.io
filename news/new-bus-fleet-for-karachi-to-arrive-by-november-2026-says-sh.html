New Bus Fleet for Karachi to Arrive by November 2026, Says Sharjeel Memon

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon has said a new fleet of buses for Karachi will arrive by November 2026, as the provincial government accelerates steps to modernise and improve the public transport system. During meetings in Shanghai with officials of bus manufacturing companies, he discussed the supply of new buses for Karachi and the promotion of environmentally friendly transport. Yutong Bus Company CEO Robin Shen met Memon and offered cooperation to introduce electric buses in Sindh.

The senior minister also met Higer Bus Company vice president Liu Zheng and stressed expediting the supply of buses required to activate double-decker buses in Karachi.
