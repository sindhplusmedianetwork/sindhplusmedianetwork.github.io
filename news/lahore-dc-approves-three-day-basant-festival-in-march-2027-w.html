Lahore DC Approves Three-Day Basant Festival in March 2027 With Strict Safety Rules

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore deputy commissioner has given formal approval for three-day Basant festivities in March 2027, ending years of uncertainty around the kite-flying festival. The celebrations will run from March 12 to 14 with detailed safety conditions in place. Kite-flying will be permitted except in prohibited zones, including aircraft landing and take-off paths.

Authorities have strictly limited kite and string sizes, allowing only cotton string coated with approved material. Metallic or sharp coatings on strings are completely banned. The manufacture, trading and sale of kite-flying material will require valid digital registration through the government's eBiz app.

Officials say the strict SOPs are designed to protect lives and property during the festival.
