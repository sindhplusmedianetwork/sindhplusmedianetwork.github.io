PM Shehbaz: PTI did 'very little, if anything' for KP in 13 years of rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Saturday launched a sweeping attack on the PTI's 13-year rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, asking where the funds made available to the now dysfunctional provincial government had gone. Speaking at a meeting of the PML-N's parliamentary party in KP held in Islamabad, he said: "They have been in power in KP for 13 years. Over these 13 years, they have raised countless slogans, delivered fiery speeches, held long marches and sit-ins, and made numerous promises." The premier questioned whether the party's pledges of hundreds of dams, solar power projects, hospitals, universities and schools had been fulfilled, and demanded to know how many universities and new hospitals had actually been built.

"From Peshawar to Kohistan and Hazara, and across the entire southern region of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the majority of people would tell you that the PTI government has done very little, if anything, to address their basic needs," he said. He noted that around 80,000 people had lost their lives to terrorism while the economy suffered losses estimated at Rs15,000 billion. The Centre imposed governor's rule and an emergency in KP on Oct 11 citing the provincial government's "deliberate disengagement" from the fight against terrorism, a move later approved by a joint sitting of parliament.

Shehbaz said the federal government had been compelled to act because it had no other option, and urged PML-N lawmakers from KP to prioritise short-term welfare projects so they could "help turn people's fortunes around within the next six months to a year."
