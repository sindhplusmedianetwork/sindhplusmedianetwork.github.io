Kane Suffers Ankle Injury as Bayern Held by Cologne in Bundesliga

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harry Kane was substituted early and is set to be sidelined for the next few months with a torn ankle ligament as Bayern Munich drew 1-1 at home with lowly Cologne in the Bundesliga on Friday. Bayern confirmed in a statement that Kane would be out due to the injury, while sporting director Max Eberl said club doctors were currently discussing whether to recommend surgery. Tottenham Hotspur loanee Mikey Moore scored for Cologne after 12 minutes, rounding goalkeeper Jonas Urbig after a counter-attack, before Nathaniel Brown levelled for Bayern with a low shot before the break.

Bayern also lost Michael Olise to injury, with the France winger leaving the stadium on crutches after failing to return for the second half. Cologne's El Mala missed a late chance to secure a first win at Bayern since 2009, pulling his shot wide with eight minutes remaining. Despite the draw, Bayern remain the only undefeated side in Europe's top-five leagues in all competitions this season, with Borussia Dortmund a point behind them in the table.
