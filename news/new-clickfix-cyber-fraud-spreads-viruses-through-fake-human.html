New ClickFix Cyber Fraud Spreads Viruses Through Fake Human Verification

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cybercriminals are using a new ClickFix technique to trick internet users into installing malware on their own computers. The trick works through fake human-verification prompts, the kind of captcha checks seen on many websites. Instead of asking victims to select images or type characters, the fake prompt instructs them to press specific keyboard buttons and hit enter.

Following those instructions runs malicious commands on the victim's computer. Cybersecurity researchers at Malwarebytes say ClickFix is now among the most widely used malware campaigns. Experts advise users never to follow verification prompts that ask them to run commands or paste anything into their computer.
