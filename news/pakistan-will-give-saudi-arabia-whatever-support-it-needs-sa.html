Pakistan Will Give Saudi Arabia 'Whatever Support' It Needs, Says Army Spokesperson

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The military's chief spokesperson has said Pakistan is providing Saudi Arabia with whatever support it needs to defend itself against attacks by Yemen's Houthi rebels. Lieutenant General Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry, the director general of Inter-Services Public Relations, made the remarks in an interview with Independent Arabia that aired on Sunday. He said Saudi Arabia's sovereignty, territorial integrity and above all the security of the two holy mosques were non-negotiable and absolute as far as Pakistan was concerned.

He added that no military alliance publicly discloses the specifics or scale of the support it provides. Describing the Makkah defence alliance with Saudi Arabia and Turkiye as purely defensive, he said any actor threatening regional peace would face its collective deterrence. He also revealed that Pakistan has been engaging all parties involved, including Iran, with the single aim of restraint and reducing tensions.

The spokesperson further noted that Riyadh had set up a multinational maritime defence coalition, with a Pakistani naval officer serving as its deputy commander, to protect shipping in the Bab al-Mandeb and the Strait of Hormuz.
