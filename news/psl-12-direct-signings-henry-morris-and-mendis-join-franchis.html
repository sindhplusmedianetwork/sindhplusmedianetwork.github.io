PSL 12 direct signings: Henry, Morris and Mendis join franchises as window closes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad United have signed New Zealand fast bowler Matt Henry, Multan Sultans have brought in Australian fast bowler Lance Morris, and Lahore Qalandars have signed Sri Lankan batter Kamindu Mendis as direct signings for the 12th edition of the Pakistan Super League. Karachi Kings have also secured England leg-spinner Adil Rashid as their marquee direct signing, completing a round of overseas signings announced as the direct-signing window closed. The 34-year-old Henry has taken 199 Twenty20 wickets and is set to make his first appearance in the league after having featured in the Indian Premier League.

Morris, nicknamed The Wild Thing for his express pace, has limited international experience but has represented Melbourne Stars and Perth Scorchers in the Big Bash League. Rashid, 38, was a key member of the England white-ball teams that won the 2019 World Cup and the 2022 T20 World Cup, and he will make his league debut with Karachi after previously being selected by Multan Sultans in a supplementary category without featuring. The direct-signing window, which opened on October 23 and closed on November 23, allowed each of the eight franchises to recruit one overseas marquee player who did not feature in either of the previous two editions.

Under the rules, a franchise making a direct signing sees its salary cap rise from 45 crore to 50.5 crore rupees, with the player auction scheduled for January 23, 2027 and the tournament set to run from March 18 to May 2, 2027, with 44 matches planned across five to six venues.
