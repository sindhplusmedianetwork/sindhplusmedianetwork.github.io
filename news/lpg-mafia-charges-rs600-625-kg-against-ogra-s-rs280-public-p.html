LPG mafia charges Rs600-625/kg against OGRA's Rs280; public paid Rs1.4bn extra in five days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

LPG sellers across Pakistan are charging between Rs600 and Rs625 per kilogram against OGRA's officially notified price of Rs280 per kg, in what the LPG Distributors Association calls unchecked profiteering. The association said on Wednesday that the public has been forced to pay more than Rs1.4 billion extra in just the last five days because of the inflated rates. Pakistan consumes an estimated 60,000 to 70,000 kilograms of LPG every day, with roughly 70 percent imported and only 30 percent produced locally.

Retailers complain they are compelled to purchase LPG at Rs550 per kg from marketing companies and plant owners, while field squads fine them instead of acting against the suppliers. OGRA has so far taken no action against the marketing companies or plant owners behind the price manipulation, the association said. Consumers, already burdened by rising inflation, are bearing the full brunt of the overcharging with no relief in sight.
