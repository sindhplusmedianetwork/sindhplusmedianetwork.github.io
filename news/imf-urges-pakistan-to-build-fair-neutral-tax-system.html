IMF Urges Pakistan to Build Fair, Neutral Tax System

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Monetary Fund has urged Pakistan to build a fair and neutral tax system, calling for broader coverage, stronger collection and fewer exemptions. IMF Regional Director Jihad Azour, speaking at a two-day tax conference in Islamabad, said the burden of taxation must be shared more evenly across sectors. He pointed out that the salaried class’s annual tax payments rose from Rs391 billion to Rs629 billion over the past two years, while retail, agriculture and real estate paid comparatively little.

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, who also addressed the conference, said Pakistan’s fiscal deficit is at a 22-year low. He added that government revenue has risen by 40 per cent and the tax-to-GDP ratio has climbed from 8.8 per cent to 10.3 per cent. The minister said the government is committed to continuing reforms to strengthen the tax system.
