Seven Everyday Foods That Help Keep Kidneys Healthy, Say Experts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Health experts say that a balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle can play an important role in keeping the kidneys functioning well. Among the foods they consider beneficial for kidney health are apples, berries and citrus fruits, along with vegetables such as cabbage, sweet potatoes, kale and cauliflower. Experts explain that these foods support the body's natural filtering processes.

They also stress the importance of limiting salt intake in daily meals. Excess sodium, they note, raises blood pressure, which in turn puts additional strain on the kidneys. Small dietary changes, combined with regular habits, can therefore help protect kidney health over time.
