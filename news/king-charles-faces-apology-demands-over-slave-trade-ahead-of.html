King Charles Faces Apology Demands Over Slave Trade Ahead of Guyana Visit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Demands for a formal apology for Britain's role in the transatlantic slave trade are growing ahead of King Charles's visit to Guyana next week. The King begins his Caribbean tour from the Bahamas on October 27. Guyana is the only former British colony on South American soil.

British officials reportedly fear that a formal apology could set a legal precedent triggering compensation claims. ITV reports that the King is unlikely to apologise but may acknowledge past injustices. Many Guyanese, however, want practical action and compensation instead of words.
