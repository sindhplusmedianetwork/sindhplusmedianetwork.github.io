US Sets Up 'Iron Gate' Task Force to Tighten Grip on Iran's Land Trade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States military has announced the establishment of a new task force to tighten restrictions on Iran. The task force, designated "Iron Gate", was announced on Saturday by US Central Command. It will work alongside the months-long naval blockade on Iran to restrict Tehran's movement of advanced weapons systems and other sanctioned material.

The task force will focus on Iran's land borders and the Caspian Sea. It will be based in Tampa, Florida, and will comprise about 20 civilian and military personnel. Central Command commander Admiral Brad Cooper announced the task force.

The move adds a land-based element to Washington's campaign of pressure on Tehran.
