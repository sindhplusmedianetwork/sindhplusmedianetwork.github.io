Unbeaten Pakistan Aim to Keep Winning Run Alive Against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Unbeaten Pakistan will look to maintain their winning momentum when they face Sri Lanka in the fifth match of the One-day International tri-series at the Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore on Tuesday. Pakistan have already secured their place in Saturday's final after winning all three of their matches, but the fixture offers another chance to assess combinations and address concerns over the top order before the title decider. Sri Lanka will be keen to strengthen their position after beating England by two wickets earlier in the series, and a victory on Tuesday would confirm their place in the final alongside Pakistan.

The hosts enter the contest after one of the tournament's most dramatic victories, chasing England's 242 after slumping to 154 for nine before Shadab Khan struck an unbeaten 113 off 96 balls to secure a one-wicket win. His unbroken 89-run stand with Haris Rauf set a record for the highest tenth-wicket partnership in a successful ODI chase. Saim Ayub, who scored six against England, has since been released from the squad at his own request to return to domestic cricket, with Sahibzada Farhan named as his replacement.

Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by 37 runs in their previous meeting in the series, with Salman Ali Agha scoring 107, and captain Shaheen Shah Afridi, who has taken seven wickets in three matches, will again lead the bowling attack.
