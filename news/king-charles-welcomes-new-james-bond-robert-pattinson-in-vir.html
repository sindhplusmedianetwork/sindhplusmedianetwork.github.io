King Charles welcomes new James Bond Robert Pattinson in viral message

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

The British royal family has welcomed Robert Pattinson as the new James Bond in its own distinctive style. A photo of the actor was shared on the official social media account of King Charles and Queen Camilla, with a caption reading: 'Welcome to the King's secret service, Mr Bond!' The message, posted after the announcement of Pattinson's selection as the new Agent 007, has gone viral, with social media users praising the royal family's playful and unusual approach. The royal family's connection with the James Bond series is not new: the late Queen Elizabeth II appeared in a memorable video sketch with Daniel Craig at the opening ceremony of the 2012 London Olympics, and members of the royal family also attended the premiere of 'No Time To Die' in 2021.

According to media reports, the new Bond film will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, known for films such as 'Dune', while Steven Knight, the creator of 'Peaky Blinders', will write the screenplay. Before Pattinson, Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig have played the famous role on the big screen.
