Only Murders in the Building Season 6 Trailer Takes Trio to London

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hulu has released the first trailer for Only Murders in the Building season 6, giving fans a glimpse of the mystery awaiting Charles, Oliver and Mabel as the beloved trio heads to London for their next investigation. The upcoming season sees the characters leave New York behind to investigate the murder of fellow true-crime podcaster Cinda Canning, and their latest case takes them to the British capital, where they encounter a new collection of suspicious characters and unexpected twists. Despite expecting an entirely different experience in London, the trio discovers that the city has more in common with their familiar surroundings than they initially imagined.

Steve Martin, Martin Short and Selena Gomez are returning to lead the comedy-mystery series, and the sixth season will also feature an impressive lineup of guest stars, including Tina Fey, Olivia Colman, Meryl Streep, David Tennant, Nicola Coughlan and Martin Freeman. According to the official season description, Cinda's murder becomes the trio's most challenging investigation yet, and the podcasters must navigate London's world of murder mysteries while attempting to follow in the footsteps of the woman whose work originally inspired their crime-solving adventures. The description also raises questions about whether Charles, Oliver and Mabel can uncover the truth without suffering the same fate as their latest victim.

Only Murders in the Building was created by Martin and John Hoffman, with Martin and Hoffman also serving as executive producers, and season 6 is scheduled to premiere on December 8, 2026, on Hulu.
