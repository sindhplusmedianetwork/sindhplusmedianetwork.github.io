FIA Arrests Three Suspects in Bank Default and Hundi-Hawala Cases in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) has arrested three suspects in Lahore in bank default and hundi-hawala cases. The FIA Commercial Banking Circle Lahore arrested Rana Shehryar in a Rs1 billion bank default case. Shehryar is a director of M/S Ashraf Poly (Pvt) Ltd and M/S Sameera Flour Mills (Pvt) Ltd.

FIRs 119, 120 and 121/2026 were registered against him on the complaint of a private bank. In a separate operation, the FIA raided an illegal hundi-hawala setup at Landa Bazaar in Lahore and arrested two suspects, Sher Muhammad and Daulat Khan. Officials recovered Rs8,385,000, mobile phones and hundi-hawala related messages from the suspects, and a case was registered against both under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act 1947.

An FIA spokesperson said further investigation of the arrested suspects is under way in both operations.
