PM Youth and Agriculture Loan Limit Raised to Rs25 Million, Age Limit Cut to 18

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The limit on personal loans under the Prime Minister's Youth Business and Agriculture Loan schemes has been raised from Rs7.5 million to Rs25 million. The age limit for applicants has been lowered from 21 years to 18 years, widening access to the schemes for younger borrowers. Thirty per cent of the loans will be reserved for women.

A total of Rs292 billion has been allocated this year for providing loans to young people under these schemes. The schemes are aimed at supporting young entrepreneurs and farmers seeking finance to start or expand their work.
