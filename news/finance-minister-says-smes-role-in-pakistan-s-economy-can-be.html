Finance minister says SMEs' role in Pakistan's economy can be expanded

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb told the SME Finance Forum 2026 in Singapore that small and medium enterprises make up 90 percent of Pakistan's businesses. They contribute 40 percent of GDP, about 20 percent of exports and 78 percent of non-agricultural jobs. He said the government aims to raise SME financing to Rs2,000 billion by June 2028.

He highlighted the SBP's SME Connect portal, launched on 1 November 2026, for business registration and access to finance. He stressed technology and data-based lending solutions beyond traditional collateral.
