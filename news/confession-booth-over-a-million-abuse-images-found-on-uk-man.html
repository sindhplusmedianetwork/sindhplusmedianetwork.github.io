'Confession booth': over a million abuse images found on UK man's phone, court hears

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Manchester court has heard that a man in his 60s who drugged and raped his wife over two decades had more than a million photographs and videos cataloguing the abuse on his phone. Prosecutors said the device functioned like a 'confession booth', with material going back to 2004. Nine men are on trial accused of sexual offences after the husband allegedly invited strangers to take part in the abuse.

The accused has pleaded guilty to about 60 offences. The prosecution said the images were systematically stored on the phone. The court heard the abuse spanned two decades.

The trial is continuing.
