Pakistan's Foreign Reserves Hit Record $21.5 Billion in First Quarter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's foreign exchange reserves have reached a record high of 21.5 billion dollars in the first quarter of the current fiscal year, according to the Ministry of Finance. The ministry's report on economic performance for July to September 2026 says reserves rose by 52 percent. Exports stood at 11.2 billion dollars and remittances at 10.9 billion dollars, while foreign direct investment improved to 720 million dollars.

Goods exports rose 8 percent to 8.5 billion dollars and services exports grew 23 percent to 2.7 billion dollars, with IT exports up 18 percent to 1.25 billion dollars. Freelancers' export earnings rose 46 percent to a record monthly high of 550 million dollars, and remittances from overseas Pakistanis rose 14 percent. The report says the Federal Board of Revenue's tax collection stood at 3.1 trillion rupees, surpassing the first-quarter target, while private sector credit reached 11.28 trillion rupees, up 16.6 percent year-on-year.

It adds that all three major global credit rating agencies have maintained Pakistan's rating in the B category, with a stable outlook.
