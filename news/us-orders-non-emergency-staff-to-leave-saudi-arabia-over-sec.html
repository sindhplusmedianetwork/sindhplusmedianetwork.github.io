US Orders Non-Emergency Staff to Leave Saudi Arabia Over Security Concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has directed its non-emergency government staff in Saudi Arabia to leave the kingdom over growing security concerns. The order, effective from October 23, also covers the family members of non-emergency staff posted at the US embassy. According to reports citing the embassy, the decision was taken over fears of possible attacks by Houthi militias on airports, civilian sites and other key installations.

The move marks a significant security advisory for one of America's closest partners in the region. Non-emergency staff have been instructed to depart Saudi Arabia as a precautionary measure. The decision comes amid heightened regional tensions and continued threats to critical infrastructure across the Gulf.
