If Cricket Routes Can Open, Why Not for Religious Festivals, Asks Sikh Body

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has completed arrangements to receive Sikh pilgrims through the Kartarpur Corridor for Baba Guru Nanak's 557th birth anniversary on November 24. India has yet to decide on restoring the corridor, which it suspended after the Pahalgam attack in May 2025. The Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee and Indian Punjab's assembly have asked New Delhi why cricket ties can resume while religious access remains blocked.

Their question has put the focus on the contrast between the reopening of cricket links and the continued closure of the corridor for pilgrims. Pilgrims are expected to start arriving from November 22. Pakistan's preparations are complete, and the pilgrims' arrival now depends on India's decision.
