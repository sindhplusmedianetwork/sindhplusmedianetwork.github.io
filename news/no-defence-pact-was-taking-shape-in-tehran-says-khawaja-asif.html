No defence pact was taking shape in Tehran, says Khawaja Asif in reply to Fazl

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif on Thursday responded in the National Assembly to Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman's remarks about his non-participation in a recent huddle in Tehran. Fazl had said a day earlier that 'a defence pact is taking shape in Iran, a meeting is under way (there), and the defence minister is sitting here', also questioning why Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi was present there. Replying on Thursday, Asif said no defence pact was taking shape in Tehran and that the discussion was not focused on Pakistan's defence or any related matter.

He said the meeting had brought together Saudi Defence Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman, Pakistan's chief of defence forces Field Marshal Asim Munir and Turkish intelligence chief Ibrahim Kalin with Iranian officials, and that the group agreed to continue dialogue to bolster cooperation among the four nations. Asif said the huddle was 'not the defence ministry's mandate' and that he was therefore 'not needed in Tehran', adding that the chief of defence forces had gone there 'as a messenger of peace and paved the ground' for the talks. He said Field Marshal Munir had assumed a role for Pakistan of 'peace and dialogue' following last year's military conflict with India, and had helped avert a confrontation over the Yemen issue, with peace possibly emerging 'in the coming days or week'.

Asif advised Fazl to be the bigger person, calling his criticism 'misplaced'.
