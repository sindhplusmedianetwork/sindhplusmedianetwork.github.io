Balochistan Train Service Restored After One-Day Suspension

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Train service in Balochistan resumed after a one-day suspension, Pakistan Railways officials said. The suspension had brought train operations in the province to a halt for a day. With the resumption, the Jaffar Express departed from Quetta for Peshawar.

The Jaffar Express travelling in the opposite direction, from Peshawar, was due to reach Quetta the same evening. The Jinnah People's Train also returned to service. That train runs between Saryab and Kuchlak.

Officials confirmed that services on these routes were running again after the brief suspension.
