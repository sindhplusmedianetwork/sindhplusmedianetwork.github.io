Pakistan Stock Exchange Opens Week on a Positive Note, KSE-100 Up 1,560 Points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Trading at the Pakistan Stock Exchange began in the green zone on the first business day of the week. The benchmark KSE-100 index rose 1,560 points at the open to cross 179,400 points. Investors welcomed the strong start after the market had also closed the previous week in positive territory.

Last week, the 100-index ended 500 points higher at 177,873 points. The positive opening set the tone for the new trading week at the exchange.
