Democrats Win Biggest House Majority in 34 Years

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Democrats in the United States House of Representatives have won their largest majority in 34 years, the biggest since 1992. Democrat candidate Chuck Hubbard defeated Republican candidate Virginia Foxx in North Carolina, a result that took the Democrats' tally in the House to 258 seats. The outcome marks a significant shift in the balance of power in the lower chamber.

In the midterm elections, the Democrats also secured control of the Senate, giving the party control of both houses. The result is being seen as a major political development in the United States. It strengthens the Democrats' position in shaping legislation in the period ahead.

The newly elected members are expected to take their seats as the new session begins.
