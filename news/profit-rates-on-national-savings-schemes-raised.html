Profit Rates on National Savings Schemes Raised

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has raised the profit rates for national savings schemes and Sarwa Islamic schemes. The National Savings Department announced the new profit rates on national savings schemes, effective from October 21, 2026. According to details, Special Savings Certificates and accounts will carry 12 percent annually on the first five profits and 12.8 percent on the sixth and final profit, giving Rs6,000 and Rs6,400 respectively every six months on Rs100,000.

Defence Savings Certificates will pay 10 percent for one year, 21 percent for two years, 34 percent for three years, 49 percent for four years, 67 percent for five years, 88 percent for six years, 113 percent for seven years, 142 percent for eight years, 176 percent for nine years and 215 percent over ten years. Regular Income Certificates will pay Rs1,010 per month on Rs100,000, or 12.12 percent annually. Under Behbood Savings Certificates for widows, senior citizens and persons with disabilities, the profit will be Rs1,080 per month on Rs100,000, or 12.96 percent annually, with investment limits of Rs5,000 to Rs7.5 million.

Pensioners Benefit Accounts for retired government employees will also pay Rs1,080 per month on Rs100,000, with limits of Rs10,000 to Rs7.5 million. The Shuhada Family Welfare Account for martyrs' families will likewise pay Rs1,080 per month on Rs100,000, or 12.96 percent annually. Short Term Savings Certificates will pay 11.12 percent annually for three months, 11.50 percent for six months and 11.72 percent for one year.

The savings account rate was left unchanged at 10 percent annually, while Sarwa Islamic term and savings accounts carry expected annual rates of up to 12.12 percent. Withholding tax, zakat and service charges will apply under the rules of each scheme.
