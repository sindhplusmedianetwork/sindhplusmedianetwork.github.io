Karachi Reports First Polio Case of the Year; National Tally Reaches Seven

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi has reported its first polio case of the year, the National Emergency Operations Centre confirmed on Monday. The case was detected in the city's East area, taking Pakistan's 2026 tally to seven cases — five from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and two from Sindh. The EOC noted that Karachi's last reported case was in December 2024.

A nationwide anti-polio campaign is scheduled from November 2 to 8, 2026, covering 97 districts including all of Karachi. The campaign will target more than 23.9 million children under the age of five. The EOC urged parents to make sure their children receive polio drops in every campaign and complete the routine immunisation course on time.

Health officials continue to warn that the virus remains a threat in parts of the country despite years of eradication efforts.
