Governor Sindh says not personally in favour of emergency, defends KP move

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Governor Nihal Hashmi has said he is personally not in favour of imposing an emergency in the country. Addressing members of the Pakistani community in Toronto, he said he wanted to see a democratic government in place. However, he added that the emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa had to be imposed to stop those who facilitate the enemies of the state.

The governor said the emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa was imposed in accordance with the constitution. His remarks come amid an ongoing debate over the emergency measures in the province. Hashmi's comments mark a rare public statement by the governor on the issue.
