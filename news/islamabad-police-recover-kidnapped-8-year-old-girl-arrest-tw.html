Islamabad Police Recover Kidnapped 8-Year-Old Girl, Arrest Two Suspects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad Capital Territory Police successfully recovered an eight-year-old kidnapped girl and arrested two suspects involved in her abduction within a short time during a swift operation conducted by Nilore Police Station, reuniting the child safely with her parents. Deputy Inspector General Islamabad Ali Waseem took immediate notice of the kidnapping incident and directed police to ensure the girl's safe recovery and the arrest of those responsible. Following the directions, special police teams were formed under the supervision of Superintendent of Police Rural Zeeshan Ali to trace the suspects and recover the abducted child.

The Station House Officer Nilore, along with his team, used modern technical methods and human intelligence to trace and arrest the two suspects within a short period. During the operation, police safely recovered the eight-year-old girl from the suspects' custody and handed her over to her parents. An ICT Police official said further investigation was under way to examine all aspects of the kidnapping incident and determine the circumstances surrounding the abduction.

DIG Islamabad Ali Waseem said the arrested suspects would be presented before the court with solid evidence upon completion of the investigation, adding that all legal requirements would be fulfilled to ensure those responsible were brought to justice and awarded punishment in accordance with the law.
