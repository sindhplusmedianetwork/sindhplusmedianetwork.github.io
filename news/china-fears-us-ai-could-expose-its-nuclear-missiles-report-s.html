China Fears US AI Could Expose Its Nuclear Missiles, Report Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China fears the United States could use advanced artificial intelligence to track and destroy its nuclear missiles and launchers, according to a New York Times report. Chinese military researchers cited in the report said AI could cut US nuclear decision-making from 15 to 30 minutes to 90 seconds or less. That speed could weaken Beijing's ability to retaliate after a nuclear attack.

China holds about 620 nuclear warheads, a number expected to reach 1,000 by 2030. It has also built about 320 underground missile silos in its northern deserts. The report says Beijing worries that AI-driven tracking could expose those weapons to attack.
