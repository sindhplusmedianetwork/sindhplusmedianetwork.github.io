Four militants killed in Quetta, Gwadar operations; army major martyred

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces killed four militants described as 'Fitna-e-Hindustan' in intelligence-based operations in Quetta and Gwadar districts of Balochistan, the Inter-Services Public Relations said. The operation in the Jattakabad area of Quetta was conducted on October 18. Separately, during a clearance operation in the Shadi Kaur area of Pasni tehsil in Gwadar, Major Wajahat Waheed was martyred in the blast of an improvised explosive device.

The 34-year-old major hailed from Lahore. The army said clearance operations continued in the areas to eliminate remaining threats.
