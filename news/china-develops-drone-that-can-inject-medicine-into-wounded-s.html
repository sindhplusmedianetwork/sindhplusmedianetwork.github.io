China develops drone that can inject medicine into wounded soldiers from a distance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China's Air Force Medical University has developed what it describes as the military's first casualty-search drone, and has made progress in using unmanned aircraft to inject medication into wounded personnel from a distance. State broadcaster CCTV reported on Wednesday that the drone can locate injured soldiers and deliver emergency medicine remotely. 'Wherever our drone can fly, we can deliver life-saving emergency medication into a wounded person's muscle, buying valuable time for treatment,' Wang Jianqi, a representative of the university, was quoted as saying.

Footage accompanying the report showed a drone releasing a dart towards a soldier lying on the ground. Military commentator Song Zhongping said the changing nature of modern warfare has made battlefield medical rescue increasingly difficult. According to Wang, air-ground unmanned treatment remains at an exploratory stage, and researchers are still working to overcome key difficulties, including how to retrieve and load wounded personnel onto drones for evacuation.
