Trump Signs Order Creating White House Task Force on Women's Health

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Donald Trump on Monday signed an executive order creating a White House task force and a new National Institutes of Health division focused on women's health. The signing took place in the White House Rose Garden alongside First Lady Melania Trump during Breast Cancer Awareness Month. The task force will be led by White House chief of staff Susie Wiles, who recently completed breast cancer treatment, and Health Secretary Robert F.

Kennedy Jr. The order directs a comprehensive federal approach to women's health across all life stages, strengthening research to speed prevention, diagnosis and treatment. It instructs the Department of Health and Human Services to establish the new women's health division within the NIH.

The division will work alongside the NIH Office of Research on Women's Health, which was established in 1990, rather than replace it.
