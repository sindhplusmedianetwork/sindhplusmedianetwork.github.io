Mashal Yousafzai Says Noreen Niazi in ICU, Demands Release of Imran's Sisters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Senator Mashal Yousafzai has said that Imran Khan's sister Noreen Niazi is presently in the intensive care unit, and demanded an end to the detention of the PTI founder's sisters. In a post on X, she said the party spent the entire day in parliament attempting to arrange a meeting between Noreen and her daughter, so that she could see her mother and the family could be informed about her health. She stated that the PTI would not leave its leader's sisters without support.

The party, she added, will continue to raise its voice for their release. Her statement adds to the growing chorus of opposition voices raising the issue of the sisters' custody.
