PMD forecasts dry weather across most of Pakistan on Tuesday, cold in upper regions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Meteorological Department has forecast mainly dry weather across most parts of the country on Tuesday. Cold conditions are expected in the upper regions, while strong afternoon winds may blow in some areas. Isolated fog and smog are likely in the morning and at night in parts of Punjab, including Lahore, Sialkot and Multan.

The department said Leh recorded the lowest temperature of minus 9 degrees Celsius during the past 24 hours. The dry spell is expected to continue across most of the country, with no major rain system in sight. People in the upper areas have been advised to prepare for cold weather as winter conditions set in.
