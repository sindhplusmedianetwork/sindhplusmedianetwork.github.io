Macron Arrives in UAE on Gulf Tour Focused on Hormuz and Energy Security

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French President Emmanuel Macron reached the United Arab Emirates on an official visit and was accorded a formal welcome at the airport by President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan. The national anthems of both countries were played and a guard of honour presented a salute on the occasion. Macron's tour of the Gulf states, which also takes in Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar from October 19 to 21, carries significance against the backdrop of growing regional tensions and strain in global energy markets.

A key aim of the visit is consultation with regional leaders on restoring freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz and securing energy supply routes. France also wants to deepen cooperation with Gulf countries in defence, security, trade and investment. Talks with the UAE leadership are expected to cover new technology and regional stability as well.

Meanwhile, Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov also arrived in Tehran as Gulf states intensify diplomatic efforts to ease tensions and keep global oil flows normal.
