PM Shehbaz chairs Food Security Ministry review, warns against delays

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif chaired a review meeting on the performance of the Ministry of National Food Security under PMOS at the Prime Minister's House in Islamabad on Thursday. He directed that PMOS be made more effective and coordination among institutions increased. He said no delay in given targets and timelines would be tolerated.

He said modern research holds a key place in strengthening the economy and sustainable agricultural development. The government would provide full patronage and financial support for research, he said. Easy provision of modern agricultural machinery to farmers was a top priority, he added.

The meeting was briefed that the Mera Kisan digital application has been launched for farmer registration, a National Agriculture and Food Security Advisory Council has been formed, and work on improving crop insurance is under way.
