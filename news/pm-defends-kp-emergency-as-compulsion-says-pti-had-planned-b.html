PM Defends KP Emergency as 'Compulsion', Says PTI Had Planned Bloodshed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Saturday defended the federal government's decision to impose an emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, insisting it was enforced 'under compulsion'. Speaking in Gujranwala, where he had gone to condole with Rashid Bashir Virk over the death of Mehmood Bashir Virk, the prime minister said the PTI's past history was 'very horrific' and that the party had this time too planned bloodshed. He revealed that during negotiations it had been agreed that the PTI would withdraw its long-march call, but the commitment was not honoured.

The premier said he had met former KP chief minister Sohail Afridi more than once, discussed the province's development with him, and assured him the federal government would provide whatever resources the province needed. He noted that terrorism emanating from Afghanistan continued unabated and that defeating it required everyone to work together, adding that the country had achieved economic stability. Separately, PML-N leader Tariq Fazal Chaudhry termed JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman's linking of the emergency to a bid to capture mineral resources 'regrettable', saying terrorism was a national issue that should not be sacrificed to political point-scoring.

On party matters, the prime minister said there was never a moment when any name other than Nawaz Sharif's was considered for the party's top nomination, describing Nawaz's thinking, experience and bond with the people as his political strength.
