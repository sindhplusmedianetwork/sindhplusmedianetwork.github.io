Bilawal calls for 'political reset', urges PM to rebuild trust with allies and opposition

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has called for a 'political reset' in which all political parties unite, urging Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to first restore trust with allies and then with the opposition. He was speaking at an event marking the completion of one million houses for flood affectees under the Sindh Flood Resilience Project. Bilawal said the housing project was the largest house-building programme in the world.

He said the country faced a severe economic crisis linked to the US-Iran war. He said political parties must work together rather than fight each other. He urged the prime minister to rebuild trust with allies first and then extend the same effort to the opposition.
