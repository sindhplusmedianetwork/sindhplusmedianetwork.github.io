Fazlur Rehman Clarifies He Criticised Policies, Not Institutions, After Ministers Allege He Sought 'Share' in KP Rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman on Saturday clarified that his party's criticism of the country's security situation was directed at policies, not institutions, and described the allegations levelled against him by federal ministers as character assassination. A day earlier, Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi had claimed, without naming anyone, that a 'head of a party' was 'extremely upset' about not being appointed the governor of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Earlier on Saturday, State Minister for Interior Tallal Chaudhry rebuked the JUI-F chief for reportedly seeking his 'share' in KP's governance after the imposition of governor's rule and emergency, while Defence Minister Khawaja Asif also criticised him in Sialkot.

Addressing a press conference in Lahore, Fazl stressed that the criticism was intended to bring about corrective action rather than undermine sacrifices made in the fight against terrorism, and called for an in-camera session of Parliament to review the security situation and formulate a consensus-based counterterrorism policy. 'Our only question is why the country has failed to eliminate terrorism,' he said, asking why decisions were taken in Rawalpindi while Parliament was simply told to follow them. He reiterated his objection to the emergency in KP, recalled his father's resignation when emergency was imposed in Balochistan in the 1970s, and noted that around 250 of his party workers had lost their lives to terrorist violence.

The Centre imposed governor's rule and emergency in KP on Oct 11, citing the provincial government's 'deliberate disengagement' from the fight against terrorism.
