TikToker Mr Patlu arrested for allegedly threatening family of stampede victim

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Well-known TikToker Ansar Mehmood, known as Mr Patlu, has been arrested on charges of allegedly threatening the family of a young man who died in a stampede at a mehndi ceremony. According to police, Mr Patlu was arrested under a new case registered at Sadar Daska police station in Sialkot. Abubakar, the brother-in-law of the deceased young man Muhammad Rehan, alleged that four armed men came to their home and pressured the family to reconcile in the case and to give a statement in favour of the accused.

He alleged that when the family refused, they were also threatened with death. It may be recalled that on October 30, a stampede at Mr Patlu's mehndi ceremony killed two people and injured several others.
