Pink Cart Bazaar Set Up in Kabirwala for Skilled Women

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Pink Cart Bazaar has been set up in Kabirwala to make women financially independent and provide them with better employment opportunities. On the instructions of the Punjab government, the district government set up the bazaar at Chowk Makhdoom Pur Chungi on Jhang Road. The aim of the bazaar is to provide business opportunities to domestic and skilled women so that they can turn their skills into income.

Women shopkeepers running businesses at the Pink Cart Bazaar say the initiative gives them a chance to take part in economic activity and increase their families' income. The women welcomed the step, saying such opportunities help them stand on their own feet and become economically self-reliant. Officials said similar markets for women are being planned in other districts as well.
