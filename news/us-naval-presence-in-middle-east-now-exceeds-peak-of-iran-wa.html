US naval presence in Middle East now exceeds peak of Iran war, says Israeli institute

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The current United States naval presence in the Middle East exceeds the level recorded during the peak of the war on Iran in February and March, an Israeli security institute said on Tuesday. The Institute for National Security Studies said it compiled open-source data on the current number of US forces and naval vessels deployed in the Gulf, the Arabian Sea, the Red Sea and the Mediterranean. It said the current naval presence exceeds the size of the forces deployed during Operation Epic Fury, providing greater offensive capabilities and broader missile-interception capacity.

According to an interactive map published by the institute, the aircraft carriers George Washington, George H.W. Bush and Theodore Roosevelt are currently deployed in the region, along with several other military vessels. The institute did not provide a figure for the current number of US forces or naval vessels deployed in the region.

Despite the presence of more than 200 aerial refuelling aircraft, US air power stationed at land bases remains below March levels, with aircraft repositioned farther west or transferred to Europe. The institute noted that aircraft can be redeployed much more quickly than naval forces, so the balance of air power in the region could change rapidly.
