DG ISPR 'Cautiously Optimistic' on Pakistan's US-Iran Mediation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Director General of Inter-Services Public Relations, Lt Gen Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry, has said Pakistan is 'cautiously optimistic' about its mediation efforts between Iran and the United States. The remarks came in his first interview since a high-level Pakistani delegation visited Tehran earlier this week. He noted that Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir has visited Tehran twice this month, most recently on Monday alongside Saudi and Turkish officials.

The DG ISPR also said the Makkah defence pact does not bar new members and affirmed Pakistan's commitment to defending Saudi Arabia. He described Pakistan's role as one of facilitating dialogue and reducing tensions in the region. The interview marks the first public comment by the military's media chief on the behind-the-scenes diplomacy between Washington and Tehran.
