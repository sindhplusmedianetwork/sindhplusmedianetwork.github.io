Lawyers Attack Suspect in Multan Murder Case of 15-Year-Old Worker

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chaotic scenes were witnessed at a Multan court on Tuesday when angry lawyers attacked the central suspect in the murder of 15-year-old worker Wasim Abbas during his court appearance. The court approved a four-day physical remand of the suspect. Police rescued the accused from the lawyers with great difficulty and shifted him to custody under tight security, with police themselves becoming the complainant in the attack case.

According to police, the contractor allegedly crushed the orphan boy under a vehicle when the teenager demanded his pending wages. One accomplice remains at large and a search is under way. The brutal killing of the young worker had sparked outrage, with labour rights activists demanding swift justice.
