US envoy Witkoff arrives in St Petersburg to meet Putin

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US special envoy Steve Witkoff arrived in Saint Petersburg early Wednesday to meet Russian President Vladimir Putin, following what Secretary of State Marco Rubio called 'positive' talks between Witkoff and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky in Oslo. Zelensky thanked Washington on Tuesday for resuming US intelligence sharing on Russian strikes, calling early warnings 'very, very important'. A US official said Trump's son-in-law Jared Kushner would take part in the Putin meeting virtually.

Witkoff was received at Pulkovo Airport by Kremlin adviser Kirill Dmitriev. President Trump said on Tuesday that he had asked Putin to release Stephen Hubbard, an American captive held by Russian forces for years. Trump also said he wants Zelensky to 'make a deal' with Russia and is ready to meet Putin and China's Xi Jinping next month to discuss denuclearisation and Ukraine.

Zelensky insisted that Kyiv must be at the table for any meeting about Ukraine's future.
