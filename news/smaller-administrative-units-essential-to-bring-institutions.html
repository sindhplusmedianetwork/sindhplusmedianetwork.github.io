Smaller Administrative Units Essential to Bring Institutions to Doorsteps, Says CM Bugti

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti has said that smaller administrative units are essential to bring state institutions to people's doorsteps. Speaking in northern Dera Bugti, he argued that along with new provinces, Balochistan needs new divisions and districts. The chief minister announced the approval of all divisional posts for the Koh-e-Suleman Division, adding that officers and staff would be deployed on them soon.

He praised the security forces for foiling the recent Gwadar attack. Bugti said terrorism incidents in Balochistan have declined since August, crediting the sacrifices of security personnel. He reiterated his government's commitment to improving governance through administrative reforms.
