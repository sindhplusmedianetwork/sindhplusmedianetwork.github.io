Notification giving Dr Akhtar Abbas additional charge as Pharmacy Council secretary declared illegal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has declared illegal the notification giving Dr Muhammad Akhtar Abbas Khan the additional charge of secretary of the Pharmacy Council. Justice Arbab Muhammad Tahir ruled on a petition by Fazalullah Farooq that the January 15, 2026 notification was issued without following the rules. The court noted that the Pharmacy Act 1967 requires federal government approval and specific professional qualifications for the secretary's appointment.

The court also noted that under the 2023 service by-laws, additional charge of a vacant post can only be given to an employee holding an equivalent post in the council. Dr Abbas is an employee of DRAP, not the Pharmacy Council, the court observed.
