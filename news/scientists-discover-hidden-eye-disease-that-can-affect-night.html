Scientists Discover Hidden Eye Disease That Can Affect Night Vision

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Scientists have identified a new genetic eye disease that can gradually affect the ability to see at night and to see objects at the edges of the visual field. The disease is hard to identify because affected people retain good vision for most of their lives and clear symptoms appear only with age. An international team of researchers, including scientists from the Scheie Eye Institute at the University of Pennsylvania's Perelman School of Medicine, linked the disease to a gene previously associated with another vision disease.

The new disease affects peripheral vision and vision in the dark, unlike the earlier-known disease which mainly damages central vision. Researchers are calling the condition EFEMP1-associated disease, after the gene involved. The discovery could help doctors identify the condition earlier and develop treatments to protect patients' sight.
