Pakistani astronauts complete space training in China, says SUPARCO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani astronauts Muhammad Zeeshan Ali and Khurram Daud have completed their space training in China, the Pakistan Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission (SUPARCO) said. SUPARCO said the achievement will prove an important milestone for Pakistan's space programme and the Pakistan-China partnership. Pakistan's first astronaut will travel to space aboard China's Shenzhou-24 mission, and one of the two trained candidates will fly on the mission.

The Chinese space agency has officially released the logo of the Shenzhou-24 mission, which includes Pakistan's national flower, the jasmine. According to SUPARCO, the Pakistani astronaut is scheduled to launch into space on November 25. The mission will be the first joint space flight of Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China's human space programme.
