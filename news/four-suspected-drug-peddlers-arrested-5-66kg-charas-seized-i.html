Four Suspected Drug Peddlers Arrested, 5.66kg Charas Seized In Nowshera Virkan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police arrested four alleged drug peddlers and recovered 5.66kg of charas during four separate operations in different areas of Nowshera Virkan. According to police, the crackdown was conducted on Tuesday under the supervision of DSP Anser Farooq Mann and SHO Ziafat Bhath. Sub-Inspector Shafqat Abbas recovered 1,520 grams of charas from Muhammad Yaseen at Babar Chowk, while ASI Muhammad Nawaz seized 820 grams from Muhammad Imran at Chand Colony Chowk.

Similarly, ASI Waheed Ahmad recovered 1,680 grams of charas from Muhammad Asghar at Khand Peer Chowk, while Sub-Inspector Waheed Abbas seized 1,640 grams from Rana Afzal Ali at Adda Jagowal. Police registered separate cases against all four suspects and launched further investigations.
