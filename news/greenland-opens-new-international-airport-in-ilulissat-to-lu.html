Greenland opens new international airport in Ilulissat to lure tourists

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Greenland has opened a new international airport in Ilulissat in a bid to attract foreign tourists and give its economy a major lift. The first direct international flight touched down from Copenhagen on October 29, carrying 169 passengers, including Greenland's Prime Minister Jens-Frederik Nielsen and Denmark's Finance Minister Peter Hummelgaard. The airport features a 2,200-metre runway and a terminal of about 5,000 square metres, allowing large passenger aircraft to reach Ilulissat directly for the first time.

Officials say the new gateway will open up Greenland's western coast — famous for its dramatic ice fjords — to international visitors like never before. The project is also being seen as a significant step in Greenland's long-term efforts toward future independence from Denmark. Tourism authorities expect the direct connections to bring a steady rise in visitors and new investment to the Arctic territory.
