Nadia Hussain Says Dar-e-Nijaat's Zaryab Needs Therapy, Not a Husband

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Model-turned-TV host Nadia Hussain has called Dar-e-Nijaat character Zaryab, played by Durefishan Saleem, 'a complete red flag' in a video posted on her Instagram on Thursday. Hussain said Zaryab did not need to get married to a man to fix her life. She said the solution to the problems facing women like her does not lie in marriage, adding: 'she needs therapy.' Hussain also spoke about Sheheryar Munawar's character Abu Bakar, saying he deserves a better wife and a balanced relationship.

She urged people not to 'promote toxic relationships'. Her remarks have added to the discussion around the drama and the way its characters and relationships are shown.
