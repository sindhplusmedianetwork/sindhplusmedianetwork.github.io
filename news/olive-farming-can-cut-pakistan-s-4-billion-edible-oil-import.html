Olive Farming Can Cut Pakistan's $4 Billion Edible Oil Import Bill, Say Experts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Expanding olive cultivation in Pakistan could significantly reduce the country's $4 billion annual edible oil import bill, experts said on Friday. The call came at an international workshop hosted by the University of Agriculture Faisalabad. UAF Vice-Chancellor Dr Zulfiqar Ali said Pakistan had launched its National Olive Value Chain Policy in collaboration with Italy.

Italian experts at the workshop shared modern high-density orchard techniques to boost productivity. The policy aims to build a complete value chain from cultivation to oil extraction. Officials hope the initiative will cut reliance on imported edible oils over time.
