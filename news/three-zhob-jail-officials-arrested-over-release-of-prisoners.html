Three Zhob jail officials arrested over release of prisoners on forged court orders

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

QUETTA: The Crime Branch Quetta has arrested three officials of Zhob Jail, including its superintendent, over the alleged release of two convicted prisoners on the basis of forged Supreme Court orders. Those arrested are Jail Superintendent Habibullah, Deputy Superintendent Muhammad Rafiq and jail clerk Sada Gul. According to prison department officials, the two prisoners, Abdul Salam and Amanullah, were serving sentences in narcotics cases when they were released on May 30 last year after the jail authorities received release orders purportedly issued by the Supreme Court.

Departmental investigations ordered by Inspector General Prisons Asim Khan led to cases being registered against the three officials, and the matter was subsequently handed over to the Crime Branch for further investigation. Crime Branch officials have now arrested all three accused over their alleged role in the unlawful release of the convicts. Officials said further investigation into the case was continuing.
