Punjab bans outdoor drone use for 30 days over security concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Home Department has issued a notification banning the flying of drones in open spaces across the province for 30 days under Section 144. The ban was imposed on security grounds, according to a spokesperson for the department. Small drones will be allowed for limited use inside halls and marquees to cover events, but the organisers will bear full responsibility for their safe use indoors.

Intelligence and law-enforcement agencies have been exempted from the ban. The department has formed Safe Drone Usage Committees at the district level, and written permission from the relevant committee will be required for the safe use of drones for agriculture, irrigation and research purposes. Drones used with that written permission will also be exempt from the ban.

Punjab Home Secretary imposed the ban under Section 144(6) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898, and the department has directed district administrations and police across the province to ensure strict implementation of the orders.
