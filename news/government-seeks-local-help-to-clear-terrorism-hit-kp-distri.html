Government seeks local help to clear terrorism-hit KP districts within three months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Peace can be restored in the restive areas of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa within three months if members of the Area Representative and Development Council, along with local residents, succeed in convincing terrorists to leave their regions, spokesperson Mehmood Jan Babar said at a news conference at the Peshawar police headquarters on Wednesday. He said the prime minister had told council members that he did not want them to fight the terrorists, but to tell them to leave their areas, and that militancy would never return if they succeeded. Babar said Afghan soil was continuously being used to carry out terrorist activities against Pakistan, with attackers crossing over under the cover of darkness to plan attacks.

Additional Inspector General Abbas Ahsan, who also attended the news conference, said security forces had arrested an Afghan national, Hikmatullah alias Hamza, during an intelligence-based operation in the Tirah valley on November 7, while he was planning an attack on a security checkpost. He said the detainee was a former member of the Afghan National Army who later taught at a seminary in Jalalabad, joined terrorist outfits, trained a suicide bomber and was associated with the banned Hafiz Gul Bahadur Group, and had confessed to involvement in terrorist activities in Pakistan. Ahsan said the detainee belonged to Mangyar village in the Chamkani area of Afghanistan's Paktia province and had crossed into Pakistan.

He added that a special funding package had been prepared for the 12 designated districts, with 40 per cent to be spent on building police capacity, and that anti-drone systems were being installed, with long-range systems already going up in Lakki Marwat, Bannu and Dera Ismail Khan.
