At least 300 schools in Italy evacuated after hoax bomb threat emails

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 300 schools across Italy were evacuated on Wednesday after receiving identical hoax bomb threat emails. The threats are the latest in a series of threats and cyberattacks reported across Europe. Schools in Scotland, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland received similar emails but were advised to stay open, as police there classed them as hoaxes.

Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said there was no indication of concrete danger. Police are investigating the source of the emails.
