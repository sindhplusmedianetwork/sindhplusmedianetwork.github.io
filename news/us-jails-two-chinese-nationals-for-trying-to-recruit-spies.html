US jails two Chinese nationals for trying to recruit spies

LOS ANGELES: Two Chinese men who admitted trying to recruit US military service members to spy for Beijing were imprisoned in California on Friday. Liren Lai and Yuance Chen were ordered to serve 30 months in a federal lockup and to undergo three years of supervised release after pleading guilty. Prosecutors said the pair attempted to enlist American service members to pass sensitive information to China.

The sentencing marks the latest in a series of US espionage cases involving alleged Chinese intelligence recruitment efforts. Both men had admitted their roles in the scheme before the court. The case underscores Washington's continued focus on countering foreign intelligence operations on its soil.
