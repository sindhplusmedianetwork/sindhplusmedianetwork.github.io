Gas Leak Blast at Islamabad CNG Station Kills One, Injures Three

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

One person was killed and three others were injured in an explosion triggered by a gas leak at a CNG station near Golra Mor on Peshawar Road in Islamabad on Friday. Rescue teams reached the scene soon after the blast and shifted the dead and the injured to hospital. Firefighters of Rescue 1122 Rawalpindi brought the fire under control, with ten emergency vehicles and 25 personnel taking part in the operation.

Rescue officials said the exact cause of the fire was not yet known and an investigation was underway. The blast spread panic in the busy commercial area near Golra Mor. Authorities cordoned off the site and cleared the area as a precaution.
