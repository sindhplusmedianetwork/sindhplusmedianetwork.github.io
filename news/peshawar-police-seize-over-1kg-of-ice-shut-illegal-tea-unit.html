Peshawar Police Seize Over 1kg of Ice, Shut Illegal Tea Unit in Separate Raids

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police and the district administration in Peshawar arrested four people in separate operations on Thursday, seizing over one kilogram of ice, or crystal methamphetamine, and a large quantity of suspected adulterated tea leaves. In the first operation, Hayatabad police arrested a suspected drug smuggler, Irfanullah, and recovered 1,010 grams of ice from his possession along with his motorcycle. A police team led by SHO Hayatabad Taimur Saleem Khan, under the supervision of DSP Raza Bacha Khan, intercepted the suspect within the jurisdiction of Hayatabad police station.

In a separate raid, officials shut an illegal tea-processing unit operating inside a house in Sethi Town, arresting three members of an alleged adulteration gang. The officials recovered a large quantity of suspicious material, henna dye, food colours, molasses and processed tea leaves suspected to be harmful to human health.
