Uproar at Daler Mehndi Concert Over Lip-Syncing Allegations

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A performance by Indian singer Daler Mehndi in Uttarakhand descended into uproar after the artist was accused of lip-syncing. Mehndi was performing at a fair in Narendra Nagar when fans and officials present noticed him mouthing along to a pre-recorded track instead of singing live. The situation turned tense, and special guest and state cabinet minister Subodh Uniyal took the stage to criticise the performance.

A heated exchange followed before police and organisers brought the venue under control. Organisers said Mehndi had been invited as a replacement just two days earlier after singer Sunanda Sharma fell ill. Mehndi said illness had prevented him from singing live, and he later performed part of the show live, which satisfied the fans.
