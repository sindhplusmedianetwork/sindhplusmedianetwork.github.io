US Army plans to destroy 5 tons of explosive chemicals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The US Army has planned to destroy by explosion approximately five tons of chemicals used in making bombs in the state of Iowa, on which the local population has expressed concerns about increased air pollution. Officials have instructed citizens to vacate the area around the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant located in Middletown, where this operation will be carried out, and people unable to leave the area have been instructed to stay inside homes and keep windows and doors closed. The explosive material had been stored at the military installation for more than the last two decades, however during a safety review at the beginning of this year it was revealed that its condition had deteriorated.

According to officials, as a result of this operation there is a risk of loud explosion sounds, ground vibration, and a dust cloud containing lead particles rising. In view of the risk of potentially harmful particles spreading in the air, officials of the state of Illinois are also monitoring the situation. Local citizens are angry at this decision of the army and are concerned about long-term environmental pollution and health risks, while health department officials say lead effects are especially dangerous for young children.
