Ideological differences should not reach a point where people cannot even greet each other, says Khawaja Asif

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has said that when Maulana Fazlur Rehman spoke about him, he replied in kind. He warned that if a fixed game is played, the price will have to be paid by us, not by Maulana. Speaking to a private television channel, Khawaja Asif said that ideological differences have their place, but bitterness should not reach a level where people are not even willing to exchange greetings.

He added that there should be no foul play even in ideological differences. Khawaja Asif said that the PTI poisoned the political atmosphere in four years. He said he has never played a fixed game and is not a player of such games.

He added that he does not think the government will close the cases it has opened. He said that if such a fixed game is played, taking things first to one extreme and then to the other, the price will have to be paid by us.
