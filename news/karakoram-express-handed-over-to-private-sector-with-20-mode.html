Karakoram Express Handed Over to Private Sector With 20 Modern Coaches

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Karakoram Express has been handed over to the private sector. The train now runs with 20 coaches equipped with modern travel facilities. It offers AC sleeper, AC business, AC standard and economy class options for passengers.

The service operates between Lahore and Karachi, departing in the afternoon. The move is part of a wider effort to bring private investment into Pakistan Railways. Officials expect the upgraded service to offer passengers more comfort and better punctuality.
