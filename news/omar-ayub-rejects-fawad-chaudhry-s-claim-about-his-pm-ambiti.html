Omar Ayub Rejects Fawad Chaudhry's Claim About His PM Ambitions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Omar Ayub Khan has rejected Fawad Chaudhry's recent claim that he wanted to become prime minister. Responding to remarks Fawad made in a television interview, Omar Ayub said a person who loses his mental balance makes ludicrous statements and tells lies. He reminded that PTI founder Imran Khan had expelled Fawad Chaudhry from the party in 2023.

Omar Ayub said he had announced his own name as the prime ministerial candidate only to contest against Shehbaz Sharif. He told Fawad to stop presenting himself as a PTI spokesman. The exchange has added to the ongoing public disagreements between the two leaders.

Party supporters on both sides reacted strongly to the remarks.
