Centre Announces Rs160 Billion Package for KP, Says Governor Kundi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has announced a Rs160 billion package for the province from the Centre. He said the package was announced at a national-level Apex Committee meeting chaired by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. Authorities were directed to take immediate steps on key provincial issues, according to the governor.

Kundi also announced a zero-tolerance policy on corruption in the province. He raised the compensation for police martyrs and said tenders for Chashma Right Bank Canal packages I and IV would be issued next month. The announcement came after the high-level meeting reviewed the province's pressing matters.
