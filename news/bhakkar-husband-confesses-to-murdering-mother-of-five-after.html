Bhakkar: husband confesses to murdering mother of five after staging fake robbery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Bhakkar have solved the blind murder of 32-year-old Humaira, a mother of five, whose throat was cut with a sharp weapon in Chak 35 ML Jandhanwala. Her landlord husband Nadeem turned out to be the killer, police said. He staged a robbery to hide the murder, himself called police on 15 and wept at the funeral.

On suspicion, police detained him, and DPO Shahid Ahmed Khan said Nadeem confessed during interrogation that he killed his wife over domestic discord. The murder weapon, a sharp knife, was recovered on his identification. Police say he will be brought to justice.
