Workers Welfare Board Case: Complaint Against Saeed Ghani, 10 Others; Court Seeks Probe Report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-corruption court in Karachi has taken up a criminal complaint alleging large-scale irregularities in the Sindh Workers Welfare Board. The complaint names provincial minister Saeed Ghani and 10 other officials. It alleges illegal recruitments from grade 1 to grade 15 in the board, the appointment of more than 500 temporary employees, financial irregularities and misuse of authority.

During the hearing, the court expressed anger after the investigating officer failed to submit the inquiry report on time. The judge directed that the report be produced and adjourned the hearing until November 4. The case has drawn attention to the management of the board, which handles welfare funds meant for industrial workers across the province.
