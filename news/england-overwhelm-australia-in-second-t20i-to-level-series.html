England overwhelm Australia in second T20I to level series

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Aneurin Donald smacked a blistering 85 and Phil Salt made 58 in a batting masterclass as England crushed Australia by eight wickets in the second Twenty20 international on Tuesday to level the series 1-1. Donald hit nine sixes and four boundaries in his stunning knock off just 32 balls, and his maiden fifty for his country came from only 18 deliveries. He was well supported by fellow opener Salt as England chased down Australia's 184 for eight on the Gold Coast after 13.1 overs for the loss of two wickets, hitting 16 sixes in all.

Donald got England's reply off to a lightning start with 19 runs from paceman Jack Edwards' first over, and reached his half-century in just the fifth over. Salt brought up his fifty from 24 balls before Ben Dwarshuis dismissed him with a stunning caught and bowled, and captain Harry Brook, 10 not out, and Tom Banton, 26 not out, steered England home. Earlier, Nikhil Chaudhary top-scored for the hosts with 45 after Australia won the toss and opted to bat, having won the opening game of the five-match series in Melbourne by three wickets.

Australia were teetering on 58 for four after Tim David fell, before Chaudhary and Lachlan Shaw steadied the innings, and the hosts finished with a flurry, taking 66 runs in the last five overs. England captain Harry Brook praised the opening partnership, saying the two batters had put on an absolute clinic, while Australia captain Mitchell Marsh admitted his side had tried everything.
