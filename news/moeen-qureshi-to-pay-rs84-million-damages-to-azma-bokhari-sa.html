Moeen Qureshi to Pay Rs84 Million Damages to Azma Bokhari, Says Lawyer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Opposition leader Moeen Qureshi will pay Rs84 million in damages to Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari, her lawyer said on Tuesday. Bokhari had filed a defamation claim against Qureshi in Lahore's Defamation Tribunal under the Defamation Act. Qureshi had alleged that Bokhari brought 150 to 200 paid people into the Punjab Assembly during the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister's visit.

He also claimed that the journalists who questioned Sohail Afridi were her salaried employees. Bokhari described the allegations as entirely false, fabricated and malicious, and Qureshi presented no evidence to prove his claims wrong before the tribunal.
