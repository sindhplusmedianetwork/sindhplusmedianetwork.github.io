Yemen's Forces Claim 336 Houthi Fighters Killed in 'Dawn of Yemen' Offensive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's armed forces said they had neutralised around 336 Houthi fighters and commanders during ongoing military operations under the 'Dawn of Yemen' offensive. Military spokesman Col. Majed Abdullah al-Nuzaili said government forces had carried out 1,087 strikes in recent hours using warplanes, drones, field artillery and rocket launchers.

He said the strikes targeted Houthi command and control centres, troop concentrations, reinforcements, firing positions, fortifications and supply routes. The claim comes amid a flare-up in the Yemen conflict, with the Houthis having taken control of the entire Red Sea coast in a lightning offensive last month, further destabilising the region and disrupting oil markets. The renewed fighting has also spilled across the border into Saudi Arabia, where the Saudi-led coalition says it has intercepted Houthi missiles and drones targeting Riyadh and other cities.

Yemen's foreign ministry has meanwhile called on the United Nations Development Programme to take urgent action to prevent the Houthis from carrying out mine operations in civilian areas.
