Somalia orders UAE ambassador to leave within 72 hours, recalls its own envoy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Somalia ordered the ambassador of the United Arab Emirates to leave the country within 72 hours. Somalia also recalled its own ambassador from Abu Dhabi. It accused the UAE of violating its sovereignty and called its actions an open attack.

Somali Information Minister Abdi Fatah Qasim Mahmoud said action would be taken against those cooperating with UAE measures against Somalia. The UAE had not yet responded to the move. The steps marked a sharp escalation in relations between the two countries.
