Historic Winchester College Bans Laptops, Brings Back Pen and Paper

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Britain's historic Winchester College has banned laptops in its classrooms and restored pen-and-paper teaching across the school. Principal Elizabeth Stone said screens distract students from learning. Laptops will now be used only in lessons where they clearly add something extra, she said.

The independent school believes handwriting helps pupils think more deeply than typing. The decision follows a growing debate in British education about screen time in classrooms. Parents and educators remain divided over screen-free policies in other schools.

The college says the change puts attention back on the lesson. The policy has drawn wide attention in education circles.
