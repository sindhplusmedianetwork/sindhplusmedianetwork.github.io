Finance ministry orders minimum wage compliance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Acting on the recommendations of a parliamentary committee, the Ministry of Finance has directed all ministries and divisions to ensure payment of minimum wages to employees of their respective attached departments, autonomous corporations and organisations, and submit compliance reports. According to an office memorandum issued by the ministry to all ministries, they were asked to confirm that the minimum wage is being paid by them and their attached departments, as well as autonomous bodies, corporations and organisations working under their administrative control, and that salaries are disbursed through banking channels. The memorandum also directed ministries to ensure that the government's decision regarding minimum wages was implemented in letter and spirit.

The directive follows repeated reminders from the National Assembly's Standing Committee on Overseas Pakistanis and Human Resource Development regarding the non-implementation of minimum wage regulations by public sector entities, which the committee believes encourages private employers to disregard government-mandated minimum wages. Earlier this month, the committee had asked the ministries of finance and overseas Pakistanis to obtain confirmation from all departments and issue fresh circulars to ensure payment of minimum wages without delay. On November 3, the committee expressed serious concern over the continued failure to implement the revised minimum daily wage rates for employees of the Overseas Pakistanis Foundation, despite the government's announcement and parliamentary approval through the federal budget for 2026-27 in June.

The committee also asked the finance ministry to consider placing workers' welfare funds in interest-bearing accounts so that the returns could be used for workers' welfare, and directed that rules be introduced to ensure revised minimum wages are paid immediately after their announcement.
