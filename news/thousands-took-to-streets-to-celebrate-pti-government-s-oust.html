Thousands Took to Streets to Celebrate PTI Government's Ouster, Says Muqam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Kashmir Affairs Ameer Muqam said on Friday that thousands of people took to the streets to celebrate the end of the PTI government. He said the people reaffirmed their support for peace and rejection of the PTI's narrative. Speaking at a public meeting in Haripur, he said people had remained frustrated during the PTI government.

He said people celebrated the party's ouster in Peshawar, Swat, Battagram and Shangla. He said the PTI had failed to address people's basic problems despite nearly 13 and a half years in power in the province.
