Muhammad Yunus's ancestral home torched in Chittagong

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The ancestral home of Bangladesh interim government's former chief adviser Muhammad Yunus was torched last night. The incident came amid a rise in violent incidents linked to the banned Awami League across the country. Foreign media reports said the ground floor of the house in Chittagong's Panchlaish area was set on fire at around 11:30pm by unknown persons.

Police on patrol reached the spot. The police extinguished the fire. No loss of life was reported.
