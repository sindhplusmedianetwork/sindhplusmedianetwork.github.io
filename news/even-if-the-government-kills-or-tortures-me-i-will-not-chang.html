'Even If the Government Kills or Tortures Me, I Will Not Change My Return Decision': Sheikh Hasina

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Bangladesh prime minister Sheikh Hasina, addressing a Washington press conference online, reiterated her intent to return to Bangladesh in December. She said she faces arrest, torture or murder threats, but standing with her supporters is her political duty — the government may arrest, kill or torture her, but she will not change her decision. Hasina said she is not afraid and that returning is her responsibility, though she gave no final date for the return.

She left Bangladesh in August 2024 after her government's fall. The interim government in Dhaka has previously said it would arrest her on her return, setting up a potentially volatile confrontation.
