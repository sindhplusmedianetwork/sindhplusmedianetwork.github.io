Businesswoman surprises her female staff with expensive foldable iPhones

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A businesswoman in the United Arab Emirates has surprised the women working in her company by gifting them expensive foldable iPhones. The company's managing director, Haseena Nishad, placed the gifts on her employees' desks without warning, and when the women opened them they found iPhones worth about 2,000 dollars each, more than 550,000 Pakistani rupees. The women were emotional on receiving the gifts.

Haseena Nishad also gave the same phone to the woman who cleans her home, who was so moved that she hugged her and began to cry. Explaining the reason for the gifts on Instagram, Haseena Nishad said she knows every woman has her own dreams, responsibilities and difficulties. She said she wants women to become strong and confident and to fulfil their dreams.

She added that a woman's success makes her feel the proudest, because when one woman progresses, she becomes an example and an encouragement for other women to move forward too.
