Two Hazara Express Coaches Derail Near Shorkot, All Passengers Safe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two coaches of the Karachi-to-Rawalpindi Hazara Express derailed near Shorkot Cantt railway station. All passengers remained safe, and no injuries or loss of life were reported. Railway staff began work to restore the affected track as soon as the derailment was noticed.

The restoration work continued until the track was ready for traffic again. The train later departed for Rawalpindi about three hours after the incident.
