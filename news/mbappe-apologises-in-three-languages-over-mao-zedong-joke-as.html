Mbappé apologises in three languages over Mao Zedong joke as China backlash grows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French football star Kylian Mbappé has apologised in French, Chinese and English for a joke he made about Mao Zedong during an interview with 'The Bridge'. The remark had drawn sharp criticism on Chinese social media, prompting a strong backlash in China. Three Chinese companies reportedly ended their sponsorship deals with the footballer over the controversy.

China Media Group also suspended its broadcasts of LaLiga matches in response. China's foreign ministry said the apology should be taken seriously and treated as a closed matter. The episode underlines how sensitive historical references remain in Chinese public discourse.

Mbappé said he regretted the joke and respected Chinese history and culture.
