Punjab sets up Pakistan's first dedicated anti-smog force with 67 quick response units

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab has set up 67 quick response units with 750 personnel to act against crop-residue burning. The provincial environment department calls it Pakistan's first force created specifically to tackle air pollution. Teams aim to reach a reported fire within 10 to 15 minutes.

In one day, 679 field-fire reports were received and 650 were brought under control. Farmers face fines of Rs15,000 per acre for burning residue, while the government subsidises 60 percent of the cost of super seeders as an alternative.
