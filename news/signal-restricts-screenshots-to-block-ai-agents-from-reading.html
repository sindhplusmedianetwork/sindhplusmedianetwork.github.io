Signal Restricts Screenshots to Block AI Agents From Reading Messages

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Encrypted messaging service Signal said on Friday it would restrict screenshots to stop AI agents from reading private chats on phones, warning that the tech industry's push to embed AI in consumer devices was eroding its ability to guarantee privacy. Signal, widely regarded by privacy advocates as a leader in secure messaging, called its new protections incomplete and urged operating system providers Apple, Alphabet's Google and Microsoft to give app developers more concrete ways to block the agents from accessing sensitive data. It also called on those companies to provide more information about how their agents function, so developers can assess what data the bots are accessing and adjust their systems accordingly.

Signal identified as particularly worrisome the AI capabilities built directly into operating systems, like Apple's Siri AI and Google's Gemini Intelligence, because of how deeply they are integrated into people's devices. To prevent agents from scanning Signal messages, the service is introducing new settings blocking screenshot access on people's phones and switching those restrictions on by default, with pop-up windows giving users options to disable the blocks when attempts are detected. Signal similarly blocked Microsoft's Recall AI from taking screenshots on Windows last year, but said it was unable to reliably offer such protections on Apple's macOS.

Signal said developers should have straightforward ways of opting their apps out of such data processing by AI assistants entirely, specifically mentioning screen reading, notifications and other file system access.
