Karachi's Heat Bill Lands on the Poor, SUPARCO Data Shows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi's poorest residents are paying the heaviest price for the city's rising heat, new data shows. A SUPARCO analysis found the city's built-up area grew by about 32 percent between 2010 and 2025, crossing 1,400 square kilometres and intensifying the urban heat island effect. IBA's Karachi Urban Lab reports the city's green cover has shrunk by nearly 40 percent over the same period.

Informal settlements, home to 62 to 70 percent of Karachi's population, bear the brunt: Baba Bhit Island suffers power outages of up to 18 hours, while North Karachi, Ajmer Nagri and Lyari face 10 to 14 hours of daily load-shedding with no fans or pumps to cope with the heat and humidity.
