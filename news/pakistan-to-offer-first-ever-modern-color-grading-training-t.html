Pakistan to offer first-ever modern color-grading training, Tarar announces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Information and Broadcasting Attaullah Tarar on Monday directed that the PTV Academy be developed into a modern training centre offering professional skills in film, drama and digital content production. Chairing a high-level meeting on the academy's revival, he announced that Pakistan will provide modern color-grading training for the first time, a facility for which people previously had to travel abroad. Federal Secretary Information Ashfaq Ahmed Khalil and senior ministry officials attended the meeting, where models for reviving the PTV Drama and Film Academy and revamping PTV Home were reviewed.

The meeting examined plans for modern classrooms, a studio lab, workstations for students, technical staff and curriculum development, with training models developed in partnership with the private sector. Tarar said a transparent pilot project would be launched with private-sector partnership so that training programmes match industry needs, adding that modern editing suites and technical equipment would give students training in a professional environment.
