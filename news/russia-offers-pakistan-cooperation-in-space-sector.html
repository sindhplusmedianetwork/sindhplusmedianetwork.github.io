Russia offers Pakistan cooperation in space sector

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian Energy Minister Sergei Tsivilev said Moscow was ready to share its expertise and experience in satellite navigation and communications with Pakistan. He said the cooperation could also cover remote sensing, human spaceflight and space technology. The offer comes as engagement between Pakistan and Russia continues to grow.

Analysts see the move as a message to India, which has watched the warming ties between Islamabad and Moscow closely. The two countries have in recent years expanded contacts in trade, energy and defence. The space offer adds a new area to that growing relationship.

It remains to be seen how the proposed cooperation will take shape in practice.
