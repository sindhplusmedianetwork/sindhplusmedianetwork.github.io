WWE star Bronson Reed dies at 38

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Well-known Australian wrestler Bronson Reed has died at the age of 38 after medical complications, and WWE has confirmed his death. Reed was injured on 7 November during a match in Saudi Arabia when he fell from the stage. Media reports say his ribs were broken and his kidney was also injured, after which he underwent surgery.

WWE has not yet confirmed any link between Reed's death and the injury he suffered during the match. According to foreign media reports, Bronson Reed's real name was Jermaine Haley. Reed began his wrestling career in 2007 and joined WWE in 2019, and in 2024 he won the Andre the Giant Memorial Battle Royal.
