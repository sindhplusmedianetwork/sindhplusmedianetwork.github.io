Rana Sanaullah rejects theories linking Imran meeting to 28th Amendment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's adviser Rana Sanaullah has rejected what he called 'conspiracy theories' linking the permission for Imran Khan to meet his lawyers to discussion of the proposed 28th Amendment. He said the meeting was a routine one committed to the Islamabad High Court. Sanaullah said the Islamabad High Court's decision would come in the 'next few days'.

After that, he added, a more informed discussion could take place on the matter. He also said Imran Khan could only be moved to a private hospital if a medical panel advised it. The remarks came as speculation grew around the jailed former prime minister's meetings and health.
