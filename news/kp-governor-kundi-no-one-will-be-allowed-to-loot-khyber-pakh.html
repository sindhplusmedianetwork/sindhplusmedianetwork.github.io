KP Governor Kundi: no one will be allowed to loot Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's natural resources

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has said that establishing peace, protecting people's rights and safeguarding the province's natural resources are the government's top priorities. He said no one will be allowed to loot the province's natural resources, and their rights will be protected in every case. Speaking to the media, he said action against companies involved in illegal activities should not be limited to their owners, but must also reach their local facilitators and financiers.

He said those paying the companies' internet and other expenses should also be traced to find out their role. The governor referred to the minerals and mining conference held in Islamabad, which was attended by delegates from many countries, where all provinces discussed their mineral resources and investment possibilities. He thanked the prime minister, the field marshal and the petroleum ministry for bringing all provincial governments and international delegates together under one roof.
