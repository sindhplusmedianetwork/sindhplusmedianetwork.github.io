Two Killed in Drone Attack on Saudi Arabia's Sheiba Oil Field

Two people were killed in a drone attack on Saudi Arabia's Shaybah oil field, the Saudi energy ministry has said. Air defence forces intercepted a drone that tried to strike an installation operated by the energy giant Aramco. Ministry officials said oil operations at the field were not affected by the attack.

In a sign of the tense security situation, the US State Department ordered its non-emergency government employees and their families to leave Saudi Arabia. The Shaybah field is one of the kingdom's most important oil facilities. The attack comes amid a widening regional conflict that has repeatedly drawn in Gulf energy infrastructure.
