Pakistani Qismat Gul Executed in Saudi Arabia Over Heroin Smuggling

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Pakistani national has been executed in Saudi Arabia after being convicted of heroin smuggling, Saudi media reported on Tuesday. Qismat Gul was apprehended while attempting to smuggle heroin into the kingdom, according to the Saudi daily Sada. He was tried and found guilty by a Saudi court, and the death sentence was carried out after his appeal was rejected and the Supreme Court confirmed the verdict.

The execution took place in the Makkah region on Monday. Saudi Arabia's interior ministry reiterated its resolve to impose the strictest punishments on drug smugglers, warning that trafficking narcotics carries the death penalty under the kingdom's laws.
