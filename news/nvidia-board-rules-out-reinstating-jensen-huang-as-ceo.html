Nvidia board rules out reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nvidia board chairman Harvey Jones said on Tuesday that the company has no plans to reinstate former CEO Jensen Huang, despite Huang's expressed willingness to return to lead the chipmaker following his resignation last week. The Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO, Jones said during a conference call on Nvidia's third-quarter financial results. Jones added that the company would not discuss the circumstances surrounding Huang's departure during the call or an ongoing investigation into alleged foreign intelligence links and potential national security concerns.

His remarks came after Huang made his first public statement since stepping down, indicating that he would be willing to return if the board determined that his reinstatement would serve the company's interests. If the board of directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honoured to answer these calls and return as CEO, Huang said in a statement. Huang resigned last week after 33 years leading the company he co-founded, and his departure followed reports of extramarital affairs, including an alleged relationship with a woman suspected of having ties to Chinese intelligence.

Jones said Nvidia will consider both internal and external candidates as it searches for a permanent chief executive, adding that the search will be thorough and the company wants to install the permanent CEO as quickly as possible, but will not expedite the choice before finding exactly the right person.
