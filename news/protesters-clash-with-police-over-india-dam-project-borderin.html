Protesters clash with police over India dam project bordering China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Protesters in India's Arunachal Pradesh clashed with police over a proposed Himalayan mega-dam, with around 40 police personnel injured by stones thrown by protesters. The violence erupted late on Friday during protests against the proposed 11,200-megawatt Siang Upper Multipurpose Project of the state-run hydropower company NHPC, Chief Minister Pema Khandu told reporters on Saturday. Khandu said the mob destroyed properties, a police station, the NHPC office and the homes of several elected representatives, and police fired tear gas to disperse the crowd.

India says the dam is needed to counter China's 167-billion-dollar Yaxia project upstream in Tibet, and says its dam would create a vast reservoir to store water and help maintain flows into the Brahmaputra river. Beijing has said its project will have no negative impact downstream. The proposed Indian dam, which would be by far its largest hydropower project, has drawn fierce opposition from local communities who fear losing their homes and land.

Khandu urged opponents to engage with the government, saying they should question and hold dialogue instead of violence.
