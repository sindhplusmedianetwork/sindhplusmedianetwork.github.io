Hamas Chief Khalil al-Hayya's Son Survives Israeli Airstrike in Gaza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Izz al-Din al-Hayya, the son of Hamas chief Khalil al-Hayya, survived an Israeli airstrike in Gaza on Friday morning, though several members of his family were wounded. Senior Hamas leader Basem Naim told the Reuters news agency that Israeli forces carried out the strike at dawn, and that Izz al-Din al-Hayya and his family remained safe. According to Hamas sources, several people were wounded in the attack, including Khalil al-Hayya's daughter-in-law, but there was no loss of life.

Naim said Izz al-Din al-Hayya, who was born in 1997, is Khalil al-Hayya's last surviving son, as his four other sons have been killed in Israeli attacks, and he added that the family of one of the late sons also remained safe in Friday's strike. Hamas said in a statement that Israeli forces targeted a residential apartment in Gaza, and the group linked the attack to threats made against Khalil al-Hayya by Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz. Hamas accused Israel of trying to undermine the 2025 ceasefire agreement in Gaza and said Israel would bear full responsibility for the consequences of its continued attacks.

Naim also said Izz al-Din al-Hayya is the twin brother of Hammam Khalil al-Hayya, who was killed in the Israeli airstrike on Hamas leaders in Qatar in 2025, an attack in which Khalil al-Hayya himself also survived. Khalil al-Hayya was appointed Hamas chief in July last year, and a day earlier he had warned of possible retaliation after at least 10 people were killed in Israeli attacks in Gaza.
