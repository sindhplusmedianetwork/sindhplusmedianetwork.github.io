Houthis launch ballistic missile attack on Aden, government forces say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's government forces say the Houthis launched a ballistic missile attack on the city of Aden on Friday evening. The attack was described in a statement as a terrorist act targeting civilians, according to Al Jazeera. A spokesperson for Yemen's government forces said the attack threatens the security and safety of residents in the city.

The spokesperson also described the attack as a dangerous escalation. Government forces said they are taking all necessary operational measures in response. Those measures, they said, are aimed at dealing firmly with the sources of the threat.
