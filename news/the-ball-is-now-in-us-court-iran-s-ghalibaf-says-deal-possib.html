'The ball is now in US court': Iran's Ghalibaf says deal possible if Washington agrees

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf says a deal with Washington is possible if the US adheres to what was previously agreed, telling Al Jazeera that Tehran has given Washington a 'clear and transparent response' outlining its demands and that the ball is now in the US's court. He added that the Strait of Hormuz will not return to its pre-war status and its future will be determined within a seven-point framework proposed by Tehran, while Iran's priority is peace provided its terms are accepted. Ghalibaf said Iran is determined to move beyond the uncertainty of 'no peace, no war', not necessarily through negotiations, but through sustained deterrence, and that it does not want the war to continue.

He said what prevents an agreement from being reached is the lack of trust in Washington, combined with its attempt to impose excessive demands. An agreement with Washington is possible, he added, provided it adheres to what was previously agreed upon and refrains from actions that undermine trust. The ball is now in the US's court, he said; they must make a decision and announce it, and as of now Washington has not provided a formal or specific response to Tehran's message.

His remarks came as the US and Israel's war on Iran continued, with Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei saying Tehran would not allow any enriched nuclear material to leave the country and warning that any fresh US attack would be met with retaliation against American bases, including distant ones.
