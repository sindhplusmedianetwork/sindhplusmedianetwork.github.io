Punjab Prepares 400,000 Cattle in a Year as Livestock Exports Rise 43%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's livestock sector prepared 400,000 animals in one year while rural farmers earned billions of rupees in profit, according to a Punjab chief minister's office statement on Sunday. Through the Livestock Card scheme, each farmer earned Rs27,000 profit per animal, and 129,581 cattle were exported under the scheme. The province has notified 8 foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) compartments, with 7 more planned this year, paving the way for exports to China, Europe and the United States.

The government announced Rs19 billion in financing subsidies and expects private investment of Rs65 billion in the meat value chain. Officials said the programme is transforming livestock farming into a profitable industry for rural households across the province.
