Jamaat-e-Islami tables resolution in Balochistan Assembly to abolish sardari system

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami member Maulana Hidayat-ur-Rehman has tabled a resolution in the Balochistan Assembly seeking the abolition of the sardari system. The resolution puts the future of the tribal system before the provincial house for formal consideration. Provincial minister Zahoor Buledi supported the move, telling the house that the sardari system can no longer continue and that rights must now be given on the basis of equality.

Assembly member Zarak Mandokhel opposed the resolution, saying he does not accept sardars and nawabs who are cruel and do not accept Pakistan. He added, however, that many sardars still play a role in education and development.
