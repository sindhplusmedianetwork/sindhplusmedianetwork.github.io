Reigns Beats Zayn as Stratton Cashes In at WWE Crown Jewel 2026 in Riyadh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WWE Crown Jewel 2026 took place at the ANB Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on Saturday, with four matches on the card. The show opened with Cody Rhodes defeating Randy Orton in a Last Man Standing match. Penta and Je'Von Evans then beat The Vision, Bron Breakker and Austin Theory, to win the World Tag Team Championship, with Oba Femi's return proving decisive.

In the women's Crown Jewel Championship match, Chelsea Green defeated Liv Morgan, but her celebrations were short-lived as Money in the Bank holder Tiffany Stratton cashed in her contract immediately and beat Green to win the WWE Women's Championship. The main event saw World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns defeat Undisputed WWE Champion Sami Zayn to win the Men's Crown Jewel Championship, ending the show with an embrace between the former Bloodline allies.
