Fazlur Rehman's detention is undemocratic, says Hafiz Naeem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem has said that the detention of Maulana Fazlur Rehman and the restrictions placed on his movement are an undemocratic step. He said that such restrictions will increase political instability in the country. His remarks came after the siege of the JUI-F leader's residence, which has drawn criticism from opposition figures.

Hafiz Naeem said curbs on a political leader's movement go against democratic norms. He warned that the restrictions could deepen the political crisis. The JUI-F chief's detention has become a fresh point of contention in the country's politics.
