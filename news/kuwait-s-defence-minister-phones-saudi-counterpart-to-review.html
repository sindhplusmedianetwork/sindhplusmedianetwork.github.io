Kuwait's defence minister phones Saudi counterpart to review defence ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kuwait's defence minister Sheikh Abdullah Ali Abdullah Al-Salem Al-Sabah has held a telephone conversation with his Saudi counterpart, Prince Khalid bin Salman. The two ministers reviewed Saudi-Kuwaiti defence relations and discussed the latest regional developments of mutual concern. According to the Saudi Press Agency, both sides reaffirmed their solidarity in confronting the challenges and threats facing the region.

They stressed the importance of continued coordination to safeguard the security and stability of both countries. The call reflects the close defence cooperation between the two Gulf neighbours.
