Pakistan to Chair IMF, World Bank Boards in 2027 — First Time Since 1953

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan will chair the boards of governors of the World Bank Group and the International Monetary Fund in 2027, the first time the country has held the position since 1953. Economic Affairs Minister Ahad Khan Cheema has been nominated to lead both boards for the year, the Ministry of Economic Affairs announced on Friday during the plenary session of the institutions' annual meetings in Bangkok, Thailand. Cheema was briefed by the World Bank's Corporate Secretariat on the chairmanship's responsibilities and met its vice-president and corporate secretary Aradhana Kumar Capoor to discuss institutional coordination during Pakistan's tenure.

He also met outgoing chairman Carlos Carvalho Spalding and congratulated him on completing his term. Cheema said Pakistan would work to amplify the voice of developing countries and ensure effective, predictable development finance, while strengthening coordination among multilateral institutions and mobilising private investment for sustainable growth. Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, who led Pakistan's delegation, highlighted the fiscal deficit falling to a 22-year low, three consecutive years of primary surpluses and improved foreign exchange reserves.

On the sidelines, Aurangzeb met World Bank Group President Ajay Banga at a dinner hosted by Thailand's prime minister for informal interaction alongside the high-level engagements.
