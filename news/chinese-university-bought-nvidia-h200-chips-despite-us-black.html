Chinese university bought Nvidia H200 chips despite US blacklist

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A Chinese university with deep ties to the People's Liberation Army bought Nvidia's powerful H200 AI chips last month despite being on a US export blacklist, procurement records show. Northwestern Polytechnical University paid $123,000 for two H200 chips through a Chinese supplier and also signed contracts to rent access to advanced Nvidia processors, according to a Reuters review of procurement records. The findings come as Nvidia faces mounting scrutiny in Washington over lobbying for looser restrictions on AI chip sales to China.

US lawmakers have questioned whether the company's sales practices are undermining export controls. The university is among several Chinese institutions barred from receiving advanced American technology. Nvidia has said it follows all export rules.
