Iran Condemns Strikes on Taiz Market, Calls for Dialogue Between Yemen and Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's foreign ministry has condemned airstrikes targeting civilian infrastructure and non-military areas in Yemen and called for talks between Yemen and Saudi Arabia. In a statement posted on X, the ministry particularly noted the recent deadly strikes on a public market in Taiz and on the village of Al-Shariyah east of Sanaa, terming them war crimes. Tehran called for an immediate halt to military attacks, an end to the blockade and respect for the Yemeni people's right to self-determination.

Both the Houthis and Yemen's internationally recognised government blamed each other for the Taiz market attack, according to Al Jazeera.
