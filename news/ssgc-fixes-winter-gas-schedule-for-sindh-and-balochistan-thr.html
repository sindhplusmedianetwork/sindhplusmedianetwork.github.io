SSGC fixes winter gas schedule for Sindh and Balochistan: three daily slots

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sui Southern Gas Company (SSGC) on Wednesday announced the gas supply schedule for the winter season for domestic consumers in Sindh and Balochistan, with immediate effect. An SSGC spokesperson told Dawn that three slots have been fixed for supply, in line with federal government directives: 6:30am to 9am for breakfast, 12pm to 2:30pm for lunch, and 6pm to 9pm for dinner. The company shared the schedule on its website and social media platforms, saying it was 'prioritising domestic consumers by ensuring supply during meal times'.

For the past some years, SSGC's consumers have not been getting round-the-clock gas supply. The announcement came a day after a severe gas crisis gripped Karachi on Tuesday, when multiple contradictory gas supply schedules attributed to SSGC began circulating on social media from Monday, while the utility issued no clear consolidated statement until late Tuesday evening, adding to the confusion.
