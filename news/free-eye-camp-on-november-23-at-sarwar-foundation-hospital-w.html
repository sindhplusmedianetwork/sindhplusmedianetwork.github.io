Free eye camp on November 23 at Sarwar Foundation Hospital with free check-ups, operations and medicines

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Patients in Toba Tek Singh district will be able to get free eye treatment at a special camp later this month. Sarwar Foundation Hospital will hold the free eye camp on November 23, 2026, at its hospital in Rajana, and the camp will run from 8 in the morning until 3 in the afternoon. Patients visiting the camp will receive free check-ups, free operations and free medicines.

The camp is being organised with the cooperation of Al-Mustafa Trust. Sarwar Foundation founder and chairman Chaudhry Muhammad Sarwar, a former governor of Punjab, said the foundation is continuing its tradition of recent years by holding the camp. He said patients will receive a full eye examination, medicines will be provided free of charge where needed, and those who require cataract surgery will also be operated on free of cost.

He said that in this time of inflation, poor people fall victim to various eye diseases but cannot afford expensive treatment, and without timely care they can lose the priceless gift of sight. He added that hundreds of people benefit from the free eye camp.
