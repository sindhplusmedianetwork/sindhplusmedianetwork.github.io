Islamabad police to hold open courts at mosques and churches to hear public complaints

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ISLAMABAD: The Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT) Police has announced a new public-outreach initiative under which senior officers will hold open courts at mosques during Friday prayers and at churches on Sundays. Inspector General of Police Capt (retd) Muhammad Sohail Chaudhry announced the plan in a special message addressed to citizens. Under the initiative, senior police officers will visit mosques on Fridays, interact directly with worshippers and hold open courts to listen to their complaints and concerns.

Similarly, open courts will be organised at churches on Sundays to facilitate members of minority communities and address their police-related complaints. An ICT Police official told APP that the initiative is aimed at improving communication between the police and the public and making senior officers more accessible to citizens. The arrangements will give people an opportunity to raise their problems directly with senior police officers at places of worship.
