Gerard Depardieu withdraws conviction appeal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French cinema icon Gerard Depardieu is set to withdraw his appeal of a conviction for sexually assaulting two women on a film set, a source close to the case told AFP on Friday, ahead of a hearing next week. A court last year handed the 77-year-old an 18-month suspended sentence and ordered him to register as a sex offender, marking a spectacular fall from grace for an actor who dominated French cinema for half a century. An appeals trial was due to begin on Monday, but the withdrawal effectively makes the original ruling final, including an additional penalty for 'secondary victimisation' stemming from his defence team's treatment of the two women.

The trial related to charges of sexual assault during the filming in 2021 of 'Les Volets Verts' ('The Green Shutters') by director Jean Becker, with the plaintiffs a set dresser identified only as Amelie and an assistant director. Depardieu has denied the allegations. The actor has also been indicted in a separate case following a rape complaint filed by actor Charlotte Arnould, 30, and has appealed against his referral to the criminal court, with a decision expected on November 26.

Depardieu, who has acted in more than 200 films and television series, is the highest-profile figure caught up in France's response to the #MeToo movement, and the verdict was delivered on the first day of the 2025 Cannes Film Festival, where he won best actor in 1990.
