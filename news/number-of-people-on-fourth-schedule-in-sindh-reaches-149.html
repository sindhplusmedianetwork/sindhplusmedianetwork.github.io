Number of people on Fourth Schedule in Sindh reaches 149

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The number of people on the Fourth Schedule in Sindh under the Anti-Terrorism Act has reached 149 this year, according to the Counter Terrorism Department. Of these, 25 are in jail, 13 are missing and six remain out of reach, while 16 more names have been recommended for inclusion. In Karachi, the highest number is in East district at 32, followed by West with 23, Central with 17, City with 13, Korangi with 10, Keamari with 4, Malir with 4 and South with 2.

Other districts include Jacobabad with seven, Larkana with six, Sukkur and Qambar with five each, and Hyderabad with three. Those on the list must report to their police station every week and to the CTD every month. The list includes people suspected of terrorism, facilitating terrorism, spreading sectarianism or links with banned organisations under the Anti-Terrorism Act of 1997.

The CTD said cases are registered for violations, and names can be kept on the schedule for three more years.
