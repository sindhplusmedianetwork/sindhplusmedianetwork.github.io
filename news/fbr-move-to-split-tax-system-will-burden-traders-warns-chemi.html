FBR move to split tax system will burden traders, warns chemicals merchants body

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Chemicals and Dyes Merchants Association (PCDMA) has rejected the Federal Board of Revenue's decision to separate sales tax and federal excise duty administration from income tax, calling it a harbinger of new difficulties for the business community. Chairman Saleem Makati warned the split would make tax compliance more complex and costly, forcing traders to visit multiple tax offices, with extra paperwork, repeated appearances, procedural delays and confusion over jurisdiction, audits, refunds and complaints. He urged the FBR to define the responsibilities of the new offices and ensure seamless coordination between income tax, sales tax and customs officials.

Makati also called on the tax authority to consult the business community before carrying out such restructuring. He further proposed an integrated one-window facility so that traders would not have to run between different offices. The association's rejection adds to the concerns raised by traders over the restructuring of the tax administration.
