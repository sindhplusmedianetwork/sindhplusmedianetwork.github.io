Port Qasim Handles 118,649 Tonnes of Cargo in 24 Hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Port Qasim handled 118,649 tonnes of cargo in 24 hours, comprising 84,431 tonnes of imports and 34,218 tonnes of exports carried in 2,480 containers. Ten ships were engaged at the port's berths during the period. Three ships, SLS Topaz, Star Oslo and Tai Star, berthed to load and offload containers, cement and coal.

The figures reflect a busy day of shipping activity at the port. Port Qasim remains one of Pakistan's key gateways for import and export cargo.
