Operations will continue at full force until foreign-backed terrorism is eliminated, says PM Shehbaz Sharif

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has said the armed forces have the full capability to foil every enemy plot, and that operations will continue at full force until foreign-backed terrorism is completely eliminated. He paid tribute to the security forces for successful intelligence-based operations against the Indian-backed terrorist organisation Fitna al-Hindustan in Kolpur, Kardagap and Nushki in Balochistan. The Prime Minister also paid tribute to Major Omer Minhas Nasir and Havildar Muhammad Adnan Khan, who were martyred during a patrol on the Quetta-Taftan National Highway N-40.

He said the enemy was making a nefarious attempt to sabotage peace in the region by targeting innocent civilians and security personnel, and he prayed for the elevation of the martyrs' ranks and patience for their bereaved families, adding that the whole nation saluted the eternal sacrifices of its martyrs. In intelligence-based operations in Kolpur and Nushki, security forces killed eight terrorists. Earlier, in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, security forces killed 16 khawarij in North Waziristan, including two Afghan nationals said to be operating with Indian backing, and in the exchange of fire two soldiers of the Pakistan Army, Havildar Ismat Ali and Naik Aftab Ali, were martyred, according to ISPR.
