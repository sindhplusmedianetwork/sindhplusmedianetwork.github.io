220 Couples Wed in Sheikhupura Under Punjab Dhee Rani Programme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A mass wedding ceremony was held in Sheikhupura under the Punjab government's 'Punjab Dhee Rani' programme. A total of 220 couples from across the district were married at the ceremony, including 200 Muslim and 20 minority couples. Provincial Minister for Social Welfare and Bait-ul-Mal Sohail Shaukat Butt attended the ceremony as chief guest.

Each couple received a salami card of Rs200,000 from the Chief Minister of Punjab, and lunch was also arranged for the participants. The minister said the Chief Minister stands with parents fulfilling their religious duty towards their daughters. He said the target of 13,000 couples' weddings will be completed by next March.
