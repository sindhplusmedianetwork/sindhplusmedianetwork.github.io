Salman Khan Announces December 4 Release for Controversial Film 'Maatrubhumi'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Salman Khan has announced the release date of his film 'Maatrubhumi', saying the film directed by Apoorva Lakhia will be presented in cinemas on December 4. The film's story is inspired by the military clash between Indian and Chinese troops in Ladakh's Galwan Valley in 2020. Explaining the delay in the film's release, Salman Khan said the team wanted to prepare the visual effects and graphics to a high standard, and the film is being made in a way that its scenes will amaze viewers.

The actor added that the shooting schedule was also affected after one of the film's actresses was injured. The release was earlier considered for January, then June and August, but it will now be presented in December. The film was initially titled 'Battle of Galwan', which was later changed to 'Maatrubhumi'.

According to Indian media, Salman Khan plays Colonel Bikumalla Santosh Babu of the 16 Bihar Regiment, who was killed in the clash with Chinese troops. Salman Khan had released the film's teaser on his 60th birthday last year, and China's state newspaper Global Times objected to it, calling it a distorted cinematic depiction of events.
