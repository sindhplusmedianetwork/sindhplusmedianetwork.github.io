US Asks China for Help to End War With Iran, Washington Post Reports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, with the backing of President Donald Trump, has asked China for help in securing a ceasefire in the war between the United States and Iran, the Washington Post reported, citing an informed American official. The report said the United States asked China to use its influence on Iran and help end the war, and that Beijing has conveyed the message to Tehran. According to the report, Vice President JD Vance is leading the administration's diplomatic efforts on Iran, and a conciliatory letter was sent to Iran's security chief, Mohsen Rezaei, last month.

Qatari official Ali Al-Thawadi, who has acted as a mediator between the White House and Tehran, said the Iranian leadership is waiting to see whether the results of the US midterm elections change Washington's policy. He said the Iranian leadership saw Vance's letter as a sign of respect and appreciated that he called the war a mistake, adding that Tehran wants to be sure the United States is a reliable party. The report also said President Trump had asked Chinese President Xi Jinping for help on Iran during a meeting in Washington in September, when Xi told Trump in a private conversation that he agrees Iran should never have nuclear weapons.

The matter is likely to be discussed again when Trump and Xi are expected to meet in Shenzhen on November 18.
