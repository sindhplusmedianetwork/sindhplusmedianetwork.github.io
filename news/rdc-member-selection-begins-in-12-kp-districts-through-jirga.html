RDC Member Selection Begins in 12 KP Districts Through Jirgas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The process of electing members of the Regional Development Council under the new administrative system has begun in 12 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. In the first phase on October 14, consultation through jirgas was initiated in North Waziristan, Khyber, Bannu and Tal in Kurram, and the exercise will be repeated in the remaining districts over the next two days. People of Bajaur, Mohmand, Khyber, Kurram, Orakzai, North Waziristan, South Waziristan Upper, South Waziristan Lower, Dera Ismail Khan, Tank, Lakki Marwat and Bannu will choose council members through the jirga system.

The district administrations organised jirgas bringing together elders, scholars, tribal leaders and traders to discuss law and order, security, public issues and development needs, along with suggestions for solving local problems. The selection takes into account the wishes of local people, tribal traditions and mutual consultation. The aim is to give representation to local elders so the area's problems, needs and development priorities reach the relevant authorities effectively.
