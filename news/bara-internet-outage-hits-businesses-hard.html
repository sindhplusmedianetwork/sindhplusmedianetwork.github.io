Bara internet outage hits businesses hard

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Internet services have remained suspended for the past two weeks in Bara tehsil of Khyber district, severely disrupting commercial activity and bringing online education to a standstill, The Express Tribune reported. Bara Traders Association president Syed Ayaz Wazir said the suspension badly affected Bara Bazaar, home to nearly 10,000 shops, with traders' losses estimated in tens of millions of rupees. Easy Paisa and other internet-based financial services — critical on weekends when banks and ATMs in the bazaar stay closed — have been disrupted, hurting remittances from overseas Pakistanis too.

Students said online classes and study resources were inaccessible; 12th-grader Lubna Afridi of Shalobar said the outage had severely disrupted her studies. The suspension followed three-day protests in Bara Bazaar from October 11 against governor's rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa; traders and students say services have still not been fully restored.
