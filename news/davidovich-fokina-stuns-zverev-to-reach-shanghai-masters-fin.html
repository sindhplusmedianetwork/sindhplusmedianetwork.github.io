Davidovich Fokina Stuns Zverev to Reach Shanghai Masters Final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Spain's Alejandro Davidovich Fokina defeated top seed Alexander Zverev 5-7, 6-4, 6-3 on Saturday to reach the final of the Rolex Shanghai Masters. The two-hour-six-minute victory was his second Masters 1000 final appearance after Monte-Carlo 2022. The world No.

26 won 82 per cent of his first-serve points, claimed his sixth career win over a top-five player, and rises to No. 15 in the live ATP rankings. He has now won nine straight matches in China, including the Chengdu Open title last month, and will face Daniil Medvedev or Rafael Jodar in Sunday's final, chasing his first Masters 1000 title.
