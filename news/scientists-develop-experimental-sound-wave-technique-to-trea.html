Scientists Develop Experimental Sound-Wave Technique to Treat Deadliest Brain Cancer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Researchers at the University of Virginia Comprehensive Cancer Center have developed an experimental technique to treat glioblastoma, the deadliest form of brain cancer. The method uses genetic molecules, nanoparticles and focused ultrasound to deliver treatment. It temporarily opens the brain's protective barrier so treatment reaches the cancer cells directly.

The technique also targets multiple genetic factors that drive tumor growth. The approach remains experimental at this stage. The research team hopes to move toward clinical trials next, as glioblastoma is the deadliest brain cancer and new treatment options are urgently needed.
