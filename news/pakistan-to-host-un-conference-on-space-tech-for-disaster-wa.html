Pakistan to host UN conference on space tech for disaster warnings and climate action

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan will host a five-day United Nations international conference in Islamabad from October 27 to 31 to explore how space technology can strengthen early warning systems, improve disaster risk assessment and support climate action. The announcement was made on Thursday by the Pakistan Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission (SUPARCO). The UN-Pakistan International Conference on Leveraging Space Technology for Early Warning for All, Climate Action and Disaster Risk Assessment will be held at the Serena Hotel.

Between 200 and 250 participants are expected, including 60 to 70 international delegates and around 150 national participants. The conference is co-sponsored by the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs through its UN-SPIDER programme, the Asia-Pacific Space Cooperation Organisation and the Inter-Islamic Network on Space Sciences and Technology. A key focus will be the UN's Early Warnings for All initiative, which seeks to protect communities and critical infrastructure from climate-related disasters.

Discussions will align with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction, the Paris Agreement and Sustainable Development Goal 13. The event will conclude on October 31 with a closing ceremony and a technical visit to the National Disaster Management Authority.
