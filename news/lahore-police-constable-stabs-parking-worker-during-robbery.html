Lahore police constable stabs parking worker during robbery attempt; booked

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A police constable in Lahore has been booked for attempted murder after allegedly stabbing a parking stand worker during a robbery attempt. The FIR states that Constable Talib stopped two parking attendants, Babar and Sarfraz, at the Lahore Development Authority parking stand in Ghaziabad and searched them. He allegedly took Rs25,000 from Babar's pocket, and when the workers tried to stop him, he stabbed Babar and injured him.

The constable and his unidentified accomplice then fled the scene. Police have registered a case against Constable Talib Hussain and his accomplice. The incident has raised fresh questions about the conduct of police personnel in the city.
