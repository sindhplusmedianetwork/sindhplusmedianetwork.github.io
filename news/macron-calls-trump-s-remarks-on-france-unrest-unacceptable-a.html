Macron Calls Trump's Remarks on France Unrest 'Unacceptable and Foolish'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French President Emmanuel Macron has called US President Donald Trump's remarks about unrest in France 'unacceptable and foolish'. Speaking during his visit to Qatar, Macron was reacting to Trump's statements blaming immigrants for violence and looting during protests against inflation and rising costs in France. He said disinformation had been spread following the looting incidents, and that foolish comments had been made by multiple figures.

When asked whether he meant Trump, Macron answered that it included him and others. Trump had earlier claimed that Paris would soon become a Muslim city and pressed Macron to close France's borders. Macron also said that blaming Muslims for the unrest in France is foolish, and that AI-generated images of incidents that never happened were circulating, with foreign leaders exploiting the tensions for their own politics.
