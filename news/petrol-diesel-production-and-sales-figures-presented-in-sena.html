Petrol, Diesel Production and Sales Figures Presented in Senate; Punjab Leads

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Petroleum Ministry on Thursday presented the July-to-September figures for petrol and high-speed diesel production and sales in the Senate through a written reply. Daily petrol production stood at 7,035 metric tons against sales of more than 21,000 metric tons. Punjab led with daily production of 4,670 metric tons and sales above 14,000 metric tons.

Sindh recorded 1,592 metric tons of production and 4,567 metric tons of sales, while Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's daily petrol sales stood at 2,127 metric tons. The figures give the house a full picture of the country's fuel supply and consumption during the first quarter of the fiscal year.
