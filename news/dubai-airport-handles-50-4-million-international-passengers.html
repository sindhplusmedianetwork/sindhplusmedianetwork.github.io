Dubai Airport handles 50.4 million international passengers in nine months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dubai Airport recorded 50.4 million international passengers in the first nine months of 2026, the report said. Pakistanis ranked fourth among the nationalities travelling through the airport during the period. The figure underlines the airport's position as one of the world's busiest international hubs.

Dubai Airport serves as a major transit point connecting travellers between Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas. The large passenger numbers reflect the continued strength of air travel through the Gulf city. Pakistani travellers form a significant share of the airport's traffic, given the large Pakistani community in the region and beyond.

The airport has consistently ranked among the busiest in the world for international passengers in recent years.
