China's First Electric Passenger Aircraft Completes Maiden Piloted Test Flight

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China has carried out the first piloted test flight of its first locally developed electric passenger aircraft, marking a milestone in the country's aviation programme. The aircraft, the CE25A built by the state-owned Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (COMAC), took off from Binzhou in Shandong province on November 14 with two pilots and a flight test engineer on board. It stayed in the air for 16 minutes and 38 seconds and covered 60.4 kilometres, while the flight and ground operations together lasted 57 minutes and 46 seconds.

The aircraft has a maximum take-off weight of 10 metric tons and can carry 19 passengers in addition to its crew, powered by 10 electric ducted fans mounted on its wings. Its expected range is 400 kilometres at a speed of 300 kilometres per hour. Chinese media reported that the aircraft had already completed 19 unmanned test flights since February 2, 2026, before this first flight with a pilot on board.

The project involves battery maker CATL and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, although COMAC has not disclosed which battery the aircraft uses. China introduced the concept in 2023 and first displayed a full-scale model in October 2026, and the company has applied to the Chinese civil aviation authority for certification.
