PSX Recovers 500 Points as Mari Petroleum's Copper-Gold Discovery Lifts Market

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange staged an intraday recovery on Friday, with the benchmark KSE-100 index gaining 500 points to close 0.28% higher at 177,873.88, as buying in oil and gas stocks, led by Mari Petroleum following its copper-gold discovery in Balochistan, helped offset early losses amid concerns over rising international oil prices. At the close of trading, the benchmark KSE-100 index posted a gain of 500.11 points, or 0.28%, and settled at 177,873.88. A Topline market review highlighted that the KSE-100 index staged a notable intraday recovery, and the benchmark remained volatile throughout the session, swinging between an intraday high of 1,910 points and a low of 508 points, reflecting mixed investor sentiment.

Market sentiment received a significant boost towards the latter half of the session following material news from Mari Petroleum, which through its wholly owned subsidiary, Mari Minerals, announced a new porphyry copper-gold discovery at the East Siah Diq (ESD-1) target in Chagai, Balochistan. The development sparked renewed investor interest and helped turn sentiment positive, providing fresh impetus to the market's recovery. On the contribution front, Mari Petroleum, Lucky Cement, Pakistan Petroleum, Pakistan State Oil and Fatima emerged as the leading positive contributors, collectively adding approximately 722 points to the benchmark index.

Meanwhile, market participation remained robust, with total traded volume reaching approximately 1,042 million shares, while traded value stood at around Rs31 billion. Overall trading volume was recorded at 1.04 billion shares compared with the previous session's tally of 1.07 billion, and shares of 497 companies were traded, of which 284 stocks closed higher, 184 fell and 29 remained unchanged.
