German Customs Catch Mexican Firefighter with 90 Venomous Snakes in Suitcase

WhatsApp Facebook X

German customs officials have foiled an unusual smuggling attempt at the airport, catching a 45-year-old Mexican firefighter with 90 venomous snakes in his suitcase. The reptiles included vipers and rattlesnakes, alongside lizards and iguanas, hidden inside socks and shampoo bottles. The man was first stopped at the airport's green channel for a drug test, where traces of cocaine were found positive.

When officers carried out a detailed search expecting to find drugs, they discovered the hidden reptiles instead. The authorities seized the animals and launched further proceedings against the suspect.
