Finance minister and Saudi envoy discuss trade, investment and economic cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb held a meeting with Saudi Arabia's Ambassador to Pakistan, Dr Rajeh Tami Albugamy, in Islamabad on Wednesday to discuss trade, investment and shared economic priorities. Aurangzeb spoke of the long-standing friendship between Pakistan and Saudi Arabia and thanked the kingdom for its continued support, stressing the need to deepen the economic relationship between the two countries. The Saudi envoy described the historic ties between the two nations as rooted in shared faith, history, trust and respect, and said stronger business-to-business engagement would bring the two peoples closer and open up mutually beneficial economic opportunities.

The finance minister highlighted Pakistan's improving macroeconomic stability, pointing to stronger foreign exchange reserves, easing inflation and recent upgrades by international credit rating agencies. He also noted Pakistan's return to international capital markets through a recent $3 billion Eurobond issuance and its access to China's capital market, saying these gains would strengthen investor confidence and support investment-led growth. Both sides explored investment opportunities in agriculture, minerals and mining, and agreed on the need to turn shared priorities into concrete results.

They also exchanged views on the regional situation and the ongoing conflict, underlining the importance of peace and stability to protect regional economic prospects, and the minister reaffirmed the government's commitment to structural reforms and a stable, predictable investment environment.
