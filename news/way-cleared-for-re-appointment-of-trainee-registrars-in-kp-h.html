Way cleared for re-appointment of trainee registrars in KP hospitals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The way has been cleared for the re-appointment of trainee registrars in all medical teaching hospitals of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Under a Policy Board notification, former trainee registrars can be re-hired as medical officers for a fixed one-year term where needed. FCPS-qualified doctors who have completed the mandatory two-year post-training will also be eligible for assistant professor posts.

The notification has been sent to the boards of governors, deans, medical directors and the health department. The decision opens a route back into teaching hospitals for doctors who had completed their registrar training. It also widens eligibility for assistant professor posts for qualified doctors who have finished the required post-training period.
