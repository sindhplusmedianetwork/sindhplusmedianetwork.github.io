Egypt and Jordan Agree to Call Urgent Arab Summit to Stop Growing War in Middle East

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The foreign ministers of Egypt and Jordan have agreed to call an emergency Arab League summit immediately to contain the rapidly spreading war in the Middle East and the tense situation in the Gulf region. In a joint statement after high-level consultations in Cairo, both countries voiced deep concern that the military confrontation is pushing the whole region towards a devastating war from which no country would be safe. Egypt's foreign minister said Arab states must set aside their differences and press world powers for a joint peace plan and a ceasefire.

His Jordanian counterpart endorsed the position. The two sides agreed that urgent joint action is needed to stop the conflict from spreading further. The call for the summit comes as tensions in the region continue to rise.
