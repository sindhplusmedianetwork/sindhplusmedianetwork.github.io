US Sanctions Indian and Turkish Companies, Individuals Over Alleged Iran Links

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has imposed fresh sanctions on companies and individuals in India and Turkiye over alleged links to Iran. According to British media, the measures target India-based Sunil Kumar Sharma and his wife Jaspreet Preeti, along with nine more entities connected to Sharma. Washington accuses them of using fraud to support Iran's secret oil-tanker network.

The US Treasury said the network supplied vessels carrying Iranian oil with fake flags and certificates. A Turkish company was also sanctioned for backing Iran's aviation sector. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said sanctions will be imposed on anyone who keeps supporting the Iranian government.
