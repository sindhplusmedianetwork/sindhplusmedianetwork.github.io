Pakistan-Iran Border Reopens as Field Marshal Asim Munir Holds Talks in Tehran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan-Iran border reopened on Tuesday morning after Chief of Defence Forces and Army Chief Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir held high-level meetings in Tehran. Munir met President Masoud Pezeshkian, Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and General Mohsen Rezaei, describing the recent border disruptions as 'temporary and technical'. He confirmed that crossings on both sides had been reopened as of Tuesday morning.

The meetings came after weeks of tension over the border trade routes between the two countries. On the nuclear front, Iranian leaders told Munir that Tehran would not give up its enriched nuclear material. They also made clear Iran would not negotiate with Washington until the United States met Tehran's conditions for talks.
