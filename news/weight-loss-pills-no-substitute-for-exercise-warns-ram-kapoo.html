Weight-loss pills no substitute for exercise, warns Ram Kapoor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian actor Ram Kapoor has said that weight-loss medicines cannot take the place of physical exercise. Speaking in a recent interview, he explained that even after losing weight through medication, keeping the body active remains essential. Focusing only on losing weight, he said, can also affect muscle mass.

Kapoor recalled that he once weighed 140 kg and took insulin three times a day, and doctors had warned him that his life was at risk. After his transformation, he said, he now feels the best fitness of his life. He also advised against using weight-loss medicine without a doctor's advice.
