Artists Stand Against Punjab Theatre Act 2026, Demand Its Repeal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Artists in Punjab have come out against the Punjab Dramatic and Theatrical Performances Bill 2026, saying the proposed law would affect artistic freedom and the right to freedom of expression. The artists and theatre-associated figures have demanded that the Governor of Punjab withhold approval of the bill and send it back to the Punjab Assembly for reconsideration. At a roundtable organised by the Human Rights Commission of Pakistan, participants expressed reservations about the bill.

They said the requirements for mandatory registration of artists, licensing of theatre halls and scrutiny of scripts before performance could limit creative freedom. The participants called for the bill to be reviewed so that the freedom of artists and theatre practitioners is protected. The artists said they would continue to press for the bill's repeal.
