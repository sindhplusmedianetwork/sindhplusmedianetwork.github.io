Foods That Keep Your Skin Looking Young as You Age

WhatsApp Facebook X

What we eat has a direct effect on how our skin ages, and certain foods can help keep it looking healthy, fresh and youthful for longer. A new feature explains the role of diet and nutrition in maintaining radiant skin as the years pass. Antioxidant-rich fruits and vegetables, healthy fats and adequate hydration are among the key dietary factors highlighted.

The article notes that a balanced diet supports the skin from within, slowing visible signs of ageing. Experts quoted in the feature advise against crash diets and ultra-processed foods, which can dull the skin. While diet alone cannot stop ageing, the right foods can make a visible difference over time.
