Iran rejects Trump's bioweapons claims, warns against repeat of disproved Iraq WMD allegations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran on Thursday pushed back against US allegations that Tehran is developing biological or chemical weapons, citing the United States' widely debunked claims before the 2000s Iraq War that Baghdad had weapons of mass destruction. Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei questioned whether Washington expects the international community to forget then-Secretary of State Colin Powell's February 2003 presentation to the Security Council on Baghdad's alleged weapons of mass destruction. He said those claims were used to justify the Iraq War, which the US launched the following month.

Baqaei said the allegations against Iraq were used to build public support for the 2003 invasion, whose consequences were catastrophic for the Iraqi people, the wider region and American society. He said Iran now faces ominous new allegations with a narrative following the same fraudulent script. His remarks came after US President Donald Trump told Fox News on Wednesday that there are indications Iran may be working to develop biological or chemical weapons due to the damage done to its nuclear program.
