20-year-old teacher arrested in Florida over alleged relationship with 17-year-old student

A 20-year-old teacher in the US state of Florida has been arrested over an alleged romantic relationship with a 17-year-old student and sending him explicit photos. Mia Acevedo Gayardo was working as a long-term substitute teacher at Grace Education Academy, a private Christian school, where the alleged relationship with the 17-year-old student came to light. The Hernando County Sheriff's Office said police were informed on November 5 that the teacher was involved in a romantic relationship with one of her underage students.

During the investigation, police checked the student's mobile phone and found explicit photos that were allegedly sent by the teacher. Authorities said Gayardo worked at the school from August 7 to November 4, and she was arrested on November 13. She faces three charges: having a romantic relationship with a student by misusing her authority, sending harmful material to a minor, and involvement in unlawful activity through electronic communication.

Her bail has been set at 105,000 US dollars, and her next court appearance is expected on December 12. Police said she no longer works at the school and that they will prove the charges against her in court.
