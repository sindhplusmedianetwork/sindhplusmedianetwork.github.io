PM voices concern over denial of humanitarian aid to people of Gaza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has expressed deep concern over what he described as Israel's illegal measures to deprive the people of Gaza of basic humanitarian aid. He said tents can be seen submerged in water, while children in Gaza face hunger and severe cold. The prime minister directed the Foreign Ministry to use every possible means to ensure an immediate increase in humanitarian aid.

He said the scenes from Gaza were tragic, with families struggling in flooded shelters and children suffering in the cold. The directive aims to step up relief for the people of Gaza as conditions for displaced families worsen.
