Punjab CM Maryam Nawaz presented with special gold crown in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz was presented with a special gold crown in Rawalpindi on Tuesday. The gold crown was presented to her by the Jewellery Association in the city. The chief minister had arrived in Rawalpindi on Tuesday, where workers and members of the public gave her a warm welcome and presented her with the gold crown.

Her visit to Rawalpindi also included the inauguration of 35 electric buses and a review of ongoing development projects in the city. The chief minister was also scheduled to distribute laptops among students at Pir Mehr Ali Shah Arid Agriculture University during the visit.
