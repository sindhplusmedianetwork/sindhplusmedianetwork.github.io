ECO Meeting: Foreign Delegations Including Iranian FM Abbas Araghchi Arrive in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign ministers and high-level delegations of ECO member states continued arriving in Islamabad for the 30th meeting of the ECO Council of Foreign Ministers. Among those who arrived was Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi. Uzbekistan's Deputy Foreign Minister Bobur Masudovich Usmanov also arrived and was received by senior Foreign Office officials.

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar hosted a welcome dinner for the visiting foreign ministers and delegation heads. Dar said Pakistan is committed to stronger regional cooperation and shared goals of peace, development and prosperity.
