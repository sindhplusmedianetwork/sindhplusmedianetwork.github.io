BLA removes chief Bashir Zeb, hands powers to Senior Command Council

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The banned separatist group Baloch Liberation Army (BLA) announced late on Tuesday that its chief, Bashir Zeb Baloch, had been removed from his position as commander-in-chief. In a press release issued by spokesperson Jeeyand Baloch, the group said that under the organisation's constitution and following the Senior Command Council's decision, Bashir Zeb would no longer serve as its head. All administrative, military and policymaking powers have been officially entrusted to the Senior Command Council, described as the BLA's highest governing body, until a new commander-in-chief is selected.

The statement said the decision followed a joint meeting of the organisation's highest bodies on October 16, where commanders unanimously approved the leadership transition to give fresh momentum to its activities. The group said the transition followed principles established by Aslam Baloch during the organisation's 2018 restructuring, which ended lifelong leadership, and that military operations would continue without interruption. Bashir Zeb, a former chairman of the Baloch Students Organisation-Azad from Nushki, had succeeded Aslam Baloch after he was killed in a suicide attack in December 2018.
