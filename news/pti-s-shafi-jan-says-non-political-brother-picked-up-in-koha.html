PTI's Shafi Jan Says Non-Political Brother Picked Up in Kohat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf leader Shafi Jan has alleged that his elder brother Ehsan, a man with no political affiliation, was detained from his office in Kohat on Thursday afternoon. In a statement posted on X, Shafi Jan said the pickup happened at around 2 PM, carried out by police personnel accompanied by masked men. He asserted that if his brother was taken in an attempt to pressure him into abandoning Imran Khan's ideology, the effort would fail.

He said he had entrusted his brother to Allah and would not move an inch from the PTI founder's ideology, whatever the consequences. Shafi Jan demanded that the authorities disclose under which case or charge his brother had been detained.
