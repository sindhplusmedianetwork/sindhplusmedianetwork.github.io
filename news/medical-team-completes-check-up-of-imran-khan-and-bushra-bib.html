Medical Team Completes Check-Up of Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A medical team visited Adiala Jail in Rawalpindi and completed the medical examination of PTI founder Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi. The team was led by Dr Arif Khan. Doctors checked Imran Khan's blood pressure and cholesterol levels and reviewed the medicines prescribed to him.

They also asked him about his health. The team carried out a full medical check-up of Bushra Bibi with the assistance of the jail hospital staff. After completing the examinations, the medical team left the jail.
