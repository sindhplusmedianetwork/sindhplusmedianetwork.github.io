Afghanistan-trained militant Hanif alias Sheena arrested in Karachi's Manghopir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Rangers arrested Hanif alias Sheena, said to be linked to Fitna al-Khawarij, in Karachi's Manghopir area. Two mobile phones and a motorcycle were recovered from the suspect. A Rangers spokesperson said the suspect received military training in Afghanistan in 2012, including in handling weapons and making improvised explosive devices.

He went into hiding during operations in 2012 and 2013, and had recently moved to Karachi, where he organised a robbery gang. The suspect and his associates allegedly carried out a robbery of six million rupees in which two citizens were injured by gunfire. The arrested man has been handed over for further investigation, and the Rangers are looking for his associates.
