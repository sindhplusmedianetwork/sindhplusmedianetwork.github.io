Muridke: Man Slits Wife's Throat Before Children; Kids Escape

In a shocking incident in the 34 Chak area of Punjab's Muridke district, an allegedly drug-addicted man attacked his wife with a sharp-edged weapon in front of their children. The victim, Rabina, was left critically injured in the assault. Police identified the accused as Sabir, who they say is addicted to ice, and said he fled the scene after the attack.

The couple's daughter Aliza told police that her father attacked her mother before her very eyes, and when she tried to intervene he chased her with a knife as well. Aliza managed to flee with her two younger brothers and save their lives. Rescue 1122 shifted the injured woman from the tehsil headquarters hospital to Lahore for treatment.

Police have registered the case and launched a search for the accused.
