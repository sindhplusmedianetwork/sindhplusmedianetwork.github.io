Nicholls' ton rescues New Zealand after Bumrah's early blitz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Henry Nicholls scored a brilliant century to rally New Zealand to 243 for 5 at the close of play after a Jasprit Bumrah-inspired India had ripped through the top order on the first day of the first Test in Wellington on Thursday. Paceman Bumrah, who had not played Test cricket for a year, dismissed New Zealand captain Tom Latham for a duck with the third delivery of the day. Nicholls ground out the runs with great application while the wickets continued to fall at the other end, accelerating a little to put on 39 for the third wicket with Rachin Ravindra.

Bumrah claimed his third victim in the shape of Tom Blundell shortly after lunch to leave the hosts on the ropes at 70-5, and Glenn Phillips came out to join Nicholls. The pair, finding the batting easier as the day wore on, transformed New Zealand's fortunes in an unbroken partnership of 173, with Nicholls claiming his 12th Test century, a fifth at the Basin Reserve, with a single late in the final session. All-rounder Phillips played an equal part in the partnership and was closing on his second Test century at 94 not out at stumps.

Nicholls will resume on day two unbeaten on 113 after a scare on 102, when he spooned a delivery into the air and the catch was spilled. India skipper Shubman Gill had won the toss and elected to send out the bowlers after looking at the grass on the wicket.
