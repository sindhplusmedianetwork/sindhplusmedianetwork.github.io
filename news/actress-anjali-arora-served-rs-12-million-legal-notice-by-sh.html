Actress Anjali Arora Served Rs 12 Million Legal Notice by Show Organisers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian social media influencer and actress Anjali Arora has been served a legal notice claiming Rs 12 million in damages by the organisers of a show she performed at. The organisers allege that despite a contract for a 45-minute performance, Arora performed for only 12 minutes and then left the stage. They have also approached the Sarop Nagar police station in Kanpur seeking registration of an FIR against the actress.

The notice demands compensation for alleged financial losses as well as damage to social and professional reputation and mental distress. The organisers claim the crowd witnessed her behaviour, and say she became upset when her song 'Kacha Badam' was played — the very song that brought her widespread fame. The 26-year-old Arora responded on October 19 by posting three videos on Instagram, stating she had no objection to the song.

She said her concerns were about security arrangements and intrusion into her personal boundaries, claiming her hotel room doorbell was rung repeatedly and several people kept entering her room for photographs. The event organiser has rejected her claims about security arrangements.
