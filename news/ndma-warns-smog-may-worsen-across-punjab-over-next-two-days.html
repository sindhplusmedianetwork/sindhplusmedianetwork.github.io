NDMA warns smog may worsen across Punjab over next two days, issues health advisory

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Disaster Management Authority has issued an alert about worsening smog in Punjab. It warned that smog intensity may increase across Punjab districts over the next two days. The districts named in the alert include Lahore, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Faisalabad and Multan.

The authority warned that air pollution can cause breathing difficulty, eye irritation and respiratory illness. Children, the elderly and asthma patients were advised to take special care. Citizens were also advised to avoid unnecessary travel during smog and fog.
