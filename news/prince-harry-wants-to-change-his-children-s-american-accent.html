Prince Harry Wants to Change His Children's American Accent to British

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

British Prince Harry has revealed that he is trying to change the American accent of his children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, into a British accent, saying he wishes the children to speak in a British style. Speaking on the British television programme This Morning, Prince Harry said both his children adopted an American accent after being raised in America, and he is now encouraging his children to adopt a British style of speaking. Harry said that because of the children he also has to change his own words at home, as he prefers to use the British word for sweets but the children understand the American word more, so he also has to change his style.

According to Prince Harry, both children recently took admission in a new school in Britain and their confidence has increased notably after studying there. Prince Harry said wearing a school uniform has also proved to be a big change for the children. Prince Archie is 7 years old while Princess Lilibet is 5 years old, and both have spent most of their lives in America.
