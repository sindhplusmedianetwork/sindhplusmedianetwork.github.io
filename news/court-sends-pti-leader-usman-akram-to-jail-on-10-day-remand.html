Court sends PTI leader Usman Akram to jail on 10-day remand in May 9 case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police on Friday arrested PTI leader Mian Usman Akram in the May 9 case of the Askari Tower attack after his release from preventive detention. Police later produced Akram before an anti-terrorism court with his face covered, and the investigating officer asked the court to allow him to be shifted to jail so that an identification parade could be conducted. Judge Manzer Ali Gill accepted the request and sent Akram to jail on a 10-day judicial remand for the identification parade.

Earlier, the Lahore High Court disposed of a petition challenging Akram's detention after the Punjab government withdrew his detention order. Justice Ali Zia Bajwa heard the petition filed by Akram's wife, Kulsoom Akram, challenging his detention under the Maintenance of Public Order law, and the Lahore deputy commissioner appeared before the court in compliance with its earlier order. A law officer informed the court that the notification regarding Akram's detention had been withdrawn, and the judge disposed of the petition in view of the withdrawal.

Mian Usman Akram is a relative of jailed former provincial minister Mian Mahmoodur Rasheed.
