Anchor Rabia Ahsan's Husband Released on Bail From Adiala Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In Rawalpindi, anchor Rabia Ahsan's husband Ahsan Tahir has been released from Adiala Jail on bail in her murder case. The court had approved the accused's bail a day earlier on bonds of Rs200,000, but according to jail sources his release could not take place that day due to incomplete documents, while the victim's family had forgiven the accused. Jail sources said the release order was received by the Adiala Jail administration, upon which Ahsan Tahir was released; a day earlier Additional District and Sessions Judge Khurram Saleem had approved the accused's post-arrest bail.

The victim's family forgave Ahsan Tahir a day earlier, as Rabia Ahsan's mother, sister and brother Khurram Ahmad also submitted affidavits in court stating that Rabia was hit by an accidental bullet and Ahsan had not murdered her intentionally. The victim's brother Khurram Ahmad is the complainant in the case, and Rabia's murder case is registered at Saddar Beroni police station in Rawalpindi.
