Shehbaz Sharif: LNG Mafia Earned Billions From Imports; Its Era Is Over

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Addressing the federal cabinet on November 4, 2026, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said an LNG mafia had earned billions of rupees through imports over the years. He said the country had finally rid itself of the network after decades. Sharif said LNG imports were now being handled competitively and transparently through Pakistan State Oil and PARCO.

He directed that petrol pumps involved in fuel-subsidy fraud be shut down and heavily fined. He also ordered officials to ensure adequate availability of LPG and petroleum products during winter. The prime minister further said Pakistan had successfully tested an air-to-air missile.

He congratulated Field Marshal Asim Munir on the achievement.
