116th Birth Anniversary of Captain Muhammad Sarwar Shaheed to Be Observed on November 10

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 116th birth anniversary of Captain Muhammad Sarwar Shaheed, the Pakistan Army hero who received the country's highest military honour, the Nishan-e-Haider, will be observed on November 10. Ceremonies will be held in Faisalabad and other cities to pay tribute to his great sacrifice. Captain Sarwar was born on November 10, 1910, in Gujar Khan in the Rawalpindi region.

He embraced martyrdom on July 27, 1948, while defending the homeland on the Azad Kashmir front. He was the first martyr of the Pakistan Army to be awarded the Nishan-e-Haider after martyrdom.
