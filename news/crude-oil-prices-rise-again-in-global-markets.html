Crude oil prices rise again in global markets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Crude oil prices rose again in global markets amid ongoing tensions in the Middle East. Brent crude climbed 0.8 percent to $106.58 per barrel. US WTI crude gained 0.9 percent to $94.94 per barrel.

Rising tensions between Yemen's Houthi group and Saudi Arabia have heightened fears about oil supply. Markets are watching the region closely for any disruption to shipping or production. Analysts say the price trend will depend on how the tensions unfold.
