Houthis Claim Missile Strikes on Saudi Refinery and Defence Ministry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Houthi militia claimed on Tuesday that it struck Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil refinery and the defence ministry in Riyadh with ballistic missiles, as explosions rocked the Saudi capital. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said the attack on Ras Tanura, one of the kingdom's largest oil-processing facilities with a capacity of 550,000 barrels per day, had caused massive fires. Saudi authorities did not confirm the refinery had been hit, but maritime security agency Vanguard reported a fire in the area that was subsequently contained.

Saudi Civil Defence said shrapnel from the interception of two Houthi ballistic missiles caused material damage in Riyadh, while the Saudi-led coalition said it had intercepted and destroyed the missiles aimed at the capital. The Houthis also claimed to have targeted two military transport aircraft with ballistic missiles, one at Najran airport and another at King Khalid Air Base in Khamis Mushait, along with a separate drone attack on Abha airport. The strikes came amid weeks of escalating Houthi attacks on Saudi oil and civilian infrastructure, including Riyadh's international airport, raising fears of a wider conflict eight months after the Iran war began.
