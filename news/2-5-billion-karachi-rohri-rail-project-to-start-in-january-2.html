$2.5 Billion Karachi-Rohri Rail Project to Start in January 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Railways will lay a new 480-kilometre railway track between Karachi and Rohri as phase one of the ML-1 project. Railways Minister Hanif Abbasi told the National Assembly that civil work is likely to start in January 2027. The project is estimated to cost $2.5 billion.

The minister said the Karachi-Rohri section is among the most accident-prone stretches in the country. Between January 2022 and September 2026, 349 train derailments were recorded on the section. Faulty tracks were identified as the leading cause of the accidents.

The new track is expected to improve safety and speed on the busy route.
