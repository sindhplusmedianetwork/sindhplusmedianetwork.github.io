Gaza's Winter Misery Deepens as Floods Hit Displaced Families, Palestine Warns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Palestine's Foreign Ministry has warned that winter is worsening the already dire situation in Gaza. Heavy rain and flooding have hit displaced people living in tents across the territory. The ministry said more than 400,000 displaced people in flood-affected areas are at the mercy of the weather.

It demanded immediate and uninterrupted humanitarian aid for those affected. Palestine appealed to the international community to press Israel to open all crossings and aid routes into Gaza.
