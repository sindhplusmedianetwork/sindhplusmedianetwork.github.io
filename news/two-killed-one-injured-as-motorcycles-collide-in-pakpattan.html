Two Killed, One Injured as Motorcycles Collide in Pakpattan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two motorcycles collided on Kacheri Road in Pakpattan, leaving two people dead and one person injured. Police said a 40-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy died on the spot in the collision. The deceased boy's 12-year-old brother was seriously injured in the accident.

The injured boy was shifted to hospital for treatment. The incident was reported from Pakpattan, where the two motorcycles struck each other on Kacheri Road.
