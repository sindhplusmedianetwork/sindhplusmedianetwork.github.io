German Gen Z Protests Descend Into Chaos as Rioters Loot Frankfurt Shops

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Youth-led cost-of-living protests across Germany descended into chaos on Saturday night as rioters looted shops in central Frankfurt on Halloween. Thousands had joined the demonstrations organised by the campaign group Gen Z regelt, meaning Gen Z takes charge, to protest underfunding in schools, the high cost of living, public spending cuts and military service. According to authorities, violence broke out in Berlin, Cologne and Hamburg, where police were forced to use water cannon to disperse protesters who targeted emergency workers with fireworks, stones and bottles.

A large section of Frankfurt's main shopping street was closed off by police, and a spokesperson said many arrests were made in the city. Gen Z regelt spokesperson Lina Friedrich said the demonstrations were meant to fight for peace and freedom rather than war and coercion, calling for better support for health and education systems and opposing compulsory military service. The unrest followed similar youth-led protests in France, where more than 320,000 people demonstrated on Saturday, while clashes were also reported in Rome and Turin, and Portuguese police cleared a protest tent camp after thousands marched against a rental bill.
