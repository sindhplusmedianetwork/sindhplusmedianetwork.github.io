Nora Fatehi Records Statement in Sukesh Case, Faces Tough Cross-Examination Ahead

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian actress Nora Fatehi appeared at Delhi's Rouse Avenue Court on Wednesday and recorded her statement as a prosecution witness in the extortion case against Sukesh Chandrashekhar. The court had summoned her twice before, and on one occasion an exemption from appearance was sought, after which the court expressed displeasure over her absence and warned her to take the proceedings seriously. In her statement, Fatehi said she was invited to perform as an artist at a charity event in Chennai in December 2020 and received no payment for it, but Sukesh's lawyers raised questions about an expensive handbag, mobile phone and a BMW car allegedly linked to the event.

Sukesh's lawyer Anant Malik said documents and evidence would be presented during the cross-examination, and, referring to Section 319 of the Criminal Procedure Code, said that if evidence of another person's involvement emerges during the hearing, the court can proceed against that person as well. The case concerns alleged extortion of around Rs200 crore, and Fatehi is a prosecution witness in the matter, not an accused. According to Delhi Police, Chandrashekhar called Aditi Singh, wife of former businessman Shivinder Mohan Singh, during 2020 and 2021 while imprisoned in Tihar Jail, posing as a government officer and taking large sums of money on the pretext of helping secure her husband's release from jail.

Fatehi's name surfaced during the investigation because she attended an event in Chennai in December 2020 organised by Sukesh's wife Leena Maria Paul, and she told investigators she received expensive gifts at the event, including a costly bag and a mobile phone, and was also offered a BMW car, after which she was named a prosecution witness in the case and has denied any involvement in money laundering.
