Iran confirms attack on Kuwait's Ali Al-Salem air base, claims US air defences targeted

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's army confirmed on Wednesday morning that it had attacked the Ali Al-Salem air base in Kuwait, where American forces are stationed. In a statement, the Iranian army said its combat drones targeted American installations at the base, and that the US air defence system and drone and missile launchers were also hit during the operation. Iran said it would not bow to any pressure in defending its security and would answer any threat or aggression in a decisive and harsh manner.

The confirmation came about an hour after Kuwait announced that its air defence system had been activated to confront incoming drones and missiles, and that explosions heard in the country were the result of intercepting them. Air-attack warnings were also issued in Bahrain on Wednesday morning, with reports of explosions in different areas, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards said the targets of the attacks in Bahrain were radar systems used by American forces. Bahraini authorities said their air defence system destroyed the incoming targets, while the United States confirmed it had struck a missile site on Iran's Qeshm Island and President Donald Trump warned Iran of an unprecedented response to any threat to Washington or American interests.
