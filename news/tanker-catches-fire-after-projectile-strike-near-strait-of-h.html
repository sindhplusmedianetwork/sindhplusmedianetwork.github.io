Tanker Catches Fire After Projectile Strike Near Strait of Hormuz, Says UKMTO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A tanker caught fire after being struck by an unknown projectile while sailing outbound through the Strait of Hormuz, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency said. The British navy-affiliated agency UKMTO said the incident occurred 8 nautical miles north of the Omani town of Khasab, adding that no casualties or environmental impact were reported. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said shortly afterwards that an oil tanker had caught fire after hitting a mine while attempting to transit the Strait of Hormuz near Khasab, Iranian state media reported.

An explosion and smoke seen on Iran's Qeshm Island were caused by disposal of unexploded munitions, Iran's semi-official Tasnim news agency reported citing an official, after having earlier reported a missile strike there. The Saudi-led military coalition also said its air defences intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by Yemen's Houthis towards Saudi Arabia's southern Jazan province, with shrapnel from the interception falling in the al-Shuqaiq area. The coalition said relevant authorities were responding at the site, giving no immediate information on casualties, damage or whether a power station the group had earlier said it would target was hit.
