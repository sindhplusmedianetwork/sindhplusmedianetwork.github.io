Fakhar Zaman Falls for Golden Duck on First Ball Against England

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan opener Fakhar Zaman was dismissed for a golden duck on the very first ball of the tri-series match against England in Rawalpindi on Tuesday. England seamer Gus Atkinson bowled a loosener on Zaman's pads, which he tried to flick, and Joe Root took a fine catch at short mid-wicket. The 36-year-old batter, recently recalled to the side, could only stare in shock before walking back to the dressing room.

England captain Harry Brook won the toss and elected to bowl first, citing possible dew in the second innings. The visitors are missing key players Jos Buttler and Jofra Archer from their squad. Pakistan captain Shaheen Afridi said the team was unfazed at batting first, noting they had also batted first in the previous game, a win over Sri Lanka, on the same wicket.
