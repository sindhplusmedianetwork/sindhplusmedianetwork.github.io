Flour gets costlier as wheat imports stay insufficient amid shortfall

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Retailers have raised flour No. 2.5 and fine flour to Rs180 and Rs200 per kg respectively, up Rs10 per kg, as imported wheat arrivals fail to ease a persistent shortfall. Stakeholders estimate a 3-4 million tonne wheat shortage this season.

Annual demand for wheat stands at 31-32 million tonnes, while production has been recorded at 29.61 million tonnes. The gap between demand and production has kept pressure on flour prices in the market. Traders warn that consumers may soon have to pay Rs2,000 for a 10kg flour bag if the situation does not improve.
