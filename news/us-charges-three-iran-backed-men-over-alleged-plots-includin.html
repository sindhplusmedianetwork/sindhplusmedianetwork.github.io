US charges three Iran-backed men over alleged plots including RAF Fairford incident

By Sindh Plus Media Network

The United States Department of Justice has announced charges against three men it alleges were backed by Iran and involved in planning multiple attacks in the United States and Europe. The indictment names Iranian national Mohsin Mahajeri, Iraqi national Mohammed Ali Al-Hasani and dual Iranian-Iraqi national Mohammed Baqir Saad Dawood Al-Saadi. The three men are accused of planning attacks in Europe claimed under an alleged new group, Harakat Ansar Al-Yaman Al-Islamiyah.

The alleged plots include a terror plot at a United Kingdom air force base. The men allegedly planned around 20 attempted attacks in total. Iran has strongly denied any involvement in the RAF Fairford incident.
