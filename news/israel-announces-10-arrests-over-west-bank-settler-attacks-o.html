Israel Announces 10 Arrests Over West Bank Settler Attacks on Westerners

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli police said on Monday they had arrested 10 people in the occupied West Bank after weekend settler attacks on an American photographer and a French mayor drew international condemnation. In a rare action against rising settler violence, police said they raided several locations across the occupied territory before dawn and seized cars and potential evidence related to the attacks. Maj Gen Moshe Finchi, the Israeli police's West Bank commander, said suspects were arrested on suspicion of involvement in the violent incidents, violating a closed military zone order and illegal construction.

The violence began with Friday's beating of veteran photographer Heidi Levine, 65, on assignment for The Washington Post, who was taken to hospital with broken vertebrae. US Ambassador Mike Huckabee called the beating an "act of terror" and demanded punishment, and called Monday's arrests a "very positive development". One day later, settlers attacked a convoy of European diplomats in Taybeh, beating Bernard Baudoux, 78, the mayor of the northern French town of Aulnoye-Aymeries, during an olive-harvest goodwill visit.

Rights groups note Israel rarely prosecutes settlers when victims are Palestinian, with the group Yesh Din documenting 561 acts of settler or military violence since September 27. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, facing a tight race for a new mandate, has cast doubt on the circumstances behind the attacks, drawing unusually strong criticism from the opposition.
