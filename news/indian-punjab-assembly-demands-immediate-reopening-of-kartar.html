Indian Punjab Assembly Demands Immediate Reopening of Kartarpur Corridor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Indian Punjab Assembly has passed a unanimous resolution demanding the immediate reopening of the Kartarpur Corridor for Sikh pilgrims. The resolution was moved by Education Minister Harjot Singh Bains and received the full support of Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann. It urged the central government to take immediate steps to reopen the corridor, ensure uninterrupted access for pilgrims and ease travel regulations for Sikh pilgrims.

The resolution described the Kartarpur Corridor as a symbol of religious harmony and shared heritage between the two countries. India has kept the corridor closed since May 7, 2025, following the Pahalgam incident. The report was attributed to KMS.
