White House Rules Out Attack on Iran Before November Midterm Elections

The White House has ruled out any American attack on Iran before the November midterm elections. Deputy press secretary Anna Kelly told reporters that there is no intention to strike Iran ahead of the polls. She said President Donald Trump believes Iran is in poor condition both economically and militarily.

Kelly added that despite the Iranian naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz, oil shipments through the waterway are continuing at record levels. The statement follows earlier indications from Trump that he would prefer to avoid a military confrontation before the midterm vote. Tensions between Washington and Tehran have remained high over Iran's nuclear programme and regional activities.
