Sindh Job Reforms Fail to Curb Merit Complaints

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Complaints over merit-based hiring continue in Sindh despite a reform drive aimed at making government recruitment more transparent. The Sindh Assembly and the Prisons Department bypassed the provincial government's own recruitment mechanism, which engages the Sukkur IBA University testing service for BPS-5 to BPS-15 posts filled through the Sindh Job Portal. Instead of routing their vacant posts through that system, the two institutions engaged a private testing company to conduct the hiring process.

The move has raised questions about whether the reforms are being applied evenly across government institutions. The Express Tribune sought an explanation from Provincial Assembly Secretary G.M. Umar Farooq and IG Prisons Fida Hussain Mastoi on why the government university's testing service was set aside.

Neither official responded to the queries.
