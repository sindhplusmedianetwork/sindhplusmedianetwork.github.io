CDF Munir, Chinese Official Liu Haixing Discuss Regional Security at GHQ

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief of Defence Forces and Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir on Monday discussed evolving regional and international security dynamics with a visiting Chinese dignitary. Liu Haixing, minister of the International Department of the Communist Party of China's Central Committee, called on Munir at General Headquarters in Rawalpindi, the ISPR said. Both sides expressed their shared resolve to further strengthen the all-weather strategic cooperative partnership between Pakistan and China.

Munir highlighted the historic and time-tested bond between the two countries and stressed sustained high-level engagement. Liu appreciated Pakistan's contributions to regional peace and stability. Separately, Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar also held a bilateral meeting with the visiting Chinese minister in Islamabad.
