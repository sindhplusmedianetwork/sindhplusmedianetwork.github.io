Cabinet ratifies defence pact with Kuwait, approves new currency note designs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal cabinet on Wednesday ratified the Pakistan-Kuwait Defence Cooperation Agreement. The agreement was finalised on June 11, 2023, to promote cooperation between the two countries in the defence sector. A statement from the Prime Minister's Office (PMO) said the Kuwaiti government had ratified the agreement on July 19.

The ratification follows a series of high-level contacts between Kuwaiti and Pakistani military officials in recent months aimed at expanding bilateral defence coordination. The federal cabinet also approved the transfer of powers delegated to the Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa chief minister to the provincial governor for the conduct of day-to-day affairs during the period of emergency, with the governor submitting a monthly report to the federal government. The prime minister said a Rs160 billion development package had been finalised for K-P, with security given priority, and praised the province's people for resisting terrorism for decades.

On the recommendation of the Ministry of Finance, the cabinet approved the designs of new currency notes after a detailed briefing, with proposals invited from artists and designers across the country. The new notes would feature enhanced security measures to prevent counterfeiting and use modern materials intended to extend their lifespan, while the cabinet also approved the introduction of a Rs10 coin to replace the existing Rs10 banknote.
