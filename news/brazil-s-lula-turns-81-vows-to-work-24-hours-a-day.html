Brazil's Lula Turns 81, Vows to Work '24 Hours a Day'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brazilian President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva turned 81 on Tuesday, promising to work 24 hours a day following his historic re-election for a fourth term. Lula won Sunday's runoff against Flavio Bolsonaro with 51.8 per cent of the vote to the right-wing senator's 48.2 per cent. He is one of the world's oldest leaders, while United States President Donald Trump, who turned 80 in June, is only a few months younger.

In a video posted on Instagram, Lula said he was going to deliver the best term any president has ever served in the history of the country, and that he would dedicate 24 hours a day to making the country into the country everyone dreams of. Amid questions over his age, he boasted while campaigning that he was fitter than ever, jogging during events and posting videos on social media of his gym workouts. Following his shock first-round defeat to Flavio Bolsonaro, the son of jailed ex-leader Jair Bolsonaro, Lula's campaign had a social media makeover.

The president bought his first-ever cellphone and declared himself the king of the selfie. In a speech after his dramatic victory on Sunday, he thanked the rebellious youth of Brazil for campaigning on his behalf, and his Workers' Party called on supporters to celebrate his birthday outside the presidential residence in Brasilia on Tuesday.
