Sindh Government Tourist Resorts Post Rs22.3 Million Loss Despite Thousands of Visitors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Seven hotels and resorts run by the Sindh Tourism Development Corporation recorded a combined loss of Rs22.3 million during 2024-25, according to the Auditor General of Pakistan's audit report. The properties incurred Rs42.1 million in expenses against revenue of just Rs19.7 million. The biggest loss came from the resorts near Keenjhar Lake in Thatta, where expenses reached Rs22 million against Rs9.073 million in revenue.

This was despite 10,000 to 12,000 visitors arriving every week, mostly from Karachi. STDC Managing Director Syed Fayyaz Shah disputed the losses. He said a new computerised online booking system had improved revenue, but provided no figures.
