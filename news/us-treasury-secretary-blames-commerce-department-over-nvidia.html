US Treasury Secretary Blames Commerce Department Over Nvidia Chip Scandal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on Friday blamed the Commerce Department for failing to blacklist Megaspeed, the Singapore company at the centre of the scandal that prompted Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang to resign. Bessent said Treasury, the Defence Department, the US Trade Representative's office and the State Department had backed adding Megaspeed to Washington's trade blacklist for months. He added, however: 'But Commerce has the pen.' Megaspeed is suspected of helping Chinese firms get around US export controls by buying Nvidia chips for its data centres in Southeast Asia and routing them to China.

The company reportedly traded more than $4 billion worth of Nvidia chips over two years. The scandal has put the spotlight on how export controls are enforced and on which department carries responsibility for adding companies to the blacklist.
