Shehbaz seeks stronger Pakistan-UK economic partnership

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Friday said Pakistan and the United Kingdom share a friendship that deserves an equally ambitious economic partnership, as their bilateral ties are anchored in shared history, trade and peace. He was addressing a reception at the British High Commission marking the 78th birthday of King Charles III. The prime minister said he had adjusted his travel plans to attend the event, citing his long association with the UK and his warm ties with the King, and extended congratulations to the King, Queen Camilla and the royal family on behalf of the government and people of Pakistan.

He said bilateral trade had reached approximately £5.5 billion and that both sides should aim higher, adding that with renewed macroeconomic stability Pakistan offered opportunities in technology, renewable energy, agriculture, minerals and textiles. He noted that Pakistan International Airlines had resumed direct flights from Islamabad and Lahore to Heathrow since March, restoring a link after six years, and described the 1.7 million British citizens of Pakistani heritage as a bridge between the two countries. He said Pakistan had just returned from COP31 in Antalya, where he was reminded that many ideas central to the global climate agenda had been championed by the King decades ago, and that Pakistan was proud to be a member of the Commonwealth, which the King heads.

He said peace was the foundation of prosperity and that dialogue must prevail over confrontation, adding that Pakistan had played a constructive role this year in facilitating dialogue between the United States and Iran, including through the Islamabad talks in April. British High Commissioner Jane Marriott, who is due to leave Pakistan next year, said the UK was Pakistan's third largest bilateral trading partner, with trade reaching £6 billion over the past year, and noted that around 78 per cent of Pakistan's exports to the UK are clothing and textiles.
