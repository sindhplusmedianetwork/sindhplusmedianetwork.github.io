Israel is pushing American administrations into wars, claims Iranian foreign minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said the American people are tired of Israel pushing American administrations into endless wars. He said US President Donald Trump also acknowledges the fact that the vast majority of Americans are unhappy with Israel. In a post on the social media platform X, Araghchi said Americans no longer want wars in which the United States keeps getting involved because of Israel, and that they have had enough, as has the region.

He said American citizens are fed up with Israel entangling American administrations in long and continuous wars and are no longer prepared to tolerate the situation, while the people of the Middle East are also tired of continuous wars. His statement came after remarks by President Donald Trump in which he said strong dislike of Israel exists among the American people. Trump had included the Iran war, rising fuel prices and anti-Israel sentiment among the reasons for his party's losses in the midterm elections.
