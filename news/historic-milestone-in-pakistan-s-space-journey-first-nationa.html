Historic milestone in Pakistan's space journey; first national human space mission near departure

Pakistan's space programme has reached a historic milestone as its first national human space mission nears departure. Pakistani astronauts Muhammad Zeeshan Ali and Khurram Daud have successfully completed special space training in China. One of the two astronauts will travel via China's Shenzhou-24 mission.

SUPARCO Chairman Yusuf Khan called the development an important day for Pakistan's space programme. He said the first Pakistani astronaut will conduct scientific experiments during the mission.
