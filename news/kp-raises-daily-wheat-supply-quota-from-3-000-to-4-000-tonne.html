KP Raises Daily Wheat Supply Quota From 3,000 to 4,000 Tonnes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Food Department has increased the daily wheat supply quota for the province from 3,000 to 4,000 metric tonnes. The move follows a high court decision and directives from the cabinet's caretaker committee. According to a notification issued by the department, the additional 1,000 metric tonnes will be supplied from the warehouses of the National Reserve Centre (NRC) at Azakhel to active flour mills.

The quota will be distributed among all districts in proportion to the province's population of 43.84 million. Peshawar will receive an additional 116 metric tonnes daily, followed by Mardan with 67 tonnes, Swabi 46, Charsadda 45, Nowshera 43 and Lower Dir 40 tonnes. The decision came after the NRC's storage and enforcement officer reported that recent floodwater had entered the warehouses and damaged the lower layers of stored wheat sacks.

Six of the centre's 11 godowns were damaged while five remained safe, prompting the department to release the stored grain to avoid further losses.
