Mirpur deworming campaign to reach more than 117,000 children on November 25–26

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 117,000 children aged five to 14 years will receive deworming medicine in Mirpur district on November 25 and 26 under the sixth phase of the Deworming Programme, official sources said on Saturday. Preparations for the campaign were reviewed at a meeting chaired by Deputy Commissioner Mirpur Syed Imran Abbas Naqvi, which discussed inter-departmental coordination, logistical arrangements and measures to ensure the campaign's effective implementation across the district. Teachers will undergo training from November 16 to 23, while the campaign will be formally inaugurated on November 24.

The Deputy Commissioner directed all departments concerned to ensure timely medicine distribution, effective teacher training and public awareness among students and parents, describing the initiative as essential for protecting children's health and supporting their physical growth. District Health Officer Dr Fida Hussain Raja said the previous five phases had been successfully completed and preparations for the sixth phase had been finalised, including the appointment of union council focal persons, control room staff, ambulance services and tehsil supervisors. Focal Person Health and Certified Lead Trainer Dr Fatima Yaqoob briefed participants on teachers' training and other preparations, while private schools representative Iftikhar Ahmed assured full cooperation from educational institutions and stressed that children should be given breakfast before receiving the medicine and that parents should be informed about the necessary precautions.
