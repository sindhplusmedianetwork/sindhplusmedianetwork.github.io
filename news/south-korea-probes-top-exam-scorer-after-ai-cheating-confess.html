South Korea probes top exam scorer after AI cheating confession

By Sindh Plus Media Network

South Korean police have launched an investigation after the top scorer in a national tax accountant exam confessed to using artificial intelligence to cheat. The candidate, who ranked first in both rounds of the qualification exam held in April and July, confessed through his lawyer that he used AI to obtain answers during the tests. His score of 397.5 out of 400 points in the first round, with only one wrong answer out of 160 questions, had sparked accusations that he cheated using smart glasses, which he initially denied.

In a video posted on his lawyer's YouTube channel, the candidate said he deeply regretted what he had done. He said he used a miniature camera to photograph the exam questions and sent them to AI, then checked the answers on his smartphone. The examination is administered by the Human Resources Development Service of Korea, which said two supervisors were present when the candidate sat the test.

The agency's president, Lee Byung-hoon, apologised for the incident and said the agency would file a criminal complaint against the test-taker for obstruction of business. With the annual college entrance test, known as the Suneung, due on November 19, the presidential office said it was treating the issue seriously and officials decided to investigate all suspected cases of AI cheating across national qualification examinations.
