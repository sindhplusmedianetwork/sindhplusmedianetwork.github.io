KP policy will come from Islamabad, not Afghanistan, says Governor Kundi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has said the province's policy will come from Islamabad, not from Jalalabad or Kandahar. Speaking at a press conference in Peshawar, he said he fully supports operations against terrorism and that intelligence-based operations are needed to establish peace. He added that there can be no negotiations with those who take up arms against the state.

Kundi said the PTI government in the province had not been serious about ending terrorism, and that the province now stands at a point of no return where defeat is not an option. He said terrorists who had fled to Afghanistan were resettled here again, and an integrated strategy is needed to end the menace of terrorism. During the press conference, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Home Secretary Amjad Ali said resources for the police and the Counter Terrorism Department will be increased, with the police to be strengthened and equipped with modern technology.

He said Rs10.30 billion will be provided to the CTD and Rs70 billion has been allocated for the police, while the martyrs' package for police is higher than in other provinces. He added that the previous government had allocated Rs6.40 billion for 22 districts but released only Rs1 billion, and that more than 1,000 soldiers have been martyred in the war against terrorism in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
