Turkish Bride Nearly Swept Away by Balloons During Bosphorus Photoshoot

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A bride in Istanbul, Turkiye, narrowly escaped being swept into the air when strong winds caught the large balloons she was holding during a pre-wedding photoshoot. The incident occurred on October 26 on the banks of the Bosphorus strait, where the photoshoot was under way. A sudden gust of wind caught the bunch of large balloons in the bride's hands, and the force pulled her off balance, dragging her toward a nearby wall.

The photographer reacted quickly, grabbing her and pulling her to safety. A video of the incident has since gone viral on social media, showing the bride being lifted into the air by the balloons. Fortunately, the bride remained unharmed and suffered no injuries.

She later managed to complete her photoshoot.
