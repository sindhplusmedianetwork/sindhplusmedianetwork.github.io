Why is the trailer of Salman Khan's 'Matrubhumi', inspired by the Galwan clashes, being discussed?

The trailer of Salman Khan's film 'Matrubhumi', inspired by the Galwan clashes, has become a topic of discussion. The film features dialogues that are popular among people in northern India, including the line 'Ek Bihari Sau Par Bhari'. It also includes lines such as 'bravery is not only in fighting but also in stopping a fight'.

The trailer has drawn attention since its release, with viewers discussing its subject and its dialogues. The film takes its inspiration from the Galwan clashes. The discussion around the trailer continues as audiences react to its portrayal of the theme.
