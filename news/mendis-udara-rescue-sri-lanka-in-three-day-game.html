Mendis, Udara rescue Sri Lanka in three-day game

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kusal Mendis and Lahiru Udara rescued Sri Lanka with solid fifties in the three-day warm-up game against PCB Patron's XI at the Oval Ground in Islamabad. Sri Lanka, who opted to bat first, had a difficult start as the hosts' pacers exploited early moisture, and at one stage the visitors were 40 for 3. Mendis and Udara, who opened the innings, then took time at the crease and revived the innings with a solid 90-run partnership for the fourth wicket.

Ali Shabir broke the stand when he held Udara off his own bowling to give the hosts some relief. Sri Lanka finished the day at 223 for 7 declared. The three-day game serves as a warm-up ahead of the Test series.
