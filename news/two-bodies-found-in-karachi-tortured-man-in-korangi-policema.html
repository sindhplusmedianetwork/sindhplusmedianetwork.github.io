Two Bodies Found in Karachi: Tortured Man in Korangi, Policeman's Brother Shot in Ibrahim Hyderi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The tortured body of a 32-year-old man, a father of four, was found near Zaman Park in Korangi 1½ on Tuesday. The victim, who hailed from a district in Gilgit-Baltistan, was reportedly called out of his house by someone over the phone at around 10pm on Monday. Later his body was found near Zaman Park in Noorani Basti, Sector 38-B, within the limits of Zaman Town police station, and was shifted to the Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre, where the deceased was identified as Sajid Hussain, son of Altaf Hussain.

The victim's cousin, Maqsood, and other relatives told reporters that Sajid worked at a garments factory and was the sole breadwinner of the family. The victim had suffered brutal torture to his head, eyes and various other parts of his body, and both mobile phones he had been carrying were also missing. Meanwhile, the body of a 28-year-old man, the brother of a police constable, was found on Rehri Road in Ibrahim Hyderi.

He had been shot three times in the head, neck and arm, and police recovered three spent 9mm pistol casings from the crime scene. The deceased was identified as Athar Ali Memon, son of Gulzar Ali, and his body was shifted to the Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre. His cousins and friends said Athar worked as a barber, while his elder brother Azhar is a police employee posted at the Qayyumabad Base and his cousin Abid also works for the police.
