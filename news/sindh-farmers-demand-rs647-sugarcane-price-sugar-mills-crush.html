Sindh Farmers Demand Rs647 Sugarcane Price, Sugar Mills' Crushing From Nov 20

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Abadgar Board, a farmers' lobbying group, has demanded a rate of 647 rupees per maund for the sugarcane crop and called for sugar mills to start the cane crushing season from November 20. At a press conference at the Hyderabad Press Club on Saturday, the board's president Mahmood Nawaz Shah warned of taking recourse to a legal battle or protests if the demands, which they have been making since October, remained unheard. He lamented that so far neither had the mills started purchasing cane which had matured in October nor were farmers being offered a good rate.

Shah said the provincial government was under obligation under the Sindh Sugar Control Act, 1950, to notify both the rates of the sugarcane crop and the start of the crushing season. He asserted that farmers have the right to protest if the provincial government does not fulfil its responsibility, and said all organisations representing farmers in the province would soon hold a meeting to chalk out a strategy. He said around 700,000 acres of agricultural land was brought under sugarcane cultivation, and an overwhelming majority of it belonged to small farmers who face rising cultivation costs and falling crop prices.

The board's general secretary Zulfiqar Yousfani said sugarcane farmers in Sindh had not been getting a reasonable price for the last two years, and warned they may stop sowing sugarcane as alternate crops are available. The board said if the mills start the crushing season, around 1,500 acres of land could be freed per day from the sugarcane crop to allow sowing of wheat, and timely harvesting would also benefit wheat cultivation as the existing wheat shortage had led to flour price hikes.
