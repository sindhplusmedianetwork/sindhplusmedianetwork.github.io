55,417 tonnes of LPG unloaded at Port Qasim in six days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Six LPG ships docked at Karachi's Port Qasim over the past six days. The ships unloaded a total of 55,417 metric tons of LPG in the country. The unloading created the possibility of a Rs150 to Rs200 per kg drop in LPG prices.

Port sources said the deliveries began on 14 November with 1,621 metric tons from the first ship and 4,387 metric tons from Victoria Kosan the same day. Pacific Star followed with 12,115 metric tons on 16 November. Leonardo Kosan with 3,877 metric tons and Alisa One with 3,551 metric tons arrived on 17 November, while Sea Shine with 21,866 metric tons is anchored at Port Qasim.

LPG is currently selling at Rs480 to Rs700 per kg in the city, but officials and market sources expect the timely arrival of import consignments before severe cold to ensure abundant availability and relief of Rs150 to Rs200 per kg for consumers.
