Unbelievable basketball blunder: player scores in her own basket with 20 seconds left

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In a US college women's basketball match, New Mexico State player Emma Cristiano intercepted a pass and scored with 20 seconds left while her team led Southern Utah 61-60, but into her own basket. The commentator initially mistook it for a New Mexico State lead before the error was pointed out. Cristiano covered her face in embarrassment when she realised what had happened.

Her teammates then staged a late comeback, with Staci Chavino scoring the winner two seconds before the end for a 64-63 victory. A similar own-basket blunder also happened in an Arkansas match the same day.
