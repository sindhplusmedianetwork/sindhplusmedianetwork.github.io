Tortured body recovered in Karachi, one injured in firing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A tortured body of 32-year-old Sajid Hussain was recovered from Noorani Basti, Sector 38-B, Zaman Park in Karachi's Korangi area. The body was shifted to Jinnah Hospital for legal formalities. Police have begun an investigation to determine the identity of the killers and the motive behind the murder.

In a separate incident, Shah Faisal Khan was injured in firing near the Qasba Islamia Colony police post. He was also taken to Jinnah Hospital for treatment. Police said they were collecting details of both incidents.

No arrests had been reported in either case at the time of filing.
