KP Governor Orders Restoration of Dilip Kumar, Raj Kapoor Ancestral Homes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has directed the authorities to initiate restoration of the ancestral homes of legendary actors Dilip Kumar and Raj Kapoor in Peshawar at the earliest. Chairing a meeting on the city's historical heritage, he described Peshawar as a living embodiment of centuries-old history. He ordered special tourist buses for heritage sites, information boards at the motorway toll plaza and night tourism plans to strengthen the city's cultural appeal.

The governor also directed that all languages spoken in the province, including Urdu and Saraiki, be represented in the proposed Khyber Star Talent Hunt programme. Officials said restoring the two historic houses would honour the subcontinent's shared cinematic legacy and draw tourists to the old city.
