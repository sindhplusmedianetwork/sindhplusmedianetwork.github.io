NA finance panel warns Pakistan's GSP+ access at risk over human rights

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Assembly Finance Committee Chairman Naveed Qamar has warned that Pakistan's duty-free access to the European Union under GSP+ could be at risk because of the deteriorating human rights situation. The government, however, insisted that there was no such threat. The committee also expressed disappointment over the failed fixed tax scheme for traders.

The scheme attracted only 222 joiners against a hoped-for one million. The committee noted that enforcement action is planned now that the scheme has expired on October 31.
