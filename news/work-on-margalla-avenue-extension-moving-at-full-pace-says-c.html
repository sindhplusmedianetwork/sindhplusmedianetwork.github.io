Work on Margalla Avenue Extension Moving at Full Pace, Says CDA

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Capital Development Authority said on Sunday that construction work on the Margalla Avenue extension from N5 to M1 was progressing at full pace. The project will serve as a gateway for people travelling between Islamabad and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. CDA Chairman Sohail Ashraf said its completion would provide easy access to Islamabad for citizens travelling within and outside the city, particularly those coming from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Abbottabad and Mansehra.

He said the project would also benefit travellers heading to Islamabad International Airport. The 2.7-kilometre road starts from GT Road and will culminate near the Sangjani Grid Station. There it will connect with another 2.5-kilometre road being built by the National Highway Authority to link with M-1.

The CDA said 35 per cent of the work had been completed and construction was underway on a 24/7 basis.
