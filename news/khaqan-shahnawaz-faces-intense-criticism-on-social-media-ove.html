Khaqan Shahnawaz faces intense criticism on social media over inappropriate remarks regarding martyrs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani actor and social media influencer Khaqan Shahnawaz has come under severe criticism for his recent remarks about soldiers and martyrs. During a podcast, while discussing respect for different professions, Khaqan Shahnawaz said all people serving the country deserve equal respect. He further said soldiers also receive a salary in return for serving on the borders.

Social media users reacted strongly to the actor's statement and described it as inappropriate regarding the sacrifices of martyrs. After the controversy grew, Khaqan Shahnawaz issued a video message and apologised. He said his statement had been presented out of context, he respects the armed forces and martyrs from his heart and his purpose was not to insult anyone.
