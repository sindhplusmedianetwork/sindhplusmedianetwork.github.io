Sindh Signs Huawei MoU to Become Pakistan's First AI-Based Digital Province

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government and global technology giant Huawei have signed a historic memorandum of understanding aimed at making Sindh Pakistan's first artificial-intelligence-based digital province. The agreement, reached during Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah's visit to Huawei, covers cooperation on artificial intelligence, cloud computing, smart cities and digital government, including a provincial cloud system, data centres and the full digitisation of government affairs. It was signed by the CM's special aide on investment, Syed Qasim Naveed, in the presence of the chief minister and Huawei Pakistan's chairman.

The two sides also agreed to cooperate on AI-based smart parks and on training young people in modern digital skills. Murad Ali Shah stressed moving beyond merely purchasing technology toward jointly developing it, calling for measurable results for citizens through joint ventures, local capacity building and youth training. He praised Huawei's sustained investment in research and development, noting the company — founded in 1987 in Shenzhen and wholly employee-owned — spent 192.3 billion yuan on R&D in 2025, or 21.8 per cent of its revenue.

A briefing on the occasion noted that Huawei's decade-long R&D spending has crossed 1.38 trillion yuan, and that the SIFC-Huawei initiative targets training 300,000 Pakistani youth. During the visit, the chief minister also pushed for modern urban management and data-driven smart governance in Karachi, a city of more than 20 million, saying advanced technology was essential for timely identification of civic problems and crisis response.
