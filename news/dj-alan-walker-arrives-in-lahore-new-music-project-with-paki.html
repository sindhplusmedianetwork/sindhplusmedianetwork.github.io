DJ Alan Walker Arrives in Lahore; New Music Project With Pakistani Singers Likely

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

World-famous DJ and music producer Alan Walker has arrived in Lahore, delighting his Pakistani fans. The 29-year-old shared videos and Instagram stories of himself walking the streets of Lahore, which revealed his visit. His stories suggest he came to work on a music project with Pakistani singers Hamza Malik and Zain Zohaib.

Walker watched the music video of the singers' song Alfaaz and playfully asked who the masked Alan Walker-like figure in it was, also sharing a screenshot of Hamza Malik's reply. Malik responded that Alan Walker remains a mystery, but perhaps they could solve it together. The DJ later posted an airport video saying Malik had replied and that the song was being heard everywhere, announcing he would complete the project in Lahore.

He also shared a photo with Malik and Zohaib, though neither he nor the singers have shared further details. Alfaaz has made it to Spotify's Top 50 Global chart, while Walker's 2015 hit Faded earned platinum certification in 14 countries.
