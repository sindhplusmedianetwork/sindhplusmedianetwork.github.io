First Secretary-General of Makkah Defence Alliance Meets PM Shehbaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first secretary-general of the Makkah Defence Alliance, Lieutenant General (retd) Noman Mahmood, called on Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday. The prime minister congratulated him on assuming the office of the alliance's first secretary-general. He described the Makkah peace treaty as a historic milestone for regional peace and stability.

Shehbaz Sharif reaffirmed that Pakistan stands firmly with Saudi Arabia for its sovereignty and defence, and said Pakistan fully supports the kingdom's legitimate right of self-defence. The prime minister was also briefed on the strategy for expanding defence and strategic cooperation under the alliance framework.
