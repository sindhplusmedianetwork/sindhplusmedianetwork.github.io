Egypt's El Hammamy and Elaraby Sweep Both Titles at Karachi's Venus ABC Open

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Egypt's Karim El Hammamy and Rowan Elaraby have claimed the men's and women's titles respectively at the Venus ABC Open International Squash Championship in Karachi, completing an Egyptian sweep of both titles. In the men's final, El Hammamy defeated top seed Curtis Malik of England 11-6, 12-10, 11-3 in 48 minutes at the Asif Nawaz Squash Complex of the Defence Authority Creek Club. El Hammamy said the tournament had been tough despite his seeding, revealing that he twisted his ankle during the quarter-final and was not sure he could continue that match.

He also spoke warmly of his time in Pakistan, saying the people are always nice and very helpful, and that he plans to return in December for the Sindh Open. In the women's final, fourth seed Elaraby outclassed compatriot and second seed Amina El Rihany 11-6, 11-8, 11-5 in 33 minutes. According to the Sindh Squash Association, both winners received prize money of 5,415 dollars each along with the trophy, and squash legend Jahangir Khan and association president Adnan Asad were among those present at the closing ceremony.
