Starc Takes Six as Australia Fight Back in South Africa Decider

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mitchell Starc said a reset at lunch led to an Australian fightback after South Africa made a flying start on the first day of the third Test at Newlands on Tuesday. Starc finished with six wickets for 72 runs as South Africa were bowled out for 329 shortly before the close in a match which will decide the series between the two leading Test nations. The fightback looked unlikely as South Africa raced to 140 without loss at lunch after being sent in to bat.

Starc was one of the bowlers punished early on, conceding 39 runs in seven overs, including four successive boundaries by Marques Ackerman, who went on to score a rapid 95. Starc said the team did not have its best stuff in the first two hours, but the last two sessions were right up there, and the side tried to change the momentum in the field after lunch. Ackerman, playing in his second Test, attacked the Australian bowlers with dazzling strokes, reaching 82 not out at lunch off just 75 balls.

He attacked veteran off-spinner Nathan Lyon, dancing down the pitch and hitting him over the stand at midwicket for six, but tried one shot too many after lunch and was stumped by Alex Carey. Ackerman, 30, said South Africa would probably have been happy if they had been offered 329 before play, but admitted the side probably left a few runs out there.
