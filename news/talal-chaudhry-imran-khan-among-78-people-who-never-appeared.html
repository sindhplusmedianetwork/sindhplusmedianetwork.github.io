Talal Chaudhry: Imran Khan Among 7–8 People Who Never Appeared Before NAB Over One Constitution Flats

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry said the National Accountability Bureau gave every holder of a flat in the One Constitution Avenue project a chance to appear and prove their claim, but seven to eight people who obtained flats in unclear circumstances never came forward, including PTI founder Imran Khan. He was speaking at a joint press conference in Islamabad alongside Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal and Information Minister Attaullah Tarar. Chaudhry said the bureau had been given three months to bring a reference, and added that those who were cheated in the scheme bore no fault.

Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal said the prime minister had formed an inquiry committee into the fraud, whose report the federal cabinet approved on Wednesday. Iqbal said the report also recommended renaming the project, claiming the One Constitution Avenue name itself had been obtained through forgery, and that every legal requirement around the project would now be fulfilled. He acknowledged that rules and regulations were ignored in the project and that the CDA was also negligent, while promising compensation for the project's victims.
