Gold Prices Slip Rs3,600 per Tola as Market Awaits US Rate Signals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices in Pakistan declined by Rs3,600 per tola to Rs435,436 on Tuesday, while 10-gram gold fell Rs3,086 to Rs373,316, according to the All-Pakistan Gems and Jewellers Sarafa Association. Silver prices remained unchanged at Rs6,234 per tola. In the international market, spot gold held largely steady at about $4,138 per ounce, with US gold futures for December delivery easing 0.1 percent to $4,157.90.

Investors remained cautious ahead of next week's US Federal Reserve meeting and the release of personal consumption expenditures inflation data, which could offer fresh direction on monetary policy. Analysts said the market was moving within a tight range, with a breakout likely in the coming days depending on inflation data and signals from the central bank. Meanwhile, the Pakistani rupee closed at 276.87 against the US dollar, gaining three paisas from the previous session.
