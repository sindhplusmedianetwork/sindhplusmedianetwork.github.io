TikTok Removed 23.2 Million Videos in Pakistan in Q2

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

TikTok removed 23,243,711 videos in Pakistan during the second quarter of 2026 for violating its community guidelines, the company reported on Thursday. It said 99.7 percent of the removed videos were detected and taken down proactively and 94.5 percent were removed within 24 hours, while 519,075 videos were later restored after further review. Globally, automated detection accounted for 99.5 percent of removals.

Sensitive and mature themes were the largest removal category at 19.5 percent, followed by dangerous activities and challenges at 13.5 percent.
