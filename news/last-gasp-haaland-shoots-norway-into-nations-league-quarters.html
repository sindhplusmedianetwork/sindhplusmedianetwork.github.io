Last-gasp Haaland shoots Norway into Nations League quarters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Erling Haaland fired Norway into the Nations League quarter-finals with a last-gasp penalty. The goal completed a stunning comeback from two goals down, the report said. Haaland scored twice in the match, carrying Norway's fightback.

His winner came in the sixth minute of injury time in Copenhagen. The late goal sealed Norway's place in the quarter-finals. It also lifted Norway above Denmark into second place in the group.

The comeback turned a two-goal deficit into a dramatic late victory for Norway.
