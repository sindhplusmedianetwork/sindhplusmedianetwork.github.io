ML-I First Phase to Cost $2.1 Billion, Senate Told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has informed the Senate that the first phase of the Mainline-I railway project, covering the 480-kilometre Karachi–Rohri section, will cost $2.1 billion. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has constituted a committee to finalise funding arrangements for the project's second phase. Railways Minister Hanif Abbasi told the House that 67 percent of the country's railway track is unsafe and that 300 accidents have occurred over the past three years.

He said construction on the first phase could begin this fiscal year, with an implementation period of two and a half years. Pakistan was unable to secure $8 billion from China for the project, forcing the government to seek financing from local banks, land collateral and multilateral creditors. The minister said upgrading the line is essential for safer and faster freight and passenger movement.
