Two planes collide while taxiing at Washington DC airport, no injuries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two aeroplanes collided while taxiing at Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport in the United States capital. One of the aircraft was an American Airlines flight bound for Dallas. It struck the wing of another aircraft belonging to the same company that was bound for Chicago.

A winglet broke off in the collision and fell onto the runway. No passenger was injured in the accident.
