Two Oil Tankers Hit Naval Mines in Strait of Hormuz, Claims Iran's IRGC

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps navy has claimed that two oil tankers struck Iranian naval mines in the southern Strait of Hormuz, caught fire and were stopped. According to Express News, the IRGC said the tankers were trying to pass through a route that Tehran deems illegal. The IRGC statement gave no time of the incident, no vessel names, ownership or crew casualty details, and the claim has no independent verification or any other party's position.

AFP cited Sepah News in reporting the claim. The IRGC also claimed it shot down a US drone on Monday night — likewise unverified in the report. The claim comes amid sharply escalated tensions in the region, with Houthi attacks on Saudi infrastructure and a US military build-up in the Gulf.
