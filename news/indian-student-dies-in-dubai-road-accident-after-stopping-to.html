Indian student dies in Dubai road accident after stopping to call father about flat tyre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Naifa Bilqis Hameed, a 21-year-old Indian architecture student from Kerala, was killed in a road accident in Dubai. She was on her way to university when her car developed a flat tyre. She stopped the vehicle and called her father for help.

Minutes later, another speeding car hit her while she was standing behind her vehicle on Al Khawaneej Street in Al Rashidiya. She died on the spot. The incident was reported by Gulf News.
