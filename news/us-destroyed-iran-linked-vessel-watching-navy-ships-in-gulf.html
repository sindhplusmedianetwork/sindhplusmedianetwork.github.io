US Destroyed Iran-Linked Vessel Watching Navy Ships in Gulf of Oman, Says CENTCOM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States says its forces destroyed an Iranian-affiliated sailing vessel in the Gulf of Oman on October 6, and announced the operation on October 14. US Central Command said the dhow was conducting surveillance and targeting American Navy ships in the strategically important waterway, and released video of the strike. According to the command, the vessel's crew was safely removed before it was destroyed and efforts to repatriate them are under way.

CENTCOM also accused Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps of indiscriminately targeting thousands of civilian mariners transiting regional waters over the past seven months, in addition to US Navy warships. The disclosure came four days after the command announced it had disabled the Panama-flagged vessel Ocean Molica, also known as Arika Sun, whose crew ignored repeated warnings and tried to bypass the US naval blockade of Iranian ports near the Strait of Hormuz. The incident has heightened already serious tensions around the Strait of Hormuz, a corridor carrying a significant share of the world's oil shipments.
