Mehfil-e-Sukhan celebrates Urdu poetry in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The fifth edition of Mehfil-e-Sukhan brought together some of Pakistan's most distinguished poets for an evening of Urdu poetry in the federal capital on Saturday. Presided over by Abbas Naqvi and Shakeel Jazaib, the annual mushaira featured prominent poets including Kishwar Naheed, Iftikhar Arif, Anwar Shaoor, Abbas Tabish, Izharul Haq, Hamid Ateeq Sarwar, Haris Khaleeq, Ahmed Salman, Wasi Shah, Adnan Baig, Shakeel Jazib, Ashfaq Nasir, Saima Aftab, Rehman Faris, Zarnigar Abbas and Saba Uddin Hunzai. Their readings, spanning classical and contemporary Urdu poetry, highlighted the richness of the language and its enduring ability to connect people across generations and backgrounds.

Organised under Serena's Cultural Diplomacy Initiative, the fifth edition continued a literary tradition that began in 2022 and has since become a regular feature of Islamabad's cultural calendar. Aziz Boolani, Global CEO of Serena Hotels, said poetry had always been a meeting place and that for five years Mehfil-e-Sukhan had opened Serena's doors to the poets who give voice to Pakistan. The initiative is part of wider efforts to promote literature and the arts in Pakistan, including support for the Lahore Literary Festival and the Quetta Literary Festival.

The event also underscored the importance of preserving Pakistan's diverse literary heritage and providing opportunities for established and emerging voices to share their work.
