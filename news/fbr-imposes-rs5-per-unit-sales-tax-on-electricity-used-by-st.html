FBR Imposes Rs5 Per Unit Sales Tax on Electricity Used by Steel Units

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue has imposed sales tax at Rs5 per unit on electricity consumed by more steel manufacturers, melters, re-rollers and composite units. Amending Sales Tax General Order No. 16 of 2026, the board added three new entities to the list: KB Enterprises, GMB Steel Furnace and Rajput Steel Industry.

According to FBR, their eligibility was determined under criteria set in SRO 1245(1)/2026 dated 31 July 2026, based on scrap and electricity-unit consumption and scrap imports under specified HS codes. The board said the list may be revised from time to time as eligibility is reassessed. The measure is part of the government's broader effort to document the steel sector and bring more units into the tax net.
