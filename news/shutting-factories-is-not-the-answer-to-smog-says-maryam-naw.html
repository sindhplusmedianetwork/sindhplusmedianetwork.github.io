Shutting factories is not the answer to smog, says Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz on Friday said that shutting down factories is not the answer to environmental problems. She said protecting the environment is necessary, but closing factories is not the right way to do it. Thousands of workers are affected whenever factories are shut down, she added.

The chief minister said the relevant departments should keep a close watch on industrial units and issue warnings where needed. She said the government should avoid stopping factory operations while still enforcing environmental standards.
