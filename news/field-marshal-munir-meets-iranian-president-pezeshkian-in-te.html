Field Marshal Munir Meets Iranian President Pezeshkian in Tehran on Mediation Push

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir on Tuesday held talks with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in Tehran, Iranian state media reported, amid persisting US-Iran tensions in the Middle East. Iranian media reported that Munir arrived in Tehran along with a delegation of Pakistani officials — including Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi — for talks on bilateral relations and stalled US-Iran negotiations. The state-run IRNA news agency reported that after an initial working meeting with the foreign minister, the Pakistani chief of army staff met President Pezeshkian.

Pakistan has been a key mediator between the United States and Iran; its earlier mediation efforts produced an interim peace agreement in June, but the arrangement faltered as fighting resumed weeks later. The two sides remain at odds over the future of the Strait of Hormuz and Iran's nuclear programme. Munir also met Iranian parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, who said Washington could not regain Tehran's trust until it fulfilled seven conditions Iran set for reopening the Strait of Hormuz.

Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei told Munir that not even a tiny bit of enriched nuclear material would leave Iran and that there would be no negotiations until Tehran's conditions were met. There has been no comment from the Pakistani side on the visit, which is the fifth by Munir since the June agreement. The visit followed one by Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov on Monday.
