NASA builds battery that works in extreme cold for future Moon missions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The American space agency NASA has developed a revolutionary new battery that can keep working in extremely low temperatures, a breakthrough that could help its plans to send humans back to the Moon. According to a recent report by the technology website Interesting Engineering, the solid-state battery could power lunar rovers, scientific instruments and astronauts' emergency equipment. During thermal vacuum tests under NASA's SPEARS project, the battery successfully withstood temperatures as low as minus 170 degrees Celsius without any damage.

It also kept working at temperatures down to minus 80 degrees, although its performance dropped, while its relatively better performance was seen between minus 60 and plus 80 degrees. The technology could in future supply energy to vehicles, research instruments and emergency gear used under NASA's Artemis programme on the Moon. For now, the batteries are small laboratory samples that have not yet been tested in space.

Experts say that if the technology is developed further into practical use, it could also lead to safer and more efficient batteries for electric vehicles and other devices on Earth.
