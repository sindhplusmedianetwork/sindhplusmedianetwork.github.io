Apple's First Foldable iPhone Duo Goes on Sale; Queues Form in Sydney and Shanghai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sales of Apple's first folding phone, the iPhone Duo, began on Friday, with the company describing customer response as "nothing short of extraordinary." Within minutes of pre-orders opening last Friday, delivery dates had slipped to late January, and Apple capped pre-orders at two handsets per customer. In Sydney, the first store to sell the phones, customers queued before 8am; engineer Simon Krishnan, 48, paid A$3,899 — about $2,734 — for a 512GB unit. In Shanghai, 26-year-old Liu Wei queued from 6am to buy one.

In Hong Kong, the 256GB model retails for HK$17,499, roughly $2,231. Early reviews praised the design and a barely visible hinge but flagged camera compromises, including no telephoto lens, and apps not yet reworked for the new display. Market researcher IDC forecast Apple would take 44 percent of foldable phone value in 2026 and lead the category by 2027.
