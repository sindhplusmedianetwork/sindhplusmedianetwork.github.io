SPSC extends CCE-2026 application deadline to November 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Public Service Commission has extended the facility for submission of online applications and uploading of deficient documents for the Combined Competitive Examination, known as CCE-2026, until 5:00 pm on November 23, 2026. The extension gives candidates more time to complete their applications and submit any missing documents. The reckoning date for the upper age limit has been revised to October 23, 2026, following an amendment to the SPSC Recruitment Management Regulations.

The change affects how the age limit is calculated for applicants. The screening test for CCE-2026 will still be held on November 28, 2026, as previously scheduled. The announcement confirms the new deadline and the revised age date while leaving the test date unchanged.
