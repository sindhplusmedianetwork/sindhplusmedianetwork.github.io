Mohsin Naqvi Backs PCB Cricket Management, Rules Out Changes to Men's Administration

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) Chairman Mohsin Naqvi on Thursday said that he was fully satisfied with the way cricket affairs were being run at the PCB and he did not see any change in the cricket administration in men's cricket. Talking to reporters during a visit to the upgradation project of the National Cricket Academy (NCA) and Lahore City Cricket Association (LCCA) at the NCA, Gaddafi Stadium, he said he would back the team's decisions by all means, whether on too many debuts in the Test team or the absolute powers wielded by the men's cricket team head coach. On the inclusion of new members in the selection committee, he said he was fully satisfied with the team working and no changes were likely.

Naqvi said he had complete trust in the entire team making these decisions, believed they were making the right decisions, and would stand behind them and support whatever decisions they made. On the default of a certain PSL team, he said the teams had bid for the franchise keeping in view the financial challenges, adding that if a franchise sells the team, the franchisee was free to go ahead with the auction. He added that the PCB sold two new franchises in the PSL last year at a record price, and that the first two or three years of their operations were difficult for them, which was included in their feasibility calculations.

On the NCA upgradation, Naqvi said the academy would have the latest machinery and equipment to benefit young cricketers and would be completed by January 31 next year, calling it a state-of-the-art academy equipped with almost all the features and machinery available in the world. He said players' central contracts would be finalised in a day or two, and that the report on Mohammad Rizwan would be finalised by the NCCI and submitted to him in a day's time.
