Online Ads Used to Hack Users' Devices Without a Click, Spyware Installed Silently: Citizen Lab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Online advertisements shown on web pages have been used to install harmful software on people's devices that later spies on them, according to the online-rights research institute Citizen Lab affiliated with the University of Toronto. Through these malicious ads, spyware can be installed on users' devices even without them clicking on any advertisement. Moreover, the ads can be targeted in such a way that they reach precisely the intended people.

Citizen Lab said this is the first known instance of this kind of method, which it has named AdIntfection. It added that attacks of this type are possibly taking place on a much wider scale. Online advertising is often decided through real-time auctions, in which websites sell their ad space to the highest bidder.

The institute said this automated process happens billions of times a day in less than a second, every time a user opens a website that has space for showing ads.
