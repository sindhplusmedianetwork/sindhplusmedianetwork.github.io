Inquiry Ordered After Vehicle Breakdown in President's Convoy in Thatta

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A high-level inquiry has been ordered after a vehicle in the President of Pakistan's convoy broke down during his visit to Thatta. Inspector General Sindh has formed a three-member committee to investigate the incident, headed by Additional IG Karachi Azad Khan. DIG Logistics Zubair Dreshak and DIG Finance Tanveer Alam Odho are the other members of the committee.

The inquiry will determine how a faulty Toyota Revo double-cabin vehicle became part of the presidential convoy. It will identify the officers responsible for the inspection and deployment of the vehicle and examine the vehicle's prior faults and repair record. The committee must submit its report and recommendations within seven days.

The use of Special Security Unit funds and spending on vehicle maintenance, fuel and purchase will also be examined.
