Trump Accuses Iran of Fabricating Hormuz Attacks to Push Up Oil Prices Before Midterms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has accused Iran of fabricating reports of attacks on oil tankers and naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz to drive up oil prices ahead of the November 3 midterm elections. Writing on Truth Social, Trump said Iran is doing everything it can to raise the price of oil before the vote, and is even inventing fake hits, mines, and everything else. He claimed that more oil is currently passing through the Strait of Hormuz than before the conflict began, citing a Wall Street Journal report on Gulf oil flows.

Iranian state media had earlier reported that seven tankers were hit in the strait, with the Revolutionary Guard navy saying four were attacked while transferring oil and three struck mines. A US Central Command spokesperson called Iran's mine claims incorrect, saying American forces had cleared the shipping lanes and the strait remains open with most vessels getting through. Trump also accused Iran's regime of killing protesters, claiming 75,000 demonstrators lay dead, without providing a source for the figure.

Speaking to reporters at the White House before departing for a campaign rally in Iowa, he acknowledged the war had a political cost but insisted Iran must never be allowed to obtain a nuclear weapon. He suggested Washington could take further military action, saying significant damage had already been inflicted on Iran.
