Alliana Sworn In as Paraguay's President After Peña Impeached

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pedro Alliana has been sworn in as Paraguay's new president just as authorities put his impeached predecessor Santiago Peña under house arrest. All 44 senators present voted to remove Peña for misconduct in office over his links to the digital bank Ueno. The crisis followed the central bank's takeover of Ueno Bank on November 3, which left around three million customers, about 60 percent of the country's adults, unable to access their accounts.

Peña had submitted his resignation a day earlier, but the Senate proceeded with the vote to remove him from office. The charges against him included alleged interference in the bank's affairs, abuse of power and conflicts of interest. Prosecutors said charges were still being determined but could include influence peddling, and Peña was ordered to appear at the prosecutor's office on Saturday morning.
