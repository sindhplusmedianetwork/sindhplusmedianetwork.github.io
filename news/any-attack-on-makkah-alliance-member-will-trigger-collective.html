Any Attack on Makkah Alliance Member Will Trigger Collective Response: DG ISPR

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Director General of Inter-Services Public Relations Lieutenant General Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry said the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement was purely defensive in nature and had no offensive intent, warning that any direct or indirect attack on any of its three member states would trigger a collective response. In an interview with Independent Arabia, he said the collective deterrence sent a strong and clear message to elements of instability and chaos in the region. He reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to Saudi Arabia's sovereignty, saying the security of the Haramain Sharifain was paramount, non-negotiable and absolute as far as Pakistan was concerned, and that whatever support was needed was being provided and would be provided.

Chaudhry noted that the alliance had evolved in a very short period of time and was open to other countries that believed in collective deterrence, peace, stability and security of the region. Responding to a question about reports of Egypt and Syria expressing interest in joining, he said the decision on who could join, and when, rested with the political and military leadership. Regarding the possibility of providing direct military support to the Yemeni government, he said he did not know whether Islamabad had received an official request, but noted that the government would evaluate and respond to any request.
