Govt moves to address Chinese visa delays for CPEC project staff

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ISLAMABAD: As Chinese enterprises raise concerns over delay in visa process and movement of Chinese nationals working on CPEC projects, the government may direct the Water and Power Development Authority to address the complaints and operationalise the airstrip at Chilas to facilitate the movement of company executives. Sources told The Express Tribune that the government was working on different proposals to address the delay in issuing visas and movement of Chinese nationals associated with China-Pakistan Economic Corridor projects. They revealed that Chinese enterprises had reported delays in visa processing for both entry and extension cases, though the visa processing and extension approval time is seven days for Chinese nationals.

The ministry concerned wants the government to direct Wapda to expedite the procurement of helicopters and operationalise the airstrip at Chilas to facilitate the movement of Chinese nationals. Sources said that the government had streamlined visa approval under the Pakistan Online Visa System to facilitate Chinese nationals by reducing the processing time while ensuring adherence to the requisite security protocols. Previously, the security clearance process for Chinese nationals took approximately four to six weeks, but the processing time has now been standardised and slashed to seven days, representing a reduction of up to 83%.

Nevertheless, delays may occur due to deficiencies in visa applications, including the selection of an incorrect visa category and submission of incomplete documents. Comprehensive security SOPs for the safety of Chinese nationals, projects and infrastructure are in place, formulated in consultation with stakeholders including the Military Operations Directorate and the Chinese Embassy. A centralised dashboard has been established at the Ministry of Interior to facilitate coordination and monitoring of Chinese nationals across the country.
