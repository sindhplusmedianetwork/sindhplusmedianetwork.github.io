Robot Builds Antenna Part in Space Without Astronauts' Help

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A robot developed by researchers at the California Institute of Technology, named ASTRA, has successfully assembled part of a reflector antenna in space without the help of astronauts. The experiment is described as an important step toward building large structures in space, which could overcome the problem of limited space in rockets. Funded by the US defence research agency DARPA, the ASTRA robot used composite fibre tubes and aluminium joints to build a 1.4-metre section of a reflector antenna.

The project is part of the NOM4D programme launched in 2022, which aims to develop new ways of manufacturing in orbit. Another experiment is expected later this year to test advanced manufacturing techniques on the International Space Station. Experts say the success shows that robots could one day build large telescopes, antennas and habitats in space without astronauts having to do the work by hand.
