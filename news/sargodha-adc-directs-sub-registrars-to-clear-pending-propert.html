Sargodha ADC directs sub-registrars to clear pending property registrations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Additional Deputy Commissioner Revenue Khawar Bashir Ahmed chaired a meeting of all sub-registrars in Sargodha on Friday to conduct a detailed review of pending registries, incomplete documents and the recovery of government dues. The meeting discussed identifying missing documents so they could be completed immediately, recovering government funds that had been received short in registries, and ensuring the early disposal of pending registration cases. The additional deputy commissioner strictly instructed the sub-registrars to resolve matters concerning incomplete documents at once, ensure the prompt recovery of outstanding amounts where dues were deficient, and dispose of pending registries without further delay.

He made it clear that no negligence or unnecessary delay would be tolerated in the collection of government dues and in registry matters. He also directed all concerned officers to improve office efficiency and to complete pending tasks on a priority basis.
