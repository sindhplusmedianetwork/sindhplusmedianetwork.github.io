John Summit's Upcoming Concerts in Question After Fatal Tour Bus Crash

A tour bus reportedly carrying members of DJ John Summit's production crew was involved in a fatal crash in Marion County, Florida, on Monday morning, leaving one person dead. Florida Highway Patrol said a car travelling east on Highway 484 left the roadway and collided with the front of the bus stopped at an intersection. The car's driver was later pronounced dead in hospital, while the bus driver and nine adult passengers were unharmed.

Summit, who was not on the bus, performed in Atlanta on November 15. He is scheduled to play two shows at Miami's Kaseya Center on November 20 and 21, with no word yet on whether they will be affected.
