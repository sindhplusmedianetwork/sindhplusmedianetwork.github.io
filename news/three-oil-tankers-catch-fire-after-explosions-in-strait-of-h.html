Three Oil Tankers Catch Fire After Explosions in Strait of Hormuz, IRGC Claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has claimed that three oil tankers caught fire after explosions in the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday. In a statement issued on Sunday, the IRGC said the tankers had been passing through a route that Iran has declared unauthorised. The statement said the vessels caught fire after striking naval mines placed along that route.

Iranian officials have not disclosed the names, ownership or flags of the affected tankers, nor any details about their crews or possible casualties. The IRGC stressed that vessels must follow the routes approved by Iran for movement through the strait. The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most important trade waterways, carrying large volumes of global oil, and tensions there have raised fears for energy supplies.
