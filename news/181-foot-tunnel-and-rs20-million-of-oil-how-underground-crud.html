181-Foot Tunnel and Rs20 Million of Oil: How Underground Crude Oil Theft Was Caught in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Rawalpindi have uncovered a crude oil theft network operating through a 181-foot underground tunnel. According to the case record, the suspects rented a house near Chakri Road and dug the tunnel to reach Pakistan Oilfields Limited's main supply line. They then clamped an illegal pipe to the line and used it to steal crude oil.

The FIR says around 1,100 barrels of oil, worth more than Rs23.2 million, were stolen through the setup. Three suspects were arrested at the scene, and 1,431 litres of stolen oil were recovered from them. A case has been registered under the Petroleum Act, and police say four more suspects are still wanted in connection with the theft.
