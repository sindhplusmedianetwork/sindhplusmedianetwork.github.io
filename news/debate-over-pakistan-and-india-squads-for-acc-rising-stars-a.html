Debate Over Pakistan and India Squads for ACC Rising Stars Asia Cup

The ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament will run from 19 November in Qatar with eight teams taking part. Pakistan and India have both announced their squads for the event. The selections have sparked debate among fans on social media, who argue that many of the picked players are not genuine emerging talent.

Critics point out that players like Haider Ali and Mohammad Hasnain featured in the 2019 Emerging Asia Cup, while India's squad includes Tilak Varma and Abhishek Sharma, who have each played more than 50 T20 internationals. Defending champions Pakistan, led by Abdul Samad, open their campaign against the UAE on 20 November. Pakistan will face India A on 22 November.
