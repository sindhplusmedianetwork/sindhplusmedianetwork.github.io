Britain puts three nuclear submarines to sea at once, first time in five years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Britain has put three of its four nuclear submarines to sea at the same time for the first time in five years, according to British media reports. The three submarines at sea are HMS Vanguard, HMS Vigilant and HMS Vengeance, while the fourth, HMS Victorious, is currently undergoing repairs. At least one of the three is armed with ballistic missiles carrying nuclear warheads, the reports said.

Britain's security guarantee rests on keeping at least one of its four submarines continuously deployed at sea, and the unusual deployment has raised questions about whether Britain has changed its defence posture after Defence Secretary Wes Streeting recently warned that the country should be ready for war. The independent Royal Navy-watching organisation Navy Lookout noted that, since 1969, at least one ballistic-missile submarine has remained continuously at sea without a break. A Ministry of Defence spokesman said the country's nuclear deterrent is the foundation of national security, protecting Britain and its NATO allies every day and every moment.
