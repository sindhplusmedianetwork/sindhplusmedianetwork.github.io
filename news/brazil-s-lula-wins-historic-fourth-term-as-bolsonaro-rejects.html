Brazil's Lula Wins Historic Fourth Term as Bolsonaro Rejects Result

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brazil's leftist President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva has won re-election, securing an unprecedented fourth term in office after a closely fought runoff vote held on Sunday. Official results gave Lula 51.8 percent of the vote against 48.2 percent for his rival, Flavio Bolsonaro, a margin of about 4.4 million votes. Bolsonaro, however, refused to accept the result and said he would seek to have the vote annulled.

Lula, who turns 81 on Tuesday, will be 85 by the end of the new term in 2030. The result marks a dramatic comeback for the veteran leftist leader, who has now won the presidency for a fourth time.
