Meghan Markle Explains Distancing From Father Amid Child Abuse Material Allegations

Meghan Markle has explained why she distanced herself from her father, Thomas Markle Sr, 82, amid reports that he is under investigation in the Philippines over alleged possession of child sexual abuse material. Speaking to the New York Post, she said she was deeply troubled by the reports and that her sympathies lie with the children who may have been harmed. She said the decision to keep her father away from her children was taken to protect them.

According to her account, a letter explaining the decision was sent to London's Metropolitan Police and royal security in 2018. Thomas Markle Sr has strongly denied the allegations, calling them false, while his daughter Samantha Markle described them as unproven.
