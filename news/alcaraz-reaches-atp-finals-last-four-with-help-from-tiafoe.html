Alcaraz Reaches ATP Finals Last Four With Help From Tiafoe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Carlos Alcaraz has reached the last four of the ATP Finals after beating world number five Daniil Medvedev 6-2, 6-4. Frances Tiafoe's straight-sets win over Flavio Cobolli sealed Alcaraz's place in the knockout stage. Alcaraz played through a mid-match thigh scare to complete the win.

He has never won the season-ending Finals. Last year he finished runner-up to Jannik Sinner. The Spaniard will now aim to lift the title for the first time.
