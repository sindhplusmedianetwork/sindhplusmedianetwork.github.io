Muammar Rana and Mehnaz part ways as court grants khula

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Lahore family court has issued a khula decree in favour of Mehnaz, ending her marriage with Pakistani film actor Muammar Rana. Family Court Judge Zahid Bashir delivered the decision, while advocate Khadija Siddiqui argued the case on Mehnaz's behalf. Earlier, in a media interview, Muammar Rana had expressed hope for reconciliation, saying he did not want the relationship to end.

The petition, cited by 24 News, alleged that Rana used abusive language after drinking and had done so in front of their daughters multiple times, causing them mental distress. Muammar Rana had married Mehnaz Pervaiz in 1998 at the peak of his film career. The couple have two daughters, Riya and Rania.
