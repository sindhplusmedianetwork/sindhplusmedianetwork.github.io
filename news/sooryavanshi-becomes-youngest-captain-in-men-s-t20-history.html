Sooryavanshi Becomes Youngest Captain in Men's T20 History

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fifteen-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi became the youngest captain in men's T20 history at 15 years and 232 days after leading Bihar in India's domestic Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy on Saturday. He passed Croatia's Zach Vukusic, who previously held the record at 17 years and 311 days. The teenager had already become India's youngest men's T20 international in July.

He finished as this year's IPL leading run-scorer with a record 72 sixes. His appointment as captain at such a young age marks a new milestone in the format's history. The record underlines how early he has risen through the ranks of the game.
