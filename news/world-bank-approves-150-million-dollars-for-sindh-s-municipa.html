World Bank Approves 150 Million Dollars for Sindh's Municipal Services

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Bank has approved a 150-million-dollar programme to strengthen municipal services and property tax collection across Sindh. Under the plan, Karachi's annual property tax collection will be raised from 8.5 million dollars to 85 million dollars, while the payment compliance rate will be lifted from 20 percent to 50 percent. Twenty-five municipal councils in Karachi, serving around 3.58 million people, will benefit from the programme.

The Bank noted that more than 95 percent of the income of Sindh's municipal councils currently depends on provincial transfers, and 70 percent of property tax revenue will be spent on municipal facilities. Drainage, sewerage, schools and road repairs are part of the package, along with the improvement of fire stations and street lights, while a modern digital tax system will be introduced in Karachi. Property surveys will also be carried out in 20 municipal councils in other cities of Sindh, indirectly benefiting 4.5 million people, and 40 million dollars have been set aside for the property tax system in other urban centres.
