PTI Agrees to Fazlur Rehman's Charter of Democracy Proposal, All-Parties Conference Planned

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam (F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman met the PTI leadership in Islamabad and proposed a new Charter of Democracy, which the PTI agreed to after a separate meeting of its own leaders. Fazlur Rehman, accompanied by a delegation, met Senate opposition leader Allama Raja Nasir Abbas, and the two sides discussed the country's political situation, constitutional and democratic matters, and cooperation among opposition parties. The participants strongly condemned the imposition of emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, calling them against the sovereignty of the people and democratic principles, and expressed concern over the arrests of the province's chief minister, the provincial assembly speaker and members of the National Assembly.

They demanded the immediate release of political prisoners and the protection of political freedoms, and held detailed consultations on convening an All-Parties Conference, which they described as an important first step towards a new Charter of Democracy. After the meeting, PTI chairman Barrister Ali Zafar said the terms of the charter would be prepared through consultation, adding that the party believed there should be peace in the country and that every stakeholder should be taken along. PTI leader Asad Qaiser said the government had completely failed on the economic, administrative and governance fronts, and that dates of November 9 and 10 were under consideration for the All-Parties Conference, though not yet confirmed.

The PTI delegation included Barrister Gohar, Asad Qaiser, Barrister Ali Zafar, Aamir Mehmood Dogar, Zain Qureshi and Akhunzada Hussain Ahmed Yousafzai, while the JUI-F delegation included Senator Kamran Murtaza, Mir Younus Zahri and Usman Badini.
