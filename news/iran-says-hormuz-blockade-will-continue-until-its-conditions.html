Iran says Hormuz blockade will continue until its conditions are met

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has reiterated that the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz will remain in place until its conditions are met. Deputy Chief of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Mostafa Izadi told Iranian state media that Iran's armed forces are fully prepared to defend the country's sovereignty. He said Iran has made significant progress in its missile programme, air defence and drones.

The report noted that the Strait of Hormuz is a critical global artery for oil and LNG shipments. The strait has been at the centre of rising tensions in recent weeks. Any prolonged disruption there carries serious implications for global energy supplies.
