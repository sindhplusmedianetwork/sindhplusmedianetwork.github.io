Composer Deva, Behind Rajinikanth's Iconic Intro Music, Dies at 75

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Renowned Indian music composer Deva, 75, passed away after suffering a heart attack at a private hospital in Chennai's Kodambakkam area. Actress and politician Khushbu Sundar confirmed his death on X, expressing shock at the news. Born Devanesan Chokkalingam on 20 November 1950, Deva went on to compose music for more than 400 South Indian films.

His career began with the 1986 Tamil film 'Maatukaara Manaru'. He is perhaps best remembered for the intro music he composed for Rajinikanth's 1992 film 'Annamalai', which is still played whenever the 'Superstar Rajni' title card appears on screen. Tributes poured in from across the film industry for the veteran composer.
