Weekly inflation rises another 0.29%, annual rate reaches 12.31%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Weekly inflation in Pakistan rose by 0.29 percent, taking the annual inflation rate to 12.31 percent, according to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics. The bureau's weekly report said prices of 21 essential items rose in one week, while 10 became cheaper and 20 remained unchanged. Compared with a year ago, LPG became 114.88 percent more expensive and tomatoes 78.89 percent.

Electricity charges rose 52.38 percent over the year, while diesel became 39.27 percent dearer and petrol 36.34 percent. Wheat flour rose 33.95 percent in a year and chicken 31.81 percent.
