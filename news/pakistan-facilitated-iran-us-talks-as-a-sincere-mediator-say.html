Pakistan facilitated Iran-US talks as a sincere mediator, says Foreign Office

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign Office Spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan has said that Pakistan, as a sincere mediator, provided every possible facilitation to resolve the dispute between Iran and the United States. He said Pakistan helped bring the two countries to the negotiating table after they had not spoken to each other for 47 years, adding that political dialogue and diplomacy, not war, are the solution to conflicts and the way to lasting peace in the region. The spokesperson said the signing of a memorandum of understanding in Islamabad is proof of Pakistan's peace efforts, and that Pakistan will continue its diplomatic efforts and facilitation for regional peace and stability.

He said the negotiation process may take time and that extreme measures should be avoided in the meantime, and he declined to speculate on the question of a nuclear attack. He said the foreign ministers of the R4 countries discussed key regional and global challenges, including the conflict involving the United States, Israel and Iran, the Israel-Palestine situation, Houthi attacks in Yemen, and maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz, Bab al-Mandab and the Red Sea. The ministers reaffirmed their commitment to dialogue, de-escalation and the peaceful resolution of disputes and agreed to continue consultations for regional peace.

The spokesperson also said the Afghan Taliban government's allegations of civilian casualties in Pakistani counterterrorism operations are baseless and unverified, and that terrorist attacks in Pakistan have caused more than 3,500 deaths, with more than 1,400 people killed in 2026 alone. He added that last Friday night an LNG carrier linked to Pakistan, sailing in international waters in the Persian Gulf, was targeted, causing a minor fire, but all 25 crew members, including Pakistani nationals, were safe and the vessel returned safely to Qatar.
