Cricket Australia Expresses Full Confidence in Pat Cummins' Captaincy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cricket Australia has expressed full confidence in captain Pat Cummins' captaincy. An important meeting of the Australian board was held over speculation about removing Pat Cummins as captain of the Australian team, in which he was unanimously supported as captain. Cricket Australia chairman David Boon and chief executive Todd Greenberg met Pat Cummins before the meeting, during which various matters were discussed in detail.

David Boon said Pat Cummins and other leaders of the Australian team had promoted the team's culture over the past eight years, expressed concern for all those affected by the events of the 2018 South Africa tour, and said an independent forum had been set up to review the events of that tour, encouraging Australian cricketers and support staff to share their experiences in it. Captain Pat Cummins said there would be difficult conversations in the independent forum but it was the need of the time, that he would participate 100 percent in its proceedings, and that captaining Australia was a very big responsibility which he never took lightly. Cummins said he was grateful that the Cricket Australia chairman and chief executive had contacted him.

In March 2018, during a Test match between Australia and South Africa in Cape Town, Australian player Cameron Bancroft was caught on camera trying to alter the ball's condition with sandpaper, after which the Australian board banned then-captain Steve Smith and vice-captain David Warner for one year and Bancroft for nine months. Recently, Usman Khawaja stated in his book that more players should accept collective responsibility in the matter.
