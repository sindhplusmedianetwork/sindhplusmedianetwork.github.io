Iran's Araghchi Phones Foreign Ministers of 12 Countries in 48 Hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi held telephone contacts with the foreign ministers of 12 countries over the past 48 hours, according to the Iranian news agency. The contacts covered the regional and international situation, including the provocative actions of the United States and Israel. Araghchi spoke with the foreign ministers of Oman, Egypt, Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Cyprus, Turkiye and Kuwait, and also held telephone contacts with the foreign ministers of the United Arab Emirates, Tunisia, Iraq and Pakistan.

During the conversations, the Iranian foreign minister stressed the need for peace to be established through regional countries, without external interference. The diplomatic outreach comes as tensions remain high in the Middle East amid the continuing standoff between the United States and Iran.
