Qaiser Ahmed Sheikh leaves for Geneva for World Investment Conference

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Board of Investment Qaiser Ahmed Sheikh has left for Geneva to represent Pakistan at the 30th World Investment Conference, scheduled for November 10 to 12 in Switzerland under the World Association of Investment Promotion Agencies. He will speak at the conference and meet foreign investment leaders and corporate executives. He will highlight incentives in information technology, agriculture, renewable energy, mining, industry and manufacturing.

The government says its priorities are economic stability and ease of doing business. One-window facilitation is building investor confidence, according to the government.
