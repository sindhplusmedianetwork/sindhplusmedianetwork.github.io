AI advances coincide with sharp rise in cyber incidents, data shows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Clients of insurance broker Marsh reported 105 cyber incidents in late October, more than double the 48 reported in May. The figures come as advances in artificial intelligence coincide with a sharp rise in cyberattacks. Marsh Risk's US and Canada cyber practice leader Meredith Schnur said attackers are using AI-enabled and agentic tools to identify vulnerabilities.

A significant number of incidents could be attributed to AI, she said. The data points to a fast-changing threat, with AI tools giving attackers new ways to find weaknesses in systems. Organisations are now facing a growing number of incidents as the technology spreads.
