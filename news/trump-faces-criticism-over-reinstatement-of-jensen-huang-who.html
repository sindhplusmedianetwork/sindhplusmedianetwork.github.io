Trump faces criticism over reinstatement of Jensen Huang, who resigned over alleged Chinese spy link

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November after reports emerged of his relationship with a woman, Alice Huang, who is alleged to have links to Chinese intelligence. After his resignation the company's shares fell sharply, and although Huang was offered a return a few days later, the company initially turned the offer down. He has now been reinstated, and US President Donald Trump said on Monday that he brokered the deal, persuading the technology company's board to change its decision and bring Huang back.

Democrats have accused the Trump administration of sacrificing national security for financial gain, and Huang is due to testify before Congress in early December. His return helped end a week-long slide in the American stock market, with Nvidia shares rising 31 percent on Monday and adding almost one trillion dollars to the company's market value. Board chairman Harvey C.

Jones said in a statement that the board believes Huang is the right person to lead Nvidia and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of shareholders, employees, partners and customers. The board said it had received input from senior levels of the US government and was confident the reinstatement posed no threat to national security, although only last Tuesday it had said it had no intention of bringing him back. Official documents also show that Trump sold some Nvidia shares after returning to the presidency, making a profit of between 3.5 and 10.2 million dollars, while Trump and the White House have repeatedly denied any financial gain, saying his businesses were moved into a trust managed by his sons.
