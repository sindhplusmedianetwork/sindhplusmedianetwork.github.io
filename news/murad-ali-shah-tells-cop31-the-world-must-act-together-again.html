Murad Ali Shah Tells COP31 the World Must Act Together Against Climate Change

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah told the COP31 conference in Antalya that Pakistan and the global community must unite and take joint action against the serious effects of climate change. He said the effects of climate change are damaging lives and the national economy. He said Pakistan is among the countries worst affected by climate change.

He said recent floods damaged homes, farmland and schools in Sindh. He said the provincial government has launched a Green Initiative Programme on an emergency basis. The programme focuses on clean and renewable energy, tree plantation and expanding mangrove forests along Sindh's coast.
