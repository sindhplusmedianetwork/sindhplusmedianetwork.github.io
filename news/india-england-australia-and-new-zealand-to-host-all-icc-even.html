India, England, Australia and New Zealand to Host All ICC Events from 2028-31

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan failed to get any International Cricket Council event at least till 2031 as the ICC announced its activity programme for the 2028-2035 cycle on Sunday. India, England, Australia and New Zealand swept all events from 2028 to 2031. India will host the 2029 Champions Trophy and the 2031 World Cup jointly with Bangladesh.

Australia and New Zealand will organise the 2028 T20 World Cup. England will continue to host the ICC Test Championship final in 2029 and 2030. England will also hold the 2030 T20 World Cup together with Ireland and Scotland.
