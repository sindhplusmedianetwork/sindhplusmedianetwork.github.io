Medicine pricing needs serious attention, says health minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Health Minister Mustafa Kamal said more than 86,000 medicines have now been registered under the Track and Trace System. He said consumers can check a medicine's authenticity and price through their mobile phones. He added that procedures at the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan are now fully digital.

The minister said medicine pricing requires serious attention. He noted that the pharmaceutical industry is seeking an increase in medicine prices. He said the matter is currently before the federal cabinet.

He cautioned that higher prices would add to the financial burden on citizens. At the same time, he said unchanged prices risk shortages of medicines used for serious illnesses.
