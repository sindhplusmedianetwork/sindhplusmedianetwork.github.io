Rs6.8 Billion National Programme Prepared Against Diabetes; Risk Check in 30 Seconds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A comprehensive national programme worth 6 billion 80 crore rupees has been prepared in Pakistan to deal with the growing diabetes crisis, and under it the disease can be detected in just 30 seconds. The programme aims to integrate public screening across the country, awareness about the disease, early diagnosis and treatment facilities. The programme is planned to start from Islamabad as a model project and later be expanded to other provinces.

The programme will also use the RAPID score to assess diabetes risk in Pakistani people, through which a preliminary assessment of the risk of diabetes can be made in about 30 seconds with the help of just three basic questions. Dr Abdul Basit, Director of the Indus Diabetes and Endocrine Centre, said the number of diabetes patients in Pakistan is rising continuously and the disease is no longer limited to older people, as cases are also appearing in children. A comprehensive strategy has been prepared under the Prime Minister's Programme for Diabetes Prevention and Control, and details about the programme's formal dates and practical launch are expected to come out soon.
