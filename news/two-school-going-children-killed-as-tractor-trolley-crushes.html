Two school-going children killed as tractor trolley crushes motorcycle in Kot Radha Kishan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two children on their way to school were killed in a road accident in Kot Radha Kishan. According to rescue officials, the accident occurred on the Bhamb Khor Bypass in Kot Radha Kishan. Officials said ten-year-old Husnain Abdullah and six-year-old Hassan Ali were riding a motorcycle to school.

On the way, a speeding tractor trolley crushed the motorcycle, killing both children on the spot. Rescue teams responded after the incident, and the bodies were handed over after legal formalities. The accident has once again raised concerns about heavy traffic movement on roads used by schoolchildren.
