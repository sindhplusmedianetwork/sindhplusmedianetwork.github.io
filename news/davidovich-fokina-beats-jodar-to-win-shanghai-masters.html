Davidovich Fokina Beats Jodar to Win Shanghai Masters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Spain's Alejandro Davidovich Fokina won the biggest title of his career on Sunday, defeating compatriot Rafael Jodar 1-6, 7-6 (7/5), 6-4 in the Shanghai Masters final. The world number 26 fought back from a set down against the 20-year-old to claim his first ATP 1000-level title. Jodar, ranked 13th, required medical timeouts for thigh injuries during the deciding set.

The victory extended Davidovich Fokina's winning streak in China to 10 matches. The all-Spanish final thrilled the Shanghai crowd with its dramatic swings of momentum. It marked a career milestone for the 27-year-old, who had never before won a title at this level.
