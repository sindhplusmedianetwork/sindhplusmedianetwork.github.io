Viral objectionable video of Asad Mehmood is fake, created with AI, investigation finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A video attributed to Asad Mehmood, son of Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam (F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman and a former federal minister, went viral on social media, but the purported objectionable video is fake. The video circulating on social media is not genuine CCTV footage but was created with the help of artificial intelligence, and it was shared on November 8 on Facebook and other social media platforms with the claim that it shows Asad Mehmood with a woman. The investigation found several technical flaws in the video, including the CCTV text and the time-stamp numbers changing in unusual ways.

When experts analysed the footage, they found a 99 per cent likelihood that it was generated with AI, and according to the report, the man seen in the video also does not resemble Asad Mehmood. JUI-F spokesperson Aslam Ghauri also dismissed the video as fake, and told AFP on November 12 that the video was fabricated.
