Bradford: Knife Attack on Councillor Abrar Ali, Suspect Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In the UK city of Bradford, Councillor Abrar Ali was attacked with a knife while on duty, as a result of which he was seriously injured. He was immediately shifted to Leeds General Infirmary Hospital, where emergency surgery was performed on him. According to Councillor Ishtiaq Ahmed, Abrar Ali's condition is stable, however he has lost a lot of blood.

West Yorkshire Police arrested a 32-year-old man on suspicion of attempted murder after the incident. According to police, the suspect had climbed onto the roof of a house in Thornbury Avenue after the incident. The reasons for the incident have not yet come to light, while further police investigations are under way.
