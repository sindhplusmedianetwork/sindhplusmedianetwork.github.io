Study Finds 1.5 Million Pakistani Children Faced Online Sexual Abuse in a Year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 1.5 million internet-using children aged 12 to 17 in Pakistan, roughly one in 16, experienced technology-facilitated sexual exploitation or abuse in a single year, according to a new study. The report, titled Disrupting Harm in Pakistan and published in September 2026 by UNICEF, ECPAT International and INTERPOL, documents abuses including unwanted sexual images and conversations, demands for intimate photos, non-consensual sharing of images, and threats or blackmail. It found that 85 percent of perpetrators were known to the victims and that 44 percent of the abuse took place on social media.

More than half of the affected children told no one about what happened, and none used formal reporting channels. The study's release coincided with the final report of UN Special Representative Najat Maalla M'jid to the UN General Assembly, which flagged growing risks from artificial intelligence. Researchers called for stronger child-protection systems, better reporting mechanisms and greater digital literacy among children and parents.
