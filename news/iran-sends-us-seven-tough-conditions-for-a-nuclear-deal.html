Iran sends US seven tough conditions for a nuclear deal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has sent the United States new proposals for a nuclear deal containing seven tough conditions. The conditions include a complete end to the war, an end to the American naval blockade and the lifting of all US economic sanctions on Iran. Tehran has also demanded the restoration of its frozen assets, a reduction of the American military presence in the Middle East and compensation for war damage.

The seventh condition calls for a complete end to attacks on Iranian soil. The proposals were sent to the United States through Qatar, and American officials are reviewing them. There has been no confirmation yet from Washington on whether the conditions will be accepted.
