PPP Threatening Bloodshed Instead of Solving Public Problems: Hafiz Naeem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman said the Pakistan Peoples Party is threatening bloodshed instead of solving people's problems. Speaking at an inauguration ceremony in Model Colony Town, Karachi, he said corruption and mismanagement in KMC and the Sindh government were preventing solutions despite heavy budgets. He said Karachi's people lack basic facilities including electricity, water, transport and education.

He said Jamaat-e-Islami wants to solve these basic issues.
