Pakistan Draw 2-2 With Jordan in AFC U17 Asian Cup Qualifiers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan produced an impressive display in their final Group D match of the AFC U17 Asian Cup Qualifiers, but despite two goals from Muhammad Musa, the match against Jordan ended in a 2-2 draw at the Laos National Stadium on Friday. Musa gave Pakistan the lead from a penalty at the end of the first half and scored again in the 52nd minute to make it 2-0. Jordan pulled one back through Yusuf Taiser Mahmood in the 55th minute and equalised in the final moments of injury time through substitute Faisal Muhammad Al-Hourani.

Pakistan faced a difficult situation late in the match when Muhammad Armaad Sarwar was sent off after receiving a second yellow card, leaving Pakistan to play with 10 men. As a result of the draw, Pakistan finished their campaign in third position in the group with seven points from four matches. Pakistan had earlier recorded victories against Sri Lanka and hosts Laos, including a 3-0 win over Laos, while losing 0-1 to group winners Iran.
