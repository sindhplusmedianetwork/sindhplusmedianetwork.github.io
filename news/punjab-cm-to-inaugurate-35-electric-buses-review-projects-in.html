Punjab CM to inaugurate 35 electric buses, review projects in Rawalpindi today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz will visit Rawalpindi on Tuesday to inaugurate 35 more electric buses and review ongoing development projects in the garrison city. The chief minister will also distribute laptops among students at Pir Mehr Ali Shah Arid Agriculture University. A senior official of the district administration said the provincial government had provided 35 more electric buses for 10 routes in the city, while 45 buses were already operating on different roads.

He said that 80 buses would now operate on different routes across the city, with buses arriving at each stop every 20 minutes, and the service would run continuously from 6am to 10pm. Women and senior citizens will be allowed to travel free of charge, while other passengers will pay a nominal fare of Rs20. The chief minister will visit different roads and areas and check development works launched by the Punjab government to uplift the city and improve the condition of roads and bazaars, including the pedestrian streets at Commercial Market Satellite Town and Raja Bazaar.

More than 4,000 police personnel will be deployed to ensure foolproof security and manage traffic during the visit, with continuous monitoring of the chief minister's route from Safe City surveillance cameras.
