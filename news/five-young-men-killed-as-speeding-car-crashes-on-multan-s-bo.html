Five Young Men Killed as Speeding Car Crashes on Multan's Bosan Road

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five young men were killed in a traffic accident on Multan's Bosan Road in the early hours of Monday, Rescue 1122 said. The victims, all in their twenties and residents of Shujaabad, were travelling in a speeding Toyota Corolla when it hit a road divider, crashed into the wall of a nearby plot and caught fire. The accident occurred at around 4am.

Three of the victims were identified as aged 22, 23 and 28. Rescue officials said all five sustained head injuries and died on the spot. Their bodies were shifted to Nishtar Hospital for medico-legal formalities.
