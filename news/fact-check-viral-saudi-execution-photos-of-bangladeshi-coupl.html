Fact-Check: Viral 'Saudi Execution' Photos of Bangladeshi Couple Are Fake

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fact-check by iVerify Pakistan has found that viral photos and videos said to show the execution of a Bangladeshi couple in Saudi Arabia are fake or misleading. The images and videos, shared widely since October 16, claimed to show a Bangladeshi man and woman executed in Makkah for immoral acts. According to the fact-checking body, several of the pictures were generated by artificial intelligence, while others were taken from older, unrelated incidents.

One widely shared photo of a man weeping over a coffin actually came from a 2019 news report about a Bangladeshi worker who died in Malaysia. Videos claimed to show the couple's daughter were found to date back to February and May 2026. iVerify ruled the claims linking these visuals to the executions false, and urged social media users to verify content before sharing.
