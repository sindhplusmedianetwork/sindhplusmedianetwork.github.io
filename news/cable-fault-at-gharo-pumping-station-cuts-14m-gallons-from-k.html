Cable fault at Gharo pumping station cuts 14m gallons from Karachi's daily water supply

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A power cable fault at the Gharo Pumping Station has suspended water supply to the station's new and old pump houses and its filtration plant, the Karachi Water and Sewerage Corporation (KWSC) said. The fault has cut roughly 14 million gallons from the city's daily water supply. Port Qasim, Bin Qasim Town, Shah Faisal Town, PNS Mehran, PAF Base Faisal and Karsaz are among the areas affected by the shortfall.

KWSC said it is in constant contact with K-Electric to restore power to the pumping station. Meanwhile, residents of Gulshan-e-Iqbal's blocks, Tariq Road, Delhi Mercantile Society, Korangi and Landhi continue to face acute shortages after more than a month of disrupted supply. The latest fault has added fresh pressure to a city already struggling with a prolonged water crisis.
