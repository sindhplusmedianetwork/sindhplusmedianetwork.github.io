Business confidence falls sharply in Pakistan; foreign investors turn negative

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Business confidence in Pakistan has fallen sharply, with foreign investor confidence now at a negative level, according to the Overseas Investors Chamber of Commerce and Industry's business confidence survey. The survey, conducted across the country in September and October 2026, found overall business confidence fell by 36 percentage points to negative 23 percent from positive 13 percent in the previous survey. Foreign investor confidence fell even more, dropping 55 percentage points from positive 28 percent to negative 27 percent.

Confidence fell in all three sectors, with retail and wholesale down 45 percentage points to negative 25 percent, manufacturing down 34 points to negative 25 percent, and services down 31 points to negative 17 percent. Expectations for investment and employment also weakened, with investment confidence falling to negative 14 percent from positive 2 percent and employment expectations dropping 23 points to negative 6 percent. The share of businesses calling conditions negative over the past six months rose from 47 percent to 78 percent, while those expecting improvement in the next six months fell to 23 percent from 47 percent in May 2026.

The chamber's president said the results are an important message for policymakers and stressed stable government policies, a competitive tax system, affordable and reliable energy and continuity in government decisions. The chamber's secretary general said investor concerns cannot be attributed only to global uncertainty and pointed to problems in Pakistan's business environment that need timely action.
