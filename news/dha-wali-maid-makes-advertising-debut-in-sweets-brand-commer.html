'DHA Wali Maid' makes advertising debut in sweets brand commercial

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistani TikToker and YouTuber known as 'DHA Wali Maid' has appeared in her first brand advertisement, for a well-known sweets brand, alongside social media influencer Amtul. The influencer, famous for videos about a domestic worker's daily life, household work and family routines, has won popularity on social media with her distinctive style. She is now seen in the sweets brand's advertisement, with Amtul appearing alongside her.

After glimpses of the advertisement surfaced, social media users expressed happiness at her success, with many fans congratulating her and wishing her more success in the future. Recently, she also won an important honour at the Pakistan Influencer Awards, leaving well-known YouTuber Iqra Kanwal behind.
