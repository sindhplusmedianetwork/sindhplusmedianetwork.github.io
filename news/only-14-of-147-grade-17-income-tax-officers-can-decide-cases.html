Only 14 of 147 Grade-17 income tax officers can decide cases: report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A test of Federal Board of Revenue officers in Karachi found that only 14 of 147 officers who took the income tax test were capable of deciding cases, while 15 of 150 passed the sales tax test in that respect. The report found officers can draft notices but struggle to understand the law and make decisions, with no officer reaching the 60 percent pass mark in the sales tax test. It recommended immediate modern training for officers and noted the department lacks an effective system to check their work.

The findings have raised questions about the quality of tax administration in the country's largest city. Officials said the report would be used to improve training and oversight.
