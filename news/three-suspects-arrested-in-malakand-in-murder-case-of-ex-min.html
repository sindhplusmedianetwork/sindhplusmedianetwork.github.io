Three Suspects Arrested in Malakand in Murder Case of Ex-Minister Liaquat Bhatti

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hafizabad police have arrested three suspects in Malakand in the murder case of former minister of state for Housing and Works Liaquat Bhatti. Police recovered three stolen vehicles and weapons from the suspects' possession. According to the FIR, four personal guards allegedly strangled Liaquat Abbas Bhatti on the night between November 18 and 19.

The incident took place at his residence attached to a petrol pump. The guards allegedly looted cash, jewellery, vehicles and weapons before fleeing. Police said details of the motive and the looted amount will emerge after the investigation is complete.
