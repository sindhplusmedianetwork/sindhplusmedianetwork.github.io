Athlete Irshadullah Khattak runs over 550 km from Karak to Lahore in seven days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Young athlete Irshadullah Khattak ran more than 550 kilometres from Karak district in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to Lahore in seven consecutive days, drawing attention for his extraordinary physical ability, determination and resolve. He was warmly received at the Sports Board Punjab in Lahore, where Director General Sports Punjab Muhammad Tariq Qureshi praised his efforts and presented him with a cheque of Rs70,000. Khattak said his run was meant to spread a message of peace, love, brotherhood and national unity and to encourage young people towards healthy activities, and he aims next to complete a historic run from Lahore to China.

Qureshi said Pakistan's young people have immense talent and need proper opportunities and encouragement, adding that the Sports Board Punjab will continue supporting players who show outstanding performance. Khattak said encouraging young people towards positive and healthy activities was the main purpose of his campaign, and he will move forward with determination to complete his mission of a historic run from Lahore to China. Earlier this year, in July, Khattak also completed a 175-kilometre run from Karak to Peshawar in 24 hours.

Deputy Director Zahoor Ahmed and other officials were also present at the reception ceremony.
