Musk Promises a SpaceX Share for Every Child in America

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tech billionaire Elon Musk has announced that he will give every child in the United States a share in his SpaceX rocket company next year. Writing on X on Monday, Musk, described as the world's richest man, said the move would be the first of many, but gave no details of how the scheme would work. There are around 73 million children under the age of 18 in the United States, according to 2024 data from the US Census Bureau.

Musk owns 38 per cent of SpaceX, according to Forbes. The announcement came after US President Donald Trump promised last month to give every American adult 5,000 dollars if his Republican party retains control of Congress in November's midterm elections. SpaceX set the record for the largest initial public offering in history, raising 85.7 billion dollars in June.

The company's Starship megarocket reached orbit for the first time in September, a milestone for the rocket central to Musk's Mars ambitions.
