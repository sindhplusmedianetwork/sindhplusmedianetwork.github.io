Pakistan calls for curbs on Security Council veto power at UN debate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan on Tuesday called for comprehensive, democratic and equitable reform of the United Nations Security Council during a General Assembly debate on the use of the veto. Its delegate, Gul Qaiser Sarwani, warned that misuse of veto power undermines the council's effectiveness, weakens the credibility of the United Nations and perpetuates inequalities within the international system. He said strategic rivalries and narrow interests among the permanent members had repeatedly obstructed collective action on international peace and security.

He highlighted the General Assembly's universal membership and democratic decision-making, saying it could respond when the Security Council was unable to act, including because of the exercise of a veto. Pakistan opposed the creation of any new permanent seats in an expanded council and opposed extending veto privileges. The delegate said Pakistan favoured abolition of the veto or, at a minimum, severe restrictions on its use, and reaffirmed support for the General Assembly debate that follows the exercise of a veto.

He added that any restrictions must remain consistent with the UN Charter and be combined with enhanced representation and a stronger role for elected members.
