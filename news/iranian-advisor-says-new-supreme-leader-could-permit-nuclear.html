Iranian Advisor Says New Supreme Leader Could Permit Nuclear Weapons 'At Any Time'

An Iranian national security advisor has said that Iran's new Supreme Leader Mujtaba Khamenei could issue a new religious decree permitting nuclear weapons at any time. The advisor added that no one could stop him from doing so. The claim follows a Financial Times report that hardline support for building a bomb has risen above 50 percent following US-Israeli strikes.

The report also noted growing calls inside Iran to withdraw from the Non-Proliferation Treaty. Iran's official position remains that its nuclear programme is entirely peaceful. The remarks are likely to deepen international concern over Tehran's intentions.

Western governments have long suspected Iran of seeking nuclear weapons capability, a charge Tehran denies.
