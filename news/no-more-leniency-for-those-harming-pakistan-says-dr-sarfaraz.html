No more leniency for those harming Pakistan, says Dr Sarfaraz Ali

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dr Sarfaraz Ali, adviser on tourism to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi, said at an Iqbal Day ceremony at Nishtar Hall in Peshawar that today's Pakistan is the realisation of the dream Allama Iqbal saw. He said terrorists wanted to deprive the youth of those dreams, and that those who harm the country and look at it with ill intent would now face action. He added that much patience had already been shown and no more would be tolerated, and that the bright future of the children had to be secured.

He said he would speak to the prime minister about reviving the Jashn-e-Khyber festival and bringing the Safe Games and the PSL to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, adding that practical development work would now be visible in the province. The ceremony was organised by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Culture and Tourism Authority, and was attended by Tourism Secretary Fazl Khaliq, Director General Shahid Khan Mohmand, officials of the Abasin Arts Council and teachers. Students from different schools presented speeches and tableaus on the life and philosophy of Allama Iqbal, while some students also drew pictures based on his life.
