Khawaja Asif Says Bureaucrats Are More Powerful Than Politicians, Demands Accountability

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has said that bureaucrats are more powerful than politicians because permanent rule rests with the bureaucracy, while politicians come and go. In an interview with a British broadcaster, he demanded accountability of all those who share in power, saying politicians have to go to jail and fight each other while the bureaucracy's power continues across governments. He stressed that the new generation should be told what the bureaucracy has done to the country over the past 78 years.

The remarks came against the backdrop of his recent social media post in which he raised questions about the dual citizenship of bureaucrats and the transfer of money abroad. He said he speaks out from within the system, and when asked whether he would raise the matter with the Prime Minister or in parliament, he said he would certainly raise it and was ready to speak on it in the house. He noted that in the country's history, bureaucrats have held the offices of president, governor-general and prime minister, and that the bureaucracy's role continued even during military governments.

He concluded that everyone who held a share of power, more or less, must be held accountable in some way.
