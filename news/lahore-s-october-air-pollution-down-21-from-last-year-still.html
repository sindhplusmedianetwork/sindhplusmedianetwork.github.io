Lahore's October air pollution down 21% from last year, still above safe limits

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Air pollution in Lahore fell 21 per cent in October compared with the same month last year, according to a new report by the Pakistan Air Quality Initiative. Despite the improvement, PM2.5 levels remained above Pakistan's prescribed limit on all 30 days of the month. The city endured 16 consecutive days of unhealthy air quality, with the average Air Quality Index recorded at 174.

Average PM2.5 stood at 100.5 micrograms per cubic metre, far above the national limit of 35. The report credited favourable weather conditions for the decline rather than emission cuts. It ranked this October as the eighth most polluted October of the past nine years, underlining how far the city still is from clean air.
