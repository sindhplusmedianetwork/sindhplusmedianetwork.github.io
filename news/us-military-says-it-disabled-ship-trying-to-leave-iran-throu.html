US Military Says It Disabled Ship Trying to Leave Iran Through Gulf of Oman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States military has said it disabled a merchant ship in the Gulf of Oman that was trying to leave Iran in breach of a blockade, while Iran has vowed to respond. US Central Command said military aircraft struck the stern of the Palau-flagged merchant vessel MV Pacific Archer with precision weapons. The action came after the ship sailed from the Iranian port city of Bandar Abbas.

The strike disabled the vessel, but the civilian crew members on board remained safe. An adviser to Iran's supreme leader, Mohammad Mokhber, said the Strait of Hormuz would not be reopened and that Iran would not allow the blockade to continue. The United States reimposed a naval blockade of Iranian ports in July.

Earlier this month it announced the formation of Task Force Iron Gate, aimed at limiting the movement of weapons and sanctioned goods through Iran's land borders and the Caspian Sea.
