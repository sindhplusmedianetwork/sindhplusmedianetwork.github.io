UAE Rescues 22 Crew After Cargo Ship Catches Fire in Sea of Oman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The UAE National Guard has rescued 22 crew members after a cargo ship caught fire in the Sea of Oman. According to the National Guard, all those on board the ship were of Arab nationalities. The ship's crew alerted the coast guard, after which special teams were dispatched immediately for the rescue operation.

The rescue teams located the affected ship and safely evacuated all 22 crew members. The rescued people were given first aid and then handed over to the relevant authorities, and all of them are in stable condition. The authorities have not yet released the ship's name, the cause of the fire, or details of any loss of life or property in the incident.
