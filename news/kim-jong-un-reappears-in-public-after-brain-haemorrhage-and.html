Kim Jong Un Reappears in Public After Brain Haemorrhage and Death Rumours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

North Korean leader Kim Jong Un has reappeared in public, putting an end to unverified rumours about his health circulating on social media. State news agency KCNA released photographs of him inspecting a national exhibition of clothing and footwear. He was accompanied by his wife Ri Sol Ju and his daughter Kim Ju Ae, according to the state media report.

In recent days, posts on X had claimed that Kim had suffered a brain haemorrhage, fallen into a coma, or even died, with some suggesting he had been rushed to Beijing for treatment. None of the claims had any verifiable evidence behind them. Similar speculation about Kim's health in 2020 also proved to be false.

His public appearance appears designed to quash the rumours.
