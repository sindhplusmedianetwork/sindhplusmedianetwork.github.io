Mustafa Kamal Calls Rs10 Billion Corruption Scandal False

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Health Minister Mustafa Kamal has rejected the alleged Rs10 billion corruption narrative circulating on social media, clarifying that the government spent not a single rupee on the production and procurement of the vaccines. During a press conference in Islamabad on Tuesday, he said social media and opposition circles had been creating the impression that Border Health Services, an organisation under the Ministry of Health, had misappropriated Rs10.5 billion in procuring meningitis and flu vaccines for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims. He said all these allegations were baseless, and that Rs183.055 million was still safely held in NADRA's official account.

The minister said that, taking notice of media reports, the prime minister had formed a high-level inquiry committee, whose report was being presented to the media. According to the inquiry report, Border Health Services bypassed the federal Ministry of Health and the health minister during the procurement process, kept the facts about the correct quantity of vaccines ambiguous, and constituted a separate committee itself one month later instead of following the committee formed by the ministry. He explained that a public-private partnership had been established so that NADRA could be involved and its certification obtained, enabling people to pay the fee online, with the relevant company providing the vaccines at its own expense while the revenue was deposited directly into NADRA's account.

He added that, since it was a public-private partnership, P3A laws should have applied, but the relevant authorities made a technical error by applying Public Procurement Regulatory Authority laws, and that the procurement committee was also responsible because it did not stop the process in time. At the end of the press conference, Mustafa Kamal also spoke on political matters and said he would not withdraw from his support for the creation of new provinces under any circumstances, and would continue to raise his legitimate concerns about the rights of provinces and the people while working to strengthen the Muttahida Qaumi Movement.
