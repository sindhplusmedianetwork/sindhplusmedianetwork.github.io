Flu Cases Double at Karachi Hospitals Within a Week as Season Changes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi is seeing a sharp rise in flu cases over the past eight to ten days, senior health experts said on Monday. At the Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre, daily flu patients have doubled from 30 to 40 a day to 70 to 80 as the season shifts towards winter and night-time temperatures fall. Dr Irfan Siddiqui, who heads the emergency department at JPMC, said most patients were aged between 18 and 32 years and belonged to the labour class.

He added that people with weak immune systems, the elderly and children were more vulnerable, and that patients were generally taking longer than usual to recover. At the Dr Ruth Pfau Civil Hospital Karachi, cases have also doubled in just over a week, rising from eight to ten a day to 30 to 40 a day. Doctors advised people to wash their hands frequently, stay home when ill, avoid close contact with sick people, stay hydrated and get adequate sleep.

They also advised against consuming cold drinks and water, and said a yearly flu shot in early autumn was the most effective step against seasonal flu.
