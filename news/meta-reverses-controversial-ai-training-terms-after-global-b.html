Meta reverses controversial AI training terms after global backlash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Meta has reversed a controversial change to its terms for business accounts on Instagram and Facebook. The change would have allowed the platforms to scrape content from users' websites and use it to train Meta's artificial intelligence models. News of the terms in mid-October drew a strong backlash around the world, including from politicians in the United States and Britain.

Critics argued that the terms showed no respect for the rights of publishers and other business owners, because they were forced to give up control over their own websites in order to keep using Meta's platforms. In the weeks that followed, Meta tried to calm the criticism through public statements and changes to the terms. The company even removed the word 'train' from the new terms to ease concerns that users' writing and other material would be used to train AI systems.
