Sindh Cabinet Makes Judicial Commission's Report on Mir Raza's Murder Public

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh cabinet on Tuesday decided to make the report prepared by a judicial commission formed to investigate the circumstances and possible negligence in businessman Mir Raza Ali's murder investigation public. The decision was taken during a meeting chaired by Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah, according to a handout issued by his spokesperson. The judicial inquiry report, presented in seven separate sealed envelopes, will be uploaded on the provincial home department's website on Tuesday, and a copy will also be provided to the aggrieved family.

CM Shah also constituted a four-member sub-committee, comprising Home Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar, Local Government Minister Nasir Hussain Shah, Labour Minister Saeed Ghani and the Sindh advocate general, to ensure implementation of the report. The commission, headed by Justice Omar Sial of the Sindh High Court, had examined 49 witnesses during 15 hearings after being formed in August with a 30-day mandate. Mir Raza, a 25-year-old businessman and Institute of Business Administration graduate, went missing on July 28 after leaving his home in PECHS, and his body with gunshot wounds was found a day later in bushes in Gulistan-i-Jauhar; a second post-mortem termed the death 'inconsistent' with suicide, saying it was caused by a gunshot wound to the back.
