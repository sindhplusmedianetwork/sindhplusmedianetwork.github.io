Three Children Injured as School Van Collides With Car in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three children were injured when a school van collided with a car near Power House Chowrangi in Karachi. The injured children were shifted to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital. Rescue sources said the children were out of danger.

Both vehicles were damaged in the collision. The children were taken to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital for treatment, rescue sources said.
