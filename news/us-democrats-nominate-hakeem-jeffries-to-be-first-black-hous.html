US Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries to be first Black House speaker

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hakeem Jeffries is on the cusp of becoming the first Black speaker of the US House of Representatives. Democrats elected him their leader on Wednesday following a sweeping midterm election triumph. The 56-year-old New Yorker secured 251 votes in a closed-door secret ballot, with 11 votes cast against his nomination.

Jeffries must still win a formal vote of the full House when the new Congress convenes on January 3. If he wins, he would become the first Black politician to hold the powerful post. He would also stand second in line to the presidency.
