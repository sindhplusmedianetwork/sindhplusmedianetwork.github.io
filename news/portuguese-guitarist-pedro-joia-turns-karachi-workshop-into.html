Portuguese guitarist Pedro Joia turns Karachi workshop into a concert

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

It is always exciting to see a workshop turn into a bit of a concert, and that is what happened on Monday evening at the World Culture Festival 2026, when Portugal's musician Pedro Joia conducted a guitar workshop at Studio 3 of the Arts Council of Pakistan, Karachi. During the workshop, Mr Joia played some beautiful compositions to the delight of the audience. The musician said the guitar is an intense instrument, adding that technique must come first and expression later, 'like you learn to walk first', and that the techniques for the guitar are immense.

To demonstrate, he told students of the music department at the council how to play an open string with one finger, explaining that once a guitarist has learned to walk, he can use four fingers to play the notes and the thumb to strum the strings. Mr Joia said he plays the classical guitar as well as other genres such as flamenco, and noted that unlike the piano, which can sustain a single note for a long time, one note of the guitar dies quickly, so the player needs to hold the note tight. He also told the audience how different kinds of tuning techniques can change the sound of an entire composition, and shared that he started learning the guitar when he was eight years old, saying his father made him learn it and that a musician needs to work hard and practise at least five minutes a day.

During the workshop, young musicians also sought tips from him. Earlier in the day, the festival began with a children's play, Treasure Hunt, directed by Hassan Malick, which attracted a large number of schoolchildren.
