Pakistan Railways Police Chief Replaced; Sarfraz Falki Gets Additional Charge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has made high-level postings in the Pakistan Railways Police, replacing Inspector General Muhammad Waseem Fakhar Sultan. According to separate notifications issued by the Establishment Division, the IG has been removed from his post and directed to report to the Establishment Division. Sarfraz Ahmad Falki, a Grade-20 officer of the Police Service posted in the Railways Police, has been given the additional charge of IG Pakistan Railways Police.

He will discharge the additional responsibilities until a permanent inspector general is appointed. The Establishment Division has issued formal notifications for the postings and transfers of both officers.
