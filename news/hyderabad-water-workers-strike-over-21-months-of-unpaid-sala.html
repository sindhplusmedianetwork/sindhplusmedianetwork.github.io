Hyderabad Water Workers Strike Over 21 Months of Unpaid Salaries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A four-hour strike by Hyderabad Water and Sewerage Corporation employees over 21 months of unpaid salaries and pensions disrupted drainage operations on Monday and triggered sewage overflows in several parts of the city. The protest was called by the corporation's CBA employees' union, which demanded pension payments and dues owed to employees who retired from 2022 onwards. Workers staged a four-hour sit-in at the main disposal pumping station in Latifabad and stopped work at offices across the corporation, halting drainage at the pumping station.

As a result, sewage overflowed in several areas, including around Khuda Hafiz Chowk on Airport Road, where standing water disrupted traffic and caused difficulties for pedestrians. Union leaders said the corporation was facing a severe financial crisis and accused its administration of failing to secure funds approved in the provincial budget for utility bills and other dues owed by government departments. They urged Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah, Local Government Minister Syed Nasir Hussain Shah and corporation board chairman Kashif Shoro to arrange payment of the workers' salaries and pensions.

CBA General Secretary Abdul Qayyum Bhatti said the first phase of the protest would continue for a week, with a daily four-hour strike and a sit-in at the Latifabad pumping station, and warned that workers would move to the next phase and shut down water supply and drainage services across Hyderabad if there was no progress on the payments.
