1,851 Govt Houses in Islamabad Found Rented Out or Under Unauthorized Occupation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A major government housing scandal rocked the federal capital after an investigation uncovered misuse of official residences, with 1,851 out of 2,441 government houses found rented out or occupied by unauthorized individuals. A report by Special Branch raised concerns as 43,000 federal government employees are still waiting for official accommodation, while thousands of residential units meant for eligible employees have allegedly been occupied, rented out or otherwise misused. In its findings, investigators claim nearly 76 percent of 2,441 residences are under unauthorized occupation or rental arrangements, while the remaining 590 properties were reportedly locked, vacant or placed in other categories.

The investigation also reportedly identified government officers associated with questionable allotments, which attract disciplinary proceedings, cancellation of accommodation privileges and legal action. A broader government investigation examined 13,555 residential properties across Islamabad, with figures reported around 1,841 cases of illegal subletting, including 914 properties categorized as fully rented out or occupied without authorization and 382 involving partial subletting, while investigators also identified 2,593 residences involving illegal extensions, encroachments or other unauthorized construction. Government enforcement efforts have already resulted in the cancellation of more than 2,200 accommodation allotments linked to unauthorized occupation, and between July 2025 and June 2026 approximately 894 residences were allotted to eligible employees in Islamabad through the merit-based General Waiting List mechanism.

Islamabad has approximately 17,400 to 17,500 federal government housing units, while an estimated 43,000 employees remain on waiting lists, forcing many eligible workers to seek private accommodation at considerably higher rental costs.
