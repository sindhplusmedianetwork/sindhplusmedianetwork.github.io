Singer Kanika Kapoor announces separation from husband after four years

Bollywood singer Kanika Kapoor has announced her separation from husband Gautam Hathiramani after four years of marriage. The singer, famed for the hit song 'Baby Doll', shared the news through an Instagram story, writing that after much thought they were parting ways and thanking him for their time together. Kapoor and Hathiramani had known each other for about 15 years before marrying in London on May 20, 2022.

This was Kapoor's second marriage — her first, to Raj Chandok in 1998, ended in 2012, and she has three children from that marriage. She asked fans and the media to respect their privacy during the difficult time.
