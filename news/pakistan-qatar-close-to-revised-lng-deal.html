Pakistan, Qatar close to revised LNG deal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Qatar are close to concluding a revised LNG pricing deal, The Express Tribune reported. PSO and QatarEnergy have two sale-purchase agreements expiring in 2031 — a 15-year contract (2016–31) and a 10-year contract (2021–31). Pakistan asked that the price be cut from 13.37% to 10.20% of Brent under the first contract, while QatarEnergy declined and ultimately offered 11.36%, then made a final offer of 11.96% of Brent for both contracts.

PSO sources said the offer is final and Pakistan is to take a decision, with the company seeking government approval to seal the deal by November 3. The revised offer covers 66 cargoes a year against the existing 108 cargoes for 2027–31, with 210 cargoes deferred over four years after 2031. Earlier, in 2023, the government had authorised PSO to negotiate a reduction of two cargoes per month due to surplus LNG in the Sui system from declining power-sector demand.
