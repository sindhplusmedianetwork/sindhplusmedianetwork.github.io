Ahsan Iqbal Inaugurates Pakistan Tourism, Sports And Family Festival In Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's largest Tourism, Sports and Family Festival kicked off at the Islamabad Sports Complex on Friday, showcasing the country's cultural diversity, traditional cuisine, handicrafts and tourism potential. Federal Minister for Planning Ahsan Iqbal inaugurated the festival, accompanied by PTDC Managing Director Aftab-ur-Rehman Rana. Iqbal said tourism and sports were vital to Pakistan's vision of becoming a $1 trillion economy by 2035.

The festival, featuring cultural stalls from all provinces, musical evenings, concerts, flying lanterns, fireworks and sports competitions, will continue until November 8.
