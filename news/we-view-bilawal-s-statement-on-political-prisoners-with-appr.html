'We view Bilawal's statement on political prisoners with appreciation': Aamir Dogar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI chief whip Aamir Dogar said they view Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari's statement regarding political prisoners with appreciation. He was speaking on Geo News programme Capital Talk. The PTI leader said such a large number of women and elderly political prisoners had not been seen as are imprisoned today.

He said the current number of political prisoners is unprecedented in this regard. He added that there is consensus on South Punjab. A fifth province should be made, he said.
