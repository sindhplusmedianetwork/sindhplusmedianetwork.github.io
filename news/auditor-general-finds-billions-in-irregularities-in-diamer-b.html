Auditor General Finds Billions in Irregularities in Diamer-Bhasha Dam Project

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Auditor General has uncovered large-scale financial irregularities in the Diamer-Bhasha Dam, the country's major water and energy project, according to its audit report for 2025-26. By June 2025, physical construction had reached only 19.95 percent while financial progress stood at 39.67 percent, showing a wide gap between money spent and work completed. The report says more than Rs73.31 billion meant for land acquisition and resettlement was transferred to the Chilas deputy commissioner's bank account between October 2010 and June 2021, and over Rs4.96 billion of those funds was moved into 24 private banks, which the audit called a violation of treasury rules.

It also found that the main dam contract, worth more than Rs442.40 billion and awarded to a joint venture of Power China and the Frontier Works Organisation, did not fully follow the required eligibility criteria, while a Rs27.18 billion consultancy contract also drew objections. The contractor was paid more than Rs10.17 billion after the payment method was changed through a contract amendment, even though the related work of installing roller-compacted concrete had not been done. The audit further noted excess payments of over Rs674.5 million, delays in recovering a Rs1.92 billion mobilisation advance, and a cost increase of more than Rs91.32 billion, pushing the expected completion to 2030.

The Auditor General has recommended a full examination of all land and contractor finances and the recovery of unauthorised payments and outstanding dues.
