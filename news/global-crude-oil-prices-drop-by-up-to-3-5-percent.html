Global Crude Oil Prices Drop by Up to 3.5 Percent

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global crude oil prices continued to fall on Monday, with Brent crude down 3.5 percent to $92.44 a barrel and US West Texas Intermediate down 3.2 percent to $85.72 a barrel. The decline was driven by recovering Gulf exports and fears of oversupply in the market. Both benchmarks have now fallen for a second straight week, extending the downward trend.

Goldman Sachs forecast that Brent would average $88 next year and fall to $78 by the end of the year. The outlook suggests prices may stay under pressure as supply concerns continue to weigh on the market.
