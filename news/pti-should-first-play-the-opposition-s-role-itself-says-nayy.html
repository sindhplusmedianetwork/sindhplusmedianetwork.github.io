PTI should first play the opposition's role itself, says Nayyar Bukhari

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Secretary General Nayyar Bukhari has said the PTI should first perform the role of an opposition itself before asking the PPP to join it. He said the PTI is neither playing that role inside parliament nor outside it. Responding to the PTI's conditional offer of an alliance, he said the party should come forward with a political agenda, not merely slogans, adding that its only demand is the release of its founder.

He noted that Bilawal Bhutto has already invited the PTI to return to the standing committees, but the party has not yet responded. Bukhari also said the PPP wants the PML-N and the PTI to clarify their policies on new provinces, and that the party believes provinces should be created, but only through the procedure laid down in the Constitution. He said the PPP has not decided to leave the government, but such a decision could be taken depending on the circumstances, and the date of the Central Executive Committee meeting has not yet been fixed.
