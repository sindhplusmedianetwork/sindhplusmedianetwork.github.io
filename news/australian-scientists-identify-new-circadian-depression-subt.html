Australian Scientists Identify New 'Circadian Depression' Subtype Tied to Body Clock

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Researchers at the University of Sydney have identified a distinct new depression subtype called 'circadian depression', with their findings published in the British Journal of Psychiatry. The team analyzed clinical and genetic data from more than 10,000 participants. They found that disruptions to the body's biological clock account for nearly a quarter of severe, persistent depression cases.

This subtype is marked by extreme fatigue on waking, longer sleep during depressive episodes and night-owl tendencies. Importantly, patients with circadian depression respond poorly to standard serotonin-based antidepressants. The discovery opens the door to targeted therapies such as light therapy and melatonin-targeted medications.
