KP Government to Audit Rs800 Billion Given in the Name of the War on Terror

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government has decided to open the accounts of the Rs800 billion the province received in the name of the war against terrorism, and a formal audit of the funds will be carried out. According to reports, the government has demanded complete details of where and how the funds provided by the federation and Punjab were spent, announcing a regular audit. Information Adviser Samar Haroon Bilour said the use of the huge sum received in the name of the terrorism-hit province, especially the tribal districts, would be fully investigated.

She said it would be determined whether the funds were genuinely spent on ending terrorism, restoring law and order and improving the tribal districts. Questioning the use of funds in previous eras, Bilour said Rs800 billion was no small amount to be blown away like a kite, and every single penny would be accounted for. She added that the public has the right to know where the billions received for the war on terror went and what results they produced.
