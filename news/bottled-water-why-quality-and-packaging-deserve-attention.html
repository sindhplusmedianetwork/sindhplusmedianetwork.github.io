Bottled Water: Why Quality and Packaging Deserve Attention

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Experts say bottled and mineral water is not automatically harmful or automatically safe, and its safety depends on quality, regulatory compliance and storage conditions. Poor-quality plastic bottles can be a possible source of plastic components leaching into the water. Mineral water can contain minerals such as potassium, calcium and magnesium in varying amounts.

However, claims that it necessarily lowers cholesterol or treats constipation are not established. Experts also advise that water bottles should not be kept in the sun or in heated places, where quality can suffer.
