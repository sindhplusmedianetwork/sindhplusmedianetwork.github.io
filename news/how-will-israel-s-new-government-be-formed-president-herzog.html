How will Israel's new government be formed? President Herzog's key role

With Israel heading toward the formation of a new government, President Isaac Herzog is set to play a decisive role in the process. In Israel's 120-member parliament, any prospective prime minister must secure the support of at least 61 members. The president will consult with the political parties and then formally nominate a prime minister-designate.

The nominated leader will be given 28 days to form a government. If the first attempt fails, a second candidate will be given the opportunity to try. And if that effort also fails, the Knesset will be dissolved and fresh elections will be held within 90 days.
