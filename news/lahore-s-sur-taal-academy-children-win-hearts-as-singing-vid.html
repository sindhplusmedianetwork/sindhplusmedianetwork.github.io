Lahore's Sur Taal Academy Children Win Hearts as Singing Video Goes Viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A short video of the children of Sur Taal Music Academy in Lahore singing a Punjabi song with enthusiasm has gone viral, winning praise in Pakistan, India and the United States. In the video, Norwegian content creator William Gamburg sits among the children, nodding to the rhythm and clapping as the music quickens, while the children sing with confidence and joy. The video was recorded in Lahore the previous week, when a person associated with Coke Studio brought Gamburg to the academy, and it has been viewed nearly 9 million times on Instagram, with Gamburg also sharing it on TikTok where it became very popular.

The academy, located in Krishan Nagar, Lahore, was founded in 2019 by musician and music researcher Zia-ul-Haq to give children with limited resources access to free music education, and it has been providing free music education for about 10 years. Students are trained in singing and playing instruments, including classical music, ghazal, pop and film music, and the academy says its students have won more than 20 national awards in recent years and appeared in more than 100 television programmes. More than 100 children were learning music there at the time of reporting, with some scouted from different areas and villages, receiving music scholarships and practising for four to five hours daily after school.

The children had previously attracted attention for a performance at a Pakistan Super League event, which also went viral on social media, and local and international figures have supported the academy's projects, including encouragement from US Ambassador Natalie Baker and a collaboration arranged by singer Ali Zafar between the academy's children and the Lahore Qalandars cricket team.
