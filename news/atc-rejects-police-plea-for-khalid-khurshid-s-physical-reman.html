ATC Rejects Police Plea for Khalid Khurshid's Physical Remand, Sends Him to Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Islamabad anti-terrorism court on Saturday rejected the police's request for a 30-day physical remand of PTI leader and former Gilgit-Baltistan chief minister Khalid Khurshid, directing that he be sent to jail on judicial remand. Judge Abul Hasnat Muhammad Zulqarnain heard the case, which was registered at Aabpara Police Station in connection with the party's November 26, 2024 protest in Islamabad. During the hearing, Islamabad police produced the PTI leader before the court and sought his physical remand for 30 days, but the court turned down the request.

Khurshid, who is the president of the PTI's Gilgit-Baltistan chapter, was arrested at the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Assembly speaker's house in Peshawar along with Babar Saleem Swati and several others, shortly after the Centre imposed emergency and governor's rule in KP on October 11. In a separate development, the court also discharged PTI workers arrested in two cases linked to the party's recent long march, rejecting the investigating officer's request for an adjournment to conduct an identification parade after the defence argued the workers had already spent more than 40 days in custody.
