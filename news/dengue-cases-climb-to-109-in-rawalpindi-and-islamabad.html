Dengue cases climb to 109 in Rawalpindi and Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dengue cases in Rawalpindi and Islamabad have risen to 109, health officials confirmed on Friday. According to the figures, 67 patients have been reported in Rawalpindi and 42 in Islamabad, with new cases emerging daily. Officials said they were hoping that heavy rainfall would help curb the spread of the mosquito-borne disease.

In Rawalpindi, the health department inspected 235,982 locations and found larvae at more than 208,000 houses and 27,160 hotspots. Authorities registered 2,800 FIRs and sealed 686 properties over violations of dengue prevention rules. In Islamabad, more than 382,000 surveillance activities have been carried out, keeping the capital's case count at a record low for the year.

Health teams have urged residents to remove standing water and cooperate with surveillance staff.
