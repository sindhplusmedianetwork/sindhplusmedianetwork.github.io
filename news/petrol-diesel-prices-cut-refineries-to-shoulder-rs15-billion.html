Petrol, Diesel Prices Cut; Refineries to Shoulder Rs15 Billion Levy Burden

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Monday slashed petroleum prices, cutting petrol by Rs3.40 and high-speed diesel by Rs7.76 per litre with effect from November 3. Petrol will now retail at Rs374.51 per litre while diesel drops to Rs395.20. Announcing the decision, Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik and Information Minister Attaullah Tarar unveiled a new arrangement under which oil refineries will contribute to the petroleum levy.

The refineries will bear a cumulative monthly impact of Rs15 billion, shielding consumers from the levy burden. The move follows Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif's strict directive against raising fuel prices. The petroleum minister revealed that petrol was due to rise by Rs15 on October 30, but the increase was averted through the refineries' cooperation.

The development comes as refineries post record profits, with Cynderco's net income jumping from Rs61.61 billion to Rs145.17 billion.
