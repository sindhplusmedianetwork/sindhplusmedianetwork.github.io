Apple Rushes to Restore Services After Internal Update Triggers Outage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apple said on Tuesday it was working to restore its systems after an internal update caused an outage that affected customer services including iCloud and Apple Pay. The company said the issue was not caused by a cyberattack, adding that many of the systems were back up and running. A spokesperson said the company was working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible.

There were around 2,300 user reports of issues with Apple Pay in the US around 12:20pm ET, according to Downdetector, which tracks outages by collating status reports from several sources. At the peak of the outage, the platform recorded around 1,800 user reports of problems with iCloud in the US, though Downdetector noted its numbers were based on user-submitted reports and the actual number of affected users might vary. The Apple outage follows a series of technology disruptions in the US in recent days, including a Microsoft outage, an FAA network failure that caused a nationwide ground stop, a United Airlines technology failure and a JPMorgan Chase software problem that disrupted some banking services.

Companies and officials have said the incidents were not linked to cyberattacks.
