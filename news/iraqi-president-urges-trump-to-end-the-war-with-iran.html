Iraqi President Urges Trump to End the War With Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iraqi President Nizar Amedi has urged US President Donald Trump to return to his election campaign promise of ending America's 'endless wars' and to bring the military conflict with Iran to an end. In an interview with the American magazine Breitbart, published on Sunday, he said Trump needs to end wars, bring peace to the region and return to his earlier position, which also covered ending other conflicts including the Ukraine-Russia war. Amedi called Trump a 'very significantly important man' who can bring peace and prosperity to the Middle East and the whole world by formally ending the war in Iran.

Describing Iraq as well placed to mediate between Tehran and Washington, he said both countries should use the current opportunity for lasting peace, as Iraq's geography and its ties with both sides put it in the best position. He said he gave the same message to Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian when he met him in Turkiye. His appeal came as the White House increases pressure on Baghdad to close its land border crossings with Iran and stop flights between the two countries.
