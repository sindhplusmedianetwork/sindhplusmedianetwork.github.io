Alcaraz Sets Up Paris Masters Final Showdown With Shelton

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Carlos Alcaraz said taking on world number four Ben Shelton is one of the best challenges in tennis after the Spaniard beat Casper Ruud to set up a Paris Masters final clash with the American. Alcaraz downed former world number two Ruud 6-4, 6-4 in the semi-final to book his spot in Sunday's final, sealing match point on the second time of asking with an ace. It will be the first meeting between the pair since Shelton ended Alcaraz's US Open title defence in the quarter-finals earlier this season.

Flushing Meadows runner-up Shelton earned his place by cutting short the fairytale run of French world number 47 Luca Van Assche, winning 5-7, 6-3, 6-4 to reach his sixth final of the season. Alcaraz said booking his first Paris Masters final berth means a lot to him after painful early losses in Paris in past years, and he promised to enjoy every second of the final. Shelton will be chasing a third Masters-level title after winning the last two editions of the Canadian Open, while Van Assche leaves with a tournament to remember after eliminating former world number five Lorenzo Musetti and US 15th seed Learner Tien on his way to the semi-finals.
