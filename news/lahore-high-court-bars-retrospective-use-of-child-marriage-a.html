Lahore High Court Bars Retrospective Use of Child Marriage Act 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has declared the retrospective application of the Child Marriage Restraint Act 2026 illegal. Justice Khalid ibn Aziz set aside a Khushab sessions court order of 20 May 2026 that had declared a girl underage and sent her to Darul Aman. The court noted that the Act came into force on 11 May 2026, while Kiran Shaheen's nikah took place on 18 April 2026, so the new law could not apply to the earlier marriage.

The court also dismissed the father's constitutional petition and allowed the girl to live wherever she wishes. The court observed that the girl had told a magistrate she married of her own free will and was not abducted. It further held that Section 552 of the Criminal Procedure Code does not authorise keeping a girl in Darul Aman indefinitely against her will.
