Hurricane Trudy Strengthens Into Major Storm Near Mexico's Pacific Coast

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hurricane Trudy rapidly intensified into a major hurricane on Friday morning as it tracked toward Mexico's Pacific coast. The storm was carrying maximum sustained winds near 120 miles per hour, with even stronger gusts, the United States National Hurricane Center said. Forecasters warned that additional strengthening remained possible before Trudy approaches the coastline on Saturday as a life-threatening major hurricane.

AccuWeather hurricane meteorologist Alex DaSilva said the system had the potential to reach Category 4 or 5 intensity. Mexico's civil protection agency urged residents to begin preparations, including securing doors and windows and removing objects that could be blown away by the winds. The storm was located about 250 miles south-southwest of the resort city of Acapulco and is expected to drop up to 12 inches of rain in the states of Michoacan, Colima and Jalisco, raising the danger of severe flooding and landslides.

The region has already been battered in recent weeks by storms that left several people dead and caused heavy flooding. Meanwhile, Tropical Storm Simon continued to weaken farther offshore after bringing heavy rain to the coast earlier.
