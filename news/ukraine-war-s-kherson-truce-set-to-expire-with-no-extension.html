Ukraine war's Kherson truce set to expire with no extension announced

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The longest truce of the Ukraine war, brokered by US First Lady Melania Trump and limited to the Kherson region, was set to expire on Sunday evening with neither side announcing an extension. The pause began early on Tuesday and enabled a humanitarian mission to the Russian-occupied town of Oleshky. Remarkably, no violations of the truce were reported during its run.

The calm allowed civilians in the area a rare respite from more than three years of war. A Ukrainian officer warned, however, that fighting could become 'worse than before', saying Russian forces had used the quiet period to amass troops and ammunition. Whether the guns stay silent now depends on talks that showed no public progress as the deadline approached.
