Karachi Safe City project phase one complete, phase two begins: Sindh Home Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Home Minister Zia Lanjar has said the first phase of the Karachi Safe City project is complete and work is now moving into its second phase. He was chairing a meeting on the project's progress and next steps, where its technical requirements and implementation were reviewed in detail. Lanjar directed that the Safe City software be aligned with international standards so that modern technology can help detect and prevent crime in time.

The Safe City project uses cameras and monitoring systems across the city to support policing and improve public safety. The completion of the first phase marks a milestone for the project, which is aimed at making Karachi safer for its residents. With the second phase now under way, the government says the system will be expanded and brought up to modern standards.
