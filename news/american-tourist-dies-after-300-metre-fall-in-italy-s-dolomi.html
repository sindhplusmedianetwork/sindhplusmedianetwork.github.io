American tourist dies after 300-metre fall in Italy's Dolomites while taking photo

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 36-year-old American tourist died after falling into a ravine about 300 metres deep in Italy's Dolomites while attempting to take a photograph, local authorities said. Michelle Alejandra Rubio Leiva, from Florida, was with her family near Forcella Pordoi when the accident happened just before 12:30pm on Saturday. She was heading to a cable car station at an altitude of about 9,000 feet when she apparently stepped off the marked trail and lost her footing.

Emergency rescue teams were dispatched to the steep mountainside, but she was pronounced dead at the scene. The Dolomites, a UNESCO World Heritage mountain range in northern Italy, draw millions of hikers each year, and officials have repeatedly warned visitors to stay on marked paths. Her death is a tragic reminder of the dangers posed by the region's sheer cliffs and narrow alpine trails.

Family members who witnessed the accident were being assisted by local authorities.
