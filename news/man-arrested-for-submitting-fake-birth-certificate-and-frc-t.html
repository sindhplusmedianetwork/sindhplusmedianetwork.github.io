Man arrested for submitting fake birth certificate and FRC to US embassy for visa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency's Anti Human Trafficking Circle in Islamabad has arrested a man who submitted fake documents to the American embassy to obtain a US visa. According to officials, the accused had prepared a fake birth certificate and a fake family registration certificate with the help of his accomplices. The forged documents were then submitted to the American embassy as part of a visa application.

The first information report in the case states that the accused had taken 12 million rupees from a citizen on the promise of getting him an American visa. The FIA took action after the matter came to light and arrested the accused. Further investigation is under way to identify the other people involved in preparing and using the fake documents.
