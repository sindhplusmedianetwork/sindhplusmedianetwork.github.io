Pakistan Navy enhancing combat capabilities for future warfare: Naval chief

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Naval Chief Admiral Naveed Ashraf said on Thursday the Pakistan Navy's combat capabilities had undergone significant enhancement across all operational domains. The enhancement is meant to meet the demands of contemporary and future warfare, he said. He was addressing the graduation parade at PAF Academy Asghar Khan in Risalpur.

Conflicts now span electronic warfare, cyber, space and cognitive domains, he said. He cited inductions including state-of-the-art warships and Hangor-class submarines. Indigenously developed long-range weapons were also cited.
