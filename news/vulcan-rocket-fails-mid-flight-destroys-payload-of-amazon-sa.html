Vulcan Rocket Fails Mid-Flight, Destroys Payload of Amazon Satellites

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Vulcan rocket owned by United Launch Alliance failed on Sunday shortly after liftoff from Florida, destroying its payload of 40 satellites from Amazon's Leo internet network. United Launch Alliance is the joint venture of Boeing and Lockheed Martin. The rocket failed roughly 10 minutes after liftoff from the Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

The cause of the failure was not immediately clear. The failure is expected to lead to a lengthy technical investigation that could keep the rocket grounded for months. The setback could deepen a launch bottleneck in the United States, and it was the launch company's first mission failure.
