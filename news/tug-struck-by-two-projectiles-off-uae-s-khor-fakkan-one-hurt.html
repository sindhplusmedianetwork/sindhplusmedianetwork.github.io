Tug Struck by Two Projectiles Off UAE's Khor Fakkan, One Hurt, Says UKMTO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) reported on Sunday that a marine tug sailing southbound was struck by two projectiles of unknown origin northeast of Khor Fakkan in the UAE. The British navy-affiliated agency said the incident resulted in one minor injury and damage to the tug, which is continuing on its route. UKMTO did not identify the vessel or provide details about the origin of the projectiles, and said authorities were investigating.

The agency advised vessels operating in the area to transit with caution and report suspicious activity. The attack comes amid a surge in strikes on commercial shipping in and around the Strait of Hormuz, a vital waterway connecting the Persian Gulf to international shipping routes. The incidents have intensified as the United States and Iran compete for control of the strategic passage following the outbreak of war between Tehran and Washington and its ally Israel in late February.
