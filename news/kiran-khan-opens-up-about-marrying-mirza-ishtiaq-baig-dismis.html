Kiran Khan Opens Up About Marrying Mirza Ishtiaq Baig, Dismisses Gold-Digger Claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Television host and actress Kiran Khan has spoken openly about her marriage to businessman Mirza Ishtiaq Baig during an appearance on Nadia Khan's morning show Rise and Shine. Khan said she had no intention of marrying again, but the two already knew each other because she had worked with Baig's foundation. According to her account, Baig proposed to her directly and only afterwards approached her family with the proposal.

Addressing the criticism that followed the announcement, Khan firmly rejected the suggestion that she married for wealth. She said she had supported herself through her own work for her entire life. The couple solemnised their marriage at Masjid al-Haram in Makkah in September this year, the report added.
