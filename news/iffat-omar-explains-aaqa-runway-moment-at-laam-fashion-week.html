Iffat Omar Explains 'Aaqa' Runway Moment at LAAM Fashion Week

WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani actress and television host Iffat Omar has responded to criticism over her runway video at LAAM Fashion Week's finale, where she was seen moving to the devotional track Aaqa by Abida Parveen and Ali Sethi. Omar appeared as the showstopper for veteran designer Nilofar Shahid, wearing a gold saree as the event approached its closing moments. After a clip from the fashion event circulated on social media, some viewers questioned the decision to play the track during a runway presentation, while others criticised the runway movements seen in the clip.

In an Instagram clarification, Omar said she was unaware of the song until its lyrics began and claimed she stopped as soon as she recognised them. She wrote that if she had known which track it was, she would never have taken part in that moment. The discussion has prompted debate about music choices at fashion events, particularly when a track carries religious significance, and has drawn comments from fellow showbiz personalities, including actress Mishi Khan.
