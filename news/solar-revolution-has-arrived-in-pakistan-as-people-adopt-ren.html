Solar revolution has arrived in Pakistan as people adopt renewable energy from their own savings, says Musadik Malik

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Climate Change Musadik Malik has said that Pakistan is among the countries worst affected by climate change and has faced devastating floods, while melting glaciers are putting the homes and livelihoods of thousands of people at risk. Speaking to the media at the Pakistan pavilion at COP31, he said the world community must move beyond announcements and offer practical cooperation. He said a large share of climate finance remains limited to countries with high emissions, and it should be distributed fairly so that developing countries affected by climate change can also benefit from it.

He said a revolution in installing solar panels on rooftops has taken place in Pakistan, and people have promoted renewable energy from their own savings without waiting for any foreign financial assistance. He added that the spread of solar energy has made it possible to reduce LNG imports, and the country's energy mix is shifting towards clean energy. Mr Malik said Pakistan has two basic responsibilities: protecting the land and protecting future generations, adding that solar and other clean energy sources help reduce climate pollution.

He also said the prime minister has announced a policy to promote electric vehicles, which will help reduce air pollution, as vehicles are among the major causes of smog and air pollution, while polluted air cuts years off human life.
