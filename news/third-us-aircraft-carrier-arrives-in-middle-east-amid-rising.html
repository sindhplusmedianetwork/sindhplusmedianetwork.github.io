Third US Aircraft Carrier Arrives in Middle East Amid Rising Tensions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The nuclear-powered US aircraft carrier USS Theodore Roosevelt has arrived in the US Central Command area of operations. Its arrival brings the number of US carrier strike groups deployed in the region to three. A US official confirmed the arrival to a British newspaper.

Earlier reports had said the ship would reach the Middle East within 24 hours. The move comes amid rising US-Iran tensions. Iran claimed on Sunday that it hit a US helicopter base in Bahrain with a drone.

Iranian state TV said five ships caught fire in the past 48 hours after hitting mines.
