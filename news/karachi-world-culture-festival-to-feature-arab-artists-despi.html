Karachi World Culture Festival to Feature Arab Artists Despite Cancellations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Palestinian actor Ahmed Tobasi and other Arab artists remain scheduled for Karachi's World Culture Festival this week, organisers said, despite withdrawals by a Palestinian group and UAE-based musicians amid the ongoing conflict in the Middle East. The third edition of the festival, organised by the Arts Council of Pakistan, Karachi, with support from Sindh's provincial government, will run from October 29 to November 22, featuring music, theatre, dance, film and visual arts. Organisers expect representation from more than 150 countries, with over 1,000 international artists and more than 500 Pakistani participants.

Arts Council President Muhammad Ahmed Shah said some performances that were completely ready had to be cancelled because of the war, including a Palestinian group that will no longer attend and UAE-based musicians unable to travel due to financial and employment difficulties. He said even recently, a musician who plays the oud had his participation cancelled at the last moment. Nevertheless, preparations for collaborations between Pakistani and international artists are continuing, including several fusion projects, and the programme shared with Arab News includes entries from the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Algeria and Morocco.
