Meta Reaches Out-of-Court Settlement with Second Teenage Girl in Addiction Lawsuit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Technology company Meta, the owner of Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp, has reached an out-of-court settlement with a second teenage girl who had sued it. The girl had accused the social media platforms of spreading mental health problems, depression and eating disorders. The trial was to hear the cases of two teenage girls, identified as SJ and KD.

SJ had already reached an agreement with Meta, and after this latest settlement the case will now continue only against Snap. The affected girls had alleged that features like autoplay and infinite scroll made them addicted to social media. Social media companies deny the allegations of deliberately causing addiction, though Meta has faced large legal settlements and billions of dollars in fines in recent months over negative effects on children and data privacy disputes.
