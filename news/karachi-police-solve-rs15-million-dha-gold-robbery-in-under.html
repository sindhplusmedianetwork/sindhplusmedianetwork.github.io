Karachi Police Solve Rs15 Million DHA Gold 'Robbery' in Under 24 Hours; Two Held

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Karachi have cracked the case of an alleged gold robbery worth Rs15 million in DHA Phase 8, arresting two suspects including a driver. Sahil police used CCTV footage and technical methods to solve the case in under 24 hours, recovering the vehicle and the stolen goods. A police spokesperson said the investigation revealed the incident was not a robbery at all but was linked to a family dispute.

According to initial findings, a woman named Ayesha allegedly staged the robbery drama through her driver Haider, who engaged two other men for the act. One suspect has been arrested so far along with the recovery of the car and goods. IG Sindh Javed Alam Odho commended DIG South Asad Raza, SSP District South Mehzour Ali, SHO Sahil and the entire police team for the successful operation.
