Truck driver arrested after running over traffic police vehicle with truck

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police have arrested the truck driver who ran over a traffic police vehicle in Defence, acting after the incident was reported in the news. The driver, Karamat, was taken into custody and a case was registered at the Defence police station. The incident took place on Saturday night near Defence Mor, when the driver drove his truck away instead of stopping after traffic police tried to stop him, and hit the traffic police vehicle.

A video of the incident went viral on social media, and after the matter was highlighted in the news, traffic police officials took notice and acted. The traffic police and the driver had earlier reached a settlement before the news report prompted action. In his statement, the arrested driver said a motorcyclist braked suddenly in front of his truck at Defence Mor, causing it to slip, and that he drove away out of fear when traffic police tried to stop him.

He admitted his mistake and apologised to Sindh Police. Police have registered the case under sections relating to interference in official duty and speeding, and have begun further legal proceedings.
