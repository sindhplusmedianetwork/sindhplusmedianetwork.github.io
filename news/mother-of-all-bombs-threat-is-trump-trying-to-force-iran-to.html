'Mother of All Bombs' Threat: Is Trump Trying to Force Iran to Submit?

Donald Trump has threatened renewed military action against Iran, warning of a 'tough attack' on the country's nuclear facilities and energy infrastructure. The US military is reportedly considering the use of the 'Mother of All Bombs' (MOAB) against Iran, adding a new dimension to the standoff. CNN had earlier reported on fresh US attack plans targeting Iranian nuclear and energy infrastructure.

According to a CSIS analysis, the United States has 10 MOABs remaining, while 14 GBU-57 bombs were used in the June 2025 strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities. The GBU-43/B bomb itself has been used in combat only once before — in April 2017 in Afghanistan, against an ISIS tunnel complex. On the Iranian side, IRGC deputy commander Maj Gen Mostafa Izadi said on 15 October that the armed forces are ready to confront any enemy on land, sea and air.

A day later he told parliament's news agency 'Ikana': 'if America has the mother of bombs, we have the father of bombs.'
