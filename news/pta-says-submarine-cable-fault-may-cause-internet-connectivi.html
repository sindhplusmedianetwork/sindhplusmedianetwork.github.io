PTA says submarine cable fault may cause internet connectivity issues

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority has said that a fault in one of the international submarine cable systems may cause internet users in the country to experience connectivity issues. The fault was reported in the SEA-ME-WE 5 system, about 11 kilometres from Pakistan's shore, and may lead to intermittent connectivity problems and a decline in service quality. Transworld Associates is coordinating with the SMW5 consortium to identify the root cause of the fault and to determine the estimated time for restoration.

In the meantime, the authority said internet traffic was being rerouted through alternative international links to minimise the impact and ensure service continuity as far as possible. It added that it was in close coordination with the stakeholders concerned and would continue to monitor the situation to facilitate the earliest possible restoration of normal services. A similar fault in the same cable system on July 1 caused widespread connectivity issues across Pakistan and was resolved on July 3.
