Globacore Minerals bets on homegrown capital to unlock Pakistan's mineral wealth

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Globacore Minerals, owned by six of Pakistan's leading business groups, is betting that domestic capital can unlock the country's mineral wealth, starting with early-stage exploration in Balochistan's Chagai belt and Washuk. The company, which holds about 2,764 sq km under exploration for copper, gold, antimony and other critical minerals, says mining still contributes less than 3% of Pakistan's GDP despite identified reserves of 92 minerals. Its CEO Ali Mukhtar said patient local capital could build a pipeline of projects behind the flagship Reko Diq mine, which has secured $3.5 billion in debt financing for its $7.7 billion first phase.

The company argues that local investors understand the country's conditions better than foreign firms. It plans to move from exploration to development over the coming years.
