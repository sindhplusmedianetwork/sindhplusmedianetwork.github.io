Sindh High Court hears case of missing New Karachi goldsmith Jahanzeb

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court has heard the case of Jahanzeb, a goldsmith from New Karachi who went missing, with the missing man, the station house officer of Gadap and others appearing before the court. Jahanzeb told the court that Gadap police had picked him up on 3 November and released him on 18 November, and that police had taken his signatures on blank and stamp papers. He said he was being told to pay four million rupees or his gold shop would be seized, and that he was too afraid to open the shop in case he was picked up again.

He said police had also taken 250,000 rupees from his brother Aurangzeb before his release, kept him hidden during the day and locked him in the police station at night. The court expressed displeasure at the police officials, while the Sindh government's lawyer described the claims as one-sided and the petitioner's lawyer said they also wanted an inquiry, as Jahanzeb remained at risk of being picked up again. The court ordered protection for Jahanzeb's life and property, noting that he had earlier approached the Malir court over his illegal detention and that a magistrate had raided the police station but had not found him.

When the court asked whether he recognised any of the policemen, Jahanzeb said he did not know them, but that those who had taken his signatures were policemen. The petitioner's lawyer said Jahanzeb lived and worked in New Karachi and that Gadap police had no jurisdiction there, after which the court directed the Sindh inspector general of police to inquire into the matter.
