Australia Confirms First H5N1 Bird Flu Case on Macquarie Island

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Australia on Saturday confirmed its first case of H5N1 bird flu on the sub-Antarctic Macquarie Island. The deadly virus was found in an elephant seal and a kelp gull, Chief Veterinary Officer Beth Cookson said. Macquarie Island is a UNESCO World Heritage-listed territory situated halfway between Tasmania and Antarctica.

It is a key breeding ground for southern elephant seals, whose pups can face mortality rates above 90 per cent when infected with the virus. Experts predict a significant elephant seal mortality event during the upcoming breeding season. The confirmation marks the arrival of the global H5N1 outbreak at one of the world's most remote wildlife habitats.
