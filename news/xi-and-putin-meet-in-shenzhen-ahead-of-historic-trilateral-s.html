Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen ahead of historic trilateral summit with Trump

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin began talks in Shenzhen on Wednesday, a day ahead of a historic three-way summit with US President Donald Trump in the southern Chinese city. Thursday's trilateral meeting has triggered fears that the three leaders could decide the fate of Ukraine over the heads of Kyiv and Europe. Also up for discussion are the Iran war and nuclear disarmament, according to Washington, after a bilateral treaty between the United States and Russia on atomic weapons expired in February.

Putin arrived in China ahead of the meeting, and the talks in Shenzhen set the stage for the first three-way summit of the three leaders. The meeting comes at a moment of high tension, with the war in Ukraine, the conflict involving Iran and the future of nuclear arms control all on the table.
