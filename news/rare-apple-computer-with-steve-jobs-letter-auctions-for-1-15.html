Rare Apple Computer With Steve Jobs Letter Auctions for $1.15 Million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A rare early Apple computer has been sold at auction for 1.15 million US dollars, setting a world auction record. Two buyers competed against each other for nearly 28 minutes before the final bid was accepted. The computer carries the signature of Apple co-founder Steve Wozniak.

It was sold together with a signed letter from 1978 in which Steve Jobs offered the owner an upgrade to the then-new Apple-2 computer. The sale reflects the enduring collector interest in the earliest machines from the dawn of personal computing.
