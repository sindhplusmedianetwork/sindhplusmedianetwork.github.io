Mystery of 150-Year-Old Westminster Abbey Skeleton Nears Solution

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A skeleton that has puzzled historians for more than 150 years may finally be about to give up its identity. The remains were unearthed at Westminster Abbey in London in 1869, buried inside a stone coffin dating back to Roman times. Because of the grand burial, experts long assumed the bones belonged to a wealthy man.

New analysis by Professor Warwick Rodwell's team has narrowed the possibilities to two medieval monks. The researchers say the skeleton could be Elfwig, who died around the year 1020, or Wulnoth, who died in 1049. Examination showed the deceased was male, stood about five feet nine inches tall, and died between the ages of 35 and 49.

Despite the progress, the team cautions that a final, proven identification has not yet been made. Further study will be needed before the abbey's long-standing mystery can be declared solved.
