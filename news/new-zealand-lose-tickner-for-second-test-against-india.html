New Zealand Lose Tickner for Second Test Against India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New Zealand seamer Blair Tickner has been ruled out of the second Test against India after suffering a right-hamstring injury during training in Wellington. The second Test starts in Christchurch on Friday. The 33-year-old will be replaced in the squad by Matt Fisher.

Tickner's absence is another setback for the home side's bowling attack. New Zealand have already lost frontline spinner Mitch Santner for the series after he fractured his right hand. The hosts will now have to reshuffle their bowling plans for the Christchurch Test.
