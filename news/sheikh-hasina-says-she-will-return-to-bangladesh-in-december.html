Sheikh Hasina Says She Will Return to Bangladesh in December Despite Death Threats

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Bangladesh prime minister Sheikh Hasina said she will return to her country in December despite the threat of death. In an email interview with the AFP news agency, she said she is fully aware of the risks she faces on returning home, but she has decided and no death threat can stop her from returning. Hasina has been in India since August 2024, after her 15-year rule ended following a student-led movement.

She said the Bangladeshi authorities have been demanding her return for months, but now that she is taking practical steps to return, they seem more interested in threatening her than in allowing her to come back. She said she has no intention of seeking India's help for her return arrangements and has decided to return voluntarily. She said extradition is a matter between two sovereign states and India will decide according to its own laws, policies and national interests.

Her lawyers have applied through the Bangladesh High Commission in New Delhi for her Bangladeshi passport and national identity card. She said she is already preparing for her return in December and added that she does not want to die but wants to live and continue serving her country.
