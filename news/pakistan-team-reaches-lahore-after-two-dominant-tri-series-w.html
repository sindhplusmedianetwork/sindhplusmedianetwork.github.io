Pakistan Team Reaches Lahore After Two Dominant Tri-Series Wins in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's men's cricket team has travelled from Rawalpindi to Lahore for the remainder of the tri-nation series, riding high on two dominant wins at home. The squad will take a full day of rest tomorrow before resuming preparations for the next stage of the tournament. The hosts won both Rawalpindi matches convincingly, first beating Sri Lanka by 37 runs and then outclassing England by 87 runs.

The series action continues in Rawalpindi with the third match between England and Sri Lanka at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Thursday. Pakistan will then face England in the fourth match of the series at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium on October 25. The green shirts will be hoping to carry their winning momentum into the Lahore leg of the tri-series.
