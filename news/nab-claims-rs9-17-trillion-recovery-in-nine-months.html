NAB Claims Rs9.17 Trillion Recovery in Nine Months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Accountability Bureau Chairman Lt Gen (retd) Nazir Ahmed Butt has claimed the bureau recovered assets worth Rs9,171.92 billion between January and September 2026. Speaking at a briefing for senior journalists in Islamabad, he said the auction of reclaimed Pakistan Railways land alone could generate more than Rs10 trillion, enough to finance the long-delayed Main Line-1 railway project without any foreign borrowing. He said NAB had recovered railway land worth over Rs6 trillion after just two and a half months of work, and that proper monitoring and auction could push the value beyond Rs10 trillion.

The chairman added that he expected even larger recoveries from the power sector, describing practices there as daylight robbery, and said the bureau would next examine entities under the Ministry of Industries and Production. He also claimed that money laundering outflows from Pakistan had stopped, accused some countries that champion democracy of shielding suspected money launderers, and called for a complete overhaul of the Capital Development Authority. Butt said NAB's total recoveries between March 2023 and December 2025 stood at Rs26 trillion.
