Indian Court Orders Takedown of Documentary on Adani and Modi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Indian court has ordered the takedown of an online documentary accusing Prime Minister Narendra Modi and billionaire Gautam Adani of cronyism. The documentary was posted by Hotmail co-founder Sabeer Bhatia, and Adani Enterprises sued, calling it defamatory. The Kolkata High Court said in its order that the film "is not a news report or an account of proceedings" but "a dramatised narrative".

In an interim order in force until January 31, 2027, the court ordered YouTube, X and Meta's Instagram to remove several posts of the 36-minute film, titled "The Friendship Files", and restrained Bhatia from disseminating it. Adani Enterprises told the court the film was riddled with false content and had prompted inquiries from some investors. Bhatia, who is based in the United States, wrote on X that the lawsuit demands 500 million rupees in damages and said "Truth is on my side".

Hearings in the case are likely to resume in January.
