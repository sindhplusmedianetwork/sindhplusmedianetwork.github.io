Pakistan, Canada advance investment treaty talks in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The third round of negotiations on the Bilateral Investment Treaty between Pakistan and Canada concluded in Islamabad, marking another step forward in efforts to strengthen economic relations and promote investment cooperation. Federal Minister for Board of Investment Qaiser Ahmed Sheikh and Canadian High Commissioner Tariq Ali Khan attended the concluding session, where both sides appreciated the efforts of their respective negotiating teams. The high commissioner emphasised the importance of continued cooperation in developing a balanced agreement that would facilitate investment, strengthen business confidence and promote mutually beneficial economic ties.

Federal Minister Qaiser Ahmed Sheikh underscored Pakistan's commitment to fostering a conducive investment environment and expanding opportunities for Canadian investors. He pointed to key sectors including renewable energy, mining, agriculture and information technology. The talks are aimed at building a treaty framework that supports greater investor confidence and enhanced economic engagement between the two countries.
