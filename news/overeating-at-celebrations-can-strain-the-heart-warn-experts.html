Overeating at Celebrations Can Strain the Heart, Warn Experts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Health experts advise extra caution with heavy meals for people with heart disease, high blood pressure or diabetes, as overeating at weddings, festivals and other celebratory occasions can increase strain on the heart. After a large meal, the digestive system needs more blood flow, which adds load on the heart; healthy bodies usually tolerate this, but those with narrowed arteries or heart disease face a higher risk of impaired blood flow to the heart. One heavy meal does not necessarily cause a heart attack, but rich, salty and high-carb foods can raise blood pressure and trigger symptoms in sensitive people.

The experts list heart-attack warning signs — chest pressure or pain spreading to the arm, back or jaw, breathing difficulty, cold sweats, dizziness and sudden weakness — and urge calling emergency services immediately. They recommend moderation, small portions, slow eating, avoiding excess fatty and salty food, not overeating after long fasting, light walking if feeling fine, and regular medication for heart patients.
