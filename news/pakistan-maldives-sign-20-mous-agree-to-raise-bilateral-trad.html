Pakistan, Maldives Sign 20 MoUs; Agree to Raise Bilateral Trade to $200 Million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and the Maldives signed 20 memorandums of understanding on Wednesday as Maldivian President Mohamed Muizzu began a three-day visit to Islamabad at the invitation of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. The visit marks the first by a Maldivian president to Pakistan in 11 years, and comes as the two countries celebrate a milestone in their diplomatic ties. Both sides agreed to enhance bilateral trade to $200 million within two years.

The prime minister also briefed President Muizzu on the Kashmir dispute and the Indus Waters Treaty issue during their talks.
