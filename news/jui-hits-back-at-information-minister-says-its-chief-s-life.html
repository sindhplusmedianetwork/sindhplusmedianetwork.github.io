JUI hits back at information minister, says its chief's life is bound to Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam has issued a strong response to the federal information minister's remarks about its chief, Maulana Fazlur Rehman, with party spokesperson Aslam Ghori saying the Maulana's life and death are bound to Pakistan and that he needs no certificate of patriotism from anyone. Ghori said the minister's statement was a crude attempt to divert attention from the facts, and he asked former prime ministers Nawaz Sharif and Shehbaz Sharif to clarify whether the minister was speaking their language. He said that before questioning anyone else's patriotism, the minister should answer for Nawaz Sharif's controversial statement on the Mumbai attacks and explain whose position it represented.

Ghori pointed out that the Maulana had brought together all schools of thought to secure a unanimous religious ruling against suicide attacks and armed struggle, and had faced three suicide attacks himself as a result. He said his party had suffered the most from terrorism, with hundreds of its workers martyred, and that lecturing a party that had given its workers' blood on patriotism was political narrow-mindedness. The spokesperson added that the Maulana's loyalty to Pakistan was proven by his struggle and sacrifices, not by political statements.
