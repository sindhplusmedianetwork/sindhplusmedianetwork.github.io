Leaders Weigh 'Huge Benefit' and Risks of AI for the Global Economy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The artificial intelligence boom has been a key driver of global growth but also poses economic risks should investment in the technology falter, experts and policymakers said this week at the IMF-World Bank Annual Meetings in Bangkok. Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh called AI the 'most consequential change' to the global economy in his lifetime with 'a huge benefit,' while warning the road ahead would be 'bumpy'. IMF chief Kristalina Georgieva told AFP that any slowdown in AI investment 'would be felt across the world'.

An IMF report released this week projected that a decline in AI investment could reduce global GDP by 0.4 per cent by 2028, and warned that weaker AI investments and profitability could trigger a market correction.
