'Wo din bhi kya din thay': Aijaz Aslam shares rare footage from his 1990s PTV days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Aijaz Aslam has taken fans on a nostalgic journey into the 1990s by sharing a rare television clip from what he remembers as an Eid special on Pakistan Television. In the video, shared on Instagram, Aslam appears alongside several prominent entertainment personalities of the era, including the late actor and comedian Khawaja Akmal and Adnan Siddiqui, with the session hosted by Laila Wasti. One amusing moment shows Aslam asking Khawaja Akmal what scope an actor has after putting on weight, to which the comedian replies with a witty reference to 'cinema scope', prompting laughter from those gathered.

Looking back at the programme, Aslam said it was an Eid show from the 1990s, although he admitted he was not entirely certain of the occasion. He wrote that there were no fancy sets and no scripts built for virality, just warmth, respect and real conversations. The actor acknowledged that television production has changed considerably since those days, but said watching the clip reminded him of simple and beautiful times he misses more than words can say.

He ended his reflection with the line that captured his longing for that period: 'Wo din bhi kya din thay.'
