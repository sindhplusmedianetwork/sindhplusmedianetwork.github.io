PM Approves 1% Sales Tax on Battery Electric Vehicles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday approved reducing the sales tax on battery electric vehicles to 1%, covering both locally made and imported EVs. The decision comes under the new five-year auto policy for 2026-31 and is pending approval from the IMF and the federal cabinet. The prime minister also approved a levy of 2% to 10% on internal combustion engine vehicles, which is expected to raise an estimated Rs232 billion over five years for export incentives.

Officials said that under the policy, the price of EVs such as the Wuling Bingo BEV would fall from Rs4 million to Rs2.7 million. The move is aimed at making electric vehicles more affordable for Pakistani buyers.
