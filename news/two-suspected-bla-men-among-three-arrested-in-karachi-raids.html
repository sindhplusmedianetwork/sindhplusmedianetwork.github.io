Two Suspected BLA Men Among Three Arrested in Karachi Raids

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Counter-Terrorism Department and a federal intelligence agency arrested three suspected terrorists in Karachi on Friday. An improvised explosive device, a hand grenade and a handgun were recovered from them. Two alleged affiliates of the banned Balochistan Liberation Army, identified as Rahim Fazal and Balach Baloch, were arrested in Gulistan-e-Jauhar.

Officials said they were allegedly planning attacks on sensitive locations. A suspected Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan member, Shah Noor Rehman Khan, was arrested near Gulbai Chowk. A loaded pistol was recovered from his possession.
