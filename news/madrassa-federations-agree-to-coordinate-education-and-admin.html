Madrassa federations agree to coordinate education and administration across Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Religious education organisations and federations from across Pakistan have agreed to work together more closely on the education and administration of madrassas. Their leaders met in Islamabad under the Muttahida Madaris Council to discuss a shared approach to religious schooling. The meeting agreed to strengthen cooperation, academic links and a joint strategy among the country's madrassa federations.

Participants called for modern subjects to be taught alongside religious studies and for education for female students to be expanded. They also agreed to improve coordination on examinations, certification and registration. The participants appreciated the government's efforts on madrassa registration and banking facilities.
