National Assembly passes record 24 bills in 30th session

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Assembly passed a record 24 bills during its 30th session, held from October 13 to 30, according to a report released by the lower house secretariat. Of the bills passed, 17 were government-sponsored and seven were private members' bills. Seven of the government bills were sent to the Senate, while the remaining 10 were forwarded to the president for assent.

Of the private members' bills, five were referred to the Senate and two were sent to the president for approval. The House also saw the introduction of 16 bills during the session, including nine government-sponsored and seven private members' bills. A motion to form a parliamentary committee on electoral reforms was also unanimously adopted during the session.

National Assembly Speaker Ayaz Sadiq facilitated the legislative process, prioritising measures relating to public interest, and convened meetings of select committees comprising representatives of parliamentary parties, ministry officials and Assembly authorities.
