US Soldier Arrested in Britain on Suspicion of Spying for Russia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

British counter-terrorism police have arrested a United States soldier on suspicion of spying for Russia. The 41-year-old senior soldier was arrested on Tuesday near Gloucester, and he has not been named. He is in custody at a police station in London.

Counter Terrorism Policing London said the soldier was arrested under sections 1 and 3 of the National Security Act 2023. Section 1 covers obtaining or disclosing protected information, while section 3 covers assisting a foreign intelligence service. Reports say the soldier was believed to be stationed at Imjin Barracks in Innsworth, Gloucestershire, which serves as the headquarters of NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps.

FBI Director Kash Patel said the arrest was significant and linked to espionage charges, adding that the FBI has no tolerance for anyone who violates their oath to the United States and puts national security at risk.
