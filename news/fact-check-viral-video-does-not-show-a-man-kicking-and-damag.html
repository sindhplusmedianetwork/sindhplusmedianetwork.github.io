Fact Check: Viral Video Does Not Show a Man Kicking and Damaging Tarbela Dam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A video circulating on social media since November 9, said to show a man kicking and damaging Tarbela Dam, is false, a fact check by iVerify Pakistan has found. The footage, shared widely on X and Facebook, actually dates from August 2026 and was filmed in Kohistan, where it shows a road safety wall rather than the dam. The false claim spread a day after a real incident at the dam, when a rock mass collapsed at the Tarbela Dam 5th Extension Project site on November 9, sending water surging into nearby work areas.

No loss of life was reported in that incident. The fact check separates the two events, confirming that the viral clip has no connection to the rock collapse or to the dam itself. The episode is a reminder of how quickly unrelated footage can be attached to a real incident online.
