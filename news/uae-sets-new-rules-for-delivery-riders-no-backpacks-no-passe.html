UAE Sets New Rules for Delivery Riders: No Backpacks, No Passengers From January 1

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Arab Emirates has introduced new traffic rules for motorcycle riders delivering food and other orders. Under the new rules, riders will not be allowed to carry orders in a bag worn on the back, and they will not be allowed to carry a passenger behind them during delivery duty. The new federal rules will come into force on January 1, 2027.

According to a November 4 report by the UAE's official news agency WAM and details published by Gulf News on November 7, the restrictions were set under Cabinet Decision No. 174 of 2026 for the enforcement of the federal traffic law. The aim is to organise delivery services and improve the safety of riders and others on the roads.

Instead of backpacks, riders will have to use specific equipment or boxes approved by the relevant authorities to deliver goods. The rules also set out eight key obligations for professional drivers and delivery services, including the use of approved equipment and a professional permit.
