Gohar seeks immediate enforcement of Supreme Court order on Imran's treatment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan on Friday expressed grave concern over the health of party founder Imran Khan and called for immediate implementation of a Supreme Court order directing his transfer to a hospital. Gohar said he had filed a petition seeking implementation of the court's August 18 order. That order directed Imran's transfer to a hospital.

It also directed the formation of a medical board. The PTI says Imran's blood pressure had reached 192 in recent days and has demanded his transfer to Shifa International Hospital. The matter of the former prime minister's medical care remains a point of contention between the party and the authorities.
