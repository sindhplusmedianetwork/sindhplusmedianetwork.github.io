Saudi Arabia Arrests Over 13,000 for Residency, Labour and Border Violations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi authorities arrested 13,357 people in one week for violating residency, labour and border laws. According to reports, 8,875 illegal residents were deported during the same period. Between October 22 and 28, 6,880 people were held for residency violations, 3,500 for illegal border crossing attempts and 2,977 for breaches of labour laws.

The interior ministry warned that facilitating illegal entry can bring up to 15 years in prison and a fine of up to one million riyals. The crackdown is part of the kingdom's ongoing campaign against residency and labour violations.
