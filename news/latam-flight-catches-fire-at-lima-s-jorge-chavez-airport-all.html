LATAM Flight Catches Fire at Lima's Jorge Chávez Airport; All Passengers Safe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A LATAM Airlines flight caught fire at Lima's Jorge Chávez Airport in Peru shortly before takeoff. Flames and black smoke erupted from the aircraft's left engine and wing. The flight, LA 230, was taxiing toward Piura in northern Peru when an unexpected technical problem triggered the emergency protocol and the plane was halted.

All passengers were evacuated safely and no one was injured. Airport fire crews brought the blaze under control. LATAM arranged alternate flights for the affected passengers.

An investigation into the cause of the technical fault is expected.
