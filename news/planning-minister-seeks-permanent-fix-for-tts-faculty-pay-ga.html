Planning minister seeks permanent fix for TTS faculty pay gaps

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal has called for a permanent, transparent and sustainable mechanism to address salary disparities among faculty members working under the Tenure Track System (TTS). Chairing a meeting on the TTS salary structure in Islamabad on Friday, the minister said a clear and objective formula was needed to ensure appropriate pay differentiation between professors, associate professors and assistant professors. He said the existing disparities needed to be resolved so that highly qualified academics received fair compensation.

The minister stressed that fair pay was essential to retaining talented faculty in the country's universities. Under the Tenure Track System, university faculty are appointed on a performance-based track, with salary packages set separately from the regular government pay scales. The meeting reviewed the salary structure and discussed ways to make it consistent, transparent and sustainable for the long term.
