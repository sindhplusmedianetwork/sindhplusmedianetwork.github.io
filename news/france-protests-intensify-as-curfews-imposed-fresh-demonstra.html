France Protests Intensify as Curfews Imposed, Fresh Demonstrations Planned

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Anti-government protests that have gripped France for several days have intensified, with demonstrators taking to the streets in Paris, Lyon, Nice and northern parts of the country. The protesters, angry over rising inflation, growing unemployment and the lack of educational facilities, vandalised property and set fire to roads in several cities. Authorities responded by imposing curfews in a number of areas and banning public gatherings in Marseille.

President Emmanuel Macron ordered that those involved in violence be dealt with strictly under the law. Labour unions have announced a nationwide strike for November 5 to press their demands. More than 2,000 people have been arrested since the unrest began, while around 1,000 police personnel have been injured in clashes.

The European Union has advised its citizens travelling to France to exercise caution.
