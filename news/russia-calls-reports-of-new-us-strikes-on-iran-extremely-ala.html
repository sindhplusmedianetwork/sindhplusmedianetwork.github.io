Russia Calls Reports of New US Strikes on Iran 'Extremely Alarming'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russia warned on Monday that reports of planned new military action against Iran were 'extremely alarming', calling for stepped-up diplomacy to resolve the crisis. Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova told a press briefing in Moscow that the situation around Iran remained complex and that the painful experience of recent months showed military adventures, which bring death and destruction, could not break the Iranians' will to defend their country's sovereignty and independence. The warning came a day before the United States midterm elections, after President Donald Trump told an Iowa rally on Sunday that he might eventually have to give Iran 'the hammer'.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Saturday that no decision had been taken on new strikes, while Gulf officials said Washington had warned them that strikes could come as early as November 4.
