Xiaomi's October EV deliveries top 50,000 in 2026 monthly high

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese smartphone maker Xiaomi said on Sunday it delivered more than 50,000 electric vehicles in October, the highest monthly total this year as it ramps up production to meet its 2026 target. Deliveries reached about 336,000 vehicles for the first ten months, after topping 30,000 units in August and 40,000 in September. The company aims to deliver 550,000 vehicles in 2026, meaning January-to-October deliveries represent about 61 percent of that goal.

Broader Chinese EV sales also surged in October: Leapmotor delivered more than 100,000 vehicles for the fourth consecutive month, setting a new record. BYD sold 490,168 new energy vehicles, up 10.97 percent year on year and 5.74 percent from September. Xpeng also posted strong October sales, about 19 percent higher than the same month last year, according to industry trackers.
