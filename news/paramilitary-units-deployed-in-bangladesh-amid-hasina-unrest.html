Paramilitary units deployed in Bangladesh amid Hasina unrest

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bangladesh has deployed hundreds of paramilitary troops across the country as the planned return of former prime minister Sheikh Hasina sparked clashes, bomb blasts and arson. The violence has been blamed on supporters of her banned Awami League, and security forces have been placed on alert in major cities and towns. A BNP activist was killed by a petrol bomb in Gopalganj, while a bus helper was burnt to death in Pirojpur.

At least 37 Grameen Bank branches have been targeted in arson attacks since Nov 10, raising concern about the safety of public institutions. Hasina, who has been in exile in India since August 2024, has asked for a new passport, saying she plans to return in December. The government has rejected her request, and the standoff has deepened political tension in the country.

Authorities say the paramilitary deployment is aimed at maintaining law and order and preventing further violence.
