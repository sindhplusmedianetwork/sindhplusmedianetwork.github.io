Pakistan to Vaccinate 24 Million Children in Nationwide Polio Drive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan is set to launch a major nationwide polio vaccination campaign from November 2 to 8, aiming to immunise around 24 million children in 97 high-risk districts. Federal Health Minister Syed Mustafa Kamal inaugurated the campaign at the National Emergency Operations Centre for Polio Eradication, personally administering the first vaccine drops. More than 200,000 frontline health workers will go door to door to give oral polio drops to children under the age of five.

The drive will run simultaneously in Pakistan and Afghanistan, the last two countries where wild poliovirus remains endemic, with coordinated cross-border efforts to stop transmission. The minister called polio eradication a national priority and said the campaign would focus on districts where coverage needs improvement, including southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Pakistan has cut polio cases by 99.8 percent since 1994, from around 20,000 to 31 last year, though seven cases have been reported so far this year.

Health officials reminded parents that polio has no cure and urged every child under five to receive the two safe drops in each campaign.
