FIA Arrests Border Health Services Chief, Three Officers in Pilgrim Vaccine Scam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency on Tuesday arrested the head of Border Health Services, Dr Abid Shah, along with three other officers in Karachi. Former BHS chief Dr Ghulam Mustafa and Dr Paryat Kumar are among those arrested. The officers stand accused of buying cheap vaccines for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims at inflated prices, causing losses to the public exchequer.

The arrests followed a notice taken by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, who has also set up an inquiry committee to examine the procurement. The arrested officers are being shifted from Karachi to Islamabad for further investigation.
