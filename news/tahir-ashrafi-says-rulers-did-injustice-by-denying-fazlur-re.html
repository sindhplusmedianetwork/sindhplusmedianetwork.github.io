Tahir Ashrafi Says Rulers Did Injustice by Denying Fazlur Rehman a State Role

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Ulema Council Chairman Tahir Mehmood Ashrafi on Sunday urged the country's rulers to engage Maulana Fazlur Rehman, saying they should answer his calls, form a committee equal to a ministry, make him its chairman, and permit him the use of an aircraft. Speaking to the media, Ashrafi said Fazlur Rehman had first been tempted with the presidency and then denied a share in the state, and that it emerged on February 5 that he had no standing in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. He said he had visited Tehran two days earlier and described Pakistan's constitution as stronger than those of 57 countries.

Ashrafi announced that the Ulema Council's executive body had decided to stand with the government's decisions regarding the expansion of Makkah. He appealed to the mothers, sisters and daughters of Balochistan to rise against terrorists who he said pick up their daughters from colleges, and called on the prime minister and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa governor to establish peace in the province. He added that the Pakistan Army was a red line, welcomed the defence pact, and noted that Iran had distanced itself from the Houthis.
