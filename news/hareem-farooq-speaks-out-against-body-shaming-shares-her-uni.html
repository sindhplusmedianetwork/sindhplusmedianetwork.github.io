Hareem Farooq speaks out against body shaming, shares her unique morning ritual

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani actress and producer Hareem Farooq has taken a clear stand against body shaming, saying she believes in accepting herself exactly as she is. She recently appeared in short kurtis and Patiala salwars during the promotional campaign for her film 'Mango Jatt', after which her dress was criticized on social media. However, the actress says she does not attach much importance to the criticism of others regarding her body and remains confident in her own personality.

She also shared an interesting detail about her morning routine: every day after waking up, she speaks positively to herself and thanks the different parts of her body. She explains that she is grateful for her arms, legs, and her entire body, which help her move forward in life and carry out her daily tasks. According to her, this practice has now become a permanent part of her daily life.

Her remarks have been widely shared, with many praising her message of self-acceptance.
