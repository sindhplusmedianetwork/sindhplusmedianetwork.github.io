Container train derails at Feroza station in early-morning accident

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Another train accident took place on the railway system early on Tuesday morning. A container train travelling from Karachi to Qila Sattar Shah overshot at Feroza station. As a result of the accident, the train's engine and seven wagons came off the track.

Six wagons overturned. A relief team from Lahore Railways reached the site after the accident.
