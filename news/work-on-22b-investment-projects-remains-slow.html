Work on $22b Investment Projects Remains Slow

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A cabinet committee has expressed concern over the slow pace of work on projects worth $22 billion signed with China, and called for regular meetings of the Special Investment Facilitation Council's executive committee to monitor progress. The Cabinet Committee on Chinese Investment Projects was informed that Chinese investors and companies signed 566 memoranda of understanding and investment agreements worth $21.9 billion during the last two years, including 424 investment agreements worth $19.275 billion. The committee chairman observed that the SIFC executive committee had not been convened since August 2025.

He asked the SIFC to review its classification of projects as 'execution', 'active' and 'in pipeline', noting that some projects with no physical work started had been wrongly listed under 'execution'.
