PTI seeks live broadcast of Al-Qadir Trust case hearing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The PTI has filed a petition in the Islamabad High Court seeking live broadcast of the appeal proceedings in the 190 million pound Al-Qadir Trust case involving Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi. The petition argues that live coverage is essential for transparency, public confidence and to dispel speculation. It invokes Article 19-A of the Constitution in support of the request.

The IHC has fixed November 25 for hearing the appeals filed by Imran and Bushra against their convictions. The petition asks the court to allow the proceedings to be broadcast so the public can follow the case directly. A decision on the request is now awaited as the hearing date approaches.
