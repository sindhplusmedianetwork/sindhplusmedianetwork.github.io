Japan's art, music and food come alive at Karachi festival

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The city got a taste of Japan as its traditions, art, music and cuisine came alive at a cultural festival held at a local hotel. From the delicate folds of paper art to the vibrant rhythms of traditional dance, the festival transformed the venue into a showcase of Japanese culture, drawing large crowds eager to explore a country where centuries-old traditions coexist with cutting-edge modernity. Organised by the Japanese Consulate in collaboration with a cultural association and a business forum, the event sought to strengthen people-to-people ties, mutual understanding and friendship between the two countries.

Visitors were treated to demonstrations of flower arrangement and paper folding, alongside traditional games, children's activities and stalls introducing the country's language and heritage. The cultural showcase extended to the performing arts, with traditional dance performances captivating audiences, while popular music struck a chord with younger visitors and a costume competition saw participants dressed as popular animated characters. Japanese cuisine was another major draw, with food stalls serving traditional delicacies, while bonsai displays, magazines and cultural exhibitions further enriched the experience.

The provincial governor, accompanied by the Japanese consul general, visited the festival and explored the cultural stalls, describing the city as a cosmopolitan place where diverse cultures found appreciation and acceptance.
