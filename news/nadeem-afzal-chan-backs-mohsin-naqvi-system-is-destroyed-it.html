Nadeem Afzal Chan Backs Mohsin Naqvi: 'System Is Destroyed, It Needs a Reset'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Peoples Party central leader Nadeem Afzal Chan has said Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi is "absolutely right" that the system has been destroyed and needs to be reset. Speaking on ARY News, Chan said he could not travel in Balochistan during the day or in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa at night, and asked how he could then claim the law-and-order situation was fine. He said the country had a crippled democracy rather an ideal one, and that the justice system, the Election Commission and governance were completely broken.

Chan demanded that the questions raised over the Election Commission be addressed, and said the bureaucratic machinery that runs the government and the state must be fixed first. The same report quoted PTI chairman Barrister Gohar as saying some words in the Maulana's speech would have been better left unsaid, and that the retaliatory attacks on it were also inappropriate. Speaking to the media outside Parliament House, Gohar said the whole nation fought external chaos together, but internal chaos harmed the country more, and urged political leaders to play their role instead of raising tensions.

He added that his party would not cross red lines, that "Pakistan first and the state first" was its position, and that electoral reforms should have equal representation of government and opposition and be impartial and in line with public aspirations.
