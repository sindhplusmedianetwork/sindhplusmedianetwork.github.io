The ancient giant birds that snatched children

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Giant prehistoric birds of prey may once have hunted our early human ancestors. Talon marks on the famous 2.8-million-year-old Taung Child skull suggest that an eagle-like predator killed the three-year-old child. Experts say these huge raptors, some with wingspans of up to seven metres, dominated the skies for millions of years.

Their threat may have shaped human evolution, including social living and even the development of language. The memory of such birds survives in myths of child-snatching birds, from the tales of Sinbad to the Maori Te Hokioi. The findings show that the skies above our ancestors were far more dangerous than they are today.
