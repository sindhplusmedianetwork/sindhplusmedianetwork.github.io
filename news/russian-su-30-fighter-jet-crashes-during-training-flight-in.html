Russian Su-30 fighter jet crashes during training flight in Kaliningrad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Russian Su-30 fighter jet crashed during a training flight in the Kaliningrad region on Tuesday. The aircraft went down while carrying out a routine training mission, according to early reports. Kaliningrad, a Russian exclave between Poland and Lithuania, regularly hosts military aviation exercises.

No official details about the crew or the cause of the crash were immediately available. Russian military authorities are expected to launch an investigation into the incident. The Su-30 is a twin-engine multirole fighter in service with the Russian Air Force.
