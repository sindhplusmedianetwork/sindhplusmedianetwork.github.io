Missile Debris Damages Aramco Tower in Riyadh, Saudi Energy Ministry Confirms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's energy ministry has confirmed that an attempted attack damaged an administrative building of the oil company Aramco in Riyadh on Sunday. The ministry said debris from missiles fell on the Aramco tower in the King Abdullah Financial District, causing minor damage and breaking windows on several floors. No one was killed or injured in the incident.

The Saudi-led coalition said its air defences had intercepted two missiles fired by Yemen's Houthi fighters towards Riyadh, and officials later confirmed that two cruise missiles were intercepted in the south of the country. A Reuters witness heard several loud explosions in Riyadh on Sunday morning, shortly after civil defence issued a warning; officials later announced that the danger had passed. The ministry's initial statement did not identify the type of weapon used or who was responsible for the attack.
