India Capital Steps Up Air Pollution Controls Ahead of Winter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India has rolled out its annual winter anti-pollution measures in the national capital, New Delhi. The Delhi government on Thursday announced a ban on open rubbish burning and ordered food stalls to switch to cleaner fuels. Air monitors recorded pollution levels of 233 micrograms per cubic metre early Thursday — about 15 times the limit recommended by the World Health Organization.

A second phase of restrictions will take effect from November 1, including doubled parking fees, work-from-home arrangements for offices and tighter construction site rules. A third phase will bring a complete construction ban from December 10 to January 20 under the city's winter action plan. Delhi's air quality typically plunges every winter as crop-stubble burning, traffic emissions and weather conditions combine to trap pollutants.

The city's residents face some of the worst air in the world during the season.
