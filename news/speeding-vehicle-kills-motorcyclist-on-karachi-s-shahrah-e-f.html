Speeding vehicle kills motorcyclist on Karachi's Shahrah-e-Faisal, driver flees

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi's busy Shahrah-e-Faisal witnessed a fatal hit-and-run early Friday when an unidentified speeding vehicle struck a motorcycle near Lal Qila. A young man riding the motorcycle died on the spot, while another person travelling with him sustained injuries. Chhipa rescue sources said both victims were immediately shifted to Jinnah Hospital, where the deceased was identified as 25-year-old Sabir.

The driver of the vehicle escaped from the scene along with the vehicle before police arrived. No further details of the incident had emerged till the filing of this report. Police are yet to identify the fleeing vehicle or its driver.
