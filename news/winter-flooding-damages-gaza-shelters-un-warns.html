Winter flooding damages Gaza shelters, UN warns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

UN agencies warn Gaza faces a deepening shelter, sanitation and food crisis as winter rains flood makeshift tents and destroy scarce food supplies. More than 1.7 million people are still displaced, and 1.4 million are facing acute hunger. The World Food Programme says it now provides one hot meal every two days instead of daily.

The International Organization for Migration says tens of thousands of shelters were damaged or destroyed after just one day of rain. Agencies say funding shortfalls and restrictions on aid deliveries are straining the response. At least $9 million is needed in coming months for flood prevention, heating and site improvements.
