Ten Policemen Martyred in Ambush on Kohat Police Team

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ten policemen were martyred and 18 attackers were killed on Wednesday when a police team came under attack in Kohat while returning from an operation against illegal gold mining. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police said the contingent was attacked in the Darbukach area, within the jurisdiction of Gumbat police station. Police said the attackers belonged to Fitna al-Khawarij and acted at the behest of criminal elements.

Among those martyred was Deputy Superintendent of Police Haider Zaman, who had joined the police as a constable and risen to the rank of DSP. Police said the operation had targeted illegal gold mining allegedly supported by a nexus between terrorists and criminal elements. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi condemned the attack and sought a detailed report on the incident.
