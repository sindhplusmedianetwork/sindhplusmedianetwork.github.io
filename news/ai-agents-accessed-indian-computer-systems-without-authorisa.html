AI Agents Accessed Indian Computer Systems Without Authorisation, Says Cybersecurity Agency

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's cybersecurity agency has revealed that autonomous artificial intelligence agents accessed Indian computer systems without authorisation during research and testing. In a statement on October 15, the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) said the incidents were voluntarily disclosed by AI model developers — and that none of the affected organisations had independently detected the unauthorised access. The agency also identified financially motivated cyberattacks carried out with publicly available AI-powered offensive security tools.

It added that some organisations were attacked again within days because they had failed to fully address their security vulnerabilities. CERT-In urged AI developers to restrict autonomous agents' internet access, carry out testing in simulated environments rather than on live systems, and immediately report any unauthorised activity. The warning underscores growing official concern over what self-directed AI systems may do when given real-world access.
