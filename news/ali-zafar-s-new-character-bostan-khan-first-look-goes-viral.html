Singer and actor Ali Zafar has shared the first glimpse of his new character 'Bostan Khan' from the upcoming web series 'Gangs of Khorasan'. In the look, he appears with a thick moustache and glasses in a stern and mysterious style, barely recognisable to his fans. The reveal has gone viral on social media, with admirers expressing surprise at the transformation.

Speculation has also begun over whether Bostan Khan will be the central character of the series or its villain. The makers have yet to reveal further details about the project.
