Six Killed, 15 Injured in Shooting in Poland's Krakow

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A gunman killed six people and injured 15 in the historic city of Krakow, Poland. The attacker opened fire on crowds at the busy Main Market Square from his car at around 3pm local time on Saturday. Police shot and arrested the suspect in an injured condition during the exchange of fire.

Panic spread through the area as people fled into nearby shops and buildings. Police said weapons and ammunition were recovered from the attacker's vehicle. The shooting took place in one of the city's busiest tourist areas.

Authorities said an investigation into the attack was under way.
