Case registered over attack on closed Korai police station in Dera Ismail Khan

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A case has been registered over the attack on the closed Korai police station in Dera Ismail Khan. Police said the first information report was lodged at the Counter Terrorism Department police station on the complaint of the police. According to Jang, the text of the case states that 15 to 20 terrorists detonated an explosives-laden vehicle at the station.

The attackers also opened fire on the police station and fired rockets. In Dera Ismail Khan, the terrorists rammed a pick-up loaded with explosives into the gate of the police station, but no one was killed in the incident, and the attackers fled after police retaliated. Police said the blast damaged the station's gate and wall as well as a nearby mosque, while windows of surrounding houses were shattered.

The terrorists could not enter the police station premises because of heavy retaliatory fire from the police personnel. Police and security forces have launched a search operation in the area to arrest the suspects.
