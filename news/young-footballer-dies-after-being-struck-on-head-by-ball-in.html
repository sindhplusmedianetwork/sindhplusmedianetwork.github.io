Young footballer dies after being struck on head by ball in Argentina

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An 18-year-old footballer in Argentina has died after being struck on the head by a ball during a match. Abel Nunez, who played for FC Ezeiza against Deportivo Muniz, was hit by a ball cleared forcefully by an opposing defender. He collapsed unconscious on the ground and was taken to hospital, where doctors operated to try to save him, but he could not survive.

An eyewitness said the match had been calm but there was tough competition for the ball. The witness described the youngster's sudden fall as resembling a knockout in a boxing match. The tragedy has shocked the local football community in Argentina.
