New Zealand's Defence Chief Resigns Over Inappropriate Relationship

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New Zealand's Defence Force has announced the resignation of its chief, Air Marshal Tony Davies, following the disclosure of an inappropriate relationship he formed while commanding a unit. Davies said he was resigning for personal reasons, accepting responsibility for past mistakes that were contrary to the Defence Force's values. He expressed regret that he had not disclosed the matter when he had the chance.

Defence Minister Paul Goldsmith said the circumstances behind the resignation would be a deep disappointment for many service personnel. Both agreed that Davies could not remain in the role, Goldsmith added. The matter has been referred to an independent oversight body for further review.

Davies had led the Defence Force as its highest-ranking officer.
