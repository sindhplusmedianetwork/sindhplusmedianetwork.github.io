Janhvi Kapoor revives mother Sridevi's 'Naagin' look for Halloween

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Bollywood actress Janhvi Kapoor has brought her mother Sridevi's iconic 'Naagin' look back to life for Halloween. Janhvi shared photos and videos on Instagram recreating the famous character from the 1986 film Nagina, appearing in a pink outfit with jewellery and makeup that closely mirror the film's scenes. The posts quickly drew attention online as fans compared her look with the late Sridevi's memorable screen presence.

Joining her tribute, influencer Orry appeared dressed as Rishi Kapoor while her friend Vishnu Praveen took on the look of Amrish Puri. The recreation has become one of the most talked-about celebrity Halloween appearances this year.
