Russian Diplomats and Military Officials to Join Abu Dhabi Ukraine Talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russia's delegation to talks with the United States and Ukraine next week will include senior military officials and diplomats, including Deputy Foreign Minister Sergei Ryabkov, Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov told the Interfax news agency. The trilateral meeting in Abu Dhabi, meant to discuss ending Moscow's war against Ukraine, is scheduled for Tuesday and Wednesday and will be the first such talks since February. Russia's delegation, headed again by Kremlin aide Vladimir Medinsky as chief negotiator, will include Ryabkov for the first time as well as Yuri Zhigarlovsky, a senior General Staff officer.

Deputy Foreign Minister Mikhail Galuzin and Igor Kostyukov, the deputy chief of the Main Directorate of the General Staff of the Russian Armed Forces, are also members of the delegation. Mr Peskov added that Yury Ushakov, the Russian presidential aide for foreign policy, would be overseeing the upcoming negotiations. Neither the United States nor Ukraine has yet announced the composition of its delegation, though Ukraine's parliamentary leader said Rustem Umerov, head of Ukraine's Foreign Intelligence Service, will lead the country's negotiation team.

Russian President Vladimir Putin met the delegation at a Security Council meeting on Friday to discuss preparations for the talks.
