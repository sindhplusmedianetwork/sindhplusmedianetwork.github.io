Bahrain Reports Fire, Material Damage Due to Iranian Attack on Civilian Industrial Facility

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bahrain's interior ministry said an Iranian attack targeted a civilian industrial facility, causing a fire and material damage. No injuries were reported in the incident, and Civil Defence teams were working to control the blaze. US President Donald Trump said he intends to secure a permanent deal with Iran so that no future American president can allow Tehran to obtain a nuclear weapon.

Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said reopening the Strait of Hormuz requires the US to accept reasonable conditions under international law. He also said the US should acknowledge its mistakes. The statements came amid continuing tensions between Iran and the United States in the region.
