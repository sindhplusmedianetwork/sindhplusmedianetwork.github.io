Larger bench to hear petition seeking restoration of PTI election symbol

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has formed a larger bench to hear a petition challenging the constitutional validity of Section 215 of the Elections Act 2017 and seeking the restoration of the PTI election symbol, the bat. Justice Abid Aziz Sheikh will head the bench, which will also comprise Justice Malik Waqar Haider Awan and Justice Malik Javid Iqbal Wains. The petition argues that Section 215 is contrary to Article 17 of the Constitution, and that depriving a political party of its election symbol seriously affects its political existence, participation in elections and democratic rights.

Article 17 of the Constitution guarantees citizens the fundamental right to form political parties and become their members, the petition states. On December 22, 2023, the Election Commission of Pakistan stripped the PTI of its symbol due to its alleged failure to hold intra-party polls transparently. The Peshawar High Court declared the ECP order illegal on January 10, 2024, but a three-member bench of the Supreme Court led by former chief justice Qazi Faez Isa set aside the PHC order on January 14, 2024.

As a result, the PTI could not participate in the February 8, 2024 general elections as a political party and its candidates had to contest as independents, which later resulted in the deprivation of reserved seats in the National Assembly.
