Pakistan invites Turkmenistan to join CASA-1,000 power project

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has offered Turkmenistan to join the Central Asia-South Asia (CASA) 1,000-megawatt electricity supply project, originating in Tajikistan. The project is designed to transmit up to 1,300 MW, mainly in summer. Of this, 300 MW is for Afghanistan and 1,000 MW for Pakistan.

Power Minister Awais Leghari invited Turkmenistan's energy minister to join the project during a meeting on Monday. Both sides also discussed the Turkmenistan-Afghanistan-Pakistan power transmission project.
