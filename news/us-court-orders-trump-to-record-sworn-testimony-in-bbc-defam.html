US Court Orders Trump to Record Sworn Testimony in BBC Defamation Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A United States court has ordered President Donald Trump to record a sworn statement in his defamation case against the BBC. Under the order, Trump must answer questions under oath as part of the legal proceedings. The order is a significant step in the lawsuit he has brought against the British broadcaster.

Courts issue such orders to ensure that testimony is given truthfully and on the record. Trump's legal team has not yet publicly commented on the ruling. The case continues to draw attention on both sides of the Atlantic.
