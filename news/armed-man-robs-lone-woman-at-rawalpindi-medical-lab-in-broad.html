Armed man robs lone woman at Rawalpindi medical lab in broad daylight; CCTV footage emerges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An armed man robbed a lone woman employee at a medical laboratory in Rawalpindi in broad daylight, taking cash and two mobile phones at gunpoint. The incident took place on October 31 at City Lab on Tulsa Road, in the Civil Lines police area, and CCTV footage of the robbery has now emerged. The footage shows the woman alone inside the lab when the suspect walks in and immediately pulls out a pistol.

He is then seen snatching a mobile phone from the frightened employee, taking cash from a drawer and a second phone from the table, and putting them in his pockets. Police say the suspect also forced the woman to unlock a mobile phone at gunpoint before fleeing on a motorcycle. A case has been registered on the complaint of Maryam Sudhir, who handles the lab's administrative affairs, and she told police she was at the lab at 9:45 in the morning when the armed man arrived.

Police say they are investigating the case and the suspect will be traced soon.
