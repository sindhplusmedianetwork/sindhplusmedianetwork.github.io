Heated Exchange Between Mohammad Amir and Indian Batter in Abu Dhabi T10 League

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Pakistan fast bowler Mohammad Amir was involved in a heated on-field exchange with an Indian batter during a match in the Abu Dhabi T10 League. According to Express News, Amir was bowling when the batter played a reverse shot over the wicketkeeper for four, after which tempers flared between the two players. On the very next delivery, the batter attempted another shot but was caught out at the boundary.

Amir celebrated the wicket enthusiastically as the batter made his way back to the pavilion. Amir is representing UAE Bulls in the league, a side that also includes Pakistan all-rounder Shadab Khan. In the previous day's match, Amir took three wickets for 38 runs in two overs.

In an earlier match, Pakistani batter Iftikhar Ahmed hit Amir for a six, and a bouncer from Amir then struck Iftikhar on the helmet, forcing the physio to come onto the field.
