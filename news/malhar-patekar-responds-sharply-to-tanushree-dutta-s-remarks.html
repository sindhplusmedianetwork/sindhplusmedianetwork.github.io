Malhar Patekar responds sharply to Tanushree Dutta's remarks after Nana Patekar's death

Malhar Patekar, son of the late Nana Patekar, has broken his silence over actress Tanushree Dutta's controversial remarks. Dutta had said the 'devil is dead' following the veteran actor's passing. Her comments have reignited the Me Too debate in Bollywood and across social media.

Nana Patekar, 75, died on October 8 from cardiac arrest in Goa. Malhar responded to the remarks with a sharp rebuttal, ending his silence on the matter. The exchange has drawn wide attention in the Indian film industry.
