NDMA Issues High Drought Alert for Districts in Sindh and Balochistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Disaster Management Authority's National Emergencies Operation Centre has issued a high alert for drought in several districts of Sindh and Balochistan. In Sindh, parts of Umerkot, Tharparkar, Mirpurkhas and Sujawal face a severe risk of drought, and the situation is being monitored continuously. In Balochistan, parts of Chagai and Washuk districts are also facing drought conditions.

The centre said below-normal rainfall, a long dry spell, rising temperatures and a changing monsoon pattern are the main causes of the drought. Temperatures in the affected areas of Sindh are running one to three degrees Celsius above normal, and the water shortage is badly affecting farm activity and crop production. The authority warned that crop losses are raising fears of food insecurity in the affected areas.

It directed the relevant institutions to ensure advance measures for effective use of water resources and protection of the affected farming and rural population, and advised the public to follow the departments' guidance to protect crops and livestock.
