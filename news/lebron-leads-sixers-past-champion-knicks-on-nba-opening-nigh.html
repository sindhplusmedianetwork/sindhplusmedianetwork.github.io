LeBron Leads Sixers Past Champion Knicks on NBA Opening Night

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

LeBron James made a winning start to his Philadelphia 76ers career, scoring 14 points and handing out seven assists as the Sixers defeated the defending champion New York Knicks 106-100 at Madison Square Garden. The game was played on the night the Knicks raised their championship banner, but the visitors spoiled the celebration. Tyrese Maxey led all scorers with 28 points, while Jaylen Brown chipped in 19 points on his own Philly debut.

In the other opening-night fixture, Detroit staged a remarkable comeback from 22 points down to beat Boston 121-113, spoiling Paul George's Celtics debut despite his 38-point effort. The results set an early marker for the new NBA season.
