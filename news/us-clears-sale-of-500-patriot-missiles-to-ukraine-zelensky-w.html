US Clears Sale of 500 Patriot Missiles to Ukraine; Zelensky Warns Delivery Will Take Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Trump administration on Friday cleared the proposed sale of 500 Patriot missiles to Ukraine, the State Department announced, in what would be one of the largest air-defence transfers to Kyiv since the war began. The approval, which now goes to Congress for review, follows weeks of complaints from Ukrainian authorities that their supplies of the interceptor missiles were dwindling under relentless Russian drone and missile strikes. "The proposed sale will improve Ukraine's capability to meet current and future threats by improving its integrated air and missile defense capabilities," the department said in a statement, adding that it would support US foreign policy and national security objectives by strengthening a partner country it described as a force for political and economic stability in Europe.

But the announcement carried a sobering caveat: no delivery timeline was given, and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said late Friday that "deliveries may begin ONLY IN A FEW YEARS." Zelensky, posting on X, said: "We are asking our American partners to find a solution for the time when there is a real danger and a real need to use PAC-3 interceptors," suggesting that Washington and its European allies could lend Ukraine missiles from their own stockpiles now and replenish them with the new purchase later. The decision comes against a complicated backdrop: US-Ukraine tensions spiked earlier this month after Washington struck a diesel fuel deal with Russia, while Kyiv complained that the United States was slowing its access to satellite internet services and had stopped some intelligence sharing — though tensions eased after Zelensky held talks with US envoys in Oslo on October 20. The State Department said the weapons would be paid for through donations from Ukraine's allies, not with US funds.
