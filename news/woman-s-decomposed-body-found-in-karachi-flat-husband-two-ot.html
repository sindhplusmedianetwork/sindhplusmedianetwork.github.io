Woman's decomposed body found in Karachi flat; husband, two others detained

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police recovered the several-days-old body of a woman named Mishal from a flat in Karachi's Ahsanabad area. The body has been shifted for a post-mortem, which police say will determine the exact cause of death. Investigators have detained her husband Usama — described as her second husband — on suspicion.

Another man has also been taken into custody as part of the probe. Officers are examining the circumstances leading to her death. Further details are awaited as the investigation continues.
