108+ Balochistan Families Disown Relatives Involved in Terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Several families in different areas of Balochistan have announced disassociation from their sons and brothers involved in terrorism, expressing intense hatred for Fitna al-Hindustan. In Quetta, the families of Jahanzeb, Noorzaib, Anwar, Mujeeb, Qasim and Obaidullah announced an end to all relations with them. The family of Younus Shahwani of Mastung also distanced itself from his actions.

In Kachhi, the families of Inayatullah, Abdul Nafi, Tayyab and Yasir likewise disowned them. The families said they have no connection with those who went missing and joined terrorist organisations or became involved in illegal activities. They made it clear that if their sons are found working on the terrorists' agenda, they will keep no ties with them either.

According to statistics, more than 108 families in various districts of Balochistan disowned their militant sons and brothers between January and October 2026.
