570 people dress as Rocky Balboa to set Guinness World Record on film's 50th anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

To mark 50 years since the release of the Hollywood film Rocky, 570 people in the American city of Philadelphia dressed as the film's central character Rocky Balboa and set a new world record. The participants gathered on Friday on the famous steps of the Philadelphia Museum of Art, where they recreated the film's memorable training scene. All the participants wore the distinctive outfit of Rocky Balboa, the character played by Sylvester Stallone, and the aim of the gathering was to set the Guinness World Record for the largest gathering of people dressed as the same film character.

According to the organisers, 570 people took part in the event in total, breaking the previous world record, which was held by 250 people who had dressed as Rocky Balboa. Under the Guinness World Record rules, every participant had to wear a grey hooded sweatshirt, grey trousers, a black woollen cap and black Converse Chuck Taylor All Star high-top shoes. In the film Rocky, Sylvester Stallone played a boxer who tries to achieve success through hard work, determination and struggle, and the scene of climbing the steps of the Philadelphia Museum remains one of the most famous scenes in film history.
