Iqrar-ul-Hassan Granted Bail, Court Orders Immediate Release

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi's City Court has approved the bail application of Awam Raj Party chief and anchorperson Syed Iqrar-ul-Hassan. The court ordered him to submit bail bonds of Rs200,000 and directed the police to release him immediately. He was facing a case over alleged anti-state material.

The case was heard by Judicial Magistrate East Gul Riz Memon. Due to security and administrative concerns, Hassan was not brought to the court in person and was instead produced through a video link. The bail approval marks a new turn in the proceedings against the anchorperson.
