Ten years on, the voice of A. Nayyar still lives in Pakistani film music

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ten years have passed since the death of A. Nayyar, one of Pakistani film music's best-known singers. Born Arthur Nayyar near Sahiwal on 17 September 1950, he began his film career with 'Behesht' in 1974.

He rose to fame with 'Pyar to ik din hona tha', sung with Naheed Akhtar. Across his career, he sang more than 4,000 film songs. He won five Nigar Awards and received the Presidential Pride of Performance.

He died of a heart attack in Lahore on 11 November 2016.
