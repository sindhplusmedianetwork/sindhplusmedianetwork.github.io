Some gifts are better left unwrapped, says Durefishan Saleem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Durefishan Saleem has asked her fans to send handwritten notes instead of expensive presents. She said words written from the heart mean far more to her than material gifts. Sharing a photograph of a handwritten message and handmade presents from a fan on her Instagram Story, she wrote that she would treasure a little handwritten note forever.

The actress said the personal message from her fan meant a great deal to her. She also shared that her mother often helps her assess scripts. Her mother reads the scripts and gives brief summaries, the actress said.
