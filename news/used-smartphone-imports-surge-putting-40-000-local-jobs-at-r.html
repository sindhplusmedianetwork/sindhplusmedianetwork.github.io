Used smartphone imports surge, putting 40,000 local jobs at risk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Imports of used smartphones surged to 98,383 units in October 2026 from just 1,434 in July, as narrowing duty differences made imports more attractive. Imports of completely built-up smartphones rose from 18,431 units worth $17.8 million in July to 150,133 units worth $71.7 million in October. Over the same period, local production fell about 17 per cent, from 906,711 units in July to 752,870 in October.

The Pakistan Mobile Phone Manufacturers Association raised the matter with the Ministry of Industries and Production and the Federal Board of Revenue, warning that the jump in used imports was the most alarming part of the trend. The industry employs a direct and indirect workforce of more than 40,000 and produces almost all global brands in Pakistan, and nearly all manufacturers have reported layoffs and retrenchment of skilled workers to cut costs. Industry sources point to two reasons for the surge: the tariff gap between imported phones and locally assembled kits has narrowed to around 11 per cent on a $100 phone and 14 per cent on a $200 phone, and a downward revision in FBR valuations in January 2026 made it cheaper to import used phones at under-invoiced values.

Completely built-up imports now hold 41.8 per cent of the smartphone market by value, and the industry says the trend is hurting job creation and investment.
