Bangladesh Reaches Historic Milestone as First Nuclear Reactor Starts Up

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bangladesh has reached a historic milestone in nuclear energy with the successful start-up of its first nuclear reactor. The reactor, the first unit of the Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant built with Russian cooperation, demonstrated its initial performance on October 11, 2026. The development brings Bangladesh closer to joining the ranks of nuclear-power-generating nations.

The plant is located about 150 kilometres west of Dhaka and has two reactors with a combined capacity of about 2,160 megawatts, built with assistance from Russia's Rosatom. Officials expect the reactor to be connected to the national grid by the end of November, after technical reviews and safety verifications are complete. They noted that the initial process does not yet mean regular electricity production has begun.
