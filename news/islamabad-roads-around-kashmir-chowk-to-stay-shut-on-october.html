Islamabad roads around Kashmir Chowk to stay shut on October 24-25; traffic police share alternate routes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two roads connecting Kashmir Chowk in Islamabad will remain completely closed on October 24 and 25, Islamabad Traffic Police have advised, as construction work continues on the Kashmir Chowk Underpass along Club Road. The closed sections are the road linking Rawal Dam Chowk with Kashmir Chowk and the section connecting Serena Chowk with Kashmir Chowk, affecting commuters travelling between Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Park Road and Lehtrar Road. Traffic police have set out alternate routes, including Srinagar Highway via Ninth Avenue for travellers from G-5, the Secretariat and Serena Hotel towards Rawalpindi, and the Lake View U-turn or Murree Road and Club Road as other options.

Commuters heading towards Murree and Bhara Kahu have been directed through Faizabad, Faisal Avenue, Zero Point, the Srinagar Highway underpass and Jinnah Square, while traffic entering Islamabad from Rawalpindi can use Ninth Avenue, Faqir Aipee Road and Srinagar Highway. The closures on the Rawal Dam Chowk route have reportedly been in place since around October 15 or 16, and the latest advisory specifies a complete closure of both sections for October 24 and 25. Traffic personnel will be deployed at key points to guide motorists and manage diversions, and police have urged drivers to allow an additional 20 to 25 minutes for their journeys.

Traffic updates are available through Islamabad Traffic Police FM 92.4 and the official social media channels of Islamabad Police.
