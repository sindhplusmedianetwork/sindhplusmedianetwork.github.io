JUI-F speeds up outreach campaign for Sargodha conference on Nov 26

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam (F) has stepped up its contact campaign for the 'Tahaffuz-e-Aain Pakistan and Awami Huqooq Conference' to be held in Sargodha on November 26. District Lahore leaders led by Maulana Abdul Rehman toured Cantt Board areas. They met local office-bearers, workers and residents to invite them to the conference.

Party leaders said all organisational resources will be used. They said this will make the conference a success.
