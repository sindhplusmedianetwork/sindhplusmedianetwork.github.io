Railway track blown up with explosives in Bolan, no casualties reported

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Unidentified persons blew up a railway track with explosives near Nabi Bakhsh Cross in Lamji, in Balochistan's Bolan district. Police and security forces reached the site soon after the blast and cordoned off the area. A search operation was launched in the surrounding area to trace those responsible.

According to initial reports, there were no casualties in the explosion. The blast damaged the track and disrupted rail movement on the section. Further investigation into the incident is under way, officials said.
