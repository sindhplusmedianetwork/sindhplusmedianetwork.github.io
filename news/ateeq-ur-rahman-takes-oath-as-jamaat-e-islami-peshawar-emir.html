Ateeq ur Rahman Takes Oath as Jamaat-e-Islami Peshawar Emir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Newly elected Jamaat-e-Islami (JI) Peshawar Emir Ateeq ur Rahman took the oath of office at a ceremony held at the party's district secretariat on Wednesday. Emir JI Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Central Abdul Wasi administered the oath. Vice Emirs Maulana Dr Muhammad Ismail, Maulana Dr Hidayatullah and Haji Muhammad Aslam, former district emir Bahrullah Khan Advocate, District General Secretary Hidayatullah, District Spokesperson Tariq Mateen and other party leaders attended the ceremony.

Addressing the gathering, Abdul Wasi claimed that JI is the country's only democratic political party where regular elections have been held since its establishment. He said the party had postponed its march towards Islamabad last month during a nationwide campaign against petroleum levy taxes, following the prime minister's assurance of relief for the public. He added that final talks were held following a meeting between Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Emir JI Pakistan Hafiz Naeemur Rehman.
