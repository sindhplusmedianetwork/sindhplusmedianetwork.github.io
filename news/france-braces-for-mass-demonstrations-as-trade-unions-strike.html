France Braces for Mass Demonstrations as Trade Unions Strike

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hundreds of thousands of people were expected to take to the streets across France on Thursday as trade unions held strikes alongside student-led demonstrations that have gripped the country for weeks. Authorities estimated 500,000 to 700,000 protesters nationwide, with as many as 80,000 marching in Paris. Education, rail, public hospital and postal unions called strike action, demanding a minimum wage increase, across-the-board pay rises, tax reforms and better learning conditions.

Interior Minister Laurent Nunez said 70,000 police and security personnel would be deployed. He promised an extremely firm response to any violence or disorder. The demonstrations are the latest in weeks of protests that have swept the country.
