France Demands UN Security Council Meeting Over Kyiv Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

France has demanded a meeting of the UN Security Council over attacks on the Ukrainian capital Kyiv. French Foreign Minister Jean-Noel Barrot made the demand on Friday and wrote in a post on social media that Russia will have to answer for this attack. According to Ukrainian officials, eight people were killed in Russian missile attacks overnight.

On Thursday, the Dutch government summoned the Russian ambassador after a missile fell near its embassy building in Kyiv, causing minor damage to the building, while the Austrian embassy was also affected by the attack. Staff of both countries were not injured or affected in the incident.
