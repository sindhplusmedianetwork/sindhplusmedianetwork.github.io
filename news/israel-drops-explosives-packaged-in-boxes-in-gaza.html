Israel drops explosives packaged in boxes in Gaza

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli forces are using quadcopter drones to drop boxes packed with explosives onto or beside Palestinian houses in Gaza, residents and witnesses say. The explosives are detonated remotely, destroying buildings and damaging properties, and residents have observed the method for about a week in areas close to Israeli military positions. Witnesses have reported boxes containing several kilograms of explosives being dropped onto houses and other buildings, and video footage showed drones dropping the boxes onto rooftops.

The operations have been reported east of Gaza City's Zeitoun and Tuffah neighbourhoods, as well as in eastern Deir al-Balah and Bureij refugee camp in central Gaza. Local sources said dozens of Palestinians fled the Zeitoun neighbourhood on Friday, fearing their homes would be targeted next, and demolitions continued into Monday near the Wadi Gaza bridge on Salah al-Din Street and north of the Bureij and Nuseirat refugee camps. At least seven such operations have been reported, causing powerful explosions and extensive destruction, and on November 10 Israeli forces used the same method to demolish two houses that had already been evacuated east of Maghazi refugee camp.

Demolitions have continued in Israeli-controlled areas along the 'Yellow Line' despite the ceasefire that took effect on October 10, 2025, and Gaza's Health Ministry says Israeli violations of the agreement have killed 1,650 Palestinians and wounded 5,975 others as of Monday.
