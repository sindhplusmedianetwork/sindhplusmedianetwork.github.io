NCCIA Multan Recovers Rs2 Million for Cyber Fraud Victim

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency's Multan circle has recovered Rs2 million for a citizen who was cheated in a cyber fraud. Victim Naveed Siddiqui said in a video message that his mobile phone was hacked, after which Rs2 million were withdrawn from his account using it. He said he then contacted the NCCIA Multan circle and submitted an application.

The agency took swift action, recovered his money and handed it back to him. Siddiqui expressed heartfelt gratitude to the DG of the NCCIA Multan circle and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi.
