Karachi roads blocked and traffic jammed for second day amid protest

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The city's traffic police announced diversions around the Karachi Press Club ahead of the expected protest, advising commuters to use alternative routes to avoid inconvenience. Traffic heading from MR Kiyani Chowk towards Saddar and Zainab Market was diverted left from Coast Guards Mess towards the Sindh High Court and the Passport Office, while Sharea Faisal could be closed temporarily at the Avari traffic signal. Traffic travelling from Sharea Faisal towards the airport was diverted right from Avari traffic signal onto Fatima Jinnah Road, towards Cantt, School Road, CSD and Kala Pul.

The road from Coast Guard towards Zainab Market and Sarwar Shaheed Road was closed to traffic at around 3:20pm in view of security and later reopened at 6:05pm. Drigh Road towards Karsaz and Sharea Faisal was closed at around 4pm, with traffic rerouted via the Rashid Minhas Flyover towards Millennium, and was opened about half an hour later, though heavy traffic pressure remained as traffic moved in only one lane. At 5:22pm, the traffic police said the flow of traffic had been restored, with all lanes opened, and noted that peak hours were still under way but traffic was being kept moving.

Commuters requiring assistance could contact the traffic police helpline at 1915, where representatives were available round the clock to provide guidance.
