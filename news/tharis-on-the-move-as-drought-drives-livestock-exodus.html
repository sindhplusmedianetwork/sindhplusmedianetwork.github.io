Tharis on the Move as Drought Drives Livestock Exodus

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Drought-hit families in Tharparkar are once again packing up and migrating with their livestock toward the irrigated barrage areas as failed rains wither crops and dry up natural grazing. Farmers who sowed millet, guar and other rain-dependent crops after this year's initial rainfall watched their hopes fade when subsequent rains never arrived on time, leaving parched fields and animals without fodder. Khemoon Bhel, a livestock owner from Vee Hangorja village in Mithi taluka, said he cultivated 20 acres after the first rains, but the dry spell destroyed his crop and fodder alike, forcing him to migrate again barely two months after returning from the barrage region, with his spending on tractor work and seeds gone to waste.

For many families the journey means carrying more than a week's provisions and surviving on dry bread, jaggery and water along the way. Beyond lost harvests, the crisis is deepened by rising food costs: with limited local wheat production, most households depend on market purchases for staples, and shrinking livelihoods make adequate meals harder to afford. Children are bearing the nutritional brunt of the crisis — a medical study of 129 children treated for severe acute malnutrition at Mithi Civil Hospital between March and June 2024 found 117 of them, or 90.7 per cent, were suffering from anaemia, with the findings published in a scientific paper in January 2026.

Meanwhile the district administration has yet to declare Tharparkar calamity-hit or seek relief from the Sindh government, prompting activist-lawyer Qurban Samejo to file a constitutional petition before the Sindh High Court's Mirpurkhas circuit, which has summoned the NDMA, relevant Sindh departments and the deputy commissioner for reports. Lawyer Ashiq Ali noted that drought-like conditions recur in Thar every three to four years, with climate change further intensifying the district's vulnerability.
