IESCO Privatisation Moves Ahead: 10 Investors Including Three Turkish Firms Prequalified

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Privatisation Commission Board has prequalified 10 domestic and international investors for the privatisation of the Islamabad Electric Supply Company (IESCO), moving the power utility closer to the next stage of the sale process. The decision was taken at the Board's 260th meeting on Thursday, chaired by Adviser to the Prime Minister on Privatisation Muhammad Ali. The prequalified parties include three Turkish energy firms — Aktor Elektrik, Genvera Enerji and Cengiz Enerji — along with Pakistani participants Engro Energy, Sapphire Fibers, Novatex and Bestway Cement.

Three consortiums also made the list: one led by Artistic Milliners with Lake City Holdings, Fatima Capital, Din Ventures and Fazal Cloth Mills; one led by Hub Power Holding with Lucky Cement, Kohat Cement and Metro Ventures; and one led by Hasnaat Brothers Construction with Dhilal Holding, Pak Steel, Bio-Labs and Farid Steel Casting. The 10 parties will now proceed to the next stage of the transaction, including access to the Virtual Data Room for detailed due diligence before preparing bids. IESCO is part of the first batch of three power distribution companies identified for privatisation, alongside FESCO and GEPCO.

In the same meeting, the Board also approved the addition of consortium members for FESCO's privatisation, changes to the restructuring plan of Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited, and selected a Deutsche Bank-led consortium as the top-ranked candidate for the financial advisory role in the State Life Insurance Corporation privatisation.
