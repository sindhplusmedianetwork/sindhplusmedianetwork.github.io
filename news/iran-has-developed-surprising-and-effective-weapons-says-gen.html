Iran Has Developed Surprising and Effective Weapons, Says General Mousavi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

General Mousavi, Commander-in-Chief of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, has spoken about Iran's military capabilities. He said Iran's war capabilities increased during both recent wars imposed on the country. According to him, the forces have developed weapons and equipment with surprising and effective capabilities.

Iranian media reported that General Mousavi and the Aerospace Commander visited a new missile city. A launcher capable of firing two missiles at the same time was also displayed during the visit. General Mousavi said there would be no hesitation in using power and confronting threats firmly.
