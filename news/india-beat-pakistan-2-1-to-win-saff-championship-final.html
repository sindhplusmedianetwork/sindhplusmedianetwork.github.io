India beat Pakistan 2-1 to win SAFF Championship final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India came from behind to beat Pakistan 2-1 in the SAFF Championship final at the National Stadium in Dhaka on Tuesday. Pakistan took the lead just before half-time through Alamghir Ghazi and dominated much of the first half. The match turned in the 57th minute when Pakistan's Junaid Shah was sent off, reducing the side to ten men.

India equalised a minute later through Sohail Ahmed Butt to make it 1-1. Sandip Mandi then scored the winner in the 87th minute to seal the title for India. The victory gave India a record-extending tenth SAFF Championship crown.

Pakistan, who had reached their first-ever final, finished as runners-up.
