Senate Tabled Bill Seeking Quarterly Gas Price Revisions Instead of Half-Yearly

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (Amendment) Bill, 2026 was introduced in the Senate on Friday, proposing that natural gas prices be determined every quarter instead of twice a year. Minister of State for Interior Tallal Chaudhry tabled the bill, which would insert Section 8A into the Ogra Ordinance, 2002. Under the proposed provision, licensees would be required to submit their revenue requirements each quarter, covering wellhead gas prices and the cost of imported gas.

One clause has already drawn criticism: it states that no public hearing will be held for price revisions under the new section, removing a long-standing layer of transparency. During the same session, PPP Senator Waqar Mehdi opposed the proposed shifting of PPL's head office from Karachi to Islamabad, calling the move unfair to the province. Senators debated whether more frequent price revisions would pass on cost shocks to consumers faster.

The bill will now go through the Senate's legislative process before any decision.
