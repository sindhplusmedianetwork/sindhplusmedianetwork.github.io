Video of Green Electric Bus conductor pushing woman sparks outrage in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A video circulating on social media has triggered widespread criticism after allegedly showing a conductor of a Green Electric Bus pushing a woman off the vehicle while she was attempting to board it on a busy road in Lahore. The footage has drawn public attention, with social media users expressing anger over the treatment of the woman and questioning the conduct of public transport staff. A user sharing the video on X described the incident as deeply disturbing and criticised the conductor's behaviour, calling for proper ethical training of staff before their recruitment.

The post also referred to Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif's emphasis on public service standards, arguing that such conduct goes against the expectations associated with the province's public transport initiatives. Other users also condemned the incident, describing the treatment of the woman as unacceptable and demanding accountability, with one user calling the episode extremely shameful and saying the behaviour deserved strong condemnation. Details surrounding the incident, including the circumstances that led to the confrontation, have yet to emerge, and no official statement or response from the relevant authorities has been reported so far.
