Air Chief Sidhu meets Uzbek defence minister, agree to strengthen defence cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Air Chief Marshal Zaheer Ahmed Babar Sidhu met Uzbekistan's defence minister and the deputy defence minister and commander of air defence and air force, discussing regional security and bilateral defence relations. According to the ISPR, the air chief is on an important visit to Uzbekistan. During the meeting, the Uzbek defence minister praised the Pakistan Air Force's professionalism and combat capability, and the two sides also discussed promoting defence cooperation between Pakistan and Uzbekistan.

The two sides discussed increasing cooperation through joint military exercises and professional exchanges, and reaffirmed their commitment to cooperate in operational preparedness and modern technology. The air chief highlighted the deep brotherly relations between Pakistan and Uzbekistan, and both countries agreed to further strengthen defence cooperation by benefiting from each other's expertise and capabilities.
