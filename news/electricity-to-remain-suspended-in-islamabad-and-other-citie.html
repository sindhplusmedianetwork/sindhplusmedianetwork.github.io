Electricity to Remain Suspended in Islamabad and Other Cities Tomorrow, IESCO Issues Schedule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad Electric Supply Company has announced that electricity supply will remain suspended in Islamabad and several other cities on November 2 under its annual power distribution system maintenance programme. According to the utility, a schedule of limited-duration temporary shutdowns has been issued to facilitate development projects, new flyovers and underpasses, construction of new 11KV feeders, road widening and the shifting of poles and other installations. The schedule spans October 15, 2026 to February 8, 2027, during which repair and upgradation of various feeders will be carried out.

For November 2, shutdowns have been approved on account of annual maintenance of 11KV feeders, construction and development works, and the upkeep of grid power transformers. The affected operation circles include Islamabad, Rawalpindi City, Rawalpindi Cantt, Attock, Jhelum and Chakwal. For consumers' convenience, the temporary shutdown schedules are also uploaded on IESCO's official website and its social media platforms.

The management has offered a sincere apology for the inconvenience and thanked consumers for their cooperation.
