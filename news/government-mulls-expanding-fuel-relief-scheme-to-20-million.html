Government mulls expanding fuel relief scheme to 20 million people

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government is weighing a proposal to widen the fuel relief scheme so that more citizens can benefit from financial relief on petrol. Under the plan being reviewed, the number of beneficiaries could rise from 12.7 million to 20 million. The prime minister's adviser on political affairs confirmed that the expansion proposal is under consideration, but stressed that no final decision has been taken and no implementation date has been announced.

The current scheme allows eligible motorbike riders, three-wheeler users and owners of cars with engines below 800cc to receive petrol relief of up to Rs 100 per litre. Officials are examining various aspects of the scheme to ease the financial burden on the public from rising travel costs. If approved, hundreds of thousands more people could benefit, though details about the relief amount, eligibility criteria and registration process have not yet emerged.

Until a formal announcement, the existing rules of the scheme will remain in force.
