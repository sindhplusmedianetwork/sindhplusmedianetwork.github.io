43 Candidates' Fake Marksheets Exposed in Sindh Medical Admissions Scandal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A major scandal has surfaced in Sindh's medical admissions after 43 candidates were found to have submitted fake intermediate marksheets. During verification of the marksheets submitted for MBBS admissions 2026-27 at Liaquat University of Medical and Health Sciences in Jamshoro, the numbers did not match the Hyderabad Intermediate Board's official gazette. The university says the admissions of all 43 candidates are being cancelled.

A five-year educational ban may also be imposed on them. The discovery has raised serious questions about the checking of documents in the admission process.
