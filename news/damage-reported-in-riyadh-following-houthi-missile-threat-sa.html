Damage reported in Riyadh following Houthi missile threat, Saudi-led coalition says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A skyscraper in Riyadh housing the headquarters of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund was damaged, witnesses told AFP, following weeks of attacks on the kingdom by Yemen's Houthis. The facade was damaged across several floors with windows blown out in the 80-storey tower, the tallest in Riyadh's financial district. The Saudi-led coalition said it dealt with a missile threat launched by the Houthis towards Riyadh, with debris falling and material damage reported.

The full extent of the damage is still being assessed. It was not yet clear whether there were any casualties.
