How Kohli can reach 100 centuries, Gavaskar explains

Former Indian cricketer Sunil Gavaskar has explained how Virat Kohli could reach 100 international centuries. Writing in his column, Gavaskar said that if a teammate sacrifices his own runs to help Kohli, the star batter could reach the historic milestone. Kohli has scored 86 international centuries so far.

The world record of 100 international centuries is held only by Sachin Tendulkar. Kohli now plays only one-day cricket, which makes the milestone a big challenge for him..
