Saudi-Led Coalition Says It Downed Houthi Drone, Missiles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Saudi-led coalition has said it intercepted and destroyed a Houthi ballistic missile targeting southern Saudi Arabia. Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki said a Houthi drone in the Riyadh region was also shot down. He added that a Houthi cruise missile targeting western Saudi Arabia was intercepted as well.

The claims come amid escalating hostilities between the coalition and the Houthi movement. Houthi military spokesperson Yahya Saree denied the coalition's account, saying the group carried out no operation toward Saudi territory on Friday. Saree said the Houthis target oil facilities and military bases.

The exchange of claims highlights the continuing volatility along the Yemen-Saudi border. There was no immediate independent verification of either side's account.
