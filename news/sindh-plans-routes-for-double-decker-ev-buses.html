Sindh plans routes for double-decker, EV buses

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh's public transport network is set for another expansion, with the government moving to finalise routes and operational arrangements for a fresh fleet of double-decker and electric buses. The new buses are expected to arrive in Karachi this month. The directives were issued by Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon while he chaired a meeting of the Transport and Mass Transit Department.

The meeting reviewed preparations for the incoming fleet and progress on bus rapid transit projects in the province. The fleet expansion will add double-decker and electric buses to the city's public transport network.
