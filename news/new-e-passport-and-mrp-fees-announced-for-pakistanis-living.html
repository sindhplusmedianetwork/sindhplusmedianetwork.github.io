New E-Passport and MRP Fees Announced for Pakistanis Living in UAE

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New passport fees for Pakistanis residing in the United Arab Emirates were announced on Thursday, according to ARY News. The fee schedule was issued by the Pakistani embassy, with all fees to be collected in UAE dirhams. Under the new rates, a 36-page five-year e-passport will cost 145 dirhams under normal processing and 235 dirhams for urgent processing, while the 72-page version is priced at 260 dirhams normal and 425 dirhams urgent.

A 36-page ten-year Machine Readable Passport will cost 215 dirhams normal and 355 dirhams urgent, and the 72-page version 390 dirhams normal and 635 dirhams urgent. Applicants will pay double the fee for a first lost passport and four times the fee for a second loss. The announcement came as e-passport issuance began at Pakistani diplomatic missions in Pakistan and around the world.

Officials said the e-passport carries modern security features that meet international standards, while already-issued MRPs will remain valid until their expiry.
