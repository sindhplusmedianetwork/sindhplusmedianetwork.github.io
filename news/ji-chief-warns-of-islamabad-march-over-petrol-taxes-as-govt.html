JI Chief Warns of Islamabad March Over Petrol Taxes as Govt Seeks Fresh Talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami has announced it will begin the third phase of its protest campaign against the petroleum levy on October 23, with sit-ins planned at district headquarters across the country. The party's leadership has said the demonstrations will continue until the government agrees to withdraw the levy and provide relief in fuel prices. The announcement follows the party's earlier decision to postpone its Islamabad long march on September 22 after a telephone request from Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, who promised a meeting with the JI leadership on his return.

That proposed meeting has not yet taken place. In response to the renewed protest call, Federal Minister for Planning Ahsan Iqbal contacted JI Vice Emir Liaquat Baloch to arrange a meeting between the negotiating committees of both sides in Islamabad on Friday. Baloch said the prime minister is also expected to meet JI Emir Hafiz Naeemur Rehman the same day.

He added that negotiations and protests would continue side by side, and that the party would not back down from its demand for the levy's abolition. JI first launched its anti-levy sit-ins in August in Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar and Quetta, and staged a nationwide shutdown strike in early September.
