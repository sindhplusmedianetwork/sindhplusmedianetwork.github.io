Electricity Likely to Get Costlier: Rs2.56 Per Unit Hike in the Works

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Electricity is likely to become more expensive for consumers as preparations are under way for a Rs2.56 per unit price hike. The Central Power Purchasing Agency (CPPA) has filed a request with the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (NEPRA), which is scheduled to take it up for hearing on October 29. Officials told the regulator that the average fuel cost for September stood at Rs10.02 per unit.

The total cost of power generation for the month was reported at Rs127.87 billion. If approved, the increase would be passed on to consumers in their upcoming bills. The development comes as households and businesses already grapple with high energy costs.
