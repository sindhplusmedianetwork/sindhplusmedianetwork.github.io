Karachi police solve sack-bound body case in six hours, victim's two brothers arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Boat Basin police in Karachi solved the case of a torture-marked body found tied in ropes inside a sack within six hours, arresting the victim's two brothers on murder charges. Police said the body, found partially buried near Umar Sharif Park in Clifton Block 2, was identified through a police search app as Kamran Khan alias Sheena, son of Gul Lalay. The initial medical report indicated that the victim was smothered with a pillow.

Police included the victim's two brothers, Sher Muhammad and Wahid Ali, in the investigation on suspicion, and during interrogation the accused confessed to involvement in their brother's killing. Police said the accused revealed during interrogation that they killed their brother at their home in Shireen Jinnah Colony during a domestic dispute, then tied the body in a sack with ropes. The victim had a criminal record, with three cases registered against him, including drug use and sale.

Police said Boat Basin police solved the blind murder case in only six hours through professional skill, modern investigation methods and timely action, and both accused brothers are in police custody while further investigation is under way.
