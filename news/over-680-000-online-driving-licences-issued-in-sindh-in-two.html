Over 680,000 online driving licences issued in Sindh in two years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh's online driving licence system has completed two years since its launch on October 24, 2024, with officials reporting a major shift toward digital public services. DIG Training and Licence Sindh Younus Chandio said 947,318 users have registered on the online system across the province. Citizens sitting at home have issued 680,522 online driving licences themselves through the system.

Of these, 601,059 citizens issued their own learner driving licences, 57,432 renewed their licences online and received them at home, and 22,031 issued their own international driving permits. Chandio said citizens are making full use of the modern facilities provided by the Sindh government, and that the system has ended bribery in the process. He added that special teams also visit people at home to issue licences, making the service accessible even to those who cannot use the online system themselves.
