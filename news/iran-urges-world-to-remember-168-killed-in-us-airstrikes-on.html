Iran Urges World to Remember 168 Killed in US Airstrikes on Minab School

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's mission to the United Nations on Friday urged the international community to remember the children killed in US airstrikes on an Iranian school in Minab and to draw attention to the suffering of survivors. In a statement on the social media platform X marking World Children's Day, the mission said 168 children and teachers were killed in two US airstrikes on the school in the city of Minab. The mission said the voice of Minab's innocent children should be heard and their memory honoured.

It also highlighted the plight of approximately 300 students who survived the attacks but continue to suffer severe psychological trauma, requiring long-term care, treatment and support. A UN-backed fact-finding mission recently said there were reasonable grounds to believe the United States had committed war crimes in the two Tomahawk airstrikes in Iran, including one that struck the school in Minab. US Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth previously said the Pentagon was investigating the incident but gave no details on accountability or a timeline.
