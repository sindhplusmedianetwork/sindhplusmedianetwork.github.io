£190m corruption case: IHC directs proper medical care, hygienic living conditions for Bushra Bibi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court on Wednesday directed prison authorities to ensure proper medical care and hygienic living conditions for Bushra Bibi, the wife of PTI founder Imran Khan. The direction came in the £190 million corruption case, amid concerns about her eyesight. The court adjourned for seven days the hearing of appeals filed by the couple against their convictions.

Imran Khan was sentenced to 14 years in prison in the Al-Qadir Trust case in January 2025. Bushra Bibi was sentenced to seven years in the same case. The court's order requires the prison authorities to provide her with proper medical attention and clean living conditions while the appeals remain pending.
