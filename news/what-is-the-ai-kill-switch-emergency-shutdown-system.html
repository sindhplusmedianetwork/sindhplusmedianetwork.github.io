What is the AI 'kill switch' emergency shutdown system?

As artificial intelligence systems grow more powerful, experts are debating the need for an emergency shutdown mechanism known as the 'kill switch,' writes Rafiq Mangat. The kill switch is an emergency button designed to stop AI immediately in a crisis or to limit its activities if it begins taking unexpected or dangerous actions. Its core purpose is to let humans intervene at once and regain control of the system.

Beyond the shutdown itself, the article lists separate protective measures: cutting off the AI's access, revoking its powers, and compensating for damage caused by its actions. The piece underscores that emergency human intervention remains essential as AI takes on more decision-making power.
