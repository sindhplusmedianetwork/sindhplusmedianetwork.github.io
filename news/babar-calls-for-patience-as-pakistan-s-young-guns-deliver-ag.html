Babar Calls for Patience as Pakistan's Young Guns Deliver Against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan captain Babar Azam has urged patience in the development of the team's young players, saying sustained opportunities and confidence will be crucial to building a consistent side after Pakistan defeated Sri Lanka by eight wickets in the second Test to clinch the two-match series 1-0. The victory was achieved despite losing frontline fast bowler Saqib Khan to injury during the match. Babar said the team had planned to introduce fresh faces following the series against England and was pleased with how the newcomers responded, but stressed that one series or a single victory would not be enough to establish the team's credentials.

'We didn't achieve anything. It's just a series. You don't achieve anything by winning a match.

It takes time for that,' he said. Pakistan's victory was built on dismissing Sri Lanka for 98 in the first innings, although a second-innings partnership involving Kusal Mendis and Sonal Dinusha briefly put Pakistan under pressure. Babar praised Aamer Jamal, Razaullah and Mohammad Abbas for absorbing the increased workload with long spells, and described Saud Shakeel as the backbone of Pakistan's Test batting.

He revealed five or six members of the emerging group were expected to feature in four-day cricket in the coming days.
