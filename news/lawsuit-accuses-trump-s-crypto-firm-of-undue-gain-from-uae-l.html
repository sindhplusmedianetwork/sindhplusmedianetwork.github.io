Lawsuit Accuses Trump's Crypto Firm of Undue Gain From UAE-Linked Deals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A lawsuit has been filed against World Liberty Financial, the cryptocurrency company linked to US President Donald Trump and his family. The suit alleges the company gained undue benefit from foreign investments and caused financial harm to a rival firm. According to US media, the case was filed by J.

Benjamin Greenberg, a shareholder of crypto company Circle Internet Group. The petition claims two UAE-linked financial deals benefited Trump's business interests while harming Circle and its investors. The first was a UAE investment fund's $2 billion investment in crypto exchange Binance, paid through World Liberty Financial's USD1 stablecoin — a move the petitioner says boosted the coin and Trump family business interests.

The second deal, agreed four days before Trump took office, saw a UAE investment institution agree to pay $500 million for a 49 percent stake in World Liberty Financial. The suit alleges a violation of the US constitutional clause barring federal officials from receiving financial benefits from foreign governments without Congress's permission, and also names US special envoy Steve Witkoff. The White House rejected the allegations, saying Trump's assets are held in a trust.
