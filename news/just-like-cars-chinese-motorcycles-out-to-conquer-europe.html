Just Like Cars, Chinese Motorcycles Out to Conquer Europe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At EICMA in Milan, Europe's biggest motorcycle show, visitors were jostling on Friday around the stand of CFMoto, one of the main Chinese brands sold in Europe. The firm from Hangzhou has gained attention by producing engines and assembling models for Austria's KTM, then by winning two Moto3 championships in 2024 and 2026. Fans of BMW, Honda and Ducati came to Milan to admire a powerful model fitted with winglets, as well as the off-road machine ridden by the brand's new ambassador, Spanish rider Mario Roman.

One Italian visitor, 64-year-old craftsman Renato Polistene, said the bikes were beautiful and that at this price, they worked, adding that he could consider choosing Chinese for his next bike as prices for other brands have gone up a lot. In 2025, China became the EU's second-largest supplier of motorcycles, behind Japan, accounting for 27.1 per cent of imports with sales at one billion euros, according to a report published Thursday by the European Association of Motorcycle Manufacturers. In six years, China has gone from a marginal supplier to a key player in the European market, mirroring the rise of Chinese carmakers.
