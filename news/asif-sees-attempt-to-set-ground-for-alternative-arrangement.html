Asif Sees Attempt to Set Ground for 'Alternative Arrangement' in Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has claimed that Chaudhry Salik Hussain's recent corruption allegations against the Punjab government are part of efforts to set the ground for an 'alternative arrangement' to discredit the incumbents and present a fresh team. Speaking on ARY News, Asif said the statement was given selectively against Punjab and alluded to wealthy 'carpetbaggers' active for six to eight months in anticipation of a political crisis. Salik, meanwhile, said he had received a legal notice from CM Maryam Nawaz and would file a reply soon.

Maryam has sent a Rs5 billion defamation notice demanding he retract his remarks.
