Punjab University to Mark Allama Iqbal's 150th Anniversary Throughout the Year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab University Vice Chancellor Dr Muhammad Ali has announced year-long events to mark the 150th anniversary of Allama Muhammad Iqbal. Special programmes will honour his thought, philosophy, poetry and national services. He made the announcement at an Iqbal Day event at the Allama Iqbal Campus, where the university's Music Society performed Iqbal's poetry.

A university delegation also visited Iqbal's mausoleum, laid a floral wreath and offered Fateha. The year-long programme will keep Iqbal's legacy in focus across the university.
