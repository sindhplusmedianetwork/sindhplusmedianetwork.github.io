Tarbela T-5 work suspended indefinitely after protective bund breach floods Tunnel Five

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Construction work on the Tarbela Dam's T-5 hydropower project has been suspended indefinitely after a protective bund was breached on Monday. A large volume of water flooded Tunnel Five following the breach. Haripur Deputy Commissioner Waseem Ahmed visited the site and confirmed that all Pakistani and foreign workers were safely evacuated.

One worker who was swept into the water was rescued, and no casualties were reported. An inquiry has been ordered into the cause of the breach.
