America Day: US Performers Highlight 22nd Day of World Culture Festival in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Music lovers were treated to a foot-tapping show on Thursday evening at the World Culture Festival 2026 when American artists Elio Villafranca, Aaron English and Caroline Cotter jammed with Pakistan's Ahsan Bari and Arman Rahim for a touch more than an hour at the Arts Council of Pakistan, Karachi. The programme, hosted by Bari, was held to celebrate 250 years of American independence. Villafranca performed with tabla player Waqas Gulab, and flute player Rahat Inayat joined for a slow number dedicated to Cuba.

Arman Rahim sang a Punjabi folk piece beginning with Bulleh Shah's words, while Caroline Cotter sang 'Let there be peace', a song she wrote after a trip to Japan with reference to Hiroshima. Earlier, US Consul-General Jai Nair addressed the audience and congratulated the Arts Council for arranging the festival. Later in the Jaun Elia lawn, Danse Macabre directed by Italy's Dr Azzurro De Gregoria, a dance theatre performance about the inevitability of death, grabbed the audience's interest.
