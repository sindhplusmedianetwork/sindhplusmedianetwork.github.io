Tallal Chaudhry Rebukes Fazlur Rehman Over 'Share' in KP After Governor's Rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

State Minister for Interior Tallal Chaudhry on Saturday criticised JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman for reportedly demanding his 'share' in the governance of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa following the imposition of governor's rule and an emergency in the province. Speaking to the media in Jaranwala, he insisted the move was not aimed at strengthening the PML-N in the province by dismissing the PTI-led government, calling it an 'administrative setup' with a one-point agenda of combating terrorism. 'This is not spoils of war,' he said, adding that the decision was an 'immensely responsible' one taken after every other option had been considered.

He maintained that the federal government had no intention of establishing a political setup in KP, noting that only one member of the KP Advisory Council had a political background, as she represents the families of martyrs. Chaudhry rejected the impression that the emergency was aimed at capturing KP's natural resources and criticised Fazl for invoking the 1971 separation of East Pakistan in his recent statements. He further claimed there was a 'relationship or mutual interest arrangement' between the TTP and the PTI, alleging that no PTI leader had been targeted by terrorists since 2015.

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif, speaking separately in Sialkot, defended the decision, shared details of mining contract holders in KP and said free and fair elections would be held when the time came. The remarks followed Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's statement a day earlier that a party head was 'extremely upset' over being denied the KP governorship, an apparent reference to Fazl.
