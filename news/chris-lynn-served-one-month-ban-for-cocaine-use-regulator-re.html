Chris Lynn Served One-Month Ban for Cocaine Use, Regulator Reveals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Australia's cricket regulator has revealed that aggressive batter Chris Lynn served a one-month ban for cocaine use. Lynn, 36, returned a positive doping test for benzoylecgonine after a sample was taken on T20 Blast Finals Day on July 18, 2026. He was playing for Northamptonshire at the time.

Lynn admitted using cocaine between July 15 and 16, and said the use was not during competition and was unrelated to performance. He completed a rehabilitation programme as part of the process. The ban ran from July 31 to August 30, 2026.

The disclosure was made public by the regulator after the suspension had already been served.
