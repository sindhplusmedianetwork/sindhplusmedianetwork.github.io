LCCI urges stronger industry-academia links to upgrade small industries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry Senior Vice President Sardar Usman Ghani has said stronger collaboration between industry and academia is essential for upgrading small industries, modernising their technology and helping them compete more effectively in export markets. The call came during a meeting with Farhan Riaz, Head of Incubation at the University of Engineering and Technology Lahore, held at the Lahore Chamber. The two sides discussed the need for practical, result-oriented linkages between the business community and academic institutions to address challenges facing Lahore's economy and support sustainable economic growth.

Ghani said Lahore had long been a vital engine of Pakistan's economy, but its small and medium-sized industries required immediate technological upgrading and stronger institutional support to remain competitive in domestic and international markets. He said institutions such as UET Lahore possessed research capacity, technical expertise and innovation infrastructure that could help local industries adopt modern technologies and improve productivity. He added that the business community and universities must move beyond symbolic collaboration and work together on concrete projects that deliver measurable economic benefits.
