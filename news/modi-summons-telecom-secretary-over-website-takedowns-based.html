Modi summons telecom secretary over website takedowns based on fake court orders

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi's office has summoned the telecommunications secretary over the takedown of websites based on fake court orders. Officials had ordered the takedown of more than 100 websites using court orders that Reuters revealed were fake. Telecom officials directed internet providers to block 129 illegal betting websites.

The orders used were fabricated High Court orders emailed by people posing as lawyers. The case has triggered a criminal investigation for forgery and cheating. It has also raised concerns that India's content-takedown system lacks safeguards against falsified court documents.

The secretary has been asked to explain how the orders were acted on without verification.
