Pakistani Products Shine on Day One of SIAL Paris 2026 as 37 Companies Showcase

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani products drew strong attention on the opening day of SIAL Paris 2026, the international food trade exhibition running in Paris from October 17 to 21, 2026. Pakistan's ambassador to France, Mumtaz Zahra Baloch, inaugurated the Pakistan pavilion, where 37 Pakistani companies are participating under the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan. The companies are presenting rice, ready-to-eat food products, pink salt and beverages, among other goods.

Biryani stood out on the first day, drawing special attention from visitors and becoming a centre of interest. Pakistan's large-scale participation comes as the country seeks to grow its exports and reach new international markets for its products. SIAL Paris counts among the world's leading international food exhibitions, giving Pakistani firms a platform to present their products to global buyers, build business links and access new trade markets.

The exhibition's first day ended with Pakistan prominent among participants.
