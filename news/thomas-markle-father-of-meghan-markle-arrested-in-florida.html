Thomas Markle, father of Meghan Markle, arrested in Florida

Thomas Markle, the 82-year-old father of Meghan Markle, was arrested in Lakeland, Florida, after FBI officials took him into custody from a retirement home on Friday. He faces charges of possessing child sexual abuse images, the Daily Mail reported, citing unnamed sources. The FBI did not immediately comment on the arrest.

Reports say such images were also previously found at his former home in the Philippines and at a rented storage unit in Mexico. His son found images last month while searching through boxes from the Philippines home. Meghan said last month that she was deeply concerned by the allegations and had cut off her children's contact with her father.

Thomas Markle moved back to the United States from Cebu in the Philippines earlier this year.
