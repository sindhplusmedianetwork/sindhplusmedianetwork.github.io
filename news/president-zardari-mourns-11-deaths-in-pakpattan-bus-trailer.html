President Zardari mourns 11 deaths in Pakpattan bus-trailer collision

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari has expressed deep sorrow and grief over the loss of 11 lives in a collision between a passenger bus and a trailer near Pakpattan. In a statement issued by the Presidency, he extended heartfelt condolences to the bereaved families. The president prayed for the elevation of the ranks of those who died in the accident.

He also prayed for the speedy recovery of the people injured in the collision. The crash took place near Pakpattan, and the president's message conveyed sympathy to all those affected.
