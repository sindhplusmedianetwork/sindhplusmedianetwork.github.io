Bus carrying restaurant staff crashes into bridge wall in Karachi, one killed and 19 injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A bus carrying 20 restaurant employees went out of the driver's control on Shahrah-e-Faisal in Karachi and crashed into the wall of the Baloch Colony bridge. One person, identified as Altaf, died during treatment after the crash. Nineteen other people travelling in the bus were injured.

The driver was trapped in the wreckage of the bus and was rescued in critical condition. Rescue workers and police reached the scene and shifted the injured to hospital. Police say they are investigating whether the crash was caused by driver negligence.
