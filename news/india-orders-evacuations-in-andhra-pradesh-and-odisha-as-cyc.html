India Orders Evacuations in Andhra Pradesh and Odisha as Cyclone Arnab Nears

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian authorities ordered nearly 100,000 people to evacuate homes in Andhra Pradesh and Odisha, ahead of a severe cyclone the national weather office expects to make landfall by Wednesday. Cyclone Arnab is forecast to hit India's southwestern Andhra Pradesh coast as a very severe cyclonic storm, with gusts of up to 150 kilometres per hour on Wednesday morning, the India Meteorological Department said. Torrential rain lashed the coast in the port city of Kakinada as heavy waves crashed on shore, and Indian media reported that as many as 50,000 people had been told to move to safe areas in Andhra Pradesh and 48,000 in Odisha.

Neighbouring Sri Lanka closed schools with warnings of torrential rain, with its Department of Meteorology warning that storms could trigger flash floods and mudslides, and the military was placed on alert. Nepal's disaster authority also issued a warning, saying that as the storm travels inland towards the Himalayas it will bring heavy rainfall and a high risk of heavy snowfall, snowstorms and avalanches. India's weather bureau said it expects the cyclone to result in the total destruction of thatched houses, localised flooding, uprooting of power and communication poles, and widespread damage to standing crops.

Odisha opened 1,735 cyclone shelters, and more than 4,700 pregnant women in potentially dangerous sites were moved to hospitals.
