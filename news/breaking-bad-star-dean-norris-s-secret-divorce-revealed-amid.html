Dean Norris and his wife Bridget Norris have quietly parted their ways. In a bombshell update, the Breaking Bad actor and his wife's secret divorce finally came to light after more than a year, as Page Six reported on Wednesday, November 4, that the couple have been separated since early 2025. As per the court documents, Bridget Norris quietly filed for divorce from her estranged husband last year on February 14.

Dean responded to the filing in May 2025. However, their divorce, according to the outlet, has not been finalised yet. The separated couple's explosive divorce update comes amid their million-dollar financial crisis, as they filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy for their restaurant Swing Inn Cafe in Temecula, California, on September 25, 2026.

For those unfamiliar, Chapter 11 is a type of bankruptcy that allows a business struggling with debt to reorganise its finances and restructure its liabilities while continuing to operate. Court documents reportedly show approximately 147,810 dollars in assets against 1.81 million dollars in liabilities, and the restaurant's revenue also declined by around 1 million dollars between 2024 and 2025. The former flames met in the late 1990s in California while the actor was focused on establishing his career in acting and Bridget was working as an entertainment attorney.

On October 27, 2001, they tied the knot in an intimate ceremony in the presence of their close friends and family, and currently share five children together.
