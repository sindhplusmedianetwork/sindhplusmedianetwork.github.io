Auditor General Flags SBCA Failures as 740 Dangerous Karachi Buildings Await Demolition

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The annual audit report of the Auditor General of Pakistan has pointed out multiple irregularities and shortcomings in the Sindh Building Control Authority's performance, financial matters and building control. The report revealed that 740 dangerous buildings in Karachi were never demolished, while contracts for demolishing dangerous buildings were awarded without the required machinery and technical expertise. It also questioned the performance of the single-window system introduced for issuing construction permits, noting that permits took an average of 50.85 days to issue despite the facility, compared to an average of 14 days under the manual system in 2019.

The report further flagged the issuance of construction permits without the mandatory NOC for sale and advertisement, warning that buyers of such units could face legal complications. Objections were also raised over renaming and relaunching two projects in Naya Nazimabad and constructing additional floors without fresh agreements with allottees. The report pointed to irregularities in the Globe Residency One and Two projects, approval of building plans by unlicensed builders, and the construction of extra floors in high-rise buildings without strength and stability tests or soil testing.
