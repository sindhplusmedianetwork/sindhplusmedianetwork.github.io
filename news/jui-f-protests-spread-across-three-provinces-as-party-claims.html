JUI-F protests spread across three provinces as party claims dozens of workers arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl staged protests across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan and parts of Punjab on Friday over what it described as the detention of its chief, Maulana Fazlur Rehman, even as police denied that he had been taken into custody. The party's Malakand chapter claimed that 38 of its workers had been detained, while leaders in Shangla said their district emir and several protesters were arrested on the Karakoram Highway at Bisham, and the party alleged that several activists were also arrested and booked in Punjab during Thursday's protests. In Balochistan, the party said its provincial emir, Senator Maulana Abdul Wasay, and the opposition leader in the provincial assembly, Mir Younas Aziz Zehri, had been placed under house arrest, and that lawmakers, office-bearers and workers had been arrested in Quetta, Chaman, Kalat, Surab, Dera Bugti, Hub and other districts.

In Peshawar, 12 of the 16 leaders named in a case over blocking the M-1 Motorway were said to be in custody, while police booked 19 leaders and 200 workers near the Chashma checkpost and arrested 32 workers from Chakdara Bridge. A rally in Quetta led by district emir Maulana Abdul Rehman Rafiq ended in a public meeting at Manan Chowk, where speakers condemned the arrests, accused the authorities of suppressing political dissent and said the party would continue its peaceful, constitutional struggle. In Sargodha, seven activists were arrested on Friday, days after more than two dozen local leaders were taken in, and police sealed the city's entry points while keeping watch on party leaders and seminaries in Sahiwal and Pakpattan.

Fazl, in a message to workers in Lahore, said the protest would continue and that arrests would not block their way, adding that the leadership would decide the next step, while he claimed he had been arrested. Lahore police chief Bilal Siddique Kamyana denied this, saying Fazl had been stuck in traffic when reports of his arrest surfaced and had since been allowed to return to his Lahore residence, and that his movement had not been restricted.
