K-P Rich in Minerals; We Will 'Extract and Sell Them', Says Governor Kundi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has announced that the province will exploit and sell its mineral wealth, promising multiple agreements to lift people's living standards. 'Our province is rich in minerals, and we will extract and sell these minerals. We will have many agreements signed so that people can prosper,' Kundi said while addressing the media during a convocation ceremony in Peshawar on October 17.

He defended the proposed exploitation of the province's mineral resources, arguing that investment in the sector could generate employment, support local businesses and improve economic conditions. 'If an international company comes and enters into a joint venture with Pakistani companies and the people of K-P, providing them with employment and economic support, why are you speaking against this?' he asked. The governor added that oil and gas reserves should also be extracted to support economic growth.

He argued that economic prosperity and access to education would make communities less vulnerable to extremist narratives, and criticised clerics who, he alleged, encouraged poor families to send their children to fight while remaining comfortable themselves. He also said the provincial government had been formed despite his opposition and continued to run successfully.
