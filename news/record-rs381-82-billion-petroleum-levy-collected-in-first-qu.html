Record Rs381.82 Billion Petroleum Levy Collected in First Quarter of Current Fiscal Year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

For the first time in the country's history, the highest petroleum levy collection in any first quarter of a fiscal year has been recorded. According to official documents, Rs381.82 billion was collected as petroleum levy during the first quarter of the current fiscal year, from July to September 2026. The documents show that the collection is Rs10.21 billion higher on an annual basis than the petroleum levy collected in the same quarter last year.

The disclosure indicates that citizens have come under the heaviest petroleum levy burden on record. The figures were reported on the basis of official documents.
