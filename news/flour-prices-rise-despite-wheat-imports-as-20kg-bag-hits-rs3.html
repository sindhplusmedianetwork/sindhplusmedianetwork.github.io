Flour Prices Rise Despite Wheat Imports as 20kg Bag Hits Rs3,600 in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Flour prices have risen across Pakistan despite wheat imports, according to documents of the Federal Bureau of Statistics. In Karachi, the 20-kilogram flour bag became costlier by Rs100 to reach Rs3,600, while the price of the 20kg bag rose by up to Rs334 within a week. The documents show that in Islamabad, Hyderabad and Bannu the 20kg flour bag also reached Rs3,600.

In Peshawar, the 20kg bag rose by Rs250 to Rs3,550, while in Rawalpindi it increased by Rs66.66 to Rs3,533. In Lahore, the 20kg bag rose by Rs333 to Rs3,467, and in Gujranwala it also rose by Rs333 to Rs3,400. In Sukkur, the price of the 20kg bag increased by Rs280 to Rs3,360, while in Quetta it rose by Rs110 to Rs3,330.
