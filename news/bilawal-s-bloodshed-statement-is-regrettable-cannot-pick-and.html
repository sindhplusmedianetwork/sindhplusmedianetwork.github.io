Bilawal's Bloodshed Statement Is Regrettable, Cannot Pick and Choose: Hanif Abbasi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Railways Minister Hanif Abbasi has called Bilawal Bhutto's statement about bloodshed regrettable, saying one cannot pick and choose and that it is not appropriate to get angry so quickly over decisions that are yet to be made. Talking to media at Fatima Jinnah University in Rawalpindi, he said Bilawal Bhutto Zardari is still respectable and an ally for them, general elections will be held in 2029 at their time, and the PPP holds big constitutional posts as well as governments at many places. He said whatever legislation was done, they stamped it everywhere, and if a 28th amendment comes, Bilawal will stamp it.

He said no decision has been made anywhere yet on provinces, and when a decision on provinces is made, there will be debate in parliament too. He added that the talk of provinces has been heard for 30 years; if they have not been made yet, they may not be made, and if provinces are to be made, they may be made in three months.
