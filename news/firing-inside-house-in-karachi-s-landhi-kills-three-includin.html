Firing inside house in Karachi's Landhi kills three including woman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three people including a woman were killed in firing inside a house near Old Muzaffarabad in Landhi, Karachi. According to an Edhi spokesperson, the deceased include a husband and wife and the woman's brother, and police reached the scene on receiving the report and also called the crime scene unit. Police said the husband shot his wife and brother-in-law before killing himself, and the deceased woman had approached the court for khula.

Police said the couple had been married for 15 years and were living separately for some days, with the woman residing at her parents' home in Muzaffarabad Colony. Police officials said the husband was residing in Shah Latif Town, and in the morning he reached his in-laws' home and opened fire on his wife and brother-in-law, after which the bodies were shifted to Jinnah Hospital. According to Quaidabad police, Nasir Ahmed son of Maqbool Ahmed shot dead his brother-in-law Muhammad Ahsan son of Muhammad Arif and his wife Asma, and later shot himself, while a pistol was recovered from the scene.
