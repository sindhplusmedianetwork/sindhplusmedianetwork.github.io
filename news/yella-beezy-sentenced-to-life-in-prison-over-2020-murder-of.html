Yella Beezy sentenced to life in prison over 2020 murder of rapper MO3

Rapper Yella Beezy has been sentenced to life in prison without the possibility of parole after being convicted of hiring the gunman who killed fellow rapper MO3 in Dallas in 2020. The verdict was delivered by a Dallas County jury on Wednesday, according to the Associated Press. Beezy, 35, whose real name is Markies Conway, was unanimously found guilty of capital murder in the death of Melvin Noble, known professionally as MO3.

Prosecutors said Conway paid gunman Kewon White $40,000 to carry out the killing following an escalating feud between the two musicians. White was convicted of capital murder in September and also received a life sentence without the possibility of parole. Noble, 28, was driving along Interstate 35 in south Dallas around midday on November 11, 2020, when White allegedly approached in another vehicle, got out carrying a firearm and chased him across the busy motorway, firing more than 40 rounds from an AK-47-style pistol.

Noble was shot and later died in hospital, while a bystander inside a vehicle was also wounded but survived. During closing arguments, prosecutors presented footage of Conway discussing revenge, while defence attorney John Gussio argued that the prosecution had presented speculation rather than sufficient evidence.
