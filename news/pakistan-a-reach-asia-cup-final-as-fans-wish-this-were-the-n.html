Pakistan A reach Asia Cup final as fans wish this were the national team

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan A qualified for the final of the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament in Doha after beating Bangladesh A by five wickets in the second semi-final. Bangladesh A, batting first after winning the toss, made 232 for eight in their 50 overs, with Shahadat Hossain carrying his bat for an unbeaten 103 off 118 balls, including seven fours and three sixes. Mahidul Islam Ankon supported him with 42 in a 95-run third-wicket stand, while Sufiyan Muqeem was Pakistan A's most successful bowler with three wickets for 29 runs.

Chasing 233, Pakistan A lost their first two wickets for 21 runs before Hasan Nawaz and Shahzeb Khan added 100 runs for the third wicket to change the course of the match. Hasan Nawaz scored 62 off 91 balls and Shahzeb Khan made 50 off 85, and captain Abdul Samad then struck a quick 44 off 33 balls while Farhan Yousuf finished unbeaten on 46 off 39. Pakistan A reached the target in the second ball of the 45th over, losing five wickets.

Former opener Ahmed Shehzad praised the win on social media, and fans said the young side looked more impressive than the national team, with several naming Hasan Nawaz, Farhan Yousuf, Sufiyan Muqeem and Ali Raza as future national players. Pakistan women's all-rounder Alia Riaz also congratulated the team, writing simply 'Shaheens!' on X.
