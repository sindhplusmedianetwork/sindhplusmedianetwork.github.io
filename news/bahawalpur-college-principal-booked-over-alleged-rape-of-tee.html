Bahawalpur college principal booked over alleged rape of teenage student

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Musafirkhana police in Bahawalpur have booked the principal of a private college for the alleged rape of a 17-year-old first-year student in mauza Khanpur Mirchan. According to the complaint, the girl was waiting at the college gate to leave for home on November 3 when the principal took her into his office and allegedly raped her. Her father said that when his daughter returned home, she told him what had happened.

He said his biradari had prevented him from lodging a complaint with the police, but he filed the complaint. No arrest had been made so far in the case. A Musafirkhana police official said the case was under investigation.

The victim's parents have appealed to the Punjab chief minister to direct police to take immediate action and arrest the suspect.
