Russia and China Call for Immediate End to War Against Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Russian and Chinese ambassadors to the United Nations have expressed concern over US and Israeli threats of renewed hostilities against Iran. Both envoys stressed the need to pursue political and diplomatic means to establish peace in the region. China's Ambassador Fu Cong said the risk of a large-scale conflict breaking out in the region remains high.

The live coverage also reported that Iran's navy said an oil tanker caught fire after hitting a naval mine in the eastern Strait of Hormuz. The developments come as fears grow of renewed fighting in the region, with diplomats calling for restraint and a negotiated solution.
