HEC Opens Undergraduate Scholarships for Balochistan and Ex-FATA Students, Apply by 14 December

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) has opened Batch-V undergraduate scholarships for students with Balochistan or erstwhile FATA domicile. The scholarships are for study in all disciplines at HEC-recognised universities in the 2026-27 academic year. Applicants must be 22 years old or younger and must have at least 60 per cent marks in intermediate.

Applications must be submitted online at scholarship.hec.gov.pk by Monday, 14 December 2026. Selection will be made through an aptitude test, on merit and on a district quota basis, with a test fee of Rs2,000. Candidates must score at least 50 per cent in the aptitude test to be shortlisted.

The scholarships will be shared equally, 50:50, between applicants from Balochistan and erstwhile FATA. The advertisement appeared in Daily Jang on 15 November 2026.
