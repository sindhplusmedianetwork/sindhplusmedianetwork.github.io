Brighton stun Man City as 10-man United fall to Chelsea

MANCHESTER: Brighton and Hove Albion inflicted a second straight Premier League defeat on Manchester City with a 1-0 win at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday. Yasin Ayari scored the decisive goal, beating City goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma to close Brighton within two points of the leaders. City's rare home defeat deepens the champions' early-season troubles.

Meanwhile, Manchester United slumped to defeat against Chelsea despite being reduced to 10 men. The results shook up the top of the Premier League table on a dramatic Saturday of action. Brighton's famous victory at the Etihad will go down as one of the results of the season.
