Liquor bottles seized at Punjab University gate, three arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab University security recovered liquor bottles at Gate No. 1 of the New Campus in Lahore. The bottles were recovered during a search of a suspicious van.

A university spokesperson said two men and one woman were arrested during the van checking. The university administration handed the liquor bottles over to Muslim Town Police. The arrested suspects were also handed over to the police for further legal action.
