CM inspection team makes surprise visits to Shahpur and Sahiwal/Sargodha district headquarters hospitals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

On the directions of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz, a Chief Minister's Inspection Team led by Special Assistant Shoaib Mirza paid surprise visits to the Shahpur and Sahiwal/Sargodha district headquarters hospitals. The team arrived at Shahpur Hospital during rain and inspected its wards and departments. It reviewed the working of medical equipment, the supply of medicines and staff attendance at the hospitals.

The team also spoke to patients and their attendants to hear about the facilities available to them. It directed improvements to stretcher and wheelchair facilities at the hospitals.
