Funeral prayers offered for anchorperson Rabia Ahsan in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Funeral prayers for anchorperson Rabia Ahsan were offered at Jamia Masjid Gulshanabad in Rawalpindi, where a large number of citizens attended. A case has been registered at Sadar Bairuni police station on the complaint of her brother, Khurram Ahmed, and her husband Ahsan Tahir is in police custody. According to the FIR, the husband has claimed that the shot was fired accidentally while he was playing with a pistol.

Police say they are investigating the case from all possible angles. Police say the investigation into the circumstances of the death is continuing.
