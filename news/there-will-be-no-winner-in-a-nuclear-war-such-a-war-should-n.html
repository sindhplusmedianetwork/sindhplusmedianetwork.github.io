There Will Be No Winner in a Nuclear War, Such a War Should Not Happen, Says Trump

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said there will be no winner in a nuclear war and such a war should not happen, while the United States, Russia and China agree on talks to reduce nuclear risks. During a press conference at the White House, US President Donald Trump said Iran is desirous of a deal and whatever deal is acceptable to the United States will be made, as the United States wants a real agreement. The US president once again expressed the position that Iran has not been ready for fair and serious negotiations with the United States for more than half a century.

He said that to stop the spread of the nuclear danger we will have to cooperate together, that he is not George Bush, and that Iran will never be allowed to obtain nuclear weapons. The US president described the recent fall in petrol prices in the United States as progress and claimed that prices will soon see a further significant fall, though he admitted that the time of a further fall in petroleum product prices also depends on how the United States settles matters with Iran. Donald Trump said his visit to China went very well, talks with the Russian president and the Chinese president remained constructive, and the best trade deals were made during the Asia tour.

Mentioning the trade talks during the tour, Trump stated that he met more than 20 world leaders in four days, finalised two new trade deals, and about 10 more deals are expected soon, while during this period he participated in four major international summits, reaffirmed relations with allies in the region, and made progress for the promotion of peace and security in the world. Trump further said that US companies will not be allowed to build data centres outside the United States, and if US companies build data centres in foreign countries, heavy taxes will be imposed on them.
