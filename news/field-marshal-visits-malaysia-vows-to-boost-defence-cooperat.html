Field Marshal visits Malaysia, vows to boost defence cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir has paid an official visit to Malaysia, the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said. During the visit, he met Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim in Putrajaya. The two sides discussed bilateral relations, defence and security cooperation, and the changing regional and global security situation.

The Field Marshal was presented a guard of honour at the Malaysian Ministry of Defence and also met the Malaysian defence minister. He was to be inducted as the 21st member of the Hall of Fame at Malaysia's National Defence Studies Centre during the visit.
