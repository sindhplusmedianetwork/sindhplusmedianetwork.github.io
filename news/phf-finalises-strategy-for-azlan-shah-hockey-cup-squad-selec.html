PHF Finalises Strategy for Azlan Shah Hockey Cup Squad Selection

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Hockey Federation has finalised its strategy for preparations and the selection of the national squad for the Sultan Azlan Shah Hockey Cup. The tournament will be played from November 20 to 28 in Ipoh, Malaysia. Invited players from the under-18, junior and senior levels will report at Islamabad's Naseer Bunda Hockey Stadium on October 30.

Three pools will be formed in the training camp, and the best-performing players will be selected for the final squad. The PHF said the aim is to give young players an equal opportunity to prove themselves at the senior level. The federation expressed hope that the selected squad would perform well at the prestigious tournament.
