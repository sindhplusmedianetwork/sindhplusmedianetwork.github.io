Skeleton Found in Mahodand Lake May Be Missing US Student Bushra Samui, DNA Test Ordered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A human skeleton recovered from the famous Mahodand Lake in Kalam, Swat, may belong to Bushra Samui, the American student who went missing in the Saif-ul-Malook lake accident in July, officials said on Wednesday. Police and the local administration shifted the remains to Kalam Hospital for legal formalities and a post-mortem examination. Because the remains are badly decomposed, facial identification is no longer possible, so authorities have ordered DNA tests and other legal and medical procedures for final confirmation.

In July, the bodies of six members of one family who drowned in Saif-ul-Malook lake were recovered, while Bushra Samui remained missing and the search for her continued. The family members who died in the tragedy included retired Colonel Aamir Aundal and his children. Family sources said Bushra Samui had returned to Pakistan just five days earlier after completing her higher-education degree in the United States and had come to Swat with her family for recreation.
