NBP law revamp keeps finance ministry's veto over bank chief's appointment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has proposed major amendments to the law governing the National Bank of Pakistan as part of an IMF condition to improve the bank's governance. The draft NBP Amendment Act, 2026 was approved by the Cabinet Committee on Legislative Cases, and its passage by Parliament is a condition of the IMF programme. However, the proposal retains the finance ministry's veto over the appointment of the bank's president.

Under the draft, the NBP president is appointed by the federal government from three candidates recommended by a three-fourths majority of the central board, a majority that must include the vote of the ex-officio Finance Division director.
