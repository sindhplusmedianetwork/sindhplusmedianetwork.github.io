Netanyahu Seeks Seventh Term as Israel's PM, Faces Former Army Chief as Rival

Benjamin Netanyahu, Israel's longest-serving prime minister, wants to become prime minister for a seventh time. He will contest the parliamentary elections scheduled for October 27. Netanyahu has dominated Israeli politics for more than a decade and a half.

However, a former army chief is being seen as a possible obstacle in his path this time. Political analysts say the election is shaping up to be one of the most closely watched in the country's recent history. Netanyahu's supporters credit him with experience and strong leadership.

His critics argue that the country needs a change at the top.
