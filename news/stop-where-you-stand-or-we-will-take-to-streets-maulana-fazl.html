Stop Where You Stand or We Will Take to Streets, Maulana Fazlur Rehman Warns Government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman has warned the government to stop where it stands, otherwise the JUI will take to the streets and, if needed, will also head to jails. Addressing workers in Peshawar on the occasion of the Friday prayers gathering, he said the Mufti Mahmood Centre is not an encroachment, the establishment commits excesses and interferes in politics, asking what its job is in Pakistan's politics and telling it to stay within its domain. He accused them of stealing the mineral resources of Pakhtunkhwa and said they know not only the thief but also its centre.

Maulana Fazlur Rehman said they are not hot-tempered people and the dream of the forces that started retaliatory actions against the JUI will not be fulfilled. He said they have no weapons and no sticks and are carrying out a political struggle, adding that even with weapons and government power on the other side, they will be defeated with bare hands. He said this government has been in place for two and a half years and kept pleading with them to join the government, saying the government could not run without them.

The JUI chief said they will protect their rights and will also protect the rights of Sindh, Balochistan and Punjab, and will stand like a lead-plated wall.
