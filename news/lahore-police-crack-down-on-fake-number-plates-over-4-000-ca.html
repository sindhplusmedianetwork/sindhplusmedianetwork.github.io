Lahore Police Crack Down on Fake Number Plates, Over 4,000 Cases Registered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore police's crackdown on fake number plates and elements concealing their identity is continuing in full swing, with more than 4,000 cases registered so far. DIG Operations Lahore Faisal Kamran shared the details during an online open court held through Facebook Live, where he interacted directly with citizens. He said criminals using fake number plates hid their identity to commit offences and flee, and the operation aims to discourage such elements and prevent crime.

Modern technology and effective monitoring are helping identify and arrest suspects, he added, and the basic aim is to close the routes through which criminals evade the law. Responding to criticism of the campaign in some quarters, he remarked that a crime is a crime whether big or small, action will be indiscriminate, and law-abiding citizens appreciate these steps. During the session, residents of Shadbagh complained about brawls and drug peddling, while the sale of ice and other narcotics in Kahna was also flagged.

The DIG directed the officers concerned to investigate the complaints and act immediately, describing the ice trade as a grave threat to the future of the young generation.
