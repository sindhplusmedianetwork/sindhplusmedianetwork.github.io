Japan stun Brazil 3-2 with late winner in Singapore friendly

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Substitute Tatsuhiro Sakamoto scored his first international goal with a late winner as Japan edged Brazil 3-2 in a thrilling friendly at the National Stadium in Singapore on Saturday. Takefusa Kubo and Keito Nakamura had put Japan 2-0 up inside 19 minutes before a Vinicius Junior brace levelled the match. Sakamoto fired home in stoppage time after Kodai Sano's deflected effort fell to him.

Brazil's Raphinha was shown a red card for protesting after VAR upheld the goal. The win gives Japan a famous result against the five-time world champions, while Brazil were left to rue a bright start that slipped away in the closing minutes.
