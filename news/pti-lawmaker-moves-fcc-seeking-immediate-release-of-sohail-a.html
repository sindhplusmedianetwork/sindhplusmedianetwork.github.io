PTI lawmaker moves FCC seeking immediate release of Sohail Afridi, other party workers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI lawmaker Shandana Gulzar on Thursday approached the Federal Constitutional Court seeking the immediate release of Sohail Afridi, the non-functional Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister, the provincial assembly speaker and other party workers. Afridi has not been seen since the Centre imposed governor's rule and emergency in KP on October 11, and a federal minister confirmed the next day that he and some cabinet members were in preventive custody. The petition asked the court to direct the authorities to disclose the whereabouts of the detained leaders, the identity of the authority holding them, and the legal provisions under which they were detained.

It also sought a declaration that Articles 232, 234 and 245 of the Constitution do not authorise arresting or detaining any person in disregard of constitutional safeguards. Gulzar further asked the court to order access to legal representation and family members for the detained leaders, along with medical examination and protection against torture or degrading treatment. Meanwhile, PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Khan also approached the court on Thursday, seeking to be made a party in the case concerning the high court being restrained from hearing cases about the detained PTI workers.

On October 13, Afridi's brother had approached the Peshawar High Court to trace his whereabouts, and on November 3 the high court referred the plea to the chief justice for a larger bench.
