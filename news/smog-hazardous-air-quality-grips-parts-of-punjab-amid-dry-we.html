Smog, Hazardous Air Quality Grips Parts of Punjab Amid Dry Weather Forecast

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Smog continued to blanket several parts of Punjab on Saturday. Lahore's Wagha Border station recorded a hazardous Air Quality Index score of 500. Vehari topped the provincial city ranking at 369, followed by Multan at 354.

The Pakistan Meteorological Department forecast largely dry weather, with smog likely at isolated places. The forecast offers little hope of immediate relief. Lahore ranked fifth among the world's most polluted major cities on IQAir's live list on Saturday night, with a US AQI reading of 204.
