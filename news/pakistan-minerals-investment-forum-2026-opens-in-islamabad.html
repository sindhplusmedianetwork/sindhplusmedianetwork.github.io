Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 opens in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 opened on Thursday at the Jinnah Convention Centre in Islamabad, making the capital the focus of global attention. The government and the people of Pakistan welcomed global investors, minerals experts and international partners to Islamabad, which the organisers described as one of the world's most beautiful capitals. The forum will brief global investors on Pakistan's vast mineral reserves and the investment opportunities available in the country.

It will highlight the prospects for global partnerships in exploration, mining, processing and value addition in the minerals sector. The aim of the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 is to turn the country's mineral resources into economic growth and new investment opportunities.
