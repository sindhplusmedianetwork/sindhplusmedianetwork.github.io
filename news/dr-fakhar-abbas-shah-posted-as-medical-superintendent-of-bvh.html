Dr Fakhar Abbas Shah posted as medical superintendent of BVH Bahawalpur

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Health Department on Saturday transferred senior medical officer Dr Syed Fakhar Abbas Shah and posted him as Medical Superintendent (MS) of Bahawal Victoria Hospital (BVH) in Bahawalpur with immediate effect. According to an official notification issued by the Health Department, Dr Syed Fakhar Abbas Shah, APMO (BS-19), was serving as Medical Superintendent at Care Hospital, Nishtar, in Multan before the transfer. He has now been transferred and posted as Medical Superintendent of Bahawal Victoria Hospital, Bahawalpur.

The notification stated that the posting would take effect immediately. The Health Department confirmed the posting through the official notification.
