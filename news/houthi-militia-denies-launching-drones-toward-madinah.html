Houthi Militia Denies Launching Drones Toward Madinah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Houthi militia has denied launching drones toward Madinah Munawwarah. In a statement, the group's spokesman said that no military operation had been carried out against Saudi Arabia today. The denial follows reports that drones headed toward Madinah and the kingdom's eastern regions had been destroyed in the air by the coalition's spokesman.

The exchange of claims and counterclaims comes as tensions between the Houthis and Saudi Arabia have intensified sharply, with the kingdom coming under repeated drone and missile attacks in recent weeks.
