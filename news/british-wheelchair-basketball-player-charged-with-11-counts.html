British Wheelchair Basketball Player Charged With 11 Counts of Rape

Lee Fryer, who won a silver medal with Britain's wheelchair basketball team at the Paris Paralympics 2024, has been charged with a total of 17 offences. The charges include 11 counts of rape. The 26-year-old from Pontefract, West Yorkshire, appeared at Leeds Crown Court on Thursday and pleaded not guilty to all charges.

The other charges include voyeurism, intentional strangulation, assault causing bodily harm and possession of Class A drugs, relating to the period from May 2021 to May 2026. The court set a seven-day trial for next year and remanded him in custody. British Wheelchair Basketball had suspended him in July pending investigation.
