Film Workers' Body Writes to PM Modi Over Daler Mehndi's Public Humiliation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federation of Western India Cine Employees (FWICE) has written to Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Uttarakhand Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami, seeking intervention over the public humiliation of singer Daler Mehndi by Uttarakhand Cabinet Minister Subodh Uniyal at a cultural event. The controversy erupted when Uniyal walked onto the stage during Mehndi's performance at the Siddhpeeth Maa Kunjapuri Tourism and Development Fair in New Tehri and questioned the singer's condition, alleging he appeared disoriented, was lip-syncing, and was under the influence of alcohol. The minister was heard saying, 'You can't even speak; how will you sing?' and claimed Mehndi had been paid Rs 35 lakh for the performance.

Mehndi's younger brother, singer Mika Singh, denied the alcohol allegation, said his brother was recovering from major surgery, and demanded an apology from the minister. Veteran actor Anupam Kher, comedian Kapil Sharma, singers Badshah and Shaan, and actor Rajpal Yadav also voiced support for Mehndi, with Kher asking whether the humiliation was necessary. FWICE's letter described Mehndi as a celebrated name in Indian music and said the incident had deeply distressed the entertainment fraternity, urging authorities to advise public representatives to treat artists with greater sensitivity.
