Massive Fire Engulfs Garment Factory Warehouse in Karachi's SITE Area

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A massive fire broke out at a garments factory warehouse near Metroville in Karachi's SITE industrial area on Monday, prompting a large emergency response. Chief Fire Officer Humayun Khan said the blaze was declared a third-degree fire so that all relevant departments would mobilise, with 11 fire tenders, two water bowsers and a snorkel deployed. He said the warehouse held cardboard, wood, thread, garments, foam, plastic and other combustible materials on its upper floors, which made firefighting extremely difficult.

The fire engulfed the factory's second and third floors, and parts of the building along with its boundary wall became structurally unsafe. Fire officials said they were struggling with a shortage of water as they battled the flames from inside and outside the building. Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah took notice of the incident and ordered the immediate deployment of additional fire brigade vehicles and Rescue 1122 units.

He also sought a detailed report from the Karachi commissioner and the industries department on the cause of the fire, and ordered a review of fire safety arrangements in industrial areas. Officials said the same premises had witnessed another fire only a few days earlier, while the cause of the latest blaze and any casualties were not yet known.
