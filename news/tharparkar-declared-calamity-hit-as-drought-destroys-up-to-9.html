Tharparkar declared calamity-hit as drought destroys up to 90% of crops

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has decided to declare Tharparkar a calamity-hit district in view of the severe drought. Officials briefed Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah that drought-like conditions prevail in all seven talukas of the district. They said 85 to 90 percent of crops have been destroyed or rendered unproductive.

Only 1 to 36 millimetres of rain was recorded across the district in September 2026, causing crops and natural grass to dry up before maturing. The chief minister directed that drinking water supply be ensured for the affected population. He also ordered that inactive RO plants be repaired and restored.

Relief measures, livestock protection and long-term water planning will also be pursued in the district.
