Sri Lanka Stun Pakistan by 8 Wickets in Final T20I; Hosts Take Series 2-1

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sri Lanka avoided a series whitewash with a commanding eight-wicket victory over Pakistan in the third and final T20I at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. Chasing a 169-run target, the visitors reached 172 for two in just 17.1 overs, led by captain Kusal Mendis's unbeaten 87 off 48 balls. Mendis struck nine fours and four sixes and was named Player of the Match, while Kamindu Mendis contributed an unbeaten 40 and Sineth Jayawardena made 38.

Earlier, Pakistan posted 168 for eight after being asked to bat, with captain Sahibzada Farhan top-scoring with 76 off 47 balls and Arafat Minhas adding an unbeaten 34. Dasun Shanaka was the pick of Sri Lanka's bowlers with three wickets for 21 runs. Despite the defeat, Pakistan clinched the three-match series 2-1 after winning the first two games at the same venue.

Farhan was named Player of the Series after scoring three consecutive half-centuries.
