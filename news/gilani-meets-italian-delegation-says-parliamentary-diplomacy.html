Gilani Meets Italian Delegation, Says Parliamentary Diplomacy Can Deepen Pakistan-Italy Ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senate Chairman Yousuf Raza Gilani met a high-level Italian delegation at Parliament House in Islamabad on Tuesday. The delegation included Riccardo Guariglia, Secretary General of Italy's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, Italy's Ambassador to Pakistan, Nicoletta Bombardiere, Director General at Italy's Foreign Ministry, and Roberto Nechia, Deputy Head of Mission at the Italian Embassy in Pakistan. Gilani said parliamentary diplomacy can further strengthen relations between Pakistan and Italy, adding that parliaments are lasting bridges of friendship, trust and cooperation between peoples.

The two sides discussed their longstanding friendly ties, parliamentary diplomacy, the regional and global situation, and cooperation in trade, investment and education. Gilani stressed exchanges of parliamentary delegations and more active parliamentary friendship groups, noting that two successful Inter-Parliamentary Speakers' Conferences have been held in Pakistan and Cambodia, with a third to be held in Indonesia. He also highlighted Pakistan's diplomatic efforts to reduce global tensions and facilitate talks between the United States and Iran.

The delegation expressed interest in expanding cooperation in education, health and other sectors, while Gilani praised Italy's support through the Pakistan-Italy Textile Centre in Faisalabad and the Footwear Centre in Lahore, and its help in preserving Gandhara heritage.
