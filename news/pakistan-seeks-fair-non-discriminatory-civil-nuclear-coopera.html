Pakistan seeks fair, non-discriminatory civil nuclear cooperation at UN

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has underscored the fundamental importance of equitable, non-discriminatory civil nuclear cooperation to help secure its national energy future at the United Nations. Counsellor at Pakistan's Permanent Mission to the UN, Syed Atif Raza, made the remarks during the UN General Assembly plenary discussion on the report of the International Atomic Energy Agency. He said efforts to prevent the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction must not impede international cooperation in the peaceful uses of dual-use technologies, which are vital for the economic development of the Global South.

Raza said all countries must retain their inalienable right to utilise nuclear energy for peaceful purposes, consistent with their non-proliferation and international legal obligations. He said Pakistan maintains that compliance or safety issues should be resolved through technical bodies like the IAEA without political bias. Pakistan operates six nuclear power reactors, all under IAEA safeguards, supplying about 20 per cent of the national energy needs and one-third of low-carbon power generation, he said.
