15 Passengers Fall Ill on Two Flights from Egypt

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fifteen passengers on two flights from Egypt fell seriously ill in the same way and were taken from airports to hospitals, Jang reported. On November 6, at least seven passengers on easyJet flight U23320 from Hurghada to Edinburgh suddenly developed high fever, flu and vomiting. The pilots declared an emergency to summon medical staff at the gate.

On October 22, eight passengers on easyJet flight U23116 from Sharm el-Sheikh to Glasgow suffered severe nausea and vomiting during the flight. EasyJet and medical authorities said the first incident was caused by food eaten at a hotel before boarding. Whether the two incidents are linked is being investigated.
