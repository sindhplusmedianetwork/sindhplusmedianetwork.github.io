EU Sets Plan to Lift 15 Million People Out of Poverty Risk by 2030

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The European Union aims to reduce the number of people at risk of poverty and social exclusion by 15 million by 2030, as part of its first anti-poverty strategy adopted earlier this year. Roxana Minzatu, the European Commission's executive vice-president for social rights and skills, said on the International Day for the Eradication of Poverty that many people across Europe still cannot afford housing, heating, food, health care or education. One in five people in Europe faces the risk of poverty and social exclusion, including 19.3 million children, while more than 1.4 million people are homeless or lack safe housing.

The 2026 theme of the day is a safe home for all, highlighting the importance of adequate housing for safety, dignity and participation in society. The strategy seeks to speed up the action plan for the European Pillar of Social Rights and help end poverty by 2050. The European Council is expected soon to endorse the Commission's proposal on housing exclusion, which would be the EU's first legal measure on homelessness and a major step toward helping those facing the most severe forms of poverty.
