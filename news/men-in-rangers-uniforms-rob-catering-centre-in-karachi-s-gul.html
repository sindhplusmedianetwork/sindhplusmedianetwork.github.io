Men in Rangers Uniforms Rob Catering Centre in Karachi's Gulshan-e-Iqbal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Robbers wearing Rangers uniforms have begun carrying out robberies in Karachi, Jang reported. In Gulshan-e-Iqbal 13-D, a suspect dressed in a Rangers uniform robbed a catering centre at gunpoint. He took mobile phones and cash from the premises before fleeing.

SSP East Dr Farrukh Raza said the incident took place within the limits of Gulshan-e-Iqbal police station. He said the affected citizen has been contacted and legal proceedings are under way. SP Gulshan Aleem Baloch is supervising the investigation into all aspects of the case.
