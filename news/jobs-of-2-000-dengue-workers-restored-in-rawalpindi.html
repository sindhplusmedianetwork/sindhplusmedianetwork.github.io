Jobs of 2,000 Dengue Workers Restored in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The jobs of nearly 2,000 male and female dengue workers in Rawalpindi district, who were dismissed last week following the expiry of their contracts, have been restored on the orders of the director general of health, Punjab, Lahore. The contracts of all the workers have been extended for another 90 days. On Friday, the Rawalpindi health department telephoned the dismissed workers to inform them about the restoration and asked them to rejoin duty on Saturday, October 31.

The extension has brought relief among the female dengue workers, who had been dismissed after their previous contracts expired, though the health department has refused to regularise their services. Meanwhile, dengue infections continue to spread in the twin cities of Rawalpindi and Islamabad, taking the combined number of patients to 134, with two reportedly in critical condition. The district's 1,400 anti-dengue teams have so far inspected over 7.9 million houses and premises, detecting dengue larvae at 241,942 locations, registering 2,798 FIRs and sealing 690 properties.

Health officials said the peak dengue season would persist until November 15, after which severe cold was expected to end the threat.
