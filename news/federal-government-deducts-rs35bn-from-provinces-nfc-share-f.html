Federal government deducts Rs35bn from provinces' NFC share for first time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

For the first time in Pakistan's history, the federal government has deducted Rs35 billion from the four provinces' NFC share as a grant under Article 164 of the 1973 Constitution. Finance Ministry officials confirmed on Tuesday that Rs34.839 billion was collected from the provinces in the first two months of the current fiscal year and shown as non-tax revenue. The provinces had agreed before the 2026-27 budget to provide Rs1,035 billion in grants during the fiscal year.

The grants are meant to help the federal government meet its financial obligations under the IMF's $7 billion programme.
