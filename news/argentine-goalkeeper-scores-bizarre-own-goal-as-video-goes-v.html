Argentine Goalkeeper Scores Bizarre Own Goal as Video Goes Viral

Argentine goalkeeper Ezequiel Bacher scored a bizarre own goal during a Primera B playoff match in Argentina. The match was between Comunicaciones and Arsenal. The 30-year-old goalkeeper caught a weak cross easily and tried to throw the ball out to a teammate.

He failed to release the ball in time, and it went straight into his own net instead. The unusual mistake stunned the players and fans in the stadium. Video of the incident is now drawing attention on social media, where viewers have been sharing the clip widely.
