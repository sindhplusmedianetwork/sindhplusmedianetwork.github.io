Man allegedly wanted by foreign agencies arrested in Karachi on FBI complaint: report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man has been arrested by the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency in Karachi's Mahmoodabad area, according to a report. ARY News claimed in its report that the arrested man, Danish alias Tito, was wanted by foreign investigative agencies and that the action was taken on a complaint by the US Federal Bureau of Investigation. The report said the available information did not make clear which countries or agencies had formally sought him or what his legal status was.

It added that the text of the FBI complaint, the nature of the alleged offence and any court appearance or remand details had not come to light, so no specific crime could be said to have been proven against him. No complete official statement from the NCCIA or direct confirmation from the FBI was available, and the arrested man's own position had not come forward either.
