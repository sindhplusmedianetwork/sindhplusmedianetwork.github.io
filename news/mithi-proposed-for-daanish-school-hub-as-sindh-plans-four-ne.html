Mithi Proposed for Daanish School Hub as Sindh Plans Four New Sites

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has proposed establishing a Daanish School hub in Mithi to expand educational opportunities for children in Tharparkar and Umerkot, where more than half of school-age children remain out of school, under a wider Sindh expansion plan envisaging four new hubs at an estimated cost of Rs12.9 billion. According to documents available with Wealth Pakistan, Mithi has been identified as a priority location under Sindh's proposed five-belt Daanish Schools expansion framework, aimed at addressing persistent educational inequalities in some of the province's most underserved districts. Tharparkar has a literacy rate of just 36.4 per cent compared with 38.7 per cent in neighbouring Umerkot, while female literacy stands at 23.5 per cent in Tharparkar and 25.1 per cent in Umerkot, and 56 per cent of children aged five to 16 are out of school in Tharparkar and 52 per cent in Umerkot.

The other proposed locations are Kandhkot in the Upper Indus region, Khairpur in the Larkana-Sukkur belt and Sanghar in central Sindh, with Mithi and Kandhkot designated priority locations for the initial phase and Khairpur and Sanghar proposed for subsequent phases. The documents estimate the combined capital expenditure for the four Sindh hubs at approximately Rs12.9 billion, based on an indicative cost of Rs3.22 billion for each standard Daanish School, with the entire cost envisaged through the federal Public Sector Development Programme. The projects remain at the proposal stage, with their inclusion in the development programme and funding arrangements subject to government approval, while five additional proposed hubs in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa take the total number of potential new hubs across the three provinces to nine at a combined estimated cost of approximately Rs29 billion, separate from the 28 Daanish Schools already included in the existing project brief.
