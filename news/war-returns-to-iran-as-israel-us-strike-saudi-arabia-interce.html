War Returns to Iran as Israel, US Strike; Saudi Arabia Intercepts Missiles Over Riyadh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The conflict between Iran and Israel has flared again with reported American strikes, according to Dawn's live coverage on Thursday. Saudi Arabia said its air defences intercepted two Houthi ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh and switched on early-warning systems in Riyadh, Al Kharj and Najran. Correspondents in the Saudi capital reported hearing at least eight explosions.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards separately claimed their forces shot down a modern American MQ-1 drone over the Strait of Hormuz with a new air-defence system. They said a US MQM-170 spy drone was seized through electronic warfare and landed undamaged in the strait's western sector, adding that Tehran believes the drones were sent from a southern Persian Gulf country to probe coastal defences. Iran also reported a fourth tanker damaged by naval mines within 24 hours, while other updates spoke of a tanker hit by an unknown projectile and a dhow sunk near Oman's Khasab.

Dockworkers in 22 countries are meanwhile planning to strike against arms shipments.
