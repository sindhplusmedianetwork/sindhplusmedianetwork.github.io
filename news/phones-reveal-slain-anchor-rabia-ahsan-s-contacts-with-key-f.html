Phones Reveal Slain Anchor Rabia Ahsan's Contacts With Key Figures

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An initial analysis of three mobile phones belonging to slain TV anchorperson Rabia Ahsan has revealed her professional contacts and communications with several prominent personalities, including a federal minister, as part of the ongoing murder investigation. Police sources said more than 1,500 photographs and videos were recovered from the phones, most related to her journalistic career and modelling. Police sources said the preliminary review found no evidence of any dispute in her conversations.

The phones are being examined as part of the continuing investigation into her murder, and investigators are studying the recovered material for further leads. Police said the recovered photographs and videos were mostly connected to her work as a journalist and her modelling assignments.
