Multan Tops Pakistan's Most Polluted Cities as Smog Worsens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Multan has been ranked the most polluted city in Pakistan as smog tightened its grip across Punjab. According to a global environmental monitoring website, particulate matter levels in Multan soared to 490 on Tuesday. Lahore, meanwhile, featured among the most polluted major cities in the world with a reading of 447.

Faisalabad recorded 461 and Rahim Yar Khan 457 as the haze blanketed the region. The thick smog forced authorities to close the M-3 Motorway from Faizpur to Jaranwala for traffic safety. Health experts have urged citizens to limit outdoor activity as air quality deteriorates with the onset of winter.
