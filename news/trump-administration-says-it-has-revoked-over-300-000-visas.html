Trump Administration Says It Has Revoked Over 300,000 Visas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The US State Department said on Tuesday that President Donald Trump's administration had revoked more than 300,000 visas during his second term, marking an acceleration from the 175,000 reported in August. The revocations have come amid a sweeping immigration crackdown and tightened social media screening of visa applicants. State Department spokesman Tommy Pigott said the administration "has revoked more visas at a faster pace than any administration in history" and maintained zero tolerance for visa holders who break the law.

Rights advocates condemned the move, arguing that revocations have targeted critics such as pro-Palestinian protesters and were being used to broadly target immigrant communities. The announcement underscores the administration's hardline approach to immigration enforcement.
