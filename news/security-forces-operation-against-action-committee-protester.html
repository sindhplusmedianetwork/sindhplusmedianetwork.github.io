Security forces' operation against Action Committee protesters in Rawalakot, exchange of fire and claims of casualties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Law enforcement agencies and workers of the recently banned Jammu Kashmir Awami Action Committee clashed in an alleged armed encounter in Rawalakot early on Thursday. The district administration has banned gatherings of two or more people in the city and advised citizens to remain cautious. Police in Poonch Division confirmed that some law enforcement personnel were injured in the clashes at the Rawalakot bus terminal, with details to be shared soon.

The Action Committee claimed three people were killed and said its workers were protesting at the city's D-Chowk with the bodies. Officials have not confirmed any deaths, though a police statement said reports indicated some armed protesters were injured in retaliatory action. The operation to clear protesters from the bus terminal began at about 5am on Thursday and lasted about two and a half hours, after which the terminal area came under the control of law enforcement agencies.

The committee's workers have been staging a sit-in in the area for the past five months over their demands, and the administration says traffic from Rawalpindi to Rawalakot has been disrupted as a result.
