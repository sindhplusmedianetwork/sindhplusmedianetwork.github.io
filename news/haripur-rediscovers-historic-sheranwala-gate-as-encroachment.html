Haripur rediscovers historic Sheranwala Gate as encroachments cleared

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A long-forgotten chapter of Haripur's history has resurfaced during an ongoing anti-encroachment operation, bringing the historic Sheranwala Gate back into public view. The stone lions that gave the landmark its name have emerged from behind shops, encroachments and years of neglect, revealing the original architectural features of the historic structure. For many residents, the sight has come as a surprise, with some saying they are seeing the stone lions for the first time in their lives, while others recall hearing stories about them from their elders.

The structure, believed to date back to before 1860, is an old temple building whose two stone lions positioned above its arch are believed to have inspired the name Sheranwala Gate, which has been associated with the locality for generations. The anti-encroachment drive is being carried out by the district administration across Haripur as part of a province-wide campaign, with government land being reclaimed in accordance with authenticated land records dating back to 1903. Owners of shops surrounding the structure are voluntarily demolishing the front portions of their buildings, though a closer examination suggests a few feet of the historic temple structure itself may also fall within the affected area.

That has raised questions about whether the front portion of the historic building will also be brought in line with the adjoining shops or allowed to retain its existing architectural projection.
