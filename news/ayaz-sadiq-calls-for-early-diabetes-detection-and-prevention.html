Ayaz Sadiq calls for early diabetes detection and prevention

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Speaker of the National Assembly Sardar Ayaz Sadiq said on Friday that diabetes has emerged as a serious public health challenge, affecting not only individuals but also their families, communities and society at large. In his message on the occasion of World Diabetes Day, he said that the global theme for World Diabetes Day 2026, 'Early Action on Diabetes', underscores the importance of timely diagnosis, access to quality healthcare and preventive measures. He stressed that greater public awareness, early detection and affordable and accessible treatment are essential to addressing the growing burden of diabetes and preventing serious complications.

Ayaz Sadiq said that unhealthy diets, physical inactivity and other unhealthy lifestyle factors can increase the risk of developing type 2 diabetes, and urged citizens to adopt balanced diets, engage in regular physical activity and make healthy lifestyle choices part of their daily routines. The Speaker also encouraged people to undergo regular health check-ups and seek medical advice without delay if they experience possible symptoms or have risk factors associated with diabetes. He emphasised that early diagnosis and timely, appropriate medical care can help prevent or delay complications and enable people living with diabetes to lead healthier and more fulfilling lives.

He said diabetes management requires sustained access to medical care, essential medicines, professional guidance and support for adopting healthy lifestyle practices. Ayaz Sadiq called upon healthcare institutions and relevant public-sector organisations to strengthen diabetes prevention, screening, diagnosis and treatment services, particularly at the primary healthcare level, and said public awareness campaigns should be expanded through healthcare facilities, educational institutions, communities and the media.
