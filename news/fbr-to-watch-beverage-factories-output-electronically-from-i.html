FBR to watch beverage factories' output electronically from its offices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) has approved seven more vendors for the electronic monitoring of production at beverage factories, in a move aimed at curbing tax evasion. FBR Chairman Rashid Mahmood Langrial issued Sales Tax General Order No 37/2026, approving the seven additional vendors for the supply, installation and maintenance of the electronic monitoring system. The order has been issued as an addendum to Sales Tax General Orders No 3, 4, 6 and 7 of 2026.

The approved vendors are the National Radio and Telecommunication Corporation (NRTC), Obsidian Technologies, Excellence Delivered (EXD) Private Limited, ISSM Labelling Solutions Private Limited, Toolnk Pakistan Private Limited, AJCL Private Limited and Authentic. Under the system, machines and electronic devices installed in factories will automatically record production, and the devices will be linked to the FBR's computer system so that tax officers can monitor production figures from their offices. The system will apply to factories producing bottled water, packaged juices, packaged milk, carbonated drinks and other related products.

The FBR has also issued a list of 54 beverage manufacturers, including Coca-Cola, Pepsi, Nestlé, Haleeb, Engro Foods, Shezan and Qarshi, but has clarified that the list is not final and that all registered manufacturers of these products will be bound by the electronic monitoring system. The FBR will be able to compare the recorded production figures with the production, sales, tax returns and tax paid as declared by companies, and investigate any difference, in order to identify hidden production and possible tax evasion.
