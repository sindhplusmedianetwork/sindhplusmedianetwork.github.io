Team Win Made Series Success Sweeter, Says Muhammad Abbas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National fast bowler Muhammad Abbas said he enjoyed it more because the Pakistan team also won along with his performance. He praised Saqib Khan and Razaullah's bowling in the series against Sri Lanka. Advising young cricketers, Abbas said they should play more four-day cricket.

He said players can also improve their white-ball skills through red-ball cricket. Abbas is the most successful fast bowler in Test cricket in 2026 with 39 wickets. He took 8 wickets in the Test series against Sri Lanka to win the Player of the Series award and was declared Player of the Match in the second Test.
