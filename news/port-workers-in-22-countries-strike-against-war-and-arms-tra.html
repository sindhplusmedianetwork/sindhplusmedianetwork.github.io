Port Workers in 22 Countries Strike Against War and Arms Transfers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dockworkers and ship crews at 113 ports across 22 countries have gone on strike in protest against war and the transfer of weapons. The workers are protesting against arms shipments and the killing of maritime workers in the wars in the Middle East and the Black Sea. The coordinated strike has affected operations at several ports and shipping companies.

The protest is a rare, large-scale show of solidarity by port and maritime workers against the movement of weapons through commercial shipping routes. Details of how long the stoppages will last and which specific ports are hardest hit continue to emerge.
