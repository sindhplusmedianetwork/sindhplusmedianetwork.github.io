Chinese Firm to Build 100km K-IV Pipeline as Sukkur Barrage Gates Near Completion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah, during his official visit to China, met representatives of HBSZ Company in Wuhan's Hanyang district to discuss the proposed 100-kilometre K-IV pipeline for Karachi. The company expressed its willingness to undertake construction of the pipeline, and the chief minister directed officials to initiate work at the earliest. He also reviewed the Sukkur Barrage Improvement Project, being executed by a joint venture of the China Road and Bridge Corporation and HBSZ.

Of the barrage's 54 river gates, 44 have been replaced along with their operating systems and made functional, while the remaining 10 gates are ready for shipment from China, each weighing over 50 tonnes. HBSZ is additionally fabricating 110 canal gates for the project. Calling the Sukkur Barrage the economic lifeline of Sindh, the chief minister said its rehabilitation after 90 years had significantly improved water flows.

Launched in February 2023, the project is scheduled for completion by June 30, 2027, with financing from the World Bank and the federal and provincial governments, while rehabilitation of the Guddu Barrage is also under way. During the visit, the chief minister also explored cooperation with Huawei in smart governance and artificial intelligence, invited Humanwell Healthcare Group to set up pharmaceutical manufacturing in Sindh, and discussed climate-resilient agriculture with Qingfa Hesheng.
