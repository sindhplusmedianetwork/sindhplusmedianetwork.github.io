England ODI Team Arrives in Islamabad for Tri-Nation Series

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

England's ODI cricket team has arrived in Islamabad for the tri-nation series, where PCB officials welcomed the players. The team will practice at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on the following day. The ODI tri-series between Pakistan, England and Sri Lanka will be played from October 18 to 31.

The opening match on October 18 will be Pakistan versus Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi, while Pakistan and England face off on October 20 at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. The tri-series matches will be played in Rawalpindi and Lahore. The final will be played on October 31 at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore.
