Karachi Swelters at 38.7°C in Hottest October Day So Far

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi sweltered through its hottest October day so far on Tuesday as the mercury climbed to 38.7 degrees Celsius. Humidity stood at 43 percent while a weak sea breeze failed to bring relief to the port city. The temperature was about 2.2 degrees above normal for this time of year, according to the Meteorological Department.

A Met official attributed the heat to a high-pressure system over the region, noting that October acts as a second summer for Karachi. The official said the temperature could touch 40 degrees in the coming days. Elsewhere in Sindh, Mithi recorded 41 degrees while Shaheed Benazirabad and Chhor reached 40 degrees.

The forecast for Wednesday predicted continued hot conditions with maximum temperatures between 37 and 39 degrees Celsius.
