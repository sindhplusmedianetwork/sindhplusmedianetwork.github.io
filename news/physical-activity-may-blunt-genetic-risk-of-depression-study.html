Physical activity may blunt genetic risk of depression, study finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Daily exercise and physical activity may help reduce the severity of symptoms associated with the genetic risk of depression, according to a new American study. The researchers analyzed survey responses, electronic medical records, genetic information, and physical activity data obtained from Fitbit devices. The results showed that in more active people, the link between genetic risk and depression severity was relatively weaker.

Experts say exercise can play a role in improving brain function, regulating bodily inflammation, and reducing the effects of mental stress. Study author Denver Brown said that understanding genetic risk can help in preventing depression and managing its symptoms through appropriate lifestyle changes. The researchers clarified that physical activity should not be considered a substitute for the complete treatment of depression, but rather a potential complementary strategy for dealing with its risks and symptoms.

The study, published in the journal Nature Mental Health, analyzed data from 23,517 individuals.
