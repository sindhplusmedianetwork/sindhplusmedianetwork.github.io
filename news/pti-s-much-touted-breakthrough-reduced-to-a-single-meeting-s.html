PTI's Much-Touted Breakthrough Reduced to a Single Meeting, Says Talal Chaudhry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister of State for Interior Senator Talal Chaudhry has said that what was presented as a major celebration and breakthrough for the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf has now come down to just one meeting. Speaking to host Shahzeb Khanzada on Geo News, he said there should be no expectation of a deal or relaxation over meetings with Imran Khan, which will take place only under the law and prison rules. He said there would be nothing out of the box, and that as long as the prison rules and the law were followed, meetings would continue under both the prison rules and court orders.

Chaudhry asked that if these lawyers had not been allowed to meet Imran Khan earlier, why had Imran Khan appointed them as his lawyers. Criticising the party's internal situation, he said there were major internal differences among them and they were not a political team, adding that many of Imran Khan's difficulties stemmed from his non-political decisions and the non-political people around him. He said the party was being reduced day by day, noting that it used to say it would change the system and did not accept the elections, the prime minister or the assemblies, and had now come down to meetings.

He added that the party's politics was now limited to meetings and a sympathy card, and dismissed claims that families were not being allowed to meet, that the founder was in solitary confinement, or that his health was not well and his life was at risk, saying none of this was actually happening. Discussing Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's statement and the matter concerning Chaudhry Salik Hussain, he said there was no link whatsoever between the corruption statements by the interior minister and Chaudhry Salik, and that time would prove this.
