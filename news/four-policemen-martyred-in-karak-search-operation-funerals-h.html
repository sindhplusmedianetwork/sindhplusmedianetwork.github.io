Four Policemen Martyred in Karak Search Operation; Funerals Held With State Honours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four police personnel, including a Counter-Terrorism Department deputy superintendent, were martyred on Thursday during a search-and-strike operation in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa's Karak district. Police launched the operation within Latamber police station limits on a tip-off, and an exchange of fire broke out with unidentified armed men in the Khurram Muhammad Zai area. The martyred officers were identified as CTD DSP Imranuddin, Station House Officer Afzal Awan and constables Amjad and Kazim.

Reports suggested two suspected terrorists were also killed in the encounter, though further details were awaited. A heavy police contingent reached the scene as intense firing continued in the area. Funeral prayers for the martyrs were later held with full state honours, attended by senior civil and military officials, police officers and the bereaved families.

The provincial police chief paid tribute to their courage, saying they had confronted the terrorists valiantly and laid down their lives without backing away from their duties. Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi also condoled the deaths, saying the nation remained indebted to the martyrs for their unparalleled sacrifices.
