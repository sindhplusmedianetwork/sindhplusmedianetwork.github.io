Television host and actress Mathira is once again under fire on social media over her choice of dress. After her recent pictures surfaced, social media users began posting critical comments about her outfit. In the photos, Mathira is seen wearing a short mauve dress, paired with a cape and dupatta of the same colour.

Her head was covered in the look, but her legs were prominently visible. Mathira, who has worked in the showbiz industry for several years, has often spoken openly about her past, personal struggles and cosmetic treatments, and she usually decides her clothing according to her own preference. This time too, her outfit choice has sparked a debate among users online.
