Trump says US does not 'need a deal with Iran'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said the United States does not need a deal with Iran, writing in a post on Truth Social that those days have long since passed. He said Vice President JD Vance should focus on corruption within the US rather than pursuing a peace agreement with Iran, adding that the US had Iran's money, hundreds of billions of dollars, and its strait, referring to the Strait of Hormuz. Separately, a vessel was hit by an unidentified projectile about 14 nautical miles northwest of the UAE state of Ras Al-Khaimah on Wednesday, causing a fire in its engine room, while all crew were evacuated.

Maritime security firm Ambrey said the vessel was struck at the stern while leaving the Strait of Hormuz, adding that no injuries or pollution had been reported. Meanwhile, the secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, Major General Mohsen Rezaei, warned that the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Aerospace Force was fully prepared to deliver new surprises on the battlefield in the event of another attack. He said Iran's missile industry was one of the country's most important deterrence tools and a firm pillar safeguarding its independence.

Reports also said Vance was considering face-to-face talks with a senior Iranian security official, after Trump signalled he was open to the vice president leading renewed negotiations with Tehran.
