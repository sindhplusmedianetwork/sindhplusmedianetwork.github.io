15-Year-Old Allegedly Run Over by Employer After Workplace Dispute in Multan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 15-year-old boy, Waseem Abbas, was allegedly run over and killed by his employer Tauqeer Nazir in Multan after trying to leave his workplace, according to an FIR registered in the case. The orphanage that had sent the boy to learn electrical work six days earlier said he wanted to quit over alleged abuse. The FIR, registered under PPC Sections 302 and 34, names Tauqeer Nazir and Amjad Ali, and alleges they pursued the boy in a Honda car (registration BVE-474) and deliberately ran him over.

Tauqeer was arrested, and a court granted him four days' physical remand on Tuesday. A video screengrab from the scene shows the car allegedly chasing the boy before the incident, and police said the investigation is continuing.
