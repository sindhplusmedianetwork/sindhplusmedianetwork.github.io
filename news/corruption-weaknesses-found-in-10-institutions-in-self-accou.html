Corruption Weaknesses Found in 10 Institutions in Self-Accountability Survey, Says Law Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has identified weaknesses that could create opportunities for corruption in ten institutions through a comprehensive National Risk Assessment of Corruption Vulnerabilities survey, Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar said on Friday. The minister shared details of the survey in a televised video message. Tarar maintained that the report was not a charge sheet.

He said weaknesses within institutions were identified to improve them, to stop corruption and to make merit and transparency possible. The exercise was described as part of an effort to undertake self-accountability in government institutions.
