Rain, Thunderstorms Likely in Upper Punjab, Islamabad, KP and Kashmir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Meteorological Department has forecast partly cloudy weather with chances of rain accompanied by strong winds and thunderstorms at isolated places in upper Punjab, Islamabad, Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan on Saturday. Dry weather is likely in most other parts of the country. In Islamabad and adjoining areas, partly cloudy weather with chances of rain and thunderstorms is expected, with a 30 percent probability of precipitation.

In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, mainly dry weather is expected in most districts, though cold conditions are likely in hilly areas during morning and night hours. Partly cloudy weather with chances of rain and thunderstorms is likely in Parachinar, Kurram, Hangu, Kohat, Charsadda, Chitral, Swat, Peshawar, Kohistan and adjoining areas during evening and night. In Punjab, most districts will see dry weather, but isolated rain and thunderstorms are forecast for Murree, Galiyat, Rawalpindi, Attock, Chakwal, Sargodha, Lahore and nearby areas.

In Sindh, mainly dry and hot weather is expected, while most of Balochistan will stay dry except for Zhob and Musakhel, where rain is possible. During the past 24 hours, dry weather prevailed across most of the country, though light rain fell at isolated places in Punjab and the Potohar region. Bahawalnagar received three millimetres of rainfall, while Murree and Chakwal recorded one millimetre each.

The PMD forecast maximum temperatures of 36 degrees Celsius in Karachi, 31 in Lahore, 31 in Peshawar and 24 in Quetta.
