PTI hands future course decisions to parliamentary party; Tuesday meeting to decide talks or protest

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

All decisions about Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf's future course of action have been handed to the party's parliamentary party. PTI's parliamentary leader in the Senate, Ali Zafar, said in a statement on X that the parliamentary party's meeting on Tuesday will settle the future course of action. The meeting will decide whether there will be negotiations or a protest, and will also decide important matters including access to Imran Khan, the release of women, and treatment.

Earlier, PTI chairman Barrister Gohar responded to reports that the core committee and political committee had been sidelined from decision-making. Gohar denied that the report was correct and said no meeting had taken place about sidelining the committees. He said the core committee, political committee and parliamentary party are separate bodies, that all party decisions are made by the core and political committees, and that their proposals are placed before the parliamentary party.

He added that the parliamentary party decides parliamentary matters and that its proposals and recommendations are always given importance, while the final authority in PTI rests with its founder Imran Khan, who has delegated powers to the relevant committees.
