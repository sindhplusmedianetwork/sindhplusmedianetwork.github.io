Joint JI-Govt Committee to Mull Ways to Expand Fuel Relief Scheme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government and Jamaat-i-Islami on Tuesday decided to form a joint committee to recommend proposals for expanding the ongoing fuel relief scheme, while JI warned of marching to Islamabad next month if no relief package was announced by then. The decision was made during a high-level meeting between Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and JI chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman at the PM House, according to a press release issued by the PM Office. The meeting was offered by PM Shehbaz last month when urging JI to postpone its Islamabad march, which it had accepted, and JI has been demanding the abolition of the petroleum levy to reduce the burden of heightened fuel prices on the public.

Tuesday's meeting decided that a committee, comprising three members each from the government and JI, will present final recommendations on two measures: expansion of the scope of the ongoing relief scheme on petrol for the public, and preparation of feasible proposals for the budget for a phased reduction in the petroleum levy. Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal and the PM's political adviser Rana Sanaullah will represent the government in the joint committee. The premier informed the JI delegation that 12.7 million people have registered in the subsidy scheme so far, and the relief scheme for owners of motorcycles, rickshaws and vehicles of up to 800cc was rolled out across the country on September 17.

In his press conference in Islamabad, the JI chief affirmed that his party will not postpone its march any further, saying it will now take place in mid-November, and that it certainly will take place if relief is not announced, while also saying the party would continue nationwide mobilisation alongside negotiations.
