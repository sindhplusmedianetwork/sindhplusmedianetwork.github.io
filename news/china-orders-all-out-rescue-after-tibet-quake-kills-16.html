China Orders 'All-Out' Rescue After Tibet Quake Kills 16

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China has ordered an 'all-out' rescue effort in Tibet after a powerful earthquake killed at least 16 people. The 7.0-magnitude quake struck Baqing County in Nagqu at 10:33pm on Thursday, local time, at a shallow depth of about 10 kilometres, 190km east-northeast of Nagqu. At least 65 people were injured and more than 300 houses were flattened, according to state media.

President Xi Jinping called for maximum efforts in search and rescue operations. Relief supplies, including 1,000 prefabricated houses and 3,239 tents, had been delivered to the affected area by Friday morning. President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif conveyed their condolences and expressed solidarity with China.
