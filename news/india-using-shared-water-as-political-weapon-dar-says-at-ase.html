India using shared water as political weapon, Dar says at ASEAN presser

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar said India's decision to unilaterally keep the Indus Waters Treaty suspended is a serious violation of international law. He called it a dangerous attempt to make shared water resources a political weapon. The move threatens the water, food and energy security of Pakistan's 250 million people, he said.

Dar was addressing a joint press conference with ASEAN Secretary General Dr Kao Kim Hourn in Islamabad on 19 November 2026. He said Pakistan and ASEAN agreed to increase cooperation in trade, investment, education, technology, climate change, food security and regional security. He welcomed the visit, the first by an ASEAN Secretary General to Pakistan in over two decades, and said Pakistan has been ASEAN's oldest sectoral dialogue partner since 1993.
