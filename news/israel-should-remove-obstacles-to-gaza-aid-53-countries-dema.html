Israel Should Remove Obstacles to Gaza Aid, 53 Countries Demand

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fifty-three countries and international organisations have demanded that Israel facilitate the fast and unhindered delivery of humanitarian aid into Gaza and allow all available land crossings to be used for the purpose. The demand was made in a joint statement delivered by Britain's permanent ambassador to the United Nations, Sarah MacIntosh, after a Security Council meeting. The statement was issued by the countries and organisations that support the joint plan of action on access to health facilities and humanitarian aid in Gaza, led by Britain, Qatar and Jordan.

MacIntosh said that with winter approaching, large numbers of people in Gaza lack proper shelter and face dangerously low body temperatures, respiratory diseases, waterborne illnesses and other preventable medical problems. The statement sets out seven demands, including keeping the aid corridors through Egypt and Jordan fully and permanently active, allowing in essential goods such as shelter, fuel, water, sanitation and medical supplies, including items Israel describes as dual-use, and restoring medical evacuation for patients and the injured who cannot be treated in Gaza. It also demands protection for medical staff, hospitals and medical transport, protection for aid centres and key infrastructure, prompt and transparent criminal investigations into attacks on medical and aid workers, and permission for high-level international delegations and journalists to enter Gaza.

The comprehensive peace plan endorsed through Security Council Resolution 2803 requires the immediate delivery of full humanitarian aid to Gaza, at a level not below that set in the agreement of 19 January 2025, but this requirement has not yet been met. Separately, the World Health Organization reported that the surgery department of the Nasser Medical Complex in southern Gaza was shelled on Thursday morning, killing six people, including a health worker, patients and their attendants, and injuring at least 27, and said that since October 2023 there have been 1,056 attacks on health facilities in Gaza, in which more than 1,000 people have been killed and 1,725 injured.
