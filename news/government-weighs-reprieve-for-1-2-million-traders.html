Government weighs reprieve for 1.2 million traders

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has digitally identified nearly 1.2 million shopkeepers who own a single shop but did not opt for the new fixed income tax scheme, and has not yet moved against them amid a view not to take adverse action for another three months. Tax authorities presented two options to the finance minister: a three-month extension with a default surcharge of up to 85,000 rupees, or enforcement under the normal income tax law. Enforcement under the normal law would mean a minimum penalty of 175,000 rupees, exposure to tax audit and full disclosure of assets.

Only 222 new traders filed returns and paid a negligible sum by the October 31 deadline, despite the scheme offering 1 percent tax on total sales in return for complete exemption from audit. The low response has left the government weighing a softer approach for small shopkeepers who stayed outside the scheme. A final decision on the extension or enforcement is still awaited, with the three-month relief option under active consideration.
