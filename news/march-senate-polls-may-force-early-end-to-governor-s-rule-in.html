March Senate polls may force early end to governor's rule in K-P

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The March 2027 Senate elections may force an end to governor's rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa before its six-month term expires, because the suspended provincial assembly must elect 12 senators. The K-P Assembly was suspended on October 11, 2026, and its six-month period runs to April 10, 2027, overlapping the Senate elections in early March. If the assembly is not restored in time, the province's 12 senators cannot be elected.

Restoration of the assembly would also revive the offices of the chief minister and the cabinet. This would open the door to a possible change of the provincial government. The coming Senate polls have thus put the future of direct rule in the province to a decisive test.
