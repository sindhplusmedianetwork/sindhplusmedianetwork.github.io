Assemblies that passed resolutions to create provinces should go ahead, we have no objection: Nasir Hussain Shah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Local Government Minister Nasir Hussain Shah said in Karachi that the assemblies which have passed resolutions to create new provinces should go ahead with them, and Sindh has no objection. He said President Asif Zardari and Bilawal Bhutto have made it clear that the people's decision will decide Sindh's fate. He added that a referendum on new provinces is possible, but the constitutional procedure must be followed, which requires a bill passed by a provincial assembly with a two-thirds majority.

He said the Sindh Assembly and the people have already given a clear decision on the province, and that decision should be respected. He also dismissed speculation about the meeting between President Zardari and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, describing it as a personal meeting.
