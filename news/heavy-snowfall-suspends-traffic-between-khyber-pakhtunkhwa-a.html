Heavy snowfall suspends traffic between Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Traffic between Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan via Babusar Top was suspended on Wednesday after Kaghan Valley received heavy snowfall for the second time in a few days. District Police Officer Mohammad Waqas Khan said traffic had been restricted beyond Bassal to the upper parts of Kaghan Valley and neighbouring Diamer district, as fresh snowfall had made the Mansehra-Naran-Jalkhad Road slippery and dangerous. It began snowing on Tuesday night in high-altitude areas, including Saiful Muluk Lake, Lulusar Lake, Battakundi, Bassal and Babusar Top, and continued the following day intermittently, turning the weather chilly.

The DPO warned that if the current snowfall spell continued, it might block the road permanently until next summer, suspending traffic between the two federating units for the whole period. He said traffic up to Naran, the commercial hub of Kaghan Valley, was still running and tourists, though in small numbers, continued to visit the area to enjoy the snowfall. Hotels and businesses in the upper parts of the valley have closed until next summer, while local communities in Battakundi, Barawai, Bassal and the upper valley have started migrating to the lower parts of Mansehra and other areas of Hazara division to spend the winter.

A resident of Battakundi said almost half of the families had already moved down, and the migration would be completed within the next 10 to 15 days.
