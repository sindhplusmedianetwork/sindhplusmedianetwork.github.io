Australian batter dismissed hit wicket in bizarre fashion, video goes viral

Former rugby star Jonathan Thurston was dismissed hit wicket in a bizarre fashion during the first T20 Gold Clash tournament in Australia, and the video of the incident has drawn wide attention on social media. The 43-year-old Thurston was batting for Team Footy and had scored 29 runs, with his team needing only two runs off the last three balls for victory. At that moment, a delivery from fast bowler James Pattinson struck Thurston in the groin.

In his sudden reaction, the bat slipped from his hand and crashed straight into the stumps, and he was out hit wicket. The unusual manner of the dismissal left the spectators in the ground astonished. The incident has since been widely shared online, with many viewers describing it as one of the strangest dismissals seen in the game.
