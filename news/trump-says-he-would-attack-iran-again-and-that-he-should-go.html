Trump says he would attack Iran again and that he should go to heaven, not hell

In his first major television interview after the Republicans' midterm election defeat, US President Donald Trump said that if given the chance he would attack Iran again. He added that he should go to heaven, not hell, and that he will always hate some of his enemies. Trump said the Iran war and expensive petrol cost the Republicans the election.

He said Iran's conventional military strength has been weakened and a deal with Tehran is near. He also said he has no intention of escalating military action further. Trump admitted some deportations were too harsh, defended his pardons, and said he may make cabinet changes.

He called the impeachment demand against Pete Hegseth unfair.
