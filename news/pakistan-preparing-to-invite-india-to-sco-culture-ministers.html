Pakistan Preparing to Invite India to SCO Culture Ministers' Meeting, Report Claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan is preparing to invite India to the SCO culture ministers' meeting to be held early next year under its chairmanship of the Eurasian bloc, the Times of India has claimed. The claim could not be independently confirmed from Islamabad. Pakistan is also preparing to host the SCO heads of state council summit in Islamabad in late August next year, which India's prime minister usually attends.

The report notes India has stressed that SCO cooperation should not be read as an improvement in bilateral ties. The meeting would be one of the first engagements under Pakistan's chairmanship of the bloc.
