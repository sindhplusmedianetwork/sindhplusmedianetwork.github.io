Pakistani architect Yasmeen Lari takes centre stage at Qatar Museum show

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani architect Yasmeen Lari's humanitarian approach to architecture features prominently in a major exhibition in Qatar that brought together the work of more than 40 architects and practices from across the Middle East, North Africa and South Asia. Titled 'Beyti Beytak — My Home Is Your Home', the exhibition opened at Fahad bin Ali Palace in Doha on October 26 and will remain on display until May 29, 2027. Lari's contribution includes her Community Centre, designed in 2024, which uses bamboo and traditional construction techniques to serve communities affected by natural disasters.

Her work is known for low-cost, sustainable buildings that put the needs of disaster-hit communities first. The exhibition places her alongside leading architects from across the region, underlining the international recognition of her approach. For Pakistan, her prominent place in the Doha show is a moment of pride, as a Pakistani architect's work is showcased at one of the Gulf's leading cultural venues.
