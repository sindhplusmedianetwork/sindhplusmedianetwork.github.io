Minibus Catches Fire After Being Hit by Passenger Train in Indonesia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A minibus caught fire after being hit by a passenger train in Indonesia. The fast-moving Siantar Express dragged the burning bus along the track after the collision. The incident took place on the northern part of Sumatra island.

According to an unconfirmed report, one person was killed in the incident. Several people were also injured, including three members of the train crew.
