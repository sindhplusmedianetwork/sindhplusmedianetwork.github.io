Real Madrid Edge AEK Athens 3-2 After Late Valverde Winner

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federico Valverde lashed home a stunning long-range effort in the 92nd minute to snatch Real Madrid a late 3-2 victory at AEK Athens on Wednesday in the Champions League. The win preserved Madrid's 100 percent start in the competition. AEK twice took the lead, first through Aboubakary Koita and then through Stavros Pilios.

Jude Bellingham and Kylian Mbappe equalised each time to keep Madrid in the match. Valverde then struck the winner in stoppage time to settle the contest. The result extended Madrid's winning run to seven straight games across all competitions.

AEK were left to rue a late defeat after leading twice at home.
