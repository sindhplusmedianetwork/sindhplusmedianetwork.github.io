Will It Rain in Karachi? Met Office Issues New Forecast

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Meteorological Department has issued a new forecast for Karachi, raising hopes of rain in the city. The department's Early Warning Centre forecast light rain or drizzle in Karachi today and tomorrow under the influence of an incoming western weather system. Partly cloudy skies are expected in the city during this period.

Light fog on Sunday may reduce visibility in Karachi. The weather system is also expected to bring rain to various districts of rural Sindh. Meanwhile, the westerly wave continues to affect the country's upper and western parts, keeping the weather unsettled in those regions.
