Irfan Pathan Distances Himself From Mohammad Amir's Selection in Afghanistan Premier League

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Indian all-rounder Irfan Pathan has distanced himself from the selection of Pakistani fast bowler Mohammad Amir in the Afghanistan Premier League, saying he has no role in player selection. Amir was picked by the Kandahar Warriors in the platinum category as their first pick. Pathan, the team's brand ambassador, said captain Rashid Khan has full authority over selection and team decisions.

The Afghanistan Premier League is scheduled to begin on December 27, 2026. Amir is among the Pakistani players taking part in the league despite the Pakistan Cricket Board's decision not to issue NOCs for the tournament.
