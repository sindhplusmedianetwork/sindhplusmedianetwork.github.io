Two Children Killed, 15 Hurt as Wedding Bus Hits Tractor-Trolley Near Peshawar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two children lost their lives and 15 people were injured in a road accident near Peshawar on Thursday. A bus carrying wedding guests collided with a tractor-trolley in the Safen area of Budh Bher in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, according to Rescue 1122. The Peshawar control room immediately dispatched four ambulances and medical teams to the site.

Fifteen injured people, including women and children, received first aid at the scene. Eight of the injured, including women and children, were shifted to hospital for further treatment. A doctor later confirmed the deaths of the two children.

Rescue teams were still operating at the site as the story developed.
