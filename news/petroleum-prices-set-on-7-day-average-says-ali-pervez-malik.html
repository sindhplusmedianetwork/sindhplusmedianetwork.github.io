Petroleum Prices Set on 7-Day Average, Says Ali Pervez Malik

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister of State for Petroleum Ali Pervez Malik has said that petroleum product prices are determined in view of international market prices, based on a 7-day average. Addressing the National Assembly, he said this mechanism is meant to maintain supply and prevent profiteering and hoarding. The minister was responding to a calling-attention notice by MNA Aliya Kamran.

He clarified that prices are only announced on a daily basis. Malik added that petroleum companies reduced their margins on the Prime Minister's instructions, which enabled the larger cut in petrol and diesel prices announced the previous day.
