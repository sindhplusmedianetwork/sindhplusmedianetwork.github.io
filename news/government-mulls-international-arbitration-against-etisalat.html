Government mulls international arbitration against Etisalat over $800m PTCL dues

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government is preparing to take the United Arab Emirates' Etisalat to international arbitration over unpaid dues from the privatisation of PTCL. The federal cabinet has asked the attorney general's office to build a case to recover the outstanding $800 million from the 2005 sale of 26 per cent of PTCL shares. The $2.59 billion deal saw Pakistan receive $1.76 billion, with the remaining payment linked to the transfer of 3,246 PTCL properties.

Thirty-two of those properties remain untransferred even today. A committee formed in July 2026 recommended arbitration to recover the $800 million plus the opportunity cost since 2008 and damages. Senators have complained they were denied access to the original sale purchase agreement.
