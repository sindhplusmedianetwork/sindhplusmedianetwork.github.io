Bilal Bin Saqib urges Pakistan to embrace digital assets and blockchain at LUMS

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ISLAMABAD: Pakistan must embrace financial innovation and emerging technologies to become a player in the evolving global financial landscape rather than remain a consumer of technologies developed elsewhere, PVARA Chairman Bilal Bin Saqib said on Monday. Addressing a dialogue on 'Digital Assets and the Future of Banking' at the Lahore University of Management Sciences, he said Pakistan needed an active role in shaping the rules of the emerging international financial system. He said digital assets and blockchain could transform the country's financial infrastructure, expand financial inclusion and open investment opportunities beyond cryptocurrency trading.

'The question is whether we want to rent our future or own our future,' he remarked. He said Pakistan needed to work with banks and the State Bank of Pakistan to build an enabling regulatory environment that encourages innovation without compromising financial stability. Noting that about $41 billion flows in as remittances each year through systems built decades ago, he said even a two to three per cent reduction in transaction costs could channel an additional $1 billion into the grassroots economy.

The discussion was held with Pakistan Banks Association Chairman Zafar Masud.
