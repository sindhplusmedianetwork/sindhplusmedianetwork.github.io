Gold price falls for second straight day, per tola rate at Rs395,236

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices in Pakistan have fallen for a second consecutive day. According to the All Pakistan Gems and Jewellers Association, the price of 24-carat gold fell by Rs3,700 per tola, bringing the rate to Rs395,236 per tola. The price of 10 grams of 24-carat gold also fell by Rs3,172 to Rs338,851.

Before the decline, the per tola price had been reported at Rs398,936 and the 10-gram price at Rs342,023. In the international market, gold fell by $37 to $3,727 per ounce, while silver in Pakistan also fell by Rs43 to Rs5,005 per tola.
