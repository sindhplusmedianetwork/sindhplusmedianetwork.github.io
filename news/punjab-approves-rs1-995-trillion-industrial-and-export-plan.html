Punjab Approves Rs1.995 Trillion Industrial and Export Plan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab cabinet on Monday approved the key features of an industrial development and export promotion programme that envisages investment of 1.995 trillion rupees and the creation of more than 162,000 jobs. The programme was presented by Senior Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb at a meeting chaired by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif. Of the total outlay, 1.089 trillion rupees will come from the provincial government while 906 billion rupees is expected from the private sector.

Agriculture processing parks will be established in Sargodha, Khanewal, Okara and Bhakkar under the plan. The cabinet also cleared a 9.5 billion rupee subsidy for 10,000 high-powered tractors in a new phase of the Green Tractor Programme, which has received more than 250,000 applications with around 25,000 tractors delivered so far. Separately, the cabinet approved a 60 billion rupee allocation for the Apni Chhat, Apna Ghar loan programme and the donation of six clinic-on-wheels vehicles to Gilgit-Baltistan and fifteen to Azad Jammu and Kashmir.
