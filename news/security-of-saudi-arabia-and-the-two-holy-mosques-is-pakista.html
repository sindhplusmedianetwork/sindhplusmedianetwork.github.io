Security of Saudi Arabia and the Two Holy Mosques is Pakistan's top priority, says army spokesperson

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Army spokesperson Lt Gen Ahmed Sharif Chaudhry has said that the security and sovereignty of Saudi Arabia is Pakistan's top priority, and that the security of Saudi Arabia and the Two Holy Mosques is Pakistan's foremost concern. In an interview with an Arab newspaper, the Director General of Inter-Services Public Relations said Pakistan is ready to take all necessary steps for Saudi Arabia's defence in the current circumstances, and will provide military personnel, military capabilities, training and logistics support for defence purposes. He said Pakistani troops are already performing various responsibilities in Saudi Arabia, with their basic duties including defence support, military exercises and training activities.

He said practical progress has begun on the responsibilities of the Makkah defence alliance after the recent attacks on Saudi Arabia, and that Pakistan has strongly condemned the attacks on Saudi cities, airports and civilian and economic installations. He also said the meeting held with Iran in Tehran on October 26 was aimed at preparing a plan to reduce tensions, and that Field Marshal Asim Munir's participation in the Tehran meeting reflects Pakistan's support for dialogue and the peaceful resolution of disputes. The Director General of Inter-Services Public Relations said the four countries agreed to continue dialogue and cooperation for regional peace and stability.
