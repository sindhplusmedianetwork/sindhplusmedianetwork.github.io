UNIDO to Mobilise $40 Million for Sindh's Climate-Resilient Uplift

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah met UNIDO Deputy Director General and Managing Director Fatou Haidara at the CM House in Karachi to discuss a new phase of development cooperation. The two sides agreed to work together on inclusive economic growth, climate-resilient infrastructure, rural development and employment generation for vulnerable communities. UNIDO plans to mobilise approximately $40 million for a dedicated development programme for Sindh under its Programme for Country Partnership 2025-2030.

The meeting also reviewed progress under the EU-funded Poverty Alleviation and Inclusive Development Across Rural Sindh (PAIDAR) programme, which carries a 49.55 million euro grant and operates in Thatta, Sujawal, Badin, Tharparkar and Larkana. Provincial planning minister Jam Khan Shoro briefed the participants on PAIDAR's work in reducing poverty, strengthening local economies and creating jobs for women and young people. The chief minister welcomed the delegation and appreciated UNIDO's continued partnership, particularly its support for rural communities and enterprise development after the devastating floods of 2022.

The initiative will focus on rural livelihoods, climate-resilient infrastructure and jobs for youth.
