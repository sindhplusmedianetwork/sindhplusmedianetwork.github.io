FIA raids PIMS surgical waste store over alleged illegal sale of medical waste

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has raided the surgical waste store at PIMS hospital in Islamabad over the alleged illegal sale of medical waste and seized its record, including the store's entry register. PIMS Deputy Executive Director Dr Irum Naveed confirmed the raid and said the FIA team is examining the record. She said disposal of medical waste is outsourced to a private company, PIMS generates 1,200-1,500 kg of medical waste daily against an incineration capacity of 900 kg, and it is the private firm's responsibility to transport and incinerate the waste outside the hospital.

The raid centres on allegations that medical waste from the hospital was being sold illegally instead of being disposed of through the proper channel. The FIA is now going through the seized record as part of its inquiry.
