Yemen Claims 1,326 Strikes on Houthi Positions in 24 Hours Under 'Operation Dawn of Yemen'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's armed forces on Sunday claimed 1,326 strikes on Houthi positions over the past 24 hours as part of an operation dubbed 'Operation Dawn of Yemen'. A military spokesman said the strikes neutralised about 370 fighters and destroyed dozens of military vehicles and installations. He said fighter jets and drones took part in the operation, and that government forces had advanced on the eastern front of Marib and seized positions in Taiz.

The spokesman described the figures as a preliminary assessment, and there was no independent verification of the claims. The reported escalation comes amid renewed fighting on multiple fronts across the country.
