Arshdeep Singh Becomes First Indian Bowler to 150 T20I Wickets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India fast bowler Arshdeep Singh etched his name into the record books on Saturday despite India's defeat to New Zealand in the fourth T20 international. The left-arm pacer became the first Indian bowler in men's T20I history to reach 150 wickets, achieving the feat in his 103rd match. He is the third-fastest to the milestone in terms of balls bowled, taking 2,143 deliveries — behind Sri Lanka's Wanindu Hasaranga (2,035) and Afghanistan's Rashid Khan (2,098), both of whom got there in 92 matches.

Arshdeep also rewrote another record in the same game, finishing 2026 with 40 T20I wickets, the most by any bowler from a full-member nation in a single calendar year. He surpassed Ireland pacer Joshua Little's previous mark of 39 wickets. The twin landmarks came in a losing cause, as India went down to New Zealand in the fourth game of the series.

Arshdeep's rise to the top of India's T20I bowling charts marks a career-defining season for the young pacer.
