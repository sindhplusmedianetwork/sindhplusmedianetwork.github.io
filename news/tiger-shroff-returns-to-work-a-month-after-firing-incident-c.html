Tiger Shroff returns to work a month after firing incident, completes shoot with Janhvi Kapoor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff has returned to work almost a month after unidentified gunmen fired at his car in Mumbai. He has completed the Pune shooting schedule of his new film 'Lag Ja Gale' with Janhvi Kapoor. The film, directed by Raj Mehta, features Shroff in a negative role.

This is his first public appearance since the October 18 firing incident in Juhu, in which he escaped with minor injuries. The shooting schedule's completion marks Shroff's return to regular film work after the attack, which had raised fresh concerns about the security of Bollywood stars.
