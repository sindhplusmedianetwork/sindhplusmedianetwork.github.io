Over 86,000 Medicines Registered Under Track and Trace System: Health Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Health Minister Mustafa Kamal has said the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan has been made fully transparent and digital, with more than 86,000 medicines registered under the Track and Trace System. Addressing industrialists during his visit to the Korangi Association of Trade and Industry, he said consumers could now scan medicines through their mobile phones to verify authenticity and check prices. Pharmaceutical companies can register their products online, and a process that previously took years is now completed within 21 days.

He added that more than 5,000 cases had been processed in the eight months since the authority's certification procedures went digital, an achievement for which the Prime Minister had awarded the body for outstanding performance. According to statistics shared at the event, pharmaceutical exports reached $457 million in FY2024-25, up about 34 percent over the previous year. The minister suggested the government should step back from direct price regulation and let market forces decide medicine prices.
