Alleged Reason Behind Prince Harry and Meghan's Return to Britain Emerges in US Media Reports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

US media reports have claimed that Prince Harry and Meghan Markle's sudden return from the United States to Britain may have been linked to problems with Harry's US visa. Sources cited in the reports said the visa had expired and was not immediately renewed. It remains unclear what type of visa the prince held.

It is also unclear whether the alleged delay affected his legal stay in the United States. The couple is reportedly planning to spend Christmas in California, according to the reports.
