Ali Rehman Khan's new look leaves fans stunned, draws Hollywood comparisons

Pakistani actor Ali Rehman Khan has become the centre of attention on social media because of his new hairstyle. Known for his work in dramas and films, the actor recently appeared at an event in a distinctive style, where his long hair caught the attention of fans. He attended the event wearing a suit, accompanied by well-known actress and friend Areeba Habib.

As soon as pictures of his new look appeared on social media, users began comparing him with famous Hollywood personalities. One user jokingly wrote that he looked like a PTA-blocked version of John Wick, while another compared him with the renowned musician John Lennon. The pictures quickly spread across social media, with fans praising the actor's changed look and sharing the comparisons widely.
