Field Marshal Asim Munir Meets Malaysian PM Anwar Ibrahim, Agrees to Strengthen Defence Ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief of Army Staff and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir paid an official visit to Malaysia, according to ISPR. During the visit, he met Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim. The two discussed bilateral relations and defence and security cooperation between the two countries.

They also discussed the changing regional and global security situation. Both sides agreed to further strengthen defence ties through regular high-level visits, increased military cooperation and continued dialogue. Field Marshal Munir was presented a guard of honour at the Malaysian defence ministry in Kuala Lumpur.

He also met Defence Minister Mohamed Khaled bin Nordin during the visit.
