Mr Patlu breaks silence after Sialkot tragedy, seeks impartial probe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

TikToker Ansar Mahmood, popularly known as Mr Patlu, has issued his first detailed video statement following the deaths caused by a stampede during a wedding event in Sialkot. In the statement, Mr Patlu denied responsibility for the tragedy and appealed to the top authorities to hold an investigation into the incident. He claimed that at the time of the accident he was under treatment in a hospital and was unable to walk because of severe back pain, and he spoke openly about his state of mind, including thoughts of suicide.

He offered condolences to the families of those who died and expressed sympathy with the injured, saying he was as saddened by the tragedy as the affected families themselves. Mr Patlu appealed to Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz and Field Marshal Asim Munir to order an impartial investigation and to review all evidence before declaring him responsible for the accident. He said his wedding functions were scheduled for the 5th, 6th and 7th, covering mehndi, baraat and walima ceremonies, adding that he had arranged one event for the public to help deserving people, arrange marriages of deserving girls and distribute ration and gifts.

He claimed that ten to fifteen days before the event he had released a video urging attendees to look after their belongings, avoid pushing each other and stay in queues, and that a formal NOC had been obtained for the event, so blaming him for the incident was not justified. He also said he had been out of public view for the past two days, that he moves to the washroom with support from the ground, that his father's health was extremely poor, and that he would present himself once his health improves, but for now he could not walk, warning that if no further video appeared, this should be treated as his last message.
