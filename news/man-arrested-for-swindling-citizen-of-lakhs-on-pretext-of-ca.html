Man Arrested for Swindling Citizen of Lakhs on Pretext of Canadian Work Visa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man accused of swindling a citizen out of lakhs of rupees on the pretext of providing a Canadian work visa has been arrested. The accused, Qasim Hussain, was arrested in Rawalpindi, and the amount involved was 2,445,500 rupees. He allegedly took the money by promising to send the citizen's son, Muhammad Abdullah, to Canada on a work visa.

During the inquiry it emerged that he had provided fake documents attributed to the Foreign Office for visa processing, which proved fake on verification. He has been handed over to the Federal Investigation Agency for further investigation. The inquiry will also determine whether a human trafficking network is involved and identify other suspects.
