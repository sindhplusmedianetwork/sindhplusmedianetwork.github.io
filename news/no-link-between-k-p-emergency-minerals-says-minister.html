'No Link Between K-P Emergency, Minerals,' Says Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Tariq Fazal Chaudhry has rubbished attempts to link the imposition of governor's rule and emergency in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa to mineral extraction in the province. Speaking on a private television programme, he said it was unfortunate that efforts were being made to link the emergency with things other than terrorism, insisting it was not a power game but a peace game imposed in extraordinary circumstances through a constitutional option. The emergency and governor's rule were declared on October 11 after negotiations between the government and opposition failed, and a joint session of parliament later adopted two resolutions ratifying them for two months.

The opposition has decried the move as an attempt to encroach on the province's mineral wealth and the forcible occupation of Pakhtun lands. Chaudhry said there were differences between the former provincial government and the federation on counterterrorism, adding that the former chief minister was not even willing to condemn terrorism and the TTP. He said Dera Ismail Khan was among 12 districts where operations would be conducted in the first phase, claiming there was no writ of the government there.

On US Charge d'Affaires Natalie Baker's statement about Pakistan's mineral wealth, he said minerals buried for 80 years should be extracted to pay off loans, with the people of K-P and Balochistan having the first right to their provinces' wealth.
