Binance Users Pull $3.64 Billion on First Day After Hack Reopening

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, saw users withdraw around $4.65 billion on the first day withdrawals reopened after the massive cyberattack that drained more than $6 billion from the platform. Deposits on the day totalled just over $1 billion, leaving a net outflow of about $3.64 billion — 2.1 per cent of the exchange's assets — the company said in a statement on X. The US Secret Service said it froze a large share of the stolen funds within minutes of the attack, while the stablecoin issuer Circle froze more than $1.1 billion in USDC linked to the hack.

Binance said it had resumed fiat withdrawals in the US dollar, UAE dirham, Vietnamese dong, Philippine peso, Indonesian rupiah and about two dozen other currencies. The exchange also published a proof-of-reserves Merkle root for an October 31 snapshot, showing coverage of 100.04 per cent for bitcoin and 100 per cent for ether and USDT.
