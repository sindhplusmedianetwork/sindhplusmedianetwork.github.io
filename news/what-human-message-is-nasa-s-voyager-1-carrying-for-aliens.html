What human message is NASA's Voyager 1 carrying for aliens?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft has travelled 25.9 billion kilometres from Earth, a distance equal to one light-day, after a journey of more than 49 years. The spacecraft is still in contact with NASA. It carries a golden record that was sent with it in 1977.

The record contains 115 images of Earth, greetings in 55 languages, music from different countries and sounds of nature. The disc also carries instructions on how to play it, in case an alien civilisation ever finds the spacecraft.
