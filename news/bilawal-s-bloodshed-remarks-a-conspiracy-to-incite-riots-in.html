Bilawal's Bloodshed Remarks a Conspiracy to Incite Riots in Sindh, Says Farooq Sattar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

MQM leader Farooq Sattar has said Bilawal Bhutto's statement about bloodshed over new provinces is a conspiracy to incite riots in Sindh. He was speaking alongside MQM leader Waseem Akhtar, who said Sindh's schools lacked education, hospitals lacked treatment facilities, and corruption was at its peak. Akhtar asked why Bilawal remembered bloodshed instead of solving the basic problems of Sindh's people, and said the people of Karachi and Hyderabad wanted an empowered system rather than fighting.

Sattar claimed with full confidence that the sun of 1 January 2027 would rise over a Pakistan with several provinces, and that a referendum would be held across the country in December. He said six administrative units would be created in Sindh, and the Karachi unit would be named after Liaquat Ali Khan. Sattar also said the worst and most corrupt governance had ruled Sindh for 18 years.

He said the party would stage a peaceful protest against the speech and hold a rally in Karachi.
