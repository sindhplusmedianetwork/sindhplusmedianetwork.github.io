Pakistan Removes Five-Year Age Limit on Commercial Used-Vehicle Imports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has abolished the five-year age restriction on the commercial import of used vehicles, the Ministry of Commerce confirmed in an official memorandum sent to the Federal Board of Revenue on October 30. Previously, commercial importers could only bring in used vehicles that were not more than five years old, a condition that applied until June 30, 2026. Under the revised policy, which took effect from July 1, 2026, registered importers may bring in older vehicles falling under Pakistan Customs Tariff headings 8702, 8703, 8704 and 8711.

The ministry clarified that importers must still meet all other import policy requirements, including the safety and environmental standards prescribed by the Ministry of Industries and Production and the Engineering Development Board. No new maximum age has been announced. The decision was approved by the Economic Coordination Committee and the federal cabinet.
