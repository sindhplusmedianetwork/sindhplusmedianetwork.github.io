Apple adds Google's SynthID watermark to AI images on iPhone

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apple has introduced Google's SynthID watermark in iOS 27.2, under which images created in Image Playground and some images edited with Apple Intelligence will carry a watermark. SynthID, developed by Google, is used to identify content created by artificial intelligence and works at the pixel level. According to Google, the watermark is designed to survive cropping, filters and compression without affecting the quality of the image.

The iPhone currently has no direct option to detect this watermark, so users will have to use the SynthID Detector website. During testing, minor clean-up edits did not include the watermark, while images with major changes did. All images created in Image Playground will include the SynthID watermark.
