Khawaja Asif Sees 'Plot' Behind Maryam-Salik Row

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has claimed that Minister for Overseas Pakistanis Chaudhry Salik's recent corruption allegations against the Punjab government are part of an effort to pave the way for an "alternative arrangement". In an appearance on ARY News, Mr Asif said the remarks were aimed at "setting the stage for some post-period", adding that corruption existed across Pakistan. "There is an attempt to create an alternative arrangement, to discredit incumbent people so that a fresh team can be presented," he said, adding that he could only share his personal assessment.

He also referred to "some wealthy people", describing them as "carpetbaggers" who had been active for the past six to eight months in anticipation of a "political crisis". Asked whether the proposed arrangement concerned Punjab or the entire country, Mr Asif said some individuals were positioning themselves behind the plan, but it was not a nationwide development, and added that the plan could eventually be abandoned. He was also asked whether the strong reaction from the Punjab CM House to Mr Salik's allegations was linked to Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's reported close ties with the Chaudhry family, given his reportedly strained relationship with Maryam Nawaz, and he said this could be a factor.

Meanwhile, Mr Salik said in a post on X that he had received a legal notice from CM Maryam and would respond soon, writing: "Transparency is never meant to exist behind closed doors."
