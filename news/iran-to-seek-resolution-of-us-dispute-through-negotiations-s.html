Iran to Seek Resolution of US Dispute Through Negotiations, Says Pezeshkian

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has announced it will try to resolve its dispute with the United States through negotiations, according to Reuters. President Masoud Pezeshkian said Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei has granted the government certain special powers regarding peace talks with America. Pezeshkian added that war cannot force Iran to surrender.

Further details of the nature and scope of the powers have not yet emerged. The announcement marks a significant shift toward diplomacy amid ongoing regional tensions.
