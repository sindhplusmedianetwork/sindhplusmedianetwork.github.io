Arrest Warrants Issued for Shaukat Yousafzai; Bisham House Raided Twice

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Arrest warrants have been issued against PTI's Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Information Secretary and former provincial minister Shaukat Yousafzai under the Maintenance of Public Order. Following the warrants, police raided his house in Bisham twice in an attempt to arrest him. According to reports, warrants were also issued for his brother Liaquat Yousafzai and other party leaders.

Reacting to the development, Shaukat Yousafzai said violating the sanctity of the home and privacy runs against Pashtun traditions. He alleged that the government deliberately wanted to create fear and unrest to worsen the situation. The move comes amid a broader crackdown atmosphere in the province, where the federal government has imposed an emergency and governor's rule.
