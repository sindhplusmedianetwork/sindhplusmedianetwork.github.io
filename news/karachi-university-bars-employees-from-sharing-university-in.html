Karachi University bars employees from sharing university information on social media

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The administration of Karachi University has barred its employees from sharing university-related information on social media. All teaching and non-teaching staff have been warned not to upload or forward any university information or official document on social media without the prior permission of the competent authority, and violators will face disciplinary action. In a notification issued by Registrar Dr Imran Ahmed Siddiqui, the administration said it had come to notice that official letters and office orders, particularly about transfers and postings, additional charges and suspensions of employees and officers, were being shared in social media groups without permission.

Staff have been directed not to upload, forward or share any official document or information with any unauthorised person without prior approval. The notification warns that any violation will be taken seriously and may lead to disciplinary action, including under Rule 18 of the Government Servants Conduct Rules 1964, and that information meant for the public will only be shared through the university's official social media pages or other authorised sources.
