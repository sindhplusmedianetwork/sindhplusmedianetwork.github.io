Kundi Says Illegal Gold Mining in Kohat Funded Terrorism, Names Politicians

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi said illegal gold mining in Kohat was funding terrorism, claiming 80 percent of the proceeds went to Afghanistan and naming 12 politicians allegedly involved. He said about 49,000 people worked in the mining, with Rs4.2 billion taken every month. He announced all gold mining leases suspended, contractors ordered to leave the province within a week, and a new mining policy within three weeks.

A task force and a judicial commission will investigate the Kohat incident in which 10 police personnel were killed. The governor's remarks set out the scale of the alleged operation and the action now planned against it. The investigation will examine the incident and the mining activity linked to it.
