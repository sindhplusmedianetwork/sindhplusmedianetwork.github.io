Wapda Under Scrutiny Over Tarbela 5th Extension Flaws

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Official documents show that Pakistan's two highest project sanctioning bodies ordered a review of lapses by the Water and Power Development Authority in the Tarbela 5th Extension Project. The review covers Wapda's selection of consultants and the weaker design of the Rs316 billion project. The Central Development Working Party issued the direction in May, and the Executive Committee of the National Economic Council followed in August.

Both bodies directed that a committee led by the water resources minister review all procedural lapses and delays in the project. No committee has been established so far. The scrutiny follows Monday's breach at the T-5 project, when water flooded Tunnel 5 and construction was suspended indefinitely.
