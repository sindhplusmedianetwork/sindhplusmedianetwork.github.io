Punjab issues new dress code for government school teachers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's School Education Department has issued a new dress code for teachers in government schools across the province. Under the new dress code, male and female teachers are barred from wearing jeans, while wearing shorts and using sunglasses indoors will also not be allowed. Male teachers have been directed to wear a waistcoat or formal pant-shirt, and wearing closed shoes has been made compulsory for them, although sandals with socks will be allowed.

Women teachers have been barred from wearing heeled shoes, and sleeveless and transparent clothing will also not be allowed. Women teachers have been directed to wear a dupatta or scarf with formal dress, while wearing hijab and abaya remains permitted. The School Education Department has directed the heads of all government schools in the province to ensure immediate implementation of the new dress code.
