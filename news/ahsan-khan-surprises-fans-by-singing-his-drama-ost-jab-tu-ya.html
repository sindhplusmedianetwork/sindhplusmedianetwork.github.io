Ahsan Khan Surprises Fans by Singing His Drama OST 'Jab Tu Yaad Aaye' at Houston Event

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani showbiz actor Ahsan Khan surprised his fans by singing at an event in Houston, USA, which he attended along with senior actress Sania Saeed, according to Geo News. During the programme, fans were present and chatted with both actors. In the middle of the event, Ahsan Khan sang the original soundtrack of his famous drama, 'Jab Tu Yaad Aaye'.

The video of him singing has gone viral on social media. Fans were amazed by his voice and thoroughly enjoyed the song. The clip continues to spread widely, drawing delight from his followers.
