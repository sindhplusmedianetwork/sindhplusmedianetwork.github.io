Margaret Qualley and Jack Antonoff File for Divorce After 3 Years of Marriage

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Hollywood actress Margaret Qualley and 14-time Grammy-winning musician Jack Antonoff have filed for divorce, ending three years of marriage. Court records show the couple filed an uncontested divorce case on Friday in New York's Kings County Supreme Court, with Qualley named as the petitioner and Antonoff as the respondent. Reports say the two separated in July; their representatives did not immediately comment.

The 32-year-old Qualley is known for her roles in 'The Substance' and 'Once Upon a Time in Hollywood'. The 42-year-old Antonoff is a celebrated songwriter and producer who has worked with Taylor Swift, Lana Del Rey and Sabrina Carpenter, and is a member of the band Bleachers. The couple began dating in 2021 and married in August 2023.
