Reports say Imran Khan supports creation of new provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Details have emerged of a meeting between PTI founding chairman Imran Khan and senior lawyers Sardar Latif Khosa and Barrister Salman Safdar in jail, in which the creation of new provinces was discussed. According to journalist Naeem Ashraf Butt's report, Imran Khan supported the creation of new provinces on administrative grounds, saying it would serve the national interest. He also instructed that all necessary measures begin immediately for the release of all women prisoners, including his wife Bushra Bibi and his sisters.

The release of the women prisoners was described as PTI's top priority. The meeting took place in jail, where the senior lawyers met the PTI founding chairman. The discussion covered the administrative case for new provinces alongside the question of the women prisoners' release.
