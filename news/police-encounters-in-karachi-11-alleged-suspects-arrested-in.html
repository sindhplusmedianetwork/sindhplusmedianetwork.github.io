Police encounters in Karachi; 11 alleged suspects arrested including 10 injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

During exchanges of fire between police and alleged dacoits in different areas of Karachi, 10 suspects were injured. Police arrested a total of 11 suspects. Police claimed to have recovered weapons, motorcycles, mobile phones and cash from them.

According to police, Ferozabad police and the Shaheen Force had an alleged encounter on Shaheed-e-Millat Road. During that encounter, a suspect named Naeem was arrested in injured condition, while his accomplice managed to escape.
