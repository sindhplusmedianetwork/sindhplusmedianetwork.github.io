Government plans $1 billion investment in LPG infrastructure over 3-5 years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has prepared a plan to invest about one billion dollars in the expansion of LPG (liquefied petroleum gas) infrastructure in Pakistan over the next three to five years. The plan covers new import terminals, storage facilities and a distribution system. It also includes new cylinders and filling plants.

The investment is aimed at expanding the country's LPG network. The plan was reported from Islamabad.
