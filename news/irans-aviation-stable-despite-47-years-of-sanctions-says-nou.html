Iran’s aviation stable despite 47 years of sanctions, says Nourian

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hassan Nourian, a senior Iranian Foreign Ministry official, has said that Iran’s aviation industry remains stable despite 47 years of global sanctions. Speaking to Geo News, he said that if the United States had spent money on infrastructure instead of war, it would have avoided the ground stop. He added that better care of American citizens would have prevented it.

Nourian said Iran is among the leading countries in technical and engineering services. He added that Iranian engineering graduates are compared with those of Russia, China and the United States.
