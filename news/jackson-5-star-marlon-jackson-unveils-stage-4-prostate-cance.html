Jackson 5 star Marlon Jackson unveils Stage 4 prostate cancer diagnosis

Jackson 5 member and Michael Jackson's older brother Marlon Jackson has revealed his secret cancer battle. Marlon shared that he has been battling Stage 4 prostate cancer for the last four years. The 69-year-old shared the health update on Farah Shammas' podcast on Tuesday, saying he has had cancer for four years.

Assuring fans that the cancer is not terminal, he said he is doing well and will be around for a long time. Marlon shared that he has already had his prostate gland removed and has undergone radiation treatment, insisting everything is fine and that he walks almost four miles four times a week. Since receiving the diagnosis, the singer has also cut out sugar from his diet, noting he is careful about what he puts in his body.

He thanked everyone who has prayed for him and said his faith in God has helped him on his cancer journey, adding that even his doctor said he was doing so much better than expected.
