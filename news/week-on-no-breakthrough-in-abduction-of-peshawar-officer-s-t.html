Week On, No Breakthrough in Abduction of Peshawar Officer's Two Children

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A week has passed since two children of grade-21 government officer Ikramullah Khan were allegedly abducted from his residence in Hayatabad, Peshawar, and the case remains unsolved. Police have recovered the officer's car, which the abductors took with them, from Kohat Road in Peshawar. Four special teams have been formed to investigate the case, and investigators are using geofencing as part of their efforts.

Despite the recovery of the vehicle, police say they have made no breakthrough so far. Neither the children have been recovered nor have the suspects been arrested. The investigation into the abduction is continuing.
