Rezaei Warns US: Starting a War Will Bring a Devastating Response

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The head of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, Mohsen Rezaei, has warned the United States that starting a war will bring a devastating response. In his statement, without naming President Donald Trump, he said that the US had lost the war and the election too. Rezaei claimed that Vice President JD Vance had conceded, that Secretary of State Marco Rubio was leaving office and that Defence Secretary Hegseth was about to be sacked.

He said the CENTCOM commander had become a podcaster, and asked whom he should convey the message to in Washington that starting a war would end in devastation. The warning comes as tensions between the United States and Iran remain high, with fresh explosions reported on Qeshm Island and the US announcing a new task force against Iran. Rezaei's remarks were carried by Iranian media and reported in Pakistan on November 8.
