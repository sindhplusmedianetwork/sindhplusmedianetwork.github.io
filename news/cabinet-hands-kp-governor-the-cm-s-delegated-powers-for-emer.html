Cabinet Hands KP Governor the CM's Delegated Powers for Emergency Period

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal cabinet on Tuesday approved the transfer of the powers delegated to the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister to the province's governor for the duration of the emergency. The decision was taken at a cabinet meeting in Islamabad chaired by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, with the stated aim of ensuring smooth day-to-day administration and better governance in the province. Under the decision, the KP governor will submit a monthly review report to the federal government on how the transferred powers are being used.

The cabinet expressed the hope that the KP Advisory Council, working under the governor's leadership, would make full efforts for the welfare and prosperity of the province's people and for the restoration of law and order. In the same meeting, the cabinet ratified the Pakistan-Kuwait defence cooperation agreement signed in June 2023, and approved new designs for currency notes on the Finance Ministry's recommendation. Officials told the cabinet the new notes would carry stronger security features and modern material to curb counterfeiting and extend their usable life.
