Pakistan's Fiscal Surplus Narrows as Interest Payments Soar 36pc: Finance Ministry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's fiscal position came under strain in the first quarter of the current financial year as surging interest payments and higher development spending eroded the surplus. The Finance Ministry said in its Monthly Economic Update and Outlook report, released on Thursday, that the overall fiscal surplus narrowed to 0.8 percent of GDP, or Rs1.198 trillion, compared with 1.7 percent in the same period last year. Interest payments jumped 36.6 percent, while development expenditure rose 69.5 percent.

On the positive side, the government slashed subsidies by 90 percent, and the Federal Board of Revenue's tax collection grew 7 percent to Rs3.086 trillion, beating its quarterly target by Rs33.2 billion. Despite these gains, the heavy cost of debt servicing weighed on the overall balance.
