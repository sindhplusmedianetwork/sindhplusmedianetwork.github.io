Four Rehri Goth fishermen die mysteriously days after month-long sea trip

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four fishermen from Rehri Goth in Karachi died under mysterious circumstances days after returning from a month-long fishing trip at sea. SHO Sukhan Irfan Ali Sehto identified the deceased as Hashim, Hussain, Usman and Siddique, saying their health deteriorated soon after they returned to Rehri Goth. They were first taken to a private hospital and later shifted to Jinnah Hospital, where all four died on separate days during treatment within a week.

The SHO said no injury marks or signs of torture were found on their bodies, and doctors termed the deaths natural. A video statement of the niece of one of the deceased also surfaced, in which she said five family members had gone fishing and complained of abdominal pain after returning. She said they were diagnosed with kidney and heart-related complications at Jinnah Hospital, where they were admitted and died during treatment.

The SHO said further investigation into the incident was under way. A day earlier, 11 fishermen reunited with their families in Karachi after returning from Indian prisons via the Wagah border.
