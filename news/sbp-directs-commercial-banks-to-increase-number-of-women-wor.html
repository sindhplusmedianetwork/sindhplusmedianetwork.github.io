SBP directs commercial banks to increase number of women workers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The State Bank of Pakistan has introduced new requirements for commercial banks to raise female participation in the banking industry, setting a target of 22 percent women representation by December 2028. The measures come under the central bank's Banking on Equality Policy 2026. Under the policy, banks must develop recruitment and implementation plans, improve promotion systems and professional development for women, and appoint more women officers in microfinance, agriculture, SME, housing and sustainable financing roles.

Figures from the central bank show that women's participation in banking rose from 13 percent in 2021 to 17 percent in 2025. Around 50,000 women are currently employed in the sector. The policy aims to build on that progress and widen women's presence across the industry over the next three years.
