Two Karachi Household Deaths in 25 Days Raise Questions Over Welfare Gaps

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The deaths of 10 people, including five children, in two separate incidents in Karachi within 25 days have renewed concern about the support available to families facing severe financial and personal difficulties. In the first incident, five bodies were recovered from a flat in Ahsanabad on September 20, and the deceased included a doctor, his wife and their three children. A second incident occurred on October 13, when five bodies were found inside a flat in Gulshan-e-Falaknaz in the Malir Bin Qasim area.

The deceased in that incident reportedly included a husband, his wife, the wife's sister and two children. Police have not conclusively classified either incident as a case of collective suicide. Investigators are examining the circumstances surrounding the deaths, while financial difficulties have emerged as a possible contributing factor in both cases.

The incidents have drawn attention to the growing pressures on low-income and middle-class households from poverty, unemployment, inflation and the rising cost of living. They have also prompted questions about whether government and charitable welfare programmes are reaching vulnerable households in time.
