US Air Force's KC-46 Pegasus Refueling Tanker Ready for Mission Amid Mideast Buildup

The United States Air Force's KC-46 Pegasus aerial refueling tanker is ready for its mission as military activity intensifies across the Middle East. According to Express News, the KC-46 Pegasus — the Air Force's modern aerial fuel-carrying aircraft — is designed to transfer fuel to other military aircraft while airborne, extending their range and endurance. The report highlights the tanker's significance at a time of heightened regional tensions, when sustained aerial support has become critical.

Aerial refueling allows fighter jets, bombers and surveillance aircraft to remain on station far longer than their onboard fuel would otherwise permit. The Pegasus has steadily replaced older tanker fleets as the backbone of America's airborne refueling capability. Its reported readiness underscores Washington's preparations for prolonged operations in the region.
