Punjab Assembly passes Theatres Act; obscene and vulgar performances banned

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Assembly has passed the Dramatic and Theatrical Performances Act 2026, a new law governing theatre in the province. The bill was tabled by Information and Culture Minister Azma Bokhari and approved by the house on Wednesday. Under the law, the registration fee for theatre artists has been abolished, while registration of every artist has been made compulsory.

The law bans harassment as well as obscene and vulgar performances in theatres, and sets out penalties for violations. A theatre's licence can be cancelled if it fails to pay artists the minimum wage, and a theatre can be sealed if it stages a performance by an unregistered artist. The government says the law is meant to protect artists and audiences and to bring order to the theatre industry, which has long operated with little formal regulation.
