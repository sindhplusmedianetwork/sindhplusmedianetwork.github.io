Saudi company Rawaf Mina to ensure Hajj 2027 services under digital system: Chaudhry Ahmad Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chaudhry Ahmad Nawaz, coordinator for South Asia of Saudi company Rawaf Mina, welcomed the Pakistan-Saudi agreement for Hajj 2027. He said the company is committed to serving 37,000 Pakistani pilgrims as well as private scheme pilgrims. He said Rawaf Mina will ensure Hajj 2027 services under a digital system.

Complaints at the Mashair sites will be handled through a computerised system, he added. He congratulated Saudi Hajj and Umrah Minister Dr Tawfiq Al-Rabiah and his team on the arrangements at the Jeddah Super Dome exhibition and conference. His remarks came as preparations for Hajj 2027 services move forward under the agreement.
