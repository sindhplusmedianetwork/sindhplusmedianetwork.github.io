Binance freezes assets after $6.3bn security breach, biggest crypto heist ever

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, has said a security breach had an estimated impact of $6.3 billion, potentially making it the biggest crypto heist in history. The company said about $3 billion in affected assets have so far been located and frozen. Investigators believe the breach may have begun with a hacked laptop belonging to one of Binance's engineers.

The news rattled digital-asset markets, with Bitcoin dropping to a six-week low of $76,368. The exchange is continuing to trace the remaining missing funds.
