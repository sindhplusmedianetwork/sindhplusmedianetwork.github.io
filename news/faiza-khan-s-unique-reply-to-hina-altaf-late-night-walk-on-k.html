Faiza Khan's unique reply to Hina Altaf: late-night walk on Karachi street

Pakistani actress Faiza Khan has given a unique response to Hina Altaf's statement describing Karachi as unsafe for women, sharing a video of herself walking on a city street late at night. Hina Altaf had recently called Karachi a jail, saying that women are not safe in the city. In response, Faiza Khan recorded a video while walking on the street at about 2:15 at night and asked who says Karachi is not safe for girls.

According to the actress, she had gone out for a walk because she was feeling anxious at home, and she felt safe while walking. Earlier, Aiman Khan and Muneeb Butt had also expressed disagreement with Hina Altaf's statement.
