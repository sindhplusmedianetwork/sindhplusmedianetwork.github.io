Christiane Amanpour to Leave CNN After 43 Years, Exits by June 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

World-renowned journalist Christiane Amanpour has announced her departure from the international news network CNN after a career spanning more than four decades, with a phased exit by June 2027. Amanpour began her 43-year career at CNN as a news desk assistant and went on to become one of the network's most recognizable and trusted faces, known for her bold reporting from war zones and interviews with world leaders. She currently hosts the famous show 'Amanpour' on CNN International.

In an emotional statement, the 68-year-old journalist said that after months of reflection and some sleepless nights, she had decided to part ways with her 'spectacular 43-year partner'. She said she looks forward to new and exciting projects and would always wish well for CNN's independent journalism. Her farewell message to journalists repeated her famous maxim: 'Remember: always be truthful, not neutral.' CNN chairman Mark Thompson confirmed the decision in an internal memo to staff, saying they had been discussing it for months.

The decision comes as David Ellison's Skydance has acquired CNN's parent company Warner Bros Discovery, and Amanpour had publicly voiced concern in May at a London journalism summit about the Ellison family's control of CNN, citing CBS News as an example.
