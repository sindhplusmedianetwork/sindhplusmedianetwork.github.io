Maryam Nawaz's badminton video with young players goes viral during Jhelum visit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A video of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz playing badminton with young people has gone viral on social media. The video was recorded during her visit to Jhelum, where she joined young players for a game of badminton. PML-N MPA Hina Parvez Butt shared the clip on social media and described it as the most beautiful video of the day.

Social media users praised the chief minister for taking part in the game and encouraging young players. The video spread quickly across platforms and drew positive comments from users. Many users said the chief minister's participation sent a positive message to young people about sports.
