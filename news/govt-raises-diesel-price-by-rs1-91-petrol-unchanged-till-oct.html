Govt Raises Diesel Price by Rs1.91, Petrol Unchanged Till Oct 26

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government raised the price of high-speed diesel by Rs1.91 per litre on Friday, keeping petrol unchanged at Rs377.71 per litre. Under the revised rates, high-speed diesel will cost Rs393.66 per litre for the October 24 to October 26 period. The revision comes under the daily pricing mechanism the government introduced on July 17, replacing the earlier weekly revision cycle.

That decision was linked to volatility in global oil markets amid renewed tensions between the United States and Iran. The latest adjustment followed a revision a day earlier in which petrol had been cut by Rs1.60 and diesel raised by Rs1.64 for October 23. Officials say the daily mechanism allows prices to respond more quickly to international market movements.
