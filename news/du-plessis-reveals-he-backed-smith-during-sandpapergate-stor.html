Du Plessis reveals he backed Smith during sandpapergate storm

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Former South Africa captain Faf du Plessis has revealed that he supported Australian captain Steve Smith when the ball-tampering controversy surfaced during the 2018 Cape Town Test. Speaking in Cricinfo's video series 'Cricketers on Cricketers', Du Plessis said that when Smith accepted responsibility at a press conference, he felt the Australian captain was under unnecessary pressure. He said he sent Smith a message, urging him to stay strong in the difficult time and assuring him of his support.

Du Plessis said the episode reminded him of his own experience in 2016, when he faced criticism in the Hobart Test over altering the condition of the ball. He also recalled that in 2016, Smith had defended him instead of taking a harsh stance against him, which earned the Australian player respect from the South African team. After the Cape Town ball-tampering scandal, Smith was banned for one year and stripped of the captaincy.
