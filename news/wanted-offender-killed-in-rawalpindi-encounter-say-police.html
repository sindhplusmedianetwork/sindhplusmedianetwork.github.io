Wanted Offender Killed in Rawalpindi Encounter, Say Police

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A wanted proclaimed offender was killed in an exchange of fire with police in Rawalpindi late at night, officials said. The incident took place on Dahi Data main road within the limits of the Jatli police station, where a Crime Control Department team came under fire from suspects riding motorcycles. In the ensuing exchange, the wanted offender Hammad alias Mado was killed while his accomplices managed to flee.

According to police, the deceased was wanted in several serious cases, including murder committed during robbery, attempted murder and the armed sexual assault of minor children, and was considered a symbol of fear in the area. A search operation is underway to arrest the fleeing suspects, and the body has been shifted to the tehsil headquarters hospital for post-mortem examination.
