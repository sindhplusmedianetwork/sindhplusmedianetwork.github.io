26-year-old issue of madrasa registration and bank accounts resolved, says Tahir Ashrafi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Ulema Council chairman and Wifaq-ul-Madaris president Hafiz Muhammad Tahir Mahmood Ashrafi says the 26-year-old issue of the registration of religious seminaries and their bank accounts has been resolved. He was speaking at a prize distribution ceremony at the Zaid Islamic Centre auditorium of Punjab University, held for students who secured prominent positions in the Board of Islamic Education's annual examinations. He said the registration process for seminaries has begun in Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Islamabad under the Directorate General of Religious Education and the Societies Act, while bank accounts of seminaries are also being opened.

He said seminary students are an asset for the future, and that giving them awareness of national responsibilities along with religious education is an important need of the time. He said linking seminaries with extremism and terrorism is not right, as seminaries are playing an important role in promoting peace, unity and national solidarity. He thanked Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir and others who played a role in resolving the issue, and said the Muttahida Madaris Council, religious education boards and other institutions also contributed.

He urged young people to stay cautious about misleading information and rumours on social media, to verify news before sharing it, and to use social media to promote national harmony, constructive dialogue and positive thinking.
