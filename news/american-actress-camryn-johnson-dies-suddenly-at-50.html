American actress Camryn Johnson dies suddenly at 50

American actress and model Camryn Johnson died suddenly at the age of 50. She died on 4 November 2026 in Los Angeles. She had suffered a sudden cardiac complication at home on 27 October and was taken to Cedars-Sinai hospital.

Doctors there diagnosed an aneurysm-related condition in a heart artery. The final medical cause of death has not yet been formally announced. She was known for her roles in the TV drama 'Beverly Hills, 90210' and the film 'Dude, Where's My Car?'.
