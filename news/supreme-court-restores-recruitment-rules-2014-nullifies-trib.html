Supreme Court Restores Recruitment Rules 2014, Nullifies Tribunal Verdict

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Supreme Court has restored the Controller General of Accounts Recruitment Rules 2014 in a written verdict authored by Justice Ayesha A. Malik. The judgment nullified the Federal Service Tribunal's verdict of October 17, 2022, which had struck down the 2014 rules.

The court held that the tribunal had no authority to cancel recruitment rules or to rewrite service policy, ruling that it had exceeded its jurisdiction under Article 212 of the Constitution. The court also observed that there is no legal vested right to a direct promotional jump from junior auditor to assistant accounts officer. It further noted that the government remains free to amend service structures as administrative needs require.

The ruling settles a long-running dispute over promotions in the accounts service.
