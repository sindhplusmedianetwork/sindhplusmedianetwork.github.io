Eisenkot Says Netanyahu's Bid to Discredit Israeli Election Results Has 'Failed'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israel's Yashar party leader Gadi Eisenkot said on Sunday that efforts by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his Likud party to discredit the results of last week's parliamentary elections and steer the country toward another election had 'failed.' In a post on X, Eisenkot called the attempt 'despicable and malicious,' writing that only someone who realises he has lost would wage such a delegitimisation campaign. He said the results for the 26th Knesset were 'firm and clear,' welcoming remarks by Central Elections Committee Chairman Justice Noam Sohlberg affirming the integrity of the vote. Sohlberg said voting had proceeded properly and that even potential irregularities being examined would not undermine the overall integrity of the results.

Netanyahu and Likud have alleged Qatari interference through funding intended to increase turnout among Arab voters, but the party has not publicly presented evidence establishing Qatari interference or widespread fraud; Qatar rejected the accusations as a 'new disinformation campaign.' With all votes counted, Netanyahu's bloc secured 53 seats in the 120-member Knesset compared with 48 for the opposition camp led by Eisenkot and 19 for Arab parties, leaving neither main bloc with the 61 seats needed for a governing majority. Final turnout stood at 77.9 per cent, the highest since 1999, and the official results are due to be submitted to President Isaac Herzog on Wednesday.
