Pakistan tourism festival ends in Islamabad after three days of culture and sport

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The three-day Pakistan Tourism, Sports and Family Festival concluded at the Islamabad Sports Complex on Sunday, drawing large crowds with cultural, recreational, sporting and adventure activities. The festival, described as one of the country's largest tourism events, brought together Pakistan's cultural heritage, traditional cuisine, entertainment and tourism attractions at one venue. The concluding night featured musical performances by renowned singers.

Fireworks marked the end of the festival. Attractions during the three days included folk dances, tent pegging, paragliding, hot-air balloon rides and cultural stalls from diplomatic missions and all provinces.
