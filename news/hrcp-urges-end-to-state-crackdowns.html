HRCP urges end to state crackdowns

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read

The Human Rights Commission of Pakistan has expressed concern over growing restrictions on freedom of expression, enforced disappearances, arrests of political activists and threats to judicial independence. In a statement issued after its 40th annual general meeting on Sunday, the commission called for a review of the 26th and 27th Constitutional Amendments and an end to military trials of civilians. The commission also raised the 10-year sentences handed to human rights lawyers Imaan Mazari-Hazir and Hadi Chattha in October 2026.

It said the shrinking space for dissent and the pressure on the judiciary were undermining fundamental rights guaranteed by the Constitution. The statement urged the state to restore the freedoms of expression, association and peaceful assembly. It also called on the authorities to end enforced disappearances and to bring those responsible to account.
