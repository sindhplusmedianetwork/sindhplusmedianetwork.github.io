India's Pollution Monitors Overwhelmed as Toxic Smog Hits 66 Times WHO Limit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's air pollution monitors were overwhelmed this week by toxic smog so severe that concentrations exceeded their upper measurement limit. Regulators said the levels were equivalent to 66 times the World Health Organisation's recommended daily maximum. The admission follows opposition accusations of deliberate gaps in official readings for several hours overnight from Sunday into Monday.

The opposition said the gaps appeared just as pollution surged with Diwali fireworks. The Central Pollution Control Board rejected accusations of deliberate under-reporting. It said the gaps reflected differences in the measurement of pollutant concentration.

Readings later hit 879 micrograms per cubic metre in Delhi's Mandir Marg locality.
