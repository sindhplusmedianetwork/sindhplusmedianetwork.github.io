Two Oil Tankers Catch Fire After Hitting Sea Mines in Strait of Hormuz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two oil tankers hit naval mines and caught fire while trying to pass through the Strait of Hormuz. The Guards claimed the vessels had switched off their GPS tracking and were trying to use an unauthorised route under the protection of US warships. Iran's Tasnim news agency reported that the Iranian navy said another tanker also caught fire on Wednesday morning after hitting a mine in prohibited waters.

Iranian authorities said their forces continuously monitor and control the eastern part of the strait. They warned ship operators to contact the Iranian armed forces and follow maritime rules for safe passage.
