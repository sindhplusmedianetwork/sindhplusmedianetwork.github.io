Bahrain Arrests Indian Man Over Planned Fatal Beating of Compatriot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 40-year-old Indian national has been taken into custody in Bahrain after another Indian man was allegedly pulled out of his car, beaten and killed in a planned attack. Bahraini daily Al Khaleej reported that the General Directorate of Criminal Investigation and Forensic Science, working with the Muharraq Governorate Police Directorate, made the arrest. According to the authorities, the suspect and his accomplices stopped the victim's car in Salman Industrial City, forced him out and assaulted him, and the man died at the scene.

Police described the killing as premeditated and said several other people allegedly involved in the incident have also been arrested. In a separate case, Bahrain's High Criminal Court handed 15-year prison terms and 10,000-dinar fines to two Asian nationals over a drug import attempt, while a female defendant was exempted from punishment for helping investigators reach the first suspect. The court ordered all the drug-case convicts permanently deported from Bahrain after serving their sentences.
