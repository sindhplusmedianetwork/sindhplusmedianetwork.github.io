Louvre's $100 Million Crown Jewels Still Missing One Year After Daring Heist

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

One year after thieves disguised as workmen broke into the Louvre on October 19 last year and stole eight French crown-jewel pieces worth an estimated $100 million, the gems are still missing. The four suspects are in jail awaiting trial. The last known sighting of the jewels was on CCTV footage from an underground lock-up in the Aubervilliers suburb of Paris.

Searches of the suspects' homes, cellars and garages found nothing. Experts told AFP that the best hope for recovery is a suspect confessing in exchange for a lenient sentence. They warned the worst case is that the jewels were dismantled and recut in Antwerp or Tel Aviv, erasing them forever.
