Nokia brings back the flip phone with the new 2360 Flip

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

HMD, the company behind Nokia phones, has launched the Nokia 2360 Flip in the Philippines. The clamshell phone is aimed mainly at elderly users and school-going children who want a simple, easy-to-use device. It features a 2.8-inch inner display, a 1,450mAh battery and a dedicated SOS button for emergencies.

A family-assist feature lets relatives keep track of the phone's location and usage. The phone supports only 2G networks, limiting it to basic calls and texts. The launch shows there is still real demand for simple phones in the smartphone age.

Price and wider availability details were not announced in the launch report.
