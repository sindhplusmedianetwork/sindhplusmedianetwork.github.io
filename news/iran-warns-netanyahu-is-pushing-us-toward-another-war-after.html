Iran Warns Netanyahu Is Pushing US Toward Another War After Trump's Backing

US President Donald Trump on Friday publicly reaffirmed his full support for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu ahead of upcoming Israeli elections. Hours later, Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi accused Netanyahu of pushing the United States toward another war in the region. Araghchi said the Israeli premier was dragging Washington into a conflict that served neither country's interests.

The remarks deepen the war of words between Tehran and Washington amid stalled diplomacy. Analysts say the exchange underscores how quickly regional tensions can escalate in the run-up to Israel's polls.
