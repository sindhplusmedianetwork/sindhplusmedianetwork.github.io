Balochistan offers rewards of up to Rs250 million for information on wanted terrorists

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Balochistan government has announced cash rewards for information that helps trace highly wanted terrorists in the province. Rewards of between Rs50 million and Rs250 million have been fixed for information about the most wanted men, according to the assistant to the home minister for media affairs, Babar Yousafzai. He said a reward of Rs250 million has been announced for information about Rehman Gul.

The same amount has been set for information about Basheer Zeb. Rewards have also been fixed for information about other highly wanted terrorists. Yousafzai appealed to the public to pass on verified information about terrorists to the relevant institutions.
