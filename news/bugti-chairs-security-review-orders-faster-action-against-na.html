Bugti chairs security review, orders faster action against narcotics and smuggling

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 29th meeting of the Hardening of the State conference was held in Quetta under the chairmanship of Balochistan Chief Minister Mir Sarfraz Bugti, reviewing the province's overall security situation, development projects and ongoing measures against illegal activities. According to Dunya News, representatives of federal and provincial institutions, relevant departments and security forces attended the meeting. Participants stressed stepping up operations against smuggling and narcotics, eradicating poppy cultivation and curbing other illegal activities.

The meeting expressed satisfaction over the process of converting B Areas into A Areas and reaffirmed the resolve to carry it forward effectively across all divisions. Directions were also issued to provide the necessary funds and resources for completing Safe City projects in Quetta and other major cities of the province. Participants reiterated their commitment to continuing joint efforts for lasting peace in Balochistan, the rule of law and effective state authority.
