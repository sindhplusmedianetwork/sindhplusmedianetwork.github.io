Fazlur Rehman says he is under state siege, vows Sargodha conference will go ahead on 26 November

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman has said he is living under a state siege, with his party workers facing state pressure. Speaking to lawyers of the Lahore High Court Bar through a video link, he said the whole city of Lahore was filled with state barriers, all roads were closed and checkpoints had been set up, preventing him from addressing the gathering in person. He said oppression never lasts, while patience has endured throughout history, adding that his party has always stood for peace and sacrificed lives for it.

He said the party needs no certificate of loyalty from any ruler or state institution, and that its workers stand firm and will not bow before pressure. He announced that the party's major conference in Sargodha on 26 November will go ahead at all costs, and urged people to make the gathering a success and demonstrate their freedom. He said workers across Sargodha division were facing state pressure, with local leaders' businesses shut, shops sealed and arrests under way in Bhakkar, Mianwali, Khushab and Sargodha.

He added that the attitude of state forces hardened after the party exposed their role over mineral resources in the tribal areas, and insisted that mineral wealth belongs to the people, not to the federation or any single powerful institution.
