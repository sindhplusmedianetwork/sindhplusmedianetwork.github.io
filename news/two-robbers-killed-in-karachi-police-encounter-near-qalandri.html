Two Robbers Killed in Karachi Police Encounter Near Qalandria Chowk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two robbers were killed in a police encounter near Qalandria Chowk on Shahrah-e-Noor Jahan in Karachi. SSP Central Dr Imran Khan said the suspects were looting citizens when a police team reached the spot. When the police signalled them to stop, the robbers opened fire, and both were killed in the retaliatory fire.

Police recovered two pistols, five mobile phones and a CD-125 motorcycle from the scene. The identification of the deceased is under way. Police said further investigation is in progress.
