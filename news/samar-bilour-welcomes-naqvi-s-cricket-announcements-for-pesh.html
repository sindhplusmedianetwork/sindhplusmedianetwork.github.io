Samar Bilour Welcomes Naqvi's Cricket Announcements for Peshawar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa information adviser and MNA Samar Haroon Bilour has welcomed Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's cricket-related announcements during his visit to Peshawar. The announcements include the restoration of the historic name of Arbab Niaz Cricket Stadium. Bilour said the stadium is a symbol of Peshawar's cricket heritage.

She said that in the past 13 years, attention had gone to changing its name and putting up plaques rather than completing its construction. She added that matches between the three national teams would give young cricketers valuable learning opportunities. She said the matches would also help bring the province's cricket talent to the fore.
