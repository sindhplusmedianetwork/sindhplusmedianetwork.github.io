Russell Brand jailed for 10-and-a-half years over rape and sexual assault

Comedian Russell Brand was jailed for 10-and-a-half years on Monday after he was convicted of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three more around two decades ago. The 51-year-old was convicted on Thursday of the offences committed between 2004 and 2009, and he had denied all the charges. Judge Joel Bennathan sentenced him at London's Southwark Crown Court to 10 years and six months, saying he would serve two-thirds in custody before being released on licence.

The judge told Brand he was a sexual predator who believed fame meant he could act how he liked, unhindered by the rules by which decent people live. Brand mouthed 'I love you' to his wife before he was led away by dock officers and down to the cells. One of his victims, whom Brand raped in 2009, said in a statement read by prosecutors that the trauma of the attack was something she carries with her every day and that it often feels never-ending.

Brand was acquitted of two other rape charges and an attempted indecent assault, while London's Metropolitan Police said he is under investigation for two other alleged rapes.
