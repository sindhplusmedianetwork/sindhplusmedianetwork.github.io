Pakistan opt to bowl against Sri Lanka in ODI tri-series final at Gaddafi Stadium

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan won the toss and elected to bowl first against Sri Lanka in the ODI tri-series final at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium on Saturday, aiming for a maiden tri-series title on home soil. Skipper Shaheen Afridi said the call was shaped by the pattern of the tournament, noting: "We opted to field first as we had in mind that Sri Lanka batted second in the last four games." Pakistan made one change to its playing eleven, bringing in all-rounder Faheem Ashraf for Shadab Khan, who was ruled out on medical grounds after being struck by a bouncer in the fifth ODI against Sri Lanka. Sri Lanka, for whom Kusal Mendis had won every toss until now, replaced Eshan Malinga with off-spinner Maheesh Theekshana.

The hosts came into the final looking to answer the eight-wicket defeat Sri Lanka had inflicted on them in their previous meeting, when Pakistan were bowled out for just 110. Sri Lanka themselves arrived after a league-stage defeat to England, setting up a final between two sides with recent wounds to avenge.
