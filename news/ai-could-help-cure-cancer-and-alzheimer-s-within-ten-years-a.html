AI could help cure cancer and Alzheimer's within ten years, Anthropic chief predicts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Anthropic chief executive Dario Amodei has expressed hope that artificial intelligence could help make major progress against cancer, Alzheimer's, heart disease and mental illness in less than ten years. He believes AI can speed up medical research and the development of new medicines at an extraordinary pace, opening the way to treatments for diseases that have long been under research. Google DeepMind chief Demis Hassabis and businessman Elon Musk have voiced similar hopes, with Hassabis saying AI could shrink a drug-development process that usually takes ten years to a matter of weeks, citing Google's AlphaFold system, which predicted and mapped more than 200 million protein structures.

Medical experts and some pharmaceutical chiefs, however, consider such predictions far from reality. Vertex Pharmaceuticals chairman Jeff Leiden said his company had tried 50 AI systems and reviewed 10,000 drug compounds through them, but none of the systems had succeeded on its own in producing an effective medicine. Experts say identifying a possible drug by computer is only the first stage of research, after which laboratory testing, animal trials, human clinical trials in several phases and approval from the authorities are all necessary.

The full process of developing and approving a medicine normally takes 10 to 12 years, and experts say it cannot be eliminated entirely simply by using AI. They also note that, by 2026, no AI-developed drug had received full medical approval and come into wide use, and that past grand predictions about ending major diseases such as cancer within a set period had also failed to materialise.
