US envoys to meet Putin and Zelensky after Abu Dhabi talks on Ukraine war

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner will separately meet Russia's Vladimir Putin in Moscow and Ukraine's Volodymyr Zelensky in Dublin on Thursday, after two days of US-hosted talks in Abu Dhabi on ending the Ukraine war. The envoys will travel first to Moscow to meet the Russian president before flying to Dublin, where Zelensky will attend European meetings, to debrief the Ukrainian leader. The sides agreed in Abu Dhabi to a major prisoner exchange, described by Kushner as the biggest ever, though no side gave any figures, and all sides said talks would continue.

The talks were the first since February, when the US war in Iran halted diplomacy, but they also revealed deep splits over the role Europe should play in future talks. Russia's delegation chief Vladimir Medinsky questioned including Europe, saying Moscow had not received a single peace initiative or concrete proposal from European countries, and later told Russian television that Moscow's aim was a 'new, modern Yalta and Potsdam'. Ukraine has warned against a deal that carves up the continent and has called for more involvement from its key European backers, while Kushner said seeking ways to get the Europeans added to the format would be on the agenda with Putin.

Hundreds of thousands of people have been killed since Russia invaded in February 2022, and Russia is demanding Ukraine withdraw from parts of eastern Ukraine it still controls, while Kyiv says a ceasefire must come before any discussions on territory and has called for a direct meeting between Zelensky and Putin, which Putin has ruled out.
