SME Lending Jumps 33% to Rs943 Billion in Second Quarter of 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Financing for small and medium enterprises in Pakistan has recorded a significant increase, according to State Bank of Pakistan data reported by a private TV channel. Loans extended to SMEs reached Rs943 billion in the second quarter of 2026, which is 33 percent higher than the previous year. The State Bank has set a financing target of Rs1.5 trillion to further increase lending to small and medium businesses.

The development is being described as important in the context of efforts to meet the financial needs of the business sector. It is also seen as a step toward expanding credit access for small and medium-sized enterprises across the country.
