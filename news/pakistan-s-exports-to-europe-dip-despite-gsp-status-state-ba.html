Pakistan's Exports to Europe Dip Despite GSP+ Status, State Bank Data Shows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's exports to European markets fell in the first quarter of 2026-27 despite the country's GSP+ status, State Bank data shows. Exports to European countries contracted by 1.84 percent to $2.350 billion in July-September, down from $2.394 billion in the same period a year ago. Trade analysts warn that the Middle East conflict and the European Union's preferential market access for India, a key textiles competitor, are weighing on demand.

Under GSP+, Pakistani goods enter the European Union largely duty-free, a scheme meant to boost exports. The figures show that even preferential access has not been enough to lift sales to Europe this year.
