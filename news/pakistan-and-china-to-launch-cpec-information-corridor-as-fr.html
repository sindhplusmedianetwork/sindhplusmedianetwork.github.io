Pakistan and China to Launch CPEC Information Corridor as Friendship Turns 75

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and China will launch the China-Pakistan Information Corridor under the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor on Monday, as celebrations mark 75 years of diplomatic friendship between the two countries. Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning has arrived in Pakistan with a high-level delegation, and was received at the airport by Chinese Ambassador Jiang Zaidong, embassy staff and Pakistani officials. Mao Ning was due to inaugurate the corridor on Monday evening at a ceremony at the Pakistan-China Friendship Centre in Islamabad, where she was to attend as chief guest.

The delegation accompanying her includes officials from China's information and foreign ministries, according to diplomatic sources. The corridor aims to strengthen Pakistan-China cooperation in media, strategic communication, information exchange, public diplomacy and cultural collaboration. Mao Ning was also due to visit Pakistan's Foreign Ministry, meet Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, and hold consultations with Foreign Office spokesperson Shajjad Haider Khan, while another Chinese delegation's visit to Pakistan was also being finalised.
