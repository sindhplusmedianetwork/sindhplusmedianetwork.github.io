Gold price plunges Rs7,000 per tola, falls below Rs425,000

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

KARACHI: Gold prices recorded a sharp fall on Friday, slipping below Rs425,000 per tola. According to the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association, the price of gold dropped by Rs7,000 per tola to close at Rs424,536. The rate for ten grams fell by Rs6,001 to Rs363,971.

The decline followed weakness in international markets, where the price slid by $70 to $4,020 per ounce.
