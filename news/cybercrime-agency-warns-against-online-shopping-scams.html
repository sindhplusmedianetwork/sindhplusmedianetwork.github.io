Cybercrime Agency Warns Against Online Shopping Scams

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has issued an advisory urging citizens to remain vigilant against online shopping scams, including fake websites, fraudulent social media stores and deceptive promotional offers. According to the agency, fraudsters operating under the guise of online shopping use fake stores and misleading discounts to fleece customers and steal their personal information. The NCCIA advised consumers to check sellers' credibility and customer reviews before placing orders, shop only through authentic and reliable websites, and exercise particular caution when unknown sellers demand advance payments.

The agency said shoppers should treat unusually cheap offers with suspicion and never share bank details or one-time passwords with strangers. It urged anyone who falls victim to such fraud to report it to the agency without delay.
