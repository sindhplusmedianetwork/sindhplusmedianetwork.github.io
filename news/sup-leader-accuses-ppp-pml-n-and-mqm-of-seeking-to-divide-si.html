SUP leader accuses PPP, PML-N and MQM of seeking to divide Sindh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh United Party central leader Syed Zain Shah has accused the PPP, the PML-N and the MQM-P of once again trying to divide Sindh. He made the remarks while addressing a Unity of Sindh rally in Kashmore district on Thursday. Shah alleged that the three parties were trying to introduce new administrative units in the province.

He also accused them of seeking a dual local government system in Sindh. The SUP leader said a public movement last year had forced the rejection of proposed canals. He urged people across Sindh to hold peaceful protests along the Indus Highway.

He described the moves as conspiracies against the province and called on the public to resist them.
