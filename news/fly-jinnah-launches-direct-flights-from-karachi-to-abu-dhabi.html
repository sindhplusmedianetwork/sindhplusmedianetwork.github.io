Fly Jinnah Launches Direct Flights From Karachi to Abu Dhabi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fly Jinnah has launched direct flights from Karachi to Abu Dhabi, with four flights a week on the route. The first flight departed carrying 112 passengers. A ceremony was held at Karachi airport before the departure of the airline's first private flight, in which the airport manager cut a cake and inaugurated the service.

The airline's airport manager and representatives of the airports authority attended the inaugural ceremony. The inaugural flight to Abu Dhabi was operated by an Airbus 320 aircraft. The flight departed on schedule, and the airline maintained its record of on-time departures.

Four flights a week will be operated from Karachi to Abu Dhabi. Abu Dhabi is the seventh international route on which the airline is starting operations, having previously operated flights to Jeddah, Riyadh, Dammam, Bahrain, Dubai and Sharjah.
