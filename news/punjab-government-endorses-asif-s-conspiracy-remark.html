Punjab government endorses Asif's 'conspiracy' remark

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has endorsed Defence Minister Khawaja Asif's suggestion that an interview by Federal Minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain may be part of a broader conspiracy against the Punjab government. Salik had said on November 2 that complaints about corruption in Punjab were commonplace and a disproportionate share of the development budget was being spent in Lahore. Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz served Salik a Rs5 billion defamation notice on November 3.

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bukhari said the government was aware of attempts to create an alternative political arrangement. The Punjab government has now backed Asif's view that the interview may have been part of a wider conspiracy.
