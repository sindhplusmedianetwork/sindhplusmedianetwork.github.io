Russia Frees American Stephen Hubbard After 4 Years on Trump's Request

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russia has freed Stephen Hubbard, a 74-year-old retired teacher from Michigan who had been imprisoned since 2022 on charges of fighting as a mercenary in Ukraine. President Trump announced the release, saying Hubbard is 'on his way back home'. Putin ordered the release during a meeting with Trump's envoy Steve Witkoff in St.

Petersburg. Trump said the United States gave Russia nothing in return for the release. Hubbard had been designated by the US State Department as wrongfully detained.

He was arrested in Izyum in April 2022 and sentenced in 2024 to nearly seven years in prison.
