Security forces foil planned attack on Dalbandin Airport, kill two militants

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces in Balochistan have foiled a planned attack on Dalbandin Airport and killed two suspected militants in a late-night intelligence-based operation in Chagai district. The operation was carried out on 14 November by a specialised Frontier Corps team from the Dalbandin Rifles near Dhadhar Nullah, along the Quetta-Taftan National Highway (N-40). According to security sources, the two armed suspects were confronted near Dhadhar Landi while allegedly conducting reconnaissance ahead of planned attacks on the airport, a rest facility for pilgrims travelling between Pakistan and Iran, and a staging area used by commercial cargo trucks.

The suspects allegedly opened fire on security personnel and tried to escape into the darkness through a seasonal watercourse, but two reserve teams blocked the surrounding escape routes and trapped them inside the nullah. Both suspects were killed in the exchange of gunfire that followed. A search of the area led to the recovery of two submachine guns, seven magazines carrying 210 rounds of ammunition, a motorcycle, a blanket and motorcycle repair equipment.

Intelligence agencies had been monitoring suspicious activity in the Dalbandin area after receiving information about a possible operation involving Fitna al-Hindustan, and reports say the banned Balochistan Liberation Army had planned the attacks. Security officials insist the airport itself was never directly attacked, and say the suspects were intercepted before they could reach any of their intended targets.
