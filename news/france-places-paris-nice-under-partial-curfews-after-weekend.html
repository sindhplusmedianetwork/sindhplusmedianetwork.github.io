France Places Paris, Nice Under Partial Curfews After Weekend Riots

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French authorities imposed curfews in parts of several cities on Monday — including Paris, Lille and Nice — after a weekend of riots and looting stemming from nationwide anti-government protests, with the prime minister blaming the 'ultra-left' for exploiting the unrest. Paris police barred protests in certain areas from 8pm and imposed an 11pm to 6am curfew around Place de la Republique and Les Halles, while Marseille banned gatherings. Unions called for new strikes on November 5 as the unrest continued to spread.

The Interior Ministry said some 2,400 people were arrested and one protester died of cardiac arrest on Saturday, while nearly 1,000 officers were injured and almost 3,000 shops were affected. The government has vowed to restore order, deploying additional security forces to the hardest-hit districts.
