OGRA Notifies Up to 15.27% Hike in RLNG Prices for October

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) has notified an increase of up to 15.27 per cent in RLNG prices for October compared with September. According to the notification, the price on the Sui Northern Gas Pipelines (SNGPL) system has risen by $2.13 per million British thermal units, taking the new rate to $17.32 per MMBtu. On the Sui Southern Gas Company (SSGC) system, the price has gone up by $2.17 per MMBtu to $16.39.

OGRA attributed the revision mainly to higher global crude oil prices. The increase follows a 20 per cent reduction in RLNG prices recorded in September. OGRA revises LNG rates every month in line with international energy prices and import costs.

The hike is expected to raise fuel costs for power generation in December. RLNG-based power generation cost about Rs40 per unit in September.
