Jet Fuel, Kerosene and Light Diesel Prices Cut Further

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The prices of jet fuel, kerosene oil and light diesel oil have been reduced further, with the reduction effective from today, November 28. Jet fuel has become cheaper by 18 rupees 21 paisa, kerosene oil by 15 rupees 55 paisa and light diesel oil by 13 rupees 1 paisa per litre. The price of jet fuel has fallen to 269 rupees 50 paisa per litre, whereas earlier the price of jet fuel was fixed at 287 rupees 71 paisa per litre.

According to OGRA, the new price of kerosene oil has become 257 rupees 67 paisa per litre, whereas earlier it was 273 rupees 22 paisa. According to PSO, the new price of light diesel oil has become 207 rupees 27 paisa per litre, whereas earlier it was 220 rupees 28 paisa per litre.
