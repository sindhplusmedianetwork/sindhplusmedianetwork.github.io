Google's parent Alphabet sheds over $500 billion in a day as AI spending spooks investors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Alphabet, the parent company of Google, lost more than $500 billion in market value in a single day after investors reacted with concern to its heavy spending on artificial intelligence. The company's shares fell nearly 13 percent to their lowest level in six months after it released quarterly results on Thursday. Revenue rose 25 percent from last year to $127.6 billion, and Google Cloud revenue jumped 103 percent to nearly $31 billion, but profit fell 25 percent and free cash flow was negative by more than $9 billion.

A major worry for investors is the use of AI-generated summaries in Google Search, which could reduce sales of search advertising, the core of Alphabet's business. By Thursday's close, Alphabet's market value had dropped from about $3.97 trillion to $3.46 trillion. Amazon's shares also fell about 6 percent after its results, with sales up 11 percent to $201 billion but free cash flow negative $42 billion.

Microsoft shares slipped less than one percent as revenue grew 19 percent to $92.3 billion, and its chief executive said daily users of the Copilot AI tool had risen 500 percent over the past year. Analysts say investors now want to see how heavy AI investment will translate into durable profit.
