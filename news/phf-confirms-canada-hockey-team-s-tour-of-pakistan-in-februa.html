PHF Confirms Canada Hockey Team's Tour of Pakistan in February 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Hockey Federation has officially confirmed that Canada's national hockey team will tour Pakistan in February 2027. PHF president Mohiuddin Wani announced the confirmation, describing the tour as an important step toward the revival of international hockey in Pakistan. He said exemplary arrangements would be made for the visiting side.

The announcement is being seen as a boost for the sport in the country, where the federation has been working to bring foreign teams back to Pakistani grounds. Further details of the tour programme, including venues and fixtures, are expected to be announced closer to the visit.
