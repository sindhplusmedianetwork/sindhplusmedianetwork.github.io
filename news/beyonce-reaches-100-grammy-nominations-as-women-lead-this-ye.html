Beyoncé reaches 100 Grammy nominations as women lead this year's honours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Beyoncé, who seemingly makes Grammy history every year, has done it again by becoming the first artist to earn 100 nominations. Her latest came in the R&B categories, for her archival release 'Morning Dew (Donk)'. It has been a year in which women have made their mark on music's biggest night, with soulful British singer-songwriter Raye topping the nominations with 11, including the three most prestigious honours for Best Album, Best Song and Best Record, and Spanish superstar Rosalía earning nine.

Rosalía's nine nominations followed a big night at last week's Latin Grammys, where her sweeping avant-garde classical pop album 'Lux' reigned supreme, and an Album of the Year win for 'Lux' would mark the second consecutive win in the category for a Spanish-language album. First-time nominee Ella Langley, the singer behind the hit 'Choosin' Texas', will enter the evening with seven nominations, as will fellow country star Miranda Lambert, who co-wrote the song and also picked up a nomination for Producer of the Year. Pop-rock star Olivia Rodrigo and musician Phoebe Bridgers also earned seven nods each.

The night will also feature strong first-time nominees such as New York indie rockers Geese, who earned five nominations in a breakout year, alongside veterans including Madonna, Paul McCartney, Bruce Springsteen and The Rolling Stones. The 69th annual Grammy gala will take place on February 7 in Los Angeles, featuring 100 competitive categories, and will air on the US network ABC and stream on Disney+ and Hulu, the first time in half a century that the ceremony will not be broadcast by CBS.
