K-P Orders Safety Audit of All School Buildings After Roof Collapse

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa government on Thursday ordered the Communication and Works Department to constitute technical teams to inspect all government school buildings across the province, following the deadly collapse of a classroom roof at a private school in Peshawar. The roof collapse at the Al-Khair Education System in Dir Colony claimed precious lives and left students and teachers injured, raising fears about the structural safety of educational institutions. In a letter issued on October 15, the Elementary and Secondary Education Department asked the department to nominate qualified teams for every district to assess school buildings in coordination with district education authorities.

The teams will examine roofs, ceilings, walls, beams, foundations and staircases to identify defects, maintenance issues and potential safety hazards. Inspected buildings will be classified into three categories: safe, requiring repairs or improvement, and potentially unsafe buildings needing urgent technical assessment. Buildings found to pose an immediate threat to students and staff must be reported at once, with precautions such as restricting access or suspending their use where necessary.

The teams have been directed to submit comprehensive district-wise reports, along with a safety clearance certificate for each school, by October 26.
