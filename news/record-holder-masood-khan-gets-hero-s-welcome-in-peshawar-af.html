Record-Holder Masood Khan Gets Hero's Welcome in Peshawar After 1,850km Peshawar-to-Karachi Run

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Record-holder athlete Masood Khan was given a warm welcome in Peshawar after completing his 1,850-kilometre run from Peshawar to Karachi in 24 days. Speaking on the occasion, the DG Sports said Masood Khan had brought pride not only to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa but to all of Pakistan through his extraordinary hard work, resolve and courage. He said covering such a long distance in 24 days was a remarkable achievement and a guiding light for the young generation.

The DG Sports also announced Rs500,000 for Masood Khan on behalf of the sports department and said a marathon race would be organised on the BRT route in November along with other sporting events. Additional Deputy Commissioner Peshawar Daud Khan said Masood's passion, courage and perseverance were a source of pride. Masood Khan said he tried to run 70 to 85 kilometres daily and had trained for about eight hours a day for 90 days for the feat.

He added that he has so far won 49 medals in full and half marathons across Pakistan, including 24 gold medals, and had earlier run the nearly 808-kilometre distance from Peshawar to Skardu in nine days.
