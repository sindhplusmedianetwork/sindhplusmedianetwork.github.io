After midterm defeat, Trump faces three options on Iran, report says

After the Republican Party's defeat in the midterm elections, US President Donald Trump faces three options on Iran: major military operations, negotiations, or a naval blockade and economic pressure. According to American intelligence assessments, military strikes may fail to change Iran's position or bring down its government. Continuing the war has become politically costly for Trump after the midterm defeat, while ending the war has become a strategically difficult decision.

The three paths before the president are a large-scale military operation, a negotiated settlement, or sustained naval blockade and economic pressure. The report presents the three routes as the options now open to the president after the election setback.
