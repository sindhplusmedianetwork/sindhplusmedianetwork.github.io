President, PM Call for Iqbal-Inspired National Renewal on Poet's 149th Birth Anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Sunday called for renewed engagement with Allama Muhammad Iqbal's intellectual and philosophical legacy, stressing his ideals of selfhood, equality, social justice, national dignity and intellectual awakening as essential to Pakistan's future. In their separate messages ahead of the national poet's 149th birth anniversary, being observed on November 9, President Zardari urged young people to draw inspiration from Iqbal's poetry to develop critical thinking and a spirit of inquiry, while emphasising his vision of an egalitarian society founded on the dignity of labour and equal opportunities. Meanwhile, Prime Minister Shehbaz announced the launch of year-long national and international commemorative activities, beginning on November 8 and culminating on November 9 next year, to promote Iqbal's intellectual legacy through academic, literary and cultural engagements.

In his message, President Zardari said, "On the 149th birth anniversary of Dr Allama Muhammad Iqbal, I pay tribute to our national poet, whose words awakened people to their dignity, their rights and their power to shape their own destiny." The prime minister said a comprehensive programme of national and international events was being formally launched, featuring international conferences, academic and research seminars, literary festivals, exhibitions, educational competitions and cultural activities. He said the government was firmly committed to ensuring that Iqbal's thought remains a dynamic and integral part of educational institutions, national discourse and development priorities.
