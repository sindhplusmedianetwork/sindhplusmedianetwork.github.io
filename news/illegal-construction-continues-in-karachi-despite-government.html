Illegal construction continues in Karachi despite government claims, over 5,000 buildings built in months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Illegal construction in Karachi has continued despite the Sindh government's claims, with more than 5,000 buildings put up in violation of the rules in just a few months, according to a report. Sources say the construction is going on openly because of alleged collusion by Sindh Building Control Authority officers, a so-called system and bribery, and even the authority's acting director general has not been able to stop it. The portion mafia remains active, turning residential houses into flats, and many areas of the city are becoming densely populated, while the government's action against illegal construction has so far been limited to claims.

According to the authority's own figures, more than 5,000 citizens filed written complaints about illegal construction in the past six months, although the actual number is said to be many times higher. District East recorded the most complaints, with 1,880 locations identified, followed by District Central with 1,432, District South with 638 and Keamari with 25, while hundreds of cases were reported in Malir and Korangi. Sources say thousands of illegal constructions are taking place that have not even been reported, all allegedly under the patronage of authority officers, and complaints have also grown that citizens are being asked for money under the name of the system even after their building plans are approved.

The authority's spokesperson said the acting director general, Dr Waseem Shamshad, has taken notice and directed the officers concerned to take immediate action against illegal construction and submit a progress report on Friday, with a meeting also called on Friday. Sources, however, say the director general has taken no action against illegal construction throughout the year, which is worrying as civic problems in the city keep growing.
