Pakistani Soldiers' Sacrifices Are Part of British History, Says Major General Harris

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

British Army Major General Carl Harris has said the sacrifices of Pakistani soldiers are an important part of British history, paying tribute to Muslim soldiers from the subcontinent, especially present-day Pakistan, in the First and Second World Wars. He was speaking at an Armed Forces Covenant signing ceremony on Armistice Day. Dr Tariq Sheikh, head of Tariq Halal Meats, signed for his company while Harris signed for the UK Ministry of Defence.

The covenant aims to give serving and former armed forces personnel and their families equal opportunities in employment and social facilities. Harris said the British Army belongs to all communities in the country, not only those in uniform.
