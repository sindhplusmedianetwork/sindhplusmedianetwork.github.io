Aima Baig Revisits 'Sajna Door' With Spontaneous Rendition for Fans

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Singer Aima Baig has revisited her much-loved song 'Sajna Door', sharing a spontaneous rendition on Instagram to thank fans for keeping the track popular years after its release. With no instrument immediately available, Baig recorded a short version and posted it with a caption explaining she wanted to show her appreciation to the listeners who had kept the song trending. "Since I couldn't think of any other way to thank those who made this track trending even after years, and had no immediate access to an instrument atm — thought it be nice to share this version of 'Sajna Door'," she wrote, calling the recording "a quick and short video" and playfully apologising for the metronome audible in it: "P.s excuse the metronome.

iykyk." She also hinted at a possible live session, promising to go live soon so she and her fans could sing the song together. Originally released as part of the soundtrack of the 2018 Pakistani romantic action-comedy 'Teefa in Trouble', 'Sajna Door' features vocals by Baig and singer-songwriter Ali Zafar, and stood out for its mellow romantic sound and blend of Urdu, Punjabi and English lyrics. Its music video, directed by Ahsan Rahim, featured Zafar and Maya Ali in a dreamy romantic sequence, including scenes of the on-screen couple dancing on water.

With her latest rendition, Baig has brought the song back into the spotlight while acknowledging the listeners who embraced it — and the singer is also rumoured to be preparing her debut album, 'Guriya', which is said to be a tribute to her late mother.
