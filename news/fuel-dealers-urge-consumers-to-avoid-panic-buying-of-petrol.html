Fuel Dealers Urge Consumers to Avoid Panic Buying of Petrol

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Petroleum Dealers Association Chairman Malik Khuda Baksh has urged consumers to avoid panic buying of petrol. He warned that excessive purchases create artificial shortages in the market. According to him, all stakeholders have maintained the balance between supply and demand, and the situation is under control.

He acknowledged, however, that pricing remains an issue for consumers. Separately, talks between Jamaat-e-Islami and the government on reducing the petroleum levy remained inconclusive. JI has asked that its proposed meeting with Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif take place "this week".

The party has been pressing the government to cut the levy imposed on petroleum products.
