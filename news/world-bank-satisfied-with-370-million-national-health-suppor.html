World Bank 'Satisfied' With $370 Million National Health Support Programme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Bank has rated the implementation progress of its $370 million National Health Support Programme in Punjab, Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa as 'satisfactory', with a 'moderate' overall risk rating, and expects the programme to meet its targets before the December 31, 2027 deadline. Effective since October 2022, the programme aims to advance universal health coverage by strengthening equitable access to and the quality of essential healthcare services at the primary level. The World Bank provided approximately $259 million in financing, while the remaining funds came through grants and co-financing from the Global Financing Facility, the Gates Foundation, the Global Fund and Gavi.

In its public disclosure, the lender said the programme 'has sustained strong implementation momentum and continues to deliver tangible results across provinces', adding that approximately 76 per cent of total programme funding had been disbursed. The World Bank has already disbursed $205 million, or 79.3 per cent, of its $259 million financing, while a $36.8 million co-financing facility has been completely disbursed. The programme uses a Program-for-Results financing instrument, the first of its kind in Pakistan's health sector, under which funding is linked to the achievement of agreed results.

According to the World Bank, the programme's improved health facility readiness score has reached 75 per cent in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, 80 per cent in Punjab and 77.5 per cent in Sindh.
