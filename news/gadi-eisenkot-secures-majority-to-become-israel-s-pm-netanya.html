Gadi Eisenkot secures majority to become Israel's PM, Netanyahu at risk

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yesh Atid leader Gadi Eisenkot has secured the backing of a majority of Knesset members to become Israel's next prime minister, in a fresh development that increases the risk for Benjamin Netanyahu. Israeli President Isaac Herzog has consulted the parties on government formation, and representatives of factions totalling 67 MPs backed Eisenkot, while factions totalling 46 MPs recommended Netanyahu. Herzog said he will hold further talks with party leaders before handing out the mandate to form a government on Sunday, to clarify the situation.

Netanyahu's chances have weakened further as his allied religious party United Torah Judaism refused to back any candidate for prime minister. The party, which holds seven seats, did not recommend any candidate during its consultations with the president, and its religious leaders also directed that no candidate be supported. The development follows an election in which Netanyahu's camp had secured 53 seats, leaving him short of a governing majority.

Eisenkot now stands as the leading contender to form the next Israeli government.
