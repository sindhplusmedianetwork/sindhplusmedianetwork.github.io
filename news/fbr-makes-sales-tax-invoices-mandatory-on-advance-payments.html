FBR makes sales tax invoices mandatory on advance payments

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue has made sales tax invoices mandatory for registered taxpayers on advance payments received against future supplies. The new procedures, notified through SRO2063 of 2026, also require taxpayers to declare output tax in the return for the tax period in which the payments are received. Under the new rules, taxpayers receiving multiple advance payments against a single supply will be required to issue a separate invoice for each payment and electronically link it with earlier invoices to ensure complete traceability of the transaction.

Taxpayers will be required to issue a final sales tax invoice when the actual supply is made, with references to earlier advance payment invoices, but the value and sales tax already accounted for on advance payments will not be counted again. Sales tax on any outstanding amount will be calculated only on the remaining value of the supply. The board has clarified that advance payments will be subject to the sales tax rate prevailing at the time of receipt, while any remaining amount will be taxed at the rate applicable when the actual supply is made.

The amended rules also provide for adjustments where advance payments or the value of supplies are increased, reduced or refunded, or where supplies are cancelled wholly or partly, in accordance with Section 9 of the Sales Tax Act. The revised procedure establishes a mechanism to account for sales tax when advance payments are received, while ensuring the same amount is not taxed again when the final supply is made.
