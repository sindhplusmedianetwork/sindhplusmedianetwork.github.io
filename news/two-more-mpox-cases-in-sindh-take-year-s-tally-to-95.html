Two More Mpox Cases in Sindh Take Year's Tally to 95

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two more cases of mpox have been reported in Sindh, taking the province's tally for 2026 to 95. Health officials confirmed that 11 people have died of the virus in the province so far this year. The two new patients are 21-year-old Salman Ali and 23-year-old Ishrat Fatima.

Their infections were confirmed through reports from the provincial public health laboratory at Dow University. Both patients are under treatment at the Sindh Infectious Diseases Hospital. Officials said surveillance and contact tracing around the new cases are under way.
