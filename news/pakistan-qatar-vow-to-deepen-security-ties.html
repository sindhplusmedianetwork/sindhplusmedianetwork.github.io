Pakistan, Qatar Vow to Deepen Security Ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Qatar on Thursday reaffirmed their commitment to further strengthening bilateral relations, particularly in the fields of security, counterterrorism and institutional cooperation. The development came as Qatar's Interior Minister Sheikh Khalifa bin Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani called on Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir in Rawalpindi, according to a statement issued by the ISPR. During the meeting, matters of mutual interest, the evolving regional and global security situation and avenues for enhancing bilateral security cooperation were discussed, the statement said.

Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to further strengthening bilateral relations between the two countries, particularly in the fields of security, counterterrorism and institutional cooperation, it added. The visiting dignitary appreciated Pakistan's role in promoting regional peace and stability and acknowledged the longstanding fraternal ties between Pakistan and Qatar. The COAS reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to further deepen bilateral engagement with Qatar and underscored the importance of close cooperation in addressing shared security challenges.

Pakistan and Qatar signed a memorandum of understanding on security cooperation between Qatar's Internal Security Force (Lekhwiya) and Pakistan's Federal Constabulary, along with an action plan to prevent drug trafficking.
