Pakistan 'unequivocally' condemns Houthi attack on Riyadh's tallest skyscraper

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan on Friday unequivocally condemned a Houthi attack on Riyadh's tallest skyscraper, which serves as the headquarters of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund. The Foreign Office said in a statement condemning Thursday's attack, without naming the tower, that this act of aggression constitutes a grave violation of the sovereignty of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Saudi state-run news agency SPA reported, citing the kingdom's civil defence, that the attack caused material damage to several floors of the building and nearby buildings, but no injuries were reported.

The SPA statement did not identify the tower, but an accompanying photograph published by the agency showed the Public Investment Fund Tower. The strike blew out windows across seven to eight floors of PIF Tower, bending parts of its metal facade, and it was the second time in less than two weeks that a Houthi attack damaged a landmark in Riyadh's financial district, with state oil company Aramco's building hit in a November 8 attack. In its statement, the Foreign Office said the continued targeting of civilian infrastructure and economic facilities endangers innocent lives and poses a serious threat to regional peace and security, and that such reprehensible attacks must cease forthwith.

It added that Pakistan stood firmly with the government and people of Saudi Arabia and supports the kingdom's legitimate right to defend its sovereignty and territorial integrity.
