Pakistani High Commissioner Tipu Usman Meets King Charles III, Presents Credentials

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's High Commissioner to the UK, Tipu Usman, presented his credentials to British King Charles III during a special ceremony held at St James's Palace in London, and his wife Saima Tipu was also with him on the occasion. In accordance with diplomatic protocol, the High Commissioner and his wife were brought by royal carriage from Lancaster House to St James's Palace under the leadership of the Vice Marshal of the Diplomatic Corps. During the meeting with King Charles III, Tipu Usman conveyed good wishes and goodwill messages from President Asif Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif.

He said the people of Pakistan hold King Charles III in great respect and the pleasant memories of the British King's previous visit to Pakistan are still fresh. The High Commissioner expressed resolve to further strengthen the longstanding friendly relations between Pakistan and the UK and the desire to increase bilateral cooperation in various fields including trade, investment, cultural cooperation and people-to-people contacts. On the occasion, King Charles III welcomed Tipu Usman and his wife and expressed good wishes for the Pakistani leadership and people.
