Petrol price cut by Rs6.59, diesel by Rs4.25 for four days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Friday cut the price of petrol by Rs6.59 per litre and high-speed diesel by Rs4.25 per litre for the next four days. Under a notification issued by the Petroleum Division, petrol will sell at Rs346 per litre and diesel at Rs378 per litre from November 7 to November 10. The cut takes petrol below Rs350 per litre for the first time since September 7, when the government had raised the rate by Rs12.90 to Rs358.77 from Rs345.87.

It comes a day after the government reduced petrol by Rs9.41 and diesel by Rs5.56 for November 6. The government had announced on November 2 that oil refineries would bear a burden of Rs15 billion a month so petrol could be sold Rs15 cheaper than in the global market, as renewed tensions between the United States and Iran drove volatility in oil markets. Since July 17, fuel prices have been reviewed and notified daily under a new pricing mechanism that replaced the weekly system.
