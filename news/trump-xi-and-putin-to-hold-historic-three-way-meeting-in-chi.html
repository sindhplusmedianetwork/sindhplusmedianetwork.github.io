Trump, Xi and Putin to Hold Historic Three-Way Meeting in China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping will hold the first three-way meeting between the leaders of the United States, Russia and China at a summit in Shenzhen next week, the White House said on Friday. The three leaders will meet on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit, White House spokeswoman Olivia Wales said. Trump wants to talk about reducing nuclear arsenals at the trilateral talks on Thursday.

He is also set to bring up the situation involving Iran during the meeting. The White House said Trump is not expected to discuss Russia's war in Ukraine at the talks. The meeting will be the first time the leaders of the three countries have sat down together in this format.
