Driver and student injured as armed men open fire on students' bus in Charsadda

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A driver and a student were injured in Charsadda when armed men allegedly opened fire on a coaster carrying students of the Institute of Computer and Management Sciences (ICMS) College, with the incident taking place late on Friday night within the limits of the Nisatta police station. According to Station House Officer Nisatta Khan Zaib, two vehicles carrying around 70 students were returning from a recreational trip to Murree, travelling on GT Road after the motorway was closed, and had reached the Nisatta area when the firing occurred. The SHO said the firing stemmed from a dispute between two students during the trip, and a case had been registered against two nominated suspects while several people had been arrested.

According to the coaster driver, who is the complainant in the case, a minor altercation took place between students during the trip, and on the way back the suspects, along with their accomplices, allegedly intercepted the vehicle and opened fire. The firing left the driver and a student with bullet wounds, and they were taken to hospital, where the driver was later referred to Peshawar for further treatment. The complainant further alleged that the armed men had also tried to enter the vehicle but teachers standing near its door resisted them, preventing further harm to the students.

District Police Officer Rafatullah Khan took notice of the incident and ordered the arrest of the nominated suspects without delay, forming a special team headed by Deputy Superintendent of Police Sardheri Bilal Haleem Khan, and a case was registered under Sections 324, 427 and 34 of the Pakistan Penal Code. The presidents of the Private Colleges Association Charsadda and the Council of Private Schools Association Charsadda condemned the firing, demanded an impartial inquiry and the immediate arrest of all those involved, and warned that educational institutions in the district would be closed in protest if the suspects were not arrested.
