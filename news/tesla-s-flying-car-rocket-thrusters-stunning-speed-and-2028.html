Tesla's 'Flying Car': Rocket Thrusters, Stunning Speed and 2028 Production

WhatsApp Facebook X

Tesla has revealed new details of its long-awaited Roadster, described by its engineering chief as a car fitted with rocket thrusters. Lars Moravy said the Roadster can accelerate from zero to 60 miles per hour in remarkable time, putting it far ahead of conventional sports cars. The vehicle is equipped with rocket thrusters that allow it to perform dramatic manoeuvres, including mid-air flips.

Moravy confirmed that production of the Roadster is scheduled to begin in 2028. The car has been in development for years and remains one of Tesla's most ambitious projects.
