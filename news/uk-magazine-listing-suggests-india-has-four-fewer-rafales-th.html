UK Magazine Listing Suggests India Has Four Fewer Rafales Than Claimed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A British aviation magazine's latest directory lists 32 Rafale fighter jets in active service with the Indian Air Force, four fewer than the 36 aircraft New Delhi has officially said it operates. FlightGlobal's 2027 World Air Forces directory, published on 10 November, records the lower figure, while the IAF had documented earlier this year that all 36 Rafales acquired from France were in service. The directory is published annually and documents the military aircraft fleets of 161 countries.

India signed a deal in 2016 to buy 36 Rafale jets from France for around 8.7 billion dollars, its first major combat aircraft purchase in two decades. The new tally comes about 18 months after the military confrontation between Pakistan and India in May 2025, during which Pakistan claimed it shot down five Indian fighter jets, including three Rafales, on the night of 6-7 May. India has repeatedly denied losing aircraft in the brief conflict, although a senior French intelligence official told CNN days later that one Indian Rafale had been shot down by Pakistan.

In August 2025, US President Donald Trump said seven aircraft had been shot down during the conflict, and the magazine's listing has renewed attention on the gap between the two figures.
