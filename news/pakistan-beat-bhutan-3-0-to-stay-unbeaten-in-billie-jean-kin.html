Pakistan Beat Bhutan 3-0 to Stay Unbeaten in Billie Jean King Cup

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan maintained their winning run in the Billie Jean King Cup Asia/Oceania Group III tournament with a 3-0 victory over Bhutan in Islamabad on Thursday. Ushna Suhail and Mahin Aftab Qureshi won their singles rubbers 6-0, 6-0 each to put Pakistan in command. Debutants Romaisa Malik and Hajra Sohail then sealed the tie with a 6-1, 6-4 win in the doubles.

Pakistan will face the Maldives in their next fixture on Friday.
