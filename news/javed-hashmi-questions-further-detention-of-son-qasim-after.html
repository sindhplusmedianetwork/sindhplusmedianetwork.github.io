Javed Hashmi Questions Further Detention of Son Qasim After 11 Days in Custody

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Javed Hashmi has raised questions over the investigation into his son Qasim Hashmi, who has been in the custody of the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency for 11 days. Qasim was presented before the Multan District Court for remand, where police sought further remand, saying more data was still needed for the investigation. Hashmi said his son's mobile phone and all electronic devices from the house are also in police custody.

He told reporters that police informed the court that nothing has been recovered from Qasim or his phone so far. Hashmi questioned why further detention is needed if nothing could be proven after 11 days in custody. His remarks have put the focus on the pace and basis of the investigation against his son.
