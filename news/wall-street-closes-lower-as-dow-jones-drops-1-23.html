Wall Street Closes Lower as Dow Jones Drops 1.23%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Wall Street closed lower on Friday as major indexes retreated. The Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 658.82 points, or 1.23 per cent, to finish at 52,215.16. The S&P 500 fell 0.52 per cent to 7,623.86, while the Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.21 per cent to 25,955.34.

For the week, the S&P 500 lost 0.5 per cent and the Nasdaq fell 0.7 per cent. The Dow, however, gained 0.4 per cent for the week, marking its third straight weekly gain. Investors weighed corporate earnings and economic data as the trading week came to a close.
