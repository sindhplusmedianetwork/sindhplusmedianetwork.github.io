Anthropic unveils new Fable, Mythos models as competition heats up ahead of IPO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Anthropic on Monday unveiled two new artificial intelligence models, Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, as the OpenAI rival seeks to strengthen its position in the fast-growing AI market ahead of its planned initial public offering. The company said Claude Fable 5.2 was designed for coding, reasoning and AI agent tasks, while Claude Mythos 5.2 was developed for approved cybersecurity and life-sciences research applications. Access to Claude Mythos 5.2 will be restricted to organisations approved through Anthropic's verification programmes, while Claude Fable 5.2 will be available more broadly.

Anthropic said the new models could generate outputs up to 30 per cent faster than their Claude 5.1 predecessors and communicate more clearly with users. The models are priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, lower than the prices of previous Fable and Mythos releases. The launch follows Anthropic's introduction of Claude Haiku 5.5 in October.
