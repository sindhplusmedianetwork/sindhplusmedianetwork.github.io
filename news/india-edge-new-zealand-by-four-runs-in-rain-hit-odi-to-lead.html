India Edge New Zealand by Four Runs in Rain-Hit ODI to Lead 2-0

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India beat New Zealand by four runs under the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern method in the rain-affected second one-day international in Wellington on Saturday. The win gave India a 2-0 lead in the five-match series. New Zealand were bowled out for 134 in 41 overs, their lowest one-day total against India on home soil.

Gurnoor Brar took a career-best four wickets for 30 runs for India. Chasing 135, India were 113 for seven in 26 overs when rain stopped play for good. India were four runs ahead of the DLS par score when the match was called off.
