Peace Talks in Geneva Push to End Sudan War

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Diplomats from two dozen countries are gathered in Geneva in a new push to end the war in Sudan and ease one of the world's worst humanitarian crises. The talks, reported by UrduPoint citing UN sources, are considering how to make progress toward an inclusive political system in Sudan. UN envoy Pekka Haavisto urged delegates to use their influence to build trust between the warring sides and move toward a comprehensive ceasefire.

The fighting between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces has now entered its fourth year. Swiss State Secretary Alexandre Fasel said the conflict has become a drone war that is killing mostly civilians. According to the United Nations, about 34 million Sudanese people now need humanitarian aid.
