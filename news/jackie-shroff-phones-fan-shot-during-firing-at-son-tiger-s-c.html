Jackie Shroff Phones Fan Shot During Firing at Son Tiger's Car

Veteran Bollywood actor Jackie Shroff has reached out to Umesh Chauhan, the young fan who was shot in the chest during a firing incident near his son Tiger Shroff's car. The incident took place outside a football stadium in Juhu, Mumbai, where gunfire struck the fan amid the commotion. In a phone call, Jackie affectionately addressed Chauhan as 'beta' (son) and assured him he should ask for anything he might need.

The actor also promised to visit Chauhan at Mumbai's KEM Hospital once the situation normalizes. Chauhan underwent surgery on October 21 to remove the bullet lodged in his chest. According to the hospital administration, his condition is now stable.

The gesture has been warmly received by fans and the film fraternity.
