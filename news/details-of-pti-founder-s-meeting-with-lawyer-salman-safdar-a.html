Details of PTI founder's meeting with lawyer Salman Safdar at Adiala jail emerge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Details have emerged of the meeting between the PTI founder and his lawyer Salman Safdar inside Adiala jail. According to sources, the founder was informed during the meeting about the imposition of governor's rule and emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He reportedly did not react strongly to the news and advised the party leadership to keep its focus on his court cases and on his transfer to a hospital.

The meeting is the first direct interaction reported between the two in months, and its details shed light on his thinking as the political crisis around the province's administration deepened.
