Norwegian Princess's Team Dismisses AI-Faked Pregnancy Images

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Princess Märtha Louise of Norway is not expecting a baby with her husband Durek Verrett, despite viral images falsely suggesting the couple are about to welcome their first child together. The manipulated pictures, created with artificial intelligence, show the 55-year-old princess with an apparent baby bump and posing alongside Verrett while holding ultrasound scans. The fake images circulating across social media raised fans' concerns about the Norwegian royal's supposed pregnancy.

However, her representative has dismissed the claims, confirming that the photographs are fabricated. Carina Scheele Carlsen, Märtha Louise's longtime manager and spokesperson, told Norwegian broadcaster TV 2 that the royal household was aware of the misleading content and was taking the matter seriously. The fabricated posts keep appearing on newly created Facebook pages, including accounts using Norwegian and German names, and some have since been removed while Meta says it has launched a probe into the matter.

Experts have warned the posts could be part of a wider online scam, with University of Oslo professor Petter Bae Brandtzæg telling TV 2 that royal figures and pregnancy rumours can attract huge attention and encourage people to share misleading content. The incident follows another wave of AI-generated royal images involving Crown Princess Mette-Marit that circulated online in June, while Märtha Louise and Verrett tied the knot in a private ceremony in August 2024.
