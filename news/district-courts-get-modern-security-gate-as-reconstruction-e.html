District Courts get modern security gate as reconstruction enters final stage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The reconstruction and renovation of the main entrance gate of Rawalpindi's District Courts has entered its final stage, nearly a year after the historic 130-year-old gate was demolished to make way for the courts underpass. The old gate was demolished on November 3, 2025, at the start of the underpass project, and although the underpass and overhead bridge were completed around seven and a half months ago, the project committee initially refused to reconstruct the entrance gate. Following this, District Bar Association President Malik Sajid Mahmood Tarar Awan and Secretary Qamarul Haq Niazi held a special meeting with Rawalpindi Deputy Commissioner Nadeem Nasir, urging him to honour the earlier commitment to rebuild the gate.

The deputy commissioner accepted the bar's demand and initiated the reconstruction using his own funds. The rebuilt gate will serve as the modern security entrance to the district courts complex.
