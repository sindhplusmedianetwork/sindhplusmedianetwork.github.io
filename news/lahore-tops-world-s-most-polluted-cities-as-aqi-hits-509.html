Lahore tops world's most polluted cities as AQI hits 509

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore has once again topped the ranking of the world's most polluted cities, with its Air Quality Index touching the hazardous level of 509. According to IQAir data, New Delhi stood second with an AQI of 221 and Cairo third at 210. In Punjab, Gujranwala and Nankana Sahib recorded an AQI of 500 each, while the Punjab AQI figures placed Lahore at 449, Hafizabad at 386 and Wazirabad at 381.

Several Lahore localities, including Barki Road, Shahdara, Kahna Nau, Multan Road, DHA Phase 6, Punjab University, Safari Park, GT Road and Gulberg 3, recorded an AQI of 500. Experts warned of serious health risks, particularly for children, the elderly and people with respiratory illnesses, and advised citizens to avoid unnecessary outdoor activity and wear masks.
