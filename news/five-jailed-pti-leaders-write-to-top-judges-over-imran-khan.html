Five jailed PTI leaders write to top judges over Imran Khan's health

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five imprisoned PTI leaders have written to the country's top judges over the health and treatment of Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi. The leaders are Shah Mahmood Qureshi, Dr Yasmin Rashid, Umar Sarfraz Cheema, Ejaz Chaudhry and Mian Mahmoodur Rashid. They wrote to Chief Justice Yahya Afridi and Islamabad High Court Chief Justice Sarfraz Dogar.

The letter was sent through lawyer Rana Mudassar Umar. It said the Supreme Court's August 18, 2026 order to transfer Imran Khan to Shifa International Hospital had not been implemented. The leaders asked the courts to ensure compliance with orders allowing Imran Khan to meet his family, lawyers and sons, and called for impartial and prompt decisions in pending cases.
