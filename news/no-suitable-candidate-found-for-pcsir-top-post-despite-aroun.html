No Suitable Candidate Found for PCSIR Top Post Despite Around 100 Applications

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Despite receiving nearly 100 applications from prominent scientists through national advertisements, the Ministry of Science and Technology has failed to find a suitable candidate to head the Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research, and decided to re-employ the previous chairman for one year. The proposal, seeking re-employment of Dr Syed Hussain Imam Abidi for one year, has been submitted to Adviser to the Prime Minister Dr Syed Tauqir Shah, and the ministry is seeking the federal cabinet's approval through circulation under Rule 17(1)(b) of the Rules of Business, 1973. Under the proposal, Dr Abidi would be re-nominated for one year, from October 19, 2026, to October 18, 2027, or until a regular chairman is appointed, whichever is earlier.

Dr Abidi was first appointed PCSIR chairman on October 19, 2020, for a standard three-year term, and was later re-nominated for a second term, which expired on October 18, 2026. The post was advertised in national dailies on October 12 with a closing date of October 27, and more than 100 scientists applied, including at least 68 candidates holding PhD qualifications. Sources said no committee was notified for the scrutiny and selection of a candidate after the applications were received, and one applicant said he was neither called for an interview nor asked to appear in any selection process.

When approached for comment, the ministry's secretary and joint secretary did not respond, and its public relations officer said he would furnish the ministry's official version but could not provide any response after about two days.
