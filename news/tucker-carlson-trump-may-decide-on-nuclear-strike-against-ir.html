Tucker Carlson: Trump May Decide on Nuclear Strike Against Iran by Nov 7

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In a post on X, Tucker Carlson claimed that under pressure from Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, the Trump administration may be within days of launching a nuclear strike on Iran, with a decision possible by Nov 7. He said the proposed target is Pickax Mountain in central Iran, where Israeli intelligence claims Iran has restarted its uranium enrichment program, and argued that conventional US weapons cannot penetrate 300 feet of solid granite to destroy tunnels inside the mountain, leaving a nuclear strike as the only option. Carlson claimed Netanyahu is in daily contact with Trump and has pressured Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth and CENTCOM Commander Brad Cooper to resume major military operations against Iran, while Secretary of State Marco Rubio and CIA Director John Ratcliffe back Netanyahu's position.

He warned that a ground nuclear blast on Pickax Mountain could contaminate the Persian Gulf and affect the water supplies of nearly 100 million people, adding that Russia has already told at least two governments it could gain justification to use nuclear weapons in Ukraine if the US strikes Iran first. Carlson also raised the risk of nuclear escalation between Pakistan and India and of North Korea targeting Seoul, and said Trump is currently wavering, listening to Vice President JD Vance and envoy Steve Witkoff, who favour a deal with Iran. He noted that after the killing of Ali Khamenei and his family in February, some Iranian circles became more hardline, though there is consensus in Tehran that further war serves no one and a deal is better.
