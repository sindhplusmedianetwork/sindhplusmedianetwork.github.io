Peshawar school roof collapse: inquiry blames owner for substandard construction material

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An inquiry into the recent deadly collapse of a school roof in Peshawar has held the school owner responsible for using substandard construction material. The inquiry report was presented to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi during a meeting chaired by him in Peshawar, according to an official statement issued by his office. The meeting was attended by the governor's adviser Dr Amir Abdullah, Chief Secretary Dr Fakhar Alam Irfan, Education Secretary Inayatullah Waseem and members of the inquiry committee.

At least 30 children and a schoolteacher were killed and dozens of others injured when the roof of a multi-storey private school collapsed in the Dir Colony area. Rescue officials said the ceiling of a classroom collapsed onto students after the roof of the floor above gave way, causing the two storeys to crumble. The report also pointed to negligence on the part of the relevant departments, and the governor was briefed on the committee's findings and recommendations.

Governor Kundi directed authorities to examine the legal aspects of the report in consultation with the law department. He said strict action should be taken against those responsible for the school collapse.
