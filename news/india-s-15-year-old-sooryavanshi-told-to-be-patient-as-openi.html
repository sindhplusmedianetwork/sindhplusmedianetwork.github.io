India's 15-Year-Old Sooryavanshi Told to Be Patient as Opening Spot Battle Intensifies

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

India's 15-year-old batting prodigy Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has been advised to be patient by senior batter Shreyas Iyer as the teenager finds himself competing for an opening spot in the national side. Sooryavanshi made his international debut for India in July but has been used sparingly against top opponents despite his glittering Indian Premier League form. Consistent performances from Sanju Samson and Abhishek Sharma have kept the youngster in a battle for the opening position.

The young batter is regarded as one of the most exciting emerging talents in Indian cricket.
