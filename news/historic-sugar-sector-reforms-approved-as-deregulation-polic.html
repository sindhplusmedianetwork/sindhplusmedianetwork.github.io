Historic sugar sector reforms approved as deregulation policy gets green light

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government and all provincial governments have approved a deregulation policy for the sugar sector, agreeing to implement historic reforms in the sector. The decision was taken in line with the Prime Minister's vision for reforming the sugar sector. Under the new policy, the sugar sector will move from government control to a competitive, market-based system.

Sugarcane growers will be free to sell their crop to any buyer, and the price of sugarcane will be set through the market mechanism. Restrictions on setting up sugar mills and on approvals for production capacity will be removed, allowing mills to decide production in line with market demand. A predictable and transparent mechanism will be introduced for the import and export of sugar, while the subsidy on sugar exports will end.

The movement of sugar between provinces will be made easier, access to loans will be improved for growers and mills, and work will be done on farm mechanisation schemes and making ethanol blending mandatory. The government says the aim is to free the sector from unnecessary controls and turn it into a centre of competition, investment and production capacity, describing the policy as a game changer for the country's sugar industry.
