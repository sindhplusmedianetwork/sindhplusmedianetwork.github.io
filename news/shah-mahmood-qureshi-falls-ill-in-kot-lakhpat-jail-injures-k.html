Shah Mahmood Qureshi Falls Ill in Kot Lakhpat Jail, Injures Knee and Arm

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf senior leader Shah Mahmood Qureshi fell ill inside Lahore's Kot Lakhpat Jail on Wednesday evening, his lawyer said. Qureshi was walking inside his barrack when he suddenly felt dizzy and collapsed, sustaining injuries to his knee and arm. His lawyer Barrister Taimur Malik said the former foreign minister lost his balance and fell to the ground.

Jail authorities collected his blood samples and sent them to Shaukat Khanum Hospital for testing. On the advice of Dr Yasmin Rashid, an application is being filed for his CT scan and necessary medical treatment.
