Hridoy’s Unbeaten 242 Powers Bangladesh Past West Indies in Second Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bangladesh batter Towhid Hridoy turned his maiden Test century into an unbeaten double century in the second and final Test against West Indies. Hridoy scored 242 off 359 balls, hitting 22 fours and 11 sixes, and rescued his team from 184 for 7. He became the fourth Bangladeshi to score a Test double century.

In doing so, he broke Mushfiqur Rahim’s record of 219 for Bangladesh’s highest individual Test innings. Bangladesh made 386 in the first innings, giving them a 30-run lead. The innings put Bangladesh in a strong position in the match, with Hridoy’s marathon effort anchoring the recovery.
