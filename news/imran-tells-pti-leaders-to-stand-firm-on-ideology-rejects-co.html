Imran tells PTI leaders to stand firm on ideology, rejects compromise, says Raja

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Central Secretary General Salman Akram Raja said on Thursday that party founder Imran Khan has told the leadership to remain steadfast in the party's ideology and principles and not to compromise its stance for political gains. Raja was speaking on the television programme Capital Talk after lawyers met Imran on November 11, a day after the Islamabad High Court ordered authorities to facilitate the meeting so the lawyers could proceed with arguments in the £190 million case appeals. He said Imran had conveyed his message through Barrister Salman Safdar, stressing that the sovereignty of the people must come before his personal comfort.

Raja acknowledged that a small group within the party was unhappy with him, believing he was too ideological and that the party should seize available opportunities, including on the 28th Amendment, but said Imran's message was the opposite. Quoting Imran, he described Allama Nasir Abbas and PkMAP chief Mahmood Khan Achakzai as people who would 'neither bow down nor be bought', adding that Imran had appointed them after careful consideration. Raja also raised concerns about Imran's health, saying his blood pressure had reached 193 on Saturday and remained uncontrolled, and that the three-hour meeting had been recorded and was not held in secret.

On the debate over new provinces, he said Imran had always supported additional administrative units but wanted clarity on the background, the process and how public opinion would be obtained, and expressed reservations about a referendum given the past referendums held by military rulers Ziaul Haq and Pervez Musharraf.
