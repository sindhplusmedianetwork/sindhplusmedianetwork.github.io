Pak-Afghan Border Closure Nears One Year, Trade in Deep Disruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pak-Afghan border crossings, including Torkham, have remained closed for nearly a year, badly disrupting bilateral trade. According to the Pak-Afghan Joint Chamber of Commerce, Torkham and other crossings were shut on October 12 last year amid tensions over terrorism and strained diplomatic relations. Local traders have estimated cumulative losses at between Rs380 billion and Rs400 billion from the prolonged closure.

Daily export losses are estimated at about $2.5 million, with around 2,500 taxi drivers and 3,000 labourers in Landi Kotal losing their livelihoods. Federal Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi said the core issue with Afghanistan was terrorism and demanded Kabul openly condemn terror incidents. Despite mediation efforts by friendly countries, trade remains suspended.
