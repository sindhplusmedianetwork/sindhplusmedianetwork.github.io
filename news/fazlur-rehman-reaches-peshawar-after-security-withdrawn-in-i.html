Fazlur Rehman reaches Peshawar after security withdrawn in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

JUI chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman has reached Peshawar after his official security was withdrawn in Islamabad. He arrived in the city late at night. He is staying at the Mufti Mahmood Centre in Peshawar.

Workers welcomed him at the centre. The security was withdrawn on the instructions of the prime minister. The withdrawn security covered Maulana Fazlur Rehman, his son Maulana Asad Mahmood and Abdul Ghafoor Haideri, including an FC platoon at his Dera Ismail Khan residence.

The JUI said Maulana Fazlur Rehman will address the Peshawar High Court Bar on Saturday.
