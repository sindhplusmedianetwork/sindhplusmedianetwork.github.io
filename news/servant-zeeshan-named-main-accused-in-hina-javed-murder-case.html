Servant Zeeshan Named Main Accused in Hina Javed Murder Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A high-level investigation committee in Rawalpindi has completed its probe into the Hina Javed murder case and named domestic servant Zeeshan as the main accused, according to ARY News. The committee's determination rested on forensic evidence: particles of Zeeshan's skin were found under the victim's fingernails. During the investigation, the committee questioned both the victim's husband Faisal Asghar and the servant Zeeshan more than four times.

Sources say the committee revealed that Zeeshan is a recorded offender in other serious cases and that he confessed to assaulting Hina Javed and strangling her to death with a bed sheet, adding that no innocent person could be declared the main accused under anyone's pressure. The victim's heirs, however, expressed no-confidence in the committee's findings and alleged that evidence in the murder had been tampered with. Sources said the committee wrapped up its investigation and left for Lahore.

The murder case is registered at Civil Line police station, and both the husband Faisal Asghar and Zeeshan remain on judicial remand in Adiala Jail.
