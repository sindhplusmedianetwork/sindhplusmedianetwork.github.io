Government Cuts Petrol and Diesel Prices by Rs2 a Litre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has cut the prices of petrol and high-speed diesel by Rs2 per litre, with the new rates taking effect from November 17. According to the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority, petrol will now sell at Rs328 per litre while high-speed diesel will cost Rs358 per litre. The All Pakistan Petrol Pump Owners Association has separately asked the prime minister to move petroleum price revisions from a daily to a monthly cycle.

The association said daily changes force dealers to sell stock bought at higher prices at a loss. The government has been revising fuel prices almost daily since July 17 amid volatility linked to the US-Iran tensions. The latest cut brings some relief to motorists and transporters, though dealers say the frequent revisions continue to strain their businesses.
