GTA 6 music album revealed with 34 songs featuring Cardi B, Dua Lipa and Travis Scott

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 34-song music album has been prepared for GTA 6, featuring Cardi B, Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey, Playboi Carti, Future, Metro Boomin, Travis Scott, Fred Again, PinkPantheress and others. The album was made with Atlantic Records and will release on 19 November, the same day the game launches for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Six radio stations have been announced for the game.

For the first time, players will be able to keep listening to radio and podcasts on foot after leaving a vehicle. The album brings together some of the biggest names in music for one of the most awaited game releases. Fans will be able to hear the new music in the game from launch day.
