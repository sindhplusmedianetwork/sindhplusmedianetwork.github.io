ICC Announces Player of the Month Nominations for October

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Cricket Council has announced the nominations for Player of the Month for October. Pakistan's Arafat Minhas was nominated alongside South Africa's Marco Jansen and West Indies' Shai Hope. In the women's category, India's Deepti Sharma was nominated.

Bangladesh's Shobhana Mostary was also nominated. Australia's Annabel Sutherland completes the women's nominations.
