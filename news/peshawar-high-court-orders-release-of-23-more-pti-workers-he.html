Peshawar High Court orders release of 23 more PTI workers held under 3 MPO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Peshawar High Court has ordered the release of 23 more PTI workers detained under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order (3 MPO). A two-member bench of Justices Sahibzada Asadullah and Inamullah heard the writ petitions filed by the workers, represented by lawyers Yasir Khattak and Azhar Rahim Khan, with the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa advocate general present. The court noted that the Mardan deputy commissioner had not submitted the required report explaining the detentions.

Each petitioner was ordered released on bail of Rs 100,000 with two sureties. The case was adjourned on merit to November 12.
