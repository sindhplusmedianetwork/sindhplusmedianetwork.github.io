Salman Khan Shot Controversial Film in Minus 7 Degree Rain Without Extra Oxygen, Director Reveals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood star Salman Khan shot for his controversial film 'Matrubhoomi' at 14,000 feet in freezing weather and in rain at minus 7 degrees Celsius without extra oxygen, director Apoorva Lakhia has revealed. The 60-year-old actor went straight to the set on arrival at the difficult high-altitude locations and filmed walking scenes in the freezing conditions without complaint. Lakhia said the toughest location was the Taro Plains near a river, where the temperature was minus 7 degrees and it was raining while soldiers had to run uphill.

The film is set against the backdrop of the India-China conflict and focuses on the human cost of war, including the separation of families and suffering on both sides. The film is scheduled for release on December 4.
