Pakistan Raises Alarm Over India's Submarine-Based Nuclear Weapons

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has sounded the alarm over India's growing sea-based nuclear arsenal, warning that it could push South Asia toward a potentially catastrophic conflict. Lieutenant General (retd) Mazhar Jamil, adviser to Pakistan's National Command Authority, raised the concern at a workshop in London. He questioned the credibility of India's declared No First Use nuclear policy in light of its expanding capabilities.

The retired general cited reports that India had begun pairing nuclear warheads with submarine-launched ballistic missiles, a development he said significantly increases the risk of miscalculation during a military crisis. He warned that the opacity surrounding sea-based deployments makes crisis management far more difficult. Pakistani officials have long argued that India's naval nuclear programme upsets the strategic balance in the region.

The remarks come amid continued tensions between the two nuclear-armed neighbours.
