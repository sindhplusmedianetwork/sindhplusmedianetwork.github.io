G7 Foreign Ministers to Meet in Paris Today

The foreign ministers of the G7 countries will meet in Paris today, and France's foreign ministry has issued a formal statement on the meeting. According to the statement, the meeting will be held in Paris on November 9 and 10. The official priorities of the meeting include support for Ukraine in the financial, defence and energy sectors, stability in the Middle East, energy supply, food security, humanitarian aid and artificial intelligence.

The important meeting of the G7 foreign ministers will take place in France on Monday and Tuesday. The gathering comes at a time when major global issues, from the war in Ukraine to developments in the Middle East, are high on the agenda of the world's leading economies.
