If PPP is serious, it should ask its KP governor to resign, says opposition leader

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senate Opposition Leader Raja Nasir Abbas has said that if the PPP is serious it should restore the unconstitutionally removed Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government and ask its governor to resign. Speaking to the media outside the Islamabad High Court, he said the PPP must prove its seriousness through practical steps, starting with removing the governor. He added that the country cannot move forward without the supremacy of the Constitution, and that the words of PPP chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari must be matched by action.

His remarks came as a direct challenge to the PPP to back its statements with concrete measures rather than words alone. The opposition leader's comments underline the ongoing political dispute over the removal of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government and the role of the governor. The issue remains a point of contention between the opposition and the ruling coalition, with constitutional supremacy at the centre of the debate.
