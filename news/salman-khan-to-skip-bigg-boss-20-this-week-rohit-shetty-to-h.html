Salman Khan to Skip Bigg Boss 20 This Week; Rohit Shetty to Host Weekend Ka Vaar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Filmmaker Rohit Shetty will host the upcoming 'Weekend Ka Vaar' episode of Bigg Boss 20 instead of Salman Khan. Salman had informed the contestants of the change during the October 18 episode. Indian media attribute Salman's absence to pre-scheduled professional commitments.

The change comes amid heightened concern over his security, following the firing on actor Tiger Shroff's car and an alleged social-media message from someone claiming to be linked to the Lawrence Bishnoi gang, warning those working with Salman. Shetty, known for directing Singham and Chennai Express, has previously hosted multiple seasons of Khatron Ke Khiladi.
