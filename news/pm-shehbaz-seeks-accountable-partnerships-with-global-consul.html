PM Shehbaz Seeks Accountable Partnerships With Global Consulting Firms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Friday said Pakistan is ready to partner with international consulting firms to strengthen economic growth, stressing the need for impact and accountability as the government pursues structural reforms and privatisation. He was speaking during a meeting with Peter Briggs, managing director of the North American operations of international consulting firm Alvarez & Marsal, in Islamabad. The prime minister welcomed the delegation and reiterated Pakistan's willingness to open its economy to promising international markets and consult globally recognised economic experts.

He said that after achieving economic and fiscal stability, the government was taking effective measures for deep-rooted structural reforms aimed at export-led growth and a productive economy. The premier highlighted the government's focus on improving the performance of the domestic manufacturing sector and attracting foreign direct investment. He also outlined a strategy to assess the economic viability of state-owned enterprises and pursue privatisation to support the national economy.

Briggs thanked the prime minister and expressed the firm's eagerness to establish coordination with Pakistan to promote a progressive business environment and a productive economy. Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, Economic Affairs Minister Ahad Khan Cheema, Power Minister Sardar Awais Ahmed Khan Leghari and other senior officials attended the meeting.
