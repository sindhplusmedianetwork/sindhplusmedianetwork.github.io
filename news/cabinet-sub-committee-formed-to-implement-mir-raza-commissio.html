Cabinet Sub-Committee Formed to Implement Mir Raza Commission Report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A cabinet sub-committee has been formed to implement the judicial commission's report on the Mir Raza murder case. Under the provincial cabinet's decision, the minister for home, law and parliamentary affairs will be the convener of the sub-committee, while its other members include Local Government Minister Nasir Hussain Shah and Labour Minister Saeed Ghani. A notification regarding the sub-committee has been issued, according to which the Advocate General Sindh will provide legal assistance to the committee.

The notification states that the cabinet sub-committee will monitor the implementation of the commission report's findings and present its recommendations to the Sindh chief minister as soon as possible. According to the notification, the sub-committee's recommendations will also be presented at the next cabinet meeting.
