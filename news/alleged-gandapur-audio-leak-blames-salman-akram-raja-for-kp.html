Alleged Gandapur Audio Leak Blames Salman Akram Raja for KP Government's Dismissal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An alleged audio recording of former Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister Ali Amin Gandapur has surfaced, in which he holds Salman Akram Raja responsible for the dismissal of the government. ARY News reported that PTI's internal differences are once again out in the open following the leak. In the audio, Gandapur says that PTI is paying the price for Raja's wrong planning.

He demanded that Raja delete his recent tweet and apologise. Raja responded by saying that undemocratic decision-makers are attacking PTI's internal unity, and reiterated that Sohail Afridi remains chief minister under the constitution.
