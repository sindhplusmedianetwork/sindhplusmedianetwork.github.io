Hyderabad road built with electric poles standing in the middle, HESCO raises questions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An under-construction road in Hyderabad has raised questions after electric poles were left standing in its middle. The road runs from Alamdar Chowk to the Old National Highway, and the Hyderabad Electric Supply Company (HESCO) has questioned why the poles were not removed before construction began. HESCO spokesperson Sadiq Kubar said that markers provided by the company must be used for road construction and the road should have been built only after the poles were removed.

He said that despite informing the relevant department, the road was constructed with the poles still in place, which he said was beyond understanding. He added that HESCO has completed work on 22 feeders from Alamdar Chowk to the Old National Highway and will take two shutdowns on Friday to shift the load of the remaining feeders, after which the 25 poles in the way will be dismantled by Sunday. Questions are being raised about why the construction was rushed ahead when HESCO objected, and how much extra it will cost to repair the road once the poles are uprooted.

The mayor of Hyderabad's spokesperson told Geo News that the road is still under construction and has only been temporarily opened for traffic.
