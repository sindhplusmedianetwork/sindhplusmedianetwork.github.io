Indonesia Detains Three Senior Generals Over Alleged Spying on President

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indonesian police have detained three senior police generals accused of illegally tapping the telephones of President Prabowo Subianto and other key government figures. The detained officers include General Listyo Sigit Prabowo, who until last month was the country’s national police chief and was later appointed the president’s chief of staff; the president removed him from the post after his name came up in the alleged scandal. The other two officers are Inspector General Ahmadie Sonta Nasution, head of the national police’s legal division, and Inspector General Eko Hadi Santoso of the police training institute.

Investigators say the three allegedly carried out illegal surveillance of the phone numbers of the president, officials, politicians, journalists, civil society representatives, NGO workers and business figures during 2025 and 2026, bypassing legal procedures and required permissions. Investigative chief Asep Saefudin said several phone numbers were added to the police communications-surveillance system without proper verification. Police say orders were allegedly given in late October to delete electronic records linked to the surveillance, raising suspicion of an evidence-tampering attempt; 11 mobile phones, one iPad and four USB drives have been seized.

Indonesia’s law and human rights minister, Yusril Ihza Mahendra, said no evidence of treason had emerged so far, though the allegations were extremely serious. The officers have not publicly responded to the charges. Under Indonesian law, illegal interception of communications, unauthorised access to electronic systems and deletion of electronic information can carry prison terms of up to 10 years.
