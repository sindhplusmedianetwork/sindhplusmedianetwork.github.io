Regenerative farming: How an Islamabad farm became a 'ray of hope' for small farmers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 2.5-acre farm on Lehtrar Road in Islamabad, owned by Supreme Court lawyer Mian Abdul Rauf, has been turned from barren land into a model regenerative farm in four years. The transformation was carried out with the help of agricultural scientist Dr Muhammad Azim Khan, who guided the work from the beginning. The 16-kanal cultivable area now has 1,500 fruit trees of 46 varieties, along with seasonal and off-season vegetables grown in tunnels.

The farm also has a small poultry and duck farm and Angora rabbits, all raised as part of the same natural system. Everything on the farm is grown without chemical fertiliser or pesticides, relying instead on regenerative methods that restore the soil. Dr Azim says the farm could soon earn Rs 7-8 million a year.

He believes it can serve as a model for small farmers, showing how they can earn more from less land.
