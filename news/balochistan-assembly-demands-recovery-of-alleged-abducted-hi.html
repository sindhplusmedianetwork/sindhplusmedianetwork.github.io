Balochistan Assembly Demands Recovery of Alleged Abducted Hindu Girl

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minority members of the Balochistan Assembly on Monday raised a strong protest over the alleged abduction of a 14-year-old Hindu girl from Sibi. PML-N MPA Dr Ashok Kumar, speaking on a point of order, said Bindiya was allegedly abducted and taken to Bakhshapur in Sindh, where she was reportedly subjected to forced religious conversion and married. The members demanded her immediate recovery and safe return to her family.

Taking notice of the matter, Speaker Captain (retd) Abdul Khaliq Achakzai directed Provincial Home Minister Mir Ziaullah Langove to contact the Sindh government. He instructed the minister to take immediate steps to resolve the issue.
