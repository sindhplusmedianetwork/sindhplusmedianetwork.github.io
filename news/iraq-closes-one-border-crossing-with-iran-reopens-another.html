Iraq closes one border crossing with Iran, reopens another

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iraq has announced the closure of one border crossing with Iran and the reopening of another. Iraqi officials said the steps are for repair and maintenance work. Border Crossings Authority chief Major General Omar al-Waeli said the Mandali crossing has been closed while the Al-Shaib crossing has been reopened.

The move comes after Iraq's foreign minister said the US Treasury had asked Baghdad to close all crossings with Iran except two. Washington seeks to increase economic pressure on Iran.
