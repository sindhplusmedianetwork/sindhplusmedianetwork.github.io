CM Maryam Nawaz inaugurates Pakistan's first fully electric sweeper truck

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has inaugurated Pakistan's first fully electric sweeper truck for washing and cleaning roads. She inspected the vehicle and observed its working during the ceremony. Officials briefed her that the electric mechanical sweeper truck will clean and wash roads on Lahore's Ring Road.

They said the truck will also absorb water from the road for the first time. The vehicle can work for up to 70 kilometres on a single charge. Officials said the truck's zero carbon emissions will reduce environmental pollution, while it will also cut dust, noise, and heavy fuel and maintenance costs.
