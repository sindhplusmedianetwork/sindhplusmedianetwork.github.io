Germany Summons Iranian Envoy Over Alleged Plot to Kill Jewish Figures

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Germany summoned Iran's ambassador to the Foreign Ministry in Berlin to deliver a strong protest over an alleged assassination plot targeting Jewish and pro-Israel figures. The move followed a Hamburg court's sentencing of two men with links to Iranian intelligence over the plot. According to the Foreign Ministry, the two men — nationals of Denmark and Afghanistan — had planned attacks on the heads of Germany's Central Council of Jews and the German-Israeli Society.

The ministry said the plot was directed by Iran's Quds Force. Berlin told the Iranian envoy that attempts to intimidate Germany's free society would not be tolerated. The summoning marks a further escalation in tensions between the two countries over the alleged operation.
