Speeding Truck Crushes Navratri Pilgrims in Gwalior, India; 12 Killed, Over 40 Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A speeding truck ran over pilgrims heading to the Shitala Mandir in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, during the Navratri festival on Saturday. Twelve people were killed in the crash and more than 40 were injured. Rescue teams rushed the injured to nearby hospitals where treatment is under way.

Several of the injured are reported to be in critical condition. Authorities fear the death toll could rise as doctors assess the seriously wounded. Police have launched an investigation into the accident.

The tragedy struck as devotees were travelling to the temple for the Hindu festival.
