Pakistan Face Sri Lanka in ODI Tri-Series Final at Gaddafi Stadium

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore — Pakistan take on Sri Lanka in the final of the ODI tri-series at Gaddafi Stadium on Saturday, aiming to finish the tournament on a high. Captain Salman Ali Agha has backed the Green-shirts to produce a 'perfect game' in the title clash, despite their batting collapse for just 110 against Sri Lanka in the final league match. Pakistan's misfiring top order remains the biggest worry heading into the final, with Saim Ayub sent back to domestic cricket after a string of failures.

Sri Lanka, by contrast, arrive in confident mood: their top order has been in commanding form, with Pavan Rathnayake scoring two centuries in the tournament and the team completing a record 430-run chase against England. The final promises a stern test for Pakistan's bowlers against a batting unit in peak form, while the hosts will hope their own batters can rediscover touch when it matters most.
