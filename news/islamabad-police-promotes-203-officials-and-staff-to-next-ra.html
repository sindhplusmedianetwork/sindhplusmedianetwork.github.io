Islamabad Police Promotes 203 Officials and Staff to Next Ranks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Inspector General of Police Islamabad Capt (R) Muhammad Sohail Chaudhry has approved the promotion of 203 Islamabad Capital Territory Police officials and employees to their next ranks, including 90 Assistant Sub-Inspectors, 107 Sub-Inspectors and six Lower Division Clerks. An ICT Police official said DIG Headquarters Muhammad Jawad Tariq and DIG Law and Order Muhammad Ateeq Tahir, along with other senior police officers, pinned the new ranks on the promoted personnel during a ceremony at Police Lines Headquarters. Family members of the promoted officers and personnel were specially invited to attend the rank-pinning ceremony and share the occasion with their loved ones.

IGP Muhammad Sohail Chaudhry said departmental promotion was a fundamental right of police personnel and a recognition of their professional services, adding that promotion also brought greater responsibilities and urging the newly promoted officials to perform their duties with increased dedication, honesty and commitment. He emphasized that ensuring timely promotions for eligible officers and personnel strictly on merit remained among his top priorities, and said measures for the welfare and professional development of Islamabad Police officers and employees would continue.
