Federal government announces public holiday across Pakistan on Monday, November 9

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has announced a public holiday across the country on Monday, November 9. The day is observed every year as Iqbal Day, marking the birth anniversary of national poet Allama Muhammad Iqbal. The State Bank of Pakistan had already announced that banks will remain closed on November 9 for Iqbal Day.

Sindh had also announced a holiday for schools across the province on the same day. With the federal announcement, the holiday now applies across the country, and government offices will remain closed on Monday.
