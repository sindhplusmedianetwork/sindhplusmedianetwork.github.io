Kohat Police Arrest Two Men Red-Handed for Aerial Firing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kohat police have arrested two men red-handed for aerial firing. SHO Thana M.R.S. Abdul Rauf Bangash caught Muhammad Shahid and Abrar Khan, both residents of Togh Bala, while they were firing into the air.

Legal proceedings against the accused are now underway. DPO Kohat SSP Rai Mazhar Iqbal said aerial firing poses a danger to human lives. He added that protecting the lives and property of citizens is the police's top priority.
