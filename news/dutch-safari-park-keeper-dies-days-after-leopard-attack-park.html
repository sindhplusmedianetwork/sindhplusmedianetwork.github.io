Dutch Safari Park Keeper Dies Days After Leopard Attack; Park Admits No Cameras

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A female keeper at the Beekse Bergen safari park in the Netherlands has died after spending several days fighting for her life following a leopard attack. The attack took place on October 24, when two male Persian leopards turned on the keeper inside their enclosure. She managed to alert her colleagues by walkie-talkie before losing consciousness, but succumbed to her injuries on Tuesday.

The park has admitted that the enclosure had no surveillance cameras installed. Police have opened an investigation into possible negligence at the facility.
