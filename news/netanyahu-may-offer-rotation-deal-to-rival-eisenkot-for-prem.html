Netanyahu May Offer Rotation Deal to Rival Eisenkot for Premiership

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is preparing to offer Yesh Atid leader Gadi Eisenkot a rotation deal for the premiership as part of efforts to form a new government, according to Israeli media reports. Under the proposal, Netanyahu would serve as the second prime minister in the rotation arrangement. Eisenkot, however, has expressed doubts about Netanyahu's proposals, even as preparations are under way for the new government to be sworn in next week.

Eisenkot has until December 6 to complete the formation of the government. His allies Yair Golan and Avigdor Lieberman have assured him that they will not form a national unity government with Likud without his approval.
