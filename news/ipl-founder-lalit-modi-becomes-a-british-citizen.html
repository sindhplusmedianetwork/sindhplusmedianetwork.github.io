IPL founder Lalit Modi becomes a British citizen

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read

Indian Premier League founder and former chairman Lalit Modi has become a British citizen. Modi announced the development during an address to the Oxford Union on 14 November, confirming that he formally became a British citizen on 15 September 2026. He described the citizenship as an important and proud milestone in his life, saying Britain has been his home for years and that he respects its people, institutions, traditions and values.

Modi said becoming a British citizen was not only an honour but also a big responsibility, which he accepts with pride and hopes to honour by playing a positive role for Britain in the future. Modi left India for Britain in 2010, during an investigation into alleged financial irregularities linked to the IPL, and has lived there since.
