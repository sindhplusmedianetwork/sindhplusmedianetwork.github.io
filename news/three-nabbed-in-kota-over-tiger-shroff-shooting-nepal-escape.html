Three Nabbed in Kota Over Tiger Shroff Shooting; Nepal Escape Plan Foiled

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three suspects in the firing on Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff's car have been detained in Kota, Rajasthan, while trying to flee toward Nepal. A joint team of the Rajasthan Anti-Gangster Task Force and Kota police caught them aboard the Pune-Jaipur Express after a tip-off from Mumbai police. Kota Superintendent of Police Tejaswini Gautam said the three were held within about 15 minutes of searching the train.

They were identified as Rehan from Haryana, Hritik from Bihar, and a minor; police said the accused planned to travel to Delhi and then escape to Nepal. Mumbai police said five to six rounds were fired at Shroff's car on Sunday evening in Juhu; the actor escaped unhurt while his driver and a bystander were injured. A social media post claimed the Lawrence Bishnoi gang's responsibility, which police are verifying.

The suspects will be handed over to the Mumbai Crime Branch for further investigation.
