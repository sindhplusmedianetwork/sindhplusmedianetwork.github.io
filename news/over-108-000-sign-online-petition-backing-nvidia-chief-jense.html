Over 108,000 Sign Online Petition Backing Nvidia Chief Jensen Huang

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An online campaign in support of Nvidia chief Jensen Huang has gained wide popularity, gathering more than 108,000 signatures by Saturday afternoon. The campaign, named 'Stand with Jensen', has drawn support from prominent figures in the technology industry, including billionaire Elon Musk and Replit chief executive Amjad Masad, who promoted the petition on the social media platform X. According to the campaign's website, those who signed include more than 58,000 shareholders, over 5,600 current Nvidia employees and more than 1,000 former employees.

A Nvidia employee who signed the petition told Business Insider that similar efforts in support of Huang are also under way inside the company. According to the employee, a message on the same demand has also appeared on the company's internal online portal, where employees can raise their questions and suggestions. The participation of technology industry figures, investors and current and former employees is being described as a significant show of support for Huang.
