Hina Dilpazir Says She Played 25 Roles in One Comedy Drama for a Single Artist's Pay

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior actress Hina Dilpazir has revealed that she played 25 different characters in a popular comedy drama but was paid the fee of just one artist. Speaking on a podcast with Ahmed Ali Butt, she said she was initially hired for three to four roles. She explained that the number kept growing as the director and writer could not find suitable actors for the other characters.

Several of the additional roles were male characters, she said. Dilpazir added that when she raised the pay issue, the producer cited financial difficulties. She said the pay she received remained equal to that of a single artist despite the 25 roles.
