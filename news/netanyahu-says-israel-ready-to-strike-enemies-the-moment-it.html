Netanyahu Says Israel Ready to Strike Enemies 'the Moment It Is Required'

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has said Israel will not hesitate to strike its enemies 'the moment it is required'. Speaking from an airbase on Monday in a video statement, Netanyahu warned Israel's adversaries: 'Do not test us — you will receive harsher blows than you have suffered to date.' The remarks followed his recent warnings directed at Iran. They come just eight days before a tight Israeli election, with regional tensions running high.

Netanyahu has repeatedly signalled that Israel retains the option of military action against its enemies. The statement is expected to draw sharp reactions across the region.
