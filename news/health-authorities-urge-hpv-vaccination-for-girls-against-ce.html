Health authorities urge HPV vaccination for girls against cervical cancer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Health authorities in Islamabad have urged parents to ensure their daughters receive the human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine under the cervical cancer prevention campaign, describing early vaccination as a vital step towards protecting girls against the disease. The Expanded Programme on Immunisation (EPI) encouraged parents to make informed decisions about their daughters' health and to take advantage of the preventive vaccination programme. The appeal came during an EPI ceremony held on Thursday to honour vaccinators.

Health experts explained that the HPV vaccine protects against the types of human papillomavirus responsible for most cases of cervical cancer. Vaccination at the recommended age of nine to 14 years provides the best protection, ideally before exposure to the virus. Cervical cancer develops in the cervix, the lower part of the uterus, and is largely preventable through HPV vaccination and appropriate screening later in life.
