Peshawar police kill two snatchers in Operation Street Fight, arrest one injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Peshawar police killed two dangerous snatchers in an exchange of fire during Operation Street Fight against street criminals and arrested one in injured condition. Police said Shaheed Gulfat Hussain police station and Sarband police station carried out a joint operation, in which the two suspects were killed and one was arrested injured. The killed suspects were identified as Abdul Samad, son of Sajjad Ahmad, resident of Afghanistan currently at Panj Katha, and Saadullah, son of Ayub Khan, resident of Shaheen Muslim Town.

The injured arrested suspect was identified as Ibrahim, son of Rambel, resident of China Dag. Weapons used in incidents and a stolen motorcycle were recovered from the suspects, who were wanted by police in several serious cases, and a search operation for other fleeing suspects is under way.
