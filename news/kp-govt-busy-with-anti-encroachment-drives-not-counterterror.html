KP Govt Busy With Anti-Encroachment Drives, Not Counterterrorism: Shaukat Yousafzai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI's Khyber Pakhtunkhwa information secretary Shaukat Yousafzai has accused the governor-rule administration of prioritising anti-encroachment drives over counterterrorism operations. He said his party had never opposed the war against terrorism, and questioned what the new administration had achieved in 16 days that the PTI government was not doing. According to him, the government's focus has shifted from fighting terrorism to demolishing encroachments, which he said has pushed the province toward ruin within days.

He added that taking away the livelihoods of the poor reflects poor judgement. Shaukat Yousafzai concluded that the only way out of the current crises is through negotiations and political dialogue.
