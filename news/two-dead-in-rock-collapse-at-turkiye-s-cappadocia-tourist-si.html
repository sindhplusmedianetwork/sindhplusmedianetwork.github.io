Two Dead in Rock Collapse at Türkiye's Cappadocia Tourist Site

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A landslide triggered by heavy rainfall caused a rock spire to collapse in Türkiye's Cappadocia tourist region on Thursday, killing two people, the local governor said. The incident occurred at around 3:15pm local time in the town of Goreme near the open-air museum, and one injured person was taken to hospital, Nevsehir Governor Huseyin Kok said. The area was closed to the public following the collapse, and search and rescue operations were underway as authorities assessed the stability of the surrounding rock formations.
