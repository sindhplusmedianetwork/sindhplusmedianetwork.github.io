Trump concedes midterm defeat, blames Iran war; Iranian speaker says Americans rejected war

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has said that the American people have given their verdict against war. In a statement on the social media website X, Ghalibaf said the American people heard the voice of conscience and clearly rejected war, adding that Iran also supports this message of the American people. The Iranian parliament speaker said that if the US government works for the interests of those who blackmail, it will face defeat.

Meanwhile, according to early results of the US midterm elections, Democratic candidates have gained a lead over Republicans in the House of Representatives, while Democratic successes have also been reported in the Senate results. US President Donald Trump announced that he accepts the election results, saying the war with Iran was one reason for the Republicans' failure in the elections. He expressed his willingness to work with the Democrats, but also made clear that unacceptable actions would be stopped through the presidential veto.
