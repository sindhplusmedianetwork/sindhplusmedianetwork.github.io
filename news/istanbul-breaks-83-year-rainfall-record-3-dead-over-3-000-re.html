Istanbul Breaks 83-Year Rainfall Record; 3 Dead, Over 3,000 Rescued in Turkey Floods

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Torrential rain across four Turkish provinces has triggered severe flooding, with Istanbul breaking an 83-year rainfall record. Floodwater entered buildings, shops and homes, swept away vehicles in several districts, toppled trees and damaged rooftops, while people were left stranded in waterlogged low-lying areas. Three people were killed in separate rain-related incidents and more than 3,000 were rescued from the affected areas.

Landslides were reported at several locations, traffic systems were thrown into disarray and motorcycles were banned from the roads. Schools remained closed in Istanbul, Kocaeli, Sakarya and Yalova, and citizens were advised against leaving their homes unnecessarily. The meteorological department has forecast more heavy rainfall in various regions through the weekend.
