Field Marshal Asim Munir to Visit Iran Today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, Pakistan's army chief, is set to visit Iran on Tuesday, according to Iranian media reports. During his stay in Tehran, he is expected to hold meetings with senior Iranian officials. The talks will cover bilateral cooperation between the two neighbouring countries.

Key regional matters and the current security situation will also be on the agenda. Pakistan's role in mediation between Iran and the United States is another important subject expected to come up. The visit comes amid heightened tensions in the region and growing diplomatic activity.

Officials on both sides are expected to discuss ways to strengthen defence and security coordination. Details of any agreements reached during the visit are likely to be announced after the meetings conclude.
