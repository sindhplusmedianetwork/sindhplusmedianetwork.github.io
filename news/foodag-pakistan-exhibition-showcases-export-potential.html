FoodAg Pakistan Exhibition Showcases Export Potential

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Commerce Minister Jam Kamal has said that despite concerns about regional tensions and disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz, the FoodAg Pakistan exhibition saw a significant increase in delegates. Talking to the media after inaugurating the 4th International Food and Agro Exhibition at the Expo Centre in Karachi on Monday, the minister said FoodAg had emerged as a highly encouraging initiative for Karachi and Pakistan over the past four years. Organised by the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan, the four-day exhibition from November 23 to 26 brings together 400 Pakistani exhibitors and 820 international delegates from 72 countries.

He highlighted the importance of business-to-business meetings in connecting Pakistan's agricultural producers, exporters and businesses with foreign buyers and investors. The minister said that for the first time, Pakistan's services exports crossed the one billion dollar mark, while agriculture, information technology and textiles also played a significant role in exports over the last eight months. He added that a number of Chinese companies are operating in Pakistan and talks are under way to sell Pakistani products to China, while Pakistan's annual imports from China hovered between 17 and 18 billion dollars against exports of around 3 billion dollars.
