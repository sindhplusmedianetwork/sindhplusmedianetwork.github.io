Netflix's The Body Scores 5.1 Million Views in Just Five Days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Netflix's teen psychological drama The Body has secured the third spot on the streaming platform's English-language TV rankings after attracting 5.1 million views in its first five days. Created by Quinn Shephard, the series premiered on November 11 and appeared on Netflix's Top 10 list for the week of November 9 to 15. Its opening performance placed it behind two returning titles, demonstrating a strong debut despite entering the rankings midway through the tracking period.

The docuseries Taken: Natalee Holloway topped the chart with 7.2 million views in its second week, followed by the second season of Black Doves, which recorded 6.6 million views. Netflix's new children's series Doozie's Doghouse claimed fourth place after collecting five million views in just four days. Other titles in the rankings included Power, which made its Netflix debut on November 9 and earned 2.9 million views, while the November 9 edition of Raw: 2026 and the third season of Nobody Wants This each recorded 2.4 million views.

Meanwhile, the docuseries The One About Matthew Perry secured ninth place with 1.7 million views, while the second season of children's series Danny Go! rounded out the chart with 1.5 million views, and the film Heartland, starring Jessica Chastain, attracted 7.3 million views during its first three days on the platform.
