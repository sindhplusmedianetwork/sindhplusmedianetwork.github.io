Kundi seeks foreign investment, joint ventures in mineral sector

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has said the province's vast mineral resources offer immense opportunities for foreign investment. He was speaking during a meeting with Lee Constable, chief executive officer of UK-based Critical Minerals Tomorrow, at KP House in Islamabad. The meeting discussed prospects for foreign investment, the development of mineral resources and the establishment of potential joint ventures between the company and the provincial government.

Kundi said such joint ventures would ensure benefits for the provincial and federal governments and local communities, and stressed the need for transparent use of the resources. He said investment in the mineral sector would accelerate economic growth, improve living standards and create sustainable livelihoods for people in mineral-rich areas. The governor said curbing illegal mining had remained a top priority since he took office, adding that illegal mining worth around Rs2 billion per month had reportedly taken place during the dysfunctional provincial government's tenure.

He said the mineral sector was being digitised to improve transparency, accountability and regulation, and that the federal government would be taken on board for future projects.
