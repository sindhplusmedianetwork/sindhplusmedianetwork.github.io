Climate Disasters Displace 1,000 Gilgit-Baltistan Families Every Year, Says Chief Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nearly 1,000 households in Gilgit-Baltistan are affected by glacial floods and other climate-related disasters every year. Chief Minister Amjad Hussain said the affected families lose their homes, crops and livelihoods. He was addressing the Mountain Women Climate Award 2026 ceremony on Tuesday.

He said glacial lake outburst floods and flash floods repeatedly threaten settlements, farmland and infrastructure. He added that billions of rupees are spent each year on restoration work. He said a Glacier Protection Authority has been established to study glacier risks and coordinate protection efforts.
