High Court bars police from naming acquitted citizens' FIRs in character certificates

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has declared that police cannot mention an FIR in the character certificate of a citizen who has been acquitted in that case, terming any such certificate unlawful and invalid. Justice Ayyaz Shaukat, deciding a public interest petition, directed the Inspector General of Police Islamabad to establish a formal mechanism ensuring that criminal cases ending in acquittal are never reflected in certificates issued to citizens. The court observed that citizens should not be compelled to approach courts to exercise rights already guaranteed under the law, and held there was no legal justification for portraying an acquitted person as an accused or criminal.

Allowing the petition, the court ordered the removal of an FIR reference registered at Shalimar Police Station from the petitioner's character certificate. The judgement clarified that while police may maintain internal records for official purposes, such information cannot be disclosed in certificates citizens need, particularly for travelling abroad. It further held that mentioning the FIR after a final acquittal violated Articles 4, 9, 14 and 15 of the Constitution, protecting citizens' rights, liberty, dignity and freedom of movement.
