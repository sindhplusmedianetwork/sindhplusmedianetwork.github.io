Farooq Abdullah urges India-Pakistan talks, says waiting for guns to fall silent will be too late

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Conference president Farooq Abdullah on Thursday made a strong pitch for dialogue between India and Pakistan, asserting that bullets and military confrontation cannot resolve political disputes. Speaking to reporters in Jammu, he said that if people kept waiting for everyone to stop firing, it would be too late by the time the guns fell silent. He was responding to a question about the BJP-led government's stated position that talks and bullets cannot go together.

The former chief minister of Jammu and Kashmir also asserted that Kashmir is, was and will remain an integral part of India. He urged Pakistan to abandon terrorism and work towards creating a conducive atmosphere for dialogue with India. He was talking to reporters after visiting the family of retired information officer Madan Mantoo, who recently died of cardiac arrest.

He was also reacting to the recent twin attacks in Kulgam and Kishtwar districts, which claimed the lives of two Special Police Officers and left three CRPF personnel injured within two days.
