Zidane calls Italy defeat 'part of the process' for France

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Zinedine Zidane was unimpressed with his side's defensive display after suffering his first defeat as France head coach against Italy on Thursday, but described the setback as 'part of the process'. France took an early lead at San Siro through Michael Olise, but were pegged back by a Francesco Pio Esposito double and a Davide Frattesi strike as Italy won 3-1. 'We started well and we were good in possession, but in the second half we ran into difficulties, especially defensively,' Zidane told a press conference.

Asked if the team lacked courage, he replied: 'No, quite the opposite. We stayed in the game, we did well in possession, especially in the first half, but in the second half we caved in a bit, especially after 2-1. It's part of the process.' France were twice denied by the woodwork and had 60 per cent possession and 18 attempts, but slipped behind Italy into second place in Group A1 on head-to-head record.

Zidane's side host Turkey in their final game on Sunday, while Italy travel to Belgium.
