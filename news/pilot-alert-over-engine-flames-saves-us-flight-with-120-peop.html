Pilot Alert Over Engine Flames Saves US Flight With 120 People Aboard

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A SkyWest pilot saved an American Airlines flight from a horrific air disaster by immediately reporting a fire in its engine just after takeoff, and all 120 people aboard the aircraft remained safe. According to flight data, the Airbus A319 aircraft had departed from Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport in the US state of Arizona for Portland. Moments after takeoff, the pilots of the SkyWest flight flying behind warned air traffic control that flames were coming from the engine of the American Airlines aircraft ahead and fluid was possibly leaking.

On receiving the immediate alert, the pilots of Flight 3230 confirmed they were facing a fault in the hydraulic system. The pilots immediately made a mayday call and the aircraft landed safely back at Phoenix Airport after remaining in the air for about 20 minutes. After landing, ground crew saw smoke rising from the aircraft's left engine and a punctured tyre, and after inspection by the fire brigade the aircraft was taken to the gate.

The aircraft was removed from service for inspection and all passengers were sent to Portland on a replacement aircraft.
