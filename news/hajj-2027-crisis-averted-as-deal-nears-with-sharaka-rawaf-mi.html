Hajj 2027 crisis averted as deal nears with Sharaka Rawaf Mina and Raji Group

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Acting Director General Hajj Muhammad Arif Aslam Rao is close to resolving the issue of tawafa companies for 107,000 pilgrims under the government Hajj 2027 scheme, averting a major crisis for the pilgrimage arrangements. Agreements are expected with Sharaka Rawaf Mina and Raji Group, with Raji Group likely to get the larger quota this year. The crisis arose after the sudden transfer of Director General Hajj Abdul Wahab Soomro, which had left the tawafa company arrangements unsettled.

Federal Minister Sardar Yousuf will sign agreements with the tawafa companies during the Hajj conference from November 8 to 11. The expected agreements are set to secure the services needed for the government scheme pilgrims for Hajj 2027.
