Selena Gomez Reveals Vaping Changed Her Voice, Not Just Illness

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Selena Gomez has admitted that her noticeably changed voice is the result of heavy nicotine vaping, saying it was not solely down to the medical reasons she had previously mentioned. In a candid Instagram Stories video posted on October 18, the singer opened up about her vaping habit and the difficulty of quitting nicotine. She described vaping as not worth it and said she hoped she could stay away from it for good.

The admission marks a shift from her earlier explanation in December 2025, when she attributed the change in her voice to medical issues, including lupus, chemotherapy and her 2017 kidney transplant. Her honest confession drew wide attention from fans and followers online. Gomez has spoken openly about her health struggles in recent years.
