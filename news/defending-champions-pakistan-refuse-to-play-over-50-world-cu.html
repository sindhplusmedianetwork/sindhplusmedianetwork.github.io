Defending champions Pakistan refuse to play Over-50 World Cup in India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defending champions Pakistan will not play the Over-50 Cricket World Cup 2027 in India after the government denied the team permission to travel. The Pakistan Veterans Cricket Association confirmed that the Foreign Ministry and the Interior Ministry were approached over the side's participation in the tournament. Association chairman Fawad Ejaz Khan said Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi conveyed that the team would not be allowed to travel across the border.

The decision ends any chance of Pakistan defending the title it lifted at the last edition of the event. Pakistan won the previous Over-50 World Cup in Sri Lanka in 2025. The 2027 edition is scheduled to be held from January 23 to February 7 in Hyderabad Deccan.
