Pakistan, Sri Lanka Docked WTC Points for Slow Over-Rates in First Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Sri Lanka have been penalised for maintaining slow over-rates in the drawn first Test of their two-match ICC World Test Championship series in Rawalpindi. Pakistan lost five points after being found five overs short of the required rate, while Sri Lanka lost 15 points for falling 15 overs behind. The players were also fined under the ICC Code of Conduct, with Pakistan fined 25 percent of their match fees and Sri Lanka the maximum 50 percent.

Match referee Dean Cosker imposed the sanctions after charges were brought by the on-field umpires. Captains Babar Azam and Kusal Mendis pleaded guilty and accepted the sanctions, avoiding formal hearings. Pakistan have slipped to four points with a points percentage of about 3.33, while Sri Lanka have dropped to eighth place with about 15.48 percent.

The penalties add to Pakistan's growing problems with over-rate violations in the current championship cycle.
