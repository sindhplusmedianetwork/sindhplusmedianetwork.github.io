Marvell to Buy Lumentum in $115 Billion All-Stock Deal for AI Data Centres

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read

US artificial intelligence chipmaker Marvell has announced the acquisition of optical technology specialist Lumentum in an all-stock deal worth about $115 billion in enterprise value. Under the agreement, Lumentum shareholders will receive 4.5 Marvell shares for each share they hold, implying a price of roughly $1,132 per share — a premium of about 23.9 percent over the October 28 closing price. The companies made the joint announcement on November 2 and said the deal should close in the first half of 2028, subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals.

If completed, it would be Marvell's biggest purchase ever, more than ten times its $10 billion acquisition of Inphi in 2021. The combined company would have annual revenue of more than $21 billion and is expected to generate over $1 billion a year in synergies. Marvell chairman and chief executive Matt Murphy called it a once-in-a-generation opportunity, saying data centres are rapidly shifting from copper wiring to optical connections.

Marvell's existing shareholders would own about 67 percent of the new company, with Lumentum's investors holding the remaining 33 percent. Reuters reports the agreement includes break fees of up to $5.8 billion and a $3.2 billion fee if regulators block the deal. Investors reacted cautiously, with Marvell shares falling about 13 percent and Lumentum's sliding nearly 19 percent after the announcement.
