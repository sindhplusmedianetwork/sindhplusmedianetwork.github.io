SpaceX launches 15th Starship flight aiming for first round trip to orbit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

SpaceX has launched the 15th test flight of its Starship spacecraft from Texas. The mission aimed for the first round-trip rocket voyage into orbit and back to its launch site. The upper stage of the spacecraft reached orbit and was due to return to the launch tower on Saturday for a catch landing.

The Super Heavy booster, however, was diverted from its return path and exploded over the Gulf of Mexico. The mission also delivered 26 Starlink satellites into orbit. It was only the second Starship flight to carry an operational payload.

The flight is part of SpaceX's programme to develop a fully reusable rocket system.
