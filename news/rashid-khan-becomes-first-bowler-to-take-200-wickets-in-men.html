Rashid Khan Becomes First Bowler to Take 200 Wickets in Men's T20Is

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rashid Khan has set a new benchmark in T20 international cricket. The former Afghanistan captain became the first bowler to take 200 wickets in men's T20 internationals during Afghanistan's second T20 against Zimbabwe in Sharjah. The 28-year-old leg-spinner reached the milestone in his 120th T20I.

He averages 13.96 with an economy rate of 6.13, remarkable figures over such a long career. He now sits 29 wickets clear of the next name on the list, England's Adil Rashid. The record cements his place among the greatest T20 bowlers of all time.
