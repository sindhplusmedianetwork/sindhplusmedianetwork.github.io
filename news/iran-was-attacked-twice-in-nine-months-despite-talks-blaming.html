Iran was attacked twice in nine months despite talks, blaming Tehran is unjust: Ismail Baghai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Ismail Baghai has said Iran was attacked twice in less than nine months despite ongoing negotiations, so blaming Tehran for the conflict is unjust. He said schoolchildren in Minab and young women in Lamerd were targeted, Iranian leaders were killed and sanctions were tightened. Those who started the war cannot present themselves as the victim, he added.

A UN fact-finding mission said in September that it found reasonable grounds regarding US war crimes in February strikes on a Minab school and the Lamerd sports center.
