Makkah Defence Alliance secretary general to assume duties in Riyadh soon, says Foreign Office

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Foreign Office on Friday said that the secretary general of the Makkah Defence Alliance, Lieutenant General (retd) Nauman Mahmood, is expected to assume his responsibilities in Riyadh soon. Mahmood was appointed as the first secretary general of the alliance between Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye on September 23. Speaking at the Foreign Office's weekly press briefing, spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan also touched upon the crisis in the Gulf, saying that Pakistan would continue its efforts to facilitate dialogue in the conflict involving Iran and the United States.

He said Islamabad had brought the two parties under one roof despite their having remained out of contact for decades and supports a peaceful resolution through dialogue and diplomacy, adding that Pakistan, Qatar and other countries were actively contributing to mediation efforts. He said Israel had played the role of a spoiler in the conflict and criticised Israel's expansionist policies, saying peace in the region was not in Israel's interest and that its expansionist ambitions were among the major causes of the current regional crisis. When asked about the recent midterm elections in the United States, the spokesperson said elections were an internal matter of that country and reiterated that Pakistan maintained close relations with Washington.
