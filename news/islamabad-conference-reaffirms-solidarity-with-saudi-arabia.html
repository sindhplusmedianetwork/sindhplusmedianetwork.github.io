Islamabad Conference Reaffirms Solidarity with Saudi Arabia and Sanctity of Haramain Sharifain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A conference on the honour and sanctity of the Haramain Sharifain was held in Islamabad under the joint organisation of the Pakistan Ulema Council and the International Tazeem-e-Haramain Sharifain Council. The participants reaffirmed full solidarity with the security, sovereignty and stability of Saudi Arabia, and pledged a collective commitment to safeguarding the sanctity of the two holy sanctuaries. The declaration adopted at the conference strongly condemned the attacks carried out by the Houthi armed group against Saudi Arabia, civilian populations, airports and pilgrims.

It also endorsed the ongoing defence cooperation between Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye. Religious scholars and political leaders attending the conference paid tribute to Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir for his efforts towards Muslim unity and global peace.
