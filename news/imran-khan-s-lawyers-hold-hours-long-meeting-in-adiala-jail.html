Imran Khan's lawyers hold hours-long meeting in Adiala jail after court order

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader and lawyer Sardar Latif Khosa said on Wednesday, after an hours-long meeting with party founder Imran Khan in Adiala jail, that his blood pressure was at a frightening level but the former prime minister was still alert and strong. Khosa and Barrister Salman Safdar held the meeting with Imran for three to four hours in accordance with an Islamabad High Court order, and Bushra Bibi was also present. On Tuesday, the court had ordered a one-hour jail meeting between Imran and his wife and their lawyers in the £190 million case, directing counsel to begin arguments on the appeals at the next hearing.

After the meeting, Khosa said they had discussed the couple's health and legal cases, adding that the party would provide further details on Thursday about what the two lawyers saw and what the couple told them. He said Imran's blood pressure and eye issues were still present, but he was completely alert and his spirit was strong. Khosa said the possible 28th constitutional amendment was also discussed but did not divulge further details.

Separately, Safdar said on X that the legal and medical issues of both were discussed in detail and comprehensive legal instructions were given by both, adding that the meeting lasted nearly three hours, from 4pm to 7pm. Access to Imran and Bushra Bibi at Adiala Jail has remained restricted, with meetings between the PTI founder and his lawyers becoming increasingly infrequent, and authorities have denied weekly meetings between Imran and his family and legal team since December 2025.
