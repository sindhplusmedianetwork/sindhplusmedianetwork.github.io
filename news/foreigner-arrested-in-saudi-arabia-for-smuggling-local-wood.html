Foreigner Arrested in Saudi Arabia for Smuggling Local Wood

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi authorities have arrested a foreign national involved in smuggling local wood, the environmental protection special forces said. According to Saudi newspaper Sada, the special forces for environmental protection arrested a foreign resident for violating environmental regulations by transporting 27 cubic metres of locally sourced wood inside the Imam Faisal bin Turki Royal Reserve. Authorities said legal proceedings have been started against the accused and the seized wood has been handed over to the relevant authorities.

The forces said the fine for transporting local wood or coal can reach up to 16,000 Saudi riyals per cubic metre. The forces appealed to the public to report any activity that harms the environment or wildlife to the relevant authorities so that timely action can be taken against offenders, adding that all information will be kept fully confidential and informants will face no legal liability. Saudi authorities had announced earlier in the day that five foreign nationals had been detained in Al Sharqiyah for violating environmental regulations, including illegal excavation of soil.
