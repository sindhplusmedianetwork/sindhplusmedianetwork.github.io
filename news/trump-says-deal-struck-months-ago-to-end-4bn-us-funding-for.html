Trump says deal struck 'months ago' to end $4bn US funding for Israel

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said he made a deal with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu months ago to stop roughly $4 billion in annual US funding to Israel after the end of 2026. In an interview with Fox News, Trump said Israel no longer needed the money, and argued that Democrats were using the funding as a talking point to criticise Republicans. He stated plainly that the agreement with Netanyahu had been reached months earlier.

Netanyahu's office did not immediately respond to requests for comment. It was also not clear precisely which US funding stream Trump was referring to in his remarks.
