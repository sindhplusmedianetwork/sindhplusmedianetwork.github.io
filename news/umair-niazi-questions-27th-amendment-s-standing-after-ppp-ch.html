Umair Niazi Questions 27th Amendment's Standing After PPP Chief's Remarks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI MNA Barrister Umair Niazi has questioned what standing the 27th Amendment retains after the PPP chief made major remarks about it. Speaking on Geo News programme Capital Talk, Niazi said Bilawal Bhutto had spoken about flaws in the whole system and admitted that mistakes were made. He said the PPP could not absolve itself by raising the issue now.

Niazi added that remarks from within the ruling coalition had exposed those involved. His comments reflected the opposition's criticism of the PPP's position on the amendment.
