One Killed, Two Injured as Part of Building Collapses in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least one person died and two others were injured after a portion of a two-storey building collapsed in Karachi, Rescue 1122 said on Thursday. The incident happened near the New Nazimabad Flyover on Manghopir Road. The roof of a small confectionery shop collapsed during ongoing construction work at the site.

Three people were rescued from the debris after the collapse. The deceased was identified as 45-year-old Abdul Bari. Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah took notice of the incident and directed speedy rescue work.

He also sought a report from the Sindh Building Control Authority on the matter.
