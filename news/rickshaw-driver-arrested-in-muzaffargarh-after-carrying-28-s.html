Rickshaw driver arrested in Muzaffargarh after carrying 28 students

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Alipur tehsil of Muzaffargarh stopped a rickshaw driver who had packed 28 students into his rickshaw. The driver was fined on the spot and arrested. Video of the incident showed students crowded inside the rickshaw and sitting on its roof.

Police helped the children climb down from the vehicle. Police said the driver had put the children's lives at risk by carrying far more passengers than the rickshaw could hold. The incident was reported in the early hours of November 14.
