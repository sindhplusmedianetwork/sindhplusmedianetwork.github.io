PPP Was the 'Last Party' to Back Governor's Rule in K-P, Says Sharjeel Memon

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon said on Monday that the Pakistan Peoples Party was the 'last party' to support governor's rule and the imposition of emergency in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, adding that the party had advocated political dialogue and engagement as the first course of action. He made the remarks during a visit to the Green Line BRT Operations Command and Control Centre in Karachi. Memon said the Green Line service now carries around 90,000 passengers daily, up from about 50,000 under the federal government.

He said Sindh aimed to begin road construction in every district within six to eight months. He also said Sindh had made significant progress in public transport over four to five years, including electric scooters, Pakistan's first electric buses and Pink Buses for women.
