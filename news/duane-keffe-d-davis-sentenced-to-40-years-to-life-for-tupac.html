Duane 'Keffe D' Davis Sentenced to 40 Years to Life for Tupac Shakur Murder

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Duane 'Keffe D' Davis, 63, has been sentenced to 40 years to life in prison for orchestrating the 1996 drive-by shooting that killed hip-hop star Tupac Shakur. A Las Vegas judge, Carli Kierny, handed down two consecutive 20-year terms on October 13 after a jury convicted Davis of first-degree murder with a deadly weapon on August 31. Prosecutors said Davis masterminded the September 7, 1996 shooting to avenge the beating of his nephew Orlando Anderson by Shakur and his entourage inside the MGM Grand casino hours earlier.

Shakur, 25, was shot four times while riding in an SUV driven by record executive Marion 'Suge' Knight and died in hospital six days later. Davis is the only person ever charged in the killing, which stood as one of America's most notorious cold cases for nearly three decades. During the sentencing hearing, Shakur's younger sister Sekyiwa Shakur gave tearful testimony about her brother's legacy.

Davis has said he will appeal the conviction.
