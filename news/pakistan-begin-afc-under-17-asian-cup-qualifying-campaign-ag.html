Pakistan Begin AFC Under-17 Asian Cup Qualifying Campaign Against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan will open their Group D campaign against Sri Lanka on Sunday as they begin their AFC Under-17 Asian Cup 2027 qualifying campaign in Vientiane, Laos. Head coach Mohammad Habib said he was satisfied with his side's preparations after a series of intense training sessions. The players were selected from the recent National Under-17 Championship, and Habib said the team would look to give it their best.

Speaking at the pre-tournament press conference, he said the side had prepared well and was looking to give its best shot. Habib acknowledged the challenge ahead, noting that Pakistan were up against strong Asian sides such as Iran and Jordan, but said he had a promising group of players who were eager to do well and shine at this stage. After Sri Lanka, Pakistan will also face Iran and hosts Laos in the group.
