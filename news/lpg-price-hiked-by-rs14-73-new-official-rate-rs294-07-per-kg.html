LPG price hiked by Rs14.73; new official rate Rs294.07 per kg for November

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority has notified an increase of Rs14.73 per kilogram in liquefied petroleum gas prices for November 2026, taking the new official rate to Rs294.07 per kg. With the revision, the price of an 11.8kg household cylinder has risen to Rs3,470.09, up from Rs3,296.23 in October. The new prices apply across the country from November 1 and have wiped out the limited relief consumers received the previous month.

The hike follows consistent increases in international crude oil and LPG prices. The report notes that despite the official rate, LPG continues to be sold at considerably higher prices in most areas because of black marketing and profiteering. Consumers have urged the government to enforce the official price list and take action against those charging above the notified rates.
