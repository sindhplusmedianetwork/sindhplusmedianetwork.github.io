Ecuador fishermen sue US over drone strike on their boat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ecuadorian fishermen have sued the US government in a federal court in Pennsylvania, seeking $5 million in damages and an end to such strikes. The case concerns their boat, La Negra Francisca Duarte II, which was allegedly hit by US drones near the Galapagos Islands on 17 March 2026. The fishermen say two small explosive-laden drones sank the boat and injured crew members.

They say they were then detained by US naval and coastguard personnel and taken to El Salvador. The fishermen argue they were not involved in drug smuggling and had already been cleared by an Ecuadorian Navy search. The US defence department had earlier denied involvement, and the US military declined to comment on the pending case.
