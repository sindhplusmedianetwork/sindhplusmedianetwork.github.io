Saudi coalition says intercepted Houthi missile, drones

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The military coalition led by Saudi Arabia fighting Yemen's Houthis said it had intercepted two drones and a ballistic missile fired toward the country's southern region. Coalition spokesperson Turki al-Malki said that air defences intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile launched by the Houthi terrorist militia toward the southern region. Two Houthi drones fired at the kingdom's southern region were also shot down, al-Malki said in X posts.

The coalition's announcement was published on Friday, November 20, 2026. Earlier the same morning, Saudi Arabia's Civil Defence issued two early warning alerts before withdrawing them quickly in the southwestern city of Abha and the nearby Khamis Mushait governorate, according to Civil Defence posts on X. These two alerts were the second and third of the day.
