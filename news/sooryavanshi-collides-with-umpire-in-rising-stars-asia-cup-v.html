Sooryavanshi Collides With Umpire in Rising Stars Asia Cup; Video Goes Viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

During the Rising Stars Asia Cup match between Pakistan Shaheens and India A, 15-year-old Indian batter Vaibhav Sooryavanshi collided with the field umpire at the end of an over. Video of the incident is spreading rapidly on social media. The footage appears to show him turning towards the umpire deliberately after speaking with captain Tilak Varma.

The collision knocked the Indian bowler's cap and glasses from the umpire's hands, which Sooryavanshi then picked up and returned. Fans online are divided over whether the collision was deliberate or accidental. The incident has become a talking point of the tournament.
