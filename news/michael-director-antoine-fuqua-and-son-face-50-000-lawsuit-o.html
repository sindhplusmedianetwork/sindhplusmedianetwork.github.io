'Michael' Director Antoine Fuqua and Son Face $50,000 Lawsuit Over Alleged Bar Assault

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Filmmaker Antoine Fuqua and his son, Brando Fuqua, are facing a lawsuit alleging assault, battery and intentional infliction of emotional distress following an alleged confrontation at a Los Angeles bar. According to court documents obtained by People, William Rosenthal, an assistant bar manager at Good Times at Davey Wayne's, filed the lawsuit on Friday, November 20, seeking damages exceeding 50,000 dollars and a jury trial. The complaint alleges that the incident occurred on February 14, when Antoine, 61, allegedly struck Rosenthal in the face with a handgun before directing a racial and antisemitic slur at him and threatening to kill him.

Brando is also accused of approaching Rosenthal and forcefully swinging a handgun at his face, and the lawsuit further alleges that both defendants pointed their weapons at the bar manager. Rosenthal claims he suffered several injuries, including a head injury, fractured nose, skin lacerations, post-traumatic stress disorder and severe emotional distress, and alleges his injuries could result in permanent disability. Fuqua's representative has rejected the allegations, saying the claims are not true.

Fuqua is known for directing films including Training Day and The Equalizer, and he directed Michael, the 2026 biographical film about late singer Michael Jackson. The allegations remain claims made in the lawsuit, and no finding of liability has been established.
