'Fake intellectuals were hurt': Maryam Nawaz defends 'slap for slap' after Faisalabad assault

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has shared a video showing an assaulted shop worker in Faisalabad slapping the detained accused. She defended the act, saying it protects the weak and oppressed, and remarked that fake intellectuals were hurt by it. The video became a subject of debate on social media.

Critics questioned whether the public humiliation of an accused person in police custody follows the rule of law. Maryam Nawaz had earlier defended the same slap-for-slap stance at an event in Mandi Bahauddin, saying she does not care about cases against her over the remark. Rights advocates, including the Human Rights Commission of Pakistan, have said the law does not allow the torture of detainees and that punishment is the courts' job.
