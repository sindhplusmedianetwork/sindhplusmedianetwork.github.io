Three terrorists killed in major security operation across Bannu

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bannu police, the Counter Terrorism Department and security forces have killed three notorious terrorists in a major joint operation in the Gareeda Shah Jehan, Kangar and adjoining villages of Bannu. The operation, carried out under the name Sarbilandi, targeted the hideouts of the Zargul alias Uncle group and people accused of facilitating them, and weapons and ammunition were recovered during the action. Police said the three men belonged to the local commander Zargul alias Uncle's group of a banned organisation, and that one of them was Zargul's nephew.

According to police, they were involved in attacks on police and security forces, the heads of local peace committees and the general public, as well as extortion, looting and other serious crimes. DIG Bannu said several suspected facilitators were also taken into custody during the operation, which he credited to close coordination between the police, the security forces and the Counter Terrorism Department. Inspector General of Police Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Zulfiqar Hamid praised the personnel involved and said joint operations to protect the public and uphold the rule of law would be stepped up further.
