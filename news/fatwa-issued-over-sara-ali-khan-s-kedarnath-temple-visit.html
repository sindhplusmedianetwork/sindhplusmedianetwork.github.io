Fatwa Issued Over Sara Ali Khan's Kedarnath Temple Visit

A religious controversy has erupted over Bollywood actress Sara Ali Khan's visit to the Kedarnath temple in Uttarakhand. Bareilly cleric Mufti Shahabuddin Rizvi Barelvi declared her participation in temple worship and rituals un-Islamic and forbidden under Shariah. The national president of the All India Muslim Jamaat, speaking to ANI, said he had received many questions about her temple visit before stating his religious position.

He advised the actress to seek repentance from Allah, saying that if she took part of her own free will, she should ask for forgiveness. Sara's connection with Kedarnath dates back to her 2018 debut film 'Kedarnath,' and media reports say she also performed rituals at the nearby Bhukunt Bhairav temple during her recent visit.
