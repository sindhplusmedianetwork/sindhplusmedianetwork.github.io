Video of Peshawar Policeman Assaulting Sweeper Goes Viral; SSP Orders Probe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An alleged incident of police misconduct has sparked public outrage in Peshawar after a police officer was accused of physically assaulting a sweeper over a minor issue at Khyber Super Market, a busy commercial area in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa capital. A video of the incident has gone viral on social media, drawing widespread criticism and calls for accountability. According to reports and the viral footage, the incident occurred while the sweeper was cleaning a building; during the cleaning, water reportedly flowed onto the road, prompting the police officer on duty to react angrily.

The footage appears to show the officer grabbing the sweeper by the hair, dragging him and pushing him, raising concerns about the alleged misuse of authority by a law enforcement official. Following the circulation of the video and the public reaction, SSP Operations Farhan Khan ordered an immediate investigation into the incident. Police officials said the matter would be thoroughly investigated and that departmental and legal action would be taken against the officer concerned if the allegations were established.

The investigation is expected to examine the circumstances surrounding the incident and determine responsibility, with police officials saying appropriate measures would be taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.
