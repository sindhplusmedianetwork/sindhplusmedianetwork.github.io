Shehbaz Sharif, Asim Munir Jointly Top World's 'Most Influential Muslims' List; Imran Khan Ranked 24th

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Field Marshal Asim Munir have been jointly ranked number one in the annual 'The Muslim 500' list of the world's 500 most influential Muslims. The list is published by Jordan's Royal Islamic Strategic Studies Centre. They share the top spot with Saudi King Salman, Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan.

It is the first time five people have jointly held first place, credited to the Makkah joint defence agreement signed on 7 August 2026. Former prime minister Imran Khan is also on the list, ranked 24th among rulers and politicians.
