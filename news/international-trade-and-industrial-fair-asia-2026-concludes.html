International Trade and Industrial Fair Asia 2026 Concludes Successfully

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Trade and Industrial Fair Asia 2026 has concluded successfully. The exhibition was organised with the cooperation of the Special Investment Facilitation Council and E-Commerce Gateway. It drew 23 exhibitors from six countries and 23,000 professionals.

Stalls featuring modern machinery, solar energy, electric vehicles and safety technology remained the focus of visitors' attention. During the three-day exhibition, memorandums of understanding, agreements and business deals worth about 35 million dollars were finalised. The organisers said the fair boosted the confidence of global investors in Pakistan and led to a marked increase in industrial activity in the country.
