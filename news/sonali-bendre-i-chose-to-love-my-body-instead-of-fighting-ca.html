Sonali Bendre: I Chose to Love My Body Instead of Fighting Cancer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Sonali Bendre has opened up about the mindset that carried her through her battle with cancer, saying she chose to love her body instead of fighting it. Speaking on the premiere episode of Kon Honar Crorepati, the Marathi version of Kaun Banega Crorepati hosted by Madhuri Dixit, Bendre said she never saw cancer as a battle to be won. Instead, she tried to understand why it was happening and what she could learn from the experience.

“These cancer cells are our own cells. So, who are we really fighting against?” she asked, adding, “how do I love my body the way it is?” She admitted it was very difficult to love something causing her so much pain, but said that approach is what worked for her. Bendre was diagnosed with stage IV metastatic cancer in 2018 and underwent extensive treatment, including chemotherapy and surgery, in New York.

The show began streaming on October 12 on Sony Marathi and Sony LIV.
