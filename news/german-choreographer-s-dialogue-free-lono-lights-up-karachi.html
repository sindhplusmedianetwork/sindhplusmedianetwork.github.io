German Choreographer's Dialogue-Free 'LONO' Lights Up Karachi Culture Festival

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 12th day of the 25-day Third World Culture Festival Karachi 2026 featured a diverse programme of sculpture, music, cinema, dance and theatre. The festival is organised by the Arts Council of Pakistan Karachi. The day concluded with a 45-minute performance titled "LONO" by German choreographer Sara Angius.

The production combined contemporary dance, physical theatre, puppetry and visual illusion. Presented without dialogue, it explored consciousness, memory and perceptions of the past and future. The story unfolded through a woman and a table lamp.
