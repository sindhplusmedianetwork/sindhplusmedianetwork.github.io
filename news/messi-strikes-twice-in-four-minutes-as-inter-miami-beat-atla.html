Messi Strikes Twice in Four Minutes as Inter Miami Beat Atlanta for Third Straight Win

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Inter Miami continued their winning run in Major League Soccer with a 3-1 victory over Atlanta United, powered by two goals from Lionel Messi within four minutes. Germán Berterame gave Miami the lead in the 45th minute after a move involving Messi and Luis Suárez. Messi then doubled the advantage in the 56th minute, finishing with his left foot after a pass from Berterame.

Four minutes later, he beat defender Júnior Alonso and rounded goalkeeper Lucas Hoyos to make it 3-0. The 39-year-old has now scored 25 goals this season, six ahead of Petar Musa and Milan Iloski in the scoring charts. He also became the first player in MLS history to register at least 15 assists in three consecutive seasons.

Since his emotional farewell from the Argentina national team, Messi has scored four goals in three matches. Under coach Cristian 'Keli' González, who took charge last month, Inter Miami have now won three matches in a row.
