Trump Invokes 9/11 in Urging Voters to Oppose El-Sayed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump, at a Sunday campaign rally in Port Huron, Michigan, urged voters to oppose Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed, comparing him to the attackers behind the September 11, 2001 attacks. Trump told the audience he was there to keep a jihadist, Muslim Brotherhood member from becoming a United States senator, and likened voting against El-Sayed to the actions of Michigan native Todd Beamer and others who fought back against the Flight 93 hijackers. If El-Sayed wins, he would be the first Muslim elected to the US Senate.

Michigan has one of the most competitive Senate contests ahead of the November 3 midterm elections, with El-Sayed vying against Republican former representative Mike Rogers for an open seat that will help decide control of the chamber. El-Sayed has denied any affiliation with the Muslim Brotherhood, calling it shameful for Rogers to question his allegiance to the United States. Rogers, speaking on NBC's Meet the Press, said he has never called El-Sayed a terrorist.

The Michigan race drew added attention after former president Barack Obama campaigned for Democrats in Detroit a day earlier, framing the attacks on El-Sayed as an attempt to make him look less American.
