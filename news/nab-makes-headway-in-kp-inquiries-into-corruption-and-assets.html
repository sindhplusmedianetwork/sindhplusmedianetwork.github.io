NAB makes headway in KP inquiries into corruption and assets beyond income

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Accountability Bureau has made significant progress in two separate inquiries in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa into corruption and assets beyond known sources of income linked to PTI leadership, issuing multiple notices on November 13. In the inquiry concerning Asad Qaiser, NAB sought details of partnership firms, deeds and business addresses registered in the names of 10 people. In the inquiry against former special assistant Dr Amjad Ali, NAB summoned nine people as witnesses.

Five of them were summoned over payments totalling Rs 24.4 million for a property in Islamabad's Eighteen Society. The two inquiries are being pursued separately in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
