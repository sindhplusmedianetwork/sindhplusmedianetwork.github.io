Investors prefer Pakistan to stay under IMF programme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

International investors are comfortable with Pakistan remaining under the International Monetary Fund programme, senior Standard Chartered officials have said. They view the programme as an anchor for policy discipline and macroeconomic stability, and favour its continuation to ensure policy continuity. The officials also flagged risks from conflict in the Middle East, which could affect energy markets.

They warned that Pakistan must demonstrate discipline beyond the programme itself to keep investor confidence. At the same time, they noted that development and social-sector spending has suffered under fiscal consolidation. Remittances, however, remain a positive source of external financing for the country.

The remarks underline how closely investors are watching Pakistan's economic policy choices in the months ahead.
