Pakistan and Turkiye Sign Energy Cooperation Agreements, Begin New Chapter of Friendship

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Turkiye have signed cooperation agreements to expand collaboration in offshore oil and gas exploration, development of key mineral deposits and the energy sector. A reception ceremony was held at Karachi Port on the arrival of the Turkish research vessel, attended by Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, the Turkish energy minister, the federal petroleum minister and the Sindh governor, where the energy cooperation agreements between Pakistan and Turkiye were signed. A shareholders' agreement was finalised between Pakistan Mineral Development Corporation and Turkiye's MTA International, an agreement was signed for the development of the key mineral antimony in Pishin, Balochistan, and a joint venture agreement was finalised between Mari Minerals and Turkiye's MTAIC for exploration and development of copper, gold and other mineral deposits in Chagai, Balochistan.

A tripartite memorandum of understanding was also signed among the Geological Survey of Pakistan, Turkiye's MTA and MTAIC, agreeing on technical exchange and capacity building in the development of mineral resources. Addressing the ceremony, Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar said relations between Pakistan and Turkiye are based on historical foundations, but the time has come to turn this friendship into practical economic projects and development opportunities for the people. He said the 87-metre-long Oruc Reis is equipped with modern seismic, acoustic and seabed survey instruments and will examine the structure of rocks beneath the seabed through acoustic waves, while according to Turkish officials the survey may take about five to six months.

He clarified that offshore energy exploration is a complex and expensive process and a seismic survey does not guarantee discovery of reserves, but it provides basic information for future exploration, and he invited Turkish investors to invest in Pakistan, saying the government will provide every possible facility in important projects. Turkish Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar said wide opportunities exist for cooperation between Pakistan and Turkiye in the energy sector and a new era in energy relations between the two countries is beginning.
