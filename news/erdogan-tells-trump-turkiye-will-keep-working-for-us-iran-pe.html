Erdogan Tells Trump Turkiye Will Keep Working for US-Iran Peace

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan has told US President Donald Trump by telephone that Ankara will continue its peace efforts and that tensions between Iran and the United States should be resolved through diplomacy, according to a readout from Erdogan's office. The two leaders spoke on Saturday and also discussed bilateral relations between Turkiye and the United States, as well as regional and global developments. Erdogan said he believed cooperation between the two countries would continue to grow and that they would work to give relations fresh momentum in the period ahead.

He also said Turkiye would keep contributing to efforts to end the war in Ukraine and that contacts with both Kyiv and Moscow should be intensified. During the call, Erdogan thanked Trump for his support for the summit of the Organisation of Turkic States. The Turkish presidency did not disclose Trump's responses to the proposals or announce any specific agreements resulting from the conversation.
