Balochistan Rejects Comparison With K-P, Says It Is Cooperating With Centre Against Terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Balochistan government on Tuesday pushed back against comparisons with the former Khyber Pakhtunkhwa administration, insisting it was working closely with the federal government against terrorism. Home Minister Mir Ziaullah Langau said Balochistan, unlike the previous K-P government, was fully cooperating with the Centre in counterterrorism efforts. Additional Chief Secretary (Home) Hamza Shafqaat told a briefing that security forces had conducted more than 60,000 intelligence-based operations and raids over the past two years.

These operations killed 1,100 suspected terrorists and led to 450 arrests, he said. However, the province also paid a heavy price: more than 300 security personnel and 551 civilians lost their lives in terrorist attacks during the same period. The statement comes amid heightened political debate over the security situation in the provinces.
