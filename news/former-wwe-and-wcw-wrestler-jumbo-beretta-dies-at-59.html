Former WWE and WCW Wrestler Jumbo Beretta Dies at 59

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former WWE and WCW wrestler Jumbo Beretta, whose real name was Julio Barrera, has died at the age of 59. The Cauliflower Alley Club announced his passing on October 25 with an emotional tribute, calling him a friend they had known for nearly four decades. Beretta began his professional wrestling career in 1986 after training under the legendary Boris Malenko.

He went on to compete for WWE, WCW, the World Wrestling Council and the NWA, facing stars such as Sting and The Road Warriors. Standing six feet five inches and weighing around 170 kilograms, he was known as a towering powerhouse of the ring. His final recorded match took place in 2013.

The cause of his death has not been publicly disclosed, and tributes from fans and fellow wrestlers have poured in on social media.
