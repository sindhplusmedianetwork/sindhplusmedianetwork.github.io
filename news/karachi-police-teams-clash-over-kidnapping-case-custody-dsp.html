Karachi Police Teams Clash Over Kidnapping Case Custody; DSP Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A dispute between two Karachi police teams over the custody of suspects turned violent in the city's North Nazimabad area. The disagreement began after the District Central police and the SIU differed over the handling of suspects in an alleged short-term kidnapping case of a young man. The Taimuria police had taken the suspects into custody, but both teams then clashed over who would retain them.

During the confrontation, DSP Shadman sustained a head injury. Talks were later held at the Nazimabad police station to settle the matter. The SHO dismissed reports about the incident as speculation.
