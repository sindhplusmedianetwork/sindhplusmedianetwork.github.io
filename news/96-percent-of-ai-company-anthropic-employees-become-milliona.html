96 Percent of AI Company Anthropic Employees Become Millionaires Overnight

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The start of sale of shares of well-known artificial intelligence company Anthropic in the stock market made most of the company's employees millionaires overnight, while the wealth of hundreds of employees exceeded $100 million. According to a CNN report, as of September this year the number of Anthropic employees was 5,300 and the company fixed the price of its shares at $83 per share under its initial public offering on 19 November 2026, after which the value of shares held by employees increased significantly. According to data from Hill, an institution estimating the value of shares of private companies, about 600 employees have shares worth more than $100 million, joining the list of extremely rich millionaires.

According to the data, about 2,800 employees of the company have shares worth more than $10 million, while about 5,100 employees, that is 96 percent of the total staff, have shares worth more than $1 million. Hill founder and chief executive Andrew Benson said the most surprising thing in this development is that all this happened extremely quickly, and according to him the rate of employees leaving jobs at Anthropic remained extremely low because people estimated the company was growing rapidly and leaving it could deprive them of a big financial opportunity. The report also gave the example of US space company SpaceX, which worked as a private company for more than two decades before starting sale of shares in the stock market in June, and about 4,400 employees became millionaires from its IPO while the number of company employees at that time was 22,000.

Anthropic's IPO was also unique in that besides the company's founders, other insider investors and employees were given the opportunity to sell their shares on the very first day, whereas usually after an IPO employees are not allowed to sell shares for three to six months, called the lock-up period, but Anthropic introduced a phased system under which insider investors and employees could sell up to 10 percent of their shares on the first day.
