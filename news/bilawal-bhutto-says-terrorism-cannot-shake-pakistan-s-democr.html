Bilawal Bhutto Says Terrorism Cannot Shake Pakistan's Democratic Resolve on Karsaz Anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari paid tribute to the martyrs of the October 18 Karsaz tragedy on its anniversary on Sunday. He called October 18 one of the darkest days in Pakistan's history, recalling the bombing that struck a homecoming rally in Karachi. Bilawal said the blood of the Karsaz martyrs testifies that terrorism, extremism and violence cannot shake democracy's resolve.

He vowed that his party would continue its struggle for constitutional supremacy, democratic institutions and the rights of the people. The PPP chairman also prayed for the martyrs and for patience for their bereaved families. The Karsaz bombing targeted the rally welcoming Benazir Bhutto on her return to Pakistan, and its anniversary is observed every year by the party.
