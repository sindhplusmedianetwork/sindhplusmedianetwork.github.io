A Few Minutes of Daily Meditation Can Reshape Brain Circuits, Experts Say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

American experts say that just a few minutes of daily meditation over two months can produce major changes in brain circuits. With busy lives, constant work and growing social media use, mental fatigue and stress have become part of many people's daily routines. Meditation is described as a practice that can help calm the mind, improve focus and manage emotions.

Morning is considered especially suitable for it, because the start of the day is usually relatively quieter and less hectic. Sitting quietly for a few minutes and focusing on the breath can create a sense of mental calm, and may help some people prepare themselves mentally for the day's activities. The report notes that meditation does not guarantee the complete removal of negative thoughts, but it can help a person pay attention to those thoughts and better understand their own reactions.

It may also assist in maintaining focus through the day's tasks, messages and responsibilities. For those starting out, three to ten minutes of daily meditation is considered a reasonable duration, which can be increased over time as needed. Experts advise that regular practice and the habit of bringing attention back to the present moment matter more than long sessions.

However, meditation is only a supportive habit for mental peace; if stress persists, disrupts daily life or anxiety becomes severe, consulting a health professional is also necessary.
