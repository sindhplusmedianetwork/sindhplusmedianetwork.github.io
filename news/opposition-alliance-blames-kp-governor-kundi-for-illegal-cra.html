Opposition alliance blames KP Governor Kundi for 'illegal crackdown' on PTI

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The opposition alliance has accused Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi of involvement in what it called an illegal crackdown against PTI. According to the alliance, the people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are being targeted as political revenge, and the governor is involved in the crackdown. The alliance said the Pakistan Peoples Party could not distance itself from the political crackdown.

It also announced a six-member committee to prepare for an all-parties conference. The alliance reaffirmed its commitment to a joint struggle for the supremacy of the Constitution and the sovereignty of Parliament. Meanwhile, the federal government issued a gazette notification about the KP governor's powers, which came into force immediately.
