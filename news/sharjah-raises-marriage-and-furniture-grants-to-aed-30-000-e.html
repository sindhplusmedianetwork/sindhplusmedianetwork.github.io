Sharjah raises marriage and furniture grants to AED 30,000 each

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sharjah's ruler Sheikh Dr Sultan bin Mohammed Al Qasimi, a member of the Supreme Council of the United Arab Emirates, has increased the government marriage grant and home furniture grant for citizens, raising both grants to AED 30,000 each. The orders have come into immediate effect. The aim of the move is to provide financial ease to citizen families in the initial expenses of marriage and setting up a home.

The ruler of Sharjah has also directed a change in the salary-related eligibility criteria for obtaining the marriage and furniture grants. Under the new rule, applicants whose monthly salary is less than AED 50,000 will now also be eligible for these grants. The decision to change the salary limit was taken keeping in view the recent increase in the salaries of Sharjah government employees, so that citizens' eligibility for the grants remains in line with the new salaries.

According to the Sharjah government, the increase in the marriage and furniture grants aims to reduce the financial burden on citizen families caused by the rise in the prices of essential goods and daily expenses. The assistance will help the eligible beneficiaries of the Sharjah Housing Department's residential support meet their marriage and home furniture expenses, and the authorities say the move will help meet families' needs and improve their economic stability.
