Punjab passes new law on stage dramas, opposition criticises

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Assembly has passed 'The Punjab Dramatic and Theatrical Performance' bill by majority, making artist registration, prior script approval, theatre licences and monitoring of performances mandatory. Under the new law, no drama or theatre performance can be held in the province without a government licence and approval of its script by the relevant authorities. The government will set up one or more script review committees, and scripts must be sent to a committee at least 15 days before a programme.

Plays deemed obscene, defamatory, hurtful to religious sentiments, inciting hatred or creating hatred against the government will not be permitted. All artists, directors, producers, choreographers and others involved in a theatre or drama must be officially registered, and unregistered artists will not be able to take part in a performance. Violations can draw fines of 300,000 to 500,000 rupees and up to six months' imprisonment, with fines of up to one million rupees for some offences.

Opposition members, including PTI-backed member Waqas Mahmood, criticised the bill as bringing art under complete government control, while Information Minister Azma Bukhari defended it, saying the purpose is monitoring and regulating the industry, not restriction.
