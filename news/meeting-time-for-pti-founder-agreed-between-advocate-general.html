Meeting Time for PTI Founder Agreed Between Advocate General Naveed Malik and Lawyers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Progress has been made on the meeting between the PTI founder and his lawyers, with the time for the meeting now fixed. The meeting at Adiala Jail will take place at 3pm tomorrow, 11 November. Advocate General Naveed Malik and the lawyers agreed on the timing of the meeting.

The meeting will be arranged under the order of the Islamabad High Court. Lawyers Latif Khosa and Salman Safdar will be among those meeting the PTI founder. The agreement on the time follows the court's directions on meetings with the PTI founder in jail.
