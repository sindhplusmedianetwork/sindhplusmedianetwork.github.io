'Smog aggravates pneumonia risk'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than one in four pneumonia deaths among people aged over 70 are attributable to air pollution and smoking, doctors warned at a workshop on World Pneumonia Day in Lahore. General Cadre Doctors Association President Dr Masood Sheikh said smog makes lungs vulnerable to infection and that wearing masks and avoiding outdoor exposure on high-AQI days is a medical necessity. He said the very young and very old are at greatest risk.

Dr Ahmad Qasim Khan said Lahore sees a surge in pneumonia cases from November to February due to smog, crowding and viral infections. The doctors urged people to take precautions as the winter smog season sets in.
