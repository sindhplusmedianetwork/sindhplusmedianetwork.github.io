Auto Policy Sets $6 Billion Export Target Over Five Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Saturday approved the draft Auto Policy 2026-31 after securing the IMF's nod, setting a cumulative export target of $6 billion for Pakistan's auto sector over five years. Adviser to the Prime Minister on Industries and Production Haroon Akhtar Khan, along with Information Minister Attaullah Tarar, announced at a news conference that the prime minister had approved the final draft of the Automotive Industry Policy. The government intends to finance incentives for electric vehicles and export incentives for the auto sector through an Automotive Development Levy on vehicles powered by internal combustion engines, which the adviser described as revenue-neutral and acknowledged by the IMF.

Vehicles with engine capacities of 1,000cc or below will be exempt from the levy, and the Ministry of Industries and Production had submitted the draft to the IMF team on Thursday before it was approved on Saturday. The policy will now be submitted to the federal cabinet for consideration and approval. Under the proposed framework, manufacturers will receive a 5 per cent drawback of local taxes and levies, along with an additional 10 per cent incentive on incremental annual export growth, but companies must meet prescribed export and localisation targets to qualify, while failure to achieve them could attract penalties.

The policy also tackles the issue of 'on-money' by making it mandatory for manufacturers not to demand more than 20 per cent of a vehicle's price as advance payment at booking, a measure aimed at protecting consumers and promoting transparency. Mr Khan said Pakistan currently has 16 car and SUV manufacturers and 100 two-wheeler manufacturers, and stressed that the government did not want the industry to remain dependent on imports of completely built units or merely assemble completely knocked-down kits without developing a strong domestic manufacturing base.
