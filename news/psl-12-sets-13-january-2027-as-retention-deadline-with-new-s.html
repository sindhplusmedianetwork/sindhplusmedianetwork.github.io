PSL 12 sets 13 January 2027 as retention deadline with new salary cap rules

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Super League has set 13 January 2027 as the deadline for player retentions ahead of its twelfth edition. The date was finalised at an operations workshop chaired by PSL chief executive Salman Naseer, where the event's framework was completed. All franchises agreed on the retention rules, player auction terms and squad sizes.

The retention date was set keeping the National T20 Cup schedule in view, so that local players can impress franchises with domestic performances. Each franchise may retain a maximum of seven players, but a franchise using the full seven must release its most expensive player from the previous auction. The salary cap for each franchise has been fixed at 450 million Pakistani rupees, about 1.6 million US dollars, rising to 505 million rupees for franchises that sign players directly.

Each franchise must spend at least 365 million rupees at the players' auction, and the direct-signing window remains open until 23 November. Salman Naseer said the day was important not only for what was agreed but for the spirit of consensus shown by all franchises.
