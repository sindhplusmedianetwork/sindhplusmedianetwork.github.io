ECP orders immediate local polls in KP, leaving out 12 districts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Election Commission of Pakistan has ordered local government elections to be held immediately in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa districts where delimitation work is complete. A five-member bench headed by Chief Election Commissioner Sikandar Sultan Raja passed the order after the provincial authorities sought a delay, citing the law and order situation, emergency measures and the deployment of troops under Article 245. Five districts — Bannu, Mohmand, Orakzai, Lakki Marwat and North Waziristan — have been excluded from the election programme.

Seven more districts — Dera Ismail Khan, Tank, Bajaur, Khyber, Kurram, Lower South Waziristan and Upper South Waziristan — have been excluded from the delimitation schedule. In the remaining districts, where delimitation stands completed, the Commission directed that the polls go ahead without delay. The order separates the province's districts into three groups: those ready for immediate polling, those held out of the election programme, and those still outside the delimitation schedule.
