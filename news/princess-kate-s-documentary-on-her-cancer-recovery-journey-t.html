Princess Kate's documentary on her cancer recovery journey to air on BBC on 29 November

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A documentary on British Princess Kate Middleton's fight against cancer and her journey to recovery will be broadcast on the BBC on 29 November. According to Reuters, the documentary shows Princess Kate speaking about the difficulties, fear and emotional moments she faced during her cancer treatment. The film shows her taking part in a challenge to climb Britain's three highest peaks within 24 hours to raise funds for a cancer charity.

During the challenge, her brother James Middleton played recorded messages from her three children, George, Charlotte and Louis, and on hearing them the princess was unable to control her emotions and began to cry. Kate said her children were a source of strength for her during her recovery. Through the documentary, she wants to give a message of hope and encouragement to cancer patients.
