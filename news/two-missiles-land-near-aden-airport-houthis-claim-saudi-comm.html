Two Missiles Land Near Aden Airport; Houthis Claim Saudi Command Centre Was Target

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two missiles landed near Aden International Airport in Yemen, according to AFP, sending loud blasts across the surrounding area. The Houthis claimed responsibility for the strike, saying the missiles were aimed at a Saudi command centre located near the airport. The site lies in territory controlled by the Saudi-backed Yemeni government, the group said.

No casualties or property damage were reported in the latest strike. The attack came a day after a Houthi strike on the presidential palace compound in Aden killed five people and wounded 16 others.
