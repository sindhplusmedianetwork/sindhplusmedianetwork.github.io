Pakistan, bad weather hold Sri Lanka to draw in first Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan held Sri Lanka to a draw in the first Test in Rawalpindi on Friday, with bad weather aiding the home side on the fifth and final day. Saud Shakeel led the resistance with a fighting 97, saving Pakistan after Sri Lanka had taken four wickets in the first session. Pakistan finished on 284-8, a lead of 272, and Sri Lanka ran out of time to force a result.

The visitors had pushed hard for victory in the morning session, but Shakeel's innings kept them at bay. The weather also played its part in denying Sri Lanka the overs they needed. The draw leaves the two-match series level heading into the second Test.

The second and final Test starts at the same venue on Tuesday.
