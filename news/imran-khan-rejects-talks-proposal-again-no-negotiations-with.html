Imran Khan rejects talks proposal again; no negotiations without apology, says government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Imran Khan's latest statement on the social media platform X effectively rejected the appeal for negotiations made by Mahmood Khan Achakzai, and PTI's information secretary made clear that Imran Khan's instructions on the protest movement will be final and will be followed. Sources close to Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif say meaningful negotiations with PTI are not possible without remorse or an apology over the 9 May incidents and the anti-military campaign on Imran Khan's and the party's social media. PTI information secretary Sheikh Waqas Akram said that since the policy on the protest movement came directly from party leader Imran Khan, it alone will be implemented, and that Achakzai may continue his political activities, including negotiations, but the PTI leadership is bound by Imran Khan's latest instructions.

According to Akram, Imran Khan has instructed the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister to prepare a protest movement for the rule of law and the supremacy of the constitution. At a national conference held the previous day by the opposition alliance, its chief Mahmood Khan Achakzai said the constitution had been torn to shreds and appealed to former prime minister Nawaz Sharif and Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman to step forward and start negotiations to pull the country out of the crisis. In the statement issued on X the same day, Imran Khan not only criticised the field marshal but also instructed Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi to prepare for the protest movement.

A senior source close to the prime minister, when asked how the government would respond to Achakzai's offer of dialogue, asked how meaningful negotiations with PTI could be possible while ignoring the serious crimes of 9 May and the party's campaign against the armed forces, adding that the government's position is that there can be no negotiations without remorse or an apology, as those involved attacked military installations, mocked martyrs and ran a character-assassination campaign against the army and its senior leadership.
