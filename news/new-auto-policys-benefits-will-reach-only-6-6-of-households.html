New auto policy’s benefits will reach only 6.6% of households, consultant warns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan’s new Auto Policy 2026-31 promises cheaper and safer cars, but its biggest benefits will go to a small, better-off group of car buyers, an independent policy consultant told a LENS virtual workshop on Saturday. Only 6.6 per cent of Pakistan’s roughly 40 million households own a car, while about 57 per cent own a motorcycle and 41 per cent own no vehicle at all. Under the plan, tariffs on built-up and knocked-down vehicles will fall in stages to 15 per cent by 2030-31.

Advance booking payments will be capped at 20 per cent of the price. An automotive development levy is expected to raise 232 billion rupees over five years.
