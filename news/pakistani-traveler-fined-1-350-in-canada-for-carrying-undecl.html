Pakistani traveler fined $1,350 in Canada for carrying undeclared goat trotters and food items

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Canada's Border Services Agency has fined a Pakistani traveler at Toronto's Pearson Airport after food items were found in his luggage. According to the agency, a trained detection dog flagged the traveler's bags, leading officers to discover the undeclared items. The search uncovered goat trotters, chicken meat, desi ghee and powdered milk, totaling 16 kilograms of food that had not been declared to customs officials.

The agency said the entire quantity of food was seized and the traveler was fined $1,350. Canadian border rules require all food, plant and animal products to be declared on arrival, and travelers can face penalties for failing to do so. The case came to light after the agency shared details on its social media.
