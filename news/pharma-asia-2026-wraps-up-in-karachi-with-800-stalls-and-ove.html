Pharma Asia 2026 Wraps Up in Karachi With 800 Stalls and Over 200 Foreign Delegates

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 24th Pharma Asia International Exhibition and Conferences has concluded at the Karachi Expo Centre after a three-day run that drew strong participation from local and foreign companies. Speaking at the event, PPMA Chairman Adnan Hirani said some 800 pharmaceutical players had set up their stalls and booths across seven halls, including more than 200 foreign delegates. This year's edition, held alongside the 14th International Pharma Convention under the theme 'Resilient Pharma Pakistan: Quality, Self-Reliance & Sustainable Growth', showcased the latest pharmaceutical technology, with industry leaders expecting a number of memorandums of understanding between local and global firms.

At a panel discussion, Neutro Pharma CEO Hamid Raza claimed that Pakistan's average life expectancy had improved by about 10 years between 1999 and 2026 due to improved access to and use of medicines. World Bank data shows life expectancy in Pakistan rose to 67.8 years in 2024 from 59.7 years in 1990. PPMA Senior Vice Chairman Dr Faisal Khokhar said the country is poised for another milestone, with a third pharmaceutical company expected to achieve Stringent Regulatory Authority status next year, potentially opening doors to export locally produced medicines to the US, UK, European Union, Japan, Canada and Australia.

He stressed that the capital-intensive sector needs to attract private equity and venture capital investment in line with global practices. Searle Company CEO Tahir Ahmed said this is the time to invest in biotechnology, adding that China can be a great help to Pakistan in value addition.
