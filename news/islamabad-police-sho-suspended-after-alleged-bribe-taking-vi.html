Islamabad Police SHO Suspended After Alleged Bribe-Taking Video Goes Viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Station House Officer of Sangjani Police Station in Islamabad was suspended on Thursday after a video allegedly showing him taking a bribe went viral on social media. In the footage, the officer can allegedly be seen receiving money in exchange for releasing a truck. Islamabad Inspector General of Police Muhammad Sohail Chaudhry took immediate notice of the viral video.

Islamabad DIG Ali Wasim suspended the SHO and directed SP Saddar Zone Kazim Naqvi to conduct a transparent inquiry, seeking an investigation report within 24 hours. Islamabad Police said all aspects of the incident would be examined and that legal action would be taken if evidence of misuse of authority or corruption was found. In the video, the police official is heard bargaining with a citizen, telling him to pay 3,000 or 4,000 rupees and adding that the normal challan would be 4,000 rupees.

The footage shows the official sitting in a vehicle, checking the driver's documents and then returning them, while calling the citizen 'brother' and insisting on receiving money. The citizen had cleverly recorded the entire conversation and the demand for payment on his mobile phone.
