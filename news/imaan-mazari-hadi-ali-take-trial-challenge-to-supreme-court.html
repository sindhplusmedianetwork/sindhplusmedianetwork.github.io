Imaan Mazari, Hadi Ali Take Trial Challenge to Supreme Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Human rights lawyers Imaan Mazari and her husband, advocate Hadi Ali Chattha, approached the Supreme Court on Monday against an Islamabad High Court decision concerning their request to halt trial proceedings in an anti-terrorism case. In their petition, the couple contended that unless the trial was immediately stayed, their pending application before the high court would become ineffective. They argued that the trial court had initiated proceedings in haste, without granting them adequate time to prepare their case.

According to the petition, the trial court recorded the statements of prosecution witnesses in a single day. The petition further said that removing security barriers did not constitute terrorism. Mazari, a lawyer and rights activist, and Chattha, an advocate, are facing trial in the anti-terrorism case at the centre of the dispute.
