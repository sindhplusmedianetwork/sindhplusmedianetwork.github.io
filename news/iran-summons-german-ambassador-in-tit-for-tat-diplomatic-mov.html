Iran Summons German Ambassador in Tit-for-Tat Diplomatic Move

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has summoned Germany's ambassador in Tehran in a tit-for-tat diplomatic move. The step came in response to Germany summoning the Iranian ambassador over an alleged Iranian role in a plot to kill pro-Israel figures, a charge Tehran called baseless. According to AFP, the row followed a German court sentencing two people for planning attacks on figures linked to Jewish organisations, with German authorities blaming Iran.

Iran's foreign ministry said giving a judicial colour to baseless political claims does not lend them credibility. With both countries now having summoned each other's envoys, concerns are growing about further diplomatic escalation between Tehran and Berlin.
