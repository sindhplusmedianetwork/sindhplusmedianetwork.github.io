Federal Constitutional Court Issues Notice in Alleged Fake-Degree Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A two-member bench of the Federal Constitutional Court has issued notices in an alleged fake-degree case. The bench, headed by Acting Chief Justice Hassan Azhar Rizvi, issued the notices to teacher Ansar Abbas and former assistant advocate general Ayub Buzdar. The notices were issued on an appeal filed by the Punjab Education Department.

During the hearing, the acting chief justice questioned what action the department had taken after the fake documents surfaced. He also asked why the intra-court appeal in the Lahore High Court was withdrawn without authority. The case will now proceed with the respondents' replies.
