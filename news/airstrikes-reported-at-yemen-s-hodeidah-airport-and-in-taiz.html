Airstrikes Reported at Yemen's Hodeidah Airport and in Taiz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Airstrikes have reportedly hit Hodeidah International Airport and an area of Taiz governorate in Yemen. Houthi-affiliated media reported that the airport in the western coastal city of Hodeidah was targeted. The reports also said the Hajjah al-Abd area of al-Shamayatayn district in Taiz came under attack.

Al Jazeera cited the Houthi reports, but no details have emerged yet about who carried out the strikes. There is also no confirmed information so far about casualties or damage. Taiz is a key centre of armed resistance against the Houthis.
