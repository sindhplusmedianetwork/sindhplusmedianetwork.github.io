Noor Zaman Stuns World No. 4 to Reach Silicon Valley Open Quarter-Final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's young squash star Noor Zaman has pulled off a major upset at the PSA Gold Silicon Valley Open in the United States. He defeated defending champion and world number four Karim Abdel Gawad of Egypt 3-1 to reach the quarter-final. The 22-year-old won the 52-minute contest in Redwood City with scores of 9-11, 7-11, 11-9 and 6-11.

The victory is one of the biggest of Zaman's career and marks his arrival among squash's elite. The tournament carries a total prize purse of $143,500.
