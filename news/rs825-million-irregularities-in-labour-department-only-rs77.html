Rs825 Million Irregularities in Labour Department, Only Rs77 Million Recovered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Public Accounts Committee-I has expressed concern that only Rs77 million of the Rs825 million in financial irregularities flagged in the Labour Department's 2022-23 audit report has been recovered. The committee raised the matter during a meeting and questioned the slow pace of recovery. It directed the department to recover the outstanding amount immediately.

The committee also directed the department to legislate to remove hurdles in the recovery process and to fix a timeline for recovery. The department told the meeting that recovery directions have been challenged at various forums, which has affected the process.
