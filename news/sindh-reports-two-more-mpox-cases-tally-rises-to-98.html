Sindh reports two more mpox cases; tally rises to 98

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Health Department reported two more cases of monkeypox on Friday, taking the provincial tally to 98. Officials said seven infections have been detected in the past week alone, prompting stepped-up surveillance, contact tracing and patient care. All health facilities across the province have been directed to strictly enforce infection prevention measures.

The department described mpox as a preventable and treatable disease, urging people to get tested early, maintain hand hygiene and rely only on official sources of information. Earlier reports had put the provincial tally at 95 with 11 deaths recorded. Health authorities said they were closely monitoring the situation as case numbers continue to rise.
