Former federal minister Liaqat Abbas Bhatti found dead at Hafizabad home

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former federal minister Liaqat Abbas Bhatti was found dead at his residence in Hafizabad in suspicious circumstances. Law enforcement agencies launched a comprehensive investigation from all angles after the incident. According to journalist Muhammad Umair's X post, the deceased's personal vehicles, security guards, personal weapons and other belongings were missing from the residence.

When police arrived, Liaqat Abbas Bhatti's body was lying on a bed. DPO Hafizabad, heavy police force and forensic teams were at the scene collecting evidence. Police have widened the investigation keeping all possible motives including murder in view and formed teams to trace the missing guards, vehicles and belongings.

The deceased was the real brother of PTI leader and former MNA Mehdi Bhatti.
