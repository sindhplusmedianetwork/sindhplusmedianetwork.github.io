UN chief urges drastic emissions cuts as COP31 wrestles with fossil fuels

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres on Friday called for an immediate and drastic reduction in greenhouse gas emissions. His appeal came as divisions over fossil fuels dominated the first week of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya, Turkiye. Major fossil fuel producers and consumers are resisting any agreement that sets firm new targets or a shift away from coal, oil and gas.

Host country Turkiye is pushing an electrification proposal that sits at the centre of an eventual deal. Australia is leading negotiations towards a draft text expected early next week. The talks will test whether countries can bridge the divide between cutting emissions and protecting fossil fuel interests.
