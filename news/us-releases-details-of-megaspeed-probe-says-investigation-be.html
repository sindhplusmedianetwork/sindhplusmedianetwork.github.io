US releases details of Megaspeed probe, says investigation began in 2024

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The US Commerce Department and Justice Department released information on Monday about Washington's investigation into Singapore-based cloud-computing company Megaspeed, Nvidia's Asia-Pacific partner, saying the investigation began in 2024. The two departments said the Commerce Department opened the probe after identifying suspicious activity through monitoring of potential diversions of sensitive semiconductors, and it centred on possible export control violations. In May 2025, Commerce investigators requested records from Nvidia concerning direct or indirect sales to Megaspeed, and in June 2025 Nvidia placed Megaspeed on its no-sell list.

US agents, together with Singaporean law enforcement officials, conducted an on-site, in-person end-use check at Megaspeed in June 2025 and interviewed Alice Huang, who was then the company's chief executive. Following that enforcement action, the Justice Department took the lead role in a criminal investigation into Megaspeed. Megaspeed said on Sunday that Alice Huang officially left the company on August 30, 2025, and that it conducted a legitimate and lawful business, while Singapore's government said it had agreed to continue discussions with Washington about the company.

Singapore has barred Huang from re-entering the country, and investigations into Megaspeed and six other individuals continue, the home affairs ministry said. The scrutiny followed reports that Nvidia's former chief executive Jensen Huang had an affair with Alice Huang, who some US authorities suspect had ties to Chinese espionage efforts, though Beijing has rejected the allegations that she is a spy.
