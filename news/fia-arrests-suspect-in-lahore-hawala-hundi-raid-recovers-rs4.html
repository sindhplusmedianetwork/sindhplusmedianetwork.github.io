FIA arrests suspect in Lahore hawala-hundi raid, recovers Rs4.665 million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency's Commercial Banking Circle Lahore arrested a suspect during an operation at Lahore's Azam Cloth Market on Tuesday. The arrested man was identified as Abu Zar. During the raid, the agency recovered Rs4.665 million in Pakistani currency, a mobile phone and other evidence relating to illegal currency exchange and hawala-hundi operations.

Officials said the suspect is in custody and further investigation is underway to trace the wider network involved in the illegal currency business.
