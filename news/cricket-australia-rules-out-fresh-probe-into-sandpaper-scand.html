Cricket Australia rules out fresh probe into sandpaper scandal after Khawaja revelations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cricket Australia's chief executive has ruled out reopening the investigation into the 2018 ball-tampering scandal after new revelations by former Test cricketer Usman Khawaja. In his new autobiography, Khawaja claims the whole Australian team knew about the plan to rub the ball with sandpaper to get reverse swing during the 2018 Cape Town Test against South Africa. At the time, only Cameron Bancroft, then-captain Steve Smith and vice-captain David Warner were punished, with Bancroft banned for nine months and Smith and Warner for one year each.

No other player accepted responsibility for the episode, and the bowling unit of Pat Cummins, Mitchell Starc, Josh Hazlewood and Nathan Lyon issued a joint statement in 2021 denying any knowledge of the plan. Khawaja's claim that 'we all knew about the plan' has now raised questions about that earlier position. David Warner has backed his former team-mate, saying he agrees with Khawaja's account 100 per cent.

Cricket Australia, however, has decided against a fresh inquiry into the affair.
