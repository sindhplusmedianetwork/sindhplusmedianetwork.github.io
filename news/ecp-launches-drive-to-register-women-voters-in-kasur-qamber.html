ECP launches drive to register women voters in Kasur, Qamber-Shahdadkot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Election Commission of Pakistan on Saturday announced a pilot project to reduce the gender gap in voter registration in Kasur district of Punjab and Qamber-Shahdadkot district of Sindh. Data collection for the initiative will be carried out in November 2026, while voter registration activities will continue from December 2026 to February 2027. The initiative aims to identify women eligible for Computerised National Identity Cards and voter registration, encourage them to register and facilitate the process.

Local social workers and enumerators will conduct door-to-door visits to prepare statistical block-wise lists and run awareness campaigns, while Nadra's Mobile Registration Vans will be deployed at people's doorsteps. Facilitation will also be provided at registration centres set up in government buildings. Through the campaign, the ECP aims to facilitate the registration of 115,689 women in Kasur and 59,156 in Qamber-Shahdadkot, prioritising areas with a wider gender gap in the initial phase.

Representatives of the education and health sectors, civil society organisations and local communities will assist the ECP in the initiative.
