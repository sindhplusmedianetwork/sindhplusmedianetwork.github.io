Approval of ML-3 railway track restoration deferred once again

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has once again deferred approval of the one-billion-dollar project to restore and upgrade Pakistan Railways' ML-3 track for transporting minerals from the Reko Diq mines. Planning Ministry officials said the Central Development Working Party deferred approval for the third time in five months. The 996-kilometre Nokundi-to-Rohri track restoration is estimated to cost about Rs278 billion.

Officials cited the unavailability of funds and a possible proposal to use Gwadar port instead of Karachi for mineral exports. Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal said approval was deferred because details of bridge financing with Barrick Gold were sought. The government has already approved a $390 million loan from Reko Diq Mining Company at about 6 percent annual interest as interim support for the ML-3 restoration.

The project is considered important for moving copper and gold from Reko Diq to ports for export.
