Referendum Should Be Held Separately in Each Province, Says Sanaullah Baloch

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former senator and Balochistan National Party leader Sanaullah Baloch has said any referendum on the creation of new provinces should be held separately in each province rather than at the national level. Citing Article 239(4) of the Constitution, he argued that a single nationwide referendum could impose one province's majority view on the others. He pointed out that Punjab's population exceeds the combined population of the other three provinces.

Sanaullah Baloch said the people of each province should decide the matter for themselves. His remarks come amid a continuing national debate over the creation of new provinces and the method for deciding the issue.
