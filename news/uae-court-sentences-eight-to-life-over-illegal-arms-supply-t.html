UAE Court Sentences Eight to Life Over Illegal Arms Supply to Sudanese Army

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Abu Dhabi Federal Appeals Court has handed life sentences to eight people convicted of illegally supplying weapons, ammunition and military equipment to the Sudanese army, along with money laundering charges. Among those sentenced are former Sudanese intelligence chief Salah Gosh and senior army official Yasser Al Atta. The court also imposed fines of one million dirhams each on six commercial companies involved in the case, ordered their licences to be cancelled and their funds confiscated.

The verdict marks one of the most significant rulings in the region against illicit arms trafficking tied to the Sudanese conflict.
