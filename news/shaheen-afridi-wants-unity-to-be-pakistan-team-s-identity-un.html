Shaheen Afridi Wants Unity to Be Pakistan Team's Identity Under His Captaincy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ODI captain Shaheen Shah Afridi said on Saturday that he wants the Pakistan team to be seen as united, with the collective goal of winning the 2027 World Cup. Speaking at a pre-series press conference at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium, Afridi urged the media to project a positive image of the side. 'The messaging from you [the media] should be good as well, to tell the people that this team is united, there is love in this team,' he said.

The left-arm pacer backed Babar Azam as the 'backbone' of the side amid ongoing debate over team selection. He added that pitches were being prepared to resemble South African conditions ahead of the World Cup. Pakistan opens the ODI tri-series against Sri Lanka on Sunday, with the series seen as an early building block for the 2027 campaign.
