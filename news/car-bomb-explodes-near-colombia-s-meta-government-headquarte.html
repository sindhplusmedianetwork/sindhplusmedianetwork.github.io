Car Bomb Explodes Near Colombia's Meta Government Headquarters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A car bomb exploded near the headquarters of the Meta Department government in the Colombian city of Villavicencio, authorities said. Dozens of people were injured and nearby buildings were damaged in the blast. The explosion struck late on Monday at the government complex located in the city's central park, causing significant damage to the building and surrounding properties, with several facades destroyed.

Among those injured were five security guards. No fatalities had been reported as of Tuesday morning. President Abelardo de la Espriella condemned the bombing and accused drug-trafficking groups of seeking to intimidate his administration.
