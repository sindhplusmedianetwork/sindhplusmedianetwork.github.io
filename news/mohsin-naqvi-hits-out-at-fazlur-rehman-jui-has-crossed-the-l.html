Mohsin Naqvi Hits Out at Fazlur Rehman: 'JUI Has Crossed the Line'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi launched a sharp attack on JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman during his address to the Senate on Thursday. Naqvi said there is no room left for respecting the Maulana after remarks that he described as spitting on the state. He declared that the JUI has crossed the line and will now receive a response.

The minister remarked that the state is treated as a mother by them only when it benefits them. He accused the party of using religion for business and politics. He further claimed that the JUI had itself asked the government to impose governor rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

In a separate remark, he said the agreements made with the province would be honoured.
