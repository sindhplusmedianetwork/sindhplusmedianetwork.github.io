PCB explores new six-a-side 'PSL Sixes' tournament for off-season

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board is exploring a new six-a-side cricket tournament, tentatively named PSL Sixes. The proposed competition would take place during the Pakistan Super League's off-season and is designed to bring franchise cricket to more cities and attract a wider audience. Salman Naseer, chief executive of the PSL, disclosed the proposal during a podcast, explaining that the board is examining a format built around shorter, entertainment-focused matches.

The plan also includes the possibility of taking games to smaller venues that have not hosted PSL fixtures. Unlike the Hong Kong Sixes, which features teams representing different countries, the proposed PSL Sixes is expected to follow a franchise-based format, with teams representing cities and regions in Pakistan. Pakistan have won the Hong Kong Sixes six times since it began in 1992 and are the defending champions ahead of the next edition, scheduled for October 31 and November 1.

Details such as the launch date, participating teams, venues and competition structure have not been confirmed, and the tournament remains under consideration rather than finalised.
