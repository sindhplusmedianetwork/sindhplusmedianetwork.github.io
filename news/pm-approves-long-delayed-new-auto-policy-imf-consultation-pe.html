PM approves long-delayed new auto policy; IMF consultation pending on sales tax

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has approved the long-delayed new auto policy in principle. However, sales-tax matters under the policy will go through further consultation with the International Monetary Fund before it moves forward. Once the IMF is taken on board, the policy will be sent to the federal cabinet for final approval along with the related tax legislation.

The proposed rates include a 1 percent sales tax on battery electric vehicles and 18 percent on hybrid and petrol vehicles. The IMF, for its part, has asked for the sales tax on electric vehicles to be raised to 18 percent. The approval marks a key step for Pakistan's auto sector, though the tax consultation with the Fund remains the decisive hurdle before the policy becomes final.
