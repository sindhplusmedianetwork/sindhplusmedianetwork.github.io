No immediate cut in petroleum levy, says Ahsan Iqbal; committee formed for phased reduction

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal has rejected any immediate reduction in the petroleum levy, saying the government charges Rs80 per litre and cannot afford a sudden cut. Speaking to the media on Thursday, he said a committee has been formed to prepare workable proposals for a phased reduction of the levy from the next budget. The committee includes Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, the planning minister and Rana Sanaullah, while Jamaat-e-Islami will nominate three representatives.

Iqbal said representatives from both sides will sit together and deliberate, as their common interest is providing relief to the public. He cautioned against seeking temporary applause with a decision that could turn into a major crisis later, noting that oil prices are rising globally. Separately, Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik told the National Assembly that levy collection was part of IMF targets and that the government has collected Rs4.4 trillion since FY2022-23, exceeding its own targets by Rs227 billion.

The government currently charges an Rs80 petroleum levy, a Rs5 climate levy and Rs20.56 customs duty per litre, taking the total tax to about Rs106.
