Rana Sanaullah welcomes new provinces if formed with public will and consensus

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's adviser on political affairs Rana Sanaullah has said that if new provinces are created with the will of the people and through consensus, it is a very good thing. He was speaking about the debate that followed remarks by Mohsin Naqvi at a ceremony, which had drawn objections from political quarters. Sanaullah said those objections no longer stand after Naqvi clarified that provinces will be formed by consensus, with all political parties included and no one left out.

He said provinces will be created if the people want them, and not if they do not. Reaching consensus could take six weeks, six months or six years, he added. Sanaullah also said that the establishment's real authority lies with the prime minister, while the civil and military leadership are working in harmony.

He added that Pakistan is a country that provides security in the region, and that the world now sees it as a guardian of peace.
