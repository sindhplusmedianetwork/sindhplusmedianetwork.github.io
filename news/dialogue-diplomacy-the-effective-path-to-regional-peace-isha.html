Dialogue, Diplomacy the Effective Path to Regional Peace: Ishaq Dar at SCO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar has reaffirmed Pakistan's strong commitment to the founding principles of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation. He said dialogue and diplomacy are the effective path for resolving regional tensions. Dar said Pakistan remains committed to promoting regional harmony, trade, transit connectivity and economic integration.

He stressed that collective multilateral solutions are essential for addressing the region's shared challenges. The remarks were made at the SCO forum, where member states discussed cooperation on security and economic matters.
