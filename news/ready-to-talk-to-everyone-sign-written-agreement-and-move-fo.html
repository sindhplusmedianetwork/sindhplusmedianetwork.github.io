Ready to Talk to Everyone, Sign Written Agreement and Move Forward, Says Raja Nasir Abbas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senator Allama Raja Nasir Abbas, vice chairman of Tehreek-e-Tahaffuz-e-Aain-e-Pakistan and Senate opposition leader, said in an interview with Geo News that his side is ready to talk to everyone. He called it a joint agenda and Pakistan's agenda, urging all sides to sit together, sign a written agreement and move forward. Abbas said there is no greed for any ministry, the presidency, the prime ministership or an adviser's post, adding that only the homeland matters to them.

He said the minimum agenda to put Pakistan back on track is supremacy of the constitution and rule of law, free and fair elections, and making parliament supreme as the source of power. He also called for reform of the Election Commission and proper appointment of its chairman, saying the vote that goes into the ballot box must be the one that comes out. Abbas said rulers in Pakistan must be chosen only by the people with no selection, and that his side would stand firmly against anyone who robs the public right to vote.
