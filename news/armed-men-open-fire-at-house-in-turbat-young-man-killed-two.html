Armed Men Open Fire at House in Turbat, Young Man Killed, Two Women Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five unidentified armed men entered a house in the Dabak area of Kalatak, Turbat, and opened fire, killing a young man and injuring two women. The dead man was identified as Raza Shah, son of Ghulam Shah of New Dehat. His grandmother Noor Bibi and Zaratun, the wife of Ghulam Shah, were injured in the firing.

Police said the armed men, travelling in a Corolla, were trying to take Raza Shah with them when family members resisted and they opened fire. The injured women were taken for treatment.
