Dar Links Regional Cooperation With Afghanistan to Action Against Terrorism at ECO Moot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar told the Economic Cooperation Organisation's Council of Ministers meeting in Islamabad on Monday that Pakistan wants a peaceful, prosperous and stable Afghanistan. He said meaningful progress towards regional cooperation requires the Afghan authorities to address the legitimate security concerns of neighbouring countries, particularly the threat of terrorism. Dar outlined five priorities for Pakistan's chairmanship of the ECO: expanding trade and investment, strengthening regional connectivity, advancing the ECO Trade Agreement, building regional resilience and promoting human development.

He said regional connectivity rests on more than the routes that link countries, and depends on trust between states and respect for international law and treaties. Dar said unresolved disputes and the non-fulfilment of international obligations can erode that trust and impede economic progress. Pakistan holds the ECO chairmanship for 2026-27 under the theme 'Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO', and the leaders will discuss and adopt ECO Vision 2035, a ten-year framework for regional connectivity, economic integration and sustainable growth.
