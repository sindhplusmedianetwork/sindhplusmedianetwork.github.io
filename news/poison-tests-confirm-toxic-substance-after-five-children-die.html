Poison Tests Confirm Toxic Substance After Five Children Die in Khyber

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Laboratory tests have confirmed that a poisonous substance was present in a sample taken from one of five children who died after a suspected poisoning incident in the Bazaar Zakha Khel area of Khyber district, officials said on Thursday. At least seven children from the same family were affected by the suspected substance, five of whom died while being taken to hospital, while two others fell unconscious. Blood, urine and other samples collected from the children were sent for laboratory analysis, and reports received from Khyber Medical College confirmed the presence of poisonous material in a sample taken from one of the children.

Deputy Commissioner Shahid Ali said the children had fallen unconscious three days earlier in the Zakha Khel area, and the two survivors were taken to Hayatabad Medical Complex in Peshawar, where they were discharged after recovering. Following receipt of the laboratory findings, the matter was referred to the Khyber district police officer for further investigation and necessary legal action. Police will now investigate the nature and possible source of the substance to establish the circumstances surrounding the incident.
