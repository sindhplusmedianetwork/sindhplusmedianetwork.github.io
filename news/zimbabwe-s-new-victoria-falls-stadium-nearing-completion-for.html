Zimbabwe's New Victoria Falls Stadium Nearing Completion for World Cup 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Zimbabwe's new Victoria Falls Stadium, being built for the ICC Cricket World Cup 2027, is in its final stages of completion. The ICC inspection team expressed satisfaction with the construction work. Tournament director Sarah Edgar inspected all three Zimbabwean venues along with her team.

The new stadium will host four World Cup matches. Main building work is complete and only finishing work remains, with a trial match planned soon. Construction will finish by mid-2027, followed by an international series.

Zimbabwe will co-host the 2027 World Cup with South Africa and Namibia.
