Seven-metre Ronaldo statue unveiled in Shanghai as fans honour football star

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fans unveiled a 7-metre-tall statue of Cristiano Ronaldo in Shanghai, China. The statue shows him in his famous celebration pose wearing Portugal's number 7 jersey. It was installed outside a museum, with a large number of football fans attending the unveiling.

Ronaldo was not present, but his elder brother Hugo Aveiro and close friend Miguel Paixao attended. A CR7 museum of about 3,000 square metres was also inaugurated with over 40 memorabilia items. Local media said the statue will remain on display until the end of December 2026, while the museum is expected to stay open for a year.
