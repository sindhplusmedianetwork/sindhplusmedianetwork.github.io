King Charles, Queen Camilla Send Condolences After Deadly Canada Base Fire

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

King Charles and Queen Camilla have expressed their shock and grief over a deadly fire at a Canadian military base that claimed four lives. In a joint message on Monday, the royal couple sent their deepest sympathy and heartfelt condolences to the families of those who lost their lives at Canadian Forces Base Wainwright on Saturday. The fast-moving grass fire, spread quickly by strong winds, killed four armed forces members and left 39 injured, 19 of them in critical condition.

The four service members were identified as Pte. Aidan Goulding, Pte. Shaun Farrell, Pte.

Hayden Lykkemark and Sgt. Morgan Wong. The King praised the courage of military and civilian emergency services, healthcare workers and all responders.

As Canada's Head of State, Charles sent special thoughts and prayers to the people of Alberta and the Canadian Armed Forces. Canadian military officials said the fire broke out during an exercise involving simulated attacks and had been thought under control.
