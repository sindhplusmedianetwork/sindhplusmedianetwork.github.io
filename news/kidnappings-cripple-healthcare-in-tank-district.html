Kidnappings cripple healthcare in Tank district

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Repeated kidnappings of polio workers and health department staff in Tank district have disrupted public healthcare. Seven polio workers were abducted in two incidents in November. Two of them were recovered on Nov 7, while efforts continue for the rest.

The abductions have hit health services in the district hard. Several basic health units have suspended operations. The Aga Khan Foundation has also halted its work over security concerns.

The continuing insecurity has left people in the district with far fewer health facilities.
