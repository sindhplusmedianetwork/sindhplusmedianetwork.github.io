Locals shoot dead 12-foot python found in Narowal fields

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A giant python that slithered out of fields in Narowal was shot dead by local residents. The 40-kilogram, 12-foot-long python emerged from the fields in the Khan Khasa area of Narowal, sparking fear among villagers. Locals opened fire and killed the reptile, after which they carried it on their shoulders.

A video of the dead python being carried away on a shoulder went viral on social media. The residents later dug a pit and buried the dead python in the ground.
