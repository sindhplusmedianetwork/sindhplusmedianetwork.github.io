Hailstorm halts South Africa's charge against Bangladesh on day three

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Heavy hailstorm and rain ended day three of the opening Test early, with South Africa on 386 for seven, just 14 behind Bangladesh's 400. Overnight centurion Marques Ackerman made 157, while Ryan Rickelton was unbeaten on 56. Captain Temba Bavuma retired hurt on 49 with a leg strain.

Hasan Mahmud took four wickets for 72 runs for Bangladesh. The weather brought play to an early close on the third day.
