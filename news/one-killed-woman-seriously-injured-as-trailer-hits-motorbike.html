One Killed, Woman Seriously Injured as Trailer Hits Motorbike on Karachi's Jam Sadiq Bridge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

One motorcyclist has been killed and his wife seriously injured after a trailer hit their motorcycle on Jam Sadiq Bridge in Karachi's Korangi area. The deceased was identified as 40-year-old Waqar, and his body was moved to Jinnah Hospital by Chhipa ambulances for legal formalities. His wife, who was riding with him, suffered serious injuries to both her legs and is being treated in the intensive care ward of Jinnah Hospital.

After the accident, angry people at the scene set fire to the container-laden trailer, while the driver fled the scene. Police reached the spot and called the fire brigade, and Korangi Industrial Area SHO Ayaz Khan said police had taken the burnt trailer into custody and moved it to the police station, with further investigation under way. Traffic on Jam Sadiq Bridge was badly affected by the accident, with long queues of vehicles causing severe difficulties for commuters.
