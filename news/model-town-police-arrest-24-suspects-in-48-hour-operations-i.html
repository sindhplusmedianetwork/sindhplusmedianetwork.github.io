Model Town police arrest 24 suspects in 48-hour operations in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Model Town police in Lahore arrested 24 suspects during a series of operations conducted over the past 48 hours. According to police, those arrested included proclaimed offenders, individuals with criminal records, drug dealers, people involved in aerial firing, beggars and those found in possession of illegal weapons. Police said proclaimed offenders and people with criminal records were traced and arrested with the help of the e-Police application and modern technology, while operations against drug dealers and illegal weapon holders were carried out on the basis of intelligence reports.

SP Model Town Asad Ali said more than two kilograms of narcotics and alcohol had been recovered from the arrested suspects. He added that people involved in aerial firing were apprehended on the spot, along with their weapons, during routine police patrols, and illegal firearms and ammunition were also seized from the suspects.
