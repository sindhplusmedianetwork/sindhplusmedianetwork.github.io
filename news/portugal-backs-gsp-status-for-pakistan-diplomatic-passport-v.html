Portugal Backs GSP+ Status for Pakistan; Diplomatic Passport Visa Waiver Agreed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Portugal has announced its support for GSP+ status for Pakistan. The two countries have also agreed to waive visa requirements for holders of diplomatic passports. The Portuguese foreign minister held meetings with President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif during his visit.

The meetings discussed expanding cooperation between the two countries in trade, investment, agriculture, information technology and tourism. President Zardari also proposed setting up a joint working group to promote trade and investment between Pakistan and Portugal.
