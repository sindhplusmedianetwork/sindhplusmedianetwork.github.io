IRGC spokesman shares image as Iran claims downing dozens of US drones

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A spokesman for Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps shared an image presented as the last view seen by an MQ-9 drone operator before destruction. The image showed a missile hitting a drone with the words THIS IS THE END. The post came as the IRGC claimed it had shot down dozens of US MQ-9 and MQ-1 drones in recent days.

According to IRGC announcements reported in the story, the claims included the 58th MQ-9 over the Strait of Hormuz on 21 November. The announcements also included the 20th MQ-1 in the eastern Strait of Hormuz on 22 November. The spokesman was identified as Brigadier General Hossein Mohebbi.
