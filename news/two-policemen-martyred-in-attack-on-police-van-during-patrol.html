Two Policemen Martyred in Attack on Police Van During Patrol in Swabi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two policemen, including police post in-charge PSI Waleed Khan, were martyred when unidentified attackers opened fire on a police van during patrol in Swabi. One constable was seriously injured in the attack. Police said the van was patrolling Mohallah India Cham in village Gohathi when the firing took place.

The martyred and injured personnel were shifted to Bacha Khan Medical Complex Shah Mansoor. Police have launched a search operation to arrest the attackers.
