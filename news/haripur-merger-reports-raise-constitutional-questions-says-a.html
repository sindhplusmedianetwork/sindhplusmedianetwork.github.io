Haripur Merger Reports Raise Constitutional Questions, Says Aimal Wali Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Awami National Party president Aimal Wali Khan said reports of separating Haripur from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and merging it into Islamabad are not merely an administrative matter. He said the reports raise a serious question about the province's constitutional, geographical, water and financial rights. He said Haripur and Swabi are both geographically linked to Tarbela Dam, whose power installations sit on both banks of the Indus.

He said the impact on Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's net hydel profit rights under Article 161(2) of the Constitution must be clarified first. He said changing the province's boundaries is impossible without the provincial assembly's constitutional approval under Article 239(4). He said the federal government should place the proposal's details, its legal basis and its full financial and constitutional implications before the public and parliament.
