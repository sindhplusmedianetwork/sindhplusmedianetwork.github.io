96 Million Pakistanis Use YouTube; Pakistan Leads World in Viewing: PTA Chairman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTA Chairman Major General (retired) Hafeez-ur-Rahman has said that 96 million Pakistanis use YouTube and that Pakistan leads the world in YouTube video viewing. Briefing the National Assembly's Standing Committee on Information Technology, he said 560 million video views are recorded on YouTube in Pakistan every day. He added that TikTok has 100 million user accounts in the country, with 150 million Pakistanis watching videos on the platform.

On WhatsApp, he said Pakistan has 100 million accounts, with users making one billion calls and sending 12 billion messages daily. Federal IT Minister Shaza Fatima told the committee that social media and AI use has greatly increased data consumption, and that no spectrum auction had taken place after Nawaz Sharif's departure — he had given the country 4G, while the current government auctioned 5G spectrum. She said 1.8 million people are now using 5G in Pakistan, the service is coming to 24 cities, and steps are being taken for better services.

She also listed reasons for poor telecom service, saying entire towers get stolen, along with diesel and generators.
