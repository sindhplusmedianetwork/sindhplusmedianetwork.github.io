CCD Head Constable Among Two Shot Dead Over Land Dispute in Rawalpindi's Changa Gali

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two brothers were shot dead during talks to settle a land dispute in the Changa Gali area of Rawalpindi. The deceased were identified as Tehseen Shah, a head constable of the Punjab Crime Control Department (CCD) Rawalpindi, and his brother Khizar Shah. According to police officials, the firing took place while negotiations over the land dispute were under way, and a patwari along with other people were present at the time.

The alleged shooter, Manzoor Shah, a cousin of the victims, was arrested on the spot. Both bodies were shifted to a nearby hospital for postmortem examination. CCD officials have confirmed the incident, and police say further investigation is under way.
