First Muslim US senator's witty reply to uncle's question about marrying an Indian woman goes viral

Abdul Rahman Mohamed El-Sayed, the first Muslim elected to the US Senate, won the Michigan seat by defeating Republican candidate Mike Rogers. In a podcast, he recalled that when he married Indian-origin Dr Sarah Joukaku, his uncle from Egypt asked why he chose an Indian woman when Egypt has 80 million people. El-Sayed replied that India has a billion people, so statistically an Indian wife was more likely.

The clip comes from the August podcast Meet the Real Abdul El-Sayed and is now circulating widely on X. The couple married in May 2006 and have two daughters.
