More Water May Be Released from Tarbela Dam into Indus Tonight

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Additional water may be released from Tarbela Dam into the Indus River tonight. River flows are expected to rise further over the next 24 hours as a result of the release. Alerts have been issued in Attock and Haripur districts in view of the expected increase in water levels.

Residents of Chhachh village near the river have been told to take precautions. The administration has also asked people in Hazro tehsil to move their livestock to safe places. Announcements have been made from mosques to inform local residents of the situation.

Authorities are monitoring the river flow and have urged people living near the river to remain careful.
