Punjab gives final deadline for vehicle token tax payment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has set a final deadline for payment of outstanding vehicle token tax. Vehicle owners can pay the tax without penalty until 30 November 2026. Fines will apply from 1 December 2026 after the deadline passes.

The Excise Department said a 50 percent penalty applies on last year's unpaid tax. A 25 percent penalty applies on the current year's unpaid tax, increasing monthly. Payment can also be made through ePay, officials said.
