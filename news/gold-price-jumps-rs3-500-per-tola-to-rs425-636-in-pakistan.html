Gold Price Jumps Rs3,500 Per Tola to Rs425,636 in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold and silver prices rose again in local and international markets on Wednesday, according to the jewellers' association. The price of 24-carat gold per tola increased by Rs3,500 to Rs425,636, while the price of ten grams of gold rose by Rs3,001 to Rs364,914. The price of silver per tola increased by Rs61 to Rs5,854.

In the international market, gold rose by 35 dollars per ounce to 4,031 dollars. The increase reversed the recent downward trend in bullion prices, which had fallen by Rs1,300 per tola only a day earlier.
