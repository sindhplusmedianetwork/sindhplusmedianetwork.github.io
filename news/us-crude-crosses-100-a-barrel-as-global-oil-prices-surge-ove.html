US Crude Crosses $100 a Barrel as Global Oil Prices Surge Over 3%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global crude oil prices recorded a major jump on Sunday, with American and British crude rising more than three percent. US crude crossed the $100 mark to reach $101.4 a barrel, while British Brent was selling at $109.9 a barrel. The UAE's Murban crude climbed even higher to $113.4 a barrel.

The rally reflects deepening supply concerns as attacks on commercial shipping around the Strait of Hormuz intensify and the US-Iran conflict disrupts regional energy flows. Amid the price surge, the United States offered to release 17 million barrels of reserve oil to calm the markets. The move underscores how the ongoing conflict in the Gulf is feeding directly into global energy costs, with US officials already warning that fuel prices could climb further if the strait is disrupted.
