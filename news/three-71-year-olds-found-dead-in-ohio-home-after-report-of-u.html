Three 71-year-olds found dead in Ohio home after report of unwanted guest

Police in the US state of Ohio found three people dead inside a home after being called about an unwanted guest, with all three victims aged 71. According to ABC News, the incident took place in North Royalton, a suburb of Cleveland, where neighbours called police on the night of Tuesday, November 10, after hearing noise from the house. Police had also received a report of an unwanted guest at the home, and when officers arrived at around 8pm they found the bodies of an elderly couple inside.

Officers then noticed a car parked outside the house that was reportedly linked to the unwanted guest, and they pulled back and called for extra personnel as a precaution. A special team later entered the home and recovered a third body. The dead were identified as Emilia Severtan, Sorin Severtan and Stephen Pasco, and police said Stephen Pasco was the person described as the unwanted guest in the initial report.

The cause of death has not yet been revealed, and the relationship between the three people is still being investigated. The Ohio Bureau of Criminal Investigation is also assisting in the inquiry.
