ATC sentences Imaan, Hadi to 10 years in security breach case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-terrorism court in Islamabad on Thursday sentenced human rights lawyer Imaan Zainab Mazari-Hazir and her husband Hadi Ali Chattha to 10 years in prison each in the security breach case. The court had reserved its verdict on October 27, after both accused recorded their statements under Section 342 of the Code of Criminal Procedure and the state counsel concluded final arguments. The two faced a jail trial in a 2024 case concerning the alleged disruption of an international cricket team's security route and incitement to public unrest, in which they were accused of removing barriers from the route and were indicted on October 7.

In their statements on Tuesday, the couple alleged that the case was 'nothing more than harassment' for their legal and human rights work, falling under a broader pattern of targeting through 'reprisals'. On Monday, they had approached the Supreme Court to challenge the Islamabad High Court's October 19 order refusing to stay the trial. The couple has been in jail since their arrest on January 23 in a case over protesting outside the Islamabad High Court; a day later a sessions court handed them 17-year sentences in a separate social media posts case.

The Supreme Court suspended those sentences on September 17, but hours later Islamabad police re-arrested them in connection with the security breach case.
