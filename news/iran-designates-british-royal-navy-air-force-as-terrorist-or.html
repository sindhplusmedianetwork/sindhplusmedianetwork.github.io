Iran designates British Royal Navy, Air Force as 'terrorist' organisations, MI6 as 'hostile group'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has designated Britain's Royal Navy and Royal Air Force as terrorist organisations and the foreign intelligence agency MI6 as a hostile group, the Iranian Foreign Ministry announced on Sunday. The ministry said the move was a direct response to London's designation of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) as a terrorist organisation under the UK's State Threats Act 2026, adopted on July 13. According to a statement carried by the state news agency IRNA, the decision was taken under a resolution of Iran's Supreme National Security Council.

Tehran also accused London of taking part in the United States-Israeli war against Iran by giving access to military and logistical bases under British jurisdiction. Separately, US President Donald Trump told reporters at his Florida residence that Washington had knocked out much of Iran's military but still had one or two targets to take care of, including a suspected nuclear site known as Pickaxe Mountain, adding that it could be done in ten minutes. Iran's military leadership warned that any renewed US aggression would be met with an immediate and decisive response on a larger scale than before.
