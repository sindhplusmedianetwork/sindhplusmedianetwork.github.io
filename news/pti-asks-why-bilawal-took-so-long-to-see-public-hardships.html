PTI Asks Why Bilawal Took So Long to See Public Hardships

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The PTI has said PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari was right in acknowledging that the country's entire system had collapsed, but questioned why it took him so long to recognise the hardships faced by ordinary citizens. In a statement, PTI Central Information Secretary Sheikh Waqas Akram said the PPP chairman had finally realised the difficulties confronting the public, but questioned the sincerity of his remarks. He argued that the PPP was part of the ruling arrangement and could not escape responsibility for the deteriorating political and economic situation.

He pointed to soaring flour prices, unaffordable electricity bills and rising petrol costs, saying they had made life increasingly difficult for citizens. Mr Akram said the ruling PML-N and the PPP shared responsibility for the country's economic conditions and the PPP could not absolve itself of accountability through political statements alone. He also questioned Mr Bhutto-Zardari's leading role in securing the passage of the 26th Constitutional Amendment, saying it had dealt a serious blow to judicial independence and constitutional supremacy.

On reserved parliamentary seats, he said retaining the PTI's seats violated democratic principles and disregarded the public mandate, and challenged the PPP chairman to ensure their return to the PTI.
