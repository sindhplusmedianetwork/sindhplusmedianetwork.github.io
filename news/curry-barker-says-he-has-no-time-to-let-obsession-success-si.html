Curry Barker Says He Has No Time to Let 'Obsession' Success Sink In

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Filmmaker Curry Barker has said he has barely had time to process the extraordinary success of his horror film Obsession. Speaking to PEOPLE at the 2026 Time100 Next Gala, where he was recognised as one of the year's rising stars, Barker said there was no way for the achievement to settle in amid the whirlwind of opportunities and industry attention. He revealed that just a year earlier he had been preparing his next film, Anything But Ghosts, while hoping Obsession would earn between 10 million and 20 million dollars at the box office.

Made on a budget of just 750,000 dollars and shot in 20 days, the film instead earned approximately 523 million dollars worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film ever made on a budget of less than one million dollars. The film stars Inde Navarrette and Michael Johnston. Barker said praise from established Hollywood figures, including Seth Rogen, meant a great deal to him.

Focus Features is reportedly campaigning for Oscar nominations for Barker in directing, original screenplay and editing, while Navarrette and Johnston are being considered for acting nominations. Barker has already completed Anything But Ghosts, starring Aaron Paul and Bryce Dallas Howard, and is also attached to write and direct a new instalment of The Texas Chainsaw Massacre franchise. Obsession is currently available to stream on Peacock.
