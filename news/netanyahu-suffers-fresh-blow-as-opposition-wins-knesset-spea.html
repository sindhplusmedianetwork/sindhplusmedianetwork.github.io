Netanyahu suffers fresh blow as opposition wins Knesset speaker vote

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has suffered a fresh parliamentary setback after the Knesset elected Matan Kahana, of Gadi Eisenkot's alliance, as its new speaker with 66 votes. The ruling Likud party's candidate, Ofir Katz, received 52 votes, while Arab parties played a key role in the opposition's victory. As a result of the vote, Amir Ohana of Netanyahu's Likud party lost the speaker's post.

The election of the new speaker became possible after nearly six hours of obstruction and delaying tactics in parliament. The speaker's office carries influence over the legislative agenda and the voting schedule on important matters, and the win may give Eisenkot's alliance the chance to advance its priorities, including rolling back laws related to judicial reforms. In last month's general election, Netanyahu's allied bloc lost its parliamentary majority, winning 53 seats in the 120-member Knesset, while Eisenkot's alliance won 48 seats.

Arab parties won 19 seats and gained decisive importance in government formation. President Isaac Herzog has invited Netanyahu's political rival Gadi Eisenkot to form a new government, though negotiations for government formation are still under way.
