4.9 million children out of school in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, including 2.9 million girls, says education adviser

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Aamir Abdullah, the adviser on education to the governor of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, said while speaking to the media in Peshawar that 4.9 million children in the province, including 2.9 million girls, are out of school. He said Peshawar tops the list of out-of-school children, while 329 schools in the tribal districts are non-functional. He said 512 government schools are closed in 12 districts, 264 schools have no teacher at all, and 107 schools have neither teachers nor students.

The adviser said the province's 35,000 schools have 44,747 vacant posts, and more than 11,000 schools have only one or two teachers. He also said that of the 1,700 laboratories in government schools, the computers in 325 have been out of order for years, and IT education is being taught on the blackboard, which he described as a tragedy. Abdullah said the access rate to higher education in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is only 8 percent, far lower than in Sindh and Punjab, and that of the province's 34 universities with 698 departments, 208 have not produced a single research paper in three years.

He said 70 projects worth 5 billion rupees have been planned for the education sector under a 180-day plan, 1,500 more schools will be outsourced while 421 have already been outsourced, and a 100-day plan is being introduced for the professional training of teachers. He added that seven Danish schools will be built in the province under the Prime Minister's scheme, 424 schools will be solarised, and the virtual school project will be expanded.
