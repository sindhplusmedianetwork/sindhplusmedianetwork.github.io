Sri Lanka Thrash Pakistan to Reach Tri-Series Final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sri Lanka thrashed Pakistan by eight wickets in the ODI tri-series match at Gaddafi Stadium on Tuesday to book a place in the final. Pakistan were bowled out for just 110 runs in 25.3 overs after winning the toss and batting first. Left-arm pacer Dilshan Madushanka produced a career-best performance, taking five wickets for 34 runs to dismantle the Pakistan batting line-up.

Sri Lanka then chased down the 111-run target in only 18.1 overs, losing just two wickets along the way. Kamindu Mendis led the chase with an unbeaten 62 runs to see his side home comfortably. The result sent Sri Lanka into Saturday's final, where they will meet Pakistan again.

Pakistan had already qualified for the final after winning their first three matches of the series.
