No Corruption Case Against Me in 34 Years of Politics, Says Fazlur Rehman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

JUI chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman has said that he has been in politics for 34 years and no corruption case has been filed against him in that time. Speaking in Islamabad, he said an atmosphere is being created in the country as if his party members are highly corrupt people. He said notices are being circulated to damage his character, while no one questions the rulers about how they became owners of lakhs of acres of land in ten to twenty years.

Rehman added that he has never appeared before any court to date. His remarks come amid an ongoing exchange of statements between the JUI chief and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa governor over the political situation in the province. The JUI chief has in recent days criticised the government and the establishment in public speeches.
