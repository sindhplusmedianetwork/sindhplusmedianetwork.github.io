Sky Turns Red Over India's Chhattisgarh as Cyclone Arnab's Effects Persist

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The sky over Kirandul in Dantewada district of India's Chhattisgarh state turned an unusually deep red this week, drawing crowds of onlookers and going viral on social media. Videos of the crimson sky were shared widely online as residents gathered to witness the rare sight. The phenomenon appeared as cyclone Arnab moved inland after making landfall on the Andhra Pradesh coast.

The storm brought rain, strong winds and persistent overcast conditions across the region. Experts said high-speed winds lifting dust, sand and red iron-rich soil particles into the atmosphere could have reddened the sky. Authorities closed schools in the affected districts on October 29 as a precaution while the cyclone's effects persisted.
