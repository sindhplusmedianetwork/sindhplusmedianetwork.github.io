Man Indicted in $126 Million US Postal Fraud Case Arrested in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has arrested Faheem Akram, a 33-year-old resident of Khanewal, over an alleged online scheme involving counterfeit US postage labels, the agency said on Tuesday. A US federal grand jury in Florida indicted Akram on September 28 on charges including conspiracy to defraud the government, counterfeit postage and wire fraud. US prosecutors allege that from August 2023 to last month he operated LabelsBank.com, which was not authorised to sell postal products and offered counterfeit labels at a fixed price of about $2, regardless of weight, size or destination.

Court records show more than 5,200 customers bought over 5.1 million counterfeit labels, allowing parcels to be shipped far below legitimate rates, with alleged losses to the US Postal Service exceeding $126 million. Investigators identified 16 bank accounts linked to the case, including accounts held by the suspect, his brother, wife, sister and maternal aunt. The agency said a case had been registered and further investigation was underway, without disclosing where or how the arrest was made.
