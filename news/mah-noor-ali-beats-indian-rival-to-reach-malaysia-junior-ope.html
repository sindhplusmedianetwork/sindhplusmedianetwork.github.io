Mah Noor Ali Beats Indian Rival to Reach Malaysia Junior Open Semifinal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Mah Noor Ali stormed into the semifinals of the Malaysia Junior Open Squash Championship 2026 with a commanding victory over India's fifth seed Divyanshi Jain in the quarterfinal. The Peshawar player, the eldest of the Ali sisters, won 3-0 at the National Squash Centre in Kuala Lumpur, taking the games 11-7, 11-6 and 11-6. She did not allow her Indian opponent to win a single game in the match, sealing her place in the final four in dominant fashion.

The championship is billed as the world's first diamond-category junior open event, according to the report carried by 24News. Mah Noor Ali will now contest the semifinal as she chases a place in the title decider.
