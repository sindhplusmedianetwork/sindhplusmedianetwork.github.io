Petroleum Dealers Warn of Another Strike Over Unmet Demands

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Petroleum Dealers Association warned on Monday that dealers would shut petrol pumps unless the government fixes fuel prices for 30 days at a time, giving it 15 days to resolve the issue. Dealers demanded their margin be raised immediately to 8 percent of the retail price, saying daily price changes were draining pump owners' working capital and refineries had cut supplies. The association said the daily pricing regime introduced in July remains in place and continues to hurt dealers.

Petrol has fallen from 369.60 rupees per litre on November 4 to 322 rupees, but dealers say the frequent changes make it impossible to plan purchases. They warned that if the government does not act within 15 days, petrol pumps across the country will be closed. The warning comes as the government has repeatedly revised fuel prices in recent months, most recently cutting petrol by 3 rupees a litre.

A strike by dealers would disrupt fuel supply for transport and businesses across Pakistan.
