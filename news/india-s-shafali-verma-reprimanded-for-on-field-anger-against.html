India's Shafali Verma Reprimanded for On-Field Anger Against Zimbabwe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian women's cricketer Shafali Verma has been reprimanded after displaying anger during the match against Zimbabwe. Match officials found that she breached Level 1 of the ICC Code of Conduct during the game. The reprimand relates to her on-field behaviour in the course of the Zimbabwe fixture.

Level 1 breaches of the ICC Code of Conduct cover minor offences relating to player conduct during international matches. The decision was announced following the standard review process for code of conduct matters in the women's game.
