New gold and copper reserves discovered in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has reported a major new copper and gold discovery in Balochistan, raising fresh hopes for the country's mineral sector. Mari Energies told the Pakistan Stock Exchange that it had found copper and gold at the East Siah Diq target, known as ESD-1, in Chagai district, about 50 kilometres east of Reko Diq. The company said the mineralisation stretches across an area of 1.4 square kilometres.

National Resources Limited separately reported copper and gold mineralisation across eight discoveries in the same district. Both announcements were made at the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026. The companies stressed that the findings are still at the exploration stage and do not yet amount to a formal mineral resource or reserve.

Mari Energies plans further drilling to a depth of 1,000 metres, and samples have been sent to an Australian company and to REAS for testing, with a technical review carried out by June Mortimer Geosciences. The company expects to begin obtaining copper and gold from Chagai in the first quarter of 2027.
