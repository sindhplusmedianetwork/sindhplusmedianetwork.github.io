YouTuber Dicky Bhai Breaks Down in Tears Playing GTA 6, Clip Goes Viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani YouTuber Saad ur Rehman, known as Dicky Bhai, could not control his emotions while playing the video game GTA 6, and clips of him crying have gone viral on social media. During a three-day live stream, he became overwhelmed at the deaths of key characters in the game's final scenes and burst into tears in front of the camera. Social media users reacted with amusement to the viral clips.

One remarked that he had not cried this much even at the time of his arrest. His father recently advised parents to keep their children away from GTA 6. The emotional moment has become one of the most talked-about clips from his recent streams.
