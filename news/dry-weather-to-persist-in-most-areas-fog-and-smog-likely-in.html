Dry Weather to Persist in Most Areas, Fog and Smog Likely in Punjab Cities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dry weather is expected in most parts of the country during the next 24 hours while cold conditions will continue in upper areas, with partly cloudy skies expected in northern mountainous regions. In Islamabad and surrounding areas the weather is expected to remain partly cloudy and cold in the morning and night hours. Most districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are expected to remain dry, with cold and partly cloudy weather in upper districts, while light rain with light snowfall on mountains may occur at a few places in Chitral, Dir, Swat, Kohistan and surrounding areas in the evening and night.

Most districts of Punjab are expected to remain dry and cold at night and in the morning, with fog and smog likely at a few places in Sialkot, Narowal, Lahore, Sheikhupura, Okara, Khanewal, Multan, Khanpur, Kot Addu, Bahawalpur and surrounding areas. Most districts of Sindh are expected to remain dry with cold nights and mornings in upper districts, while most districts of Balochistan are expected to remain dry with cold and partly cloudy weather in northern districts. Gilgit-Baltistan and Kashmir are expected to remain cold and partly cloudy, with light rain and light snowfall on mountains possible at a few places in Gilgit-Baltistan at night, and the lowest temperatures recorded were minus 7 degrees Celsius in Leh, minus 5 in Skardu and minus 1 in Gupis, Gilgit and Astore.
