Maryam Nawaz Calls Husband Safdar's Remarks on Women 'Heartbreaking'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has commented on the remarks about women made by her husband, retired Captain Safdar, which had come under criticism on social media. In a post on X, she titled her message 'Heartbreaking' and wrote that it described the silent battle a woman, a wife, fights behind closed doors. She wrote that such a woman must find strength even when she feels like breaking.

According to her post, some battles are fought in silence and some wounds are invisible. Captain Safdar's remarks about women had earlier drawn widespread criticism on social media. Maryam Nawaz's own comment on the controversy adds a personal dimension to the debate, as she is both the chief minister of Punjab and the person at the centre of the remarks made by her husband.
