PM Vows to Restore Progress in KP, Slams PTI's 'Callous' Record on Terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Thursday chaired the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa provincial apex committee in Peshawar, marking his first visit to the province since emergency and governor's rule were imposed there on October 11. Addressing the meeting, the prime minister vowed that the federal government would bring back progress to the province, which he said had suffered during the PTI's tenures. He accused the previous provincial governments of 'callousness' and 'criminal negligence' in the fight against terrorism, claiming that terrorists were allowed to return after peace had been established at great sacrifice.

The prime minister said the sacrifices made in the war against terror had been washed away by those decisions. The session was attended by Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir, KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi, federal ministers and newly appointed provincial advisers. The meeting carried out a detailed review of the province's security situation and law and order.

Officials said the decisions taken at the meeting would be implemented to restore lasting peace in the province.
