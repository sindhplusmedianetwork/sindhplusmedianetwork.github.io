Three Die After Consuming Toxic Substance in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three people died in Karachi after consuming a toxic substance in separate incidents in Landhi, Gadap City and Lyari. In Landhi, a 22-year-old woman, Aqsa, wife of Anas, died after mysteriously consuming a toxic substance at flat number 5 of Benazir Society near Landhi railway station in Qaidabad, and her body was shifted to Jinnah Hospital. In Gadap City, an 18-year-old girl, Azra, daughter of Akram, died after consuming a toxic substance at her home near the Baqai toll plaza on the Superhighway, and her body was taken to Jinnah Hospital after legal formalities.

Gadap City SHO Mumtaz Marwat said he had no knowledge of any such incident. In Lyari, a 30-year-old man, Abdul Rehman, died after consuming a toxic substance at home near Khaliq Juma Hall, and his body was shifted to Civil Hospital. Police are investigating the incidents.
