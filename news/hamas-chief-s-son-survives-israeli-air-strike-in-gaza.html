Hamas Chief's Son Survives Israeli Air Strike in Gaza

WhatsApp Facebook X

In Gaza, the son of the Hamas chief remained safe along with his family in an Israeli air strike. Izz al-Din al-Hayya, son of Khalil al-Hayya, survived the strike, though his daughter-in-law and several others were injured. Senior leader Basem Naim said Israeli forces targeted al-Hayya's son and his family in an early-morning air strike.

He said there was no loss of life in the attack. According to Basem Naim, Izz al-Din al-Hayya, born in 1997, is Khalil al-Hayya's last surviving son. His four sons have been killed in Israeli attacks.
