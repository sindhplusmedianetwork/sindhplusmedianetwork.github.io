Educational institutions in Nankana Sahib to remain closed for Baba Guru Nanak birth anniversary celebrations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A notification has been issued to keep government and private educational institutions closed in Nankana Sahib in connection with the 557th birth anniversary celebrations of Baba Guru Nanak Dev Ji. According to the notification, all government and private schools in the city will remain closed from November 22 to 25. Government MC Girls High School and Government Girls High School Fatima Jinnah will remain closed from November 20 to 25.

The buildings of both schools will be used to accommodate Sikh pilgrims. Government Guru Nanak Graduate College Boys will also remain closed from November 20 to 25, while Government Guru Nanak Graduate College Women and private colleges will remain closed from November 23 to 25. The notification clarified that the closure of educational institutions will not affect examinations and all examinations will be held as scheduled.
