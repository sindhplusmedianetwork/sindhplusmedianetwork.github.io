Defending Champions Kipruto, Obiri Eye Back-to-Back New York Marathon Titles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defending champions Benson Kipruto and Hellen Obiri of Kenya will chase history and back-to-back titles in Sunday's New York Marathon. About 60,000 runners from 140 nations are expected to compete over 26.2 miles through the five boroughs of New York. Kipruto won the closest New York men's marathon in history last year, edging compatriot Alex Mutiso by three-hundredths of a second in 2:08:09, and will try to become the first back-to-back men's winner since Geoffrey Mutai of Kenya in 2013.

Obiri, who won the New York Marathon in 2023 and 2025, will try to become only the fourth woman to win a third career New York title after Grete Waitz, Mary Keitany and Paula Radcliffe. The 36-year-old also won the Boston Marathon in 2023 and 2024 and took bronze at the 2024 Paris Olympics marathon. Also in the field is Ethiopia's Deresa Geleta, making his North America marathon debut, and Swede Andreas Almgren, who set a European half-marathon record of 58:06 last month and is making his marathon debut.

Clear conditions and perfect running temperatures are expected for the 50th edition of the race.
