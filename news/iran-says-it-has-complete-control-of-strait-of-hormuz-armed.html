Iran Says It Has Complete Control of Strait of Hormuz, Armed Forces Chief Claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's armed forces chief of staff, Major General Ali Abdollahi, has said that Tehran has complete control of the Strait of Hormuz, according to reports citing Iran's Tasnim news agency. He said Iran's army and naval forces have complete control and oversight of the coasts of the Persian Gulf, the islands and the Strait of Hormuz, contrary to what he called propaganda by United States leaders. Abdollahi added that the strategic waterway is completely closed, and that no vessel is allowed to transit it outside of Iranian regulations.

The Strait of Hormuz is a vital route through which around a fifth of the world's oil and liquefied natural gas used to travel, and control of it has been fiercely contested since war engulfed the region in late February. The United States has imposed a naval counter-blockade on Iranian ports, while Iran claims control over the strait and regularly targets vessels that attempt to cross without its authorisation. Oil prices have stayed near six-month highs as attacks on Saudi Arabia keep investors wary, even as diplomatic efforts to resolve the Middle East conflict continue.
