Lahore Court Seeks PM Counsel's Arguments in Rs10bn Defamation Case Against Imran Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An additional sessions court in Lahore on Saturday sought arguments from Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif's counsel on a miscellaneous application in his Rs10 billion defamation suit against former prime minister Imran Khan. Additional Sessions Judge Yalmaz Ghani heard the case, in which Shehbaz alleged that the PTI founder levelled false allegations against him in connection with the Panama Papers controversy. Imran Khan's counsel Muhammad Hussain Chotya presented arguments on the miscellaneous application.

After hearing him, the court sought arguments from Shehbaz Sharif's lawyers on the same application. The proceedings will continue as the court takes up the next stage of the long-running defamation case.
