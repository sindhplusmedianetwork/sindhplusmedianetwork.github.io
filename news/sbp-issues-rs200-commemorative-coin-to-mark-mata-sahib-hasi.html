SBP issues Rs200 commemorative coin to mark Mata Sahib Hasi Devi's 200th birth anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The State Bank of Pakistan has issued a Rs200 commemorative coin to mark the 200th birth anniversary of Pujya Mata Sahib Hasi Devi, the bank said on Friday. Mata Sahib Hasi Devi was the fifth head of the historic Shadani Darbar in the Hayat Pitafi area of Sukkur division's Ghotki district. The coin is made of cupro-nickel and measures 30 millimetres in diameter.

It weighs 13.5 grammes. The commemorative coin is available through the exchange counters of all field offices of the SBP Banking Services Corporation. The issue marks the 200th birth anniversary of Mata Sahib Hasi Devi.
