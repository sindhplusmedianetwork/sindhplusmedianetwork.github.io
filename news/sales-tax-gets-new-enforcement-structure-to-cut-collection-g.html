Sales tax gets new enforcement structure to cut collection gap

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has decided to reverse the 17-year functional integration of sales tax and income tax collection after the sales tax revenue gap widened to Rs3.4 trillion. A dedicated member for sales tax and federal excise duty will report directly to the Federal Board of Revenue chairman. Separate sales tax zones headed by commissioners will be set up to focus only on sales tax collection.

The restructuring will begin at Large Taxpayer Offices on December 1. Officials expect the changes to be completed within two months. The move is aimed at closing the large gap between the sales tax that should be collected and the amount actually recovered.

The government believes a separate enforcement structure will improve compliance and raise revenue.
