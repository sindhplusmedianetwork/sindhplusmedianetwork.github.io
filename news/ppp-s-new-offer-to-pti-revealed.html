PPP's new offer to PTI revealed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Journalist Syed Zeeshan Aziz has reported, citing internal sources, that the PPP has held four meetings with opposition leaders and indicated a new offer to the PTI. He said the understanding reached in the meetings is that if the PTI does not support the 28th Amendment, the PPP will walk out of parliament when governor's rule comes up for a vote rather than backing it. Aziz shared the claim on social media platform X, saying the move could create difficulties for the ruling coalition.

He also reported details of Barrister Gohar's meeting at Mohsin Naqvi's residence on October 27, saying Gohar and Allama Nasir Abbas were initially refused a meeting with PTI founder Imran Khan at Adiala Jail. He said the authorities agreed after Barrister Salman Safdar's name was added to the list, and Imran Khan's first question to Safdar after he entered the jail was how he had managed to get in. Aziz further claimed that two figures in Punjab who are considered very close to the establishment are eyeing the chief ministership, one of them silent for now under compulsion, while the other is powerful, has a business background and has set up an active social media cell.
