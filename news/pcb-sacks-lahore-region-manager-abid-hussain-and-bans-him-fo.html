PCB sacks Lahore region manager Abid Hussain and bans him for 10 years over corruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has imposed a 10-year ban on Abid Hussain, manager cricket operations of the Lahore Region Cricket Association, and dismissed him from his job. In a notification, the PCB said Abid Hussain faced allegations of corruption and mismanagement, as well as allegations of threatening regional and zonal coaches. According to the board, Abid Hussain could not give a satisfactory answer to the charges against him before the PCB panel.

The board said he will not be able to work with the PCB, regional cricket associations or affiliated departments for 10 years. The action is one of the strongest disciplinary measures taken against a regional cricket official, and it comes as the board continues to oversee domestic cricket administration in the regions.
