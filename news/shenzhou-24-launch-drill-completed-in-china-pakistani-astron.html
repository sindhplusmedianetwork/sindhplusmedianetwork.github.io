Shenzhou-24 launch drill completed in China, Pakistani astronaut to fly after training

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A successful launch drill for the Shenzhou-24 spacecraft was conducted at the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre in northwestern China. All three astronauts performed operations correctly in the pre-flight joint exercise. A China Astronaut Research and Training Centre official said the astronauts were determined and completed all operations accurately.

The exercise was part of preparations for the Shenzhou-24 mission. After completing training, a Pakistani astronaut will travel to space aboard China's Shenzhou-24 spacecraft.
