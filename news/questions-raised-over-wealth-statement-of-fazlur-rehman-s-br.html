Questions raised over wealth statement of Fazlur Rehman's brother

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Major discrepancies have surfaced in the wealth statement of Ziaur Rehman, the brother of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman and a central party leader. The statement records Rs 12.5 million in cash received from his wife's father in 2022, with no banking record, written agreement or legal document to support it. It also shows 375 kanals of inherited agricultural land valued at zero for five straight years.

A two-kanal house in Dera Ismail Khan is shown valued unchanged at Rs 7.59 million since 2011. Questions are also raised about salary deposits of over Rs 6.6 million in 2024.
