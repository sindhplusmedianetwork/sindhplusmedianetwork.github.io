Mailsi: accused in missing girl's abduction and murder case dies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In Mailsi, the alleged accused in the abduction and murder case of a missing girl has died. According to police officials, the incident took place during a search operation in the case. Police said the accused was moving the girl's body to another location when officers began chasing him.

As the police closed in, the accused shot himself, officials said. He died from the shot.
