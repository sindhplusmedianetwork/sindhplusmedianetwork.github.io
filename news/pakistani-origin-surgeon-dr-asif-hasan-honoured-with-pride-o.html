Pakistani-Origin Surgeon Dr Asif Hasan Honoured With Pride of Britain Lifetime Award

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Pakistani-origin heart surgeon Dr Asif Hasan has been honoured with the Pride of Britain Lifetime Achievement Award. The award recognises 35 years of service in children's heart treatment and surgery in the United Kingdom. Over his long career, Dr Hasan has performed more than 4,000 children's heart operations and nearly 400 heart transplants.

The award ceremony turned emotional when several of his recovered patients made a surprise appearance on stage. Colleagues described him as a surgeon whose hands gave thousands of children a second chance at life. The honour adds to a career already celebrated across Britain's medical community.
