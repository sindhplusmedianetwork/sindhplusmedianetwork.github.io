Way paved for satellite internet launch in Pakistan as six companies register

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Interest from foreign and local companies in providing satellite internet services in Pakistan is growing, with six satellite internet companies so far registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan, including four new companies registered after the establishment of the Pakistan Space Activities Regulatory Board. The SECP informed the Senate Standing Committee that registering a company alone does not authorise the commercial provision of satellite internet services, for which a PSARB no-objection certificate is mandatory. The SECP said the security clearances of the foreign directors and shareholders of Starlink, Galaxy Space and Space Cell Pakistan remain pending with the Ministry of Interior, due to which the process could not move forward.

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority will issue licences after clearance from the Ministry of Interior and issuance of the PSARB NOC. Satellite internet services can begin only after all regulatory requirements and security procedures are completed.
