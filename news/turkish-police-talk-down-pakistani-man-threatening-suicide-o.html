Turkish Police Talk Down Pakistani Man Threatening Suicide on Istanbul Bridge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 30-year-old Pakistani man brought traffic to a standstill on Istanbul's 15 July Martyrs Bridge after stopping his car, climbing onto its roof and threatening to take his own life. He waved Pakistani and Turkish flags and held a pistol while shouting anti-Israel slogans, drawing a large police response to the busy crossing over the Bosphorus. Turkish police officers spent about an hour and a half negotiating with him, working to calm him and persuade him to come down.

Their efforts succeeded, and the man was brought down safely before being taken into custody. Traffic on the bridge, one of Istanbul's busiest crossings, was badly disrupted throughout the standoff. No injuries were reported during the incident, and the man was handed over to the authorities for further questioning.
