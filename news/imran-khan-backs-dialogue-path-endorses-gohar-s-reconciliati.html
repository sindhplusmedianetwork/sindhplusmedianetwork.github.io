Imran Khan Backs Dialogue Path, Endorses Gohar's Reconciliation Policy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The founder of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf, Imran Khan, has agreed to move matters forward through talks instead of confrontation, endorsing Barrister Gohar's reconciliatory policy. Details emerged from a meeting between the party founder and his lawyers, Barrister Salman and Latif Khosa, in which the founder and his wife backed Gohar's policy of reconciliation. The founder directed party leaders to decide the future course of action and instructed Gohar to continue his role, with the tone of the meeting described as conciliatory and an agreement reached that matters should be advanced through negotiations rather than reaching a dead end.

The founder said he watches the news on television and reads newspapers, and is aware of the current political situation and television programmes. He stopped members from returning to the standing committees of the National Assembly and Senate for now, though no discussion took place on resignations or leaving parliament. On the 28th constitutional amendment, he said a decision would be taken after deliberation once the details of the amendments emerged.

The founder also directed efforts for the release of Aleema Khan, and was told that if matters proceeded well, Bushra Bibi could receive treatment and be shifted to hospital. In a separate development, the responsibilities of the Insaf Lawyers Forum were taken from Intizar Panjutha and handed to Niazullah Niazi.
