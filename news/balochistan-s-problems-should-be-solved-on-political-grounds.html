Balochistan's Problems Should Be Solved on Political Grounds: Liaquat Baloch

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami naib ameer Liaquat Baloch met a delegation of Baloch students in Lahore. During the meeting, the students discussed educational problems and the situation in Balochistan with him. Speaking on the occasion, Liaquat Baloch said the artificial nature of the public mandate has deepened the crises in Balochistan.

He said the problems of Balochistan should be resolved on political grounds. He added that Baloch students are very dear to us, and respecting them is a national duty.
