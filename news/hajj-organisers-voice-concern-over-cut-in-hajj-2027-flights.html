Hajj Organisers Voice Concern Over Cut in Hajj 2027 Flights From Peshawar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Hajj Group Organisers Association of Pakistan (HOAP) Khyber Pakhtunkhwa zone has expressed deep concern over the airlines' decision to reduce flights and seats from Peshawar International Airport for Hajj 2027. The association said the cut could cause difficulties for thousands of pilgrims from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It warned that pilgrims may be forced to travel from other cities at extra cost.

HOAP urged the federal Ministry of Religious Affairs, the Civil Aviation Authority and airline managements to take immediate notice of the matter. It called on the authorities to ensure an adequate number of flights and seats from Peshawar for the pilgrims.
