Emaar approves nearly AED 12 billion offer to take subsidiary private

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dubai real estate giant Emaar Properties has approved a cash offer of about AED 12 billion, around 3.3 billion US dollars, to buy the remaining minority shares of its subsidiary Emaar Development. The offer is priced at AED 15 per share and aims to bring the residential development business fully under group control and pave the way for delisting Emaar Development from the Dubai Financial Market. Emaar Properties and its subsidiary Emirates Property Holdings already own 80.1613 percent of Emaar Development, and buying the remaining 19.8387 percent will cost about AED 11.9 billion.

The offer price is about 19.4 percent higher than Emaar Development's closing share price of AED 12.56 on November 2, valuing the company at around AED 60 billion in total. The cash offer opened on November 6, and shareholders can accept it until 3pm UAE time on December 10, with the results expected to be announced on December 11. Emaar said it has enough funds to pay shareholders who accept, with payment due within three business days once the purchase becomes unconditional.

Emaar Development's third-quarter profit rose about 8 percent to AED 3.5 billion, while its revenue increased 14 percent to AED 8.8 billion.
