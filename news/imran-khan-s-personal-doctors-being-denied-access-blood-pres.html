Imran Khan's personal doctors being denied access, blood pressure reached 193, says Salman Akram Raja

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja has said that the personal doctors of party founder Imran Khan are not being given access to him. Speaking to the media on Adiala Road, he said arranging a meeting is not a favour or a relief, and questioned why the doctors were being kept away. He said they were told that Khan's blood pressure had reached 193, which he described as alarming.

He added that humanity demands Khan be treated in a hospital, as a court order exists in this regard. Raja said Khan is not seeking any deal and only wants his wife's operation to be discussed. Imran Khan's sister Uzma Khan said relief will only come when he is treated in a hospital under Supreme Court orders.
