UAE eases work visas for Pakistanis, scrapping document rule for 13 permit types

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Arab Emirates has removed the requirement for supporting documents for 13 types of work permits, including golden visa, freelance and overseas recruitment permits, under its zero-bureaucracy campaign. The Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation took the major reform step under the government's zero-bureaucracy programme, removing the compulsory supporting-document condition for the 13 permit types. Under the new reforms, the data fields required on application forms for the specified work permits have been cut by 75 to 97 per cent, while the time taken to issue permits has also been reduced significantly, which should make recruitment faster and easier for foreign workers, including the roughly 30,000 Pakistanis who obtain UAE visas each month.

All 13 services are now available through the ministry's unified digital channels, allowing employers to apply completely online. The 13 types include two-year overseas recruitment and transfer permits, the golden residency work permit, the freelance work permit, and permits for family-sponsored residents, part-time work, temporary work, missions, private tutoring, student training, juveniles aged 15 to under 18, Emirati and GCC citizens, and national trainees. The ministry said the reforms aim to facilitate private businesses, protect workers' rights and make the labour market more effective, and that a new phase of reforms has begun to provide simpler procedures and wider digital services.
