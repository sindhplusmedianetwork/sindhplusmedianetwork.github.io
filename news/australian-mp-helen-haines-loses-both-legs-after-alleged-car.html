Australian MP Helen Haines loses both legs after alleged car attack in Wangaratta

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read

Australian MP Helen Haines has lost both legs after a man allegedly drove his car into her with intent to kill in Wangaratta, Victoria, on Monday. Police say 43-year-old Brenden Nugent deliberately targeted the parliamentarian in the shocking attack. Her husband Phil Haines confirmed that while she lost her second leg during treatment, her mental state and heart health remain stable.

Nugent was arrested and appeared in court on Wednesday on three charges, with police saying attempted-murder charges may follow. His lawyer told the court that he has mental health issues. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese and other political leaders expressed deep regret over the incident.

The attack has sparked a national conversation in Australia about the safety of elected representatives.
