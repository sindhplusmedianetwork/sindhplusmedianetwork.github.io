Sindh, Hubei Agree to Deepen Cooperation in Agri, Tech and Trade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah met Hubei Governor Li Dianxun and Party Secretary Guan Zhi'ou in Wuhan to deepen cooperation between the province and the Chinese province. The talks covered agriculture, technology, trade, investment and education, marking 75 years of Pakistan-China diplomatic ties. Both sides discussed hybrid rice, high-yield seeds, farm machinery and a joint digital-agriculture laboratory, along with cold-chain development.

Port logistics, industrial automation and Karachi's traffic management also featured in the discussions. The meeting noted that 1,000 agricultural graduates had been trained at the Huazhong Agricultural University under existing cooperation. The two sides agreed to take the partnership into the next phase of CPEC.
