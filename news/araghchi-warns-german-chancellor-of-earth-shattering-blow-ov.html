Araghchi warns German chancellor of 'earth-shattering blow' over new attack

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has warned German Chancellor Friedrich Merz of an 'earth-shattering blow'. He said Europe could pay a far heavier price than energy costs if there is any new attack on Iran. The warning came after Merz complained that the war against Iran had raised Europe's energy costs by more than 100 billion euros.

Writing on X, Araghchi recalled that Merz had welcomed the US-Israeli attacks on Iran last June. He noted that Merz had said Israel was 'doing the dirty work for all of us'. Araghchi declared that red lines were now a thing of the past.
