Babar Azam's Controversial Caught-Behind Dismissal in Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Goes Viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A video clip of national Test captain Babar Azam being given out caught behind under controversial circumstances in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy has gone viral on social media, drawing criticism of the umpiring from cricket fans. Playing for Lahore Whites against FATA, Babar was dismissed for 28. The footage shows Lahore Whites at 120 for three with Babar at the crease, when a delivery from fast bowler Afaq appeared to pass the bat without touching it and carry through to the wicketkeeper.

On appeal, the umpire raised his finger and gave Babar out. Fans shared the clip widely, questioning the decision. The Pakistan captain's poor run of form continues; he had also failed to score big in the President Trophy earlier.

Meanwhile, wicketkeeper-batter Mohammad Rizwan made a strong domestic comeback with a double century.
