Ronaldo's stunning hat-trick takes him to 985 career goals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cristiano Ronaldo produced another masterclass as Al-Nassr beat Al-Kholood 5-3 in the Saudi Pro League on October 29. With the scores level at 2-2 at half-time, the Portuguese star transformed the game in the second half. He completed his hat-trick within just 15 minutes, scoring in the 66th, 72nd and 81st minutes.

His first came from a Joao Felix pass, the second was a header, and Sadio Mane set up the third. The treble took Ronaldo's career goal tally to 985, leaving him only 15 short of the historic 1,000-goal mark. It was the 67th hat-trick of his career and his seventh for Al-Nassr.

He also had two first-half goals ruled out for offside. The goals have come for Sporting Lisbon, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al-Nassr and Portugal.
