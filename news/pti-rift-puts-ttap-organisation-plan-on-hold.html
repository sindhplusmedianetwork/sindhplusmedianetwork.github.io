PTI Rift Puts TTAP Organisation Plan on Hold

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Differences within the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf leadership have forced the Tehreek-e-Tahafuz-e-Aain-e-Pakistan (TTAP) to put on hold its plan to establish an independent organisational political arm, with the two sides offering conflicting accounts of whether PTI had endorsed the proposal. In mid-October, TTAP had told The Express Tribune that, with PTI's backing, it planned to establish its own political arm and develop a formal organisational structure at the provincial level, to address the organisational challenges facing PTI and strengthen TTAP's political role. According to PTI sources, the publication of The Express Tribune mid-October report triggered friction between the two sides, with PTI leaders expressing strong reservations about the proposed arrangement.

The sources said TTAP leaders Asad Qaiser and Akhunzada Hussain had faced criticism from within PTI, with party leaders reminding them of their political standing and warning that they could be called back from TTAP if required. TTAP spokesperson Akhunzada Hussain, however, disputed this account, saying the proposal had been discussed at a meeting attended by PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan and Secretary General Salman Akram Raja, both of whom had endorsed the suggestion to allow TTAP to establish its own political arm. He said TTAP later learnt through intermediaries that there was resistance within PTI to the understanding reached at the meeting, but maintained that PTI had never formally communicated any reservations to TTAP.

PTI's central information secretary disputed the spokesperson's account, saying Barrister Gohar had told him that the proposal had not been approved as a formal agenda item during the meeting and that PTI would not allow the formation of such a structure under any circumstances. Hussain confirmed that the plan to establish a formal organisational structure had been put on hold because of the differences within PTI.
