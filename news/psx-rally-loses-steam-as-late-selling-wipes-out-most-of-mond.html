PSX Rally Loses Steam as Late Selling Wipes Out Most of Monday's Gains

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Early optimism at the Pakistan Stock Exchange gave way to selling pressure on Monday, as the benchmark KSE-100 index surrendered most of its intra-day gains in the second half of the session. The index opened strongly, climbing to 177,976.16 in the opening minutes, and later touched an intra-day high of 178,746.45 points. Selling in fertiliser, commercial banks, technology and communication, leather and tanneries, and refinery stocks then dragged the index down from its high.

It eventually settled at 177,473.86, rising just 91.29 points, or 0.05 percent, from the previous close of 177,382.57. Engro Holdings, Hub Power and Mari Energies closed in positive territory, while Engro Fertilisers, Oil and Gas Development Company and Systems Ltd remained under pressure. Trading volume decreased to 551.5 million shares, compared with 620.7 million on Friday, and the value of traded shares stood at Rs22.5 billion.

Of the 496 companies traded, 199 rose, 270 fell and 27 remained unchanged, while foreign investors sold shares worth Rs426 million.
