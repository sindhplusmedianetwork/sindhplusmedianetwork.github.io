Young Fan's Banner Claims He Sold Sister's Mobile Phone for Tri-Series Ticket

A young cricket fan stole the show during the Sri Lanka-England match in Rawalpindi with a hilarious banner. The banner read: 'I sold my sister's mobile to get this seat,' drawing laughter from the crowd and viewers alike. The on-air commentator could not resist a joke of his own, quipping: 'must have been a cheap phone.' The tri-nation ODI series between Pakistan, England and Sri Lanka is pulling large crowds to the stadium.

The first three matches are being played in Rawalpindi. The remaining matches, including the final, are scheduled to be held in Lahore.
