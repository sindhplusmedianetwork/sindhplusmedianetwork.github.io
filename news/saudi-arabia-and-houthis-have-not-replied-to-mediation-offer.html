Saudi Arabia and Houthis Have Not Replied to Mediation Offer, Says Araghchi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said that no response has been received from Saudi Arabia and Yemen's Houthis to the mediation offer. Speaking in an interview with Iraqi television, he said the offer was made early this month at a time when the Houthis had stepped up attacks on Saudi Arabia. On the other side, Yemeni government forces claimed to have killed 558 Houthi fighters in the last 24 hours.

The Yemeni forces also said they had destroyed 135 military vehicles of the Houthi militia and halted the Houthi advance in Taiz province. The claims come amid continued fighting between the Yemeni government forces and the Houthi militia, alongside the wider regional tensions involving Saudi Arabia, Yemen and Iran.
