SPI inflation accelerates past 12% on surging energy prices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Sensitive Price Indicator climbed 12.31 per cent year-on-year for the week ended November 5, the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics reported, driven by sharply higher energy prices amid Middle East tensions. LPG prices soared 114.88 per cent, electricity charges for the lowest quintile rose 52.38 per cent, diesel 39.27 per cent and petrol 36.34 per cent, while tomatoes rose 78.89 per cent. On a week-on-week basis, the indicator edged up 0.29 per cent.

Of the 51 essential items tracked, 21 became costlier, 10 became cheaper and 20 remained unchanged. The figures show that energy costs remain the main force pushing weekly inflation higher for households.
