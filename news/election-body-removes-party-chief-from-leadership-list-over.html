Election Body Removes Party Chief From Leadership List Over Missed Internal Polls

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Election Commission of Pakistan has removed Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui's name as the convener of Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan from its official website, leaving the party among 13 political parties without a recognised head in the commission's records. The commission removed Siddiqui over the party's inability to hold an intra-party election. The list also includes the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf, Tehreek-e-Labbaik Pakistan, Pakhtunkhwa Milli Awami Party and Pakistan Sunni Tehreek, along with eight relatively lesser-known political organisations with no representation in the national or provincial assemblies.

The commission has issued a show-cause notice to MQM-P over its failure to hold intra-party elections, and the party has submitted its response. MQM-P last held intra-party elections on October 15, 2021, and since then the commission has issued several notices to the party, which secured extensions totalling one year at different stages to fulfil the requirement. The latest extension expired on October 15, 2026, and under the relevant legal and regulatory requirements, political parties are required to hold intra-party elections at intervals not exceeding five years.

The removal of Siddiqui's name comes amid the party's failure to hold fresh intra-party elections within the prescribed timeframe, raising questions about its leadership status in the commission's official records.
