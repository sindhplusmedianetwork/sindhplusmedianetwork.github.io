11-Month-Old Baby Found Alive After Being Buried for 24 Hours in the US

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An 11-month-old baby was found alive in the US state of South Carolina after being buried underground for nearly 24 hours. The baby, Koah Sharp, was wrapped in a towel inside a dog cage in the woods. He was found during a large search operation on Tuesday, about 50 yards from his grandfather's home, after searchers spotted freshly dug soil.

The baby was taken to hospital for medical care. His father, Michael Comer, had been arrested on Monday night on charges of theft and resisting arrest.
