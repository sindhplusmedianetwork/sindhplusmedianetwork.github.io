Three Convicted of Taking Woman from UK to Pakistan for Forced Marriage

Three people have been convicted of taking a young woman from the United Kingdom to Pakistan and forcing her into marriage, according to British police. West Midlands Police said the victim, because of mental and other vulnerabilities, did not have the capacity to consent to marriage. The case is said to be the first of its kind in the United Kingdom.

Muhammad Bashir, Woman B and Mubeen Akhtar, all from Birmingham, took the woman to Pakistan in the autumn of 2024 and married her there. Following a trial at Birmingham Crown Court, all three were found guilty. They will be sentenced at a later date.
