Pakistan and Saudi Arabia agree to deepen cooperation in oil and energy sector

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Saudi Arabia on Friday agreed to further strengthen cooperation in oil storage, refinery upgradation, liquefied petroleum gas supplies and minerals. The understanding was reached during a meeting in Islamabad between Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik and Saudi Ambassador to Pakistan Dr Rajeh Tami Albugamy. Malik highlighted investment opportunities in Pakistan's bonded oil storage policy and the proposed Hub petroleum complex in Balochistan, offering Saudi Arabia greater flexibility in energy supply routes and access to Asian markets.

He said the proposed complex would have facilities to accommodate very large crude oil carriers. The minister said Pakistan had approved a tax-neutral bonded storage policy, offering Saudi companies an opportunity to establish commercial oil storage facilities in the country. During the meeting, the Saudi envoy announced the introduction of same-day visa processing for Pakistani citizens, saying it would promote business, cultural and people-to-people contacts.

Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening energy security, investment and bilateral ties. A day earlier, the Saudi envoy had met Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb to discuss expanding economic cooperation in agriculture, minerals and mining.
