LHC Declares Taking MDCAT Papers Back From Candidates Illegal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has declared the practice of taking MDCAT question papers back from candidates after the examination illegal. The Rawalpindi bench allowed the students' petitions and ordered that all examination question papers be uploaded to the app immediately, with compliance directed the same day. Justice Ahsan Raza Kazmi announced the verdict, ruling that the PMDC could not hand question papers to candidates and then take them back.

Assistant Attorney General Malik Zaheer Arshad represented the federation and presented its position, while students Rehan Shahid and Anabia Tahir had filed the petitions. Their counsel, Barrister Imran Afrasiab, argued that keeping the question papers was the candidates' constitutional and legal right, calling the PMDC's move unconstitutional, illegal and unethical. The court directed the PMDC administration to release all question papers on the app without delay.
