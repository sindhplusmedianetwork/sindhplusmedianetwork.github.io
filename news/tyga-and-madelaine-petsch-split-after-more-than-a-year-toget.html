Tyga and Madelaine Petsch Split After More Than a Year Together

Rapper Tyga and 'Riverdale' actress Madelaine Petsch have reportedly ended their relationship after more than a year of dating, with multiple sources confirming the split to PEOPLE. The pair, aged 36 and 32, first sparked romance rumours in February 2025. They were spotted courtside at a Los Angeles Lakers basketball game at the time.

Neither star publicly confirmed the relationship. Their reported separation comes two months after they were seen enjoying a date at Knott's Scary Farm in California. The reports bring an end to a relationship that the pair had kept largely private.
