Hasina seeks new passport in clearest sign of Bangladesh return

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ousted Bangladeshi prime minister Sheikh Hasina has requested a new passport and national identity card from Bangladesh's embassy in Delhi, according to a letter seen by Reuters. The request would allow her to return from self-imposed exile in India, where she has lived for more than two years. It is the clearest step yet towards her stated plan to return to Bangladesh in December.

Hasina's rule ended in an uprising more than two years ago, and she has since been sentenced to death in absentia by Bangladesh's war-crimes court. She has said she plans to return to surrender before a court. Her Awami League party remains banned in Bangladesh.
