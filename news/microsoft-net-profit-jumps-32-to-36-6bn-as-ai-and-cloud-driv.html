Microsoft net profit jumps 32% to $36.6bn as AI and cloud drive growth

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global technology company Microsoft has announced a sharp rise in net profit for the first quarter of its financial year, with total revenue crossing $92 billion. Net profit grew to $36.63 billion from $27.75 billion in the same quarter last year — an annual increase of about 32 percent. Earnings per share rose from $3.72 to $4.92, reflecting stronger business performance.

Microsoft's total revenue stood at $92.28 billion, with cloud services playing a major role: revenue from Microsoft Cloud reached $63.9 billion, up 30 percent from last year. Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer Amy Hood said the company beat expectations on revenue, operating profit and earnings per share. Chairman and CEO Satya Nadella said the company remains focused on delivering greater capacity and better performance for customers, helping them build their artificial-intelligence systems and strengthening trust through security.

He said the goal is to turn technological innovation into practical benefits and measurable results across industries. Microsoft's fresh financial results come at a time when global technology companies are increasing investment in artificial intelligence and cloud computing.
