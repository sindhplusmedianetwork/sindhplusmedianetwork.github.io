China Extends Trade Truce With US by Two Months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China on Monday effectively extended its trade truce with the United States by two months, announcing it would continue to suspend certain trade measures that were due to expire on Tuesday. A 24 per cent additional tariff on US imports will remain suspended for two months while a 10 per cent tariff stays. Export-control waivers for certain critical minerals will run until January 10, 2027.

The extension comes as Presidents Xi Jinping and Donald Trump are expected to meet for a third time this year at the APEC summit next week. It follows a proposed 60-billion-dollar package of reciprocal tariff cuts agreed after their September summit in Washington.
