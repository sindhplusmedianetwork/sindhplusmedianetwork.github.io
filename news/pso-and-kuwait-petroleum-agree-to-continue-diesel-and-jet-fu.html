PSO and Kuwait Petroleum Agree to Continue Diesel and Jet Fuel Supplies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan State Oil (PSO) and Kuwait Petroleum Corporation (KPC) have agreed to continue the supply of gasoil and jet fuel from Kuwait to Pakistan, while expanding cooperation across the energy sector. The understanding was reached during a visit to Kuwait by a PSO delegation led by Chief Executive Officer Jawwad Ahmed Cheema, which held discussions with KPC's senior leadership, including Chief Executive Officer and Deputy Chairman Sheikh Nawaf Saud Al-Sabah and Sheikh Khaled Ahmed Al-Sabah, Managing Director of International Marketing at KPC and Chief Executive Officer of Kuwait Oil Tanker Company. The two sides renewed their commitment to a 50-year energy partnership and agreed to widen cooperation across supply, storage, shipping, refining, lubricants and investment, with the shared aim of strengthening energy security and promoting long-term growth.

They also agreed to improve the terms of the existing supply arrangements in line with prevailing market conditions and discussed wider business opportunities in base oil supply and lubricants marketing. A key outcome was KPC's plan to use Pakistan's storage and port facilities under the government's recently revised Customs Bonded Storage Policy, with imported products to be sold in the local market and re-exported to regional markets. The remaining formalities and documentation for implementing the initiative are expected to be completed soon in coordination with the relevant Pakistani departments, while the two sides will also explore KPC investment in gas, refining, storage infrastructure, shipping and logistics.

Cheema said KPC's interest in Pakistan's storage and port infrastructure offered important opportunities to strengthen energy security, promote regional trade and attract investment, adding that PSO was committed to turning the talks into solid practical results over the next fifty years.
