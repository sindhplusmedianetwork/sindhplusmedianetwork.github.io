Lahore High Court Issues Key Ruling on Defamation Cases, Sends Matter Back to Tribunal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has set aside the order dismissing a defamation claim on the grounds of limitation, in a significant ruling on defamation cases. The court remarked that if the special tribunal has not been constituted, a citizen cannot be deprived of the right to file a defamation case. Justices Mirza Waqas Rauf and Anwar Hussain allowed the appeal filed by Muhammad Farooq Malik and sent the matter back to the defamation tribunal.

The judgment noted that if the government itself delays the establishment of the forum, the citizen should not have to suffer for that administrative delay.
