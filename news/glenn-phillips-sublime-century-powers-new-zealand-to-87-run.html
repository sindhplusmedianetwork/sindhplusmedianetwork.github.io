Glenn Phillips' Sublime Century Powers New Zealand to 87-Run Win Over India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New Zealand defeated India by 87 runs in the fourth T20 International at Auckland on Friday, powered by a stunning century from Glenn Phillips. Phillips smashed 107 off just 49 balls as New Zealand posted a record 259 for 7, the highest T20I total against India. Michael Bracewell provided strong support with a 25-ball 65, dismantling India's bowling attack.

In reply, India's chase never gained momentum, with Jacob Duffy claiming 4 wickets for 23 runs. Mitchell Santner also reached a personal milestone, passing 150 wickets in T20 internationals. The victory gave New Zealand a commanding position in the five-match series.

The fifth and final T20 will be played in Hamilton on Sunday.
