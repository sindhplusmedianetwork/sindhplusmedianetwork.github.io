Lahore man arrested after beating woman on scooty on busy road

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore police have arrested a man accused of brutally beating a young woman riding a scooty on the busy Mozang Link Road late on Thursday. The attacker, identified as Ghulam Mustafa, was tracked down using data from dozens of Punjab Safe Cities Authority cameras alongside traditional investigation techniques. CCTV footage that sparked widespread public outrage showed the suspect overtaking the woman, placing his motorcycle in front of her scooty, and then subjecting her to brutal torture in the presence of several passersby.

In a shocking detail, none of the onlookers intervened as the man pulled her hair and slapped her repeatedly. Initial police reports say the woman suffered facial injuries, including a nasal bone fracture. A police official said the attacker also threw the victim's bag, containing a laptop worth around Rs90,000, onto the ground, severely damaging it.

A special police team under the supervision of Civil Lines SP Maniza Karamat arrested the suspect, and a case has been lodged on the complaint of the victim's father.
