Police Launch Active Probe Into Epstein's Balmoral Stay With Andrew

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police Scotland has confirmed it has launched an 'active' investigation into Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell's stay at Balmoral Castle with Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. Speaking on Friday, October 30, the police said they had received 'information' and that 'active enquiries are ongoing but these are at a very early stage.' The force added that all enquiries are evidence-led, with the needs of victim survivors at the heart of the investigation. Back in February, Police Scotland said it was keen to speak to anyone with information about Epstein's private jet that flew from London Luton Airport to Edinburgh carrying the former Duke of York and Maxwell.

Epstein was not listed as a passenger on that 2006 flight, prompting the appeal as part of wider inquiries following the release of the Epstein files. According to the Daily Express, the inquiries relate to claims that women were trafficked to the Scottish estate. Balmoral Castle in the Scottish Highlands has belonged to the British Royal Family since 1852, and was where Queen Elizabeth II died in 2022.

The update comes amid King Charles' nine-day Caribbean tour, set to conclude on November 4.
