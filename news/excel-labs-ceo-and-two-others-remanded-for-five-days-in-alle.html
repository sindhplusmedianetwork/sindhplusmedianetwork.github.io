Excel Labs CEO and Two Others Remanded for Five Days in Alleged Fake Vaccine Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A judicial magistrate has approved a five-day physical remand of Excel Labs CEO Dr Nasir Ahmed and two other accused in a case involving the alleged administration of fake flu vaccines to patients. The accused were produced before the court following their arrest by the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA), and Judicial Magistrate Shaista Kundi approved the five-day physical remand of the three suspects, including the company's CEO, Marketing Manager and Assistant Manager, against the FIA Anti-Corruption Circle Islamabad's request for a 14-day remand. According to the FIA, the three accused were arrested over allegations of administering substandard and counterfeit Influvac Tetra vaccines bearing batch number P13 to patients.

The agency said a joint team comprising a drug inspector and FIA officials recovered 27 injections of the suspected fake vaccine from a laboratory of Excel Labs in Islamabad's Blue Area. The FIA informed the court that, according to the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) and the Punjab government, vaccines carrying batch number P13 had neither been imported into Pakistan nor met the required quality standards, and the batch had been declared counterfeit. Records of 26 patients who had received the vaccine, including the daughter of complainant Muhammad Zareef, were also seized.

A case has been registered at the FIA Anti-Corruption Circle Police Station Islamabad under relevant provisions of the Drugs Act 1976 and the DRAP Act 2012, while the accused have not been convicted.
