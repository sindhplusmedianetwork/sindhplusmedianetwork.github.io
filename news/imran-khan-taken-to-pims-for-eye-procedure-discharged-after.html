Imran Khan Taken to PIMS for Eye Procedure, Discharged After Treatment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former prime minister and PTI founder Imran Khan was taken from Adiala jail to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences in Islamabad during the night between Nov 10 and 11. He was brought to the hospital for a follow-up eye check-up and treatment on the advice of his ophthalmologist. The hospital official said Imran underwent a medical examination, including an ocular assessment, before the procedure.

His vital parameters were found normal and he was clinically stable. The ophthalmology team then administered an intravitreal anti-VEGF injection after obtaining informed consent. He remained clinically stable before, during and after the procedure and was discharged at around 2:30am with instructions for further care.

PTI chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan said the party learned of the check-up through media reports and reiterated the demand that Imran be shifted to Shifa International Hospital under the Supreme Court's order of Aug 18.
