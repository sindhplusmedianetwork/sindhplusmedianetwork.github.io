Karachi Port Handles Over 192,000 Tons of Cargo in 24 Hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Port recorded a busy 24 hours, handling 192,559 metric tons of cargo in the period ending at 7am on October 21. Import cargo accounted for 125,469 tons of the total, while export cargo made up 67,090 tons. The cargo mix included container, bulk and liquid cargo alongside cement, clinker, DAP fertilizer and rice shipments.

Thirteen ships were at berth during the 24-hour window, and ten vessels sailed after completing operations. Port authorities said 29 arrivals and three departures are expected over the next 24 hours. The figures reflect the steady flow of trade through Pakistan's busiest seaport.

Karachi Port Trust releases the daily handling update as part of its routine shipping report.
