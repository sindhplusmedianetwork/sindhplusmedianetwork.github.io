Kerala Girl Develops Serious Kidney Disease After Using Fairness Cream, Police Register Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In Kerala, India, police registered a case against a fairness cream brand after a 17-year-old girl allegedly developed a serious kidney disease from using the cream. Her doctor said the cream allegedly contained a toxic amount of mercury. Lab tests at Aster MIMS Mother Hospital found 12,500 parts per million of mercury in the cream.

The permissible limit for mercury is less than 1 part per million. Experts warned that some unregistered fairness creams sold through social media may contain toxic metals such as mercury and lead. Police are investigating the brand in connection with the case.
