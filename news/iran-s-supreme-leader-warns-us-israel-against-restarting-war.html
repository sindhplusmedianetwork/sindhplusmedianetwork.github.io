Iran's Supreme Leader warns US, Israel against restarting war

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Supreme Leader has issued a written warning to the United States and Israel not to restart the war. The statement said that if the two countries attack again, Iran's armed forces and nation will decide when and on what terms the fighting ends. The warning was released after a meeting with the country's top military commanders.

At that meeting, the Leader ordered an immediate and effective response "beyond geographical boundaries" to any new threat. He also directed that an updated list of American and Israeli military targets be prepared. The statement comes as a Qatari delegation arrived in Tehran and Iran reportedly received a letter from US Vice President JD Vance aimed at restarting diplomacy.
