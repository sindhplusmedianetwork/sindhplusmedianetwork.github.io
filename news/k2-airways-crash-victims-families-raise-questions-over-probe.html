K2 Airways crash victims' families raise questions over probe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Families of the victims of the K2 Airways crash near Ormara on July 7 have expressed reservations over the investigation into the disaster. They have demanded a comprehensive probe into the airline's operational history, its management and the maintenance of its aircraft. The families also want scrutiny of the decisions that kept the aircraft in continuous operation before the crash.

They said the airline's management has given no clear assurance about the search for the flight data and cockpit voice recorders. More than three months after the crash, the families say they have received no clear timeline for insurance payments and compensation. They urged the authorities to ensure a transparent and thorough inquiry.
