CDA and Railways in dispute over ownership of billion-rupee plot in Islamabad's I-9

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Railways and the Capital Development Authority are at loggerheads over the ownership of a piece of land worth billions of rupees and the construction of godowns and warehouses in Sector I-9, Islamabad. Pakistan Railways last year auctioned plots situated at Dry Port Road in I-9, Margalla Railway Station, for the construction of godowns and warehouses, and Pakistan Railways Rawalpindi approved the building plans. However, when the matter came to the CDA's notice, the civic agency sealed the under-construction buildings and termed them unauthorised construction and encroachment on the CDA's right of way along Service Road North, Sector I-9.

The leaseholder later filed a petition in the Islamabad High Court, claiming the land belonged to Pakistan Railways and remained under its administrative control, having been acquired from the CDA after payment, and that the building plans were approved by the Railways as the competent authority. The court, however, observed that it was unable to accept the petitioners' contention that the CDA was wholly without jurisdiction to regulate construction on the land, noting that ownership or administrative control by Pakistan Railways did not exclude the planning and building control laws governing the Islamabad Territory. IHC Judge Mohammad Asif last month directed the CDA to examine the petitioners' representations together with a letter dated July 9, 2026, issued by Pakistan Railways, and pass a speaking order in accordance with law within 30 days of receiving the certified copy of the judgement.

Following the court orders, the CDA board, headed by its chairman Sohail Ashraf, on Thursday heard Pakistan Railways officials and the petitioners' lawyers, while the CDA's lawyer maintained that the land was part of the right of way and construction could not take place there.
