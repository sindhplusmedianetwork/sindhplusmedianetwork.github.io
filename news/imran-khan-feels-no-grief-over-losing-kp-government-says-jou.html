Imran Khan Feels No Grief Over Losing KP Government, Says Journalist Azaz Syed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior journalist Azaz Syed has said that Imran Khan and the PTI leadership feel no grief over losing their government in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Speaking on Geo News' programme 'Report Card', Syed said the provincial government had become a headache and a burden for the party. He argued that the PTI is pleased to have shed that burden and to have cast itself once again as a political victim.

According to Syed, the party now sees advantage in being out of government rather than carrying its weight. He also said that Shah Mahmood Qureshi's return to active politics looks increasingly likely. A path is being cleared, he added, for Qureshi to contest the Senate elections in March.
