NCCIA advises job seekers to verify call centres before applying

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) has advised young men and women to verify a call centre's legal status before applying for a job there, so they do not end up with a police record. The advisory says applicants must confirm registration with SECP, FBR, PSEB and PTA, including the company's NTN and tax registration and PTA IP whitelisting, and ensure the firm is not involved in fraud, identity theft or illegal financial schemes. The agency warned that even a registered company can be involved in unlawful activity, so verification must come before accepting any job.

The guidance is aimed at young job seekers who may join call centres without checking the employer's legal standing. According to the agency, a job taken at an unlawful operation can leave a worker with a police record even if they were unaware of the wrongdoing.
