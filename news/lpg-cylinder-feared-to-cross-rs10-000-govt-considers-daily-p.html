LPG Cylinder Feared to Cross Rs10,000; Govt Considers Daily Price Setting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

LPG prices in Pakistan have surged to Rs 750 per kilogram in the northern areas, raising fears that the price of a domestic cylinder could cross Rs 10,000. The government is considering replacing the monthly pricing system with daily price setting to curb hoarding ahead of winter. Geo News reported that a daily-price mechanism could push the domestic cylinder's price past the Rs 10,000 mark.

Importers have demanded a revision of the pricing formula, suggesting weekly or daily prices instead of the current monthly system. The proposal comes as gas shortages bite harder across the country with winter approaching.
