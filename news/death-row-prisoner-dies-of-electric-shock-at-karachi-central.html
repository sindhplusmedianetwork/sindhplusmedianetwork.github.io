Death-row prisoner dies of electric shock at Karachi Central Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A death-row prisoner named Amjad died after receiving an electric shock in a barrack at Karachi Central Jail. Jail officials said cases of sexual assault were registered against him and his mercy appeal was pending before the President. The body was shifted to the mortuary and will be handed over to his family after legal formalities are completed.

The jail administration said an investigation into the incident is under way. Officials said the prisoner died inside the barrack where the electric shock occurred.
