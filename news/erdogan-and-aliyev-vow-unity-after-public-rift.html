Erdogan and Aliyev Vow Unity After Public Rift

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan and Azerbaijan's President Ilham Aliyev vowed on Sunday that their countries' alliance would remain solid, in a show of unity aimed at overcoming a public rift between the close neighbours. Tensions flared in September, when Baku revised school uniform regulations to prohibit clothing deemed incompatible with secular education, effectively barring Islamic headscarves in classrooms. Azerbaijan's move sparked protests at home and drew criticism from Erdogan's Justice and Development Party, while Baku declared 31 Turkish nationals persona non grata over critical social media posts.

Erdogan subsequently dismissed his presidential adviser Oktay Saral, but Aliyev stayed away from an October 30 summit of Turkic leaders in Ankara, sending Foreign Minister Jeyhun Bayramov instead. On Sunday, as Azerbaijan marked Victory Day commemorating its 2020 war with Armenia, Erdogan arrived in the country's Fuzuli district, which Baku recaptured from Armenian forces six years ago. At a joint press conference, Erdogan said their unity would endure forever and described the alliance with the motto 'One Nation, Two States' as an everlasting torch.

Aliyev said the alliance remained unshaken and that Erdogan's visit showed the whole world their unity.
