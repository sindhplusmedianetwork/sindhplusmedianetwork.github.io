Islamabad Hosts Foreign Dignitaries for Two-Day ECO Ministerial Meeting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign dignitaries arrived in Islamabad on Sunday as Pakistan hosts the two-day 30th meeting of the ECO Council of Ministers. Delegations from Turkiye, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Turkmenistan are attending the meeting. The leadership of the OIC and the SCO is also in town for the gathering.

The meeting is chaired by Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar. Pakistan, which holds the ECO chairmanship for 2026-27, will seek to advance regional connectivity and economic integration. The meeting is being held under the theme 'Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO'.
