Huge Boulder Smashes Into Moving Car in China's Sichuan, Passengers Miraculously Unhurt

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A huge boulder rolled down a mountain slope and smashed into a moving car in China's Sichuan province, but the passengers miraculously survived unhurt. Dashcam footage shows the boulder rolling quickly down the hill and crashing onto the rear of the white Peugeot 308 car. The force of the impact completely destroyed the rear of the car, and the rear windscreen shattered.

Fortunately, all the people in the car survived the terrifying accident without even a scratch. The car owner told the Chinese newspaper Beijing News that two of his relatives were sitting in the back seat at the time of the accident. A woman pedestrian near the car also sensed the danger in time and jumped away moments before the boulder struck, saving her life.

The car was badly damaged, but the survival of its passengers and the nearby woman was nothing short of a miracle. The video of the incident has also become the centre of attention on social media.
