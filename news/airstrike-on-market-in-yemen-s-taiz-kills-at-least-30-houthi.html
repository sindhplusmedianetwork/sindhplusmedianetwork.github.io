Airstrike on Market in Yemen's Taiz Kills at Least 30, Houthis Say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 30 people were killed when airstrikes hit a market in Yemen's Taiz province. Eight more people were injured in the attack in the Al-Waziyah area of southwestern Yemen. The Houthi military spokesman said three airstrikes hit Al-Hadarah market at around 7am.

The Houthi group blamed Saudi-backed government forces for the attack. Government officials, in turn, blamed the Houthis. The attack is being described as one of the deadliest in the area in recent months.
