Maryam Nawaz Attends Information Minister Uzma Bukhari's Daughter's Wedding

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's Information and Culture Minister Uzma Bukhari's daughter Wadiat Fatima has entered into matrimony with Barrister Syed Musa Raza. Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif attended the wedding ceremony. During the event, Uzma Bukhari introduced the groom to the chief minister.

A number of political and government personalities were also present at the ceremony. Photographs from the event showed the chief minister meeting the newlywed couple.
