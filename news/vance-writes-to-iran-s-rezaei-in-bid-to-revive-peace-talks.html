Vance writes to Iran's Rezaei in bid to revive peace talks

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States Vice President JD Vance has written to Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council and a former commander of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, in an effort to revive stalled peace negotiations, The Wall Street Journal reported, citing US officials and people familiar with the matter. One person said the letter was sent at President Donald Trump's direction and that the president is aware of the outreach. The aim, according to the report, is to test whether enough common ground exists to dissuade Trump from ordering a short, intense strike on Iran in the coming weeks.

Contacts so far have focused on a possible framework for negotiations rather than the details of a peace proposal. Rezaei said last week that Tehran would not negotiate until certain core demands were met. The outreach sits awkwardly with Trump's public stance, as he said last week that Washington was not negotiating with Iran and suggested an outcome would come soon, one way or another.

Trump, when later asked whether he had told Vance to start negotiations, answered that he had not, describing the contact as concerning help for Iranian civilians, while Tehran had not publicly confirmed a letter when the first reports ran.
