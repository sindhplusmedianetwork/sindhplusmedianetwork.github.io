OGRA Raises Diesel Price by 85 Paisa, Keeps Petrol Unchanged

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's fuel prices for the next one-day period have been announced. The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority has raised the price of high-speed diesel by 85 paisa per litre, taking it to Rs 400.67. Petrol prices remain unchanged at Rs 377.91 per litre.

The new rates take effect from October 30. The one-day notification means diesel becomes marginally costlier for transport and agriculture, while petrol consumers see no relief. OGRA issues the pricing revision for the short period under its regular mechanism.
