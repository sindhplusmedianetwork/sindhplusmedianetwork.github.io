Board of Peace urges immediate implementation of Trump's Gaza plan after hospital strike

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Board of Peace said on Thursday that US President Donald Trump's Gaza peace plan must be implemented 'immediately without further delay' after a deadly Israeli strike on Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis, while stopping short of criticising Israel. The strike hit the second floor of the Al-Yasin building at Nasser Medical Complex, killing six people and injuring 27, and caused extensive damage to the hospital's intensive care and surgical wards, according to the Gaza Health Ministry and hospital officials. Those killed included medical staff, patients and their companions, Gaza's Civil Defence said, while Israeli Army Radio described the attack as an 'assassination operation' without giving further details.

The board said the 'tragic loss of life' showed why the plan must move forward, adding that it deplores the death of any civilians and denounced Hamas, and urged all parties to honour their commitments under the plan. It said it had established a strategic framework to ensure Gazans 'can live safely and access essential medical care in secure, demilitarised facilities' and called on all parties to give it the authority and operational space to implement it without obstruction. The United Nations expressed deep alarm at the strike, with UN officials warning that the ongoing violence jeopardised the increasingly fragile ceasefire and calling for rapid, safe and unimpeded access for humanitarian assistance.

The attacks came amid continued Israeli violations of the ceasefire that took effect on October 10, 2025, which have killed 1,622 Palestinians and wounded 5,878 since then, while the war since October 2023 has killed more than 74,600 Palestinians and wounded nearly 176,000, and destroyed 90 percent of the territory's infrastructure.
