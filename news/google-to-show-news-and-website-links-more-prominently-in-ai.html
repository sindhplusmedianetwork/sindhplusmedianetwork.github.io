Google to Show News and Website Links More Prominently in AI Search

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Google announced on Thursday that it will start displaying links to websites and articles more prominently and higher up in search results powered by artificial intelligence, as brands and news publishers warn of a sharp decline in their traffic. The Alphabet subsidiary began integrating AI features into search over two years ago, launching AI Overviews in May 2024 with summarised answers and adding AI Mode in March 2025, which opens in a separate window with more comprehensive answers and links. Now, contextual links will be featured more frequently and higher up in AI-generated search content, and links at the end of AI summaries will be made more engaging and prominent for users who want to dig deeper.

Google will also expand worldwide its link carousel, which displays cards directly below AI Overviews showing headlines with images for relevant articles. The algorithm will prioritise articles from sites a user has previously identified as preferred sources, and it will also be able to pull in content from relevant social media posts and other forums. During its second-quarter 2025 results, Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai said AI Overviews had more than two billion monthly users in over 200 countries.

Several studies have shown that replacing traditional search with AI summaries significantly reduces the rate at which users open the suggested links, and consultancy Chartbeat reported that news publishers saw a 40 per cent drop in traffic coming from Google between July 2025 and July 2026. In August, Apig, a group representing daily newspapers in France, alerted the Competition Authority that AI summaries were in breach of agreements reached with news publishers in 2022.
