Climate change is Sindh's top development priority, says Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has urged departments to build infrastructure that can resist threats posed by climate change, saying it will remain a key priority of Sindh's development agenda. Chairing a high-level meeting at the Chief Minister's House in Karachi on Thursday, he said the government had decided to restore mangrove forests and extend natural protection to the coastal areas. He stressed water conservation, the optimum use of water resources and improvement in irrigation practices.

He said climate change was not only an environmental challenge but the fundamental development problem for Sindh, pointing to threats from flooding, drought, water scarcity and coastal erosion. He reviewed the restoration of mangrove forests on 126,000 hectares of land under the Delta Blue Carbon plan and said the Sindh Coastal Resilience Plan would extend protection to more than 500,000 people in Thatta, Sujawal and Badin districts. He also reviewed projects for an additional 2,360 megawatts of wind power generation and solar energy projects for 275,000 low-income and off-grid households.

He directed officials to promote private sector investment in the renewable energy sector and urged departments to complete priority projects as early as possible.
