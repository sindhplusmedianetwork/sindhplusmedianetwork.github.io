Amir Muqam Says He Asked Field Marshal to Share His 'Talisman'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister Engineer Amir Muqam said people in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa had high expectations from the new political and administrative setup in the province. He added that strict accountability must accompany the change. Addressing an event in Swat, Muqam said he often told Field Marshal Asim Munir to share his 'talisman', because no one could stand before him due to his and his team's clean intentions.

He said all state institutions were on the same page. The minister said the federal government would not leave the people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa alone. He said the government was committed to supporting the province and addressing the concerns of its people.
