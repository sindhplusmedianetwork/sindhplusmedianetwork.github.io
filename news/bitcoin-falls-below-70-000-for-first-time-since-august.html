Bitcoin Falls Below $70,000 for First Time Since August

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bitcoin has fallen below $70,000 for the first time since 20 August, according to data from the Binance digital currency exchange cited by Tass. At 5:52 in the morning Moscow time, the cryptocurrency was down 2.18 percent and trading at $69,900. By 6:09 in the morning it had fallen further to $66,910, while Ethereum was down 1.8 percent at $1,983.44.

Data from Coinglass showed that positions worth $901.89 million belonging to 125,760 traders were liquidated across global crypto exchanges in the past 24 hours. The largest share of the liquidations was on the Hyperliquid exchange, at $317.88 million. Around $772 million of the liquidated amounts came from long positions and $129.8 million from short positions, while the largest single liquidation order, worth $31.09 million, was placed on Hyperliquid in the BTCUSDT pair.

According to CoinMarketCap, total crypto market capitalisation stood at $2.36 trillion, with Bitcoin accounting for $1.41 trillion and Ethereum for $243.26 billion.
