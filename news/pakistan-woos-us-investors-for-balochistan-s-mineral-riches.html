Pakistan Woos US Investors for Balochistan's Mineral Riches

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan is planning to encourage the United States to take the lead in investing in the mineral-rich province of Balochistan, despite the security risks in the region. The push will be on display at the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum scheduled for November 12 and 13. Currently, no American company operates in Balochistan.

However, the US Export-Import Bank approved $1.25 billion in December 2025, a step that could unlock up to $2 billion in American mining equipment and services exports. Pakistan's mineral reserves are estimated at around $8 trillion, spread over 600,000 square kilometres. Officials believe foreign investment could transform the country's economy if managed properly.
