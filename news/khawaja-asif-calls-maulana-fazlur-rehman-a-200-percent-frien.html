Khawaja Asif calls Maulana Fazlur Rehman a '200 percent' friend

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has said he enjoys good relations with JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman and considers him '200 percent' a friend, insisting that politics does not damage personal relationships. He made the remarks while speaking outside parliament. Asif said he had differences with the Maulana in the past but had not wanted to react immediately.

He added that if Fazlur Rehman has a clean record, he should face the proceedings that have been initiated against him. The comments came a day after Fazlur Rehman described Khawaja Asif as 'like a brother' whom he had merely 'tickled'. The remarks add a noticeably softer note to their recent public exchanges.
