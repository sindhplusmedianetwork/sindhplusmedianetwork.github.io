Pakistan, UK agree to deepen cooperation in security, trade and migration

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and the United Kingdom agreed to deepen cooperation in security, law enforcement, migration, trade and investment, the Foreign Office said. Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar received visiting UK Minister of State for Security Dan Jarvis in Islamabad. The two sides also discussed further meetings under the R4 + E4 framework.

The meeting came a day after both countries expressed concern over terrorism emanating from Afghanistan at their annual bilateral counter-terrorism dialogue in Islamabad. Officials said the dialogue reflected the shared commitment of both countries to regional security. The visit is part of ongoing engagement between the two countries on security and economic cooperation.
