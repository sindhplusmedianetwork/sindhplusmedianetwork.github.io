Man shoots wife and brother-in-law dead in Landhi before killing himself

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man allegedly shot dead his wife and brother-in-law before taking his own life in Muzaffarabad Colony in Landhi, police said. The incident took place within the jurisdiction of Quaidabad police station, where 40-year-old Nasir Ahmed arrived at his in-laws' house and opened fire. His wife, 35-year-old Asma, and her brother, 31-year-old Muhammad Ahsan, were killed, and the suspect then shot himself and died.

Police said the couple had been married for around 15 to 16 years, had three children, and had been living separately amid domestic disputes, with the woman residing at her parents' home and her husband in Shah Latif Town. The victims' father, Muhammad Arif, alleged that Nasir had previously issued death threats and that complaints to police had brought no action. The woman had reportedly approached a court seeking khula.
