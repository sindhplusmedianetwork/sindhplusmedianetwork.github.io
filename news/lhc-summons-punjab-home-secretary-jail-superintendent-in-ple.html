LHC Summons Punjab Home Secretary, Jail Superintendent in Plea for Imran's Sisters' Release

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court on Thursday summoned the Punjab home secretary and the Kot Lakhpat jail superintendent over the detention of Imran Khan's three sisters. The summons came in a fresh plea filed by Shahrez Khan, the son of Aleema Khan, challenging the detention of Aleema Khan, Noreen Niazi and Uzma Khan. The three women were detained in September under the Punjab Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance, 1960.

The plea contends that the detention orders expired on October 19 and 20 and asks the court to declare them void and order the immediate release of the women. Justice Abher Gul Khan heard the initial arguments and adjourned the hearing to October 28. The development adds a new legal front to the ongoing tussle between the PTI leadership and the Punjab government over the detentions.
