Missing US girl rescued after 17 months after seeking help through a video game

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A teenage girl who had been missing for nearly 17 months in the United States was rescued after she reached out for help while playing the online game Among Us. The girl had gone missing from Garden City in the state of Utah in May 2025, when she was 15 years old. She had a phone on which she could not use mobile data, but she could connect to the internet through Wi-Fi, and while playing Among Us she told another player that she was the long-missing girl.

When the matter reached the other player's family, they informed the relevant authorities, and the FBI received information about her possible location on 1 November. A federal criminal complaint was filed on 2 November against Adam Hunter Harmel, a 39-year-old resident of Oregon, who is accused of first contacting the girl online, taking her from Utah to Oregon, and transporting a minor across state lines with the intent of criminal sexual activity. Court documents allege that the girl was not allowed to leave the house, that the accused kept a knife under his pillow, and US media citing court records have also reported allegations of violence and sexual abuse, but all of these allegations are still at the court proceedings stage and have not yet been proven.

The accused was later taken into custody during a vehicle stop in Ontario, Oregon, and the Department of Justice confirmed the rescue and the case in a statement on 4 November.
