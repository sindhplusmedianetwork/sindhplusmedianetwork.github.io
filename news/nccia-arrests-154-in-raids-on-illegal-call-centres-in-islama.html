NCCIA arrests 154 in raids on illegal call centres in Islamabad and Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency arrested 154 suspects, including 53 women, after busting two illegal call centres in Islamabad and Rawalpindi. The arrests were made during a three-day operation against international cyber fraud and online blackmailing networks. The suspects allegedly lured citizens of South Asian countries through fraudulent loan apps, stole personal data and photos from their phones, and used manipulated images and videos to harass and blackmail victims.

NCCIA Additional Director Shoaib Khan said cases have been registered against the suspects and investigations are underway. He added that a crackdown on online betting and gaming fraud will be launched soon. The operation is part of an ongoing effort against organised cybercrime networks operating from the twin cities.
