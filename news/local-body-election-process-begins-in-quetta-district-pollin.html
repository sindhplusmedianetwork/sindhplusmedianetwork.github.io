Local body election process begins in Quetta district; polling on December 28

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The local body election process has begun in Quetta district, provincial election commissioner Ali Asghar Sial said. Candidates can collect nomination papers from November 13 to 17, with scrutiny and appeals to follow. Final symbols will be allotted on December 6, polling will be held on December 28, and final results will be announced on December 31.

Elections will be held in 172 union councils and 641 wards in Quetta. The Election Commission of Pakistan had announced the schedule on November 3. Local elections in Balochistan's other 35 districts were completed in phases before the Quetta schedule was announced.
