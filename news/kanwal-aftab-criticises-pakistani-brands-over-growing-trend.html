Kanwal Aftab Criticises Pakistani Brands Over Growing Trend of Bold Clothing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Popular Pakistani social media influencer Kanwal Aftab has criticised local clothing brands over the growing trend of bold clothing. In a video posted on social media, she said brands are increasingly making clothes with inappropriate cuts and short lengths. She said brands should also consider customers who prefer full and modest clothing.

Kanwal said that when she asks a brand to lengthen the sleeves or fully cover the back, she is told the design cannot be changed. Many social media users supported her stance after the video was shared. Her remarks have started a debate about clothing designs offered by Pakistani brands.
