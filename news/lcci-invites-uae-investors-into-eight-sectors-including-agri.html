LCCI invites UAE investors into eight sectors including agriculture, textiles and IT

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry has invited investors from the United Arab Emirates into eight sectors of Pakistan's economy. The sectors are agriculture, food processing, textiles, information technology, construction, logistics, minerals and value-added manufacturing. LCCI President Ali Hussam Asghar extended the invitation during a meeting with UAE Ambassador Salem Mohammed Al Zaabi in Lahore.

He proposed regular exchanges of trade delegations and the holding of joint business forums. He said joint ventures between Pakistani and Emirati businesses could drive industrial growth and create employment.
