Sindh Sees First-Ever November Cotton Sowing in Coastal Districts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cotton sowing has begun in the second week of November in Sindh's coastal districts for the first time in the country's history, thanks to favourable temperatures. The early sowing has started in areas of Thatta and Sujawal, including Chuhar Jamali, Mirpur Sakro, Gharo, Baharo, Keti Bandar and Chhachh Jan, and reports suggest a record crop if the current temperatures continue. The Punjab crop reporting service has claimed a production of 3.7 million bales in Punjab this year.

Two of the country's leading cotton research bodies, the Central Cotton Research Institute and the Pakistan Agricultural Research Council, have decided to jointly research new cotton seeds and increase technical cooperation. Ehsan-ul-Haq, chairman of the Cotton Ginners Forum, said unusual changes in temperature and its duration in recent years have harmed per-acre yields and the quality of crops and fruit, affecting farmers and the local farm economy. He noted that cotton in Sindh's coastal districts was traditionally sown in February and March, while in other cotton zones sowing ran from May 1 to the first week of June, but changing temperatures have shifted the pattern.
