Electronic comb regrows nearly all hair in mice, study finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese researchers have developed an advanced electronic comb for treating hair loss and baldness that regrew nearly 100 percent of hair in mice within one month. The research was published in the scientific journal Nature Communications. The device, named De-Tangle, uses special electrodes that create an electric field around hair roots.

The field helps deliver medicines to the roots and stimulates regrowth through electrical stimulation. The results were better than the commonly used hair-loss drug minoxidil, which restored about 70 percent of hair. Researchers say the technology could one day be used to treat baldness in humans, though so far it has only been tested on mice.
