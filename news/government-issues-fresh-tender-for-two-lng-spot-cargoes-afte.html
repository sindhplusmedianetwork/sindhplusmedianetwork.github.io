Government issues fresh tender for two LNG spot cargoes after expensive bids rejected

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has issued a new tender for the purchase of two LNG spot cargoes. It has invited bids from international companies by November 18, with all bids to be opened the same day. One cargo is sought for delivery between December 14 and 16, 2026.

The second is sought for delivery between January 9 and 11, 2027. The move follows the rejection of expensive bids received under the earlier tender for the same delivery windows. Those bids ranged from 27.27 to 31 dollars per MMBtu.
