Consumer courts can't hear insurance policyholder plaints, LHC holds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has ruled that consumer courts lack jurisdiction to hear complaints filed by policyholders against insurance companies. Justice Jawad Hassan issued the ruling on a petition filed by the United Insurance Company of Pakistan against an order of the District Consumer Court in Bhakkar. The consumer court had overruled the insurance firm's objection and held that the policyholder's complaint was maintainable because the dispute stemmed from insurance services.

Setting aside that order, Justice Hassan observed that the lower forum had failed to properly examine Section 122 of the Insurance Ordinance, 2000. He noted that Section 122(3) explicitly bars any court other than a specialised tribunal from hearing claims brought by a policyholder against an insurance company. Referring to constitutional principles and settled precedents, including the Adamjee Insurance Company case, the judge said federal legislation took precedence over provincial enactments under Article 143 of the Constitution.

He remanded the matter to the consumer court with directions to re-examine the question of maintainability strictly in accordance with the law and issue a fresh, reasoned decision within a month.
