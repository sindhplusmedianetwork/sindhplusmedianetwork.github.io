Revenue Board Told to Explain Massive Tax Waivers in Former Tribal Areas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Senate subcommittee has directed the Federal Board of Revenue to present complete records of consumption certificates, payments and verification documents at an in-camera meeting on November 16. The committee is investigating the alleged misuse of the tax exemption scheme for the former tribal areas, involving imports worth Rs1,120 billion, and the recovery of evaded taxes. The meeting was chaired by Saifullah Abro, who expressed displeasure over the absence of the head of the cybercrime investigation agency, who had been expected to brief the committee.

The committee was told that total imports between 2018 and June 2026 amounted to Rs1,120.66 billion, against which consumption certificates covered imports worth Rs389.86 billion, leaving an apparent unreconciled difference of Rs730.79 billion. Sector-wise gaps were reported in steel at Rs148.248 billion, textile and fabric at Rs146.95 billion, food at Rs100.88 billion and ghee and oil at Rs76.48 billion. The investigation agency informed the committee that 512 notices had been issued to various companies, of which 19 joined the proceedings with partial records, while more than 100 notices were returned or remained undelivered.

The chairman also sought a full explanation of 20 certificates involving approximately Rs64 billion and questioned the release of payments worth around Rs74 billion without adequate verification.
