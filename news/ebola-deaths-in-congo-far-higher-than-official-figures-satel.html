Ebola deaths in Congo far higher than official figures, satellite images reveal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The real death toll from Ebola in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo is far higher than official figures, with satellite images showing rapidly expanding cemeteries and hundreds of new graves, according to a Reuters investigation. In the eastern city of Bunia, excess deaths by the start of November were more than 50% above official counts, and cemetery workers said up to 15 people a day were being buried at the peak. Since the outbreak was formally declared in mid-May, official figures put cases above 11,600 with more than 5,600 deaths.

But many victims are buried without tests or registration, so the true toll is believed to be much higher. The findings point to a far more severe outbreak than the official numbers suggest. Health workers and burial teams in the affected areas continue to face a heavy burden as the outbreak goes on.
