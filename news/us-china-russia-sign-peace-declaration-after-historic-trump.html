US, China, Russia sign 'peace declaration' after historic Trump, Xi, Putin meeting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China, the United States and Russia issued a Declaration toward Peace after their leaders met in Shenzhen. Presidents Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin took part in the historic meeting. The declaration says nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.

The statement calls for reforming the United Nations. It also says the International Criminal Court must not abuse its jurisdiction. The three countries further pledged increased production and supply of refined petroleum products.
