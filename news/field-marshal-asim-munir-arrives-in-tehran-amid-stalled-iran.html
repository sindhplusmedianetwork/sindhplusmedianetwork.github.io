Field Marshal Asim Munir Arrives in Tehran Amid Stalled Iran-US Diplomacy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's army chief, Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, arrived in Tehran on Tuesday alongside a delegation of officials from his country, Iranian state television reported. Earlier, the spokesman for Iran's foreign ministry, Esmaeil Baqaei, confirmed the visit, saying talks would 'relate to issues concerning the negotiations, but they are mainly focused on bilateral relations and strengthening interactions and cooperation'. The visit comes as diplomatic efforts between Iran and the United States remain stalled over issues including the future of the Strait of Hormuz, which Iran has sought to blockade during the war launched by the US and Israel in February.

Pakistan has played a key mediating role in the so far unsuccessful attempts to end the war, and in June it helped broker a short-lived memorandum of understanding, which Tehran insists should still form the basis of any peace deal. The trip is the fifth by Munir since the June agreement, and it follows a visit by Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov on Monday. According to reports, the visit will cover bilateral defence cooperation, border management and Pakistan's mediation efforts to de-escalate Iran-US tensions.
