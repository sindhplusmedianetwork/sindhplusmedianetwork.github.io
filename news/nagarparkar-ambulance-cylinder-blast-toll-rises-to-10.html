Nagarparkar ambulance cylinder blast toll rises to 10

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The death toll from the cylinder explosion inside an ambulance in Nagarparkar, Tharparkar has risen to 10, after a child who was under treatment at the Trauma Centre in Karachi succumbed to his injuries. The blast tore through the ambulance earlier, killing a pregnant woman, health centre staff and attendants of patients among the victims. According to the head of PPHI Sindh, the cylinder that exploded was an oxygen cylinder, while the LPG cylinder installed in the ambulance was not in use at the time.

Rescue and health officials said several of the injured remain under treatment. The tragedy has raised fresh questions about safety standards in ambulances operating across the province.
