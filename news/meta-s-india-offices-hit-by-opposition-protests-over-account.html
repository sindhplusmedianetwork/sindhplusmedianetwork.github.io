Meta's India Offices Hit by Opposition Protests Over Account Removals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The youth wing of India's main opposition Congress party clashed with police outside Meta offices, protesting the company's removal of some Instagram accounts of its members, according to footage released by the group on Saturday. Meta declined to comment on the protests in the company's biggest market by users, where debate is growing about potential risks to free speech from the Indian government's efforts to remove social media content it objects to. The government of Prime Minister Narendra Modi says it orders content taken down only if it breaches local laws or can harm public safety or national security, while Congress has long accused Meta and other platforms of curbing dissent online by removing party accounts.

In Hyderabad on Friday, protesters stormed into the building housing Meta's offices, smashing plants and clashing with police, while in Mumbai Congress workers spray-painted anti-Meta slogans on walls outside the building housing its office. In Gurugram near New Delhi, Congress workers chanted slogans and held banners reading restore youth Congress accounts. The Indian Youth Congress warned it would storm Meta offices across the country unless Instagram accounts of its leaders are restored, and two such accounts were restored after Friday's protests.

Meta disclosures show it restricted more than 5.4 million posts or accounts in India under local laws in the first half of 2026, up from about 70,000 in the preceding six months.
