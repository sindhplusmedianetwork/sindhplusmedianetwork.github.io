US Has Proposed Next Round of Trilateral Talks: Zelensky

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said the United States has proposed holding the next round of trilateral peace talks before Christmas. Zelensky suggested the talks could be held on the occasion of the G20 summit. He suggested President Donald Trump's golf resort in Miami as the venue.

The first round of trilateral peace talks between the US, Russia and Ukraine was held on November 10 and 11 in Abu Dhabi.
