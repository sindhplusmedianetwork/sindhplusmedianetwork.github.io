IHC Orders All Islamabad Sheesha Cafes Shut Until New Rules

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has ordered the complete closure of all sheesha cafes in the federal capital until the government frames new rules to regulate them. Chief Justice Sardar Muhammad Sarfraz Dogar issued the direction while hearing a petition seeking action against illegally operated shisha centres in Islamabad, and also ordered the withdrawal of no-objection certificates previously issued to such establishments. The court observed that no sheesha cafe should operate until relevant rules and regulations are framed, remarking that the absence of rules cannot justify illegal activity.

The chief justice said an earlier court order, which had only allowed establishments operating in accordance with the law, had been misinterpreted as blanket permission for the cafes. Following the order, the Islamabad district administration sealed four cafes in the E-11 area, and the deputy commissioner directed assistant commissioners across the capital to take immediate action against all such establishments, indoor and outdoor alike. The court's detailed written order is expected later, while the government informed the court that draft rules have been prepared and sent for approval.
