Overseas Pakistanis are valuable asset of homeland, says High Commissioner

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's new High Commissioner to Britain, Tipu Usman, has said overseas Pakistanis are a valuable asset and ambassadors of their homeland. He addressed a ceremony in Manchester hosted in his honour by Consul General Imtiaz Feroz Gondal. Usman said their hard work in different fields is bringing Pakistan's identity to the fore.

He also mentioned his recent meeting with King Charles III. He said the meeting was held in a very pleasant atmosphere. The King expressed good wishes for Pakistan's progress and prosperity and for better relations with Britain.
