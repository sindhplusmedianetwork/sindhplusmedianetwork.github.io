Gold Price Falls by Rs15,400 Per Tola in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices fell sharply in Pakistan amid expectations of a rise in the US interest rate. According to the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association, the price of gold per tola dropped by Rs15,400 to Rs379,836. The price of 10 grams of gold fell by Rs13,203 to Rs325,648.

In the international market, gold fell by $154 to $3,573 per ounce. The previous day, the price of gold per tola in Pakistan had fallen by Rs3,700 and the price of 10 grams of gold had fallen by Rs3,172. The decline came as estimates of an increase in the US interest rate weighed on gold prices in the international market.
