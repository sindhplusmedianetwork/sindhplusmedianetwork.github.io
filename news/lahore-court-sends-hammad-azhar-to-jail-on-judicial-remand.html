Lahore Court Sends Hammad Azhar to Jail on Judicial Remand

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Lahore anti-terrorism court on Tuesday rejected a police request for further physical remand of PTI leader Hammad Azhar in the Jinnah House attack case. The court instead sent Azhar to jail on judicial remand. Police had sought further remand to conduct a polygraph test on the PTI leader.

Azhar's counsel argued that a polygraph test requires the suspect's consent, and Azhar said he did not want to undergo the test if the law allowed him to refuse. His lawyer said the 50-day physical remand had finally ended with the court's order. Azhar was subsequently sent to Kot Lakhpat jail on judicial remand.

The case relates to the attack on Jinnah House in Lahore.
