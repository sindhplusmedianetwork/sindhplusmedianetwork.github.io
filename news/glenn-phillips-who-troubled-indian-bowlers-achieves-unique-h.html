Glenn Phillips, who troubled Indian bowlers, achieves unique honour

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New Zealand's aggressive batter Glenn Phillips made history by scoring centuries against India in Test, ODI and T20 International cricket in a calendar year. Glenn Phillips has become the first player in the world to achieve this feat against India. Phillips claimed this honour by scoring a brilliant century in the first innings of the ongoing first Test match at Wellington's Basin Reserve.

Earlier, he had scored 106 runs in the ODI match played in Indore on 18 January 2026, while during the T20 match in Auckland on 30 October he played a blistering innings of 107 runs off just 49 balls. In this way, Phillips became the second player to score centuries in all three formats against a single opponent in one year, after Indian captain Rohit Sharma achieved the feat against Sri Lanka in 2017. Phillips also became the first New Zealand batter to score centuries in all three formats against a single team.
