Warriors Continue Unbeaten Streak With Third Straight Win

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Muneeba Ali's Warriors registered their third consecutive win in the PCB Women's Super-3 T20 Tournament on Monday. They defeated Tuba Hassan's Champions by 24 runs at Iqbal Stadium in Faisalabad. Hafsa Khalid's 47 had earlier lifted the Warriors to 154 for 8.

Chasing 155, the Champions were bowled out for 130. Left-arm spinner Ayesha Bilal starred with the ball, taking 4 wickets for 15 runs. The unbeaten run has put the Warriors in a commanding position in the tournament.
