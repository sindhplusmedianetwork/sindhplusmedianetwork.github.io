Pakistan Medical Association Seeks Forensic Audit of Disease Control Programmes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Medical Association has demanded a transparent, sweeping and comprehensive forensic audit of all government departments, vertical programmes and non-governmental organisations operating in the fields of polio, tuberculosis, HIV/AIDS and hepatitis eradication. In a statement, the association regretted that Pakistan continued to lag disastrously behind global health milestones despite billions of rupees funnelled through international grants, donor funding and national exchequers over the decades. The association welcomed recent administrative steps, such as the Economic Affairs Division ordering a special audit of Global Fund-backed trusts managing HIV programmes.

Dr Abdul Ghafoor Shoro of the PMA said that while a huge amount of money is disbursed annually under the banner of disease eradication, public health infrastructure remains dilapidated, healthcare providers are overworked, and disease burdens continue to multiply. He blamed chronic institutional inertia, lack of transparency, lack of grassroots execution and administrative malpractices for rendering these vertical programmes ineffective. On behalf of the association, he demanded the urgent implementation of independent forensic and financial audits, overseen by credible, non-partisan financial watchdogs, of all federal and provincial disease control programmes.
