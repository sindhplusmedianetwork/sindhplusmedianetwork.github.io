Israeli settlers storm village after army dismantles outposts

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli settlers stormed a Palestinian village and injured five people on Thursday, a rights group said, after the military dismantled illegal outposts in the occupied West Bank. The Israeli military, which has come under international criticism over settler violence, said it intervened and took down two outposts built by settlers without permission. According to the Israeli rights group Yesh Din, dozens of settlers armed with clubs, tear gas and stones stormed the village of Atara after military forces left the area, and five residents were injured and hospitalised, one in serious condition with a brain haemorrhage.

The Palestinian Red Crescent said one of the injured people was 67 years old. A resident of Atara, Abdelhadi Atari, said settlers broke into and vandalised Palestinian houses soon after the army arrived, ripping doors off houses and smashing windows, and that children were terrorised. In a separate incident on Thursday, settlers committed acts of vandalism in the Palestinian Christian village of Taybeh, east of Ramallah, setting fire to a vehicle and spray-painting slogans in Hebrew on the walls of a house, according to a watchdog organisation.

More than 500,000 Israelis live in settlements across the West Bank, all of which are deemed illegal under international law, and more than 1,100 Palestinians have been killed in the West Bank since the October 7, 2023 attack, mostly by Israeli soldiers but also by settlers, according to a tally based on data from the Palestinian health ministry.
