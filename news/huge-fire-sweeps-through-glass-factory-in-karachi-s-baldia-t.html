Huge Fire Sweeps Through Glass Factory in Karachi's Baldia Town

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A massive fire broke out at a glass factory in Rasheedabad, Baldia Town, destroying goods worth millions of rupees and a fuel tanker parked inside the premises. Firefighters battled the blaze for more than three hours before bringing it largely under control, and fortunately no casualties or injuries were reported. The fire erupted at a glass marble manufacturing factory located at Plot No.

F-168 in the jurisdiction of Site B Police Station, where the upper floors contained electrical panels, plastic materials, clothing, shoes and other goods, and the presence of highly combustible materials caused the fire to spread rapidly, sending thick black smoke into the sky that could be seen from several kilometres away. Police, rescue workers and fire brigade teams rushed to the scene, and more than 10 fire tenders, two water tankers and two snorkels were deployed to extinguish the blaze, with Rescue 1122 and Karachi Metropolitan Corporation fire brigade personnel working continuously for over three hours. Keamari SSP Singhar Malik and Deputy Commissioner Abdul Sattar Hakro also reached the site to supervise rescue operations, and nearby factories were evacuated as a precaution.

KMC Chief Fire Officer Humayun Khan said the fire broke out while oil was being transferred from a tanker into an underground storage tank, and the presence of different combustible materials made firefighting difficult, requiring both foam and water. Meanwhile, Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah took notice of the incident and directed the fire brigade and rescue agencies to deploy all available resources, ensure the safety of workers and expedite firefighting operations, and ordered a comprehensive investigation into the cause of the fire and the factory's safety arrangements.
