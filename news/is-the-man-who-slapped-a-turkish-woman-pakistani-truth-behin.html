Is the Man Who Slapped a Turkish Woman Pakistani? Truth Behind the Viral Claim

A video circulating on social media claims that a Pakistani man suddenly slapped a Turkish tourist woman standing on a street in London, after which he was arrested. The viral post, shared by the Instagram account 'India First Post', presents the claim as fact. However, Express News found that no authentic or verified report has confirmed the identity or nationality of the accused.

There is also no confirmation from any credible source that the incident actually took place in London. The claim appears to be another example of an unverified viral video being used to spread a misleading narrative online.
