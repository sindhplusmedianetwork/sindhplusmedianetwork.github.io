PM unveils revised LPG allocation plan through transparent auctions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said on Wednesday that the government was making all-out efforts to ensure the availability of energy supplies, including liquefied natural gas (LNG), liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) and oil, during the upcoming winter season amid challenges posed by rising international prices and maritime routes. Chairing a federal cabinet meeting, he said the government was holding meetings every two to three days to review energy requirements and prepare contingency and coordination plans. The prime minister announced that the decades-old monopoly in the LPG sector had come to an end, with the government introducing a competitive and transparent mechanism for its allocation.

Under the new system, locally produced LPG would be offered through competitive auctions under proper supervision, while importing companies would also be required to follow prescribed procedures. Referring to rising international crude oil prices, he said Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik, OGRA and the relevant committee had taken a timely decision to approach refineries for sharing their windfall profits to avoid passing on a proposed Rs15 per litre increase in petrol prices to consumers. He appreciated the decision and said the government's subsidy programme, which had benefited around 15 million people, would continue at least until the end of December.

He also noted that intelligence agencies had identified irregularities at a few petrol pumps and directed the petroleum minister to ensure their closure and impose heavy financial penalties.
