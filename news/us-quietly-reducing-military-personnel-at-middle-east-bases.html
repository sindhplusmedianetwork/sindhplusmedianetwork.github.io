US Quietly Reducing Military Personnel at Middle East Bases, CBS Reports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has been quietly drawing down its military personnel at bases across the Middle East, CBS News reported on Wednesday. The report, citing a US official and a senior military official, said the reductions are a response to ongoing threats from the Houthis and direct threats from Iran. CENTCOM spokesperson Captain Tim Hawkins told CBS that the United States routinely repositions personnel and assets to optimise mission readiness.

He said the military does not discuss specific movements for operational security reasons. Hawkins added that American forces remain vigilant and fully ready to protect US personnel and interests in the region. The reported drawdown comes amid a sharp escalation in the region, with Houthi attacks continuing and warnings from Tehran of further retaliation.
