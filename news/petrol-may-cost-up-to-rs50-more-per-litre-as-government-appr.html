Petrol May Cost Up to Rs50 More Per Litre as Government Approves New Pricing Formula

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Petrol may become up to Rs50 per litre costlier as import premiums on November cargoes could climb to $35 per barrel amid Iran-US tensions, compared with $19.88 at present. To handle the spike, the government has approved a new 30-day pricing formula. Under the plan, refineries will deposit the difference between the import price and the ex-refinery price into a levy account.

The petroleum levy will then be reduced by an equal per-litre amount, so consumers are shielded from the full impact of the higher premiums. Officials said the mechanism is meant to absorb the volatility in import costs without passing the entire burden to the public. The formula will remain in place for 30 days.
