France: people join anti-government protests dressed as superheroes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An unusual scene was witnessed during anti-government protests in France, with videos and photographs going viral on social media. In the viral videos, two people dressed as Black Panther and Captain America can be seen taking part in a protest in France. The two not only joined the protest but also bravely faced a water cannon and chanted slogans alongside other demonstrators.

Other protesters kept filming and photographing the two during the protest. People dressed as Spider-Man, Superman and Deadpool have also been seen during protests in different cities of France. Earlier, videos of people dressed as Napoleon Bonaparte and Batman had also gone viral on social media.

Many protesters were seen wearing helmets, masks and protective glasses to guard against possible baton charges and tear gas by police.
