Punjab replaces newly appointed Rawalpindi CPO within hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government on Tuesday reversed its appointment of a new Rawalpindi city police officer within hours of issuing the initial transfer orders, naming Sardar Ghiyas Gul to the post instead of Muhammad Sohail Ashraf in a swift reshuffle of senior police officers. Under the revised postings, Deputy Inspector General of Police Hassan Mushtaq Sukhera, who had been serving as Rawalpindi CPO since July, was transferred as DIG operations at the Counter-Terrorism Department in Lahore. Ashraf, initially appointed as his successor on Monday night, was instead posted as Gujranwala CPO, while Gul was directed to take charge in Rawalpindi.

Sukhera, a grade-20 police officer, had served in Rawalpindi for more than four months and had reportedly expressed a desire to return to Lahore for personal reasons, conveying his preference to the Punjab inspector general of police and other senior officials. Following approval of his transfer, the provincial government issued a notification appointing grade-19 officer Ashraf as the new Rawalpindi CPO, but within hours the government withdrew Ashraf's posting and issued fresh orders appointing Gul. Gul has previously served in Rawalpindi as Saddar superintendent of police and chief traffic officer, besides holding the post of Attock district police officer.

During Sukhera's tenure, Rawalpindi witnessed several high-profile murder cases, including the mysterious killing of Hina Javed at her home and the recent murder of private television anchor Rabia Ahsan, and both cases remain under investigation.
