Tesla to end Cybertruck production in Texas to make room for robotaxi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tesla will stop building the Cybertruck at its Texas factory around the middle of 2027 to free up space for its Cybercab robotaxi, Elon Musk said on Friday. Musk did not say whether the truck would be built elsewhere or discontinued altogether, nor what would happen to outstanding orders. The angular pickup was unveiled in 2019, with deliveries starting in late 2023.

It sold far below its planned capacity of 250,000 trucks a year. The decision brings the future of the futuristic pickup into question.
