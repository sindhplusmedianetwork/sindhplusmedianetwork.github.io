LPG Marketers Call Off Planned Strike After Talks With Governor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan LPG Marketers Association has postponed its planned strike after a meeting with the Sindh governor on Monday. The association had announced a strike for November 16 over the lack of consultation on the LPG policy. The meeting discussed LPG supply and related policy matters in the context of the evolving Middle East situation and prevailing energy challenges.

The governor emphasised that ensuring an uninterrupted supply of LPG was essential, and assured the delegation that its concerns would be conveyed to the relevant ministry and that necessary coordination would be facilitated. The association's senior vice president stressed the importance of consulting all stakeholders while formulating the LPG policy, saying their input could help make the policy more effective and practical. Consumers have been paying Rs500 to Rs550 per kg for LPG for the last few months, and the gas is extensively used by the transport sector, households and outdoor dining outlets.

The country imported LPG worth $285 million in the first quarter of the current fiscal year, up 22 per cent from $235 million in the same period last year. The association said the contract price of imported LPG was likely to drop in December if the war in the Middle East either ended or its intensity subsided, but warned that demand would surge in winter and the government needed a solution to avert a deep gas crisis.
