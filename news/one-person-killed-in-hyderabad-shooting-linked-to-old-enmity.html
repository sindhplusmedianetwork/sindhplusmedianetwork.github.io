One person killed in Hyderabad shooting linked to old enmity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man was killed in a shooting in Hyderabad’s GOR Colony near Moti Masjid on Wednesday, and police have attributed the killing to an old enmity. The victim was identified as Allah Bachayo Malani, whose body was shifted to Civil Hospital for medico-legal examination. According to police, the suspected attacker, Asif, opened fire and managed to flee the scene after the incident.

Police reached the spot shortly after and began collecting evidence as part of the investigation. Reports also surfaced that the suspect’s wife and daughter had been taken into custody, but police officials declined to confirm them. A case is expected to be registered as further investigation continues.
