Sikh Pilgrims Arrive in Nankana Sahib for Baba Guru Nanak's 557th Birth Anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sikh pilgrims from around the world continued arriving in Nankana Sahib for the 557th birth anniversary celebrations of Baba Guru Nanak Dev Ji. Two special trains carrying pilgrims from Sindh reached the city. The district administration welcomed the pilgrims with flower petals and garlands and arranged shuttle service to the gurdwaras.

The celebrations formally begin on 22 November and run until 24 November. Around 25,000 pilgrims are expected to take part, including 3,091 from India.
