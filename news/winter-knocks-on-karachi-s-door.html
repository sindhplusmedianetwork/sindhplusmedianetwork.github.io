Winter knocks on Karachi's door

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi is set to get its first taste of winter from November 18, with temperatures expected to fall by 1 to 3 degrees Celsius. The change will come as cooler nights arrive under a western weather system affecting Balochistan. The Pakistan Meteorological Department's Early Warning Centre expects dry days and cooler nights across the city.

The first winter chill is expected to last around four days. No winter rainfall is expected in the city for now, the Met Office said. Residents can expect noticeably cooler nights after weeks of mild weather.

The department will continue to monitor the system as it moves across the region.
