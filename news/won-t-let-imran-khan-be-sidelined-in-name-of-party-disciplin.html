Won't Let Imran Khan Be Sidelined in Name of Party Discipline: Mashal Yousafzai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Senator Mashal Yousafzai has declared that the party will not allow Imran Khan to be sidelined in the name of party discipline. Speaking on the party's internal situation, she said the real test for the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa parliamentary party is that the political committee has offered no action plan. She alleged that the demand for a fact-finding committee on the long march is being suppressed.

She further claimed that paid social media accounts are running planted narratives against the party's position. Her remarks highlight the deepening rifts within the PTI over strategy and leadership.
