Public's complaint on inflation is right, but it's Trump's fault, not ours: Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's adviser on political affairs Rana Sanaullah has acknowledged that the public's grievance over inflation is justified, but insisted the fault lies with Donald Trump rather than the government. Addressing a ceremony in Faisalabad, he said Iran had answered Trump in the war, while Pakistan had appealed to both sides for a ceasefire, adding that the country's role in stopping the Iran war had enhanced Pakistan's standing in the world. He maintained that Pakistan played a major part in halting the Iran-US war, including efforts by Field Marshal Asim Munir.

Sanaullah argued that once the Iran war ends, the national economy will improve and Pakistan will move toward prosperity. He also linked the fuel crisis to the conflict, saying the closure of the Strait of Hormuz had cut off gas supplies from Qatar, though gas had reached Pakistan under its agreement with Doha. An end to the war, he claimed, would also bring down petrol prices.
