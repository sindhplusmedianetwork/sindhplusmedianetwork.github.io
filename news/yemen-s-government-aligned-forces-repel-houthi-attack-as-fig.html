Yemen's Government-Aligned Forces Repel Houthi Attack as Fighting Displaces Over 340,000 People

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's government-aligned Giants Brigades said they repelled a Houthi attack in Al-Mudharaba district of southwestern Lahj province. State media reported airstrikes on Houthi reinforcements in neighbouring Taiz. The International Organisation for Migration said the fighting since August has displaced more than 340,000 people.

The escalation has ended a period of relative calm in Yemen that began in April 2022. The fighting in Lahj and Taiz marks a sharp rise in hostilities after that long period of calm. The displacement figures show the human cost of the renewed fighting since August.
