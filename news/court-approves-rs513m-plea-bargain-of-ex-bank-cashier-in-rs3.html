Court approves Rs513m plea bargain of ex-bank cashier in Rs37bn Kohistan graft case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An accountability court in Peshawar on Thursday approved a plea bargain of Rs513.138 million by a former bank cashier arrested by the National Accountability Bureau in the case involving the alleged embezzlement of over Rs37 billion in the Upper Kohistan district accounts office. Judge Mohammad Zafar ruled that the suspect, Musharraf Shah, a former cashier at the National Bank of Pakistan's Dassu branch, was discharged from his liability as he had voluntarily surrendered the crime proceeds, and ordered his release if he is not required in any other case. The judge ruled that the suspect had admitted his guilt through the plea bargain and availed the benefit of Section 25(b) of the National Accountability Ordinance, and was therefore deemed convicted and disqualified from holding public office under Section 15.

The plea bargain application had been accepted by the NAB chairman on November 4, 2026, and the matter was referred to the accountability court for final approval. Prosecutors told the court that Shah served as cashier at the NBP Dassu branch from October 7, 2021, to March 5, 2025, and misused his authority in connivance with others to misappropriate government funds through the illegal clearance of treasury cheques. In his statement, the suspect conceded that he had purchased two houses in his wife Nabila Shah's name in Abbottabad and Shangla, worth Rs84.223 million and Rs21.435 million, transferred Rs200 million to his friend Mohammad Imlaq, and deposited Rs207.480 million in the account of Speen Ghar Construction, a company in his brother Arshad Khan's name.

NAB has arrested around 40 people in the case, which began with a source report on April 8, 2025, alleging the illegal withdrawal of over Rs37 billion from the contractor's security deposit head of Account G-10113.
