Rs2.1 Million Recovered From Prime Suspect in Laiba Ijaz Case, Says FIA

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has recovered Rs2.1 million from Nauman, the prime suspect in the Laiba Ijaz suicide case, officials said. Laiba, a young student, died by suicide after being defrauded over a promised admission and scholarship at Harvard University in the United States. Investigators say Nauman, along with Fatima Sarwar and a woman known as Aiza, lured her with fake admission documents, offer letters and fee receipts.

According to the FIR, the suspects took Rs9.3 million from the victim in exchange for a US visa and scholarship. FIA sources said the agency is now collecting Nauman's records from relevant departments, adding that the Islamabad Excise Department has no record of him. Police records show he is already a proclaimed offender in a separate fraud case in Mansehra.

Authorities plan to hand him over to Mansehra police once the recovery process is complete.
