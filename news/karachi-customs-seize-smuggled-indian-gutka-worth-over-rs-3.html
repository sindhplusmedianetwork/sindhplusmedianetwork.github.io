Karachi customs seize smuggled Indian gutka worth over Rs 3.4 crore in Kathore raid

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Customs Enforcement Collectorate Karachi seized a large quantity of smuggled Indian gutka from a suspicious container at Kathore during an anti-smuggling operation. Customs officials said a container passing through Kathore was stopped and searched on a tip-off, and 12,480 boxes of Safeena-brand Indian gutka were recovered from it. Collector Enforcement Karachi Fareed Salarzai said the seized gutka is worth Rs 3 crore 47 lakh 50 thousand.

Officials confiscated the Indian gutka and the container and began legal proceedings. The operation was carried out as part of the customs department's ongoing campaign against smuggling.
