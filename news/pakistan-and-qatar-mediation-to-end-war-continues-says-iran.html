Pakistan and Qatar Mediation to End War Continues, Says Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran said Pakistan and Qatar's mediation efforts to end the war are continuing. Tehran said the United States is not showing seriousness in negotiations. Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmail Baghaei said Pakistan and Qatar, concerned about the regional situation, are continuing diplomatic efforts to end the war.

He said both countries are maintaining contacts between the parties. He accused the US of failing to recognise the other party's rights. He said diplomacy can only be effective when both sides' positions are heard fairly.
