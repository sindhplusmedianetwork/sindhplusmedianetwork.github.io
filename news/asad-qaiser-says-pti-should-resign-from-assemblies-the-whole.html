Asad Qaiser says PTI should resign from assemblies: 'the whole system has collapsed'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf leader and former National Assembly speaker Asad Qaiser has openly said he believes his party should quit the assemblies. Speaking on Hamid Mir's program on Tuesday, Qaiser said his personal view is that the PTI should walk out of the assemblies and resign en masse. He added, however, that the final decision rests with the party's collective consultation.

Qaiser said he was deeply disappointed with parliament, noting that speeches are not aired on the media and courts do not deliver justice. 'The entire system has collapsed,' he declared. He also claimed that the PTI still has the street power to topple the federal government through a long march.

But, he said, the party is holding back because it wants to avoid bloodshed.
