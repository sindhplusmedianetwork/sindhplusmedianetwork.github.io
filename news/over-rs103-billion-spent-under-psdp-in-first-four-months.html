Over Rs103 Billion Spent Under PSDP in First Four Months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than Rs103.87 billion was spent under the Public Sector Development Programme in the first four months of the current fiscal year, the Planning Ministry said. That is 10.38 percent of the Rs1,000 billion annual development budget. Federal ministries' projects received Rs74.65 billion.

Provinces and special areas received Rs23.8 billion. Water resources projects got Rs18.72 billion. The figures cover spending from the start of the fiscal year to the end of October.
