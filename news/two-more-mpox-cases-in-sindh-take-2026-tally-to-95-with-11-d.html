Two More Mpox Cases in Sindh Take 2026 Tally to 95 With 11 Deaths

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh has reported two more cases of mpox, taking the province's 2026 caseload to 95 with 11 deaths. The two new patients are 21-year-old Salman Ali and 23-year-old Ishrat Fatima. Health officials said 71 of the total cases are men, the 30-39 age group has been hit the hardest, and 23 children under the age of nine have also tested positive.

Authorities have urged the public to follow precautions and seek early treatment, as the virus continues to spread in the province. Surveillance and contact tracing are being strengthened in affected districts.
