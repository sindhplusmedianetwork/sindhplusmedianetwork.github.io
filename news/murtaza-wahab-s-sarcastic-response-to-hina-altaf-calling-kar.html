Murtaza Wahab's Sarcastic Response to Hina Altaf Calling Karachi a Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Mayor Murtaza Wahab responded sarcastically to actress Hina Altaf's statement describing Karachi as a jail. When a journalist asked him about her remarks during a press conference, Wahab said that before commenting on Karachi's conditions, one should also remember the past. He remarked that she must have recently seen the Sindh chief minister walking on the street, accompanied by ministers, local government representatives and ordinary citizens.

The mayor said that opposition to the Pakistan Peoples Party makes some people forget what the city was like nearly 20 years ago. He recalled that in that era, the city faced problems like bodies found in gunny bags, strikes, extortion and kidnappings. He said the situation in the city had changed since then.
