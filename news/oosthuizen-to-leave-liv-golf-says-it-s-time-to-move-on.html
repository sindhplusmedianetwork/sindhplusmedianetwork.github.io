Oosthuizen to Leave LIV Golf, Says It's 'Time to Move On'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Louis Oosthuizen is walking away from LIV Golf, declaring it is time to move on from the breakaway circuit. The former British Open champion told the South China Morning Post of his decision as the league grapples with an uncertain future. The 44-year-old South African was among the first golfers to join LIV, featuring in its inaugural event in June 2022.

Oosthuizen said he wanted a more manageable schedule with less international travel going forward. Meanwhile, LIV is fighting to emerge from Chapter 11 bankruptcy after Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund cut its funding in April. The league has since secured investment from BC Partners Credit and will decide this month whether to wind down entirely or press ahead with a 2.0 model.
