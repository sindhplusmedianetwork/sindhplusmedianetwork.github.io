Maryam Nawaz directs best possible treatment for Jalalpur accident victims

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has expressed deep sorrow over the loss of lives in a traffic accident near Jalalpur. The accident near Jalalpur claimed precious lives and left passengers injured. She directed the authorities concerned to ensure the best possible medical treatment for the injured passengers.

The chief minister offered condolences to the bereaved families. She also prayed for the speedy recovery of the injured.
