NASA's simulated Mars crew emerges after 378 days in isolation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four volunteer crew members of NASA's CHAPEA Mission 2 have emerged after spending 378 days inside a 3D-printed simulated Mars habitat at the Johnson Space Center in Houston. The crew — Ross Elder, Matthew Montgomery, Ellen Ellis and James Spicer — exited the habitat on November 1. During their isolation, the crew grew crops in simulated Martian soil and tested delayed communication systems like those that would be used on Mars.

NASA says the data collected from the mission will help prepare for a possible human mission to Mars as early as the 2030s.
