Five abducted on Indus Highway return home; 17 still missing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five people who were abducted by armed men from buses on the Indus Highway have returned home, while 17 others remain missing. The abductions took place near Cheera Minor, where armed men stopped a passenger coach and a bus belonging to Chashma Sugar Mills and took several people away. Relatives of the missing blocked the road at Parwa Adda in protest against the incident.

The five released men — identified as Arsalan, Majid, Kashif, Sheraz and Hamad — told their families they walked for about four hours to reach home. Police say a search and recovery operation for the remaining 17 missing people is underway.
