Love-Marriage Couple Shot Dead at Pano Aqil Bus Stand; Father, Cousin Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A young man and woman were shot dead in firing at the Pano Aqil bus stand in Sukkur. According to SSP Sukkur Ahmed Chaudhry, the two had married for love some time ago and were about to board a coach for Lahore when armed men pulled them off and killed them. The girl's father and cousin have been taken into custody, and the murder weapon has been recovered.

Police are investigating the case as a suspected honour killing. The incident has once again raised concern over the continued practice of so-called honour crimes in parts of Sindh.
