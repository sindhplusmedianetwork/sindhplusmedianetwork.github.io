Global oil supply to outpace demand, says energy agency

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global oil supplies are expected to exceed demand in the fourth quarter of this year, after increased Gulf exports led to a market surplus in October for the first time since the Iran war, the International Energy Agency said on Friday. Even though attacks on tankers trying to transit the Strait of Hormuz have continued, Gulf shipments reached 18.4 million barrels per day, though that was still 26 per cent below pre-war levels, the Paris-based agency said in its monthly report. 'The oil market is showing tentative signs of rebalancing, with global oil supplies surpassing demand for the first time since the outbreak of the war in Iran,' the IEA said.

The agency's assessment points to a recovery in Middle East exports that is running faster than the growth in global oil demand, despite continued attacks on shipping. The surplus marks a shift in the market after months of disruption caused by the conflict, which had cut supplies from the region and pushed prices higher.
