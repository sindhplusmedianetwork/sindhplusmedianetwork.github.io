Singer Waqar Ali, Brother of Sajjad Ali, Reveals Stage IV Lung Cancer Diagnosis

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Renowned Pakistani singer, music composer and producer Waqar Ali has revealed that he has been diagnosed with Stage IV lung cancer. The 60-year-old, younger brother of celebrated vocalist Sajjad Ali, shared the news in an emotional Instagram post, describing it as one of the most difficult chapters of his life. Waqar Ali composed hit drama soundtracks including Parizaad, Mayi Ri, Khuda Mera Bhi Hai, Cheekh and Gul-e-Rana.

He said he faces the illness with faith, courage and hope, and asked fans to remember him in their prayers. Fellow artists including Saad Hayat, Bushra Ansari, Shagufta Ejaz and Zhalay Sarhadi, along with fans, flooded his post with prayers and messages of support.
