Afghanistan Education Crisis Deepens as Over 2.6 Million Girls Barred From Secondary School, UN Report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Afghanistan's education crisis has become severe, with 7 million of the country's 11.5 million children — 53 percent — out of school, according to a new report by UNICEF and UNESCO. The report says more than 2.6 million Afghan girls have been deprived of secondary education because of restrictions imposed by the Taliban regime. Boys' enrolments have also fallen sharply under the same restrictions.

Schools face an acute shortage of basic facilities, trained teachers and quality learning materials, leaving the education system in an extremely poor state. The findings underline the scale of the challenge facing Afghan children, particularly girls, as access to learning continues to shrink. The report warns that the crisis is deepening with each passing year.
