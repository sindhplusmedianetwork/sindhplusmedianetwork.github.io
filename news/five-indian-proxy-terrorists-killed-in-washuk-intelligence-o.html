Five Indian-Proxy Terrorists Killed in Washuk Intelligence Operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five terrorists affiliated with Fitna al-Hindustan, described by officials as an Indian proxy, were killed in an intelligence-based operation in Balochistan's Washuk district. The operation was conducted on the night of October 12 and 13 on a reported terrorist hideout, which security forces busted during the raid. According to the ISPR, weapons, ammunition and explosives were recovered from the site.

A sanitisation operation was launched in the area to eliminate any remaining threats. The military's media wing said the action was part of the ongoing Azm-e-Istehkam campaign against foreign-sponsored militancy. State media reported that four other terrorists were injured in the exchange and that advanced weaponry was seized under Operation Radd-ul-Fitna 3.

President Asif Ali Zardari paid tribute to the security forces for the successful operation.
