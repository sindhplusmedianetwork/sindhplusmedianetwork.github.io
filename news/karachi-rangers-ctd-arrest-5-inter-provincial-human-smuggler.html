Karachi: Rangers, CTD Arrest 5 Inter-Provincial Human Smugglers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rangers and the Counter-Terrorism Department arrested five inter-provincial human smugglers in a joint operation in Karachi's Surjani Town on Sunday. A Rangers spokesperson said the gang was illegally bringing Afghan nationals into Karachi, charging up to Rs200,000 per family and housing them temporarily in hotels and homes. The operation was carried out after the authorities received intelligence about the smuggling network.

Arms, drugs and a vehicle were recovered from the suspects during the raid. The arrested men are accused of running the network across provincial lines. Officials said further investigation is under way to trace the rest of the network and its facilitators.
