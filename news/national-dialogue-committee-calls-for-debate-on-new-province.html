National Dialogue Committee Calls for Debate on New Provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Dialogue Committee on Tuesday said a national government made up of political parties should be formed to pull the country out of economic crisis. It also called for a debate on creating new provinces to fix administrative problems. At a press conference at the National Press Club, former federal minister Fawad Chaudhry, former Sindh governor Imran Ismail and Mahmood Maulvi said the country could no longer afford confrontation.

They said a joint national strategy based on consensus was needed. They also said existing administrative units needed review because of population growth, expanding cities and growing demands for better services.
