Funeral Held for Slain Muzaffargarh Boy Waseem; Family Demands Justice

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The funeral prayer of Waseem, a teenage boy from Muzaffargarh, was offered at night in Mauza Daulatpur, and he was buried in the ancestral graveyard. Only close relatives and officials of the orphanage attended. His body had been brought to Muzaffargarh by his uncle and maternal uncle.

The boy's uncle said Waseem's mother, a younger brother and a younger sister survive him, and that he had lived in an orphanage for about five and a half years. The family has demanded justice from the government. The 15-year-old was crushed under a vehicle by influential suspects in Multan, who attempted to present the killing as an accident.

However, CCTV footage from multiple angles established that it was a deliberate act.
