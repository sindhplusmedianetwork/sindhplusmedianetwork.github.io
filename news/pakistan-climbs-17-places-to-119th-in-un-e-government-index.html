Pakistan Climbs 17 Places to 119th in UN E-Government Index 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has achieved a notable success in the United Nations E-Government Development Index 2026 by improving 17 places to rank 119th globally. Federal Minister for Information Technology and Telecommunication Shaza Fatima Khawaja, in a statement on the social media website X, called the achievement the best ranking in the country's history. The minister said Pakistan has continued its journey of improvement in the index over the past four years, gaining a total of 31 places during this period.

According to the figures, Pakistan's global ranking was 150th in 2022 and 136th in 2024, which has now reached 119th in 2026, with a 23 percent improvement recorded in the overall index. Telecommunication infrastructure saw the highest improvement of 39 percent, with its score rising from 0.4745 to 0.6590, while online government services increased 20 percent with the score rising from 0.7042 to 0.8422. The human capital index also improved 8 percent, with its score rising from 0.3500 to 0.3778.

The minister credited Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif's leadership and the tireless efforts of relevant teams for the progress, and reiterated that the government's vision is to build a Digital Pakistan where modern technology makes every citizen's life easier.
