Aurangzeb Outlines Pakistan's Priorities as G-24 Chair

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb has outlined Pakistan's priorities as it takes the chair of the G-24 group of developing countries. In a virtual briefing with G-24 Secretariat Director Iyabo Masha on November 4, 2026, Aurangzeb pledged an inclusive and results-oriented agenda. He said the agenda would focus on economic resilience and affordable development financing.

He also stressed greater representation for developing economies in global decision-making. Pakistan is expected to host the G-24 Technical Group Meeting in February 2027. The chairmanship comes at a challenging time for developing nations facing debt and climate pressures.
