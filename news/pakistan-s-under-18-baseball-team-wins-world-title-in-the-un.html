Pakistan's Under-18 Baseball Team Wins World Title in the United States

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's under-18 baseball team has won the world title in the United States after beating Paraguay 11-0 in the final of a tournament in Florida. The final was played as part of the event called World Comes to the Palm Beaches, powered by Prospect Wire, which ran from October 21 to 25 at Cacti Park in West Palm Beach. Cacti Park is the spring training home ground of the Houston Astros and the Washington Nationals.

The event began in 2017 and has featured players from more than 30 countries across six continents, with under-12, under-15, under-18 and open age categories. Pakistan's under-18 team was organised by the Pakistan Baseball Association North America. The association congratulated the team on social media, saying the success was proof of the programme's growth and that its aim was to give talented Pakistani players opportunities and a platform to play at a high level.
