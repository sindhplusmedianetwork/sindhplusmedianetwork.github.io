Poland Mourns Krakow Shooting Victims as Security Fears Grow

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Poland began two days of national mourning on Sunday after six people were killed in a mass shooting in Krakow. Authorities said the attack was likely inspired by far-right ideology. Seventeen people, including two police officers and the gunman, were wounded in the attack.

The 24-year-old suspect's parents and sister were later found dead at their home. Krakow's main market square remained closed as mourners placed candles at the scene. Police in Warsaw strengthened security in busy tourist areas as security fears grew.
