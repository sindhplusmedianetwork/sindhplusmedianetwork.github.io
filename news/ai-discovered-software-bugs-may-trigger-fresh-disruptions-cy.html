AI-Discovered Software Bugs May Trigger Fresh Disruptions, Cyber Experts Warn

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Advances in artificial intelligence have uncovered a wave of new software vulnerabilities that are almost certain to trigger further disruptions, seven cybersecurity professionals told Reuters. The warning comes after flaws discovered in PHP, a widely used web programming language, caused outages at government websites across Europe this week. According to research by VulnCheck due to be published in the coming days, AI has driven a doubling of the number of digital flaws discovered year-on-year.

Cloudflare chief executive Matthew Prince said he was aware of 'a bunch of additional severe vulnerabilities' in other critical software and forecast busy months ahead for security teams. Experts say the speed at which AI tools can find bugs means defenders have less time to patch systems before the flaws are exploited. The findings point to a new phase in cybersecurity, where automated discovery is expanding the workload for organisations faster than they can respond.
