Fuel-Price Anger Bolsters Europe's Far Right as Unrest Sweeps France

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rising fuel prices are driving cost-of-living protests across Europe and strengthening the continent's far right ahead of elections next year in France, Italy and Poland. Some far-right parties are calling for a return to Russian energy supplies. In France, demonstrations that began over school facilities in the northern suburbs of Paris have become some of the country's worst unrest in decades.

Shops were looted, buildings were torched, and a 19th-century town hall in Limoges was set on fire. President Macron acknowledged the protesters' 'legitimate demands' while condemning the violence. The European Commission estimates that the Iran conflict has cost Europe roughly 100 billion euros through disrupted trade and energy markets.
