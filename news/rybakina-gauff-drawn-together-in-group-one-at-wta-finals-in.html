Rybakina, Gauff drawn together in Group One at WTA Finals in Indian Wells

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

World number one Elena Rybakina and American Coco Gauff were drawn in Group One for the WTA Finals, while second-ranked Aryna Sabalenka will face third-ranked Mirra Andreeva in Group Two at the season-ending championship. Defending champion Rybakina will begin her title defence against Gauff, four-time WTA Finals participant Jessica Pegula and reigning Wimbledon champion Linda Noskova, who is making her tournament debut. Sabalenka's group also includes 19-year-old Andreeva, 2018 WTA Finals champion Elina Svitolina and Czech Karolina Muchova.

The event, which brings together the season's top eight qualifiers, will be held at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden from November 8 to 15, with the top two players from each round-robin group advancing to the semifinals. Rybakina, who claimed the world number one ranking after September's US Open, said the position brought both added scrutiny and motivation. 'It has been an unbelievable couple of months,' she said at Tuesday's draw ceremony.

Sabalenka will be looking for vengeance after losing her top ranking to Rybakina at the US Open, having won both Indian Wells and Miami earlier this year to complete the 'Sunshine Double'.
