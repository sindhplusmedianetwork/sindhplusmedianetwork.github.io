Gandapur Returns to PTI Parliamentary Politics, Named Negotiation Representative

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister Ali Amin Gandapur has made a return to the parliamentary politics of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf. A meeting of PTI Khyber Pakhtunkhwa decided to name him the party's representative in the central negotiation process. The meeting also expressed concern over a recent statement by party secretary Salman Akram Raja and demanded that he withdraw the statement and offer an apology.

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar said that internal party matters should not be discussed in public. The decisions were announced after the party's provincial meeting concluded.
