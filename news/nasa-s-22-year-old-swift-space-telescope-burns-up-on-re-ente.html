NASA's 22-Year-Old Swift Space Telescope Burns Up on Re-entering Earth's Atmosphere

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

NASA's Swift space telescope ended its 22-year mission after re-entering Earth's atmosphere on 4 November and burning up over Indonesia. There were no reports of casualties or injuries on the ground. The roughly 1,452 kg telescope was launched in 2004 to study gamma-ray bursts.

It entered the atmosphere at about 22 times the speed of sound. Most of it burned up, though small fragments may have reached the ground. A July 2026 rescue mission by the startup Catalyst, using a spacecraft called Link to lift Swift to a safer orbit, failed after the craft spun out of control.

The rescue attempt was abandoned in mid-August.
