Pakistan High Commission in London Marks Kashmir Black Day

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan High Commission in London organised an event to mark Kashmir Black Day, attended by British parliamentarians, members of the Kashmiri community, civil society activists and media representatives. Among those present were Lord Qurban Hussain, Lord Shaffaq Mohammed, Baroness Nosheena Mubarik, Adnan Hussain, Afzal Khan and Ayub Khan. In his keynote address, High Commissioner Tipu Usman condemned what he described as the fear and brutal repression imposed by Indian forces in Jammu and Kashmir, saying the region's people have long faced a strict military siege marked by violence, enforced disappearances and extrajudicial killings.

He said India's actions violate international law and basic moral principles and threaten regional and global peace and security, urging the international community to hold India accountable for violating United Nations Security Council resolutions guaranteeing the Kashmiri people's right to self-determination. British parliamentarians at the event expressed solidarity with the people of Jammu and Kashmir, saying the dispute is not merely a bilateral matter between Pakistan and India but a longstanding international dispute, and they called for an end to systematic human rights violations and for the pledge of a free and fair plebiscite to be honoured.
