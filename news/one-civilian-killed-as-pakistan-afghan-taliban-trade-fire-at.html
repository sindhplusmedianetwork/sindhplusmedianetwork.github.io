One Civilian Killed as Pakistan, Afghan Taliban Trade Fire at Angor Adda

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A peace jirga at Angor Adda on the Pakistan-Afghanistan border turned into a night of explosions and fire as Pakistani forces and the Afghan Taliban traded conflicting accounts of who fired first. Pakistan said unprovoked fire by the Afghan Taliban killed a civilian labourer, Umar Khan, and injured five others during the jirga on Tuesday evening. The Afghan Taliban's defence ministry countered that Pakistan had drone-attacked a bazaar and the commissioner's office in Paktika, forcing retaliation.

Local residents said the bazaar, several houses and the timber market caught fire in the shelling, and one man, Rahmanullah, was seriously wounded and operated on in DI Khan. The BBC said it could not independently verify either side's claims. Angor Adda, straddling South Waziristan and Paktika, has seen repeated cross-border flare-ups despite ongoing efforts to settle tensions through tribal jirgas.
