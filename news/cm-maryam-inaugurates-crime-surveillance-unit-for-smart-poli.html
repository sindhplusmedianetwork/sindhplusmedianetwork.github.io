CM Maryam inaugurates crime surveillance unit for smart policing across Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has formally inaugurated the CM Crime Surveillance Unit in Lahore. The unit will carry out smart policing and digital monitoring of police officers' performance at the district, circle and police station levels across the province. The head of the unit, Usman Tahir, briefed the chief minister on the unit's work and systems, saying the latest crime data for every police station in Punjab will be available at the click of a button.

The performance of officers, including DPOs, DSPs and SHOs, will be analysed, and the unit will compile data on 20 different categories of crime and prepare analysis reports immediately. The system will also monitor whether citizens' complaints and applications are resolved on time, with action against the officials concerned in case of delay, and it will identify noteworthy social media posts in line with national security requirements. Maryam Nawaz asked the inspector general of police for a police reform plan and directed that data from the Police Station Record Management system be used properly.

She expressed displeasure over unjustified delays in registering FIRs and directed that negative propaganda against the police be responded to immediately. She said she wants to rid Punjab of all kinds of crime, adding that the historic downward trend in the crime rate resulting from effective reforms in the province is encouraging.
