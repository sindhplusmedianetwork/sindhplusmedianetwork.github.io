First-Ever Digital Yellow Card Used in Football Match in Uruguay

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A referee in Uruguay has used a smartphone-like electronic device instead of traditional yellow and red cards during a Copa Uruguay match, the first such use in football history. In the 12th minute of the October 15 match between Nacional and Rentistas, referee Eduardo Varela showed Rentistas player Juan Pablo Plada a digital yellow card for a foul. Nacional won the match 3-0.

The device logs match events automatically and works without Wi-Fi. However, spectators and television viewers struggled to distinguish the yellow screen from the red one. Football authorities will review the trial before deciding whether to expand its use.
