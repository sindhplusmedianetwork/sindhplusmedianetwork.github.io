Hafizabad: Sons Allegedly Kill Father and Mediator Over Land Dispute

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two brothers allegedly killed their father and a mediator with axe blows during a reconciliation meeting over a land dispute in Kolo Tarar, Hafizabad. Police identified the deceased as Muhammad Siddique and Nazeer Ahmed, the latter having come to mediate between the feuding family members. The suspects, Qasim and Asif, had been locked in an ongoing land conflict with their father, police said.

Both men fled the scene after the double murder, leaving the bodies at the meeting spot. A case has been registered and a police search for the suspects has begun.
