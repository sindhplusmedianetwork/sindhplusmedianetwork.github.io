Alcaraz confident ahead of ATP Finals despite stomach problem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Carlos Alcaraz cut short a training session in Turin on Friday because of a stomach problem, but he said he was feeling "better and better" ahead of the ATP Finals. The Spaniard was runner-up in Turin last year and is now seeking a first ATP Finals crown in his fourth appearance at the event. He arrives full of confidence after winning titles in Tokyo, Vienna and Paris since returning from a wrist injury.

Alcaraz said his recent run of victories had left him in strong shape for the season-ending tournament. He also spoke about parting ways with long-time coach Juan Carlos Ferrero, who will coach Ben Shelton from next season. The split marks the end of a long partnership that guided Alcaraz to the top of the men's game.
