Eleven Killed as Bus Overturns on Indus Highway in Rajanpur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least eleven passengers were killed and 34 others injured when an inter-city bus overturned on the Indus Highway in Rajanpur district of Punjab on Wednesday. The bus was travelling from Karachi to DI Khan when it struck the concrete divider between two under-construction roads near Bangla Hidayat, a locality in the Rojhan tehsil. Eyewitnesses said the impact was so intense that the bus turned over.

Rescue 1122 officials, along with local people, reached the site and began shifting the dead and the injured to Rajanpur's DHQ hospital. Hospital sources said eleven people succumbed to their injuries while 34 sustained injuries, seven of them critical. Five of the critically injured were later referred to DI Khan and two to Rahim Yar Khan hospitals.

Most of the dead and injured belonged to DI Khan, Taunsa and various areas of Balochistan, and the bodies were sent to their native areas after legal formalities. Police in Umarkot registered a case against the bus driver, and officials said the apparent cause of the accident was over-speeding.
