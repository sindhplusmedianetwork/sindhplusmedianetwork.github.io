Actress Aymen Saleem joins PPP to serve Pakistan and its people

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani actress Aymen Saleem has stepped into politics by joining the Pakistan Peoples Party. The 33-year-old is the daughter of former cricketer Saleem Yousuf. A video surfaced online showing her announcing the decision at an event.

She said she had been a devoted admirer of former prime minister Benazir Bhutto since childhood. She added that Benazir Bhutto's life and legacy had inspired countless women. Calling the decision a moment of pride and happiness, she said joining the party was more than a political choice.

She described it as a promise to her country and its people. She said she intended to serve through the party platform with sincerity, good intentions and wholehearted dedication.
