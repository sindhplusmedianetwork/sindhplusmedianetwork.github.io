Leak Reveals Key Feature Tested for Foldable iPhone Duo

SP

A leak has revealed that Apple has tested new lock-screen widgets for its expected foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo. The widgets could be introduced through a future software update. According to tipster pdfu, Apple tested a 'complication column' fitting two circular widgets along the right edge of the cover screen.

The feature was first seen in iOS 27.1 under a disabled SpringBoard/Ionic flag. The report said Apple has made no code changes to the feature since iOS 27.1. It is therefore unclear whether the company is still working on it.
