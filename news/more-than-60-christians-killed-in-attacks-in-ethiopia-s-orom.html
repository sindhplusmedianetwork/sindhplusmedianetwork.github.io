More Than 60 Christians Killed in Attacks in Ethiopia's Oromia Region

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 60 people have been killed in a series of attacks on Christians in Ethiopia's Oromia region, according to the country's Orthodox Church. A church official described attacks on three areas in the Shirka district as a massacre, saying security forces arrived at the scene but were unable to stop the assault. One resident, whose 75-year-old father was killed, told BBC Amharic that the attackers surrounded residents from all four directions and that they had no prior warning.

Eyewitnesses said five of those killed were children, and the Holy Synod of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church said 20 houses were burnt to the ground. A local official blamed the attacks on a newly formed rebel coalition, the Alliance for Survival, which includes the Oromo Liberation Army, though the group has not commented and has denied carrying out previous such attacks. The regional government said it would take action against those responsible, while many residents have fled the area.

The Orthodox Church said attacks against Christians have been increasing recently and that it has repeatedly raised security concerns with authorities without a solution so far.
