Azma Bokhari says Hina Pervaiz Butt's remarks embarrass the Punjab government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari has said that the statements of PML-N lawmaker Hina Pervaiz Butt create difficulties for her and embarrassment for the government. Speaking on a television talk show hosted by Shahzeb Khanzada, Bokhari said she did not want to comment on how serious Butt's tweets were. She said police in Punjab are given two hours to arrest suspects in crimes against women and children.

She said the Crime Control Department was set up to deal with serious crimes, and that crime in Punjab had fallen after its creation. She said that over the past year, seven CCD personnel had been martyred and more than 100 injured in encounters. She added that the chief minister had convened a meeting on the rising violence against women and children in the province.
