Cyberattack on supermarket fridges sweeps across Europe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A hacking attack on European supermarkets spread across the continent on Monday, striking refrigeration and freezer units from Sweden to Belgium. The attack raised temperatures in refrigerator and freezer units at dozens of stores, forcing supermarkets to throw away large quantities of food. The incident echoed a similar attack in France last month, when hackers seized control of cooling units at hundreds of supermarkets, causing fires and food losses.

The latest attack hit supermarket groups Ahold Delhaize and Colruyt in Belgium and Luxembourg, as well as Swedish grocers ICA and Coop, while furniture retailer IKEA also saw at least one store affected. An ICA spokesperson said the company was aware that a limited number of stores were affected overnight, impacting their refrigeration and freezer systems. At least some of the affected units used temperature control technology from Danish firm Danfoss, which was also targeted in the French attack.

No one has claimed responsibility for either attack, and investigators could not establish who was behind Monday's incidents or whether they were linked to the French ones. EU agriculture commissioner Christophe Hansen said the incidents highlighted how digital threats were becoming increasingly disruptive, calling them a deliberate attempt to disrupt the food supply chain and undermine consumer confidence.
