Trump Casts Early Vote in Florida Ahead of November 3 Midterm Elections

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States President Donald Trump has voted early in Florida for the midterm elections scheduled for November 3, a contest in which his Republican Party faces a difficult battle to retain its majority in Congress. According to a foreign news agency, Trump cast his ballot with his wife, Melania Trump, at an election office in West Palm Beach. After voting, he said he had voted for his party's candidates, and then departed to address an election rally in the state of Georgia.

Speaking to journalists, Trump criticised Democrats over corruption in the United States and claimed that the real problem of corruption lies in cities and districts run by Democrats. On the occasion, he also repeated his earlier claims about the 2020 presidential election, saying once again that he had won it, although no credible evidence has emerged in support of that claim. It is noted that the Democratic candidate, Joe Biden, won the 2020 election.

Only a few days remain before the American elections, and various public surveys suggest the Republican Party faces the risk of losing its majority in the House of Representatives, while its position in the Senate is also described as uncertain. Losing its congressional majority could make it difficult for Trump to carry out his political and legislative agenda.
