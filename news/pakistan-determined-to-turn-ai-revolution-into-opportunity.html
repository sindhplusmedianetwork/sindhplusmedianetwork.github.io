Pakistan Determined to Turn AI Revolution Into Opportunity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan is determined to ensure the global technological transformation, particularly the rapid advance of artificial intelligence, becomes an opportunity for national development, economic growth and technological sovereignty, IT Minister Shaza Fatima Khawaja said on Wednesday. She was speaking on the second day of the Margalla Dialogue 2026 during a panel discussion on technology and the transformation of state and society. The minister said artificial intelligence was expected to become a 4.8 trillion dollar sector by 2033 and to affect about 40 per cent of global employment.

She said Pakistan wanted to position itself to benefit from the change rather than be left behind by it. The discussion focused on how technology is reshaping the state, society and the economy. The Margalla Dialogue brings together policymakers, experts and officials to discuss regional and global challenges.
