AVCC investigation officer suspended over bribery, negligence in kidnapping case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An investigation officer of the Anti-Violent Crime Cell (AVCC) has been suspended in Karachi following a complaint by the father of Zainul Abideen, who was kidnapped from Steel Town and brutally murdered. The victim's father, Muhammad Khan, accused IO Rizwan of receiving Rs22,000 from him in bribes, including Rs2,000 allegedly taken in the name of food expenses. He said the officer had met him only three times, showed little interest in the case, and failed to recover the ransom money and other belongings from the suspects.

Khan lodged the complaint with DIG CIA Muqaddas Haider on Thursday; the DIG suspended the officer and appointed a new investigation officer, though the new officer had not yet contacted the family. The family had paid Rs500,000 in ransom to secure his son's release, but he was not recovered, and the kidnappers later sent the family a severed hand of his son to intimidate them. Zainul Abideen, who had returned from Saudi Arabia, went missing from Steel Town on October 14; his body was recovered on October 16 from Chhatri Ground in Bin Qasim on the identification of arrested suspects, including his close relative Faisal and an accomplice, Ansar.
