Additional SHO Martyred in Miranshah Bypass Firing, North Waziristan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An additional station house officer was martyred on Tuesday when unidentified gunmen opened fire on Miranshah Bypass Road in North Waziristan. Police identified the fallen officer as Mir Akhan Darba Khel, the Additional SHO of Miranshah police station, who died at the scene of the attack. According to police officials, the attackers managed to flee immediately after the shooting, escaping into the surrounding area.

Police launched search raids shortly after the incident to track down the assailants involved in the targeted killing. Officials said further investigation into the attack is underway, with security in the area tightened in the aftermath.
