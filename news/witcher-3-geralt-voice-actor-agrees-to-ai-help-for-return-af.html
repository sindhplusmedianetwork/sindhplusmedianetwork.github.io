Witcher 3 Geralt voice actor agrees to AI help for return after stroke

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jacek Rozenek, the Polish voice actor behind Geralt in The Witcher games, has approved the use of artificial intelligence to help recreate his original voice for the upcoming The Witcher 3 expansion, Songs of the Past. A stroke in 2019 left the actor unable to speak for months. Although he returned to acting, his voice changed, becoming lower and gruffer.

With his approval, the studio contacted an AI company it had previously worked with. The aim is to make his new dialogue sound closer to his original performance. The expansion will bring Rozenek back as the voice of Geralt with AI assistance.
