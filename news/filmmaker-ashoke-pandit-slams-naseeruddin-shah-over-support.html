Filmmaker Ashoke Pandit slams Naseeruddin Shah over support for protesting students

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian filmmaker Ashoke Pandit has launched a sharp attack on veteran actor Naseeruddin Shah for supporting students protesting at Jamia Millia Islamia. In a special interview with news agency ANI, the 69-year-old filmmaker described Shah as psychologically troubled and habitually foul-mouthed. Pandit said there was nothing surprising about the language used by Shah, claiming he has been behaving inappropriately for the past several months.

He said Shah's words and on-camera expressions reflect his frustration and mental turmoil, adding that the more he vents his frustration, the more his personality stands exposed. The criticism followed Shah's praise for the courage of Indian youth and his accusation that the central government, led by the BJP, is trying to destroy the education system. On Tuesday, Shah shared a video on Facebook in which he proudly described himself as a 'mental Naxal.' He said he did not know what other titles government supporters would give him, and asked what more the government would do to demoralize the country's youth, destroy the education system and endanger democracy.

The student-led protest demonstrations began on Monday, October 12, and are still continuing.
