Exchange of fire between police and dacoits in Karachi; three dacoits arrested in injured condition

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three alleged dacoits were arrested in injured condition after an exchange of fire between police and armed dacoits in Gulshan-e-Zia, in the Pakistan Bazaar police station area of Karachi. Police recovered illegal weapons along with ammunition from their possession. Mobile phones, cash and a motorcycle were also recovered from the suspects.

According to the spokesperson for SSP District West, the arrested suspects are Musharraf son of Ashraf, Salman son of Usman and Shahzad Alam son of Mahmood. The injured suspects were shifted to hospital for medical treatment.
