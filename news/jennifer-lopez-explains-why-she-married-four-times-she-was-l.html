Jennifer Lopez explains why she married four times: she was looking for security and stability

American singer and actress Jennifer Lopez has finally explained why she married four times, saying she was looking for security in relationships and stability in life. Speaking on a podcast, the 57-year-old revealed that because of fame she often felt lonely and directionless, and that was why she wanted a partner who genuinely wanted to spend time with her. According to Lopez, people think famous personalities are always surrounded by people, but life can still be lonely.

She said she now realises she was looking for a strong support and stability in her relationships. The singer, who recently separated from Ben Affleck, also spoke about the experience of divorce, saying divorce is difficult and disappointing, but even in difficult circumstances a person should try to keep the best side of themselves. Lopez married for the first time in 1997 to Ojani Noa, a marriage that ended in 1998, then married Cris Judd, from whom she separated in 2003.

Her third marriage was to singer Marc Anthony, with whom she has twins Max and Emme and with whom she now shares their upbringing, and she married Ben Affleck in 2024, a marriage that did not last long and ended in divorce in 2025.
