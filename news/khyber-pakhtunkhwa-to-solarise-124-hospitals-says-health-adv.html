Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to solarise 124 hospitals, says health adviser

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa's health adviser Jauhar Nagar has said that 124 hospitals in the province will be solarised, as part of efforts to improve the health sector. Speaking at a briefing in Peshawar, he said more than 200 hospitals in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa have remained closed for years. He said that 30,697 health staff are deployed for a population of 40 million, and that the province needs 64,000 hospital beds against the 18,000 available in proportion to the population.

Jauhar Nagar said Rs2 billion will be spent on the purchase of medical equipment for hospitals in 25 districts of the province. He said the health card will not be abolished in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, and its shortcomings will be removed. He added that Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has promised Rs1,462 million for a children's hospital in Hayatabad.

The provincial health adviser also said the federal government will provide Rs850 million for the establishment of breast cancer centres in five divisions of the province.
