Moscow car bombing: 21-year-old Russian woman arrested over attack on ex-Ukrainian PM's adviser

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian authorities have arrested a 21-year-old Russian woman on suspicion of involvement in a car bombing in Moscow that seriously injured Sergei Zavorotny, an adviser to former Ukrainian prime minister Mykola Azarov. The blast occurred on November 13. The FSB alleges the woman was manipulated through a fake call centre since July by someone posing as a central bank official, and claims the operation was directed by Ukrainian intelligence, which Kyiv has not commented on.

The incident was the second car bombing in Russia in two days, after a Russian soldier was killed in a similar blast. The arrest adds to the series of attacks inside Russia that Moscow has blamed on Ukrainian intelligence.
