Hospital Sealed in Karachi After Mpox Detected in Newborn

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Healthcare Commission has sealed Ibn-e-Hassan Hospital in Malir 15 after a 20-day-old newborn from Korangi tested positive for mpox. The action followed a complaint by the District Health Officer Korangi. Investigators found that the instruments used for the infant's circumcision were contaminated with the mpox virus, a finding confirmed by Dow University laboratories.

Officials said the hospital was operating illegally, with unqualified staff performing circumcisions. The alleged quack fled during the sealing operation. Health authorities have urged parents to ensure medical procedures for newborns are carried out only at licensed facilities.
