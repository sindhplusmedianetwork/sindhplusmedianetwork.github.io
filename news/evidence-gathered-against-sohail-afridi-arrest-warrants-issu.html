Evidence gathered against Sohail Afridi, arrest warrants issued

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Evidence of alleged financial corruption and irregularities worth billions of rupees has been gathered against former Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister Sohail Afridi. According to a private TV channel's report, citing KP government sources, arrest warrants for Sohail Afridi have also been issued, but he has not been arrested so far. The report said that according to the provincial government, Sohail Afridi is in protective custody and in safe hands, while his care is being ensured.

Officials said records have been sought from all relevant government departments, and after the initial inquiry the cases will be referred to the National Accountability Bureau and the anti-corruption department for further action. The government's statement also mentions alleged corruption and administrative irregularities in the Sehat Card project, saying different matters are being examined and records are being collected. The charges remain unproven, and the reported actions reflect the KP government's claims; Sohail Afridi's response was not included in the report.
