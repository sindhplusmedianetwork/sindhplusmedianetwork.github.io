Health expert urges smog precautions to protect children in Muzaffargarh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Health expert Dr Fakhar ul Islam has urged parents and schools in Muzaffargarh to take precautions as severe smog grips the South Punjab city, warning that children are especially vulnerable because their respiratory systems are still developing. He said prolonged exposure to smog can harm children's respiratory health and aggravate existing breathing problems, and can also cause persistent coughing, throat irritation, eye inflammation and allergic reactions. He advised parents to discourage unnecessary outdoor activities, restrict outdoor sports and games when pollution levels are high, and ensure children wear properly fitted, good-quality masks while travelling to and from school.

He also recommended that children drink enough water, maintain personal hygiene and wash their eyes with clean water if they feel irritation, and that windows and doors of homes and classrooms stay closed when outdoor pollution is high. Parents and teachers should watch for symptoms such as persistent coughing, breathing difficulties and chest discomfort, and children with such problems should receive timely medical attention. He also called on school administrations to monitor air quality, limit outdoor activities during severe smog and raise awareness among children about preventive measures.
