Beardman, Marsh lead Australia to crushing win over England

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Debutant Mahli Beardman took 6-24 as Australia crushed England by seven wickets in the first ODI in Perth. England were bowled out for 131 in 33.4 overs after Beardman's superb spell on debut. Mitchell Marsh then smashed a 29-ball 50 to seal the win for the hosts.

Beardman's figures were the best ODI bowling figures by an Australian on debut. The result gave Australia a winning start to the series against England.
