Ramayana, India's most expensive film, opens in cinemas worldwide

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ramayana, India's most expensive film, has opened in cinemas around the world. The two-part adaptation, reportedly made at a cost of around $500 million, is directed by Nitesh Tiwari and stars Ranbir Kapoor as Ram. Sai Pallavi plays Sita, while Yash plays Ravan, the ten-headed king of Lanka who abducts Sita.

The film's music is a collaboration between Oscar-winning composers A. R. Rahman and Hans Zimmer, and its producer Namit Malhotra heads the visual effects company DNEG.

The film was released a few days before Diwali, the Hindu festival of lights that marks Ram's return to Ayodhya after exile. Reviews have been mixed, with some praising the visual spectacle and others saying it overshadows the story. Ranbir Kapoor said he was filled with fear about playing a figure worshipped by millions, calling the role a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.

The second part, which will show the great war between Ram and Ravan, is due to be released next year.
