Sindh must get its fair share of water, no one should tamper with it: Nisar Khuhro

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Senator Nisar Khuhro has said his party never opposed building dams, but dams should be built where agriculture needs them. Speaking at an event of a private university in Lahore, he said Sindh only wants its fair share of water and does not want anyone tampering with it. According to Jang, he recalled that the water issue in the region is a hundred years old and Sindh was once accused of wasting water.

He said the Indus water pushes back the sea, and even today 22 lakh acres of land in Sindh lie barren. Khuhro acknowledged that he had led the movement against the Kalabagh Dam and had proposed the Bhasha Dam instead 26 years ago. He added that the Bhasha Dam has still not been completed after all these years.
