Iran's senior Shia cleric Ayatollah Wahid Khorasani dies at 108

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prominent Iranian Shia religious leader Ayatollah Wahid Khorasani has died at the age of 108, his office confirmed in a statement. According to Iran's Mehr News Agency, the statement expressed deep sorrow and said Ayatollah Khorasani spent decades associated with the teaching, research and instruction of religious sciences. It said he also played an important role in protecting religious rituals and in training religious scholars.

A report of his death had also surfaced on 5 November, but his office had rejected it as false and said he was under treatment. This time, his office formally announced his death. According to his office, details of the arrangements for his funeral prayers, burial and mourning gatherings will be announced later.
