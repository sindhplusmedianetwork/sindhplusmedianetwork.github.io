Iran's Armed Forces Warn US to Abandon Hostile Policies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's armed forces have warned the United States to abandon what they called its hegemonic and hostile policies towards Iran. Armed forces spokesman Brigadier General Abolfazl Shekarchi said any power that stands against Iran will ultimately face decline and disarray. He said recent political and electoral developments in the United States reflected Washington's failures in confronting Iran.

Shekarchi said the United States and its allies should reconsider their policies towards Tehran. The armed forces spokesman said Washington's approach towards Iran had not succeeded.
