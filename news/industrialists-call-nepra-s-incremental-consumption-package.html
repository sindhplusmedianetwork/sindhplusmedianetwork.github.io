Industrialists Call NEPRA's Incremental Consumption Package a Burden of Billions, Demand Its Suspension

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The president of the SITE Association of Industry Karachi, Abdul Hadi, has demanded the immediate suspension of NEPRA's incremental consumption package, describing the revised rate of Rs25.30 per unit as unfair. He said the package places a burden of billions of rupees on ordinary electricity consumers. According to Abdul Hadi, a four-month delay in the first half-yearly review cost consumers about Rs13.6 billion in April and Rs9.4 billion in May 2026.

He demanded that any losses be recovered only from consumers who benefit from the package, rather than from all consumers. The Karachi Chamber, the Korangi Association of Trade and Industry and the Federal B Area Association of Trade and Industry have also raised serious concerns about the package. The trade bodies say the package's terms need to be reviewed so that ordinary consumers are not made to carry costs they did not incur.
