Azad Jammu and Kashmir marks Foundation Day

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Azad Jammu and Kashmir observed its Foundation Day on Saturday, commemorating the establishment of the region's provisional government on October 24, 1947 under Sardar Muhammad Ibrahim Khan. The day marks the historic moment when the people of the region set up their own government during the freedom struggle against the Dogra Raj. Commemorative events recalled the resolution of July 19, 1947, in which the region's leadership affirmed accession to Pakistan, and the sacrifices made by Kashmiris in the liberation movement.

Speakers at the events said the freedom struggle and the ideology of accession to Pakistan remained alive, and that the Kashmiri people's demand for their right to self-determination continued to deserve the world's attention. The day was observed with renewed pledges to keep the freedom movement and its ideals alive.
