Alibaba's Qwen AI to Power Apple Intelligence on Chinese iPhones

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese tech company Alibaba Group has partnered with Apple to integrate its Qwen AI model into Apple Intelligence for the Chinese market. The feature will be available for testing in the latest iOS developer beta starting November 4, and will launch officially with iOS 27.3 in December, Alibaba said. Qwen's capabilities will be integrated into several Apple features, including Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence.

Users can choose whether to enable the integration and what information to share, and Alibaba said user requests and generated responses would not be used to train its AI models. The feature will be available on compatible iPhones whose device location and Apple Account region are both set to China. Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu said the partnership would bring new AI-powered services to Chinese users.

An AI expert at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications said this marked the first time a major global consumer hardware maker had adopted a Chinese large language model as its primary system-level AI backbone in the Chinese market.
