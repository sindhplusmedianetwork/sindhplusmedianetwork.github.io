NADRA launches digital Nikah registration through Pak ID, trial begins in Islamabad and Quetta

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Database and Registration Authority has introduced a digital system for Nikah registration. The system also covers the issuance of marriage registration certificates through Pak ID accounts. Nikah registrars will register marriages through Pak ID under the new arrangement.

The marriage record will be entered in the relevant union council. Digital certificates will be available to the registrar, the bride and the groom. NADRA said the facility has been launched on a trial basis in Islamabad and Quetta.
