China Renews Push at UNSC to Sanction BLA and Majeed Brigade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China on Thursday renewed its call at the United Nations Security Council to place the Balochistan Liberation Army (BLA) and its suicide wing, the Majeed Brigade, on the UNSC's 1267 sanctions list. China's Acting Permanent Representative Sun Lei urged committee members to set aside political considerations and build consensus as soon as possible. A previous joint Pakistan-China bid in June was blocked by the US, France and the UK on procedural grounds.
