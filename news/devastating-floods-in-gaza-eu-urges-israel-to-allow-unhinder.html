Devastating floods in Gaza; EU urges Israel to allow unhindered aid delivery

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The European Union has urged Israel to ensure immediate and unhindered access for humanitarian aid to Palestinians affected by heavy rain and devastating floods in Gaza, and to reopen all of Gaza's border crossings. The EU said aid agencies need immediate and unconditional access so flood victims can receive food, clean drinking water, medicines and temporary shelter. Displaced families' tents have been submerged by rainwater, leaving people without dry shelter.

Poor drainage, damaged infrastructure and a shortage of clean water are raising fears that disease could spread among the displaced population. The EU stressed that humanitarian organisations must be able to reach those in need without delay. The appeal comes as winter weather adds to the hardship of families already displaced by the war.
