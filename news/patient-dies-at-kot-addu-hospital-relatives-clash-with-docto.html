Patient Dies at Kot Addu Hospital, Relatives Clash With Doctors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A patient died in the emergency ward of the Tehsil Headquarters Hospital in Kot Addu, sparking a protest by the patient's relatives. The relatives created a disturbance and allegedly assaulted two doctors after the death. Police have taken three people into custody over the alleged assault on the doctors.

The relatives allege that the patient died because timely medical care was not provided, while the hospital administration has rejected the charge of negligence. According to the hospital administration, the patient was given timely medical treatment and was suffering from a heart condition. The relatives have demanded the release of the detained people and action against the doctors they blame for negligence.

The incident disrupted work at the hospital, and the matter is being looked into by the hospital administration and the police.
