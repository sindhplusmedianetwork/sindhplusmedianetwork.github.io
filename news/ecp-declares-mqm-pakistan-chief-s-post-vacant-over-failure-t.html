ECP declares MQM-Pakistan chief's post vacant over failure to hold intra-party polls

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The difficulties of MQM-Pakistan have increased after the Election Commission declared the post of party chief vacant for not holding intra-party elections. The Election Commission has issued a new list of registered political parties. The name of the MQM-Pakistan chief has been removed from that list.

The Election Commission had given MQM-Pakistan time until October to hold intra-party elections, and after the deadline passed, a show-cause notice was issued to MQM-Pakistan. In the Election Commission's list, PTI and TLP are also recorded without heads.
