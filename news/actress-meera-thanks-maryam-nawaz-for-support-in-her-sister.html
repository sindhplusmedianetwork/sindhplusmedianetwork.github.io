Actress Meera thanks Maryam Nawaz for support in her sister's treatment and funeral arrangements

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actress Meera has thanked Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz for supporting the treatment of her younger sister and the arrangements for her funeral in London. Meera's younger sister, Shaista Abbas, had been suffering from cancer for a long time and passed away in London after battling the illness. The actress shared a picture of her late sister on social media and expressed her gratitude to Maryam Nawaz.

In her post, Meera wrote that she is thankful to Maryam Nawaz Sharif, who helped with her sister's medical treatment. She said that although her sister is no longer in this world, her family will always remember the help and kindness shown by the Punjab chief minister in a difficult time. The actress further said that Maryam Nawaz also contacted the British High Commissioner in Pakistan so that arrangements for her sister's burial in England could be facilitated.

According to Meera, her late sister's last rites have been performed with the chief minister's efforts and support. She prayed for Maryam Nawaz, saying that may God grant her the ability to continue serving humanity in the same way and reward her for this good deed.
