Arshdeep Singh gets engaged to Samreen Kaur in New Zealand

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian fast bowler Arshdeep Singh got engaged to his girlfriend, actor and model Samreen Kaur, in New Zealand. The couple announced the engagement on Instagram on Wednesday, November 11, sharing pictures of the proposal. The ring is reported to be a Tiffany piece worth more than INR 5.5 million, about 59,000 US dollars.

The engagement came on the same day Arshdeep's move from Punjab Kings to Mumbai Indians for the 2027 Indian Premier League season was confirmed. The transfer fee was reported at INR 180 million. Arshdeep and Samreen went public with their relationship about four months ago.

The couple have not announced a wedding date.
