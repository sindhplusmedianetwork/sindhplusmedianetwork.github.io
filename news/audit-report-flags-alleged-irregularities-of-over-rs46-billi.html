Audit Report Flags Alleged Irregularities of Over Rs46 Billion in Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The latest report of the Auditor General of Pakistan has identified alleged financial irregularities exceeding Rs46 billion in Punjab, senior journalist Kamran Khan has revealed. Speaking on his programme, he said only 200 of Punjab's more than 5,000 government offices were audited. The audit covered just 19% of expenditures.

Punjab's budget stands at Rs5.904 trillion. The report's summary shows irregularities of Rs5.25 billion, while its overall calculation exceeds Rs46 billion. The figures were revealed in the auditor general's latest report.
