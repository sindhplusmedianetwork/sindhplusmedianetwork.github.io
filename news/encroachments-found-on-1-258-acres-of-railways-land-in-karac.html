Encroachments Found on 1,258 Acres of Railways Land in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 951 acres of Pakistan Railways' land in Karachi Division are under private encroachment, while another 307 acres have been encroached upon by government departments. A parliamentary panel was informed of the figures on Tuesday. The National Assembly Standing Committee on Railways was told that out of 19,594 acres of railway land in the division, 1,258.28 acres were under encroachment.

The Ministry of Railways said 363.907 acres had been retrieved over the past four years. About 8.992 acres worth an estimated Rs305.67 million were cleared between July and October 2026. The committee was briefed on the ongoing efforts to recover the remaining encroached land.
