Khelo Karachi Girls Games Begin with Over 500 Students

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The two-day Khelo Karachi Inter-School and Collegiate Girls Games 2026 began on Tuesday at Khatun-e-Pakistan Government Degree Girls College. More than 500 female students from over 35 government and private schools and colleges are taking part in the event. The students will compete in tape-ball cricket, badminton, futsal and table tennis over the two days.

The games are being held for inter-school and collegiate girl students of Karachi. The event will continue for two days.
