Colombia's ex-president Petro under investigation over 'sex contracts' claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colombia's Supreme Court is investigating former president Gustavo Petro over allegations that state contracts were awarded in exchange for sexual relationships. The case follows an interview given on 25 October by Deicy Jiménez, a former state contractor, to the magazine Semana, in which she said she had a relationship with Petro that began in 2019, when he was a senator, and that she later received public contracts in return. Jiménez said about 30 other women were involved in similar arrangements.

Petro does not deny a relationship with the woman, but he denies the allegation that he awarded government contracts in exchange for sexual relations. In a post on his X account, he wrote: 'If I had ever done such a thing, I would have vomited on myself.' Supreme Court president Mauricio Lenis has confirmed that the former president is under investigation, saying some of the alleged events date to his time in Congress and others to his presidency. No charges have been filed, and the court has announced no findings.

Petro served as Colombia's president from 2022 until August 2026 and now leads the opposition to President Abelardo de la Espriella.
