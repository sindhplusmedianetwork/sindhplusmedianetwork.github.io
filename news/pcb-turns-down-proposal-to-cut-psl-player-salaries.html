PCB Turns Down Proposal to Cut PSL Player Salaries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has rejected a proposal to cut the salaries of Pakistani players in the Pakistan Super League. The proposal was put forward by a franchise owner during a virtual meeting with the board on Thursday, with the argument that player salaries were too high. The board did not approve the move.

The PCB also directed all stakeholders with outstanding dues to clear their payments by October 31. An official of one defaulting company said his firm would meet its obligations and would arrange a bank guarantee against his land to submit to the PCB. The meeting also discussed the PSL Season 12 auction, which is expected to take place on January 23, with one owner suggesting it be held in China.

January 13 was set as the deadline for player retentions, though one owner suggested completing the process in December. The PSL 12 final will be played at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium on May 2, with the opening match scheduled for March 18. Since the South Asian Games will be held in Islamabad, Lahore and Peshawar from April 4 to 11, no PSL matches will take place in these cities during that period.

The tournament schedule will be finalised at the next meeting of the cricket committee. PCB officials also briefed franchise owners on promising talent emerging from school cricket and urged teams to nurture young players.
