Global crude oil prices fall sharply as producers signal higher output

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global crude oil prices have fallen sharply, bringing new signs of stability to the energy sector and industrial markets around the world. According to foreign media economic reports, prices dropped to their lowest level in recent months after signals from major oil-exporting countries about increasing supply and production. Energy experts said prices had swung sharply in recent months due to geopolitical tensions.

That volatility fuelled a fresh wave of inflation in developing and industrial countries. Experts said that if the decline continues, transport and industrial production costs could fall noticeably. Investors are now watching closely for the final decisions of the next meeting of the oil-exporting countries.
