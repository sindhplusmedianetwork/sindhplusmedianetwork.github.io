Six killed, ten injured in suspected gas blast at Hanoi eatery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least six people were killed and ten injured in a suspected cooking-gas explosion in the crowded Dong Da area of Hanoi, Vietnam, at about 8am today. The blast occurred at an eatery, partly collapsing the building and shattering the windows of nearby houses. Rescue teams searched through the rubble for people who may have been trapped inside.

The injured were taken to nearby hospitals for treatment. Residents living around the affected building were moved away as a precaution while the rescue operation continued. The cause of the explosion is suspected to be cooking gas, and the incident has left the surrounding neighbourhood shaken.
