Asif Hits Back at Bilawal: 'He Who Claims Equity Must Come With Clean Hands'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif responded on Wednesday to PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari's hard-hitting speech on new provinces, constitutional tweaks and the treatment of the opposition. In a post on X, Asif reminded Bilawal that he who claims equity must come with clean hands, calling it a centuries-old maxim of equity. He asked whether Bilawal, whose party has been a partner of the PML-N in government since 2022, had the right to lecture or threaten.

Asif said no system could be reset without public support and that politicians were not above accountability. He backed a public say on the new-provinces question, saying the pinnacle of legitimacy would be reached if public opinion was taken into account along with Parliament. Taking a jibe at 18 years of PPP rule in Sindh, he said Sindh had turned into paradise and the world admired the condition of Karachi.

He said the fight now was for devolving governance to the grassroots, where public opinion and Parliament's role were entirely democratic and constitutional.
