Iran Wants Peace but US Must Accept Our Demands, Says Speaker Qalibaf

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian parliament speaker Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf has said Iran wants peace and does not want the war to continue, but the United States must accept Iran's demands. In an interview with Al Jazeera, Qalibaf said Iran gave Washington a clear written reply on its demands. The reply included seven points and new conditions on the Strait of Hormuz.

He warned that if the US or Israel restart the war, Iran will confront both together. The remarks set out Tehran's position that peace depends on Washington accepting its stated demands. Qalibaf's interview comes as the two sides continue to exchange positions over the conflict.
