Morocco Calls for Expanded Mining Cooperation with Pakistan at Investment Forum

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Morocco has called for expanded mining cooperation with Pakistan at the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 in Islamabad. Mohamed Ouhmed, Secretary General of Morocco's Ministry of Energy Transition, addressed the government dialogue on the second day of the forum on November 13. He said the two countries could build on Morocco's mining expertise and explore investment, partnerships and expertise-sharing in the mineral sector.

Ouhmed noted that Morocco ranks first in the world in phosphate exports, with production of about 35 million tonnes in 2025. The forum brings together government officials and investors to explore opportunities in Pakistan's mineral sector, and the Moroccan delegation's remarks signalled interest in closer ties between the two countries in mining and related industries.
