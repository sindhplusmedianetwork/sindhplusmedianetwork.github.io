British Woman Spends Over Rs10 Million on University Degree to Become a Social Media Influencer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 21-year-old woman in Britain has finished a three-year university degree specifically designed to prepare her for a career as a social media influencer. Destiny McGowan studied Digital Content Creation at Nottingham Trent University, a programme that cost her about £30,000 — more than Rs10 million. The course taught practical skills including video editing, building an online following and negotiating brand deals.

McGowan says the structured training gave her a professional foundation she could not have assembled alone. Online reactions have been mixed, with many users surprised that someone would pay such a large sum for skills they believe can be picked up for free on the internet. Supporters of the programme argue that formal study brings discipline and credibility to an increasingly competitive field.

The story has reopened debate over whether influencer careers need formal education at all.
