Rana Sanaullah Responds to Mohsin Naqvi, Says Provinces Decision Will Be Made in Parliament

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senator Rana Sanaullah has responded to Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi. Rana Sanaullah, who serves as adviser to the prime minister on political affairs, made the remarks during a Geo News programme. He said there will be no need to hide faces.

He said the decision will now be made in parliament. He said Naqvi should accept that he mishandled the new provinces issue. He said Naqvi made the issue controversial instead of reacting to his own failures.
