Sindh Assembly demands uninterrupted gas supply to domestic consumers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Assembly on Friday unanimously passed a motion demanding uninterrupted gas supply to domestic consumers in the province and an immediate end to unannounced loadshedding, with members across party lines accusing the federal government of denying the gas-producing province its constitutional right. Moving the motion after a detailed discussion on an adjournment motion on the issue, Law and Parliamentary Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar said Sui Southern Gas Company must ensure uninterrupted supply to domestic consumers, urging the gas utility to inform consumers in advance through SMS and media before any gas suspension. The adjournment motion on prolonged and unannounced gas loadshedding across the province was moved by PPP lawmaker Sumeta Afzal Syed, who said the issue was not that of one party but of the entire province, and that under Article 158 of the Constitution the gas-producing province should get priority.

JI member Muhammad Farooq said the province produced 65 per cent of the country's gas, yet loadshedding continued in every season, summer or winter. PPP's Khairunnisa Mughal said resolutions and motions demanding the province's constitutional rights were repeatedly moved, but smaller provinces had faced injustice for years, while PTI MPA Sajid Hussain said the house had already passed unanimous resolutions on the gas issue. MQM-P's Rashid Khan demanded that President Asif Ali Zardari take immediate notice, summon the relevant federal ministers and officials and form a committee to ascertain the reasons for the shortage, while MQM-P member Bilquis Mukhtar said the assembly's resolutions and motions should not end up in the dustbin and that resolutions sent to the federation must also be implemented.

Earlier, during the Question Hour, opposition members took up the water and sewerage crisis, with JI MPA Farooq saying only 45 to 46 per cent of Karachi's citizens got water through supply lines while 54 per cent were not being supplied water through taps. Later, Speaker Owais Qadir Shah welcomed Bangladesh Parliament Speaker Hafiz Uddin Ahmed and a parliamentary delegation to the assembly, and the house was adjourned to Saturday at 12 noon.
