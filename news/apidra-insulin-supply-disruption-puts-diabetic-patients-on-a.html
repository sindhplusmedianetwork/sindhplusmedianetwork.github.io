Apidra Insulin Supply Disruption Puts Diabetic Patients on Alert in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Diabetic patients in Pakistan have been put on alert amid reports of a supply disruption affecting Apidra, a widely used rapid-acting insulin. Health authorities moved quickly to clarify that the disruption is limited to one brand and does not amount to a nationwide insulin shortage. Health ministry spokesperson Sajid Shah said Sanofi's Apidra is currently unavailable because of international supply constraints affecting the manufacturer.

He stressed that other insulin products, including NovoRapid and Humalog, remain accessible to patients. Doctors have cautioned patients against switching insulin products on their own, warning that different formulations can affect blood glucose levels differently. Patients have been advised to consult their physicians for suitable alternatives if they are unable to find Apidra.
