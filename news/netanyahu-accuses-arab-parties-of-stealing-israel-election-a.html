Netanyahu Accuses Arab Parties of 'Stealing' Israel Election as 19 Seats Make Them Kingmakers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Benjamin Netanyahu has accused Arab-led parties of "stealing" Israel's general election, hours after partial results showed them winning around 19 seats and emerging as potential kingmakers. In a post on X, the Israeli prime minister alleged large-scale irregularities, prompting his Likud party to demand investigations by the Central Election Committee, the Shin Bet security service and the police. Likud cited alleged cash payments, threats against polling staff, multiple voting, dual identities and the use of banned software.

The partial count put Netanyahu's right-wing bloc at 53 seats and Gadi Eisenkot's change bloc at 48, leaving neither camp with a clear route to the 61 seats needed for a parliamentary majority. With turnout reported above 77 percent, a record high, the Arab-led parties now hold the balance of power in the tightest Israeli election in years. The allegations set the stage for a bitter legal and political fight over the vote's legitimacy as coalition talks loom.
