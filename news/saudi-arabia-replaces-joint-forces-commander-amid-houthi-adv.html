Saudi Arabia Replaces Joint Forces Commander Amid Houthi Advances in Yemen

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia has removed Lieutenant General Fahd bin Hamad Al-Salman as commander of the joint forces and appointed General Fayyaz bin Hamid Al-Ruwaili in his place. The change comes as Houthi rebels advance in Yemen. A royal decree issued by King Salman also made Al-Salman an adviser to the defence minister on military affairs.

Reuters reported that the move comes as government forces retreat and the Houthis threaten to advance towards the southern city of Aden. The report described the situation as a setback for the six-week-old military campaign against the Houthis. The Saudi-led coalition has been backing Yemeni government forces in the conflict.
