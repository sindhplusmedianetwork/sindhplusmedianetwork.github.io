'Apartheid automation': report details Israel's AI surveillance of Palestinians

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Anadolu report has detailed Israel's expanding use of artificial intelligence to monitor and control Palestinian land, movement and lives. The AI tool ImiSight reportedly uses satellite imagery to detect changes to Palestinian buildings and land, flagging structures for demolition; Israeli media reported it surveyed about 10,735 square kilometres in 2024 and led to about 495 enforcement proceedings. Across the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, more than 1,100 structures were demolished in 2026 by September, according to OCHA, displacing over 1,500 Palestinians.

Amnesty International's May 2023 report documented the Red Wolf facial recognition system at Hebron checkpoints and the Blue Wolf app with its Wolf Pack database. A +972 Magazine and Local Call investigation reported that the AI system Lavender marked up to 37,000 Palestinians as bombing targets in the early months of the Gaza war, with human approvals taking about 20 seconds. Microsoft disabled specified cloud-storage and AI services used by an Israeli Defence Ministry unit in September 2025 after a review found evidence of mass surveillance of civilians.
