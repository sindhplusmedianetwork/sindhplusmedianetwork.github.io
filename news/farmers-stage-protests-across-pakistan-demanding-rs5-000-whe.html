Farmers Stage Protests Across Pakistan Demanding Rs5,000 Wheat Support Price

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Small farmers across Pakistan took to the streets on Sunday, demanding that the government fix the wheat support price at Rs5,000 per maund. The demonstrations, organised by the Pakistan Kissan Rabta Committee, also called for an end to corporate land grabbing and a halt to genetically modified crops. Protesters pointed to the rising costs of diesel, electricity, fertilisers and seeds, saying crop returns had become non-viable for small growers.

The farmers opposed market deregulation backed by the IMF and the World Bank, arguing it would leave growers at the mercy of market forces. They demanded the restoration of PASSCO and a robust public procurement mechanism to ensure fair prices. The committee warned that without a guaranteed support price, the coming wheat season would push more farmers toward ruin.
