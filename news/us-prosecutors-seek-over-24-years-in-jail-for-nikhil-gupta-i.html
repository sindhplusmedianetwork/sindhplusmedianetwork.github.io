US prosecutors seek over 24 years in jail for Nikhil Gupta in alleged plot to kill Sikh leader

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US prosecutors have recommended more than 24 years in prison for Indian national Nikhil Gupta, who is accused of involvement in an alleged plot to kill Sikh leader Gurpatwant Singh Pannun in the United States. Gupta is due to be sentenced on November 20. According to the US Justice Department, Gupta was recruited in May 2023 by Vikash Yadav, an agent of India's intelligence agency RAW, to arrange the killing.

Prosecutors say RAW also offered Gupta extra money, clearance for a drug-carrying aircraft at Manipur airport and help in buying and selling weapons. During a hearing last year, it also emerged that Gupta and Yadav had planned another killing in Pakistan and Nepal, and that both men were linked to the killing of Sikh separatist leader Hardeep Singh Nijjar in Canada. Gupta pleaded guilty on February 13, 2026, to charges including conspiracy to kill the Sikh leader, murder for hire and money laundering.
