Protesters Slam Anthropic After IPO Debut, Demand Ban on Self-Improving AI

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around a hundred people braved the rain on Thursday to protest in front of Anthropic's offices in San Francisco, the same day the artificial intelligence lab went public, to denounce its race toward ever more powerful AI models. The protesters chanted against recursive self-improvement, a term the industry uses for building advanced AI models that can autonomously create newer versions of themselves, which critics worry will hasten doomsday scenarios. One protester said people will profit from a technology that even its makers admit has a chance of eliminating human life on Earth, while another described the situation as a black box.

The protesters' first demand was to ban recursive self-improvement, with researcher Jacob Coxon saying there are only a few places in the world where it is possible in 2027 and this office is one of them. Anthropic, which on Thursday pulled off the largest IPO in history, says it has not yet reached the recursive self-improvement stage. Critics questioned whether a company that talks about prioritising safety over profits can stick to its stated values under pressure from shareholders who want growth and returns.

Anthropic was founded by several former OpenAI employees who disagreed with its business decisions and vowed to always prioritise safety, and many protesters acknowledged they still use AI tools but feel the technology is advancing too quickly without sufficient safeguards.
