Court Stops Issuance of Final Appointment Notifications at Sheikh Zayed Hospital

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court, hearing a petition against alleged irregularities in recruitments to administrative posts at Sheikh Zayed Hospital, has issued an order stopping the issuance of final appointment notifications to candidates. According to Dunya News, the Lahore High Court heard the petition against alleged irregularities in the recruitment process at the administrative level in Sheikh Zayed Hospital, and Justice Shirin Imran issued a written order on the petition of Muhammad Akhtar and other petitioners. Advocate Zubair Kundi appeared in court on behalf of the petitioners, and the petition took the position that tests for recruitments to administrative posts at Sheikh Zayed Hospital were taken through CTS, however serious irregularities were committed in the test process.

According to the petitioners, CTS declared successful even those candidates who had not even submitted applications for the recruitments, and the petition adopted the position that the current procedure adopted for recruitments is not transparent. The petitioners requested the court to declare the entire recruitment process at Sheikh Zayed Hospital null and void and order fresh recruitments under a transparent procedure. The court, issuing an order on the petition, stopped Sheikh Zayed Hospital from issuing the final appointment notifications of candidates.
