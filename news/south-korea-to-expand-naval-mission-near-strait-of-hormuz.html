South Korea to Expand Naval Mission Near Strait of Hormuz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

South Korea's defence ministry said it will expand the operational area of its Cheonghae anti-piracy naval unit from the Gulf of Aden to waters south of Oman near the Strait of Hormuz. The move is intended to help keep the shipping lane open. The unit will deploy a destroyer, a P-8A maritime patrol aircraft, an explosive ordnance disposal team and unmanned mine-clearance vessels.

The unit's personnel ceiling rises by 75 to 395, and the mandate would run to 31 December 2027, subject to parliamentary approval. Seoul stressed that the mission is strictly non-combat and under independent command. The aircraft will not monitor Iranian territory, waters or the inside of the strait.

Tehran has warned Seoul against the deployment.
