PTI Voices Concern Over Imran Khan's Health, Urges Chief Justice to Enforce Court Orders on Treatment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The PTI's core committee has expressed grave concern over jailed party founder Imran Khan's health. It urged the chief justice of Pakistan to ensure court orders on his treatment are implemented. The committee met after reports that Imran's blood pressure recently surged to 192.

It called the reports extremely alarming. The committee demanded his personal physicians be given immediate access. It also demanded that all necessary medical tests be arranged without delay.

It further demanded that he be transferred to Shifa International Hospital for a complete medical evaluation.
