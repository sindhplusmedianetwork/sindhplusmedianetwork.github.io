It Was Our Mistake to Bring You From London and Put You in Power: Muhammad Aslam Ghouri

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam central information secretary Muhammad Aslam Ghouri said it was a mistake to bring someone from London and place him in the seat of power, and bringing someone from Dubai and making him president was also a mistake. Speaking on a private television programme, he said if Maulana Fazlur Rehman had not placed a shawl over Maryam Nawaz's head, perhaps Maulana's home would not have been desecrated today, adding, "Don't worry; we have now recognized you properly." He recalled that when a shawl was being removed from Maryam Nawaz's head at a hotel in Karachi, her husband was being arrested at the time. Ghouri said he knew what the other side's position was when Pervez Musharraf imposed martial law, and when their prime minister was handcuffed, put on a plane and sent out of the country, he knew how many of their people were present at Rawalpindi Airport.

He said, "Leave us alone and do not provoke us; we know a great deal." He added that every party engages in politics, but the character assassination of Maulana and his colleagues at this time is ingratitude by the other party. He said they had brought them out of jails and their homes and helped them reach the seat of power, and today they are spitting on them and making allegations against them. He said videos are still being broadcast on social media and national media showing who praised Maulana, when and from where, sometimes Maryam Nawaz, sometimes Nawaz Sharif and sometimes Shehbaz Sharif.
