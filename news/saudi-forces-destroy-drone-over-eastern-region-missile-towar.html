Saudi forces destroy drone over eastern region; missile towards Madinah intercepted

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's military has destroyed a drone over the country's eastern region. The Saudi-led coalition confirmed the interception on November 6. Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al-Maliki said the drone was destroyed over eastern Saudi Arabia.

Earlier, Saudi air defences had intercepted a ballistic missile fired by Yemen's Houthi militia. The missile had been fired towards the Madinah region.
