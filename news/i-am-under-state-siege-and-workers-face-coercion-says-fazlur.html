I am under state siege and workers face coercion, says Fazlur Rehman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman says he is currently under a state siege and that his party workers are being subjected to state coercion. Addressing lawyers of the Lahore High Court Bar through a video link, he said the whole city of Lahore was full of state barriers, with all roads closed and checkpoints set up, and that a civilised citizen was being stopped from attending a gathering of intellectuals. He said coercion never lasts, but patience always has, adding that his party advocates peace and has sacrificed lives for it, and that it has not come to seek a certificate of loyalty to the homeland from any ruler or state institution.

He said his party's loyalties to Pakistan are beyond doubt, its workers stand firm like mountains of steadfastness, and they will not bow before coercion. He said the JUI will hold a major conference in Sargodha on November 26, and that workers across the Sargodha division are facing coercion, with businesses of local leaders closed, shops sealed, and arrests and pursuit under way in Bhakkar, Mianwali, Khushab and Sargodha. He said his party is fighting for the rights of the people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Gwadar, Sindh, the coastal areas and Punjab, and that mineral resources belong to the people, not to the federation or any single powerful institution.

He said his party cannot allow the people's resources to be looted, and that it will hold on to patience while remaining firm on its stance.
