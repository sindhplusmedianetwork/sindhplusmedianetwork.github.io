Faisalabad Air Turns Hazardous as AQI Hits 600

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Authorities across Faisalabad division were directed to immediately halt the sale of uncovered construction material, with violating shops facing sealing, as the city's live Air Quality Index was recorded at 600, in the hazardous category, on Wednesday. Commissioner Dr Imran Hamid Sheikh ordered suction hoods at barbecue outlets to be double-checked, night inspection teams to monitor compliance, regular water sprinkling on roads, and a halt to crop residue burning. The Environment Protection Agency said it had conducted 812 inspections of industrial units over the past month and a half, identifying 79 violations.

The agency imposed fines of Rs6.8 million, sealed five units and registered an FIR against one. The measures come as the city struggles with dangerously polluted air, and officials said enforcement would continue to bring the air quality under control.
