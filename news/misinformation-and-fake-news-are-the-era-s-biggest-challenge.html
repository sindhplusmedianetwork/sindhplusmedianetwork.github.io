Misinformation and Fake News Are the Era's Biggest Challenge: Tarar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Information Minister Attaullah Tarar has said that misinformation and fake news are the biggest challenge of the present era. Speaking at an event in Islamabad, he stressed the need to make digital literacy a part of the school curriculum. He said labelling of AI-generated content must be made mandatory, and proposed the formation of a National Cyber Security and Social Media Awareness Council.

The minister noted that the country also faces the challenges of deepfakes and artificial intelligence, adding that continuous efforts on cyber security and social media awareness are needed. He said children must be taught how to protect themselves from the unsafe use of social media, and announced that a Digital Communication Department has been set up in the Information Ministry. He also said the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has started working and will support the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority.
