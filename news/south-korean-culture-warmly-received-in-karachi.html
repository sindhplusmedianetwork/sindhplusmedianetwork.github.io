South Korean Culture Warmly Received in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi witnessed a vibrant celebration of South Korean culture. A Korean festival at a local mall showcased traditional heritage, modern trends and entertainment. It included K-pop performances, traditional Hanbok attire, food and beauty products.

Young performers entertained audiences with energetic Korean dance routines, while visitors dressed in traditional outfits and posed for photographs. South Korean musical group nuMori also performed at the ongoing World Culture Festival in Karachi. The events reflect the growing popularity of Korean music, fashion and entertainment among Pakistani audiences.
