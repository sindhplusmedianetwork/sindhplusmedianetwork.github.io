Oil dives almost 5 percent as Trump signals restraint on Iran strikes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brent crude futures were down almost 5 percent on Monday after hitting their lowest since early September. The fall came as US President Donald Trump downplayed the likelihood of renewed strikes on Iran, though attacks continued on ships near the Strait of Hormuz. Brent was down $4.57, or 4.65 percent, at $93.75 a barrel by 1704 GMT.

The decline followed a plunge of 16 percent last week. On Sunday, Trump said he was highly unlikely to order fresh attacks on Iran as long as talks to end the conflict progressed.
