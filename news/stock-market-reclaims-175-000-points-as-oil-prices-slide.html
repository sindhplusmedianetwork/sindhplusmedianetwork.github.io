Stock market reclaims 175,000 points as oil prices slide

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Equity investors remained in a bullish frame of mind on Thursday, as expectations of a quick end to the Middle East conflict allowed the Pakistan Stock Exchange to reclaim the 175,000 milestone. The benchmark index gained 3,366.38 points, or 1.96 per cent, to close at 175,380 points, extending its bullish momentum for the fourth consecutive session. The index touched an intraday high of approximately 176,605 points before witnessing some profit-taking at higher levels.

Strong buying interest across major sectors continued to support the market, further strengthening investor confidence and market sentiment. A sharp retreat in oil prices improved investor sentiment, with WTI crude falling below $87 per barrel and Brent falling to $96 per barrel from $102, a decline of over 8 per cent. The market reclaimed the 175,000 level for the first time since September 1.

The US election results, in which Democrats made gains in the midterm elections, fuelled optimism about a potential de-escalation in the Middle East conflict, easing concerns about further geopolitical escalation and supporting a broader recovery in global risk appetite.
