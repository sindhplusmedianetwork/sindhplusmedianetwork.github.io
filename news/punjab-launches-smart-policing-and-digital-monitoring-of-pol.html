Punjab Launches Smart Policing and Digital Monitoring of Police Performance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif formally inaugurated the CM Crime Surveillance Unit, through which smart policing and digital monitoring of police officers' performance will be carried out at district, circle and police station level across the province. On the occasion, the head of the Crime Surveillance Unit, Usman Tahir, gave the chief minister a detailed briefing on the unit's affairs and system. Under the new system, the latest data on crime in every police station of Punjab will be available with one click, while the performance of police officers including DPO, DSP and SHO will also be analysed.

Data of 20 different categories of crime will be compiled in the Crime Surveillance Unit, and experts there will immediately prepare data analysis reports. Through this system, timely redressal of citizens' complaints and applications will also be monitored, and in case of delay, action can be taken against the relevant authorities. The unit will also identify posts on social media that are noteworthy in accordance with the requirements of national security.

Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif asked the IG Punjab for a police reforms plan and directed that the data of police station record management be used correctly. The chief minister expressed displeasure over complaints of unjustified delay in registering FIRs and directed an immediate response to negative propaganda about the police, saying they want to make Punjab free of all kinds of crime and the purpose of establishing the Crime Surveillance Unit is to make the campaign to end crime effective.
