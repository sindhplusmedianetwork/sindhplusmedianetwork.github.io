Drug Trafficking Network Busted, 20 Nigerians Arrested in Twin Cities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Anti-Narcotics Force has busted an international drug trafficking network that was targeting young people in the twin cities of Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Twenty Nigerian nationals were arrested during a crackdown carried out in both cities, according to an ANF spokesperson. The force recovered 4.432 kilograms of narcotics in the operation.

The recovered drugs comprised 1.682 kilograms of cocaine, 2 kilograms of methamphetamine, also known as Ice, and 750 grams of weed. Preliminary investigations revealed that two of those arrested were already on bail in a previous narcotics case. The ANF has expanded its probe to identify the facilitators, supply sources and financial supporters behind the network.
