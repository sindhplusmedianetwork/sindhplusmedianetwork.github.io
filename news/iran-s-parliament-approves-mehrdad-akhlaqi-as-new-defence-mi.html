Iran's Parliament Approves Mehrdad Akhlaqi as New Defence Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's parliament has approved Mehrdad Akhlaqi as the country's new defence minister. Akhlaqi previously headed Iran's aerospace industry and is regarded as a key figure in the country's missile and defence programmes. President Masoud Pezeshkian welcomed parliament's decision and congratulated the new minister.

Akhlaqi is under American sanctions and appears on the United States' target-killing list. He succeeds Aziz Nasirzadeh, who was killed in a US attack earlier this year. The appointment comes at a time of heightened tensions between Iran and the United States over the nuclear programme and regional security.
