PM hails alliance unity on security, sovereignty

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Friday reiterated Pakistan's strong commitment to the Makkah Alliance for Defense, as top Saudi and Turkish officials called on him in Islamabad on the sidelines of the second meeting of the Strategic Political and Defence Committee. The Saudi delegation was led by Defence Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman bin Abdulaziz Al Saud and included Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud and Chief of the General Staff General Fayyadh bin Hamed Al-Ruwaili. Turkiye was represented by Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan, Defence Minister Yasar Guler and Chief of the General Staff General Selcuk Bayraktaroglu.

The prime minister welcomed what he called the strong unity and determination of the three countries to defend their security, sovereignty and territorial integrity under the alliance agreement, and expressed satisfaction at the outcome of the committee meeting. He assured Saudi Arabia of Pakistan's full support for the kingdom's sovereignty, territorial integrity and security, and the three sides exchanged views on the latest regional developments and resolved to keep working together for lasting peace and stability. Separately, the prime minister directed the authorities to simplify loan procedures for small and medium enterprises and to treat their export data, supply-chain participation and cash flow as alternative criteria for bank financing.

Banks expressed their commitment to provide Rs2,000 billion in loans to SMEs by June 2028 and to increase SME borrowers to 775,000, including Rs886 billion in new loans for 449,000 new borrowers.
