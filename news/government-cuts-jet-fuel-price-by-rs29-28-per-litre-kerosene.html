Government cuts jet fuel price by Rs29.28 per litre; kerosene and light diesel also cheaper

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has cut the price of jet fuel by Rs29.28 per litre. Kerosene oil is cheaper by Rs24.88 per litre and light diesel oil by Rs19.65 per litre. The new price of jet fuel is Rs299.77 per litre, down from Rs329.05.

Kerosene has fallen from Rs308.52 to Rs283.64 per litre, while light diesel has dropped from Rs251.37 to Rs231.72 per litre. The price cuts follow continued reductions in petrol and high-speed diesel prices. The revised prices took effect from November 14.
