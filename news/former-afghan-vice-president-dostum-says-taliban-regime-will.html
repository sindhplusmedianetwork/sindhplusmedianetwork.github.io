Former Afghan Vice President Dostum Says Taliban Regime Will Not Last

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Afghan vice president Marshal Abdul Rashid Dostum has sharply criticised the Taliban regime, saying its so-called emirate will not last long and will ultimately come to an end. Dostum, who heads the Afghan National Movement Party, said the Afghan people will never forget the sacrifices of those who gave their lives in the struggle for freedom. He said Afghan youth are conscious and are pursuing higher education to carry the martyrs' mission forward.

Dostum added that it is the educated Afghan youth who will now decide the future of Afghanistan. His remarks come amid growing resistance against the Taliban regime in Afghanistan.
