Russia ready to send nuclear-armed satellite into space

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read

NATO member states' intelligence agencies have warned that Russia is preparing to send a nuclear-armed satellite into space next month. According to German media reports, Germany's federal intelligence service (BND) and armed forces, along with the intelligence agencies of other Western countries, have shared information suggesting that Russia began work on the satellite, named 'Sputnik S', several years ago. The satellite is said to be complete and is reportedly kept in an area near Moscow.

Intelligence reports indicate that the satellite is now ready for launch. The warning comes from intelligence agencies of NATO member countries, which have exchanged information about the programme. No official confirmation of the launch plan has been given by Russia in the reports.
