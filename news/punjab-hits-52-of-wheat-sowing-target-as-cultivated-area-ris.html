Punjab Hits 52% of Wheat Sowing Target as Cultivated Area Rises 7.7%

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab has achieved 52 percent of its wheat sowing target, with the area under wheat cultivation up 7.7 percent compared with the same point last year, agriculture officials told a provincial review meeting. Certified seeds have so far covered 44 percent of the cultivated land, officials said. Agricultural loans worth Rs100 billion have also been approved for one million farmers under the Kisan Card scheme.

The figures were shared at the review meeting, where officials assessed progress in the province's wheat cultivation. The increase in sown area and the wider use of certified seed point to a stronger wheat season in Punjab.
