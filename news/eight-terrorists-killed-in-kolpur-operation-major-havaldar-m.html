Eight terrorists killed in Kolpur operation; major, havaldar martyred

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces have killed eight terrorists, including a suicide bomber, in intelligence-based operations around Kolpur, Kardagap and Nowshera in Balochistan, the military's media wing said. A large cache of arms and ammunition was recovered during the operations, according to the Inter-Services Public Relations. Major Umar Minhas Nasir and Havaldar Muhammad Adnan Khan were martyred when terrorists attacked a military patrol vehicle with armed quadcopters on the Quetta-Taftan highway on November 14.

The prime minister paid tribute to the fallen soldiers and said operations would continue until foreign-backed terrorism was eliminated. The ISPR said the operations were carried out on intelligence reports about the presence of terrorists in the area. The attack on the military patrol vehicle took place on the Quetta-Taftan highway.
