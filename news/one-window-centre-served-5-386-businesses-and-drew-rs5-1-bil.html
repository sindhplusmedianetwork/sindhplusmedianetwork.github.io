One-Window Centre Served 5,386 Businesses and Drew Rs5.1 Billion, Minister Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Board of Investment Qaiser Ahmed Sheikh told the World Investment Conference in Geneva that a one-window Business Facilitation Centre in Islamabad served 5,386 businesses, processed 4,555 approvals and no-objection certificates, facilitated investment worth Rs5.08 billion and supported 15,680 jobs during its first eight months, with an investor satisfaction rating of 9.8 out of 10. The centre brings 22 federal government agencies under one roof through a one-window mechanism, he said. He said more than 500 regulatory reforms had been undertaken across about 30 sectors under the Pakistan Regulatory Modernisation Initiative and the Asaan Karobar Act 2025.
