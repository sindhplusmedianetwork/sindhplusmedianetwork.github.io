Mir Raza Was Murdered, Not a Suicide: Judicial Commission's Report Released

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has released the judicial commission's report on the death of businessman Mir Raza Ali, declaring it a murder and not a suicide. The commission, headed by Sindh High Court's Justice Umar Sial, found that the final post-mortem identified the cause of death as a gunshot wound inflicted from behind. Acid marks were found on the body, and an anaesthetic drug was detected in his blood.

The report slammed serious police negligence during the first 10 days of the investigation, covering evidence collection, forensics, CCTV footage and the medical board. It recommended forming a joint investigation team under DIG Irfan Baloch, including officers from the ISI, Intelligence Bureau, Rangers and the cyber-crime agency. The commission further recommended an FIA inquiry against negligent officials, the suspension of three officers, and a public apology by the police.
