KSE-100 climbs 1,116 points on Wall Street rally

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange carried its bullish momentum into Tuesday as the benchmark KSE-100 Index climbed 1,116.77 points, or 0.63 percent, to 178,590.62 by 1:19pm. The market drew confidence from a powerful rally on Wall Street and improving sentiment across Asian markets. The market opened strongly, with the index rising 1,490.50 points within minutes of the opening bell.

Brent crude fell to $89.10 per barrel, offering additional support to the market. The gains continued the positive trend seen in recent sessions. Investors took encouragement from the strong global cues as trading progressed through the day.
