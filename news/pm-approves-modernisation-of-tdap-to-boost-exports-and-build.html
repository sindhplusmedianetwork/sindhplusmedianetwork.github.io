PM approves modernisation of TDAP to boost exports and build farm export value chain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has approved modernising the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan on modern lines to boost exports. The aim is to improve Pakistani products' access to global markets. At a review meeting in Islamabad, he directed that a complete ecosystem and value chain for agricultural exports be built.

He directed that TDAP's scope be limited to eight core functions. He directed that the reforms be completed and enforced by the middle of next year. The approval also aims to align local industries, including small and medium enterprises, with export standards.
