ICC Gets Over 8.3 Million Ticket Applications for Pakistan-India World Cup 2027 Clash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Cricket Council has received a record number of ticket applications for the ICC Cricket World Cup 2027. According to the ICC, around 3 million fans from 193 countries registered for tickets, while total applications crossed 120 million. The Pakistan-India match alone attracted more than 8.3 million ticket applications, and the final has drawn nearly 7.9 million requests.

The World Cup will be jointly hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia. The tournament is scheduled to run from October 2 to November 21, 2027.
