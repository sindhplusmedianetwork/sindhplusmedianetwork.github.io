Apple's first foldable iPhone Duo lands in Pakistan with prices up to nearly Rs1.48 million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apple's first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, has landed in Pakistan with a price tag reaching nearly Rs1.5 million, putting a single smartphone in the same price range as some used cars. Pakistani retailers are advertising the device after its international release on October 23, 2026, with the top 2TB model listed at Rs1,479,599 on the market and Rs1,390,000 through an authorised distributor, a gap of nearly Rs100,000. The 256GB model is listed at Rs999,599 on the market and Rs899,999 through the authorised distributor, while the 512GB model costs Rs1,079,599 and Rs980,000 respectively, and the 1TB model costs Rs1,239,599 and Rs1,145,000.

Supply in Pakistan appears limited, with several sellers advertising restricted availability and some listings showing sold-out stock, while certain retailers are taking orders for deliveries expected in the final week of November. Even the entry-level model, at Rs899,999 through the authorised distributor, remains beyond the budget of most consumers, and the most expensive advertised model sits just Rs20,401 short of Rs1.5 million. The handset combines a 5.4-inch outer display with a larger 7.6-inch inner screen, runs on Apple's A20 Pro chip with iOS 27.1, and carries a 48-megapixel main camera alongside a 48-megapixel ultrawide lens.

It uses a side-mounted Touch ID sensor instead of Face ID, weighs about 254 grams, and comes with IP68-rated water resistance. Apple unveiled the foldable on September 9, 2026, opened international pre-orders on October 16, and a leading dealer announced its arrival in Pakistan offering PTA-approved units through the authorised distribution channel.
