Christiane Amanpour to Leave CNN After 43 Years as Ellison Era Begins

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

CNN chief international anchor Christiane Amanpour will leave the network next year after 43 years, according to an internal memo seen by Reuters on Monday. Her daily show ends this year, and she will host 'Amanpour' weekly through June 2027. The departure follows Skydance's $110 billion takeover of Warner Bros Discovery, putting CNN and CBS News under CEO David Ellison.

Amanpour has been one of the most recognised faces in international broadcast journalism, known for reporting from conflict zones and interviewing world leaders over four decades. Her exit marks the end of an era at the network as new ownership reshapes its direction.
