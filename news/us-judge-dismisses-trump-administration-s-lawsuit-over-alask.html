US Judge Dismisses Trump Administration's Lawsuit Over Alaska Senate Election

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A federal judge in the United States has thrown out a Trump administration lawsuit over Alaska's Senate election. US District Judge Sharon Gleason issued the ruling in Anchorage on Sunday. The administration had sought to block Alaska from certifying a midterm loss by Republican Senator Dan Sullivan if votes for a same-name rival could have altered the outcome.

The judge's decision was the latest setback for Trump's efforts to expand federal control over state-run elections. Under the US Constitution, the conduct of elections is left to the states. The Alaska race pitted Democrat Mary Peltola against Sullivan.

The outcome could determine whether Republicans retain control of the Senate.
