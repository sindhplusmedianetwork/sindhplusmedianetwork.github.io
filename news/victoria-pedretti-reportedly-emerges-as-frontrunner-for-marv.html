Victoria Pedretti Reportedly Emerges as Frontrunner for Marvel Mystique Role

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Marvel Studios' upcoming X-Men reboot could have a new contender for the role of Mystique, with The Haunting of Hill House actress Victoria Pedretti reportedly emerging as a frontrunner amid earlier speculation surrounding Hunter Schafer. According to recent reports, Pedretti is being considered to portray Raven Darkholme, better known as Mystique, in the Marvel Cinematic Universe's upcoming mutant-focused film. Entertainment scooper James Mack claimed that the actress is Marvel's preferred choice, although the studio has not confirmed the casting report.

The development comes after Schafer, known for playing Jules Vaughn in HBO's Euphoria, was previously linked to the character, with earlier reports suggesting that the actress was in serious discussions for the role, but no official agreement was announced. Pedretti is best known for her performances in Netflix's The Haunting of Hill House, The Haunting of Bly Manor and You, and she also appeared in Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. Mystique is a shape-shifting mutant known for her ability to assume other people's appearances, and the character has previously been portrayed by Rebecca Romijn in the original X-Men films and Jennifer Lawrence in the franchise's prequel instalments.

The upcoming reboot is being directed by Jake Schreier and already features several notable actors, including Sadie Sink, Kit Connor, Samara Weaving, Maya Boyd and Christopher Abbott, while the untitled X-Men film is currently scheduled to arrive in cinemas on May 5, 2028.
