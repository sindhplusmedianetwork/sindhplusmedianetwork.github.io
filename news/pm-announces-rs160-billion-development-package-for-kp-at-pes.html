PM Announces Rs160 Billion Development Package for KP at Peshawar Apex Committee

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has announced a development package of Rs160 billion for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The announcement was made during a meeting of the federal apex committee at the Governor House in Peshawar. The prime minister said the federal government would work together with the military leadership to resolve the province's problems.

Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir attended the session. The meeting reiterated the resolve to completely eliminate terrorism from the province. Officials said the gathering reviewed the security situation and development priorities in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
