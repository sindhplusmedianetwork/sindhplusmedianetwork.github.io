Microsoft's AI Diagnoses 'Sequentially' Like a Doctor, Twice as Accurately

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read

Microsoft researchers have developed an AI tool that diagnoses patients step by step, in the way doctors do. According to a study published on Thursday in the journal Science, the tool was more than twice as accurate as general physicians at solving complex medical cases. The model uses an 'orchestrator' that coordinates how the AI tackles a problem, employing a simulated panel of clinical experts collaborating to reach a diagnosis.

Unlike existing medical AI models that examine all symptoms and test results at once, the new system first decides what to ask about the patient's history and what matters in the physical exam, then orders tests, considers the results and updates its working hypotheses. In tests on more than 300 highly unusual medical dilemmas, the orchestrator model outperformed general physicians using textbooks and the internet, and it also did better on emergency-room admission scenarios. The researchers found that diagnostic work-ups designed by the orchestrator also cost less than those by human doctors.

Dr Dominic King, corporate vice president of Microsoft AI's health unit, said real-world diagnosis never comes with a neat summary. The team will next test the tool's ability to triage patients in emergency department waiting rooms in collaboration with Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Centre in Boston.
