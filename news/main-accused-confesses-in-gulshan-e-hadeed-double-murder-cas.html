Main accused confesses in Gulshan-e-Hadeed double murder case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The arrested main accused in the November 5 Gulshan-e-Hadeed Phase Two case has confessed during interrogation. In the incident, 45-year-old Samina and her 16-year-old daughter Bakhtawar were shot dead after entering their home. The accused said he had befriended the girl on Snapchat about three years ago.

He stated that a dispute over unkept promises had been going on for two months. He further said he bought the pistol used in the crime for Rs20,000. He added that he had planned the murder a week earlier.
