Magnitude 4.5 earthquake jolts Swat and surrounding areas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tremors were felt in Swat and its surrounding areas on Wednesday evening, with the seismological centre recording the earthquake at a magnitude of 4.5. According to the centre, the quake struck at a depth of 231 kilometres below the earth's surface, with its epicentre located in the Hindu Kush region. Residents, frightened by the shaking, came out of their homes as the tremors were felt across the area.

The seismological centre confirmed the depth and epicentre after recording the event. No further details about the tremors were given in the initial report, which focused on the magnitude, depth and location of the earthquake.
