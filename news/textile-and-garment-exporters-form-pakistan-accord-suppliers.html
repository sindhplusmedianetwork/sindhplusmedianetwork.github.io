Textile and garment exporters form Pakistan Accord Suppliers Advisory Board

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 450 medium and small textile and garment exporters have formed the first Suppliers Advisory Board under the Pakistan Accord to strengthen the competitiveness of the country's exports. Aamir Riaz Chhotani has been elected the board's first chairman, while Habib Anwar has been elected co-chairman. The board includes around 450 exporters from Karachi, Lahore and Faisalabad.

The Pakistan Accord programme has 145 international brands as signatories. The formation of the board gives the country's small and medium exporters a shared platform within the Accord, through which they aim to work together on improving the standing of Pakistani textiles and garments in international markets.
