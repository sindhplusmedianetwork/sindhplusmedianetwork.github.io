Shah Rukh Khan among world's 100 greatest film stars, only Indian actor on list

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood superstar Shah Rukh Khan has been included in US magazine Variety's list of the world's 100 greatest film stars. He was placed at number 92 on the list. He is the only Indian actor selected in the list.

The magazine called the 61-year-old a giant star of Bollywood cinema. His upcoming action film King is scheduled for release in cinemas on 24 December 2026. The film is among his most awaited upcoming releases.
