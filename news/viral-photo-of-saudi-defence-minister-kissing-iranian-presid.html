Viral photo of Saudi defence minister kissing Iranian president's hand turns out to be fake

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A viral photo claiming to show Saudi Defence Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman kissing Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian's hand during a meeting in Tehran has turned out to be fake. The image began circulating on Facebook and X on October 26, with posts claiming it was taken during the Saudi minister's recent visit to Tehran. A fact-check found unusual alterations around the hands, fingers and faces in the picture, and tools that detect artificial intelligence indicated the image had been generated or altered using AI.

A reverse image search traced the original photo to July 2026, when it showed Saudi Deputy Foreign Minister Waleed Al-Khuraiji shaking hands with the Iranian president. The original image contains neither the Saudi defence minister nor any scene of a hand being kissed. The fact-check concluded that the claim attached to the viral image is false.
