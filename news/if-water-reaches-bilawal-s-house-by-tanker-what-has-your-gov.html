If Water Reaches Bilawal's House by Tanker, What Has Your Government Done in 18 Years, Asks Hafiz Naeem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman has said Karachi should receive 76 percent of the province's revenue but gets only 19 percent. He asked that if water reaches Bilawal Bhutto's house by tanker, the PPP should explain what its government has done in Sindh for 18 years. Hafiz Naeem was presenting a three-year performance report of the JI's nine town chairmen in Karachi.

He said the towns had revived 234 neglected parks and rebuilt 73 government schools. The towns also built 42 playgrounds and restored 29 dispensaries, he added. The performance report was presented at a ceremony held at a local hotel in Karachi.
