India Steps Up Push for Fifth-Generation Fighter Jets as China-Pakistan Air Power Grows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India has stepped up its efforts to acquire fifth-generation fighter jets as concern grows over China's advances in stealth technology and air combat capabilities. Among the options under consideration is the Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft programme, India's plan to build its first indigenously developed stealth fighter. The programme would give India a home-grown stealth aircraft as its neighbours strengthen their air forces.

The report examines how India hopes to close the air-power gap with China, which has made rapid progress in stealth technology. Pakistan is also strengthening its air power and is pursuing the Chinese J-35 stealth fighter. India's search for a fifth-generation jet reflects the changing balance of air power in the region.
