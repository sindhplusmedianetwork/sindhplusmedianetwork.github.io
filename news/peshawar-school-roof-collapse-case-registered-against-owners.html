Peshawar School Roof Collapse: Case Registered Against Owners, Contractor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A criminal case has been registered over Thursday's deadly school roof collapse in Peshawar's Dera Colony, with the FIR lodged at the Agha Mir Jani Shah police station naming the school's owners, shareholders and the contractor. According to the FIR text reported by Dunya News, 30 children and a woman teacher were killed and 52 people injured when the roofs caved in. The roofs of the nursery class on the second floor and rooms on the ground floor collapsed, causing the heavy loss of life.

The FIR blames the use of substandard construction material and describes the building as dilapidated and unfit. It states that complaints about the crumbling walls and roofs had been made earlier — including by the children themselves — but the school administration and owners allegedly paid no heed. The case notes that the 16-marla plot, owned by Waheed and Javed, was leased in 2024, after which the school building was constructed on it.

Police have registered the case under culpable-homicide provisions, sections 322, 148 and 149 of the Pakistan Penal Code, and say arrests of the nominated accused are underway.
