Four Injured as Land Dispute Triggers Clash Near Shikarpur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four people were injured in a violent clash between two rival groups in Noor Muhammad Kalhoro village near Rahimabad, Shikarpur, on Tuesday. The confrontation broke out between members of the Pahor and Kalhoro communities over an unresolved dispute concerning a piece of land. What began as a disagreement over ownership escalated quickly into a physical fight, leaving four people hurt.

All of the injured were taken to a nearby hospital, where they received medical treatment. Police and local authorities are expected to look into the circumstances that led to the violence. Land disputes of this kind have repeatedly triggered clashes in rural Sindh.
