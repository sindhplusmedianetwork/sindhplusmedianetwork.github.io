Sri Lankan Delegation Visits Taxila Museum to Explore Gandhara Heritage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A high-level delegation from Sri Lanka visited the Taxila Museum to explore the region's Buddhist heritage and the Gandhara civilisation. Deputy Director SRO Taxila Muhammad Asim Dogar and Assistant Curator of the Taxila Museum Nauman Akhtar welcomed the visitors and briefed them on the historical, religious and cultural heritage of Gandhara. The delegation included Sri Lanka's Director General of Archaeology along with professors from various universities.

During the visit, the delegates performed religious rituals before the sacred Buddha Tooth Relic and viewed Gandhara art artefacts preserved at the museum, including Buddhist sculptures, stone carvings and remains of ancient civilisations. They were also briefed on the historical significance of Taxila, the spread of Buddhism in the ancient Gandhara civilisation, and the region's academic and cultural role. The delegation members appreciated the efforts of Director General of Archaeology Punjab Zaheer Abbas Malik and the Punjab government, calling the initiatives to highlight Gandhara civilisation and Buddhist historical heritage at the international level commendable.

They expressed satisfaction with the arrangements for their reception and briefing, describing the visit as a positive step towards promoting cultural and historical ties between Pakistan and Sri Lanka.
