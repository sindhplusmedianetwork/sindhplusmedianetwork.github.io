Seven-year-old girl abducted in Sargodha, Islamabad police told by phone call

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A seven-year-old girl has been abducted in Sargodha, and the incident was reported to Islamabad police through a phone call. According to Islamabad police, a call was received about the alleged abduction of a girl within the limits of Sabzi Mandi police station in Sector G-14. The caller said the girl had been abducted from Tala Chowk to the G-14 double road, adding that three men in a car had stopped the girl's father on the pretext of asking for directions and then forced the girl into the vehicle and fled.

However, the police said investigation showed the incident had taken place in Sargodha, not within the limits of Sabzi Mandi police station. The girl's father has recorded his statement, saying the incident did not happen in Islamabad and that he had gone to Sargodha with his daughter, where she was abducted. He also said he was embarrassed about making the false call.
