Tarbela Dam Tunnel 5 Closed After Fault in Extension Project Intake

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Movement through Tunnel 5 of the Tarbela Dam has been closed as a precaution after a fault developed in the intake of the dam's extension project, officials said on Thursday. Work is under way to plug a hole in the intake, with heavy water pumps being used to remove water from the powerhouse. According to WAPDA sources, a decision has been taken to install a heavy gate in front of Tunnel 5 to stop the outflow of water, and movement through the tunnel has been closed as a safety measure.

Sources said work is continuing on an emergency basis to ensure safety at the dam. The fault appeared in the intake of the fifth extension project of the Tarbela Dam, and the closure will remain in place while the repair work is completed.
