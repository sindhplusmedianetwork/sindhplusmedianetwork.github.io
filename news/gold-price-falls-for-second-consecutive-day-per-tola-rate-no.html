Gold price falls for second consecutive day, per tola rate now Rs427,436

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The price of gold in Pakistan fell for the second consecutive day, according to the All Pakistan Gems and Jewellers Association. The price of 24-carat gold fell by Rs3,400 per tola to Rs427,436, after it had previously been reported at Rs430,836 per tola. The price of 10 grams of 24-carat gold also fell by Rs2,915 to Rs366,457, from the previously reported rate of Rs369,372.

In the international market, gold fell by $34 per ounce to $4,049, from the earlier reported rate of $4,083 per ounce. Silver prices, on the other hand, rose in the country. The price of silver per tola increased by Rs45 to Rs5,768, from the previously reported rate of Rs5,723 per tola.

Gold had fallen by Rs1,500 per tola the previous day, taking the total decline over two days to Rs4,900 per tola. The latest rates were reported in Karachi on 11 November 2026.
