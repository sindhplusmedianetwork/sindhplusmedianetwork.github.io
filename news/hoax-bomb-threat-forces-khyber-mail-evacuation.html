Hoax bomb threat forces Khyber Mail evacuation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A false bomb threat aboard the Khyber Mail prompted authorities to halt the train at Khanpur Railway Station in Rahim Yar Khan on Thursday. All passengers were evacuated from the train after the threat was received. Security agencies, police and rescue workers reached the station to deal with the situation.

The bomb disposal squad also arrived and inspected all the coaches. After a thorough search, no explosive material was found anywhere in the train. The threat turned out to be a hoax.
