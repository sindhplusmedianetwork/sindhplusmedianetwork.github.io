UN Warns Gaza's Humanitarian Situation Keeps Getting Worse

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) has said the humanitarian situation in Gaza continues to deteriorate. Thousands of Palestinian families lost shelters and belongings as rain and strong winds lashed Gaza from Friday night, flooding displacement sites and disrupting services across all five governorates. An infant reportedly drowned, and five Gazans, including three children, were killed when a sand dune collapsed on their tents.

UN Deputy Special Coordinator Ramiz Alakbarov said most Gazans live in worn-out tents. He called for the urgent lifting of restrictions blocking aid, tents, tarpaulins, blankets and winter clothing. The UN warning underlines the worsening conditions facing displaced families as winter approaches.
