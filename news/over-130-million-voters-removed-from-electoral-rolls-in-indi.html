Over 130 Million Voters Removed From Electoral Rolls in India, Says Report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A report says more than 130 million voters were unlawfully removed from electoral rolls in India under the guise of revision. The report describes it as the worst electoral rigging in the country's history, with Muslims, India's largest minority, the biggest target. In West Bengal, about 9 million voters were removed before the state elections, 70 percent of them Muslims, and the report says the BJP captured control of the state for the first time through electoral engineering.

The report cites the case of Shahida Faqir of Mumbai, who was allegedly pushed into Bangladesh despite holding Indian identity documents. According to the report, courts and tribunals are moving slowly on appeals against the revision. The findings have raised serious questions about the fairness of the electoral process in the country.
