Faisal Vawda Warns of More Political Upheaval in KP, Gives Setup 180 Days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senator Faisal Vawda has warned that the political situation in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is far from over, hinting that more developments could follow. Speaking at a news conference, he said, “Part one has come; part two and part three will also follow,” adding that a complete reboot was under consideration to present Pakistan's story to the younger generation. Vawda said the people of KP would receive relief within 180 days, arguing the public would back whoever delivered results.

He expressed hope that the KP governor would perform well but cautioned that another person could take his place if expectations were not met. The senator also claimed the PTI had links with terrorists, alleging this led to the removal of the party's provincial government and the imposition of governor's rule. The report noted these allegations were presented as Vawda's claims and had not been independently verified.

He reiterated that a possible reboot was being weighed as a way to explain Pakistan's narrative to the youth, without giving further details.
