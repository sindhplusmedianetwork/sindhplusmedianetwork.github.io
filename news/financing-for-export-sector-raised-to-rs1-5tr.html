Financing for export sector raised to Rs1.5tr

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has raised financing under the Export Finance Scheme to Rs1.5 trillion from Rs1 trillion, with Rs300 billion earmarked for small and medium enterprises and new exporters, as it seeks to drive export-led growth. The revised schemes, which took effect on November 1, were reviewed at a meeting held at the Ministry of Finance on Monday, chaired by Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb and attended by several federal ministers and secretaries. The meeting assessed progress on the SME financing initiative, under which eligible small businesses can now apply for funding through the State Bank of Pakistan's SME Connect Portal.

To support SMEs, the government has also increased the collateral-free financing limit from Rs10 million to Rs50 million. Outstanding SME financing rose from Rs691 billion in June 2025 to Rs1.114 trillion in June 2026 and reached Rs1.148 trillion by October. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has set a target of Rs2.2 trillion by 2028 for SMEs, while the State Bank aims for Rs1.5 trillion by June 2027 and the finance ministry for Rs2 trillion by June 2028.

The government also aims to increase SME borrowers from about 350,862 in September to 775,000 by June 2028.
