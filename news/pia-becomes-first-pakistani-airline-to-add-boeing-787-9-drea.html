PIA becomes first Pakistani airline to add Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner as new jet lands in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan International Airlines' first Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner, acquired on lease last month, arrived in Islamabad from London as flight PK786 on Sunday. A PIA spokesperson said the carrier was the first Pakistani airline to add a Boeing 787-9 to its fleet. PIA Chief Executive Aamir Hayat, accompanied by his team, welcomed the Dreamliner at Islamabad airport, according to a statement.

The aircraft is equipped with modern technology, including in-flight entertainment, and features a spacious and comfortable cabin. The spokesperson said the inclusion of the Dreamliner was part of a strategy to modernise PIA's fleet and strengthen its international network, with the objective of providing international-standard travel facilities to passengers. Last month, PIA signed an ACMI lease agreement with Norse Atlantic Airways to acquire two state-of-the-art Boeing 787-9 Dreamliners, one of which arrived on Sunday.

The aircraft will operate direct long-haul flights from Pakistan to the United Kingdom and support PIA operations between different points in the UK and Pakistan. PIA was privatised in December last year, when a consortium led by Arif Habib Corporation won the auction for 75 per cent of the airline's shares with a bid of Rs135 billion.
