CAS declares Senegal winners of Africa Cup of Nations after appeal upheld

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Court of Arbitration for Sport has declared Senegal the winners of the Africa Cup of Nations 2025. The court upheld Senegal's appeal and annulled the decision of the African Football Confederation's appeal board, which had declared Morocco the winners. Senegal had beaten Morocco 1-0 in the final of the tournament.

In its ruling, the court held that the authority to restart or end a match rested only with the referee. The decision restores Senegal as the champions of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.
