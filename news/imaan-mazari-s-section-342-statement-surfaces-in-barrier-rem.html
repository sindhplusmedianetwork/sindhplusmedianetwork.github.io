Imaan Mazari's Section 342 Statement Surfaces in Barrier-Removal Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lawyer Imaan Mazari's statement recorded under Section 342 of the criminal procedure code has come to light in the case over removing a barrier and interfering with official work. Mazari said she was submitting the entire statement under protest, adding that the cricket team had never used that route and police themselves had said the team had already passed through another route. She said police had no magistrate's order for the temporary road closure, and that she and others had waited 15 minutes before asking for the road to be opened so they could reach the anti-terrorism court as lawyers.

Mazari said the prosecution had deliberately hidden the original transcript of the video, which she said would expose its false claims, and alleged that a plainclothes policeman named Ibrahim threw an iron barrier at her, injuring her. She said her husband acted only to protect her from assault, that a medical report from PIMS hospital had been submitted to the court, and that there was no evidence on record of the policeman being injured. Mazari further said all the witnesses were policemen with no independent witness included, called the cases retaliation for her human rights work, and said she and her husband were innocent.
