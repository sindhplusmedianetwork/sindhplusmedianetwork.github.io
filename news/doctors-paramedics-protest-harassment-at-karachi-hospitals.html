Doctors, Paramedics Protest 'Harassment' at Karachi Hospitals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Doctors and paramedical staff held protest demonstrations at Karachi's Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre and the National Institute of Child Health in response to a nationwide protest call by the Pakistan Medical Association, the Joint Action Committee and other medical bodies. The protesters demanded safeguards for their professional rights, dignity and security in the workplace. They pointed to what they described as harassment of medical professionals, the registration of FIRs against them without justification, and health policies enforced without consulting stakeholders.

The demonstrators also rejected the Federal Board of Revenue's policy of making the Point of Sale invoicing system compulsory for private clinics and healthcare facilities. The protest remained peaceful, according to the organisers.
