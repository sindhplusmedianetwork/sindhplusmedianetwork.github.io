Kuwait Sentences Three to Death Over Plot to Kill Deputy PM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kuwait's Criminal Court on Monday sentenced three men to death and eight others to five years in prison with hard labour in a case involving espionage for Iran and a plot to assassinate the country's First Deputy Prime Minister and Interior Minister. The three men sentenced to death were identified as Hussein Abdullah Al-Matouq, Hassan Abdulhadi Hajieh and Abdulreza Haidar Dehqani. They were convicted of harming Kuwait's national interests, spying for Iran, plotting to assassinate First Deputy Prime Minister and Interior Minister Sheikh Fahad Yousef Saud Al Sabah, and joining the Lebanese group Hezbollah.

The verdict was issued by the court's State Security Circuit, presided over by Judge Nasser Al-Badr. According to case details, defendants four through ten met to plan the assassination and divided responsibilities among themselves, while the eleventh defendant was tasked with providing the equipment needed for the operation. The defendants were also accused of attempting to use explosives in an operation intended to spread fear in Kuwait, and the eleventh defendant was charged with attempting to bring explosives into the country.

The court said the plot sought to undermine Kuwait's security, spread unrest and erode public confidence in the authorities' ability to protect lives and property, adding that national security is a right belonging to society as a whole. The verdict is not final and remains subject to appeal.
