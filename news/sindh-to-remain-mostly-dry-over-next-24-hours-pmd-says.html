Sindh to Remain Mostly Dry Over Next 24 Hours, PMD Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Meteorological Department on Friday forecast dry weather in most districts of Sindh province during the next 24 hours. According to the forecast, cold conditions are predicted in upper districts of the province during the morning and night. Mainly dry weather is likely to prevail across Sindh during the forecast period.

The department issued the forecast from Karachi on Friday, covering the next 24 hours for most districts of the province. No rain has been forecast for Sindh in this outlook, with dry conditions expected to continue in most areas while upper districts experience cold during morning and night hours.
