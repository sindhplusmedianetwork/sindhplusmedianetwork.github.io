UMT announces Rs50 million scholarships for KP Police personnel, martyrs' children

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The University of Management and Technology Lahore allocated Rs50 million in scholarships for higher education of serving Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police officers and personnel and children of police martyrs. Eligible candidates will be offered a 90 percent tuition fee discount. UMT will also provide free digital skills training to 1,000 serving officers and martyrs' children through its digital learning platform LiveX.

An MoU was signed at the Central Police Office Peshawar. DIG Welfare Sher Akbar Khan signed for KP Police. Director Syed Bilal and Senior Advisor Professor Sajjad Qamar signed for the university.

Selection will be on transparency and merit.
