Leaders urge interfaith harmony and tolerance

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif have called for greater religious tolerance, interfaith harmony and protection of minority rights. The two leaders issued separate messages ahead of the International Day for Tolerance, observed on 16 November. Both stressed equal citizenship and peaceful coexistence for all Pakistanis.

President Zardari quoted Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah's address of 11 August 1947 and Article 25(1) of the Constitution, which guarantees equality before the law. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said the teachings of Islam provide an enduring guide for patience, tolerance and respect for humanity. The messages said tolerance and harmony among faiths are essential for national unity and progress.
