Australia Face Selection Headaches After 'Shambolic' South Africa Defeat

Australia captain Pat Cummins admitted it was difficult to paper over the cracks after his team suffered an innings-and-31-run defeat to South Africa at St George's Park, Gqeberha on Thursday. The heavy loss left the three-Test series level at 1-1. Cummins described the performance as shambolic and conceded that serious questions needed to be answered.

Doubts hang over at least three players ahead of the decider in Cape Town next week. Marnus Labuschagne has gone three years without a Test century, while opener Nic Maddinson and all-rounder Beau Webster are also under pressure. Australia were bowled out for just 99 in the first innings of the match.
