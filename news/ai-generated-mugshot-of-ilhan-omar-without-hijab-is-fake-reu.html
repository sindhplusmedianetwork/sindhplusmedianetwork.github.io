AI-Generated 'Mugshot' of Ilhan Omar Without Hijab Is Fake, Reuters Fact Check Finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A viral image purporting to show a mugshot of US Congresswoman Ilhan Omar without her hijab is altered and was likely created with artificial intelligence, according to a Reuters fact check. The image was shared online after Homeland Security Investigations agents raided Omar's Washington, DC residence on October 12 as part of an ongoing Justice Department investigation related to alleged immigration fraud. The altered image depicts Omar with a balding head and was presented as an authentic mugshot taken after the raid.

Reuters found it was created by combining an old Associated Press photograph of Omar — in which she is seen wearing the same earrings as in the altered image — with a mugshot of another man published in 2019 and credited to a police force in Wales. There are no credible reports of Omar being detained after the raid; a spokesperson in her Congressional office told Reuters in an email, 'The Congresswoman was not detained,' a claim also confirmed by a source close to the investigation. An analysis using OpenAI's verification tool, which detects invisible watermarks embedded in generated media, determined that the image was likely edited or created using the company's AI models.

Reuters noted it had previously addressed the same altered image when it was shared in 2020.
