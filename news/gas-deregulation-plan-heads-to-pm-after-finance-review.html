Gas Deregulation Plan Heads to PM After Finance Review

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government is weighing a phased deregulation of the gas sector, with a proposed restructuring roadmap to be presented to the prime minister after a high-level review chaired by Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb. According to a Finance Ministry statement issued on Monday, the framework aims to build a financially sustainable, efficient and competitive gas market that supports businesses, encourages private investment and protects consumer welfare. Participants reviewed deep sectoral challenges including circular debt, cross-subsidies, unaccounted-for-gas losses and supply-demand imbalances.

A sustainable resolution of legacy circular debt and a workable approach to cross-subsidies were identified as central to the viability of the restructured entities, with the ministry noting that legacy liabilities had long weighed on the sector's finances and the government's fiscal position. Expected outcomes from the reform include stronger energy security, more private-sector investment, reduced fiscal and sovereign risks, greater business-to-business transactions, more stable gas pricing and better-priced gas for exporters. The framework draws on international experience, including gas-sector reforms in Turkiye and the United Kingdom, while accounting for Pakistan's own institutional structure and consumer interests.

Officials stressed careful sequencing so that supply stays secure, regulated consumers remain protected and private investors get a predictable environment.
