Pochettino backs Manchester United players after Liverpool thrashing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mauricio Pochettino said Manchester United's players showed fight and commitment despite a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool at Anfield in his first game in charge. United, 19th with one win from 11 games, played more than 80 minutes with 10 men after Leny Yoro's eighth-minute red card, with Liverpool goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz. Pochettino rejected relegation suggestions and said the dismissal fundamentally altered the contest.

He questioned whether Alexander Isak had the ball under control when Yoro brought him down. The defeat was the joint heaviest for a United manager in his first match, equalling Lal Hilditch's 4-0 loss to Bolton Wanderers in 1926.
