UN Rights Office: Israeli Actions Squeezing Palestinians Into Ever-Smaller Gaza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Nations human rights office has warned that Israeli forces are squeezing Palestinians into an ever-shrinking area of Gaza while destroying civilian infrastructure and striking camps for displaced people, raising fears of ethnic cleansing. The Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR) said Israel had announced it would expand its troop deployment to 70 percent of Gaza's territory, triggering fresh displacement near the Israeli-drawn 'yellow line'. The office reported that over the past two weeks, Israeli forces expanded their deployment zones westward in Gaza City's Shejaiya district and central Gaza, with at least two reported attacks on displaced-person camps in Shejaiya and the use of explosive-laden robots to destroy the few remaining buildings.

On October 25, an Israeli drone dropped explosives on the tents of displaced families near the Shejaiya junction, sparking a fire that burned for five hours and displaced 200 families — 800 people, mostly women, children and the elderly. OHCHR's head for the occupied Palestinian territory, Ajith Sunghay, said the prospects of return for the displaced were close to zero because most of their areas lay beyond the yellow line and were inaccessible, and he stressed that forced displacement could amount to war crimes and crimes against humanity. He noted that almost Gaza's entire population had been displaced multiple times, buildings and urban infrastructure had been damaged or destroyed, and Israeli restrictions on humanitarian aid needed for shelter construction had deepened the crisis, leaving most residents to face a fourth winter in tents under continued attacks.
