Four family members killed as truck crushes motorcycle near Sindhri

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four members of the same family, including two women, were killed near Sindhri when a speeding truck loaded with straw ran over their motorcycle. The accident took place near village Kalaro on the Phulhadiyoon-Tagusar Road. All four people on the motorcycle died in the accident.

The truck driver fled the scene after the accident. Police and rescue teams reached the site and shifted the bodies to Civil Hospital. The bodies were taken for post-mortem examination.
