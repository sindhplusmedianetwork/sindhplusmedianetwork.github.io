Gold Slips Below $4,000 to Hit One-Year Low Globally

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices in Pakistan declined on Friday, tracking losses in the international market where the metal fell to its lowest level in a year. In the domestic market, the price of gold per tola fell by Rs5,100 to reach Rs420,536, according to the All-Pakistan Gems and Jewellers Sarafa Association. Spot gold fell 3.1% to $3,897.09 an ounce, marking its lowest level since October 2025 and slipping below the key $4,000 level.

Silver dropped 6% to $49 an ounce, breaking below $50 for the first time in 11 months, while palladium slid below $1,000 an ounce. The sell-off in precious metals coincided with a sharp drop in technology shares, as investors expressed concern that heavy spending on artificial intelligence may not generate sufficient returns in the near term. In the local silver market, the price declined by Rs238 to Rs5,530 per tola.

Meanwhile, the Pakistani rupee closed the day at 276.75 against the US dollar, posting a marginal gain.
