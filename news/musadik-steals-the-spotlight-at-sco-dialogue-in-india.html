Musadik Steals the Spotlight at SCO Dialogue in India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister for Climate Change and Environmental Coordination Dr Musadik Malik addressed the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi on Saturday. He warned that climate change posed a serious threat to civilisation and cultural heritage. He described Pakistan's cultural identity as a product of diverse civilisations.

He said diversity and free exchange of ideas were essential to understanding heritage. On the sidelines of the forum, he held bilateral meetings with Tajikistan's Minister of Culture. He also met the President of Iran's Islamic Culture and Relations Organisation.
