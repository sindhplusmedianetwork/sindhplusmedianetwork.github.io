Apni Chhat Apna Ghar: 219,000 Families Get Rs289.11 Billion in Interest-Free Loans

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Under the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar programme, 219,000 families have so far received interest-free loans totalling Rs289.11 billion for home construction. More than 126,000 houses have been completed under the programme, while over 61,000 are under construction. A review meeting chaired by Housing Secretary Punjab Noorul Amin Mengal was briefed that more than 169,000 families have also received the second loan instalment.

The meeting was told that Rs22.15 billion has been repaid so far. The programme's online portal has registered more than 2.462 million citizens and received over 1.54 million applications.
