FIA Karachi Returns Rs1.1 Million to Immigration Fraud Victims, Arrests Suspect

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency's Anti-Human Trafficking Circle in Karachi has returned Rs1.1 million to victims of immigration fraud after recovering the amount during an inquiry. Officials said an immigration consultant had taken the money from complainant Ajmal Khan on the promise of assisting with immigration to Canada. The recovered sum was handed back to the affected citizen, providing him financial relief, the FIA spokesperson said.

In a separate action, the circle arrested an accused, Yaseen Khan, in a human-smuggling case registered under sections 3 and 4 of the Prevention of Smuggling of Migrants Act 2018. Initial investigation revealed he had settled Rs800,000 for providing a Bahrain work visa and allegedly collected Rs520,000 in advance. The traveller was denied permission to travel after doubts emerged over the visa and was handed over to the FIA, which says further investigation is under way to identify other possible facilitators.
