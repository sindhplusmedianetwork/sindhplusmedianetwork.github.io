Major Progress Toward Polio Eradication in Balochistan: All 23 Environmental Samples Negative

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan has recorded major progress toward polio eradication, with all 23 environmental samples testing negative. Health Minister Bakht Muhammad Kakar announced the development, describing it as the fruit of two years of continuous effort. He said no new polio case has been reported in the province during the past two years.

The minister credited the chief minister's and the chief secretary's constant monitoring for the campaign's success. He also praised polio workers for reaching children with vaccines despite terrorism and difficult terrain. The province's achievement marks an important step in Pakistan's long fight against the crippling disease.
