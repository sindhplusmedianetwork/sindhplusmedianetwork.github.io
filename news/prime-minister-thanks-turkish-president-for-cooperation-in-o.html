Prime Minister Thanks Turkish President for Cooperation in Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration Project

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif hosted a dinner in honour of Turkish Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar. Addressing the occasion, the prime minister said historical relations with Turkiye are based on long-standing friendship, mutual trust, brotherhood and common interests. He further said he is grateful to the Turkish president for the arrival of the Turkish research vessel Oruc Reis in Pakistan and full cooperation in the offshore oil and gas exploration project, and welcomes the growing cooperation between Pakistan and Turkiye in the energy sector.

He said Pakistan wants to further expand cooperation in the energy sector with Turkiye's experience and technical expertise, and there are vast possibilities to increase bilateral cooperation in the minerals and renewable energy sectors as well. The prime minister said the search for oil and gas reserves in maritime boundaries is important progress for the country's energy security and economic development. On this occasion, Turkish Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar said Pakistan and Turkiye are entering a new era of cooperation in the energy sector and Turkiye is committed to bringing its technical expertise and scientific experience into use with Pakistan.

The Turkish energy minister said the Turkish research ship will conduct a 3D seismic survey in the search for oil and gas reserves in the offshore areas of Sindh and Makran. Turkish Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar said scientific data will help determine the prospects of oil and gas exploration and drilling in the future.
