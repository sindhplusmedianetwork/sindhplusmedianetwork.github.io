KP governor rules out talks with anti-state elements, announces Rs160bn package

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi on Friday ruled out negotiations or concessions for elements that do not accept the Constitution. He said decisive action against terrorism was the only way to restore peace in the province. The governor said the federal government was ready to provide weapons and financial resources to the KP police.

He announced a Rs160 billion federal package for the province. Kundi also raised the province's water share, saying KP was not getting its due water quota. He made the remarks while speaking to reporters about the security situation in the province.
