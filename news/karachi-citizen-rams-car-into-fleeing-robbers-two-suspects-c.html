Karachi Citizen Rams Car Into Fleeing Robbers; Two Suspects Caught

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two suspected robbers were caught in Karachi after the citizen they had robbed chased them down in his car and rammed their motorcycle. The incident took place near Centrum Shopping Mall on Rashid Minhas Road, where two armed men snatched a purse from a citizen and tried to flee on a motorcycle. The victim immediately gave chase in his car and struck their motorcycle near the UBL Complex, throwing both suspects to the ground.

Both men were injured in the collision, after which people in the area caught them, beat them and handed them over to the police. Police said both suspects are alive and in custody. A weapon, the stolen purse and other items were recovered from their possession.

Further investigation is under way.
