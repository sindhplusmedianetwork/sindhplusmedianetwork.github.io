Reliance says Jio also awaiting satellite clearance amid Starlink row

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mukesh Ambani's Reliance has broken its silence on Elon Musk, saying it welcomed competition from Starlink and that its own satellite services were also awaiting clearance. The statement responds to Musk's accusations that Ambani was blocking Starlink's launch in India. It comes just days before Jio Platforms seeks a $108 billion valuation in what would be India's largest initial public offering.

Starlink replied that it had worked with Indian regulators for over five years and was ready to connect India. The exchange highlights the intensifying race for satellite internet in one of the world's largest markets.
