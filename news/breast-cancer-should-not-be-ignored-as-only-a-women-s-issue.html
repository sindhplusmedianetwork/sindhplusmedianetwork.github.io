Breast Cancer Should Not Be Ignored as Only a Women's Issue: Sherry Rehman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP vice-president Senator Sherry Rehman has said that breast cancer should not be ignored as only a women's issue. Addressing a breast cancer symposium organised by the Rawalpindi Chamber of Commerce via video link, she praised the chamber's efforts for awareness. She said one in nine Pakistani women faces the risk of breast cancer, and many cases go undiagnosed, especially in rural and remote areas where access to diagnosis and treatment facilities is a major problem.

She noted that social stigma and cultural barriers stand in the way of awareness, and the cancer registry statistics do not reflect the full picture because many cases are not reported. She urged businesses to make breast cancer screening part of their corporate social responsibility and arrange free or discounted screening for women employees and the families of male employees. She said that if three major laboratories provided screening free or at discounted rates, it would be major progress.

She proposed that the chamber move forward with partnerships with laboratories, start a pilot screening programme in the twin cities of Rawalpindi and Islamabad, and later expand it nationwide. She also proposed a special free breast cancer screening campaign for women in October 2027, adding that her own mother had suffered from breast cancer.
