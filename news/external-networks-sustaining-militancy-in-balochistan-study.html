External Networks Sustaining Militancy in Balochistan, Study Finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A study published in November 2026 examined how outside facilitation is believed to sustain militancy in Balochistan. It was based on interviews with 16 people working in security, counterterrorism, politics, governance, development, media, border management and CPEC. The study identified six linked ways outside facilitation sustains militancy, including money, cross-border sanctuary and propaganda.

The researchers said counterterrorism must also disrupt the networks enabling attacks. They also called for better governance. They stressed visible local benefits from development.
