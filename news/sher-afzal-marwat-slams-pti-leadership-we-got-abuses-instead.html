Sher Afzal Marwat slams PTI leadership: 'we got abuses instead of honour for our sacrifices'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI lawmaker Sher Afzal Marwat has publicly criticised his party's leadership, saying the sacrifices he made for PTI in difficult times are known to the whole nation. In a statement issued in Islamabad on Friday, he lamented that instead of honour, he faced abuse and slander in return for those sacrifices. He said his services and struggle for the party were hidden from no one, and he did not want to keep repeating his record.

Marwat added that if the injustices done to him were addressed, he was still ready to make any sacrifice for the party. He urged PTI to get rid of self-serving elements whose politics had always centred on personal gain and money, and who had fled in difficult times. He questioned how anyone could pin hopes of Imran Khan's release on such people.
