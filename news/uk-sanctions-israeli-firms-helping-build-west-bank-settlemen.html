UK sanctions Israeli firms helping build West Bank settlements

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Britain on Tuesday announced sanctions against eight Israeli companies helping to plan and build settlements in the West Bank, in a bid to halt new construction. Foreign minister Ed Miliband urged Israeli companies not to bid, not to build, and not to profit from the destruction of the two-state solution. The Foreign Office said the sanctions target construction and property development firms involved in settlement expansion, and will disrupt their flows of finance and ability to support further construction.

The move comes as European leaders condemned plans to construct more than 1,200 homes in the occupied West Bank, which were put out to tender in August. The sanctions result in asset freezes for the companies as well as director disqualifications. The Foreign Office also sanctioned the Israeli activist group Tzav 9 on Tuesday for blocking aid deliveries into Gaza.

Miliband said the government will not stand by while those blocking desperately needed aid deepen the suffering of civilians in Gaza. The Foreign Office said heavy flooding in Gaza had left nearly 4,000 households without shelter and 1.4 million people living in tents or makeshift shelters.
