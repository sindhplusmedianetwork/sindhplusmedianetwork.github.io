Health Minister Flags Alarming Rise in Child HIV Cases; Sindh Worst Hit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Health Minister Syed Mustafa Kamal has told the National Assembly that child AIDS cases are rising alarmingly across the country. Responding to a question by PPP MNA Dr Sharmila Farooqui, the minister said Sindh is the worst-hit province with 3,900 affected children, followed by Punjab with 1,765, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa with 139 and Balochistan with 46. Islamabad has 30 child cases, Azad Kashmir four and Gilgit-Baltistan one.

A September 2026 review of the national HIV programme puts the total number of HIV patients in the country at 417,214, of whom 92,148 are registered and 67,878 are receiving anti-retroviral therapy. The minister's figures underline the scale of the challenge facing the country's health authorities. Child health advocates have long called for stronger screening and prevention programmes, particularly in Sindh.
