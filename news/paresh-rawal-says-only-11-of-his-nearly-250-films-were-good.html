Paresh Rawal Says Only 11 of His Nearly 250 Films Were Good

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Veteran Bollywood actor Paresh Rawal, who has ruled the film industry for four decades, has spoken candidly about his film journey during the recent premiere of his film 'Past Tense'. In a question-and-answer session at the film festival, when asked about the number of films he has done, he laughed and said he does not remember the total but only remembers his good films, which number just 11. His son Aditya Rawal also confirmed that he has kept a count of these favourite films.

According to Paresh Rawal, working in 'Sardar' (1993) was closest to his heart and he worked hard for that role. He also called the 1997 film 'Tamanna' good, saying he loved its first half and the real-life-based character 'Tiku', though the second half could have been better. Others on his list of 11 include 'Oye Lucky!

Lucky Oye!' (2008), 'Mumbai Meri Jaan' (2008), 'The Storyteller' (2025), 'Hera Pheri' (2000) and 'Dacait' (1987). Rawal began his film career in 1984 with 'Holi', gaining fame in negative roles in films like 'Naam' (1986), 'Kabzaa' (1988) and 'Sir' (1993), winning National Film Awards for Best Supporting Actor for 'Sir' and 'Woh Chokri'. He later moved from serious and villainous roles to comedy, where his 'Babu Rao Ganpat Rao Apte' in 'Hera Pheri' (2000) became extremely popular.
