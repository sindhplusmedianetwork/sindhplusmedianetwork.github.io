Copper and gold found at eight sites in Balochistan's Chagai district

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mining company National Resources Limited has announced that it has identified copper and gold deposits at eight sites in Balochistan's Chagai district. The finds came after nearly 25,000 metres of drilling, covering 5.6 square kilometres in Darigwan, adjoining the company's earlier Tang Kaur discovery. One drill hole revealed a 295-metre stretch containing 0.43 per cent copper and 0.13 grams of gold per tonne.

The company said it plans to carry out environmental and feasibility studies in 2027. It also reaffirmed its previously announced $100 million commitment to mineral exploration in Pakistan. The announcement adds to a series of recent mineral discoveries in the Chagai region.
