Five Arrested, Four Injured in Karachi Police Encounters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi police arrested five street criminals, four of them in injured condition, during three separate encounters in different parts of the city on Saturday. In District West's Mominabad Sector 11-E, police and the Shaheen Force exchanged fire with armed suspects, arresting two men — Dunya Zeb and Qasim Khan — one of them injured. SSP West Tariq Elahi Mastoi said illegal weapons, ammunition, spent shells, mobile phones and cash were recovered from them, and their motorcycle was impounded.

In District Central's Khwaja Ajmer Nagri Sector 2, near Juma Bazaar, police arrested two more suspects in injured condition, identified as Ismail and Waheed Ahmed, recovering two 30-bore pistols, five mobile phones, cash, a wallet and a motorcycle. SSP Central Dr Muhammad Imran Khan said both injured men were shifted to hospital while their previous criminal record is being obtained. A third alleged encounter near Jail Chowrangi ended with one more injured suspect in custody.

Police said investigations are underway in all three cases.
