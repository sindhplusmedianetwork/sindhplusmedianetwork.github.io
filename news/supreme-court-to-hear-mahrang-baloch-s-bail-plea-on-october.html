Supreme Court to Hear Mahrang Baloch's Bail Plea on October 21

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Supreme Court has scheduled October 21 for hearing the bail petitions of Dr Mahrang Baloch, Beebo Baloch and Beberg Baloch in a terrorism case. A three-member bench headed by Justice Muhammad Ali Mazhar, also comprising Justices Naeem Akhtar Afghan and Shahid Bilal Hassan, will take up the petitions. The court has already issued notices on the petitions.

Dr Mahrang, a well-known rights activist, has been in detention since March 2025 on various charges. Her lawyer, Imaan Mazari, is herself facing multiple charges including terrorism and is currently detained along with her husband, and has separately challenged her jail trial in an anti-terrorism court case.
