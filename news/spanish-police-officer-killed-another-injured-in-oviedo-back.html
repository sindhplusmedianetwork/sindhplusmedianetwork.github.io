Spanish police officer killed, another injured in Oviedo backpack explosion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Spanish police officer was killed and another seriously injured on Tuesday when an explosive device in a backpack blew up in the northern city of Oviedo. Officers were responding to a violent dispute between two families when they found the backpack at the entrance of a building in the Villafria area. The device detonated as the officers carried the bag into the street to move it away from residents, at about 8:50am.

The officer who died was a 60-year-old sub-inspector from the National Police canine unit, named by the force as Juan Jose. The injured officer, from the bomb disposal unit, remained in a very serious condition after losing a leg. The dispute had begun earlier in the morning when a man shot and injured a 17-year-old girl, who was later said to be out of danger.

Police said the suspected gunman, a man in his sixties, had fled the scene and was still at large, and that a terrorist motive had been ruled out for now.
