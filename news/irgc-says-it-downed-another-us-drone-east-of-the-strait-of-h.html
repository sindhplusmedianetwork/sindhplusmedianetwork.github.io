IRGC says it downed another US drone east of the Strait of Hormuz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) says it has intercepted and shut down another US MQ-1 drone using an advanced air defence system east of the Strait of Hormuz, state media reported on Thursday. The claim marks the latest escalation as regional tensions remain high around the strategic waterway. Separately, the Saudi-led coalition fighting Yemen's Houthis said it had intercepted a ballistic missile fired by the group at the kingdom's eastern region.

Coalition spokesman Turki al-Malki said air defences intercepted and destroyed the missile launched toward the Eastern Province. Meanwhile, US media reported that Washington has warned Gulf allies to prepare for possible renewed strikes on Iran after the November midterm elections. Officials quoted by Al Jazeera warned of a short, intense campaign and potential Iranian retaliation.

Oil prices fell on Thursday, paring gains made a day earlier, as hopes for peace talks took the edge off renewed concerns over Middle East supply disruptions. Brent crude futures declined $1.22, or 1.1 per cent, to $114.10 a barrel, while US West Texas Intermediate futures were down $1.33 at $101.45.
