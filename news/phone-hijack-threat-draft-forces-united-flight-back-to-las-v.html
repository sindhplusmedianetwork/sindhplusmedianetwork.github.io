Phone Hijack Threat Draft Forces United Flight Back to Las Vegas

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read

A United Airlines flight from Las Vegas to New Jersey was turned back shortly after take-off after a flight attendant spotted a threatening message drafted on a fellow passenger's phone. Flight UA1681 had been airborne for only 20 minutes and was at 24,000 feet when the attendant, passing through the cabin, saw 25-year-old passenger Dante Millan's phone screen. The draft message read “gonna hijack this”, along with abusive wording and a further line threatening to crash only the passenger's row.

The attendant immediately alerted the pilot, who locked down the cockpit and informed air traffic control of the unusual situation before turning the aircraft back on instructions. The crew deliberately gave the passenger no sign that he was under suspicion so that he would not cause any disturbance during the return. After a safe landing back at Las Vegas airport on October 24, police boarded the aircraft and arrested Millan on charges of making terror threats and spreading false information.

All 150 passengers and six crew members remained safe, and aviation security protocol treats any such written threat as an extremely serious risk requiring immediate action.
