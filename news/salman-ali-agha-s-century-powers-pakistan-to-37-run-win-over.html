Salman Ali Agha's century powers Pakistan to 37-run win over Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by 37 runs in the ODI tri-series opener in Rawalpindi on Sunday. Salman Ali Agha was the star of the match, scoring 107 off 90 balls — his third ODI century. Babar Azam contributed 45 and debutant Saad Baig added 39 as Pakistan posted 305.

Sri Lanka looked comfortable during a 119-run stand between Pavan Rathnayake and Janith Liyanage. But Liyanage and Dasun Shanaka fell within four balls to trigger a dramatic collapse. Pakistan's bowlers held their nerve in the closing overs to seal the win.

The victory gives Pakistan a strong start to the tri-series campaign.
