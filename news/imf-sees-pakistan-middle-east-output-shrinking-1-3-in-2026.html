IMF Sees Pakistan, Middle East Output Shrinking 1.3% in 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Monetary Fund has forecast a sharp contraction in economic output across Pakistan, the Middle East and North Africa this year. The Fund attributed the downturn to the US-Israeli-led war against Iran, which has disrupted energy production, shipping, tourism, trade and business confidence across the region. The region's output is expected to shrink by 1.3 per cent in 2026.

The IMF said growth could rebound to 6.6 per cent in 2027 if conditions stabilise. The assessment was released on Thursday as part of the Fund's latest outlook for the region.
