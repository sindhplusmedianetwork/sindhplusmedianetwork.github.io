Government to privatise three power distribution companies in early 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government is planning to privatise three power distribution companies during the first quarter of 2027, Prime Minister's Adviser on Privatisation Muhammad Ali has said. The companies are the Faisalabad Electric Supply Company, the Gujranwala Electric Power Company and the Islamabad Electric Supply Company, he said in an interview on Saturday. Ali, who also serves as chairman of the Privatisation Commission, said due diligence for the three companies was already under way.

He added that potential investors had requested additional time to assess the complex transactions involved in the sale of the power utilities.
