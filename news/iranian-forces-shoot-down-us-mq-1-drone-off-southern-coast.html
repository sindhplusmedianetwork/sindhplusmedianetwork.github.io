Iranian forces shoot down US MQ-1 drone off southern coast

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's navy says it has shot down a United States MQ-1 drone near Kuhestak, off the coast of Sirik in Hormozgan province, using a new naval air defence system. Iranian media reported that the drone was intercepted near the Strait of Hormuz and destroyed by the country's air defence network. The drone was detected near Kuhestak and disabled by the air defence system.

The MQ-1 is a medium-altitude unmanned aircraft built by General Atomics and used by the US Air Force for military and surveillance operations in western Asia and other regions. The US Air Force retired its MQ-1 Predator fleet in 2018, replacing it with the more advanced MQ-9 Reaper. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said last week that they had shot down a 16th MQ-1 drone since the start of the US-Israel war.

Iran closed oil transit through the Strait of Hormuz on 28 February 2026 after attacks by the US and Israel, reopened it temporarily in April for a ceasefire, and later suspended it again after further US attacks.
