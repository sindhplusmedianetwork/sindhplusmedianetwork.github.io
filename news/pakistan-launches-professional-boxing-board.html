Pakistan launches professional boxing board

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has established a professional boxing board aimed at developing the sport, giving local fighters international opportunities and introducing a transparent regulatory system, officials said. The Pakistan Professional Boxing Board, chaired by Amir Khan, will oversee the registration, licensing and training of boxers, referees and judges. The board will seek affiliations with the WBC, WBA, IBF, WBO and Commonwealth Boxing.

The first professional boxing event under the initiative is expected on February 10 next year. A registration portal, Showboxing.com, has been launched for boxers and officials. Officials say the board will provide a structured pathway for Pakistani fighters to compete internationally.
