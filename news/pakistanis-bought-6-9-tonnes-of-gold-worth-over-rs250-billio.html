Pakistanis Bought 6.9 Tonnes of Gold Worth Over Rs250 Billion in Three Months, Says World Gold Council

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistanis bought 6.9 tonnes of gold in the three months from July to September 2026, worth more than Rs250.64 billion at current rates, according to a World Gold Council report cited by 24News. Of this, 3.1 tonnes were bought for jewellery-making, worth over Rs112.61 billion or 264,957 tolas. A further 3.8 tonnes were bought as bars and coins for investment, worth over Rs138.03 billion.

Total gold purchases in the first nine months of 2026 reached 25.6 tonnes, worth more than Rs929.91 billion. That total included 10.1 tonnes of jewellery and 15.5 tonnes bought for investment, so investment buying ran ahead of jewellery buying over the nine months.
