ILT20 Season 5 Kicks Off Today in UAE with Pakistani Stars in Action

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The fifth season of the International League T20 (ILT20) is set to begin today in the United Arab Emirates, with Pakistani stars including Babar Azam and Shadab Khan set to feature in the tournament. The opening match will see defending champions Desert Vipers take on MI Emirates in Dubai. Afghanistan spinner Rashid Khan described the ILT20 as a major driver for the development of cricket in the Emirates, while Sikandar Raza welcomed the decision to provide opportunities to players from associate nations in the league.

South Africa's Faf du Plessis will make his first appearance in the ILT20 this season, and Heinrich Klaasen will lead Gulf Giants. Cricket legends Wasim Akram, Waqar Younis and Shoaib Akhtar will be part of the commentary team for the event.
