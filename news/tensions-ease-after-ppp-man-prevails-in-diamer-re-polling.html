Tensions ease after PPP man prevails in Diamer re-polling

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A controversy over re-polling in Gilgit-Baltistan Assembly constituency GBA-16 Diamer-II was finally put to rest on Monday night. The GB Election Commission declared PPP candidate Attaullah Shah victorious, with a margin of 85 votes. According to the final consolidated result, he secured 6,758 votes against 6,673 garnered by his rival, Imam Malik of the Istehkam-i-Pakistan Party.

Re-polling at three polling stations was completed on Sunday, but official results for two stations were not issued until Monday night. Earlier in the day, supporters of both men had blocked the Karakoram Highway at different locations in Diamer for around 10 hours amid claims of rigging and irregularities. Police also claimed to have recovered a ballot box allegedly snatched from the Wora female polling station.

Counting at the combined male and female polling station in Gechi was completed, where Mr Shah secured 427 votes while Mr Malik received 53.
