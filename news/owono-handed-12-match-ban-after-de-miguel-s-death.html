Owono handed 12-match ban after De Miguel's death

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono has been suspended for 12 matches by the Spanish football federation's disciplinary committee after his challenge on Tenerife striker Jesus de Miguel ended in the player's death. Owono, an Equatorial Guinea international, was sent off during the second-division match on November 2 after racing into midfield and colliding with De Miguel while trying to clear an aerial ball. De Miguel, 30, was taken to hospital in intensive care and died on November 3.

The committee said the referee's report recorded that Owono was sent off for violently striking an opponent in the chest with his leg, using excessive force, while the ball was in play. Given the nature of the conduct and the circumstances, it concluded that the maximum sanction of 12 matches was justified. Spanish police have opened an investigation into the incident under judicial supervision.

Owono has described the period since De Miguel's death as the hardest days of his life, while Andorra coach Carles Manso said it would be Owono who decides if and when he returns to football. Tenerife fans and players gathered at the club's stadium on Saturday for a memorial and farewell to De Miguel, whose funeral was attended by around 400 mourners last Thursday.
