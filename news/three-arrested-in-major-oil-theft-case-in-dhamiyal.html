Three Arrested in Major Oil Theft Case in Dhamiyal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police uncovered a major crude oil theft operation in Dhamiyal, where suspects allegedly dug a tunnel from a rented house to tap a Pakistan Oilfields Limited pipeline. The suspects stole about 1,100 barrels of crude oil worth over Rs23.2 million. Police recovered 1,431 litres of crude oil during a raid on the site.

Three suspects, Muhammad Mumtaz, Shaukat and Muhammad Akram, were arrested at the scene. A case has been registered, and five more suspects were nominated in the FIR.
