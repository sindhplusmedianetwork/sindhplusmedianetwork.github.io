Forces Repulse Cross-Border Attack in South Waziristan, Taliban Flee Posts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani security forces gave an immediate and effective response to unprovoked firing from Afghan border posts on Pakistani positions in Angoor Adda, South Waziristan. The deputy commissioner of South Waziristan Lower said in a post on X that firing from Afghan border posts targeting Pakistani border posts was continuing, prompting the strong response. Security forces targeted several Afghan border posts, mortar positions and installations, after which Afghan Taliban personnel were forced to abandon their posts and flee.

The deputy commissioner said no casualties had so far been reported on the Pakistani side in the border areas, adding that Pakistani forces remained prepared to respond to any form of aggression. Earlier on Thursday, the army had given a befitting reply to unprovoked firing by the Afghan Taliban in the Angoor Adda area, forcing the Taliban personnel to abandon their posts. Several personnel of the Afghan Taliban regime were killed during that operation, according to security sources.
