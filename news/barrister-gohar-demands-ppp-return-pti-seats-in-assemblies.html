Barrister Gohar demands PPP return PTI seats in assemblies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar demanded that the PPP return PTI's seats in the National Assembly, Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa assemblies. He called on all sides to free the judiciary and the Election Commission. He also urged protection of the sanctity of the vote.

Barrister Gohar said cases against Imran Khan were baseless and politically motivated. He added that even political opponents now accept that Imran Khan is a political prisoner. He asked the court to hear PTI's stance in open court.

On new provinces, he said the matter was premature and PTI would give its position after a draft appears and after meeting the PTI founder.
