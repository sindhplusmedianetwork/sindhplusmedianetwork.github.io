French flavours come to Islamabad with Gout de France dinner

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Movenpick Hotel Centaurus Islamabad, in collaboration with the Embassy of France, the Bank of Punjab and Alliance Francaise Islamabad, hosted Gout de France - A French Culinary Affair at Embers, Level 20, bringing an authentic taste of French gastronomy to the capital. French guest chef Maxime Delaboudiniere prepared a specially curated five-course menu showcasing classic French techniques, carefully selected ingredients and refined flavours, accompanied by live jazz performances. The menu began with scallop tartare with cucumber, lime, Penja pepper and pomegranate, followed by roasted butternut squash cream with toasted walnuts and morel emulsion.

The savoury courses continued with slow-cooked chicken served with carrots prepared in various ways, followed by confit beef chuck with reduced meat jus, mushrooms, roasted cauliflower and potato mousseline. The experience concluded with chocolate mousse complemented by virgin olive oil, fleur de sel and toasted hazelnuts. The gathering reflected the spirit of Gout de France, an initiative celebrating French culinary heritage and cultural exchange through gastronomy, and the hotel said it would continue introducing global flavours and culinary talent to Pakistan.
