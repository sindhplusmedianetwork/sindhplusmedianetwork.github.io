Punjab education minister responds to Chaudhry Salik Hussain's corruption claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Education Minister Rana Sikandar Hayat has responded to federal minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain's corruption claims against the Punjab government. Speaking on the Centre Stage programme, he asked what action Salik had taken in his own ministry and whether there was no corruption there. He said Salik offered no evidence for his claims against the Punjab government.

He noted that Salik's brother Shafay Hussain had praised Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's governance on national television just 48 hours earlier. He added that Punjab had made more than 400 corruption arrests in the past four months and removed 400 people over corruption.
