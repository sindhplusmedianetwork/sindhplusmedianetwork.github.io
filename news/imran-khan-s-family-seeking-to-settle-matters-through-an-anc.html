Imran Khan's Family Seeking to Settle Matters Through an Anchor, Claims Analyst

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Analyst Syed Imran Shafqat has claimed in a social media post that the family of PTI founder Imran Khan is making behind-the-scenes efforts to normalise strained relations with the establishment. He said a well-known Lahore-based media anchor was reportedly being brought in to help find a way out of the political deadlock. According to Shafqat, the anchor is in regular contact with an important federal minister who is also from Lahore.

A report by journalist Ansar Abbasi separately claimed that the release of Imran Khan's sisters from Kot Lakhpat Jail was the first tangible outcome of recently initiated backchannel contacts. The report said the release followed assurances that the sisters would remain non-controversial and avoid statements against the military leadership.
