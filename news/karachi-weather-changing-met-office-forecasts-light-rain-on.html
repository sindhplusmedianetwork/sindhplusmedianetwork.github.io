Karachi Weather Changing: Met Office Forecasts Light Rain on November 13

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi's weather has begun to change under the influence of westerly winds, the Pakistan Meteorological Department said on Wednesday. Light drizzle is expected in the city on November 13. Met Office spokesman Anjum Nazir Zaigham said mornings will feel noticeably colder from November 18.

Daytime temperatures will also start falling gradually from the beginning of December. The changing weather marks the start of proper winter in the city. Residents can expect cooler mornings in the coming days as the westerly system affects the region.
