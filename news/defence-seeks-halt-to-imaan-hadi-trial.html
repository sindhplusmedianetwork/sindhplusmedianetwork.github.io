Defence Seeks Halt to Imaan, Hadi Trial

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The defence on Monday urged the Islamabad High Court to halt trial proceedings against lawyer Imaan Mazari and her husband, advocate Hadi Ali Chattha. The defence alleged procedural violations in the framing of charges and argued that the accused had not been physically present when they were indicted. The court was hearing appeals filed by Imaan and Hadi against the anti-terrorism court's dismissal of their applications challenging their indictment and the terrorism charges.

The case relates to the removal of road barriers. Imaan and Hadi were rearrested on September 17, shortly after the Supreme Court granted them bail and suspended their sentences in a separate case concerning social media posts. The high court will now decide whether the trial proceedings should be stayed while the appeals against the indictment are pending.
