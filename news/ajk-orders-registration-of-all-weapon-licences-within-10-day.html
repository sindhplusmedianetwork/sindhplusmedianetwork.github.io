AJK orders registration of all weapon licences within 10 days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Azad Jammu and Kashmir government has directed all weapon licence holders in the region to register their licences at the nearest police stations within 10 days. The Home Department notification, approved by the AJK president, warns that unregistered licences will be treated as cancelled. The order applies to all types of licences regardless of where they were issued.

Anyone found possessing weapons or ammunition without a registered licence will face arrest and confiscation of the weapon. The government has asked licence holders to complete the registration within the deadline to avoid action.
