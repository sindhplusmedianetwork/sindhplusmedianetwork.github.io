Babar Azam Hits 86 on ILT20 Debut as Desert Vipers Beat Sharjah Warriors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Babar Azam made a sparkling ILT20 debut, scoring 86 off 51 balls to guide Desert Vipers to a six-wicket win over Sharjah Warriors. Chasing 167, the Vipers reached the target in 17.3 overs at Sharjah Cricket Stadium in the second match of the 2026 season. Babar hit 13 fours and two sixes at a strike rate of 168.62 and finished as the match's top scorer.

His 13 fours set a new ILT20 record for the most boundaries in a single innings. Sharjah Warriors had been bowled out for 166 before the Vipers chased the target down. Babar was named player of the match for his debut innings.
