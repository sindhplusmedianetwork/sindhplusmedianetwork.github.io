Protest grows against Nehr-e-Khayyam project in Clifton, Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Opposition to the Nehr-e-Khayyam project in Clifton, Karachi, intensified on Sunday as residents, activists, lawyers and politicians held a rally demanding an immediate halt to construction. The protesters want all environmental approvals, technical studies and project details made public before work continues. They warned that the construction could compromise the historic waterway's drainage capacity and increase the risk of urban flooding in the area.

The rally demanded completion of the mandatory Environmental Impact Assessment and approval from the Sindh Environmental Protection Agency. Concerns were also raised over alleged plans to use parts of the land for six flat sites and two multi-storey commercial plots. The project has become a flashpoint between development plans and environmental protection in the coastal neighbourhood.
