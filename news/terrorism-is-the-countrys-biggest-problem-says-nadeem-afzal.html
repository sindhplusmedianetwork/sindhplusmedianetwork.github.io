Terrorism is the country’s biggest problem, says Nadeem Afzal Chan, urging government to stop fighting opposition

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP leader Nadeem Afzal Chan has said terrorism is currently the country’s biggest problem, along with unemployment, inflation and the energy crisis, and the government should stop fighting the opposition and focus on solving the country’s problems. Speaking to journalists in Lahore, he said PTI should also give up stubbornness and play its role in assemblies and committees to solve public issues. He added that the bureaucracy’s system has collapsed and ministers themselves are levelling corruption allegations.

He said the government should call all stakeholders to fix the system and work on electoral reforms, as elections are due in two years. He also called for local body elections to transfer powers to the grassroots, describing Punjab’s upcoming local government system as a fraudulent system and the 2001 system as better.
