NCA Adviser Warns India's Military Doctrines Endanger South Asia at London Workshop

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Retired Lieutenant General Mazhar Jamil, adviser to Pakistan's National Command Authority, told an IISS workshop in London that India's military doctrines show a 'poor strategic sense of nuclear realities' and are endangering South Asia. He blamed what he described as a Hindutva-driven ideology that marginalises Muslims for shaping Indian strategic thinking. Jamil warned that military adventurism between two nuclear-armed powers invites catastrophe and cited Kashmir as a nuclear flashpoint.

He recalled the accidental firing of a BrahMos missile into Pakistan in 2022 and the May 2025 conflict as examples of what he called reckless Indian behaviour. He stressed that Pakistan remains committed to peace and strategic stability but reserves the right to safeguard its national security.
