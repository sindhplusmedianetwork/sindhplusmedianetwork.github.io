Speaker Seeks Names for 30-Member Electoral Reforms Committee

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Assembly Speaker Ayaz Sadiq has written to top political leaders seeking nominations for the parliamentary committee on electoral reforms. Letters were sent to Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Senate Chairman Yousaf Raza Gilani and opposition leader Mahmood Achakzai. The 30-member committee will include 15 members nominated by the Leader of the House, 10 senators and 5 members from the opposition leader.

The committee will review and propose reforms to the country's electoral laws. Its formal notification will be issued once all names are received. The move is seen as a step toward building consensus on election-related legislation.
