What Sanctions and War Could Not Achieve, More War Will Not Either: Iran's Foreign Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has declared that whatever could not be gained through sanctions and war will not be gained through more war either. In his statement, he said Iranian forces are conducting defensive operations at locations used by the Americans. Araghchi revealed that he had contacted Pakistan's Field Marshal Asim Munir along with senior officials from six countries, exchanging views on the latest situation in the region.

He warned that any hostile move would be met with an immediate and decisive response. The foreign minister's remarks came at a time when both the United States and Iran have been making claims of attacks against each other. Tensions between the two sides have remained high amid warnings of renewed military action after the American midterm elections.

Regional countries have been watching the developments closely, with Gulf states preparing for a possible escalation.
