PML-N UK Office-Bearers to Continue Till 2027 With Nawaz Sharif's Approval

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The current office-bearers of Pakistan Muslim League (N) UK will continue in their responsibilities until September 22, 2027. According to a notification issued by Ishaq Dar, the decision was taken with the approval of party leader and president Mian Muhammad Nawaz Sharif. Ahsan Dar will continue as president of PML-N UK, while Khurram Butt will continue as president of PML-N Youth UK, the notification said.
