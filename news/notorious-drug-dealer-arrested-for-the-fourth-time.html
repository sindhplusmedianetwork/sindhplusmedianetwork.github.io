Notorious Drug Dealer Arrested for the Fourth Time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ferozabad police, in a joint operation with a federal civil sensitive agency, arrested an alleged notorious drug dealer for the fourth time. The accused, Qasim alias Qasoo, was taken into custody along with ice worth hundreds of thousands of rupees. Officials said the suspect was a repeat offender involved in the supply of narcotics in the area.

A case has been registered against him and further investigation is under way. Police said the crackdown on drug dealers would continue.
