27 mineral water brands declared unsafe for drinking, PCRWR report says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Council of Research in Water Resources (PCRWR) has declared 27 mineral water brands unsafe for drinking in its quarterly report covering July to September 2026. The council collected samples from 223 brands in 20 cities and tested them for chemical and microbiological quality. Of these, 195 brands passed the tests, while 27 failed.

Six brands failed the chemical tests and 22 failed the microbiological tests, according to the report. The brands declared unsafe include Aab-e-Haram, Imperial Pure Drinking Water, Aab-e-Makran, Water Verse Purified, Jail, Sup, AB Blue Water, HR Water Healthy Life, Safi, Pure Aqua Tech, Aqua Fine, Water Time, Al-Saha and Aab-e-Hayat, among others. The council publishes the quarterly report to inform the public about the quality of bottled water sold across the country.
