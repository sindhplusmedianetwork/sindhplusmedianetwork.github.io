Hanif Abbasi Says Fazlur Rehman Is Targeting State Institutions for Politics

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Railways Minister Hanif Abbasi has accused JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman of targeting state institutions for political motives. Speaking in Islamabad on Monday, Abbasi said Fazlur Rehman's statements were causing pain to the nation. He described using institutions for political gain as condemnable.

The minister dismissed rumours about mineral agreements in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa as baseless and challenged critics to name which country such a deal was signed with. He said the army and police were rendering sacrifices and paid tribute to the families of the martyrs. Abbasi added that the province's law-and-order situation had led to the governor's rule and that corruption worth billions had been ended.

He concluded that no one would be permitted to cross red lines or put the country's interests at stake for politics.
