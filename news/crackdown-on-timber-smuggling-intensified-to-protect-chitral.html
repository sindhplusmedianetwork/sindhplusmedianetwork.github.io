Crackdown on Timber Smuggling Intensified to Protect Chitral Forests

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Chitral forest division has stepped up its crackdown on timber smugglers to stop illegal tree felling and protect the mountainous region from flash floods caused by rapid deforestation. Divisional Forest Officer Asif Ali Shah told reporters that preserving the valley's ecological balance remained the department's priority. He said unchecked deforestation directly contributed to soil erosion and catastrophic flooding during the monsoon and glacial melting seasons.

Over the past couple of months, forest teams seized 4,164 cubic feet of illegally cut timber, impounded 21 vehicles used for smuggling, and imposed fines of 5 million rupees. The department has sent the names of 48 habitual forest offenders to the district magistrate, recommending their detention under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order ordinance. Vigorous prosecution in courts has left 25,184 cubic feet of confiscated timber secured across various forest compartments under judicial orders.

Shah also clarified that timber from designated guzara community forests is lawfully allotted to eligible residents from Arandu in Lower Chitral to Broghil in Upper Chitral for building houses, and urged people to verify facts before raising concerns about lawful timber transport.
