Fans saddened by frail condition of 'Josh' actor Sharad Kapoor in viral video

Fans expressed sadness after recent pictures and a video of Bollywood actor Sharad Kapoor, who starred alongside Shah Rukh Khan in the film 'Josh', showed him in poor condition. In the video circulating on social media, Kapoor appears emotional as his four pet dogs are being moved to an animal welfare centre. Reports say the actor is facing health problems, which is why the dogs are being shifted from his home to the centre.

Hema Chaudhry, a representative of the Mumbai-based Be Zuban Public Welfare Trust, shared the video and explained the reasons for the move. She said Kapoor is dealing with some health issues these days and needs to focus on his own care. She added that his wife and newborn baby are also at home, so looking after four dogs had become difficult in the present circumstances, which is why the decision was taken to move them to the welfare centre.
