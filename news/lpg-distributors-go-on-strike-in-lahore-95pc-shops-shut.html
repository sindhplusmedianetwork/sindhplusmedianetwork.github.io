LPG distributors go on strike in Lahore, 95pc shops shut

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The LPG Distributors Association in Lahore has gone on strike, raising fears of an LPG crisis in the city. Shops selling LPG are closed in most areas of Lahore, and the distributors have announced that the strike will continue until their demands are accepted. At a meeting on Sandha Road, attended by chairman Mian Ajmal, president Abdullah Khan and other members, it was claimed that 95 per cent of shops in the city are closed, creating a crisis-like situation.

The association said the strike is against the district administration's operations against LPG refilling and decanting, during which shops have been sealed and goods confiscated. The distributors claimed that goods worth Rs140 million have already been damaged during the operations, and they also levelled allegations of bribery and extortion against officials. They have demanded that Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz take notice, that clear and workable standard operating procedures be set for shops, and that operations by the Para Force, Civil Defence and the municipality be stopped.

The association's chairman said the operations should be halted and the issues resolved through negotiations.
