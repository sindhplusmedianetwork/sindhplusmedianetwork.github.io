Authorities warn of rising Facebook, Instagram account theft; urge two-factor authentication

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cybercrime authorities and security experts have issued a fresh advisory warning citizens about a surge in Facebook and Instagram account theft. According to ARY News, hackers are using fake lookalike websites and panic-inducing links shared on WhatsApp and Messenger to trick users into handing over their login credentials. A single wrong click can cost users their entire account, the advisory warns.

Once an account is compromised, criminals use it to send fake emergency messages to the victim's friends and family, demanding money. Citizens have been advised to use strong, unique passwords and keep two-factor authentication enabled at all times. Experts also urge people never to click unknown links and never to share one-time passwords or verification codes with anyone.

Reporting suspicious messages to the authorities promptly can help prevent wider fraud.
