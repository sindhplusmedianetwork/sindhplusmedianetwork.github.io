The FBI has reportedly decided to search Thomas Markle's homes in Los Angeles, where material allegedly linked to the sexual abuse of children is feared to be present, according to reports in The Sun and the Daily Mail, carried by The News. The move is said to follow a revelation by his son, Thomas Markle Jr, about a possible third controversial stash. The reports say similar material was allegedly recovered in Mexico.

The rented homes in California are also said to hold Meghan Markle's old belongings and her father's personal memorabilia, which may now be searched. The alleged material has not been proven, and the claims remain unproven allegations. Markle Sr, 82, denies any wrongdoing.
