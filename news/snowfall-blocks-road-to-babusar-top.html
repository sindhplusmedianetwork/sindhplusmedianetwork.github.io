Snowfall blocks road to Babusar Top

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Heavy snowfall from Baiser to Babusar Top in Naran Valley has covered the mountains in snow and blocked the road. Traffic in the upper Kaghan Valley has been affected by the snowfall. The Meteorological Department has forecast the season's first snowfall in the northern mountains this week.

Rain and thunderstorms are expected in several regions during the same period. Snowfall is also expected over higher elevations in upper Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Kashmir. The forecast covers November 10 to 14.
