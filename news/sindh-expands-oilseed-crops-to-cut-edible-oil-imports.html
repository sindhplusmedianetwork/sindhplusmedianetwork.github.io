Sindh Expands Oilseed Crops to Cut Edible Oil Imports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon has welcomed a rise in the cultivation of mustard, sesame and canola in the province, saying higher domestic production will help reduce dependence on imported edible oil and save foreign exchange. He said the area under mustard cultivation in Sindh has increased from 51,870 hectares to 61,165 hectares, while sesame and canola cultivation has also expanded. The minister said lower Sindh offers considerable potential for further mustard cultivation.

He added that the provincial government is taking practical steps to support growers, including financial assistance through the Benazir Hari Card and subsidies on DAP fertiliser under an additional Rs14.9 billion support package for farmers. The promotion of modern agricultural machinery and the efficient use of irrigation resources are also among the government's priorities. Memon said the measures aim to raise farmers' incomes, strengthen the local agricultural sector and increase local production so the province can cut its dependence on edible oil imports.
