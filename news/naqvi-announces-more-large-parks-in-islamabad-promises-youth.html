Naqvi announces more large parks in Islamabad, promises youth jobs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi has announced that the government plans to build one or two more large parks in Islamabad modelled on F-9 Park, while also improving the capital's existing parks. He made the announcement while speaking to the media during a visit to the Kashmir Chowk underpass, where he reviewed ongoing development work in the company of the CDA chairman and senior officials. Naqvi said the government wanted to take more steps for the youth, and that new development projects would create employment opportunities.

He added that the Safe City project in Islamabad would be expanded, with cameras currently being installed for security and vehicle identification. On the redevelopment of F-9 Park, the minister said Islamabad still resembled a jungle and that 400 acres of the park remained unused, adding that he wanted it developed on the pattern of London's Hyde Park at minimum cost. He also said the capital should be freed of plastic and other environmentally harmful items, noting that the record would show whether the number of trees had grown or shrunk.

Asked about the Laiba case, Naqvi said it was a sensitive matter he did not want to go into, adding that the police would brief on its details.
