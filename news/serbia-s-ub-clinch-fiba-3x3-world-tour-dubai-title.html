Serbia's Ub Clinch FIBA 3x3 World Tour Dubai Title

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Serbia's Ub won the FIBA 3x3 World Tour Dubai 2026 title after defeating Germany's Baskets Bonn 20-19 in Saturday's final at Festival Bay, Dubai Festival City. The narrow victory secured Ub's fourth World Tour title of the season. Ub's Strahinja Stojacic was named the Most Valuable Player after scoring 34 points and recording 11 key assists across the tournament.

The two-day event was organised by Dubai Sports Council under the supervision of the International Basketball Federation, in cooperation with the UAE Basketball Association, and featured 14 teams in Dubai's first hosting of a World Tour event. In the side competitions, Toronto's Adris De Leon of Canada won the Shootout Contest with 12 points, while Poland's Piotr Grabowski claimed his third Slam Dunk Contest title of the season. The 2026 World Tour concludes in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on December 12 and 13, while Dubai's hosting forms part of a three-year partnership running through 2028.
