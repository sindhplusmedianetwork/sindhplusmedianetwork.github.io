Modest rise in exports to US, imports surge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's merchandise exports to North America grew modestly by 4.65 per cent to $1.826 billion in the first quarter of 2026-27, up from $1.745 billion a year ago, according to data compiled by the State Bank of Pakistan. Imports from the region, however, surged 60.58 per cent to $841.31 million from $523.91 million over the corresponding period last year, indicating a high inflow of goods from North America. The rise in imports reflects Islamabad's efforts to ease trade imbalances with the region under an agreement reached with the US, and comes amid policy shifts by US President Donald Trump, who has imposed additional tariffs while extending limited relief to Pakistan under a reciprocal arrangement.

The US remained the dominant market, accounting for nearly 94 per cent of Pakistan's total exports to North America, with the remaining share going mainly to Canada and other countries. Pakistan's exports to the US rose 4.44 per cent to $1.716 billion in the July-September quarter of FY27 from $1.643 billion a year ago, while imports from the US surged 45.44 per cent to $659.23 million from $453.28 million. Exports to Canada rose marginally by 7.53 per cent to $109.72 million in the first quarter of FY27 from $102.03 million a year ago.

Under the new agreement, Pakistan will import additional goods from the US to balance the trade deficit, and as a result imports from the region, especially the US, grew sharply during the year under review. In the same region, exports to Latin America surged 118.64 per cent to $21.58 million in the first quarter of FY27, up from $9.87 million a year ago.
