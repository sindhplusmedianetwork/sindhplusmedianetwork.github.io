Consumers to Face Higher Power Tariffs in December

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Electricity consumers across Pakistan, including Karachi, may face an additional financial burden in December. Quarterly and monthly adjustments are expected to raise the combined cost to Rs4.22 per unit. Consumers currently pay Rs2.89 per unit under quarterly and monthly adjustments.

The amount is expected to rise by Rs1.33 per unit compared to November. Power distribution companies have filed a petition with Nepra seeking an increase of Rs1.34 per unit under the quarterly adjustment for July-September 2026 for the next three months.
