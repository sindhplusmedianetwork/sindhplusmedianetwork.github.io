Imran's Sister Getting All Necessary Medical Care, Senate Told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry informed the Senate on Friday that Noreen Niazi was being provided all necessary medical care and had been shifted to hospital on the recommendation of doctors. Responding to a point raised by Senator Mohsin Aziz about her health, he said the government was particularly sensitive to the health and welfare of individuals in detention and would ensure their rights and medical needs were fully protected. Chaudhry said decisions regarding her treatment, hospitalisation and any further medical intervention would be taken by the medical professionals concerned, adding that her family was present at the hospital.

Meanwhile, Senator Azam Swati referred to a telephone conversation with Hafeez Ullah Niazi, who appreciated the government for facilitating Noreen Niazi's transfer to hospital and arranging a meeting with her elder daughter. Swati described the arrangements as a positive humanitarian step and urged the government to also allow her younger daughter to meet her, saying family visits should be facilitated on humanitarian grounds. He said the matter should be viewed from a humanitarian perspective, beyond political differences, and called for consideration of similar relief for other family members.
