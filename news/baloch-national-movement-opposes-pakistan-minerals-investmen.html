Baloch National Movement opposes Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026, warns foreign investors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Baloch National Movement (BNM) has opposed the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026, scheduled to be held in Islamabad on November 12 and 13, alleging that Pakistan is seeking to bring foreign investors and multinational corporations into the exploitation of Balochistan's natural resources without the consent of the Baloch people. In a statement shared on X, a BNM spokesperson described the initiative as an attempt to turn the exploitation of Balochistan's mineral wealth into an international economic enterprise, while perpetuating what the organisation termed the region's poverty, deprivation and economic subjugation. The spokesperson said the forum was being presented under the banners of investment and development, but argued that its underlying objective was to deprive the Baloch nation of control over its natural resources, terming the alleged arrangement 'mineral colonialism'.

The BNM maintained that no foreign investment, mining project or natural resource extraction activity in Balochistan could be considered acceptable without the free, genuine and collective consent of the Baloch nation, and warned that any agreements concluded without that consent would remain illegitimate and unacceptable. The organisation urged international mining companies, investors and financial institutions to refrain from investing in Balochistan, and called on the United Nations and the wider international community to make the free consent of the Baloch nation a fundamental condition for any project involving the region's natural resources. The spokesperson said, 'Balochistan is not a mineral colony; it is the homeland of the Baloch nation,' and maintained that the authority to decide the fate of Balochistan's natural resources belonged to the Baloch people.
