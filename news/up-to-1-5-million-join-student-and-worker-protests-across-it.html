Up to 1.5 Million Join Student and Worker Protests Across Italy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thousands of students and workers took to the streets across Italy on Friday in largely peaceful demonstrations demanding greater funding for schools and relief from the soaring cost of living. The USB trade union estimated participation at up to 1.5 million people nationwide. The protests follow a recent wave inspired by demonstrations in France and Spain, with students setting up tent camps and occupying school buildings.

A general strike drew in truck drivers, taxi drivers and farmers protesting fuel price spikes and rising defence spending. Demonstrations in Rome, Milan and Turin saw a few clashes, while Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's government toughened laws on public gatherings after earlier confrontations. The government also announced a 175.2 million euro aid package aimed at cushioning households from fuel costs.

Organisers say the movement will continue until concrete measures address education funding and living standards.
