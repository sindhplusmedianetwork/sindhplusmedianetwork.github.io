Uzma Khan Becomes Emotional Outside Adiala Jail Over NRO Question

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Imran Khan's sister Uzma Khan became emotional outside Adiala Jail on Tuesday when a journalist put a question to her about an NRO. She asked who was even talking about an NRO and said those who deceive people were the ones raising the matter. Uzma Khan said that despite an order of the Supreme Court, Imran Khan had been taken to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences instead of Shifa International Hospital.

She described the move as a great deception. The report recalled that a day earlier Imran Khan had written an open letter to the chief justices, in which he alleged that his health was poor and that he was facing political victimisation in jail. In the letter, he also complained that basic rights were being denied to him behind bars.
