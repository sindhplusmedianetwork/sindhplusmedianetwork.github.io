Pakistan Rejects Taliban's 'Misguided Approach' on Terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's information ministry has rebuked the Afghan Taliban's stance on the conflict between the two countries, calling it a deeply misguided approach. The statement came in response to remarks by Taliban spokesperson Zabihullah Mujahid, who told Hurriyat Radio that Islamabad's 'wrong policies' were preventing a resolution. Mujahid claimed that 'Pakistan does not want to resolve the conflict with Afghanistan through talks' and that Islamabad lacked readiness for a peaceful resolution of differences.

The ministry said his remarks exposed the regime's misguided approach and its unwillingness to confront the root cause of deteriorating relations with Pakistan. It said the Taliban leadership blamed Islamabad's policies while ignoring the single most important issue poisoning bilateral ties: its persistent failure to address terrorism emanating from Afghan soil. The ministry added that Pakistan will not tolerate terrorist sanctuaries across its borders that threaten its security, nor allow its people to remain targets of terrorism.

Shifting blame, making hollow assurances and questioning Pakistan's policies, it said, would neither resolve the crisis nor absolve Kabul of its responsibilities.
