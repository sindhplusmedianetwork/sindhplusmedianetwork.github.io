ECC Lowers Export Threshold for SMEs, Approves Supplementary Grants

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Economic Coordination Committee (ECC) of the cabinet on Friday approved measures to facilitate and promote SME exports, along with technical supplementary grants (TSGs) for various ministries and divisions. A meeting of the ECC, chaired by Minister for Finance Muhammad Aurangzeb at the Finance Division, approved amendments to the Long-Term Export Growth Financing Facility, administered through Exim Bank of Pakistan, to facilitate small and medium enterprises and support emerging exporters. The amendments have lowered the minimum threshold of 80 percent exports to 50 percent within four years, and the ECC also directed a review of progress after six months.

The committee also approved a summary for the provision of TSG for execution of National Economic Initiatives projects, and on a summary of the Ministry of Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony, approved a TSG of Rs100 million for the Minorities Welfare Fund to support financial assistance, scholarships, marriage grants for orphan minority girls, emergency relief and repair of minority places of worship. The ECC also cleared three summaries of the Ministry of Interior and Narcotics Control, including Rs26.500 million for helicopter spare parts for Pakistan Rangers (Punjab), Rs100 million for salary requirements of the Social Media Protection and Regulatory Authority and the Social Media Complaint Council for FY27, and Rs67 million for annual repair and maintenance of helicopters under use by Frontier Corps Balochistan (North). It further approved modification of its earlier decision regarding the release of Rs2 billion for the Daanish Schools Authority, allowing utilisation of Rs500 million for operational expenses during FY27, and approved a TSG of Rs1 billion to settle the outstanding guaranteed throughput claim of Pak-Arab Pipeline Company Ltd.

The ECC also considered recommendations of a cabinet committee headed by the deputy prime minister regarding sugar exports to safeguard farmers' interests, reiterating the importance of deregulating the sugar sector.
