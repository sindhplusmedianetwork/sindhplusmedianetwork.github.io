Ten Pakistani climate businesses seek 51 million dollars after UK-backed support

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ten Pakistani businesses are seeking a combined 51 million US dollars over the next 12 months to expand practical climate solutions, following specialist support from the United Kingdom government. The businesses, working on cleaner energy, transport, construction, farming and waste solutions, presented their strengthened investment proposals directly to investors, banks and development finance institutions at a two-day Climate Finance Accelerator Pakistan Investor Roadshow in Karachi on October 27 and 28. The ten businesses span five sectors: agriculture and land use, waste and the circular economy, clean energy, building and construction, and electric mobility, and together they have a three-year investment pipeline worth 120 million dollars.

The programme, implemented by DAI on behalf of the UK government, gave each business technical, financial and gender equality and social inclusion support to prepare for investment. Between 2022 and 2024, the programme supported 22 projects in Pakistan and helped mobilise 40 million dollars in investment for climate projects. British Deputy High Commissioner in Karachi Alison Blackburne said the UK's climate partnership with Pakistan was increasingly focused on mobilising climate finance and strengthening adaptation and resilience.

Sindh Minister for Local Government Syed Nasir Hussain Shah, welcoming guests on behalf of the government, reaffirmed the commitment to working with the private sector and development partners to mobilise finance for climate action.
