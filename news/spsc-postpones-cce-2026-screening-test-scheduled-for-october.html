SPSC Postpones CCE-2026 Screening Test Scheduled for October 24

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC) has postponed the screening test of the Combined Competitive Examination-2026 (CCE-2026), which was scheduled to be held on October 24. According to a public notice issued by the Commission on Thursday, October 22, the revised date, time and venue of the screening test will be announced later. The Commission has advised all candidates to await further announcements regarding the examination schedule.

The screening test is the first stage of the CCE, the competitive examination for provincial management and other Sindh government posts, with passing marks earlier set at 40 percent under Advertisement 04/2026. The postponement follows major reforms approved by the Sindh cabinet on October 20, which raised the upper age limit for the CCE from 30 to 33 years, increased the number of attempts from three to five, reduced the minimum qualifying marks for the screening test from 40 percent to 33 percent, and approved computerised testing for single-paper examinations. Thousands of aspirants across Sindh had been preparing for the October 24 test, and the revised schedule is now awaited.
