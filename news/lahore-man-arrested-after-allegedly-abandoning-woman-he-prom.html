Lahore Man Arrested After Allegedly Abandoning Woman He Promised to Marry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Liaquatabad police have arrested a man accused of sexually assaulting a woman on the false promise of marriage. The FIR states that the accused, Ahmed, befriended the woman in a bazaar and assaulted her after promising to marry her. A child was later born, after which he refused to marry her.

The complaint says that when the woman insisted he honour his promise, he threatened her with serious consequences. Police traced and arrested the accused from Pindi Rajputan. A case has been registered and the investigation has been handed to the Gender Cell.

DIG Operations Faisal Kamran said those who exploit women will face strict action, adding that the protection of women and justice for them are the top priority.
