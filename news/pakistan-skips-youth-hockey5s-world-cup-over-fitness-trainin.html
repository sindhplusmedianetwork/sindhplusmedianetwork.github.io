Pakistan Skips Youth Hockey5s World Cup Over Fitness, Training Concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Hockey Federation has decided to stay out of the FIH Youth Hockey5s World Cup scheduled in Egypt from December 7 to 12, even though the national team had qualified for it. Federation sources say the decision is not about a shortage of funds but about gaps in fitness and specialised hockey5s training. The federation has decided to prioritise traditional hockey, the under-15 team and overall player fitness instead.

International fitness coaches will be brought in, and the under-18 format is being replaced by under-15. Officials view hockey5s as a supporting pathway, not a substitute for 11-a-side hockey.
