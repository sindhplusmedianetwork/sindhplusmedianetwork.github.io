Veena Malik returns to television with new morning show; fans surprised by her changed look

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Pakistani showbiz host and former actress Veena Malik has made a comeback to television after a long break with a new morning show titled 'Veena Hai Na'. The programme is being aired on a private news channel and follows the end of Fiza Ali's morning show. Malik's return has drawn wide attention on social media, where her recent videos and pictures are being shared and discussed extensively.

Many users have commented on her changed appearance and weight gain, with reactions ranging from surprise to criticism. At the same time, a large number of fans welcomed her back to the screen, expressing love and support for the host. Malik, who has remained a familiar face in Pakistani entertainment, appears to be easing back into the spotlight with the new programme.

Her return has once again made her one of the most talked-about personalities in the country's showbiz circles.
