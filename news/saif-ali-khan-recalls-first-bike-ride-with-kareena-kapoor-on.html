Saif Ali Khan recalls first bike ride with Kareena Kapoor on KBC

Bollywood star Saif Ali Khan shared a light-hearted memory from the early days of his relationship with Kareena Kapoor during his appearance on Kaun Banega Crorepati. Responding to host Amitabh Bachchan, Saif recalled taking Kareena for her first bike ride on the sets of Tashan, where the two were working together. He said Kareena, who had claimed to love bikes, clutched him in fear throughout the ride and kept shouting 'Stop stop stop' every few seconds, before finally admitting that she actually hated bike rides.

The anecdote drew laughter from the audience. Saif and Kareena married in 2012 and are parents to two sons, Taimur and Jehangir.
