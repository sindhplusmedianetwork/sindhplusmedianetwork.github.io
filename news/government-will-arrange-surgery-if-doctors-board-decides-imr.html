Government will arrange surgery if doctors' board decides Imran Khan needs it, says Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The prime minister's adviser, Rana Sanaullah, has said the government will provide the facility if the doctors' board decides that the PTI founder, Imran Khan, needs surgery. Speaking to Geo News, he said there has been no negligence in the treatment of the PTI founder. He added that Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi are being provided medical facilities in line with doctors' advice.

Sanaullah said the PTI had wanted disruption and unrest, and turned to talks after failing in that effort. He said the government tried to settle matters in a proper way, but the other side refused, adding that the impression that any deal or relief is on the table is wrong. He said the government's policy is clear that issues should be resolved through dialogue while sitting together.

On the provinces' matter, he said it will come before parliament, where it will be debated, and that it is not an issue one person can settle alone.
