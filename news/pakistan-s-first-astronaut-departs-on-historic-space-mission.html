Pakistan's first astronaut departs on historic space mission

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's first astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, is set to depart on a historic space mission from China's Jiuquan Space Centre. The departure phase of the mission will begin at 5am Pakistan time, while a farewell ceremony will be held at 1:40am. The farewell ceremony and the different stages of the mission will be broadcast live on Pakistan Television.

Around four to five hours after the flight, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali will enter China's space station, where he will stay for about a week along with two Chinese astronauts. During his stay, he will carry out various scientific experiments at the Tiangong space station. The flight is being described as an important milestone in Pakistan's space journey, giving the country's participation in space research new importance at the global level.
