UK Government Takes 32 Services Offline Over Global PHP Security Flaw

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read

The British government has taken 32 of its services offline and partly disabled four more amid a suspected global cybersecurity issue. Minister Baroness Lloyd confirmed the development in the House of Lords on Thursday. She said the action followed the discovery of a serious security flaw in PHP, the programming language used by most websites around the world.

Work is underway to address the security issue, and precautionary measures are being taken for security updates, she added. Downing Street said no major service available to the British public had been affected. Some limited government websites were shut down until the issues are resolved, but officials stressed this did not mean they had been hacked.
