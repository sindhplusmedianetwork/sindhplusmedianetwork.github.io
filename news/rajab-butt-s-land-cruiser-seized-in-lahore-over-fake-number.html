Rajab Butt's Land Cruiser seized in Lahore over fake number plate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore police have seized TikTok star Rajab Butt's Land Cruiser after discovering it was fitted with a fake and non-standard number plate. The vehicle was stopped near the NFC Bridge, where officers found the irregular plate on the SUV. The Land Cruiser was being driven by Musa Khan, who could not give police a satisfactory explanation about the number plate.

Police registered an FIR against the driver and shifted the vehicle to the Sundar police station. According to the DIG Operations spokesperson, initial information confirms that the Land Cruiser belongs to YouTuber Rajab Butt, and further legal proceedings on its ownership and registration are underway. No criminal charges have been announced against Butt personally at this stage.

DIG Operations Faisal Kamran said Lahore police are running a city-wide crackdown on fake and non-standard number plates, under which 1,508 cases have been registered so far, and warned that no one would receive preferential treatment regardless of public profile.
