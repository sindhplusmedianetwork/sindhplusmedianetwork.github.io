Daniyal Zafar Announces Temporary Break From Showbiz Over Health Strain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani singer and actor Daniyal Zafar has announced a temporary break from showbiz, saying continuous work has put his health under strain. In an Instagram story, he wrote that he feels he has reached the last limit of his strength, after travelling constantly for two years and rarely staying in one place for more than a few days. He said working in acting and music at the same time is extremely difficult, and that the busy routine has affected his health.

He said he has been advised to give priority to his health, and has decided to pause all professional activity for now so he can focus on his recovery and return fully healthy.
