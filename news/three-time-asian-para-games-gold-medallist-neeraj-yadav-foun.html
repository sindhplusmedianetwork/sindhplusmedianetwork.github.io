Three-time Asian Para Games gold medallist Neeraj Yadav found dead at New Delhi home

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three-time Asian Para Games gold medallist Neeraj Yadav, 42, has been found dead at his residence in New Delhi, according to Indian media reports. The incident is initially being described as suicide, though the actual cause of death has not yet been confirmed. Indian para athletics coach S Satyanarain confirmed Yadav's death and said he was shocked by the news.

Yadav, who belonged to Uttar Pradesh, suffered a physical disability after contracting polio at the age of seven. He competed in the F55 category of para athletics in javelin throw and discus throw, winning javelin gold at the Jakarta 2018 and Hangzhou 2022 Asian Para Games and discus gold at Hangzhou. He played wheelchair tennis from 2005 to 2012 before switching to para athletics.
