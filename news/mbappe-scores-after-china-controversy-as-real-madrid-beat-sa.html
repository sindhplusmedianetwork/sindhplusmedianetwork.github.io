Mbappe Scores After China Controversy as Real Madrid Beat Santander

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kylian Mbappe scored from the penalty spot as Real Madrid eased to a 3-0 win over Racing Santander in La Liga. Jude Bellingham and Yan Diomande were also on target for the hosts. The victory keeps third-place Real Madrid within three points of leaders Barcelona in the title race.

The win came after a turbulent week for Mbappe, who lost the Ballon d'Or award to Harry Kane. The French forward also apologised over a social media storm surrounding a video that joked about former Chinese leader Mao Zedong. Chinese state television subsequently dropped this weekend's Spanish league fixtures from its broadcast schedule.

Mbappe answered the controversy with a composed performance and a goal that helped seal an important win. Real Madrid will look to carry this momentum into the next round of fixtures.
