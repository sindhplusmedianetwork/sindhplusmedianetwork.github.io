NA Sets Aside Day's Agenda for General Debate; PPL Headquarters Shift Opposed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Assembly set aside the day's agenda to allow a general debate in the house. During the debate, PPP legislators strongly opposed the proposed relocation of the Pakistan Petroleum Limited headquarters from Karachi to Islamabad. Members from Punjab complained about the rising costs of DAP fertiliser, urea and agricultural machinery, saying farmers were under severe pressure.

The debate covered a wide range of public issues as lawmakers used the open floor. The session reflected growing parliamentary concern over economic pressures facing ordinary citizens.
