Woman Gifts Boyfriend GTA 6 — With a Signed Agreement of Conditions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman has gifted her boyfriend the video game GTA 6 along with a signed agreement setting out conditions for playing it, and the story has gone viral on social media. The game is due to be released on November 19, 2026, thirteen years after GTA 5, which has sold about 230 million copies so far. A user named Ed shared a photo on social media platform X of the gift he found when he reached home, including the game code, a Vice City poster and the paper agreement.

The agreement says that in return for the game, he must take a break every few hours to check on his girlfriend, and pause the game immediately whenever she speaks to him. It also says he will not spend more than 50 percent of his free time playing GTA 6, and that he will remember that Lucia, the game's main character, is not his real girlfriend. The agreement, dated November 11, 2026, carries the signatures of both of them.
