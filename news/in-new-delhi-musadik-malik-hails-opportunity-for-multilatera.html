In New Delhi, Musadik Malik Hails Opportunity for Multilateral Dialogue

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

After arriving in New Delhi to attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation's Civilisational Dialogue Forum, Minister for Climate Change Dr Musadik Malik hailed the occasion as a tremendous opportunity to have a multilateral dialogue. This is the first visit to India by a Pakistani minister since Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari attended the SCO meeting in the Indian state of Goa in May 2023. Pakistan has assumed the rotating chairmanship of the SCO Council of Heads of State for the 2026-27 term and is scheduled to host the organisation's summit in Islamabad in August 2027.

Speaking to a news agency, Mr Malik said cultures and civilisations cut across boundaries and called it a very welcoming sign that the two sides were talking about their history, geography and civilisations. On terrorism, he said both sides must ensure transparency and bring forward evidence, adding that Pakistan's objective is to establish a transparent mechanism that would help restore trust. He said the challenges facing the entire neighbourhood need to be addressed through a structured mechanism based on concrete evidence rather than finger-pointing.
