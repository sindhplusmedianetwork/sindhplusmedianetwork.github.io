Pakistani Three-Wheelers Go Global as Sazgar Opens Plant in Philippines

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Sazgar Engineering Works Limited has opened a new assembly plant in the Philippines, marking a major step for the country's automotive exports. The plant was inaugurated by Philippines President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.

The Lahore-headquartered manufacturer will assemble passenger and commercial three-wheelers along with electric vehicles at the facility. The plant is designed to produce about 12,000 three-wheeled vehicles every year. Company officials said the expansion will strengthen Sazgar's presence in Southeast Asia.

Sazgar already exports three-wheelers and auto parts to more than 20 countries, including Japan, the UAE, Sri Lanka and Nigeria.
