Award show organisers admitted their mistake, says YouTuber Iqra Kanwal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Popular Pakistani YouTuber Iqra Kanwal has made an important revelation about the dispute over a recent award show, claiming that the management has admitted its mistake. In a video that appeared on social media, Iqra Kanwal said the award show's management team conceded that an error occurred while finalising the award categories. According to Iqra Kanwal, the management acknowledged that a mistake was made in the process of giving final shape to the different categories.

That error caused the controversy, she said. The report does not name the award show or give further details of the dispute beyond her account in the video.
