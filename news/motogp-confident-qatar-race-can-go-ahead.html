MotoGP confident Qatar race can go ahead

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

MotoGP is set to go ahead with the Qatar Grand Prix even as a group of riders demand more assurances about travelling to the region, with organisers insisting that all available safety measures are in place. The race at the Lusail International Circuit was originally scheduled for April but was postponed to November 6-8 due to the US-Israeli war against Iran. MotoGP deputy CEO Carlos Ezpeleta visited Qatar on Thursday to review preparations.

Despite the riders' concerns, championship officials remain confident the event can be held safely.
