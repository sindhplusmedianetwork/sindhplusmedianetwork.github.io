Pakistan Worst Hit by Hunger in South Asia, Says 2026 Global Hunger Index

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan recorded the highest score in South Asia on the 2026 Global Hunger Index released this week, reflecting a worsening food security crisis driven by economic instability, climate shocks and global conflicts. The report was published jointly by Welthungerhilfe, the Institute for International Law of Peace and Armed Conflict at Ruhr University Bochum, and the Swiss organisation Helvetas under the theme No Security Without Food Security. It reveals that progress in South Asia has largely stagnated, with conditions in Pakistan and Afghanistan deteriorating since 2018.

In Pakistan, economic instability and sharp food price inflation have eroded households' purchasing power and constrained access to food. Undernourishment in the country has climbed from low in 2018 to medium, while child wasting has risen toward the high threshold. Across South Asia, the situation remains serious with a regional score of 23.5, though most progress occurred before 2018.

The report warned that high levels of acute food insecurity will persist across the region, and noted that heavy rains and flooding caused localised crop losses in parts of Pakistan.
