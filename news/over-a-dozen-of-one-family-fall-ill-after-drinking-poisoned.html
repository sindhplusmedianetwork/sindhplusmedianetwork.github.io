Over a dozen of one family fall ill after drinking 'poisoned' milk in Larkana

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 12 members of a single family have fallen ill in Larkana after allegedly drinking poisonous milk. The affected family members were rushed to the emergency ward of Chandka Hospital. Police said the family had bought the milk from a milkman, Abdul Majeed Chandio, and used it at home.

The family has demanded that samples of the milk be sent for laboratory tests. They have also called for a transparent investigation into the incident..
