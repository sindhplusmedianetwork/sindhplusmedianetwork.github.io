Eight foreign ministers oppose military action against Iran at Istanbul meeting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign ministers of eight regional and European countries held a closed session in Istanbul and opposed military action against Iran, calling for the Strait of Hormuz to be reopened. The meeting brought together Turkey, Egypt, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia with Britain, France, Germany and Italy. Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan told reporters that the first four regional countries met first, discussing fears of a possible attack on Iran and recent Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia.

The four European countries then joined the session, which reviewed the wider effects of the ongoing conflicts. Fidan said the region's two major conflicts threaten key shipping routes including the Strait of Hormuz, the Bab el-Mandeb Strait and the Black Sea, severely affecting energy supplies, food security and free trade and increasing pressure worldwide. He said all participating countries agreed that negotiations were needed to stop the destruction from spreading further, and that no country supported military action against Iran.

Fidan also stressed the need for a ceasefire and a comprehensive peace agreement between Ukraine and Russia, and called for safe navigation of ships in the Black Sea to be ensured.
