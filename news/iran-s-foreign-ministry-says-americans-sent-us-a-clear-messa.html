Iran's foreign ministry says Americans sent US a 'clear message' in midterms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's foreign ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei said on Wednesday that the American people had sent a 'clear message' to Washington in the US midterm elections, a day after Democrats captured control of Congress from President Donald Trump's Republicans. In a post on X, Baqaei said Washington had dragged the country into an 'unlawful and atrocious war' against Iran and that its calculations had failed because it had 'fundamentally misunderstood Iran'. He warned that if the United States persisted with the war, 'the consequences of this grave miscalculation will extend far beyond what you have already witnessed'.

The remarks came as international investors weighed the fallout from the election results, with oil prices slipping in volatile trading as markets awaited Washington's next move in the US-Israeli war with Iran. The Democratic sweep of both chambers of Congress has been widely interpreted as a rebuke of Trump's foreign policy, including the eight-month-long war with Iran that has sent fuel prices soaring. Baqaei added that Iran was not a nation that could be intimidated through threats or brought to its knees through aggression.
