New e-passport system brings long waits and hardship for citizens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The new e-passport system, introduced to make the process easier, is instead causing hardship for citizens, who are being forced to wait for hours at passport centres in Lahore and elsewhere. Slow processing and repeated delays have left applicants standing in long queues, while staff are also struggling to cope with the technical requirements of the new system. As large numbers of people continue to apply, crowds at the centres keep growing.

Citizens pointed out that the five-year, 36-page ordinary e-passport costs Rs9,000, while the urgent version costs Rs15,000, yet timely service is still not available. They have demanded proper training for staff, fixes to the technical problems, and prompt processing of applications so that passport seekers are no longer made to wait for hours.
