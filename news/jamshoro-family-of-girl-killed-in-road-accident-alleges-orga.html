Jamshoro: Family of Girl Killed in Road Accident Alleges Organs Removed During Post-Mortem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The family of a young girl killed in a road accident on the Indus Highway in front of Sindh University, Jamshoro, last night has accused a doctor of removing her organs during the post-mortem. Police surgeon Dr Waseem rejected the charge, saying the post-mortem was carried out according to the rules and principles and calling the allegations baseless. He explained that the girl had died in the accident at night and her body was brought for post-mortem in the morning.

The family's claim has raised concern in the area. Police have not announced any further inquiry into the matter so far.
