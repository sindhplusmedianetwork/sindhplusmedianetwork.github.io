Brazilian Court Gives Meta 24 Hours to Prove AI Neutrality Before Runoff

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Brazilian court has given Meta 24 hours to demonstrate that its artificial intelligence systems do not favour either candidate ahead of next Sunday's presidential runoff. The order by the Superior Electoral Court covers Meta's algorithmic systems across Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. It follows a complaint from President Lula's campaign alleging that Meta suppressed material favourable to him while permitting hostile AI-generated posts against him.

Meta denied giving preferential treatment and said its rules apply equally to all political figures. The ruling adds to global scrutiny of how AI systems shape election campaigns.
