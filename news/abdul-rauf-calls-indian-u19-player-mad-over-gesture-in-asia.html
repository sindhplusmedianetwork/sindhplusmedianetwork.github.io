Abdul Rauf Calls Indian U19 Player 'Mad' Over Gesture in Asia Cup Final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Pakistan fast bowler Abdul Rauf has criticised an Indian Under-19 player's inappropriate gesture towards a Pakistani player during the Under-19 Asia Cup final. Sharing a video of the incident on social media platform X, Rauf wrote that the player looked completely mad and abnormal. He said such players should be completely banned from cricket grounds, or the grace of cricket would be lost, and the Indian cricket board should ban him immediately.

Former Pakistan batter Ahmed Shehzad also commented on the incident, saying a victory celebration should express happiness and joy and should not become a means of aggressive behaviour or unnecessary provocation. He said these are young players and they should be taught that the spirit of the game is not limited to winning or losing but goes beyond it.
