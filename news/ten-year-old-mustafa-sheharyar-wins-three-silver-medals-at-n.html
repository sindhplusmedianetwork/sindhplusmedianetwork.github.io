Ten-year-old Mustafa Sheharyar wins three silver medals at National Swimming Championship

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ten-year-old Punjab swimmer Mustafa Sheharyar Rana has won three silver medals at the National Swimming Championship. The championship was held at the KMC Sports Complex in Karachi from November 13 to 15. He claimed an individual silver in the 50m backstroke in the Under-10 category.

He was also part of the silver-winning 4x100m freestyle relay team. His third silver came in the 4x50m medley relay. The young swimmer's three-medal haul marks an impressive performance at the national event.
