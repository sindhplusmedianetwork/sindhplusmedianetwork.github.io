Army Successfully Test-Fires Extended-Range Fatah-2 Hypersonic Ballistic Missile

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Army on Tuesday conducted a successful test launch of the extended-range version of its Fatah-2 hypersonic ballistic missile, the military's media wing announced. According to the Inter-Services Public Relations, the weapon system is equipped with advanced navigation and state-of-the-art avionics. It is capable of engaging targets with high precision and defeating enemy missile defence systems due to its unique trajectory and manoeuvrability features, the ISPR said.

The extended-range variant will further enhance the Army's reach and its ability to engage targets deep inside enemy territory. The launch was witnessed by Army Rocket Force Command chief Lt Gen Nauman Zakaria, senior army officers, scientists and engineers involved in the programme. The ARFC commander appreciated the dedication and technical prowess of the scientists and engineers behind the system.

The president, the prime minister and the chief of defence forces congratulated the scientists and engineers on the successful test. Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi also congratulated the nation, calling the test a significant development in the country's defence sector. The ISPR did not disclose the missile's maximum range or flight details during the test.
