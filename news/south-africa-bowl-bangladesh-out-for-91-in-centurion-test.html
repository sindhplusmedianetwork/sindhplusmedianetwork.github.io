South Africa bowl Bangladesh out for 91 in Centurion Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

South Africa bowled Bangladesh out for 91 runs in 33.3 overs on the opening day of the second Test at Super Sport Park in Centurion. Captain Aiden Markram won the toss and elected to field. Kagiso Rabada took 3-23, Marco Jansen 3-17 and Anrich Nortje 2-10.

Mahfijul Islam top-scored for Bangladesh with 23, as only three batters reached double figures. The match was reported on November 23.
