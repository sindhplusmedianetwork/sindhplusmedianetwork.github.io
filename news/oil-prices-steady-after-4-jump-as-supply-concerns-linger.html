Oil Prices Steady After 4% Jump as Supply Concerns Linger

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Oil prices were little changed in early Asian trade on Friday after a sharp rally in the previous session. Prices had risen 4 percent on Thursday to a five-month high. The jump was driven by concerns that Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia could disrupt global oil supplies.

Traders remained cautious as the security situation in the Middle East stayed tense. Market analysts said investors were watching for any further attacks or escalation. The stability in prices on Friday suggested the market was pausing after Thursday's surge.

Any new development in the region could quickly move prices again.
