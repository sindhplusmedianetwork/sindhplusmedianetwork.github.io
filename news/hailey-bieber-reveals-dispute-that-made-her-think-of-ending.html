Hailey Bieber Reveals Dispute That Made Her Think of Ending Her Life

Well-known American model Hailey Bieber has revealed that a continuous hate campaign and threats on social media over her dispute with singer Selena Gomez caused her so much mental suffering that she began thinking about ending her life. Speaking at an event organised by The New York Times, Hailey Bieber said strangers on social media used to send her messages every day telling her to end her life, and she was also abused in public places. She said the intensity of the hate forced her to think that it would be better if she were not in this world.

Hailey Bieber said criticism on social media still affects her and she cannot pretend that these things make no difference to her. During the conversation, she said she tries to deal with negative behaviour on social media by limiting her use of her mobile phone and focusing on her present. The dispute between Hailey and Selena had basically started from speculation on social media that there was rivalry between Hailey Bieber and Selena Gomez over Canadian singer Justin Bieber, who married Hailey Bieber in 2018 after being in a relationship with Selena Gomez for about eight years.
