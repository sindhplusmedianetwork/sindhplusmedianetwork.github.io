'School Meals Foster Healthy Learning Environment,' Says Punjab Education Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government's initiative in South Punjab to address nutritional deficiencies among primary schoolchildren by providing them milk and fortified biscuits is part of efforts to create a healthier learning environment, according to Punjab Education Minister Rana Sakandar Hayat. Under the initiative in South Punjab districts, children enrolled in government primary schools are provided milk and fortified biscuits to help meet their dietary requirements. The minister said the programme aimed to improve children's health so they could learn better at school.

Officials said the meals were distributed in government primary schools across the South Punjab districts. The initiative is part of the provincial government's wider effort to support children's health and education.
