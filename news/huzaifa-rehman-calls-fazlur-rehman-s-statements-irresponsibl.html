Huzaifa Rehman Calls Fazlur Rehman's Statements Irresponsible, Defends KP Governor's Rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Special Assistant to the Prime Minister Huzaifa Rehman has termed Maulana Fazlur Rehman's statements irresponsible, saying the JUI-F chief turned the anti-terrorism operation into a 'minerals war'. Speaking at a press conference in Islamabad, he said making the operation controversial was an injustice to the sacrifices of the martyrs, and added that no one is bigger than the state. He said the imposition of governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa was aimed at restoring peace and order in the province.

He praised the sacrifices rendered by the KP police and the armed forces in the fight against terrorism. He stressed that national security and public safety must take precedence over political point-scoring.
