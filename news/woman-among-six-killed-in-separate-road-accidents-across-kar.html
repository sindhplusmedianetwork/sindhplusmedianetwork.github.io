Woman Among Six Killed in Separate Road Accidents Across Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Traffic accidents claimed six lives in different areas of the city on Friday. In one road mishap, a woman pedestrian was killed after being hit by a speeding motorcycle near Darul Sehat Hospital in Gulistan-e-Jauhar. The woman was shifted to Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre in seriously injured condition where she was pronounced dead, and the deceased was identified as 26-year-old Kausar, while police took the motorcyclist Hasnain into custody and launched an investigation into the accident.

In another traffic accident, a 65-year-old man, identified as Qasim Khan, son of Bilal Khan, was killed when an unidentified vehicle hit his motorcycle near Ayub Goth Sunday Bazaar in the Sohrab Goth area, and his body was shifted to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital while the driver fled the scene along with the vehicle. Two people were killed when the motorcycle they were riding skidded near Bismillah Market at New Sabzi Mandi on the Superhighway in the SITE Super Highway Industrial Area. The identity of one youth could not be immediately established, although he was believed to be around 30 years old, while the other victim was identified as 16-year-old Abbas, son of Mumtaz.

A young motorcyclist was killed after being hit by a passenger bus near Urdu Chowk in Sector 10, Orangi Town. The deceased, believed to be around 25 years old, had no identification documents in his pocket, and the bus driver fled the scene along with the vehicle.
