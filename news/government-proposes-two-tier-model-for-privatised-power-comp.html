Government Proposes Two-Tier Model for Privatised Power Companies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has proposed a two-tier business model for privatised power distribution companies under which a successful bidder would retain poles and wires but could auction the functions of selling electricity, billing and collection to other private contractors, including legacy capacity payments. The model, subject to approval by the power sector reform steering committee and the federal cabinet, envisages regulating only the buyer of the distribution companies, to be called the supplier of last resort. Third parties, dubbed competitive suppliers, would operate in a deregulated fashion, but only after buying the legacy capacity that costs consumers Rs1.7 trillion in annual capacity payments.

The model will be placed before the power sector reform steering committee and the federal cabinet for approval. Under the model, only the buyer of the distribution companies would be regulated.
