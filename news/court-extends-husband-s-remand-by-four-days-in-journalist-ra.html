Court extends husband's remand by four days in journalist Rabia Ahsan's murder case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Rawalpindi court on Tuesday extended the physical remand of Ahsan Tahir, husband of television anchorperson and journalist Rabia Ahsan, by four days in connection with her murder. Senior Civil Judge Muhammad Nasrullah Wattoo approved the extension after the investigating officer sought ten days of further remand for interrogation. The court had granted police a one-day physical remand of the suspect on Monday.

The court directed police to complete the investigation and produce the suspect before it again on November 13, and also directed the investigating officer to submit a complete investigation report and the post-mortem report at the next hearing. No lawyer appeared on behalf of the suspect, and police took Ahsan back to the police station after the hearing. The case was registered on the complaint of Rabia's brother, Khurram Ahmed, who accused Ahsan of shooting his sister at their home in Gulshanabad Sector II, on Adiala Road, on Sunday.

According to the FIR, Rabia's mother and sister were in the lawn at around 2:30pm when they heard a gunshot from the bedroom, and her sister rushed inside to find Rabia lying on the floor in a pool of blood while Ahsan was holding a pistol. The FIR stated that Ahsan told police he had fired at his wife with the same weapon, and police said he had failed to produce a licence or permit for the pistol. The case was registered under Section 302 of the Pakistan Penal Code and Section 13(2)(a) of the Punjab Arms Amendment Ordinance.

Rabia, a 41-year-old anchorperson associated with 92 News, had presented her last programme on a mainstream television channel on Saturday night, and she was survived by two daughters.
