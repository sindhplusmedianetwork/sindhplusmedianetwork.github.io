Court orders child labour charges added in Fatima death case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Judicial Magistrate Naima Iffat ordered that Section 3 of the law on child labour and exploitation be added against the accused in the minor Fatima death case. Provisions on violence against children and abandoning minor children will also be added, and a further three-day physical remand was approved. According to a NADRA assessment cited in the investigating officer's report, the girl's age was 11 years and 11 months.

The court directed determination of the relationship and purpose for which the girl was at the FIA officer's house. It also sought any financial benefit, statements of the accused's husband, employees and neighbours, and the husband's role. The court further directed inquiry into keeping the girl at an official residence for three years and the circumstances of her death.
