Ambassador Urges US Tech Firms to Tap Pakistan's Investment Opportunities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Ambassador to the United States, Rizwan Saeed Sheikh, has urged American technology companies to seize broad and profitable partnership opportunities in Pakistan. Addressing "Pak-US Tech Connect 2026" in Dallas, Texas, he pitched Pakistan as an attractive destination for tech investment. The event was organised by the Pakistan Embassy in Washington DC, the Houston consulate, the IT Ministry and the Pakistan Software Export Board, and drew Pakistani tech firms, US business leaders, investors and members of the Pakistani-American community.

The ambassador noted that more than 60 percent of Pakistan's population is under 30. The PSEB chief executive said IT exports had grown 21 percent annually over four years, reaching about $435 million in September 2026. Follow-up conferences are scheduled for October 21 in Washington DC and October 23 in Chicago.
