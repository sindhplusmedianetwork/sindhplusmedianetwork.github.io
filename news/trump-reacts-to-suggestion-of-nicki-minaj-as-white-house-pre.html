Trump Reacts to Suggestion of Nicki Minaj as White House Press Secretary

US President Donald Trump has weighed in on the surprising suggestion that rapper Nicki Minaj could become the next White House press secretary. Speaking to podcast host Benny Johnson, Trump called it a good idea and said he would have to think about it. The speculation started after Minaj appeared alongside Trump at an election rally in Michigan on October 25.

White House communications director Steven Cheung added fuel to the rumours by posting on X that they may have found their new press secretary in Minaj. No formal announcement has been made so far. The idea has drawn mixed reactions on social media, with supporters cheering the unconventional pick and critics questioning her suitability for the role.
