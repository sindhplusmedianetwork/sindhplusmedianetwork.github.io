Actress Momina Iqbal's sister Ramsha Iqbal completes graduation in Australia

Pakistani actress Momina Iqbal's sister Ramsha Iqbal has completed her graduation from a university in Australia. The actress expressed happiness and pride over her sister's achievement. According to 24 News, Momina shared an emotional message on social media while celebrating her sister's graduation in Australia.

In her message she remembered her late father, saying this was a day he had eagerly awaited. She expressed pride in her sister's success along with good wishes for her. The graduation marked a proud moment for the family, celebrated publicly by the actress on social media.
