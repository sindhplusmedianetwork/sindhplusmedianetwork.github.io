Sindh govt moves to stop rival rallies over new provinces on November 29

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Senior Minister Sharjeel Memon said the provincial government had formed a committee over planned rallies on November 29. The committee will approach a nationalist party and the MQM-P. It will urge them to cancel their rallies planned over the issue of new provinces.

Memon warned that rival demonstrations could lead to clashes and unrest. Addressing a press conference in Karachi, he said the committee would urge both parties not to bring their opposing positions to the streets. He also defended Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari's remarks on a proposed "system reset", saying the PPP chairman had been warning about consequences, not threatening anyone.
