Pakistan take control after bowling Sri Lanka out for 98 in second Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's fast bowlers dismissed Sri Lanka for just 98 on the opening day of the second Test at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Tuesday. Mohammad Abbas, Razaullah and Aamer Jamal claimed three wickets apiece as Sri Lanka were bowled out in 35.3 overs. Pakistan won the toss and chose to field on a green-tinged pitch, and the total was the lowest by any team at the venue.

In reply, Pakistan reached 121 for 3 at stumps to take a 23-run lead. The reply was built on an 80-run opening stand between Tayyab Arif, who made 46, and Azan Awais, who remained unbeaten on 47. Pakistan will resume on the second day looking to build a big first-innings lead.
