Pakistan, Qatar Close to Resolving Iran Crisis, Claims US Envoy Tom Barrack

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States' ambassador to Turkiye, Tom Barrack, has claimed that diplomatic efforts on the Iran crisis are close to a breakthrough. He said Pakistan, Qatar and other allies are near bringing the parties together to resolve the standoff. According to Barrack, major steps toward finalising a framework to end the crisis are likely within the next week or two.

The claim comes amid continued tensions in the region. Neither Iran nor the United States has yet confirmed the timeline he described.
