Severe Typhoon Devastates Philippines, Over a Million Moved to Safety, Emergency Declared

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An extremely powerful typhoon has struck the northern and eastern coastal provinces of the Philippines, with winds of 200 km/h and torrential rain causing widespread destruction. More than one million people were moved to official shelters before the storm arrived. Emergency was declared in the affected provinces, and schools and government offices were closed.

Falling trees and power poles cut electricity to hundreds of thousands of homes. Air and sea transport were suspended, leaving thousands of passengers stranded. The army and rescue teams have begun relief operations in the affected areas.
