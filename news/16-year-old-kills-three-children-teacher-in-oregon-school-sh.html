16-Year-Old Kills Three Children, Teacher in Oregon School Shooting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 16-year-old boy opened fire at a school in the US state of Oregon, killing three children and a teacher, according to a foreign news agency. The children who died were aged 5, 8 and 11, while the teacher was 54 years old. The teenage shooter was also killed — shot dead by a person who was nearby at the time of the attack.

US authorities say further investigation into the shooting is under way. No further details about the motive or the circumstances of the attack have been reported so far.
