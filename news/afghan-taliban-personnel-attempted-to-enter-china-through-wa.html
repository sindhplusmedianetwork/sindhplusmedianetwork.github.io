Afghan Taliban Personnel Attempted to Enter China Through Wakhan Corridor, Stern Warning Issued

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Afghan Taliban personnel attempted to infiltrate the Chinese border through the Wakhan Corridor, the narrow strip of Afghan territory that touches China. According to a report based on the Afghan journal Afghanistan International, Chinese border authorities responded with a stern warning, ordering the Taliban personnel to leave the area immediately. China has kept the Wakhan border route closed over fears that terrorists could use it to slip into Chinese territory.

Beijing has also tightened security along its border with Afghanistan, installing barbed wire and surveillance cameras at sensitive points. The incident comes amid rising concern among Afghanistan's neighbours over cross-border terrorism and infiltration attempts under the Taliban regime. Regional countries have been placed on alert as such incidents continue to surface along shared frontiers.
