Trump's New Press Secretary Admits She Failed the Bar Exam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New White House Press Secretary Katie Zacharia has admitted that she failed the bar exam, after questions were raised about the legal credentials she had claimed on television and social media. Zacharia, 41, had presented herself as a lawyer and listed herself as general counsel of a California-based conservative nonprofit. In a Fox News interview on Thursday, host Sean Hannity asked her directly whether she had taken the bar exam but not passed it, and she answered yes.

She said she never attempted the exam a second time. Speaking separately to Real America's Voice, Zacharia said she had been distracted by plans to move to China with her husband, where she later worked at a Beijing law firm. She said many people in the legal field had told her she could describe herself as a lawyer because of her law degree and business-law experience in China, and stressed that she only offered legal analysis rather than legal advice.

The Campaign for Accountability, a political ethics organisation, has called on the California Bar Association to investigate, saying that calling oneself a lawyer without a licence in California is a crime. Zacharia, who was appointed by President Donald Trump last week, held her first briefing on Tuesday, answering questions from only three journalists. She succeeded Karoline Leavitt, who stepped down in August to care for her young children.
