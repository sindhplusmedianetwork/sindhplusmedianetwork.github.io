Frontier flight cabin flooded after bathroom tap fails at 33,000 feet

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Frontier Airlines flight travelling from Denver to Detroit was forced to make an emergency landing in Chicago after a faulty bathroom tap flooded the cabin with water. Water poured from a tap at the front of the aircraft and spread across the cabin floor and the aisle. The spreading water raised fears of an electrical short circuit on board.

A passenger who worked as a plumber stepped in and helped stop the water. The aircraft landed safely in Chicago, and the emergency ended without harm to anyone on board. No passenger or crew member was reported injured.
