Israel Strikes Gaza's Nasser Hospital, Says Three Hamas Members Killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israel's military said it struck Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis in southern Gaza. It claimed it targeted only one room and floor where Hamas commanders were present. The military named three Hamas members it says were killed, including an engineering unit commander involved in the 7 October abduction of an Israeli woman.

Gaza officials said six people were killed and at least 20 wounded in the strike, including a surgeon and a young boy. The report said the main target, the new commander of Hamas's Khan Younis brigade, Mohammed Abu Ismail, survived but is missing. Nasser Hospital is one of the main medical facilities still operating in southern Gaza.
