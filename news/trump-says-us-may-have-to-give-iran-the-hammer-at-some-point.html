Trump Says US May Have to Give Iran 'the Hammer' at Some Point

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has reiterated his threat to resume military strikes against Iran, telling a campaign rally that he may have to give Tehran "the hammer" at some unspecified time. "And the only reason why I'm not finished with Iran is I want to be nice. I want to try and save all those lives if I can," Trump was quoted as saying by AFP.

According to Axios, Trump also held a phone call with Israel's Benjamin Netanyahu to discuss the next move in the US-Israeli war on Iran, with Netanyahu pressing for strikes immediately after the US midterm elections. Trump is said to be eager to let negotiations play out instead. Meanwhile, Iranian and Qatari officials held a third straight day of talks aimed at pushing for a deal to end the war.

Oil prices slipped more than 3 per cent on the talks, with Brent crude futures falling to $109.75 a barrel. The developments come amid heightened military activity across the region, including tanker attacks in the Gulf and escalating exchanges between Saudi Arabia and the Houthis.
