Lahore High Court Orders End to Parchi System in Punjab Hospitals Within Four Weeks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has ordered the abolition of the traditional parchi, or token, system in all government hospitals of Punjab within four weeks. The court directed that patients must receive immediate treatment without first standing in long queues for registration slips. Justice Malik Javed Iqbal Wains issued the order while hearing a petition about the lack of facilities in public hospitals.

The special secretary for health assured the court that the order would be implemented. The court adjourned the case for six weeks. The decision is expected to bring major relief to thousands of patients who visit government hospitals daily.
