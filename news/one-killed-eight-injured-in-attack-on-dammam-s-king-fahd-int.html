One Killed, Eight Injured in Attack on Dammam's King Fahd International Airport

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

One person was killed and eight others were injured in an attack on King Fahd International Airport in the Saudi city of Dammam. Saudi Arabia's General Authority of Civil Aviation confirmed the casualties and said the attack had also caused material damage, the extent of which was still being assessed. The Saudi military coalition said its forces had intercepted and destroyed four drones in the kingdom's Eastern Province.

Meanwhile, the US State Department ordered non-emergency US government staff posted in Saudi Arabia and their family members to leave the kingdom. The airport strike followed an attack on the Shaybah oil field, where falling debris from a drone intercepted by air defences killed two people at an Aramco facility. The civil aviation authority said further updates would be issued once inspections and damage assessments were complete.
