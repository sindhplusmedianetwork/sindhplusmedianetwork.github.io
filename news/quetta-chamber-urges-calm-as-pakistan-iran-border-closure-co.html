Quetta Chamber Urges Calm as Pakistan-Iran Border Closure Continues

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Quetta Chamber of Commerce and Industry on Sunday urged traders affected by the Pakistan-Iran border closure to remain calm and disregard what it called 'negative propaganda'. The chamber said the Ministry of Commerce had assured it that the shutdown was technical and temporary, aimed at addressing incidents on the Pakistan-Iran highway and certain customs issues. The FIA immigration gate at Taftan was closed on Saturday, halting movement of people and goods through the key crossing.

The closure also followed the withdrawal of an import waiver, though exact reasons were not officially disclosed. No timeline has been announced for reopening. Traders in the region have voiced concern over mounting losses as the stoppage continues.
