NCCIA nominates officer for JIT probing Mir Raza murder case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has nominated Deputy Director Arslan Manzoor to the joint investigation team probing the murder of young Karachi trader Mir Raza. According to a notification, Manzoor will represent the NCCIA in the JIT on the recommendation of the judicial commission. The JIT is headed by DIG Irfan Baloch and also includes officers from the ISI, Intelligence Bureau and Sindh Rangers.

The judicial commission had earlier called Mir Raza’s death a murder and recommended the formation of the JIT. The commission’s report had pointed to serious failures in the police handling of the case. The JIT will now investigate the murder and fix responsibility.
