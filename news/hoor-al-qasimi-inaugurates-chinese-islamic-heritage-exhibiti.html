Hoor Al Qasimi inaugurates Chinese-Islamic heritage exhibition in Sharjah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sheikha Hoor bint Sultan Al Qasimi, President of Sharjah Museums Authority, inaugurated the exhibition 'Entwined Legacies: Dialogue and Interaction between Chinese and Islamic Civilisations' on Wednesday morning at the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilisation. The exhibition represents Shanghai Museum's first organised exhibition in the Gulf region and reflects a cultural collaboration between Sharjah Museums Authority and Shanghai Museum. It features 124 artefacts, including 87 from Shanghai Museum and 37 from Sharjah Museums Authority collections, including items from the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilisation, Hisn Khorfakkan and Bait Sheikh Saeed Bin Hamad Al Qasimi.

The artefacts highlight the historical connections and cultural exchange between China and the Islamic world dating back to the seventh century, when the overland and maritime Silk Roads connected the two sides and carried goods, knowledge, technologies and artistic influences. Among the highlights are a polychrome-glazed earthenware figure of a woman riding a horse from the Tang dynasty, a Jingdezhen dish bearing an Arabic inscription from the Zhengde reign of the Ming dynasty, and a faceted blue-and-white Jingdezhen candlestick from the Yongle and Xuande reigns. The exhibition is on view at the Al Majarrah Gallery for Temporary Exhibitions until 9 May 2027, with a panel discussion and a series of museum programmes accompanying it.
