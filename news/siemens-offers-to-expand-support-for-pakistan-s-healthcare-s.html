Siemens offers to expand support for Pakistan's healthcare system in Berlin meeting with Zardari

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Technology company Siemens has expressed its readiness to expand support for strengthening Pakistan's healthcare infrastructure, during a meeting between its executives and President Asif Ali Zardari in Berlin. The meeting took place on the third and final day of the president's official visit to Germany, as the two countries prepare to mark 75 years of diplomatic relations. Siemens Executive Vice President Daniel Frisch briefed the president on the company's work in Pakistan and praised the difference made by the Sindh health department's networked steps.

According to the Presidency, the delegation said its representatives would soon visit Pakistan to sign memoranda of understanding with partners in capacity building and health technology, with cooperation extending to cancer, stroke and other diseases. President Zardari called for research-based and technology-driven solutions to improve health services, saying affordable and standardised care remained fundamental for every citizen to realise their potential. He also urged Siemens to diversify its investment across other sectors in Pakistan and assured the company of full institutional cooperation through the Special Investment Facilitation Council.

The president later departed Berlin, with the Presidency describing the visit, the first by a Pakistani president to Germany in more than two decades, as a significant advancement in bilateral ties.
