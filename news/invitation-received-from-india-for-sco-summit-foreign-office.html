Invitation received from India for SCO summit, Foreign Office says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has said that an invitation has been received from India for the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) summit. Foreign Office spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan confirmed that the invitation had arrived. He said a decision on participation would be taken by the leadership after institutional consultation.

The meeting is scheduled to be held in New Delhi from November 20 to 22. The spokesperson also said that permission for the Indian Punjab chief minister's visit to Pakistan was for the Indian government to grant.
