Saudi airline announces extra flights for Pakistan amid Riyadh airport disruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's national airline has announced additional flights for Pakistan in the wake of the disruption at Riyadh airport. Pakistan's Consul General in Jeddah, Syed Mustafa Rabbani, said extra flights from Jeddah to Lahore and Islamabad are being operated today and tomorrow, and that Pakistani passengers can contact the consulate for help and guidance. Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport had been temporarily closed since October 10 following attacks by Houthi militias.

The airport reopened for air traffic yesterday, but only under limited and very strict conditions. Under the latest notice to airmen issued by Saudi Arabia's civil aviation authority, prior permission from the authority is mandatory for all arrival and departure flights at Riyadh airport.
