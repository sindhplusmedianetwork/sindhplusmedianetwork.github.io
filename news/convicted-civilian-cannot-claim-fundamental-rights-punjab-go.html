Convicted civilian cannot claim fundamental rights, Punjab govt tells LHC in Hassaan Niazi case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has told the Lahore High Court that Hassaan Khan Niazi, the nephew of the PTI founder, cannot claim the protection of fundamental rights because he is a convicted civilian tried under the Pakistan Army Act. In a report submitted through the home secretary and the provincial police chief, the government sought the dismissal of Niazi's petition challenging his handover to the army and his military trial in the May 9 riots case. The report stated that Niazi was a proclaimed offender in FIR No.

96/2023 relating to the attack on Jinnah House in Lahore and was sentenced to ten years' imprisonment in December 2024. It added that he has already filed an appeal under Section 133-B of the Pakistan Army Act, 1952. The government argued that no judicial order was required for his handover because he was neither in judicial custody nor on remand when police handed him over to the commanding officer of the 54 Electrical and Mechanical Engineers Battalion on August 17, 2023.

The court will take up the petition in the coming days.
