Halal food and instructions in two languages: preparations complete for first Pakistani astronaut's mission to China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Preparations for Pakistan's first astronaut to travel to China's Tiangong space station are in their final stages. Halal food has been arranged for the mission. Operational instructions have been prepared in Chinese and English.

Pakistan has shortlisted Muhammad Zeeshan Ali and Khurram Daud for the Shenzhou-24 mission, and both have completed training in China. One will travel as a payload specialist to the Tiangong station while the other will serve as the backup astronaut.
