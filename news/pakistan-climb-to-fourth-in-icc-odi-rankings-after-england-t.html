Pakistan Climb to Fourth in ICC ODI Rankings After England Triumph

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's one-day side has climbed above Australia to fourth place in the latest ICC Men's ODI team rankings. The team now holds 103 rating points after consecutive victories over Sri Lanka and England in the tri-series at Rawalpindi. Australia slip to fifth with 102 points, while South Africa sit third on 104.

India continue to lead the table with 115 points, followed by New Zealand on 109. The decisive result was Tuesday's commanding 87-run victory over England. All-rounder Arafat Minhas starred with 59 runs off 58 balls before claiming career-best figures of six for 40.

Hasan Nawaz top-scored with 79 as Pakistan posted 302 for nine, before England were bowled out for 215. There was further good news in the individual charts: Salman Ali Agha moved up to 12th in the batting rankings, Haris Rauf climbed to 20th among bowlers, and Naseem Shah rose to 45th. Spinner Abrar Ahmed held on to second place in the bowling rankings, behind Afghanistan's Rashid Khan.
