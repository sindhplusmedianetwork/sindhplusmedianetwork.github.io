Naqvi suggests referendum as 'solution' to debate on new administrative units

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi on Thursday suggested that political parties hold a referendum to gauge public opinion on the creation of new administrative units, describing it as the solution to the ongoing debate. Speaking to reporters at the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency office in Lahore, he said every party had the right to disagree, but since all parties stressed democracy and the supremacy of the people, the public should decide. He said that if the people said new units should not be created, he would fully agree with them, and if they said they should be made, then they should be made.

Naqvi has argued for months that the current system of governance has collapsed and needs a reset, and he first raised the idea of new administrative units in July. He also said he and PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari were nearly on the same page, noting that Bilawal had called for a political reset and unity among parties. The PPP, which rules Sindh and is an ally of the PML-N government at the Centre, has strongly opposed the creation of a new province within Sindh and insists any decision must come through constitutional and parliamentary processes.

Naqvi added that nothing would happen against the will of the coalition allies and any step would follow consensus. He also said corruption existed across the entire country and it was the government's responsibility to end it.
