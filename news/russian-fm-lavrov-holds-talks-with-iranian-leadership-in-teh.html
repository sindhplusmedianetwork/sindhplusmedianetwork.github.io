Russian FM Lavrov Holds Talks With Iranian Leadership in Tehran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov met his Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi in Tehran on Monday, Iran's state broadcaster IRIB reported. Diplomatic delegations from both countries began consultations immediately after the meeting. Lavrov arrived in Tehran on October 19 for an official visit to discuss bilateral relations and regional and international developments.

He is also expected to meet Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian during the visit, according to Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei. The spokesperson said the visit is part of ongoing bilateral consultations and follow-up on agreements between the two countries. Iran and Russia have strengthened political, economic and military cooperation in recent years amid Western sanctions on both.

In January 2025, the two countries signed a 20-year Comprehensive Strategic Partnership Treaty in Moscow.
