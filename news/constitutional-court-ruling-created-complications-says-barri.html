Constitutional Court Ruling Created Complications, Says Barrister Gohar; PTI to Seek Stay Withdrawal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar said the Federal Constitutional Court's 5 November decision has created complications affecting political figures and ordinary citizens. The decision halted proceedings on certain constitutional petitions in the Peshawar High Court. He said the Peshawar High Court has delisted PTI cases that were scheduled for 10 November.

The party will approach the Constitutional Court to seek withdrawal of the stay order. Barrister Gohar said the stay has disrupted the progress of cases before the high court.
