Decline in Journalism Extends Beyond News Quality, Says Nasir Baig Chughtai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior journalist Nasir Baig Chughtai spoke at a special dinner held in his honour by Pakistan Journalists Forum Jeddah. He said the decline of journalism is not limited to the quality of news. According to him, journalists and journalism as an institution are also facing serious deterioration.

He said the traditional spirit of collective struggle for journalists' rights, professional freedom and ethical values has gradually weakened. Nasir Baig Chughtai said hundreds of professional journalists are being made unemployed, but no voice is being raised. He is currently visiting Saudi Arabia to perform Umrah.
