Punjab CM Maryam Nawaz Meets New French Ambassador

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz held an important meeting with the new French Ambassador to Pakistan, Jean-Noel Poirier, in Lahore. The chief minister warmly received the envoy and congratulated him on assuming his new diplomatic responsibilities. Both sides discussed strengthening trade, economic and investment cooperation between Pakistan and France, stressing the need to explore new business opportunities.

The French ambassador invited Maryam Nawaz to visit France to advance trade relations, for which she thanked him. She also invited French investors, through PIVOT, to invest in Punjab's agriculture, industry and livestock sectors. The meeting agreed to expand trade, investment and industrial partnership, while GSP Plus matters and Pakistan's export prospects were also reviewed.

Maryam Nawaz thanked France for providing a 180-million-euro loan for NTDC transmission lines and grid stations in Punjab and offered French investors opportunities in urban wastewater governance projects. She described GSP Plus as a lifeline for Pakistan's exports, noting that Pakistan's exports to the EU reached around $8.96 billion in 2025-26.
