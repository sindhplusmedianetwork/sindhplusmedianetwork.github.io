Zendaya Says Tom Holland Helped Her Through Nerves on The Odyssey Set

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Zendaya has opened up about feeling nervous while filming Christopher Nolan's upcoming epic The Odyssey, crediting her husband and co-star Tom Holland for helping her feel more confident on set. Speaking during a recent question-and-answer session at the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures in Los Angeles, the actress discussed the pressure of performing alongside actors and filmmakers she had long admired. Zendaya plays Athena in the highly anticipated film, and she said she felt overwhelmed during a night shoot while preparing for a scene.

She recalled thinking that she did not want to mess up the performance, and she also felt nervous about performing alongside the many background actors and stunt performers involved in the production. Holland, who plays Telemachus, the son of Odysseus and Penelope, visited the set that day and offered her encouragement from the corner, telling her she was doing well. Director Christopher Nolan also praised Zendaya's performance, describing her as always perfect in comments to Fandango.

The couple previously worked together on several Marvel films, including Spider-Man: Homecoming, Far From Home and No Way Home, and their latest collaboration marks another chance to share the screen in a major production.
