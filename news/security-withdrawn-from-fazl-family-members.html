Security withdrawn from Fazl, family members

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police and Federal Constabulary personnel deployed to protect JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman and other members of his family have been withdrawn from the residences they guarded in Islamabad and Dera Ismail Khan, police and party sources said on Thursday. JUI-F spokesman Aslam Ghouri confirmed the development, saying FC and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa police officials deployed at the Maulana's homes in Dera Ismail Khan and Islamabad were simultaneously withdrawn on Wednesday evening. He alleged that the Maulana's house in Islamabad had been cordoned off by the capital police for some time, that security personnel had harassed some visitors, and that the party's Central Secretariat in Islamabad had also been besieged by police.

An Islamabad police official, however, said that only one police guard had been called back. JUI-F leaders from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa alleged that security personnel deployed to protect the JUI-F chief and members of his family, including his son Asad Mehmood and brother Maulana Attaur Rehman, had also been withdrawn and directed to report back to police lines, with some instructed to leave their posts within 15 minutes, though there was no official word from the authorities. Opposition leaders in the National Assembly and Senate, Mehmood Khan Achakzai and Allama Raja Nasir Abbas, expressed concern over the reported withdrawal and called for restoring the security detail in view of threats to the lives of JUI-F leaders.

Separately, Islamabad police registered a case under the Anti-Terrorism Act, the Pakistan Penal Code and the Peaceful Assembly and Public Order Act, 2024 against two JUI-F leaders and more than 200 unidentified workers over a November 7 gathering in Sector I-9/4, and an anti-terrorism court issued a non-bailable arrest warrant for Mufti Abrar in connection with the case.
