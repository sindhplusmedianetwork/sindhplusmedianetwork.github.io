High Court Moved to Allow Aleema Khan Meetings With Family and Lawyers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court (LHC) has been approached seeking permission for family members and lawyers to meet Aleema Khan, sister of incarcerated PTI founder Imran Khan, in jail. The petition, filed through Advocate Rana Mudassar Umar, challenges the authorities' failure to allow family members and lawyers to meet Aleema despite repeated requests, and asks the court to direct the authorities to facilitate the meetings. It alleges that several applications seeking permission for meetings have been submitted to the relevant authorities, but no action has been taken so far.

Aleema and her sisters, Dr Uzma Khan and Noreen Niazi, were detained in September under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO) Ordinance as the PTI geared up for a planned protest march towards Islamabad. Uzma and Noreen were released after their detention orders were withdrawn last week, while Aleema remains under preventive detention. Meanwhile, the LHC granted the prosecution another month to produce the case record in appeals challenging the acquittal of former foreign minister and PTI Vice Chairman Shah Mahmood Qureshi in May 9, 2023 violence cases.

A two-member bench headed by Justice Shehram Sarwar Chaudhry heard the appeals, and a law officer informed the bench that the prosecution did not have the case record and sought more time, after which the bench directed the prosecution to submit the record on Dec 19. An anti-terrorism court had acquitted Mr Qureshi in August and September 2025 in cases including the attack on Lahore's Shadman police station and the burning of police vehicles near Jinnah House, and another court had acquitted him in July 2025 in a case of violence at Sherpao Bridge, though he remains behind bars and faces trial in other cases stemming from the violence that erupted after the arrest of the PTI founder.
