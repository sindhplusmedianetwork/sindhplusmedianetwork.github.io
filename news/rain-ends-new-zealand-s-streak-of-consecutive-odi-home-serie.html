Rain ends New Zealand's streak of consecutive ODI home series wins

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The third one-day international between New Zealand and India in Hamilton was abandoned because of persistent rain. The toss was delayed and the match was first reduced to 40 overs per side, but further rain made play impossible. The washout ended New Zealand's run of 11 consecutive bilateral one-day series wins at home.

India already lead the five-match series 2-0 and can no longer lose it. The last two one-day internationals will be played in Mount Maunganui on Friday and Sunday.
