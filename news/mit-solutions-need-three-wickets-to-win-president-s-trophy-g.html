MIT Solutions Need Three Wickets to Win President's Trophy Grade-I Final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Markhor IT Solutions needed three wickets and State Bank of Pakistan another 72 runs after stumps were drawn on day three of the five-day President's Trophy Grade-I final at the Bagh-e-Jinnah Cricket Ground in Lahore on Thursday. SBP, set a target of 237 runs to win, were cruising at 119 for 2 in 39.5 overs before chaos set in, and they closed the day at 165 for 7 in 59.4 overs after losing five wickets for 46 runs in a 19.5-over passage dominated by MIT. Hasan Raza top-scored with 73 off 136 balls, while Abdullah Fazal made 25 and Faham-ul-Haq 13.

Aamir Jamal, whose 7 for 32 in the first innings had given SBP a slim lead, broke a 41-run stand by removing Kamran Ghulam, and MIT skipper Khalid Usman's left-arm spin accounted for Rameez Aziz and Shahid Aziz. Mubasir Khan (17 not out) and Muhammad Abbas (5 not out) survived the remaining overs at stumps. Earlier, MIT were bowled out for 296 in their second innings, with Aamir completing his fifth first-class fifty, while Muhammad Abbas completed his 60th first-class five-for with figures of 5 for 70.

On Friday, MIT will look to complete a dream red-ball run that began with the Grade-II title earlier this year, while SBP chase their first major title in first-class cricket.
