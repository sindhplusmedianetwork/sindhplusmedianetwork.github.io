Bilawal did not take President Zardari into confidence over his remarks, says Hamid Mir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior journalist and analyst Hamid Mir has said Bilawal Bhutto did not take President Zardari into confidence over the remarks he made at the Karachi Press Club and in interviews. Speaking on Geo News programme Aaj Shahzeb Khanzada Kay Saath, Mir said the meeting between President Zardari and the prime minister took place on the president's initiative. He said the president told the prime minister that efforts should be made to remove bitterness and the prime minister agreed, though it cannot yet be said that a ceasefire has happened.

Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry, confirming the ceasefire efforts, said a ceasefire was appropriate and mature political parties act this way, with talks held whether issues are small or big. Mir said that after the meeting Bilawal presented and defended his position before the president, saying some ministers were speaking in favour of a referendum while no discussion had yet taken place in the cabinet or parliament, and that if a referendum could be talked about, he also had the right to talk about elections in six months.
