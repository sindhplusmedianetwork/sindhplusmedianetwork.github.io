Melania Trump secures Russia-Ukraine ceasefire for Oleshky from October 29, White House says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WASHINGTON: The White House said on Friday that US First Lady Melania Trump has secured a localized ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine in the city of Oleshky, to begin on October 29. According to the White House statement, both nations agreed to temporarily halt all military action in Oleshky to allow humanitarian aid to enter and to prepare a civilian evacuation through a designated safe passageway. Oleshky, in southern Ukraine, has been under Russian control since February 2022, and residents have reported a deepening food crisis; Melania Trump said the city's remaining population was mostly elderly people in urgent need of medical attention, medicine, food and clean water.

Non-military civilian leaders from both countries are to visit residents in their homes to assess their needs, with evacuees promised coordinated support and transport to their preferred destination. Al Jazeera noted, however, that neither Russia nor Ukraine had immediately confirmed the ceasefire, making it a developing story.
