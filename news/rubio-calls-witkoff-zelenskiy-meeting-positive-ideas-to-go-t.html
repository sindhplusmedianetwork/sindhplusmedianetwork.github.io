Rubio Calls Witkoff-Zelenskiy Meeting 'Positive'; Ideas to Go to Russia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Wednesday that special envoy Steve Witkoff's meeting with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy in Oslo was positive. Rubio said Witkoff would carry the ideas discussed in Oslo to Russia. Two sources told Reuters that Witkoff arrived in St Petersburg on Wednesday to meet Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Rubio also denied that the United States had any role in slowing Starlink services for Ukraine's military. The diplomatic shuttle comes as Washington seeks a path to end the war in Ukraine.
