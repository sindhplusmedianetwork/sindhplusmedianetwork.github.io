Hamza Zahoor's Unbeaten Century Powers Pakistan U19 to Win Over India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Under-19 defeated India by three wickets in an Asia Cup match in Al Amerat, Oman, thanks to an unbeaten century by Hamza Zahoor. Before the match, Zahoor had written himself a note, which later went viral. India, batting first after winning the toss, were bowled out for 208 in 46.3 overs, with Arjun Rajput scoring 107 off 85 balls.

Pakistan's chase began poorly, with five wickets falling for just 61 runs. Zahoor then held one end and added a decisive 100-run partnership with Hasnain Abbas. He finished on 100 not out off 108 balls, hitting three sixes and 10 fours, and was named player of the match.

With four runs needed for both his century and the win, he struck a cover drive off Kavya Paresh Patel's full toss to seal both. Pakistan, who had earlier beaten the UAE and Japan, moved to the top of Group A.
