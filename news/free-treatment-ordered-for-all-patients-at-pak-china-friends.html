Free treatment ordered for all patients at Pak-China Friendship Hospital

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The prime minister has ordered 100 percent free treatment for patients at the Pak-China Friendship Hospital in Islamabad, with the government covering all medical costs. He said financial hardship must never stand in the way of any citizen's treatment. The 182-bed facility was built with China's cooperation during the COVID-19 pandemic, and its revival will give patients from Islamabad, Rawalpindi and other parts of the country access to quality care.

The hospital will be managed jointly by the government and the Indus Hospital and Health Network. China's ambassador, Jiang Zaidong, welcomed the hospital's naming as a symbol of friendship between the two countries. Federal Health Minister Mustafa Kamal also addressed the ceremony, outlining ongoing reforms in the health sector.

The Indus network's chief executive, Dr Abdul Bari Khan, said his organisation runs 15 hospitals, 140 primary health centres, four blood centres and four rehabilitation centres across the country, serving around 700,000 patients every month.
