Iran says it will use all available tools to confront US naval blockade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran will use all its tools to confront the US naval blockade, Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei said in a weekly news conference on Monday. He called the blockade a military aggression against Iran. The US blockade on Iran-related oil exports was imposed on April 13 and lifted in June after Washington reached a draft understanding with Tehran.

It was then restored on July 14 after the agreement collapsed. Baghaei said Iran will only reopen the Strait of Hormuz when Washington meets its conditions, including halting hostilities, lifting the blockade and waiving sanctions.
