Smog season: how to protect your lungs and immunity, and which foods help

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Air pollution can become a serious danger to human health, especially for the lungs, and in such conditions a balanced and nutritious diet also plays an important role in keeping the body healthy. In view of the current smog situation in different parts of Pakistan, medical experts advise citizens to take special care of their health and to include foods in their diet that are useful for the immune system and overall health. According to experts, foods rich in antioxidants can help the body deal with the effects of oxidative stress, which are caused by air pollution.

People living in smog-affected areas can include fish, turmeric, garlic, ginger, green tea, fruits, vegetables and dry fruits in their daily diet. Fish is an important source of omega-3 fatty acids, which help reduce inflammation, while the natural compound curcumin in turmeric has antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Garlic contains a compound called allicin, valued for its natural antibacterial properties, and ginger can help reduce inflammation and ease discomfort in the airways.

Green tea is rich in antioxidants, berries are a good source of vitamins and antioxidants, and leafy green vegetables provide vitamins and minerals that support the lungs. Dry fruits such as almonds, pistachios and sunflower seeds provide magnesium, while probiotic foods such as yogurt support gut health, which is linked to the immune system. However, experts stress that no specific food can provide complete protection from the harmful effects of smog, so avoiding unnecessary outings, using a quality mask and seeking medical advice for breathing difficulty or a persistent cough remain essential.
