Pakistan's biggest AI hackathon ends with grand final as 300 teams compete for $60,000 in prizes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The grand final of AI Hackathon Pakistan 2026, described as the country's biggest AI hackathon, was held at the Pak-China Friendship Centre in Islamabad. The contest was organised jointly by Alibaba Cloud and Al-Khidmat Foundation Pakistan to connect young people's skills with global technology opportunities. Young participants from across the country presented AI-based projects offering modern solutions to problems in health, education, agriculture and other sectors.

More than 16,000 AI projects were submitted for the hackathon, and after initial selection and regional contests in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, 300 teams reached the national final. A total prize package of 60,000 US dollars was set aside, including 30,000 dollars in cash prizes and 30,000 dollars in cloud credits. The team Salix secured first place and won 7,000 US dollars, while the second- and third-place teams and runners-up in six categories also received prizes.

The competition focused on six areas: health, financial inclusion, education, smart agriculture, open innovation, and Urdu and regional languages. Participants were also given training, mentorship and access to modern digital tools, with the aim of helping them build their own products and workable technology solutions rather than only using artificial intelligence.
