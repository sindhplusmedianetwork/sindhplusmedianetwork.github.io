Encroachments choke Tando Jam roads as ambulances and schoolchildren struggle

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mounting encroachments, roadside vendors and an allegedly illegal Friday cattle market near the railway crossing have turned traffic congestion into a recurring ordeal for residents of Tando Jam. Commuters, schoolchildren and even emergency ambulances are struggling to navigate the choked roads, amid alleged inaction by the municipal authorities. Residents said the illegal cattle market, set up every Friday near the railway crossing, together with shopkeepers extending their businesses beyond their designated limits and vendors occupying roads with pushcarts, had severely narrowed major thoroughfares and disrupted the flow of traffic.

The situation is particularly dire around Main Workshop Stop, Nawan Hatt Bazaar and the railway crossing, where roadside encroachments have made commuting increasingly difficult. According to residents, traffic gridlocks frequently leave ambulances carrying patients stranded, potentially delaying their access to medical care. Students travelling to and from schools and colleges also face considerable difficulties, while pedestrians struggle to cross the roads safely.
