FIA Busts Hundi Network in Karachi Bank, Seizes 300,000 Saudi Riyals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has smashed an alleged hundi-hawala network operating out of a private bank in Karachi, arresting five people including the branch manager. The FIA's Anti-Corruption Circle Karachi raided the bank's branch in Federal B Area, Block 4, under Inquiry No. 107/2026, according to an agency spokesperson.

Those arrested were identified as Farhan Ahmed, Faisal Ahmed, Kashif Alvi, Muhammad Naseem and Junaid Ahmed, with Alvi identified as the manager of the branch. During the raid, officials recovered 300,000 Saudi riyals in cash, seven smart devices and mobile phones, and cheque books belonging to several banks. The FIA said the seized phones allegedly contain WhatsApp chats, digital ledgers and records of financial transactions linked to the hundi-hawala operation.

The cash, devices and records were formally taken into custody under a seizure memo. Proceedings are under way to register a case against the suspects under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act and other applicable laws, while investigators work to trace the sources of the transactions and other possible facilitators.
