PM meets Saudi and Turkish delegations, reaffirms commitment to Makkah defence pact

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif met high-level delegations from Saudi Arabia and Turkiye in Islamabad on the occasion of the second session of the Strategic Political and Defence Committee under the Makkah defence pact. Saudi Defence Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman and Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan called on the prime minister, and the prime minister reaffirmed Pakistan's strong commitment to the Makkah defence alliance. According to the statement issued after the meeting, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye expressed their resolve to stand united in defence of security, sovereignty and territorial integrity.

The prime minister expressed satisfaction with the outcomes of the committee's second session and said Pakistan would continue to extend full support for Saudi Arabia's sovereignty, territorial integrity and security, expressing unwavering solidarity with the brotherly country. The meeting also discussed the latest regional situation and peace and stability in the region, and the three countries reaffirmed their resolve to continue working together for lasting peace and stability. The Saudi delegation also included Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan and Chief of General Staff General Fayyad al-Ruwaili, while the Turkish delegation included Defence Minister Yasar Guler and Chief of General Staff General Selcuk Bayraktaroglu.

Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, Field Marshal Asim Munir, Defence Minister Khawaja Asif and the National Security Adviser also attended the meeting, where the three countries agreed to promote defence cooperation under the Makkah defence alliance.
