IHC Slams Customs Over Missing Gold, Issues Show-Cause

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has expressed strong dissatisfaction with an offer to pay Rs4.8 million to a petitioner in place of seized gold bars, issuing a show-cause notice to the customs collector in a contempt of court case. A division bench comprising Justice Sardar Ejaz Ishaq Khan and Justice Arbab Muhammad Tahir issued the written order after hearing a contempt petition filed by citizen Waqarun Nisa over the failure to return gold bars recovered from a passenger's bag. The petitioner's counsel submitted that the court had ordered the return of the gold bars themselves and not payment of money, adding that the offered amount had been calculated using 2023 gold prices rather than the prevailing market rate.

The court was informed that the gold bars had been handed over to the State Bank of Pakistan and could not be retrieved. While customs authorities said they had acted under Section 169(4) of the Customs Act, the court observed that the provision does not mention handing confiscated goods to the State Bank. The bench said no satisfactory explanation had been given for why the taxpayer should bear the loss from the difference in the gold's value, and adjourned the hearing until November 2.
