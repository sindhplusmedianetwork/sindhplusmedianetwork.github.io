8.4 Million Ticket Requests for India vs Pakistan World Cup Clash at 34,000-Seat Wanderers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Organisers of the ICC Men's World Cup have received nearly 8.4 million ticket applications for the group-stage fixture between India and Pakistan. The blockbuster match is scheduled for October 10, 2027, at Johannesburg's Wanderers Stadium, which holds just 34,000 spectators. The demand has outperformed every other fixture in the tournament, including the final at the same venue, which drew nearly 8 million applications.

Tournament-wide ticket requests have crossed 120 million across 193 countries, according to the ICC. The showcase is being co-hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia. With millions competing for limited seats, allocations are being managed through a randomized ballot window that stays open until November 21.
