Former minister launches drive for Bahawalpur, South Punjab and Hazara provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former federal minister for information and broadcasting Muhammad Ali Durrani on Wednesday launched a nationwide movement for the immediate establishment of Bahawalpur, South Punjab and Hazara provinces and the restoration of empowered local governments. He was addressing a news conference at the Multan Press Club on Wednesday. Durrani said that for the past 80 years Pakistan had been run on one wheel, where 70 per cent of the budget was showered on just one city, Lahore, while the people of the remaining areas were completely deprived of basic rights, employment and funds.

He said that to get rid of the large provincial system created by the British and the luxuries of the rulers, new provinces should be formed immediately. Durrani said the people of Bahawalpur and Multan had struggled continuously for their rights, and the movement would press for new provinces along with empowered local governments. He described the campaign as a nationwide movement aimed at securing the three new provinces and stronger local governments.
