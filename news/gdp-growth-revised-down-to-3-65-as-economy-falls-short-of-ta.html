GDP Growth Revised Down to 3.65% as Economy Falls Short of Target

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's National Accounts Committee has revised the country's GDP growth estimate for the current fiscal year down to 3.65 per cent, trimming the earlier projection of 3.7 per cent. The committee attributed the downgrade to slower-than-expected performance in industry, which grew 3.34 per cent, and services, which expanded 3.81 per cent. Agriculture posted growth of 3.5 per cent, offering some support to the overall figure.

With the revision, the size of the economy now stands at 450.4 billion dollars. Per capita income was recorded at 1,896 dollars, which remains the lowest in the region. The committee's assessment reflects an economy growing below its target pace as key productive sectors lose momentum.
