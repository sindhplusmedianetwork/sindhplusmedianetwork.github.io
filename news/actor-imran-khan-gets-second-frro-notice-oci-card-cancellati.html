Actor Imran Khan Gets Second FRRO Notice; OCI Card Cancellation Proceedings Begin

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Imran Khan is facing fresh trouble after taking part in a protest demanding the resignation of India's Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Mumbai's Foreigners Regional Registration Office has issued him a second notice, asking him to respond within 15 days, and has sought his explanation in proceedings to cancel his Overseas Citizen of India card. The notice states that Khan joined the protest at August Kranti Maidan without obtaining police permission.

A case of unlawful assembly has also been registered against him and the other protesters. The office clarified that the registration of a case or his detention does not constitute final proof of the charges, and that merely participating in a protest is not automatic grounds for cancelling an OCI card. This follows an earlier notice issued on October 14, which had directed him to leave India by October 28, 2026.
