New implant lowers blood sugar using electricity, offers hope for diabetes and obesity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Scientists in Switzerland have developed a modern experimental device that can lower blood sugar levels with the help of electricity after being implanted in the body. The device, developed by researchers at ETH Zurich University, converts excess glucose in the blood into gluconate, which the kidneys excrete from the body through urine. According to the research, the wireless device is linked to a continuous sugar-monitoring system and showed automatic sugar control results in experiments on mice.

The experiments also showed the device helps increase the use of stored fat in the body, raising hopes for simultaneous treatment of diabetes and obesity. Experts say more than 2.5 billion people worldwide suffer from the combined problem of diabetes and obesity, while current treatments using insulin and medicines have some side effects. Scientists caution, however, that the device is still in the experimental stage and its results were obtained on mice, so further research and clinical trials are needed to prove its effectiveness and safety in humans.
