Security forces foil major terror bid in Gwadar; 10 terrorists killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces have foiled a major Indian-sponsored terrorism attempt at the Gabd border crossing and in Gwadar city, the military's media wing said. In clearance operations conducted in and around Gwadar, ten terrorists, including a female terrorist, were killed. One civilian was martyred during the operations and some vehicles were damaged, while no security force personnel suffered casualties.

The attempt was aimed at creating unrest in the strategically important port city, officials said. President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif paid tribute to the security forces for their swift and successful action. The prime minister said the nation stood united behind its armed forces in the fight against terrorism.

Security officials said clearance and combing operations were continuing in the area to ensure no further threat remained.
