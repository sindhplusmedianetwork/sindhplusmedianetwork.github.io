Africa's First Olympics: Dakar 2026 Youth Games Open Today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 2026 Youth Olympic Games open in Dakar on Saturday, the first Olympic event ever held on African soil. The opening ceremony takes place this evening in Diamniadio, one of three host cities along with Dakar and Saly. Around 2,665 athletes aged 14 to 17 will compete across 25 sports and 153 medal events at eight venues.

Senegal has spent years preparing for the landmark event, which was postponed from 2022. The Games also mark the return of Russian athletes competing under their own flag and colours for the first time since the 2022 invasion of Ukraine. Organisers say the event will leave a lasting sporting legacy for African youth.
