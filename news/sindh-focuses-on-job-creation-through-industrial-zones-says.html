Sindh focuses on job creation through industrial zones, says Sharjeel Memon

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon has said the Sindh government is working to provide essential facilities and employment, with the establishment of industrial zones and other initiatives creating new job opportunities. In a statement issued in Karachi, he said the Pakistan Peoples Party's politics are centred on serving the public and upholding democracy and the Constitution. He said people want solutions to issues such as inflation, unemployment and access to basic services, and the provincial government is also seeking to attract international investors to these zones.

'If Sindh develops, Pakistan's economy will also grow stronger,' he said. He urged all political parties to respond seriously to the challenges facing the country and to focus on public relief and practical measures rather than rhetoric. Sharjeel said Shaheed Benazir Bhutto had dedicated her life to democracy, people's rights and a strong federation, and that the PPP remained committed to her principles.
