PM Shehbaz Approves Expansion of Karachi and Lahore Airports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Thursday approved the expansion of Karachi and Lahore airports under their respective master plans. Chairing a meeting in Islamabad on airport expansion and passenger facilities, he directed authorities to complete the projects within the stipulated timelines. He stressed that there would be no compromise on quality or international standards.

The prime minister ordered the engagement of internationally renowned institutions for third-party validation of the projects. He also called for regular staff training, better inter-agency coordination and the hiring of leading catering companies to ensure high-quality food for passengers. Officials briefed him that the runway expansion at Karachi airport was complete and that 14 airports had been activated for domestic flights in the past year.

He was also told that e-gate installation was under way and that Hyderabad's aviation institution would be upgraded into an international-standard aviation university by February 2028.
