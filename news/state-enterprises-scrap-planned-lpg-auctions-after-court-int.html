State Enterprises Scrap Planned LPG Auctions After Court Intervention

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) auctions planned by state-owned enterprises Oil and Gas Development Company Limited (OGDCL), Pakistan Petroleum Limited (PPL) and Pak-Arab Refinery Limited (PARCO) have been scrapped, the Pakistan LPG Marketers Association (PLPGMA) announced. The three enterprises confirmed before the Lahore High Court on November 20 that the auctions had been abandoned. The association had challenged the auction notices and invitations to bids issued under advertisements dated July 26, 2026, arguing that LPG producers cannot charge any premium over the price notified by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA).

The Lahore High Court had suspended the auction process on August 7, 2026. After the enterprises confirmed the auctions were scrapped, the association consented to withdraw its petition and the court disposed of the case. Separately, the LPG Distributors Association of Pakistan has challenged the federal cabinet's LPG pricing guidelines approved on November 4, 2026, objecting that they transfer price-setting powers from OGRA to state-owned entities without following the constitutional procedure under Articles 153 and 154.

The distributors contend the guidelines unlawfully discontinue OGRA's monthly price notifications and introduce auction-based pricing of indigenous LPG at import-parity-linked rates.
