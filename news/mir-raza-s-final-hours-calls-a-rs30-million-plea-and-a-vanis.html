Mir Raza's Final Hours: Calls, a Rs30 Million Plea and a Vanishing Act

New details have emerged about the final hours of Mir Raza Ali, whose murder in Karachi is being investigated by a judicial commission. Phone records and CCTV footage trace his movements through the city on the night of July 27-28, when he disappeared from view at 4:38 in the morning. A forensic examination of his mobile phone showed a WhatsApp message sent to a friend in which he asked for Rs30 million.

His social media accounts were suddenly deactivated around 3am that night. Records also show he took a Bykea ride to Cheese Valley in the PECHS area. His body was recovered on July 29.

The judicial commission has already ruled out suicide, concluding that Mir Raza was murdered, and has recommended a joint investigation team to probe the case.
