Harry Kane signs two-year Bayern Munich contract extension until 2029

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harry Kane has signed a two-year contract extension at Bayern Munich, keeping the England captain at the German champions until 2029, the club announced on Thursday. The 33-year-old joined Bayern from Tottenham in 2023. He has scored 156 goals in 161 games in all competitions for the club.

Kane said he feels right at home at the club. He won the men's Ballon d'Or last month. Since joining Bayern, he has won two Bundesliga titles and one German Cup.
