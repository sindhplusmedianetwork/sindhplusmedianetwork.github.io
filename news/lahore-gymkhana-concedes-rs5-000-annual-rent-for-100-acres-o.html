Lahore Gymkhana Concedes Rs5,000 Annual Rent for 100 Acres of State Land Is 'Inadequate'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The chairman of the Lahore Gymkhana Club has conceded that the Rs5,000 annual rent paid for more than 100 acres of prime state land in the provincial capital is wholly inadequate. Appearing before Punjab Assembly Special Committee No. 6, chaired by MPA Samiullah Khan, on Wednesday, Chairman Salman Siddique said he was himself surprised by the nominal rent given the vast land occupied by the club and its improved financial position.

Contrasting the club's present finances with the constraints it faced during the 1990s, he said the annual rent should be at least Rs10 million, and expressed willingness to provide records and cooperate with the inquiry. The committee questioned how successive governments had allowed the arrangement to continue for decades without proper valuation, government oversight or scrutiny of the lease terms, after being informed that the land's estimated value ranged from Rs76 billion to Rs218 billion. The Member Colonies of the Board of Revenue told the committee that then chief minister Sardar Muhammad Arif Nakai, who was himself a member of the club, had verbally directed that a token Rs5,000 annual rent be fixed for the lease extension in 1996, bypassing proper valuation and legal principles.

Lawmakers demanded the complete documentary record of the approval behind the 1996 lease deed, and the committee formed a fact-finding team to frame specific queries, verify records and present a comprehensive report within 10 days.
