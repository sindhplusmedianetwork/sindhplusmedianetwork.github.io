Miss Universe unveils new crown studded with 3,150 diamonds ahead of 75th final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new Miss Universe crown has been unveiled for the pageant's 75th anniversary, studded with 3,150 diamonds set in 18-carat white gold. The crown, called The Radiance of an Eternal Legacy, was designed by jeweller George Wittels together with Paris house Maison D'Or. It was presented at The Miss Universe Experience in Plaza Las Americas, Puerto Rico, ahead of the grand final on 24 November in San Juan.

Three prominent diamonds in the crown symbolise the pageant's past, present and future. More than 100 contestants will take part in the final, where the winner will be crowned with the new piece. The unveiling marks a milestone celebration for one of the world's best-known beauty pageants.
