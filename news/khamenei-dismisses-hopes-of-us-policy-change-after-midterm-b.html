Khamenei dismisses hopes of US policy change after midterm blow to Trump

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's supreme leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei has dismissed any hope that Washington's policy towards Tehran will change after the US midterm elections, calling such expectations 'seriously mistaken'. In a written statement published on his official website on Wednesday, he said those who believe concessions to the United States could end its hostility towards the Iranian nation were wrong. The remarks came a day after US voters handed President Donald Trump a heavy defeat, with Democrats seizing both the Senate and the House.

In a separate message, Khamenei warned that the Middle East war would become 'much wider' if the United States resumed its attacks on the Islamic republic. An adviser to the commander-in-chief of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps also warned that Tehran would target 'enemy leaders' if Iranian commanders were struck, adding that Iran's response would no longer be 'reciprocal or proportional'. The statements, reported by AFP and Iranian state media, came amid an eight-month-old conflict centred on US-Israeli strikes on Iran and the Strait of Hormuz.
