Sunlight at night? US firm plans giant space mirrors to light up Earth after dark

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A US space technology company has unveiled an ambitious plan to reflect sunlight onto Earth even after sunset using giant mirrors placed in orbit. Reflect Orbital, based in California, aims to deploy mirrors measuring 18 by 18 metres in low Earth orbit to direct sunlight onto selected areas of the planet at night. According to research published in The Astrophysical Journal Letters, the company plans its first test with the 'Earendil One' satellite in the second quarter of 2027.

The firm says the technology could extend solar power generation into the night and provide light to disaster-hit areas where power supplies have failed. Reflect Orbital is targeting a network of 50,000 satellites by 2035 if the concept proves workable. However, astronomers and environmentalists have raised concerns about the plan, warning it could add to light pollution and disturb the natural darkness of the night sky.

The debate highlights the tension between a novel energy idea and the protection of the night environment.
