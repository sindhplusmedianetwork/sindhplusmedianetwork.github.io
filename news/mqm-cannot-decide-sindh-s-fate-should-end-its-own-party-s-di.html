MQM Cannot Decide Sindh's Fate, Should End Its Own Party's Disunity: Murtaza Wahab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mayor Karachi Murtaza Wahab has said MQM cannot decide Sindh's fate and should end disunity in its own party. He asked whether they want to impose their thinking on people, saying Nine Zero no longer exists and the dispute is now between Bahadurabad and Ravi Road, and questioned whether those whose own party is in disunity will decide Sindh's fate. Addressing a press conference, he termed MQM a 'disunited national movement' and said the people and representatives of Sindh are saying Sindh is united, and the decision should be what the people of Sindh want.

He said Sindh has seen very bad times, peace and order was established in Karachi after immense sacrifices, and they do not want to go back to the 1980s. On this occasion, Sukkur Mayor Arsalan Sheikh said the Peoples Party is not against the creation of new provinces, and new provinces should be made where constitutional requirements are not fulfilled and people's problems are not being solved. Arsalan Sheikh said the Peoples Party established peace in the city, double standards should not be adopted on the matter of Punjab and Sindh, and the series of threats of linguistic division and mocking Sindh's culture should stop.

He warned that if the same attitude continues, the next press conference will be held at Nine Zero with the people of Sindh's urban constituencies.
