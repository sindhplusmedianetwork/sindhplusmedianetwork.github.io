Jordan king orders iron-fist response after deadly church attack kills nine

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jordan's King Abdullah has ordered the security forces to respond with an iron fist after gunmen opened fire during a funeral service at a church in Karak governorate, killing nine people and wounding 40 others. The attackers fired for several minutes in broad daylight on Friday at Saint George's Greek Orthodox church, about 120 kilometres southwest of the capital, Amman, sending mourners diving to the ground in panic. A priest who was present described the gunfire as indiscriminate, saying those inside never knew where the next bullet might land.

Chairing a National Security Council meeting, the king condemned what the royal court called a despicable terrorist act and said there could be no leniency when Jordanian lives were at stake. Security has since been stepped up at shopping malls, foreign embassies and other sites across the country, while a manhunt for the gunmen continues. Officials said it was still too early to say whether the attackers were linked to any group inside or outside Jordan, and no one has yet claimed responsibility.

Attacks of this kind are highly unusual in Jordan, a relatively stable Middle Eastern state where Christians form a small minority.
