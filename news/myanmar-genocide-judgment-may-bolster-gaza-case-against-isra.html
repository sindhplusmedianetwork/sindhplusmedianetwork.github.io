Myanmar genocide judgment may bolster Gaza case against Israel, experts say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Legal experts said a ruling by the International Court of Justice that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya will strengthen South Africa's genocide case against Israel over Gaza. The experts said the judgment establishes that genocidal intent can coexist with a military objective. South Africa's Foreign Minister Ronald Lamola welcomed the judgment as an important precedent.

The case against Israel is not expected to be argued until 2029. The analysis was published as legal debate continued over the implications of the Myanmar ruling. No new court date for the Gaza case was announced in the report.
