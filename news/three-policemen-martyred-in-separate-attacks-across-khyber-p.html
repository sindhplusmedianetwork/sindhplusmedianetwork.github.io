Three Policemen Martyred in Separate Attacks Across Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three policemen were martyred in separate incidents in Bannu, Lakki Marwat and Bajaur on Friday, while unidentified armed men abducted a head constable in Bannu. In a retaliatory operation in Lakki Marwat, police and Counter Terrorism Department personnel killed two suspected target killers allegedly involved in the murder of a police constable. In Bajaur, unidentified armed men launched a late-night attack on the Salai Pati Police Post, martyring Constable Khaista Main Syed, whose funeral prayers were offered at Police Lines with senior officials in attendance.

In Bannu, unidentified gunmen opened fire on CTD Constable Sadiq Zaman at Barmashil Chowk, killing him on the spot; he was visiting his native area on leave from duty in North Waziristan. In another incident, armed men abducted Head Constable Ziaullah from the Karam Garhi area near Baran Dam. Meanwhile, in Lakki Marwat, unidentified armed men opened fire on Constable Mumtaz near a bank, martyring him, after which police and the local peace committee launched an immediate response.
