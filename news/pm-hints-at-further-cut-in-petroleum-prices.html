PM Hints at Further Cut in Petroleum Prices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has indicated that petroleum product prices may be reduced further in the coming days. He said prices are being brought down gradually, and the government intends to pass on relief to the public wherever possible. The prime minister made the remarks during a meeting with Pakistan Muslim League-N leader Khawaja Ahmed Hassan in Model Town, Lahore.

He said the government’s relief measures for the public would continue, with petroleum prices among the areas under review. The remarks follow the recent cut in petrol and diesel prices announced by the oil regulator. No new prices were announced during the meeting, and any further reduction would be decided through the regular fortnightly review.
