Central Park rape suspect arrested in Miami five years later with help of DNA

A man accused of raping three women in New York's Central Park has been arrested in Miami after five years, with the help of DNA evidence. According to American media, the suspect, Derek Rivera DeLeon, was taken into custody by police and US Marshals while he was working at a Burger King restaurant in Miami Beach. New York police said he faces charges of raping three women in Central Park in October 2026, October 2023 and July 2021.

The latest incident took place on October 24, when the suspect allegedly took a 39-year-old woman to a wooded part of the park at knifepoint, raped her and fled. Investigators found that a DNA sample obtained from the 2021 incident matched evidence from the recent sexual assault, after which police reopened the old cases. The victim of the 2023 incident also identified the suspect.

He has been held in the Miami-Dade County jail, and court proceedings are under way to transfer him to New York. Police said five rape cases have been reported in Central Park so far this year.
