Iran Claims Missile and Drone Production Has Moved Underground and Cannot Be Stopped

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Revolutionary Guards have claimed that the country's missile and drone production lines have been moved underground so that production can continue in any situation. Spokesman Brigadier General Hossein Mohibbi told a press conference that the lines were shifted to underground cities to keep the production chain running. He said the production system is flexible and the enemy has never been able to stop Iran from making missiles, adding that all of the aerospace force's weapons are made using local knowledge, skill and technology without importing arms from any other country.

Mohibbi also claimed that Iran has built a vast network of underground missile bases across the country's geography, and that the aerospace force shot down 260 enemy aircraft during the recent conflict with Israel. He said production of missiles and drones continued despite enemy bombing, and the facilities were successfully moved from above ground to underground. The United States and Israel have said the attacks launched in late February aimed to badly damage Iran's missile programme, and President Donald Trump has repeatedly claimed its missile capability suffered a major blow.

Iran rejects those claims, and its officials say production of missiles and other weapons has increased in recent months.
