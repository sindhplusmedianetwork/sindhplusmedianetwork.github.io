PSB schedules coordination meetings for 14th South Asian Games

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Sports Board will hold a series of discipline-wise coordination meetings from November 16 to 20 to finalise preparations for the upcoming 14th South Asian Games. The Games are scheduled to be held from April 3 to 11 in Islamabad, Lahore and Peshawar. The meetings will be chaired by the Director of Games and Events and will take place daily from 1pm at the Games Secretariat in Islamabad.

Federations are required to provide complete lists of sports equipment with estimated costs. They must also share requirements for foreign coaches and country-wise estimates of athletes and officials. Detailed plans for national training camps and pre-Games international exposure competitions are also required from the federations.
