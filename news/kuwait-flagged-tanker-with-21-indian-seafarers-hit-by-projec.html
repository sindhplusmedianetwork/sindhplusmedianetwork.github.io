Kuwait-Flagged Tanker With 21 Indian Seafarers Hit by Projectile in Strait of Hormuz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Kuwait-flagged oil product tanker carrying 21 Indian seafarers was struck by an unknown projectile while passing through the Strait of Hormuz on October 21. The vessel, M/T Al Dasma, was hit in the narrow waterway linking the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman, but all crew members on board are safe. The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations tracked the strike about 11 nautical miles west of Bukha, Oman.

According to reports, the strike sparked a fire on board the tanker, which was later brought under control, and the vessel anchored. India's Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal confirmed the incident on X, writing that all 21 Indian crew members were completely safe. He said the government was continuously monitoring the situation and remained in close contact with the ship's management company.

The attack is the latest in a surge of strikes on commercial shipping in the region.
