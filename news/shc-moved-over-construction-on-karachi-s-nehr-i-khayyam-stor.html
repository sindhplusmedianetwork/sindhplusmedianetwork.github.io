SHC moved over construction on Karachi's Nehr-i-Khayyam storm-water drain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Jamaat-i-Islami and some Clifton residents have moved the Sindh High Court against the provincial government over construction and development activities on Karachi's Nehr-i-Khayyam. Leader of the Opposition in the City Council, Advocate Saifuddin, and three others filed the petition, naming the chief secretary, the local government secretary, the Karachi Metropolitan Corporation, the Karachi Development Authority, the Karachi Water and Sewage Corporation, the Sindh Environmental Protection Agency and the Boat Basin SHO as respondents. The petitioners said the construction in and around the waterway, including adjacent amenity plots, raised serious concerns about preserving the natural watercourse and the city's rainwater drainage.

They contended the project was proceeding rapidly without a proper technical and environmental assessment, which could disrupt rainwater flow during the monsoon and endanger public safety. They also noted that the judiciary had halted construction near Nehr-i-Khayyam on several occasions in the past, but work had resumed after brief suspensions. The petition asks the court to declare the conversion or commercial use of the drain illegal, to halt all ongoing construction immediately, and to order the removal of encroachments and structures within the waterway's lawful boundaries.
