Fresh Arrest Warrants Issued for Sohail Afridi in May 9 Radio Pakistan Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-terrorism court in Peshawar has issued fresh arrest warrants for Sohail Afridi and Amir Chamkani in the May 9 Radio Pakistan attack case. Judge Wali Muhammad Khan heard the case, with the investigation officer present in court. Sohail Afridi, Taimur Saleem Jhagra, Kamran Bangash, Irfan Saleem and Amir Chamkani are named in the case.

Taimur Saleem Jhagra, Kamran Bangash and Irfan Saleem have filed applications for pre-arrest bail. The court extended the interim bail of Kamran Bangash, Taimur Saleem Jhagra and Irfan Saleem until November 17. The case will be heard again on the next date fixed by the court.
