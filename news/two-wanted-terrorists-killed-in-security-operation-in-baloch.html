Two Wanted Terrorists Killed in Security Operation in Balochistan's Chaghi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces have killed two wanted terrorists in an intelligence-based operation in Dalbandin, a town in Balochistan's Chagai district. According to security sources, the forces carried out the targeted operation against terrorist hideouts in the area. The killed terrorists were wanted for harassing passengers on the Quetta-Taftan highway and for spreading fear among local residents.

Security officials said the operation was planned after receiving credible intelligence about the presence of the suspects. No security personnel were harmed during the operation. The action is part of the continuing campaign against terrorism in the region.
