Government to Keep Wheat Reserves of Up to Six Million Tonnes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Food Security Minister Rana Tanveer Hussain announced on Monday that the government would maintain strategic wheat reserves of up to six million tonnes, saying adequate arrangements were in place and there would be no shortage of wheat in Pakistan this year. He said the reserves would help ensure uninterrupted supplies and protect the country against potential market disruptions. His remarks came days after Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif announced an indicative wheat price of 4,500 rupees per maund for 2027, up by 1,000 rupees from last year, following consultations with all provincial governments.

The minister said imported wheat would also cost about 4,500 rupees per maund, making domestic procurement the better option, and that the government would prioritise Pakistani farmers rather than rely unnecessarily on imports. He disclosed that more than 1.3 million tonnes of wheat had already been provided to the provinces from the stocks of the Pakistan Agricultural Storage and Services Corporation, and that up to 550,000 tonnes could be imported if provincial demand required it. Describing the recent price rise as artificial and manipulated by certain market elements, he said sufficient stocks were available and expressed confidence that prices would return to normal within the next few days.

He added that the government was closely monitoring the market and would act against artificial shortages, speculation and unjustified price increases, while Pakistan Kissan Ittehad welcomed the indicative price and the consultation process.
