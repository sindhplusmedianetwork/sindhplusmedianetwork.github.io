NCCIA Lahore Zone Crackdown on Cyber Crimes: 13 Suspects Arrested Including 6 Women

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency's Lahore Zone, in a major crackdown against cyber crimes, arrested 13 suspects including six women in various operations. During the operations, two illegal international call centres were exposed in Lahore, Multan and Gujranwala, where networks involved in online fraud and other serious cyber crimes were active. In the operation carried out on the instructions of Additional Director NCCIA Lahore Zone Atif Amir, a raid on Model Town Link Road led to the arrest of eight suspects including six women from a call centre selling fake packages in the name of British telecom companies, and laptops and CPUs were also seized during the operation.

Similarly, two more suspects were arrested from an illegal call centre established at Park View Broadway Commercial Plaza, and according to preliminary investigations the suspects were involved in financial fraud by calling British citizens through dialer software. The NCCIA also arrested a suspect named Muhammad Hashim Tahir in an operation in Johar Town, who is accused of sexually exploiting and threatening a nine-year-old girl residing in the United States online, while in Multan a suspect was arrested on allegations of harassing a minor girl and sharing objectionable pictures on WhatsApp, and the Gujranwala team arrested a suspect named Sawan Mahmood in Jabran area of Sheikhupura district, who is accused of converting a girl's pictures into objectionable material and blackmailing her. According to NCCIA officials, operations against elements involved in international cyber fraud networks, online harassment and sexual exploitation of children will continue, and Additional Director NCCIA Lahore Zone Atif Amir expressed the resolve that indiscriminate operations will continue against elements involved in cyber crimes and effective measures will be taken to protect citizens, especially children, from online crimes.
