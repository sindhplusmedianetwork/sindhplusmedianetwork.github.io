Makkah Pact Is for Defence, Not War, Says Naqvi; Pakistan Stands With Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi has said the Makkah defence agreement is meant for defence, not for war. Addressing the Qatar Security Forum in Doha, Naqvi assured that Pakistan will stand with Saudi Arabia in every difficult time. He said the purpose of the pact is to strengthen collective defence, and Pakistan will back the Kingdom through all challenges.

Naqvi added that Pakistan will continue its efforts for peace between Iran and the United States. He shared three lessons he drew from the region's conflicts: never close the doors of dialogue, trust is essential for negotiations, and sincere efforts by all sides are needed for lasting peace. He linked regional stability to the fight against terrorism and underlined Pakistan's contributions to United Nations peace missions.

The minister's remarks come as Pakistan continues to explain the aims of the Makkah defence pact at international forums.
