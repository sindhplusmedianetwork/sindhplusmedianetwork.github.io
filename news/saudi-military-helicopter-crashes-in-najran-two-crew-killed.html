Saudi Military Helicopter Crashes in Najran, Two Crew Killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Saudi military helicopter has crashed in the Najran region, killing two crew members on board. Coalition spokesman Brigadier General Turki al-Maliki said the aircraft went down because of a technical fault. He confirmed that both crew members died in the crash.

In a separate announcement, the Saudi-led coalition said its forces had destroyed drones over the capital Riyadh and the Eastern Province. The coalition also said it intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles fired by the Houthis toward Khamis Mushait. The announcements come amid continued cross-border exchanges between the coalition and Houthi forces from Yemen.
