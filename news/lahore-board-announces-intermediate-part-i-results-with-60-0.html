Lahore Board Announces Intermediate Part-I Results With 60.03% Pass Rate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education has announced the results of the Intermediate Part-I annual examinations for 2026. The overall pass rate stood at 60.03 percent, marking a 7 percent improvement over last year. Out of 165,245 candidates who appeared, 99,191 passed while 66,054 could not clear the exams.

Female students outperformed their male counterparts, recording a 68.23 percent pass rate compared to 47.32 percent for boys. Among academic groups, pre-engineering led with 79.23 percent, followed by pre-medical at 74.16 percent. General science stood at 61.05 percent, commerce at 52.66 percent and humanities at 50.17 percent.

Board officials congratulated the successful candidates and advised those who did not pass to prepare better for the next attempt.
