Cotton Bales Gutted in Warehouse Fire in Karachi, Multiple Blazes Reported

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Multiple fire incidents were reported in different parts of Karachi, prompting responses from Rescue 1122 fire and rescue teams. A fire broke out at a cotton warehouse on the seventh floor of Rampa Plaza on MA Jinnah Road, destroying cotton bales and other stored goods, with no casualties reported. Sources said the fire erupted while cotton bales were being transported through a lift, and all employees inside managed to evacuate safely, though the blaze also damaged the lift.

A house fire in Nazimabad No. 4 was extinguished by the fire brigade with no injuries reported, while solar panels on a building on Khalid Bin Waleed Road also caught fire and occupants were safely evacuated. In Liaquatabad, a massive fire at shops and a warehouse dealing in wooden doors and sheets destroyed a large quantity of timber and other goods, and a hybrid car was gutted by fire in the parking area of Jinnah Terminal while being towed, reduced to ashes before the fire brigade could reach the scene.

A fire in the kitchen of a restaurant in DHA Phase VI also led to the evacuation of residents in apartments above, with no casualties reported.
