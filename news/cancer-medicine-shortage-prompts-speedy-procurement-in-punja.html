Cancer medicine shortage prompts speedy procurement in Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's health secretary has ordered the speedy procurement of cancer medicines after shortages were reported at six government hospitals in the province. The order followed a report in The Express Tribune about the shortage of medicines for more than 3,200 registered cancer patients. The medicine Nilotinib, used by chronic myeloid leukaemia patients, had stopped reaching Mayo Hospital in Lahore since June this year.

Mayo Hospital is the supply centre for the other five hospitals, so the disruption affected patients across the province. Officials have been directed to complete the procurement in the shortest possible time. The free home delivery of medicines for registered patients is also to be restored.

The health secretary warned that no negligence in the matter would be tolerated.
