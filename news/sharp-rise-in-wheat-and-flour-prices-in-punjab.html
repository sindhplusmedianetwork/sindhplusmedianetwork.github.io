Sharp Rise in Wheat and Flour Prices in Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Wheat and flour prices have risen sharply in Punjab's open market. Wheat has reached Rs5,900 per maund generally, and Rs5,920 per maund in Rawalpindi and Islamabad. A 15-kg flour bag now costs Rs2,500 wholesale in Lahore and Rs2,650 in Rawalpindi and Islamabad.

The agriculture department has confirmed a wheat production shortfall of 2.5 million tonnes this year. Flour mills have demanded emergency permission to import 500,000 tonnes of wheat. They have warned that prices could rise further and that the price crisis could turn into an availability crisis.
