China's Communist Party Opens Key Plenum Ahead of Expected Xi Fourth Term

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China's Communist Party began a four-day meeting of its highest-ranking members on Monday, targeting concrete measures for full and rigorous party self-governance, as President Xi Jinping seeks to reinforce his authority and enforce ideological uniformity. Xi, 73, who is in his third five-year term as head of the party, the state and the military, briefed the party's Central Committee on a draft document that state news agency Xinhua said addressed the challenges the party faced, including growing its governing capacity, guarding against complacency and combating corruption, his signature campaign. The Central Committee will meet behind closed doors from Monday to Thursday in the fifth plenum since the current cohort was formed in 2022, and analysts say it could be the last major party gathering before its 2027 leadership reshuffle, when Xi is expected to hold on to the top job for a fourth term.

The plenum comprises 205 full members and 171 alternate members, and the draft submitted to it summarises the achievements and experience of strict party self-governance and offers an analysis of party building at present and for some time to come. Xi has carried out a widespread anti-corruption campaign since 2013, punishing or removing thousands of officials, including more than 100 generals, a drive that has helped him consolidate his rule. Kerry Brown, director of King's College London's Lau China Institute, said the plenum would present Xi as in control and able to continue his command, and that appearances would matter as much as substance at this gathering.

With substantive policy issues largely set in previous gatherings, the meeting is expected to focus on party organisation and discipline rather than a new economic plan, whose basic direction was already set at last year's fourth plenum.
