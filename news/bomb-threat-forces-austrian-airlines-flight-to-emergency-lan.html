Bomb Threat Forces Austrian Airlines Flight to Emergency Landing in Bulgaria

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Austrian Airlines flight travelling from Tel Aviv to Vienna was forced to make an emergency landing in Sofia, Bulgaria, after the crew received an anonymous bomb threat. The aircraft was cruising at around 36,000 feet when the threat came in, and the pilots diverted toward the Bulgarian border as a precaution. A youth delegation on its way to a United Nations event was among those on board.

Security forces surrounded the plane on the tarmac and carried out a full search of the aircraft, the baggage and the passengers. The operation continued for more than five hours, but no explosives were confirmed. Once authorities declared the aircraft clear, the flight was allowed to continue its journey to Vienna.

Passengers resumed their travel after the long security ordeal.
