Listed Companies' Profits Rise 25% in First Nine Months of 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's listed corporate sector recorded a 25% year-on-year increase in profits during the first nine months of calendar year 2026, the strongest growth for this period in three years, Adviser to the Finance Minister Khurram Schehzad said on Thursday. In a post on social media platform X, Schehzad said the improvement reflected stronger business performance and improving economic fundamentals across major industries. He said corporate profits grew 18% year-on-year in FY26, while profits increased 23% year-on-year in the first quarter of FY27, from July to September.

Non-bank corporate earnings jumped 37% year-on-year in the latest quarter, showing momentum beyond the banking sector. In the energy sector, profits of exploration and production companies rose 43%, while earnings of oil marketing companies surged 144%. Among industrial sectors, engineering companies recorded 304% growth, followed by automobiles at 19% and cement at 13%, while the technology sector grew 31% and pharmaceutical and fertiliser companies rose 14% and 11% respectively.

Schehzad attributed the improving performance to greater macroeconomic stability, easing financing pressures, and ongoing fiscal, energy and business reforms, along with targeted tax relief for businesses and exporters in the FY2026-27 budget. He said the corporate sector was moving beyond recovery, and stressed that the next phase should turn improved earnings into productive investment, industrial expansion and job creation through greater private-sector participation.
