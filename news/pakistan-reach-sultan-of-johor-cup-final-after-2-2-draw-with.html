Pakistan Reach Sultan of Johor Cup Final After 2-2 Draw With Japan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's junior hockey team has reached the final of the Sultan of Johor Cup after a 2-2 draw with Japan in their last league match. Hamza Fayaz and Ghulam Mustafa scored the goals for the young Green Shirts. Both Pakistan and Japan finished level on seven points, but Pakistan qualified ahead on more goals scored — 18 against Japan's 16.

The result set up a final against hosts Malaysia. The tournament has been a strong showing for Pakistan's junior side, which held its nerve in the decisive league fixture. The final will decide the champion of this year's edition.
