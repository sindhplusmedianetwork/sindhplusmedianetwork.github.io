Minister Insists Referendum on New Provinces Will Happen

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Parliamentary Affairs Minister Tariq Fazal Chaudhry on Friday insisted that a referendum on the formation of new provinces will happen. The remarks come after PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari delivered a speech on Tuesday that covered the question of new provinces, in which Bilawal expressed opposition to the creation of provinces and the idea of holding a referendum and cautioned that any forced attempt to divide provinces, particularly in Sindh, could lead to bloodshed. The federal government maintains that a referendum is a legal and constitutional method of gauging public opinion on new provinces and that no political party should have reservations regarding it.

Speaking to the Geo News programme Naya Pakistan on Friday, Chaudhry, when asked about the referendum, insisted that developing consensus on the matter should be a priority for the ruling coalition and said national consensus is very important on the issue of new provinces, highlighting that the PTI-led opposition was also in favour of it. Chaudhry said the process of taking the public's opinion cannot be stopped if a single party opposes it, insisted the referendum will happen, and added that the PML-N would undertake every last effort to convince the PPP. He remarked that a referendum is neither non-constitutional nor undemocratic and the public's opinion can be asked on any important matter, and on being asked about the timeline said it was premature to talk about it however things will be clarified soon.

Asked if PML-N supremo Nawaz Sharif was on board with the referendum, Chaudhry answered in the affirmative and added that the prime minister does not make any major decisions without his opinion.
