Fireworks explode near Army helicopter before Seattle NFL game, alarming 70,000 fans

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three US Army Black Hawk helicopters were flying over Lumen Field before the Seattle Seahawks vs Chicago Bears Monday Night Football game when pre-game fireworks exploded alarmingly close to the last helicopter in the formation. The incident, which unfolded before more than 70,000 spectators, left fans shaken as the fireworks lit up the sky dangerously near the aircraft. The helicopter remained safe and no casualties were reported.

Videos of the near-miss went viral on social media, with fans questioning the fireworks arrangements at the stadium. The episode has raised concerns about the coordination of aerial displays during large sporting events.
