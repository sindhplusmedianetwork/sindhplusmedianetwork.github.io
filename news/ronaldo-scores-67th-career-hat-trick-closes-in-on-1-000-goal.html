Ronaldo Scores 67th Career Hat-Trick, Closes in on 1,000 Goals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cristiano Ronaldo completed his 67th career hat-trick on Friday, firing Al Nassr to a 5-3 victory over Al Kholood in the Saudi Pro League. The Portuguese star scored all three goals within 15 minutes, underlining his enduring sharpness in front of goal. The treble took his career goal tally to 985, leaving him just 15 short of the historic 1,000-goal milestone.

It was his seventh hat-trick for Al Nassr and the 67th of his professional career. At 40, Ronaldo continues to defy age with consistent scoring form. Al Nassr's win keeps them firmly in the title race.
