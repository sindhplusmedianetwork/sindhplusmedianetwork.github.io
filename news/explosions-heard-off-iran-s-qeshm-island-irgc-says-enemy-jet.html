Explosions heard off Iran's Qeshm island; IRGC says enemy jet forced back

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Several explosions were heard in the maritime area off Iran's Qeshm island, according to Iranian news agencies. The reports said the aerospace force of the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps (IRGC) intercepted an enemy fighter jet in the area and forced it to leave. The IRGC further claimed that three tankers caught fire after striking sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most critical shipping lanes.

Separately, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency reported a vessel on fire off the coast of Oman. The developments add to the mounting tensions in the region, where shipping traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has faced repeated attacks and disruptions. Iranian state media has carried similar claims in recent days, including images it says show interceptions of advanced drones over the strait.

Independent verification of the latest claims was not immediately available.
