Federal JIT demanded to probe Valika Hospital HIV cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Advocate Tariq Mansoor, representing children allegedly infected with HIV at Valika Hospital in Karachi, has demanded that the federal government constitute a high-level Joint Investigation Team to conduct an independent and impartial inquiry, accusing the Sindh government of attempting to suppress the case. He claimed that no report had been submitted by the inquiry committee formed to investigate the incident, while no FIR had been registered so far. He demanded a transparent investigation to identify those responsible and ensure justice, lifelong medical treatment and financial assistance for the affected children.

He urged the federal government to constitute a JIT under Section 3 of the Pakistan Commissions of Inquiry Act and appealed to Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir to intervene. Parents of the affected children complained that they continued to face serious difficulties in securing medical treatment, financial assistance and justice. According to the parents, four children from one family have been diagnosed with HIV, but only two are receiving financial assistance from the Auqaf Fund.

The lawyer said he had also written to the World Health Organization, UNICEF, the International Labour Organization and European organisations seeking support for the affected families.
