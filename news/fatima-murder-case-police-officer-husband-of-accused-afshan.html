Fatima Murder Case: Police Officer Husband of Accused Afshan to Be Included in Investigation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

After CCTV footage emerged of the alleged torture of 13-year-old domestic worker Fatima, who was killed at Islamabad Police Lines Headquarters, it was decided to further widen the scope of investigation, and the arrested suspect Afshan's husband, a grade-19 police officer, will also be included in the investigation. The CCTV footage in the case of Fatima's alleged torture and murder at Islamabad Police Lines Headquarters has provided important evidence related to the incident. In the footage, Fatima can be seen being tortured with a stick; during the torture the girl goes out of the room but is brought back into the room and tortured again, and she can also be seen crying and pleading in the video.

Photographs of torture and burn wounds on Fatima's body have also emerged, and police sources say a match has been found between the CCTV footage and the medical report, while evidence has been sent to the laboratory for forensic analysis. In light of court orders, police have decided to include Afshan's husband, a grade-19 police officer, in the investigation and his statement will be recorded soon. The arrested suspect Afshan and Qari Ali are already part of the investigation and both will be produced in court again on Monday.
