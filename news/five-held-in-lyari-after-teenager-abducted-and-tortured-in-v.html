Five Held in Lyari After Teenager Abducted and Tortured in Viral Video

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five suspects, including a son of notorious Lyari gang-war commander Baba Ladla, have been taken into custody over the alleged abduction and brutal torture of a teenager in Karachi's Lyari area. A disturbing mobile phone video of the assault surfaced on social media and was aired by Express News, showing the teenager stripped, tied up with ropes and repeatedly beaten with a leather belt and a stick. The incident reportedly took place on October 11 in Phool Patti Lane, and police officials said it stemmed from a dispute among young men rather than a clash between gang-war groups.

A case was registered at the Baghdadi police station on October 14 on the complaint of the victim's maternal uncle, Kabir Ahmed, under charges including attempted murder, abduction and assault. In his complaint, Kabir Ahmed said his 17-year-old nephew Ghulam Nabi, a Class VIII student, returned home that night in a frightened state with visible marks of torture on his face. The teenager told his uncle he had been seized by seven armed men near Dongi Chowk while walking to work, taken to a small room, tied up and severely tortured, with the assault filmed on a mobile phone.

He also alleged that one of the suspects threatened to kill him and drew a pistol, but another pushed his hand up so the shot missed. The suspects untied him after he pleaded for his life and warned him not to return to the area.
