WHO Approves Clinical Trials for Two New Ebola Vaccines

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Health Organisation has recommended adding two new vaccine candidates to ongoing clinical trials in the Democratic Republic of the Congo to combat an Ebola outbreak caused by the Bundibugyo virus. The two vaccines, ChAdOx1 BDBV, developed by the University of Oxford with the Serum Institute of India, and Moderna's mRNA-1469, are the first vaccines developed specifically for the Bundibugyo virus. The DRC Ministry of Health confirmed that the death toll from the virus had risen to 5,163, with 10,603 confirmed cases as of November 2, a case fatality rate of 48.7 per cent.

The WHO announcement was made in Geneva on November 5. The trials aim to test the vaccines' safety and effectiveness as health workers continue to battle the outbreak in the country.
