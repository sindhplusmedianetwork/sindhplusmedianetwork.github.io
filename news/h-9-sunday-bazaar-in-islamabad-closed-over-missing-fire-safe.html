H-9 Sunday Bazaar in Islamabad Closed Over Missing Fire-Safety Arrangements

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad's H-9 Sunday Bazaar has been shut down for lacking fire-safety arrangements. Shopkeepers protested the closure, raising slogans against the Capital Development Authority. The traders claim they have spent crores of rupees on safety measures.

Between 200 and 250 shopkeepers staged a sit-in outside the bazaar, demanding that it be reopened immediately. The closure has affected vendors while also depriving citizens of the chance to buy low-priced goods.
