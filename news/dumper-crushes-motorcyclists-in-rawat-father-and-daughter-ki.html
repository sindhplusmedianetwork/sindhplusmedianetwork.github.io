Dumper crushes motorcyclists in Rawat; father and daughter killed on the spot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A dumper crushed a motorcycle near Dera village in the Rawat area of Rawalpindi, killing a father and his daughter on the spot. The victims were 49-year-old Jehanzeb Khan and his 28-year-old daughter Sehrish Haseeb, who were travelling with Jehanzeb's six-year-old grandson Amin Haseeb. Officials said the dumper driver allegedly drove negligently and struck the motorcycle, leaving the father and daughter critically injured, and they died before they could receive treatment.

The six-year-old boy was injured in the collision. Teams from Rescue 1122 Rawalpindi and Rawat police reached the scene, gave the injured child first aid and shifted him to hospital. The bodies of the father and daughter were also moved to hospital after legal formalities were completed.
