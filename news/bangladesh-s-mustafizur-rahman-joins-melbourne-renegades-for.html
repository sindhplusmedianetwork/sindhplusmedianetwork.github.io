Bangladesh's Mustafizur Rahman Joins Melbourne Renegades for Big Bash League

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bangladesh fast bowler Mustafizur Rahman has become part of the Melbourne Renegades squad for the 16th edition of the Big Bash League. It will be the 31-year-old's first appearance in the BBL. Mustafizur has taken 423 wickets in 331 T20 franchise matches so far in his career.

The Renegades signed him as a replacement for fast bowler Jason Behrendorff, who has been ruled out of the upcoming season with injury. He will not be available for the Renegades' opening match against the Perth Scorchers due to prior commitments in the International League T20. Expressing his delight at playing the BBL for the first time, Mustafizur said he was looking forward to the tournament.

Renegades head coach Luke Ronchi described Mustafizur as a T20 match-winner, saying his ability to take wickets in the powerplay and at the death would strengthen the team's bowling attack.
