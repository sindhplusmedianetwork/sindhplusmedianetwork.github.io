Karachi Kings Secure Direct Signing of England Leg-Spinner Adil Rashid for PSL 12

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Kings have announced the direct signing of England leg-spinner Adil Rashid for the 12th season of the Pakistan Super League. The franchise confirmed Rashid will represent the team in HBL PSL 12. Rashid has played more than 330 international matches for England during his career.

Across formats, he has taken over 470 wickets, including 244 wickets in One Day Internationals and 172 wickets in T20 Internationals. Karachi Kings owner Salman Iqbal said Rashid's experience, skill and winning spirit will be valuable for the team in the upcoming season.
