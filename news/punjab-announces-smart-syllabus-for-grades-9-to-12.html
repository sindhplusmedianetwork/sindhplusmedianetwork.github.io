Punjab announces smart syllabus for grades 9 to 12

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Education Minister Rana Sikandar Hayat has announced a smart syllabus for students in the province. The syllabus for grades 9, 10, 11 and 12 will be issued by PECTA on Saturday. According to the minister, the syllabus is in its final stages of preparation.

He said the smart syllabus is designed to make next year's board examinations easier for students. The announcement covers students from grade 9 through grade 12 across Punjab.
