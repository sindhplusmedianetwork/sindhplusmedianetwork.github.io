Russian Missiles, Suspected Drone Come Down in Poland During Ukraine Bombardment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland on Friday during a major Russian bombardment of neighbouring Ukraine, prompting Warsaw to describe the incidents as a likely provocation and NATO to call them a violation of Polish airspace. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk said the incidents appeared to be an intentional provocation timed to coincide with European military exercises in southeastern Poland, where troops are preparing for a possible postwar mission in Ukraine. There were no immediate reports of injuries or damage.

One Russian missile is believed to have crashed about 35 kilometres from the exercise area, while a Kh-101 cruise missile came down roughly 50 kilometres from the Ukrainian border. A suspected jet-powered drone struck around 30 kilometres from the border. The incidents occurred as European forces were taking part in an unprecedented two-week exercise that began on November 9, intended to prepare for a possible mission to help stabilise Ukraine and rebuild its military after the war.

There was no immediate comment from Moscow on the missiles, the suspected drone or the military exercises, and the developments heightened tensions between Europe and Russia as the United States seeks to advance negotiations to end the Ukraine conflict.
