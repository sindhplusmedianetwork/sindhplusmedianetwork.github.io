Diwali Celebrated Across Sindh Cities as Muslims Join Hindu Festivities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Hindu community celebrated the religious festival of Diwali with great enthusiasm in cities across Sindh, lighting up temples and homes and filling the air with fireworks. In Sujawal, the Hindu neighbourhood of Hindustan Mohalla glowed with lights as families decorated their homes and temples and celebrated through the night. In Naushahro Feroze, special ceremonies were held at the historic Baba Jamna Das temple, where large numbers of people gathered, lit lamps and set off fireworks.

In Sukkur and surrounding areas, special prayers were offered at temples, including the central Napar Darbar, where a large gathering exchanged greetings and shared sweets and gifts. Senior officials, including the commissioner, the deputy inspector general of police and the deputy commissioner, attended the Sukkur ceremony, where the Hindu Panchayat presented them with turbans and shawls. Muslims in several cities joined the celebrations and congratulated the Hindu community, and prayers were offered for the country's security, stability, progress and prosperity.
