Five trainees fall ill at Quetta Police Training College; two died of heart attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five under-training police personnel at the Police Training College in Quetta were shifted to hospital after suffering from high blood pressure. A spokesman for the Balochistan Police said the condition of the personnel taken to hospital is out of danger. He said two personnel at the college, Samiullah and Mirza Khan, suffered heart attacks on November 5 and 7 and died.

The spokesman said no incident of substandard food or food poisoning has come to light at the Police Training College. The college administration and police officials are monitoring the health of the trainees.
