Microsoft's medical AI model outperforms doctors in diagnosing complex cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new study on the medical use of artificial intelligence says Microsoft's 'MAI-DxO' model showed remarkable performance in diagnosing complex diseases. According to the company, the system is not meant to replace doctors but to help physicians analyse difficult medical cases, compare possible illnesses and reach correct diagnoses. The AI was tested on medical cases in which identifying the illness required combining the patient's symptoms, medical history and various test results.

The company says that in some experiments the model diagnosed more accurately than human doctors and reached conclusions with relatively fewer tests and lower costs. In one diagnostic experiment, the AI's accuracy was about 1.7 times that of doctors, and in another comparison the model made correct diagnoses 83.2 percent of the time versus 36.1 percent for doctors, while in emergency cases it reached 80.3 percent against 46.8 percent for doctors. Microsoft says that in some cases the AI helped reach the correct diagnosis at about half to one-third of the cost compared to doctors.

However, the report notes these results apply to the specific cases and experimental conditions used in the research and cannot be applied equally to all patients and medical situations. Experts say such systems could in future help doctors review symptoms and histories in an organised way, but further research and validation are needed to test their effectiveness and safety in real clinical settings.
