Crumbling Jauhar flyover leaves commuters driving over danger

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A flyover in Karachi's Gulistan-e-Jauhar neighbourhood is crumbling, leaving commuters to drive daily over a damaged structure that residents describe as dangerous. Thousands of people use the route every day to reach their homes, workplaces and schools. The flyover's poor condition is part of a wider pattern of neglect in the area, where main roads and inner streets have been scarred by broken surfaces, deep potholes and years of disrepair.

University Road, one of the area's main arteries, remains choked by construction work on the Bus Rapid Transit Red Line project, pushing more traffic onto already damaged alternative routes. On Pehlwan Goth Road, another key approach to the neighbourhood, a journey that should take ten minutes often stretches to between 30 and 40 minutes because of the road's condition. Residents say repair work has begun on some stretches, but the pace remains slow, and they fear the flyover's deterioration could end in a serious accident if it is not fixed soon.
