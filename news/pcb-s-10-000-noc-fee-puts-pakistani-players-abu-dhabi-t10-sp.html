PCB's $10,000 NOC Fee Puts Pakistani Players' Abu Dhabi T10 Spots in Doubt

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board's demand of $10,000 per player for No-Objection Certificates has thrown the participation of Pakistani cricketers in the 2026 Abu Dhabi T10 league into fresh uncertainty. The league, which begins on November 7, has reportedly refused to foot the bill, and organisers have told contracted players they must pay the amount from their own pockets if they wish to take part. Players who fail to pay in time face removal from drafts and squads, with replacements to be brought in.

Among the Pakistanis signed up are Shadab Khan, Naseem Shah, Fakhar Zaman, Salman Irshad, Sufyan Moqim, Iftikhar Ahmed, Abbas Afridi and Faheem Ashraf, spread across sides including the UAE Bulls, Royal Desert Champions, Emirates Eagles, Arabian Aces and United Lions. The dispute follows a similar row over the recent Nepal Premier League, where organisers alleged the PCB demanded $25,000 per player, and an earlier disagreement with Major League Cricket franchises over the same amount. The PCB has not commented on the reported fee, though chairman Mohsin Naqvi had announced a separate contract category for T20 specialists who would not need NOCs — contracts that are yet to be distributed.

With the league refusing to cover the charge, the contracted cricketers may have to pay personally or miss the competition.
