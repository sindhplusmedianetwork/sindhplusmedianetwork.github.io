Hina Pervaiz Butt's Remarks Embarrass the Government, Says Azma Bokhari

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's information minister has said that Hina Pervaiz Butt's remarks become a problem for her and an embarrassment for the government. Speaking on a television program, she said she did not want to discuss how serious the lawmaker's tweets were, adding that the PML-N MPA perhaps said such things out of naivety, which later became a problem for her and a source of embarrassment for the government. The minister also said police in Punjab had only two hours to arrest suspects in crimes against children and women, and that a special crime-control department was created for serious crimes, after which crime had declined in the province.

Referring to the Multan boy's killing, she said it was only CCTV footage that exposed the case, otherwise the child's death would have been passed off as a traffic accident. She said the boy received no salary and worked for two meals a day, tried to flee his employer three times, and that the accused had fled custody with two men coming to free him. She added that seven personnel of the crime-control department had been martyred and more than a hundred injured in encounters over the past year, while the second man who was in the car with the accused was still at large.

She further said the chief minister had held a meeting the same day asking why violence against women and children was rising.
