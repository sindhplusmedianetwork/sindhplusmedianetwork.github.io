Xbox Says Project Helix Is a Console First, Not a PC

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Microsoft has clarified that its upcoming Project Helix hardware is being designed as a traditional Xbox console with PC gaming capabilities, rather than a gaming PC carrying the Xbox brand. The clarification came from Jason Ronald, Xbox vice president of next generation, in a new article published by the company as part of its 25th anniversary coverage. Ronald said the design goal was clear, describing Helix as a console that is also capable of playing games from a PC, and not a PC that can also play console games.

He said players should be able to access their games immediately without having to manage operating system updates, graphics drivers or complicated configurations. Users who want greater control over performance settings and customisation will still have those options, according to the company. Microsoft also pointed to the ROG Xbox Ally and its dedicated Xbox mode as important steps towards Project Helix, with feedback from combining PC and console libraries on the handheld helping to shape the next-generation interface.

Backward compatibility is another major priority, with Project Helix intended to support four generations of Xbox games so players can keep their existing libraries and carry progress between devices.
