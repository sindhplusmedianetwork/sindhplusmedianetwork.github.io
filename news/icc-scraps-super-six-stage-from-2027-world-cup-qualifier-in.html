ICC Scraps Super Six Stage From 2027 World Cup Qualifier in Major Format Change

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Cricket Council has announced major changes to the format of the 2027 ODI World Cup qualifier, scrapping the Super Six stage. The qualifier will be played in February and March 2027 between 10 teams, with 23 matches instead of 34. The tournament will comprise two groups of five, with the top two teams from each group reaching the semi-finals.

The winner of the final will qualify directly for the 2027 World Cup group stage, while the other three semi-finalists will compete in a Super Series to decide the final group-stage place. The ICC said the changes were made with increasing opportunities for full and associate members in mind, strengthening qualification pathways and enhancing competitive standards. The new format gives every team a shorter and clearer route to the World Cup.
