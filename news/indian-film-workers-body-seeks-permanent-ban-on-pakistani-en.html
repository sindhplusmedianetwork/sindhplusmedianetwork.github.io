Indian Film Workers Body Seeks Permanent Ban on Pakistani Entertainment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Indian film workers association has urged the Indian government to permanently ban Pakistani entertainment content. The association has also sought a block on the release in India of an upcoming Pakistani original series, 'Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo'. In a letter dated November 24, it sought legal action against streaming platforms carrying Pakistani films, dramas, songs and podcasts.

The series is the first Pakistani original series on the streaming platform. Neither the streaming platform nor the Indian government has announced a final decision on the series. The series is due for global release on December 4.
