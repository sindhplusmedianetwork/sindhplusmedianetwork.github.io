Government gives further Rs1.65 per litre relief in petroleum levy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has given further relief of Rs1.65 per litre in the petroleum levy on petrol. According to official documents, the levy burden on citizens has fallen to Rs63.64 per litre. The relief follows a change in the government's petrol pricing formula, and the positive effects of the change are continuing.

Petrol prices have fallen in stages over the past week, and petrol has become cheaper by more than Rs35 per litre during the week so far. The latest relief in the levy is expected to ease the burden on consumers further.
