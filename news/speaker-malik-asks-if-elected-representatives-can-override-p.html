Speaker Malik asks if elected representatives can override people's verdict in a referendum

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Assembly Speaker Malik Muhammad Ahmad Khan has questioned whether elected representatives could override the people's will if they expressed support for new provinces through a referendum. He described the matter as a constitutional question that required interpretation, rather than one with a simple answer. Malik was responding to Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's proposal for a referendum on creating new provinces.

He said the people were sovereign and their representatives could not negate their expressed wishes. Malik also played down the controversy over minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain's remarks on corruption in Punjab, calling it a storm in a teacup. He praised Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's development initiatives in the province, presenting them as evidence of the government's work.
