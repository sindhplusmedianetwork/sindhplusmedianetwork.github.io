Jam Kamal Opens Pakistan Products Export Display Centre in Doha

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Commerce Minister Jam Kamal Khan has inaugurated an export display centre for Pakistani products in Doha, the capital of Qatar. According to the commerce ministry, the centre aims to promote Pakistani products in the Qatari market and to boost the country's exports. During his visit, the minister also met members of the Pakistani business community living in Doha, where ways to strengthen Pakistan-Qatar trade relations and increase export opportunities were discussed.

Jam Kamal Khan said stronger business links are necessary to expand the access of Pakistani products to the Qatari market, adding that overseas Pakistanis can play an important role in promoting exports. He also stressed increasing direct links between Pakistani exporters and Qatari business enterprises. The ministry said the display centre will help introduce Pakistani products to Qatari buyers.

The minister said Pakistan is committed to expanding trade and economic cooperation with Qatar, and that increasing access for Pakistani products to world markets and finding new export markets are key government priorities.
