Silicon Valley elites 'bankrolled' news platform Visegrad24 publishing pro-Israel content: report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

An extensive investigation by an American magazine revealed on Thursday how an anti-migrant, pro-Israel propaganda machine took form. The report said the machine gained traction via bankrolling by Silicon Valley elites. It detailed the rise of Polish platform Visegrad24 and its founder Stefan Tompson.

The account pumped out nearly 400 posts within three days after the Hamas attack on Oct 7, 2023, the report said. Those posts racked up 370 million views, according to the report. The information in the report has not been independently verified.
