Keenan Hat-Trick Powers Ireland to 42-12 Rout of Argentina

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ireland beat Argentina 42-12 in their Nations Championship match at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin on Friday. Full-back Hugo Keenan scored a hat-trick in his first home Test appearance in over a year, as Ireland ran in six tries against a disappointing Pumas side. Argentina took an early lead through Mateo Carreras, but Keenan crossed either side of captain Dan Sheehan's try to put Ireland 21-5 ahead at half-time.

Sam Prendergast scored his first international try and kicked five conversions, while Ronan Kelleher also crossed late to complete the rout. The win was Ireland's third from four matches in the Nations Championship, after summer victories over Australia and Japan and a defeat against New Zealand. Head coach Andy Farrell said he was delighted with the win but wanted his side to be more ruthless, and Ireland moved to the top of the northern hemisphere standings.
