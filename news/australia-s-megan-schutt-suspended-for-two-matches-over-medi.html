Australia's Megan Schutt Suspended for Two Matches Over Medication Rules Breach

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cricket Australia has suspended women's fast bowler Megan Schutt for two matches over violations of regulations covering prescribed medications and sports science and medicine guidelines. The board described it as the first out-of-competition incident of its kind, and clarified that Schutt had not used any medication for performance enhancement. The suspension applies to both international and domestic cricket.

Schutt accepted the sanction and issued an apology. As a result, she will miss the third T20 international against New Zealand and the Adelaide Strikers' Women's Big Bash League opener against the Melbourne Stars on October 31. The case has drawn attention because of its unusual nature, but officials stressed there was no doping element involved.
