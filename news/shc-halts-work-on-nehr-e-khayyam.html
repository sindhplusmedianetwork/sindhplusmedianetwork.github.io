SHC halts work on Nehr-e-Khayyam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court's constitutional bench on Thursday ordered a halt to construction work on Nehr-e-Khayyam until the next hearing while hearing petitions filed by residents against the ongoing development work. The petitions were taken up after counsel for the petitioners argued that the Supreme Court had previously restrained construction on the canal. During the hearing, Justice Hassan questioned who had authorised the construction work on Nehr-e-Khayyam.

Counsel argued that the canal had originally been developed as a stormwater drain but construction was now being carried out there using public funds, and that the ongoing work was reducing the width of the drainage channel, potentially affecting its capacity to carry rainwater. Referring to heavy rainfall a few years ago, counsel said an underpass had been inundated resulting in the deaths of eight people, and contended that no approval had been obtained from SEPA for the construction. The bench directed the KDA to suspend all construction work on Nehr-e-Khayyam until the next hearing.
