PM Seeks Roadmap for '100pc' Digital Payments System, Stresses Countrywide Internet Access

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Friday directed officials to prepare a clear roadmap with time-bound targets for establishing a 100 per cent digital payments system in Pakistan. Chairing a high-level meeting on the cashless economy in Islamabad, he stressed that internet access across the country and easy access to technology must be ensured. He also ordered a media campaign to encourage electricity and gas consumers to pay their bills through QR codes.

The meeting was told that payments in most government institutions would be fully digitised by December 2026. Officials said all Benazir Income Support Programme payments were already being made through digital wallets. They added that cheque-based payments through Asaan Assignment Accounts will be discontinued from November 15 in favour of the Raast system.
