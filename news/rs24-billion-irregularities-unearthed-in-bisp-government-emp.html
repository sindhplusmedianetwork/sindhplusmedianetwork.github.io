Rs24 Billion Irregularities Unearthed in BISP; Government Employees Drew Funds Meant for the Poor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Financial irregularities worth Rs24 billion have been exposed in the Benazir Income Support Programme, where about 140,488 government employees allegedly received funds meant for deserving families. According to documents presented by the Ministry of Poverty Alleviation in the National Assembly, only around Rs71 crore has been recovered against the massive irregularities. In the fiscal year 2019-20 alone, government employees and pensioners drew Rs23.13 billion from BISP.

Despite the irregularities being flagged in 2019, payments reportedly continued for six years, and even after employees were identified, Rs90 crore more was paid out. In 2020-21, over 12,000 employees received Rs14.67 crore; the figures were 2,447 employees in 2021-22, 2,235 in 2022-23, 227 in 2023-24, and 12,078 in 2024-25. The documents reveal that employees' spouses were also involved, and that 236 of BISP's own staff were among the recipients.

The FIA recovered Rs54 crore from implicated employees, other departments recovered Rs14.5 crore, and BISP recovered Rs2.15 crore from its own staff — roughly Rs70.65 crore in total. BISP has forwarded the records of the implicated employees to the relevant institutions and decided to scrutinize the records of more than 600,000 government employees and pensioners for further investigation.
