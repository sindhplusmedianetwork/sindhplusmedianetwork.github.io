Sindh decides to replace petrol vehicles with electric vehicles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has decided to gradually replace conventional vehicles used in government departments with electric vehicles (EVs), in a move aimed at cutting expenditure, saving fuel and reducing pollution. The decision was taken at a meeting of the Cabinet Committee on Austerity, chaired by Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon and attended by Local Government Minister Syed Nasir Hussain Shah. The meeting reviewed measures to reduce government expenditure, save fuel and assess the vehicle requirements of various departments.

According to a press statement, the meeting decided to purchase vehicles for the home department, the Anti-Corruption Establishment and the universities and boards department, and agreed that electric vehicles would be given preference in future purchases for government departments. Mr Memon, who also holds the portfolios of information, transport and mass transit, said saving official resources was among the provincial government's priorities. He said the provincial government wanted EVs to be given preference over conventional vehicles in future purchases and to gradually shift the entire government vehicle system to environment-friendly technology.

The senior minister said the gradual transition to EVs would also help reduce the day-to-day expenditure of government departments. The local government minister said smoke emitted by conventional vehicles affected the urban environment and air quality, adding that the government also wanted to promote the use of EVs instead of conventional vehicles in local government institutions.
