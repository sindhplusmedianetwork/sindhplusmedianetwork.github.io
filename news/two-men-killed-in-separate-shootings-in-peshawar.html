Two men killed in separate shootings in Peshawar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two young men lost their lives in separate incidents of violence across Peshawar on Saturday, prompting public protests. In the first incident, a mobile-snatching attempt turned fatal in the OPF Colony area within the jurisdiction of Shahpur Police Station. According to police officials, a young man identified as Muawia was shot and killed during a robbery.

News of his death sparked outrage among his family and relatives, who took to the streets in protest and blocked the motorway, demanding swift justice and immediate action against the perpetrators. In a separate incident within the limits of Rehman Baba Police Station, a man identified as Farhanullah was gunned down in a targeted shooting stemming from personal enmity. Upon receiving reports of firing, a police contingent led by DSP Rehman Baba Murtaza Khan arrived at the scene and shifted the body to a local hospital for a postmortem.

Police have registered cases for both killings and launched widespread raids across various localities of the city to track down and arrest the suspects involved.
