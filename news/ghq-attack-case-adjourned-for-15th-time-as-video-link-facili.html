GHQ attack case adjourned for 15th time as video-link facility stays out of order

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The hearing of the GHQ attack case was adjourned for the 15th time without any proceedings on Thursday, the Anti-Terrorism Court in Rawalpindi fixing November 19 as the next date. ATC Special Judge Amjad Ali Shah adjourned the hearing because the video-link facility required for the trial remains non-functional. Although the Punjab government had ordered the case to be conducted through WhatsApp video link, the system at both the courtroom and the jail court has remained out of order for nearly a year.

The failure has prevented the production of PTI founder Imran Khan before the court, even through video link. Imran Khan, a former prime minister, was marked present on the basis of the jail record, while former foreign minister Shah Mahmood Qureshi's attendance was also recorded through the jail authorities. Former interior minister Sheikh Rashid Ahmed appeared in court, accompanied by his lawyers.

The trial has remained stalled for 14 months.
