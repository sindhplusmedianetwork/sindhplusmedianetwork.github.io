Pakistan's Maliha Ali beats Russia's Drozdova at MMA world championships

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Maliha Ali pulled off a stunning victory at the IMMAF World Championships in Tbilisi, Georgia, defeating Russia's Veronika Drozdova by split decision in their featherweight (65.8kg) bout on Tuesday. The 24-year-old from Hunza, Gilgit-Baltistan, beat a two-time European champion from a country that arrived with more than 60 athletes, government backing and one of the best-funded programmes in the sport. Maliha, also a wushu champion and national kickboxing gold medallist, started cautiously from a southpaw stance before pinning the Russian against the cage and finishing the opening round strongly.

She dominated the second round with a takedown and full mount, landing heavy blows from the top, but tired in the third as Drozdova took her down and controlled the ground action. The judges awarded the first two rounds to Maliha. After her name was announced, she roared, hugged her opponent, then dropped to the mat in prostration with the Pakistan flag draped over her shoulders.

Pakistan MMA Federation president Omar Ahmed said a six-week camp with an international jiu-jitsu and grappling coach had fixed weaknesses spotted during her silver-medal run at the IMMAF World Cup in Angola earlier this year. Maliha now faces Mexico's Andrea Gabriela Guzman Salazar in Wednesday's quarter-finals, while her twin sister Manesha, a gold medallist in Luanda, fights Poland's Martyna Caruk the same day.
