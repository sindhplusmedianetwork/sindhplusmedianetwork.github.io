Smog crisis spirals in Lahore; masks made mandatory for students

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore's District Education Authority has made masks mandatory for students in all public and private schools amid worsening smog. Schools have been directed to strictly enforce smog-protection SOPs. They have also been told to encourage students to drink water and to hold awareness seminars.

The order covers every public and private school in the city. Citizens and environmental experts have criticised the government over the crisis. They said a month of statements has produced no practical measures.
