Why did Karachi's Jewish families settle in Israel, and how did Hindu names vanish from the city's streets?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Before the creation of Pakistan, many places and streets in Karachi were named after Hindu figures. After independence, numerous Hindu names were replaced with those of Muslim personalities and national leaders. Karachi once had a small Jewish community settled in the old city.

That community gradually left for Israel. In the process, the city's streets and neighbourhoods lost their Hindu names.
