Murad Orders Major Drinking Water Project for Sukkur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has reaffirmed his government's commitment to ensuring clean, safe and uninterrupted drinking water supply to the people of Sukkur. He said a city situated along the mighty River Indus cannot be left thirsty. Presiding over a high-level meeting at CM House on Sunday, he reviewed the Sukkur Water Supply Project and its proposed public-private partnership structure.

He directed departments to plan the project in line with population growth, noting that Sukkur's population had grown by about 68 per cent between 1998 and 2023. The project envisages a water treatment capacity of 40 to 50 million gallons per day, with improvements to facilities at Bukkur Island, Bunder Road, Numaish Gah and Airport Road, alongside pumping and distribution upgrades. The existing distribution network serves an estimated 60 to 70 per cent of demand, while an ongoing Rs2.14 billion ADP scheme is expected to raise coverage to 80 to 90 per cent.

The chief minister also said a proposal to declare Sukkur a sister-city of China's Yichang, on the Yangtze River, was under consideration.
