UN urges faster winter aid into Gaza as 150,000 tents wait outside

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Nations humanitarian team has urgently appealed for winter supplies to be allowed into Gaza, saying lives are at stake after deadly rainfall flooded or damaged thousands of family shelters. The appeal came on Friday in the wake of a rainstorm over the past weekend that killed children and adults, suspended critical services and left thousands of family shelters flooded, swept away or torn apart. The humanitarian team, which brings together heads of UN agencies and international and local aid groups, said the devastating impact of the storm was preventable and was caused not by the storm alone but by policy decisions.

It recalled that several months ago it had presented a plan to help residents prepare for winter, covering shelter, health and protection services, drainage and sandbag reinforcement, along with the supplies that need to enter. These include tents, warm clothes and blankets, as well as equipment for pumping water, removing debris and clearing explosive ordnance. The team noted that although funding has fallen far short of what is needed, more than 150,000 tents are currently outside Gaza waiting to be dispatched, while needs inside keep growing as every rainstorm destroys more tents.

It called on the authorities to expedite the entry of life-saving supplies, lift restrictions, enable the repair of water and wastewater networks, let all humanitarian partners operate and allow meaningful commercial activity. The partners also urged member states to use their influence to ensure these steps are taken swiftly and to provide the necessary humanitarian funding without delay, stressing that lives are at stake.
