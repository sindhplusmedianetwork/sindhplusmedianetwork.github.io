Pakistan Suspends Trade With Iran Via Gabd-Rimdan and Taftan Crossings

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has suspended trade with Iran through the official Gabd-Rimdan (250) and Taftan border crossings, it emerged on Saturday. The Ministry of Commerce issued a memorandum on Friday, seen by Dawn, withdrawing its earlier order dated October 3 with immediate effect, without stating a reason. Separately, the Quetta Chamber of Commerce and Industry advised its members and businesses trading with Iran to stop placing new import and export orders until further notice.

Hundreds of freight vehicles carrying liquefied petroleum gas, rice, fresh fruit and other essential commodities are reportedly stranded on both sides of the border. The Gabd-Rimdan crossing had become a major route for meeting Pakistan's energy needs, with over 4,000 tonnes of LPG imported daily, while rice exports through it had surged in recent months. Trade and energy sector sources warned that the closure could aggravate LPG shortages in Karachi and push up prices in the open market.

Iranian officials confirmed that commercial operations at the crossings were halted, while passenger traffic remains operational.
