Punjab MPA Hina Parvez Butt Defends Deleted Post on Alleged CCD Encounter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Assembly member Hina Parvez Butt has defended herself over a social media post praising an alleged encounter by the Punjab Crime Control Department, after the post drew sharp criticism online. The now-deleted post concerned the killing in an alleged encounter in Bahawalpur of a suspect accused of running over a teenage boy, Waseem, in Multan. Critics accused the lawmaker of celebrating extrajudicial killings and questioned a legislator endorsing such action.

Butt responded that her tweet reflected the 'feelings of an ordinary citizen' and said she respectfully disagreed with Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari, who had criticised the post. The controversy has reignited debate in Pakistan over the legality and ethics of alleged police encounters. Butt's post has since been removed from her social media account.
