Diwali smog in Indian cities raises fears for Lahore's air

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pollution in New Delhi and cities across Indian Punjab has climbed to very high levels in the days after Diwali, raising concern that the smog could reach Lahore. Of the 34 air monitoring stations in Indian Punjab, 24 reported pollution at hazardous levels. The highest reading was recorded in Faridkot, where the air quality index reached 1,588.

Low wind speed and easterly winds are behind the fear that the polluted air could move towards Lahore. Teams of the Punjab government are on alert over the situation, and the Environmental Protection Agency is advising citizens about precautions..
