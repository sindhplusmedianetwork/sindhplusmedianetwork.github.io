LPG price rises again in Karachi, up Rs 50 per kg

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The price of LPG in Karachi has risen again, this time by Rs 50 per kilogram. According to Express News, after the latest increase, LPG is being sold at Rs 550 per kilogram in the city. Sources say Iranian gas supply is not reaching Karachi.

Marketing companies are taking advantage of the shortage to sell LPG at arbitrary prices. The repeated hikes continue to add to the burden on households that rely on LPG for cooking, especially as prices keep climbing with no relief in sight.
