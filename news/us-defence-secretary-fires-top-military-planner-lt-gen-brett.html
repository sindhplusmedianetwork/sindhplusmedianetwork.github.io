US Defence Secretary Fires Top Military Planner Lt Gen Brett Sylvia Amid Iran War Planning

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth has removed Lt Gen Brett Sylvia, the Joint Staff's head of strategy, plans and policy (J-5), from his post, officials said on Wednesday. According to CBS, Sylvia is the second senior officer to be removed from the position. A Pentagon official claimed Sylvia was accused of leaking classified information on Iran and the Russia-Ukraine war to journalists, and said the matter had been referred to the Justice Department.

However, two other US officials disputed the charges, and the Pentagon has not formally released full details of the reasons for the removal. The move comes as President Donald Trump weighs fresh strikes on Iran, having said a day earlier that the US military was planning possible new operations against Tehran. Sylvia served 32 years in the US Army and had previously commanded the 101st Airborne Division.

Multiple senior military officers have been removed from their posts during Hegseth's tenure as defence secretary.
