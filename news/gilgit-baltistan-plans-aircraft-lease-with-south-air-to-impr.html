Gilgit-Baltistan plans aircraft lease with South Air to improve air links

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Gilgit-Baltistan government has announced plans to lease aircraft under a joint venture with South Air to improve air connectivity in the region. Chief Minister Amjad Hussain discussed the proposal with South Air management, which agreed to operate the flights under the arrangement. The chief minister said the aircraft would be leased against government guarantees, while South Air would run the services under the joint venture.

The announcement came on Monday, and the plan is aimed at easing the region's long-running air travel problems. Flights between Islamabad and Gilgit are particularly vulnerable to weather disruption, while the short runway at Gilgit Airport limits aircraft operations, leaving passengers facing frequent delays and cancellations. Gilgit Airport handled around 4,000 passengers between March and June 2026, compared with more than 50,000 at Skardu Airport during the same period.

The chief minister has previously urged the federal government to build a modern airport in Gilgit that can handle larger aircraft.
