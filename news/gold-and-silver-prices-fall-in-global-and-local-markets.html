Gold and silver prices fall in global and local markets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold and silver prices recorded a decline in both international and local markets on Wednesday. In the international bullion market, gold fell by nine dollars an ounce, taking the new global price to 4,120 dollars per ounce. Reflecting the global trend, the local market saw the price of gold fall by 900 rupees per tola to 434,536 rupees on the third business day of the week.

The price of ten grams of gold dropped by 772 rupees to 372,544 rupees. Silver also weakened, with the per-tola price down 27 rupees to 6,207 rupees and the ten-gram price down 23 rupees to 5,321 rupees.
