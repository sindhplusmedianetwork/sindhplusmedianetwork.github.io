Zia Lanjar Orders Police to Step Up Action Against Street Crime

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Home Minister Zia ul Hassan Lanjar has ordered police to intensify their crackdown on street crimes, robberies and dacoities. The directive came during a law-and-order meeting chaired by the minister. Senior officers attending the meeting included the Additional IG Karachi, the DIGs of East, West and South zones, and the SSPs of Keamari, West and Malir districts.

The minister directed officers to increase patrolling and surveillance in sensitive areas. He also instructed police to coordinate operations based on secret information. Officers showing negligence in their duties would face accountability, he warned.

The meeting reviewed the overall law-and-order situation in Karachi.
