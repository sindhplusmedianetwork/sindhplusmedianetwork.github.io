Wawrinka ends 24-year tennis career with farewell defeat in Basel

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Stan Wawrinka brought the curtain down on a decorated 24-year tennis career after a first-round exit at his home tournament, the Swiss Indoors in Basel, on Tuesday. The 41-year-old Swiss, who had announced last December that 2026 would be his last year on tour, was beaten 7-5, 7-6 (7/2) by Czech eighth seed Jiri Lehecka, with his final shot as a professional, a backhand, landing wide to the disappointment of the home crowd. Wawrinka, a former world number three, won three Grand Slam titles, beating the then world number one in each final: Rafael Nadal at the Australian Open in 2014, and Novak Djokovic in the French Open final in 2015 and again in the US Open final in 2016.

Alongside Roger Federer, the Lausanne native won the Davis Cup for Switzerland and claimed gold in the doubles at the 2008 Beijing Olympics, and he won 16 tour-level titles in all. On court in Basel, every point he won, including several trademark booming one-handed backhands, was greeted by wild cheers from the partisan home crowd, and after the match he did a lap of the court to thank the crowd before disappearing down the tunnel for the last time. Wawrinka had told the ATP Tour website ahead of his final match that all he had ever wanted was to give everything, to have no regrets, to always fight and to always give 100 per cent in every match, out of respect for himself, his opponent, the tournament and the fans.

Federer sent a video message for his farewell ceremony, Djokovic, Andy Murray and Martina Navratilova also sent messages, and Lehecka said it was a pleasure to be part of Wawrinka's moment and that he would never forget the match. Fans will have one more chance to see the Swiss in action this year, at a farewell party on December 19 in Geneva with Federer and Murray also on the card of the 'One last backhand' event.
