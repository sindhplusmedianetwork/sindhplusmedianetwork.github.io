Rangers and SIU Police Kill One Bandit, Arrest Two in Karachi Operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi's Rangers and the police's Special Investigation Unit (SIU) killed one suspected bandit and arrested two others in an injured condition during an overnight operation near Jinnah Ground by Mazar-e-Quaid. The operation took place during the night between Wednesday and Thursday, police said. The deceased was identified as 35-year-old Zuhaan, son of Salmanuddin, while the two injured suspects were named as 24-year-old Hassan, son of Shahid, and 24-year-old Syed Hussain, son of Irshad.

Police said the suspects were involved in extortion and other serious crimes. Two pistols with ammunition, mobile phones, cash and a motorcycle were recovered from them. The killed and injured suspects were shifted to Jinnah Hospital, and police said further investigation is under way.
