Gill Faces Good Headache as India Line Up Four Pacers for Wellington Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India will field four frontline pacers in the opening test against New Zealand this week, but with a surfeit of fast bowling options at his disposal captain Shubman Gill is having a hard time deciding who to leave out. Jasprit Bumrah returns to lead the pace attack, and Mohammed Siraj is likely to keep his place for the tourists, who won the preceding Twenty20 and one-day international series. Prasidh Krishna and Gurnoor Brar are also in contention, but Akash Deep and Anshul Kamboj could have the edge as they can contribute with the bat lower down the order.

Gill told reporters on Tuesday that it was a bit of a headache but a good kind of headache to have, and he said India definitely wanted four fast-bowling options while still deciding which bowlers those would be. He said the team wanted to cover all aspects, noting that on day three, four and five the wicket, especially with the old ball, could go a little bit flat. Gill said the fast-bowling unit needed someone with pace, someone who could hit the areas consistently, and one spinner to control from one end, because it would not be easy to bowl against the wind if the wind was strong.

Rishabh Pant will return from a side strain to take his place behind the stumps for the opener in the two-test series beginning in Wellington on Thursday, and Gill said he was not worried about the wicketkeeper's lack of recent game time because Pant had scored hundreds all over the world.
