President Zardari Accepts Resignation of Lahore High Court Justice Mohsin Akhtar Kayani

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari has accepted the resignation of Lahore High Court Justice Mohsin Akhtar Kayani. After the acceptance of the resignation by the president, Justice Kayani's name was removed from the Lahore High Court website. Justice Kayani had resigned from the judgeship on October 28, 2026.

He stood 10th on the high court's judges seniority list. He was appointed a judge of the Islamabad High Court on December 23, 2015. He was the Islamabad High Court's first local judge.
