Gujranwala Bride-Murder Prime Suspect Killed in Alleged Police Encounter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The prime suspect in the murder of a bride on her valima day in Gujranwala has died in an alleged police encounter, the Crime Control Department said. The suspect, identified as Sarfraz, was accused of shooting dead his bride Saba as she sat in a car while returning home from the reception. According to the CCD, Sarfraz was killed by the firing of his own accomplices during a shootout while the arrested suspects were being brought back.

Co-accused Arooba and other suspects remain under arrest. Sarfraz had gone into hiding after the murder and was tracked down after reportedly changing locations repeatedly. Police say the motive was a rejected marriage proposal.

The case was registered on the complaint of the victim's father at the CCD police station in Gujranwala.
