Kane misses penalty as Dortmund snatch 2-2 draw at Bayern

MUNICH: Fabio Silva's 89th-minute goal earned Borussia Dortmund a dramatic 2-2 draw at Bayern Munich after Harry Kane missed a penalty in a see-saw Bundesliga clash on Saturday. Bayern were 1-0 up when Kane failed to convert from the spot. Dortmund capitalised on the miss to fight their way back into the contest.

The late equaliser silenced the Allianz Arena crowd. The result keeps the Bundesliga title race finely poised. Kane's penalty miss will dominate the post-match discussion after a night of high drama in Munich.
