Bavuma set to extend Test captaincy record against Bangladesh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Temba Bavuma is set to extend one of Test cricket's most remarkable records when he captains South Africa in the first of two Tests against Bangladesh at the Wanderers Stadium on Sunday. With 13 wins in his first 15 Tests as captain, Bavuma has had more success than anyone in the history of the game at an equivalent stage of their captaincy. Only Lindsay Hassett of Australia can match Bavuma's record, as they both had 13 wins, one draw and one loss in their first 15 matches in charge.

Hassett's fortunes took a downturn in his 16th match at the helm, when Australia lost to South Africa in Melbourne in 1952/53, and he had only one more win in his last nine matches as captain. South Africa are the reigning World Test champions, and their wins under Bavuma include three against Australia and three against India, of which two were in India last year. South Africa have won 14 of their 16 previous Tests against Bangladesh, with the other two matches having been rain-affected draws in Bangladesh in 2015.

Bangladesh pulled off a surprise win against Australia in Darwin in August, and as Bavuma noted in a recent interview, "the target is on our back and everyone wants to have a go at us." Bangladesh lost all eight of their previous Tests in South Africa, five of them by an innings, and the closest of the other three defeats was by 220 runs in Durban in 2021/22.
