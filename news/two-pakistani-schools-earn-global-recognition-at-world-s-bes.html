Two Pakistani Schools Earn Global Recognition at World's Best School Prizes 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two Pakistani schools, a private institution in Lahore and a government-run high school in Hyderabad, have earned international recognition at the World's Best School Prizes 2026, organised by T4 Education. TNS Beaconhouse in Lahore was announced as the winner of the Supporting Healthy Lives category; the school has more than 1,100 students and was recognised for reimagining how mainstream schools include neurodivergent learners. Govt Girls High School Tando Mir Ghulam Hussain in Hyderabad secured a place among the world's top three schools in the Community Collaboration category, won by the United Kingdom's Swiss Cottage School.

The Hyderabad school has employed a dual-shift system, allowing girls who work or support their families during the day to attend in the afternoon. Within one year, school enrolment increased by 90 percent and attendance improved by 80 percent. Parents are now increasingly supportive of girls' education, with 80 percent prioritising education over child labour, and teachers conduct home visits for counselling sessions.
