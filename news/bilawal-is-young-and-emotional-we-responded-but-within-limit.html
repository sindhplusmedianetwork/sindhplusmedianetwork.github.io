Bilawal is young and emotional, we responded but within limits: Talal Chaudhry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhry said Bilawal Bhutto Zardari is young and emotional. He said the government did respond to him but within appropriate limits, as it also has to answer to its own people. Speaking on a TV programme, he said the President and Prime Minister always want to move things forward through talks.

He said no decision is taken without consensus. He added that the discussion on administrative units is about improving governance. The discussion concerns the whole of Pakistan, not any single province, he said.
