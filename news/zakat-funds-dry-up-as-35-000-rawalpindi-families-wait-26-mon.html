Zakat funds dry up as 35,000 Rawalpindi families wait 26 months for aid

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Rawalpindi Zakat Department has been crippled by an unprecedented financial crisis and a prolonged suspension of funds, bringing its operations to the brink of collapse. Around 10,000 Zakat beneficiaries in Rawalpindi district and 25,000 families, including widows, across the six divisional districts of Murree, Attock, Chakwal, Jhelum and Talagang have not received a single penny in financial assistance or subsistence allowance for the past 26 months. The beneficiaries were also denied Eid assistance of Rs3,000 each on all four Eid occasions in 2025 and 2026 because of the severe financial crisis.

Financial assistance to the families of leprosy patients has also remained suspended for two years, leaving patients suffering from the disease in severe distress. Rising unemployment has further aggravated the plight of the affected families, who have no one else to turn to for support.
