Pakistan Accepts Invitation for November's Sultan Azlan Shah Hockey Cup

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's hockey team will take part in the Sultan Azlan Shah Cup hockey tournament in November after the country accepted the invitation to compete. According to Geo News, Pakistan Hockey Federation president Mohiuddin Wani confirmed the development on Saturday, saying Pakistan had accepted the invitation for the November event. Wani said that competing in the Sultan Azlan Shah Cup would give the Pakistan team more opportunities to play against international sides.

He added that featuring in international competition would give Pakistani players extraordinary experience at the top level. The PHF chief also noted that Pakistan's under-21 team was already in Malaysia to participate in the Sultan of Johor Cup. He said the tournament would provide the young players with valuable international exposure and allow their abilities to be properly assessed.
