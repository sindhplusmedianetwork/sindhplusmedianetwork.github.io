Imran Khan's Sister Noreen Niazi Moved to Cardiology Hospital From Kot Lakhpat Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Noreen Niazi, the sister of PTI founder Imran Khan, has been shifted from Lahore's Kot Lakhpat jail to the Punjab Institute of Cardiology after experiencing heart-related discomfort, her lawyer Rana Mudassar Umar said on Wednesday. Doctors were conducting her initial medical tests, he added. Noreen and her sister Uzma Khan have been detained under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance, 1960, since September 21, after being taken into custody in Lahore and shifted to Kot Lakhpat jail.

A third sister, Aleema Khan, was arrested under the same law a day earlier. The detentions came ahead of PTI's planned nationwide protest, including a long march towards Islamabad on September 27, demanding Imran Khan's release and the supremacy of the Constitution. The Punjab government recently extended the detention of all three sisters by another 30 days, according to a report submitted to the Lahore High Court.
