India-China Border Dispute: Why the 3,488-Kilometre Line of Actual Control Remains Unresolved

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

India and China share a 3,488-kilometre border running from Arunachal Pradesh through Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh to Ladakh. The demarcation of this border is unclear and disputed, and the line is known as the Line of Actual Control. Talks between the two sides since 1993 have failed to resolve the dispute.

China is pressing its claims along the border, and military activity in the area has increased. Experts cited in the report say neither side is ready to make territorial concessions. The unresolved demarcation remains at the centre of the decades-old dispute.
