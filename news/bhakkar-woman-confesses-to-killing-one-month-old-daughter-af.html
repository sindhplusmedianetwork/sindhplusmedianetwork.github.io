Bhakkar Woman Confesses to Killing One-Month-Old Daughter After Staging Fake Robbery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman in Bhakkar's Darya Khan area has confessed to killing her one-month-old daughter after staging the infant's death as a robbery. District Police Officer Shahzad Rafiq said the family had reported a week earlier that unknown intruders broke into their home near Sunehri Masjid, threw red chilli powder into the woman's eyes, stole cash, tore up the couple's nikah nama, and threw the infant into a water drum. Police found the circumstances suspicious and detained the husband and wife for questioning.

During interrogation, the mother allegedly confessed that she wanted to separate from her husband, had torn the nikah nama herself, staged the fake robbery, and killed the baby because she saw the child as an obstacle. The DPO said the investigation had exposed the staged crime scene and that legal proceedings were underway. The case has sent shockwaves through the district, with residents demanding justice for the infant.
