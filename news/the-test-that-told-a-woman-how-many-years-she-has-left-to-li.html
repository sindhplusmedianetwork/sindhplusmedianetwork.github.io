The test that told a woman how many years she has left to live

If you could find out whether you carry a gene that could shorten your life, would you want to know? Laura Sides faced just such a difficult decision when she was offered a test to find out whether she had inherited the rare form of early-onset dementia that had been diagnosed in her father when he was 40. She spoke about why she decided to take the test and how that decision has shaped the rest of her life.

The test gave her an indication of how much time she may have left, and she says she hopes to spend a few more beautiful years in this world. Among the things she looks forward to are drinking champagne and eating oysters, her two favourite things.
