Taylor Red Singer Nicole Taylor Dies at 35 After Battle With Breast Cancer

Country singer Nicole Taylor, one-third of the musical trio Taylor Red, has died at the age of 35 after a battle with breast cancer, her family has confirmed. Her triplet sisters, Nika and Natalie Taylor, announced the news in a YouTube video, saying Nicole — who died on September 28 — was "at peace and no longer suffering". Natalie revealed that Nicole was diagnosed with breast cancer in 2022, while Nika said the cancer was Stage 3 and "very aggressive".

Despite undergoing chemotherapy, Nicole encouraged those around her to enjoy life, even doing CrossFit during her treatment. In a memorial statement, the sisters wrote that Nicole, the eldest of the three, "will be greatly missed" and was "our leader and our best friend", adding that a private funeral had already taken place. The identical triplets began as the musical act Red Roots in 2011, later building a large online following with comedy and musical videos.

Taylor Red released the track "Country Money" in March 2021, which went viral on TikTok, and the trio amassed more than 13 million TikTok followers and 16 million YouTube subscribers.
