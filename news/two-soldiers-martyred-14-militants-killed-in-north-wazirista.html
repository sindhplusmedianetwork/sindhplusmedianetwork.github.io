Two Soldiers Martyred, 14 Militants Killed in North Waziristan Operations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two soldiers were martyred and 14 terrorists killed in operations in North Waziristan district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the military's media wing said on Saturday. Twelve terrorists, including two Afghan nationals, were killed in Ali Khel in the last twelve hours, while two more each were neutralised in Mir Ali and Datta Khel. Havildar Asmat Ali, 43, and Naik Aftab Ali, 32, embraced martyrdom during the operations.

Weapons and ammunition were recovered from the slain terrorists. The operations are part of the ongoing efforts to eliminate terrorism from the region. Security forces remain committed to clearing the area of militant presence.
