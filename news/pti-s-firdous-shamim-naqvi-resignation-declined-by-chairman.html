PTI's Firdous Shamim Naqvi Resignation Declined by Chairman Gohar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan has declined the resignation of the party's deputy general secretary, Firdous Shamim Naqvi. Gohar told the media that Naqvi had submitted his resignation five days ago. The chairman said he refused to accept the resignation and returned it to Naqvi.

He added that Naqvi has been associated with the party for 30 years and has rendered great services to it.
