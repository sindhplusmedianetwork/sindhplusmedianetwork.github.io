TTAP Suspends All Talks Until Meeting With Imran Khan, Health Details Provided

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Tehreek-e-Tahafuz-e-Ain-e-Pakistan (TTAP) has decided to suspend all negotiations with the government until it is given access to PTI founder Imran Khan and details about his health are provided. The decision was taken at a leadership meeting chaired by TTAP head Mahmood Khan Achakzai. The alliance also condemned the imposition of an emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, terming the move a 'robbery' on the province's mandate.

It demanded the release of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi, Babar Saleem Swati, Junaid Akbar, arrested lawmakers and leaders, and Imran Khan's sisters. The alliance announced it would challenge the emergency measures in the Peshawar High Court. No further talks with the government will be held until these demands are met, the meeting decided.
