Back-channel contacts under way as talks possible on Bushra Bibi case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Back-channel contacts between PTI and the authorities are continuing, and sources familiar with them say talks on Bushra Bibi's case and possible relief measures are possible, including a possible move to Bani Gala. However, there is no room at this stage for relief for former prime minister Imran Khan. There is also no room for any change in the political setup in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, including the return of Sohail Afridi as chief minister.

Sources say Imran Khan's sisters, whose recent release opened a new channel of contact, have assured they will avoid political activities that fuel confrontation. They have also assured that they will not target the military leadership.
