Muhammad Asif beats Indian rival to reach World Snooker Cup knockout round

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former world champion Muhammad Asif comfortably defeated his Indian opponent to reach the knockout round of the World Snooker Cup in Oman, with the other Pakistani players also advancing to the next stage. On the second day of the event in Oman, Pakistan's Muhammad Asif, Asjad Iqbal and Mubashir Raza won both of their matches. Asif first beat Bangladesh's Nizamuddin Ahmed and then overcame Indian rival Malkeet Singh 3-0 with ease.

Pakistan's Rana Arif lost to India's Dhita Mehra but still managed to reach the knockout stage. The strong showing from the Pakistani cueists has put four players from the country into the knockout round of the tournament.
