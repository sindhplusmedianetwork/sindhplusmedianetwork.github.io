Pakistan, Qatar sign security cooperation MoU and anti-drug action plan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Qatar signed a memorandum of understanding on security cooperation, the Prime Minister's Office said. The MoU is between Qatar's Internal Security Force (Lekhwiya) and Pakistan's Federal Constabulary. An action plan to prevent drug trafficking was also signed.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif witnessed the signing ceremony in Islamabad. Sheikh Khalifa bin Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi signed the agreements on behalf of their countries. Shehbaz appreciated Qatar's continued supply of liquefied natural gas and said around 300,000 Pakistanis were working in Qatar.
