Nahyan bin Mubarak visits World Congress of Military Medicine in Abu Dhabi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sheikh Nahyan bin Mubarak Al Nahyan, the UAE Minister of Tolerance and Coexistence, visited the 46th ICMM World Congress of Military Medicine in Abu Dhabi. The congress, organised by the UAE Ministry of Defence, is being held under the theme 'Ready When Needed. Resilient When Tested'.

During his tour of the exhibition held alongside the event, he was briefed on the latest solutions, technologies and innovations in military medicine and healthcare. Sheikh Nahyan said the UAE's hosting of the congress reflects its commitment to promoting coexistence and dialogue. He added that it also shows the country's position as an international hub for exchanging knowledge.
