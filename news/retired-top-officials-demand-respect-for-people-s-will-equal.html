Retired top officials demand respect for people's will, equal provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A group of retired civil servants, diplomats and military generals has called for equal treatment of all provinces and warned against any constitutional amendment imposed against the wishes of the people, saying the country's unity and future are at risk amid a deepening political and economic crisis. In a statement shared on Saturday, the Roedad Khan Club — often described as Islamabad's 'retired establishment' — said constitutional rule must be restored as a reality rather than maintained as a facade. The statement was signed by former air chief Abbas Khattak, former bureaucrat Shakil Durrani and former diplomats Ashraf Jehangir Qazi, Humayun Qazi and Asif Durrani.

It declared that 'the military needs to return to its sacred mission of protecting the country, its oath of allegiance, and [Quaid-i-Azam Mohammad Ali Jinnah's] clear instructions'. The intervention comes amid renewed debate over restructuring the provincial system after senior government figures proposed creating new provinces — a move critics say could weaken provincial autonomy. The club rejected what it called hybrid governance, arguing that a system in which elected civilian institutions operate alongside an unelected centre of power deepens resentment.

It called for 'a restitution of the people's verdict in the last general elections' — or, alternatively, a fresh election with all parties and their chosen leaders — as the first step towards national reconciliation and stability.
