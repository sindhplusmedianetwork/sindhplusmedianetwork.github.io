Pakistan to Host Four Consecutive International Squash Tournaments

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has secured the hosting rights for four consecutive international squash tournaments following the Asian Team Squash Championship currently under way in Islamabad. The four events, part of the Professional Squash Association's annual calendar, carry a combined prize money of $439,000, with three of them to be staged in Karachi. The Silver-category Chief of Air Staff International Squash Championship 2026 will be played in Islamabad from November 24 to 28, featuring a $40,000 men's event with Pakistan's world U23 champion Noor Zaman, ranked 25th in the world, as top seed and Ashab Irfan as fourth seed.

Muhammad Asim Khan, now ranked 68th, will be seventh seed, while Nasir Iqbal will also be in action. Karachi will host the $18,000 Chief of Naval Staff Championship from December 1 to 5, including a $1,000 women's PSA event, and the $130,000 Sindh Open at the Creek Club from December 6 to 10, with Egypt's Youssef Soliman and Salma Hany headlining. The series concludes with the Gold-category Karachi Open from January 5 to 10, 2027, carrying a $250,000 purse split equally between the men's and women's events.
