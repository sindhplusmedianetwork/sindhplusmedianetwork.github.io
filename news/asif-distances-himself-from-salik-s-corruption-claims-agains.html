Asif Distances Himself From Salik's Corruption Claims Against Punjab Government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has distanced himself from PML-Q minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain's corruption allegations against the Punjab government, saying the remarks were 'beyond my understanding'. In a post on X, Asif pointed out that Salik sits in governments led by the PML-N at both the federal and Punjab levels, making his attack on the Maryam Nawaz-led provincial government difficult to comprehend. The row escalated after Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz served Salik a Rs5 billion defamation notice over the allegations.

Punjab Senior Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb also weighed in, demanding an apology and claiming Salik had admitted he possessed no evidence to back his claims. The episode has exposed rare public friction within the ruling coalition, with a sitting federal minister openly questioning a cabinet colleague's statements. Neither Salik nor the PML-Q leadership had issued a detailed rebuttal by late Tuesday.
