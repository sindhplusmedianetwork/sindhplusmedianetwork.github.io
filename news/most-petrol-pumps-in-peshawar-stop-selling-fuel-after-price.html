Most Petrol Pumps in Peshawar Stop Selling Fuel After Price Cut, Citizens Suffer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

After the reduction in petroleum prices, most petrol pumps in Peshawar have stopped supplying petrol. Citizens are facing severe difficulties as a result. Supply was halted at pumps in Haji Camp, Sardar Garhi, GT Road, Gulbahar and Warsak Road.

The pumps displayed repair-work boards to avoid serving customers. Citizens allege that pump owners colluded to stop supply despite having thousands of litres of petrol in stock. They say the owners are waiting for a possible further change in prices.

Citizens have demanded that the Peshawar commissioner and deputy commissioner visit the pumps and take action against those halting supply.
