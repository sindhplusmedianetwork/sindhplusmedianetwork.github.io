Terror attacks down 66 percent over five weeks amid security operations, report says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Successful operations by security forces in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan have led to a clear decline in terrorist attacks, according to a new report. The report by the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies says terrorist attacks fell by about 66 percent over five weeks. Attacks dropped from 61 during 18–24 September to 21 during 16–22 October.

Compared with the previous week, militant attacks fell by 38 percent in the latest week covered. The number of people injured in terrorist attacks also fell, from 20 in the previous week to 11 during 16–22 October. Security forces killed 33 militants during 10 operations in the period covered.

The report also examined the effects of a leadership dispute within Fitna al-Hindustan and the changing propaganda strategy of Fitna al-Khawarij.
