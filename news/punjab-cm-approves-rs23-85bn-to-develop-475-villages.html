Punjab CM approves Rs23.85bn to develop 475 villages

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has approved Rs23.85 billion for the first phase of the Khushhal Dehat Program, which will develop 475 villages across the province. Under the program, each village will receive Rs50 million for development projects, including paved streets and a proper sewerage system to improve rural infrastructure and living conditions. A Khushhal Dehat Committee will oversee the completion of the work in each village, headed by the respective Additional Deputy Commissioner (Social Services) and comprising five government and four private members, along with the Union Council chairman and the relevant sub-engineer.

The chief minister directed the authorities to release the first installment of the grant to the committees by November so that development work can begin on time. She also asked officials to prepare a development plan for another 2,000 villages under the second phase of the program. Speaking on the occasion, she said providing paved streets and an organised sewerage system in every village of Punjab was her commitment.

She added that development would now be ensured across every part of Punjab, whether urban or rural, and that political affiliations would not be allowed to become a hurdle.
