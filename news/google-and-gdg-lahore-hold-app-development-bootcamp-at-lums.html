Google and GDG Lahore Hold App Development Bootcamp at LUMS, Spotlight Pakistani Apps' Global Success

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Google experts joined Pakistan's developer community at the Lahore University of Management Sciences for the "Pehla Qadam: App Edition Bootcamp 2026", an event hosted by Google Developers Group Lahore to train students and young developers in app creation, marketing, revenue models and artificial intelligence. LUMS Provost Dr Tariq Jadoon welcomed the Google team and congratulated it on the opening of Google Pakistan's first office in Karachi. Speakers pointed to Sensor Tower data showing global in-app spending reached $167 billion in 2025, up 10.6 percent, with roughly 149 billion downloads worldwide.

They highlighted Pakistani success stories including Game District, whose game "My Supermarket Simulator" passed 60 million downloads in 2025. The Google delegation is also set to hold the "Think Apps" event and Pakistan's first YouTube Festival.
