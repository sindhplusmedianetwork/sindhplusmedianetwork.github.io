A new video of Salman Khan has left fans worried, with marks visible on his right hand resembling drip marks prompting speculation about his health, according to the report. The report notes he has previously faced serious illnesses including trigeminal neuralgia, a brain aneurysm and an arteriovenous malformation. It also notes he underwent gamma knife surgery in the US.

No confirmation about his current health was given in the report.
