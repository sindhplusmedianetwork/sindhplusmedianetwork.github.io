Banks, utilities put on alert against AI-enabled cyber threats

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Emergency Response Team has warned of increased use of artificial intelligence and automated bots to carry out credential-based and coordinated cyberattacks, with critical infrastructure and financial services at particular risk. In a November 5 advisory, the team said banks could face attacks on core banking systems, digital banking platforms and payment systems, while oil and gas facilities, water utilities and power networks could also be exposed. It urged financial institutions to use multi-factor authentication and advised utilities to limit internet exposure and segment their operational and information technology networks.

The warning comes as attackers increasingly use AI tools to automate large-scale attempts to steal login credentials and breach protected systems.
