JI Launches Third Phase of Protests Against Petroleum Levy in KP

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami on Friday launched the third phase of its nationwide protest movement against petroleum price increases and the petroleum levy. The party held sit-ins, rallies and public gatherings across Peshawar, Mardan, Nowshera, Swabi, Charsadda and Khyber. The demonstrations were organised on the call of JI amir Hafiz Naeemur Rehman.

Party leaders vowed that the movement would continue until the government abolishes the levy. They also demanded meaningful relief for citizens burdened by rising fuel costs. The protest campaign has been running in phases across the country.
