Elderly Man Survives Murree Road Crash, Then Falls into Nullah Lai and Dies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An elderly man in Rawalpindi died after falling into Nullah Lai, shortly after surviving a traffic accident on Murree Road. The accident took place near Mareer Chowk. Bystanders seated him by the roadside after the crash.

He then suddenly fell into the stream from the edge of a bridge. Rescue officials said he died before rescuers arrived.
