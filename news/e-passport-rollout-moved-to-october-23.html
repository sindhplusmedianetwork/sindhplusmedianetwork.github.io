E-Passport Rollout Moved to October 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Directorate General of Immigration and Passports has changed the e-passport rollout date from October 15 to October 23, 2026. From October 23 onwards, all passport offices and Pakistani missions abroad will issue only e-passports to new applicants. The change was confirmed through a notice issued by a Pakistani consulate in New York.

Officials said the new date applies across the country and at every diplomatic mission. Passports already issued in the machine-readable format will remain valid until their expiry. Citizens therefore do not need to replace their current passports immediately.
