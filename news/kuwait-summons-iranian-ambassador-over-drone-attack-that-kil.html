Kuwait Summons Iranian Ambassador Over Drone Attack That Killed Two Italians

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kuwait has summoned Iran's ambassador, Mohammad Totounji, to the foreign ministry to protest against what it described as continued Iranian attacks on the country. According to the AFP news agency, the Kuwaiti foreign ministry said the ambassador was handed a protest note condemning the Iranian attacks on Kuwait. The ministry said that in the latest incident, a drone attack on Thursday targeted an important site in Kuwait, killing two Italian nationals.

In a statement posted on the social media platform X, the Kuwaiti foreign ministry said a formal protest had been recorded with the Iranian ambassador over the continued attacks, particularly the recent drone strike. The report said the attack was the first deadly attack in the Gulf country of Kuwait in several months.
