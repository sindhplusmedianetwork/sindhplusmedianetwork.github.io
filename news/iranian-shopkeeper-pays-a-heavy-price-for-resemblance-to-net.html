Iranian Shopkeeper Pays a Heavy Price for Resemblance to Netanyahu as Customers Boycott His Shop

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A shopkeeper in Tehran who sells dried fruits, nuts and sweets has gone viral on social media because of his striking resemblance to Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu. After the video surfaced, users compared his facial features, hairstyle and expressions to Netanyahu. Some users called for a boycott of his shop.

Reports say the boycott has affected his business and left him distressed. The shopkeeper's identity, the original date of the video and the claimed business losses could not be independently verified.
