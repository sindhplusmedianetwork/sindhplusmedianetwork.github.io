Imaan Mazari and Hadi Ali Sentenced to 10 Years Each in Barrier-Removal Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad's anti-terrorism court on Thursday sentenced human rights activist and lawyer Imaan Mazari and her husband, advocate Hadi Ali Chatha, to ten years in prison each. Judge Tahir Abbas Supra announced the verdict at Adiala Jail in the case concerning the removal of security barriers and interference in state affairs. The court also imposed fines on the couple under three different legal provisions.

The case stemmed from the security arrangements during the England cricket team's October 2024 tour. The verdict is separate from their January 2026 conviction in the controversial-tweets case, which the Supreme Court suspended in August.
