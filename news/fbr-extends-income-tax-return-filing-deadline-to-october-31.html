FBR Extends Income Tax Return Filing Deadline to October 31

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue has further extended the deadline for filing income tax returns for tax year 2026 to October 31, exercising its powers under Section 214A of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001. The notification, posted on the FBR's X account, applies to persons who were required to file by September 30, 2026, and whose deadline had earlier been extended to October 15. The board said the extension came "in view of requests received from various trade bodies and tax bar associations." Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce and Industry President Atif Ikram Sheikh had formally urged the government to extend the deadline to October 31 to facilitate trade, industry and other filers, while the Karachi Tax Bar Association submitted a five-page letter citing delays in draft notifications and persistent technical glitches in the IRIS portal.

Trade bodies and professionals had sought the extension, pointing to delays in the issuance of return forms and heavy online traffic as major hurdles. Official data available with Dawn shows total returns filed until October 14 reached 6.71 million, up 34.38 per cent from 4.99 million in the corresponding period last year, while filings by September 30 stood at 5.76 million against 3.98 million a year earlier — an increase of around 45 per cent. Total tax paid alongside returns until October 14 stood at Rs66.81 billion, about 14.61 per cent more than last year's Rs58.29 billion.

Returns by non-salaried individuals — traders, shopkeepers, professionals and the self-employed — rose 46.35 per cent to 4.45 million from 3.04 million, while salaried individuals' returns rose 16.16 per cent to 2.18 million from 1.88 million.
