Philippines, Australia sign defence pact with eye on China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Philippines and Australia signed a defence cooperation arrangement in Manila on Sunday to streamline joint military activities under their Status of Visiting Forces Agreement. Philippine Defence Secretary Gilberto Teodoro said it would cut red tape and pave the way for closer maritime, air and land cooperation and humanitarian response. He said the arrangement would bolster deterrence, including in the South China Sea.

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, in Manila for the ASEAN summit, also announced new investment in maritime domain awareness. The pact comes as both countries deepen security ties in the region. Officials said the agreement would make joint exercises and operations easier to organise.
