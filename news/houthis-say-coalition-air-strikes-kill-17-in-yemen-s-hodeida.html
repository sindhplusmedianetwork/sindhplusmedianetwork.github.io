Houthis Say Coalition Air Strikes Kill 17 in Yemen's Hodeida

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's Houthis said Saudi-led coalition air strikes on telecommunications facilities in the Red Sea governorate of Hodeida on Monday killed 17 workers and wounded 11 others. The strikes caused the total destruction of administrative buildings, staff housing, a mosque and other sites. Telecom and internet services were disrupted for tens of thousands of people, according to the Houthis.

The report also noted that Brent crude fell to $89.32 amid higher Middle East flows and the restoration of a Libyan pipeline. The strikes targeted facilities in a governorate on Yemen's Red Sea coast. Further details about the damage and the affected services were not given in the report.
