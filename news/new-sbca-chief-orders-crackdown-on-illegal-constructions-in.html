New SBCA Chief Orders Crackdown on Illegal Constructions in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The new Director General of the Sindh Building Control Authority has taken charge of his office and ordered a crackdown on all illegal constructions in Karachi. He was briefed on the building-collapse incident in the Admin Society area soon after assuming office. He directed that rescue and debris-removal work at the site be speeded up without delay.

He also ordered strict enforcement of safety procedures and a full inquiry into the collapse. Lists of all illegal floors and buildings are to be prepared so that immediate demolition can follow under a policy of no more floors and no illegal construction. The appointment follows the Admin Society collapse, after which the Chief Minister removed the former Director General and suspended 16 officers of the authority.
