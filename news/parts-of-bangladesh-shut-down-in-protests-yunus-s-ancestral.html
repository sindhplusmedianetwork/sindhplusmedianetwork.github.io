Parts of Bangladesh shut down in protests, Yunus's ancestral home set ablaze

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Parts of Bangladesh shut down as Sheikh Hasina's Awami League called a nationwide lockdown. Police said two ruling party members were killed during the protests. The ancestral home of former leader Muhammad Yunus in Chattogram was set ablaze.

The protests follow the government's rejection of Hasina's request for a new passport and identity card that would allow her return from exile in India. Ninety-seven platoons of the Bangladesh Border Guard have fanned out across seven districts to deter violence.
