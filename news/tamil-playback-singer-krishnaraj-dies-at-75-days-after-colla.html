Tamil Playback Singer Krishnaraj Dies at 75, Days After Collaborator Deva's Death

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tamil playback singer Krishnaraj Ramasamy passed away in Chennai on Thursday, October 22, at the age of 75, marking the third loss for the Tamil film community in less than a week. The reason for his death is not yet known. Krishnaraj sang more than 300 songs in Tamil cinema, including 'Paruthiveeran', 'Arunachalam' and 'Kadhal Kottai', and was best known for his collaborations with composer Deva in the 1990s and 2000s on hits such as 'Vellarikka', the sad version of 'Meesakkaara Nanbaa', 'Kathadikkuthu Kathadikkuthu' and 'Gopala Gopala'.

He received the Tamil Nadu State Government Award for Best Playback Singer for 'Thanjavooru Mannu Eduthu' from the 1997 film 'Porkkaalam', a song that was played at the 2016 state funeral of former Singapore President S R Nathan. His death comes five days after Deva's passing on October 17 and two days after Deva's nephew, actor Karthik Sabesh, died on October 20. Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C Joseph Vijay, actor Sarath Kumar and singer Chinmayi Sripaada were among those who paid tribute, with Vijay calling his demise a great loss to Tamil cinema.
