Tyra Banks says she has not spoken to Law Roach since TV clash

Tyra Banks has revealed that she has not spoken to Law Roach since their disagreements on Project Runway, while acknowledging that the celebrity stylist continues to discuss her in interviews. Speaking to TMZ on Thursday, November 12, Banks reflected on their relationship following the show's 22nd season, which aired over the summer. She credited Roach's early television success to his ability to capture audiences' attention, saying she saw him as a star and told him so because he speaks in soundbites.

Banks also recalled giving him his first chance on television. During a separate conversation with Entertainment Tonight, she again addressed their lack of communication, joking that Roach seemed to have something to say about her in every interview. Roach had previously discussed their professional history during an appearance on Ts Madison's Outlaws podcast in September, crediting Banks with giving him his first reality television opportunity on America's Next Top Model.

Following Banks' latest comments, Roach responded in the comments section, writing that it was so true and that she told him he was a star.
