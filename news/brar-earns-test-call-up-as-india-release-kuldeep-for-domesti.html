Brar earns Test call-up as India release Kuldeep for domestic cricket

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fast bowler Gurnoor Brar has been added to India's squad for the two-Test series against New Zealand, while spinner Kuldeep Yadav has been released to play domestic cricket, the country's cricket board said on Saturday. Brar, 26, will join the Test squad in New Zealand and will not be available for Sunday's fifth and final one-day international in Tauranga. India will not name a replacement for Brar, with the visitors leading the five-match one-day series 2-1.

Wicketkeeper-batter Rishabh Pant has linked up with the squad in Wellington after receiving fitness clearance. The two-match Test series begins on November 19, with Shubman Gill leading a squad that includes senior names such as Jasprit Bumrah, Ravindra Jadeja and Mohammed Siraj alongside the new inclusion.
