Judicial Commission Recommends JIT for Mir Raza Murder Probe, FIA Inquiry Into Police Conduct

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A single-member judicial commission headed by Sindh High Court Justice Omar Sial has recommended forming a joint investigation team under DIG Irfan Baloch to re-investigate the murder of 25-year-old Mir Raza Ali. The proposed JIT would include representatives of the ISI, the Intelligence Bureau, the Sindh Rangers and the NCCIA. The commission concluded that the earlier police investigation failed in its most basic duty, which was to establish the truth.

Its executive summary, presented to the Sindh cabinet, also recommended an FIA-led inquiry into whether police officials breached Pakistan Penal Code provisions on destroying evidence and framing false records during the first ten days of the probe. The commission confirmed that the final post-mortem found the cause of death to be a perforating firearm injury from the back, ruling out the police's initial theory of suicide. It warned that the trust deficit between the police and the public is now nearly unrectifiable.
