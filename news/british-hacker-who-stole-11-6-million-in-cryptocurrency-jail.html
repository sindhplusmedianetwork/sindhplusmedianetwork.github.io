British hacker who stole $11.6 million in cryptocurrency jailed for six years in US

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A US court has sentenced British hacker Tyler Buchanan to six years in prison for stealing cryptocurrency worth more than $11.6 million. The 24-year-old from Dundee, Scotland, was part of the cybercrime group known as 'Scattered Spider'. Between 2021 and 2023, he sent hundreds of fake messages to employees of major technology and telecom companies.

The messages tricked the employees into entering their credentials on lookalike websites, allowing the group to break into company systems. Buchanan had pleaded guilty to the charges against him. He was extradited from Spain to the United States, where the court handed down the 72-month sentence on 17 November.

One victim alone lost $1.67 million in December 2022.
