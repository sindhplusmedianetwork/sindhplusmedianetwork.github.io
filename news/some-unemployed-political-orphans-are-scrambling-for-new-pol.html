Some unemployed political orphans are scrambling for new political shelters, says Azma Bokhari

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari has said that Punjab is progressing rapidly under Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz. She said some political elements feel threatened by this progress. Bokhari said Maryam Nawaz has made good governance, transparency and merit the basic model of Punjab's rule.

She added that in just two years, Maryam Nawaz has carried out development work unmatched in the country's history. Bokhari said some unemployed and politically orphaned people are now scrambling to find new political shelters. She added that the public stands with progress, performance and good governance.
