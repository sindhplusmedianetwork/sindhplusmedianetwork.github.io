Pakistan and China to Set Up Engineering and Technology Institute in Gwadar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and China will collaborate on establishing a new engineering and technology institute in Gwadar to train young people in skills required by the port, shipping and maritime industries. Federal Minister for Maritime Affairs Muhammad Junaid Anwar Chaudhry announced the plan while speaking at a pre-board meeting of the Gwadar Port Authority, where development projects and technical education initiatives were reviewed. Gwadar Port Authority Chairman Noorul Haq Baloch told the meeting that the port authority, the Pak-China Technical and Vocational Institute, the University of Gwadar and China's Qingdao Harbour Vocational and Technical College had signed a memorandum of understanding to establish the Pakistan Overseas Engineering and Technology Institute in Gwadar.

Under the five-year cooperation framework for 2026-2030, the partner institutions will work together on academic programmes, curriculum development, skills training, faculty development, joint research and practical learning. The proposed curriculum will cover navigation technology, marine engineering, maritime safety, shipping technology and port machinery. The minister said technical education should be aligned with the operational requirements of ports and the maritime industry, adding that the agreement would improve employment prospects for young people in Gwadar and Balochistan.
