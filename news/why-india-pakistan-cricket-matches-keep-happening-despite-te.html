Why India-Pakistan cricket matches keep happening despite tensions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India and Pakistan have played 14 matches across senior and age-group cricket in as many months since September 2025, even though bilateral cricket between the two countries has been suspended since 2012-13. The latest meeting was Pakistan Shaheens' 48-run win over India A in Doha on Sunday. Indian policy rules out bilateral ties while permitting matches in multilateral events, a distinction the ICC and the Asian Cricket Council have built into their calendars.

The clearest explanation for the continued fixtures is money, with industry estimates putting the commercial value of one India-Pakistan ICC match at roughly $200 million to $250 million. Some analysts place the figure at around $500 million when wider spending is included.
