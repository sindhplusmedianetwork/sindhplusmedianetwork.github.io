'Shabnam': Coke Studio Nu.WAV's New Song Explores Love and Suspicion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Coke Studio Nu.WAV has released its newest song 'Shabnam', featuring Maanu and Zoha Waseem. The track, produced by Talal Qureshi, explores how two people experience the same relationship in different ways, as love turns into suspicion. Released on October 18, the song began as an unfinished collaboration between Qureshi and Maanu before Zoha joined the project.

The music video was directed by Fida Moin, with production design and art direction by Tanya Mirza. Executive Producer Kamal Khan said the collaboration evolved into two people telling different versions of the same story. 'Shabnam' is the fourth of six planned Nu.WAV songs, following 'Dil', 'Rangeen' and 'Maheya'.
