No Compromise on Law and Order in KP; No Peace, No Development — Governor Kundi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has said that establishing law and order in the province is his top priority and there will be no compromise on it. He was speaking during a visit to the Jamaat-e-Islami center in Peshawar, where he met Naib Amir Professor Ibrahim and other party leaders. The meeting discussed the overall provincial situation, development and youth employment.

Kundi spoke about promoting agriculture in the southern districts and creating IT sector jobs for young people. He also sought the JI's support on hydel net profit, NFC and other federal-linked issues. He added that indiscriminate action against encroachments continues in Peshawar.
