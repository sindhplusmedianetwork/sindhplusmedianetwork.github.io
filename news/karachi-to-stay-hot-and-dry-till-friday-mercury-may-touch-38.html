Karachi to Stay Hot and Dry Till Friday, Mercury May Touch 38°C

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Meteorological Department has warned that intense heat is likely to persist in Karachi until Friday. According to a private TV channel, the department says the city is in the grip of hot and dry weather. Temperatures in the city may reach 38 degrees Celsius by Friday, and could stay between 34 and 36 degrees after Friday.

The Met Office said the coolness of the night has begun in Karachi, and night-time temperatures may drop to 22 degrees in the coming days. It added that November's normal night temperature is 17.7 degrees and the day temperature 32.6 degrees, but day and night temperatures in November may remain above normal this year.
