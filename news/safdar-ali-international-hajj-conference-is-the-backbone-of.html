Safdar Ali: International Hajj Conference Is the Backbone of Hajj 2027 Preparations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Safdar Ali, director of Saudi company Nazhat Al-Mushtaqin, has welcomed the annual international Hajj conference and exhibition to be held at the Jeddah Superdome from November 8 to 11, describing it as the backbone of Hajj 2027 preparations. He said the event is awaited throughout the year by companies and operators involved in Hajj services. According to Safdar Ali, all Saudi companies and the operators for Hajj 2027 will set up stalls at the conference, where services, facilities and arrangements for pilgrims will be showcased.

He welcomed participants arriving from Pakistan and from countries around the world. The four-day exhibition is expected to bring together service providers, transporters and accommodation companies serving Hajj pilgrims. Organisers say the gathering helps standardise services and improve the pilgrim experience ahead of the 2027 season.

Pakistani operators attending the event hope to finalise service contracts with Saudi companies.
