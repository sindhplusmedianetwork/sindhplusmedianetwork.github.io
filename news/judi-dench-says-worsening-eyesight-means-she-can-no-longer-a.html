Judi Dench Says Worsening Eyesight Means She Can No Longer Act

Dame Judi Dench has said her worsening eyesight means she can no longer act. The 91-year-old British actress told BBC Radio that the macular degeneration she has lived with for more than a decade has deteriorated badly. "I can't be on stage, I can't do a play anymore, I can't do a film anymore, I can't read anymore," she said.

Dench added that she can no longer recognise faces and often comes close to hugging strangers by mistake. One of Britain's most decorated performers, she has won an Oscar, a Tony Award and seven Olivier Awards, and played the spymaster M in eight James Bond films.
