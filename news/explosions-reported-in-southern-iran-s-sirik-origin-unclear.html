Explosions Reported in Southern Iran's Sirik, Origin Unclear

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Explosions have been heard in Sirik in southern Iran, Reuters reported citing Iranian media. The nature and origin of the explosions are unknown, and no further details have been confirmed. The report appears on Dawn's live-updates page tracking the war between Iran and Israel and the US.

The same updates page carries news of US Treasury sanctions against a Greek law firm and shipping agents linked to Iranian networks. Separately, the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted and destroyed a Houthi drone that was headed for the kingdom's southern regions. The region remains tense as developments continue to unfold.
