Krakow Gunman Who Killed Six Dies in Hospital, Polish Police Say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Polish police say a suspected gunman involved in the killing of six people in Krakow, the country's second-largest city, has died in hospital. According to prosecutors, the 24-year-old suspect was influenced by far-right extremist views. He was injured in firing in the central area of Krakow.

Police said armed officers reached the scene immediately and shot him within three minutes of the attack beginning, preventing a larger-scale massacre. Six people, including four women and two men, were killed in the attack, while a search of the attacker's home found the bodies of his father, mother and sister.
