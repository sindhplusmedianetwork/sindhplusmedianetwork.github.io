Pakistan rejects ex-US envoy Khalilzad's claim it is arming Afghans against Taliban

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Ministry of Information has categorically rejected allegations by former US envoy to Afghanistan Zalmay Khalilzad that Pakistan is arming former Afghans to fight the Taliban and terrorist groups. The ministry said in a statement on X that the allegations are baseless and misleading and merely recycle speculative narratives. The statement came in response to a post by Khalilzad in which he claimed that Pakistan was arming Afghans to fight the Taliban and that the Pakistani military was recruiting Afghans to fight anti-Pakistan insurgents.

The ministry said it was misleading to conflate Pakistan's legitimate counterterrorism operations against groups operating from areas under Taliban control with recruiting Afghan nationals or sponsoring armed groups inside Afghanistan. It said Pakistan remains at the forefront of the fight against terrorism and has made enormous sacrifices for regional peace and security. The ministry added that successive UN monitoring reports have documented the continued presence of terrorist organisations, including the TTP and Al-Qaeda, inside Afghanistan.
