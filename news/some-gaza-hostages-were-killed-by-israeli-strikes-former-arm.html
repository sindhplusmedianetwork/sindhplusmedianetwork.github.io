Some Gaza hostages were killed by Israeli strikes, former army chief admits

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Israeli army chief Lieutenant General Herzi Halevi has admitted that some of the Israeli hostages held by Hamas in Gaza were killed by Israeli strikes, in his first public remarks since resigning. Halevi, who commanded the military during the October 7, 2023 Hamas attack and stepped down in March 2025, was speaking at a conference in New York. He said the army did not know the hostages were at the locations that were bombed.

Halevi added that the hostages' return was impossible without heavy military pressure in Gaza. He described October 7 as a preventable failure while denying any internal treason, and said the commission of inquiry into the attack should complete its work. The admission is the first time a former military chief has publicly acknowledged that Israeli fire killed some of the hostages.
