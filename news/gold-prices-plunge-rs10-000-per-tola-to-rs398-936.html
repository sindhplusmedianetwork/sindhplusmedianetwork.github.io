Gold prices plunge Rs10,000 per tola to Rs398,936

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices fell sharply in local and international markets, with gold down 100 dollars per ounce globally and 10,000 rupees per tola in Pakistan. In local markets, gold fell to 398,936 rupees per tola and 342,023 rupees per 10 grams. Silver also became cheaper, dropping by 180 rupees per tola to 5,048 rupees.

The decline came after a one-day pause in the recent downward trend in bullion prices, with the fall resuming in both the global and local markets. Traders and buyers watch gold prices closely, as the metal is widely bought in Pakistan for weddings and as a store of value. The fresh drop brings some relief to buyers, though prices remain far above their levels of a year ago.

Market watchers will now be looking to see whether the downward trend continues in the coming days.
