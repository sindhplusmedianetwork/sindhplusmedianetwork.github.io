ABAD Alleges SBCA Officers Charge Up to Rs5 Million Per Floor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ABAD chairman Hanif Gohar has alleged that Sindh Building Control Authority officers are involved in illegal construction. He claimed the officers charge Rs2.5-5 million per floor to allow illegal construction. According to Gohar, legal builders are targeted instead of those carrying out illegal construction.

He called the Sindh chief minister's order to seal all under-construction buildings unfair. He said the order hurts lawful builders.
