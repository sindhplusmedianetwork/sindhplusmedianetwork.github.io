US Quietly Reducing Troop Presence in Middle East, Reports CBS

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States is quietly scaling back the presence of its personnel at military bases across the Middle East, according to a report by the American broadcaster CBS. The report cites a US official and a senior military officer, who link the move to threats from the Houthis and direct threats from Iran. US Central Command spokesman Captain Tim Hawkins responded that the US military routinely conducts movements of personnel and equipment.

He added that details of such movements are not disclosed for security reasons. The reported drawdown comes amid heightened tensions in the region, where the risk of a wider conflict has raised concerns about the safety of American forces.
