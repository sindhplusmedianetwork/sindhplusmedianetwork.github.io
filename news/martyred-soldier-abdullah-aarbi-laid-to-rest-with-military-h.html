Martyred soldier Abdullah Aarbi laid to rest with military honours in Qutubpur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Army soldier Abdullah Aarbi, who was martyred during an operation against terrorists in Noshki district of Balochistan, was laid to rest with full military honours in his native area Qutubpur. Locals welcomed his body by showering flower petals. His funeral prayers were attended by the Corps Commander Bahawalpur, police officers, political and social figures and a large number of citizens.

After the funeral prayers, he was buried with military honours in Shah Chiragh cemetery, where the Corps Commander Bahawalpur presented his uniform and cap to his father Abid Aarbi. The father expressed pride in his son's sacrifice and said he would also enlist his younger son in the Pakistan Army.
