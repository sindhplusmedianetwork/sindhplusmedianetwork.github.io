Apple services hit by sudden disruption worldwide

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Several online services of technology company Apple suffered a sudden disruption on Tuesday, causing difficulties for users around the world. According to a foreign news agency, Apple's iCloud, Apple Pay, App Store and other services were affected. Users were unable to log in to iCloud accounts, download apps or complete payments through Apple Pay.

Some iPhone and Mac users were automatically logged out of their accounts. Apple said the problem was caused by an internal software update and was not a cyberattack. The company said most services had been restored by Tuesday afternoon.
