Khalil-ur-Rehman Qamar's daughter Hijab faces criticism over dance videos

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hijab Khalil, the daughter of renowned Pakistani drama writer Khalil-ur-Rehman Qamar, is facing criticism on social media over dance videos in which she appears wearing an outfit that exposes part of her shoulders and stomach. Hijab is building her own identity as a social media influencer on Instagram and TikTok, and recently shared dance videos that drew comments from users. Some users linked her clothing choices to her father's past statements criticising actresses, including Saba Qamar, over their attire, while others pointed out that he has often given instructions to other women about dress.

The comments from users are their personal opinions, and debate continues on social media over connecting her father's statements with her personal clothing choices.
