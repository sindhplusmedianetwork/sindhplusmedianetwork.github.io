SECP Seeks Bank Records in SE Fruits IPO Probe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has sought export records from seven banks as its investigation into the proposed initial public offering of SE Fruits and Vegetable Limited progresses. The banks were asked to provide export details for the company, formerly known as Shaheen Enterprises, the SECP said in a news release. The SECP also sought information from 30 banks about foreign remittances, export proceeds and other foreign exchange transactions received in accounts of the company's sponsors and directors, and some banks have responded, with their submissions under review.

The SECP initiated the investigation on October 21 under Section 29 of the SECP Act, 1997, read with Section 139 of the Securities Act, and it has received information from the IPO's joint lead managers, Topline Securities and Growth Securities, while the response from consultant Parker Russell is also under review. The investigation team is examining the IPO application and shareholder information, and the Central Depository Company provided an investor list and later received a request for shareholder lists and transfer histories for people named in the prospectus, with its response, received on November 2, partial and still under review. The probe follows concerns about the company's financial information and audit.

SE Fruits had planned to raise up to Rs1.92 billion by offering 30 million shares at Rs40 each, but the SECP halted the IPO in October and the company subsequently withdrew the offering.
