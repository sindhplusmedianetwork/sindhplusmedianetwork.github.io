People's will cannot be overridden, says Punjab Assembly Speaker

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Assembly Speaker Malik Muhammad Ahmad Khan has said the will of the people cannot be overridden if they directly express their desire for the creation of new provinces through a referendum. He questioned whether elected representatives could go against the people's wishes, stressing that the people are sovereign. The speaker raised the question after Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi proposed a referendum to seek public opinion on creating new provinces.

Malik said he supported the creation of smaller administrative units, arguing that they would improve service delivery to the public. He also strongly advocated a provincial finance commission and effective local governments. His remarks come amid a renewed debate over Naqvi's proposal for a referendum on new provinces.
