October 20 Last Date to Deposit Second Hajj Installment, Ministry Warns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Ministry of Religious Affairs has announced that October 20 is the last date for collecting dues under the government Hajj scheme. The ministry's spokesperson urged intending pilgrims to deposit the second installment promptly so their applications are not cancelled. So far, 97,000 government Hajj pilgrims have paid the second installment, and Rs 61 billion has been received through the digital Hajj portal.

Of these payments, 45,245 were made through OneLink, 3,685 through bank cards and 47,563 through manual bank challans. The ministry also said that medical fitness certificates must be downloaded from the Hajj portal, verified by a doctor and then re-uploaded to complete the process. Officials said the digitized system was introduced to make the process transparent and convenient for pilgrims.

Pilgrims who miss the deadline risk losing their place in the scheme.
