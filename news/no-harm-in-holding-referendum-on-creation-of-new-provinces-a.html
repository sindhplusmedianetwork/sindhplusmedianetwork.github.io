No harm in holding referendum on creation of new provinces: Attaullah Tarar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Information Minister Attaullah Tarar has said the government's position on the creation of new provinces is clear, public opinion should be taken in this regard, and if the public decides in favour of new provinces they should be created, while if they oppose it, it should not be done. Talking to the media outside the Election Commission, Attaullah Tarar said the path of public opinion should be paved and decisions should be according to the people's will, adding there is no harm in holding a referendum and the Constitution clearly provides a clause for holding a referendum on any matter of national importance. He said debate on this matter should continue across the whole country and public opinion should be sought, and only after public opinion comes forward will it be decided what to do next.

The information minister said President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif had met the previous day, in which various matters were discussed and moving forward with mutual understanding was talked about. On the matter of his appearance before the Election Commission, Attaullah Tarar said Gujranwala is his native city and he had visited there on the invitation of the District Bar's general secretary. He said the invitation was given before the election schedule was issued and no candidate was present during the visit, and he has tendered an unconditional apology to the Election Commission on this matter.
