No Majority for New Provinces Right Now: CM Murad Ali Shah at Jam Chakro Landfill Inauguration

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah on Monday inaugurated the Jam Chakro landfill site in Karachi, marking a step toward improved waste management in the city. Speaking on the occasion, he said that at present there was no majority in favour of creating new provinces. The chief minister described terrorism as the biggest problem facing the province and said it needed to be addressed on a priority basis.

Commenting on the political situation in another province, he termed the imposition of governor's rule as constitutional but not democratic. He also announced that the provincial cabinet would open the sealed judicial commission report on the Meer Raza case and take a decision on it. The inauguration of the landfill site is expected to help streamline the disposal of the city's solid waste.
