PM assures Balochistan communities will benefit from mineral projects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Friday directed the relevant authorities to ensure that local communities, particularly the people of Balochistan, directly benefit from mineral development projects through employment, skills development and improved economic opportunities. Chairing a meeting with a delegation of Barrick Mining Corporation, led by its Chairman John Thornton, the prime minister emphasised that effectively utilising Balochistan's natural resources and mineral wealth was essential for the province's development and public prosperity, a Prime Minister's Office news release said. He said the government's priorities included creating employment and skills development opportunities for local youth, ensuring their participation in the benefits of mineral projects and promoting the development of communities surrounding project sites.

Welcoming Barrick Mining Corporation's participation in the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum, the prime minister described the Reko Diq project as a major milestone for investment in the country's mineral sector and a project of strategic importance to Pakistan's economic future. He said the successful implementation of Reko Diq would establish a benchmark for future investment in the country's mining sector and help create new opportunities for international investors. The prime minister also assured Ivanhoe Mines of full government facilitation for investment in Pakistan's mineral sector, particularly in the exploration and development of copper, gold and other critical mineral resources, during a meeting with its Founder and Executive Co-Chairman, Robert Friedland.

He said Pakistan was rich in mineral resources and committed to working with international partners to harness its vast mineral potential, and that the recognition of Pakistan as a preferred destination for mineral investment reflected growing confidence among international investors in the country's mineral potential and investment climate.
