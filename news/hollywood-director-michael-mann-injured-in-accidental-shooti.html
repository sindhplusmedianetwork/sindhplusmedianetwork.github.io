Hollywood Director Michael Mann Injured in Accidental Shooting at Los Angeles Range

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Renowned Hollywood director Michael Mann, 83, was injured in an accidental shooting at a Los Angeles shooting range on Saturday and taken to hospital. According to foreign media reports, the incident occurred at around 4pm at the 'Range LA' facility, where Mann was engaged in firearm training. Sources said he was attempting to place his pistol into its holster when it suddenly discharged and the bullet struck his leg.

The Los Angeles Police Department confirmed the incident, stating that the Hollywood director was accidentally shot during firearm training. Medical staff immediately transported him to hospital by ambulance. Attempts to reach Mann's spokesperson for comment received no immediate response.
