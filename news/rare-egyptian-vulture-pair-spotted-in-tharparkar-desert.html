Rare Egyptian Vulture Pair Spotted in Tharparkar Desert

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A rare pair of Egyptian vultures has been spotted in the desert between Mithi and Nagarparkar in Tharparkar, and a video of the pair has now surfaced. Wildlife conservationist and environmental expert Syed Rizwan Mehboob shared the video. He said the species faces severe threats, and its population has declined significantly due to the indiscriminate use of pesticides.

He noted that Egyptian vultures play an important role in cleaning the environment by feeding on dead animals.
