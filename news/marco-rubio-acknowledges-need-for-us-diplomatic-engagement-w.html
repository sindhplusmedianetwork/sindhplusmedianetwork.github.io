Marco Rubio acknowledges need for US diplomatic engagement with Pakistan

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio has acknowledged the need for continued diplomatic engagement with Pakistan. Rubio said Washington should maintain its ties with Islamabad, underlining Pakistan's standing as a nuclear-armed state of strategic importance in the region. The remarks signal that the current administration considers Pakistan an essential partner in the region's diplomatic landscape.

Rubio's comments came during a round of diplomatic engagements in which he also met Indian External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar. The meetings reflect Washington's balancing act in South Asia, as it engages with both Islamabad and New Delhi.

Observers see the acknowledgement as a sign that the US intends to keep Pakistan firmly within its diplomatic outreach in the region.
