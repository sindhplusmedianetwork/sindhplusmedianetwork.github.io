LESCO Reports 1,428 Power Theft Cases in October, 545 Registered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore Electric Supply Company has shared details of its crackdown on electricity theft during October 2026, reporting 1,428 cases across all its circles. The cases included 1,418 domestic, one commercial, six agricultural and three industrial connections. Applications were filed for 1,331 cases, 545 cases were registered and five people were arrested.

The company charged 1,932,870 units worth over Rs116 million and recovered nearly Rs10 million. The Eastern Lahore circle reported the highest number of cases at 778.
