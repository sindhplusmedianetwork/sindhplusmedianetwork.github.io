Government Unveils New Auto Policy to Cut Prices and Boost EV Adoption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has unveiled the final draft of its new Automotive Industry Policy, which aims to gradually reduce vehicle prices and turn the auto sector into a competitive, export-oriented industry. Adviser to the Prime Minister on Industries and Production Haroon Akhtar Khan and Information Minister Attaullah Tarar announced the policy at a press conference in Islamabad on Saturday, saying it had been approved by the prime minister and would now be submitted to the cabinet. The government has set a five-year target of increasing automotive exports from 200 million dollars to 6 billion dollars, with manufacturers assigned export targets and a reward equal to five percent of their export value in the first year for meeting them, while those who fail will face penalties.

The policy introduces 45 additional safety standards, caps advance booking payments at 20 percent of a vehicle's price, and provides for third-party audits to improve transparency. To encourage electric vehicles, the financing limit has been raised from Rs2 million to Rs10 million, electric vehicles will attract one percent sales tax, and machinery imported for charging stations will face one percent sales tax and zero customs duty. Tarar clarified that the policy still needs cabinet approval and that its final announcement and implementation would follow the required approvals, including from the International Monetary Fund.
