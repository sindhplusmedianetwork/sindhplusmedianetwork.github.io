US loses measles-free status after 26 years as cases spread

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has lost its measles-free status after 26 years because of rising measles cases. Public health experts voiced concern about the disease spreading again. Measles is highly contagious, and experts stressed that vaccination is the key protection against it.

Unvaccinated people and areas with low vaccination coverage face a greater risk. Experts urged parents to complete their children's vaccination schedules.
