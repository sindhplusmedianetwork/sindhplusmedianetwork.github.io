CDF Munir, Singapore's FM Discuss Regional Security and Bilateral Cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir met Singapore's Foreign Minister Dr Vivian Balakrishnan at the General Headquarters in Rawalpindi on Friday. The two leaders discussed regional security, evolving global security dynamics and avenues for expanding bilateral cooperation between the two countries. Dr Balakrishnan also called on Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, who praised Singapore's remarkable economic transformation.

He later held delegation-level talks with Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar, covering trade, direct flights and a memorandum of understanding on disaster management. Both sides agreed to deepen cooperation in trade, investment, the digital economy, education and people-to-people contacts. The visit coincides with the 60th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Pakistan and Singapore.
