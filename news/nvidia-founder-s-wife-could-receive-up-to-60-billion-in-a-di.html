Nvidia founder's wife could receive up to $60 billion in a divorce, estimates suggest

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Reports about the married life of Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang have sparked fresh debate about the wealth of his wife, Lori Huang, after claims about his personal life began circulating. Various reports estimate Jensen Huang's total wealth at around $144 billion, placing him among the richest people in the world. According to those estimates, his wife could receive up to $60 billion in the event of a divorce, though the figure is only an estimate and the final amount would depend on how the couple's assets are divided under the law.

The reports have drawn wide attention because of the sheer scale of the fortune involved and the long marriage behind it. The couple married in 1985 and have two children together. No formal divorce announcement has been made by either of them, and the discussion remains based on estimates and unconfirmed reports rather than any legal filing.
