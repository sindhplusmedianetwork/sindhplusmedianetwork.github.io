China unveils new video and details of its GJ-11 stealth attack drone

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China's People's Liberation Army Air Force has released new video of its GJ-11 stealth attack drone, codenamed Xuanlong. The flying-wing drone, which was developed locally in China, entered service in November 2025, when the air force announced that it had formally joined service. The drone has air intakes on its upper side and internal weapons bays underneath, and it can carry several types of air-to-ground weapons, with high stealth features, long flight endurance and highly accurate targeting capability.

It can carry out various missions, including air strikes and the destruction of enemy air defence systems. The released footage also shows its jet nozzle, low-visibility coating and a modern optical window fitted at the front of the drone, which resembles the advanced electro-optical targeting system installed on China's J-20 stealth fighter. According to a recent report, the GJ-11 was operated in coordination with another drone, and it is also capable of conducting coordinated operations together with manned aircraft.

State broadcaster CCTV reported that the drones can act as the eyes of manned fighter aircraft and carry out advanced reconnaissance to identify enemy air defence systems, and that they can also be used as decoys to draw enemy fire and expose their positions, or as weapons platforms to launch guided missiles under the guidance of manned aircraft.
