Salman Khan faces backlash over romantic song in Galwan-inspired patriotic film

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Salman Khan is facing criticism on social media after releasing the romantic song 'Teri Sexy Beauty' from his upcoming patriotic film 'Matrubhumi: May War Rest in Peace', marking his return to playback singing after 11 years. The song, released on the morning of 4 November and featuring actress Chitrangda Singh, drew objections from viewers who questioned a romantic party song in a film inspired by the June 2020 Galwan Valley clash in which Colonel Santosh Babu and 19 other Indian soldiers were killed. Some fans defended the song's melody and welcomed Khan's return to singing, but critics said audiences expected a patriotic song in a film about a sensitive military episode.

The debate continues on social media, with supporters praising the music while others say a film about the Galwan clash should carry a patriotic song instead.
