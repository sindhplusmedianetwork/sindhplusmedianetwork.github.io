Courts cannot pre-empt future laws, remarks Islamabad High Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has remarked in a judgment that courts cannot decide in advance to block possible future laws. According to Express News, the court disposed of a constitutional petition against the NAB ordinance that had extended the period of physical remand in custody from 14 to 40 days, as infructuous. Justice Inaam Ameen Minhas held in the written judgment that the NAB amending ordinance had lapsed on its own after its legal tenure expired, so no live dispute remained before the court.

The judgment noted that the petitioner could not prove any action or punishment against him under the ordinance, and that courts cannot issue advance rulings to prevent possible laws that may be made in the future. The NAB amending ordinance of 2024 had extended physical remand from 14 to 40 days. It was issued by the acting president on May 27, 2024, but was never extended by parliament nor made a regular act.

The Lahore High Court had earlier disposed of a similar petition on the expiry of the NAB ordinance.
