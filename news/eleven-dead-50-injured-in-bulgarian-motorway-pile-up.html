Eleven dead, 50 injured in Bulgarian motorway pile-up

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Eleven people died in a traffic accident involving dozens of vehicles on both sides of a foggy stretch of highway in eastern Bulgaria on Tuesday, Prime Minister Rumen Radev said. Police, firefighters and ambulance crews rushed to the scene on the A1 road that links the capital Sofia with the eastern city of Burgas, where cars and trucks were piled up across six lanes of highway, many crushed, overturned or veered off to the side. The interior ministry said 50 injured people were taken to hospital.

Briefing reporters at the scene, the interior ministry's general secretary, Lyubomir Nikolov, said fog was the likely cause of the accident, which started with a collision between a bus and a truck on the side of the highway heading towards Burgas. Radev described the accident as a tragedy during a visit to Bulgaria by European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, and said he had deployed aircraft to evacuate the wounded and planned to visit the scene. Von der Leyen said she would cut her visit short and expressed her deepest condolences, paying respects to the rescue teams.

Chief Inspector Todor Mishinev of the Yambol regional directorate said some people were still trapped in their cars, adding that rescue operations were the priority and that procedural and investigative work would follow once they were completed.
