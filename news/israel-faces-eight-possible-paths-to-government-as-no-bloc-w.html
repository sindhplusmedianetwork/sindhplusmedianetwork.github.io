Israel faces eight possible paths to government as no bloc wins Knesset majority

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Eight possible scenarios for forming a government have emerged after the Israeli elections, in which no bloc secured the 61 seats required for a majority in the 120-member Knesset. Prime Minister Netanyahu's 53-seat bloc could try to stay in power through a national unity arrangement, by breaking away opposition members, or through a compromise involving a rotation of the premiership. Gadi Eisenkot's 48-seat bloc, meanwhile, could reach a numerical strength of 67 seats with the support of the Arab parties' 19 seats.

Political analysts say each path carries its own complications, and intense negotiations are expected in the coming days. The outcome will determine the shape of Israel's next governing coalition.
