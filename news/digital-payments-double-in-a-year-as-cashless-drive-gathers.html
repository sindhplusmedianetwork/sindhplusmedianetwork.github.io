Digital Payments Double in a Year as Cashless Drive Gathers Pace

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The volume of digital payments in the country has doubled in a year, and the number of active digital merchants has risen from 500,000 to over 2.03 million. The figures were shared at a follow-up meeting on the cashless initiative chaired by the state minister for finance, Bilal Azhar Kayani, who issued directions to promote digital payments, advance the Raast QR merchant discount rate scheme and improve the dashboard indicators. The meeting reviewed progress on the decisions of the cashless committee headed by the prime minister, and considered finalising the framework of the Raast QR merchant discount rate scheme and making the prime minister's dashboard more comprehensive.

It was decided that the dashboard would cover not only the total volume of digital payments but also their value and the number of users making payments through unique accounts. From November 16, all payments from Asaan Assignment Accounts across the country will be made through the Raast digital system, for which the training of drawing and disbursing officers has been completed. Raast QR codes have been provided on more than 45 million gas and electricity bills, allowing citizens to pay their utility bills digitally from home, and all centres of the national database authority have also been equipped with Raast QR codes.

The state minister directed the central bank to prepare a working paper to move ahead the approval process for the Raast QR merchant discount rate scheme, and announced he would meet bank presidents and chief executives in Karachi to discuss the role of banks in the national initiative.
