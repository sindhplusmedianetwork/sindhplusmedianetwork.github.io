Pakistan Among Countries With Highest Diabetes Rates, Doctors Warn

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Doctors have warned that Pakistan is among the countries with the highest prevalence of diabetes, with the number of patients continuing to rise. The warning came at a World Diabetes Day seminar in Lahore titled 'Early Action on Diabetes', organised by the Pakistan Society of Internal Medicine and the Sir Syed College of Medical Sciences Karachi in collaboration with the Express Media Group. Patron-in-Chief of the society, Professor Javed Akram, said around half of the people living with diabetes do not know they have the disease.

He stressed that diabetes is preventable and can be controlled through a healthy lifestyle, weight management and regular exercise. Other speakers at the seminar warned that Pakistan is moving towards having the highest number of diabetes patients in the world. They also noted that the country ranks sixth in the world for diabetes among pregnant women.
