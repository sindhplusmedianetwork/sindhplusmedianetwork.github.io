Nine young Pakistani players shine on day two of World Junior Tennis Championship in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nine young Pakistani players won their round-robin second-round matches on the second day of the J-30 Subh-e-Nau Pakistan World Junior Tennis Championship 2026 at the PTF Tennis Complex in Islamabad. The wins brightened their chances of reaching the quarter-finals. The winners were Mohammad Yahya, Ehtisham Humayun, Zohaib Afzal Malik, Abdul Basit, Aamir Mazari, M Hassan Usmani, Inam Qadir, Mohammad Moazz and Umar Jawad Malik.

The event features 55 players from 16 countries, including 33 boys and 22 girls. The strong showing on home courts has raised hopes for the young Pakistani players as the tournament moves towards its later rounds.
