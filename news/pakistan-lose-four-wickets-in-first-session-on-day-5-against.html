Pakistan lose four wickets in first session on day-5 against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan reached 151-5 at lunch in their second innings on the fifth and final day of the first Test against Sri Lanka at the Pindi Cricket Stadium in Rawalpindi, leading by 139 runs. Conceding a 12-run lead, Pakistan resumed their innings with the overnight score of 57-1, with Azan Awais on 22 and Mohammad Taha on 24 at the crease, but both fell without adding to their overnight scores, as Lahiru Kumara dismissed Awais while Asitha Fernando removed Taha in quick succession. Babar Azam then combined with Saud Shakeel to rebuild the innings, adding 50 runs for the fifth-wicket partnership, before Milan Rathnayaka removed Babar, who fell for 34 off 48 balls with six fours.

Rathnayaka continued to add to Pakistan's troubles when he had Salman Ali Agha caught for just 13 off 19 balls, with two fours. At lunch, Saud Shakeel was batting on 23 and with him at the crease was Ghazi Ghori on seven, while Asitha Fernando, with figures of 2-20, and Milan Rathnayaka, with 2-32, were effective with the ball. Due to Friday prayers, the session was extended for half an hour and lunch was taken at 12:30pm instead of 12pm.

Earlier on Thursday, debutant pacer Saqib Khan claimed 7-130 to help Pakistan bowl Sri Lanka out for 446 in response to the hosts' first innings total of 434-9 declared, with Dhananjaya de Silva top-scoring with 167 and captain Kusal Mendis contributing 119.
