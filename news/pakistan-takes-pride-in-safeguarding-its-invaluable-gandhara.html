Pakistan takes pride in safeguarding its invaluable Gandhara heritage, culture minister tells global dialogue

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for National Heritage and Culture Aurangzeb Khan Khichi has said Pakistan takes pride in safeguarding its invaluable Gandhara heritage, describing it as a shared responsibility to the world. He was speaking at the Gandhara Strategic Dialogue 2026 in Islamabad on Wednesday, an event that brought together senior Buddhist monastics from eight countries, along with diplomats and experts. The dialogue focused on preserving and promoting Gandhara sites such as Taxila, Takht-e-Bahi and Swat, which hold deep significance for Buddhist heritage and for Pakistan's cultural history.

Among the proposals discussed were a dedicated Buddhist visa category for pilgrims, meditation facilities near heritage sites and the creation of a Gandhara heritage zone in Taxila. The minister said Pakistan values its ancient heritage and wants the world to see it, adding that the dialogue would help build stronger cultural links with Buddhist communities abroad. The event concludes on November 20.
