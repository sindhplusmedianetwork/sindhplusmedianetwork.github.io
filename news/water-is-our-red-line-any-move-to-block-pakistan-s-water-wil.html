Water Is Our Red Line; Any Move to Block Pakistan's Water Will Be Treated as an Act of War: PM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has declared that water is Pakistan's red line, warning that any attempt to block the country's water will be treated as an act of war. Speaking at a joint press conference with Maldives President Dr Muizzu, he termed India's unilateral suspension of the Indus Waters Treaty unacceptable. The two sides signed 20 memorandums of understanding and agreed to raise bilateral trade from $13 million to $200 million within two years, while Pakistan will provide IT technical support to Maldives.

Shehbaz noted that the visit was the first by a Maldivian president in 11 years. President Muizzu was conferred an honorary doctorate during the visit. The prime minister reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to strengthening ties with the island nation.
