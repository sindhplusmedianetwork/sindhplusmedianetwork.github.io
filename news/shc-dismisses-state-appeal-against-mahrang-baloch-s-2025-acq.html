SHC Dismisses State Appeal Against Mahrang Baloch's 2025 Acquittal in Sedition Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court has dismissed the provincial government's appeal against the 2025 acquittal of Baloch Yakjehti Committee leader Mahrang Baloch in a sedition case. A two-judge bench headed by Justice Shamsuddin Abbasi declared the state's plea non-maintainable and rejected it. The court observed that the complainant of the FIR was a private individual, and questioned how the prosecution could file an appeal in his place.

Mahrang Baloch was acquitted in the sedition case in 2025. She remains in Quetta prison in connection with other cases. The ruling closes the state's attempt to challenge her acquittal in the sedition matter.
