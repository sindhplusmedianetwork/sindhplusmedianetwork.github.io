Security withdrawn from both PML-N, 'Q' MPs in Gujrat, claim officials

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gujrat district police have withdrawn 30 policemen deployed for the security of PML-Q and PML-N ministers, lawmakers and former lawmakers, officials say. Only two of the withdrawn policemen were guarding PML-N former MNA Chaudhry Abid Raza Kotla, while the rest, including 12 Elite Force commandos and a vehicle guarding federal minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain, were with PML-Q figures. Officials say the District Intelligence Committee decided the withdrawal under the provincial home department's standard operating procedure, as the politicians faced no threat.

The decision was taken at a meeting a couple of days ago after a review of the security situation. Local authorities said the ministers would be given security on visits as needed, but the parliamentarians were not entitled to keep permanent police security. MPA Musa Elahi criticised the withdrawal in a recent media talk.
