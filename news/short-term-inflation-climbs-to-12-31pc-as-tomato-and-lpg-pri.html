Short-term inflation climbs to 12.31pc as tomato and LPG prices surge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's short-term inflation rate rose to 12.31% on an annual basis, according to Pakistan Bureau of Statistics data for the week ending November 5. The rise was driven mainly by higher LPG and perishable food prices. Tomato prices rose 54.18% in a week and LPG prices increased by 3.72%.

LPG prices are up 114.88% year-on-year. Of 51 essential items, prices of 21 rose, 10 fell and 20 remained stable. Petrol fell 4.20% and diesel 2.97% during the week.
