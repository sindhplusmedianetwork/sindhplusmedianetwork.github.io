Bahrain and France agree to deepen cooperation on AI, clean energy and industry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prince Salman bin Hamad Al Khalifa, Bahrain's Crown Prince and Prime Minister, met French President Emmanuel Macron in Paris on November 14. The meeting focused on strengthening the strategic partnership between Bahrain and France and expanding bilateral cooperation. The Crown Prince highlighted the longstanding friendship between the two countries and the importance of closer work in modern technologies, artificial intelligence and renewable energy.

President Macron reaffirmed France's commitment to developing cooperation with Bahrain, particularly in the industrial sector. The talks also covered collaboration with Aluminium Bahrain, known as Alba, as part of the wider industrial agenda. Both leaders presented the meeting as a step towards deeper ties between the two countries in technology, energy and industry.
