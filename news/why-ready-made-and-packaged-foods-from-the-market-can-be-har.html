Why ready-made and packaged foods from the market can be harmful to health

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ready-made and packaged foods bought for convenience are not a substitute for fresh home-cooked food, and regular use can create health problems. Many such products lack essential nutrients in adequate amounts, even though they may look filling. They often contain preservatives, artificial flavours and colours, and excess salt, which can raise blood pressure and in turn increase the risk of heart disease and stroke.

They can also be low in fibre, vitamins and minerals while being high in unhealthy saturated and trans fats and calories. A typical ready meal can contain about 500 to 800 calories. Over time, eating such foods regularly can contribute to weight gain and obesity.

Health experts advise eating fresh, home-cooked meals whenever possible and reading food labels before buying packaged items.
