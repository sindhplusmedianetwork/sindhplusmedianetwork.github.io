Sindh Government Moves to Make Shaheed Bhutto Expressway a Model Road

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has taken major decisions to develop the Shaheed Bhutto Expressway as a model expressway. Interior Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar chaired an important meeting on the project and said it is extremely important, stressing that its security strategy and action plan must be re-evaluated. He directed the authorities to prepare two mobile mechanic vans at the earliest and to arrange tow vehicles for broken-down cars on the expressway.

He also ordered regular marking of entry and exit points and the installation of CCTV cameras to be linked with the Safe City project. The minister said the expressway is a new project on which the Sindh government has a special focus, and that such model roads would later be extended to other districts of the province. IG Sindh briefed the meeting that mobile patrols for the expressway's security continue round the clock, with traffic, thana and highway police actively deployed.

Senior officials including the Special Assistant for Investment, the finance, transport and municipalities secretaries and senior police officers attended the meeting.
