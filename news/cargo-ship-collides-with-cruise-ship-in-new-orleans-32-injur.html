Cargo Ship Collides With Cruise Ship in New Orleans, 32 Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A cargo ship collided with a passenger-packed cruise ship in the American city of New Orleans, injuring 32 people. After the collision, the cruise ship tilted to one side, but everyone on board was safely evacuated. According to American media reports, more than 120 people were on the cruise ship.

After the collision, rescue teams acted promptly and evacuated all passengers and crew from the vessel. Traffic on the Mississippi River near the collision site was temporarily suspended following the accident. No further details about the cause of the accident have been released by the authorities.

US President Donald Trump confirmed that there were no deaths in the accident, while the 32 injured people are under treatment in hospital. He praised the performance of the Coast Guard and other teams in the rescue operation.
