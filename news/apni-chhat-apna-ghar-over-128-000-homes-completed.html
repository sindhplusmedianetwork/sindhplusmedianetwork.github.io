Apni Chhat, Apna Ghar: Over 128,000 Homes Completed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's Apni Chhat, Apna Ghar housing programme has crossed a new milestone with more than 225,000 loans disbursed to applicants. Of these, 128,718 houses have been fully completed so far. A total of 171,597 borrowers have received the second installment of their loans.

The programme's recovery rate stands at an impressive 97.6 percent. Around 22.29 billion rupees have been collected in repayments. Officials confirmed that fresh applications have also been approved under the scheme.

The initiative continues to provide interest-free housing finance to low-income families across the province.
