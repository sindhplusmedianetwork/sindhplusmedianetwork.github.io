Government Asks PPP, MQM to Name Members for Electoral Reforms Committee

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has moved to form a Parliamentary Committee on Electoral Reforms, asking the PPP and the MQM to nominate their members. Federal Minister Tariq Fazal Chaudhry sent letters to the chief whips of both parties seeking names for the proposed body. Under the agreed formula, the PML-N will hold nine seats, the PPP five and the MQM one, while the smaller allies BAP and PML-Z will each get one seat from the PML-N's quota.

Chaudhry said the initiative was launched on the proposal of National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq. Opposition parties, he added, will forward their nominations directly to the speaker's office. The PPP has already submitted its names, though they have not been made public yet.

The committee is expected to deliberate on reforms to the country's electoral system once fully constituted.
