Kohli becomes first to 13,000 ODI runs at number three in Auckland

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Virat Kohli added another world record to his name on Wednesday, becoming the first cricketer in history to score 13,000 one-day international runs while batting at number three. The 37-year-old Indian star needed just 12 runs in the series opener against New Zealand at Auckland's Eden Park and got there off the 13th ball of his innings. He reached the milestone in only 251 innings, maintaining an average of 61.55 at the position with 48 centuries and nearly 70 half-centuries to his name.

Former Australia captain Ricky Ponting sits second on the list with 12,662 runs, followed by Sri Lanka's Kumar Sangakkara with 9,747. South Africa's Jacques Kallis (7,774) and New Zealand's Kane Williamson (6,504) complete the top five. With the top four closest challengers all retired from international cricket, and active players like Pakistan's Babar Azam (6,054) and England's Joe Root (5,525) nearly 7,000 runs behind, the record looks almost unbreakable.

Kohli has been in outstanding form in 2026, scoring 552 runs in nine innings before the New Zealand series with two centuries and three fifties.
