Pakistan Stock Exchange hits record 681,965 investors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The number of investors at the Pakistan Stock Exchange has reached a record 681,965, the highest in the bourse's history. Federal adviser on economic and financial reforms Khurram Shehzad said a record 25,747 new investors joined in October 2026 alone, a 66 percent increase over October last year. He said 218,894 new investors joined during the first ten months of 2026 — more than the total increase recorded over the previous four years combined.

Around 85 percent of the new investors belong to Generation Z and millennials, reflecting growing youth interest in equity markets. Shehzad attributed the surge to the government's economic policies and financial reforms.
