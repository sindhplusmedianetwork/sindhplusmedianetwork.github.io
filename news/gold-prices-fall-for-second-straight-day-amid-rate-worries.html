Gold prices fall for second straight day amid rate worries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices fell for a second consecutive session on Friday, losing 4,900 rupees per tola as the metal remained under pressure in international markets. The price per tola settled at 411,636 rupees, while 10 grams of gold fell by 4,201 rupees to 352,911 rupees, according to the national jewellers association. The latest decline followed a sharp correction a day earlier, when gold had lost 10,900 rupees per tola to close at 416,536 rupees.

Silver also weakened, falling 111 rupees to 5,467 rupees per tola in the domestic market. Internationally, spot gold edged up slightly to 3,885.09 dollars per ounce, but it remained down more than four per cent for the week, heading for its steepest weekly fall since early June. Market watchers linked the pressure to expectations of further monetary tightening in the United States and persistent geopolitical uncertainty.

One analyst said international prices could test the 3,700 to 3,600 dollar range in the coming sessions, with the central bank's December policy decision and regional developments seen as key factors ahead.
