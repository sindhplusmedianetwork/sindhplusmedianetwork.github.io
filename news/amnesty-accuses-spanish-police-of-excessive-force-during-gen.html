Amnesty Accuses Spanish Police of Excessive Force During General Strike

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Amnesty International has accused Spanish police of using excessive force against protesters and journalists during last week's general strike. The human rights organisation said police used batons, tear gas, pepper spray and rubber bullets without any prior warning in six major cities including Madrid and Barcelona. According to media reports, 36 people were injured in the police action, five of whom had to be admitted to hospital.

The organisation's report is based on interviews with 24 victims and eyewitnesses, and Amnesty International has demanded a deep review of the police code of conduct. Following the incident, the Spanish Interior Ministry announced that the use of pepper spray would be limited, while Madrid police have started an internal investigation into the actions of their personnel.
