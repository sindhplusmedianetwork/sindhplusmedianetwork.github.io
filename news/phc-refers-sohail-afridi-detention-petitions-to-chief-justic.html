PHC Refers Sohail Afridi Detention Petitions to Chief Justice for Larger Bench

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A division bench of the Peshawar High Court has referred petitions challenging the detention of senior PTI leaders, including non-functional Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister Sohail Afridi, to the chief justice for the constitution of a larger bench. The court took the step after the provincial government and police failed to submit their replies despite clear directives issued on October 20. The petitions also cover the alleged disappearance of KP Assembly Speaker Babar Saleem Swati, several members of the national and provincial assemblies, and other PTI figures.

Afridi has not been seen in public since governor's rule and emergency were imposed in the province on October 11. During the hearing, the bench questioned why no response had been filed even after the court's earlier order. The advocate general sought additional time, saying reports had been called from the police, law-enforcement agencies and deputy commissioners, and also supported the request for a larger bench.

The matter will now be placed before the chief justice for further proceedings.
