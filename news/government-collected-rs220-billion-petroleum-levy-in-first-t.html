Government collected Rs220 billion petroleum levy in first two months of fiscal year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government collected Rs220 billion through the petroleum levy in the first two months of the current fiscal year 2026-27. The full-year target for the levy is Rs1.676 trillion. Last fiscal year, the initial budget estimate for the petroleum levy was Rs1.468 trillion.

Actual collection during that year rose by Rs100 billion. By 30 June 2026, total collection had reached Rs1.567 trillion.
