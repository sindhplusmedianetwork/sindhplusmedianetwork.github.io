Two ships carrying fertilizer and wheat berth at Karachi Port

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two ships loaded with fertilizer and wheat reached Karachi Port and were given berths on priority. A Karachi Port Trust spokesperson said more than 101,000 tonnes of import cargo was handled during the operation. The cargo included 50,000 tonnes of DAP fertilizer and 51,000 tonnes of wheat.

Maritime Affairs Minister Junaid Anwar Chaudhry said the timely delivery of essential goods is vital for food security. He added that the government is focused on keeping the supply chain of key commodities running smoothly. The berthing of the two vessels marks a fresh addition to the country's food and agriculture supply reserves.
