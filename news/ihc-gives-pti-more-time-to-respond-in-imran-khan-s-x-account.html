IHC Gives PTI More Time to Respond in Imran Khan's X Account Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court on Thursday granted PTI interim chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan additional time to file a written response to a petition seeking a ban on party founder Imran Khan's official X account. Justice Arbab Muhammad Tahir, who heard the petition filed by Ghulam Murtaza, adjourned the hearing until November 23. Barrister Gohar appeared in person before the court.

The court observed that he could have submitted his response in writing. The PTI leader told the court that his party did not claim ownership of the X account in question, but sought more time to file a detailed reply in light of changed circumstances. Separately, Judicial Magistrate Shaista Kundi issued arrest warrants for the accused who failed to appear in cases registered against the PTI founder and others in connection with the 2014 sit-in.

Imran Khan could not be produced in court as he is currently incarcerated in Adiala jail, after which the magistrate adjourned proceedings till November 25 and directed that evidence be presented at the next hearing.
