Bomb hoaxes target 8 passenger planes across Europe in two days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Europe's aviation system faced severe disruption after eight passenger planes received fake bomb threats within two days. Thousands of passengers were affected as emergency measures were imposed at several airports and flight operations were badly hit. One flight was diverted for an emergency landing at Warsaw Modlin airport in Poland, while a flight from Copenhagen to Warsaw also received a threat.

A Ryanair flight from Spain to Manchester was evacuated immediately after landing and surrounded by the anti-terrorism squad, which searched the aircraft. Flights arriving from Sofia in Bulgaria, Tel Aviv and Bergamo in Italy received bomb threats through email, and commandos carried out detailed searches of planes at Sofia airport. Two flights arriving in Rome from Lisbon and Dublin were also threatened and were brought down under the supervision of Italian air force jets.

The wave began on November 2, when a Wizz Air flight from London to Sofia was forced into an emergency landing in Budapest under the escort of Hungarian fighter jets. Security agencies confirmed that all the alerts turned out to be hoaxes and no damage was reported, but experts warn that targeting flights in several countries within such a short time could be an organised campaign or a hybrid security threat aimed at paralysing European air travel.
