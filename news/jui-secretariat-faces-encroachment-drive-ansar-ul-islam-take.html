JUI secretariat faces encroachment drive; Ansar-ul-Islam takes over security

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ansar-ul-Islam volunteers have taken over security at the JUI provincial secretariat, the Mufti Mahmood Markaz on Peshawar's Ring Road, and cordoned off the centre. The move followed reports that the secretariat could be affected by the ongoing anti-encroachment operation in the area. A day earlier, the Peshawar Development Authority and the district administration had marked structures around the JUI offices.

No markings were placed on the Markaz itself. The volunteers' takeover came as the marking of nearby structures raised concern about whether the centre would be affected.
