Gold Price Falls by Rs3,500 Per Tola to Rs376,336 in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices in Pakistan recorded another sharp fall on Saturday. According to the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association, the price of gold per tola dropped by Rs3,500 to Rs376,336. The price of 10 grams of gold fell by Rs3,001 to Rs322,647.

In the international market, gold declined by $35 to $3,538 per ounce. With the latest decrease, the per-tola price of gold has fallen by Rs34,600 in one week.
