Errors in NADRA Records Causing Trouble for Citizens, Correction Rules Explained

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Errors in identity records are increasingly causing problems for Pakistani citizens, as mismatches in names, parentage, dates of birth and other details create hurdles in getting passports, registering marriages and verifying education documents. Under NADRA's rules, a citizen can change their name only once in a lifetime. Corrections to the date of birth are also subject to fixed limits and specific conditions laid down by the authority.

Citizens seeking corrections can apply through NADRA registration centres or the Pak ID app along with their original documents. Officials advise applicants to carefully check all details before submitting their records. The authority says the rules are meant to keep the national database accurate while still allowing genuine mistakes to be fixed.
