Over a Billion Tonnes of Food Wasted Each Year While 645 Million Go Hungry, UN Data Shows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than one billion tonnes of food is wasted around the world each year while about 645 million people face hunger, according to United Nations data. The figure represents 7.8 percent of the world's population who faced food shortage in 2025, down from 8.1 percent in 2024 but still above pre-COVID levels. The Maldives has the highest per-capita food waste at roughly 260kg per person per year, followed by Syria, the Dominican Republic, Seychelles, Mexico and Tunisia, each wasting more than 210kg per person.

Spain wastes about 39kg or less per person. Food waste costs the global economy more than $1 trillion a year and consumes around 1.4 billion hectares of farmland. Experts say planning purchases, buying only what is needed, storing food properly and freezing surplus can significantly reduce waste.

The data highlights a stark imbalance between surplus and scarcity in the global food system.
