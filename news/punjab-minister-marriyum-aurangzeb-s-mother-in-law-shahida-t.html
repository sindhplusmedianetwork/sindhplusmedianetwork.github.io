Punjab Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb's Mother-in-Law Shahida Taseer Passes Away in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Shahida Taseer, the mother-in-law of Punjab Senior Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb, passed away in Lahore on Wednesday after a brief illness. She had been receiving medical treatment at a hospital in the city before her death, according to information shared by the family. Her funeral prayers were offered at 1:30pm at the GOR-I complex in Lahore and were led by Maulana Abdul Khabir Azad, chairman of the Ruet-e-Hilal Committee Pakistan.

The funeral was attended by provincial ministers, members of the Punjab Assembly, senior government officials and police officers. Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz visited Marriyum Aurangzeb to express her grief and offer condolences. Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari also expressed deep sorrow over the demise and prayed for the departed soul.

She was laid to rest at a graveyard in Model Town, Lahore, while the Quran Khwani was scheduled for Friday at 4pm.
