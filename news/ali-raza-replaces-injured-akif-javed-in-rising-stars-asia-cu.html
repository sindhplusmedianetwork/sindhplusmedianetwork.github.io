Ali Raza Replaces Injured Akif Javed in Rising Stars Asia Cup Squad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has announced that fast bowler Ali Raza will replace Akif Javed in the Pakistan Shaheens squad for the upcoming ACC Rising Stars Asia Cup 2026 in Qatar. Based on medical reports and MRI findings, Akif Javed has sustained a knee injury and has been advised to undergo a rehabilitation programme before returning to competitive cricket. Accordingly, the selection committee recommended Ali Raza as Akif Javed's replacement for the tournament.

The change comes as the Shaheens prepare for the regional tournament in Qatar. Akif Javed will now focus on his rehabilitation programme before he can return to competitive cricket.
