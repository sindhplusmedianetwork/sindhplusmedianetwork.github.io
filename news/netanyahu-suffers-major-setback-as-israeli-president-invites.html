Netanyahu Suffers Major Setback as Israeli President Invites Eisenkot to Form Government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli President Isaac Herzog has announced that he will ask former army chief and opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot to form the next government, dealing a major political blow to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. Netanyahu had appealed for a national unity government, but that demand was rejected. In the 27 October elections, Netanyahu's alliance won only 53 of the 120 seats in the Knesset, while opposition parties won 48 seats and Arab parties won 19.

Herzog is expected to formally invite Eisenkot on Sunday evening, after which he will have 28 days to put together a coalition. The president has warned that if the government-formation efforts fail, the Knesset will be dissolved and the country could head back to elections. Netanyahu's political position was already under pressure over the Gaza war and the war with Iran, and the election result has left his alliance without a clear majority.
