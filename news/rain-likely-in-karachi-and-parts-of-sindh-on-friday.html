Rain Likely in Karachi and Parts of Sindh on Friday

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Meteorological Department says rain is likely in Karachi and several other parts of Sindh on Friday. The rain is expected under a westerly wave affecting the country's western and upper regions. Light rain is expected at a few places in Karachi, Thatta, Qambar Shahdadkot, Jamshoro and Naushahro Feroze.

Most of Sindh will otherwise stay dry and cool during the period. Karachi's daytime temperature is expected to reach up to 34 degrees over the next three days. The department has advised residents to keep the forecast in view while planning travel on Friday.
