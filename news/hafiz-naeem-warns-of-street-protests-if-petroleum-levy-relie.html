Hafiz Naeem Warns of Street Protests if Petroleum Levy Relief Is Not Given

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman has said that matters with the government on the petroleum levy are in the final stage, and if the public is not given relief, decisions will be made on the streets. Speaking at a press conference at Idara Noor-e-Haq in Karachi, he said the levy is unacceptable in any form, and the government is ready to end the unjust tax gradually, though talks are still under way and no final decision has come from the government. He said the government is talking to oil refineries after repeated insistence by Jamaat-e-Islami, and that public relief on petroleum products followed those talks.

He asked the government to extend the current petrol relief to 1,300cc vehicles and to find a way to give relief on diesel used in agriculture and public transport. He said the government has sought a few days' time, which has been given, but there will be no further delay, and if decisions are not taken in the public interest, there will be protest. He also said there is political chaos in the country, the present central and provincial governments are not representative of the people, and Karachi has become the worst city in the world to live in.
