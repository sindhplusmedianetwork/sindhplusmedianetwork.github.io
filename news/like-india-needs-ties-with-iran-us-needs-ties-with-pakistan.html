Like India Needs Ties with Iran, US Needs Ties with Pakistan: Marco Rubio

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said in an interview with an Indian broadcaster in Mumbai that the United States needs to maintain relations with Pakistan, just as India needs ties with Iran. He said Pakistan's nuclear capability and geographical importance make engagement with Islamabad necessary. Rubio added that America's ties with Pakistan would not come at the expense of its partnership with India.

He denied that his remarks signaled any new US assistance or policy change toward Pakistan. The comments came during his visit to India, where he addressed the balance Washington seeks between its relationships with New Delhi and Islamabad.
