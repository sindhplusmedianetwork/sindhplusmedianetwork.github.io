Muzaffargarh: accused who killed 25-year-old woman walks free a month later, family alleges police bribe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The accused in the killing of 25-year-old Zoya in Muzaffargarh's Shah Jamal area has been released from custody just one month after the crime. According to Express News, the victim's husband Sabir Khokhar said a local landlord had shot Zoya dead at his dera (outhouse) a month ago after failing to sexually assault her. Despite the husband and the victim's family coming forward, police registered the case on the state's complaint, which the family says weakened the case.

The family alleges that poor police investigation and the police-complainant status of the FIR led to the release of the alleged killer within a month. They have also accused police of taking a heavy bribe. According to the family, the accused was welcomed back in the area with flower showers and a convoy of dozens of vehicles after his release, prompting them to protest.
