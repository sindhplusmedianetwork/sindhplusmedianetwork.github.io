Fire at US Nuclear Centre Laboratory Prompts Emergency Declaration

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An emergency was declared at a nuclear centre in the US state of South Carolina after a fire broke out inside a laboratory, officials said. One employee was slightly injured and taken to hospital. The fire was extinguished immediately, and the emergency alert was then withdrawn.

The US Department of Energy said a radiological survey found no evidence of nuclear material release or radiation. The incident briefly raised safety concerns at the sensitive facility before authorities confirmed the situation was under control.
