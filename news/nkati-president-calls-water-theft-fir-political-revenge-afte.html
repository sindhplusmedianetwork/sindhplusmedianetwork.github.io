NKATI President Calls Water-Theft FIR Political Revenge After Raising City Issues Before Karachi Mayor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

North Karachi Association of Trade and Industry president Faisal Moiz Khan has called the water-theft case against him an act of political revenge. He said the FIR was registered after he raised the city's and the industrial area's problems before the Karachi mayor. Khan said the case should have been heard at the Water Corporation's own police station.

He called the FIR fake and bogus. An FIR was registered at New Karachi Industrial Area police station on the complaint of a Water Corporation employee. The complaint alleges that illegal connections were laid in the corporation's lines under Khan's patronage.
