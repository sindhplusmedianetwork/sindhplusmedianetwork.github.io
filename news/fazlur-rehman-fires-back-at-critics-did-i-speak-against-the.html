Fazlur Rehman Fires Back at Critics: 'Did I Speak Against the Army, or Did Nawaz Sharif?'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, on reaching Garden Town in Lahore to condole with central party leader Bilal Mir on the death of his brother Zahid Mir, responded to his critics with a pointed question: 'Did I talk against the army, or did Mian Nawaz Sharif himself?' He said he was loyal to the state but disagreed with the use of coercion after such sacrifices, and called for a consensus national policy against terrorism, adding that the public must be taken into confidence to be included in the war on terror. Replying to criticism from Maryam Nawaz, Talal Chaudhry, Khawaja Asif and Azma Bokhari, he asked who had called the army the 'agriculture department' and who had kept naming former army chief General Bajwa in rallies. He said Maryam Nawaz was like his daughter but her past statements about the institution were also on record, and it would be better not to reopen those chapters of the past.

Fazlur Rehman claimed he had never criticised the military institution itself, though he did discuss state policies.
