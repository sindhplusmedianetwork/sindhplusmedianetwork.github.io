Alert for Pakistanis in UAE Over Social Media Use and Privacy Laws

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read

Pakistan's Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment in Islamabad has issued an important alert for Pakistanis living in or travelling to the United Arab Emirates about social media use and privacy protection, warning that violations can lead to imprisonment and fines running into lakhs of dirhams. Citizens have been directed to strictly follow local laws when sharing photos, videos, audio recordings and others' personal information. Under Article 44 of the UAE's Federal Decree-Law No.

34 of 2021, unlawful interference in a person's private or family life is a punishable offence, including photographing someone without permission, saving or transmitting electronic images, and recording private conversations. The alert notes these restrictions are not limited to public social media posts — forwarding someone's photo or video even in a private WhatsApp group can breach privacy laws in certain circumstances. According to the UAE Public Prosecution, violating Article 44 can bring a minimum of six months in jail and a fine of 150,000 to 500,000 dirhams, while doctoring images to defame someone carries at least one year in jail and fines of 250,000 to 500,000 dirhams.

The advisory also warns against photographing accident victims or the injured and sharing workplace secrets, with Article 45 setting up to one million dirhams in fines for disclosing confidential job information. Officials clarified that this is a reminder of existing laws — in force since January 2022 — not a new law, and that the rules apply to everyone in the UAE.
