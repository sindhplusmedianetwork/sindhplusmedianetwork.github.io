YouTube Viewership in Pakistan Records Surge; TikTok Reaches 100 Million Users

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's National Assembly Standing Committee on Information Technology and Telecommunications was told on Tuesday that TikTok has around 100 million users in Pakistan, with nearly 150 million videos watched and uploaded daily in the country. PTA Chairman Major General (retd) Hafeez ur Rehman shared the figures during a briefing, saying 96 million Pakistanis use YouTube and watch about 560 million videos a day, making Pakistan the world's leading country in YouTube viewership. He added that Pakistanis also make and receive millions of calls and messages on WhatsApp every day.

The meeting, chaired by MNA Syed Aminul Haque at Parliament House, also took up complaints of poor telecom services and repeated internet disruptions. Committee members questioned why connectivity had not improved despite years of discussion, with one member saying mobile networks barely worked in his constituency and along the route to Karachi. Federal Minister for IT Shaza Fatima Khawaja said data consumption had risen sharply with social media and artificial intelligence use, adding that 18 million people were using 5G services now being rolled out in 24 cities.

She said service disruptions had several causes, including theft of telecom infrastructure, noting that in some areas whole mobile towers, diesel and generators had been stolen.
