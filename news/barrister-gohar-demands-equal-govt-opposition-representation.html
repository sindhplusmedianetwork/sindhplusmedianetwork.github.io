Barrister Gohar Demands Equal Govt-Opposition Representation in Electoral Reforms Committee

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan has demanded equal representation for the government and the opposition in the electoral reforms committee. Speaking to reporters outside Parliament House, he rejected a proposed split of 15 government members against 5 opposition members. He said it would be better not to sit on the committee at all than to accept such an imbalance.

Barrister Gohar said electoral reforms must be neutral and must reflect the aspirations of the public. He added that internal discord harms the country more than external threats. He called on all sides to ensure the reform process is fair and transparent.

The committee is expected to take up key electoral changes in the coming weeks.
