Maryam Nawaz meets new Saudi ambassador, agrees over Rs500b export target

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz met the newly appointed Saudi ambassador, Dr Rajeh Albugamy, welcoming him and congratulating him on assuming his diplomatic responsibilities. She thanked the Saudi leadership for its economic and financial support to Pakistan, and the two sides described the Makkah defence agreement as the start of a new chapter in bilateral ties. The chief minister strongly condemned Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and expressed grief over the loss of lives.

Both sides also discussed increasing exports of livestock, beef, poultry, fruit and vegetables from Punjab, agreeing on a target of more than Rs500 billion. She noted that Saudi Arabia was Pakistan's largest source of remittances, contributing about $9.8 billion. The meeting reflected the two countries' resolve to deepen economic and defence cooperation.
