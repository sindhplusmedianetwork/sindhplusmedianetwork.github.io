Used coffee grounds can now be turned into fuel, scientists discover

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Researchers at Spain's Universitat Rovira i Virgili have found an effective way to extract oil from used coffee grounds. The oil can be used to produce renewable fuel and useful chemicals. The results were published in the scientific journal Biomass and Bioenergy.

Used coffee grounds contain about 15 percent lipids that can serve as raw material for biodiesel. The discovery offers a new way to turn a major global waste stream into an eco-friendly energy resource. Coffee grounds are produced in huge quantities around the world every day.

Turning this waste into fuel could reduce waste while providing a renewable source of energy.
