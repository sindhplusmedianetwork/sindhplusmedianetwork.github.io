Bangladesh prosecutor says ex-PM Hasina will be executed if she returns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Bangladeshi prosecutor who secured the death sentence against ousted leader Sheikh Hasina said on Tuesday she will be executed if she returns home. Hasina, whose 15-year rule was ended by a mass uprising she tried to crush, has vowed to return in December to Bangladesh, where she was sentenced to death for crimes against humanity over the 2024 crackdown on protesters. Prosecutor Md Aminul Islam told reporters that Sheikh Hasina has no option but to face the execution of the verdict, responding to reports she planned to return from self-imposed exile in India.

He added that she missed the deadline to appeal her conviction, leaving her with no further legal recourse, as the statute says an appeal must be filed within 30 days of the delivery of the judgement. Hasina, 79, fled to neighbouring India by helicopter in August 2024 as angry crowds stormed her palace, and she has remained there ever since, hidden from public view. Hasina has repeatedly said she intends to return to Bangladesh in December, telling AFP she is fully aware she faces being hanged if she returns and that fear cannot decide her duty to the people.

Her 2024 crackdown left up to 1,400 people dead, according to the United Nations. In November 2025, a court in Dhaka sentenced her to death in absentia after finding her guilty on three counts of crimes against humanity, including incitement, ordering killings and failing to prevent atrocities. Bangladesh's foreign ministry has sought her extradition from India, which says the request is under legal review, and her continued presence there has strained relations between the neighbouring countries.

She also faces a string of other cases, including corruption charges.
