Calling referendum a 'backdoor' is not democratic thinking, Abid Sher Ali tells Bilawal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PML-N leader Abid Sher Ali has said that calling a public referendum a 'backdoor' is not democratic thinking, arguing that the path to reforming and changing the system runs through the opinion of the people. In a post on X, he said parliament should hold a debate on the matter and the people should also have the constitutional right to express their opinion, because the real strength of the system is the trust and support of the public. He said PPP chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has governed Sindh for the past 18 years and has shared power at the federal level since 2022, so criticism of the system must be accompanied by a review of his own governance and performance.

Abid Sher Ali added that accountability is not meant for one party or one politician alone, and that every political party and every ruler must answer to the people. He said the goal is not a tug of war for power but the prosperity of 250 million people, the dignity of the common man and the transfer of powers to the grassroots level. He added that Pakistan has earned respect on the diplomatic and defence fronts, and now the governance system must also reach the doorstep of the people, because governance is not anyone's property but a trust of the people.
