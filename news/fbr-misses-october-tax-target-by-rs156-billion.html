FBR Misses October Tax Target by Rs156 Billion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue has missed its tax collection target for October 2026 by a wide margin. Against a target of Rs1,131 billion, the board has collected Rs975 billion so far, leaving a shortfall of Rs156 billion. FBR officials remain hopeful of narrowing the gap, expecting an additional Rs40 billion as designated tax offices and bank branches remain open today (Saturday) to receive payments.

Even so, the monthly shortfall would still stand at around Rs116 billion. The miss adds to the pressure on the tax machinery, which has faced repeated questions over its ability to meet ambitious revenue targets.
