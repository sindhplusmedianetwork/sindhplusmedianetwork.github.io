Big drop in global oil prices likely after Trump party's election defeat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global oil prices are likely to record their biggest fall in a week since 2020 after the defeat of US President Donald Trump's Republican Party in the midterm elections. The report says the election result has raised expectations of a sharp weekly decline in oil prices. Hopes of an end to the war with Iran and of reconciliation have grown following the outcome.

At the same time, attacks on ships in the Middle East are preventing prices from falling below a certain level. The expected fall would be the largest weekly drop in oil prices in more than five years.
