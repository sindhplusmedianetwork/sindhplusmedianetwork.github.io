Shadab Khan Fined for Dissent at Umpire's Decision in Abu Dhabi T10 League

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani all-rounder Shadab Khan has been fined 5 percent of his match fee for showing dissent at an umpire's decision in the Abu Dhabi T10 League. The incident took place in the 15th match between UAE Bulls and Arabian Aces at Sheikh Zayed Stadium. Shadab thought a bouncer should have been called wide and slammed his bat on the ground in frustration.

He was found guilty of breaching Article 2.8 of the Code of Conduct, a Level 1 offence. He admitted his mistake and accepted the sanction imposed by the match referee. Shadab scored 22 runs off 14 balls in the match, which UAE Bulls lost by 8 runs, leaving the defending champions fifth on the points table.
