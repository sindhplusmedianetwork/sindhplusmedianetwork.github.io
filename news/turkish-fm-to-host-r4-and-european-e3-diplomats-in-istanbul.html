Turkish FM to Host R4 and European E3 Diplomats in Istanbul on Friday

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan will host top diplomats of the R4 group and the European E3 in Istanbul on Friday, including in an unprecedented combined R4+E3 format convened at Türkiye's own initiative. The R4 comprises Türkiye, Egypt, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, while the E3 brings together the United Kingdom, Germany and France. Among the expected attendees are Pakistan's Ishaq Dar, Germany's Johann David Wadephul, Britain's Ed Miliband, France's Jean-Noel Barrot, Egypt's Badr Abdelatty and Saudi Arabia's Faisal bin Farhan.

The agenda covers the Palestinian question, the Strait of Hormuz, Yemen and the Russia-Ukraine war. Fidan is expected to stress regional ownership of security and to underline that the dispute between the United States and Iran can only be resolved through diplomacy.
