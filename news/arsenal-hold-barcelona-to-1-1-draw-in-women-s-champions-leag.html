Arsenal hold Barcelona to 1-1 draw in Women's Champions League

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Arsenal earned an impressive 1-1 draw at Women's Champions League holders Barcelona on Thursday in a league phase clash between two of the competition's heavyweights. Frida Maanum fired the visitors into a surprise lead midway through the first half at Camp Nou, pouncing on a rebound after Cata Coll saved from Caitlin Foord. Barcelona dominated possession and eventually found an equaliser through Caroline Graham Hansen in the 59th minute, set up by Esmee Brugts.

Neither side could find a winner in a tense conclusion, and the draw leaves Barcelona level on 10 points with leaders Lyon after four matches. Arsenal are fourth, level on eight points with third-place Bayern Munich and Chelsea, and have extended their unbeaten start in the competition to four matches. The match was a repeat of the 2025 final, which Arsenal won in Lisbon.
