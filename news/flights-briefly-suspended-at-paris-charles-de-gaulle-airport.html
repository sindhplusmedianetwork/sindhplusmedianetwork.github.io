Flights briefly suspended at Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport over suspected drone

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Flights were briefly suspended at Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport over a suspected drone. The suspension followed a possible drone sighting in the airport's airspace. Charles de Gaulle is a busy airport serving Paris.

Authorities said normal flights resumed shortly after security clearance was given. The clearance allowed operations to restart at the airport. Further investigation into the incident is under way.
