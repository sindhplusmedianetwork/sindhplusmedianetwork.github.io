Lahore Ring Road traffic fines raised sharply; driving without a licence now fined 5,000 rupees

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Traffic fines on Lahore's Ring Road have been increased under the Safe Roads and Safe Punjab vision, on the instructions of Punjab Ring Road Authority chairperson Safiullah Gondal. The fine for driving without a licence has been raised from 500 rupees to 5,000 rupees. Wrong overtaking will now cost 5,000 rupees for a car and 10,000 rupees for vehicles above 2,000cc, while using the wrong lane carries a 2,000-rupee fine and one-way entry a 5,000-rupee fine.

A smoke-emitting car will be fined 4,000 rupees and a commercial vehicle 5,000 rupees, overloading has been raised from 2,000 to 7,000 rupees, and wrong parking from 1,000 to 2,000 rupees. Underage driving will be fined 10,000 rupees, while black tinted windows and a missing or non-standard number plate each carry a 5,000-rupee fine, as does entering the Ring Road on a motorcycle. Crossing the road on foot will now cost 1,000 rupees instead of 200, bringing an animal onto the road will cost its owner 5,000 rupees, and using a mobile phone while driving or violating the seat-belt rule has been raised from 500 to 3,000 rupees.

Ring Road Police Lahore urged road users to follow traffic rules to keep their own lives and others' lives safe.
