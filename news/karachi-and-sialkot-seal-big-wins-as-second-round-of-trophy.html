Karachi and Sialkot Seal Big Wins as Second Round of Trophy Concludes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Blues and Sialkot Blues secured convincing victories as the second round of the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Gold concluded on Thursday, while the other two matches ended in draws. Karachi Blues defeated Multan by 275 runs at the Diamond Cricket Ground in Islamabad, declaring their second innings at 407 for 5, with Saifullah Bangash unbeaten on 111 and Rameez Aziz having earlier contributed 170. Multan, who had made 216 in their first innings, were bowled out for 130 in their second attempt, falling well short of the 406-run target, as Mohammad Raza led the Karachi bowling attack with four wickets for 42 runs and Abrar Ahmed claimed three for 14.

At the Shoaib Akhtar Cricket Stadium in Rawalpindi, Sialkot Blues overcame Faisalabad by five wickets after chasing down 187, with Mohammad Huraira guiding the chase with a fluent 105 and Abdul Rehman contributing 48 as the winners reached 187 for 5 in just 21.3 overs. At Abbottabad, Lahore Whites and Islamabad played out a draw, with Zain Bin Farooq scoring 103 for Lahore and Azeem Bari making 105 in Islamabad's first innings. The match between FATA and Peshawar Blues at the National Ground in Islamabad also ended in a draw, with Mohammad Sarwar Afridi narrowly missing a century on 97.

The third round of the tournament is scheduled to begin on Sunday, November 15.
