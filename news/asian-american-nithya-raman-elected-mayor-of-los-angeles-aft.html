Asian-American Nithya Raman elected mayor of Los Angeles after New York's Zohran Mamdani

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nithya Raman has defeated Karen Bass to win the Los Angeles mayoral seat, becoming another Asian-American mayor after New York's Zohran Mamdani. Raman's victory in Los Angeles follows Mamdani's win in New York, placing another Asian-American at the head of one of America's major cities. The win is being described as another major success for the Democratic Socialists of America.

US President Donald Trump reacted to the result, calling Raman's victory a very shameful event. The outcome adds to the string of notable results in the recent American elections, where candidates backed by the Democratic Socialists of America have made gains.
