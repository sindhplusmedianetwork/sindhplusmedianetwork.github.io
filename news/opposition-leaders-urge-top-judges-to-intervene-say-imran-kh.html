Opposition Leaders Urge Top Judges to Intervene, Say Imran Khan's Life Is in Danger

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Opposition leaders Mahmood Khan Achakzai and Allama Raja Nasir Abbas have written to the chief justices of the Supreme Court, the Federal Constitutional Court and the Islamabad High Court, urging immediate intervention to protect the life and health of jailed PTI founder Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi. The letter says the Supreme Court's August 18 order shifting Imran to Shifa International Hospital has been flouted for three months. It also cites UN human rights chief Volker Turk's call for his release.

Imran's doctor has warned that he must see a specialist within days to avoid irreversible heart damage. The appeal adds to the growing concern voiced by opposition parties over the former prime minister's health and his access to medical care in custody. The letter asks the top judges to act to safeguard his fundamental rights.
