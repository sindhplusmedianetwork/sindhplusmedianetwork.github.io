Tom Cruise's 'Digger' Ends Run as One of Hollywood's Biggest Box Office Failures

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tom Cruise's latest film 'Digger' has ended its theatrical run with approximately $50.3 million in worldwide ticket sales, making it one of Hollywood's biggest box office disappointments. Directed by Alejandro González Iñárritu, the satirical black comedy arrived in cinemas on October 2, 2026. It took only around $20 million worldwide in its opening weekend.

That figure stood against a reported production budget of $160-180 million. Cruise plays Digger Rockwell, an ageing oil tycoon whose drilling triggers an environmental catastrophe. He stars alongside Riz Ahmed, John Goodman, Sandra Hüller, Michael Stuhlbarg and Jesse Plemons.

The gap between its cost and its ticket sales has left the film counted among Hollywood's biggest box office failures.
