Election Commission decides to issue fresh schedule for Islamabad local government elections

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Election Commission has decided to issue a new schedule for local government elections in Islamabad. It directed its secretariat to prepare the election programme under the ICT Local Government Act 2015, the Elections Act 2017 and the rules. In a detailed order on the delay, the Commission noted the local government's term ended on 14 February 2021.

Delimitation was done six times and the election programme issued four times, with the last programme halted at the symbol-allotment stage in January 2026 after a federal ordinance. The Commission said the Supreme Court has held elections cannot be postponed indefinitely and that the expiry of the ordinance does not end its constitutional authority.
