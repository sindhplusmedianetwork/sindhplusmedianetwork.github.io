No member will be allowed to use the floor against state institutions, says National Assembly Speaker

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq said on Wednesday that every elected representative has the constitutional right to speak in parliament and present the concerns of the people, but no member will be allowed to use the floor of the house to speak against the state, the judiciary or the armed forces of Pakistan. He was speaking to reporters in Sahiwal after offering condolences to the family of the late former member of the National Assembly, Chaudhry Muhammad Ashraf, at his residence, where he met the deceased leader's son-in-law, Mian Ghaffarul Haq, and prayed for the departed soul. Referring to the functioning of parliament, Mr Sadiq said the National Assembly is committed to promoting democratic values and encouraging meaningful participation in the legislative process by both the treasury and the opposition benches.

He said the National Assembly is not a rubber stamp, but a vibrant democratic forum playing an effective role in strengthening democratic traditions, safeguarding parliamentary norms and ensuring representation for all political voices. He said the opposition is being given an ample opportunity to express its views in the house, adding that speaking time has been increased from eight to thirteen hours on important occasions to ensure broader participation and to uphold the principles of fairness and parliamentary justice. He said it would be unfortunate if opposition parliamentarians did not join parliamentary committees and contribute to these bodies, and added: "I want to be a non-controversial speaker." Commenting on recent political developments, Mr Sadiq dismissed claims regarding any attempts being made to oust the government, saying such narratives are often promoted by individuals and groups that had failed to secure public support through the electoral process.

He stressed that democratic institutions must be strengthened through constitutional and parliamentary means, and paid tribute to the late MNA, saying Mr Ashraf stood firmly with the PML-N leadership during challenging times.
