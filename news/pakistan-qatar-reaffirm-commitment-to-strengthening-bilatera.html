Pakistan, Qatar reaffirm commitment to strengthening bilateral relations in security, counter-terrorism: ISPR

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Qatar reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening bilateral relations in institutional cooperation, security and counter-terrorism, the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said. The reaffirmation came after Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir met Qatar's Interior Minister Sheikh Khalifa bin Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani in Rawalpindi. The two countries earlier signed a memorandum of understanding on security cooperation between Qatar's Lekhwiya and Pakistan's Federal Constabulary.

An action plan to prevent drug smuggling was also agreed. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif witnessed the signing. He appreciated Qatar's continued supply of liquefied natural gas to Pakistan.
