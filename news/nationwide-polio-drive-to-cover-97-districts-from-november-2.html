Nationwide Polio Drive to Cover 97 Districts From November 2

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan will launch a week-long polio vaccination campaign in 97 selected districts on November 2, the National Emergency Operations Centre announced on Monday. The campaign, running until November 8, aims to vaccinate more than 23.9 million children under the age of five. Over 9.278 million children are to be covered in 13 selected districts of Punjab, 6.883 million in 20 districts of Sindh, 4.808 million in 27 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and 2.217 million in 31 districts of Balochistan.

More than 447,000 children will be immunised in Islamabad, over 153,000 in three districts of Gilgit-Baltistan and over 183,000 in two districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir. Pakistan has reported six wild poliovirus type-1 cases so far this year, including four in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The NEOC described a polio-free future for children as a shared national responsibility and urged parents to fully cooperate with polio teams.

Pakistan and Afghanistan are the only two countries where polio remains endemic. Last month, Pakistan vaccinated 29.3 million children in a nationwide drive.
