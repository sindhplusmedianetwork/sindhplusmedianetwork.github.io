NAB Opens Inquiry Into Alleged Irregularities in KP's Sehat Card Programme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Accountability Bureau has taken notice of alleged financial irregularities in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's Sehat Card Plus programme. Former Sehat Insaf Card chief executive officer Dr Riaz Tanoli has been summoned to appear at the NAB Peshawar office on October 29. The bureau has sought records of the release and expenditure of Rs12 billion during the 2023-24 financial year.

The inquiry also covers Rs9 billion in funds allocated between July 2025 and July 2026. Investigators have raised questions regarding unverified expenditures and claims, including alleged irregularities in Sehat Card data such as the issuance of duplicate cards. NAB is further investigating the verification of high-value claims submitted by hospitals under the programme.

As part of the investigation, the bureau has requested bank statements, audit reports, vendor agreements, beneficiary lists and asset declarations. The inquiry is focused on examining financial records, expenditure claims and administrative processes to assess the alleged irregularities.
