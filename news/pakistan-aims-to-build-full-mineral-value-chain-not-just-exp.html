Pakistan aims to build full mineral value chain, not just export raw minerals, says Ishaq Dar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar has said Pakistan aims to develop its full mineral value chain, not just extract and export raw minerals. Addressing the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 in Islamabad, he welcomed the delegates and said the holding of the forum was welcome for the development of the mineral sector and reflected foreign investors' interest in Pakistan. Dar said the confidence shown by global investors in a commitment to long-term partnership in the mineral sector was commendable, and that the Foreign Ministry and diplomatic missions had played an important role in presenting Pakistan's mineral potential.

He said Pakistan wants an active role in the world's clean energy, digital technology and industrial production sectors, and that the country stands at an important stage of its economic development, with significant progress towards economic stability through financial discipline, reforms and careful financial management. He added that Pakistan wants to gradually increase its capacity for mineral processing, refining and manufacturing products, and that investment is needed in mining, technology, processing and energy in the mineral sector. Dar said mineral development would create opportunities in engineering, automation, services and product manufacturing, and that Balochistan is Pakistan's mineral future, as demand for copper and other minerals has risen with clean energy and electric vehicles.
