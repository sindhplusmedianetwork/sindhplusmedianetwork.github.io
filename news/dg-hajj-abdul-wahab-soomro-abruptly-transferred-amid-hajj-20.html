DG Hajj Abdul Wahab Soomro Abruptly Transferred Amid Hajj 2027 Operations

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Grade-21 DMG officer Abdul Wahab Soomro, the Director General Hajj Makkah, has been abruptly transferred. He was ordered to immediately hand over charge and report to the Establishment Division. The notification came while he was on a one-week visit to Pakistan.

The move stirred the religious affairs ministry, since Hajj 2027 operations have already begun, with hiring underway in Saudi Arabia and an international Hajj conference due on November 8. His transfer is being linked to his failure to appear three times before a parliamentary standing committee. His request to join the committee via Zoom from Saudi Arabia was rejected, with a demand for his immediate transfer instead.
