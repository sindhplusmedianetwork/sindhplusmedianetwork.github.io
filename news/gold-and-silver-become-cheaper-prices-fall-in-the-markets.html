Gold and silver become cheaper; prices fall in the markets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold and silver prices continued to fall in local and international markets on Saturday. In the international bullion market, gold fell by 7 dollars an ounce to 3,884 dollars. The fall pushed the local price down by 700 rupees per tola to 410,936 rupees.

Silver also became cheaper, falling by 1 dollar 24 cents an ounce internationally to 48 dollars 65 cents. In the local market, silver fell by 124 rupees per tola to 5,343 rupees. Traders said the decline in the international market was reflected in local prices.

The fall comes after a period in which gold and silver prices had been moving lower in both markets.
