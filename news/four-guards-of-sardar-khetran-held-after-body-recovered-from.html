Four Guards of Sardar Khetran Held After Body Recovered from Barkhan Property

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police said they have detained four guards of former Balochistan minister Sardar Abdul Rehman Khetran following the reported recovery of a body from his property in Barkhan district. A separate case has also been registered against the MPA and his two sons over the alleged murder, kidnapping and illegal confinement of one of his former guards in a private jail. Koh-Suleman DIG Ayaz Baloch said the four security guards were taken into custody during a search operation at Khetran's fortified residence in Haji Kot on Nov 20, and weapons were also recovered from their possession.

Two decomposed bodies were recovered from a graveyard in the Tumnai area, around 40 kilometres from Barkhan, on Nov 19, and a third body was recovered during excavation at Khetran's residence the following day. Police have shifted the three bodies to Quetta for DNA testing, while Khetran has rejected the allegations, describing the police action as politically motivated. On Nov 18, Barkhan police registered a case against Khetran, his two sons Islam Shah and Abdul Rauf, and three other accomplices on charges of murder, kidnapping and illegal confinement, on the complaint of Pyara Khan Bugti, father of former guards Sahib Din Bugti and Qamar Din.
