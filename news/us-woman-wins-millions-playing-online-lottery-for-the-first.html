US woman wins millions playing online lottery for the first time

By Sindh Plus Media Network

A woman in the US state of North Carolina won a jackpot of $1,939,727 after playing an online lottery game for the first time in her life, Express News reported, citing foreign media. Donna Gray, a resident of Mount Pleasant, tried her luck in a digital instant lottery game for the first time on August 1 and won nearly $1.94 million in prize money on her very first one-dollar attempt. Gray told officials of the North Carolina Education Lottery that she had been buying traditional scratch cards for many years but this was her first experience of playing the lottery online.

She said she chose the Power Max Cash game and the jackpot was hers on the first one-dollar turn. According to Donna Gray, the prize amount kept growing and at first she thought she might have won only $100. The woman said she immediately went to her husband to share the good news and told him she had won a big prize.

She added that it took both her and her husband nearly two days to believe the big win.
