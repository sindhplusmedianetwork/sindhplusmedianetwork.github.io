South Africa's Series Win Over Australia Boosts WTC Final Hopes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

South Africa's 2-1 home series victory over Australia has boosted their hopes of reaching a second straight World Test Championship final. The Proteas now sit at the top of the world rankings with a points percentage of 71.43, narrowly behind New Zealand in the WTC table. South Africa need five wins from their seven remaining Tests — against Bangladesh, England and Sri Lanka — to qualify for the June final at The Oval.

Australia, meanwhile, dropped down the standings after the series defeat. The win underlines South Africa's strong form in the current championship cycle.
