SECP cuts cost of digital nano loans via mobile apps

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) has cut the maximum annual cost of digital nano loans via mobile apps. The cost was reduced from 274% to 90% for new users. For users who repay their first loan on time, the maximum annual cost was cut to 70%.

Under the new circular, a user can borrow up to Rs50,000. The borrowing period can be up to 90 days. There will be no late penalty for the first three days after the due date.
