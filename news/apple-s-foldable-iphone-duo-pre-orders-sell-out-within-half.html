Apple's Foldable 'iPhone Duo' Pre-Orders Sell Out Within Half an Hour

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apple's first foldable iPhone has seen its pre-order bookings close within half an hour of opening, in a sign of strong early demand. According to Express News, Apple introduced the device — dubbed the 'iPhone Duo' — at an event last month, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro. The company opened pre-order bookings to eager customers, but the initial allocation was claimed within 30 minutes.

Buyers placing new orders now face a long wait: deliveries for fresh orders are not expected before January 22. The rapid sellout marks a notable debut for Apple's entry into the foldable phone segment, where rival manufacturers have been competing for years. Analysts will be watching closely to see whether Apple can maintain momentum once wider availability begins.
