Performing Arts Take Centre Stage at World Culture Festival

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Performing arts took centre stage on the fourth day of the 3rd World Culture Festival Karachi 2026 at the Arts Council of Pakistan. The day's programme included the 'VIOLA & CELLO' music masterclass, a screening of the Pakistani film Wakhri, and Uruguayan theatre group Grieta Teatro's play 'Chicha'. The international 'Crossfade' music programme and the 'Beats Galore' dance showcase drew performers from the USA, the UK, Ecuador, Brazil, Singapore and Croatia.

The 25-day festival brings together more than 1,000 international artists from over 150 countries alongside 500 Pakistani artists. It runs until November 22 and offers free public registration. The festival has turned Karachi into a hub of global culture, with theatre, music, dance and film filling the Arts Council's halls daily.

Organisers say the event aims to build bridges between Pakistani artists and performers from around the world.
