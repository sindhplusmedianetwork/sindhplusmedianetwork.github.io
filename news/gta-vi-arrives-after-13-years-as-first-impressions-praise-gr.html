GTA VI Arrives After 13 Years as First Impressions Praise Graphics, World and Driving

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally out after 13 long years, billed as Rockstar Games' magnum opus, with hype that has raised it from being just another game to a full-on cultural event, and it is priced higher than other AAA blockbusters. In first impressions the reviewer says the game is delivering and more, looks absolutely beautiful with superb lighting and texture work, and feels mechanically sound with satisfying driving mechanics, while the world of Leonida, including Vice City and the starting island of Key Lento, has its own character and personality. The reviewer says GTA VI is graphically one of the best games ever seen, at times photorealistic, with realistic skin textures and hair that moves naturally, and a lighting system that illuminates the environment and characters so well that it looks like natural light.

The map is described as bigger than GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 put together, with high non-player character and traffic density, and the game runs at 30 frames per second, though the reviewer notes the framerate noticeably dropped when moving through vegetation and went below 20 FPS in one instance while backing up through small trees during a car chase. Driving is described as heavier and more planted than the arcade-style system of GTA V, requiring delicate control to avoid wheelspin, while the revamped police system is compared to Red Dead Redemption 2, marking the crime area red on the map while police search for someone matching the player's description. The reviewer says the game has met most expectations so far, is technically stable with no bugs or glitches encountered, and is worth the money, while noting it is still very early days and a full review will follow.
