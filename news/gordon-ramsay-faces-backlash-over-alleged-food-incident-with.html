Gordon Ramsay Faces Backlash Over Alleged Food Incident With Waiter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gordon Ramsay has faced criticism following a report alleging that he pushed a canape into a waiter's mouth during a reception at a high profile London awards ceremony. The incident was reported by the Daily Mail and is said to have taken place on November 9 at the Walpole British Luxury Awards, held at The Dorchester in Mayfair, where Ramsay attended the event and received the Visionary Award for his contribution to British hospitality. The chef was offered a cone-shaped canape by a waiter carrying a serving tray, and Ramsay allegedly took the snack and pushed it into the employee's mouth instead of eating it himself, while the waiter was reportedly holding the tray with both hands.

A guest quoted in the report described the alleged behaviour as appalling and very disrespectful, saying they were shocked by the interaction. The waiter, who was not identified, also spoke to the newspaper about the encounter, saying he felt embarrassed because staff were not supposed to eat while serving guests, and he questioned why Ramsay had put him in that position. The report further claimed that Ramsay gave the waiter a double thumbs up before walking away, although these details have not been independently verified and the account is based on statements from people cited by the newspaper.

When contacted, Ramsay's spokesman did not directly address the allegation, and instead criticised the newspaper for focusing on the incident during an event celebrating British luxury brands and talent.
