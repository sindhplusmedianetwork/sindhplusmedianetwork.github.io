LHC acquits Quran desecration convict, cites lack of proof and mental illness

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Justice Tariq Mehmood Bajwa of the Lahore High Court's Rawalpindi bench has acquitted Shahidur Rehman, setting aside his life sentence under Section 295-B of the Pakistan Penal Code. The court ruled that a burnt copy of the Quran alone could not prove a wilful and deliberate act, noting that prosecution witnesses admitted they had not seen him set fire to it. The court gave weight to his 13 to 14 years of mental illness, bringing Section 84 of the PPC into play.

It also noted investigation gaps, including the absence of any scientific examination of the burnt pieces and the fact that the alleged matchbox was never taken into possession.
