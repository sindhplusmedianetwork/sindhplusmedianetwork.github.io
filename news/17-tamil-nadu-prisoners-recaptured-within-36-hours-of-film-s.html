17 Tamil Nadu Prisoners Recaptured Within 36 Hours of Film-Style Jailbreak

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Seventeen prisoners who staged a film-style jailbreak in Tamil Nadu's Theni district have been recaptured within 36 hours. The escape began when one inmate faked a seizure, overpowered the guard and seized the cell keys; one of the escapees returned on his own shortly after. Police tracked the fugitives through bus-stand raids, rickshaw chases and drone surveillance, while two of the prisoners surrendered.

Officials said the jail was holding 334 inmates against a sanctioned capacity of 200, with only four guards on duty at the time. Nine prison staff members have been suspended over the incident.
