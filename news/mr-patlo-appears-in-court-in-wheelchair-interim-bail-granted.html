Mr Patlo appears in court in wheelchair, interim bail granted till November 10

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

TikToker Ansar Mahmood, known as Mr Patlo, appeared before a local court in Daska in a wheelchair. Additional Sessions Judge Muhammad Irfan Naseem Tarar heard the case and granted him interim bail until November 10. The court appearance follows a stampede at Mr Patlo's mehndi ceremony in Sialkot, in which police said two people were killed.

The incident had drawn wide attention after reports of deaths and injuries at the event. The court will take up the matter again after the interim bail period ends.
