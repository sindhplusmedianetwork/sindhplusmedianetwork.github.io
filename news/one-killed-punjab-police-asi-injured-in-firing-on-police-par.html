One Killed, Punjab Police ASI Injured in Firing on Police Party in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Punjab Police team that had come to Karachi to arrest an accused was fired upon in the Site Super Highway area, in which one person was killed and an assistant sub-inspector was injured. According to reports, firing was carried out by accused persons during a raid on the police party that had come from Multan at Khadim Solangi Goth, in which one person was killed and another was injured. After the incident, rescue volunteers reached the spot and shifted the body and the injured to hospital.

Officials said Punjab Police ASI Asghar was seriously injured in the firing, while the Multan police had arrived to arrest an accused. Rescue officials said the deceased was identified as 35-year-old Hamza and the injured as 35-year-old Asghar, who is an ASI of Punjab Police. Police officials said ASI Asghar was shot in the leg and his condition is out of danger, and medical aid is being provided to the injured officer.
