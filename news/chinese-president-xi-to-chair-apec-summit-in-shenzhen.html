Chinese President Xi to Chair APEC Summit in Shenzhen

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese President Xi Jinping will chair and address the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, China's foreign ministry has said. The meeting will be held in the Chinese city of Shenzhen on 18 and 19 November, under the theme of building an Asia-Pacific community for shared prosperity. The announcement was made by a foreign ministry spokesperson, citing Xinhua.

The APEC CEO Summit will also be held in Shenzhen on 17 and 18 November, under the theme of moving together towards prosperity and a bright future. President Xi will attend the opening ceremony of the CEO Summit and deliver a keynote address. He will also host a welcome dinner for the world leaders and representatives attending the meeting, and will hold bilateral meetings with various heads of state on the sidelines of the summit.
