Maryam Nawaz Attends Azma Bukhari's Daughter's Wedding; Photos Go Viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bukhari's daughter Wadiat Fatima married Barrister Syed Musa Raza at a private ceremony in Lahore on Sunday. Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz attended the nikah ceremony, congratulated the newlywed couple and was introduced to the groom by the bride's mother. Health Minister Khawaja Salman Rafiq, Deputy Speaker Zaheer Iqbal Chanar, Salma Butt and Shazia Rizwan were among the guests present at the event.

Pictures from the wedding, shared by Bukhari on Instagram, have gone viral on social media. The ceremony remained a private family affair attended by close relatives and senior government figures.
