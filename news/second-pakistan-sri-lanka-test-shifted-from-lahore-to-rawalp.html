Second Pakistan-Sri Lanka Test shifted from Lahore to Rawalpindi over smog

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has confirmed that the second Test between Pakistan and Sri Lanka will move from Lahore to Rawalpindi. The match, scheduled for November 17 to 21, was to be played at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium but will now be held at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. The decision was taken because of rising smog in Lahore, which has raised concern for the board.

The first Test of the series is already under way in Rawalpindi, where the intensity of smog is much lower than in Lahore. PCB officials had discussed shifting the match as the weather situation in Lahore worsened. The board confirmed the change late on Tuesday after the proposal was discussed.
