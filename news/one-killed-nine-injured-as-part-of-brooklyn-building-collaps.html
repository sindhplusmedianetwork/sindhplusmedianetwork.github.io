One Killed, Nine Injured as Part of Brooklyn Building Collapses

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Part of a three-storey building collapsed in the Flatbush area of Brooklyn, New York, killing one person and injuring at least nine. One person remained trapped under the rubble as rescue work continued. The fire department said the building at 1321 Flatbush Avenue suddenly collapsed onto an adjacent construction site in the afternoon.

At least two of the injured were in critical condition. Emergency teams worked at the scene to search for anyone still trapped and to clear the debris. Building inspectors arrived to check the structure of nearby buildings for safety.
