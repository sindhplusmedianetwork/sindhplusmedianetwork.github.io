Salik toll fees change in Dubai on Sunday: AED 6 from 6 to 10 a.m.

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dubai will charge different Salik toll fees on Sunday, November 15, during the Dubai T100 Triathlon. Each toll-gate crossing will cost AED 6 between 6 a.m. and 10 a.m., instead of the usual Sunday rate of AED 4.

At other chargeable times on the day the fee will be AED 4. Travel will be free from 1 a.m. to 6 a.m.

Salik announced the special one-day schedule on its official social media account. Authorities stressed that it is not a permanent increase, and with 5% VAT an AED 6 crossing totals AED 6.30.
