Kandara T20 Cricket League opens at Sindh Agriculture University

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first Kandara Cricket Club and Academy T20 Cricket League 2026 commenced at the cricket ground of Sindh Agriculture University, Tandojam. Two Pool A matches were played on the opening day of the league. Vice Chancellor Altaf Ali Siyal said sports play a vital role in the physical and mental development of young people.

He said sports also promote discipline, teamwork, tolerance and leadership among the youth. The league has begun with matches between the participating teams. The opening day featured two matches from Pool A at the university cricket ground.
