Australian MP Helen Haines Critical After Car Attack Outside Electorate Office

Independent Australian MP Helen Haines, 65, is fighting for her life after being struck by a four-wheel drive outside her electorate office in Wangaratta, Victoria. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese described the incident, which occurred around 3:30pm, as a deliberate attack. A male suspect was arrested at the scene.

Witnesses said a bystander confiscated a knife from the driver before police arrived. The Joint Counter-Terrorism Team, comprising the Australian Federal Police, Victoria Police and ASIO, has been activated to investigate the attack. The incident has shocked Australia's political community.

Further details about the suspect's motives have not yet been released.
