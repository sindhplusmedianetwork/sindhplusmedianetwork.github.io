Sui Gas Companies Seek Big Hike in Prices, File Petitions with OGRA

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sui Northern and Sui Southern gas companies have filed petitions with the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) seeking an increase in average gas prices. The companies have asked for the hike to apply from July 1 of the current financial year. Sui Northern proposed a rise of Rs10 per MMBTU, taking the price to Rs1,712.40, including Rs60.14 billion in LNG service costs.

Sui Southern sought a much larger increase of Rs366.75 per MMBTU, which would take its price to Rs2,071.75. OGRA will decide on both petitions after holding hearings. The regulator's decision will determine whether consumers face higher gas prices from the start of the current financial year.
