Sindh Orders Annual Inspection of Electrical Systems in Private Buildings, Industry Objects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has decided to conduct annual inspections of electrical installations in all private-sector buildings and has issued notices to that effect. According to Express News, the provincial Energy Department suspended a notification from 2003 and issued notices for the annual inspection of electrical installations in private-sector buildings. However, the Federal B Area Association of Trade and Industry described the annual inspection of industrial units as a new burden on the industrial sector.

The association demanded that the notices sent by the Electric Inspectorate be withdrawn, saying government interference in the sector is not acceptable in any form. The dispute highlights the tension between the government's safety enforcement drive and industry's concerns about added costs and inspections.
