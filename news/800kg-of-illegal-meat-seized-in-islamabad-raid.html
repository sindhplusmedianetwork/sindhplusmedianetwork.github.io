800kg of illegal meat seized in Islamabad raid

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad Food Authority seized about 800 kilogrammes of suspected illegal meat during a raid on a property in Sector D-14 of the capital on Monday night. Officials also recovered several animals from the property, including donkeys, dogs, turtles and a pangolin. They recovered 12 live donkeys, five dogs and 17 turtles, along with the carcasses of a donkey, a jackal and a pangolin.

The meat was destroyed at the site. The live animals were handed over to wildlife authorities. A case was registered at Golra Police Station under the Islamabad Food Safety Act.
