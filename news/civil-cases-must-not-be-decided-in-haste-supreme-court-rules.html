Civil cases must not be decided in haste, Supreme Court rules

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Supreme Court has issued a written judgment in a case concerning the restoration of a civil case, ruling that courts should not decide cases in haste merely to dispose of them quickly. The judgment, authored by Justice Muhammad Ali Mazhar, states that the scales of justice neither automatically favour dismissing a case nor automatically favour restoring it. The court noted with surprise that the civil case had remained pending since 2016, yet there was no earlier record to place the responsibility on the petitioner or his lawyer.

It said that when a lawyer does not appear before the court, the court may dismiss a case for non-prosecution at its discretion, but if a lawyer shows no sense of professional responsibility, the client ends up bearing the loss. The judgment stressed that clients must stay alert and follow their cases regularly instead of leaving all responsibility to their lawyers, and that lawyers must keep an accurate record of hearing dates, cause lists and the relevant benches while informing their clients about progress, possible risks and any settlement offers. It also held that a case dismissed for non-prosecution can be restored if a reasonable cause exists and justice demands it, and it restored the civil revision applications filed in the Lahore High Court subject to a fine of ten thousand rupees in each case, to be deposited in the account of the Lahore High Court Bar Association's medical centre with the receipts presented before the Registrar of the Lahore High Court.
