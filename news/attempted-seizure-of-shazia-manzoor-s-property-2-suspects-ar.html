Attempted seizure of Shazia Manzoor's property, 2 suspects arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police have arrested two suspects on charges of allegedly attempting to seize the property of Pakistan's famous singer Shazia Manzoor and issuing threats. According to the spokesperson of DIG Operations, Lahore's Millat Park Police immediately registered a case on singer Shazia Manzoor's application and took action, arresting the nominated suspects Sohail and Beenish. According to police, the suspects are accused of forcibly getting the singer to sign property papers and going to the disputed property and threatening the tenants.

A case has been registered against the suspects under sections for issuing threats and harassing women. Police have also obtained CCTV footage of the incident, while investigation is ongoing for the arrest of other suspects. DIG Operations Faisal Kamran says strict action will be taken against the land-grabbing mafia and those harassing citizens in Lahore.
