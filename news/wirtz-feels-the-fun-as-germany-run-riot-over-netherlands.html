Wirtz feels the fun as Germany run riot over Netherlands

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Florian Wirtz scored twice as Germany cruised to a 5-0 win over the Netherlands in Berlin on Monday to reach the Nations League quarter-finals, their biggest win over their neighbours since 1959. Brajan Gruda added a goal and two assists, while Derry Scherhant and Nico Schlotterbeck also scored. It was the most impressive match of Jurgen Klopp's six-game stint as coach.

Despite the defeat, the Netherlands also qualified for the final eight, although the last German goal dropped them to second in the group. The result underlines Germany's strong form under Klopp as they head into the knockout stage of the competition.
