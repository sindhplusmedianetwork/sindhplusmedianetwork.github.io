If governor's rule is imposed in Karachi, Bilawal's face will really be worth watching, says Taimur Jhagra

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Taimur Saleem Jhagra has said that if governor's rule is imposed in Karachi tomorrow, Bilawal Bhutto Zardari's face will really be worth watching. Speaking on the programme "Sawal Yeh Hai", he argued that Bilawal himself gave political legitimacy to the practice by installing his own governor for governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He asked with what moral right Bilawal would protest if the same measure were used in Karachi.

Jhagra also criticised calls for election reforms, saying the problem is not the absence of laws but their violation by the powerful. He said the law promises parties their electoral symbol, freedom of public gatherings, transparent counting of votes and results, and tribunal decisions within 180 days, yet none of these were delivered. He added that the powerful turn the law into a piece of paper whenever they wish, and that existing laws should be implemented first.
