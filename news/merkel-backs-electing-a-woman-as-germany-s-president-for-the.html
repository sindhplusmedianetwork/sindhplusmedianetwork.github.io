Merkel Backs Electing a Woman as Germany's President for the First Time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former German chancellor Angela Merkel has backed the idea of electing a woman as Germany's president for the first time in the country's history. Speaking in a Magenta TV interview, Merkel noted that no woman has ever held the office in the 77-year history of the Federal Republic. She said it was worth asking whether the time had come for a woman to take the post, though she declined to name any candidate.

The next president will be elected by the Federal Convention on January 30, with incumbent Frank-Walter Steinmeier stepping down in March after two terms. Names circulating in conservative circles include Ilse Aigner, Julia Klöckner, Karin Prien and Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer, all from the CDU/CSU. The debate adds momentum to wider discussion about representation at the top of German public life.
