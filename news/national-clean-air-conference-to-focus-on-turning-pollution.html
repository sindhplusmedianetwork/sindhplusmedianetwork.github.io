National Clean Air Conference to Focus on Turning Pollution Policy Into Action

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal and provincial representatives, development partners and technical experts will gather in Islamabad on October 28 and 29 for the two-day Pakistan National Clean Air Conference 2026, with the aim of turning clean-air policies into practical measures. The opening day will treat clean air as a national development priority, linking air quality with health, productivity, climate and economic competitiveness. The environment and climate change minister is scheduled to deliver the keynote address, setting out the national air quality situation, major emission sources, pollution exposure hotspots and airshed-related challenges.

A session led by UNICEF will examine the effects of air pollution on people, children, productivity and vulnerable populations. A separate session involving the Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency and the Asian Development Bank will look at moving the National Clean Air Policy from formulation to implementation. Punjab's experience will feature prominently, with the province presenting its implementation model under the Punjab Clean Air Programme.

The second day will focus on sector-specific action covering transport, agriculture and waste, industry and brick kilns, clean cooking, and governance, before concluding with deliberations on clean-air financing, carbon markets and a closing compact intended to turn the discussions into commitments.
