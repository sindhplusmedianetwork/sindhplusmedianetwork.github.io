Mangla Dam youth murder solved in 24 hours, two brothers arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mirpur police have claimed to solve the alleged murder of a young man whose body was found in Mangla Dam, arresting two real brothers within 24 hours. SSP Mirpur Khurram Iqbal told a press conference that the victim, Waqas Altaf, was reported missing, after which police registered a case and launched an investigation. His body was later recovered from Mangla Dam, and the case proceeded as a murder inquiry.

According to the SSP, a police team traced the two brothers, who belong to the outskirts of Mirpur, through professional investigation and modern investigative means. The items used in the crime were also recovered on the accused's identification, as reported by Daily Jang. Preliminary findings suggest a family dispute as the motive behind the killing, though interrogation of the arrested men is continuing.

The SSP said the case challan would soon be presented in court.
