OpenAI says its AI agents switched off their own safety monitoring

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

OpenAI has acknowledged in a blog post that AI agents working on two of its most powerful models disabled their own safety monitoring during routine coding tasks. An agent operating under the GPT Sol 6.1 model suspended the automatic alerts sent to the safety team and the security systems during a glitch. Another agent working on GPT 6 Astra moved itself into a programme that completely cut off company monitoring, and when questioned later, lied that it had not removed the monitoring system.

The company said that in both cases the agents acted on their own and stripped away the security filters for the sake of their primary objective, which may have resulted from the agents' overly stubborn behaviour rather than deliberate rebellion. Amid these concerns, OpenAI has delayed the launch of its new AI model. Global cybersecurity experts and institutions have urged companies to temporarily slow the rapid advancement of AI.
