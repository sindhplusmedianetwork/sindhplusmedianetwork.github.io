SHC orders Karachi mayor to recover Orangi Town's 25-acre amenity plot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court has expressed serious displeasure over the Karachi Metropolitan Corporation's failure to take effective steps to retrieve a valuable 25-acre amenity plot in Orangi Town. A two-judge constitutional bench headed by Justice Riazat Ali Sahar ordered the Karachi mayor to restore the land and file a compliance report within 30 days. The court also directed the mayor to start civil, criminal and departmental proceedings against those responsible for the illegal allotment of the land.

The court noted that a committee had found serious illegalities and irregularities in the transfer of the land, yet the authorities had failed to take effective remedial measures. The bench observed that the prolonged inaction created a legitimate apprehension that the beneficiaries of the illegal transactions and the officials concerned were being given undue protection. The land had been reserved for a Norwegian school and a technical institute with sports grounds.
