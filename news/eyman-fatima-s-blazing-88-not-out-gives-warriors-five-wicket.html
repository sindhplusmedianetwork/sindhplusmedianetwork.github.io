Eyman Fatima's blazing 88 not out gives Warriors five-wicket win

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Eyman Fatima struck a blazing 88 not out as Warriors overhauled the 165-run target on the penultimate ball of the match against Champions in the second match of the PCB Women's Super-3 T20 Tournament at the Iqbal Stadium, Faisalabad on Thursday. Champions, who began the tournament with a win against Incredibles on Wednesday, posted a total of 164-4 in 20 overs on the back of Shawaal Zulfiqar's 40 and unbeaten thirties from Najiha Alvi and Saira Jabeen. Tuba Hassan-led Champions failed to defend the total as Eyman took the chase by storm after walking in to bat at 48-3, while none of the batters from her team crossed the 20-run mark.

In the chase, Warriors' top four batters stuttered, with Sadaf Shamas (20) and Ravail Farhan (16) both getting run out, Muneeba Ali scoring 11 and Gull Rukh departing for just four as Warriors slipped to 58-4. Eyman continued to pace the chase with timely boundaries, striking five fours and eight sixes, sharing a 35-run stand with Rameen Shamim (13) and a match-winning unbeaten 75-run stand with Hafsa Khalid (10 not out). Warriors needed 72 off 28 balls before Eyman launched her charge, completing her fifty off 30 balls and hammering a 24-run 19th over off Tania Saeed.

Warriors needed 10 off the last over and five off the last three balls when Eyman finished the game with consecutive fours. For Warriors, Rameen, Hafsa, Shahar Bano and Aleesa Muktiar picked up one wicket each. Warriors will now face Incredibles on Friday at the same venue with the first ball expected to be bowled at 10am PKT.
