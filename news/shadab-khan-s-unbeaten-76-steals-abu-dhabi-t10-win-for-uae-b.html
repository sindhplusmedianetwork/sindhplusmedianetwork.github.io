Shadab Khan's Unbeaten 76 Steals Abu Dhabi T10 Win for UAE Bulls

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan all-rounder Shadab Khan produced a blazing unbeaten 76 off 21 balls to turn certain defeat into victory for UAE Bulls in the Abu Dhabi T10 League. Chasing against Royal Desert Champions, the Bulls needed 45 runs from the last two overs and won with a ball to spare. Shadab reached his fifty in just 16 balls, the second-fastest T10 fifty by a Pakistani batter after Shahid Afridi's 14-ball fifty in 2018.

His innings included nine sixes and four fours. The knock proved decisive as the Bulls completed a remarkable chase in the closing overs.
