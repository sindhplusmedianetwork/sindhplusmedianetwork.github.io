Shehbaz Pledges Full Resources for Daanish Schools; 4,000 Free AI Subscriptions Gifted

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday pledged that the government would provide all possible resources to Daanish schools. He was speaking at an MoU signing ceremony between the Education Ministry and Gauth AI, under which the company gifted 4,000 free subscriptions — including 1,000 for Daanish school teachers. IT Minister Shaza Fatima Khawaja said TikTok had made its AI technology available for Pakistan, and added that paid versions of Gauth AI were being launched in Pakistan, the first country after the United States.

On the occasion, the prime minister also announced a national-level plant to produce livestock vaccines from indigenous resources. He further ordered the restructuring of the Livestock and Dairy Development Board.
