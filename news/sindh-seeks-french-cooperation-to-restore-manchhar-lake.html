Sindh Seeks French Cooperation to Restore Manchhar Lake

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah on Saturday sought French cooperation and technology to restore Manchhar Lake and eliminate its pollution during a meeting with French Ambassador Jean-Noël Poirier at the CM House. He said freshwater inflows into the lake had declined over the past 25 to 30 years due to upstream diversions, dams and low rainfall. As a result, the lake, once Asia's largest freshwater lake, became dependent on polluted water from the Main Nara Valley Drain and the Right Bank Outfall Drain carrying agricultural, industrial and municipal effluent.

The contamination led to the collapse of fisheries and the spread of waterborne diseases. The ambassador assured that French companies were ready to invest in the development and environmental sectors, and both sides agreed to hold a joint meeting with French companies. The chief minister also noted that Karachi generates 15,000 tonnes of solid waste daily and the government is considering generating electricity from it.

He said 35 million gallons of wastewater are currently treated daily in Karachi, with a plan to enhance capacity to 200 MGD so treated water can be made fit for industrial use.
