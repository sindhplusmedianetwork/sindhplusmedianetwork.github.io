Former Olympian Zahid Sharif, Left Homeless and Jobless, Appeals for Help

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former hockey Olympian Zahid Sharif has appealed to Pakistan's top leadership for help after severe financial hardship left him without a home or a job. He told a private television channel that a heart attack and stroke in October 2015 left him bedridden for two to three years. A restaurant venture he started later failed, leaving him with heavy debts.

He said his medicines cost Rs10,000 to Rs11,000 a month and he cannot pay his children's school fees. The Punjab Sports Board stopped his Rs35,000 monthly stipend, he added. Sharif appealed to the President, the Prime Minister, the Punjab Chief Minister, the Army Chief and the PCB chairman.

He was part of the 1984 national junior team, won bronze at the 1985 Junior World Cup, played for the senior team from 1987 to 1992, and competed at the 1988 Seoul Olympics.
