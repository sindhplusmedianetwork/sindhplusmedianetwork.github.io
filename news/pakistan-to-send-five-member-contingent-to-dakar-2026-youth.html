Pakistan to send five-member contingent to Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan is set to field a five-member contingent, including four athletes, in the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games in Senegal, with the competition scheduled to run from Oct 31 to Nov 13. The Pakistan Olympic Association announced the contingent on Friday after the International Olympic Committee allocated Pakistan quota places in athletics and swimming. Pakistan will be represented by Shahzain Safdar in athletics, while Hareem Malik and Syed Muhammad Daniyal Hatim will compete in swimming.

Safdar will be accompanied by athletics coach Rehan Yousaf. Lt Col (retd) Ahmed Ali Khan has been named Chef de Mission and will lead the national contingent at the Games. According to the association, Pakistan received one male quota place in athletics and two swimming places, one each for male and female athletes, through allocations made by the IOC.

The athletes' nominations were reviewed and vetted by the respective international federations in line with the age limits and eligibility requirements for the event. The association expressed confidence that the contingent would uphold the values of sportsmanship, discipline and integrity while representing the country.
