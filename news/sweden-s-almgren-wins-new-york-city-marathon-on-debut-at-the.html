Sweden's Almgren wins New York City Marathon on debut at the distance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sweden's Andreas Almgren won the New York City Marathon in 2 hours 6 minutes 1 second on his debut at the distance, pulling away in the final mile. The 31-year-old beat Italy's Yemaneberhan Crippa to claim one of the biggest prizes in world athletics. Kenya's Sheila Chepkirui defended her women's title in 2:24:36, finishing ahead of American Jessica McClain and last year's winner Hellen Obiri.

Almgren became the first Swedish athlete ever to win a major marathon. His victory capped a breakthrough year in which he set the European half-marathon record in Copenhagen in September. The win marks a remarkable transition for the former track specialist, who only moved up to the marathon this year.
