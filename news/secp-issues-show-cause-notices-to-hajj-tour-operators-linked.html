SECP issues show-cause notices to Hajj tour operators linked to Fazlur Rehman's close ally

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) has issued show-cause notices to Hajj tour operators allegedly linked to Mufti Abrar, a close associate of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman. The notices concern alleged irregularities in the ownership records of three companies linked to Mufti Abrar's family and associates: Sajjad Travels, Al-Rehab International and Shukr Al-Faizan Travels. The companies are accused of failing to submit beneficial ownership information as required under the Companies Act 2017.

The three companies have been given 14 days to submit their explanations to the regulator. Records show that Al-Rehab International filed relevant documents in 2023, but the regulator has raised questions about the ownership information provided. The notices are part of the SECP's enforcement of corporate disclosure rules for companies operating in the Hajj and Umrah travel sector.
