Lahore Safe City Traces Family of Bangladeshi Woman Separated for 40 Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore Safe City has traced the family of Haleema, a Bangladeshi woman who had been separated from her loved ones for 40 years. According to a Safe City spokesperson, Haleema was found in Islamabad in 2013 in a destitute condition, and because she did not know Urdu, she was unable to give information about her identity or her family. She was moved to the Edhi Centre for temporary shelter, where she had been living for several years.

The Safe City team kept searching for her family by contacting the local administration. Using the name of her ancestral village in Bangladesh and the names of her family members given by Haleema, the team traced her son and nephew in Bangladesh. The spokesperson said the family has now been found after four decades of separation.
