Nobel Laureate Navi Pillay Calls Trump's ICC Sanctions 'Unlawful'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nobel Peace Prize winner Navi Pillay on Wednesday hit out at US President Donald Trump's sanctions against the International Criminal Court, calling them unlawful and saying he needed a lesson in international law. In an interview with AFP in The Hague, the 85-year-old judge said it was no coincidence that the sanctions were announced just hours after her Nobel award, calling it a reaction to the fact that the prize was not given to President Trump. On Friday, the United States announced sanctions aimed at crippling the day-to-day operations of the court, which tries individuals for war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide.

Trump's administration has vowed to dismantle the court brick-by-brick, furious that its judges issued an arrest warrant for Israel's Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu over alleged crimes in Gaza. Pillay noted that previous US administrations had welcomed an ICC arrest warrant against Russian President Vladimir Putin, calling the difference selective and double standards. The sanctions, which will hit the court's technology and banking operations, take effect in six months, during which Dutch Prime Minister Rob Jetten has urged talks with Washington.

Pillay said talk of reforms to the court was euphemistic, arguing that what Washington really wanted was an undertaking that the court would not investigate US citizens.
