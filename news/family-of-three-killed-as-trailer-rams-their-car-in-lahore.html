Family of Three Killed as Trailer Rams Their Car in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man, his wife and their teenage son were killed when a 22-wheeler trailer rammed into their car on Allama Iqbal Road in Lahore's Garhi Shahu area. The car was stationary at a traffic signal when the heavy trailer, reportedly loaded with cement, struck it from behind and crushed it completely. Rescue 1122 teams reached the scene and had to cut through the wreckage and use a crane to recover the bodies, which were shifted to a morgue for medicolegal formalities.

The deceased were identified as Muhammad Asim, 42, his wife Huma Asim, 35, and their son Talha Asim, 15, residents of the Shah Jamal area of Ichhra. The trailer driver and other crew members fled the scene, and police seized the trailer while launching efforts to trace them. A case has been registered at Garhi Shahu police station on the complaint of the couple's surviving son, Muhammad bin Asim, who told police he had lost his father, mother and brother.

The FIR states the driver was driving with extreme negligence and recklessness. The provincial police chief took strict notice of the incident and sought a report from the chief traffic officer, directing action against drivers involved in overspeeding.
