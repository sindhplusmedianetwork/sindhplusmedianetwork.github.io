Samantha Prabhu Calls Vijay Her 'Lucky Charm' on Celebrity MasterChef Tamil

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

South film industry superstar Samantha Ruth Prabhu has called fellow actor Vijay her 'lucky charm'. The remark came during an appearance on Celebrity MasterChef Tamil, where she praised Vijay's hard work. Samantha said she had worked with him in three super-hit films.

The films she named were Kaththi (2014), Theri (2016) and Mersal (2017). She also praised Vijay's political journey. Her comments have drawn wide attention from fans of both stars.
