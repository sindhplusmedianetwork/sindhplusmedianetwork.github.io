Viral Video Shows Girl Swept by Sea Waves as Couple Films Reel on Beach

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A video circulating rapidly on social media shows a couple busy filming a reel on a beach while a small girl nearby slips on the sand and is caught by the incoming sea waves. In the footage, the girl can be seen suddenly slipping and falling on the sand as the waves wash over her, while the couple appears to remain occupied with making their video. According to the woman who shared the video, a passerby intervened immediately and rescued the child.

The video has drawn widespread criticism over the couple's alleged negligence. The location and date of the incident, as well as the authenticity of the video, could not be independently verified.
