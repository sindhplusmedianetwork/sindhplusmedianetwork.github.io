Gadap Farmhouse Case: Main Accused Arrested, 40 Phones Recovered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Karachi have arrested the main accused in the Gadap farmhouse case, in which 59 girls were lured with the promise of jobs and had their mobile phones taken before the accused fled. Police said the arrested man, Babar, was found in possession of 40 mobile phones and a pistol. According to police, Babar had planned the crime, calling the girls to the farmhouse on the pretext of employment and then fleeing with their phones.

The other accused in the case had already been arrested. Police said the arrested man's criminal record was being checked.
