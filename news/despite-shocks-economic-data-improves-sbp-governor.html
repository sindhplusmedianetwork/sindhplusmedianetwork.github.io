Despite shocks, economic data improves: SBP governor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Governor Jameel Ahmad has said Pakistan's macroeconomic recovery since 2022 has been remarkable, with economic fundamentals significantly improving despite severe global and regional shocks, The Express Tribune reported. Addressing ICMA Pakistan's annual convocation, he said inflation remained close to the target range in FY26 and, though it has risen since, is projected to gradually ease towards the target range. The current account deficit is expected to stay within 0–1% of GDP, while real GDP growth recovered from a contraction in FY23 to 3.7% in FY26 and is expected to rise further this fiscal year.

He credited timely, decisive action in FY22–23 — raising and holding the policy rate for almost a year alongside temporary measures on non-debt forex outflows — for averting a deeper external financing and debt crisis. He stressed that lasting progress cannot come from short-term fixes at the expense of long-term stability.
