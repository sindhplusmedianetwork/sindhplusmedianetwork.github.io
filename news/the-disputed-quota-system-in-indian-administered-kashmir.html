The Disputed Quota System in Indian-Administered Kashmir

In Jammu and Kashmir, 70 percent of the population is limited to only 30 percent of opportunities in government jobs and higher education. The story examines why this is the case under the region's disputed quota system. The quota system determines how government jobs and places in higher education are shared out.

For the majority of the population, that leaves a smaller share of the available opportunities. The report looks at how the system works and why it has become a subject of dispute in the region.
