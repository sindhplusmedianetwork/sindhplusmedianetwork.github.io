Ban on girls' education under Taliban regime continues, UN issues new warning

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Taliban regime's severe restrictions on women's education remain in place, and the United Nations has issued a new warning. UNESCO warned that the ban on girls' education will deprive Afghanistan of another generation of professionals in education, health and other fields. A UN report says 2.4 million girls have been removed from secondary education.

It also says no girl entered university in 2026. The figures underline the scale of the education crisis facing Afghan girls.
