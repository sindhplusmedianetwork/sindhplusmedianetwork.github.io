UAE Launches AI Assistant 'Mira' for Government Services

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Arab Emirates has launched 'Mira', an AI-powered personal assistant designed to handle government services. The assistant was inaugurated by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum. It will be rolled out in phases from March 2027, beginning with residence permits, identity documents, passport renewals, education and health services.

All government services are targeted to be covered by the end of 2027. Officials say Mira uses agentic AI to understand what citizens need, gather information from the relevant departments, and complete service procedures automatically. The launch places the assistant at the centre of the country's plan to make public services faster and simpler for residents.
