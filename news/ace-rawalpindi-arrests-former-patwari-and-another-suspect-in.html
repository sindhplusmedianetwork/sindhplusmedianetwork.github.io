ACE Rawalpindi Arrests Former Patwari and Another Suspect in Murree Fraud Cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Anti-Corruption Establishment (ACE) Rawalpindi has arrested two suspects, including a former patwari of Bara Hotar in Murree, in two separate cases involving alleged fraud, forgery and the use of fake documents. Officials said the arrests were made on November 6 by Circle Officer Mansoor Mazhar along with ASI Umar Farooq and his team. The former patwari was identified as Raja Mumtaz Ali, while the second accused was identified as Muhammad Irfan Khan.

Case No. 31/2026 was registered on a reference forwarded by Deputy Commissioner Murree Agha Zaheer Abbas Shirazi, and judicial action has also been approved in the case. The Anti-Corruption Establishment said both suspects were arrested in separate cases registered under charges of fraud, forgery and the use of fake documents.
