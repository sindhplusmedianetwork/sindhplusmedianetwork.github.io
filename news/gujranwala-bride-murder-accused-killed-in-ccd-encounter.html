Gujranwala bride-murder accused killed in CCD encounter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The main accused in the murder of a bride on her valima day in Gujranwala was killed in an alleged encounter with the Crime Control Department (CCD) near Khalil Road on Saturday. The CCD said the accused, Sarfaraz Mado, was shot dead in firing by his own accomplices after he was cornered. He was wanted for shooting dead bride Saba in broad daylight on October 24, 2026, reportedly over one-sided love and rejection of a marriage proposal.

According to the CCD, the accused had fled to Karachi and then to the border areas of Balochistan, where he remained in hiding. He returned to Gujranwala, where officials surrounded him before the exchange of fire.
