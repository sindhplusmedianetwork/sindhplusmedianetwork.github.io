Netanyahu's departure sealed as allies abandon him and Arab parties back Eisenkot

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's departure appears sealed after his allies abandoned him and Arab parties backed his rival to take over. His ally, the United Torah Judaism party, has refused to support him for prime minister. Arab parties have announced their support for opposition leader and former army chief Gadi Eisenkot as the new prime minister.

Eisenkot has the support of 67 members of the Knesset, more than the 61 needed for a majority. The Yesh Atid party has rejected Netanyahu's offer to form a government together and has asked President Isaac Herzog to give Eisenkot the mandate to form a government. The developments signal the end of Netanyahu's tenure at the head of the Israeli government.
