U19 Asia Cup: India Bat First Against Pakistan After Winning Toss

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India won the toss and decided to bat first against Pakistan in their ACC Under-19 Asia Cup Group A match, the eighth match of the tournament, being played in Muscat. India had reached 86 for five when the latest update came in. Pakistan had won their opening match against the United Arab Emirates by nine wickets.

Both Pakistan and India have won their opening matches in Group A, while Afghanistan have won two of three matches in Group B. Two teams from each group will advance to the next stage. Defending champions Pakistan beat India by 191 runs in last season's final.
