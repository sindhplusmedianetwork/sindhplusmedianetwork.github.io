Government struggles to enrol out-of-school children in Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

AI-powered surveillance cameras flagged 17,946 instances of children outside schools during school hours in Punjab in October 2026. The figure included 12,378 children without uniforms. It also included 1,052 suspected child labour cases.

The 2023 Punjab census counted 9.6 million out-of-school children aged 5-16. That is about 27 per cent of the school-age population in the province. The numbers underline how large the problem remains, and the government continues to struggle to bring these children into schools.
