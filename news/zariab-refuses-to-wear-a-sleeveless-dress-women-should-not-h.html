'Zariab' refuses to wear a sleeveless dress: 'Women should not have to justify their clothing choices'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A scene in the popular Pakistani drama 'Dar-e-Nijat' has sparked a wide debate on social media after the character Zariab refused to wear a sleeveless dress. In the scene, Zariab, played by Dur-e-Fishan Saleem, is sitting with her grandmother preparing for a wedding function when her sister brings a white sleeveless dress and urges her to wear it. Zariab first refuses, then changes at her sister's insistence, but seeing her bare arms in the mirror she feels uncomfortable and changes back into her old clothes, saying simply that it is sleeveless.

Responding to the debate, Dur-e-Fishan wrote in a detailed post that the change in the character's dress may reflect changes in Zariab's own thinking, and that women who wear sleeveless clothes and those who do not should both be free from having to justify their choice. She said faith begins with inner change, and that what she wears is part of her personal journey and her relationship with God. Senior actress Marina Khan said people should take the drama as a drama, and that criticism should not affect how dramas are written, while writer Fasih Bari Khan said ideas were being thrust into dramas and that judging a character by her clothes was superficial.
