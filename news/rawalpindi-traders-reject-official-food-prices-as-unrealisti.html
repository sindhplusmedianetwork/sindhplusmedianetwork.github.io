Rawalpindi Traders Reject Official Food Prices as Unrealistic

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Traders' associations in Rawalpindi have rejected the new official prices fixed by Deputy Commissioner Nadeem Nasir for pulses, rice, milk, meat, yoghurt and bread, describing them as unrealistic, drawing-room prices. The Karyana Merchants Association, Milkmen Union, Naanbai Association and Mutton and Beef Retailers Union demanded a meeting of the District Price Control Committee to fix rates in line with market conditions, warning of a shutter-down strike and protests otherwise. Under the new list, new Basmati Super rice is fixed at Rs240 per kilogram against a market price of Rs300 to Rs400, milk at Rs220 per litre against Rs250, and beef at Rs1,200 per kilogram against Rs1,500 to Rs1,700 in the market.

The traders said the official rates ignored the actual cost of buying and selling, and they urged the administration to sit with them before enforcing the new price list. The traders warned that they would go on a shutter-down strike and hold protests if their demand for a committee meeting was not met.
