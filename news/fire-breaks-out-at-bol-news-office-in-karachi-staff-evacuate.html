Fire breaks out at Bol News office in Karachi, staff evacuated safely

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A sudden fire broke out at the private TV channel Bol News office near Korangi Creek in Karachi. Flames rose from parts of the building and black smoke spread across the area. All staff and employees were safely evacuated and no loss of life was reported.

The building also houses Asia One News, 365 News and Awaz News offices, and part of the building was reported to have collapsed. Fire brigade vehicles and firefighters reached the spot and began operations to control the blaze. The cause of the fire and the final estimate of financial and technical losses will be determined after complete control and cooling.

IG Sindh directed police officers to reach the scene on an emergency basis, fully cooperate with fire brigade and rescue agencies, and clear all roads around the site.
