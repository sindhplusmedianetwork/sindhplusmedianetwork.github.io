FIFA Unveils $6.35 Billion Global Football Plan; Pakistan Could Get Up to $25 Million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

FIFA has presented a plan to invest 6.35 billion dollars in football development around the world between 2027 and 2030. Under the proposed plan, each of FIFA's 211 member associations, including Pakistan, could receive up to 25 million dollars in additional funds. The proposal for a major increase in development funding follows the record commercial success of the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Final approval of the new plan is subject to the FIFA Congress in March 2027, while the FIFA Council will finalise its details at its December 2026 meeting. The proposed funding will prioritise the promotion of women's and youth football as well as the development of sporting infrastructure, and it also proposes maintaining 1.7 billion dollars in emergency reserves. FIFA president Gianni Infantino apologised for mistakes made in the controversial FIFA Forward Enterprise project, announcing that it had been permanently ended and would not be presented again in any form.

He said FIFA's funds belong to its 211 member associations, and the plan aims to raise per-member funding from one million dollars to 25 million dollars over ten years. A working group will review possible governance reforms, the relevant committee will review the FIFA Peace Prize with a decision expected in December, and the council will also review player disciplinary procedures after American footballer Folarin Balogun's one-match suspension was conditionally deferred.
