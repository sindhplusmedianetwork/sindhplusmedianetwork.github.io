The Mystery of Mars's Giant Recurring Cloud May Finally Be Solved

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Scientists may finally be closing in on the answer to one of Mars's strangest mysteries. A new study published in the journal Nature Geoscience takes a close look at the unusually long white cloud that stretches across the sky above Mars's Arsia Mons volcano every summer. Led by Dr Jorge Hernández Bernal of Sorbonne University, the research focuses on the cloud that appears daily above the giant volcano and can stretch for nearly 1,800 kilometres.

The study suggests the cloud forms through homogeneous nucleation, a cloud-formation process that scientists had largely considered only theoretical until now. If the finding holds, it would represent the first real documented case of clouds forming outside the conventional mechanism. The cloud's daily appearance and enormous length have made it one of the most watched features of the Martian atmosphere.

Researchers say understanding it could reshape how clouds are understood on other planets as well.
