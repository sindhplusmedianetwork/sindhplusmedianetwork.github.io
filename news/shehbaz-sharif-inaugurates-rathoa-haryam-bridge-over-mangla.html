Shehbaz Sharif Inaugurates Rathoa Haryam Bridge over Mangla Reservoir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif inaugurated the Rs10 billion Rathoa Haryam Bridge over the Mangla Dam reservoir in Azad Jammu and Kashmir. It is the region's longest bridge. The three-kilometre bridge connects Mirpur city with Islamgarh town and provides a direct link to Kotli district.

The bridge will improve access to markets, schools and healthcare for communities around the reservoir. The project was financed by the federal government under the PSDP. It was completed six months ahead of schedule.

The bridge is expected to boost tourism, trade and socio-economic activity in the region.
