US Senators Urge Rubio to Press Israel on Winter Aid for Gaza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two senior Democratic senators have urged US Secretary of State Marco Rubio to press Israel to allow more winter supplies into Gaza. The appeal came after heavy rain flooded more than 70,000 tents in the enclave. Senators Jeanne Shaheen and Brian Schatz wrote that Israel's 'extensive review and approval processes can delay or block many supplies'.

They noted that Kerem Shalom 'remains the only crossing open for aid'. The senators asked Rubio to press for immediate clearance of tents, tarpaulins, blankets and other winter supplies already staged outside Gaza. They also called for the opening of additional crossings.
