Trump Says Progress Is Being Made in Talks With Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said that Washington is in talks with Tehran and that matters are moving forward. Speaking to reporters as he left the White House, Trump said the United States has full control of the situation but is talking to Iran and will see which way things go. He also claimed that America has not lost any soldier in the past six or seven months.

His remarks came at a time when Iranian media reported attacks on Saturday morning in southern Iran, including on Qeshm island, although there has been no official confirmation so far about any damage from those attacks. Trump has been giving mixed signals in recent days, speaking at times about continuing diplomatic efforts with Iran and at other times about restarting military strikes. The talks come against the backdrop of months of tension between the two countries, with the US maintaining a naval blockade against Iran and both sides exchanging warnings.
