Italy Sees Nationwide Strikes as Students and Workers Protest Over Inflation and Housing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thousands of students, workers and trade unionists took to the streets across Italian cities on Friday in a nationwide general strike over rising inflation, education funding and housing costs. The USB union claimed between one and 1.5 million people joined the protests, most of which remained peaceful, though police used water cannon in Turin and clashes broke out in Rome. Demonstrators demanded cheaper housing, better schools and measures against the rising cost of living.

Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's government last week approved a 175.2 million euro relief package for truck drivers, farmers and fishermen, and proposed an annual bonus of 2,500 euros for workers under 30. Union leaders said the package was not enough and called for deeper structural reforms. The strike shut down public transport and schools in several cities.
