Indian court issues bailable warrant against filmmaker Anurag Kashyap over Brahmin-community remark

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mumbai — An Indian court has issued a bailable warrant against filmmaker and actor Anurag Kashyap in connection with his controversial remarks about the Brahmin community on social media last year. Kashyap had failed to appear before the court despite repeated summons. The court had ordered a case registered against him in May on the petition of a lawyer associated with the Bajrang Dal and Vishwa Hindu Parishad.

The petitioner cited Kashyap's message posted on April 16, 2025, describing it as an insult to the Brahmin community. While issuing the warrant, the court ordered police to arrest Kashyap and present him before the court. The next hearing in the case has been fixed for December 11.

The 54-year-old filmmaker is known for acclaimed films such as 'Gangs of Wasseypur' and 'Black Friday'.
