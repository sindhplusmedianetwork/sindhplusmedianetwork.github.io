A timely jab can save a child from pneumonia, says Sindh CM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has said that a timely vaccination can save a child from pneumonia, underlining the importance of routine immunisation for young children. Pneumonia remains the leading cause of death in children worldwide, and in Pakistan more than 350,000 children under the age of five die every year, with almost one in five of those deaths caused by pneumonia, according to the Aga Khan University. Health experts say almost two-thirds of the children who die of pneumonia in the country die at home because they do not receive appropriate and timely care.

The pneumococcal vaccine, which protects children against pneumonia and meningitis, is being included in the routine immunisation programme. Parents have been urged to make sure children under one year of age are inoculated on schedule, so that thousands of young lives can be saved from a preventable illness.
