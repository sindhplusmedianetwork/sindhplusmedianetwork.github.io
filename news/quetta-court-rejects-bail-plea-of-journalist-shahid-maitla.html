Quetta Court Rejects Bail Plea of Journalist Shahid Maitla

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A local court in Quetta on Saturday rejected the bail application of Islamabad-based journalist Shahid Maitla. Judicial Magistrate Akhtar Shah announced the decision on the application. The application had been filed in the court of Judicial Magistrate-VII.

Maitla was arrested under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act on a complaint by former caretaker prime minister Anwarul Haq Kakar. He is currently on judicial remand in District Jail, Quetta. The court turned down his plea for release on bail.
