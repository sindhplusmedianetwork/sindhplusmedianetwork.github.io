Masked Men Smash Windows of Nawaz Sharif's Car Outside Avenfield

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two masked individuals smashed the windows of former prime minister Nawaz Sharif's car outside Avenfield in London, according to Jang. Police have taken the incident's CCTV footage into custody for investigation. This is the third such incident involving Nawaz Sharif's cars.

Earlier, speaking to Geo News, the former prime minister called the Peshawar school roof-collapse tragedy "regrettable" and said Maryam Nawaz is already working on the poor condition of Punjab's private schools. He added that Pakistan understands the problem of terrorism and is taking notice of it. Asked about his health, Nawaz Sharif said he is doing fine, thank God.
