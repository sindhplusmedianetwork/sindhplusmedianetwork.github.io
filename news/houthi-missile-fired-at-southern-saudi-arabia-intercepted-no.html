Houthi missile fired at southern Saudi Arabia intercepted, no damage reported

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Houthi militia in Yemen fired a missile at the southern region of Saudi Arabia, the Saudi-led coalition said. According to the coalition, the missile fired by the Houthi militia was intercepted and shot down. Saudi officials said there were no reports of any loss of life or damage to property in the missile attack.

The attack came a day after a cruise missile fired from Yemen towards the Saudi capital Riyadh was also destroyed. The latest incident adds to the continuing exchange of strikes between the Saudi-led coalition and the Houthi movement despite efforts to calm the conflict in Yemen. No further details about the launch site or the exact target of the missile were given in the reports.
