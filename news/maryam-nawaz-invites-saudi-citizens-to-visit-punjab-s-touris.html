Maryam Nawaz invites Saudi citizens to visit Punjab's tourist spots; Rs500bn export target agreed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz met Saudi Ambassador Dr Rajah Al-Baqmi on Wednesday and invited Saudi citizens to visit Punjab's tourist centers. The two sides discussed investment cooperation and a planned roadshow in Riyadh to promote Punjab's economic potential. They agreed on an ambitious target of more than Rs500 billion in exports from Punjab, covering livestock, beef, poultry, mangoes, kinnows, potatoes and shrimp.

Maryam Nawaz noted that Saudi Arabia, with $9.8 billion in annual inflows, is the largest source of remittances to Pakistan. She also voiced Pakistan's full support for the Kingdom's right to self-defense. The ambassador appreciated Punjab's efforts to deepen economic and cultural ties between the two countries.
