PDMA warns of rain, thunderstorms and hail in 15 Balochistan districts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Provincial Disaster Management Authority (PDMA) Balochistan has warned of rain, windstorms, thunderstorms and possible hail in 15 districts of the province. The weather is expected on November 11 and 12 as a westerly system approaches Balochistan. The districts under the warning include Quetta, Zhob, Chaman, Ziarat and Khuzdar.

The PDMA said strong winds could damage vulnerable structures in the affected areas. Temperatures in the province may fall by 2 to 4 degrees Celsius during the spell. The authority advised residents to take precautionary measures and stay away from weak structures during strong winds.
