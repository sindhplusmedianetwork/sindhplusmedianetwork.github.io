Tax exemptions on sales tax, income tax and customs duties surge extraordinarily, says report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A major cause of the growing fiscal pressure in Pakistan is the extraordinary increase in the exemptions and concessions given in sales tax, income tax and customs duty, which have risen rapidly over the past decade. According to a fresh report by the Pakistan Institute of Development Economics, tax expenditure in Pakistan, the possible loss to the national exchequer due to tax exemptions and concessions, has continued to rise and reached nearly 4 to 5 percent of gross domestic product in recent fiscal years. The report further says that during this period the tax collection rate in the country has stagnated at around 10 percent of GDP.

The exemptions cover three main heads, sales tax, income tax and customs duties, and the report links the rise in these exemptions over the past decade directly to the growing pressure on public finances. With tax expenditure now close to 4 to 5 percent of GDP and collections stuck near 10 percent, the report presents the exemptions as a central part of the country's fiscal challenge.
