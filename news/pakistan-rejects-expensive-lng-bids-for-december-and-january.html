Pakistan Rejects Expensive LNG Bids for December and January

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has rejected the expensive bids received for LNG supply in December and January. Pakistan LNG Limited had sought bids for two spot LNG cargoes and received three bids in total. The bids were sought for delivery windows of 14-16 December and 9-11 January.

Bids for the December cargo ranged from $27.27 to $31 per MMBTU. Only one bid of $27.01 per MMBTU was received for the January cargo. The documents show prices of Rs24.43 billion to Rs27.77 billion sought for the December cargo, and Rs24.28 billion for the January cargo.
