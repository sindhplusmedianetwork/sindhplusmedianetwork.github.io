Middle East Crisis Brings 'Record Profit' for Pakistani Ports

When war in the Middle East closed the Strait of Hormuz, ships turned towards Pakistani ports. The shift gave Pakistan's shipping industry a chance for economic growth after decades. The surge in traffic brought record profits to the country's ports.

The piece looks at the record profits reported by Pakistani ports during the crisis. It also examines what drove the surge in traffic.
