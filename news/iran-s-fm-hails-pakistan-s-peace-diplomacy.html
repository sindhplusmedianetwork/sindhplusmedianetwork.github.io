Iran's FM hails Pakistan's peace diplomacy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir held separate meetings with Iranian, British and Turkish military officials to discuss regional security and ways to strengthen bilateral defence cooperation, according to the ISPR. A high-level Iranian delegation led by Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi called on Field Marshal Munir at GHQ in Rawalpindi. During the meeting, Araghchi appreciated Pakistan's constructive role in promoting regional peace and stability.

The CDF also met Turkish Air Force Commander General Rafet Dalkiran. He separately met a British military delegation led by General Sir Charles Roland Vincent Walker.
