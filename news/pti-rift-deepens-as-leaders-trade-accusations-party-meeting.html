PTI Rift Deepens as Leaders Trade Accusations; Party Meeting Asks Raja to Apologize

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cracks have appeared inside Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf as senior leaders openly traded accusations and threats. The row began after a social media post by party secretary Salman Akram Raja, in which he told followers not to believe any conspiratorial narrative, said Ali Asghar was acting provincial president after Junaid Akbar, and called those spreading rumours 'snakes and their offspring' who would face defeat. Former Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister Ali Amin Gandapur hit back in a leaked audio message shared in a party group, declaring the party 'is not anyone's father's property' and that decisions are made by the founding chairman.

He warned Raja against calling people snakes, saying 'if you don't understand, we have ways of making you understand.' Members of the KP Assembly also expressed strong reservations, demanding that the parliamentary party's opinion be given weight in provincial decisions. In a separate development, a PTI Khyber Pakhtunkhwa meeting decided to name Gandapur as the party's representative in the central negotiation process and asked Raja to withdraw his statement and apologize. PTI chairman Barrister Gohar told reporters outside Parliament that Sohail Afridi 'was and will remain our chief minister', and asked Sheikh Waqas Akram, Gandapur and others not to take internal party matters outside.

Raja, speaking to Geo News, denied that Mahmood Achakzai had been removed as opposition leader.
