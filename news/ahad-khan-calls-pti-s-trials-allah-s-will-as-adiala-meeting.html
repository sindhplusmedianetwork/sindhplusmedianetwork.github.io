Ahad Khan Calls PTI's Trials Allah's Will as Adiala Meeting Fails

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Imran Khan's brother-in-law Ahad Khan reached the Dahgal checkpoint outside Adiala jail on the scheduled meeting day, accompanied by Niazullah Niazi, Muhammad Faisal Malik, Khalid Yousaf Chaudhry and Mustafa Baloch. Speaking to the media there, he said the trials facing the PTI were Allah's will and suggested that events surrounding Imran Khan could be a form of retribution. He also declared that imposing governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa would prove to be the final nail in the rulers' coffin.

However, the delegation could not meet anyone inside because the allotted meeting time had expired, and neither family members nor party leaders were allowed in. Barrister Gohar, though named on the meeting list, did not turn up. Niazi used the occasion to allege that a no-confidence move was being planned against the KP government, that Sher Afzal Marwat had petitioned to disqualify Chief Minister Sohail Afridi, and that the province's two percent NFC share remained unpaid.

Ahad Khan, who is not a PTI member, also raised concern over the heart condition of his wife Dr Uzma Khan, noting that the Lahore High Court had ordered her medicines to be provided inside the jail.
