RDA reimburses 2026 medicine bill on four-year-old prescription, audit questioned

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

RAWALPINDI: The Rawalpindi Development Authority has reimbursed a senior officer Rs31,980 for medicines purchased in 2026 against an unauthorised medical prescription issued back in 2022, casting doubt on the professional responsibilities of its internal audit and finance directorate. The prescription dated to a time when the officer concerned was not posted at the RDA, and the private hospital that issued it is not on the authority's approved list. The prescription covered multivitamin medicines generally considered strength supplements rather than treatment for a specific illness, and the medicines were bought in 2026 for the officer's father.

The reimbursement bill was approved by the RDA's deputy director finance and director finance, and was also cleared by the authority's internal audit. The episode stands in contrast to the difficulties subordinate employees routinely face in getting their own medical claims reimbursed. It also comes after more than Rs2 billion was transferred from the RDA's official account to private firms and individuals through cash deposit receipts over five to six years, transactions the internal audit had failed to trace.

The authority currently has no full-time director general, and its public relations officer declined to comment on the matter.
