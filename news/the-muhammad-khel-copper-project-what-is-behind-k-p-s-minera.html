The Muhammad Khel Copper Project: What Is Behind K-P's Mineral Contract Debate?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A heated debate over mineral contracts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa began after JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman alleged at a 16 October rally in Multan that emergency — not governor's rule — was imposed in the mineral-rich former tribal districts and 'the nation was deceived'. Rehman argued that since the 2018 merger, the mineral deposits belong to the people of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and questioned why governor's rule was not imposed across the whole province. K-P Governor Faisal Karim Kundi and federal ministers disagreed with his claims.

At the centre of the discussion is the Muhammad Khel copper project in North Waziristan, which spans about 30 square kilometres. A 20-year mining lease for the project was granted on 15 July 2016. Between 2016 and 2024, mineral production of 952,878 metric tons was recorded.

FWO says it is Pakistan's second-largest copper mining project, and it was declared a special export processing zone in 2023.
