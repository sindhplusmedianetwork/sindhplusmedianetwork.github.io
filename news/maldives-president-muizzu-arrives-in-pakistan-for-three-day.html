Maldives President Muizzu Arrives in Pakistan for Three-Day Visit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Maldives President Dr Mohamed Muizzu arrived in Islamabad on Tuesday on a three-day visit to Pakistan, state-run Radio Pakistan reported. Muizzu is visiting Pakistan at the invitation of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. The visit marks the first by a Maldivian president to Pakistan in 11 years and comes as the two countries celebrate the 60th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations in 1966.

According to the Foreign Office, Muizzu will meet President Asif Ali Zardari, who will host a state banquet in his honour, and will hold 'restricted and delegation-level' talks with PM Shehbaz. During the talks, the two leaders will 'review the entire spectrum of bilateral relations and explore avenues for further strengthening cooperation in trade, investment, health, education, tourism, agriculture, defence, maritime security, climate change and people-to-people exchanges'. A Pakistan-Maldives Business Forum will also be held during the visit to promote interaction between the two countries' business communities, and a number of agreements and memorandums of understanding are expected to be signed.

The Maldivian delegation, led by Foreign Minister Iruthisham Adam and comprising cabinet ministers and senior officials, arrived in Islamabad earlier on Tuesday ahead of the president's arrival.
