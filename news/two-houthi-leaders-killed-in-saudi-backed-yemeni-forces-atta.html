Two Houthi Leaders Killed in Saudi-Backed Yemeni Forces' Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two Houthi militia leaders, Ibrahim al-Nunu and Abu Abdullah al-Sufyani, have been killed in attacks by Saudi-backed Yemeni forces. The deaths mark the latest development in the ongoing conflict in Yemen between the Houthi movement and forces loyal to the internationally recognised government, which is backed by a Saudi-led coalition. Separately, the Saudi-led coalition said it had destroyed a Houthi drone headed toward Makkah.

Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree denied the claim on X, saying the group had carried out no operation toward Saudi land. Saree said the Houthis' targets were Saudi oil installations and military bases. The exchange of claims comes amid continued tensions across the region.
