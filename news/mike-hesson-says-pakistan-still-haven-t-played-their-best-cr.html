Mike Hesson says Pakistan still haven't played their best cricket ahead of tri-nation final

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan head coach Mike Hesson believes the national team is yet to produce its best cricket, and he hopes the side saves that performance for Saturday's tri-nation series final against Sri Lanka at Gaddafi Stadium. Speaking to Geo News, Hesson praised his players for winning three straight matches even without standout contributions from the top order. He singled out Salman Ali Agha, Hassan Nawaz, Shaheen Shah Afridi, Naseem Shah and Haris Rauf among the performers who have carried the team so far.

The coach said the squad remains focused on lifting the trophy in front of home fans. He also thanked supporters in Lahore and Rawalpindi for backing the team throughout the series. The final will be played in Lahore on Saturday.
