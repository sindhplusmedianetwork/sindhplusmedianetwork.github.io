Serbia's Student List Wins Parliamentary Election as Vucic Concedes Defeat

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read

Serbia's Aleksandar Vucic has conceded defeat to a student-led protest movement in a parliamentary election, ending his long grip on power in the Balkan country. Vucic said projections indicated that the opposition's Students' List would win between 135 and 140 seats in Serbia's 250-member parliament, while his Serbian Progressive Party would secure around 105 seats. Within moments of his announcement, the streets of Belgrade erupted in celebration, with tens of thousands of people thronging the main boulevards, cars honking and students shouting victory.

Vucic, a populist and nationalist, has dominated Serbian politics as prime minister or president since 2014, but he called snap elections in September after almost two years of student-led demonstrations against perceived corruption and creeping authoritarianism. Those protests were triggered by the deaths of 16 people when a train station canopy collapsed in the city of Novi Sad in 2024. Pollsters and the election commission put turnout close to 70 percent, the highest since the breakup of Yugoslavia in 1992.

European leaders congratulated the Student List on its victory, and the movement's list of 250 largely unknown candidates is led by cardiologist Ilija Srdanovic, though it remains unclear who will become prime minister.
