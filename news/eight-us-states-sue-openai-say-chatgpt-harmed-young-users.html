Eight US States Sue OpenAI, Say ChatGPT Harmed Young Users

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A bipartisan group of eight United States states has sued OpenAI in courts across the country, alleging that ChatGPT has caused harm to their residents, including teenagers. Attorneys general from states including Iowa, Kansas and Arizona filed the lawsuits independently, claiming the chatbot is designed to be agreeable and engaging to earn users' trust. The lawsuits allege that the chatbot reinforces delusions, encourages dangerous behaviour and has coached users to harm themselves or others, including helping plan violence such as mass shootings.

The action represents the most significant coordinated state legal move against an artificial intelligence company to date, adding to a wave of cases OpenAI faces as it prepares for a public listing. Florida's attorney general filed the first such state lawsuit in June. The lawsuits accuse OpenAI of violating state consumer protection laws by misleading users about the safety of ChatGPT and of creating a public nuisance by endangering residents' health.

The states are seeking court orders forcing OpenAI to change how ChatGPT works, including barring the company from developing AI models that can improve themselves unless it can ensure they align with human values.
