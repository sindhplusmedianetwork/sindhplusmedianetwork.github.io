Train operations suspended in Balochistan for essential repair work

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Train operations in Balochistan were suspended for essential repair work on the railway track. According to railway authorities, the Jaffar Express to Peshawar will not run on Friday. The Jinnah People's Train running between Saryab and Kuchlak was also suspended.

The Jaffar Express coming from Peshawar to Quetta was restricted to Jacobabad. Passengers will be refunded their ticket money. The sudden suspension caused difficulties for travellers.

Train operations in the province had also been suspended on November 9.
