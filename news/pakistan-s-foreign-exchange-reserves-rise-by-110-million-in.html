Pakistan's Foreign Exchange Reserves Rise by $110 Million in a Week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's total foreign exchange reserves rose by $110 million in a week to $26.864 billion, the State Bank said. Official reserves rose by $39 million to $21.56 billion. Commercial banks' dollar deposits rose by $71 million to $5.393 billion.

The increase took total reserves to $26.864 billion. The figures were shared by the State Bank.
