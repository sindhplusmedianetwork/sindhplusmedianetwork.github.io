Jailed PTI Leaders Say Country Cannot Exit Crises Without Accepting Public Mandate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leaders imprisoned at Kot Lakhpat Jail have issued a statement saying the country cannot come out of its crises without national reconciliation, respect for the Constitution and acceptance of the public mandate. The statement was issued through their lawyer, Rana Mudassar Umar, by Shah Mahmood Qureshi, Dr Yasmin Rashid, Umar Sarfraz Cheema, Mian Mahmoodur Rasheed and Ejaz Chaudhry. They said the present government had harmed the Constitution and democracy, and that the 26th and 27th constitutional amendments had damaged the judiciary.

They also said the media's voice was being suppressed through the PECA law, and that inflation, unemployment and poverty had made life difficult for the public. The statement further claimed that more than 3,000 people had been killed in encounters by the Crime Control Department in Punjab, and said political instability and economic difficulties were affecting domestic and foreign investment. The leaders demanded confidence-building steps with the opposition and respect for the Constitution, the rule of law and the public mandate.
