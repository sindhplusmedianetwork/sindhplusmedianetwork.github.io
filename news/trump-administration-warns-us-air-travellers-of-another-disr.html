Trump Administration Warns US Air Travellers of Another Disruptive Day

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Trump administration warned on Sunday that disruption to air travel in the United States could drag on for another day after a nationwide ground stop on Friday. Hundreds more flights were cancelled as faulty work to upgrade air traffic control communication cables continued to affect operations. Friday's stop was the longest nationwide halt since the attacks of September 11, 2001, with more than 9,200 flights cancelled in a single day.

Officials said the problems began during work to replace ageing copper cables with a fibre network. President Donald Trump blamed the previous administration for the state of the system, while Transport Secretary Sean Duffy said full normality would return by Monday. Airlines and passengers were urged to check flight status before travelling, as delays continued at major airports across the country.
