Did Rajab Butt Send His Wife a Divorce Notice? New Details Emerge

Fresh claims about the troubled marriage of YouTuber Rajab Butt surfaced on Tuesday after a social media page alleged that he had sent his wife Emaan Fatima a divorce notice through the union council. Reports say Emaan had been trying to reconcile for the sake of their son. She has now filed a maintenance suit demanding Rs500,000 per month for the son and Rs200,000 for herself, along with the return of dowry articles worth Rs8.7 million.

She has also claimed that Butt bore none of the child's expenses. The claims circulating online have not been independently verified, and neither party has issued an official statement in the published report.
