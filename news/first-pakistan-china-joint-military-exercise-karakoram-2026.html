First Pakistan-China Joint Military Exercise 'Karakoram 2026' Begins in Xinjiang

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first-ever joint Pakistan-China military exercise, 'Karakoram 2026', is under way in China's Xinjiang region, with the opening ceremony held in Korla. The exercise covers drone operations, detecting and engaging moving targets, firing practice, long-range shooting, joint command procedures and practical combat drills. Troops from both armies are exchanging counter-terrorism experience, and Pakistani soldiers observed Chinese weaponry on display.

According to the report, the exercise aims to strengthen bilateral counter-terrorism cooperation and operational coordination between the two forces.
