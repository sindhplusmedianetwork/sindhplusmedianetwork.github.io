If Maryam Nawaz gets PML-N presidency, Shehbaz Sharif would quit as PM and from party, claims journalist

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior journalist Muhammad Malik has claimed that if the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz party presidency is handed to Maryam Nawaz, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif would resign from both the premiership and the party and go home. Speaking on a private television channel's programme 'The Reporters' with anchor Khawar Ghumman, Malik discussed the possible move to make Maryam Nawaz the party president. He said Maryam Nawaz wanted the presidency matter resolved as soon as possible, because her group believed the next general elections could prove to be the best opportunity for them.

According to him, the group also believed that no major political relief was in sight for the PTI by 2029 and that the chances of Imran Khan's release before the next elections were low, though nothing could be said with certainty about the future. The senior journalist added that the effort to make Maryam Nawaz the party president had once again begun in earnest, and that, according to his information, Nawaz Sharif had resisted the move earlier and still wanted to delay it, but the pressure on the matter was now very high. When asked what the reaction of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Hamza Shehbaz would be if Maryam Nawaz became party president, Malik claimed that, according to his observation and information, Shehbaz Sharif would resign from both offices and go home.

He added that similar signals had appeared earlier, which was why the decision on the PML-N presidency had been held back.
