Initial Post-Mortem Says 13-Year-Old Girl Died Due to Torture at Police Officer's Home

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The initial post-mortem report of 13-year-old domestic worker Fatima Asghar, who worked at a senior police officer's home in Islamabad, says she died as a result of torture. The report says severe beating caused severe inflammation in her body, and blood clots formed in her body. She then suffered thromboembolism, which finally led to cardiopulmonary arrest as her heart and lungs stopped working.

Police have recovered the iron and stick allegedly used to beat her. Accused Ali Raza told police in his statement that the accused woman often beat the girl with a stick. He said that when her condition worsened, she was first taken for a check-up at the Police Lines dispensary.
