Tharparkar ambulance fire toll rises to nine; President orders probe, CM announces Rs2m aid

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The death toll from the ambulance fire in Tharparkar rose to nine on Thursday, a district official confirmed. The oxygen cylinder exploded inside a PPHI ambulance at the basic health unit in Dano Dhandhal, Nagarparkar, on Wednesday night while it was being fitted for a woman who had given birth. Several patients and attendants were caught in the blast and the resulting fire.

President Asif Ali Zardari ordered a transparent inquiry into the incident to fix responsibility. Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah announced compensation of Rs2 million for the family of each victim. Health authorities said the cause of the explosion was being investigated and safety protocols for medical oxygen use were being reviewed.
