Houthi Attacks on Saudi Airports Alarm Pakistanis in the Kingdom

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fear and anxiety are growing among Pakistanis living in Saudi Arabia after a series of Houthi missile and drone attacks on the kingdom's airports. The deadliest strike hit King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on October 10, killing at least 12 people and wounding more than 300, in what officials called the worst attack on the kingdom in over a decade. Earlier, attacks on October 6 and 7 targeted Abha and Riyadh airports, killing three people and injuring 36, including a Pakistani national.

Saudi Arabia's civil aviation authority said the injured included Saudi citizens as well as foreign residents, with wounds ranging from minor to critical. Pakistan strongly condemned the attacks, with the Foreign Ministry calling the targeting of a civilian airport a heinous act of terrorism. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif reaffirmed Pakistan's solidarity with Saudi Arabia under their bilateral defence agreements.

Foreign embassies in Riyadh have advised their citizens to avoid the airport area as operations were temporarily suspended.
