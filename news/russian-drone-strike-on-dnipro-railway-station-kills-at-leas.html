Russian Drone Strike on Dnipro Railway Station Kills at Least 14 in Ukraine

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 14 people were killed and 10 injured in a Russian drone strike on the railway station in Dnipro, Ukraine, officials said on Wednesday. According to an international news agency, a jet-engine drone struck the station platform where a passenger train bound for Zaporizhzhia was standing. Ukrainian railway authorities had issued a warning about the heightened threat level before the attack, but passengers remained on the platform and the casualties were higher.

The injured were taken to hospital, and officials fear the death toll could rise as three of the wounded are in critical condition. President Volodymyr Zelensky called the attack horrific and demanded that the United States, European countries and the G7 impose tougher sanctions on Russia. He also criticised the easing of sanctions on Russia's energy sector and some Russian billionaires, saying pressure on Moscow and its war machinery must increase.

Dnipro mayor Boris Filatov called the incident a major tragedy for the city. Ukrainian railway officials said more than 1,700 railway installations, including stations, engines and passenger coaches, have been targeted in Russian attacks this year.
