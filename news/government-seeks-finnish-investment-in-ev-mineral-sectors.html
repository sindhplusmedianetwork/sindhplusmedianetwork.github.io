Government seeks Finnish investment in EV, mineral sectors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government is seeking Finnish investment in electric vehicles and minerals, the report said. The 14th Finland-Pakistan Business Summit was held in Islamabad on November 16. Officials said the summit explored new trade and investment opportunities between the two countries.

The electric vehicle and mineral sectors were the focus of the investment sought from Finland. The summit provided the setting for discussions on expanding economic ties between Pakistan and Finland. Officials presented the event as a step towards identifying fresh opportunities for trade and investment.
