Missile strike cripples bridge linking Russia with Crimea

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A section of the bridge connecting Russia to the annexed Crimean peninsula appeared to be lying in rubble in the water on Tuesday after Ukraine hit the road-and-rail link with missiles in an overnight attack. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky hailed the strike as unprecedented and a justified retaliation for Russia's barrages of its infrastructure in the almost five years since Moscow invaded. Road traffic was completely halted, with Russian-installed officials on the peninsula urging locals not to panic and saying the delivery of food and essentials would not be affected.

Moscow also launched attacks on key Ukrainian bridges, firing ballistic missiles at two major river crossings in Kyiv. Reporters saw crater-like holes on Kyiv's Northern Bridge and people crossing on foot during the morning rush hour, and both the Northern and Southern bridges in the capital, key bridges across the Dnipro river that form part of the city's ring road, were hit, with traffic suspended and gridlock on Tuesday morning. Kyiv's Mayor Vitali Klitschko posted a video from the Northern Bridge showing debris strewn across the road and later said traffic was partially resumed through the Southern Bridge, while the strikes also disrupted heating and water in parts of the capital.

Russia said on Tuesday it was aware of French troops having been sent to Ukraine, calling it direct participation in the war, but France rejected the claim as absurd, saying there is a very limited deployment of French troops in Ukraine for training and equipment maintenance, with no intention to fight. The wave of strikes comes as the United States renews its push for an end to the war, but diplomatic efforts have so far failed to reach a breakthrough, with fighting grinding into another winter.
