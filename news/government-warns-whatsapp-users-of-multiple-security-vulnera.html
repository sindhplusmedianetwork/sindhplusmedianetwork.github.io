Government Warns WhatsApp Users of Multiple Security Vulnerabilities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Computer Emergency Response Team (National CERT) has issued an advisory warning WhatsApp users about multiple vulnerabilities affecting the platform across Android, iOS, Windows and Mac desktop versions. The advisory urges users to update WhatsApp immediately. The three-page advisory covers five vulnerabilities fixed in the May and November 2026 updates.

The flaws could allow the deletion of locally stored files, limited leakage of chat database information, disclosure of a device's IP address, and the execution of a malicious file disguised as a harmless document. CERT advised government institutions, public and private organisations and individual users to update the app from official app stores, avoid unofficial or modified clients, and disable automatic media downloading as a defence-in-depth measure.
