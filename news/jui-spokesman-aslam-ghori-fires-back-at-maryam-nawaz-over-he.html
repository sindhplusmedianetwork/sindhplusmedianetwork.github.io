JUI Spokesman Aslam Ghori Fires Back at Maryam Nawaz Over Her Remarks on Fazlur Rehman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam spokesman Aslam Ghori has fired back at Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz over her recent statement about Maulana Fazlur Rehman, reminding her of the JUI chief's past support for her family. Speaking on ARY News programme 'Sawal Yeh Hai', Ghori said Maryam could have conveyed her message to Fazlur Rehman over the phone instead of making the remarks publicly. He recalled that when Maryam's room doors were broken down, Fazlur Rehman was the first person to address her as his daughter, and that JUI workers had prevented her bail from being cancelled when she was being arrested.

'The chair is a cruel thing — whoever sits on it forgets everything,' he remarked, adding that before using harsh words, Maryam should have considered that Fazlur Rehman was like a father to her, and should have remembered his role in bringing the family from London and getting people out of jails. Ghori also took aim at Defence Minister Khawaja Asif, saying he makes such statements merely to stay in the news and should now retire, and at State Minister Tallal Chaudhry, telling him to assess his own stature first. He demanded an in-camera session of Parliament to explain the reasons behind the emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and claimed the PML-N and PPP had no role in the province — criticising the appointment of a governor who, he said, had contested National Assembly elections nine times and won only once, and had even lost a mayoral contest.
