Aadam, Hamza share lead at CNS Open Golf Championship

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Aadam Syed and Hamza Taimur Amin share the lead after the opening round of the 30th Chief of the Naval Staff Open Golf Championship at Karachi Golf Club, both carding matching three-under-par 69s. Aadam reached the joint lead with a bogey-free back nine of 33 after an even-par 36 on the front nine, picking up birdies at the 11th, 15th and 17th holes. Hamza took a contrasting route to the same score, going out in 34 with a birdie on the 8th and adding birdies at the 11th and 13th before a double-bogey six on the 15th stalled his momentum, and he came home in 35.

Three golfers sit one stroke behind on two-under-par 70: Arshad Rasheed and Matloob Ahmad, who both closed with inward 34s, and veteran Muhammad Munir, who went out in 34 and came back in 36. Akber Mehroz and Waheed Baloch are tied sixth at one-under-par 71, while Muddasir Iqbal and Muhammad Saqib share eighth place on level-par 72. In the men's amateur category, Waleed Bilal leads by two strokes after an opening-round two-over-par 74, recovering from 42 on the front nine with a back-nine 32 that included back-to-back twos on the 11th and 12th holes followed by six consecutive fours.

Abdullah Ansar Raja is second alone at 76, Gibran Asghar Khan is third at 78, Muhammad Barik Ayub is fourth on 81, and Muhammad Hassan Shahid, Shawaiz Ahmad Latki and Ali Hamza share fifth place at 82.
