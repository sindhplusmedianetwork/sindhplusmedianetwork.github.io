Seven Palestinians Killed in Gaza Attacks, Including Father and Son

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least seven Palestinians were killed in Israeli air operations and firing in Gaza, including a father and his 13-year-old son. Medical officials said an air strike on a shelter roof in Gaza City's Zeitoun area killed Fadi Rajab and his 13-year-old son Abu Bakr. Further strikes and firing in Daraj, Khan Younis and Sabra raised the day's toll to at least seven.

A 14-year-old Palestinian was also shot dead by Israeli forces in Barqa in the occupied West Bank. UNICEF warned that flooding, poor sanitation and inadequate health and water facilities are raising the risk of infectious disease. The agency said recent heavy rains have flooded thousands of tents of displaced people.
