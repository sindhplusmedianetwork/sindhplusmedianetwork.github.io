Man Robbed of Rs700,000 Mobile Phone at Karachi Food Walk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two men on a motorcycle snatched a mobile phone worth Rs700,000 from a citizen at the food walk in Karachi's Sindhi Muslim Society and fled, and a case of the robbery has been registered. The victim, Salal, said he was recording a video at the food walk when two men arrived on a motorcycle, and the man sitting behind snatched his mobile phone and fled with his accomplice. According to police, the incident took place at 1am on the night of November 4, and the case has been registered at Ferozabad police station on the complaint of the victim.

The citizen had also reported the incident to police helpline 15. Police have obtained CCTV footage from the scene, while the search for the suspects and further investigation into the incident are under way.
