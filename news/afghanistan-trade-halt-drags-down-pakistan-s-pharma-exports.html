Afghanistan Trade Halt Drags Down Pakistan's Pharma Exports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's pharmaceutical exports continued to decline in the first quarter of fiscal year 2026-27, falling 25.53 percent in value to $73.301 million, with the industry largely blaming disrupted trade with Afghanistan. Exports had already contracted 30.26 percent in FY26, dropping to $319.012 million from $457.456 million in FY25, while volumes fell 26.14 percent. Afghanistan was once one of Pakistan's main medicine markets; official data shows Pakistan exported $155.68 million worth of medicines to Afghanistan in FY25, about 34.1 percent of total pharmaceutical exports that year.

Former Pakistan Pharmaceutical Manufacturers Association chairman Tauqeer ul Haq said the suspension of trade caused heavy losses for pharmaceutical companies and the national exchequer. He added that Afghan patients were also suffering as they had to turn to more expensive medicines. He said the association raised the issue with the government and Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal promised to discuss it with the relevant authorities, but there had been little serious follow-up.
