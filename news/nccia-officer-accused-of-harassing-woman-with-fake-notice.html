NCCIA Officer Accused of Harassing Woman with Fake Notice

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A case of harassment of a woman through a fake notice by the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has come to light in Karachi. According to the woman's lawyer, NCCIA Sub-Inspector Chaudhry Imranullah issued a notice to the complainant woman. The sub-inspector had first sent her a message asking her to meet at a nightclub, and sent the notice after she refused.

The authorities have started departmental action, and the NCCIA filed its reply in the matter. The petition against the alleged harassment was disposed of on Thursday after a hearing in the High Court, where NCCIA officials and the petitioner's lawyers appeared. The episode has raised questions about the misuse of official notices to pressure a complainant.
