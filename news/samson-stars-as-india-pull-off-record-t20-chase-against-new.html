Samson Stars as India Pull Off Record T20 Chase Against New Zealand

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sanju Samson's 33-ball 67 powered India to a four-wicket win over New Zealand in Christchurch, chasing 226 — India's highest-ever T20 total — with two balls to spare. The match was a rematch of the T20 World Cup final. Shreyas Iyer contributed 42 before Naman Dhir's 15-ball 42 sealed the victory.

India now lead the five-match series 1-0. The record chase thrilled fans as India overhauled a daunting target in the final overs. It was a statement win for the visitors in the series opener.
