State Bank injects Rs 1,049.85 billion to maintain market liquidity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The State Bank of Pakistan injected Rs 1,049.85 billion into the market on Friday through open market operations to maintain liquidity. The injection was made through reverse repo purchases and Shariah-compliant Mudarabah-based operations. Of the total, Rs 854.65 billion came through reverse repo purchases, while Rs 195.2 billion was injected through Shariah-compliant Mudarabah operations.

Funds were offered for seven- and 14-day tenors. For the 14-day reverse repo, the central bank accepted Rs 722 billion at a 12.06 percent rate of return.
