Pakistan to Take $600m World Bank Loan Tied to Reform Laws

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan is set to take a $600 million World Bank loan under a new reform-linked programme. The loan falls under the 'Bold Reforms for Investment-Driven Growth and Employment (BRIDGE) Programme', with an estimated approval date of January 29, 2027. In return, the government has assured the lender it will enact the Investment Act and the Factoring Law.

It has also committed to building a database of overseas workers, allowing free international trade in sugar and wheat, and increasing the private sector's role in the economy. The package ties financing to structural reforms aimed at investment-driven growth and employment.
