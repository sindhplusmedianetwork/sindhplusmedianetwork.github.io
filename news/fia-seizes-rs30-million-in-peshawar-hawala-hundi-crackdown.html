FIA seizes Rs30 million in Peshawar hawala-hundi crackdown

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has arrested two suspects and recovered Rs30 million in a crackdown on illegal hawala-hundi business in Peshawar. The FIA spokesperson said the Commercial Banking Circle Peshawar team carried out the raid in Peshawar Saddar Cantt on November 2. The suspects were allegedly running an illegal money-transfer network from the area.

Cash recovered from their possession has been taken into custody as evidence. A case has been registered against both suspects and further investigation is under way. The FIA said operations against hawala-hundi networks will continue.
