Hollywood actress Anne Hathaway gives birth to third child

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hollywood actress Anne Hathaway and her husband Adam Shulman have welcomed their third child. The actress is already the mother of two sons, 10-year-old Jonathan and six-year-old Jack. According to a report in The News International, Hathaway had expressed her happiness during a TV interview while pregnant, saying she was delighted to become a mother again and that the new addition to the family would bring more joy to her life.

She said in the interview that the birth of her third child would bring a big change to her family life, and that she wanted to enjoy every moment of the new phase to the fullest. Hathaway had recently taken part in promotional activities for five of her films. She had also indicated that she would stay away from social media for a while after the birth of her third child.
