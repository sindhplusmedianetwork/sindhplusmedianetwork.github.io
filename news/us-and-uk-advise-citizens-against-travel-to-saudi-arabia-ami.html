US and UK Advise Citizens Against Travel to Saudi Arabia Amid Attack Fears

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States and Britain have advised their citizens to exercise caution and avoid unnecessary travel to Saudi Arabia amid security concerns and fears of further attacks on airports. Britain's Foreign and Commonwealth Development Office warned of possible flight cancellations and advised avoiding all travel within 10km of the Yemen border and only essential travel elsewhere in the kingdom. The US embassy in Riyadh issued a security alert after recent missile and drone attacks, saying operations at King Khalid International Airport remain suspended while Jeddah and Dammam airports face delays and cancellations.

The advisories reflect growing alarm over the security situation in the kingdom as attacks on Saudi airports continue. Both governments urged their nationals already in Saudi Arabia to stay alert and follow local guidance.
