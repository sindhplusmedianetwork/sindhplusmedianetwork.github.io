My Daughter Studied the Quran at 15, Reveals Bill Clinton as He Criticises Trump's Anti-Muslim Rhetoric

Former US President Bill Clinton has criticised President Donald Trump's anti-Muslim rhetoric, saying he is tired of those who defame Muslims merely for being Muslim. Clinton was addressing an event at a church in South Florida during the campaign for Democratic gubernatorial candidate David Jolly ahead of the midterm elections. He criticised what he called Americans' inappropriate behaviour toward Muslims and cited Quranic teachings in his remarks.

Clinton revealed that his daughter had studied the Quran at the age of 15 and had told him it teaches that God divided humans into nations and tribes. He said people should not belittle one another, adding that every religion has bad elements and everyone has flaws.
