Bad light ends day four of Rawalpindi Test as Pakistan lead Sri Lanka by 45 runs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Play on the fourth day of the first Test between Pakistan and Sri Lanka at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium was ended early because of bad light, with Pakistan reaching 57 for one in their second innings and an overall lead of 45 runs. Sri Lanka had resumed their first innings on 404 for four but were bowled out for 446, adding only 42 runs and losing their last six wickets, after debutant fast bowler Saqib Khan produced a sensational spell. Saqib finished with magnificent figures of seven wickets for 130 runs from 34 overs, removing the well-set Dhananjaya de Silva for 167 and captain Kusal Mendis for 119, ending their massive 249-run partnership, and then dismissing Sonal Dinusha, Lahiru Kumara and Asitha Fernando, while Mohammad Abbas claimed two wickets and Razaullah picked up one.

Sri Lanka still secured a narrow 12-run first-innings lead over Pakistan's first-innings total of 434 for nine declared, in which Saud Shakeel scored 93, Tayyab Arif 57, Arafat Minhas 52, Mohammad Taha 51 and captain Babar Azam 50. In Pakistan's second innings, Tayyab Arif was dismissed for 11, while Mohammad Taha, on 24, and Azan Awais, on 22, remained at the crease when deteriorating light stopped play. The interruption has sparked fresh debate over whether the second Test at the same venue should be played with a pink ball to minimise interruptions caused by poor visibility, although there has been no official announcement about any change to the format.
