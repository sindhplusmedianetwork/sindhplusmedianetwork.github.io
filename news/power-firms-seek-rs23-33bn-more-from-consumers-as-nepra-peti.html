Power firms seek Rs23.33bn more from consumers as NEPRA petition filed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Power distribution companies have applied to the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (NEPRA) to recover Rs23.33 billion from consumers under the quarterly adjustment for July to September 2026. The petition seeks Rs38.92 billion in capacity charges and Rs5.597 billion more for operations and maintenance. At the same time, the companies have requested a Rs29.25 billion reduction on account of incremental package effects, which brings the net additional burden to Rs23.33 billion.

NEPRA will hear the petition on November 17, after which it will decide how much the per-unit tariff will rise. The adjustment will also apply to K-Electric consumers. If approved, the increase will add to the electricity bills of consumers across the country, at a time when power costs remain a major burden on households and businesses.
