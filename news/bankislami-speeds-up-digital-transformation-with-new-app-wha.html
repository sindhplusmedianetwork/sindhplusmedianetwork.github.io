BankIslami Speeds Up Digital Transformation With New App, WhatsApp Banking and Payments Platform

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

BankIslami has accelerated its digital transformation with the launch of a redesigned mobile banking app offering more than 60 features, along with WhatsApp banking. The bank has also migrated to Euronet's payments platform. According to the bank, 61 percent of its transaction volume is now digital, and about Rs557 billion moved through the mobile app in the last quarter.

The new app allows transaction limits up to Rs5 million, scheduled bill payments, bulk payments and card management. It also offers Urdu language support and an Islamic hub with a Qibla locator and prayer reminders. The bank plans to extend its digital offering to Sharia-compliant deposits, financing, investment and takaful solutions.
