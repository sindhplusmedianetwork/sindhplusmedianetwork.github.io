Alia Bhatt Says Motherhood Has Reshaped Her Career Priorities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian actor Alia Bhatt has said that while she loves being in the spotlight, spending time with her daughter remains a priority, explaining how motherhood has reshaped her approach to work and professional commitments. Speaking to TIME100, Bhatt reflected on balancing her passion for cinema with her responsibilities as a mother, stressing that she did not want to give up either. Since welcoming her daughter in 2022, Bhatt has adopted a more measured approach to her career and said that when she decided to return to work after giving birth, she was already clear about the number of days she could dedicate to a film each month.

While she remains willing to accommodate genuine production requirements, Bhatt believes flexibility should not come at the expense of reasonable working hours and called for a more organised approach to filmmaking with better scheduling and clearly defined working hours. Her comments echoed the wider conversation around working conditions in Bollywood, particularly following Deepika Padukone's remarks about the industry's demanding 15-to-18-hour shooting schedules. Bhatt acknowledged that her professional standing had helped her secure accommodating producers, but stressed that fair working hours should become standard across the industry rather than a privilege reserved for established stars.

The actor also spoke about using her position as a producer to bring lasting changes including better facilities for women and clean toilets for crew members, saying a woman being able to use a clean bathroom on a film set should not be a conversation in 2026, and her TIME100 Next India recognition came after more than a decade in the film industry since her debut in Student of the Year in 2012.
