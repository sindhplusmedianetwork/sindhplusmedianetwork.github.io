OpenAI Expands ChatGPT Ads to Pakistan and 25 More Countries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

OpenAI is taking ChatGPT advertising to dozens of new markets, including Pakistan. The company announced that ads on ChatGPT will expand to 26 more countries — among them Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, South Africa, Serbia, Ukraine and Cambodia, plus 14 Latin American nations — bringing the total to 89 markets. The move comes as OpenAI pushes to grow revenue ahead of a potential public listing.

Advertisements will appear only for users on the Free and Go plans; Plus, Pro and Enterprise subscribers will continue to see no ads. OpenAI says more than 100,000 advertisers have run campaigns on the platform since testing began in the United States. The ads business has reached a $1 billion annualised revenue run rate as of August.

The expansion marks one of the most aggressive monetisation steps yet for the world's most widely used AI chatbot.
