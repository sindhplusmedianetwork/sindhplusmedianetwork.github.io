LHC Seeks Reports From Lahore, Sheikhupura DCs on Detention of PTI Workers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has sought reports from the deputy commissioners of Lahore and Sheikhupura on the detention of PTI workers. Justice Abher Gul Khan heard petitions filed by Rawal Shahzad and others against the detentions. Advocate Irfan Klar told the court that PTI workers, including Rashid and Javed Iqbal, had been detained and were not released even after their detention periods ended.

He said the deputy commissioners of Lahore and Sheikhupura had issued fresh detention notifications, calling continued detention without cause unconstitutional. The lawyer prayed that the court order the workers' release and declare the detentions void. The court directed both deputy commissioners to submit their reports on the matter.
