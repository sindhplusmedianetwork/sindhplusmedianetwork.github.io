Child among six killed by Israeli forces across Gaza Strip

Israeli forces killed six people across the Gaza Strip on Tuesday, including a six-year-old boy, the civil defence agency and hospitals in the war-ravaged Palestinian territory said. Israel and Hamas regularly accuse each other of violating the ceasefire that came into effect in October 2025, after two years of conflict. Mahmoud Bassal, spokesman for Gaza's civil defence agency, said a total of six people were killed and several injured as a result of Israeli fire on Tuesday, while Israel's military said it was looking into the reports, adding that it struck several terrorists in Gaza on Tuesday and was not aware of any six-year-old killed by its fire.

Bassal said two people, including a 21-year-old and a six-year-old boy, were killed by Israeli fire in the central Gaza city of Deir el-Balah, and Nasser Hospital, in Gaza's south, confirmed the two deaths and identified the child as Iyas al-Namnam. Bassal said two people were killed in an Israeli drone bombing near northern Gaza's Jabalia camp, with Al-Shifa hospital in nearby Gaza City confirming the two deaths, and one man was killed by bombing and another by Israeli live fire in southern Gaza, with Nasser Hospital confirming both deaths. More than 1,600 Palestinians have been killed since the start of the ceasefire, according to a tally based on data from the Gaza health ministry, whose figures are considered reliable by the United Nations.

The UN migration agency warned on Tuesday of further deaths from hypothermia in Gaza and called for more funding and shelter supplies ahead of winter after a baby died from cold, with heavy rain already flooding tens of thousands of tents housing displaced Palestinians. The agency has a 173 million dollar funding shortfall for its winter shelter programmes, and its Gaza programme head Karl Baker said some 100,000 tents were currently waiting to enter Gaza, with flows hindered by crossing closures and lengthy Israeli security checks.
