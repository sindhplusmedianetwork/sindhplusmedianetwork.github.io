UK Warns Citizens in Saudi Arabia of Missile and Drone Debris Risk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Kingdom has advised its citizens in Saudi Arabia to remain cautious and follow the instructions of local authorities in view of possible missile and drone attacks. In safety guidance issued on social media, the British Embassy in Riyadh and the British Consulate in Jeddah warned that after missiles and drones are intercepted and destroyed in the air, their debris falling to the ground can also become a major threat to human lives. The British diplomatic missions advised their citizens that if they are instructed to move to a safe place or take shelter, they should immediately go inside a safe building and stay there.

According to the guidance, staying inside a strong building is comparatively safer, and for this purpose inner staircases or rooms with fewer outer walls and windows can be used. British authorities urged citizens to follow the safety guidance issued by local Saudi authorities and to avoid staying in open places in case of danger. The guidance has been issued at a time when, amid fears of missile and drone attacks in the region, different countries are taking steps for the safety of their citizens.
