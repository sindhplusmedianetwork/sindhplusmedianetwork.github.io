AI Decodes Napoleon-Era Secret Letter in Just Six Hours

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Artificial intelligence is helping researchers solve historical codes that have remained unbroken for centuries. A coded letter commissioned by Napoleon Bonaparte in 1809 was deciphered in just six hours using OpenAI's latest model. Carter Church, a 27-year-old AI engineer at US cybersecurity firm SentinelOne, decoded the letter, which detailed French and allied troop positions.

The document had sat overlooked in an online academic archive for more than 200 years. Beyond code-breaking, AI is also being used to virtually unroll carbonised papyri from the Roman-era eruption of Mount Vesuvius, recovering texts once thought lost forever.
