Meeting on Country's Situation Expected at Aiwan-e-Sadr With Important Personality

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A meeting on the country's situation at Aiwan-e-Sadr with an important personality was expected shortly, Daily Pakistan reported on Friday. According to private TV channel ARY News, a meeting of an important personality with President Asif Zardari and Bilawal Bhutto over dinner was expected after a short while. The country's situation will be discussed in the meeting to be held at Aiwan-e-Sadr.

Federal Minister for Information and Broadcasting Attaullah Tarar had said that an important meeting between Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and President Asif Ali Zardari on the referendum had already taken place the previous day. Addressing a press conference in Islamabad, Attaullah Tarar had said public opinion on new provinces cannot be stopped, the public's opinion on provinces should come and should be given precedence, and whatever opinion comes from the public, the next process will be carried out after that. The previous day, an important meeting between President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif was held at Aiwan-e-Sadr in which the current political situation was reviewed in detail, and both leaders agreed to resolve all matters through consultation.
