Government to impose up to 10% levy on petrol and diesel vehicles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has decided to impose an Automotive Development Levy of between 2% and 10% on imported and locally manufactured petrol and diesel vehicles. The levy is part of a push to speed up the adoption of electric vehicles across the country. The lowest rate will apply to public service vehicles and vehicles of up to 2,000cc.

Two- and three-wheelers, tractors and vehicles of up to 1,000cc will be exempt from the levy altogether. New energy vehicles will continue to benefit from lower sales tax rates of 1% for battery-electric vehicles, 6% for range-extended vehicles and 9% for plug-in hybrids. The State Bank may also raise the limit on vehicle financing to Rs10 million.

The measures are aimed at making electric and new-energy vehicles more attractive compared with conventional petrol and diesel models.
