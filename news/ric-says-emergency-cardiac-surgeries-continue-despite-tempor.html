RIC Says Emergency Cardiac Surgeries Continue Despite Temporary Valve Procurement Disruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Rawalpindi Institute of Cardiology has clarified that its cardiac surgery services are continuing without interruption, rejecting reports aired on a private television channel about alleged delays in emergency procedures. In an official statement, a spokesperson explained that the patient featured in the news report was awaiting an elective surgery, where immediate surgical intervention is not medically urgent, rather than an emergency case. The spokesperson emphasised that essential cardiac valves remain readily available for all emergency and urgent patients, and confirmed that no emergency procedure has been halted due to a shortage of valves.

Surgical prioritisation is determined strictly by clinical condition and registration seniority, without any discrimination based on payment mode or healthcare programme, the spokesperson said. Addressing supply logistics, the spokesperson noted that procurement for elective cases had been temporarily disrupted by a legal stay order on the centralised tender, but a fresh tendering process has been completed under the Punjab Procurement Rules. A Letter of Intent has already been issued to the successful bidder, and the new consignment of valves is expected to arrive within 15 days.

Highlighting recent clinical performance, the spokesperson stated that since July 2026 the institute has performed 320 adult open-heart surgeries and 236 paediatric cardiac procedures free of cost under government health initiatives. The spokesperson urged media outlets to refrain from broadcasting patients' confidential details, including names, CNIC numbers and contact information, and requested journalists to obtain the hospital's official version before airing or publishing reports.
