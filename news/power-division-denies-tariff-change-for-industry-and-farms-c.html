Power Division Denies Tariff Change for Industry and Farms, Cites Rs43.5bn Relief

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Power Division has clarified reports about changes to electricity tariffs for industrial and agricultural consumers, saying the government has made no change to their current tariffs. A spokesperson said the price of Rs22.98 per unit applies to additional electricity use, while only a slight increase on extra consumption has been proposed. Under the incremental package, 209,335 industrial and agricultural consumers benefited by September 2026, about 40 percent of the 518,316 eligible consumers.

Between December 2025 and September 2026, consumers used an extra 6,940 gigawatt-hours of electricity, receiving relief worth Rs43.5 billion. Industrial consumers received relief of Rs5 to Rs10 per unit on extra use, while overall electricity bills fell by an average of Rs1.50 to Rs2.50 per unit. The division said fuel has become expensive in the global market and regional tensions have raised generation costs, but the aim is to avoid placing an extra burden on other consumers.

It added that industrial electricity rates have fallen by Rs18.94 per unit compared with March 2024, and the average price for industrial consumers in October 2026 was Rs34.40 per unit.
