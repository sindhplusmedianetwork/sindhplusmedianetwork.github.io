Joe Biden Speaks Openly About Living With Cancer for the First Time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former United States president Joe Biden has spoken openly for the first time about his fight against cancer. The 83-year-old said the illness and the treatment he is receiving have limited his activities. He explained that he is undergoing continuous treatment as part of his care.

Biden also said that his pace has slowed since the illness and treatment began. Despite these limits, he said his doctors are satisfied with how things are going. His doctors are also hopeful about his progress, he said.

The remarks mark the first time Biden has discussed living with the illness in this open way. He spoke about the effect the illness has had on his daily life and the steady course of treatment he continues to receive.
