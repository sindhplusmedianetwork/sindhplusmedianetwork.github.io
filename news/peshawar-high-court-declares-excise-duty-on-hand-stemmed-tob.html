Peshawar High Court declares excise duty on hand-stemmed tobacco illegal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Peshawar High Court has declared that federal excise duty could not be imposed on green leaf and hand-stemmed tobacco unless it has been processed and converted by a green leaf threshing unit. A bench consisting of Justice Syed Arshad Ali and Justice Mohammad Faheem Wali disposed of 14 petitions filed by tobacco growers, tobacco companies and threshing units, which had challenged the levy of duty, the sealing of warehouses and the confiscation of un-manufactured tobacco and machinery. In its 29-page detailed judgment, the bench ruled that green leaf and hand-stemmed tobacco which had not undergone processing through a threshing unit did not fall within the definition of unmanufactured tobacco under the Federal Excise Act, 2005, and was therefore not liable to duty.

The court directed the authorities to release the seized tobacco, vehicles, equipment and records and de-seal the premises and machinery forthwith. It also set aside show-cause notices demanding duty, including one of 123.992 million rupees from Royal Tobacco Company, and ordered its godowns to be de-sealed and 628,145 kg of hand-stemmed tobacco and 130,000 kg of green leaf tobacco to be released. The bench also ordered the release of 3,375 kg of hand-stemmed tobacco and a vehicle belonging to Pak Hills Tobacco Company, while leaving any sales tax liability on the consignment to be determined in accordance with law.

The bench held that hand-stemming, even when carried out with the use of tools or machinery, could not by itself be treated as a manufacturing process to justify the levy of federal excise duty.
