PM Warns Tax Officers: No Resistance to FBR Reforms Will Be Tolerated

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Monday warned tax officers that resistance to the ongoing reforms in the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) would not be tolerated, directing the authorities to implement measures to minimise the country's tax gap. The warning came during a weekly briefing on tax reforms, following a call for a pen-down strike by the Inland Revenue Officers Association, which was later withdrawn after intervention by senior FBR officials. FBR Chairman Rashid Mahmood Langrial briefed the prime minister on the pilot project for faceless assessment and audit.

The prime minister said officers unwilling to work would be asked to leave, reminding them that their salaries had been increased fourfold and that they had been provided with vehicles and accommodation. The FBR has established the National Faceless Centre in Islamabad, initially staffed by 27 officers, to handle audits and assessments through a faceless system aimed at reducing official discretion and direct contact between tax officials and taxpayers. The centre has been tasked with auditing third-party data relating to 300,000 to 400,000 individuals under a pilot project that became effective on October 1.

Meanwhile, the FBR suspended two Inland Revenue Service officers and repatriated two others to FBR headquarters for further investigation over an unauthorised press statement and a call to suspend official work.
