Italian Court to Decide First Climate Lawsuit Against the State

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Italian court is expected to deliver its verdict in the country's first climate lawsuit filed against the state, known as 'Giudizio Universale' or 'Last Judgment'. The case was launched in June 2021 by the environmental group A Sud together with more than 200 other associations and individuals. The claimants accuse the Italian state of violating fundamental rights by failing to take sufficient action under the 2015 Paris Agreement.

They are asking the court to order the government to cut emissions by 92 percent compared with 1990 levels by 2030. Legal observers say the ruling could set a major precedent for climate litigation across Europe. Environmental groups have called the case a landmark moment for holding governments accountable on climate policy.
