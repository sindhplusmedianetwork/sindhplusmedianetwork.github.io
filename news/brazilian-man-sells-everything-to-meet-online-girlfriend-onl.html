Brazilian Man Sells Everything to Meet Online Girlfriend — Only to Find She Was AI

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 49-year-old Brazilian labourer has been left heartbroken after travelling nearly 2,000 kilometres to meet his online girlfriend — who turned out to be an artificial intelligence creation. Edmar Rocha Martins sold his belongings and boarded a bus for the long journey to meet 'Jessica Oliveira', a woman he had been speaking to on social media. The account portrayed a woman who claimed to live on a farm and was looking for a life partner.

When Martins arrived, he discovered that Jessica did not exist: the persona was AI-generated and run by an operator who said he had created it only to gain attention and had never invited the man to visit. Stranded far from home, Martins was assisted by local authorities, who arranged a bus ticket for his journey back. The incident has drawn attention to the growing risks of AI-generated personas and online romance scams.
