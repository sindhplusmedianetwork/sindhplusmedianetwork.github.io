Govt drafts marble policy to curb wastage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government is developing the Marble Policy 2026-29 to modernise the industry through advanced technology and value addition, covering the value chain from quarrying to exports. Adviser to the Prime Minister for Industries and Production Haroon Akhtar Khan chaired a meeting with Marble Association representatives to discuss a policy to make the sector globally competitive, the Ministry of Industries said on Wednesday. Khan said Pakistan possessed potential in marble resources, but this required a shift towards scientific mining practices and competitive production standards.

He said the priority was to prevent the wastage of valuable raw materials, introduce modern mining techniques and improve the overall quality and productivity of the industry, highlighting the need to equip it with modern machinery, skilled manpower and quality testing laboratories. He said sustainable access to raw materials and processing technologies was essential for strengthening the sector and improving the quality of Pakistani marble in international markets. The policy would also address infrastructure and logistics requirements, including road and rail connectivity and port facilities, and Khan said Pakistan needed to strengthen links to international buyers, investors and technology providers, noting that a visit by Italian experts would assess the industry's potential and technological requirements.

The government would work on a proposal to organise an international marble exhibition, with a committee directed to develop a comprehensive proposal and give recommendations within one week, while consultations on the policy would be completed within four weeks before implementation.
