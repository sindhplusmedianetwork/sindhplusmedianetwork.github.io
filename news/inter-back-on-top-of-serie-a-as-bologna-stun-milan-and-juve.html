Inter back on top of Serie A as Bologna stun Milan and Juve lose again

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Inter Milan returned to the top of Serie A with a 2-0 win at Venezia on Wednesday, drawing level with Lazio on 25 points ahead of Saturday's Milan derby. The champions needed two own goals within two minutes — from Matias Moreno and Redouane Halhal — to overcome the bottom-placed hosts after a nervy display that coach Cristian Chivu admitted was not ideal derby preparation. AC Milan suffered a first league defeat of the season, losing 4-2 at home to Bologna after Samuel Mbangula's hat-trick on his full debut.

Mbangula struck inside eight minutes, again just after the hour and completed the treble past Mike Maignan, while Alphadjo Cisse's double and a Davide Bartesaghi own goal shaped the scoreline. Milan coach Ruben Amorim blamed fatigue and an injury list that included Adrien Rabiot, Diego Moreira and Mario Gila. Juventus slipped to a 2-0 defeat at Genoa through early goals from Kingsley Ehizibue and Tommaso Baldanzi, and have still not won back-to-back league games since the opening fortnight.

Napoli thrashed Monza 4-1, with Rasmus Hojlund, Matteo Politano, Rafa Marin and substitute Kevin De Bruyne on target, despite coach Massimiliano Allegri being suspended and midfielder Stanislav Lobotka being stretchered off. Napoli, who visit Juventus on Sunday, moved sixth, level on 14 points with Juve.
