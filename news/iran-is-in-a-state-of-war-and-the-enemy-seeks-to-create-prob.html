Iran is in a state of war and the enemy seeks to create problems inside the country, says Pezeshkian

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian has said that Iran is currently in a state of war and the enemy is trying to create various problems inside the country. He said people must show patience and perseverance in the current situation. Pezeshkian stressed that it is necessary to inform the public about the problems and challenges facing the country so they can understand them better.

He said he will continue supporting the armed forces in line with national resources. He also said he will keep working to solve public problems with the resources available. The president's remarks underlined the need for public awareness and steadfastness at a time of serious challenges for the country.
