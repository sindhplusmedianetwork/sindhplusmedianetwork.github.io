Research Finds Hundreds of Satellites Above UK Cities at Night

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Research by engineering company Accu Components has examined satellites visible above Britain at night. The research says that over 186 cities and towns in Britain, an average of 710 satellites are above the horizon at any moment during the night. It also says that about 13,000 different spacecraft pass over a typical city in one night.

According to the research, about 70 percent of active satellites in low Earth orbit are part of Elon Musk's Starlink network. The number of Starlink satellites rose from 120 in December 2019 to more than 11,000 in October 2026. Experts say bright satellites are affecting telescope images and astronomical research.
