Maryam Nawaz's 'Slap for a Slap' Stance: Do Laws Allow It?

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's recent tweets and speeches endorsing the slapping of suspects have drawn sharp criticism from the Human Rights Commission of Pakistan and legal expert Abid Saqi. They say the law permits neither the torture of detainees nor punishment by anyone except the courts. Maryam defended herself at a Mandi Bahauddin rally, saying she does not care about criticism, that women and children are her red line, and that she would speak to the Lahore High Court chief justice about ensuring criminals are properly sentenced.

The debate followed cases such as the assault on a schoolgirl in Lahore.
