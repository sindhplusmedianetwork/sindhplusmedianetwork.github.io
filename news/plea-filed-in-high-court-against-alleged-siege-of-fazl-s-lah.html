Plea filed in high court against alleged siege of Fazl's Lahore residence

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A plea challenging the alleged siege of the JUI-F chief's residence in Lahore was filed in the Lahore High Court on Tuesday. The petition, filed by party lawyer Advocate Abrar Hussain, has yet to be fixed for hearing. In the petition, the lawyer alleged that around 3,000 police personnel had been deployed outside the residence, effectively confining the party chief to his house.

The petitioner said he had sought a copy of the legal order authorising the detention or confinement, but the authorities did not share any such document, and police had also denied him permission to meet the party chief. He asked the court to direct the police to lift the siege of the residence. According to the party, the JUI-F chief had reached Lahore on Monday night and was scheduled to address lawyers at the invitation of the bar association, but personnel deployed outside his house restricted him from leaving.

Separately, Section 144 was imposed in Lahore for three days, from November 24 to 26, banning the assembly of five or more people, processions and gatherings in public places, after reports of a serious and imminent threat to public peace and safety.
