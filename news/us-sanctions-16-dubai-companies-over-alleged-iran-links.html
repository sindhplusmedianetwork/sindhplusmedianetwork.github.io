US sanctions 16 Dubai companies over alleged Iran links

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has imposed sanctions on 22 entities, including 16 companies based in Dubai, and eight individuals over alleged links to Iran's informal banking network. The US Treasury said the action, taken under Operation Economic Outcast, targets firms that allegedly helped Iran conduct foreign trade and financial transactions despite sanctions. The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network separately identified about $14.7 billion in suspected transactions linked to the network.

The sanctions form part of Washington's wider campaign against the financial channels it says Iran uses to bypass restrictions. The targeted companies and individuals are accused of facilitating trade and money flows for Iran through informal banking routes. The action adds further pressure on the networks the US says support Iran's economy despite international sanctions.
