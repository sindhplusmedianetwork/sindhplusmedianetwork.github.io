Gaza tents flood in heavy rain, children among those killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Heavy rains in Gaza have devastated refugee settlements, with an infant reported drowned and four children and a woman killed after floodwater and a collapsing sand dune hit tents. UN Deputy Spokesperson Farhan Haq said UN aid teams are pumping out floodwater and improving drainage at the sites. He said the teams are reinforcing sites with sandbags and delivering emergency supplies to the affected families.

The UN has called for restrictions on the entry of aid and machinery into Gaza to be lifted urgently.
