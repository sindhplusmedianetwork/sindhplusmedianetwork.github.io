UN nuclear watchdog says US-Saudi nuclear agreement 'satisfactory'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The head of the International Atomic Energy Agency, Rafael Grossi, has welcomed the agreement between the United States and Saudi Arabia on equipping the kingdom with a civilian nuclear programme, calling it a very satisfactory outcome. Speaking to reporters after opening a meeting of the IAEA board of governors, Grossi said that once nuclear power development gets under way in Saudi Arabia, the agency will be there. He said the two countries had asked the IAEA in October to take the necessary steps to implement safeguards and verification measures for their agreement.

Under the accord, the IAEA will be given information about, and access to, any sites for conversion, enrichment, fuel fabrication and reprocessing in Saudi Arabia. Grossi said the agreement was fully aligned with the legal framework of the IAEA and welcomed what he called the open and transparent approach of both governments. He added that Saudi Arabia had also agreed to additional verification measures on a voluntary basis.

The deal, announced in July, had raised questions about the risks of nuclear proliferation, and US media have reported it would allow Washington to export technology without the usual safeguards preventing future military use.
