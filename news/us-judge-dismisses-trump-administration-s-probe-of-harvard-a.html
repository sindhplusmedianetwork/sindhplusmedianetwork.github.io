US Judge Dismisses Trump Administration's Probe of Harvard Admissions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A day before the US midterm elections, a federal judge in Boston dismissed the Trump administration's bid to obtain Harvard University's student admissions records. District Judge Myong Joun threw out the Justice Department's lawsuit on Monday. The administration had sought years of admissions records to investigate whether Harvard still gives weight to race in admissions, arguing the data was needed to enforce the 2023 US Supreme Court ruling that barred race-conscious admissions.

Harvard countered that the government filed its case without meeting legal requirements and demanded the private information of thousands of students, calling it part of a broader campaign of pressure on the university. Courts had previously ruled against the government on freezing more than two billion dollars in federal research funds and restricting Harvard's ability to host foreign students. The midterms on Tuesday will decide all 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats, with Trump facing close contests in 11 Senate races.
