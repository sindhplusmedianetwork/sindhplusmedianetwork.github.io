US Man, 64, Arrested for Taping Wife to Chair to Watch Football in Peace

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A 64-year-old man in the US state of Virginia has been arrested after allegedly taping his wife to a chair so he could watch a football match undisturbed. Terry Ward is accused of binding his wife to a chair in the basement of their home and leaving her there. According to the woman's statement to police, she remained tied up for about 45 minutes.

Officers found marks and tape residue on both of her arms. Police said Ward admitted to tying his wife up and leaving her in the basement. He has been charged with abduction and domestic violence.

Ward is due to be produced in court on November 10. It remains unclear which football match he was so determined to watch in peace.
