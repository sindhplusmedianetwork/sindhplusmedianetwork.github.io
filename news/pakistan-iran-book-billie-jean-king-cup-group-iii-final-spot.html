Pakistan, Iran Book Billie Jean King Cup Group III Final Spots

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Iran have qualified for the final of the Billie Jean King Cup Asia/Oceania Group III in Islamabad. Hosts Pakistan defeated top seeds Maldives 2-1 in Group A, with singles victories from Ushna Suhail and Mahin Aftab Qureshi sealing the tie. Iran cruised past Qatar 3-0 in Group B without dropping a match.

The two sides will meet in Saturday's final at the PTF Tennis Complex to decide the Group III champions. The final offers both teams a chance to finish the tournament on top of their group. Home fans will be backing Pakistan to claim the title on home courts.
