Ball-Tampering Row: Former Australian Bowler Chad Sayers Backs Usman Khawaja

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Australian fast bowler Chad Sayers has challenged Pat Cummins' account of the ball-tampering scandal and backed Usman Khawaja. Sayers, who was the 12th man during the 2018 Newlands Test, made the remarks while speaking to SEN radio. He said he believes every member of the team knew sandpaper was being used to get the ball to reverse swing and that it was discussed in the dressing room several times.

He revealed he was handed the sandpaper to take off the field, as the plan was for Cameron Bancroft to tamper with the ball and for the sandpaper to be removed while the umpires had the ball on the ground. Sayers said the team debated for 60 to 90 minutes over publicly accepting responsibility, with disagreement over Cummins' position. He said he now feels relieved to speak openly about what happened.
