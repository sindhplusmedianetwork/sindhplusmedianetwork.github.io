Islamabad ATC Sends Former G-B CM Khalid Khurshid to Jail on 14-Day Judicial Remand

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-terrorism court (ATC) in Islamabad on Saturday rejected the police's request for a 30-day physical remand of former Gilgit-Baltistan chief minister and PTI leader Khalid Khurshid, sending him to jail on a 14-day judicial remand. Khurshid, who was arrested by the federal police, was produced before ATC Judge Abul Hasnat Muhammad Zulqarnain. A case has been registered against Khurshid at Islamabad's Aabpara police station in connection with a protest held on November 26.

In December 2024, an ATC had sentenced Khurshid to 34 years in prison on charges of threatening security agencies, stemming from an incident on May 26, 2024, when he allegedly threatened G-B's chief secretary, chief election commissioner and security agencies at a PTI public gathering. Khurshid served as the region's chief minister from 2020 until his disqualification in July 2023, after the G-B Chief Court disqualified him for submitting a fake degree — purportedly from the University of London and later verified as fake by the Higher Education Commission — during his nomination process. Khurshid, who joined PTI in 2018, has also been booked in multiple cases, including charges related to the party's protests at D-Chowk in Islamabad in October.
