Rain and snowfall forecast for northern areas; cold winds likely to reach Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Meteorological Department has forecast rain with strong winds and thunderstorms in the country's northern areas over the next two to three days, with snowfall on the mountains. Rain with thunder is also expected in Balochistan districts including Barkhan, Zhob, Musa Khel, Quetta, Ziarat, Chaman, Qila Saifullah, Qila Abdullah, Kalat and Khuzdar. Cold winds after the rain in Balochistan may affect Karachi.

Weather in Sindh is expected to stay dry for the next few days. The forecast covers the next two to three days.
