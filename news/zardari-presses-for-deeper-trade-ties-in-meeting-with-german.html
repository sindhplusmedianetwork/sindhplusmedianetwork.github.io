Zardari presses for deeper trade ties in meeting with German president

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari on Friday stressed the need to realise the available potential to expand political, economic and defence cooperation between Pakistan and Germany, with a greater focus on trade, emerging technologies, climate resilience, skills development and people-to-people and cultural exchanges. During a meeting with German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier in Berlin, President Zardari thanked him for his invitation and the warm hospitality extended to him and his delegation. He said the visit reflected Pakistan's commitment to deepening its longstanding cordial and friendly relations with Germany and to turning them into a stronger and mutually beneficial partnership through sustained engagement.

President Zardari said Pakistan was prepared to expand cooperation into scientific development, healthcare and education, vocational training, technological advancement, trade and investment, agriculture, commerce and industry, and climate resilience. The two presidents also discussed the challenges related to Afghanistan, with President Zardari saying Pakistan had no conflict with the Afghan people but would not allow terrorists to use Afghan soil to attack its citizens or violate its sovereignty. He also told his German counterpart that the Makkah Defence Pact was aimed at ensuring regional security and was not directed against any specific country.

The German president appreciated Pakistan for facilitating the verification of 500 Afghans destined to be repatriated to Germany, and for its humanitarian assistance to millions of Afghans over more than four decades.
