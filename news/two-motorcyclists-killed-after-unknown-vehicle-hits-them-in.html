Two motorcyclists killed after unknown vehicle hits them in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people riding a motorcycle have been killed in Karachi after an unknown vehicle struck them on a bridge near Five Star Chowrangi in North Nazimabad. The two men were aged between about 25 and 30 years. Their identities could not be established immediately after the incident.

Police are using the Talash app and CCTV cameras to identify the victims. The police are also trying to trace the vehicle responsible for the accident.
