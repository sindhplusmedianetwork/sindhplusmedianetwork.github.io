GB Announces Local Body Elections in Urban Areas for Dec 5 in Four Phases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gilgit-Baltistan will hold local body elections in its urban areas on December 5, following necessary amendments to the Local Government Act, after which the election schedule was issued. Chief Election Commissioner of Gilgit-Baltistan Raja Shahbaz Khan said the local body elections would be conducted in four phases, adding that all preparations for holding the polls were complete. In the first phase, direct elections will be held on December 5 in the urban areas of Gilgit, Skardu and Chilas.

Gilgit's municipal corporation will see direct contests on 42 seats, Skardu's on 21 seats and Chilas's on 13 seats. Gilgit will also have 7 reserved seats for women, Skardu 4 and Chilas 2, along with one reserved seat each for non-Muslims, youth and persons with disabilities. The chief election commissioner said the elections would be completed across all four phases.
