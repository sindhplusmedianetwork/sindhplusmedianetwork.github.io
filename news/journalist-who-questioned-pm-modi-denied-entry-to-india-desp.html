Journalist who questioned PM Modi denied entry to India despite visa

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read

Norwegian journalist Halla Leng, who was due to attend the India Summit 2026, has been denied entry to India despite holding a visa. The decision has sparked a controversy. Congress claims around 116 foreign delegates were also refused entry.

The case has raised questions about whose orders were behind the decision. Leng had travelled to India to attend the summit.
