Fire at Cardboard Factory in Karachi's SITE Area Brought Under Control After 8 Hours

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A major fire that broke out at a cardboard factory in Karachi's SITE area was brought under control after eight hours of firefighting, Chief Fire Officer Humayun Khan said. More than 12 fire tenders, three snorkels and two water bowsers were used in the operation, though officials warned the cooling process could take several more hours. The fire was declared third-degree after starting on the factory's second floor and engulfing the third floor, whose walls developed cracks.

The blaze intensified because of large stocks of plastic granules, along with plastic rolls, wood, thread, garments and foam plastic stored on the upper floor. According to fire brigade officials, the factory's staff was evacuated immediately after the fire broke out. Officials also revealed that a fire had broken out in one part of the same factory ten days earlier.

Water and foam were used to finally bring the fire under control.
