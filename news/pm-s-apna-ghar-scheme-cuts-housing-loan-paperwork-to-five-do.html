PM's Apna Ghar Scheme Cuts Housing Loan Paperwork to Five Documents

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The number of documents required for a housing loan under the Prime Minister's Apna Ghar programme has been reduced to five. According to a private TV channel, the required documents are the transfer deed, position letter, map, mortgage permission letter and mortgage registration, and instructions have been issued to banks in this regard. A spokesperson for the Ministry of Housing and Works said the PM's Apna Ghar programme is an important nationwide housing-loan project.

He said that under the programme, the government is providing loans of up to Rs10 million for building a home for the first time.
