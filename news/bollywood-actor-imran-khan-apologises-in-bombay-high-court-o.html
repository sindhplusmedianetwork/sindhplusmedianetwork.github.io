Bollywood actor Imran Khan apologises in Bombay High Court over protest presence

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Bollywood actor Imran Khan has tendered an unconditional apology to the Bombay High Court over his presence at a protest against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar in Mumbai. In an affidavit filed before a bench comprising Chief Justice Mahesh Chandra Tripathi and Justice Advait Sethna, the actor said he had only gone to see the situation and had no intention of joining the protest. He assured the court that he would neither take part in nor encourage any protest in future.

The actor had earlier been issued a 'leave India' notice by the Foreigners Regional Registration Office and a show-cause notice seeking cancellation of his OCI status. The court took the apology affidavit on record.
