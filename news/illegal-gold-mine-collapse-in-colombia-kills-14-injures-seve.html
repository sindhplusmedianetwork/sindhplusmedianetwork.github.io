Illegal Gold Mine Collapse in Colombia Kills 14, Injures Seven

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An illegal gold mine collapsed in southwestern Colombia, leaving 14 people dead and seven injured. The accident took place in Chambiro, a rural area of the Santa Barbara de Iscuande municipality in Narino province. Governor Luis Alfonso Escobar said rescue and search operations had been completed.

He urged the federal government, the national mining agency, and the ministry of mining and energy to accelerate the process of bringing illegal mining under the legal framework. Unregulated mining is common in Colombia's rural areas, where inadequate safety arrangements frequently lead to fatal accidents.
