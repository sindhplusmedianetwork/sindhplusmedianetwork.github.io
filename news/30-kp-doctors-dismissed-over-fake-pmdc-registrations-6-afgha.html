30 KP Doctors Dismissed Over Fake PMDC Registrations; 6 Afghan Doctors Told to Leave

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Health Department has dismissed 30 doctors over fake PMDC registrations and illegal appointments. The action followed show-cause notices and personal hearings conducted under Rule 15 by Additional Secretary Finance Tauseef Khalid, with the approval of the chief secretary. Officials said the dismissed doctors held fraudulent registration certificates, and orders were issued to recover salaries and dues paid to them.

Criminal cases are also expected to be registered against those involved. In a separate move, six Afghan doctors doing house jobs at Saidu Sharif Hospital in Swat were barred from duty, asked to vacate the hostel and ordered to leave Pakistan. The step was taken on a PMDC letter in line with the prime minister's directives regarding Afghan nationals.

The department said the measures were part of a wider effort to clean up irregular appointments in the province's health sector.
