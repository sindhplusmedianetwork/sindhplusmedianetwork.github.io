FIA busts hawala-hundi network in Karachi, arrests two suspects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has busted a hawala-hundi network in Karachi, arresting two suspects in a raid on Tuesday. The FIA's State Bank circle, acting on the orders of Director Karachi Zone Muntazir Mehdi, raided Jami Cloth Market near Glass Tower on MA Jinnah Road. The arrested men were identified as Altaf Ahmed and Muhammad Nadeem.

Officials recovered Rs2.5 million in cash, digital devices and other evidence from the premises. A case has been registered under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act 1947, as amended in 2020. The FIA said the suspects were running an illegal money-transfer business disguised as legitimate trade.
