Nursing council hikes registration fees; building-shifters face mandatory inspection

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Nursing and Midwifery Council has raised registration fees for nursing institutions and approved revisions to several fee heads, with the changes taking effect immediately. According to the official notification, the application processing fee for nursing institutions is now set at Rs 200,000. The fee for each degree programme is Rs 500,000, for each diploma programme Rs 150,000, and the inspection fee is Rs 500,000.

The council also made inspection mandatory for nursing institutions that changed buildings without prior approval. It warned that recognition will be suspended for institutions that fail to submit their applications within 15 days.
