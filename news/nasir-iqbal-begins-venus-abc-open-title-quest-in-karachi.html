Nasir Iqbal Begins Venus ABC Open Title Quest in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's seasoned squash campaigner Nasir Iqbal will begin his bid for the Venus ABC Open 2026 title on Wednesday at the DHA Creek Club in Karachi. Iqbal faces Canada's Salah Eltorgman in the men's singles preliminary round of the tournament, which is being held under the banner of the Sindh Squash Association. Squash legend Jahangir Khan is attending the event, adding prestige to the competition.

The draw features seven Pakistani men and three Pakistani women, giving home fans plenty to cheer. The tournament will run until October 25 and carries a total prize purse of $41,000. Iqbal, one of Pakistan's most experienced players on the circuit, will be aiming to add another title to his name in front of the home crowd.
