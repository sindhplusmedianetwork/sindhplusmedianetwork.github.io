Hina Javed Murder Case: Suspect's Family Speaks for the First Time, Makes New Claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The family of Zeeshan Arshad, the domestic servant arrested in the Hina Javed murder case, has come forward publicly for the first time with new claims. His father Muhammad Arshad told Express News that his son said during jail visits that whatever he did in the case was done under force. The father claimed that on the night of August 20, Hina Javed's husband Faisal Asghar himself killed her and hung her body in the bathroom.

He alleged that Zeeshan was tortured in custody to accept guilt in the case. The family maintains that Zeeshan is innocent and rejects claims of a prior criminal record. These are the family's claims; the case remains under investigation and the courts will decide the matter.

Police and prosecution have previously named the servant as the main accused.
