Fire Breaks Out at Will Smith's $42 Million Residence

WhatsApp Facebook X

Fire broke out at Hollywood actor Will Smith and his wife Jada Pinkett Smith's $42 million residence in California on Monday night. Firefighters found the blaze in a storage shed on the property. No casualties were reported in the incident.

It is unclear whether the couple was home at the time of the fire. Authorities have begun investigating the cause of the blaze. A similar fire was reported at the same home in 2021.
