Federal Constitutional Court dismisses 1,178 review petitions against Income Support Levy Act verdict

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Constitutional Court has dismissed 1,178 review petitions filed against the verdict on the Income Support Levy Act 2013. A written decision by the court's Chief Justice was issued in the matter. The court upheld the 28 December 2021 decision of former Justice Qazi Faez Isa.

It noted that a two-member bench had already examined the constitutional status of the levy in the Commissioner Inland Revenue versus Muhammad Mustafa case and dismissed the appeal on 28 December 2021. The court observed that, even while review petitions were pending, a three-member bench allowed an appeal on a nearly identical question in another case on 21 June 2022, raising serious concerns about judicial discipline and institutional order. It held that benches must respect an earlier decision already given, and that allowing an appeal in a separate case does not automatically review that earlier decision.

The court further said legislation cannot be passed by bypassing the Senate, as Articles 70 and 73 set out the legislative procedure, and the levy could not be justified as a money bill. It added that a court decision remains effective until the law is amended, repealed or declared void, and that review corrects only an error apparent on the record.
