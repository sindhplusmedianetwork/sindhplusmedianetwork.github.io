Punjab E-Challan System Becomes a Burden for Citizens Instead of a Convenience

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's e-challan system, run by the Punjab Safe Cities Authority, is causing distress for citizens instead of making their lives easier, according to complaints now coming to light. People have reported receiving several challans for the same vehicle on the same day for the same offence, and say their review requests are being rejected. Figures from Punjab Police show that more than 4.93 million traffic challans had been issued in the province by July 2026.

The total amount of fines imposed crossed Rs 9.75 billion. Citizens are now demanding an independent audit of the e-challan system and the monthly publication of details about review decisions. They say the system needs greater transparency so that people are not fined repeatedly for a single violation.
