Petrol price increased, diesel price unchanged

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government announced petroleum product prices for the next one day. A notification was issued for the new prices. The petrol price was increased by one rupee to Rs323 per litre.

The diesel price remained unchanged at Rs355 per litre. The OGRA notification said these prices are applicable for November 20. The prices were announced for one day only.
