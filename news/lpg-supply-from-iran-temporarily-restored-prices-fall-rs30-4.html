LPG supply from Iran temporarily restored, prices fall Rs30-40 per kg

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) supply from Iran has been temporarily restored, bringing prices down by Rs30 to Rs40 per kilogram over the past two days, with a further fall expected. The senior vice chairman of the LPG Distribution Association said the restoration was temporary and warned that the risk of an LPG crisis in winter still looms. Only parcels loaded before October 16 that had reached the Pak-Iran border have been allowed into Pakistan, and only bowzers loaded from Iranian refineries by October 16 were permitted to cross.

He said the quantity loaded by October 16 was 8,000 to 9,000 tonnes, which would meet only three days of demand, so the temporary supply would not resolve the crisis. Rates are falling gradually, with a further cut of Rs30 per kilogram expected in the next two to three days. The All Pakistan LPG Distribution Association has proposed to the federal government a six-month EIF exemption on LPG imports from Iran, and has told Prime Minister's Adviser Haroon Akhtar that the old mechanism's exemption should be restored for six months to meet winter consumption, warning that without restored supply the winter rate could reach Rs1,000 per kilogram.
