Security Guard Martyred in Khawarij Attack at Kohat Tunnel Toll Plaza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A security guard was martyred in an attack by Khawarij at the Kohat Tunnel toll plaza. Police personnel retaliated strongly and repelled the attack. After the attack, heavy police contingents were dispatched to the area and a search operation was launched.

DPO Kohat SSP Rai Mazhar Iqbal praised the police personnel for their response. He said all available resources were being used to maintain law and order.
