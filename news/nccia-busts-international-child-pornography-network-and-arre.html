NCCIA busts international child pornography network and arrests ringleader from Skardu

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has busted an international child pornography network, the agency said on Sunday. Islamabad NCCIA Additional Director Mohammad Shoaib Khan told a press conference that the network's ringleader, who belongs to Skardu, was arrested with the assistance of international agencies. He said a father was found involved in making and selling explicit videos of his own minor children, which were traded in secret Telegram groups through cryptocurrency.

Important links and evidence were recovered from Islamabad, Multan and South Punjab, and raids were being conducted to arrest other facilitators of the network. In a separate case, the agency arrested four members of a gang after investigating videos recovered from a child's mobile phone, saying the suspect befriended children on Instagram, lured them with money and sold the videos internationally through WhatsApp groups. The additional director urged citizens not to remain silent if they are victims of cybercrime and said complaints can be made on the agency's 24-hour helpline 1799.
