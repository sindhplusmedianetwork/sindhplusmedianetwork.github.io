Dark Web and White House Data Access Claim: How Pakistani 'Batman' Was Arrested With US, China Help

Pakistan's National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency said a 25-year-old man was arrested in Karachi on a tip from a US law enforcement agency. He was accused of allegedly selling data of agents and government institutions via the dark web. It was his second arrest in about six months.

He had earlier been arrested on information from China in a similar data-leak case, in which he was later granted bail.
