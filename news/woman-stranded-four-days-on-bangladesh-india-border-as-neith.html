Woman stranded four days on Bangladesh-India border as neither country accepts her

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman has been stranded in the border zone between Bangladesh and India for four days, with neither country willing to accept her as its citizen. She has been sitting at the zero line in the Pathar Char border area of Dewanganj in Jamalpur district since Tuesday morning, caught in the narrow strip of land between the two countries. Bangladesh's Border Guard says Indian border forces tried to push her into Bangladesh, a claim the Indian side denies.

The woman, said to be around 30 years old, has not given clear information about her name, address or identity when questioned, leaving officials unable to establish where she belongs. With both sides refusing to take responsibility, she remains stuck in the open border area with no resolution in sight. The case has drawn attention to the human cost of border disputes, where a person's citizenship can become a question neither side is prepared to answer.
