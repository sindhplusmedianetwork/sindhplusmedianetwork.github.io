Lahore air worse than Delhi and Kolkata as AQI hits 481 at Kahna Nau

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore's air quality was worse than Delhi and Kolkata on Monday. The Punjab Environmental Protection Department's dashboard recorded an AQI of 481 at Kahna Nau. That reading placed Kahna Nau in the hazardous category.

Shahdara registered a reading of 330, while Multan Road registered 326. Dera Ghazi Khan, Kot Addu, Nankana Sahib and Sahiwal also recorded hazardous air quality.
