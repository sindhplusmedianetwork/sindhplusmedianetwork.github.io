Some federal ministers active to become PM candidates, carrying their CVs: Asif Kirmani

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former senator Dr Asif Kirmani said some federal ministers are active to become candidates for prime minister. He said the ministers are carrying their CVs in pursuit of the premiership. He said if the new provinces issue is deferred until the 2029 elections, it could benefit those aspiring to become prime minister.

He added that speculation on social media suggests some ministers are presenting themselves as PM candidates. His remarks came amid ongoing debate over new provinces and the political future. No minister was named in his statement.
