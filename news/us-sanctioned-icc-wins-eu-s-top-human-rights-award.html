US-Sanctioned ICC Wins EU's Top Human Rights Award

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Criminal Court has won the European Union's top human rights award, the Sakharov Prize, less than two weeks after US President Donald Trump announced sanctions against the court. European Parliament President Roberta Metsola announced the award in Strasbourg, saying the European Union is built on respect for multilateralism and international institutions, and that the prize recognises the important work of the court in upholding international law and justice. ICC President Tomoko Akane welcomed the honour, saying it comes at a time when the court and its officials face unprecedented challenges.

The prize is the second international recognition for the tribunal this month, after former ICC judge Navi Pillay was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize. Trump's campaign against the court escalated after it announced arrest warrants against Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his former defence minister.
