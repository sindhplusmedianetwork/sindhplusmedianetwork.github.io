Court to hear CDA plea against stay on Bari Imam House demolition

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court on Thursday accepted an application filed by the Capital Development Authority, seeking vacation of an interim order restraining it from demolishing a structure known as Bari Imam House. The court, presided over by Justice Ayyaz Shaukat, allowed the CDA's application for an early hearing and fixed the matter for Monday. The authority argued that the disputed property was part of land acquired by it in 1963 and had no legal connection with the shrine of Hazrat Bari Imam, and that no building named Bari Imam House existed in its official records.

In its response, the CDA stated that the land in Noor Pur Shahan had been acquired under Award No. 4/DC CDA/1963 and that the original owners had been duly compensated, and that the disputed structure, spread over more than 300 kanals, had no connection with the actual shrine site, which was situated across the road under different Khasra numbers in the name of Darbar Aalia Hazrat Bari Abdul Latif Shah. The dispute arose after the CDA and the district administration sealed Bari Imam House on October 30 during an anti-encroachment operation, claiming the structure had been raised on government land.

Mohammad Akmal, the caretaker of the premises, subsequently approached the IHC through Advocate Syed Shajjar Abbas Hamdani, maintaining that the premises comprised a mosque and a Langarkhana and constituted exempted shrine property that had never been acquired by the authority. On November 3, the IHC issued notices to the respondents and directed them to submit reports within two weeks, besides ordering the authorities to immediately de-seal the premises and restraining them from demolishing the structure until the next hearing. Following that order, the CDA decided to challenge the interim relief, maintaining that the directions had been issued at the initial hearing without first examining its position and official records.
