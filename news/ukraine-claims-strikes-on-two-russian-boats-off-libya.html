Ukraine claims strikes on two Russian boats off Libya

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Volodymyr Zelensky has said Ukraine was behind strikes on two Russian vessels off Libya. The strikes took place on Sunday. He said seven Ukrainian-made naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury and hit the dry cargo vessel MV ANGARA.

The United Nations mission in Libya expressed serious concern over the attacks. The mission called for an immediate, independent and transparent investigation into the strikes.
