Eight million girls out of school in Punjab, says Hafiz Naeem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman has said that eight million girls in Punjab are currently out of school. Speaking at an exhibition in Lahore, he blamed the provincial government for the situation and said providing free, quality education was the state's responsibility. He said 15,000 schools in the province had been outsourced, while private schools operated a dual standard and charged very high fees.

The Jamaat-e-Islami leader said the gap in girls' education showed that the government had failed to meet its duty to children. He also spoke about his party's Bano Qabil programme, saying it had registered 1.6 million students. Of those students, 32 per cent were women, he said.
