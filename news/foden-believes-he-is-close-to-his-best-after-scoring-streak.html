Foden believes he is close to his best after scoring streak

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Manchester City midfielder Phil Foden says he is beginning to rediscover the form that made him one of the Premier League's standout players, after a recent scoring streak boosted his confidence ahead of Wednesday's Champions League clash at RB Leipzig. Foden, who won the Premier League Player of the Season and the Professional Footballers' Association Player of the Year awards in 2024 after scoring a career-best 27 goals in all competitions, endured a dip in form that ultimately cost him a place in England's 2026 World Cup squad. The 26-year-old scored his first Premier League goal of the season against Ipswich Town last month after serving a suspension following a red card in the Manchester derby.

He followed it up with a hat-trick and an assist in City's 5-1 Champions League win over AEK Athens. "Being involved in goals gives you a lot of confidence and when you're playing regularly you get into a rhythm and a groove," Foden said. He added that he had not spoken to England manager Thomas Tuchel since his World Cup omission and was focusing on his own game.
