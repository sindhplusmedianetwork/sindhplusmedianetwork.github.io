Work-Visa Holders Are Not Offloaded, Visit-Visa Stops Have Reasons, Says Immigration Official

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Director Immigration at Karachi Airport Shahzad Akbar said there are solid reasons behind the offloading of any passenger, including financial status, paid hotel booking, required currency and not having a return ticket, and the impression of offloading on work visas is incorrect. He said in his statement that offloading cases on work visas are extremely limited because the documents of work visas are already checked by the concerned ministry, while most offloading cases occur with passengers travelling on visit visas where their financial status and ability to bear expenses of stay abroad are assessed. Shahzad Akbar said not having a paid hotel booking, required foreign currency and return ticket are also important reasons for offloading of passengers, and the FIA has directed passengers to ensure their financial resources, paid hotel booking, required foreign currency and return ticket before departing abroad on visit visas so they do not face trouble during immigration.

He said the number of Pakistanis deported from abroad has risen to four times compared to offloading, while many deported Pakistanis also face criminal charges abroad, deportation of Pakistani citizens from Saudi Arabia on drug charges is higher, and expiry of iqama and overstay beyond the stipulated period are also major reasons for deportation from Saudi Arabia. According to Shahzad Akbar, the FIA pre-departure facilitation desk at Jinnah International Airport Karachi for guidance and facilitation of passengers going abroad is fully active where information is being provided to passengers about travel requirements and immigration procedures. The purpose of this desk is to provide guidance to genuine passengers going abroad about travel documents and immigration matters before departure and to remove their concerns, and FIA officials said this initiative is helping make passengers' travel easy, smooth and trouble-free.
