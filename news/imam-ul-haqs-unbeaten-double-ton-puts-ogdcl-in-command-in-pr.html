Imam-ul-Haq’s Unbeaten Double Ton Puts OGDCL in Command in President’s Trophy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Imam-ul-Haq struck an unbeaten double century for OGDCL on the third day of the seventh-round President’s Trophy Grade-I match at Lahore’s LCCA Ground on Wednesday. Resuming on 118 not out, Imam advanced to 200 not out, an innings studded with 19 fours and two sixes. His effort helped his side declare at 410 for nine, securing a 178-run first-innings lead over Ghani Glass.

For Ghani, Ubaid Shah took five wickets for 131 runs. In another match, centuries from Waqar Hussain, who made 171, and Taimur Khan, who made 151, helped PTV fight back against MIT Solutions. MIT Solutions now need 47 runs to win that game.

Elsewhere, SNGPL and Kingsmen Academy also strengthened their positions on day three of their respective matches.
