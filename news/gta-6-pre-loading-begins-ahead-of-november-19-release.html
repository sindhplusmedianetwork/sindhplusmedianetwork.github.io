GTA 6 Pre-Loading Begins Ahead of November 19 Release

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rockstar Games has opened pre-loading for Grand Theft Auto 6 ahead of its scheduled release on November 19. The early download option allows players to install the game before launch day. This is intended to help players avoid server pressure when the game officially becomes available.

Grand Theft Auto 6 will be released on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. The game requires 212GB of storage space. Its standard edition is priced at $69.99.
