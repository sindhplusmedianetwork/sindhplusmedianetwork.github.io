Qatar, Germany Urge Iran to End Houthi Support, Cooperate With IAEA

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Qatar and Germany have urged Iran to end its support for the Houthis in Yemen and increase cooperation with the International Atomic Energy Agency. In a joint statement, the two countries called on Iran to fulfil its obligations under the nuclear non-proliferation treaty and its safeguards agreement with the IAEA. They urged Tehran to resume full cooperation to ensure the peaceful nature of its nuclear programme.

The statement was issued during Qatari Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani's official visit to Germany on 20 and 21 November. It also condemned Iranian attacks and threats against commercial ships. Qatar and Germany demanded that Iran stop supplying weapons and other support to the Houthis in Yemen.
