War Returns to Iran as Israel and US Launch Strikes, Live Updates

War has returned to Iran as Israel and the United States carried out strikes, according to live coverage. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted and destroyed a Houthi drone headed toward the southern regions of the kingdom. The Israeli prime minister said the only way to end the Iranian threat is to topple the Iranian government.

The deputy commander of the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed the country has the missile capacity to target US Navy vessels. French President Emmanuel Macron visited Riyadh and secured extra Saudi diesel supplies along with liquefied natural gas from Qatar. The Iranian foreign minister warned that any Egyptian interference in the Yemen war would make the situation more difficult.

The developments marked a sharp escalation on multiple fronts.
