Rs100.6 Billion Tax Collected as 7.56 Million Returns Filed This Fiscal Year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Documents show that 7,556,387 income tax returns have been filed so far this fiscal year, with Rs100.605 billion collected in tax. That is more than 1.6 million additional returns compared with the same period last year. However, the tax collected fell by Rs11.58 billion year-on-year.

Returns from non-salaried individuals rose to 5.07 million, while salaried-class returns reached 2.37 million. Company returns dropped from 18,051 to 15,243 over the same comparison.
