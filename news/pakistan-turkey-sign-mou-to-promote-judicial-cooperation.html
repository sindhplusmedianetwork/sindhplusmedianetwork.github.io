Pakistan, Turkey sign MoU to promote judicial cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Turkey have signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) in Islamabad. The MoU aims to promote judicial cooperation between the two countries. It was signed between the Supreme Court of Pakistan and Turkey's Court of Cassation.

Chief Justice Yahya Afridi and the president of Turkey's Court of Cassation signed the agreement. Both sides said modern technology and e-courts would be promoted to modernize the judicial systems and improve access to justice.
