After Republicans' midterm defeat, Marco Rubio prepares to resign

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Reports have emerged that United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio is preparing to resign after the Republican Party's heavy defeat in the midterm elections. According to the reports, Rubio is also considering seeking the Republican Party's nomination in the 2028 presidential election. The development comes in the wake of the Democrats' victory in the midterm polls, which dealt a major setback to the Republican Party.

Rubio serves as the country's top diplomat in the Trump administration. The reports have not been officially confirmed.
