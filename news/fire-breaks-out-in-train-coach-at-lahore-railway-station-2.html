Fire breaks out in train coach at Lahore Railway Station

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fire broke out in an empty, disused economy-class coach parked at platform 7 of Lahore Railway Station. Rescue 1122 teams reached the site and brought the blaze under control. The fire started at 12:40 pm and was extinguished by 1:20 pm.

No loss of life was reported, and train operations were not disrupted. Railway police detained a suspect with the help of station staff. A case is being registered at Lahore Railway Police Station, and further investigation continues.
