Punjab reports 43 percent drop in smog-related patients in first 10 months of 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab has reported a 43 per cent decline in smog-related patients in the first 10 months of 2026. The figures cover five smog-related disease categories, according to provincial government data. The number of reported patients fell to 7.03 million between January and October.

In the same period last year, the number stood at 12.4 million. The government also said Lahore recorded its cleanest October in five years. The decline was reported as pollution curbs expand in the province.
