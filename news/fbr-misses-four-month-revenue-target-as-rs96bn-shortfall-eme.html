FBR misses four-month revenue target as Rs96bn shortfall emerges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Board of Revenue has failed to meet its tax collection targets for October 2026 and for the first four months of the fiscal year, official sources say. The four-month shortfall stood at Rs96 billion, while October alone recorded a shortfall of Rs129 billion against its monthly target. Sources said taxpayers filed 7.7 million income tax returns, and overall collections grew by only 6 percent compared to the same period last year.

The figures point to persistent pressure on the tax machinery as the government chases ambitious revenue goals agreed under its economic programme.
