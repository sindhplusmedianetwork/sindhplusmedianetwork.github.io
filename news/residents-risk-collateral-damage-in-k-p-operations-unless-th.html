Residents Risk Collateral Damage in K-P Operations Unless They Move Away From Terrorists, Warns Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's adviser Rana Sanaullah warned on Friday that civilians risk collateral damage during planned intelligence-based operations in designated Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa districts. Speaking on Naya Pakistan, he said residents must either distance themselves from terrorists or drive them out of their areas. Sanaullah claimed that about 70 percent of terrorist groups are concentrated in those districts, with each group having between 3 and 15 members.

He further alleged that training camps funded by India operate across the Afghan border, with suspected Israeli involvement. He expressed confidence that the armed forces could neutralise these networks within three to five months. He also paid tribute to the sacrifices of the security forces and the people.
