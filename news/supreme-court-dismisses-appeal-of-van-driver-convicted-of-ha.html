Supreme Court Dismisses Appeal of Van Driver Convicted of Harassing Two Girls

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Supreme Court has dismissed the appeal of a van driver against his sentence in a case of sexually harassing two girls. The court declared the appeal non-maintainable and upheld van driver Hameed Khan's sentence of 14 years' imprisonment and a fine of Rs1 million. Justice Shahzad Malik remarked that the petitioner had received a lenient sentence, as Section 376 of the Pakistan Penal Code carries the death penalty.

Justice Shahzad Malik also remarked that if courts started deciding cases on the accused's say, all jails would empty. The defence lawyer argued in court that the accused has been in jail for seven years and the remaining sentence should be remitted and he should be released. The defence also said the accused did not go to the scene that day and Safe City camera video was not summoned either.

The court remarked that the prosecution had succeeded in proving the crime beyond doubt, and no enmity or bad faith against the petitioner was proved in the statements of both girls.
