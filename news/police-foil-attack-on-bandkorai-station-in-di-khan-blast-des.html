Police foil attack on Bandkorai station in DI Khan; blast destroys nearby mosque

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Militants attacked the Bandkorai Police Station in Dera Ismail Khan by ramming an explosives-laden pickup into its main gate. The powerful explosion was followed by intense firing last night. Police said the attackers belonged to Fitna al-Khawarij.

Prompt retaliatory firing by police prevented the attackers from entering the station, and no casualties were reported. The blast completely destroyed the station's main gate and a section of the boundary wall. A nearby mosque was also completely destroyed, and several houses were damaged.
