NCCIA Busts International Child Abuse Gang; Four Key Suspects Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four central suspects of an international gang involved in blackmailing innocent children, assaulting them, and selling their indecent videos in Pakistan and at the international level have been arrested. Indecent videos of between 50 and 60 children were recovered from the suspects' possession. Additional Director NCCIA Islamabad Muhammad Shoaib Khan told a press conference on Friday that the agency exposed the international gang during a nationwide crackdown against elements involved in child pornography, online financial fraud, and cyber harassment.

He said the investigation began with nearly 60 videos found on one child's mobile phone, which led to the arrest of the four suspects. The accused befriended children aged 6 to 14 on Instagram and other social media platforms, lured them with money, made indecent videos, and sold them through WhatsApp groups and internationally. He added that two Chinese nationals had previously been convicted in connection with this same crime.

Shoaib Khan further said that raids on call centers across the country led to the arrest of 473 foreigners who posed as bank representatives in Malaysia, China, and India to commit fraud. In the past week, 22 cyber-harassment suspects were also detained, and their inquiries and investigations were entrusted entirely to women officers.
