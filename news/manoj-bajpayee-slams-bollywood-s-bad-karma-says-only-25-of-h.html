Manoj Bajpayee Slams Bollywood's 'Bad Karma', Says Only 25% of His Talent Has Been Used

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Manoj Bajpayee has accused Bollywood of wasting talented performers, saying the Hindi film industry carries a 'bad reputation and bad karma' for demolishing great talent. Speaking to Zoom alongside co-stars Divya Dutta and Shraddha Das while promoting his new Netflix film Gajab Raat, the Satya star said stereotyping had ruined many actors by reducing them to 'nothing on the set' in terms of roles and treatment. He said many gifted performers had withered away without any recognition because the industry kept handing them similar characters.

Reflecting on his own career, Bajpayee admitted that only about 25 per cent of his acting abilities have been used so far, calling the gap an actor's natural 'greed' and 'hunger' and adding that the fire was still burning in his belly. He credited the rise of OTT platforms with bringing a welcome shift, saying streaming services had given audiences a stronger voice and opened up more meaningful opportunities for actors. Divya Dutta echoed the criticism, saying she detests being labelled a 'supporting actor' or 'character actor', which she called a lazy shortcut that becomes a ceiling for performers.

Gajab Raat, earlier titled Ghooskhor Pandat, is streaming on Netflix and also stars Nushrratt Bharuccha, Saqib Saleem, Akshay Oberoi, Kiku Sharda and Shraddha Das.
