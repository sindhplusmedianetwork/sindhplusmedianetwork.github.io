Attack on Aman Mela check post in DI Khan foiled; area cleared

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police and security forces foiled an attack on the Aman Mela check post in the Darazinda area of Dera Ismail Khan. According to police, firing was opened on the check post from nearby mountains. Police said the attackers fled after police and security forces returned fire.

A search operation was carried out in the area, after which it was declared clear. No casualties were reported in the incident.
