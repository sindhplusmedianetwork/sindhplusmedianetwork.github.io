Pakistan, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Turkiye to Meet European Trio in Istanbul

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkiye will host meetings of the R-4 and R-4+E-3 foreign ministers in Istanbul on October 23, the Turkish foreign ministry announced on Thursday. The R-4 group, comprising Pakistan, Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye, will meet the E-3 of France, Britain and Germany for the first time at Turkiye's initiative. The meetings come as wars involving Iran, Yemen and Ukraine threaten shipping routes and regional security.

Ministers are expected to discuss the Israeli-Palestinian issue, the Strait of Hormuz, Yemen and the Russia-Ukraine war. The gathering reflects Turkiye's growing diplomatic role in regional crises.
