Microsoft Services Hit by Worldwide Outage as Users Report Disruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Microsoft services have been disrupted around the world, leaving users facing serious difficulties in accessing the company's platforms. The affected services include Microsoft Teams, Outlook and the artificial intelligence software Copilot. Microsoft confirmed the disruption on Thursday, saying the company was working to resolve the connection problems.

According to the company, unusual pressure built up on one part of its infrastructure, which made it difficult for users to reach the services. Microsoft said the problem was not caused by a rise in traffic from users, but by several servers suddenly becoming unavailable, which increased the load on the remaining servers. The outage monitoring website Downdetector reported that large numbers of users in Britain and the United States lodged complaints about Microsoft services, with more than 131,000 complaints about Outlook alone in the United States.

Microsoft added that it was testing changes to server settings to remove a fault that arises during automatic checks of the servers.
