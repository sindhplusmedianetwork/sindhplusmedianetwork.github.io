27 illegal housing schemes found on Kashmir state properties in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The convener of the Senate Standing Committee on Kashmir Affairs, Gilgit-Baltistan and Frontier Regions, Nasir Mahmood Butt, along with members of the sub-committee, on Thursday visited various properties of Jammu and Kashmir State in Lahore to review their current status and assess issues related to their management and protection. Members of the sub-committee, Senator Naseema Ehsan and Senator Nadeem Bhutto, accompanied the chairman during the visit, and the delegation inspected Jammu and Kashmir State properties located in Sultanpura, Rehmanpura and Naulakha Bazaar. According to an official statement, during the visit it emerged that 27 allegedly illegal housing societies have been established on the official land of Jammu and Kashmir State, where plots are reportedly being sold to citizens, while several individuals have also constructed houses on the land.

The statement said a large number of citizens are reportedly unaware that the land on which they are purchasing plots or constructing houses is, in fact, the official property of Jammu and Kashmir State, a situation that could expose them to potential legal complications, financial losses and possible fraud. The committee expressed concern over the matter and emphasised the need for a comprehensive examination of the legal status of these housing societies, land ownership, the sale and purchase of plots, and relevant official records, to safeguard citizens' rights and prevent the illegal use of state-owned land. The authorities concerned briefed the committee on the current status of the properties, administrative matters and available records, and the members also reviewed illegal constructions, rental income generated from state properties, unauthorised occupation and encroachments.

The committee members emphasised that the properties of Jammu and Kashmir State were important state assets, and their protection and transparent management must be ensured.
