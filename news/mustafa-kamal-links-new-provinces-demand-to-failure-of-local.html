Mustafa Kamal Links New Provinces Demand to Failure of Local Governments

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Health Minister Mustafa Kamal said on Friday that MQM-P was left with no option but to demand the creation of new provinces because Karachi and urban Sindh had been denied facilities and representation according to their population. Speaking to media on the sidelines of a Startup-Government Dialogue at the National Institute of Cardiovascular Diseases in Karachi, he said the failure to empower local governments had compelled the party to support the demand. Kamal said local governments lack authority even over matters such as manhole covers, and accused the Sindh government of resisting devolution of powers.

He cited Article 239(4) of the Constitution as the mechanism for creating new provinces, saying the Constitution has been amended 27 times and can be amended further. He said MQM-P was not seeking confrontation and would withdraw its 29 November rally if there was even a slight risk of a clash. Kamal also disputed Karachi's population figures, saying the city was counted at 16 million in 2017 and 13 million in the 2023 census, while a recount put the figure at 20.3 million, and claimed 7.7 million people had been excluded.

In the health segment of the dialogue, he said around 11,000 mothers die every year in pregnancy and childbirth, 43 percent of children suffer impaired growth due to malnutrition, and about 400,000 children aged one to five die annually. He said around 11 million people have been identified with Hepatitis C through screening, with free screening and treatment being provided and Rs68 million allocated for an elimination programme.
