Nora Fatehi Tells Delhi Court She Lives in India on a Work Visa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Moroccan-born Canadian artist Nora Fatehi has told a Delhi court that she lives in India on a work visa. She is a prosecution witness in the Rs200 crore extortion case. Appearing on Friday before Additional Sessions Judge Gagan Deep Singh at Rouse Avenue Court, she said she stays in India on a work visa linked to music company T-Series.

She gave details of her Chennai visit and her meeting with Leena Paulose, wife of accused Sukesh Chandrashekhar. Chandrashekhar is accused of duping Aditi Singh, wife of Fortis Healthcare promoter Shivinder Mohan Singh, of Rs200 crore. Prosecutors say he posed as a senior government official to arrange her husband’s bail.

The court recorded her statement as part of the case proceedings.
