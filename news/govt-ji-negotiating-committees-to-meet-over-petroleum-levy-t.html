Govt, JI Negotiating Committees to Meet Over Petroleum Levy Tomorrow

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government and Jamaat-e-Islami negotiating committees are set to meet in Islamabad on Friday, October 23, to discuss the petroleum levy. The meeting was arranged after a telephone conversation between the two sides, signalling a fresh attempt to break the deadlock over the federal levy on petroleum products. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif is also expected to meet JI Emir Hafiz Naeemur Rehman during the same round of engagement.

JI leader Baloch said nationwide protests would resume on Friday and that negotiations and protests would continue side by side until the levy is abolished. The dispute escalated after JI staged sit-ins in four cities in August and called a shutdown strike on September 3 over rising fuel costs. Talks between the two sides have been stalled since then, with the party demanding the complete abolition of the levy.

Friday's meeting is being seen as a make-or-break moment for the negotiation track.
