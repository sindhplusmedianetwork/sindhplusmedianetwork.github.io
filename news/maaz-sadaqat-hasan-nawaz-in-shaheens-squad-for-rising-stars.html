Maaz Sadaqat, Hasan Nawaz in Shaheens Squad for Rising Stars Asia Cup

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has announced a 15-member Pakistan Shaheens squad for the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026. Abdul Samad will lead the side in the 50-over tournament, which will be played in Qatar from November 19 to 29. Batters Maaz Sadaqat and Hasan Nawaz headline the selections in a squad built around emerging talent.

Pakistan have been placed in Group B alongside India A, Nepal and the UAE. The defending champions open their campaign against the UAE on November 20 before taking on India A two days later. The tournament is seen as a key platform for the country's next generation of international cricketers.
