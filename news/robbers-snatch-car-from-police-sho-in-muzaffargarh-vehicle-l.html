Robbers Snatch Car from Police SHO in Muzaffargarh; Vehicle Later Recovered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Armed robbers snatched a car at gunpoint from a police officer in Muzaffargarh, police said on Monday. The officer was Hamza Ghafoor, Station House Officer of Qasba Gujrat Police Station. The armed men intercepted the SHO and took his car before fleeing.

The vehicle was later recovered, according to police. The incident was reported after the armed men stopped the officer and drove away with his car.
