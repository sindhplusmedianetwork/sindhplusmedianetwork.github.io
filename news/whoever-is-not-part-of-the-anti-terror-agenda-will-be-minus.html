Whoever Is Not Part of the Anti-Terror Agenda Will Be 'Minus', Warns Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PML-N leader and Adviser to the Prime Minister Rana Sanaullah has said that whoever is not part of the agenda to eliminate terrorism will be 'minus'. Speaking on Geo News's morning programme, he said any political party not standing wholeheartedly with the forces would also be eliminated. Sanaullah said eradicating terrorism is the top priority, adding that people's lives cannot improve without ending it and the entire nation must unite against it.

He said terrorism would be crushed out of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He added that the KP governor had demanded Rs160 billion, which the prime minister approved immediately. He also said there has been no talk or draft of a 28th constitutional amendment so far.
