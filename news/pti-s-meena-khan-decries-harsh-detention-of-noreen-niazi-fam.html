PTI's Meena Khan Decries 'Harsh Detention' of Noreen Niazi, Family Kept in Dark

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Meena Khan Afridi has strongly criticised the authorities over the treatment of Imran Khan's sister Noreen Niazi, describing her continued detention and the lack of information to her family as deeply alarming. He said Noreen was shifted from Kot Lakhpat Jail to the Punjab Institute of Cardiology after she complained of sudden chest pain, yet her family was not informed about the development. Meena Khan demanded that her daughter, Hajra Bibi, be allowed to meet her immediately, calling it a basic human requirement.

He pointed out that Noreen has not been arrested in any case and is only held in detention on apprehension. Keeping her in harsh conditions while keeping the family uninformed, he said, raises serious questions about the treatment of detainees.
