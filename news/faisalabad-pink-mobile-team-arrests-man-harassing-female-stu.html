Faisalabad: Pink Mobile team arrests man harassing female student

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A team of the Pink Mobile police station in Faisalabad's Medina Town area arrested a man accused of harassing a female student. According to reports, the spokesperson for Punjab traffic police said a BS IT student had filed a harassment complaint at the Pink Mobile police station, on which the SHO Medina Town took immediate action and arrested the accused. The spokesperson said that during October, mobile police stations received 2,663 complaints, of which citizens were provided relief at their doorstep in 2,348 cases, while more than 900 FIRs were registered.

More than 130 FIRs were registered on women's requests last month, while over 20,000 character certificates were issued through the mobile police stations. According to the spokesperson, over 900 tenants and more than 5,000 employees were registered in October, while over 31,000 citizens were provided driving license facilities. DIG Traffic Punjab Muhammad Waqas Nazir said providing facilities to citizens and delivering police services to their doorstep is the top priority.
