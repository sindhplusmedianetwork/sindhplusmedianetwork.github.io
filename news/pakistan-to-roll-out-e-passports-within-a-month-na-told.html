Pakistan to Roll Out E-Passports 'Within a Month', NA Told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has decided to discontinue Machine Readable Passports and replace them with electronic passports, the interior ministry informed the National Assembly on Friday. Responding to MNA Malik Shakir Bashir Awan during the question and answer session, the ministry said the issuance of MRPs would be discontinued within a month, after which only e-passports would be issued by passport offices and Pakistan missions abroad. Passports already issued would remain valid until their respective dates of expiry.

Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Dr Tariq Fazal Chaudhry said Pakistan was moving towards e-passports in line with rapid technological changes in global immigration systems, adding that the electronic chip embedded in the new passports would make forgery impossible. He cautioned, however, that the country's large population and the operational challenges faced by passport offices could cause some delay, while reiterating the government's commitment to introducing the system as soon as possible. Responding to a supplementary question, the minister agreed that the existing machine-readable passport system needed to be upgraded in view of changing global immigration requirements, noting that e-passport technology was being used worldwide.
