Macron Rejects Interior Minister's Resignation After Violent Protests

French Interior Minister Laurent Nunez has submitted his resignation to President Emmanuel Macron amid the political and administrative crisis following the violent protests of October 17 in France. However, the French president has refused to accept the resignation in view of national security and the current circumstances. According to details, the interior minister was under severe pressure from political and public circles over the alleged failure to control the violent riots and restore law and order.

In this situation, Nunez accepted moral responsibility and offered his resignation to the president, but was instead directed to continue working in his office. According to presidential sources, President Macron believes that maintaining continuity in the command of security and law-enforcement institutions is essential in the current tense situation. He urged the minister to focus all his attention on tackling the crisis and restoring state authority and peace in the country rather than resigning.
