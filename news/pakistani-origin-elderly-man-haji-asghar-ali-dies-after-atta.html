Pakistani-Origin Elderly Man Haji Asghar Ali Dies After Attack in Halifax, UK

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Haji Asghar Ali, a Pakistani-origin elderly man seriously injured in an attack in Halifax, United Kingdom, has died. According to reports, the man, aged over 60, was found seriously injured last Tuesday evening shortly before 5pm. He was discovered at the junction of Gibbet Street and Queens Road in Halifax.

He was taken to hospital in critical condition but could not survive his injuries. He is survived by his wife and four children. He also leaves behind a brother, an uncle and six grandchildren.
