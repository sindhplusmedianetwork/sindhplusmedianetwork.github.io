PSX Plunges Over 2,000 Points as Oil Fears Rattle Investors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange came under heavy selling pressure on Thursday, October 22, with the benchmark KSE-100 Index losing 2,006.67 points, or 1.21 percent, to close at 163,924.31, falling below the 164,000-point mark. The index hit an intra-day high of 166,180.32 in early trade but slid to a low of 163,782.41 as selling intensified after midday. Escalating geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and surging international oil prices, following fresh Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia, rattled investor sentiment and raised concerns over Pakistan's energy import bill and external account, analysts said.

ENGROH, UBL, HUBC, HBL and PTC were among the major laggards, collectively dragging the index down. On the corporate front, key banks announced their nine-month results, with HBL, MEBL and MCB reporting earnings per share of Rs36.84, Rs41.02 and Rs37.26 respectively, in line with expectations. Market activity saw 364.3 million shares traded with turnover of Rs17.9 billion, with PRL leading volumes.

The decline followed Wednesday's recovery, when the index had gained 779.34 points to close at 165,930.99 on renewed institutional buying and optimism over diplomatic efforts to resolve the US-Iran conflict.
