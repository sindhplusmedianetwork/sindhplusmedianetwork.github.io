Punjab Labour Code 2026 extends legal cover to workers in offices, homes and online platforms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Labour Code 2026 is being viewed as a significant development in protecting the rights of employees and workers in the province, extending legal coverage to people employed in offices, shops, factories, households, the construction sector, agricultural activities and through digital platforms. The new labour code gives importance to issues such as minimum wages, timely payment of salaries, working hours, overtime compensation, leaves, termination of employment, discrimination and workplace safety. Under the new law, employees have been provided with legal recourse in cases involving delayed salaries, non-payment of overtime, unlawful dismissal and other labour-related issues.

The objective is not only to protect workers' rights but also to clarify the responsibilities of employers and institutions to ensure a dignified and safe working environment. According to legal guidance, an employee facing unpaid wages, denied overtime or sudden dismissal should first preserve evidence such as an appointment letter, salary record, bank statement, attendance record, duty hours, WhatsApp messages or a written dismissal order, and then submit a written application to the employer specifying the outstanding amount and the months or overtime hours involved. Workers can also register complaints through the Punjab Labour Helpline 1314 and seek guidance from the Labour Department, though they are advised to follow up a phone call with a formal written complaint and legal claim.
