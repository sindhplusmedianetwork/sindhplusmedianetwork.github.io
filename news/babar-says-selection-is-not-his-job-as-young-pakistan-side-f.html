Babar says selection is not his job as young Pakistan side face Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan captain Babar Azam has said team selection is the responsibility of the selectors and the coach, and his role is to get the best out of the players available to him. He was speaking as a youthful Pakistan side prepared to face Sri Lanka in the opening Test of a two-match series in Rawalpindi. Pakistan, ninth and last in the 2025-27 World Test Championship standings, fielded four debutants for the match.

The opening Test runs from November 9 to 13 at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. The second Test of the series will be played in Lahore from November 17 to 21. Babar's remarks came as the young side looked ahead to the challenge against Sri Lanka.
