Hajj 2027: Pakistan secures second-largest quota among top pilgrim countries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

For Hajj 2027, Pakistan has secured the second-largest quota among the ten countries receiving the most pilgrims, with 179,210 places. Indonesia tops the list with 210,000 pilgrims, followed by India in third place with 175,000. Iran ranks fourth with 87,550 places and Turkiye fifth with 84,942.

Bangladesh, Nigeria, Ethiopia, Algeria and Egypt complete the top ten, with dozens of other countries receiving smaller quotas. The figures show Pakistan among the largest contributors of pilgrims to the annual pilgrimage. The quota determines how many Pakistanis will be able to perform Hajj in 2027 under the country's allocation.
