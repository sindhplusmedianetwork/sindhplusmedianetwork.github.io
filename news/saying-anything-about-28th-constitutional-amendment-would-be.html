Saying Anything About 28th Constitutional Amendment Would Be Premature, Says Aamir Dogar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chief Whip Aamir Dogar has said that at present the 28th constitutional amendment is only in media and social media, and saying anything until the draft of the 28th constitutional amendment comes to parliament would be premature. Aamir Dogar said the PTI founder has always talked about smaller provinces, referendum is the best solution to any issue, and taking the people's opinion is the best solution. He said the creation of provinces should be according to the people's will, and the decision regarding the creation of provinces will be made by the PTI founder.

Aamir Dogar said at present there are cracks in the government's ranks, the opposition will make its own decision, and first the government and allies should be on one page. He said Bilawal Bhutto's recent statements are before everyone, government chief whip Tariq Fazal has again invited them to join the electoral reforms committee, and they will decide after mutual consultation of opposition leaders and the parliamentary party.
