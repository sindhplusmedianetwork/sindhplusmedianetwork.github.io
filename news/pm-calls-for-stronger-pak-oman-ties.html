PM calls for stronger Pak-Oman ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has called for further strengthening Pakistan's bilateral cooperation with Oman, particularly in trade, investment, energy and defence. During a meeting with the Omani ambassador at the Prime Minister's House, the prime minister highlighted the longstanding brotherly relations between Pakistan and Oman, rooted in shared history, faith and culture. He stressed the importance of translating the positive momentum in bilateral relations into tangible results through increased economic engagement and institutional cooperation.

The two sides also discussed recent regional and global developments, and the prime minister emphasised the importance of maintaining peace and stability through dialogue and diplomacy. The Omani ambassador thanked the prime minister for receiving him, conveyed the greetings of the Omani leadership and reaffirmed his country's commitment to further strengthening its close relations with Pakistan. Separately, the prime minister directed the Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research to enhance its research capabilities and contribute more effectively to industrial development, export growth and the promotion of small- and medium-sized enterprises.

He directed all of the council's laboratories to obtain accreditation in accordance with international standards to facilitate internationally recognised certification of Pakistani export products, and called for stronger cooperation between the council and the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission to improve industrial and technical training opportunities for young people.
