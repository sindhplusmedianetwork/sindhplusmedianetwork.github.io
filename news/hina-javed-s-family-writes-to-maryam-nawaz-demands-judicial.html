Hina Javed's family writes to Maryam Nawaz, demands judicial commission

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The family of the late Hina Javed has written to Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz demanding that a judicial commission be formed to investigate the murder case. According to the letter, the family rejects the findings of the police investigation committee and says repeated attempts to contact the chief minister directly were unsuccessful. The family says it raised its reservations about the committee through various forums and government representatives.

The letter says the severity of the violence against Hina Javed and the mutilation of her body reflect hatred, and that DNA of Faisal and his father was found on her neck. It says her father-in-law initially reported a theft at the house, that forensic reports of Hina Javed's and the accused's mobile phones are still pending, and that important messages were deleted from her phone. The family says the investigation was turned towards a domestic worker before forensic and fingerprint results arrived, and that while the worker is involved in the murder, evidence relating to Faisal is being ignored.

The letter asks the chief minister to review the case file in detail and argues that a committee cannot declare anyone guilty or innocent before the case reaches court, calling self-investigation and self-judgment a conflict of interest. The family says it no longer expects justice from the committee's process and has appealed for an impartial judicial commission, for no protection to the accused, and for justice under the law.
