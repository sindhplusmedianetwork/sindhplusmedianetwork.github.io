PTI Links Support for 28th Amendment to Imran Khan's Release

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf parliamentary party has decided to conditionally support the proposed 28th Constitutional Amendment if party founder Imran Khan is released, sources said on Tuesday. A majority of members at the parliamentary party meeting supported linking the party's backing for the amendment to Imran Khan's release. The meeting, which continued for seven to eight hours at Parliament House, also decided to form a six-member steering committee to address matters related to access to Imran Khan and his medical treatment.

Opposition leaders Mahmood Khan Achakzai and Allama Raja Nasir Abbas will head the committee, which will also negotiate with the government. The meeting discussed governor's rule and the imposition of an emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, which the parliamentary party unanimously condemned. In a resolution read out by Asad Qaiser, the party demanded the immediate release of Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi, unhindered meetings with family, lawyers and party representatives, and full implementation of the Supreme Court's August 2026 directives, including Imran Khan's independent medical examination and treatment at Shifa International Hospital.

The resolution also demanded an end to governor's rule and the emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the restoration of the elected provincial government.
