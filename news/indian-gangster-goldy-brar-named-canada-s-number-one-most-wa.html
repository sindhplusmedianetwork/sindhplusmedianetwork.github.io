Indian Gangster Goldy Brar Named Canada's Number One Most Wanted Suspect

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Canada has placed Indian-origin gangster Goldy Brar at number one on its list of 25 most wanted fugitive suspects and announced a reward of 250,000 Canadian dollars for help in his arrest. According to foreign media, Canada's public safety programme 'Bolo' has appealed to the public to provide information for Goldy Brar's arrest. Goldy Brar, whose real name is Satinderjit Singh, is wanted in the case of the murder of Sukhdul Singh Gill in the Canadian city of Winnipeg in September 2023.

Police issued arrest warrants in February 2026 against Goldy Brar and one of his associates on charges of murder and conspiracy to murder. The US investigative agency FBI has also included Goldy Brar in its list of most wanted persons and set a reward of one million US dollars for help in his arrest. According to officials, efforts to find the suspect are continuing at the international level.
