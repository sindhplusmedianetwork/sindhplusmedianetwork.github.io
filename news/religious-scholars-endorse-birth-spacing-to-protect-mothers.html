Religious scholars endorse birth spacing to protect mothers and children

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Religious scholars from different schools of thought have endorsed healthy birth spacing, known as waqfa, as an effective way to protect maternal and child health, declaring it consistent with Islamic teachings and the principles of responsible parenthood. The endorsement came at a high-level consultative dialogue in Rawalpindi organised by the Population Council in collaboration with the United Nations Population Fund, bringing together religious scholars, health experts and policymakers. Participants signed a joint declaration recognising birth spacing as a beneficial health intervention and pledged to promote awareness through mosques, educational institutions and community networks across the country.

Speaking at the event, Population Council Pakistan Country Director Dr Ali Mohammad Mir said Pakistan faced serious maternal health challenges, with a mother dying every 37 minutes. Central Ruet-e-Hilal Committee Chairman and khateeb of Lahore's Badshahi Mosque, Maulana Abdul Khabeer Azad, said protecting mothers and children was fully aligned with Islamic teachings, and urged religious scholars to carry the message to mosques, households and communities across the country.
