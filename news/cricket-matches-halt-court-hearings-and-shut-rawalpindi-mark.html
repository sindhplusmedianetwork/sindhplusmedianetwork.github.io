Cricket Matches Halt Court Hearings and Shut Rawalpindi Markets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cricket matches at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium have virtually paralysed the system of producing suspects from Adiala Jail before the city's courts. Police guards who transport suspects and the prisoner vans have both been deployed at the stadium, so the process of producing suspects before courts has been completely suspended. As a result, hearings before 50 courts across Rawalpindi district have been delayed, and in the past three days alone hearings in 7,500 cases could not take place.

Traders have also suffered heavily, with shops on both sides of Double Road and stalls in the Food Market shut, reportedly causing daily losses worth millions of rupees. The Metro Bus station on Double Road has been completely closed, nearby petrol pumps have been sealed, and the city's largest recreational facility, Allama Iqbal Park, has been closed because of the security arrangements. With the Karachi Test also shifted to Rawalpindi, the same situation is expected to continue for the next 10 days.

Traders and residents have appealed to the administration to address their problems and demanded that the stadium be shifted to Islamabad.
