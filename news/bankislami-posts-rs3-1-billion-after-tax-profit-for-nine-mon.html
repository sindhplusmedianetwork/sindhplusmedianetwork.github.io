BankIslami Posts Rs3.1 Billion After-Tax Profit for Nine Months of 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

BankIslami has announced an after-tax profit of Rs3.1 billion for the nine months ending 30 September 2026. The bank's total deposits reached Rs630.7 billion during the period, reflecting continued customer confidence. Current accounts grew 9.7 percent since December 2025, lifting their share of total deposits from 43.6 percent to 50 percent.

The improved deposit mix strengthened the bank's CASA ratio to 74.7 percent, up from 71.2 percent in December 2025. Total income for the period stood at Rs31.9 billion, while the capital adequacy ratio was recorded at 14.9 percent, above regulatory requirements. Earlier in the year, the bank was recognised as Pakistan's best Islamic digital bank at the Euromoney Islamic Finance Awards.

President and CEO Imran H Sheikh said the results reflect growing trust in interest-free banking as the country moves towards a riba-free financial system.
