Iran unveils giant Tehran billboards threatening to kill Trump

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran unveiled giant billboards across Tehran on Tuesday bearing the message 'We will kill Trump', alongside an image of the US president behind bars with his head bowed, in a sharp escalation of official anti-American rhetoric. A second display showed Trump with US Vice President JD Vance, special envoy Steve Witkoff and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu with bloodied faces and torn clothes, next to damaged American and Israeli flags. The displays at Enghelab Square and Tajrish Square came amid renewed diplomatic efforts to bring Tehran and Washington back to the negotiating table, with Trump saying a deal 'could happen'.

Iran's government accuses Trump of deliberately targeting civilian sites, including a school and a wedding ceremony, during the ongoing conflict. The billboards drew international attention as tensions between the two countries remained high.
