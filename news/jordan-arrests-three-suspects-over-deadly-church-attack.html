Jordan Arrests Three Suspects Over Deadly Church Attack

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jordanian security forces on Monday arrested three suspects over an attack on a church last week that killed nine people, with the trio wounded in a dawn raid and shootout, authorities said. The kingdom's Public Security Directorate said the group were apprehended at an abandoned house where they had barricaded themselves inside and opened fire on security forces. One of the suspects was taken to hospital in critical condition, while the other two were stable, and there were no casualties among security forces.

On Friday, gunmen stormed the compound of the Greek Orthodox St George's Church in the village of Hammoud in Karak governorate during a funeral procession, authorities said. Karak is home to a significant Christian community in Muslim-majority Jordan, where Christians make up between two and three per cent of the population. Jordan's King Abdullah II said security forces chased the attackers to the very end, writing that the terrorists would not escape punishment and that no proponent of ideologies dividing Jordanians would be tolerated.

Interior Minister Mazen al-Faraya said the attackers were all Jordanians, had been holed up in a building near the site of the attack, and that there was no indication of any external contacts or collaboration with foreign parties. Earlier, the prime minister ordered a three-day period of national mourning, eight of the nine victims were laid to rest on Sunday next to the church in a funeral that drew a huge crowd, and the ninth victim was to be buried on Monday.
