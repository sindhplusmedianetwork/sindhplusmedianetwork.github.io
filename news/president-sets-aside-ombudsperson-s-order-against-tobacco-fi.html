President sets aside ombudsperson's order against tobacco firm ex-employee

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The president of Pakistan has accepted a representation by a former employee of a multinational tobacco company, setting aside a federal ombudsperson's order declaring him guilty of subjecting a female colleague to sexual harassment, and ordered his reinstatement. In an order, the President's Secretariat allowed the representation of Raza Pervez Fateh under Section 14 of the Federal Ombudsmen Institutional Reforms Act, 2013, setting aside the July 24, 2026, order of the Federal Ombudsperson for Protection Against Harassment at Workplace, Lahore. The ombudsperson had held that Fateh subjected a colleague to sexual harassment through persistent unwelcome verbal and written communication.

The order was signed by retired Justice Irfan Qadir, Consultant (Legal Affairs) at the President's Secretariat. The president accepted the representation, setting aside the ombudsperson's order and issued the direction that the petitioner be reinstated. The decision overturns the workplace harassment finding against the former tobacco company employee and restores his employment.
