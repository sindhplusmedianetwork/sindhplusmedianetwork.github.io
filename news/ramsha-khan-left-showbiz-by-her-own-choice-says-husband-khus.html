Ramsha Khan left showbiz by her own choice, says husband Khushal Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Khushal Khan has said that his wife, actress Ramsha Khan, stepped away from showbiz entirely by her own choice. Speaking on host Hassan Chaudhry's programme, he made clear that he had put no pressure on her to quit acting. He rejected the speculation that he had forced her to leave the industry, saying Ramsha was firm in her decision.

According to Khushal, Ramsha had even jokingly warned him not to question her decision. He said she also distanced herself from social media because of the negative attitudes and unpleasant experiences she faced there. Khushal added that he would not impose such decisions on his wife or on any member of his family.
