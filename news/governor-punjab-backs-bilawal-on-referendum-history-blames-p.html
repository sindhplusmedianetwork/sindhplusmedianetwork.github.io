Governor Punjab backs Bilawal on referendum history, blames PTI for most mistakes behind KP emergency

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Governor Sardar Saleem Haider Khan has said Bilawal Bhutto is right that the history of referendums in Pakistan has been terrible, and that the decision about Sindh cannot be made by others because the people of Sindh will decide it themselves. Speaking to the Journalists Association at Punjab House in Islamabad, he said he is not in favour of governor's rule, but circumstances had made it a need of the time, and the emergency was imposed because of the PTI's mistakes, which he put at 80 percent, adding that the matter is now being taken to Parliament for debate, which is a good thing. He repeated the Sindhi saying that the people of Sindh have always said they would die but never give up Sindh, and said that whatever happens should happen by consensus.

He said that as you sow, so shall you reap is God's law, and that the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa governor will leave office and go to Dera Ismail Khan whenever the party asks him to. He said a provincial chief minister being in jail is certainly against the democratic system, but Imran Khan had done the same things during his own tenure, recalling that Imran Khan had said in America that he would remove the air conditioning and take back the facilities given in jail. He also said a specific narrative was used to sideline the PPP from Punjab, while Sindh's health system is better than Punjab's, with heart, liver and kidney transplants carried out on a ten-rupee slip, and emergency services that can take a patient from any corner of the province to a central hospital within one to one and a half hours.

He added that a government school in Sindh has won the honour of being the best school, and said the narrative that Sindh has been ruined is wrong, because visiting Sindh showed how good its roads, education system and health system are.
