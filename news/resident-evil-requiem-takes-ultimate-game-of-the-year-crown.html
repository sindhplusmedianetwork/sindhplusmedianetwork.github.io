Resident Evil: Requiem takes Ultimate Game of the Year crown at Golden Joystick Awards

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Resident Evil: Requiem has taken home the Ultimate Game of the Year crown at the 2026 Golden Joystick Awards, the first awards show of the season. The survival horror title also won Console Game of the Year, while the indie action-adventure Crimson Desert was named PC Game of the Year. Angela Sant'Albano, who portrayed protagonist Grace Ashcroft, won Best Lead Performer, beating Liam McIntyre for Marvel's Wolverine and Patrick Gibson for 007: First Light.

The Golden Joysticks could be an early indicator of which games will earn recognition later in the season, including at The Game Awards in December, according to the gaming outlet Polygon. The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI was not in competition, since it is due to release on November 19, nearly a week after the awards were handed out. Indie title Big Walk, developed by House House, won the Critics' Choice Award, but the game has been overshadowed by controversy over its reported use of AI large language models in development.

The developer confirmed the use of AI this week, after the November 5 cut-off date for voting, and said under 0.1 per cent of the game's code involved generative AI tools.
