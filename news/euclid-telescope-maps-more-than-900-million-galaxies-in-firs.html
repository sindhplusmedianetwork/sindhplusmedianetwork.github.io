Euclid telescope maps more than 900 million galaxies in first big data release

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The European Space Agency (ESA) has released the first major collection of data from its Euclid space telescope, covering observations of more than 900 million galaxies. The data, unveiled on Thursday, covers 13 months of observations and totals 1.7 petabytes, equal to about 1.7 million gigabytes. The survey covers a part of the sky equal to about 9,500 times the size of the moon as seen from Earth.

Scientists will use the data to study dark matter and dark energy, the unseen forces shaping the universe. The mission's goal is to complete the largest-ever three-dimensional atlas of the cosmos, covering more than a third of the sky, by 2031. ESA's Euclid project scientist Valeria Pettorino called the release a major milestone for the mission and for everyone involved.
