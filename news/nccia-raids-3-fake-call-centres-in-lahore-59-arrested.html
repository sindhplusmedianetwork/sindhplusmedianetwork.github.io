NCCIA Raids 3 Fake Call Centres in Lahore, 59 Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency raided three fake call centres in Lahore's Gulberg area and arrested 59 suspects. Those arrested include 52 men and seven women, the agency said. The NCCIA seized 61 laptops, 21 computer systems and 52 mobile phones during the raids.

According to the agency, the suspects lured foreign citizens with fake mobile upgrades and cheap packages, and hacked restaurant data through a fake app to divert weekly payments into their own accounts. NCCIA chief Syed Ali Nasir Rizvi said the crackdown against international cyber fraud would continue.
