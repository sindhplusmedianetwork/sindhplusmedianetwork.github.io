Trump Tells Saudi Arabia to Accept Israel's Help Against Houthis

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States President Donald Trump has called on Saudi Arabia to take Israel's help against Yemen's Houthis. Speaking to journalists outside the White House on October 16, the president said Israel could be very helpful to Riyadh and expressed the view that the prospects of normalization between Saudi Arabia and Israel were brighter than ever before. Trump, however, announced no new agreement between the two countries.

His remarks came days after the Israeli newspaper Haaretz reported on October 13 that Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman had shown no interest in such cooperation. According to that report, the crown prince feared that open military cooperation with Israel would provoke a negative reaction from the public.
