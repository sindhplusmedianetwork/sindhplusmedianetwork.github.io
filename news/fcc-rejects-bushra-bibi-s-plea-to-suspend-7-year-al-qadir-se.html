FCC Rejects Bushra Bibi's Plea to Suspend 7-Year Al-Qadir Sentence

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Federal Constitutional Court on Wednesday rejected a plea by Bushra Bibi, wife of jailed former prime minister Imran Khan, to suspend her seven-year prison sentence in the £190 million Al-Qadir Trust case. A two-member bench headed by Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan and including Justice Ali Baqar Najafi ruled there was no sufficient ground to interfere with the Islamabad High Court's April 30 order at this stage. The plea had argued that Bushra Bibi's status as a woman and her serious medical condition, including rapidly deteriorating eyesight, warranted suspension of the sentence.

Her counsel Salman Safdar also complained that the National Accountability Bureau had repeatedly sought adjournments, prolonging the proceedings. The court, however, observed that the main appeal was already fixed for hearing and should be decided expeditiously instead. It directed both sides to extend full cooperation for an early disposal of the appeal while discouraging unnecessary adjournments.

The bench had reserved its ruling earlier in the day and asked the defence to submit written arguments before a detailed judgment.
