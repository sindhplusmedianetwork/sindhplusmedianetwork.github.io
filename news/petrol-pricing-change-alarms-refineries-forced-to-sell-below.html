Petrol pricing change alarms refineries; 'forced to sell below cost'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal cabinet's surprise change to the petrol pricing mechanism has set off alarm bells among Pakistan's oil refineries. The cabinet directed refineries to deposit Rs49 per litre into the petroleum levy account to offset a Rs14.71-per-litre reduction in the levy, a decision announced on Friday night without prior consultation with the industry. Refinery executives have called the move a 'serious setback', warning it could force them to sell petrol below production cost and push gross refining margins into negative territory.

Attock Refinery CEO Adil Khattak said the decision undermines the assurance given under the Brownfield Refineries Up-gradation Policy that no adverse changes would be made during project execution.
