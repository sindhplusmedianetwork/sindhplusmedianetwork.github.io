CDA Calls Bari Imam House an Illegal Occupation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Capital Development Authority has described the building known as Bari Imam House as an illegally occupied property, maintaining that no structure under this name exists in official records and that the land housing the Bari Imam shrine is separately recorded under different khasra numbers. The authority has filed a miscellaneous application before the Islamabad High Court seeking vacation of a stay order in the pending case, stating that the land in Noorpur Shahan had already been acquired and compensation paid to its owners. The CDA argued that merely naming a building Bari Imam House or associating it with the shrine did not confer any legal rights over the land on the petitioner.

The case will now proceed before the court, which will decide on the authority's request to lift the stay. The authority said the land had already been acquired and compensation paid, so the petitioner held no legal right over it.
