Rupee gains further ground against dollar in both forex markets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistani rupee remained stronger against the US dollar on Wednesday in both foreign exchange markets. In the interbank market, the dollar fell to as low as Rs276.62 during trading before closing at Rs276.86. In the open currency market, the dollar closed at Rs278.61, down by two paisas.

Currency dealers attributed the rupee's strength to the defence-industrial cooperation accord signed with Turkiye, Qatar and Indonesia. Stable foreign exchange reserves also supported the local currency, according to market watchers.
