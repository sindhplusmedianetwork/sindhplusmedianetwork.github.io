Punjab IGP Orders Intensified Action Against Crime Hotspots

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Inspector General of Police Abdul Kareem chaired a crime review meeting at the Central Police Office in Lahore on Friday. He directed all regional and district police officers to intensify action against crime hotspots across the province. The IGP ordered faster arrests of proclaimed offenders and improved rates of challan submission to courts.

He also directed daily follow-up on pending forensic reports, particularly in cases of sexual violence. The DIG Investigation briefed the meeting that heinous crime had declined during the current week. The IGP stressed that there would be no compromise on public safety and that officers failing to deliver results would face accountability.

The meeting reviewed district-wise crime data and ongoing operations against organised criminal gangs.
