Aurangzeb Meets Moody's, Discusses Pakistan's Rating-Upgrade Prospects

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb met Moody's officials in Bangkok on Wednesday on the sidelines of the IMF-World Bank Annual Meetings to discuss Pakistan's rating-upgrade prospects. The meeting covered foreign exchange reserves, the current account position, forward liabilities and the government's structural reform agenda. Aurangzeb said the government was targeting an upgrade to B+ by the first quarter of fiscal year 2027-28.

Moody's had upgraded Pakistan's sovereign rating to B3 from Caa1 in August 2026, while S&P raised it to B with a stable outlook in July 2026. On the sidelines, the finance minister also met OPEC Fund President Abdulhamid Alkhalifa, where financing for the M6 motorway and the ML-1 railway project came under discussion.
