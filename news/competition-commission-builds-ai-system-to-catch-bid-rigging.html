Competition Commission Builds AI System to Catch Bid Rigging

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Competition Commission of Pakistan has developed an artificial intelligence-powered system to detect hidden bid-rigging and cartelisation in public procurement. The Bid-Rigging Detection System, known as BRDS, is designed to screen large volumes of procurement data and flag suspicious bidding patterns for investigation. Pakistan's public procurement market is estimated at 60 billion dollars a year, accounting for roughly 15 to 20 per cent of the country's gross domestic product.

The system links data from the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority, provincial procurement bodies and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. It can identify complex practices such as bid rotation, cover bidding and the participation of related companies across multiple tenders. CCP Chairman Farid Ahmad Tarar said procurement worth trillions of rupees could not be effectively monitored through complaints and manual checks alone.

He stressed that the system's alerts would serve only as investigative leads, with every case requiring independent verification and legal assessment under the Competition Act 2010. The initiative has been featured in Stanford Computational Antitrust's Fifth Cross-Agency Report and is at an advanced pre-deployment stage, with data integration work still under way.
