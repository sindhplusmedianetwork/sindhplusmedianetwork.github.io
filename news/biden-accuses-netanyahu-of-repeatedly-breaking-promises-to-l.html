Biden Accuses Netanyahu of Repeatedly Breaking Promises to Limit Civilian Deaths in Gaza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former US president Joe Biden has accused Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of repeatedly breaking promises to reduce civilian deaths during the Gaza war. Biden made the revelation in his new memoir, 'Promise Me, America', excerpts of which have been released in the American media. His 480-page book is due to be published on November 17.

Biden says Netanyahu would assure him that he would heed US demands, yet military operations continued in which more Palestinian civilians were killed. According to Biden, he had pressed Israel since the start of the war in Gaza to take greater precautions to protect civilian lives during military operations. He wrote that he raised the matter in several private meetings and telephone conversations with Netanyahu.

The Israeli prime minister also acknowledged that the rising number of Palestinian civilian deaths was harming Israel's global standing and American support for the war.
