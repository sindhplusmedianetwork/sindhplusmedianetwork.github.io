Japanese Minister Blames X Translation for Diplomatic Embarrassment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Japanese Trade Minister Ryosei Akazawa blamed Elon Musk's X on Friday after its automatic translation feature implied he did not know the names of world leaders he was meeting. The tool wrongly rendered his post about meeting Bangladeshi Prime Minister Tarique Rahman as a meeting with ousted premier Sheikh Hasina. Akazawa said it was the third such translation error, after similar mistakes involving Kuwait's oil minister and US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick.

He appealed to X to fix the tool. The mistaken rendering turned a routine meeting post into a diplomatic embarrassment.
