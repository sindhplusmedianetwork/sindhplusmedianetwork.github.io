Pension is a legal right, not charity, rules Islamabad High Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has directed the Finance Ministry and the concerned authorities to immediately release the pension and other benefits of National Savings Centre officer Naveedullah on his retirement. Justice Shah Rukh Arjumand issued the written decision in the case. The court remarked that pension is not charity, concession or donation, but an employee's legal and constitutional right.

The Finance Ministry had argued that the officer's appointment on contract was not made through the Federal Public Service Commission. The court record showed that Naveedullah began service on 28 May 1995 and was later regularised after his transfer to the National Savings Centre. The court directed that his pension and benefits be released without further delay.
