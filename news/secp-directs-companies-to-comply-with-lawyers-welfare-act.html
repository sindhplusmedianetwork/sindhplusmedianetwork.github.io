SECP Directs Companies to Comply With Lawyers Welfare Act

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has issued directions to all registered companies in the country. The companies have been told to ensure compliance with the requirements of the Lawyers Welfare and Protection Act 2023. The directions specifically relate to the appointment of legal advisers by the companies.

SECP said the move was aimed at strengthening legal compliance and protecting the welfare of lawyers associated with corporate entities. Companies failing to comply may face regulatory action under the law.
