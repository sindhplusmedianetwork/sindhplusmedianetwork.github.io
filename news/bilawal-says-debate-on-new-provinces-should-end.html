Bilawal Says Debate on New Provinces Should End

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari on Tuesday called for a full stop to conversations about creating new provinces in Pakistan. He made the remarks while speaking to the media at Spencer Eye Hospital in Karachi's Lyari area, alongside Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah. Bilawal said the people of Sindh and their elected representatives had already presented their decision on the matter.

He pointed out that the Sindh Assembly had made its stance evident at least seven to eight times. He also said the federal government should pay attention to Karachi, which he described as the country's economic hub. His remarks come amid an ongoing national debate about new administrative units, sparked by Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's call in July for dialogue on governance reforms.

The PPP, which rules Sindh, has strongly opposed suggestions to divide the province, while the MQM-P has demanded that Karachi be made a separate province.
