Iran Claims It Shot Down One US Drone, Captured Another Over Strait of Hormuz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Revolutionary Guards have claimed that their naval electronic warfare unit shot down an advanced American MQ-1 drone over the Strait of Hormuz and seized control of an MQ-9 spy drone, landing it intact. According to Tasnim news agency, the Guards said the drones were mapping Iran's air-defence systems along the Persian Gulf coast. Data recovered from the captured drone showed it had flown in from a southern Gulf country, the report added.

The claim follows an earlier IRGC statement that another MQ-1 had been downed in the Strait of Hormuz.
