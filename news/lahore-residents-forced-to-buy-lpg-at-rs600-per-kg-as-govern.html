Lahore Residents Forced to Buy LPG at Rs600 Per Kg as Government Fails to Control Mafia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

People in Lahore are being forced to buy LPG at up to Rs600 per kg despite OGRA fixing the official price at Rs295 per kg. The government has failed to rein in the LPG mafia, according to the report. Shopkeepers say OGRA has taken no action against profiteers in two months.

They say enforcement agencies seize pumps from small retailers instead of acting against the mafia. Shopkeepers say they buy gas at Rs550-600 per kg, so they cannot sell at the official rate. In Karachi too, LPG is selling at Rs500-600 per kg, with no area offering it below Rs500, even though OGRA set the November price at Rs294 per kg.
