Quetta's Iconic Hanna Lake Losing Its Charm Amid Severe Water Shortage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Quetta's iconic Hanna Lake, once the jewel of the city's natural beauty, is losing its charm as a prolonged drought and falling water levels turn the 1,818-acre water body into cracked, barren land. Located about 18 kilometres from the city, the lake was long known for boating, water sports and the migratory birds that visited its shores each year. Today much of the lake bed lies dry, leaving tourists disappointed and raising fears that the lake may be slipping toward permanent ruin.

The drying lake has also hit the livelihoods of the boatmen and local businesses that depended on visiting tourists. Environmental experts warn that the lake's ecosystem, the birds that visit it and the orchards in its surroundings are all at serious risk. They are urging the authorities to take immediate restoration and water-management measures before the damage becomes irreversible.
