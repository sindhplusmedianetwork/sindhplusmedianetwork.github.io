UK to Deploy RAF Typhoons to Defend Saudi Cities from Houthi Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Britain will deploy Royal Air Force Typhoon fighter jets on defensive counter-air missions to help protect Saudi cities and critical infrastructure from Houthi aerial attacks, the Ministry of Defence said, according to Reuters. Saudi forces intercepted a ballistic missile aimed at the kingdom's capital, said a spokesman for the Riyadh-led coalition backing Yemen's government, after authorities issued a danger warning and AFP journalists reported hearing blasts. US special envoy Tom Barrack said Washington is providing logistical support to Saudi Arabia, but a US ground intervention in the conflict involving Yemen's Houthis 'appears pointless'.

Oil prices advanced on the escalation, with Brent crude futures climbing to $110.10 a barrel, near their highest since May 20, as traders priced in a geopolitical risk premium. The United Nations said more than 250,000 people have been internally displaced in Yemen since the civil war reignited in July. After an uneasy four-year truce, Yemen's conflict surged back to life when the Houthis seized strategic territory along the Red Sea coast last month, widening a Middle East crisis already fuelled by the US-Iran conflict.
