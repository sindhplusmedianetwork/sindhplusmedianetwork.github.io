Bushra Bibi's Interim Bail in 29 Cases Extended Till November 26

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A special anti-terrorism court in Rawalpindi has extended the interim bail of Bushra Bibi in 29 cases until November 26. Bushra Bibi is the wife of former prime minister Imran Khan. Judge Amjad Ali Shah heard the bail petitions, with her lawyer Muhammad Faisal Malik and the prosecution team present in court.

The cases relate to the November protests and were registered in Rawalpindi, Taxila and Attock. The court extended the interim bail and adjourned further hearing of the petitions without proceedings.
