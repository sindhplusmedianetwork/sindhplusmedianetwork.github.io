Trump Says Fresh US Strikes on Iran Highly Unlikely if Talks Progress

US President Donald Trump has said fresh American strikes on Iran are highly unlikely as long as negotiations with Tehran continue to make progress. He told CNN the decision would depend on whether Iran signs what he called a very strong agreement with the United States. Trump added that Washington had already identified 38 bridges inside Iran as potential targets, and warned that Iran would be in a tough situation if the talks collapse.

The remarks came as Iran resumed talks with the United States in Muscat, following US and Israeli strikes on Iran's missile and air defence systems. Trump said Iran had agreed to halt the killing of protesters and was ready to negotiate, adding that the situation there was under control. He also said the United States would be ready to provide food and medical assistance to Iran if needed.
