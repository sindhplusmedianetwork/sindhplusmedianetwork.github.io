Asian woman arrested in Bahrain over fatal red-light crash

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Authorities in Bahrain have arrested an Asian woman and presented her before a court on charges of running a red light and crushing a motorcyclist, with the criminal court beginning the hearing of the case. According to the Bahraini newspaper Akhbar Al Khaleej, prosecutors allege that her negligence on October 8, 2026 caused the death of the motorcyclist. The 52-year-old accused told the court she did not stop her car because the driver behind her was honking and forcing her to cross the intersection.

Her lawyer sought an adjournment to review the case files and prepare a formal defence. The report said the incident happened when she violated the red traffic signal and failed to exercise the necessary care and attention while driving, causing injuries that ultimately led to the victim's death. Authorities also announced this week the arrest of three Asian nationals for selling drugs, with narcotics worth more than 166,000 Bahraini dinars seized in two separate operations.
