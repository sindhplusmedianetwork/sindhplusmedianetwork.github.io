Punjab Safe City Authority wins global police award in the US

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Safe City Authority has won the global award of the International Association of Chiefs of Police (IACP) in the United States. According to the authority's spokesman, Managing Director Muhammad Ahsan Younas received the award during the global police conference held in Florida. The global honour was given to the Safe City Authority for the effective use of modern technology in preventing vehicle theft and crime.

The spokesman said the authority's modern security systems and digital surveillance methods are gaining recognition at the global level. Managing Director Muhammad Ahsan Younas said the Safe City Authority is providing world-standard services for crime prevention and the protection of citizens. He said this achievement has highlighted the positive image of the country and established Punjab's prominent identity in the field of modern policing.
