NAB issues second notice to JUI-F chief's son over alleged 'illegal appointments' in Pakistan Post

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Accountability Bureau on Tuesday issued a second call-up notice to Asad Mahmood, son of Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, directing him to appear before the anti-graft watchdog on November 13. The notice relates to an inquiry into alleged illegal appointments in the Pakistan Post Office Department during Mahmood's tenure as federal minister for communications and postal services from 2022 to 2023. Mahmood was required to appear before NAB on November 10 but failed to do so, instead submitting a written response through his lawyer, which NAB officials declared unsatisfactory.

The fresh notice warned that failure to appear could lead to legal proceedings under Section 31 of the National Accountability Ordinance, 1999. NAB had earlier summoned him on November 5 over alleged irregularities in appointments at the department. According to the earlier notice, the Establishment Division had authorised recruitment against 3,938 posts at the Pakistan Post Office, but 4,252 people were allegedly appointed.

The inquiry has also flagged appointments against 2,646 abolished posts, allegedly in violation of cabinet committee directives, and NAB maintains that many permanent appointments were made despite recommendations for contractual recruitment through a testing agency.
