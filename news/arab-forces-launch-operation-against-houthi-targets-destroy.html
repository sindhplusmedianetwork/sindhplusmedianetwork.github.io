Arab Forces Launch Operation Against Houthi Targets, Destroy 7 Military Sites

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Arab joint forces spokesman Major General Turki al-Maliki has said a military operation has begun against those endangering the lives of civilians in Yemen and Saudi Arabia. In a statement issued from Riyadh, the spokesman said important military installations of the Houthi militia will be targeted during the operation. He said joint forces targeted Houthi bases at Yemen's Hodeidah seaport, and the operation will continue in Sanaa, Taiz and Saada until the Houthi militia's military targets and ballistic capabilities are eliminated.

Major General Turki al-Maliki said the operation against Houthi installations in Yemen is under way and joint forces have destroyed seven Houthi military targets. He said the destroyed targets were used for attacks, and six factories preparing launch pads for ballistic missiles and drones were destroyed. Important Houthi barracks in Sanaa were also destroyed, he added.
