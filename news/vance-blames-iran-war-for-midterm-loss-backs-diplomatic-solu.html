Vance Blames Iran War for Midterm Loss, Backs 'Diplomatic Solution'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Vice President JD Vance has admitted that the war with Iran cost the Republicans control of Congress in the midterm elections. In a post on X, Vance backed a 'diplomatic solution' to the conflict, writing: 'Everyone knows why we lost.' The remarks mark a notable shift in tone from the administration after the November 3 vote. Meanwhile, thousands of people rallied outside the former US embassy in Tehran to mark the anniversary of its 1979 seizure, chanting against America and Israel.

Oil prices fell by more than $3 a barrel as investors waited for Washington's next move. Iran's supreme leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei warned that the scope of the war would widen if the United States resumed its attacks.
