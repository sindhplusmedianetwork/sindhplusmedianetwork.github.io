Samantha Ruth Prabhu Gives Birth to a Son

Indian actress Samantha Ruth Prabhu and filmmaker and director Raj Nidimoru have been blessed with a son. According to Indian media reports, the actress shared the good news of her first child with fans via social media, along with the baby's name and photo. The couple from the Indian film world named their newborn son 'Dev'.

Samantha wrote in an Instagram post that their beautiful son, Dev Nidimoru, was born on November 19, 2026, calling it the happiest moment of their lives so far. The professional relationship between Samantha and Raj Nidimoru began in 2021 when the two worked together in the web series The Family Man 2, and later both also worked together in the web series Citadel: Honey Bunny. Speculation about their close relationship gained strength when they were seen together at various public events.

The couple married on December 1, 2025 at the Isha Yoga Centre in Coimbatore.
