Attempt to impeach War Secretary Pete Hegseth failed badly, says Donald Trump

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said the attempt to impeach War Secretary Pete Hegseth failed badly. The impeachment motion had been tabled by Republican Congressman Thomas Massie. Trump claimed that, apart from Massie, every Republican voted in support of Hegseth.

He credited Hegseth with successes in Venezuela and Iran. Trump also pointed to record military recruitment under Hegseth. He said the House had cleared the War Secretary.
