NCCIA Busts Illegal Call Centre in Karachi; 11 Held for Targeting Foreign Citizens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has raided and shut down an illegal call centre operating from the first floor of Zafar Centre on Sharea Faisal in Karachi. The centre was running a cyber-fraud operation aimed at foreign citizens, particularly Americans. Eleven people, including the owner Yasir Gilgiti, were arrested during the raid.

According to investigators, the suspects posed as representatives of US banks and loan-settlement companies to trick victims into revealing their social security numbers, card details and banking information. An FIR, numbered 106/2026, has been registered under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016, as amended in 2025, along with relevant sections of the Pakistan Penal Code. Officials said the operation is part of a wider crackdown on cyber-fraud networks operating from the country.
