SBP Governor Says Exports and Economic Resilience Are Key to Sustainable Growth

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

State Bank Governor Jameel Ahmad said stronger exports, savings and investment are needed to break Pakistan's recurring boom-and-bust cycles. He said the country needs to build sustainable export-led growth. Speaking at Standard Chartered Bank's Annual Global Research Briefing in Karachi, he said macroeconomic stability had been restored.

He added that the economy was better placed to absorb external shocks, including those from the Middle East conflict. He said real GDP growth reached 3.7 percent in FY26. Inflation averaged close to target during the year.

The current account deficit is expected to stay within 0-1 percent of GDP.
