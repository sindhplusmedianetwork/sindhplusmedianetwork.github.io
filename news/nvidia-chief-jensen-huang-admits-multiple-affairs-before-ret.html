Nvidia chief Jensen Huang admits multiple affairs before returning as CEO

Nvidia co-founder and chief executive Jensen Huang admitted to multiple extramarital affairs before the company's board of directors ahead of his return to the role. According to a report in the Wall Street Journal, the 63-year-old told board members in a meeting lasting several hours about relationships that had not previously come to light, and expressed remorse over his conduct. The report said that after a long discussion in which Huang acknowledged his mistakes and spoke about the importance of openness and honesty, the directors decided to hand him the company's leadership again.

Earlier, news of two of Huang's extramarital affairs had surfaced, one with company vice president Kimberly Powell and another with a Chinese businesswoman named Alice Huang, whom US authorities have said is a spy whose links with Chinese intelligence are under investigation. Later, reports of a relationship with a third woman also emerged, after which Huang admitted to further relationships with more women in the board meeting, expressed remorse and assured the board it would not happen again. Huang had admitted infidelity to his wife in a message sent to employees on 8 November, and he had led the company since 1993 before resigning after the affairs came to light.

The board has now unanimously reappointed Huang as president and chief executive, and the company says that after consultation with US authorities it is satisfied his return poses no risk to national security.
