Spain Holds First Nationwide General Strike in 14 Years Over Rents and Cost of Living

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Spain held its first nationwide general strike in 14 years on Wednesday in protest against rising rents and the cost of living, bringing transport, industrial activity and other public services to a halt. According to the BBC, the country's two largest trade unions took part in the strike along with tenants' associations. Roads were closed in Madrid, Barcelona and several other cities, traffic and public transport were disrupted, and protesters burned tyres at some places.

Police said 40 people were arrested in incidents linked to the strike. A major trigger for the protest was the eviction of 87-year-old Maricarmen Abascal, who had lived in her Madrid home for almost 70 years but had to leave after the new owners raised the rent; she died in hospital a few weeks later. According to the report, the average monthly rent for a 50-square-metre home in Madrid reached 1,170 euros in October, about half of Spain's average gross monthly salary.

Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez supported the strike, while the opposition described it as a political campaign ahead of the parliamentary elections on November 29. Trade unions claimed around four million people took part in the strike, while business organisations said participation was limited in different regions and sectors.
