US woman, 31, dies after cutting medication on ChatGPT's advice; parents sue OpenAI

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 31-year-old American woman lost her life after reducing her epilepsy medication on the advice of ChatGPT, her parents have alleged in a lawsuit against OpenAI. Bowen Wan, a resident of Seattle, lowered the dose of her medicine without medical supervision following the chatbot's guidance. She was found dead in her apartment in February.

Her parents have now taken the company to court over the tragedy. OpenAI expressed sorrow over the incident and stated that ChatGPT is not a substitute for medical treatment. The case has raised fresh questions about the risks of relying on artificial intelligence for health decisions.

Medical experts continue to warn that medication changes should only be made under a doctor's supervision.
