Bilawal Says State Must Ensure Every Child Has Access to Education and Healthcare

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chairman of Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Bilawal Bhutto Zardari, in his message on World Children's Day, said it is the fundamental responsibility of the state to ensure that every child has access to education, healthcare, protection, equal opportunities and a future filled with hope. He expressed concern, saying children were bearing the brunt of the country's unprecedented inflation, unemployment, poverty, climate change and growing social inequalities. The PPP Chairman said that, in line with the vision of Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto, the party had consistently taken practical steps to secure a better future for children, promote free education, improve healthcare facilities and provide a safe environment for their growth.

He reaffirmed the PPP's commitment to continuing its struggle for effective legislation to safeguard children's rights and ensure improved educational and healthcare facilities. Bilawal Bhutto Zardari urged parents, teachers, civil society and state institutions to fulfil their shared responsibility towards the protection, upbringing and bright future of children. He concluded that simply showing children dreams of a better tomorrow is not enough, and when children have books in their hands, dreams in their eyes and hope in their hearts, Pakistan will move towards new milestones of progress, peace and prosperity.
