Saba Qamar praises Bushra Ansari for heartwarming gesture

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Saba Qamar has praised veteran actress Bushra Ansari for her kindness after Ansari arranged a specially prepared home-cooked meal for her. The meal, of daal chawal and kebabs, was prepared by Ansari's chef after she learned that Qamar was craving home-cooked food. Qamar, who had been away from home for almost a month, shared a picture of the meal on Instagram.

She called Ansari a phenomenal actress and an even more wonderful human being. Qamar wished her happiness, good health and peace.
