SHC orders production of 14-year-old Bindya Kumari in forced marriage case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court's Larkana bench heard the case of 14-year-old Bindya Kumari, whose alleged abduction, marriage and religious conversion are under scrutiny. During the hearing, the court ordered police to produce both Bindya Kumari and Yasir Domki before it. The petitioner's lawyer, Athar Abbas Solangi, cited the Sindh Child Marriages Restraint Act 2013, arguing that under Sindh law no girl or boy under 18 can be married and that the law must be enforced in such cases.

Speaking to journalists after the hearing, Solangi said he was fighting the case on the basis of humanity, not religion, and that the law was equal whether the girl was Hindu, Muslim, Sikh or Christian. He demanded a strict law against forced religious conversion and questioned why Bindya was not being produced in court even though her whereabouts were known. Bindya's mother Kashish alleged that her daughter was abducted and forced to give a statement under pressure, and that the family was receiving threats.

According to the lawyer, the court said no notices would be issued until Bindya's statement was recorded, and recovering the girl was the responsibility of the Balochistan government.
