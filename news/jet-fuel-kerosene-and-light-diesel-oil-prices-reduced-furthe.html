Jet Fuel, Kerosene and Light Diesel Oil Prices Reduced Further

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prices of jet fuel, kerosene and light diesel oil have been reduced further, with the cut effective from today. Under the OGRA notification, the price of jet fuel fell by Rs12.06 per litre to Rs287.71. Kerosene became cheaper by Rs10.42 per litre, bringing its price to Rs273.22 per litre.

Light diesel oil fell by Rs11.44 per litre to Rs220.28 per litre. The reduction applies under the latest OGRA notification issued for these petroleum products.
