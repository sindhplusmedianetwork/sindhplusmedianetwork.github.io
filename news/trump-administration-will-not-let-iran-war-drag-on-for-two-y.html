Trump administration will not let Iran war drag on for two years, says JD Vance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Vice President JD Vance has said the Trump administration will not let the war with Iran become a conflict lasting two years, saying Iran's nuclear programme remains a threat to the United States. In an interview with Fox News, Vance said Americans were promised that the US would not join more long, endless wars, and that while President Trump believed destroying Iran's nuclear programme was necessary, the US would not let the war drag on for 10, 15 or even two years. Vance appeared to be referring to the two years remaining in President Trump's second term.

The United States and Israel began the war against Iran in late February, and President Trump has repeatedly said Iran must not be allowed to obtain nuclear weapons, while Iran has long maintained that its nuclear programme is for peaceful purposes and that it is not trying to build an atomic bomb. Vance added that although some neoconservatives and other circles want the US to remain in Iran permanently, that will not happen. He said the US has promised not to get entangled in a long war and expressed confidence that President Trump will end the conflict with outcomes very good for America.

His remarks came days after President Trump announced that he had assigned Vance to focus on alleged corruption in Democrat-run states and removed him from the process of negotiations and oversight related to the Iran war.
