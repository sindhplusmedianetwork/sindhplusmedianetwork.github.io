R4 Ministers' Meeting in Istanbul Expresses Solidarity with Saudi Arabia, Condemns Houthi Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At the Regional Four (R4) Foreign Ministers' Meeting held in Istanbul on Friday, Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar and other foreign ministers expressed solidarity with Saudi Arabia over the recent Houthi attacks targeting the kingdom, the Foreign Office said. The R4 group, comprising Pakistan, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and Turkiye, emerged in March and has been focusing on regional security and mediation efforts between the United States and Iran. Dar participated in the sixth R4 foreign ministers' meeting alongside Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan, Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty, and Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud.

The ministers reviewed regional developments since their last meeting in New York in September, with a particular focus on the Middle East, and expressed solidarity with Saudi Arabia while reaffirming their support for its sovereignty and territorial integrity. They condemned the Houthi attacks and underscored the importance of respecting state sovereignty. The R-4-plus meeting with France, Germany, Italy, and the United Kingdom was also held in Istanbul.

On the sidelines, Dar held bilateral meetings with his Turkish, Egyptian, and German counterparts, and welcomed the European countries' engagement with the R4 framework. British foreign minister Ed Miliband's office said the talks would essentially revolve around the Strait of Hormuz and the situation in Saudi Arabia.
