Seven-Year-Old Girl Strangled in Peshawar Over Mobile Phone Dispute, Niece Held

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A seven-year-old girl was strangled to death in the Khazana area of Peshawar, police said. The victim, Haleema, was killed by her niece after the girl's uncle stopped the niece from using a mobile phone excessively, which led to a grudge. A missing report for the child had been filed at Khazana police station on November 6.

Police traced the blind murder within 24 hours and recovered the body. The accused has been arrested and confessed to the crime during initial investigation, citing a domestic dispute and resentment, police said.
