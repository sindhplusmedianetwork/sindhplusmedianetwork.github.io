Rs37.5m robbery case takes a U-turn as police recover Rs25m

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police have recovered Rs25 million in the Rs37.5 million robbery case. The recovery came after an employee identified the suspects involved in the robbery. The case, involving Rs37.5 million, has now taken a U-turn with the recovery of a large part of the stolen money.

Police have recovered Rs25 million of the total amount so far. A hunt is under way for the other suspects in the case. Police are continuing their efforts to trace the remaining suspects and recover the rest of the money.
