US Measles Cases Hit 35-year High

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US measles cases have surpassed 5,000 for the first time since 1991, reaching their highest annual level in 35 years amid continuing outbreaks nationwide. The US CDC reported 5,182 cases on Friday. Unvaccinated people or those whose vaccination status was unknown accounted for 96 percent of infections in 2026.

The figures mark the worst measles year in the United States in more than three decades.
