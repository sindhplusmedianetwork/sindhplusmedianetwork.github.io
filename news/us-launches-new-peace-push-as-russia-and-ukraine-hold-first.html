US Launches New Peace Push as Russia and Ukraine Hold First Talks in Nine Months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian and Ukrainian officials met in Abu Dhabi for their first peace talks since February. US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff launched a new push to end the war. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio called the US-brokered meeting very productive.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the talks constructive and quite substantive. The talks covered prisoner releases, security guarantees and de-escalation. A second day of negotiations was scheduled for Wednesday, though the sides remain far apart on territory and security terms.
