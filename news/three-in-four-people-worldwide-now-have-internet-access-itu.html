Three in Four People Worldwide Now Have Internet Access, ITU Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three out of four people in the world now have access to the internet, the UN's International Telecommunication Union has reported. For the first time, mobile internet has become affordable in landlocked and small-island developing countries. However, significant differences in internet speed and quality remain.

In high-income countries, 86 percent of the population has access to 5G networks, while in low-income countries the figure is just 4 percent. A mobile internet user in those countries consumes an average of 3.4 GB of data per month — roughly six times less than users in wealthy countries. ITU Secretary-General Doreen Bogdan-Martin said it is more necessary than ever to expand internet access and ensure that every person everywhere genuinely benefits from it.
