'It should be clear where we stand after Pahalgam': India reacts to Mann's Lahore invitation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's foreign ministry has reacted to the invitation for Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann to visit Lahore, restating India's position after the Pahalgam attack. Foreign ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said it should be completely clear to everyone where India stands on Pakistan after the Pahalgam terrorist attack. Mann was invited to attend the World Punjabi Conference in Lahore on 27 November, an event bringing together Punjabi voices from both sides of the border.

Pakistan has said it would consider issuing him a visa if he decides to travel, keeping the door open for the visit. Any travel, however, would also need clearance from the Indian government, which has now made its position plain. The exchange underlines how the Pahalgam incident continues to shape relations between the two countries, even around a cultural gathering.
