Federal Constitutional Court marks first anniversary, issues commemorative stamp

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A ceremony was held in Islamabad to mark one year since the establishment of the Federal Constitutional Court. Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan said the court had decided 9,000 cases during its first year, and remarked that the year had passed so quickly they were already marking its first anniversary. The ceremony was attended by all the judges, the attorney general and office-bearers of the Pakistan Bar and the Supreme Court Bar.

A special documentary on the court's first year was shown at the start of the ceremony. Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan unveiled the commemorative postage stamp issued for the occasion. He said the bench and the bar work together to deliver justice, and urged those working at the court to work with dedication.

An e-court was also inaugurated at the ceremony, and a book containing Urdu translations of the court's important decisions was launched.
