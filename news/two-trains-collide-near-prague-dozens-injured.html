Two Trains Collide Near Prague, Dozens Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two trains collided near Prague's main railway station on Tuesday morning, leaving dozens of passengers injured. Several of the injured were in serious condition, the Czech News Agency reported. Prague emergency services said all seriously injured passengers were taken to hospital.

Everyone aboard both trains was evacuated after the collision. Transport Minister Ivan Bednarik called it a very serious incident and said the Railway Inspectorate was investigating. Rail traffic on the affected line was suspended, with disruptions expected for several hours.
