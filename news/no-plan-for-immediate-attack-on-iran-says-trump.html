No Plan for Immediate Attack on Iran, Says Trump

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has said that the United States is not considering any immediate attack on Iran, while keeping the military fully ready for action if needed. In an interview with a British magazine, Trump said America's position on Tehran is strong and that Washington appears to be getting what it wants. He added that an attack may not even be necessary.

Trump claimed that the US naval blockade has badly damaged Iran's economy, while more oil is now passing through the Strait of Hormuz than before the war. He also confirmed that indirect negotiations with Iran are under way, with the enriched uranium stockpile among the issues being discussed. The president made clear once again that if circumstances demand it, the United States would not hesitate to take military action — but for now it is seeking its objectives through diplomatic channels.
