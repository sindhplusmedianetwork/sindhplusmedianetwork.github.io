Attacks on Gulf Energy Infrastructure Threaten Pakistan's Economic Outlook, Aurangzeb Warns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb has warned that attacks on energy infrastructure in Saudi Arabia and other Gulf countries pose a risk to Pakistan's longer-term economic outlook. Speaking to AFP on the sidelines of the IMF-World Bank Annual Meetings in Bangkok, he said Pakistan had so far withstood the shock from skyrocketing energy prices. However, he said the attacks on infrastructure in the Gulf were worrying for the country's economic future.

Aurangzeb said Pakistan had procured enough energy supplies to last through November and was planning ahead for the winter months. On the sidelines of the meetings, he also met Jihad Azour, the director of the IMF's Middle East and Central Asia Department, for talks on Pakistan's economic performance and ongoing reforms.
