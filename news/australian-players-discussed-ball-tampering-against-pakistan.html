Australian players discussed ball-tampering against Pakistan in Dubai Test, report claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New revelations have emerged about Australia's famous ball-tampering scandal, according to an investigative report by Australian sports broadcaster Gerard Whateley. The report claims that just six months after the Cape Town incident, Australian players tried to change the condition of the ball in the Test match against Pakistan. It claims that during the Dubai Test in October 2018, players allegedly discussed tampering with the ball using lollies or mints.

Former captain Tim Paine and coach Justin Langer are said to have intervened at the time and stopped the attempt. The report claims Langer severely reprimanded the players in the dressing room after the match and expressed strong anger at the damage to Australian cricket's reputation. It also claims differences arose in the Australian dressing room after the Cape Town scandal, while Mitchell Marsh had demanded that David Warner accept responsibility for the incident.

In 2018, Cameron Bancroft, David Warner and Steve Smith faced bans over ball-tampering in the Test match against South Africa. Fresh revelations in Usman Khawaja's autobiography, 'Sabar', have brought the controversy back to life.
