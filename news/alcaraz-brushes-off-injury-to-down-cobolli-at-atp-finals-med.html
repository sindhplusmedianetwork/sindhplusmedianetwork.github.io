Alcaraz Brushes Off Injury to Down Cobolli at ATP Finals, Medvedev Advances

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Carlos Alcaraz made light of potential hamstring issues as he eased to a 6-4, 6-1 victory over Flavio Cobolli at the ATP Finals in Turin on Friday, booking a last-four meeting with Ben Shelton. The third seed had an MRI scan for a thigh issue on Thursday but showed little sign of being hampered by heavy strapping on his left leg as he needed just over an hour to dismiss the world number six. With three victories from three Ivan Lendl Group outings, Alcaraz topped the pool and will face Shelton on Saturday in a repeat of the Paris Masters final earlier in November, which Alcaraz won 7-6(7/5), 7-5.

Russia's Daniil Medvedev later battled past American Frances Tiafoe 6-3, 6-4 to snatch the final semi-final berth and set up a clash with world number one Alexander Zverev on Saturday. Medvedev reached the ATP Finals semi-finals for the fourth time and first since 2023, while Cobolli finished bottom of the group with three defeats on his Finals debut.
