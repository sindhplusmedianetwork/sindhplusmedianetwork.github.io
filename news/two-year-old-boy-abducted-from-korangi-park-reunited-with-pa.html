Two-Year-Old Boy Abducted from Korangi Park Reunited with Parents

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A two-year-old boy, Hamdan, who was allegedly abducted from a park in Korangi Awami Colony, Karachi, has been reunited with his parents. Police said photos of the child and a video of a woman taking him went viral after the incident. A family from Khokhrapar then contacted police and said the child was with them.

The father told police the women said they had taken the crying child home after finding him alone in the park. The child was handed over to his parents after legal formalities were completed.
