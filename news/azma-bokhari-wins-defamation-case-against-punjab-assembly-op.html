Azma Bokhari Wins Defamation Case Against Punjab Assembly Opposition Leader

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari secured a legal victory on Tuesday when the Punjab Defamation Tribunal ruled in her favour in a defamation suit against Punjab Assembly Opposition Leader Moeen Riaz Qureshi. The tribunal awarded Bokhari Rs3 million in general damages, while dismissing her claims for special and punitive damages. Bokhari had filed the case seeking Rs84 million in damages over Qureshi's allegations made on a television programme that she had brought more than 100 paid individuals into the Punjab Assembly during the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister's visit.

The tribunal found the allegations unsubstantiated. Reacting to the verdict, Qureshi said on social media platform X that the fine would not silence him, signalling he may challenge the decision.
