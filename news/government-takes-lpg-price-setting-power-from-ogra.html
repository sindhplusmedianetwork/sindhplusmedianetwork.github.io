Government Takes LPG Price-Setting Power from OGRA

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has taken the power to set LPG prices away from the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority. A new company made up of PSO, Sui Gas and other oil companies will now fix LPG prices every month, and the official rates will be binding on all distributors and shopkeepers. Only legally established LPG pumps and shops will be allowed to sell gas, while action will be taken against illegal sellers.

A new cylinder-exchange system will give consumers approved filled standard cylinders of 2, 3 and 5 kilograms in place of their empty ones, to prevent cylinder-blast accidents. LPG Distributors Association secretary general Rana Abdul Jabbar welcomed the steps and said the association will fully cooperate with the new arrangements.
