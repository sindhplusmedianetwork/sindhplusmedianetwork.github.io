Dhabeji shutdown to cut Karachi water supply by 130 million gallons on November 13

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi's water supply is set to fall sharply on November 13, when K-Electric suspends power to the Dhabeji grid station from 10am to midnight for its annual maintenance. According to a Water Corporation spokesperson, the shutdown will render nine of the pumping station's 21 pumps inoperative, reducing the city's daily supply from approximately 650 million gallons to 520 million gallons. The disruption is likely to affect residents of Landhi, Korangi, Malir, Shah Faisal, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Clifton, Saddar and Lyari, as well as parts of Nazimabad, Liaquatabad, North Karachi and Gulberg Town.

The Landhi and Sherpao hydrants will remain closed during the power shutdown. Once K-Electric's maintenance work is complete, six of the nine suspended pumps will be restored, bringing supply back towards normal in most affected areas except Landhi, Korangi, Malir and Shah Faisal. Relief for those four areas will remain limited, as the Water Corporation will then carry out its own annual maintenance lasting another 22 hours, during which three pumps at Dhabeji will stay non-operational.

Water distribution will be gradually restored once electricity supply resumes. Meanwhile, the Water Corporation has urged residents to store sufficient water in advance and use their existing reserves judiciously throughout the maintenance period.
