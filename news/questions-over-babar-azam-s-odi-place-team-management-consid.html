Questions Over Babar Azam's ODI Place; Team Management Considering Dropping Him, Says Shoaib Akhtar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Pakistan fast bowler Shoaib Akhtar has said questions are being raised over Babar Azam's place in the ODI team and that the team management is considering dropping him from the format. Speaking on a national television programme, Akhtar asked the host whether he would select Babar in the ODI side, adding that the team management was also thinking along the same lines. He clarified that he did not want to create a controversy and believed Babar Azam should keep playing, but said the batter would have to prove through his performance that he belongs in ODI cricket.

Akhtar also spoke about the challenges facing Aqib Javed in 50-over cricket, saying it would be difficult to handle both the batting and bowling departments, though he hoped his assessment would prove wrong. The Pakistan Cricket Board has included Babar in the tri-nation ODI squad against England and Sri Lanka, with the series starting on Sunday at Rawalpindi.
