Araghchi Warns US of Severe Military Response if War Continues

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said that positive and peaceful outcomes would follow if the United States and its allies met key regional peace demands. Among those demands, he named ending the war on all fronts. He said Iran's two-day diplomatic proposal remained on the table and was still valid.

The proposal, he said, had not been withdrawn. At the same time, he warned that if Washington chose to continue the war, it would face severe military operations. Those operations, he said, would be carried out by Iran's alert and battle-hardened fighters.
