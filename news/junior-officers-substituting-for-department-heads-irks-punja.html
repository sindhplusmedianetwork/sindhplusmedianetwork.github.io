Junior officers substituting for department heads irks Punjab watchdog

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior government officials are increasingly skipping meetings of the Punjab Assembly's Public Accounts Committee, sending junior officers in their place despite being summoned to explain billions of rupees in audit objections. Instead of department secretaries, Additional Secretaries, Deputy Secretaries and Section Officers frequently attend the meetings, raising questions about whether they have the authority and complete records to give satisfactory answers on public spending. Public Accounts Committee Chairman Iftikhar Chachar has taken notice of the matter, maintaining that an officer specifically summoned for the examination of audit objections is required to attend in person and that those who fail to appear may face reprimands.

Chachar said the situation had started improving since he began questioning officials about their absence. Departments are now expected to inform the committee before a meeting if a summoned officer is out of the country or city, and to provide evidence explaining the absence along with naming the officer who will attend instead. The Punjab Assembly is currently examining audit objections relating to the financial year 2023-24 involving amounts running into billions of rupees, and the absence of senior officials can delay the settlement of objections when junior officers lack decision-making authority.
