Petrol down Rs3, diesel down Rs4 in fresh fuel price cut

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has reduced the price of petrol by Rs3 per litre and high-speed diesel by Rs4, marking the eighth consecutive decrease, according to a notification by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority. Following the revision, petrol will retail at Rs332 per litre, while high-speed diesel will cost Rs360 from Friday, November 13. The government continues to levy Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel.

Under an arrangement announced on November 2, oil refineries will contribute to the petroleum levy by bearing a cumulative impact of Rs15 billion over 30 days. The government said this allows a Rs14.71 reduction in the petrol price at the consumer end. Fuel prices have been revised almost daily since July 17 because of fluctuations in international prices after renewed hostilities between the United States and Iran.
