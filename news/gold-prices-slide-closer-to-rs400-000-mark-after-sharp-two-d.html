Gold prices slide closer to Rs400,000 mark after sharp two-day sell-off

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices in Pakistan slid further as the week opened, edging closer to the Rs400,000 per tola mark. Twenty-four-karat gold fell by Rs10,800 per tola to Rs409,736, while the rate for 10 grams dropped Rs9,259 to Rs351,282. The slide extends a sharp sell-off at the end of October that stripped Rs15,900 off the per-tola price in just two trading sessions, a fall of about 3.7 percent.

On October 29, gold had stood at Rs425,636 per tola before declining on each of the following two days. Silver also weakened, with the per-tola rate quoted at Rs5,388. Market participants are now watching whether the Rs400,000 threshold will be tested as selling pressure continues.
