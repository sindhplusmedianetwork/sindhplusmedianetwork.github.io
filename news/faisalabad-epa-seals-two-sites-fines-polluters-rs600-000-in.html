Faisalabad EPA Seals Two Sites, Fines Polluters Rs600,000 in Anti-Smog Drive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Environmental Protection Agency in Faisalabad conducted 65 inspections in a single day during its anti-smog crackdown. The drive detected six violations, imposed fines of Rs600,000 and sealed two sites for polluting the air. Inspection teams checked 34 industrial units, 22 brick kilns and nine under-construction sites.

One unit's boiler and one kiln house were sealed over non-compliance. Deputy Director Dr Tahir said the drive was being carried out under the supervision of Divisional Commissioner Dr Imran Hamid Sheikh on the directions of Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz. He said indiscriminate action against polluters would continue.
