PTI internal differences surface openly as Mishal Yousafzai demands core committee, parliamentary party meetings

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Internal differences within Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) surfaced openly as party leader Mishal Yousafzai demanded that core committee and parliamentary party meetings be convened. She said a previously called core committee meeting was suddenly cancelled for unknown reasons after Imran Khan's letter about his and Bushra Bibi's health. According to her, the core committee has not been convened since that cancellation.

She said party parliamentarians have been demanding a parliamentary party meeting for ten days. The purpose, she said, is for leaders of the opposition to show a way forward and help resolve the issue of Imran Khan's treatment. She also stated that 'minus one' is unacceptable.

Her remarks brought the party's internal disagreements into the open.
