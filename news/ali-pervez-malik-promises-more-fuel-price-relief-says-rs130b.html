Ali Pervez Malik promises more fuel price relief, says Rs130bn gas subsidy given

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister Ali Pervez Malik has promised further relief in petroleum product prices, saying the benefit of cheaper oil in the global market will reach the people. At a press conference, he said global oil prices have fallen and their effects will be transferred to the public as relief. He said that on Friday the rise in petrol prices was stopped with the help of refineries, and that good news on petroleum product prices would come today as well.

The minister said the country has 10 million gas connections, and Rs130 billion in subsidy has been given under the public relief scheme. He said some elements tried to profiteer on LPG rates, noting complaints of LPG being sold at up to Rs800 per kg in various cities, while local LPG production is 2,000 to 2,500 tons. Ali Pervez Malik said fuel-importing companies will publish their rates on their websites and that fair distribution of the hard-area LPG quota will be ensured.

He added that the government is strengthening energy security infrastructure, working on a winter gas supply strategy, and seeking BISP's help for gas-sector relief, noting that for the first time in history, money was taken from refineries and given to the people.
