How PML-N got Punjab is an open secret, says Faryal Talpur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Women's Wing President Faryal Talpur has urged party workers to capitalise on the weakness of the PML-N-led Punjab government, saying the way the PML-N secured its mandate in the province in the February 8, 2024 general elections was an open secret. Talpur, who is on a week-long visit to Punjab, held three meetings at the party's Central Secretariat in Lahore, including separate sessions with office-bearers from Lahore Rural, Kasur and Sheikhupura and representatives of civil society. During the meetings, some office-bearers complained about the attitude of the party's provincial leadership and about access to the Governor House.

Punjab Governor Sardar Saleem Haider Khan, who belongs to the PPP, was called by Talpur and arrived at the party office to meet her. Addressing workers from Sheikhupura and Kasur separately, she urged them to overcome despondency, strengthen grassroots organisation and prepare for the upcoming local government elections. She said political parties never cease to exist and the PPP knows how to play the role of an opposition party.
