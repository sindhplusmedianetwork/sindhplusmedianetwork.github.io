22 killed, 1,668 injured in 1,421 traffic accidents across Punjab in past 24 hours, says Rescue 1122

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In the past 24 hours, 1,421 traffic accidents across Punjab left 22 people dead and 1,668 injured, according to Rescue 1122. A spokesperson for Rescue said the Emergency Services Department responded to 1,421 traffic accidents during the period. Of those injured, 754 people were seriously hurt and were shifted to nearby hospitals.

Another 914 people with minor injuries received medical aid at the scene of the accident. The highest number of accidents was recorded in Lahore, where 251 calls were received and 298 people were affected.
