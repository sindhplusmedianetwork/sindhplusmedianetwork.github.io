Case of spreading anti-state material, charges framed against Iqrar-ul-Hassan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Judicial Magistrate East in Karachi has indicted Awami Raaj Party chief Iqrar ul Hassan in the case of promoting anti-state content. The accused Iqrar ul Hassan appeared in court with his lawyers and denied the charges, and the court ordered the prosecution to present witnesses at the next hearing. The court said the next appearance will be on 9 December, the prosecution should present evidence, and directed the accused to ensure attendance at the next hearing.

The accused's lawyers filed an application in court for the return of mobile phones, stating that the mobile phone and iPad taken into custody at the time of arrest have not yet been returned. Iqrar ul Hassan's lawyer said the applicant's wife's mobile phone was also taken into custody and it was said the phones would be returned after forensic analysis, but the phones have not yet been returned. The court reserved its decision on the application, adjourned the hearing until 9 December, and the accused Iqrar ul Hassan is on bail in this case.
