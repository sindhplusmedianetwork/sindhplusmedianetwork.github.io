Terrorist Killed in Intelligence-Based Operation in Kech, Balochistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces killed a terrorist belonging to Fitna al-Hindustan in an intelligence-based operation in Balochistan's Kech district on Thursday. The operation took place in the Minaz area under Operation Radd-ul-Fitna 3, according to state media citing security sources. Sources said the terrorists had attempted to set up a blockade while taking two women with them.

The forces pursued them and one terrorist was killed in the ensuing exchange of fire. Weapons and ammunition were recovered from the site. A day earlier, the National Assembly was informed that security forces had neutralised 2,410 terrorists in 5,143 intelligence-based operations during the first nine months of 2026.

The operations remain part of an ongoing counter-terrorism campaign in the region.
