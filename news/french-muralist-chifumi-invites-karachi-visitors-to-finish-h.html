French muralist Chifumi invites Karachi visitors to finish his art

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French muralist Chifumi's retrospective exhibition opened at Alliance Française Karachi on Thursday, November 12. The exhibition is titled 'Chifumi – Vous Laissez La Main (Let's You Take the Lead)'. Its concept is interactive: visitors pick up paintbrushes and add colours to murals drawn directly on the exhibition room's walls.

Chifumi deliberately left his drawings unfinished so that people visiting the show can complete them. He said Pakistani culture had attracted him, especially its traditional clothing, food and hospitality. Chifumi recalled painting a mural at the Arts Council of Pakistan Karachi during his previous visit last year.

The exhibition invites Karachi's art lovers to become part of the artwork itself rather than simply looking at it.
