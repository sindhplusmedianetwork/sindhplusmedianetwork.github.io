Anthropic Logs $80.7 Billion First-Half Loss Ahead of Planned IPO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Artificial intelligence company Anthropic recorded a net loss of $80.7 billion in the first six months of 2026, according to a draft initial public offering prospectus reviewed by Reuters and reported on October 20. The loss widened from $13 billion a year earlier, but about $72 billion of it reflected a non-cash accounting charge linked to changes in the valuation of convertible notes and other financial instruments, rather than direct operating expenditure. The company generated $16.3 billion in revenue between January and June 2026, compared with $1.1 billion in the corresponding period last year.

The San Francisco-based developer of the Claude AI assistant is expected to go public in November, and some investors are now weighing a lower valuation of around $1.5 trillion following the disclosures. The filings underscore the heavy costs of the AI race: Anthropic has committed to spending at least $518 billion over a decade on cloud services, computing and infrastructure, and spent nearly $9 billion on computing capacity in the first half, up from $2.3 billion a year earlier. At an investor meeting on October 14, the company told prospective IPO investors that third-quarter revenue jumped 45 percent to $16.8 billion and that it was profitable on an adjusted basis.
