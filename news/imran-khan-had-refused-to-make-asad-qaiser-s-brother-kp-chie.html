Imran Khan Had Refused to Make Asad Qaiser's Brother KP Chief Minister, Says Barrister Saif

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Khyber Pakhtunkhwa information adviser Barrister Muhammad Ali Saif has said that former National Assembly speaker Asad Qaiser once asked Imran Khan to make his brother Aqibullah the chief minister of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, but PTI founder Imran Khan did not agree to the proposal. Speaking to a private TV channel, Saif said that after the elections and before Ali Amin Gandapur's nomination as chief minister, he and Asad Qaiser had a joint meeting with Imran Khan in which Qaiser expressed the wish to make his brother chief minister. According to Saif, Imran Khan refused to make Qaiser's brother the chief minister, and in another meeting held in Junaid Akbar's presence, he again did not support Aqibullah.

The former information adviser added that Imran Khan had initially directed that Aqibullah be made the speaker of the KP Assembly, but later changed the decision and nominated Babar Saleem Swati as speaker. Saif's remarks came in the context of Senator Mishal Yousafzai's statement about Qaiser wanting his brother as chief minister. PTI KP information secretary Shaukat Yousafzai also responded, saying Mishal had alleged that Qaiser lobbied for his brother, and that Saif himself had announced Aqibullah's nomination as speaker while Ali Amin Gandapur had already been nominated as chief minister before Qaiser's meeting.
