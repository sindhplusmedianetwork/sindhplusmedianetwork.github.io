Residents irked by road closure for Sindhi Muslim Food Walk in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Residents of Sindhi Muslim Cooperative Housing Society (SMCHS) and nearby PECHS say the new Sindhi Muslim Food Walk in Karachi has disrupted their daily lives by closing a main road to traffic. The food walk was inaugurated by Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah last week. Advocate Irfan Aziz says the closure violates a February 2023 Sindh High Court order restricting road closures for food streets.

Chanesar Town chairman Farhan Ghani rejects any violation and says alternative routes exist for residents. Residents complain their streets have become parking lots for visitors' cars from sunset to midnight, blocking even ambulances. The town chairman says a residents-only parking system will be introduced soon.
