Sinner Opens Up on Painful Recovery from Knee Injury

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jannik Sinner has opened up on his difficult recovery from a knee injury, saying there were days when he could barely move at all. The former world number one has not played a competitive match since winning Wimbledon in July, and he ended his season early in October to focus on being fit for the 2027 Australian Open. In a new episode of his YouTube series, Jannik Sinner Unfiltered, the 25-year-old showed himself travelling to Lyon for further medical examinations as his team tried to understand the problem, and working on physiotherapy and training in Monaco.

Sinner said his knee felt different from day to day, with some days when he felt nothing and could play without any problems, and then days when he could not move at all. He also revealed that he had his tonsils removed some weeks ago and lost a lot of weight and muscle, which has added another challenge to his recovery. Sinner said he is now working every day to rebuild his muscles, and his goal is to be in Australia in January, hopefully playing a tournament before the first Grand Slam of the year, although he has not yet returned to training on the tennis court.
