CTD claims arrest of suspected RAW agent in Okara

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Counter Terrorism Department (CTD) claimed to have arrested a man it described as a terrorist belonging to Indian intelligence agency RAW near the Rao Sikandar Iqbal Road in Okara city on Wednesday. CTD officials said that on a tip-off, Under Officer Muhammad Naeem conducted a raid along with a five-member team and arrested the man, later identified as Adnan Ali of Mohala Kareenabad, Shakriyal, in Rawalpindi district. The CTD claimed to have recovered cash, explosive material, IEDs, an iron box, electric wires, devices and buttons, a prima card, dry batteries, mobile phones, detonators and tape from the suspect.

Okara civil defence personnel were later called to defuse the detonators and devices, and a sample of the explosive material powder was taken for forensic analysis. A case was registered against the suspect, and a duty judge granted the CTD a seven-day physical remand of the suspect.
