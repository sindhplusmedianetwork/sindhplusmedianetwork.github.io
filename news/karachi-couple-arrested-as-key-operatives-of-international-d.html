Karachi Couple Arrested as Key Operatives of International Drug Supply Network

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

East Zone police in Karachi have arrested a husband and wife said to be key operatives of an international drug supply network, following a tip-off near Chakar Hotel. Police said a bag recovered from the couple contained drugs concealed inside 14 slippers, along with two packets of hashish. The arrested man and woman were identified as Ali Raza, son of Safdar Ali, and Maryam, wife of Ali Raza.

During initial questioning, police said, the couple disclosed that drugs were brought from Quetta to Karachi by bus through a man named Shoaib, after which Ali Raza collected the drugs with his wife and arranged for them to be sent to Australia. Police said the network's central figure in Australia received the drugs, sold them, and transferred the proceeds to operatives in Pakistan. Cases have been registered against the couple under the Control of Narcotic Substances Act, and police said investigations into the wider network and its further links are continuing.
