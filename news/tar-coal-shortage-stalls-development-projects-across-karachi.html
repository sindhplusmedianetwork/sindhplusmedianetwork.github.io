Tar coal shortage stalls development projects across Karachi and Sindh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A sharp fall in tar coal supply has slowed or halted work on several development projects in Karachi and other districts of Sindh. Contractor companies say the price rose suddenly by Rs31,000 per ton within a week, while tar coal imported from a neighbouring country has completely stopped arriving. They were earlier getting tar coal from a Karachi refinery at Rs170,000 per ton, but the price has climbed to Rs201,000 per ton in the last week, and supply is also short.

The imported variety used to be available at around Rs140,000 per ton from a nearby country. Small contractors with projects worth Rs50 to 100 million say they have almost shut down work, because unlike big projects, their agreements do not allow cost escalation and working at old rates is no longer possible. Karachi Contractors Association chairman Naeem Kazmi confirmed to Jang that prices have risen and work has stopped, urging the government to take immediate notice.

He pointed out that all of Sindh is affected because there is only one refinery in the whole province.
