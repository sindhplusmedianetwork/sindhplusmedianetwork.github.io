Doctors Confirm Pope Leo XIV Needs No Further Treatment After Lung Surgery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Doctors treating Pope Leo XIV have confirmed that he requires no further treatment following surgery to remove a nodule from his lung. The pontiff underwent the operation on October 13 at Rome's Gemelli Hospital, where surgeons removed part of the lower lobe of his right lung. The procedure, carried out through video-assisted surgery, lasted 90 minutes and was successful.

The tumour was identified as a localised lung adenocarcinoma. Pope Leo XIV was discharged from the hospital on Saturday. The reassuring update follows days of concern over the pontiff's health.
