Up to Rs60 Billion Laundered Through Solar Panel Imports, Senate Body Told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Money laundering of up to Rs60 billion took place under the guise of solar panel imports. The disclosure was made by Mushtaq Sukhera, chairman of the National Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Authority, before a Senate standing committee on the cabinet secretariat. He said banks acted as conveyor belts in the scheme and bank managers were involved.

He told the committee that Rs12 billion was recovered from the account of a bulldozer driver. The committee also sought details of measures to prevent money laundering through bank accounts and online fraud. The revelations point to large-scale abuse of the solar import trade.
