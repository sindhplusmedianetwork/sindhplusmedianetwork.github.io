Shan Masood's Availability for Sri Lanka Test Series Uncertain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan captain Shan Masood's availability for the upcoming Test series against Sri Lanka is uncertain, according to an update published by Daily Pakistan on October 21. The report flags the doubt over the skipper's participation but does not specify the reason behind it. No further details were given in the report about whether the uncertainty relates to fitness, selection or any other matter.

The development comes at a time when Pakistan's red-ball side is preparing for its next home assignment. Team officials have not yet issued any statement on the matter in the published report. Fans will be watching closely for an official word from the Pakistan Cricket Board on the captain's status for the series.
