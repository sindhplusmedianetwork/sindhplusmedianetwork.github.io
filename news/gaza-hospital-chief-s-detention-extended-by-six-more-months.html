Gaza hospital chief's detention extended by six more months without charge

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Israeli court has ordered the detention of Dr Hussam Abu Safiya, head of Gaza's Kamal Adwan Hospital, extended by another six months, although no formal charge has been filed against him. His lawyer said the court in Beersheba ruled on Wednesday that he was a threat to the state of Israel. Dr Abu Safiya was detained in December 2024 during an Israeli raid on the hospital in northern Gaza, along with other staff and patients.

The UN human rights office called in July for his immediate release or a fair trial on an identifiable charge, and raised concern about alleged torture, prolonged isolation and denial of medical facilities. According to an Israeli human rights organisation, a recent medical review found weight loss, low haemoglobin levels and chest pain. Last week, the Israeli Supreme Court also rejected a petition for the release of 14 Palestinian doctors held without charge or trial.
