Pipeline damage leaves three Chitral neighbourhoods without water for eight days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three neighbourhoods of Chitral town have been without clean drinking water for eight days after damage to a supply pipeline. The affected areas are Gankorini, Balach and Singoor. A six-inch pipeline was damaged by an excavator during house construction work in Singoor.

Local elders said the Water Supply Unit and the local administration have failed to repair the line properly in more than a week. They shared photos showing a tree branch inserted into the damaged pipe as a makeshift fix. Residents have urged the district administration to order an emergency technical intervention.

They want the water supply to the three neighbourhoods restored without further delay.
