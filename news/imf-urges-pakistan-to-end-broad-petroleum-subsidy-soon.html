IMF urges Pakistan to end broad petroleum subsidy soon

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Monetary Fund has urged Pakistan to end its broad petroleum subsidy scheme soon. The IMF said any future relief should be limited, temporary and targeted only at deserving groups. Pakistan launched the scheme in September, giving motorcycle and rickshaw users weekly petrol tokens worth 500 rupees.

Small-car owners receive relief of 100 rupees per litre on up to 10 litres every 10 days. The IMF also warned that the war in the Middle East is a major challenge for Pakistan's economy. An IMF board meeting on Pakistan's programme reviews is expected in December.
