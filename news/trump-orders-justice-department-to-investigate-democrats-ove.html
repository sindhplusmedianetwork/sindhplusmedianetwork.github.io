Trump orders Justice Department to investigate Democrats over alleged threats

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States President Donald Trump has announced that he has ordered the Justice Department to immediately investigate alleged criminal actions by Democrats. Trump accused Democrats of seeking political revenge, threatening to send Republican leaders and their supporters to jail, and using the law as a weapon against political opponents. In his statement, he said Democrats were threatening many people with impeachment and various punishments, including unjustified threats of impeachment against the attorney general and others.

Trump described the Democrats' conduct as high-level extortion, saying they must be held accountable for their actions. He said Republican donors were being targeted simply because of their political affiliation, while Republicans and their supporters were being threatened with jail. Citing Senator Elissa Slotkin, the president alleged that she was talking about targeting even young people who hold Republican views.

He said punishment should fall on those who issue unlawful threats to others. Trump further alleged that Democratic leaders had sent threatening letters to innocent people, and announced that he was ordering the Justice Department to immediately investigate the Democrats' alleged criminal actions.
