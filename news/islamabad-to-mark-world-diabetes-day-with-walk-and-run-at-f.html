Islamabad to mark World Diabetes Day with walk and run at F-9 Park

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The District Health Office Islamabad, in collaboration with the Ministry of National Health Services, will organise a walk and run event on November 14 to raise public awareness about diabetes prevention and promote healthy lifestyles. The event, titled Walk and Run to Defeat Diabetes, will be held at F-9 Park in the capital from 2:30 pm to 5:00 pm on the occasion of World Diabetes Day, with participants invited to take part in 2.5-kilometre and 5-kilometre races. According to the organisers, online registration has been opened through the official event portal, while participants will also have the facility of on-site registration at the park before the event, with the starting point at Bara Dari, near the F-10 Gate.

The initiative aims to encourage physical activity, promote healthier lifestyle choices and highlight the importance of preventing and managing diabetes through regular exercise, balanced nutrition and timely health screening. The health office has invited citizens, families, health professionals and fitness enthusiasts to participate in the activity and contribute to building a healthier capital.
