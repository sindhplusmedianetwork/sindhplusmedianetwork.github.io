Young Man Shot Dead in Swabi Over Rs600,000 Dispute

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A young man was shot dead in the Naru Banda Maneri Bala area of Swabi district in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa over a dispute involving Rs600,000. Hasnain, a resident of Maneri Bala, told police in his first information report that he was with his 22-year-old brother Sudais the previous night at Rasta Landa Naru Maneri Bala. The two were talking with Farhan, Arslan and Rehan about the Rs600,000 matter.

According to the police report, the three brothers then opened fire on them with their pistols. Sudais was hit by a bullet and died on the spot, while Hasnain survived the attack. Police said the motive behind the enmity was the Rs600,000 dispute.

Police have registered a case against the accused and started an investigation.
