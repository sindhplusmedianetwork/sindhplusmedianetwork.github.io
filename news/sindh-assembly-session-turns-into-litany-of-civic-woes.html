Sindh Assembly Session Turns Into Litany of Civic Woes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Public grievances over gas load-shedding, water shortages, electricity bills and deteriorating civic facilities dominated the Sindh Assembly proceedings, with MPAs highlighting problems facing residents. Members raised the difficulties people face in their daily lives because of unreliable gas and water supplies and high electricity bills. The session also heard that land vacated after the evacuation of Afghan settlements had been handed over to the Malir Development Authority.

The chief minister's adviser told the house that the government would not allow anyone to occupy the vacated land. Lawmakers also discussed the poor state of civic facilities in several areas and urged the government to act on the complaints. The proceedings reflected the growing concern among members over the basic problems of people across the province.
