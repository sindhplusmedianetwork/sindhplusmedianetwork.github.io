Mengal and Raja Nasir seek to join case against Khyber Pakhtunkhwa governor's rule in Federal Constitutional Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sardar Akhtar Mengal and Senator Raja Nasir Abbas have filed miscellaneous applications in the Federal Constitutional Court seeking to become parties to the appeals against the imposition of governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and arrests under 3 MPO. The applications, filed through Advocate Sardar Muhammad Latif Khan Khosa, say the President imposed emergency and governor's rule on 11 October 2026 under Articles 232 and 234, which they had already challenged in the Peshawar High Court. They ask the court to let them argue and file written submissions.

They also ask the court to amend or withdraw its 5 November 2026 order that suspended proceedings on related petitions.
