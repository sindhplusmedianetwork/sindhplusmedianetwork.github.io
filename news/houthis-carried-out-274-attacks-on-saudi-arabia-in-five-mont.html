Houthis carried out 274 attacks on Saudi Arabia in five months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The attacks on Saudi Arabia have increased gradually over the past five months, reaching a total of 274, as the Houthis gained the ability to strike deep inside the kingdom. The rise in attacks has put the spotlight on the security situation in the Saudi capital and on how daily life in the city has been affected. A reporting team given permission to report from Riyadh visited the city's streets to see the situation for itself.

The team spoke to local people about what they had seen and heard and how they viewed the continuing attacks. The account offers a ground-level picture of life in the capital at a time of heightened tension in the region. The pattern of attacks reflects the Houthis' growing reach beyond Yemen's borders.

The situation remains under close watch as the attacks continue.
