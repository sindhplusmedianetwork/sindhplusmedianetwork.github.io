Ishaq Dar Meets ECO Secretary General, Agrees to Boost Regional Connectivity and Economic Cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar met ECO Secretary General Dr Asad Majeed Khan in Islamabad on Sunday. The meeting took place on the sidelines of the 30th ECO Council of Ministers meeting. The two sides agreed to strengthen regional cooperation, connectivity and economic integration.

Dar reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to hosting the ECO summit in 2027. The ECO secretary general praised Pakistan's arrangements and hospitality for the council meeting. He also congratulated Pakistan on assuming the organisation's upcoming chairmanship.
