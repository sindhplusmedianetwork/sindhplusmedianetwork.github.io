Claims About Political Groupings Carry More Rumour Than Truth, Says Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's Adviser on Political Affairs Rana Sanaullah has said that various things and claims about political groupings keep coming forward, but in practice they contain less truth and more rumour-making, and solid evidence is necessary to level an accusation naming a person. Speaking to a private TV channel, Rana Sanaullah said what federal minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain said was inappropriate for him, adding that people often claim something is happening but have no evidence for it. He said Khawaja Asif had told him that when people talk in this manner, certain wishes and plans lie behind them.

Rana Sanaullah said he asked Khawaja Asif whether any practical effort was also being made in this regard, to which Khawaja Asif replied that wishes exist, but whether anyone can act on them is a separate matter. He said that in such talk, the element of rumour-making is greater than practical activity, and if he were to speak naming someone who forms a group, it would be the same kind of talk as Chaudhry Salik Hussain's. He said Chaudhry Salik Hussain spoke as if the cupboard was full, but he had no evidence, and until a person himself comes forward and speaks about a matter, no one's name can be taken.

He said that in political matters, rumour, rumour-making and propaganda have always existed, and on a question about Senator Faisal Vawda's remarks, he said Faisal Vawda also speaks on various matters but has no evidence for those claims, though sometimes part of what he says, or a situation similar to it, comes to light later.
