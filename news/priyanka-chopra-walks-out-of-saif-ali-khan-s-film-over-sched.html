Priyanka Chopra walks out of Saif Ali Khan's film over schedule clash

By Sindh Plus Media Network

MUMBAI: Bollywood actress Priyanka Chopra has opted out of the upcoming film opposite Saif Ali Khan, directed by Ritesh Batra. According to Indian media reports, Chopra was to play the lead role and had been keen on the project, but her prior commitments made it impossible to spare time for the shoot. Reports say the production team tried adjusting the shooting dates to accommodate her schedule, but no workable arrangement could be found, leading the actress to part ways with the film.

Shooting on the project began on October 21 at a museum in Mumbai's Byculla area. Two actresses are now in talks for the lead role, though their names have not been disclosed. The team is currently filming Saif Ali Khan's scenes, while the film's title and story have not yet been officially revealed.
