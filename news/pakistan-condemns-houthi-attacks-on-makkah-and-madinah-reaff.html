Pakistan condemns Houthi attacks on Makkah and Madinah, reaffirms solidarity with Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan on Friday strongly condemned Houthi ballistic missile attacks targeting Saudi Arabia, particularly the Makkah and Madinah regions, describing the strikes as a violation of the kingdom's sovereignty and reaffirming its unwavering solidarity with Riyadh. During the Foreign Office's weekly press briefing, the spokesperson said Pakistan had condemned the October 26 attacks and reiterated its commitment to the Makkah Alliance for Defence. He described the strikes as a blatant disregard for the sanctity of Islam's holiest sites and civilian safety, reaffirming Pakistan's support for protecting the two holy mosques against external aggression and threats.

The condemnation came amid a series of diplomatic engagements by Pakistan and its regional partners aimed at addressing tensions in the Middle East and promoting peace and stability. On October 23, the foreign ministers of Pakistan, Turkiye, Egypt and Saudi Arabia met in Istanbul for the R4 ministerial meeting, where they discussed developments in the Middle East, including Palestine, Yemen, the conflict involving the United States, Israel and Iran, and tensions surrounding the Strait of Hormuz. The four ministers condemned Houthi attacks against Saudi Arabia and commercial shipping in the Red Sea and Bab al-Mandab, while expressing solidarity with the Yemeni government and Saudi Arabia.

Separately, Pakistan condemned a terrorist attack during a funeral procession at a church in Jordan on October 24 and reaffirmed its solidarity with Jordan in the fight against terrorism.
