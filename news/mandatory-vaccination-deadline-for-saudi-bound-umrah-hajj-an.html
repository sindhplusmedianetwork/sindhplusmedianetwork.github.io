Mandatory Vaccination Deadline for Saudi-Bound Umrah, Hajj and Work Visa Travelers Extended by a Month

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The deadline for mandatory vaccination of passengers traveling to Saudi Arabia on Umrah, Hajj and work visas has been extended by one month, and a circular to that effect has been issued. Under the new instructions, enforcement of mandatory vaccination and its verification for the relevant passengers at Karachi airport will begin on November 20, 2026 at 10am. Meningitis, influenza and polio vaccinations have been declared mandatory for these travelers, who are directed to complete the required jabs at least ten days before travel and keep their vaccination cards with them as per the prescribed rules.

Previously, travel without mandatory vaccination and the vaccination card was to be banned from October 15, but that period has now been extended by a month. For the convenience of passengers, the government dispensary at Karachi airport was providing round-the-clock vaccination so travelers could get vaccinated before their flights, and as an emergency measure a vaccination facility was also set up in the departure lounge for pilgrims and other passengers arriving from remote areas. Officials have advised travelers to ensure their vaccinations and travel-document verification are complete before the new enforcement date to avoid any trouble or delay at the airport.
