Fullkrug scores again as Bremen hammer Hamburg 4-0 in Bundesliga

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Niclas Fullkrug returned to the top of the Bundesliga scoring charts as Werder Bremen beat Hamburg 4-0 on Sunday to climb to sixth place. Fullkrug scored in stoppage time for his 10th goal in 10 league games, after Marco Friedl opened the scoring and Justin Njinmah scored twice. Fullkrug, recalled to Jurgen Klopp's Germany side in November, now has 13 goals in 12 matches in all competitions this season after scoring three in 18 months with parent club West Ham United.

Bremen sit in the European places, three points outside the top four. The win was Bremen's biggest margin over Hamburg since 2004.
