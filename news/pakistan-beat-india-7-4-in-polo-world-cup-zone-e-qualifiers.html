Pakistan beat India 7-4 in Polo World Cup Zone E qualifiers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan beat traditional rivals India 7-4 in their second match of the 13th Polo World Cup Zone E qualifiers in Abuja, Nigeria. The win made it two victories in a row for Pakistan, after they beat Iran 10-6 in the opener. The four-team qualifiers also include hosts Nigeria.

After two straight wins, Pakistan's chances of qualifying for the World Cup in Uruguay have grown much stronger. Pakistan play their final qualifier against hosts Nigeria on Sunday.
