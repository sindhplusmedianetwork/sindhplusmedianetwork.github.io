International boxing championship concludes in Quetta; Najibullah wins gold

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Late Abdul Ghani Mengal Memorial International Boxing Championship 2026 concluded successfully at Ayub Stadium Quetta on Saturday, with 60 boxers from Pakistan, Afghanistan and Iran taking part in the event organised by the Pakistan Professional Boxing Association. Balochistan Governor Jafar Khan Mandokhail, who was the chief guest, said Pakistan has the honour of being a world champion in several sports and called for better opportunities and modern training facilities for the athletes of Balochistan. He noted that significant funds have been allocated to the province's sports department for this purpose.

In the bouts, Pakistan's Najibullah claimed the gold medal, while Ali Ahmed, Iran's Daniyal Shah and Pakistani female boxer Bus Bano delivered outstanding performances. The organisers said the championship provided young boxers a valuable platform to compete at the international level.
