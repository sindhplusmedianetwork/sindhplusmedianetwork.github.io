13 years' account cannot be settled in 11 days, says KP Governor Kundi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has said that thirteen years of governance cannot be judged in eleven days, announcing that a 100-day plan is being drawn up for the province. Speaking to the media at the Governor House in Peshawar, he said the prime minister would soon visit Peshawar. Kundi accused some parties of spreading false news about the province's minerals and described Afghanistan's silence as surprising.

He assured that the police's demands for ending terrorism would be met. The governor announced free government land for Pakistan Sweet Homes to build a cadet college, while chairman Zamarud Khan announced 50,000 scholarships for children across the country, with priority for the children of martyrs.
