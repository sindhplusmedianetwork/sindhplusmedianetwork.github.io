SDPI awards Ishrat Husain Lifetime Achievement Award as IBA bids farewell to former SBP governor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sustainable Development Policy Institute (SDPI) on Tuesday awarded former State Bank governor Dr Ishrat Husain its Lifetime Achievement Award in recognition of his public services that began in 1964 as a civil servant, while IBA bid farewell to its dean and director. Husain served as SBP governor from 1999 to the end of 2005, when he inherited just $300 million in foreign exchange reserves and left with $13 billion. Speaking at the event, SBP Governor Jameel Ahmad said Pakistan's economic situation remained challenging, while Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb said he took inspiration from Husain's reports as chairman of the Cabinet Committee on State-Owned Enterprises, admitting that 'we still have a long way to go'.

Aurangzeb also recalled that Husain, as adviser to the PM on institutional reforms, 'put a lot of good and hard work into governance reform reports'. Husain noted that as a World Bank official he had advised Ghana, Tajikistan and Azerbaijan, pointing to their sovereign wealth funds and reform successes, while Pakistan's own sovereign wealth fund, established in 2023, had not been made operational amid IMF concerns. In his message to the youth, Husain said, 'your beginning need not determine your destination', sharing his journey from a shy boy to a towering figure in policymaking.
