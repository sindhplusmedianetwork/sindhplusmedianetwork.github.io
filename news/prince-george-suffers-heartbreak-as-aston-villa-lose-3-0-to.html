Prince George Suffers Heartbreak as Aston Villa Lose 3-0 to Barcelona in Spain Trip

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Prince George joined his father Prince William on a trip to Spain during his half-term break from Eton College to cheer on Aston Villa. On Tuesday, November 3, the pair watched their team lose 3-0 to Barcelona at the Spotify Camp Nou. FC Barcelona welcomed the royals at the stadium, sharing a photo of the Prince of Wales in a navy blazer and crewneck sweater.

William and George sat in the front row of their box; George wore a black athletic jacket carrying the Aston Villa crest. William had previously confirmed his son's allegiance, telling Villa fans he would not force a team on his children as long as it was not Chelsea. He also discussed his lifelong support of Aston Villa on the Graham Norton Show earlier this month, joking that childhood friends introduced him to the club hoping for free tickets in later life.
