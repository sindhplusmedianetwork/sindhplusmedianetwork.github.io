Firing on Tiger Shroff's Car in Mumbai, Driver Among Two Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Tiger Shroff's car came under fire from unidentified armed men in Mumbai, leaving his driver and one other person injured, while the actor himself remained safe. The incident took place on Sunday evening near Pushpa Park in Juhu when Tiger Shroff was returning in his BMW after playing football, and attackers fired about three shots at the vehicle. His driver Firdous was shot in the hand and another person was also injured; both were rushed to hospital where treatment is underway.

A large number of fans had gathered to catch a glimpse of the actor, and the sound of gunfire caused panic in the area, with a purported video of the incident circulating on social media. Messages from accounts claiming links to the Lawrence Bishnoi gang, in the names of Anil Pandit and Harman Sandhu, claimed responsibility for the attack and threatened film personalities working with Salman Khan, but Mumbai police said the claim has not been verified. Deputy Commissioner Mohit Garg confirmed that Tiger Shroff was completely safe, and police have collected evidence from the scene, obtained nearby CCTV footage and formed special teams to arrest the attackers.

The actor's father, Jackie Shroff's team, also confirmed Tiger was unharmed, while Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Eknath Shinde strongly condemned the firing and said gang wars would not be tolerated in Mumbai.
