Power Shortfall Deepens as Lesco Faces 800MW Gap After Tarbela Mishap

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Load-shedding has increased across the country due to a decline in electricity generation, with consumers in the Lahore Electric Supply Company (Lesco) region facing three to four hours of outages. Lesco's shortfall has reached 800 megawatts against a demand of 3,100MW, while it is receiving 2,350MW from the national grid. The Tarbela Dam accident has also affected power generation, after a breach in a protective embankment at the Tarbela Dam's Tunnel Five (T-5) hydropower project on Monday caused a large volume of water to flood the tunnel.

The shortfall has left consumers facing prolonged outages across the country. The Tarbela mishap added to the generation shortfall behind the increased load-shedding.
