Multan Sultans Sign Australian Fast Bowler Lance Morris for PSL 12

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Multan Sultans have signed Australian fast bowler Lance Morris through a direct signing for the 12th season of the HBL Pakistan Super League. Morris has represented Australia in international cricket. He has previously played for Melbourne Stars, Perth Scorchers and Western Australia.

Morris said he had thought before about being part of Multan Sultans. Sultans CEO Gohar Shah welcomed Morris, calling him the world's fastest bowler. The team announced the signing as the fastest bowler in HBL PSL 12, introducing Morris as 'The Wild Thing'.
