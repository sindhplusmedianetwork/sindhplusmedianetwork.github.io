Kundi Warns Afghan Taliban After Angoor Adda Firing: Pakistan Will Never Tolerate Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has condemned the firing from across the Afghan border in Lower South Waziristan's Angoor Adda area on Tuesday night, saying Pakistan will never tolerate attacks on its people or violations of its sovereignty. The firing targeted Pakistan Army border posts and residential areas, killing a local resident and injuring five members of a family when a mortar landed on their house, while several shops were also damaged. The Lower South Waziristan deputy commissioner confirmed the casualties, saying Pakistan's forces on the border responded effectively and heavily.

In a statement on Wednesday morning, the governor saluted the armed forces for their swift, forceful and decisive response and told the Afghan Taliban regime to have no illusions about Pakistan's resolve. He said Khyber Pakhtunkhwa had sacrificed thousands of lives in the fight against terrorism and would not allow anyone to threaten its people or undermine its security. Adviser to the KP governor Samar Haroon Bilour also condemned the night attack, saying those responsible would have to face consequences.

Officials said the firing came from posts inside Afghan territory using sniper, small and heavy weapons, and that Pakistani forces engaged multiple Taliban border posts, mortar positions and defensive bunkers with artillery, forcing the attackers to abandon their posts and flee.
