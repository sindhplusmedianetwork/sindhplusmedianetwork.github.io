University of Karachi issues new academic calendar, sets result dates for the first time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The University of Karachi has issued a new academic calendar for the academic session 2027 that, for the first time in the university's history, fixes the dates and the time frame for announcing semester examination results. The decision was taken to ensure that results are announced on time and transparently, instead of being delayed, and the result dates have now been made part of the academic calendar. The calendar was re-prepared during the two-month tenure of acting vice chancellor Dr Tufail Ahmed, and the academic session 2027 was rescheduled so that teaching will begin on January 7 instead of December 15, 2026, while orientation classes will now be held on January 6 instead of December 14.

The decision was taken at a meeting of deans and advisers chaired by Vice Chancellor Dr Waseem Qazi, and the acting registrar issued a notification with the vice chancellor's approval. Under the new calendar, teaching for the first semester will run from January 7 to Friday, May 7, 2027, semester examinations will be held from May 12 to 28, and the results will be announced by June 19, with the deans bound to make teachers meet the deadline. Semester holidays will run from June 21 to July 2, the fall semester will begin on Monday, July 5 and continue until November 3, its examinations will be held from November 8 to 26, results will be announced by December 14, and the winter break will run from December 15 to January 2, 2028.
