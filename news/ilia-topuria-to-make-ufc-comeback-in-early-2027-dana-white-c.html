Ilia Topuria to Make UFC Comeback in Early 2027, Dana White Confirms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

UFC chief Dana White has confirmed that former featherweight and lightweight champion Ilia Topuria is expected to make his return to the Octagon in early 2027. Topuria has been away from competition since suffering the first defeat of his professional mixed martial arts career against Justin Gaethje. In that gruelling contest, Topuria sustained serious facial injuries, including fractures around the eye socket, and his corner stopped the bout before the fifth round.

Since then, the fighter known as "El Matador" has returned to his home country of Georgia to recover, rebuild and train for his comeback. Speaking on "The Jim Rome Show", White said the promotion is actively working on scheduling Topuria's next fight, adding: "He'll come back in 2027." White also praised the lightweight division, calling its top ten "straight killers" and the most competitive division in the sport right now. He did not name a specific opponent, but possible bouts include rematches or meetings with leading lightweight contenders such as Arman Tsarukyan, Charles Oliveira, Paddy Pimblett and Max Holloway.

Topuria himself recently hinted at his return on social media, writing: "The Matador is coming back."
