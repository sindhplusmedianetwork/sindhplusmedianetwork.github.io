Saudi Arabia Executes Five Nationals Convicted of Terrorism Charges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia carried out the death sentences of five Saudi nationals in the Eastern Province on Saturday after they were convicted of terrorism-related offences, according to an interior ministry statement reported by Geo News. The ministry said the men were linked to a terrorist organization and had attempted to attack security forces. They were also found in possession of illegal weapons and accused of providing financial support for terrorist activities inside the kingdom.

Security agencies arrested the suspects and, after investigations, produced them before the relevant court, where the charges were proven and all five were sentenced to death. The sentence was subsequently upheld by both the appeal court and the Supreme Court. The interior ministry reiterated that the verdicts followed full legal proceedings before the executions were carried out.
