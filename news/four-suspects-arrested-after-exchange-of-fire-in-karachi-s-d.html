Four suspects arrested after exchange of fire in Karachi's Defence area

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence police in Karachi arrested four alleged members of a gang that carried out robberies while travelling in cars and on motorcycles, District South Police said. The arrests followed an exchange of fire during routine patrolling in the Defence area. One suspect was arrested in an injured condition, while three others, including two women, were taken into custody without resistance.

Police also found a three-year-old child with the two women. Officers recovered a white Suzuki Alto, two motorcycles, two pistols with ammunition, cash and artificial jewellery from the suspects. Six alleged accomplices managed to escape, police said.
