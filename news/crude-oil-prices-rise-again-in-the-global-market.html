Crude oil prices rise again in the global market

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Crude oil prices have risen again in the world market amid continuing tensions in the Middle East. Brent crude rose 0.8 percent to $106.58 a barrel, while US WTI crude rose 0.9 percent to $94.94 a barrel. Tensions between Yemen's Houthi group and Saudi Arabia have raised fears about oil supplies.

The concern over supplies has pushed prices higher in the global market. Traders are watching the situation in the region closely as the tensions continue.
