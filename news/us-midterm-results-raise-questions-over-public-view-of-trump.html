The results of the US midterm elections have raised the question of whether they reflect public confidence or distrust in President Trump's policy towards Iran. The outcome is being read as a signal of how Americans view the administration's handling of Iran. The results have prompted debate about what the vote says about public feeling on the Iran issue.

Attention is now on whether the outcome shows support for the administration's approach or doubt about it. The midterm verdict is therefore being seen through the lens of the Iran policy.
