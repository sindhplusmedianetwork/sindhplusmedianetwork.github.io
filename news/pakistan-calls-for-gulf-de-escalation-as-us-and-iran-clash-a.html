Pakistan calls for Gulf de-escalation as US and Iran clash at UN

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan on Friday called for an immediate end to military provocations in the Gulf and renewed regional diplomacy, as the United States, Iran and Gulf Arab states exchanged sharply conflicting accusations at a UN Security Council meeting. The meeting focused on attacks against shipping and threats to maritime security in the region. Pakistan's Permanent Representative, Ambassador Syed Haider Shah, condemned attacks against Gulf countries and Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia.

He urged protection for civilians, commercial shipping and freedom of navigation. The Gulf states said their members had faced more than 7,000 Iranian missiles and drones since February 28. Iran, in turn, blamed American and Israeli strikes for the insecurity in the region.
