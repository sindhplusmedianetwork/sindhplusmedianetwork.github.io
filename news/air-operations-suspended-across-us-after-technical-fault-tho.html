Air operations suspended across US after technical fault, thousands of flights affected

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Air operations in the United States were suspended after a technical fault in the air traffic control system. Thousands of flights were affected by the disruption. The Federal Aviation Administration said the fault hit radar and air traffic control capacity across the country.

International flights arriving in the United States would be assessed individually, the FAA said, and no time was given for the ground stop to end. US media reported no official confirmation of a cyberattack or sabotage. The FAA and the FBI are investigating the cause of the fault.

The US weather service also faced nationwide internet and communications problems at the same time.
