PIA Unearths Rs5 Million Fraud as Staff Pocketed Premium Seat Fares

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan International Airlines has unearthed a fraud of over Rs5.06 million involving the sale of premium seats at major airports. Staff at Lahore's Allama Iqbal International Airport — and also at Islamabad and Karachi — allegedly collected the higher fares for attractive seats from passengers but entered them as normal seats in the system, pocketing the difference. Documents show that around 20 officers and employees, including three women, were part of the scheme, carried out with the collusion of post-flight officers.

After the audit and finance committee detected the fraud, the station manager ordered the misappropriated amounts to be deducted from the salaries of those involved. Surprisingly, the employees caught in the fraud are reportedly still serving at the same posts, and further proceedings under PIA rules have been halted. Sources say the action was stopped following intervention by senior officials.

When contacted, the airline's spokesperson said the matter had been referred to the officer concerned and a response would be shared once received.
