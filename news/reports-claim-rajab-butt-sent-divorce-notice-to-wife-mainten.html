Reports claim Rajab Butt sent divorce notice to wife; maintenance case filed

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

New claims have emerged in the ongoing marital dispute between Pakistani YouTuber Rajab Butt and his wife Eman Fatima. A well-known social media page is claiming that Butt has allegedly sent his wife a divorce notice through the union council, while reports say Fatima had also attempted reconciliation for the sake of their son. In parallel, Fatima has filed a maintenance case against her husband, demanding five lakh rupees per month for their son and two lakh rupees per month for herself, along with the return of dowry that is said to be worth 8.7 million rupees.

Her side has further claimed that Butt bore none of their son's expenses. None of these claims or the reported legal proceedings have been independently verified, and the dispute continues to generate intense discussion on social media.
