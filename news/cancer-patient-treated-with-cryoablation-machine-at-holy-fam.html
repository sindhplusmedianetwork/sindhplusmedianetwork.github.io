Cancer patient treated with cryoablation machine at Holy Family Hospital

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first cancer patient was successfully treated with a cryoablation machine at Holy Family Hospital in Rawalpindi. A 50-year-old woman was relieved of liver cancer. Hospital administration said the liver cancer was eliminated in a one-hour process using a probe worth Rs1.6 million.

Treatment for liver, kidney and chest cancer had been activated, the administration said. Sixteen probes were available in the first phase for 16 patients.
