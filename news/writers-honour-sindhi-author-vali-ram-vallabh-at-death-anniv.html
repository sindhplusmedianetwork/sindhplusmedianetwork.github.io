Writers honour Sindhi author Vali Ram Vallabh at death anniversary seminar in Jamshoro

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Writers and intellectuals honoured renowned Sindhi author and translator Vali Ram Vallabh at a seminar held at the Institute of Sindhology in Jamshoro on his third death anniversary. Speakers described his 60 years of literary work as unique and exemplary. Sindh University Vice Chancellor Prof Dr Fateh Muhammad Marri said Vallabh was a silent contributor to Sindhi literature.

He said Vallabh introduced masterpieces of world literature into Sindhi through translations that read like original works. Speakers including Shabnam Gul, Dr Tahmina Mufti and Dr Fayaz Latif praised his prose, poetry and translations. His children Dr Kamleshwar and Dr Pushpa Vallabh also spoke at the event.
