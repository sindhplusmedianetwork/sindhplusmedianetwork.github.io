Being close to the powerful does not mean trampling others, says Khawaja Asif

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has said that being close to the powerful does not mean a person should trample over others to get ahead. Speaking to a private television channel, he said threats should not be issued, as that is not political language, and that political criticism could be made as many times as one wished, but it was wrong to say that if he spoke, the other side would not be able to show its face. Praising Rana Sanaullah, Khawaja Asif said he had always faced difficulties with courage, and that in a difficult time a companion should feel that a friend stands beside him.

He explained that he was speaking of the past, when there was neither difficulty nor a time of trial, and recalled what had been done to Rana Sanaullah 26 years ago and the imprisonment he had endured in the PTI era, saying anyone in his place would have knelt down. The defence minister said Nawaz Sharif did not involve himself in such matters, adding that they were political workers who made mistakes but stood by what they said and faced every risk behind it. Khawaja Asif said he had attended the meeting on the last amendment, whose biggest beneficiary was Asif Ali Zardari, as the entire amendment revolved around the president and gave him lifetime immunity, and that calling it forced through coercion was political dishonesty.

He said it was not right to start bidding at every opportunity, as it left a bad impression and stained politics. He added that he remained hopeful that whatever happened would be good for the country, and that give and take, compromise and new ways forward were possible.
