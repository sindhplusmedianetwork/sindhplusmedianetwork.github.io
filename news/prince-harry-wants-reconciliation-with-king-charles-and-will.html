Prince Harry Wants Reconciliation With King Charles and William

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Britain's Prince Harry has expressed the wish to improve relations with his father King Charles and elder brother Prince William, saying the world already had many problems and there was no benefit in continuing family disputes. The Duke of Sussex said this in an interview with the American magazine GQ in New York. He added that conflicts between parents and children and strained sibling relations were problems many families went through.

The prince said people often watched his family's disputes and wondered how such conflicts would end, and perhaps that could help others resolve their own family problems. Prince Harry made it clear that he himself wanted to improve relations and that reconciliation mattered most to him. Asked about the possibility of old wounds healing, he said scars could always remain, but one should try to leave the past behind and move forward.

He said that if past grievances kept affecting daily life, they became harder to forget. Prince Harry also called a return to Britain and the education of his children Archie and Lilibet in British schools important. He expressed the heartfelt wish that his children gain experiences and exposure to the different cultures of Britain and America.

Speaking about coping with grief after his mother Princess Diana's death, Harry said he shared his experiences not to gain sympathy but to help others, adding that the incident had left deep wounds and therapy and support had helped him move forward. His spokesman later clarified that his remarks were not criticism of Prince William or any royal family member but were made in a general context.
