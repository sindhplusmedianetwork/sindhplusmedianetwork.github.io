Honey, ginger and other home remedies for relief from a persistent cough

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

With the change of season, complaints of colds, flu and cough become common, and sometimes a cough lingers for many days even after the infection ends, affecting sleep and daily activities, Express News reported. Smoke, dust, allergies and mucus in the airways can all worsen a cough, and some home remedies can give temporary relief for throat irritation and cough — though it is important to know the real cause of a persistent cough. Honey can help reduce throat irritation and the intensity of coughing, especially at night: one to two teaspoons of honey can be mixed into a cup of lukewarm water, though it should not be considered a guaranteed cure — and honey must never be given to children under one year of age.

Ginger contains compounds whose anti-inflammatory properties have been researched; a few pieces of ginger can be boiled in water for a while and drunk like tea, which may soothe the throat, though there is no strong evidence that ginger ends every kind of cough. A drink of cinnamon and cloves — ginger pieces, a small piece of cinnamon and one or two cloves boiled in water — may give temporary throat relief, but claims that cinnamon or cloves cure coughs or infections are unproven. It helps to stay away from smoke, dust or specific smells that trigger the cough, keep reasonable humidity in the room and drink adequate water to ease throat dryness — while avoiding very hot steam because of the burn risk.

A medical checkup is advised if the cough lasts more than three weeks, breathing becomes difficult, there is chest pain or blood, or a high fever persists. Relying only on home remedies without knowing the cause of the cough is not appropriate, the report concluded.
