Eleven killed as passenger van plunges into river in Ghizer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Eleven people, among them an infant and two women, lost their lives on Thursday evening when a passenger van travelling from Gilgit to Yasin Valley plunged into the Yasin river in Gilgit-Baltistan's Ghizer district. Police said the driver lost control of the vehicle near Morkah Bridge, sending it into the water below. Rescue teams pulled the bodies of the victims from the river and moved two injured passengers to Gupis Civil Hospital for treatment.

The dead and injured were all residents of Yasin Valley, according to police, who added that an investigation was under way to determine what caused the crash. Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Minister Amjad Hussain expressed deep grief over the loss of life and directed the district administration, rescue agencies and health officials to ensure the injured were taken to hospital at once and given the best possible care. He also instructed officials to make sure there was no negligence during the rescue work and that the treatment of the injured was closely monitored, while offering condolences to the bereaved families and praying for the speedy recovery of the injured.
