Taylor Swift Stuns in Cinderella-Inspired Dior Gown at Academy Museum Gala

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Taylor Swift turned heads at the sixth annual Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles on Saturday night. The singer wore a custom icy-blue Dior ballgown inspired by Cinderella, featuring silver-blue pleating and a crystal-embellished bow. The 14-time Grammy winner paired the look with statement earrings, vintage-style curls and classic winged eyeliner.

The fundraising gala honoured filmmaker John Carpenter, Colman Domingo and Charlize Theron. Swift closed the evening with a live performance for the guests.
