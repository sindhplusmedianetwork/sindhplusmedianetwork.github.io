Ronaldo in Line for Portugal Return After Fallout With Coach

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cristiano Ronaldo is in Portugal's preliminary squad for next month's Nations League matches, opening the door to an international return after his fallout with coach Jorge Jesus, a federation source told AFP on Tuesday. The 41-year-old abruptly left the Portugal camp on September 30 during the opening qualifiers for the Nations League, before accusing Jesus of breaking his word about giving him game time. A spokesperson for the Portuguese football federation confirmed reports that Ronaldo was in line for a return ahead of games at home to Denmark and in Wales on November 14 and 17.

Jesus will announce his final squad on November 6, the spokesperson added. The federation last week announced it had handed Ronaldo a 30-day suspension for walking out, but the suspension was backdated, meaning he will be free to return by the end of this week. Ronaldo apologised to supporters earlier in October, adding that he would accept any punishment imposed on him.

In a social media post shortly after leaving his team-mates, he accused Jesus of promising him playing time against Norway before failing to honour it, but insisted he was not retiring from international duty. Reigning champions Portugal won all four of their Nations League matches, three of them without Ronaldo, and qualified for the quarter-finals with two games to spare.
