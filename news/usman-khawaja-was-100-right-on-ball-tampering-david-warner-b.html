'Usman Khawaja was 100% right on ball-tampering': David Warner backs former team-mate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Australian batter David Warner has said Usman Khawaja was '100 percent right' about the 2018 ball-tampering scandal, and described Cricket Australia's investigation after the incident as rushed and poorly conducted. In a video statement posted on Instagram on Monday, Warner said he fully supported Khawaja, adding that everything Khawaja had said was 100 percent correct and that now everyone knew the truth. Khawaja has claimed in his autobiography, 'Sabr', and in a newspaper interview that the whole Australian team knew about the plan to use sandpaper on the ball during the third Test against South Africa in Cape Town in 2018, although the bowlers have repeatedly denied any knowledge.

Warner questioned the investigation, saying Khawaja sat right next to Cameron Bancroft but was never interviewed, and asking how it could be called a complete investigation. He said the issue was about accountability and transparency, not about blaming anyone or reopening old wounds. Khawaja, who retired from international cricket in January after 88 Tests and 6,229 runs, has said no player was made a scapegoat and that the team should collectively accept responsibility.

Cricket Australia has not indicated it will reopen the investigation, saying all relevant information provided to it has been appropriately considered.
