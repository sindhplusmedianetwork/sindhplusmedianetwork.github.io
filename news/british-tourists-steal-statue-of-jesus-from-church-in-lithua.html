British Tourists Steal Statue of Jesus from Church in Lithuania and Smash It

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two alleged British tourists stole a golden statue of Jesus Christ from a cross in the courtyard of a church in Vilnius, Lithuania, and smashed it. The statue stood in the courtyard of the 15th-century Shrine of Divine Mercy church. CCTV footage showed the men laughing as one of them carried the statue away while the other filmed on a mobile phone.

The statue's arms broke off and it shattered after falling to the ground before the pair fled the scene. The church priest expressed regret over the incident and appealed to the public for help in identifying the men. Local police have started an investigation and are assessing the damage caused to the statue.
