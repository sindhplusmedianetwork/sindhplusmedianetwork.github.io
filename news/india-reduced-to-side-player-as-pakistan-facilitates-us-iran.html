India Reduced to 'Side Player' as Pakistan Facilitates US-Iran Talks: Rahul Gandhi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian opposition leader Rahul Gandhi has expressed surprise at Pakistan's role in facilitating negotiations between the United States and Iran, saying India had been reduced to a 'side player' in global affairs. Speaking at the University of Chicago, he pointed to Pakistan's diplomatic engagement with Washington and Tehran as an example of India's diminishing influence in international affairs. In a post on X on Friday, he said he was pretty shocked that Pakistan is busy negotiating with the Iranians and the Americans while India is just looking.

Gandhi stated that India had traditionally maintained an independent foreign policy since independence, guided by its national interests. He argued that by India's size, history and international relationships, it should be involved in global conversations, but said the country had stepped back from the stage. He also highlighted that the war involving Iran had exacerbated existing hardships in India, and called for a more inclusive global security framework in which a nation of 1.5 billion people must help shape the emerging order.

Pakistan's mediation efforts gained momentum in March and April, with Islamabad facilitating negotiations on April 11 and the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding signed on June 18.
