Contacts With Gulf States Ongoing Despite Regional Tensions, Says Iran's Deputy FM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said on October 17 that Tehran is still in contact with littoral Gulf states and that contacts with the region's foreign ministers are ongoing 'at the highest level', ISNA reported. He acknowledged that the regional situation is not favourable, attributing it to 'the aggression of the United States and the Zionist regime (Israel)'. The remarks came as Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met his Turkish counterpart Hakan Fidan on the sidelines of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation foreign ministers' meeting in Baghdad.

The comments signal Tehran's effort to keep diplomatic channels open with its Gulf neighbours despite heightened tensions in the region. Iran has in recent days repeatedly denied involvement in Houthi attacks on Saudi targets while offering to mediate.
