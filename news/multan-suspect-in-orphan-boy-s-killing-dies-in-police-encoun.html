Multan: Suspect in Orphan Boy's Killing Dies in Police Encounter

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The contractor accused of crushing 15-year-old orphan Waseem Abbas with his car in Multan was killed in a police encounter, officials said. Tauqeer, who was on a four-day judicial remand, had fled custody, according to the Crime Control Department. Police signalled two motorcyclists to stop on the Northern Bypass in Bahawalpur; the riders fled and opened fire.

Tauqeer was killed by his own companion's firing while the companion escaped, police said. Abbas, who had lived at the orphanage for five years, died after the contractor allegedly chased him in a car and ran him over following an argument over work. The boy's death had earlier led the Child Protection and Welfare Bureau to reject the orphanage's registration request.
