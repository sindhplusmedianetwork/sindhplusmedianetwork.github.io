DG ISPR Visits Three Lahore Universities, Fields Students' Questions in Open Sessions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Director General of ISPR visited three universities in Lahore and attended special sessions with students and faculty members. He visited the University of Lahore, the University of Central Punjab and King Edward Medical University, where students and faculty spoke openly and asked questions on various issues, and the army spokesperson gave detailed and reasoned answers. The meetings included detailed discussion on the regional geopolitical situation, including Afghanistan, and the Pakistan Army's role in establishing peace.

Students appreciated the DG ISPR's university visits and direct dialogue, stressing the need for more such sessions in future. On the occasion, students paid tribute to the armed forces on their success in Marka-e-Haq and remembered the martyrs of the Pakistan Army, praying for the elevation of their ranks.
