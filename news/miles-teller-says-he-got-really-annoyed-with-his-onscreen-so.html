Miles Teller Says He Got 'Really Annoyed' With His Onscreen Son While Filming 'Paper Tiger'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Miles Teller has revealed that he became genuinely annoyed with one of the young actors playing his teenage son while filming his upcoming crime drama 'Paper Tiger', admitting that the experience gave him an unexpected taste of fatherhood. The 'Top Gun: Maverick' actor shared the story during an appearance on Jimmy Kimmel Live! on November 4, where he discussed working with the young cast members and playing a father of teenagers for the first time.

Teller recalled that during the first day of filming, one of the actors playing his sons used a swear word while the cast was improvising a scene. The actor said he found himself reacting as though he were actually the boy's father. In another moment, the young performer delivered a line suggesting that he had learned the behaviour from his onscreen dad.

Teller said he immediately called for a cut and questioned whether he could discipline the actor playing his son. The actor joked about how quickly he became invested in correcting the teenager's behaviour, explaining that the experience was particularly unusual because he is not yet a father himself.
