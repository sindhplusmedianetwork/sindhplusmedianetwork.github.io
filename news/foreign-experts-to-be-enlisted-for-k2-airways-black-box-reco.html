Foreign Experts to Be Enlisted for K2 Airways Black Box Recovery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nearly four months after a K2 Airways aircraft plunged into the sea off Ormara, the search for its black box, wreckage and cockpit crew is entering a new phase, with Pakistan stepping up efforts to enlist international expertise for a deep-sea recovery operation. With the aircraft believed to lie around 3,000 metres below the surface, the search is expected to draw on advanced sonar systems, autonomous underwater vehicles and remotely operated robots equipped with high-definition cameras and specialised recovery tools. The government has intensified contacts with foreign experts and several international companies to determine how best to locate and recover the aircraft's remains from the seabed, according to sources.

The black box and wreckage are considered crucial to establishing what happened in the aircraft's final moments. The aircraft belonging to the privately owned domestic cargo operator crashed into the sea on July 7 this year after encountering adverse conditions near Ormara, and sources said the transmitter beacon fitted to its black box stopped emitting signals around a month after the accident, making the search more difficult. The Pakistan Navy had earlier used specialised equipment and side-scan sonar to identify a possible location of the black box on the seabed, but further progress now depends on more sophisticated underwater technology and specialised deep-sea salvage expertise.
