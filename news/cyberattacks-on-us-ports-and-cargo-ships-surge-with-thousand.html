Cyberattacks on US ports and cargo ships surge, with thousands of daily attempts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ports and cargo ships in the United States are facing a sharp surge in cyberattacks. Officials and experts report a more than 300 percent increase in attempted breaches in recent years. Some facilities face between 3,000 and 5,000 attempts every day.

Criminal gangs and hostile states are targeting the maritime sector. Experts warn that automated systems at ports could be vulnerable to attack. They also caution that artificial intelligence could help attackers exploit weaknesses faster.
