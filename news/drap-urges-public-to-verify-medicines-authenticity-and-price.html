DRAP Urges Public to Verify Medicines' Authenticity and Prices Through New App

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan has urged consumers to use its Mehfooz Dawa application to verify the authenticity and prices of medicines as part of its efforts to curb the sale of counterfeit drugs. About 800 pharmaceutical companies and more than 90,000 medicines are registered with the authority, a spokesperson for the Ministry of National Health Services said. The authority has set up a 2D barcode system that allows consumers to scan medicine packs to ensure they receive safe, high-quality and effective products.

The centralised track-and-trace system for pharmaceutical products became operational on October 9. The app is part of the authority's efforts to protect consumers from fake medicines and to help them check that the medicines they buy are genuine and fairly priced.
