US Supreme Court Rejects Bid to Restore 9/11 Plea Deals; Death Penalty Back on Table

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The US Supreme Court has rejected a bid to restore plea agreements for 9/11 mastermind Khalid Sheikh Mohammed and two co-defendants. The ruling means the three men could once again face the death penalty. Under the deals, they would have pleaded guilty in exchange for life imprisonment without parole.

The agreements were approved by military prosecutors but were cancelled by then-Defence Secretary Lloyd Austin in 2024. The defendants' lawyers had argued that Austin lacked the authority to cancel them, but the appeals court upheld his decision. The military trial is now expected to begin in 2028.

Families of the 9/11 victims remain divided, with some preferring a full trial and others favouring the plea deals.
