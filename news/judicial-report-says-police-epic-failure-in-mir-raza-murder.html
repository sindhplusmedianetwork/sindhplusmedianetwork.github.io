Judicial report says police 'epic failure' in Mir Raza murder case, backs JIT

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A judicial commission led by Sindh High Court Justice Omar Sial has concluded that Karachi businessman Mir Raza Ali was murdered and was not a suicide victim. The 62-page report delivered a scathing assessment of the police investigation, calling it an "epic failure". It backed the family's demand for a joint investigation team comprising the ISI, the IB, the Rangers and the NCCIA.

The findings mark a decisive turn in a case that had been treated as a possible suicide. Following the report, the Sindh cabinet decided to make it public. A three-member committee will oversee the implementation of its recommendations.
