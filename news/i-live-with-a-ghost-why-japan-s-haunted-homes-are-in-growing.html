'I Live With a Ghost': Why Japan's 'Haunted' Homes Are in Growing Demand

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rising property prices in Japan are pushing more people toward homes with a dark history. So-called 'jiko bukken' — properties where a death occurred — are seeing growing demand because they sell or rent at steep discounts. A 2025 survey found that more than half of respondents would rent such a home if the discount was large enough.

Among them is idol Reiva, who pays around 30 percent less for her Tokyo flat, where a previous resident died by suicide. Since 2021, agents have been required to disclose such deaths to buyers and renters. For many young renters, the savings now outweigh the superstition.
