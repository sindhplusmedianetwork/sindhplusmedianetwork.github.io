UN Inquiry Says Israel Kept Committing Genocide in Gaza After October 2025 Truce

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read

Israel continued to commit genocide in Gaza after last October's US-brokered ceasefire in the enclave, a UN commission said in a report published on Monday. The report by the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry examined violations against Palestinian civilians, including children, from the start of the war between Israel and Hamas on October 7, 2023, up to September this year. It said Israeli authorities had kept committing genocidal acts with the genocidal intent to destroy, in whole or in part, the Palestinians in Gaza, and that the situation had also continued to deteriorate in the occupied West Bank.

The commission found that Israeli forces continued using heavy munitions, drones, quadcopters, artillery and sniper fire in densely populated areas during the ceasefire in Gaza. The Israeli mission in Geneva rejected the report, calling it part of a misinformation campaign to demonise Israel. The commission also concluded that Israeli authorities and forces had committed crimes against humanity in the West Bank, including extermination, torture, rape and other forms of sexual violence, and persecution.

Palestinian authorities say more than 74,000 people have been killed in Israel's assault on Gaza, launched after Hamas-led fighters killed about 1,200 people in southern Israel on October 7, 2023. The report said military operations since January 2025 had led to the largest displacement of Palestinians in the West Bank since 1967, with 34,176 Palestinians remaining displaced as of August 2026.
