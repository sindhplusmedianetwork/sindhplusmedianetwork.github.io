FCC Bars Peshawar High Court From Hearing PTI Workers' Detention Cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Constitutional Court on Thursday restrained the Peshawar High Court from hearing cases related to the preventive detention of PTI workers and activists under the Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance, following the imposition of emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. A two-judge bench headed by Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan and including Justice Aamer Farooq took up petitions challenging interim bail granted by the high court to the detainees against surety bonds of Rs100,000 each. The high court had issued the interim orders without a report from the provincial government explaining the reasons and circumstances behind the detentions, and had directed that the detainees should not be arrested while their cases were pending.

More than 60 appeals challenging the high court's interim orders have been fixed before the constitutional court, moved by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Home and Tribal Affairs Department. The constitutional court restrained the high court from proceeding further and issued notices to the parties, directing that they may also file their pleas before the superior court. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Advocate General Sanaullah Khan argued that the high court's orders had been challenged under Article 245 of the Constitution, which bars a high court from exercising jurisdiction under Article 199 in areas where the armed forces are acting in aid of civil power.

Scores of PTI workers and leaders have been detained since emergency and governor's rule were imposed in the province on October 11, including lawmakers, while former chief minister Sohail Afridi has also been detained.
