Houthis Claim Six Strikes on Saudi Targets, Including Aramco Sites and Riyadh Airport

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said in a statement on X that six military operations were carried out on Saturday, targeting sites inside Saudi Arabia and in Yemen. He said ballistic missiles, cruise missiles and drones were used in the operations. The claimed targets included King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, oil storage tanks and a distillation tower in the capital, Aramco facilities in the oil-producing Khurais region, and a pumping station in Dawadmi.

The Houthis also claimed to have targeted a gathering of Saudi National Guard personnel in Najran and Saudi-backed military targets in Yemen's Al-Jawf and Marib provinces. Saree claimed some targets were hit successfully and Saudi personnel were harmed, but no full details or independent evidence were provided. The Saudi-led coalition said on Saturday that its air defences intercepted and destroyed a missile fired from Yemen toward Riyadh province before it reached its target, with no immediate reports of casualties.

Fighting between the Houthis and the Saudi-backed internationally recognised government of Yemen has intensified again, with the Houthis presenting their recent operations as a response to Saudi air strikes.
