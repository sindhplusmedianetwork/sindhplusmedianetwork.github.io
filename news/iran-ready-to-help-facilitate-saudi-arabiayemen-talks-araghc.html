Iran Ready to Help Facilitate Saudi Arabia–Yemen Talks, Araghchi Tells Saudi FM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said Iran is ready to provide every possible help to end the conflict between Saudi Arabia and Yemen. According to Iran's official news agency IRNA, Araghchi made a telephonic contact with his Saudi counterpart Faisal bin Farhan, during which the two ministers discussed recent tensions in the region. Araghchi said that insisting on a military approach while ignoring a political solution would further complicate the current situation, adding that the region's crises have no military solution.

Referring to a 12-year deadlock, he said it was now proven that Yemen had no military solution and that only political negotiations could bring lasting peace. Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei also expressed deep concern over developments in the Arabian Peninsula, saying two Muslim countries should not remain hostile, especially when the region was already tense due to the United States and Israel. The contact comes as Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia have intensified, with the October 10 attack on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport killing 12 to 13 people and injuring more than 300, followed by a second attack on October 11.
