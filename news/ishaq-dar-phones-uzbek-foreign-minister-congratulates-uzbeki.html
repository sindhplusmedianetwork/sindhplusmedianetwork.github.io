Ishaq Dar Phones Uzbek Foreign Minister, Congratulates Uzbekistan on SCO Chairmanship

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Senator Ishaq Dar on Saturday held a telephone conversation with the Foreign Minister of Uzbekistan, Bakhtiyor Saidov, the Foreign Office spokesperson said. During the call, Dar congratulated Uzbekistan on taking over the chairmanship of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization's Council of Heads of Government for 2026–27. He assured Uzbekistan of Pakistan's complete cooperation and support during its chairmanship.

The two foreign ministers also discussed the dates and preparations for the SCO summit scheduled to be held in 2027. They further exchanged views on the SCO's calendar of upcoming activities. According to the spokesperson, the Uzbek foreign minister praised Pakistan's continued efforts for the promotion of regional peace and security.

Both sides reiterated their commitment to further strengthening bilateral ties.
