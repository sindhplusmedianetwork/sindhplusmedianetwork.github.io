Jewellery collection reaches Shanghai expo venue in 35 minutes under CIIE Express facility

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new jewellery collection has been moved to the venue of China's ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai under the 'CIIE Express' facility. The collection was transferred from a bonded warehouse in the city centre to the National Exhibition and Convention Center. Pakistani exhibitor Aqeel Chaudhry said China had this time brought the port to the companies' doorstep.

He said goods now reach the exhibition venue in just 35 minutes. The system has made the transport of exhibits, customs clearance and access to the market faster and safer than before.
