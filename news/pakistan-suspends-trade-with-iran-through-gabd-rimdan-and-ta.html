Pakistan Suspends Trade With Iran Through Gabd-Rimdan and Taftan Crossings

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan suspended trade with Iran on Saturday through the official Gabd-Rimdan and Taftan border crossings with immediate effect, after the Ministry of Commerce issued a memorandum withdrawing its earlier order dated October 3. The directive did not specify a reason for the closure. The Quetta Chamber of Commerce and Industry has advised businesses involved in trade with Iran to stop placing new import and export orders until further notice.

Hundreds of freight vehicles carrying LPG, rice, fresh fruit and other essential commodities were left stranded on both sides of the border. The Gabd-Rimdan crossing had become a major route for Pakistan's energy needs, with over 4,000 tonnes of LPG reportedly imported daily through it, while rice exports had also surged in recent months. Trade and energy sector sources warned that the closure could aggravate LPG shortages in Karachi and other parts of the country and push up prices in the open market.

Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmail Baghaei said cross-border movement had been temporarily suspended due to technical issues and precautionary measures, adding that authorities from both countries were in close consultation to restore normal operations.
