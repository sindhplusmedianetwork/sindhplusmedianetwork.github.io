Bangladesh's Ex-Air Chief Sheikh Abdul Hannan Arrested in Qatar on Graft Charges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Bangladesh Air Force chief Air Chief Marshal Sheikh Abdul Hannan has been arrested in Qatar on corruption and money laundering charges, Bangladeshi media reported. The Anti-Corruption Commission (ACC) says Qatari authorities detained him on October 15, and Interpol formally informed the commission of the arrest by email. The ACC's deputy director Muhammad Akhtar-ul-Islam said two cases were filed against Hannan and his wife in July 2025 over illegal wealth accumulation and money laundering.

He is accused of building assets worth more than 32.8 million taka beyond his known sources of income. The commission also found suspicious transactions of 276.3 million taka through six bank accounts in his personal name. His wife, also named in the cases, faces allegations of acquiring assets worth over 19.1 million taka beyond known income and suspicious transactions of 11.8 million taka through 32 bank accounts.

Despite facing multiple corruption cases and a travel ban, Hannan managed to leave Bangladesh, and investigators have traced his alleged foreign assets to Canada and Dubai. A Dhaka court in March ordered the seizure of the couple's income tax records, after earlier orders to confiscate two apartments and freeze bank accounts.
