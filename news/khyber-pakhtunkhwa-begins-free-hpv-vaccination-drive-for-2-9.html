Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Begins Free HPV Vaccination Drive for 2.9 Million Girls

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has begun a free HPV vaccination campaign targeting 2.9 million girls aged 9 to 14. The campaign covers girls in schools, religious seminaries and communities, including those who are out of school. It runs through November 24.

The Abbottabad district campaign alone aims to reach 75,000 girls across all 54 union councils. Abbottabad is using 98 mobile vaccination teams for the drive.
