Imran Khan's letter based on his exact words, not my recollection: Salman Safdar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Barrister Salman Safdar, the lawyer of PTI founder Imran Khan, has clarified that a letter he drafted on behalf of the former prime minister was based on Imran's exact words during their meeting, rather than his own recollection of the conversation. Safdar said he was documenting every word and every sentence Imran spoke during the meeting, and that senior counsel Sardar Latif Khosa was also present and had independently taken notes of Imran's remarks. On Monday, Imran had appealed to the country's top judges to ensure the implementation of Supreme Court orders concerning his medical treatment and prison conditions, warning that his health was deteriorating.

According to Safdar, Imran said his blood pressure had remained uncontrolled despite medication, reporting readings of 150, 160, 170, 190 and 192, and a reading of 193 the previous day, which he said was approaching 200. Quoting Imran, the lawyer said: 'I am prone and vulnerable to brain haemorrhage,' adding that doctors had told him persistently high blood pressure had already affected his heart to some extent. Safdar said Imran had repeated the same statement three times during their meeting of about one hour and five minutes, and had said: 'If I remain alive, I will fight my cases and prove my innocence.' The lawyer said Imran had asked that judges be informed that both he and his wife, Bushra Bibi, urgently needed medical treatment, and complained that while many people were receiving treatment at hospitals established during his tenure, he himself was not being given access to a hospital, a doctor or adequate medical care.
