Middle East tensions cut Pakistan's mango exports by 36% to 78,000 tonnes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's mango exports fell 36% this season to 78,000 tonnes as Middle East tensions hit demand, the fruit exporters' association PFVA said. The country missed its 80,000-tonne export target. Export earnings dropped to $58.5 million from $92.2 million last season, when 123,000 tonnes were shipped.

Exports to Gulf markets fell sharply, particularly the UAE and Saudi Arabia. The closure of the Afghan border cost an estimated 24,000 tonnes. Climate change has also cut mango production about 35% below its 1.8 million-tonne potential.
