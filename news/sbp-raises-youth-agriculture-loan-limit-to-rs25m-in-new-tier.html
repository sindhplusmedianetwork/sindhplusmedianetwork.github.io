SBP raises youth, agriculture loan limit to Rs25m in new Tier 4

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The State Bank of Pakistan has raised the credit limit under the Prime Minister's Youth Business and Agriculture Loan Scheme to a maximum of Rs25 million, according to a circular issued by the central bank. The government has introduced a new Tier 4 under the scheme, covering loans of Rs7.5 million to Rs25 million. Long-term loans under the tier will have a tenor of up to eight years, with a grace period of up to one year.

The rate for end users will be 8 per cent, significantly below the SBP policy rate of 12 per cent. The move is aimed at widening access to finance for young entrepreneurs and farmers. The scheme supports young people seeking funds to start or expand their businesses and farm work.
