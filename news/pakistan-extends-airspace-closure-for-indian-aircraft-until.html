Pakistan extends airspace closure for Indian aircraft until December 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has decided to extend the airspace closure for India by another month. The Pakistan Airports Authority issued a new NOTAM in this regard. The closure for Indian aircraft has been extended until December 23, 2026.

All Indian-registered commercial, passenger and military aircraft cannot use Pakistan's airspace under the notice. Pakistan's airspace has been closed for India since April 23, 2025.
