Realty Sector Gets Rs34 Billion Tax Relief as Its Contribution Falls 45 Percent

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government's decision to cut real estate income taxes by half has so far extended 34 billion rupees in benefits to the influential sector in this fiscal year, contrary to the marginalised salaried class whose tax payments jumped by 13.5 billion rupees to 193 billion rupees. According to provisional figures, the realty sector paid only 42 billion rupees in withholding taxes during the July to October period, which was 34 billion rupees, or 45 percent, less than the same period of last year, despite an uptick in property transactions and values after the government reversed its policy to discourage speculative investment. As a result, the gap between tax payments by the salaried class and the realty sector, which was favoured by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif through the policy reversal, increased by 360 percent in just four months.

In June, on the request of the prime minister's government, the International Monetary Fund endorsed a 50 percent cut for real estate and a fixed tax scheme for retailers. The advance income tax collection on the sale of property decreased from 52.7 billion rupees to 27.3 billion rupees in four months, a reduction of 25.4 billion rupees or 48 percent, while collection on purchases dipped from 23.1 billion rupees to 14.7 billion rupees. The Federal Board of Revenue has also booked a 95 billion rupee tax shortfall for the July to October period, registering only 6.5 percent growth in revenues, less than half of the prevailing nominal economic growth rate.
