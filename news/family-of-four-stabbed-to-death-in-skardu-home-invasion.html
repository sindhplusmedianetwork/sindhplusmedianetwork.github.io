Family of Four Stabbed to Death in Skardu Home Invasion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A family of four has been brutally murdered in a home invasion in Skardu district of Gilgit-Baltistan, where unknown assailants stabbed a husband, his wife and their two young children to death with repeated knife blows. Police sources said the bodies were discovered at a house behind Madina Colony in Skardu, and the gruesome nighttime attack has spread fear and panic across the area. The deceased husband belonged to Talu Broq in Skardu while his wife was from Parachinar; the two had contracted a court marriage a few years ago and had two children.

Police said the killers also targeted the couple's daughter and son, aged five and two-and-a-half years. Officers reached the scene after being informed, collected forensic evidence and launched an investigation. The victims' relatives told police they had no enmity with anyone, though they suspect the woman's family members could be involved in the killings.

Police have sealed the entry and exit routes of Skardu to arrest the culprits.
