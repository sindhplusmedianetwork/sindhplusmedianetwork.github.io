Health ministry scraps flawed vaccine tender, halts pilgrim vaccinations for now

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Ministry of National Health Services has abolished the vaccination setup run through Border Health Services and halted the current vaccination process for religious pilgrims and overseas job seekers, after a high-level inquiry ordered by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif exposed severe flaws and financial irregularities in a previously awarded contract. Federal Health Minister Mustafa Kamal confirmed that officials involved in floating an unauthorised tender have been arrested for bypassing ministry oversight, and said a new procurement tender for meningococcal meningitis and influenza vaccines will be issued within a month. Until then, pilgrims have been directed to arrange their mandatory vaccinations directly through their travel agents.

The inquiry was triggered by allegations that the new contract inflated vaccination costs to about Rs4,500 per pilgrim, up sharply from around Rs1,250 per dose under the previous arrangement. Seven companies have also petitioned the Islamabad High Court challenging alleged irregularities in the bidding process. A five-member inquiry committee chaired by the Cabinet Division secretary examined the legal basis, rationale and approvals for the contract, and documents reviewed by Dawn showed that Border Health Services officials created parallel committees to bypass federal oversight.
