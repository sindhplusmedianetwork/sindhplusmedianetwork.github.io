Finance Ministry Issues Monthly Outlook: Remittances $10.9bn, Inflation 10.2% in Q1

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Finance Ministry has released its monthly economic outlook report with a mixed picture of the economy. In the first quarter of the fiscal year 2026-27, remittances reached $10.9 billion, up 14 percent year-on-year, while exports grew 7.7 percent to $8.5 billion. Imports rose faster at 14.1 percent to $17.8 billion, and foreign direct investment grew 19.9 percent to $719.7 million.

FBR tax collection stood at Rs3,086.2 billion in the quarter, with September alone up 11.5 percent. Inflation clocked 10.2 percent in the quarter and 10.3 percent in September, well above last year's 5.8 percent. The State Bank's reserves stood at $21.5 billion in October, the rupee was at 278.6 per dollar on Oct 28, and the policy rate was 12 percent.
