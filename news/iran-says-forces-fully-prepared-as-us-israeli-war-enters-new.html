Iran says forces fully prepared as US-Israeli war enters new phase

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fighting in the renewed US-Israeli war on Iran continues with fresh developments reported on Sunday. Brigadier-General Yadollah Javani, the IRGC's deputy for political affairs, told Fars news agency that Iranian forces are fully prepared for any scenario the enemy may devise and will not be caught off guard. Iran has designated Britain's Royal Navy, Royal Air Force and MI6, citing London's participation in the war and its decision to place the IRGC under the State Threats Act 2026.

A Qatari gas tanker was attacked with unidentified objects while transiting Gulf waters, the country's state news agency reported. Two air raids hit an oil facility in Yemen's Hodeidah, with Houthi-affiliated Al Masirah TV blaming the Saudi-led coalition. Iranian Army spokesperson Brigadier General Mohammad Akraminia warned that renewed US aggression would be met with an immediate, decisive response on a larger scale, adding that the scope of any new war would not be confined to West Asia.
