Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake sells 5.2m copies in four days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nintendo's remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has sold 5.2 million copies worldwide within its first four days on sale, according to figures released by the company. The achievement makes it the highest-selling game globally for the Nintendo Switch 2. The strong sales performance has prompted discussion among fans about the remake's development costs, staffing and production timeline.

Estimates circulating online put the budget at $40-50 million, with around 200 core staff. The figures show the remake has made a powerful start on the new console. Fans continue to discuss how the game was made and what it cost to produce.
