Shawn Mendes Expresses Solidarity with Palestine, Calls for End to Gaza Violence

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Famous Canadian singer Shawn Mendes has expressed solidarity with the Palestinian people and called for an end to the violence in Gaza. The singer shared a detailed message on his Instagram account, in which he expressed concern over the difficulties faced by Palestinian children and families. In his statement, Mendes described the situation in Gaza as genocide and said the killing of innocent people must stop immediately.

He said Palestinian children and families have faced difficulties for a long time and need help on humanitarian grounds. Mendes added that he himself is trying to learn more about the conflict and encouraged other artists, friends and fans to become informed as well. He also shared the names of charities he has donated to, including the Palestine Children's Relief Fund (PCRF), Watermelon Pictures and the Woven Futures Fund.

Mendes urged his fans to take part in steps such as donating, raising awareness and demanding answers from the responsible authorities. He expressed hope that through collective efforts, it is possible to help the affected people.
