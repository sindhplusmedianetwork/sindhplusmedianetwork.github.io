Dubai Court Fines Foreign Driver AED 3,000 for Hit-and-Run Injuring Motorcyclist

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A traffic court in Dubai has fined a foreign driver AED 3,000 after he hit a motorcyclist and fled the scene, according to media reports. The court found the driver guilty of negligence, inattention and failing to keep a safe distance between vehicles. His careless driving injured a motorcyclist, who is an Emirati citizen, and caused considerable damage to the motorcycle.

The driver is accused of fleeing the scene without stopping even though the motorcyclist was injured in the collision. Police told the court the accused was responsible for the crash because of his reckless driving and failure to keep a proper distance. Prosecutors charged him with negligently causing bodily harm to another person, damaging another person's property, and leaving the scene without reasonable cause.

The court announced the fine in the defendant's presence, and enforcement records show the fine has been paid and the driver released through the traffic and roads prosecution.
