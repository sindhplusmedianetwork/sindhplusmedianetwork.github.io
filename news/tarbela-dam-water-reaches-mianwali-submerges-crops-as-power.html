Tarbela Dam Water Reaches Mianwali, Submerges Crops as Power Shortfall Hits 4,232 MW

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Water released from Tarbela Dam has reached the Indus River at Mianwali, submerging crops in the district's riverine areas and raising water levels at the Jinnah and Chashma barrages. Inflows at both barrages in Mianwali have increased, according to officials. The development follows the rockfall during excavation at the dam's fifth extension project, known as T-5, a few days ago, after which water had to be discharged from the reservoir.

The dam's main structure remains completely safe, but the technical fault and the discharge of water have deepened the power crisis across the country. Tarbela's total generation capacity was 4,888 megawatts, but output has fallen to 656 megawatts, creating a shortfall of 4,232 megawatts. As a result, people in large parts of the country are facing widespread unannounced loadshedding.
