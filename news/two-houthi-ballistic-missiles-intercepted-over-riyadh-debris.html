Two Houthi Ballistic Missiles Intercepted Over Riyadh; Debris Injures Civilian

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's Civil Defense said on Friday that debris from two ballistic missiles launched by the Houthi militia fell over Riyadh after the missiles were intercepted and destroyed on Thursday. One citizen sustained minor injuries when the fragments fell, and a house and a vehicle suffered material damage. Civil Defense teams reached the affected areas in the capital and handled the situation under standard emergency procedures.

Coalition spokesman Turki al-Malki attributed the launches to the Houthi militia, saying air defences had destroyed both missiles before they could reach their targets. The Saudi-led coalition separately announced that a Houthi drone had been intercepted and destroyed over the Makkah region. Civil Defense had earlier issued warnings for Riyadh, the nearby town of Kharj and the southern city of Najran, which were lifted shortly afterwards.

The incident is the latest in an intensifying campaign: a missile strike on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport on October 10 killed at least 13 people and injured more than 300, and an attack on Dammam's airport last week killed two people.
