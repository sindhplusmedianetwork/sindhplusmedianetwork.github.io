New York Bans AI Chatbots From Acting as Companions to Children

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New York Governor Kathy Hochul has signed one of the broadest AI safety laws in the United States, barring AI chatbots from acting as companions to children. Under the law, chatbots such as ChatGPT must verify the ages of their users and strip down features for anyone under 18. The bots are forbidden from pretending to be human, alive, capable of feeling emotions or an authority figure, and may not frame their answers as personal opinions.

The technology industry criticised the law's vague language, while the advocacy group Common Sense Media praised it as the strongest kids' AI safety bill in the country.
