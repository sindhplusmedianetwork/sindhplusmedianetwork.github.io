World Record 51 Red Cards Shown in Brazilian Youth Game After Mass Brawl

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A total of 51 red cards were shown during an under-20 semi-final first leg in Minas Gerais state between Uberlândia and Mamoré, setting a new world record in football. The cards went to 43 players and eight members of the coaching staff. The brawl began in the second half after Mamoré player David gestured towards the opposition bench following a goal.

Uberlândia goalkeeping coach Paulo Sérgio Dias dos Santos then entered the pitch, and the disorder spread. There was also damage to the stadium, police were called, and the match was halted for about 40 minutes before being abandoned. The previous record was 36 red cards in an Argentine match in 2011.

Uberlândia later withdrew from the second leg, handing Mamoré a place in the final as a fair-play gesture.
