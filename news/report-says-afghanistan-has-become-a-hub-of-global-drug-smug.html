Report says Afghanistan has become a hub of global drug-smuggling networks

A video report says Afghanistan has become a hub of global drug-smuggling networks, posing risks to neighbouring countries. The report presents the country as a centre through which international drug-smuggling networks operate. It says the scale of the trade threatens the stability and security of the countries around Afghanistan.

The report frames the drug trade as a growing problem for the wider region, with neighbouring states facing the effects of smuggling across their borders. No further text details were published on the video page beyond the report's central claim.
