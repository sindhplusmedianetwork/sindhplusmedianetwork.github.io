Veiled women face more harassment, says actress Hina Bayat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior Pakistani actress and host Hina Bayat has said women are not safe only outside their homes but also within their homes and families, and that veiled women face more harassment. Speaking on a morning show about harassment in joint and extended family systems, she said girls attending weddings and gatherings face harassment from some male relatives. Bayat said sons must first be taught to respect women, and that respect means genuinely honouring women, not merely treating them as the family's honour.

She added that men need to adopt civilised behaviour to create a better environment in society. The actress also said women should wear dignified and appropriate clothing, which she said adds to their confidence. She described a social experiment in which she sent two girls to a market in different dress: first in ordinary eastern dress, then in dupatta and then in full hijab, and found that the girls in dupatta were touched and those in full hijab were teased.

Bayat said the result surprised her, adding that some men assume a woman in full veil cannot defend herself, while women in dignified ordinary dress are seen as more confident.
