Gohar Demands End to Harassment of PTI Workers, Says 29 Lawmakers Arrested or Missing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan on Friday demanded justice and an end to the harassment of political workers, stating that nearly 29 parliamentarians, including members of the National Assembly and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Assembly, had been arrested or gone missing. Speaking to the media at the Peshawar Press Club, Gohar said the party was not seeking any concessions or special treatment but was only demanding justice, and called for an immediate halt to further arrests and the harassment of political workers, as well as violations of the sanctity of people's homes and private lives. He said every accused person had a fundamental and constitutional right to seek bail, adding that rearresting individuals after a court had granted them bail amounted to a violation of the Constitution, the law and fundamental rights.

Referring to the arrests of PTI leaders Taimur Saleem Jhagra, Irfan Saleem and Kamran Bangash, Gohar said their rearrests under other laws despite being granted bail were unlawful and condemnable, and appealed to the chief justice of Pakistan and chief justices of the relevant high courts to ensure that pending cases were heard, restraining orders were lifted where applicable, and justice was delivered to those arrested. Gohar also criticised the withdrawal of security provided to Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, saying every political leader had a role to play and it was the state's responsibility to ensure their security, and demanded that the authorities inform the families of Bangash, Saleem, Jhagra and others about their whereabouts and well-being, while also calling for the immediate provision of necessary medicines and medical facilities to PTI founder Imran Khan. Responding to a question about constitutional amendments, Gohar said it was not the appropriate time to discuss the matter and the party would hold consultations internally and with its allied parties at an appropriate time before deciding its future course of action in light of a meeting with Imran and his instructions.

On November 17, PTI leaders Jhagra and Bangash were arrested under the Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance after the Peshawar High Court granted them interim pre-arrest bail in the May 9, 2023, Radio Pakistan attack case, while party spokesmen describe the pattern as systematic targeting of elected representatives and grassroots workers in the province and official confirmation of the status of each individual remains limited.
