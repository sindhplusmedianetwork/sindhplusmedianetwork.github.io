Northern Ireland School Offers Prize Draw to Stop Students Skipping Classes for GTA 6

A school in Northern Ireland has introduced a prize offer to stop students from staying absent from school to play the video game Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6). According to the administration of Belfast Boys Model School, students who came to school on Thursday, that is on the game's release day, were included in a draw through which two lucky students were given Xbox or PlayStation store gift cards worth 91.76 dollars each. The school administration had earlier also sent a letter to parents, in which it was stated that the game's release date matches the mathematics GCSE examinations of eleventh and twelfth grade students.

In the letter the administration wrote that activities of playing games like GTA can be postponed until the weekend. The school's principal Mary Montgomery, speaking to the BBC, said the basic purpose of sending the letter was to encourage students to come to school and be ready to show their best performance.
