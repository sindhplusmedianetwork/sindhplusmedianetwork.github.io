Karachi to Host International Conference on AI for Climate Action and Ecosystems

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An international conference titled Smart Solutions for a Green Future: AI in Ecosystem and Climate Action 2026 will be held in Karachi from October 27 to 29, 2026, focusing on the application of Artificial Intelligence in environmental protection, climate change mitigation, biodiversity conservation and sustainable development. According to a press release issued by the Federal Urdu University spokesperson, the conference aims to bring together artificial intelligence, environmental science and emerging technologies to explore effective and practical solutions to climate change and the challenges facing ecosystems and natural resources. The event is being organised through collaboration among the Federal Urdu University of Arts, Sciences and Technology, the Pakistan Academy of Sciences and the International Center for Chemical and Biological Sciences at the University of Karachi, along with other national and international scientific institutions.

The initiative also involves the Alliance of International Science Organizations for the Belt and Road Regions and the China-Pakistan Joint Research Center on Earth Sciences and other research organisations. The three-day gathering will bring scientific institutions together to examine AI's role in climate mitigation, biodiversity conservation and sustainable development, with the discussions intended to support practical action for a greener future.
