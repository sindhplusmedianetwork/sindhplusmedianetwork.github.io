Pakistan Launches Nationwide Schools Hockey League to Find Young Talent

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has launched a nationwide Schools Hockey League to discover young talent and rebuild the country's standing in international hockey. The league was inaugurated at the Naseer Bunda Hockey Stadium in Islamabad. An exhibition match marked the opening, which Islamabad Model College for Girls, Tarlai, won 2-0.

Pakistan Hockey Federation president Mohyuddin Wani said the league will run simultaneously in Islamabad, all four provinces, Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan. It will be held for both boys and girls. He said 100 boys and 100 girls will be selected for specialised training camps.

The league is aimed at giving young players a clear path from school hockey to the national game.
