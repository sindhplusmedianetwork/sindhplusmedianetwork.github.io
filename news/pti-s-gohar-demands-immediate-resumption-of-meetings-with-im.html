PTI's Gohar Demands Immediate Resumption of Meetings With Imran Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan has called for the immediate resumption of meetings with party founder Imran Khan, saying access restrictions have continued for 375 days. Speaking outside Parliament House in Islamabad on Tuesday, which he noted was a designated meeting day, Gohar said keeping Imran jailed without family meetings amounts to inhuman treatment. He said political tensions are rising and accused political opponents of taking undue advantage of the emergency and governor's rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa.

Gohar added that political problems should be solved through meaningful talks with the government rather than through restrictions on meetings.
