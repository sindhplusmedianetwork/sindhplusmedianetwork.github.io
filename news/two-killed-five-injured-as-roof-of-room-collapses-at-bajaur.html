Two Killed, Five Injured as Roof of Room Collapses at Bajaur Tablighi Centre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people were killed and five were injured when the roof of a room collapsed at a Tablighi centre in Bajaur. According to Rescue 1122, seven people were trapped under debris when the roof of a mud room collapsed at the Tablighi centre in Nawi Dand and Alizo Filling area of Khar tehsil. Rescue 1122 Bajaur teams pulled out the people trapped under the debris, provided immediate first aid and shifted them to District Headquarters Hospital Khar.

Two people died in the tragic incident, including Dost Ali, a resident of Khar. The injured include Muhammad son of Mashi Khan of Dag Alizo, Mansoor son of Muhammad of Alizo, Younas son of Badar Munir of Shamozai, Noor Ali Jan son of Bakht Yar of Khar, Rehanullah of Alizo and Marwan Khan of Alizo.
