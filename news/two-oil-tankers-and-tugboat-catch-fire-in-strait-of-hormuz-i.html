Two Oil Tankers and Tugboat Catch Fire in Strait of Hormuz, IRGC Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps says two oil tankers and a tugboat caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz. The vessels caught fire after attempting to pass through an unauthorised route, the IRGC said, according to Al Jazeera. The incident was reported on Wednesday morning, November 5, in the latest shipping disruption in the strategic waterway.

The IRGC also said its electronic warfare units had captured two US LUCAS drones intact. The Strait of Hormuz has seen a series of attacks on tankers in recent weeks, with several vessels hit by mines. The waterway is a vital route for global oil shipments, and the repeated incidents have raised concerns about the safety of shipping in the region.
