WAPDA Dominate National Athletics Championships in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WAPDA continued their dominance at the National Athletics Championships at NED University in Karachi on Friday. Yasir Sultan claimed javelin gold with a throw of 74.63 metres in the absence of Olympic champion Arshad Nadeem, who skipped the event. Fatima Hussain won the women's javelin title for the department.

WAPDA also secured gold in the men's pole vault, the men's 400 metres through Salman Ahmed, the men's 110m hurdles, the women's 10,000 metres and the men's shot put. Army did not participate in this year's championships. The commanding medal haul underlined WAPDA's depth across track and field disciplines.
