Free visa on arrival restored for Pakistani citizens in Qatar — but what are the conditions?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Qatar has restored the visa-on-arrival facility for Pakistani citizens, allowing eligible travellers to obtain a free tourist visa for up to 30 days on arrival. The facility had been suspended in March 2026, when the Pakistani embassy in Doha advised travellers to obtain a visa before travelling. Under the conditions published on the website of Qatar's interior ministry department for passports and border affairs, travellers need a passport valid for at least three months, a confirmed return ticket, a hotel booking through the Discover Qatar platform and health insurance from an approved institution.

Pakistan's foreign ministry says travellers must obtain health insurance for at least one month, costing 50 Qatari riyals, and should prefer the Qatar Insurance Company and QLIM. After the insurance is completed, travellers receive an email with the receipt and insurance certificate, which they must carry during travel. On arrival at Hamad International Airport, passengers must visit the Discover Qatar desk before the immigration counters, where their documents will be stamped after verification.

The visa process, eye scanning and biometric steps are then completed at the immigration counters.
