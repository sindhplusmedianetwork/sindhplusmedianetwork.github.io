Oil prices rise as Brent reaches $116.08 a barrel

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Oil prices rose on Friday, heading for a fourth consecutive monthly gain, according to Reuters. Brent crude futures for December delivery advanced 1.20 dollars, or 1.04 percent, to 116.08 dollars a barrel as of 0323 GMT. The more active January contract gained 1.39 dollars to 112.19 dollars.

US West Texas Intermediate crude futures climbed 1.37 dollars, or 1.34 percent, to 103.37 dollars. The rise came amid ongoing Middle East tensions and their impact on supply expectations. Markets are watching geopolitical developments closely for further direction.
