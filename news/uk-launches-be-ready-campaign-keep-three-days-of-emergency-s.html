UK Launches 'Be Ready' Campaign: Keep Three Days of Emergency Supplies at Home

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The British government has advised the public to keep three days of emergency supplies at home under its newly launched 'Be Ready' campaign. The advice covers three days of bottled water and non-perishable food for every household. Officials said the message was reinforced by this week's PHP software glitch and a record-setting summer of heat, drought and wildfires.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham said Britain had been 'on the back foot' in responding to emergencies and needed to become more proactive. The government also announced a new wildfire response plan, including the deployment of military aircraft. Defence Secretary Wes Streeting warned that cyber incidents like the PHP outage would become more common.

Britain's guidance mirrors similar advice issued by the European Commission to EU citizens.
