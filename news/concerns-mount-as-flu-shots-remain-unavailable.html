Concerns mount as flu shots remain 'unavailable'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Concerns are mounting in Pakistan as flu shots remain unavailable ahead of winter, leaving many people scrambling for vaccines. For many, the onset of winter brings sniffles and blocked noses, and many opt for flu shots to strengthen their immunity for the cold weather. Naeem Ahmed, whose aged mother has received a flu shot every October for the past several years, said it seemed his mother would become vulnerable to influenza this year because the vaccine was unavailable.

According to a pharmacist, Anflu and TetrAnflu from Sinovac, French Vaxigrip from Sanofi, and Influvac from Abbott were the three primary choices for influenza vaccines in the local market. The pharmacist said none of them were available this year, adding that he used to sell at least 1,500 shots in October but could not arrange a single one this year.
