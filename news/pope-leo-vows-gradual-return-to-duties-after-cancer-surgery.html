Pope Leo Vows Gradual Return to Duties After Cancer Surgery

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pope Leo pledged on Sunday to slowly resume his usual activities as leader of the world's 1.4 billion Catholics, greeting crowds in St Peter's Square in his first public event since surgery for lung cancer five days ago. The 71-year-old pontiff had an operation on Tuesday to remove the lower lobe of his right lung, and has been diagnosed with lung adenocarcinoma, the most common form of lung cancer. "Having overcome this delicate phase...

I will now gradually resume my activities, always with the help of God and your prayers," Leo said, speaking in a firm, clear voice from the Apostolic Palace window; he walked unaided to the window. He was discharged from Rome's Gemelli hospital on Saturday after blood tests and chest imaging that day showed him well enough, and doctors said he could perform normal daily activities independently. About 15,000 people gathered in St Peter's Square, according to the Vatican; one nun knelt and cried, holding her hand over her face, while other nuns held a sign reading "Best wishes, Holy Father.

We love you, and we are praying for you." He urged pilgrims to set aside selfish ambitions and serve God "without compromise." The Vatican cancelled his other public events until at least Wednesday, when he is due to hold a weekly general audience. The pope plans to visit Uruguay, Argentina and Peru from November 6 to 17; lead surgeon Dr Stefano Margaritora said he could be fully recovered in time, while Cardinal Pietro Parolin expressed scepticism he could keep the pace.
