Faisalabad-Lahore rail link to reopen on Nov 15 after flood repairs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Railways will restore the rail link between Faisalabad and Lahore from November 15, 2026, after rebuilding track and bridge infrastructure damaged by floods at an estimated cost of Rs455 million. The link had been suspended since August 2026, after the Massan Kalar Bridge near Sheikhupura collapsed in the floods and cut the route. Repair work covered the damaged track and the bridge structure, allowing trains to run safely between the two cities again.

Faisalabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry president Arif Ehsan Malik welcomed the restoration, saying the link matters for trade and for passengers travelling between the two industrial cities. He urged the authorities to add faster trains on the route and to double the track so more trains can run. The restoration is one of several repair projects on the railway network after this year's floods damaged lines and bridges in Punjab.
