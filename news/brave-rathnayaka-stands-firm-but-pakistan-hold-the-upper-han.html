Brave Rathnayaka stands firm, but Pakistan hold the upper hand in Pindi Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan held the upper hand on day three of the second Test against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi. Sri Lanka reached 309-9 at stumps in their second innings. The visitors held an 82-run lead at the close of play.

They had earlier conceded a 227-run lead to Pakistan. Milan Rathnayaka remained unbeaten on 60 for Sri Lanka. His innings helped Sri Lanka avoid an innings defeat.
