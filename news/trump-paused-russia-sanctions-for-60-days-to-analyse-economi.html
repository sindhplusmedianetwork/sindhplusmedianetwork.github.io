Trump Paused Russia Sanctions for 60 Days to 'Analyse' Economic Impact, Rubio Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Saturday that President Donald Trump's waiver of sweeping sanctions and tariffs on Russia would be a 60-day pause to 'analyse' their impact on the global and American economies. Rubio said the president wanted to assess what imposing both sanctions and tariffs would mean for the world and the US economy, noting that the measures involved a number of countries, not just Russia. Trump said late on Saturday that the decision would benefit the US and the world, though he appeared uncertain about its final shape.

The waiver, signed through an executive order on Friday, set aside measures adopted by Congress last month that targeted Russian officials, oligarchs and financial institutions and authorised sanctions on major buyers of Russian oil and gas. The law, which Trump himself signed in September, was aimed at cutting off funding to supporters of President Vladimir Putin and his war on Ukraine, but Trump exempted Russia two days before it was due to take effect. The move drew sharp criticism from both parties: Senator Thom Tillis called it 'beyond irresponsible', while Senator Ted Cruz termed it 'a mistake' on Fox News.

Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky said he feared the US would lift more sanctions and that Trump's action 'rewards' Putin. Rubio said various conflicts were impacting global energy prices, including the US war on Iran and attacks on Saudi Arabia by Yemen's Houthis, and that diesel price hikes were beginning to filter into the US economy ahead of next month's midterm elections.
