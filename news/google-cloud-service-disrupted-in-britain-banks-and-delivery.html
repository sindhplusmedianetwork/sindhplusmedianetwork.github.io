Google Cloud Service Disrupted in Britain, Banks and Delivery Firms Hit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Google Cloud suffered a technical fault in Britain on Tuesday, knocking out the online systems of multiple institutions, Jang reported citing British media. Banks and delivery companies were among those affected; users struggled to transfer money or make online payments, and even withdrawing cash from ATMs became difficult. The outage disrupted digital services relied upon by businesses and consumers across the country.

The company's technical teams are working on restoring the services, and reports suggest online services in several other European countries risk being affected as well. Google has not yet issued a full statement on the cause of the fault or the timeline for complete restoration.
