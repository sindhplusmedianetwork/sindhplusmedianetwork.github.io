US Bars Cloudera From Green Card Sponsorship, Reaches $2 Million Settlement

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US authorities have barred California technology company Cloudera Inc. from sponsoring employment-based green cards for two years. The action accompanies a $2 million settlement over allegations that the firm discriminated against qualified American workers during recruitment.

The settlement was announced jointly by the US Department of Justice and the Department of Labour. It resolves a lawsuit filed in April accusing Cloudera of violating the Immigration and Nationality Act while recruiting for seven positions under the PERM programme. Under the agreement, Cloudera will establish a $1.4 million fund to compensate qualified US workers who were denied fair consideration, and will pay $600,000 in civil penalties.
