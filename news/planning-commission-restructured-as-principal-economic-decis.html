Planning Commission restructured as principal economic decision-making forum

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has restructured the Planning Commission for the third time in 20 years. It is now the "principal economic decision-making forum", with a new top-heavy policy wing chaired by the prime minister. The cabinet-approved resolution divides the commission into a 22-member policy wing and a 16-member development and analysis wing.

Both wings are headed by the prime minister. Provinces were again excluded from the forum. Their exclusion comes despite calls for representation after the 18th Amendment.
