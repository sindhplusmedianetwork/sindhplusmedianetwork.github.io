Shehzad Poonawalla Wins Rise and Fall Season 2, Donates Rs 35 Lakh to Flood Relief

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former BJP spokesperson Shehzad Poonawalla has won the second season of reality show Rise and Fall, taking home the trophy and a cash prize of Rs 35 lakh. Actor Priyanka Chahar Choudhary finished as the first runner-up, while Bigg Boss 8 winner Gautam Gulati secured the third position. The second season of the show was hosted by former India cricketer Virender Sehwag, and the grand finale took place on November 6.

Poonawalla announced that he will donate his entire prize money of Rs 35 lakh towards flood relief work in Bihar and Assam. After the win was announced, Tej Pratap Yadav questioned the result and claimed Gautam Gulati deserved to win, an allegation Poonawalla dismissed. With Shehzad's win, Rise and Fall has its second champion after Arjun Bijlani won the first season.
