Trump warns Iran over 'plot' to target US officials

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Donald Trump said on Tuesday that US authorities take very seriously any possible threat to Washington, following reports Iran was planning to target officials, possibly with a radioactive bomb. Asked about the potential threat during a visit to the Philippines, Trump warned that if there is such a threat, Iran will pay a price the likes of which no other country has ever paid. Trump was speaking to reporters during his visit to the Philippines when he was asked about the reports.

The report also notes US strikes on Qeshm island. It further notes Iran targeting a Bahrain radar system.
