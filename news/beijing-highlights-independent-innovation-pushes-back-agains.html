Beijing highlights 'independent innovation,' pushes back against 'Chinese spy narrative' in Nvidia case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

China has rejected what it called the United States' 'Chinese spy narrative', with Foreign Ministry spokeswoman Mao Ning saying China's scientific progress relies on independent innovation and open cooperation rather than espionage. Her remarks came as Nvidia's board was reported to be weighing the future of CEO Jensen Huang over reported extramarital affairs, including one involving a woman suspected of links to Chinese intelligence. Mao also accused Washington of overstretching national security concerns to justify restrictions on advanced semiconductor exports to China.

She said China's advances came from its own research and cooperation with others. The Nvidia case has drawn growing attention in Washington as scrutiny of the chipmaker intensifies.
