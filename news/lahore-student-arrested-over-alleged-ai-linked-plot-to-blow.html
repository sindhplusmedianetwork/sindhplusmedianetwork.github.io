Lahore Student Arrested Over Alleged AI-Linked Plot to Blow Up Educational Institution

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 17-year-old student has been arrested in Lahore in connection with an alleged plan to blow up the building of an educational institution. The arrest was made by Pakistan's National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency. The agency said the youth, a first-year student, is accused of using an AI tool in the planning.

According to the agency, it received an alert about the prompt linked to the alleged plan. The agency said it then traced the youth. He was subsequently taken into custody.
