Rubio rejects Indian objections to US-Pakistan ties during India visit

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio has rejected Indian objections to America's relationship with Pakistan, speaking during his visit to India. In an interview with India's Network18, Rubio said the United States needs to maintain ties with Pakistan from a geographic standpoint. He pointed to Pakistan's nuclear weapons and its geographic importance as reasons for engagement, noting that India itself engages Iran.

Rubio suggested that the US-Pakistan relationship could help address some of India's own security concerns. His remarks underline Washington's balancing act between its strategic partnership with New Delhi and its working relationship with Islamabad.
