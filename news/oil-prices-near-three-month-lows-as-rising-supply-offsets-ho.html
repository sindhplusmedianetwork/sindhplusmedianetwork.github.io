Oil Prices Near Three-Month Lows as Rising Supply Offsets Hormuz Threat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Oil prices extended their falls into a second day to trade near three-month lows on signs of rising crude supply and a potential market surplus, though risks to shipping through the Strait of Hormuz persisted. Brent crude futures fell 66 cents, or 0.7%, to $88.71 a barrel, after posting their lowest close since August 31. US West Texas Intermediate crude slipped 60 cents to $79.57 a barrel.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said three 'violating' oil tankers had caught fire after hitting naval mines in the strait. The US is investigating whether Iran was involved in cyberattacks on as many as 50 energy tankers.
