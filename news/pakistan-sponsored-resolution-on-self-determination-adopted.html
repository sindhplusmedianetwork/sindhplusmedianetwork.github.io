Pakistan-sponsored resolution on self-determination adopted by consensus at UN

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The UN General Assembly's Third Committee adopted by consensus a Pakistan-sponsored resolution reaffirming the right to self-determination of peoples living under colonialism, foreign domination and alien occupation, with 62 countries co-sponsoring the initiative. The resolution, titled Universal Realisation of the Right of Peoples to Self-Determination, was introduced by Pakistan's Permanent Representative to the United Nations, Ambassador Syed Haider Shah. Pakistan has been sponsoring the resolution since 1981.

Ambassador Shah said the promise of self-determination remains unfulfilled for many peoples still living under foreign occupation, drawing attention to the continuing denial of this right to the people of Palestine and Indian-occupied Jammu and Kashmir. He said that in several situations the legitimate aspirations of occupied peoples continued to be met with military oppression, excessive use of force, extrajudicial killings, arbitrary detentions and enforced disappearances. He said such practices constituted grave violations of international law and undermined the credibility of the international legal order.
