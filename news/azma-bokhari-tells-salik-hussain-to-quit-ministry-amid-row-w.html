Azma Bokhari tells Salik Hussain to quit ministry amid row with Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The war of words between leaders of the PML-N and the PML-Q took another turn on Wednesday, when Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari called on Federal Minister for Overseas Pakistanis Chaudhry Salik Hussain to relinquish his portfolio rather than criticise Maryam Nawaz's provincial government while sitting in the PML-N-led federal government. Bokhari accused Salik of hypocrisy, asking how he could allege corruption in the Punjab government while at the same time seeking development funds from it. She said a week had passed without any reply from Salik to the defamation notice issued by the chief minister, and challenged him to resign and sit on the opposition benches.

Salik, in an interview on Geo News, said he had tried to mend fences through a colleague in the federal cabinet and had explained the context of his remarks to Federal Information Minister Atta Tarar, but Bokhari's outburst had spoiled the matter. He said neither Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif nor any other senior PML-N leader had contacted the PML-Q about the dispute. The row began when Salik alleged corruption against the Punjab chief executive and discrimination in the award of development funds, saying Gujrat had been deprived of the development it saw during the tenure of Parvez Elahi.

Salik said he had not directly levelled corruption allegations against the chief minister and vowed to contest her Rs5 billion defamation suit, saying he would not retract his statement.
