Two Killed as Crush Plant Wall Collapses in Upper Dir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people were killed and one injured when the boundary wall of a crush plant collapsed in the Akhgram area of Upper Dir. Rescue 1122 said labourers loading crushed stone were buried under the rubble along with their vehicle. Rescue workers and local residents pulled the bodies and the injured man from the debris and shifted them to Wari Hospital.

The injured labourer is receiving treatment, and the authorities are looking into the incident.
