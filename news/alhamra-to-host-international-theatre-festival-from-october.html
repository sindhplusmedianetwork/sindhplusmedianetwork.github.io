Alhamra to Host International Theatre Festival From October 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore Arts Council Alhamra is set to host the Third Punjab MAAS International Theatre Festival 2026 from October 23 to 28. The six-day festival will bring together theatre artists from Pakistan, Kazakhstan, Indonesia, Norway and France to showcase diverse theatrical traditions and promote cultural exchange. The programme includes local and international performances, creative workshops and panel discussions exploring artistic freedom, digital culture, social transformation and the role of theatre in engaging younger generations.

The international programme begins on October 24 with the University of the Punjab's production of 'Ak Boota Ambi Da', followed by 'Gangster Hunting with Daisy Detective' by Norway's ERLER Teater Kompani. On October 25, a French theatre group will present 'Zig Zag'. Kazakhstan's Ilyas Omarov Kostanay Regional Kazakh Drama Theatre will present 'Sholpan's Sin' on October 26, while Indonesia's Sanggar Obrog Selagiling Seni will close the festival on October 28 with 'Kintir'.

The organisers say artists are coming together with one shared belief: 'theatre has no borders'.
