Toxic Air Pollution in Delhi Surges to 30 Times the WHO Limit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Toxic air pollution in India's capital New Delhi surged on Wednesday to more than 30 times the World Health Organisation's recommended daily limit, air monitor data showed. PM2.5 levels hit over 461 micrograms per cubic metre in parts of the city, according to monitoring firm IQAir. The tiny particles are among the most harmful pollutants because they can penetrate deep into the lungs.

Pollution is expected to worsen ahead of Sunday's Diwali, when firework displays add to the already poisonous air. The WHO daily limit for PM2.5 is far below the levels recorded in the city on Wednesday.
