US Envoy Warns Strike on Iran Could Come as Early as This Weekend

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States could strike Iran as early as this weekend, US Ambassador to Turkiye Tom Barrack has said. Barrack made the remarks in an interview with podcast host Mario Nawfal, as threats from Washington and Israel against Tehran continued to mount. President Donald Trump separately repeated his claim over the Strait of Hormuz, saying it is 'totally ours and that is the way we will keep it'.

The comments came as global oil markets reacted nervously to the rising tensions in the Middle East. Brent crude prices moved near six-month highs on fears of possible disruption to energy supplies. The developments were reported in a live blog updated late on Friday night.

No official confirmation of any planned military action has been issued beyond the envoy's remarks.
