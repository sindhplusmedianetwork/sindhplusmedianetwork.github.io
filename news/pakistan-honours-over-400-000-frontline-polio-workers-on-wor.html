Pakistan Honours Over 400,000 Frontline Polio Workers on World Polio Day

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Polio Eradication Initiative marked World Polio Day 2026 by honouring frontline workers for their dedication and sacrifices in protecting children against the crippling disease. The event was held under the theme 'Shukriya Polio Frontline Workers - Celebrating the Champions of a Polio-Free Pakistan'. It was attended by Minister of State for National Health Services Dr Malik Mukhtar Ahmad Bharath and the Prime Minister's Focal Person for Polio Eradication, Ayesha Raza Farooq.

Dr Bharath paid tribute to Pakistan's more than 400,000 polio frontline workers and reaffirmed the government's commitment to polio eradication through sustained vaccination campaigns, effective disease surveillance and collective action. Ayesha Raza Farooq highlighted the indispensable role of the frontline workers, over 60 per cent of whom are women, in advancing the country's polio eradication efforts. The event underscored the importance of reaching every missed child and strengthening collective efforts to protect every child and achieve a polio-free Pakistan.
