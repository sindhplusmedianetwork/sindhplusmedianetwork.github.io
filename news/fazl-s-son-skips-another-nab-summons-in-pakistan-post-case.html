Fazl's Son Skips Another NAB Summons in Pakistan Post Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Asad Mehmood, son of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, skipped a second National Accountability Bureau summons on Friday and did not appear before its investigation team. The case concerns alleged irregularities in appointments during his 2022-23 tenure as federal minister. Sources in the bureau said he had already ignored the first notice issued on November 10.

A third notice will follow, after which the bureau could take stern action including arrest. The case concerns alleged irregularities in 4,252 appointments at Pakistan Post. The investigation team is pursuing the matter, and the next notice will set out the further steps the bureau may take.
