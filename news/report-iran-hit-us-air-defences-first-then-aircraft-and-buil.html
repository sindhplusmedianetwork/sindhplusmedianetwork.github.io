Report: Iran hit US air defences first, then aircraft and buildings on Middle East bases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian strikes on more than two dozen US bases across the Middle East targeted early-warning systems before hitting aircraft and buildings, causing billions of dollars in equipment damage, according to an analysis by The Associated Press. The AP said it had independently verified damage to US facilities using satellite imagery and by speaking to four regional officials briefed on what was hit. It found that more than 115 drone and missile strikes in August, claimed by Iran or pro-Iran militias, damaged or destroyed structures on 26 military bases and other sites holding American equipment.

The analysis found Iran initially targeted aircraft hangars and air defence systems that detect incoming strikes, clearing the way for further attacks on warehouses, troop housing, hangars and other facilities. Two regional officials said the US has moved many of its assets out of the Persian Gulf, including aircraft, air defence systems, communications systems and other equipment. Iran's armed forces chief of staff, Major General Ali Abdollahi, said Tehran is 'continuously updating our target database', warning that any confirmed hostile act would be met with an 'immediate, effective' response 'without geographical limitations'.
