Pakistan, EU Agree to Deepen Counterterrorism Cooperation at Multilateral Forums

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and the European Union on Thursday agreed to deepen counterterrorism cooperation at multilateral forums, including the United Nations. The understanding came during the 10th round of the Pakistan-EU Bilateral Counterterrorism Dialogue, held in Islamabad. Pakistan's delegation was led by Director General for Counterterrorism Dr Fareha Bugti, while the EU side was headed by Special Envoy Anna Strazska.

Both sides reaffirmed their condemnation of terrorism in all its forms and voiced concern over the security implications of the situation in Afghanistan. The two sides also discussed tackling violent extremism, terrorist financing and the misuse of cyberspace for radicalisation. The European Union condemned the recent terrorist attacks in Pakistan and expressed solidarity with the affected civilians.

The next round of the dialogue will be held in Brussels next year.
