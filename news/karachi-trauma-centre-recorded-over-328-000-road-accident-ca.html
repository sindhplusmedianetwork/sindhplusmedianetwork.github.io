Karachi Trauma Centre Recorded Over 328,000 Road Accident Cases in a Decade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 328,000 road traffic injury cases and 5,045 brought-dead cases were reported at Karachi's Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Institute of Trauma over the past decade, experts revealed. The figures were shared at an event marking Trauma Awareness Week 2026 and World Trauma Day. The institute's emergency department recorded a total of 1,448,624 patient visits in ten years, including 9,634 firearm injury cases.

Speakers at the event stressed that preventing accidents, promoting road safety and ensuring a prompt emergency response are essential to saving lives. They called for stronger measures to reduce the heavy toll of road crashes in Karachi. The hospital remains one of the city's busiest trauma care facilities.
