Seven injured in Kallar Syedan jeep crash; RPO orders probe into triple murder

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Seven people were injured after a jeep plunged into a ravine near Dubairan village in Kallar Syedan, and Rescue 1122 shifted them to the local THQ hospital. The injured were given first aid before being moved for treatment. In a separate development, Rawalpindi's regional police chief ordered a thorough investigation into a triple murder in Fateh Jang, directing forensic evidence collection and the immediate arrest of those involved.

Police said the investigation would be completed on merit and without delay. The two incidents were reported on the same day in the Rawalpindi region.
