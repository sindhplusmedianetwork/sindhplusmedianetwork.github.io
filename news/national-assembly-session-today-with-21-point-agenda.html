National Assembly Session Today With 21-Point Agenda

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read

The National Assembly will meet on Friday, October 23 at 11 am at Parliament House after a one-day break. The house's 21-point agenda has been issued for the sitting. It includes legislative business and a motion of thanks on the president's address to the joint sitting of parliament.

The session comes amid a tense political environment in the country. Opposition members are expected to raise issues of public concern during the proceedings. The sitting will be chaired by the speaker.
