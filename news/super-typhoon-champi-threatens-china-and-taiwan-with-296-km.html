Super Typhoon Champi Threatens China and Taiwan With 296 km/h Winds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Super Typhoon Champi, recorded as the strongest tropical cyclone on Earth in 2026, is tracking northwest across the Pacific with winds peaking at 296 km/h. The storm poses a severe Category 5 threat to Taiwan and eastern China. Taiwanese authorities have alerted coastal regions, and official sea and land warnings are expected as the storm approaches.

Forecasters warn of heavy precipitation, flash flooding, landslides and dangerously high waves across northern and eastern Taiwan. The system is then forecast to track along China's eastern seaboard toward Shanghai and Japan.
