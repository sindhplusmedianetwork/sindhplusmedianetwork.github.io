Suthra Punjab: cleaning contracts cancelled in four divisions over poor performance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has decided to cancel the contracts of cleaning companies in four divisions under the Suthra Punjab programme over poor performance and negligence in cleanliness arrangements. According to sources, the contracts will be ended in the affected tehsils of Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad and Multan divisions. The Suthra Punjab Authority will itself take over cleaning operations in those areas.

Delays in paying workers' salaries and poor cleanliness arrangements were cited as the main reasons behind the decision. Concern had also been expressed in a source report about the performance of all four divisions. Sources said the cancelled contracts will not be given to any other company, and the authority will start its own operation in the areas concerned immediately.

The authority had earlier cancelled the contracts of cleaning companies in 29 tehsils on the same grounds.
