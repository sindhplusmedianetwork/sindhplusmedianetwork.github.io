Türkiye to help Pakistan produce foot-and-mouth disease vaccine

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Türkiye will extend technical cooperation on producing foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) vaccine in Pakistan, and a Turkish technical delegation will visit to explore collaboration, Türkiye's ambassador in Islamabad told Federal Minister for National Food Security and Research Rana Tanveer Hussain. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif had decided to seek Turkish support for developing domestic FMD vaccine production capacity, the minister said. A key focus of the meeting was capacity building and technical training.

Pakistan plans to send an initial group of about 30 participants to Türkiye under a proposed training programme for 100 trainees. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has identified potential Turkish institutions, and coordination with the Higher Education Commission is under way to finalise universities, priority disciplines and arrangements. Both sides discussed findings from recent exchanges between livestock experts, including a Pakistani delegation's visit to study Türkiye's sector.

The cooperation on vaccine production and disease control was linked to Pakistan's ambition to establish disease-free zones and widen access to international meat markets, particularly Türkiye. The meeting also reviewed Türkiye's earlier support through TIKA for an olive oil facility in Muzaffarabad, which has reportedly benefited about 5,000 farmers, with Pakistan expressing interest in similar projects in Balochistan, Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, Chakwal and Attock.
