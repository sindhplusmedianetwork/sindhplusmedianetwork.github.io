Amitabh Bachchan's Home, Diljit Dosanjh's Concert Receive Threats After Tiger Shroff Firing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood legend Amitabh Bachchan's residence and Punjabi singer Diljit Dosanjh's concert have received grave threats in an email sent to the Mumbai Mayor's office. The message mentioned an attack on Bachchan's Jalsa bungalow and sabotage during Dosanjh's Mumbai concert scheduled in November. Mumbai police and related agencies immediately searched the named locations.

The initial investigation confirmed no suspicious item or threat at either venue. Security arrangements around Bollywood personalities and major cultural gatherings have been tightened. The threats follow recent incidents including the firing on actor Tiger Shroff, putting Mumbai's entertainment circles on edge.
