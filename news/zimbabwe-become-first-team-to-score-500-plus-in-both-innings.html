Zimbabwe become first team to score 500-plus in both innings of a Test match

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Zimbabwe have set a world record in the first Test against Afghanistan in Sharjah. They have become the first team in Test history to score more than 500 runs in both innings of the same match. Zimbabwe scored 504 in the first innings.

They then declared their second innings at 523 for 5. Craig Ervine remained unbeaten on 206 at the age of 41 years and 82 days. He became the third-oldest double centurion in Test cricket.

Wesley Madhevere made 100 not out. The match ended in a draw.
