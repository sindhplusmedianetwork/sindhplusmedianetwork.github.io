Tenders for 71 Khyber development projects worth Rs8.92 billion cancelled amid kickback claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tenders for 71 development projects worth 8.92 billion rupees in Khyber district have been cancelled after complaints of kickbacks, sources say. According to the sources, the tenders were issued during the tenure of Chief Minister Sohail Afridi, allegedly on a political basis, with advance kickbacks of 5 to 10 percent charged on contracts. Complaints were received that between 500 million and 900 million rupees had been collected in alleged kickbacks, prompting the authorities to cancel the tenders.

Under the Annual Development Programme, projects worth five billion rupees in the district were attributed to Naveed, a close aide of the chief minister, while projects worth 3.9 billion rupees were attributed to MPA Abdul Ghani, the sources said. Authorities have begun an investigation to identify the alleged corruption and kickback network. An SDO from Bara, Ziaullah, has been detained for questioning and has provided what officials described as important information about the distribution of tenders.
