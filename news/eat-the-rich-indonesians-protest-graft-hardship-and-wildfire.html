'Eat the rich': Indonesians protest graft, hardship and wildfire haze

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hundreds of Indonesians gathered in central Jakarta on October 28 to protest against economic hardship, graft and the government's handling of devastating wildfires that have produced a toxic haze sickening thousands. Many demonstrators gave the three-finger salute from the Hunger Games franchise and carried posters reading 'Hungry? Eat the Rich!' and 'No Clean Air Under Dirty Government', while one sign aimed at President Prabowo Subianto read 'Hey Wok!

Nobody paid us. We all hate you for free.' Protester Sekar, 37, told AFP her heart was shattered by compatriots battling months of wildfires and haze while also facing a weakening currency and higher prices, and said she had come to protect the students who called the demonstration. Police said about 14,000 officers, as well as soldiers, had been deployed in Jakarta, and there was pushing and shoving between front-row protesters and officers near the central Bundaran HI square.

The organising committee, which calls itself SADAR, meaning 'aware' in Bahasa Indonesia, demanded that the government declare a national emergency to unlock more resources to fight wildfires across the archipelago. Deforestation monitor Nusantara Atlas says the blazes have destroyed more than 1.9 million hectares in the nine months to September. Protester Annette Mau, 47, of the Indonesian Mothers' Alliance, said hundreds of thousands of people were suffering and tens of thousands of babies had health problems because they could not breathe, while Singapore's Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan said the haze was not a natural phenomenon and the fires were caused by human action.

Protests were also reported in other cities, and reports noted that Prabowo's popularity two years into his term had reached record lows amid claims of widespread corruption and growing authoritarianism.
