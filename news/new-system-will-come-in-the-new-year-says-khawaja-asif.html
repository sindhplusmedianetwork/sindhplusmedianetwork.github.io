New System Will Come in the New Year, Says Khawaja Asif

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has said that a new system will come in the new year. He was speaking on Geo News programme 'Naya Pakistan'. Asif said some shape of the new system will emerge in 2026, with about a month and a half still remaining in the year.

He said there could be a window of opportunity for Imran Khan and the PTI, similar to the one the PML-N received at the time of General Bajwa's extension. The defence minister also said consensus is being built on the creation of new provinces, with Asif Ali Zardari, Nawaz Sharif and others involved in the discussion. He added that the establishment never asked him to speak about new provinces.
