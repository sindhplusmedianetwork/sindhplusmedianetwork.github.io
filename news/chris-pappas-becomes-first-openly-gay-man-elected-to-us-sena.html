Chris Pappas Becomes First Openly Gay Man Elected to US Senate

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Democratic candidate Chris Pappas has won the US Senate seat from New Hampshire in the midterm elections, becoming the first openly gay man ever elected to the United States Senate. The 46-year-old defeated Republican former senator John Sununu to win the seat left vacant by the retirement of three-term senator Jeanne Shaheen. Pappas was born on June 4, 1980, in Manchester, New Hampshire, to a Greek immigrant family.

He studied at Harvard University and began his working life in his family's restaurant business before entering politics. Before politics, he managed the family business and was later elected to the state executive council; in 2018 he won a US House of Representatives seat for the first time and has been a member of Congress since 2019. He was also the first openly gay member of Congress from New Hampshire, and lives in Manchester with his partner Wayne Bentley.

His campaign focused on ordinary citizens' economic problems, healthcare facilities, protecting family businesses, and opposing the Trump administration's policies. After his victory, Pappas said the American people were not satisfied with the current direction and wanted change in the country. His win is being described as a historic advance for the political representation of different communities in America.

Notably, openly gay women have previously served in the US Senate, but Pappas will be the first openly gay man to reach the chamber.
