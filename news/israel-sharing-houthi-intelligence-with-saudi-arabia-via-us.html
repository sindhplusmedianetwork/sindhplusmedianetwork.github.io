Israel Sharing Houthi Intelligence With Saudi Arabia via US, Media Claim

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israel has begun sharing intelligence about Houthi fighters in Yemen with Saudi Arabia through the United States, Israeli media reports have claimed. The intelligence is meant to help counter Houthi threats and to keep shipping moving freely through the Bab el-Mandeb strait. According to the reports, Saudi Arabia had sought American help on the matter, and Washington passed the request to Israel, which described the Houthis as a broader regional threat.

Representatives of the US Central Command also took part in the security talks. The reports say the United States has proposed a joint regional system to counter the Houthi threat. The claims have not been officially confirmed by the governments involved.
