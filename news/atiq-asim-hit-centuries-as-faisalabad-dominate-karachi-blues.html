Atiq, Asim hit centuries as Faisalabad dominate Karachi Blues in Quaid-e-Azam Trophy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Atiq-ur-Rehman and Asim Ali Nasir struck centuries to put Faisalabad in a commanding position against Karachi Blues on day two of the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Gold fourth round at Abbottabad. Atiq remained unbeaten on 135 while Asim scored 132 as Faisalabad reached 426 for four in reply to Karachi Blues' 185, taking a lead of 241 runs. In the Silver competition, Peshawar Whites' Sajjad Ibrahim set a national record by hitting 17 sixes in a first-class innings, scoring 139 off 77 deliveries against Larkana.

Lahore Blues beat Bahawalpur by an innings and 62 runs. AJK defeated DMJ by nine wickets in another match.
