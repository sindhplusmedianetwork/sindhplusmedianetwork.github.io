Pirwadhai Bus Stand Revenue Rises to Rs140 Million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Regional Transport Authority Rawalpindi has raised the annual income of Pirwadhai General Bus Stand from 25 million rupees to 140 million rupees by bringing 500 additional buses to the facility. RTA Secretary Asad Shirazi told Dawn that the authority closed three illegal bus stands and directed their operators to shift services to Pirwadhai, where they pay a departure fee of 500 rupees per bus. He said the move ended illegal bus stands, raised the income of a government-run facility, and allowed people to travel to other cities at fixed fares.

Around 850 buses currently operate from the stand to different parts of the country, and the number will rise to 1,350 with the additional buses. The provincial government has asked Nespak to prepare a plan to improve the bus stand, and the additional revenue will be spent on its improvement and operational services. The Rawalpindi Waste Management Company has provided 30 workers for round-the-clock cleanliness at the stand in two shifts, and the aim is to develop it into a model bus stand.

Established in 1976, the stand spans over 210 kanals, handles about 40,000 passengers daily, and has 37 active stands for 65 transport companies.
