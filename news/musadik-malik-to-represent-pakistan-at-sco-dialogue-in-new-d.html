Musadik Malik to Represent Pakistan at SCO Dialogue in New Delhi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik has been nominated to represent Pakistan at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Dialogue in New Delhi from November 20 to 22, the Foreign Office said on Saturday. The gathering will bring representatives from SCO member states together to discuss civilisational dialogue and cultural understanding. The Foreign Office said the event provides a platform for member states to exchange views on shared heritage and mutual understanding.

The statement added that Pakistan remains committed to promoting dialogue among civilisations and people-to-people contacts within the SCO framework. The dialogue is part of the SCO's programme of engagement among its member states.
