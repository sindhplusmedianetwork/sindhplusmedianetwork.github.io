Ronaldo's 2008 Season Voted Greatest Ballon d'Or Campaign Ever

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cristiano Ronaldo has beaten Lionel Messi in fan voting for France Football's inaugural 'BOAT' award, held to mark the 70th anniversary of the Ballon d'Or. Ronaldo's 2007-08 season with Manchester United was voted the greatest Ballon d'Or-winning season ever. Messi's 2012 season, in which he scored a record 91 goals in a calendar year, came second.

Ronaldo's 2017 season with Real Madrid was voted third. The voting system, which charged one euro per vote with unlimited votes allowed, drew questions about fairness, with more than 5,000 people casting multiple votes. In that 2007-08 campaign, Ronaldo scored 42 goals and led United to both the Premier League and Champions League titles.

The award adds another chapter to the legendary rivalry between the two football greats.
