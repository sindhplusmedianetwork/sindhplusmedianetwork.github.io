Never share your photos with anyone, Mohsin Naqvi warns women against online fraud

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi has advised women never to share their photographs with anyone, issuing a strong warning against online fraud. Speaking at a press conference in Lahore, he said fraudsters create fake photos and videos of women to blackmail them, and most cybercrime harassment cases relate to girls. He urged people never to transfer money to anyone making requests online.

Naqvi revealed that 600 people were arrested from call centers in Lahore over the past 10 to 12 days in the crackdown on fraud networks. He said cyber police will be made more active to curb such crimes. The minister's warning comes amid a growing number of complaints about online blackmail and financial fraud targeting women.
