Qatar offers 30-day visa on arrival to Pakistani travellers under conditions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani nationals travelling to Qatar can obtain a 30-day tourist visa on arrival, provided they meet conditions set by Qatar's Ministry of Interior, Doha News reported. Applicants must show at least 5,000 Qatari riyals in cash or credit as proof of funds. They must also hold a confirmed return ticket and a passport valid for six months.

Travellers arriving directly from Pakistan must additionally provide a polio vaccination certificate.
