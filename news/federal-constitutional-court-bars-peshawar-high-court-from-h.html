Federal Constitutional Court Bars Peshawar High Court From Hearing MPO Arrest Cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Constitutional Court has barred the Peshawar High Court from hearing cases on arrests made under the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO) ordinance. A two-member bench headed by Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan heard the matter. The bench suspended the various orders of the high court that had been challenged before it.

Notices were issued to the parties involved in the case. The court then adjourned the case indefinitely. The provincial government had challenged the high court's orders in the constitutional court.

The case will remain pending until the court takes it up again.
