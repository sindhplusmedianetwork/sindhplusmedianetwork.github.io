Netanyahu does not deserve to remain in power for even one more minute, says Eisenkot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's political rival Gadi Eisenkot has sharply criticised him, saying Netanyahu's conduct is a direct threat to Israel's democracy. Eisenkot said Netanyahu should not remain in power for even one more minute. Speaking on Sunday, November 15, he said the behaviour of Netanyahu and his close associates has become a clear and immediate danger to Israeli democracy.

He said no institution or sector has remained safe from corruption, distortion of facts and use for Netanyahu's personal political interests. Eisenkot is among the leaders trying to form a new government in Israel. The Israeli president has tasked him with forming the government after the elections.

His strong criticism comes as efforts continue to put together a new government in the country.
