Sindh launches digital gateway for youth to access banking, education and jobs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has described the Sindh Youth Card as a landmark youth empowerment initiative designed to connect young people with education, employment, financial inclusion, skills development and entrepreneurship opportunities through a single digital platform. The initiative was formally launched by Pakistan Peoples Party Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari at a ceremony held at Chief Minister House on Monday. According to a press statement issued by the CM House, registration began on Monday across the 30 districts of the province, and it will initially benefit 100,000 young people aged 15 to 29, with a plan to expand coverage to one million beneficiaries.

The card offers an integrated package including digital bank accounts and Visa debit cards, scholarships, vocational and digital skills training, discounts at more than 180 partner businesses, transport concessions, job placements, paid internships and entrepreneurship support, including proposed interest-subsidised loans of up to Rs500,000. Speaking at the launch ceremony, the chief minister said the future of Sindh and Pakistan depended on the opportunities created for young people today, adding that the card was much more than a card — it was a platform of opportunity, inclusion and empowerment. He said the programme would bring together government departments, educational institutions, financial organisations and the private sector to establish a comprehensive support system for young people, and stressed that young women, minority communities and persons with disabilities must have equal access to its benefits.

The initiative has been launched through the Sports and Youth Affairs Department in collaboration with Sapphire Consulting Services and JS Bank's Zindigi platform, and Sports and Youth Affairs Minister Sardar Muhammad Bux Khan Mahar described it as a historic day for Sindh's youth, saying: 'One Card, One Youth, One Sindh.'
