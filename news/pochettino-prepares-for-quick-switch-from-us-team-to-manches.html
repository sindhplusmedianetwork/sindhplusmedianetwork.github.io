Pochettino Prepares for Quick Switch From US Team to Manchester United

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US men's national team coach Mauricio Pochettino insisted on Monday that his focus was not divided ahead of his departure for Manchester United, as he prepares to coach the American side for the last time on Tuesday. The Argentine signed a contract to lead the US through to the 2030 World Cup but last week agreed to take the helm at the Premier League club. Pochettino will have little time before his first game with United, joining them on Wednesday with the team due to play arch-rivals Liverpool on Sunday at Anfield, and his decision to stay with the US for their CONCACAF fixture against Haiti has been criticised.

He told reporters ahead of the US match in Cincinnati that the team was well prepared to switch quickly, comparing it to a player moving from the national team to a club side. He said the business on the day was to focus on the game against Haiti, but once that game finished, the focus would turn fully to the next project. Manchester United are bidding to get out of the relegation zone with Pochettino, who improved a US men's team that had long trailed their female counterparts in terms of on-field success.

He led the Americans to the 2025 Gold Cup final, where they lost to Mexico, and steered them to the final 16 of the World Cup this year on home soil, while members of his coaching staff have already gone ahead to Manchester to prepare for his arrival.
