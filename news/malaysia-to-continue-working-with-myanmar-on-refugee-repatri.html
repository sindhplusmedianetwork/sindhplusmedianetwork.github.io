Malaysia to Continue Working With Myanmar on Refugee Repatriation, PM Says After Genocide Ruling

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Malaysia will continue engaging with Myanmar's military-backed government on repatriating refugees, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said. He spoke after a ruling by the UN's top court last week that a 2017 crackdown by Myanmar security forces on the Rohingya ethnic minority amounted to genocide. Anwar said his engagements with Myanmar's former junta chief-turned-President Min Aung Hlaing were part of efforts to safeguard Malaysia's interests.

He said the engagements were in line with the ICJ ruling, which was 'to guarantee a safe and dignified return' for those fleeing persecution. Malaysia has sent back over 10,000 Myanmar nationals since January. It plans to send back another 1,500 on three navy vessels on December 6 to 7.
