IT earnings push services exports up 22.6pc to $2.72 billion in first quarter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Services exports recorded robust year-on-year growth of 22.60 per cent in the first quarter of 2026-27, largely driven by higher earnings from information technology. The sector has maintained uninterrupted growth since the start of FY27, according to official data compiled by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics. In absolute terms, services exports reached $2.72 billion in July-September 2026-27, up from $2.22bn over the corresponding period last year.

In September, services exports grew 15.35pc to $929.87 million from $806.14m in the same month last year. The increase was led by telecommunication, computer and information services, which have remained the main contributors since the beginning of the fiscal year. According to data compiled by the State Bank of Pakistan, exports of telecommunications, computer and information services reached $1.246bn in 1QFY27 against $1.059bn over the corresponding months of last year, indicating a growth of 17.7pc.

Exports of other business services grew 27.98pc to $622m in 3MFY27, up from $486m in the corresponding months last year, while exports of transport services increased by 9.66pc to $295m. At the same time, services imports surged 16.32pc to $3.55bn in 1QFY27 from $3.05bn in the same period last year. The services trade deficit declined by 0.33pc to $832.16m in 1QFY27, compared to $834.88m in the corresponding months last year, although the deficit widened by 84pc to $282.76m in September on a year-on-year basis.

Pakistan's services exports rose by 18.81pc to $10.04bn in FY26, up from $8.45bn in the previous fiscal year.
