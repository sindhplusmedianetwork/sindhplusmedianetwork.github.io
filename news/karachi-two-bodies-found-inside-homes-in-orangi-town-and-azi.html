Karachi: Two Bodies Found Inside Homes in Orangi Town and Azizabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two decomposed bodies were discovered inside homes in two areas of Karachi, rescue officials confirmed. In Orangi Town's Sector 11, Rahim Shah Colony, the body of a man was recovered from a house situated next to a mosque. Rescue officials said the body was three to four days old, adding that the man had been living alone in the house.

Separately, another body was recovered from inside a house in Azizabad. Rescue officials said both bodies were shifted for legal formalities. The causes of death have not yet been determined.

Police said further details would come to light after post-mortem examinations.
