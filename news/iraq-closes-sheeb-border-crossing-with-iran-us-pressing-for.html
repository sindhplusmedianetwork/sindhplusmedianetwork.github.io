Iraq Closes Sheeb Border Crossing With Iran; US Pressing for More Closures

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iraq has closed the al-Sheeb border crossing with Iran to passengers, vehicles and commercial trucks until further notice, two sources at the crossing told Reuters. Iran's judiciary news website Mizan Online confirmed the closure, saying it came following a decision by the Iraqi prime minister's office. Another source said the decision had been taken to reconsider the security arrangements inside the crossing.

Separately, the United States is pressuring Iraq to close more border crossings to tighten pressure on Tehran, AFP reported citing two anonymous sources, after Baghdad had already closed the Sheeb crossing to all traffic.
