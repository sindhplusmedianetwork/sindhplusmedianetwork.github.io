Pakistan attaches great importance to its relations with Bangladesh: DPM Dar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar said Pakistan remained committed to taking its bilateral partnership with Bangladesh forward in mutual respect, sovereign goodwill and trust. He was addressing a delegation of 35 Bangladeshi civil servants in Islamabad on November 18. Dar said both countries shared a relationship rooted in history and cultural affinities.

He highlighted the Pakistan-Bangladesh Knowledge Corridor during his address. The deputy prime minister said Pakistan attached great importance to its relations with Bangladesh.
