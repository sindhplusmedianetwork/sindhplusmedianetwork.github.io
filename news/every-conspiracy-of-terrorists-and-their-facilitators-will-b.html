Every Conspiracy of Terrorists and Their Facilitators Will Be Foiled: President

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari has said that every conspiracy of terrorists and their local facilitators will be foiled. In a statement issued by the Presidency's press wing, he paid tribute to the security forces for killing four terrorists of the Indian-sponsored Fitna al-Hindustan in different areas of Balochistan's Kalat and Gwadar districts. The president expressed grief over the martyrdom of Major Wajahat Waheed during an operation against terrorists in the Shadi Kor area of Gwadar district.

He paid homage to the major's great sacrifice for the defence of the homeland and the elimination of terrorism. President Zardari also extended heartfelt condolences and sympathy to the bereaved family of the martyred officer.
