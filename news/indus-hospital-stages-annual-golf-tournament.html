Indus Hospital Stages Annual Golf Tournament

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indus Hospital and Health Network hosted its 12th annual golf tournament in Lahore on Sunday. The tournament attracted 104 golfers and prominent business leaders, philanthropists and other guests. The event at the Royal Palm Golf and Country Club aims to raise awareness about the health risks of smog and air pollution.

The focus is particularly on respiratory and ear, nose and throat conditions, while supporting free healthcare for underserved communities. Atique-ur-Rehman and Muhammad Abid secured first place with a score of 55. The Lahore leg will be followed by tournaments in Islamabad on November 29 and Karachi on December 20.
