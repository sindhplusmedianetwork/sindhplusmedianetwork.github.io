Lower Dir: Woman Shoots Co-Wife Dead Over Domestic Dispute

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman shot and killed her co-wife in the Tourmang Darra area of Lower Dir following domestic disputes, police confirmed. The accused woman was arrested soon after the incident. Khal police completed the post-mortem formalities and handed over the victim's body to her heirs.

A case has been registered under various sections of the law. The accused was produced before a court, which ordered her transfer to District Jail Timergara. Police say further investigation into the incident is under way.
