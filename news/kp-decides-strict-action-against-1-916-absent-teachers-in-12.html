KP Decides Strict Action Against 1,916 Absent Teachers in 12 Districts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has decided to take action against 1,916 absent teachers in 12 designated districts, directing all concerned officers to seek explanations within three days. Important decisions were taken in a meeting chaired by Additional Chief Secretary Designated Districts Yahya Akhunzada, in which all district education officers were directed to seek written explanations from absent teachers within three days. The meeting directed that disciplinary action under the rules be taken against persistently absent teachers who do not give a satisfactory explanation, noting that teachers' absence is affecting children's education and the performance of educational institutions.

According to the official report, the total number of absent teachers in the 12 designated districts is 1,916, including 1,042 women and 874 men. The highest number of absent teachers, 419, is in North Waziristan, followed by Bannu with 348 and South Waziristan Upper with 243. Education Secretary Dr Qasim expressed serious concern over teachers' absence in educational institutions and said absent teachers are playing with children's future.
