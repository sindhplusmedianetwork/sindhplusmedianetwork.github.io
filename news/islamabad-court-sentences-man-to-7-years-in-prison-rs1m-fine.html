Islamabad court sentences man to 7 years in prison, Rs1m fine in student harassment case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Islamabad court on Monday sentenced the main accused in a cybercrime case involving the harassment of a 16-year-old student to seven years of rigorous imprisonment. Additional Sessions Judge Muhammad Afzal Majoka also imposed a fine of Rs1 million on the convict, Raja Haris Naveed, ruling that he would serve an additional six months in prison if the fine is not paid. According to the prosecution, the accused had lured the matric student, locked him in a drawing room and harassed him at gunpoint while recording an indecent video without his consent.

The court acquitted co-accused Shahzeb Meraj, giving him the benefit of the doubt after the charge of abetting the crime could not be proven against him. The verdict was reported by Dunya News as the conclusion of the PECA case heard in the federal capital.
