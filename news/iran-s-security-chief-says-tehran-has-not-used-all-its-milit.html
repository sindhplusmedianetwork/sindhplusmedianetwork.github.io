Iran's security chief says Tehran has not used all its military options yet

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's top security official has declared that Tehran has held back part of its military arsenal during the recent conflict. Major General Mohsen Rezaei, Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, told US-based outlet DropSite News that Iran had not deployed all its capabilities in the previous stages and was prepared for the next one. He said Iran had spent decades preparing for a confrontation with the United States, focusing on dispersed production, underground facilities, different launch systems and the ability to repair damage and replace losses.

Rezaei argued that while a prolonged blockade would hurt Iranian families and damage investment, economic pain and strategic collapse were not the same thing. He said Tehran's goal was to end the war on terms that reduce the likelihood of another war. The security chief also warned that negotiations with Washington had been halted, and that if fighting continued, undersea internet cables, data centres and AI facilities could be among Iran's targets.
