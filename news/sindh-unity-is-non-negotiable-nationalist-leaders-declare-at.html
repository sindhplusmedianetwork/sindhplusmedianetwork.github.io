Sindh unity is non-negotiable, nationalist leaders declare at Karachi rally

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A rally by the Jeay Sindh Rahbar Committee in Karachi on Sunday drew nationalist leaders, activists and lawyers opposed to dividing Sindh or creating new provinces. Speakers at the rally declared that Sindh's unity is non-negotiable. They rejected the proposed referendum on the issue.

The speakers also warned the federal government against imposing any decision on Sindh. The rally was held against proposals to divide the province or create new provinces.
