Minhas stars with bat and ball as Pakistan crush England by 87 runs in tri-series

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

RAWALPINDI: All-rounder Arafat Minhas produced a brilliant all-round performance as Pakistan beat England by 87 runs in the ODI tri-series on Tuesday. Minhas scored 59 runs off 58 balls and then claimed six wickets for just 40 runs to dismantle the English batting line-up. Pakistan had posted 302 for 9 in their 50 overs, with Hasan Nawaz contributing 79 and Minhas scoring a fifty.

In reply, England were bowled out for 215 in 38.4 overs. The victory was Pakistan's second straight win in the series and took them to the top of the standings with four points.
