Maryam Nawaz Orders Ban on Camera Glasses, Crackdown on Wheat Smuggling

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has ordered a ban on camera-equipped glasses and other spying devices in the province, alongside a wider law-and-order push. Chairing a meeting, she directed police to stop wheat smuggling from all districts of Punjab. She also ordered the anti-bribery campaign within the police force to be intensified, with action against personnel found involved.

The chief minister directed pre-emptive action and recovery operations against holders of unlicensed weapons. She called for increased police patrolling across the province and approved new motorcycles for Lahore's Dolphin Force. Maryam Nawaz said the culture of 'badmashi', or hooliganism, would not be tolerated in Punjab.

She further ordered full-scale action to eliminate narcotics and to dismantle crime hotspots across the province.
