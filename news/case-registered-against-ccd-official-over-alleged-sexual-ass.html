Case Registered Against CCD Official Over Alleged Sexual Assault of Woman in Lahore's Kahna

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore police have registered a case against CCD official Muhammad Shabbir over the alleged sexual assault of a young woman in the Kahna area. According to the FIR, the complainant, a resident of Kot Lakhpat, had visited the Kahna police station to report her lost mobile phone. She alleged that the official lured her to a house on Ahlu Road on the pretext of tracing the phone, where he assaulted her at gunpoint and recorded her video in a compromising state.

The woman claimed the assault happened twice. Her initial attempts to get the case registered were unsuccessful. She then approached the open court of DIG Operations Faisal Kamran, after which the case was formally registered and investigation ordered.
