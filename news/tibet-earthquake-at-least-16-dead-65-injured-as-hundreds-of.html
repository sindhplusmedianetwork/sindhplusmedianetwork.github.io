Tibet Earthquake: At Least 16 Dead, 65 Injured as Hundreds of Houses Destroyed; Pakistan Offers Help

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 16 people were killed and 65 injured while hundreds of houses were destroyed in a severe earthquake that struck Tibet's Nagqu region, and Pakistan has offered help. The earthquake measured 7.1 in magnitude, and at least 339 houses were destroyed in northern Nagqu, with rescue operations continuing amid fears the death toll could rise as people may be trapped under rubble. More than 1,600 rescue workers are taking part in relief operations, and 1,000 temporary houses, nearly 3,000 tents and other supplies including folding beds, food items, torches and winter clothing have been sent for the victims.

According to the US Geological Survey, the quake struck at 10:33pm China time with its epicentre 191 kilometres northeast of Nagqu city at a depth of 18 kilometres. At least seven aftershocks of magnitude 4.3 and 5.2 have followed the main quake. Chinese media reported that communications were affected in some areas and railway authorities stopped trains passing through the affected region as a precaution.

A landslide triggered by the quake closed part of National Highway 317, where only relief and government vehicles are being allowed, and intermittent snowfall is expected in the affected area over the next three days. It is the strongest earthquake to hit the area in the last five years. Chinese President Xi Jinping directed all-out efforts to rescue victims and provide medical aid to the injured.

Nagqu city is located more than 300 kilometres north of the Tibetan capital Lhasa. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said on X that he was deeply saddened by the loss of lives, extended heartfelt condolences to President Xi Jinping and the Chinese people, prayed for the swift recovery of the injured, and said Pakistan stands in solidarity with its Chinese brothers and sisters and is ready to extend every possible support.
