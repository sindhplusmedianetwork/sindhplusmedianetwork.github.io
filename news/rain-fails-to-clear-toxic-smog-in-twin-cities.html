Rain fails to clear toxic smog in twin cities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Despite a day of rain in Islamabad and Rawalpindi and other parts of Punjab, air pollution levels have declined only marginally. The Air Quality Index remains in the unhealthy to hazardous range across several cities, including Lahore at 291, Multan at 400 and Faisalabad at 500. Nearly 2,700 patients suffering from respiratory problems, sore throats, eye irritation, colds and asthma visited the three allied hospitals in Rawalpindi over the past two days.

The Rawalpindi deputy commissioner has made face masks mandatory for schoolchildren. Health officials have advised residents to limit outdoor activity while the smog persists.
