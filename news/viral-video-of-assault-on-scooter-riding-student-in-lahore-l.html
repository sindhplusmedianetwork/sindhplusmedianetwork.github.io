Viral Video of Assault on Scooter-Riding Student in Lahore Leads to Arrest

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A university student in Lahore was publicly assaulted while riding her scooter home from campus, and a video of the attack went viral on social media. The victim's father told BBC Urdu that his daughter was on her way home from university when the assault took place. The footage sparked widespread outrage online, with many calling for strict punishment.

Police acted on the viral video and arrested the suspect. Officials described the case as crossing Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's stated red line on the protection of women. The investigation is continuing and the suspect remains in custody.
