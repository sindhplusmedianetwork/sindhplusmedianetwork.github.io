PSX Extends Rally to Fourth Session, Gains 1,456 Points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The KSE-100 Index extended its winning streak to a fourth consecutive session on Thursday, closing at 167,193 points after gaining 1,456 points, or 0.88 percent. The rally was led by commercial banks, fertilizers, investment banks, oil and gas and refineries, with better-than-expected corporate earnings supporting sentiment. Brent crude eased around one percent to near 114 dollars a barrel.

Analysts warned of possible consolidation and profit-taking after four straight positive sessions.
