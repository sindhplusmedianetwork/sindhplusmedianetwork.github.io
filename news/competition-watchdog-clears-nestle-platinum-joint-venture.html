Competition Watchdog Clears Nestle-Platinum Joint Venture

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Competition Commission of Pakistan has approved a proposed joint venture between Nestle and Forward I, an entity controlled by Platinum Equity, following a phase-I assessment under the Competition Act, 2010. The transaction covers Nestle's waters and beverages business across multiple jurisdictions, including its bottled water operations in Pakistan. Under the arrangement, Nestle will transfer the relevant business to WPB JVco, a Luxembourg-incorporated special purpose vehicle, through an internal reorganisation.

Forward I will subsequently acquire a shareholding in the vehicle, establishing joint control of the business with Nestle. The approval clears the way for the two sides to combine their interests in the waters and beverages business, with the joint venture set to operate across the jurisdictions covered by the transaction. The commission's phase-I clearance means the deal has passed its initial competition review, allowing the parties to proceed with the reorganisation and the transfer of the business to the new joint venture company.
