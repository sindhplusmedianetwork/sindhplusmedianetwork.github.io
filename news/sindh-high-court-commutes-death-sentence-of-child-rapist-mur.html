Sindh High Court commutes death sentence of child rapist-murderer to life imprisonment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court has converted the death sentence of an accused in the rape and murder of a minor girl into life imprisonment. The court heard the appeal of the accused, Irfan, whose lawyer Liaquat Gabol argued that legal requirements had not been fulfilled in the sentence and that there were contradictions in the statements of witnesses. According to the prosecution, an additional district and sessions judge in the East had awarded him the death sentence in June 2026.

The prosecution said the accused had entered a house in the Mobeen Town police limits on December 8, 2022 and subjected the girl to sexual assault before strangling her to death. The accused worked as a plumber and forced his way into the house after finding the girl alone. A case had been registered at the Mobeen Town police station on the complaint of the victim's father.
