Burnt Car Skeleton Mystery: Police Trail Leads to Woman Constable in Gujarat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A human skeleton recovered from a burnt car on the banks of the Hathmati river in Sabarkantha district of India's Gujarat state was identified as that of Assistant Police Sub-Inspector Ashwin Tambad, who was posted in the same district. A week after the recovery near Padra village, it emerged that the death was allegedly a planned murder conspiracy, and four people, including a woman police head constable, were arrested in the case. According to initial information, 45-year-old Ashwin Tambad, posted at Chithoda police station, was allegedly in a romantic relationship with 37-year-old woman head constable Alfa Klasva, who served at the same station.

Ashwin had gone on duty in his car on November 8, and when he did not return home for two days, his elder brother Sudhraj filed a missing-person report, the same day the burnt car with a human skeleton was found near the river. A DNA test matched with the sample of their father Babubhai confirmed the skeleton was Ashwin's, after which police began further investigation. Superintendent of Police Manohar Singh Jadeja said CCTV footage showed a white Echo car and Ashwin's Ertiga car heading towards the spot on November 9, but the Ertiga was not seen returning, while the Echo returned at night.

Forensic analysis indicated petrol had been sprinkled inside and outside the car, leading police to conclude the person who set it ablaze would also have been burnt, and checks of hospital and petrol pump records showed the Echo's occupants had bought petrol in a can, while head constable Alfa had received first aid and later treatment at a hospital in Himatnagar. Police then took into custody Alfa's brother Kalpesh Klasva, cousin Pankaj and their friend Manoj, along with Alfa from the hospital, and said the four initially did not cooperate but later allegedly confessed during questioning. According to the police officer, the accused stated that Alfa wanted to end the relationship while Ashwin was pressuring her to continue it, and on November 8 Alfa called Ashwin to her home where he was killed, after which the body was placed in a sack in his car parked by the river and set on fire with petrol, burning Alfa's hands and part of her body in the blaze.
