Pakistan Railways Logs Over 55 Accidents This Year as Safety Concerns Mount

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Railways has recorded more than 55 accidents involving passenger and freight trains since the start of the year, raising concerns over railway safety and maintenance standards. The accidents have reportedly left several people dead and dozens injured, while also causing millions of rupees in damage to railway infrastructure. Passengers say travelling by train remains a necessity for them despite the risks, while repeated incidents have raised questions about the implementation of safety measures under Federal Minister for Railways Muhammad Hanif Abbasi.

The minister visited the Steel and Carriage Shops in Lahore's Mughalpura area on Saturday, where he inspected production facilities and directed officials to ensure quality standards in manufacturing railway components. According to railway sources, the Yusufwala coal train alone has been involved in several accidents: ten wagons of a freight train derailed near Khanpur on August 27, the same day the Rawal Express engine and two coaches went off the tracks near Kala Gujran, and the Yusufwala coal train met with an accident near Khairpur on September 27. Other incidents include three coal wagons derailing at Kotri yard on September 9, the Khyber Mail's power van derailing near Sanjani on September 2, and seven wagons going off the tracks on September 6.

Railway sources identified the Sukkur, Karachi and Multan divisions as the areas where most train accidents have occurred. On May 24, a shuttle train in Quetta was also targeted in an act of sabotage that damaged three coaches.
