IMF: Gulf Economies Won't Return to Pre-War Growth Anytime Soon

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Monetary Fund has said Gulf economies will not return to their pre-war growth rates in the medium term, warning of lasting economic scarring from the Iran war. IMF regional director Jihad Azour told reporters in Bangkok that the shock would scar various sectors, disrupt infrastructure and require additional resilience spending. The IMF projected the Middle East and Central Asia region would contract 0.3 per cent in 2026.

Saudi Arabia was projected to shrink 3.3 per cent, a rare recession for the kingdom, amid continued fighting with the Houthis that has hit airports, energy facilities and the Aramco headquarters. IMF chief Kristalina Georgieva had warned a day earlier that ongoing attacks targeting Saudi Arabia could affect people 'everywhere'.
