Light Poles on New Road Pose Landing Danger at Jinnah Airport

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Street-light poles on a new road being built between Gulistan-e-Johar and Shahrah-e-Faisal, next to the runway at Karachi's Jinnah International Airport, have been declared a serious danger to landing passenger aircraft. Karachi Air Traffic Control has formally advised the Pakistan Airports Authority administration to act to prevent a possible accident. ATC officials say the bright lights and rows of poles at night make the road look exactly like a runway to pilots coming in to land.

That confusion could prove extremely dangerous during landing. The warning puts the focus on the road project and the urgent need to remove the risk to flights.
