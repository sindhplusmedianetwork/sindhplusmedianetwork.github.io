Pakistan Ambassador Opens International Culture Day at Embassy College in Beijing

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's ambassador to China, Dr Syed Ali Asad Gilani, inaugurated the annual International Culture Day at the Pakistan Embassy College in Beijing. The event celebrated global diversity, promoted intercultural dialogue and strengthened the spirit of international friendship. On arrival, the ambassador visited stalls at the exhibition and spoke with students and participants from more than 50 countries.

The programme featured performances highlighting the rich cultural diversity of Pakistan's provinces, along with Chinese and African artistic presentations, and an international cultural walk showcased the range of national identities among the college's students. Addressing the gathering, Ambassador Gilani underlined the historic significance of the Pakistan Embassy College as Beijing's first international school. He acknowledged its long-standing contribution to educational excellence and its important role in strengthening the educational and cultural pillars of the Pakistan-China partnership.
