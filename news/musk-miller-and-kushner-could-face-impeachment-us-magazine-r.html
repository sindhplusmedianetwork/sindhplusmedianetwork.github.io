Musk, Miller and Kushner could face impeachment, US magazine reports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A US magazine report says senior figures linked to President Donald Trump's administration could face impeachment proceedings once the new Congress takes shape in January. The report says the new lawmakers, many of whom support far-reaching changes, are continuing to coordinate their strategy. It follows a move by Congressman Thomas Massie, who introduced a motion to impeach Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth.

According to the report, the new Congress may also summon senior administration officials, including Stephen Miller and Elon Musk, for investigations. The report further says the Securities and Exchange Commission could be directed to look into allegations of financial irregularities involving the president, his family and senior officials. Jared Kushner is also named among those who could face impeachment proceedings, the report says.
