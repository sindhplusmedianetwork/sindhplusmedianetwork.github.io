Health Ministry Denies Insulin Shortage, Says Only One Brand Unavailable

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Health authorities on Tuesday denied reports of a widespread insulin shortage in Pakistan, clarifying that only one brand of insulin is currently unavailable. According to the health ministry and the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan, insulin glulisine — sold as Apidra by Sanofi — is unavailable because of international supply constraints. Officials said alternatives remain readily available in local markets, including insulin aspart (NovoRapid) and insulin lispro (Humalog).

Health ministry spokesperson Sajid Shah advised patients prescribed Apidra to consult their healthcare provider before making any change to their treatment regimen. He said the reports of a general shortage were not accurate and that the ministry was monitoring the supply situation. The authorities said there was no disruption in the availability of other insulin products across the country.
