October 27 Is the Darkest Day in Kashmir's History, Says Azad Kashmir Prime Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Azad Kashmir Prime Minister Iftikhar Gilani has said that October 27, 1947 is the darkest day in the history of the state of Jammu and Kashmir, when India occupied Kashmir. In a special message from Muzaffarabad on Black Day, he paid tribute to the Kashmiri people's freedom struggle, their sacrifices and their resolve in the face of Indian atrocities. He said the people of occupied Kashmir have kept the flame of freedom burning despite Indian brutality, and that their spirit of freedom is passing from generation to generation.

The prime minister said Indian atrocities have not weakened the resolve of the Kashmiri people, and saluted their steadfastness, courage and the sacrifices made for freedom. He expressed the hope that one day the entire state of Jammu and Kashmir will become part of Pakistan.
