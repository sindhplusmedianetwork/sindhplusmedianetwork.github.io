Careless accusations and hateful messages can put lives at risk, warn President and Prime Minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif have said that careless accusations and hate messages can pose a danger to human lives. In his message on the International Day for Tolerance, the president said Islam teaches compassion, justice and respect for human dignity. He said all citizens are equal before the law, and public institutions must act firmly against discrimination and incitement to violence.

The prime minister said that respecting others' beliefs and views despite differences is the true meaning of tolerance. He urged religious scholars, teachers, the media and especially young people to reject prejudice. Both leaders stressed that tolerance and respect for others are essential for protecting lives and keeping society peaceful.
