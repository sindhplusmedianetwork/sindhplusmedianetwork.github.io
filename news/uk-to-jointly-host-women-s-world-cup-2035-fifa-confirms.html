UK to Jointly Host Women's World Cup 2035, FIFA Confirms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

FIFA has confirmed that England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland will jointly host the Women's World Cup in 2035. It will be the first time a World Cup tournament has been held in Britain since the men's World Cup in 1966. The 48-team tournament will run for 39 days and feature 104 matches.

The four home nations will share hosting duties across the United Kingdom. FIFA made the announcement as part of its long-term hosting plans for the women's game.
