MQM-P says VIP protocol for Faryal Talpur brought Hyderabad to a standstill

By Sindh Plus Media Network

The MQM-P has criticised the VIP protocol given to PPP leader Faryal Talpur during her visit to Hyderabad. The party said the protocol paralysed the city. According to the MQM-P, the arrangements during the visit reflected feudal-style governance.

The party also said the protocol reflected VIP culture. Its criticism focused on the disruption caused in Hyderabad during the visit.
