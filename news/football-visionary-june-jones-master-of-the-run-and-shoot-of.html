Football visionary June Jones, master of the run-and-shoot offense, dies at 73

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Football legend June Jones has passed away at the age of 73, his death confirmed by former colleagues and programs. Jones was an offensive visionary who revolutionized modern passing attacks with the iconic run-and-shoot offense, a wide-open system that changed how teams thought about the aerial game. His coaching journey spanned four decades across the NFL, college football and alternative leagues, including memorable work in Hawaii.

Jones had recently entered hospice care following a diagnosis with a spinal tumor. Tributes have poured in from across the football world for a coach remembered as one of the great offensive innovators of the modern era.
