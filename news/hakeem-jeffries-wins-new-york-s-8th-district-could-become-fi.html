Hakeem Jeffries wins New York's 8th District, could become first Black House speaker

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Democrat leader Hakeem Jeffries has won from New York's 8th congressional district. According to preliminary figures compiled by a US news agency, Jeffries secured 79.2 percent of the vote, defeating Republican rival Lewis Mizrahi. If the Democrats gain control of the House of Representatives in the US midterm elections, Jeffries could be elected speaker, which would make him the first Black speaker in American history.

Addressing his supporters after the victory, Jeffries declared 'America is Back', saying the American people had given their verdict and made clear that they are tired of extremism and corruption. He described the results as a mandate for change, saying Democrats would have to deliver significant change for the public because many things were not working properly. He said that after gaining a majority in the House, Democrats would work to reduce rising prices of housing, groceries and petrol, and try to ensure health facilities for all Americans at the time of need.
