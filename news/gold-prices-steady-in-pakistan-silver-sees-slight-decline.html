Gold prices steady in Pakistan, silver sees slight decline

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices remained stable in Pakistan's local bullion markets on Saturday, while silver prices recorded a slight decline. In the international bullion market, gold held steady at 4,066 dollars per ounce, keeping the price of one tola of gold unchanged at 429,136 rupees. The price of 10 grams of gold also stayed unchanged at 367,914 rupees.

Silver fell by 10 rupees per tola to 5,668 rupees, while 10 grams of silver dropped by 8 rupees to 4,859 rupees. Traders said the local market followed the steady trend in the international market, where gold showed no major movement during the day.
