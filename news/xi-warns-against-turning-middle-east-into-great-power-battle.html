Xi Warns Against Turning Middle East Into Great-Power Battleground

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese President Xi Jinping told Oman's Sultan Haitham bin Tariq al-Said on Monday that the Middle East and Gulf region should not become an arena for great-power rivalry and geopolitical conflict, according to China's Foreign Ministry. Xi said the world is undergoing profound changes and that peace and stability in the region require joint nurturing and protection by all parties. The remarks came during the Omani sultan's state visit to Beijing.

Separately, Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said Beijing will send special envoy to the Middle East Zhai Jun to Iran, Oman and Saudi Arabia in early and mid-November. The statements came as the region faces escalating hostilities, with Saudi Arabia's civil defence giving the all-clear after alerts in Khamis Mushait, Abha, Taif and Najran, and the Saudi-led coalition saying it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile toward the kingdom's southern region. Meanwhile, Iran's foreign ministry said it was considering a two-day plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz if the United States agrees to its conditions.
