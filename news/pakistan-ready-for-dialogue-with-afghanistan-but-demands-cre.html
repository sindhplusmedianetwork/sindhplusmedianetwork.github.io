Pakistan Ready for Dialogue With Afghanistan but Demands Credible Action Against Terror Threats: FO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan remains ready for dialogue with Afghanistan, but any meaningful engagement must be backed by a credible mechanism to stop terrorist groups operating from Afghan soil, the Foreign Office said on Friday. Briefing the media, spokesperson Sajjad Haider Khan said more than 1,300 Pakistanis had lost their lives to terrorism in the past year. He cited United Nations and SIGAR reports which state that over 20 terrorist organisations operate in Afghanistan.

Khan said Pakistan's position was that dialogue could only move forward if Kabul took verifiable action against the groups threatening Pakistan's security. The spokesperson also addressed Pakistan-India tensions, the Kashmir issue and the Indus Waters Treaty during the briefing. He added that Pakistan would take part in upcoming foreign ministers' meetings in Istanbul involving Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Turkiye and Egypt.
