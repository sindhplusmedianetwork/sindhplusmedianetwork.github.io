French cricket 'dying' as federation crisis and chaos deepens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cricket in France is at risk of disappearing altogether, less than two years before the sport makes its long-awaited return to the Olympics. The sport was last played at the Olympics in Paris in 1900, when England beat France by 158 runs to win the gold medal, but France will not be at the Twenty20 tournament at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. Clubs suspended the national league this year as they try to force president Prebagarane 'Prebou' Balane to resign following a series of scandals.

The organisation lost its recognition by France's sports ministry early this year, while the International Cricket Council in June suspended development funding and placed France Cricket on notice for non-compliance with membership criteria. The crisis began when a 2023 report exposed clubs faking women's matches, apparently so the federation could benefit from hundreds of thousands of dollars of ICC funding, and the national women's team was suspended after demanding an investigation. In November, 34 clubs asked France Cricket for an extraordinary general meeting to submit a motion of no confidence in Balane and the executive committee, but the president refused.

Player and administrator Miriam Romero, who at 65 still plays, warned that cricket in France would die if nothing was done, saying: 'We must not let cricket die in France, it's a shame.'
