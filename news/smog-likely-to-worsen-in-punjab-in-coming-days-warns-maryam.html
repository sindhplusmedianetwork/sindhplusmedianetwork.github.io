Smog likely to worsen in Punjab in coming days, warns Maryam Aurangzeb

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior Punjab minister Maryam Aurangzeb has warned that the intensity of smog in Punjab may increase over the next few days. She said fine particles from fireworks across the border could add to air pollution in the province. In a statement, Aurangzeb said operations against smog have been further intensified across Punjab.

She said the use of smog guns has been stepped up near the eastern corridor bordering India. The minister urged the public to support the government in controlling local sources of pollution and to avoid burning crop residue, especially rice stubble. She also called for strict action to ensure that garbage, plastic and other waste are not burnt in the open.

Aurangzeb added that controlling smoke and harmful emissions from vehicles is essential to reducing smog, and said vehicle fitness and emission standards must be strictly enforced.
