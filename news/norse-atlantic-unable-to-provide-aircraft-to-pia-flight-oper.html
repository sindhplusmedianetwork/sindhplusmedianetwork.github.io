Norse Atlantic Unable to Provide Aircraft to PIA, Flight Operations Feared Disrupted

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Norwegian airline Norse Atlantic's financial difficulties have created new problems for Pakistan International Airlines, as the unavailability of a leased aircraft threatens disruption to its UK and Europe flight operations. Sources said PIA has begun preparing changes to its flight schedule, and internal instructions were issued to Boeing 777 pilots and crew on November 23. For Monday's Islamabad to Manchester flight, Norse Atlantic's leased aircraft was unavailable, forcing PIA to use its own Boeing 777 on an emergency basis.

The London to Islamabad flight PK-786 is also expected to be delayed by more than two hours. According to FlightRadar24, PIA's own Boeing 777 aircraft are scheduled for London to Islamabad flights on November 24 and 25 instead of Norse Atlantic's Dreamliner. PIA signed a wet-lease agreement with Norse Atlantic on October 9 for two Boeing 787-9 Dreamliners, and the first arrived in Islamabad on November 1.

Aviation sources said Norse Atlantic faces heavy financial losses and payment difficulties, and if the agreement ends PIA could face losses of several million dollars.
