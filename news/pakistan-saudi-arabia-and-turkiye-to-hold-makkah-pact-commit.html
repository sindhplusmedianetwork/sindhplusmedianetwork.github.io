Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye to hold Makkah pact committee meeting in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A two-day meeting of the Strategic Political and Defence Committee of Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkiye will begin in Islamabad on November 26 under the Makkah defence agreement. Senior political, defence and military officials from all three countries will attend. The meeting is expected to discuss practical arrangements for implementing collective defence commitments.

These include possible military assistance, intelligence sharing and air defence cooperation. Diplomatic sources say the session will review progress on the agreement's institutional framework. It will also look at ways to strengthen military coordination among the three countries.
