Fact-Checkers Reject Claim That Army Officer Died at Rawalpindi Match

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting's fact-checker on Monday rejected claims circulating on social media that a Pakistan Army officer died of a heart attack during the Pakistan-Sri Lanka one-day international at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. The ministry clarified that the deceased, identified as Muhammad Farooq, was a PCB employee and not an army officer. It said Farooq died of electrocution at his home while checking an electrical plug and was not even present at the stadium on the day of the match.

Hospital records confirmed electrocution as the cause of death, the ministry added. The fact-checker urged the public to verify information before sharing it online. The clarification came after the false claim gained traction on social media platforms.
