'Forensic audit of refineries could cut petrol by Rs50/litre': petroleum dealers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Petroleum dealers have demanded a forensic audit of refineries' profits, saying such an audit could make petrol cheaper by Rs50 per litre. According to ARY News, the dealers held an emergency meeting in Karachi to discuss profits and public relief. Dealers' official Malik Khuda Bakhsh said a transparent forensic audit of refineries' profits is needed to reveal the truth about their earnings.

He claimed refineries earned a whole year's profit in just three months, and said the petroleum minister's statement confirms their concerns. Official Anwar Kamal said the public could be given Rs40 to Rs50 per litre relief on petrol, alleging refineries earned unjustified profits and the people should get their due. The dealers demanded a comprehensive forensic audit of all refineries' financial matters and representation for dealers in the petroleum sector's decision-making committees.

They also said the audit would reveal the truth about past profits and stop the path of additional financial burden on the public in the future.
