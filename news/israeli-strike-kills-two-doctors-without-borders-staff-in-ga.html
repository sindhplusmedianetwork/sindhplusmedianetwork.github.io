Israeli strike kills two Doctors Without Borders staff in Gaza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Doctors Without Borders (MSF) said on Saturday that an Israeli strike killed two of its staff members at a clinic in Gaza, while the Israeli army alleged that the pair were members of Hamas. The Israel Defence Forces claimed the men, who worked as security guards at the clinic, were Hamas artillery cell commander Moaz Yousef Alitsbach and Ahmed Sufyan Muhammad Ali Abu. MSF said it was devastated by the killings and denounced a direct hit on the organisation.

It said its premises were clearly marked with MSF logos, and their location had been communicated to and was known by the Israeli military. World Health Organisation chief Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus condemned the attack, posting that attacks on health facilities, health workers and humanitarian personnel in Gaza were outrageous and unacceptable. The IDF denied targeting a hospital or damaging medical infrastructure, though the Israeli military has regularly targeted health facilities in the besieged Palestinian enclave, and the latest attack comes amid a series of Israeli strikes targeting senior Hamas security officials in Gaza.

On Wednesday, an Israeli air strike killed Mohammad Abuelouf, a Hamas colonel who headed the group's internal security service in northern Gaza, along with his wife and two daughters at their apartment in Gaza City's Beach refugee camp. On November 24, Israeli air strikes killed at least three people in Gaza, including Adel Al-Helo, a worker with the UN Palestinian refugee agency (UNRWA), according to the agency and local health officials.
