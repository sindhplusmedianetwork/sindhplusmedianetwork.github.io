Independent media not as independent as it could be, says journalist Mehdi Hasan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

British-American journalist Mehdi Hasan spoke at the Doha Film Festival on November 22 in a talk titled The New Gatekeepers, saying independent media is not as independent as it could be because subscriber-funded outlets face audience capture. He said gatekeeping power has shifted to Silicon Valley executives such as Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg. He cited a Reuters Institute survey showing more people now get news from social media than from news websites and apps.

Hasan said every journalist needs to be a content creator now. He also stressed fact-checking and professional standards.
