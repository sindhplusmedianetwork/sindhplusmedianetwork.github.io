High-powered panel admits $18bn trade data gap to IMF

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A high-powered committee has admitted to the International Monetary Fund that Pakistan's trade statistics carried a discrepancy of more than 18 billion dollars between fiscal years 2020-21 and 2024-25. The gap was identified and has since been rectified, the committee's report said. The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics report has been shared with the Ministry of Planning, the Ministry of Finance and the IMF.

The admission came in a report presented to the Fund as part of ongoing engagements. Officials said corrective measures had been taken to ensure accuracy going forward.
