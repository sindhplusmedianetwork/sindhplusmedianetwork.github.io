Traders Press FBR to Extend Tax Return Deadline to December 2

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Trade bodies and tax bar associations across Pakistan pressed the Federal Board of Revenue to extend the last date for filing income tax returns and wealth statements for Tax Year 2026 to December 2. The demand was raised as the October 15 deadline approached, which itself was an extension from the original September 30 cutoff granted under Section 214A of the Income Tax Ordinance. The Sialkot Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry both sought the December 2 extension, citing persistent technical problems with the FBR's IRIS online portal, including glitches, service interruptions and slow processing.

The Pakistan Tax Bar Association made a similar demand, pointing to errors in return forms and problems with the minimum tax calculation formula. Tax experts also argued that the final return form was notified only on September 2, which in their view required giving taxpayers a full three-month filing window. The Korangi Association of Trade and Industry in Karachi likewise urged an extension of at least a month over the portal's problems.

The FBR later extended the deadline to October 31 in response to requests from trade bodies and tax bar associations.
