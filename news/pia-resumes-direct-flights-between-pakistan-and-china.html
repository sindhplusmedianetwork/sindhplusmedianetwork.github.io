PIA resumes direct flights between Pakistan and China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

BEIJING: Pakistan International Airlines has resumed its flight operations between Pakistan and China, reconnecting Islamabad and Beijing through direct air services. The first flight under the renewed schedule operated on October 30. The resumption marks a significant milestone in strengthening aviation links between the two countries and reflects the airline's commitment to restoring regular operations in the Chinese market.

Under the renewed schedule, PIA will operate weekly flights between Islamabad and Beijing every Friday, offering passengers a direct and convenient travel option between the two capitals. The airline also aims to offer competitive fares while ensuring consistent and reliable services. In a major step toward expanding its network across China, PIA has strengthened its cooperation with three leading Chinese airlines — Air China, Shenzhen Airlines and Hainan Airlines.

Through its partnership with Air China, passengers will benefit from onward connections to various domestic destinations through Beijing, a major aviation hub.
