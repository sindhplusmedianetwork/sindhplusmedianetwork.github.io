Seven Killed, 34 Wounded as Gunmen Open Fire at Church Funeral in Jordan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gunmen killed at least seven people and wounded 34 others when they opened fire during a funeral at a Greek Orthodox church in the village of Hammoud in Jordan's southern Karak governorate on Friday. The public security directorate said the assailants got out of a vehicle and started shooting during the funeral procession before fleeing, and a manhunt is underway for the perpetrators. The death toll was revised several times through the day, settling at seven dead with 27 of the 34 wounded still receiving treatment.

CCTV footage aired by state broadcaster Al-Mamlaka showed two gunmen in casual clothes brandishing pistols and a third carrying a long gun as they opened fire on the church. A witness said the attackers fired continuously for about five minutes while between 150 and 200 people were in and around the church at the time. The attackers remain unidentified and no group has claimed responsibility; the interior minister described them as a group of extremists.

King Abdullah II vowed the 'harshest retaliation', declaring on X that Jordan would 'strike with an iron fist against those who dare to threaten our nation', and arrived in Karak on Friday evening to visit the injured. The assault is a rare attack on Christians in the majority-Muslim kingdom and has been described by officials as unprecedented in Jordan's history.
