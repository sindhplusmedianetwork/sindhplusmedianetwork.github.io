Alcaraz to Face Zverev for ATP Finals Title

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Carlos Alcaraz beat Daniil Medvedev in the ATP Finals semi-final in Turin. Alcaraz won the match 6-4, 6-3 to reach the final. Alexander Zverev beat Alex de Minaur in the other semi-final.

Zverev won his match 7-6 (8-6), 6-2. Alcaraz and Zverev will meet in the final on Sunday.
