US Accuses Canada of Election Interference Ahead of Midterms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has accused Canadian politicians of attempting to influence its upcoming midterm elections, citing Canada's resistance to President Donald Trump's tariff campaign. In a fact sheet titled 'Monitoring Canadian Activities in US Politics,' released on Wednesday, the US embassy in Canada listed actions it described as attempts to sway American politics. The fact sheet singled out Industry Minister Melanie Joly's meetings with Democratic officials in Boston and Chicago and her remarks about targeting US states with retaliatory tariffs.

It also pointed to Ontario Premier Doug Ford's advertising campaign against Trump's tariffs. The embassy further cited 11 Canadians charged with illegally voting in US elections between 2012 and 2026, without linking the cases to the Canadian government. Canadian leaders rejected the allegation; Joly said Canada would never meddle in US elections, and Ford said he had no interest in interfering.

The accusations come as the two countries remain locked in a trade war.
