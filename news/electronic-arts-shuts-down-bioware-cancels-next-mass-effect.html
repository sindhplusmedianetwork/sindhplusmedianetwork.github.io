Electronic Arts Shuts Down BioWare, Cancels Next Mass Effect in First Post-Buyout Cuts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Electronic Arts will lay off roughly 400 employees, shut down its BioWare studio and cancel the next game in the Mass Effect series, in the company's first major round of cuts since going private in a $55 billion buyout. BioWare confirmed the closure in a social media post on October 21, saying that after more than three decades of shared adventures, the studio is closing and early development on the next Mass Effect project is ending. The buyout by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake and Affinity Partners — a private equity fund owned by President Trump's son-in-law Jared Kushner — was completed in August 2026 and saddled EA with $18 billion in debt, with executives promising investors $700 million in annual cost savings.

EA acquired BioWare in 2007; the studio behind Baldur's Gate, Mass Effect, Dragon Age and Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic suffered the commercial failure of Anthem in 2019 and sales of Dragon Age: The Veilguard that fell short of expectations. EA said it will now concentrate on sports games, Apex Legends, Battlefield, the next Star Wars Jedi game and Marvel's Iron Man, while a Sims project code-named Project Lotus is paused in favour of one code-named Evergreen. The cuts represent about 3 percent of EA's roughly 14,500 staff.
