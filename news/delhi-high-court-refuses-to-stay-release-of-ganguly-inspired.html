Delhi High Court Refuses to Stay Release of Ganguly-Inspired Film 'Doosra'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Delhi High Court on Thursday refused to stay the theatre release of 'Doosra: India's Other Freedom Struggle', a Hindi sports drama inspired by former India captain Sourav Ganguly, scheduled to hit cinemas on Friday. A vacation bench of Justice Dinesh Bhatt orally observed that the balance of convenience favoured the makers of the film, described as the story of a middle-class Rajasthani girl inspired by sporting events involving the former captain. The petition was filed by Ganguly and Luv Films LLP, which alleged unauthorised commercial exploitation of Ganguly's personality rights, citing the use of events from his life including his captaincy in the 2002 NatWest Series final and his shirtless celebration at Lord's.

According to the suit, Ganguly had granted Luv Films exclusive rights in August 2021 to develop a biographical film, the authorised biopic 'Dada – The Sourav Ganguly Story' being scheduled for May 14, 2027. The filmmakers' counsel argued the film was not a biopic, that due permissions had been taken including a BCCI licence for match footage, and called the last-day move blackmail, noting over a hundred theatres had been booked. Justice Bhatt told the plaintiff's counsel that while they may have a valuable right, the film would not be stopped today.

The court listed the matter for hearing on Friday.
