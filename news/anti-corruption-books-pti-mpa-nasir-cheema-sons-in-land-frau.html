Anti-Corruption Books PTI MPA Nasir Cheema, Sons in Land Fraud Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Gujranwala Anti-Corruption Establishment has booked nine people, including a PTI MPA, his two sons and three land revenue officials, over the alleged fraudulent transfer of a valuable piece of land. The land, measuring 119 kanals and 11 marlas near a rice mills along the main GT Road in Wazirabad, belonged to late businessman Bilal Arshad and its market price is said to run into billions of rupees. Complainant Akhtar Shahzad told the ACE that he had been in Gujranwala district jail after being implicated in the murder of his father Muhammad Arshad and brother Bilal Arshad, and that in February 2022 his other brother Amjad Shahzad, PTI MPA Nasir Cheema of the Gakhar Mandi area, the MPA's sons Jamal Nasir Cheema and Bilal Nasir Cheema, Naib Tehsildar Shabeer Hussain, Patwari Khawar Mureed, Gardawar Najamul Hassan, Imran Ali and Asif Abdullah Tarrar fraudulently transferred the land.

He alleged ownership was moved to Jamal Nasir Cheema through fake documents showing Amjad Shahzad as the sole owner, depriving him and other legal heirs of their rightful ownership. The case has been registered under sections 420, 468 and 471 of the Pakistan Penal Code and section 5/2/47 of the Prevention of Corruption Act, and an ACE official said the role of Arazi Record Centre officials may also come under scrutiny.
