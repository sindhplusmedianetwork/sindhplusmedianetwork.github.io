NAB summons Fazl's son Asad Mahmood in Pakistan Post appointments case

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Accountability Bureau has summoned former federal minister for communication Asad Mahmood over alleged irregularities in appointments at Pakistan Post during his 2022-23 term. Mahmood is the son of JUI-F president Maulana Fazlur Rehman. The summons came after Maulana Fazl's fresh criticism of the government and state institutions over issues concerning the PTI and terrorism.

According to a NAB notice, the bureau is investigating alleged irregularities in 4,252 appointments made at Pakistan Post during his tenure. The Establishment Division had authorised recruitment against 3,938 posts, but 4,252 people were allegedly appointed. The bureau said 2,646 appointments were made against posts that had already been abolished, in violation of the directions of a cabinet committee.

It also said a large number of permanent appointments were made even though recruitment had been recommended on a contractual basis only. NAB has directed Mahmood to appear before its investigation team on November 10 at 11am.
