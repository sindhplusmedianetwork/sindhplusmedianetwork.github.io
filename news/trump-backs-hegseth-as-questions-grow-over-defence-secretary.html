Trump Backs Hegseth as Questions Grow Over Defence Secretary's Future

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump said he is not considering replacing Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth, telling reporters "No, I think he's doing a very good job." His comments came a day after Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress in midterm elections shaped by anger over the war on Iran. Sources said Trump could still replace Hegseth as Republicans assess their losses, with one adviser saying he "could be on the chopping block". The Defence Department said Hegseth was "100 percent committed" to leading the Pentagon until the end of Trump's term.

The contrasting signals leave the defence secretary's future uncertain despite the president's public backing. The midterm results have intensified scrutiny of the administration's handling of the war.
