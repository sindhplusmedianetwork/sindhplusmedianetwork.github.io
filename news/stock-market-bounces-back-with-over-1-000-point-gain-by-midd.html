Stock market bounces back with over 1,000-point gain by midday

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The stock market staged a strong recovery on Friday, with the benchmark index climbing more than 1,000 points before trading paused at midday for Friday prayers. The index stood at 178,434.59, up 1,060.83 points or 0.60 per cent from the previous close. Trading began on a weaker note, but the index recovered steadily through the morning, touching an intraday high of 178,434.59 and a low of 176,865.05.

By the midday break, the market had fully reversed the previous session's sharp decline. Buying was visible in commercial banks, oil and gas exploration companies, oil marketing companies and refineries, with several leading shares trading higher. The recovery followed a volatile Thursday session in which the index climbed early towards the 180,000 level before renewed selling, linked to domestic political uncertainty, pushed it down 1,710 points to close at 177,373.76.

Trading volume reached about 90 million shares, with the value of shares traded at roughly 9.83 billion rupees. Investors will now watch whether the morning recovery holds in the afternoon session amid lingering caution near recent highs.
