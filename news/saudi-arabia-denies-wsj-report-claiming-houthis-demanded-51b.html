Saudi Arabia denies WSJ report claiming Houthis demanded $51b to end hostilities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia has categorically rejected a Wall Street Journal report claiming Yemen's Houthis demanded $51 billion in compensation and formal recognition as Yemen's rulers in exchange for ending hostilities with the kingdom. Saudi Deputy Minister for Public Diplomacy Rayed Krimly called the claims fabricated in a post on X. Krimly said the Houthis had turned against Yemen's internationally recognised government and caused famine.

The Wall Street Journal had reported that Riyadh approached the Houthis about negotiating an end to the renewed conflict, and that the group responded with the $51 billion demand. Saudi Arabia's denial is a new development after the newspaper's report about the alleged demand.
