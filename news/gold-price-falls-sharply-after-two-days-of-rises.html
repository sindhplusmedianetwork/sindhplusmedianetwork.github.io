Gold Price Falls Sharply After Two Days of Rises

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The price of gold per tola in Pakistan fell sharply by 2,300 rupees on Saturday after rising for two consecutive days. According to the All Pakistan Gems and Jewellers Association, the price of gold per tola fell by 2,300 rupees to 387,836 rupees. Similarly, the price of 10 grams of gold fell by 1,972 rupees to 332,506 rupees.

According to the association, the price of gold in the international market fell by 23 dollars to 3,653 dollars per ounce. It is worth noting that on the previous day, the price of gold per tola had risen by 11,500 rupees. The association had reported on the previous day that the international gold price had risen by 115 dollars to 3,676 dollars per ounce.
