Previous government lacked ability to fight province's case effectively, says KP governor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has said the previous provincial government lacked the ability to fight the province's case effectively, but now, together with the federal government, the province's rights will be secured. Addressing a ceremony held at Shaheed Benazir Bhutto Women University Peshawar on the occasion of Iqbal Day and later speaking to the media, the governor said steps are being taken to resolve the financial difficulties facing the province's public universities and to improve the performance of educational institutions. He said unnecessary recruitments in universities will be stopped, while there will be no interference in the administrative affairs of vice-chancellors.

He said the province has 36 universities facing various financial problems, efforts are under way to resolve them, and universities will be run in accordance with the decisions and orders of the vice-chancellors, with a relationship of respect and trust restored with university administrations. He said the prime minister has assured all possible cooperation from the federal government, for which he is thankful, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa will receive its due share in all federal public welfare schemes, with the province's rights raised forcefully at every relevant forum, including the NFC. He said a zero-tolerance policy against terrorism will be followed, and the province's policy will be decided in consultation with the federal government in Islamabad, not from Jalalabad or any other place.

Addressing the Iqbal Day ceremony, he said Allama Muhammad Iqbal gave Muslims awareness of selfhood, self-respect, freedom of thought and a distinct national identity, and it is now the responsibility of the young generation to turn that great dream into reality. He said Iqbal was not merely a poet but a great thinker and visionary leader whose message is of knowledge, character, self-confidence and continuous struggle, and that young people must trust their abilities and set big goals, while the daughters of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have full ability to move ahead and lead in every field, and providing them with quality education, equal opportunities and institutional support is the government's responsibility, as the education of a woman is an investment in the progress of the entire family and society.
