Mir Raza murder case: Former SHO dismissed over misconduct and mishandling of evidence

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Gulistan-i-Jauhar station house officer Kamran Qureshi was dismissed from service on Saturday over misconduct in the Mir Raza Ali murder case. The dismissal order said Qureshi failed to secure the crime scene where Ali's body was found and did not call in the Crime Scene Unit. The delay in forensic examination and the possible loss of evidence were cited as the reasons for the action against him.

A judicial commission had earlier concluded that the 25-year-old businessman's death was murder, not suicide. A joint investigation team was subsequently formed to probe the case. The dismissal marks the latest development in a case that has drawn attention to the handling of the investigation.

The case remains under investigation by the joint investigation team.
