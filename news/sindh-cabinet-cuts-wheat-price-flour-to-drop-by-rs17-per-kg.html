Sindh cabinet cuts wheat price, flour to drop by Rs17 per kg

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh cabinet on Tuesday approved a reduction of Rs500 in the wheat release price, cutting it from Rs5,000 to Rs4,500 per 40 kilograms following a fall in market prices. Briefing journalists after the cabinet meeting, Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon said the decision would bring down the price of flour by Rs15 per kg, and flour mills were directed to pass the benefit on to consumers. The provincial cabinet also announced a public holiday on Monday on account of Diwali for the Hindu community.

It approved additional funds of Rs502.409 million for five public health engineering and rural development department schemes in Jamshoro, including the main water supply scheme for Sehwan. The cabinet meeting also fixed November 15 as the start date for the 2026-27 sugarcane crushing season.
