Jhagra asks why PML-N stays silent on KP's constitutional, financial rights

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Taimur Saleem Jhagra said that while Rs160 billion was verbally announced for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Rs170 billion was being taken back from the province. He said a Rs220 billion surplus was being left with the province so that its own money would not be spent on development and the public. Jhagra said former FATA's Rs50 billion in current expenditure was not paid this year, and Rs70 billion in hydel net profit was not being paid.

He said KP's NFC share would be about Rs150 billion more if it were based on a new census instead of the 1998 census. Jhagra added that the PML-N, which he said promotes provincialism the most, lectures PTI on provincialism and new provinces while staying silent on KP's constitutional and financial rights.
