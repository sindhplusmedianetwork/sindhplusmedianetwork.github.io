Father-in-Law Confesses to Murdering Pregnant Daughter-in-Law in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Lahore have solved the murder of 32-year-old Shazia, who was five months pregnant and a mother of two daughters, within hours of the crime. The woman was murdered in the Factory Area of the city. DIG Investigation Syed Zeeshan Raza took notice of the murder, after which SP Investigation Cantt Asim Iftikhar Kamboh supervised the investigation led by Factory Area investigation in-charge Zafar Iqbal.

During questioning, the victim's father-in-law confessed to killing his daughter-in-law. Police said the accused resented that the victim's brother had eloped with his daughter and married her in court, and he strangled Shazia with a dupatta when he got the chance. The accused has been arrested, and police are preparing a challan against him on the basis of the evidence.

Officials said those involved in the killing of women deserve no concession.
