Imran Khan's sisters Uzma and Noreen released from jail as detention orders withdrawn

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dr Uzma Khan and Noreen Niazi, sisters of incarcerated PTI founder Imran Khan, were released from Kot Lakhpat jail on Thursday after their extended detention orders were withdrawn, PTI legal counsel Rana Mudassar Umer told Dawn. The two women had been detained in September under the Punjab Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance, along with their sister Aleema Khan, ahead of a protest call by the party. In a post on social media, the lawyer said the two had returned home safely.

PTI lawyer Taimur Malik also confirmed the release, saying it followed the withdrawal of what he called the unlawful detention order. He added that everyone else detained under such orders should also be released.
