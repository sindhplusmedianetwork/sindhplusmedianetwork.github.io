Turkiye's Farmers Bear the Cost of a Changing Climate Alone

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Farmers in Turkiye are being forced to cope with a changing climate largely on their own. Drought and rising costs are squeezing agriculture across the country. The country's cotton output is expected to fall 38 percent this year.

Farmers in the Soke region are cutting cotton acreage by around 60 percent. They are switching to wheat and sunflower, which need less water. Experts say adaptation is no longer optional but expensive.

Farmers today largely pay for it themselves.
