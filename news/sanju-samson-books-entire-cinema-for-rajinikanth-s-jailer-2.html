Sanju Samson Books Entire Cinema for Rajinikanth's 'Jailer 2'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian T20 team player Sanju Samson has booked an entire cinema for Rajinikanth's film Jailer 2, saying that all team members who understand Tamil will come to watch it. He had missed the film's first screening because of engagements. Samson, who wins the hearts of fans on the cricket field, counts actor Rajinikanth among his favourites, and booked the whole cinema to enjoy the film with family and friends.

In a video circulating on social media, Samson expressed his admiration for the South superstar, saying he always waited for the senior actor's next film. About his plan, Samson said he loved watching Rajinikanth's films and tried to watch as many as possible, adding that he had a programme in Delhi the previous day which was why he could not watch the film. He said he had arranged a special show for his loved ones but fortunately got the chance to book the entire cinema, had invited his friends, and his wife was also present.

He said he was very excited to watch Rajinikanth on the big screen, and all team and support staff members who knew Tamil were coming. Jailer 2 is the sequel to Rajinikanth's 2023 blockbuster Jailer, in which he returns as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian, a retired law-enforcement officer. Ramya Krishnan, Yogi Babu, Mirna and Rithu return in their earlier roles, while SJ Suryah, Mithun Chakraborty, Suraj Venjaramoodu, Vidya Balan and others are also in the cast.
