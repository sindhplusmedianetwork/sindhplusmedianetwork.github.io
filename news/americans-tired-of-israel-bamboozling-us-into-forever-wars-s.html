Americans tired of Israel 'bamboozling' US into forever wars, says Araghchi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said the US president clearly recognises that the vast majority of Americans are disgusted with Israel. In a post on X, Araghchi wrote that Americans are evidently sick and tired of Israel bamboozling US administrations into forever wars. He added that they had enough, and so has the region.

The remarks came as a senior Emirati official warned that a comprehensive political settlement between the United States and Iran to end the more than eight-month war appears difficult. US President Donald Trump, meanwhile, told CNN that renewed fighting with Iran was highly unlikely, while warning that his decision depends on whether a very strong agreement is signed. Earlier in the day, Saudi authorities briefly sent a text message to residents of Riyadh warning of a hostile aerial threat, before lifting the alert minutes later.
