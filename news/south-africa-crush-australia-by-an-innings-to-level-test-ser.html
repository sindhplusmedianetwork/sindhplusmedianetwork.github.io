South Africa Crush Australia by an Innings to Level Test Series

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

South Africa produced a commanding comeback to beat Australia by an innings and 31 runs in the second Test at St George's Park, Johannesburg, on Friday. The victory levelled the three-match series at 1-1, with the decider to be played in Cape Town from Tuesday. South Africa posted 425 in their first innings, powered by captain Temba Bavuma's 179 and Marco Jansen's 85.

Australia were bowled out for just 99 in reply. In their second innings, the visitors resumed the final day on 156 for 4 but were dismissed for 295 after the morning session was affected by rain. Travis Head made a fine 121 before being bowled by Wiaan Mulder, who took three wickets, while Kagiso Rabada claimed four.

Australian captain Pat Cummins conceded his side had failed to match their performance from the previous Test.
