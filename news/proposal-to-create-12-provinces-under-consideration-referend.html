Proposal to create 12 provinces under consideration, referendum eyed before 28th Amendment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A proposal to redraw Pakistan's administrative map and create 12 provinces is under consideration, with a nationwide referendum being weighed before a possible 28th constitutional amendment. Sources familiar with the thinking say the idea is part of an effort to improve governance, strengthen administrative performance and ensure a fairer distribution of development across the country. Under the proposed structure, Punjab could be divided into four provinces, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa into three, Balochistan into three and Sindh into two, taking the total number of provinces to 12.

Early discussions envisage progress towards the proposed amendment during the first half of 2027, with initial steps possibly taken in the first quarter. However, the proposal is still at the consideration stage and no final decision has been taken on the proposed boundaries, administrative structures or the constitutional procedure required for such a reorganisation. A key feature of the proposed process is holding a nationwide referendum before the amendment begins, so that public opinion can be sought on creating additional provinces and public support secured for the reorganisation.
