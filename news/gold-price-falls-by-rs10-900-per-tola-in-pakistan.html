Gold Price Falls by Rs10,900 Per Tola in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gold prices recorded a sharp fall in Pakistan on Thursday as reports of rising interest rates in the United States weighed on the market. According to the All Pakistan Sarafa Association, the price of 24-carat gold per tola dropped by Rs10,900 to Rs416,536, while the price of ten grams fell by Rs9,345 to Rs357,112. The price of silver per tola also declined by Rs190 to Rs5,578.

In the international market, gold slipped below the key level of $4,000 per ounce, falling by $109 to $3,940 per ounce. The decline came as investors reacted to expectations of higher borrowing costs in the US, which typically reduce demand for gold.
