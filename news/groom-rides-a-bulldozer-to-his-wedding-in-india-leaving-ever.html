Groom rides a bulldozer to his wedding in India, leaving everyone amazed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A groom in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh arrived at his bride's home riding a bulldozer instead of a luxury car or a horse. Abdul Kalam, dressed in traditional wedding attire, drove the bulldozer through the streets as part of his wedding procession. His family had tried to change his mind, but he insisted on the bulldozer.

Hundreds of people gathered along the route to watch the unusual procession. A large crowd also gathered outside the bride's home as the groom arrived. Videos of the procession were shared widely on social media, where they quickly went viral.
