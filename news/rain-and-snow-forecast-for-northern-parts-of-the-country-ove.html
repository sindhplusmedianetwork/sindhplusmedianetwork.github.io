Rain and snow forecast for northern parts of the country over next three days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Meteorological Department has forecast rain, wind and thunderstorms, with snowfall over the hills, in the upper parts of the country over the next two to three days, urging the authorities concerned to take precautionary measures to prevent any untoward incident, particularly in hilly areas. According to the Met Office, a shallow westerly wave is approaching the western and northern parts of the country, and the system is likely to intensify on November 12 and may persist over the upper regions until the morning of November 14. In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, rain, wind and thunderstorms, accompanied by isolated heavy falls and hailstorms, are expected in districts including Dir, Chitral, Swat, Kohistan, Mansehra, Abbottabad, Peshawar, Mardan and Kohat from the evening of November 10 until the morning of November 14, with snowfall also likely over the mountains.

In Punjab and Islamabad, light rain, wind and thunderstorms are expected on Wednesday, with more rain forecast for Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Murree, Lahore, Sialkot and other districts on November 12 and 13. Rain, wind and thunderstorms, accompanied by snowfall over the hills, are also expected in Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan until the afternoon of November 14. Mainly dry weather is expected across most districts of Sindh and Balochistan, though isolated rain and hailstorms are expected in parts of Balochistan on the night of November 11 and November 12.

Daytime temperatures are expected to fall by 2 to 4 degrees Celsius during the forecast period, and the department has urged provincial and district authorities to remain vigilant and take all necessary precautionary measures.
