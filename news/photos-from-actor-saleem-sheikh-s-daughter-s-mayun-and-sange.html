Photos from actor Saleem Sheikh's daughter's mayun and sangeet ceremonies surface

Photos from the mayun and sangeet ceremonies of Saleena Sheikh, the second daughter of Pakistani actor Saleem Sheikh, have surfaced on social media. At the mayun ceremony, Saleena wore a henna-coloured outfit, while her fiance was seen in a white shalwar kameez. Family members and friends took part in the traditional ubtan ritual during the ceremony.

At the sangeet, Saleena appeared in a beautiful golden saree with matching jewellery. Saleem Sheikh and the rest of the family took a full part in the wedding festivities, and the pictures quickly drew attention online. Saleem Sheikh has three daughters, and his eldest daughter Nashmia was married in 2022.

Saleem Sheikh married Nosheen Sheikh in 1999.
