10 'India-Sponsored' Terrorists Killed as Gwadar Attacks Foiled, ISPR Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The military's media wing ISPR said on Friday that security forces killed 10 India-sponsored terrorists in separate engagements in Balochistan's Gwadar district. According to the statement, the terrorists of what the state calls 'Fitna al Hindustan' attempted attacks in the general areas of the Gabd Border Crossing and Gwadar city on Oct 22. The ISPR said the attackers sought to harm the very people they claim to represent and to obstruct Balochistan's development and prosperity.

In the exchanges of fire, ten terrorists, including one female attacker, were killed, while one civilian embraced martyrdom and a few vehicles were damaged. The military said operations were concluded without any loss to security forces, restoring order and the writ of the state. It described the coercion of a Baloch woman into the attack as a crime against the state and the honour of Baloch women, calling it a cynical exploitation of Baloch women for propaganda.

President Asif Ali Zardari commended the security forces for foiling the attempts and expressed grief over the martyrdom of a citizen. The ISPR added that the counter-terrorism campaign would continue at full pace.
