Lahore Again Ranks World's Most Polluted Major City as Smog Worsens

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore was ranked the world's most polluted major city on Wednesday morning as smog thickened over the city with the onset of colder weather. According to the live ranking by Swiss air quality monitoring platform IQAir, Lahore recorded an Air Quality Index of 296 at 10am on October 28, placing it at the top of the list of major cities. Cairo ranked second with an AQI of 289, followed by Delhi at 272, while Tashkent stood fourth at 186 and Karachi fifth at 173.

The readings placed Lahore and Cairo in the 'very unhealthy' category, which can pose health risks, particularly for children, older people and those with respiratory or heart conditions. Residents were advised to limit prolonged outdoor exertion when pollution levels were high, particularly those with asthma and other respiratory conditions. Environment Protection Agency spokesperson Sajid Bashir told Dawn that authorities were monitoring industries and had installed devices to record pollution levels, and that measures were being taken to prevent stubble burning.

He said easterly winds had been consistently pushing polluted air from India's border regions into Lahore, worsening smog across central Punjab. The government was procuring and installing 30 more modern fog cannons mounted on electric trucks, with anti-smog guns deployed on a priority basis in areas with higher readings, particularly where PM10 levels were elevated.
