Shah Rukh Khan marks 61st birthday with surprise 'King' song 'Tu Mil Jaaye'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Shah Rukh Khan turned his 61st birthday on November 2 into a gift for fans, releasing the music video of 'Tu Mil Jaaye' from his upcoming film King on Instagram. November 2 always holds a special place for the superstar's fans, who gather outside his Mumbai home Mannat every year, but this time it was Khan who had the surprise in store. The romantic song features Khan and Deepika Padukone and is crooned by Arijit Singh and Ed Sheeran — with Khan himself lip-syncing to Sheeran's voice, a crossover that stunned fans.

In the caption, Khan wrote that the era of love is granted only to the fortunate, announcing the song's release and declaring that King arrives in cinemas on December 24, 2026. Fans flooded the comments with birthday wishes and excitement, one writing that the birthday belonged to the king but the gift belonged to them. King is an upcoming action thriller co-written and directed by Siddharth Anand, with a star-studded cast including Shah Rukh Khan, Suhana Khan, Abhishek Bachchan, Deepika Padukone, Anil Kapoor, Jackie Shroff, Rani Mukerji, Arshad Warsi and Raghav Juyal.
