Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi meets Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi met Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi in Islamabad, where they discussed increasing bilateral cooperation and promoting Pakistan-Iran relations. They also discussed the latest regional situation and joint steps for regional peace and stability. Araghchi praised Pakistan's positive role in reducing tensions in the region.

The meeting took place during Araghchi's visit to Pakistan, which comes as regional countries work to ease tensions. Naqvi later saw the Iranian foreign minister off at the airport after the talks.
