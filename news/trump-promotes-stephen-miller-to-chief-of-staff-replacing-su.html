Trump promotes Stephen Miller to chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump replaced chief of staff Susie Wiles with Stephen Miller on Friday, elevating the architect of some of his toughest domestic policies to run the White House. Trump called Miller one of his most trusted advisers and praised Wiles for doing an outstanding job. Miller, best known for driving Trump's immigration crackdown, takes over on Monday.

Wiles, who submitted her resignation on Thursday night, will move to Trump's political operation. The change came after Republicans lost control of Congress in this week's midterm elections. Miller currently serves as deputy chief of staff and has been one of the most influential voices in the administration.
