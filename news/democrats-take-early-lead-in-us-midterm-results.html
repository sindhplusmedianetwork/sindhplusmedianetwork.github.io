Democrats Take Early Lead in US Midterm Results

November 04, 2026 · 1 min read

Early results in the US elections have given Democrats the upper hand, with vote counting still under way in many states. According to US media, Democrats have won 45 seats in the Senate compared with 34 for Republicans, while in the House of Representatives, early calls put Democrats ahead with 73 seats to the Republicans' 34. Several leading Democrats successfully defended their seats, including Senator Mark Warner in Virginia, who defeated Republican Bert Mizusawa to secure a fourth term, and Senator Jon Ossoff in Georgia, who beat Republican Mike Collins.

Democrats also won Senate seats in Delaware, Illinois, New Jersey, Rhode Island and Massachusetts. In the House, Debbie Wasserman Schultz was re-elected from Florida's 20th district, Morgan McGarvey won Kentucky's 3rd, Jasmine Clark took Georgia's 13th, and Nikema Williams retained Georgia's 5th. Among governors, Democrat JB Pritzker was re-elected in Illinois, Maura Healey held Massachusetts, Wes Moore won Maryland, and Josh Shapiro took Pennsylvania.

Republicans also secured seats, including Hal Rogers in Kentucky's 5th House district. As counting continues, the final position of both parties in Congress will become clearer.
