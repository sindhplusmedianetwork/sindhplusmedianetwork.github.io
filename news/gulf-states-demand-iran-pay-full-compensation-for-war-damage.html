Gulf States Demand Iran Pay Full Compensation for War Damage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Gulf Cooperation Council has demanded that Iran pay full compensation for war damages. GCC Secretary General Jasim Al-Budaiwi told the UN Security Council that Iran launched thousands of missile and drone attacks on Gulf states. The attacks hit airports, ports, oil and gas centres, power plants and residential areas across the region.

He said the Gulf states were not party to the war and had tried to prevent escalation, so Iran should be held responsible for the attacks and their consequences. He added that any agreement with Iran must cover its nuclear programme, missiles, drones and support for armed groups. The demand places the question of compensation at the centre of the Council's discussion of the conflict.
