JI Announces Third Phase of Anti-Levy Protests With Friday Sit-Ins Nationwide

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Jamaat-i-Islami has announced the third phase of its protest movement against the petroleum levy, with nationwide sit-ins set for Friday. The party said on Wednesday that demonstrations would be held in all districts across the country. JI reiterated its clear and unchanged demand: the complete abolition of the petroleum levy.

The party also claimed that the government had made no further contact regarding a planned meeting between JI chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. According to the announcement, the government currently levies Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel. The Friday sit-ins will mark the third wave of the party's anti-levy campaign, signalling that the protest movement is set to continue and expand.
