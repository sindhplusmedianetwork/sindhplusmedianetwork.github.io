Karachi Destroyed After 18 Years of PPP Rule; New Administrative Units Inevitable, Says Waseem Akhtar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

MQM-P leader Waseem Akhtar has said that 18 years of PPP rule have failed and destroyed Karachi. He was speaking with the Mutahida Lawyers Forum in the city. Akhtar demanded the creation of new administrative units instead of new provinces.

He alleged fake recruitments at Shah Abdul Latif University and claimed that 1,184 lawyers hold fake degrees. He also spoke of fake domiciles and what he called the murder of merit. Akhtar said Karachi's population has crossed 4 crore, which makes governance under the current system impossible.

His remarks reflect the party's long-standing demand for a new administrative setup for the city.
