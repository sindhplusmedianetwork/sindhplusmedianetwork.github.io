Tech outage wave reaches US government as NSA reports network disruption

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read

Technology outages that shut down email for many companies and grounded thousands of flights over the past few days have now extended to the US National Security Agency. The NSA said its network experienced a 'temporary degradation' during routine maintenance, but that mission-critical capabilities remained operational. The disruptions began on Thursday, when a Microsoft outage affected many of its services, including email, and continued on Friday with a Federal Aviation Administration ground stop lasting four and a half hours.

On Monday, a United Airlines technology failure delayed about 150 flights, while a JP Morgan Chase software problem disrupted some banking services, and on Tuesday Apple said an internal update had caused an outage affecting iCloud and Apple Pay. President Donald Trump played down the incidents while attending meetings in Manila, saying 'It's fine', and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the incidents were unrelated and that 'we are not under systemic attack'. US Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton said there was no sign of a coordinated cyberattack or state-sponsored activity, and attributed the NSA outage to patches deployed for a PHP vulnerability discovered using artificial intelligence last month.

Some lawmakers, however, demanded more information from the White House about the string of disruptions.
