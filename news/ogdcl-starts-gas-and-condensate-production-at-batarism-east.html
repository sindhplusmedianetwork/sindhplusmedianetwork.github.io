OGDCL starts gas and condensate production at Batarism East-1 well in Khairpur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gas and condensate production has begun at the Batarism East-1 well in Khairpur, Sindh, the Oil and Gas Development Company Limited (OGDCL) announced on Thursday. According to the company spokesperson, the well is currently producing seven million cubic feet of gas and 200 barrels of condensate per day. The gas produced from the well has been injected into the SSGC network.

A 28-kilometre gas pipeline was laid to connect the well with the Sinjhoro plant. The spokesperson said production is expected to increase further as output stabilises. The company described the new discovery as a boost to the country's gas supply and energy security, coming at a time when Pakistan is working to shore up domestic energy resources.
