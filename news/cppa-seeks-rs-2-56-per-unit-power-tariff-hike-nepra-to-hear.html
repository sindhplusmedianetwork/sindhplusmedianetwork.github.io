CPPA seeks Rs 2.56 per unit power tariff hike; NEPRA to hear plea on Oct 29

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Central Power Purchasing Agency has requested NEPRA to approve a Rs 2.56 per unit increase in electricity prices. The request has been filed under the fuel price adjustment for September. NEPRA is set to hear the plea on October 29.

According to the agency's workings, September's average fuel cost was estimated at Rs 7.46 per unit. Total fuel costs stood at Rs 127.87 billion for 12.7 billion units received by distribution companies. If approved, the adjustment will reflect in consumers' bills for the September billing cycle.
