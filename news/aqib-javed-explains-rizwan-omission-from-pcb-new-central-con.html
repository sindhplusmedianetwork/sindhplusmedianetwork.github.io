Aqib Javed Explains Rizwan Omission from PCB New Central Contracts

By Sindh Plus Media Network

PCB Director High Performance Aqib Javed has explained the omission of Mohammad Rizwan from the new central contracts. Speaking at a press conference in Lahore, Aqib Javed said Mohammad Rizwan has great services for Pakistan cricket. He said it is not necessary that only those who have received contracts can be selected.

The report examines which big players have been left out of the PCB's new central contracts and the reasons given for Rizwan's exclusion. Rizwan's absence from the contract list has raised questions among fans and experts about the selection criteria. Aqib Javed's statement suggests that non-contracted players remain eligible for selection on merit.
