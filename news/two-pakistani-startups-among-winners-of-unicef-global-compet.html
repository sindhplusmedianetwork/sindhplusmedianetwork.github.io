Two Pakistani startups among winners of UNICEF global competition

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two Pakistani startups, Boltay Haroof and Rovila Foods, are among the 14 winners of UNICEF's 2026 global competition, in which startups from 10 countries took part. According to UNICEF Pakistan, Boltay Haroof prepares documents under the Braille system for blind people that sighted and blind people can read together, and the organisation also works on assistive technology for people with disabilities. Rovila Foods, meanwhile, prepares food products from dates and their discarded pits, with the aim of reducing food waste and helping meet nutritional needs.

The 14 winners of the global competition will receive financial support, training, expert guidance and access to an international business network to expand their businesses. Boltay Haroof founder H S Umar Farooq said the organisation's aim is to make access to information easy for everyone. He said the organisation worked with the Sindh government on the curriculum for blind children and, with the support of the State Bank of Pakistan, also promoted the use of Braille documents in the banking sector.

UNICEF's Generation Unlimited programme provides young people with financial support, training and guidance to start new business ventures and turn them into sustainable businesses.
