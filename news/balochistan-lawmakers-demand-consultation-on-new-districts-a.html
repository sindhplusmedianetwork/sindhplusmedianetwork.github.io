Balochistan Lawmakers Demand Consultation on New Districts and Divisions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan lawmakers have demanded that the government consult elected representatives before creating new divisions and districts in the province. They called for a uniform formula based on population and area to guide the creation of new administrative units. Opposition members raised the issue in the provincial assembly, objecting to the shifting of Pishin division's headquarters from Suranan to Pishin town without consulting them.

The lawmakers said such decisions should not be taken without taking elected representatives into confidence. Speaker Abdul Khaliq Achakzai summoned the Board of Revenue's senior member for next week to explain the change. The senior member is expected to brief the assembly on the reasons behind shifting the division's headquarters.
