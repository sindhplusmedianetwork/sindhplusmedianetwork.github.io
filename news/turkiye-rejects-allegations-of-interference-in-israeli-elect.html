Türkiye rejects allegations of interference in Israeli elections

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

After Qatar, Türkiye has also rejected allegations of interfering in Israel's recent elections. Türkiye's Directorate of Communications Center for Countering Disinformation said in a statement on X that claims circulating on some international media outlets and social media platforms — that Türkiye interfered in the Israeli elections — are completely baseless. It said the allegations are part of a deliberate and open disinformation campaign aimed at influencing public opinion, and appealed to the public not to believe the false news and misleading claims meant to lead people astray.

Türkiye's response came as Israel's internal security agency, Shin Bet, was tasked with investigating allegations that funds were moved from Türkiye and Qatar into Israel to influence Arab voters' decisions. The allegations had been levelled by Benjamin Netanyahu and his allies.
