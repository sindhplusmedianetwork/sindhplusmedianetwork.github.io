Muneeba Ali to Lead Pakistan in Women's Emerging Nations Trophy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Muneeba Ali will captain Pakistan in the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy 2026, with the Pakistan Cricket Board announcing a 14-member squad on Tuesday for the tournament to be held in Ajman, United Arab Emirates, from November 12 to 25. Batter Ayesha Zafar has been appointed vice-captain of the side, which will feature 12 players retained from Pakistan's previous assignment, while Sadaf Shamas and Waheeda Akhtar have been recalled to the squad. Regular captain Fatima Sana will miss the tournament as she prepares to feature in her maiden Women's Big Bash League season in Australia.

The squad announcement comes after Pakistan secured a bronze medal at the Asian Games in Japan, and wicketkeeper-batter Gull Feroza, named the best wicketkeeper-batter at the recently concluded National Women's T20 Tournament, has also been included. The batting line-up comprises Ayesha, Eman Naseer, Eyman Fatima, Sadaf, Saira Jabeen and Shawaal Zulfiqar, while Muneeba and Gull provide the wicketkeeping options. Left-arm spinner Nashra Sundhu will spearhead the bowling attack, with Momina Riasat, Tuba Hassan and Umm-e-Hani completing the spin contingent, and Tasmia Rubab and Waheeda are the two frontline pace options.

The 30-match tournament will feature 10 teams divided into two groups, with Pakistan placed in Group B alongside Bangladesh, Thailand, the Netherlands and Zimbabwe. The teams will first compete in a single-league round-robin stage, after which the top three sides from each group will advance to the Super Six stage, with the top two progressing to the final on November 25. The Pakistan squad will assemble in Lahore on November 6 and attend a pre-tournament training camp on November 7, 8 and 9 before departing for the UAE.
