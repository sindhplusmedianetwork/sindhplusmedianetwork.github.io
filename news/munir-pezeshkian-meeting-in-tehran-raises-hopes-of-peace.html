Munir-Pezeshkian Meeting in Tehran Raises Hopes of Peace

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir held talks with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and other top leaders in Tehran on Tuesday. Munir also met parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and Mohsen Rezaei. Iranian leaders told him that the United States must take the first steps to rebuild trust, and that Tehran's seven-point proposal could serve as a starting point for renewed negotiations.

Iran also stated that no enriched nuclear material would leave the country. No breakthrough or formal agreement was announced. In a positive sign, the Pakistan-Iran border crossings reopened on Tuesday.
