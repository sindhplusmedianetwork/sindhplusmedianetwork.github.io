Trump to meet Putin and Xi together on APEC sidelines

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The White House has announced that the American president will hold a trilateral meeting with the Russian and Chinese presidents next Thursday on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen, China. A White House spokeswoman told reporters that the talks would address a number of issues, including reducing nuclear weapons worldwide. The meeting was announced in Washington on Friday, adding a high-profile diplomatic gathering to the summit's schedule.

The three leaders are set to discuss shared concerns at a time when global powers are seeking channels for dialogue on security and economic matters. Further details of the agenda and the format of the meeting were not immediately disclosed.
