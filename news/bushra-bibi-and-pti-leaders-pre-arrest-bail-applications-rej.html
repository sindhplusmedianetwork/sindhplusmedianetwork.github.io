Bushra Bibi and PTI leaders' pre-arrest bail applications rejected

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Islamabad anti-terrorism court has dismissed the pre-arrest bail applications of Bushra Bibi and Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf leaders in cases linked to protests. Judge Abu Al-Hasnat Zulqarnain heard a total of 250 bail applications of PTI leaders and workers. The court rejected the bail applications of Bushra Bibi, Junaid Akbar, Latif Khosa, Naeem Panjotha, Asad Qaiser, Ali Zaman, Iman Waseem, Atif Khan and Fazal Muhammad.

During the hearing, Raoof Hasan and Shehryar Afridi appeared before the court and marked their attendance. The court extended the bail of PTI workers who appeared until February 11. The judge rejected the bail applications of PTI leaders who failed to appear before the court.
