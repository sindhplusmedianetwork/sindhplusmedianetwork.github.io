Gaza Charities Thank Macklemore as Free Palestine Tour Wraps Up

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gaza charities have expressed deep gratitude to American rapper Macklemore as his sold-out Free Palestine Tour concludes. Proceeds from the three concerts will be distributed among 12 organisations, including Medical Aid for Palestinians, Anera and UNRWA USA. Macklemore launched the three-concert European tour in Dublin on October 26.

The tour came after he was dropped as Ed Sheeran's support act in September over his comments on Gaza. The final performance is scheduled at London's OVO Arena Wembley.
