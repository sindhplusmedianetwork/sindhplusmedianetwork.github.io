Firing on Nomads' Huts in Faisalabad Kills Three Women, Including a Girl

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gunmen opened fire on the huts of nomadic families in Faisalabad. Three women were killed in the firing. One of the victims was a young girl.

The attack targeted the huts where the nomadic families were living. The report did not give further details about the attackers or the circumstances of the firing.
