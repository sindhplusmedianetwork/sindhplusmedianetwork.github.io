Umrah Pilgrim Vaccinations Stopped at Dispensary Near Karachi Airport

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Vaccination of Umrah pilgrims and other travellers to Saudi Arabia has been stopped at a dispensary near Karachi airport. Sources say Border Health halted meningitis and influenza vaccinations on Saturday, acting on verbal orders issued the previous day. The orders are reportedly linked to an inquiry by the Federal Investigation Agency.

The vaccine counter at Karachi airport itself was closed last month, and a tender for vaccine procurement has also been cancelled. Border Health Services had, however, issued a letter for mandatory vaccination to resume from November 20. It is not yet clear how the latest halt will affect pilgrims preparing to travel in the coming days.
