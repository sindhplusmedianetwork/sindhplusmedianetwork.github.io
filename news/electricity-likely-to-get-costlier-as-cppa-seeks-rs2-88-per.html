Electricity likely to get costlier as CPPA seeks Rs2.88 per unit hike

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Central Power Purchasing Agency has filed a request with the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority seeking an increase of Rs2.88 per unit in electricity prices under October's fuel price adjustment. If approved, consumers are likely to face an additional burden of Rs30 billion. According to the application, 10.35 billion units of electricity were generated in October at a total production cost of Rs111.29 billion.

The request has been filed under the monthly fuel price adjustment mechanism, through which changes in fuel costs are passed on to consumers. NEPRA will hear the request on November 26, after which a decision on the proposed increase will be announced.
