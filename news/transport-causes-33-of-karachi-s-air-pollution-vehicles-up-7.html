Transport Causes 33% of Karachi's Air Pollution; Vehicles Up 770% in 35 Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Transport is responsible for 33 per cent of Karachi's air pollution, while Lahore loses seven years of life expectancy to smog, a new PIDE study has found. The study 'More Vehicles, Less Mobility', authored by Raheel Hasan Qadar and Irem Batool, says Pakistan's vehicle count surged more than 770 per cent in 35 years, from 4.47 million in 1990 to over 39 million in 2025. Private vehicles jumped from 1.42 million to 35.87 million in the same period, while transport CO2 emissions rose from 13.8 million tonnes to 43.6 million tonnes by 2023.

The transport share in air pollution was 81.3 per cent for carbon monoxide in Lahore, 53 per cent in Islamabad-Rawalpindi and 51 per cent in Peshawar. The researchers called for urgent curbs on private vehicle growth and investment in mass transit.
