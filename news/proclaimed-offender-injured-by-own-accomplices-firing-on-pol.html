Proclaimed Offender Injured by Own Accomplices' Firing on Police Team in Islamabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Armed men fired on a police team near Shakarparian in Islamabad in an attempt to free a detained proclaimed offender, injuring the accused with their own bullets. An Islamabad Police spokesman said a Kohsar police team was taking the detained accused, Sher Ali alias Ali, for recovery on his identification when armed accomplices hiding near Shakarparian opened indiscriminate fire within the limits of Aabpara police station. During the firing, the proclaimed offender was injured by his own accomplices' bullets, while the police team remained safe due to bulletproof jackets and other protective measures.

The injured accused was shifted to hospital for medical treatment. Police said the accused is a previous record holder wanted in multiple cases of robbery and other serious crimes in Rawalpindi and Islamabad. The accomplices fled taking advantage of darkness, and a search operation is under way for their arrest.
