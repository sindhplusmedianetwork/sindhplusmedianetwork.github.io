Saudi-Led Coalition and Houthi Militia Threaten to Attack Each Other's Ports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Saudi military coalition and the Houthi militia have threatened to attack each other's ports. The Saudi-led military coalition directed Yemeni citizens to leave Hodeidah, while in response the Houthi militia said if Hodeidah is attacked, it will target Jeddah port and all Saudi ports along the Red Sea. The Saudi-led military coalition, without mentioning an attack, had told Yemeni citizens to leave Hodeidah to ensure their safety.

Hodeidah is under the control of the Houthi militia, and this port is the main source of food and fuel supply to Houthi militia areas. Along with the threat of attacks on the port, the Houthi militia also warned international airlines using Saudi airspace and said in the next few hours Saudi airspace will be the field of their operations. Therefore airlines should not use Saudi airspace, and the Houthi militia has also told shipping companies to withdraw their ships from Saudi ports.
