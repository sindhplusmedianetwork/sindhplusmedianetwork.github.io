150 Killed in Drone Attack on Market in Disputed Abyei Region

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

South Sudanese officials said Sudan's military carried out drone strikes that hit a market in the disputed Abyei region, killing more than 150 people. The strike ranks among the deadliest attacks in a war that erupted in April 2023 between the Sudanese Armed Forces and their rivals. The attack demonstrated the growing lethality of airstrikes in Sudan's war, which has increasingly spilled across the country's borders.

The Abyei region has long been contested between Sudan and South Sudan. International observers have condemned the targeting of a civilian market.
