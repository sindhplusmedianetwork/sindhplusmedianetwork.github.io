Anchorperson Rabia Ahsan's husband gets four more days in police custody

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Rawalpindi court on Saturday remanded Ahsan Tahir, husband of television anchorperson Rabia Ahsan, to a further four days in police custody in connection with her murder. The court also directed that a polygraph test be conducted at the Punjab Forensic Science Agency in Lahore on November 16. Rabia, a 41-year-old anchorperson associated with 92 News, was found shot dead at her home in Rawalpindi last week.

The case was registered on the complaint of her brother, who accused Ahsan of shooting her. Police said he failed to produce a licence for the pistol.
