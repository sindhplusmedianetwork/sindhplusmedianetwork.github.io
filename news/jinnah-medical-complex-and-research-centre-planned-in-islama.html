Jinnah Medical Complex and Research Centre planned in Islamabad at Rs212.734 billion

By Sindh Plus Media Network

The Jinnah Medical Complex and Research Centre is being planned in Islamabad at an estimated cost of 212.734 billion rupees. The project aims to strengthen specialised healthcare services, medical research and professional training. According to official sources, the complex will bring advanced clinical facilities, research and professional learning under one integrated institution.

The project is aligned with the health pillar of the government's URAAN Pakistan programme. Officials say the centre will improve access to advanced treatment and help train more medical professionals. Work on the project is expected to move ahead as part of the government's plan to upgrade health infrastructure in the capital.
