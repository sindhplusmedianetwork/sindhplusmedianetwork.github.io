KCCI Seeks Joint Platform of Chambers to Resolve Business Issues

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Chamber of Commerce and Industry President Talat Mehmood has called for a joint platform of five to six major chambers across Pakistan to raise a strong collective voice to resolve common business and industrial issues. Addressing a meeting hosted by the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry on Wednesday, he said the proposed forum should meet every month on a rotational basis. He noted that individual representations often failed to achieve results.

He also cautioned against excessive reliance on overseas remittances, noting that exports stood at about $30bn against remittances of around $41bn. He urged policies encouraging overseas Pakistanis to invest at home.
