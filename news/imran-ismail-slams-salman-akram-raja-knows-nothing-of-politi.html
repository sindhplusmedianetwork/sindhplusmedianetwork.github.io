Imran Ismail Slams Salman Akram Raja: Knows Nothing of Politics

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Sindh governor Imran Ismail has launched a scathing attack on PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja. In a statement on X, Ismail said Raja has ruined the party and does not know the ABC of politics or the true spirit of PTI. He expressed regret over the resignation of Firdous Shamim Naqvi, describing him as a sincere worker of the party.

The criticism highlights the growing internal rifts within PTI. Neither Raja nor the party's central leadership has responded to the remarks so far.
