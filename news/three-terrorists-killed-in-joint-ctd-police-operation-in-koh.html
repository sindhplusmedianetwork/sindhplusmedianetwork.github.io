Three Terrorists Killed in Joint CTD, Police Operation in Kohat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Counter Terrorism Department Kohat Region of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Police conducted an operation near Mankara hill on the basis of secret information and three terrorists were killed in an exchange of fire, while weapons were recovered from their possession. According to the statement issued by CTD Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, CTD Kohat Region received secret information that armed terrorists linked to Ittehad-ul-Mujahideen Pakistan, who are involved in various terrorist activities, had set up a blockade near Mankara hill on MRS Bazid Khel Road in Kohat district. The statement said on the basis of confirmed information, the Counter Terrorism Department Kohat Region and District Police Kohat started a joint intelligence-based operation against the terrorists at 6:30pm and when police and CTD teams reached the required place, the terrorists present there started indiscriminate firing on seeing the personnel, upon which the personnel took effective retaliatory action in self-defence.

CTD said after the firing stopped, a search operation was conducted in the area during which three terrorists were found dead while their other accomplices succeeded in escaping from the spot, and a search operation is continuing in the area to trace and arrest the escaped terrorists. The statement said three SMGs and nine magazines were recovered from the possession of the killed terrorists and according to initial information, two of the killed terrorists were identified as Abdul Hai and Abdul Rasheed. CTD said the identified terrorist Abdul Hai belongs to Kohat district and was wanted as the mastermind of the Kohat Police Lines suicide attack and in the Billitang bank robbery incident, while the second terrorist Abdul Rasheed was also wanted in the Billitang bank robbery incident, while the third killed terrorist has not yet been identified for which necessary identification and legal action is continuing.

CTD said the killed terrorists belong to Ittehad-ul-Mujahideen Pakistan while further investigation is continuing regarding the incident and all available resources are being used for the arrest of the escaped terrorists, and Inspector General Police Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Zulfiqar Hameed and Additional Inspector General CTD appreciated the bravery, professional skill and dutifulness of the police personnel on the successful operation.
