British Bodybuilder, 33, Dies After Collapsing on Stage in Telford

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

British bodybuilder Matus Khumora, 33, has died after collapsing on stage during the PCA British Finals in Telford, England. Khumora was competing in the classic bodybuilding medium category when he suddenly fell, and medics rushed to treat him at the venue. He was airlifted by helicopter to a nearby hospital, where doctors were unable to save him.

Organisers of the event expressed deep condolences to his family. Khumora was new to competitive bodybuilding — he had entered his first event only about two weeks earlier and finished second. He had married just two months before his death.

The exact cause of death has not yet been established, and organisers said further details would follow.
