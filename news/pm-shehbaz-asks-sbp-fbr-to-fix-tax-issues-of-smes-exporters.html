PM Shehbaz asks SBP, FBR to fix tax issues of SMEs, exporters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Monday directed the State Bank of Pakistan and the Federal Board of Revenue to work together to resolve tax-related issues faced by small and medium-sized enterprises and exporters. Chairing a meeting in Islamabad to review lending facilities for priority sectors, he also directed the finance ministry to address financing problems faced by stakeholders in the SME, housing and agriculture sectors and formulate a comprehensive strategy. He called for a comprehensive risk-coverage mechanism to make it easier for small and medium businesses to obtain loans, saying the development of SMEs, housing and agriculture was among the government's foremost priorities for economic growth and youth employment.

The prime minister praised banks that had performed well in extending loans under government initiatives, but directed the SBP and finance ministry not to extend concessions to banks slow in financing farmers, SMEs and beneficiaries of the Prime Minister's Apna Ghar Scheme. Officials briefed the meeting that Rs511 billion in loans had been approved under the Apna Ghar Programme covering 86,899 applications, with Rs91 billion disbursed to 15,584 applicants, while agriculture lending reached Rs1,329 billion for about 3.5 million people and SME financing stood at Rs1,148 billion for 342,458 businesses. The government set a target of raising agriculture and SME lending to Rs1,500 billion by the end of fiscal year 2027, and noted that the SBP had launched the SME Connect digital portal for loan applications.
