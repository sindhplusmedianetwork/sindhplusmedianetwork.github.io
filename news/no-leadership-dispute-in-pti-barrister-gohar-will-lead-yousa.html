No Leadership Dispute in PTI, Barrister Gohar Will Lead: Yousafzai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Khyber Pakhtunkhwa information secretary Shaukat Yousafzai said there is no disagreement over party leadership and Barrister Gohar will continue to lead. Speaking on Geo News, he said Ali Amin Gandapur has demanded inclusion in the negotiation process. Yousafzai added that Mahmood Achakzai has shown flexibility.

He said campaigns by YouTubers against institutions had harmed the party. The party will not bow to pressure from YouTubers abroad, he added. His remarks come amid speculation about internal rifts within the PTI leadership.

Yousafzai's statement sought to project unity as the party navigates ongoing political challenges.
