PSX Slides 1,335 Points as Saudi-Houthi Conflict Rattles Investors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange remained under pressure through the first half of Friday's session. The benchmark KSE-100 Index slid 1,335.01 points, or 0.81 per cent, to 162,589.30 by the Friday prayers break. The index had opened near 163,869 and touched an intraday high of 163,966.86 before sellers took control, dragging it to a low of 162,407.61.

Selling was visible across major sectors, including commercial banks, oil and gas exploration, oil marketing companies, power generation, cement, automobile assemblers and refineries. Heavyweights such as HUBC, PPL, POL, UBL and MCB traded lower. Market activity stood at 144,593 trades with 159.1 million shares changing hands at a value of roughly Rs7.66 billion.

Market participants attributed the weakness to intensified fighting between Saudi Arabia and Yemen-based Houthis, which has lifted Brent crude to near multi-month highs around $113-114. Higher energy prices are raising concerns over Pakistan's import bill, external account pressures and the inflation outlook.
