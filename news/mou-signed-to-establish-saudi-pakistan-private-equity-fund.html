MoU signed to establish Saudi-Pakistan Private Equity Fund

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Saudi-Pakistan Joint Business Council signed a memorandum of understanding on Monday for the establishment of the Saudi-Pakistan Private Equity Fund. Speaking at a ceremony held in Karachi, Prince Mansour bin Mohammed Al Saud said Saudi Arabia and Pakistan are bound by bonds of friendship and brotherhood that are far deeper than any agreement, and the fund will give that relationship an equally strong commercial foundation. He said Pakistan is a natural partner of the Kingdom and Saudi investors are looking for partners and markets where they can grow with confidence, adding that after a careful review of Pakistan, a more stable economy and real opportunities are now visible.

He said that by investing alongside their Pakistani brothers in both countries, years of deliberation and study are being turned into practical investment that will benefit the people of both countries and lay the foundation of a lasting partnership. Arif Habib, head of the Arif Habib Group, said the fund reflects confidence in profitable investment opportunities in Pakistan, and that perceptions about Pakistan have long clouded reality, while this agreement shows that serious investors are ready to take practical steps. He said Pakistani investors are leading the way in confidence in the national economy and investing their share, while Saudi brothers are investing alongside them, and directing capital towards the monetisation of the government's land bank will turn idle assets into productive investment.

Under the memorandum, a joint proposed fund worth hundreds of millions has been established, with Saudi and Pakistani private investors contributing equally, while 75 percent of the fund will be invested in projects in Pakistan and 25 percent in Saudi Arabia. The fund intends to invest in fast-growing private businesses, including agriculture, mining and construction, and will also review commercial projects emerging from the Government of Pakistan's land bank monetisation programme, including airports, motorways, ports and maritime development, as well as commercial investment opportunities across Saudi Arabia, including the holy cities.
