Salman Akram Raja says meeting with Imran Khan had set purposes and political discussion was allowed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf leader Salman Akram Raja has said his meeting with party founder Imran Khan had specific purposes and that political discussion was allowed during it. Speaking on a television programme, Raja said more favoured people may be sent for meetings in future and that Imran Khan has said he will name the people for meetings, after which they will be arranged accordingly. Raja said Imran Khan has expressed confidence in Mahmood Achakzai and Raja Nasir Abbas, and that no demand for an alternative to him was made.

He added that if Imran Khan gives even the slightest hint, he will step aside and move on. Raja said Imran Khan favours smaller provinces and units for better governance, but not through force in an environment where votes are not respected. He said creating provinces through a referendum would require a basic change in the Constitution, and added that no clear instructions have come from Imran Khan on accepting this assembly's constitutional amendment, which is why the party did not accept the 26th and 27th amendments.
