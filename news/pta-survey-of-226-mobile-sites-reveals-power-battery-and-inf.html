PTA Survey of 226 Mobile Sites Reveals Power, Battery and Infrastructure Flaws

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In a nationwide survey by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) to check the quality of telecom services, non-supply of electricity, flaws in alternative arrangements, defects in devices, faults in batteries and generators, issues related to the protection of fibre optic cables, and other management and infrastructure flaws have been revealed. According to the PTA, under the ongoing nationwide field monitoring to improve the quality and provision of telecom services, the PTA inspected 226 mobile sites from October 29 to November 11, 2026, and in this way the total number of telecom sites inspected so far has exceeded 1,127. These inspections were carried out in Karachi, Lahore, Bahawalpur, Quetta, Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Pindi Gheb, Charsadda, Peshawar, Mirpur, Skardu, Kharmang, Haripur, Mansehra, Gwadar and Sargodha, in which contacts were made with the district administration, elected representatives, local stakeholders and telecom operators so that help could be taken in identifying flaws in services and remedying them in time.

During the inspections, flaws in electricity supply and alternative arrangements, defects in devices, faults in batteries and generators, issues related to the protection of fibre optic cables, and other management and infrastructure flaws were identified. The PTA is taking necessary steps through continuous field monitoring and contact with relevant stakeholders for the remedy of these flaws so that the overall quality of telecom services across the country improves, their provision becomes more reliable, and in case of a fault it becomes possible to maintain the service or restore it quickly.
