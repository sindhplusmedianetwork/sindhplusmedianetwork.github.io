Man Pulled Alive From Rubble Two Days After Karachi Building Collapse

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man trapped under the rubble of a collapsed under-construction six-storey building near Karachi's Baloch Colony was rescued alive on Friday, two days after the building came down. Rescue 1122 said 28-year-old Muhammad Adil was pulled out alive and called the rescue a breakthrough. The building collapsed on Wednesday, killing three people and injuring 14 others.

Four more bodies were recovered from the debris on Friday, taking the death toll to eight. The search for anyone else who may still be trapped under the rubble was continuing. Rescue workers remained at the site as the operation went on.
