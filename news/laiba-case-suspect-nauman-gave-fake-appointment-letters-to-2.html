Laiba case: suspect Nauman gave fake appointment letters to 21 people

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New revelations have surfaced in the suicide case of student Laiba Ejaz, as investigators found that the deceased suspect Nauman had defrauded 21 people besides Laiba. During the investigation, 21 fake appointment letters were recovered from Nauman. According to sources, he issued the fake Ministry of Defence appointment letters to 21 different people and collected money from them in return for the letters.

Four of his own cousins were also among those given the fake letters. Meanwhile, Islamabad Inspector General of Police Syed Ali Nasir Rizvi visited the home of gold-medalist student Laiba Ejaz and offered condolences to her bereaved family.
