Hafiz Naeem Says People's Power Will Expose Mafias, Announces Rallies on Nov 22 and 29

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman has said his party is emerging as a major political force in the country. Speaking at a press conference in Lahore, he said the power of the people will expose mafias and bring them under control. He said the party's struggle has brought relief to the public on electricity and petrol prices, and no further unjust agreements with independent power producers will be tolerated.

He said the economy is being damaged to benefit bank mafias, while land, sugar, flour and bank mafias are linked to one another. He said Jamaat-e-Islami has always supported small administrative units and the transfer of powers to local governments under the Constitution. He noted that local body elections have not been held in Punjab since 2015.

He announced that the party will hold a rally in Balochistan on November 22 and a historic rally at Minar-e-Pakistan in Lahore on November 29.
