Karachi Mayor Lays Foundation Stone of Rs60 Million Clifton Road Project

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Mayor Barrister Murtaza Wahab has laid the foundation stone of a development project aimed at improving and rehabilitating Adam Road in Clifton. The work will be carried out along the stretch from Chaudhry Khaliq-uz-Zaman Road to McNeil Road, and according to an official statement the project will cost Rs60 million. It envisages the construction of a 2,500-running-foot-long single carriageway and a 45-foot-wide road, along with a 500-running-foot-long paver block road with an average width of 24 feet.

The project also includes the rehabilitation of the sewerage system on Adam Road. Addressing the ceremony, the mayor said providing improved transportation facilities to citizens and rehabilitating the city's basic infrastructure were among the government's top priorities. He said an announcement had been made at the beginning of the current year to spend Rs68 billion on improving the city's roads, work on these projects had already commenced, and the target was to complete them during the current financial year.

He also announced that construction of a bridge on Nishtar Road had commenced, which would facilitate commuters arriving from the Lyari Expressway and was expected to be completed within 100 days and opened to the public by the middle of January next year.
