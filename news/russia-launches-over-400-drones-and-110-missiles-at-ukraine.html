Russia Launches Over 400 Drones and 110 Missiles at Ukraine, Eight Killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russia carried out a large-scale air attack on Ukraine, launching more than 400 drones and over 110 missiles, in which at least eight people were killed and 28 were injured. According to British media, the Russian attacks also included 27 ballistic missiles. Ukrainian officials said Russia targeted nine regions of the country including the capital Kyiv, as a result of which the embassies of Austria and the Netherlands were also affected.

According to media reports, Poland has accused Russia of violating its airspace. On the other hand, the French foreign minister demanded an emergency meeting of the United Nations Security Council on the situation in Ukraine and said Russia will have to answer for these attacks.
