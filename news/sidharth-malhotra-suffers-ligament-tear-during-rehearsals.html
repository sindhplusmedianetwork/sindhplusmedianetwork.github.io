Sidharth Malhotra Suffers Ligament Tear During Rehearsals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Sidharth Malhotra has revealed he suffered a ligament tear in his leg during rehearsals, sharing a photograph of the limb fitted in a protective walking boot. Posting on Instagram on October 13, the actor joked, “I took break a leg too seriously,” alongside a hospital emoji. According to reports, the injury occurred just hours before Reliance's The Grand Navratri celebration in Jamnagar, where he had been rehearsing for a performance.

Despite the injury, Malhotra went ahead with his appearance and made sure the show went on, later thanking the hospital staff who treated him. He has, however, been advised to skip the following day's performance to allow the injury time to heal. The update comes shortly after Malhotra and his wife Kiara Advani announced on October 7 that they are expecting their second child.

The couple, who married in February 2023, welcomed their daughter Saraayah in 2025. On the work front, Malhotra recently starred in the fantasy folklore film The Vvaan: Force of the Forrest, released on September 25.
