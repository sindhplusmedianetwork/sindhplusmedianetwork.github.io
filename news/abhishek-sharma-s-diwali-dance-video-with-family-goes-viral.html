Abhishek Sharma's Diwali dance video with family goes viral

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian opener Abhishek Sharma celebrated Diwali with his family in Amritsar, dancing to singer Daler Mehndi's popular song 'Bolo Ta Ra Ra'. A video of the celebrations, shared by his sister Komal Sharma, has gone viral on social media, showing him dancing with his family. Komal captioned the video 'Bolo Ta Ra Ra', and the celebrations included traditional prayers, music and dance.

After a successful T20 international series against New Zealand, Abhishek has joined the India A squad for the ACC Men's Rising Stars Asia Cup in Doha from November 19 to 29. Tilak Varma will captain the side, while Vaibhav Suryavanshi, Rinku Singh and Prabhsimran Singh are also part of the squad.
