Lahore Air Pollution Stayed at Dangerous Levels in First Week of November

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Air pollution in Lahore remained at unhealthy to dangerous levels during November 1 to 7, with the city's average Air Quality Index recorded at 268, according to the Pakistan Air Quality Initiative. The index peaked at 335 on November 2, while average PM2.5 over the seven days stood at 215.5 micrograms per cubic metre, about 14 times the World Health Organization's limit of 15. Five of the seven days were rated very unhealthy and two were rated hazardous.

The report links the pollution to fire incidents, weather conditions and other local sources. It notes that PM2.5 levels were 25 percent higher than the same period last year. The week was the third worst for air pollution in Lahore in a nine-year record.
