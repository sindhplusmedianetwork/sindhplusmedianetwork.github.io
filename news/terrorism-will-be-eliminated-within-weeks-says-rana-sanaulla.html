Terrorism Will Be Eliminated Within Weeks, Says Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rana Sanaullah, the prime minister's adviser on political affairs, has said that terrorism in the country will be eliminated within the next few weeks. He said the military leadership, under Field Marshal Asim Munir, conveyed in a briefing that the task was not a difficult one for the armed forces and law-enforcement agencies. Sanaullah was speaking on Geo News' programme Capital Talk.

He said the prime minister did not discuss banning the PTI at the Peshawar apex committee meeting. According to him, terrorist formations would be expelled from the area within two to three months. He said the military leadership reported the presence of 201 terrorist formations across 12 districts, and that local residents were being persuaded to expel them, with support ready if they did.
