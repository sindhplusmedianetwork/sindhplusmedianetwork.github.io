Smog Chokes Lahore and Other Punjab Cities as Pollution Hits Alarming Levels

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Air pollution has reached alarming levels in Lahore and several other cities across Punjab, according to a global environmental website. The report placed Multan as the most polluted city in Pakistan, while Lahore ranked first among the world's major polluted cities. Lahore's overall Air Quality Index has crossed 400, with the Paragon area recording a severe 728.

Readings of 615 in Allama Iqbal Town and 435 in Model Town underlined how widespread the smog has become. Other Punjab cities were also engulfed, with Multan at 566, Faisalabad at 455 and Rahim Yar Khan at 483. Health experts warned that pollution at these levels poses a serious threat to public health.

Citizens have been urged to take precautionary measures to protect themselves from the smog.
