New Zealand Cling On in Wellington After India Build Mammoth Lead

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

New Zealand were 119-2 at close on day four in Wellington as they fought to save the first Test after India declared on 720-9 with a first-innings lead of 352. Rishabh Pant scored 128 for India in the innings. Devon Conway was unbeaten on 59 for the hosts at stumps.

Rachin Ravindra was unbeaten on 27 alongside him. New Zealand still trail by 233 runs to make India bat again heading into day five.
