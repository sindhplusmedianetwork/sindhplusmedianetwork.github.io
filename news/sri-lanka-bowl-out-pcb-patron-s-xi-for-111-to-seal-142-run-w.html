Sri Lanka bowl out PCB Patron's XI for 111 to seal 142-run warm-up win

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Keshara Nuwantha and Eshan Malinga bowled Sri Lanka to a 142-run victory over PCB Patron's XI in their three-day warm-up game at the Oval Ground in Islamabad on Friday. Chasing 254, Patron's XI were bowled out for 111, with Nuwantha taking 4 for 26 and Malinga 3 for 6. The warm-up match gave Sri Lanka a comfortable win ahead of the Test series.

The first Test of the two-match series begins on November 9 at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. Pakistan will look to regroup after the heavy defeat in the practice game.
