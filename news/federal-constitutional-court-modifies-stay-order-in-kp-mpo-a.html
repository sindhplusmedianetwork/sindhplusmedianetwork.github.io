Federal Constitutional Court Modifies Stay Order in KP MPO Arrest Cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Constitutional Court has modified its stay order in the case of arrests under 3MPO in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. A two-member bench headed by Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan and comprising Justice Aamer Farooq heard the case. The court accepted PTI's application and amended the stay order issued on 5 November.

Barrister Gohar argued that the 5 November order had affected the proceedings of the Peshawar High Court and halted lawyers' practice. The court observed that the stay applied to governor's rule and MPO-related matters, and that the Peshawar High Court can hear other cases under Article 199. The court allowed the Peshawar Bar Council to become a party to the case, and adjourned the hearing for an indefinite period.
