Komal Rani's new romantic pictures with Govinda go viral

Pictures of Bollywood actor Govinda with actress Komal Rani are circulating on social media, drawing mixed reactions from users. In the viral pictures, Komal Rani is seen in a purple sari while Govinda wears a coat and trousers. Some fans reacted with red heart emojis, while one user commented that a man with money can fall in love at any age.

Reports note that rumours of separation and divorce between Govinda and his wife Sunita Ahuja had surfaced when news of his friendship with Komal Rani began circulating. Govinda had also visited a temple with Komal to pray for the success of his new film 'Roopa'. The actor said he had earlier visited Mumbai's well-known Lalbaugcha Raja temple for the same purpose, as he has done with Karisma Kapoor and Raveena Tandon in the past.

Govinda also reacted to the discussion around his new film, saying work on it has only just begun, and claimed that a planned propaganda campaign is being run against him to distance him from his family and damage his reputation.
