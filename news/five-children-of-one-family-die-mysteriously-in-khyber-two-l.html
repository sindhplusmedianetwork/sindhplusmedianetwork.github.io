Five children of one family die mysteriously in Khyber, two left unconscious

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five young children from one family have died and two others fell unconscious after a mysterious illness in Khyber district. The seven children from Qaziano Killi in Bazaar Zakha Khel tehsil fell unconscious and were being taken to a hospital in Peshawar when five of them died on the way. The other two children are being treated at Hayatabad Medical Complex in Peshawar.

Police said the children belonged to the family of a man named Shah Jee. All five children who died were under five years of age, including three girls and two boys. Deputy Commissioner Khyber Shahid Ali said a special team of the district administration immediately shifted the affected children to Peshawar, where they are receiving full care and treatment.

Initial medical tests of the children came back clear, but the actual cause of the illness is still unknown, and medical experts at Hayatabad Medical Complex are investigating it.
