England Cricket Team Arrives in Pakistan for Tri-Nation ODI Series

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The England cricket team has arrived in Pakistan to take part in the tri-nation ODI series, where officials of the Pakistan Cricket Board welcomed the visiting side. The ODI tri-series featuring Pakistan, England and Sri Lanka begins on October 18, with the opening match between Pakistan and Sri Lanka scheduled at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. Pakistan and England will face each other on October 20 at the same venue in Rawalpindi.

Matches of the tri-series will be played in Rawalpindi and Lahore, while the final is set for October 31 at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium. Sri Lanka's ODI squad had already reached Islamabad two days earlier to participate in the series. Captains Shaheen Shah Afridi, Harry Brook and Kusal Mendis will unveil the trophy on October 17, and all three skippers will hold pre-series press conferences after the unveiling ceremony.
