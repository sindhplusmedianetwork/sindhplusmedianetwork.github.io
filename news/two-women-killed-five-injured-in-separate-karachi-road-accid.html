Two Women Killed, Five Injured in Separate Karachi Road Accidents

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two women, including a teenage girl, were killed and five other people were injured in separate road accidents across Karachi. In Bhains Colony, a dumper hit a motorcycle carrying siblings who were returning from tuition, killing the teenage girl, while rescue officials said others on the motorcycle were also hurt. Angry bystanders stoned the dumper and broke its windscreen after the crash, and police said the driver fled the scene while the vehicle was seized.

In a separate incident near Tariq Road, a woman pedestrian who was seriously injured in a traffic accident died during treatment at hospital. A pedestrian was also seriously injured after being hit by a water tanker on main Korangi Road, while a truck overturned near the Northern Bypass and a taxi hit a footpath on the Gulbai Shershah bridge, leaving several more people injured. Police have taken the vehicles involved into custody and are searching for the drivers who fled.
