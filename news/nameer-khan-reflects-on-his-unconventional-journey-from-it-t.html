Nameer Khan reflects on his unconventional journey from IT to acting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actor Nameer Khan has reflected on his move from information technology into acting, describing himself as a slow learner who succeeds through hard work. Speaking with Ahmed Ali Butt, he said he tries to stay away from fame and glamour. He recalled leaving his software engineering job in 2015 and starting with commercials, including a Coke advertisement.

He later moved to Montreal in 2018 for a master's degree. Khan, currently known for playing Zamil in Dar-e-Nijaat, said his inspiration to enter the field came from cinema.
