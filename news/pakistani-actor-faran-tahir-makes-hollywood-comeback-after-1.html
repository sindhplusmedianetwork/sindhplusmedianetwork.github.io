Pakistani actor Faran Tahir makes Hollywood comeback after 18 years in Marvel's 'Vision Quest'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani-American actor Faran Tahir has made a major Hollywood comeback, returning to the Marvel universe 18 years after his memorable debut. Tahir appears as the villain in the sixth episode of Marvel Studios' series 'Vision Quest,' once again proving his mettle on the international stage. He first won global recognition in the 2008 blockbuster 'Iron Man,' where he played Raza, the dangerous militant who kidnapped Tony Stark.

Eighteen years on, he has reprised the same role — this time with a markedly different look and style. In the new episode, he is seen alongside armed companions attempting to capture Vision. Tahir's long-awaited return is being celebrated as a proud moment for Pakistan's showbiz industry, as he continues to represent Pakistani artists at the highest level of international entertainment.
