Petrol price cut by Re1 per litre, diesel unchanged

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has announced petroleum prices for the next three days, and a formal notification has been issued. According to the notification from the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority, the price of petrol has been reduced by one rupee per litre, while the price of diesel will remain unchanged. Petrol will now sell at 312 rupees per litre, and diesel will continue at 346 rupees per litre.

The small cut in petrol prices brings some relief to motorists amid high fuel costs, though diesel users will see no change. The new prices apply for the coming three days, after which the rates will be reviewed again.
