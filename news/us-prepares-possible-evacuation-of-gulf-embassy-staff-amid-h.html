US Prepares Possible Evacuation of Gulf Embassy Staff Amid Houthi Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States is preparing for the possible evacuation of some diplomatic staff and their families from its embassies in Gulf countries, Reuters reported on October 21, citing three American officials. The State Department is said to be drafting ordered-departure directives as a precaution against renewed military action and escalating attacks by Yemen's Houthi group. Two officials told Reuters that the plans under discussion include the possible full evacuation of the US diplomatic mission in Saudi Arabia, though no final decision or formal orders have been confirmed.

The US embassy in Riyadh separately issued a security alert warning of further possible Houthi attacks after recent strikes, including one near the capital's airport. The Houthis denied launching a drone toward Medina or carrying out any operation against Saudi Arabia that day. The development comes only two months after Washington began returning staff to several regional missions in August, signalling renewed concern in the American administration over the region's security.
