People badly distressed by heavy electricity bills, taxes and inflation; running a household on one income has become difficult, says Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman said that had they not raised their voice, the price of petrol would have exceeded Rs500 per litre. He said their struggle against petroleum prices continued despite mockery. Addressing a ceremony, he said that in today's times a household cannot run on one person's income.

He said people are badly distressed by electricity bills, taxes and expenses, with even middle-class life becoming miserable. He also said working women must be protected and the state should build institutions where women get justice. He said most women are earning dignified livelihoods through the Bano Qabil programme.

He urged women to start businesses rather than seek jobs.
