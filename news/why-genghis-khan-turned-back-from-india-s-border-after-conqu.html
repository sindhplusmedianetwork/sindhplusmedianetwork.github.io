Why Genghis Khan turned back from India's border after conquering half the world

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

There was a time when Genghis Khan, having conquered half the world, reached the border of India while pursuing Jalaluddin. The final battle between the armies of Genghis Khan and Jalaluddin took place on November 24 on the banks of the River Indus. A BBC Urdu feature looks back at that moment in history and asks why the conqueror, who had swept across so much of the world, chose to turn back from India's border.

The piece traces the pursuit that brought Genghis Khan to the Indus and the battle that was fought there on that day. It examines the reasons behind his decision not to press on into India after the battle on the Indus. The feature recalls how far the Mongol conqueror had come before reaching the river, and how the campaign ended at the edge of the subcontinent.
