Indian Father Gifts 15-Year-Old Son a BMW Worth Millions for Good Marks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In India, a businessman has gifted his 15-year-old son a BMW M4 Competition worth INR 23 million. Shamsuddin Madia, from East Bardhaman district in West Bengal, gave the car to his son Muhammad Talha for scoring good marks in his ninth-grade half-yearly exams. Madia travelled to Mumbai to buy the car, which was delivered to their hometown in a special container.

The teenager cannot drive the car himself yet because Indian law does not allow a driving licence at his age. He says he will travel in it with his father for now. The unusual gift has drawn wide attention on social media, with many users debating whether such an expensive reward is appropriate.
