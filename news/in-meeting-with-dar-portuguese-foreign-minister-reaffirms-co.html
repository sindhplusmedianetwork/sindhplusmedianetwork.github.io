In meeting with Dar, Portuguese foreign minister reaffirms commitment to bolster Islamabad-Lisbon ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Portuguese Minister of State and Foreign Affairs Paulo Rangel has reaffirmed Portugal's commitment to further strengthening bilateral relations with Pakistan. The reaffirmation came during his meeting with Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar at the Foreign Office in Islamabad, according to the Foreign Office. Rangel arrived in Pakistan on Sunday on a two-day visit.

The two sides reviewed 77 years of diplomatic relations between the two countries. They also exchanged views on regional developments and cooperation at the United Nations and within the European Union. Rangel was also scheduled to hold delegation-level talks and attend the inaugural Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum.
