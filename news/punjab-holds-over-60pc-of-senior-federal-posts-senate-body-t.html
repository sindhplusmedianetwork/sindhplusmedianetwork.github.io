Punjab Holds Over 60pc of Senior Federal Posts, Senate Body Told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab holds 60.17 per cent of senior posts in the Federal Secretariat, a Senate subcommittee was told on Monday. The disclosure prompted a demand for immediate rectification and strict enforcement of the constitutional shares of all four provinces. The subcommittee said the over-representation violated Article 38(g) of the Constitution, which requires provincial shares to be maintained in federal services and bodies.

Senators demanded strict enforcement of provincial quotas in federal employment. They also demanded strict enforcement of provincial shares in natural resources. The subcommittee pressed for the imbalance to be corrected without delay.
