Khuhro rejects Mohsin Naqvi's referendum proposal

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Sindh President Nisar Ahmed Khuhro has said his party will neither allow a division of Sindh nor agree to any alteration of its boundaries. He was speaking to media personnel in Hyderabad on Saturday after chairing a meeting of the party's divisional and district bodies. Rejecting Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's proposal for a referendum on new provinces, he said there is no need for a referendum when the Constitution offers a solution.

The meeting also discussed arrangements for Sindh-wide celebration of PPP foundation day on November 30. Khuhro said the party stands firmly against any move to alter Sindh's boundaries.
