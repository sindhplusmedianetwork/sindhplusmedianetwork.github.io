Overseas job opportunities for Pakistanis shrinking as departures fall 25% this year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The number of Pakistanis going abroad for employment has fallen by 25% in one year, according to data from the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment. From January to October this year, 476,240 Pakistanis went abroad for work, compared with 636,270 in the same period last year. The figures were received by Samaa from the bureau.

The data also shows a steady decline in overseas employment over the past three years. The fall has raised fears that the expected $45 billion remittance target could be affected. Remittances sent home by overseas Pakistanis are a major source of foreign exchange for the country.
