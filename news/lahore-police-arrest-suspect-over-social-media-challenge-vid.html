Lahore Police Arrest Suspect Over Social-Media Challenge Videos Involving Children

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Lahore have arrested a suspect in a case involving videos of underage children that were made and spread online under the guise of social media challenges. Garhi Shahu police made the arrest after a case was registered over the making of an obscene video of minors and its circulation on the internet, and investigations are continuing into other elements behind the video going viral. Police have written to the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency for analysis of digital evidence and identification of suspects.

Deputy Inspector General Operations Faisal Kamran said the trend of giving children tasks involving crime and presenting them as challenges is deeply concerning, and those who push children towards crime will be brought to justice. He appealed to parents to keep an eye on their children's online activities and to inform police immediately about any suspicious activity.
