Not Even 10 Percent of 500 Crore, I Live in a Rented Apartment: Anupam Kher

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior Bollywood actor Anupam Kher has reacted to claims circulating on social media that his total wealth is 400 to 500 crore rupees. Rejecting these figures, the 71-year-old actor said he does not even have 10 percent of the claimed amount, adding that he currently lives in a rented apartment and had sold his house to actress Alia Bhatt. During a conversation on a YouTube channel, Anupam Kher said that from the day he decided to live in a rented apartment, he has felt free, a decision he made 10 years ago, and now he has no worry about looking after property.

He said he bought a house in Shimla only for his mother, while he and his brother both live in rented apartments and are happy in them. Expressing disappointment at the social media claims, he said people have the impression that he is very rich, but he does not have even 10 percent of 400 to 500 crore rupees, and his friends nowadays joke with him after seeing these figures on the internet.
