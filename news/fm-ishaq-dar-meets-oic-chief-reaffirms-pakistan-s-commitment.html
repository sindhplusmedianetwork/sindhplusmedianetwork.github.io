FM Ishaq Dar Meets OIC Chief, Reaffirms Pakistan's Commitment to Muslim Ummah's Unified Voice

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar met the Secretary General of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC), Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed, on Sunday, discussing regional cooperation, economic integration and matters of mutual interest. In the meeting, Dar reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to strengthening the OIC as a unified voice of the Muslim Ummah. Dar also met the Secretary General of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO), Dr Asad Majeed Khan, on the sidelines of the 30th ECO Council of Ministers meeting in Islamabad.

In that meeting, he underscored the importance of strengthening regional cooperation, connectivity and economic integration. The two meetings were held separately on Sunday.
