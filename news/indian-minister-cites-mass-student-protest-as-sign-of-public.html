Indian Minister Cites Mass Student Protest as Sign of Public Unrest Against Modi Government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jharkhand Health Minister Dr Irfan Ansari has described a protest by four million students as clear proof of public unrest in India, according to the Times of India. Ansari said the policies of the Modi government have caused economic and social hardship for farmers, young people, minorities and all sections of society. He said the government has put public welfare aside and continues to promote measures that work against the people.

The report presents the growing anger among the public as a sign of deep internal tension in the country. Ansari's remarks reflect the opposition's criticism that the government's policies have fuelled discontent across India.
