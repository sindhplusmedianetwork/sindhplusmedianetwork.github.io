Fire breaks out at world's largest offshore gas field in Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fire broke out on a platform of Iran's South Pars gas field, and officials say it has been brought under control while an investigation into the cause has begun. According to Iran's Fars news agency, the deputy petroleum minister said the fire erupted on Tuesday and was later extinguished. Initial reports suggest a gas leak or an accident during operations as possible causes of the blaze.

The affected platform produces 14.5 million cubic metres of gas a day, while the South Pars field as a whole produces 700 million cubic metres a day. South Pars is the world's largest offshore natural gas reserve, shared by Iran and Qatar; in Qatar the same reserve is called the North Dome. According to data from the Gas Exporting Countries Forum, Iran produced a total of 276 billion cubic metres of gas in 2024, of which 94 percent was used inside the country.

The joint reserve holds about 1,800 trillion cubic feet of usable gas, enough to meet the world's needs for 13 years.
