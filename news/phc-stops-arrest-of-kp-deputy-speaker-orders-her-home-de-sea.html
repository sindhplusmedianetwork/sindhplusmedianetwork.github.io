PHC Stops Arrest of KP Deputy Speaker, Orders Her Home De-Sealed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Peshawar High Court has barred police from arresting Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Assembly Deputy Speaker Surraiya Bibi under 3MPO. The court also suspended the notification sealing her official residence. A two-member bench comprising Justices Ijaz Anwar and Muhammad Fahim Wali heard her petition.

The petition challenged the October 14 raid on her house and its subsequent sealing. The action had followed the imposition of governor's rule in the province. At the next hearing, the court sought replies from the chief secretary, the IG police and other respondents.
