Democrats take another Senate seat in Alaska as Mary Peltola wins

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Democratic candidate Mary Peltola has defeated incumbent Republican Senator Dan Sullivan to win a Senate seat in Alaska, the Associated Press confirmed. With the announcement of the result, all Senate seats in the US midterm elections are now decided and the Democrats' majority has been further strengthened. Democrats increased their Senate seats to 56, while Republicans hold 44.

In the midterm elections, Democrats won a total of nine Senate seats from the Republicans, the most won by one party since 1958. Peltola said the support of Alaska's people was a great honour for her and she would work against corporate monopoly, cut costs and serve the interests of the state. Her victory gives the Democrats a stronger majority in the Senate as the results of the midterm elections are now complete.
