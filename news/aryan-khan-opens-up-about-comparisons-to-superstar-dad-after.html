Aryan Khan opens up about comparisons to superstar dad after TIME100 milestone

Shah Rukh Khan's eldest son Aryan Khan has opened up about his superstar father's influence on his work and craving his own identity in Bollywood. Addressing the constant comparison with his father, Aryan, who made his directorial debut last year, said he does not have any problem with it, as he takes it as a compliment to be compared to the number one guy. The comment came as Aryan was named in the inaugural TIME100 Next India 2026 list, which recognises emerging talent and influential voices across different fields.

During the interview, Aryan also discussed his creative process and what he enjoys most about filmmaking, explaining that he likes taking an idea from the writing stage to its final execution. He said he is good at bringing ideas to life, from getting them down on paper to seeing things through to execution, and that how you make a story different and add a spin on it is what filmmaking really is. The 29-year-old made his writing and directorial debut with The Ba**ds of Bollywood, which premiered on Netflix in 2025.

Produced by his mother Gauri Khan under the Red Chillies Entertainment banner, the seven-episode Hindi series is a satirical action comedy set in the film industry.
