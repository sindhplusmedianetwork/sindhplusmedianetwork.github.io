Bilawal's 'rivers of blood' remark unbecoming of a national leader, says Hafiz Naeem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami Pakistan chief Hafiz Naeem ur Rehman has said that Bilawal Bhutto's remark about rivers of blood flowing over the demand for new provinces is not befitting any political leader at the national level. He said such statements damage national unity. The remarks came in response to Bilawal Bhutto's comments on the demand for new provinces.

Hafiz Naeem said leaders at the national level should speak with restraint on sensitive questions. He warned that such language weakens the bonds that hold the country together.
