World on Cusp of Autonomous Weapons Revolution as Calls Grow for Curbs on 'Killer Robots'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lethal autonomous weapons powered by artificial intelligence are set to revolutionise warfare, but there is no proper regulatory or legal framework governing them. A United Nations conference in Geneva starting Monday will discuss curbs on so-called killer robots. UN chief Antonio Guterres and the president of the Red Cross have warned that the world is dangerously close to crossing a moral red line, with machines autonomously targeting humans.

Experts say artificial intelligence is already being used in target generation, including in the US-Israeli war against Iran and Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Analysts doubt the Geneva talks will keep pace with the fast-changing technology. They warn that swarms of autonomous drones and a race towards full autonomy are already under way.
