DRAP, WHO Workshop Targets Detection and Recall of Falsified Medicines

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan, in collaboration with the World Health Organization, organised a national workshop in Islamabad. The workshop aims to strengthen regulatory capacity for the sampling, identification and recall of substandard and falsified medicines. Speakers at the event stressed that the quality, safety and efficacy of medicines and vaccines are essential to protect public health.

They said families must be protected from harmful and fake medical products. The initiative continues the ongoing collaboration between DRAP and the WHO. It is part of broader efforts to improve the detection and management of substandard medical products in the country.
