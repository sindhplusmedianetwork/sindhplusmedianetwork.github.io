Pakistan, China Kick Off First Joint Army Exercise 'Karakoram-2026' in Xinjiang

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and China on Wednesday began their first-ever joint army training exercise, Karakoram-2026, in northwestern China's Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region. The opening ceremony was held at a training base in Korla city, according to Chinese state media. The exercise focuses on joint counter-terrorism clearance operations in high-altitude and mountainous terrain, and includes skills training in UAV operation, on-the-move target search and firing, and long-range sniping.

It also covers joint command and live-force exercises. Chinese Defence Ministry spokesman Senior Colonel Chen Xi said the drill would help the two sides deepen exchanges and cooperation and strengthen their ability to jointly respond to terrorist threats. According to China Central Television's military channel, all weapons and equipment used in the training were provided by China, including Type 191 rifles, Type 201 squad machine guns, Type 11 armoured assault vehicles and Type 08 infantry fighting vehicles, along with artillery and unmanned systems.

China is Pakistan's largest arms supplier, accounting for more than 80 per cent of the country's arms imports in recent years. The two countries previously conducted counter-terrorism drills mainly through special forces; Karakoram-2026 marks the first time regular army units are training together.
