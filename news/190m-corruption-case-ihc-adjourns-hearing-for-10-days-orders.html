£190m corruption case: IHC adjourns hearing for 10 days, orders meeting between Imran and legal team

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court adjourned for 10 days the hearing of appeals by PTI founder Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi against their convictions in the £190 million Al-Qadir Trust case. Imran Khan was sentenced to 14 years in the case, while Bushra Bibi was sentenced to seven years. The appeals challenge those convictions before the high court.

During the proceedings, the court directed the Islamabad advocate general to arrange meetings between Imran and his legal team. The legal team includes Sardar Latif Khosa and Barrister Salman Safdar. The hearing will resume after the 10-day adjournment.
