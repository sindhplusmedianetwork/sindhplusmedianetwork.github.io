Multan Orphanage's Registration Bid Rejected After Child's Death, Says Sarah Ahmad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Child Protection and Welfare Bureau Punjab chairperson Sarah Ahmad has said the registration request of a Multan orphanage was rejected after it failed to ensure the safety and supervision of a child in its care. The move follows the death of 15-year-old Waseem Abbas, an orphan who had been living at the facility for five years and was sent to a contractor to learn electrical work just days before he died. Ahmad demanded that Punjab Police take strict legal action against the orphanage's owner and hold everyone responsible for the teenager's death accountable.

She also asked the Punjab Charities Commission to review the orphanage's registration and licence and cancel them where warranted under the law. Ahmad said minimum care standards for children living in orphanages across Punjab had already been issued, and no institution would be allowed to neglect its duty of care. 'Every child deserves protection, dignity and safety,' she said, adding that no orphan should be left at the mercy of those entrusted with their care.
