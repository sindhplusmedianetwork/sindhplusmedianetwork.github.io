US religious freedom panel says India is targeting minorities in voter roll revisions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India has escalated its targeting of religious minorities during a voter list revision that struck off tens of millions of names nationwide, a US panel on religious freedom said on Thursday. Asif Mahmood, the chair of the US Commission on International Religious Freedom (USCIRF), said the Indian government is targeting Muslim citizens by removing them from voter rolls, calling them foreigners, and now illegally deporting them. India's voter list revision removed 130 million names, roughly the population of Mexico, in the largest deletion of voters in the country's history.

Data shows the omissions disproportionately affect Muslims, India's largest minority. India's Election Commission says it was removing duplicate, deceased, shifted and otherwise ineligible voters and that anyone excluded in error can pursue appeals. Commissioner Gene Mills urged the State Department to designate India as a country of particular concern, while USCIRF's recommendations are not binding and Washington has not acted on its recent recommendations on India.
