Over 340,000 kanals of Auqaf land missing from official records in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 340,000 kanals of Auqaf Department land in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is missing from the department's own official records, it has emerged. The department's record lists only about 67,000 kanals. The administrations of 20 districts, however, have confirmed a total of 407,374 kanals and 8 marlas of Auqaf land in their districts.

Official sources say the valuable land, worth billions of rupees, has been under the occupation of influential people for years. The land remains outside government oversight. Mardan has the largest share at 45,462 kanals and 16 marlas.
