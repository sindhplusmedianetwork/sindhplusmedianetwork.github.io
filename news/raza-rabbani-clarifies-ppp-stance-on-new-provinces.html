Raza Rabbani Clarifies PPP Stance on New Provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Senate chairman Raza Rabbani said the Pakistan Peoples Party's position on new provinces or administrative units is clear and without contradiction. He said the PPP supports giving practical shape to the will of a province's people under Article 239(4) of the Constitution. He added that the people of Sindh have rejected any change to the province's geographical boundaries.

He said a two-thirds majority approval from the provincial assembly is mandatory before any change. The statement comes amid an ongoing debate over the creation of new provinces in the country. Rabbani's remarks set out the party's constitutional position on the question.
