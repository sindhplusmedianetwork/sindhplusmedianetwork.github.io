Magnitude 7 Earthquake Jolts Tibet's Nagchu Region

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Strong earthquake tremors were felt in China's autonomous Tibet region late on Thursday night. The China Earthquake Networks Center recorded the magnitude at 7 on the Richter scale. The epicentre was located in Baqen County of Nagchu city in southwestern Tibet, at a depth of 10 kilometres.

The quake struck at night, when most residents of the remote mountainous county were indoors. Local authorities began assessing the situation in the affected areas. Further details about damage were awaited at the time of reporting.
