Yemeni government forces fortify defences around Aden

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's internationally recognised government is fortifying positions on the outskirts of Aden province, home to its de facto capital, as Houthi fighters seize hilltops overlooking the area, military officials told AFP. The officials said they had received high-level directives to establish military fortifications west and north of Aden governorate as the threat to the government's interim capital drew nearer. The fortifications include stacking up sandbags and digging tunnels.

Fighting has flared since a 2022 truce fell apart over the summer, with violence intensifying after the Saudi-backed government lost control of the Red Sea coastline along the strategic Bab al-Mandab strait in September. The Houthis are now advancing east, consolidating gains near the Red Sea while moving closer to the Gulf of Aden and taking hold of strategic hilltops in the Al-Madaribah wal Ara district overlooking Aden province. Government forces have repeatedly tried to regain control of the Red Sea coast without success, with battles killing dozens on both sides in the past couple of days, according to government and Houthi sources.

One military official described the fortification outside Aden as a long-overdue precautionary measure.
