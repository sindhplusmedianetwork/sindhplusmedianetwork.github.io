AI Could Pose Existential Threat Within Three Years, Warns Leading Scientist Geoffrey Hinton

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nobel laureate Professor Geoffrey Hinton, a leading scientist in the field of artificial intelligence who played a key role in developing artificial neural networks, has warned that superintelligent AI could become so powerful within the next three years that it poses a threat to all of humanity. Hinton, who is also known as the godfather of AI, is due to present his concerns to European Union leaders at an informal meeting in Dublin. According to him, if highly intelligent machines are not properly supervised and controlled, they could soon go beyond human control and become more intelligent than humans.

The warning comes against the backdrop of concerns raised during the Hinton Lectures organised by the AI Safety Foundation in Toronto, where experts considered the possibility that artificial intelligence could in future gain the ability to destabilise even governments. Alignment Research Center founder Paul Christiano has also warned that the risk of AI dominating humans is higher over the next three years. He added that a notable risk of this kind could also exist within the next six to 12 months.

The experts' concerns highlight the rapidly growing capabilities of artificial intelligence and the need for effective human oversight. However, these predictions relate to possible risks, not to events that are certain to happen in the future.
