ECP notifies delimitation schedule for Quetta, Kharian cantonment boards

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ISLAMABAD: The Election Commission of Pakistan has notified delimitation schedules for the Quetta and Kharian cantonment boards following their reclassification by the federal government. The federal government reclassified the Quetta Cantonment Board from Class-II to a higher category, necessitating fresh delimitation of its wards. The ECP's notification sets out the timeline for the delimitation exercise in both cantonment boards.

Delimitation is a prerequisite for holding local government elections in the cantonment areas. The Commission said the process would be completed in accordance with the notified schedule. Cantonment boards across the country are administered separately from the provincial local government systems.
