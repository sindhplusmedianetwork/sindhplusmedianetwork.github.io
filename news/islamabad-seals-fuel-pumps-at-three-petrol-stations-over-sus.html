Islamabad Seals Fuel Pumps at Three Petrol Stations Over Suspected Meter Tampering

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad district administration has issued show-cause notices to three petrol stations on Lehtrar Road after electronic devices allegedly used to manipulate fuel measurements were found installed in their dispensing machines. The notices were issued on the basis of a report submitted by the Assistant Commissioner Nilore following inspections of the three filling stations, during which the devices were allegedly found fitted in fuel dispensing machines, raising concerns that customers were receiving less fuel than the quantity displayed. The administration has sealed the machines concerned and secured the recovered devices for technical examination, stating that supplying less fuel than the quantity shown could cause financial losses to consumers.

The management of all three filling stations has been directed to submit written explanations within seven days of receiving the notices, and to explain why their No Objection Certificates should not be cancelled and why legal and administrative proceedings should not be initiated against their owners or management. The owners have also been asked to submit copies of their NOCs, relevant licences and Form K certificates, along with inspection and maintenance records of the fuel dispensing machines and any other documents supporting their defence. The district administration has scheduled personal hearings for the management of the three filling stations at 10 am on November 18, 2026, where the owners may appear personally or through their authorised representatives with all relevant documents.

The notices further state that failure to submit a reply within the prescribed period or to appear for the hearing will result in ex parte proceedings based on the available record, and the administration has warned that if the allegations are established, action may be taken under the relevant laws, including cancellation of the filling stations' NOCs.
