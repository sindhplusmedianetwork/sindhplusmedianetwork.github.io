New LPG mechanism aims to rein in high prices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government on Saturday moved to overhaul liquefied petroleum gas distribution through a transparent auction mechanism, after the first meeting of the LPG Committee chaired by the Petroleum Secretary. Indigenous producers will allocate 10 per cent of LPG production to state-owned marketing companies for supply to hard areas, while the remaining 90 per cent will be offered through transparent auctions. Each company will be allowed a maximum of 20 tonnes per day under the new mechanism.

LPG is currently selling for up to Rs600 per kg in Lahore and Rs500 to Rs540 in Karachi, against Ogra's notified November rate of Rs294.08 per kg. The new system aims to bring down prices by making LPG supply more transparent.
