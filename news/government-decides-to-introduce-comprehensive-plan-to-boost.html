Government Decides to Introduce Comprehensive Plan to Boost Exports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has decided to introduce a comprehensive plan to increase the country's exports. The Ministry of Industries and Production has sent the plan to the government, and it aims to expand trade and investment with support from the Special Investment Facilitation Council. The plan proposes removing extra duties and tariff flaws on raw materials used by export industries, ending delays in customs clearance, and ensuring timely payment of refunds to exporters.

It also suggests reviewing electricity and gas prices to cut production costs, expanding shared testing laboratories and certification facilities, and introducing modern machinery, automation and new technology in industrial clusters. Exporters would get easier access to loans, working capital on the basis of invoices, and supply chain financing under the plan. The plan further calls for faster clearance of goods at ports, alternative trade routes, a simpler digital system for raw material import permits, and better food processing, cold chain and packaging facilities to raise agricultural exports.
