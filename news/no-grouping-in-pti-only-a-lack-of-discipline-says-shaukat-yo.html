No Grouping in PTI, Only a Lack of Discipline, Says Shaukat Yousafzai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Khyber Pakhtunkhwa information secretary Shaukat Yousafzai has said there is no grouping in the party, but a lack of discipline. He said he was saddened by party leaders making statements against each other. According to Yousafzai, mutual understanding and unity are essential to pull the party out of its current difficulties.

He urged Barrister Gohar to play his role in keeping the party united. Yousafzai said a disciplinary committee has become necessary to protect the party from chaos. He added that the issue of new provinces has not yet been discussed within the party.

He said new provinces should be created through the constitutional process.
