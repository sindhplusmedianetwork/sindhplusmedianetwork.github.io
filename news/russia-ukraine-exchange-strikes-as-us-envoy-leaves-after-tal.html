Russia, Ukraine Exchange Strikes as US Envoy Leaves After Talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russia and Ukraine exchanged heavy strikes overnight as American mediation efforts continued without a pause in the fighting. Missiles hit Kyiv early on Thursday, wounding 15 people and damaging subway infrastructure in the Ukrainian capital. Hours earlier, Ukraine launched hundreds of drones toward Moscow, with the city's mayor reporting two people wounded near the capital.

The attacks came despite the latest round of mediation talks by US envoy Steve Witkoff with Ukraine's Zelensky and Russia's Putin. Zelensky said he supported three-way talks at the negotiator level. The Kremlin, meanwhile, said Putin had agreed to resume US-mediated talks as early as next month.

The continued strikes underline how far the two sides remain from a ceasefire.
