Rana Sanaullah Says Terrorism Will Be Eliminated Within Four Months

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Adviser on Political Affairs Rana Sanaullah has claimed that terrorism in the country will be eliminated within four months. Speaking on Samaa News, he said it was not a matter of months or years, asserting that the menace would be wiped out in four months. He cited intelligence reports indicating that 201 terrorist formations were present in 12 affected districts of Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa.

Sanaullah said the military leadership briefed the apex committee meeting in Peshawar on the ongoing operation and expressed resolve to eliminate the terrorists within four months. The statement comes amid a renewed wave of terrorist attacks in KP, including Thursday's deadly attack on police personnel in Karak. Security forces have intensified intelligence-based operations across the province in recent weeks.
