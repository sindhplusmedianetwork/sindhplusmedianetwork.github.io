Nepal Refuses Screening of Anti-Pakistan Indian Film 'Udta Tir'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nepal has refused to allow the public screening of the Bollywood film "Udta Tir", which stars Ayushmann Khurrana and Sara Ali Khan. According to Nepal's communications and IT ministry, the central film censor board cannot permit the film under Section 8(3) of the National Cinema (Production, Exhibition and Distribution) Act 2026. The law bars films that could harm national security, law and order, or friendly relations with friendly countries, and the board concluded after review that the film falls in the prohibited category.

Directed by Aakash Kaushik, the film has drawn criticism ahead of its release over its anti-Pakistan theme. It is scheduled to release in Indian cinemas on October 23.
