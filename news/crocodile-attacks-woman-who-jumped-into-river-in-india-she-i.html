Crocodile attacks woman who jumped into river in India; she is rescued

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A crocodile attacked a woman in India's Karnataka state after she jumped into the Kumaradhara river in an apparent suicide attempt, but police and local people rescued her in time. The incident happened near a bridge over the river on Wednesday night, when the woman, distressed over personal problems, jumped into the water. A crocodile in the river bit her, causing minor injuries, and her cries for help alerted people nearby.

Local residents informed the police, and officers reached the spot with their help to pull her out of the river safely. She was taken to a nearby hospital for first aid and her injuries were described as minor. Police later contacted her family and, after counselling, handed her over to them.

No case was registered in connection with the incident, and her identity was not disclosed at the family's request.
