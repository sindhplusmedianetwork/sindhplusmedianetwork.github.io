As long as PPP exists, no one can look at Sindh with ill intent, says Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has said that as long as the Pakistan Peoples Party exists, Sindh will endure and no one can look at it with ill intent. The chief minister visited Jilani House in Makli, Thatta, where he was accompanied by Pakistan Peoples Party Member of the National Assembly Sadiq Memon. On his arrival, he was welcomed by Pir Ghulam Rizwani, Pir Ghulam Haqqani, Pir Ghulam Rahmani and Zain-ul-Abideen, provincial minister Muhammad Ali Malkani, Riaz Shah Shirazi and members of the provincial assembly.

The chief minister attended the urs of Pir Syed Ghulam Jilani Shah Jilani and also addressed the gathering at the shrine of Pir Syed Ghulam Jilani Shah Jilani. He said Sindh is the land of Sufis, and that because of the Sufi saints there is peace and brotherhood here. He added that as long as the Peoples Party exists, Sindh will remain established and enduring, and no one can look at it with an evil eye.

The chief minister said a referendum is held on an issue in which there is ambiguity, and that a procedure for it exists in the Constitution, adding that even after a referendum, the final decision will be made by the assemblies. He said the teachings of the Sufi saints are to respect one another and to live with love and brotherhood.
