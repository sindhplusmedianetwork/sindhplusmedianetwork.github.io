Declared Dead: Indian Voters Fight to Get Back on Electoral Rolls

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 130 million names have been removed from India's electoral rolls under the Special Intensive Revision (SIR), sparking near-daily protests in New Delhi and a major political storm. The opposition alleges that the revision benefits Prime Minister Modi's BJP, a charge denied by both the Election Commission and the BJP. The report follows Kamta Prasad, a 53-year-old Delhi tailor listed as 'Kanta Prasad' and declared dead, who has been shuttling between government offices to prove he is alive.

The Election Commission says duplicates and deceased voters were removed from the rolls. It adds that erroneous exclusions can be appealed before the rolls are finalised. The controversy has become one of the most heated political issues in the country.
