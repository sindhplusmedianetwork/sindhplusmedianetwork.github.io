Trump Promises 'Big' Petrol Price Cuts Soon, Unveils Diesel Relief Measures

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has told Americans that petrol prices will soon come down in big and significant numbers, offering the promise as fuel costs weigh heavily on voters ahead of the November 3 midterm elections. He has announced a series of relief measures aimed at diesel prices, including expanded access to cheaper red-dyed diesel, temporarily eased restrictions on Russian diesel imports, and a one-off $8 billion subsidy for farmers to help with the harvest. Trump described lower fuel prices for farmers, ranchers and truckers as his greatest priority, writing on social media that diesel prices would come down in record numbers and fast.

According to the American Automobile Association, the national average price of petrol stands at about $4.29 a gallon, more than $1.25 higher than a year ago, while diesel has climbed by nearly 75 percent to $6.30 a gallon. Trump has said the Iran war could end soon and that once it is over, oil prices would drop like a rock, calling higher fuel costs a small price to pay for keeping nuclear weapons out of Iran's hands. Energy analysts, however, have dismissed the likely impact of the new measures before the election, with one describing it as zero.

Polls indicate that high fuel prices are hurting Republicans, with one survey finding that even a one-dollar drop in petrol prices would be unlikely to win most voters over.
