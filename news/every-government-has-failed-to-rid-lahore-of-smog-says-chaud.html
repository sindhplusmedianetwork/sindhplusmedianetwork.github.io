Every government has failed to rid Lahore of smog, says Chaudhry Sarwar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Punjab governor Chaudhry Sarwar said Lahore, once famed for knowledge, saints and good food, has become the world's most polluted city. He said breathing has become difficult in the city and respiratory diseases are now common. Sarwar said smog has gripped Lahore and its surroundings for the past two decades, and every government so far has failed to rid the city of it.

He said pollution cannot end with policies and statements alone. Clean air is a basic constitutional and human right, he said, and a comprehensive long-term policy is needed with public participation and the involvement of experts. He added that cutting forests and trees is a national and human crime, and it has made Pakistan one of the most polluted countries in the world.
