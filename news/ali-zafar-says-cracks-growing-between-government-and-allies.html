Ali Zafar says cracks growing between government and allies, 'something is about to happen'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Barrister Ali Zafar has said the differences between federal minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain and Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz are no small matter. Speaking on Geo News programme Capital Talk with host Hamid Mir, he said a federal minister had levelled an accusation against the Punjab chief minister. He described the development as the first card falling from a house of cards, adding that the entire house could collapse soon.

Zafar said it appeared that cracks were growing between the government and its allies and that something was about to happen. The prime minister's adviser on political affairs, Rana Sanaullah, also took part in the programme. The remarks came as the row between the Punjab chief minister and the federal minister continued to draw attention.
