Kashmir Black Day Observed at Pakistan Consulate General in Barcelona

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Consulate General of Pakistan in Barcelona commemorated Kashmir Black Day on October 27, 2026, in an event aimed at expressing solidarity with the people of Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir. The day marks October 27, 1947, when Indian forces entered Jammu and Kashmir, triggering a long-standing dispute that remains unresolved despite relevant United Nations Security Council resolutions. Kashmiri leaders, representatives of the Pakistani and Kashmiri community, and members of the local community in Spain attended the ceremony.

Participants strongly condemned the ongoing atrocities and human rights violations against Kashmiris and urged the international community to take effective steps for the resolution of the dispute. The event opened with the recitation of the Holy Quran by Tariq Mahmood, followed by special messages from the president, prime minister, and deputy prime minister and foreign minister of Pakistan, read out by Consul General Murad Ali Wazir, Sajeel Aqdas, and Muhammad Sarwar respectively. A special video message from Kashmiri leader Dr.

Ghulam Nabi Fai was also screened, along with a documentary on the history of the Kashmir issue and the human rights situation in the region. In his address, Consul General Murad Ali Wazir reaffirmed Pakistan's unwavering moral, political, and diplomatic support for the Kashmiri people and stressed that the Jammu and Kashmir dispute must be resolved in line with UN Security Council resolutions and the aspirations of the Kashmiri people. A special photographic exhibition depicting Kashmir's history, culture, and the Kashmiri struggle was also held at the consulate.
