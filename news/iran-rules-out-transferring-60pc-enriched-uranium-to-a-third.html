Iran rules out transferring 60pc enriched uranium to a third country

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has rejected the possibility of transferring its 60 per cent enriched uranium to a third country, state broadcaster IRIB reported. Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmail Baghaei said Tehran would not hand over its enriched uranium to any other country. He was asked whether moving the 60 per cent enriched uranium to a third country could pave the way for an agreement between Iran and the United States, and whether the matter had come up during the talks in Doha.

In his reply, the spokesman made clear that Iran would not transfer its 60 per cent enriched uranium to a third country. The statement did not clarify what was discussed in the Doha talks or whether Iran was ready to offer any alternative proposal. The 60 per cent enriched uranium remains a key subject in the negotiations over Iran's nuclear programme, and the Iranian position has emerged while diplomatic efforts continue for a possible deal to resolve differences, including nuclear matters, between the two countries.
