American Runs Half Marathon Carrying a Pizza in World Record Attempt

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An American man has completed a half marathon while carrying a pizza box in an attempt to set a world record. Steven Erickson, from Lubbock, Texas, ran the Williams Route 66 Half Marathon in Tulsa, Oklahoma on Sunday carrying a 16-inch pizza box in his hand. The box had been specially designed by Andolini's Pizzeria in the style of the album cover of the music band Metallica.

Erickson completed the almost 21-kilometre race in one hour, 44 minutes and 40 seconds, and he is hopeful that his time will be enough for the Guinness World Record for the fastest half marathon completed while carrying a pizza. He wrote on social media that he had never imagined trying to get his name into Guinness World Records, but he is now ready for this unusual goal. Remarkably, he carried the pizza box safely with him throughout the race and brought it to the finish line intact.
