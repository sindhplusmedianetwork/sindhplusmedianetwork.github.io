Barrister Gohar Meets Shah Mahmood Qureshi, Inquires About His Well-Being

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan met the party's Vice Chairman Shah Mahmood Qureshi in Lahore and inquired about his well-being. During the meeting, Gohar briefed Qureshi on developments regarding the release of Imran Khan's sisters. He also updated him on meetings between lawyers and the former prime minister.

Zain Qureshi was also with Barrister Gohar during the meeting. Shah Mahmood Qureshi has been receiving treatment at the Punjab Institute of Cardiology for the past two days. The meeting took place in Lahore, where Qureshi is under medical care, and the discussion focused on the party's current matters, including the situation of Imran Khan's sisters and the lawyers' engagement with the former prime minister.
