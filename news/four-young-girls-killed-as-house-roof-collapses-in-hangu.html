Four Young Girls Killed as House Roof Collapses in Hangu

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four young girls were killed and three people were injured when the roof of a house collapsed in Hangu. The incident took place in Jaded Banda, within the limits of Doaba police station, where the roof of a room caved in and buried the children under rubble. Rescue 1122 said its teams reached the spot as soon as the control room received the call and started rescue work.

The rescuers pulled the four girls from the rubble and shifted their bodies to Abdul Rauf Hospital. The girls were aged between two and twelve years. The injured include the house owner, Fazal Ghani, and two women, who were taken to hospital for treatment.

According to SHO Doaba, Wahid Noor, initial reports suggest that debris from construction work in a room had been dumped on the roof, and the roof could not bear the extra weight, causing the tragic collapse.
