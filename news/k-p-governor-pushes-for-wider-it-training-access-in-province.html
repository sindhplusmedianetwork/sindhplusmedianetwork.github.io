K-P Governor Pushes for Wider IT Training Access in Province

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has called for expanding the province's access to federal IT training programmes so young people can acquire modern digital skills and economic independence. He made the call at a meeting with Federal IT Minister Shaza Fatima Khawaja, where officials reviewed the Digital Master Plan. The meeting noted that 150 of the country's 500 digital platforms operate in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa.

The minister said 800,000 young people in the province had already been trained under the Digital Skills Programme. Governor Kundi stressed that wider access to federal programmes would help more young people in the province build digital careers. The discussion focused on how the province can benefit further from national digital training initiatives.
