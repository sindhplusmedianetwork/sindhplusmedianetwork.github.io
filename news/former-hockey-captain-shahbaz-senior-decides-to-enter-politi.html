Former hockey captain Shahbaz Senior decides to enter politics

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Pakistan hockey captain Shahbaz Ahmed Senior has decided to enter politics, according to a private television channel report. He is expected to make a formal announcement soon. Sources say Shahbaz Senior intends to contest the next general elections from his hometown, Faisalabad.

He is counted among the finest forwards in the history of Pakistan hockey and world hockey, known for his speed, remarkable body dodge, brilliant dribbling and agility, which earned him the titles 'Maradona of Hockey' and 'Man with Electric Heel'. He scored 92 goals in his international career, and under his captaincy Pakistan last won the Hockey World Cup in 1994. He is the first player to be named Player of the Tournament at two consecutive World Cups, in 1990 and 1994, and the only hockey player to have been awarded the Hilal-i-Imtiaz by the Government of Pakistan.
