Japan's PM Takaichi Faces Backlash Over Remarks on Housework and Makeup

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Japan's Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi has drawn criticism after telling parliament that housework and makeup cost her three to four extra hours a day compared with her male predecessors. Critics said the remarks reinforced the idea that housework was traditionally women's responsibility. Some also questioned Japan's long working hours, which the remarks highlighted.

Data cited in reports says Japanese women spend about five hours a day on housework, while men spend roughly 40 minutes. The backlash has added to a difficult stretch for Takaichi, whose comments have been seized on by opponents as evidence of entrenched gender expectations in Japanese public life.
