Imran Khan ready for political deal on 28th Amendment, says Durrani

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior journalist Fakhar Durrani has said that PTI founder Imran Khan is ready to make a political deal on the 28th Amendment. Speaking on Geo News programme Report Card, Durrani said Khan's message, conveyed through Salman Akram Raja, was that provinces should be created while raising objections to the current mandate. Durrani said the message showed that Khan was ready for a deal and that he had sent the same message to his party.

He said divisions already existed within the PTI over Khan's recent meetings and the amendment message. It remained to be seen whether the government would accept Khan's position or the PTI would cooperate under the current mandate, he added. Durrani also said some relief might be possible for Bushra Bibi and Khan's family members, but there was currently no relaxation for Imran Khan himself.
