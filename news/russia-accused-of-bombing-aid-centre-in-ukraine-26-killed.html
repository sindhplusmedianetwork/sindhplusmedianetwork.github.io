Russia Accused of Bombing Aid Centre in Ukraine, 26 Killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ukraine has accused Russia of bombing an aid-distribution centre in the southern Zaporizhzhia region, killing at least 26 people. The strike hit the centre in the village of Komyshuvakha, where humanitarian supplies were being handed out, and left five others wounded. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy described the attack as 'extremely horrific'.

He said nine aerial bombs weighing 3,000 kilograms each were dropped on the site. A local school was struck in a separate second attack, according to Ukrainian officials. The Russian military has not yet commented on the allegations.

The incident marks one of the deadliest recent strikes on civilian aid infrastructure in the conflict.
