SpaceX Starship booster explodes and falls into the sea during return attempt

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

SpaceX's Super Heavy booster exploded over the Gulf of Mexico and fell into the sea during the first attempt to fully return a Starship rocket and catch it with the launch tower's mechanical 'chopsticks' arms. The booster was diverted away from the Boca Chica launch site in Texas, hovered briefly, and then burst into a fireball. The attempt was part of SpaceX's plan to make Starship fully reusable, so that both the booster and the spacecraft can be flown again.

A reusable rocket would cut the cost of future space flights. The booster had lifted off as part of the Starship test flight before the return attempt ended in the explosion. SpaceX said the test still provided data for the next attempt.
