Horticulture Agency's tree-washing campaign under way in Lahore to tackle smog

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Horticulture Agency Lahore's tree-washing campaign is under way across the city to tackle smog. The campaign is continuing on major roads, green belts and parks to reduce the effects of smog and air pollution. Managing Director Raja Mansoor Ahmed said trees and plants are being washed regularly.

The washing is aimed at removing dust and pollution layers from leaves. He said the effort is being carried out under Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's 'Green Lahore' vision. The campaign forms part of the city's measures against deteriorating air quality.
