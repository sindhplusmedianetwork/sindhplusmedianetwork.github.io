Lahore's October Air Improves 27% as AQI Stays Below 300 for First Time in Five Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore's air pollution fell by 27 percent in October 2026 compared with the same month last year, Senior Provincial Minister Maryam Aurangzeb said. She said that for the first time in five years, no day in October saw the air quality index (AQI) cross 300, making October 2026 the city's cleanest October since 2023. The average PM2.5 level in Lahore dropped from 136.3 micrograms per cubic metre in October 2025 to 100.1 micrograms in October 2026, while the Pakistan Air Quality Initiative recorded lower pollution at 26 monitoring sites across the city.

According to Punjab Climate Watch, overall pollution improved by 15 percent across six major indicators, and the month's highest AQI level fell by 28 percent. Aurangzeb noted that October 2025 had four days with AQI above 300, while October 2026 had none. She added that smog must be assessed on scientific facts and reliable data, that weather conditions also affect pollution, and that every improvement should not be credited to government policies alone.
