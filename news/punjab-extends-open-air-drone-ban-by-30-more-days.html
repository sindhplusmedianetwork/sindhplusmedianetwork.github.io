Punjab Extends Open-Air Drone Ban by 30 More Days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has extended the ban on flying drones in open spaces across the province for another 30 days, issuing a formal notification to that effect. The notification states that drones may not be flown in open places anywhere in Punjab for the next 30 days, with the restriction imposed in view of security concerns. However, limited use of small drones has been allowed inside wedding halls, marquees and other enclosed venues for covering events held there.

Organisers of indoor events will be responsible for ensuring safety measures while drones are in use, and for making sure that no participant, staff member or other person faces any risk from the drone. Intelligence agencies and law-enforcement institutions have been exempted from the ban and may use drones in the performance of their duties, while all other persons and institutions must follow the prescribed rules. District-level Safe Drone Usage Committees have also been formed, and drone use after written permission from these committees will be exempt from the ban.

The government says the procedure is meant to bring drone use under regulation and to reduce security concerns.
