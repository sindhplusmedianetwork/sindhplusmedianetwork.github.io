IMF Flags AI, Inflation and Debt as Major Risks to Global Economy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Monetary Fund has warned that artificial intelligence, inflation and mounting debt pose major risks to the global economy. According to Express News, the Fund's assessment puts the disruptive potential of AI alongside persistent price pressures and heavy debt burdens as the key threats to global growth. The IMF chief was quoted as saying that central banks should focus fully on bringing inflation under control and keeping prices stable.

The warning reflects unease that rapid advances in AI could unsettle labour markets and financial systems even as many economies still battle elevated inflation. High public and private debt levels, the Fund cautions, leave countries with less room to respond if conditions deteriorate. Policymakers around the world will be watching the Fund's upcoming meetings for further guidance on navigating the three risks.
