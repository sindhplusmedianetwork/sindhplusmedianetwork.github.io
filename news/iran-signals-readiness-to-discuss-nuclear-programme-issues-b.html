Iran signals readiness to discuss nuclear programme issues, Bloomberg reports

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Bloomberg has reported that the United States and Iran are close to a possible breakthrough in talks to end the war. According to the report, Iran has shown readiness to discuss aspects of its nuclear programme that were previously not on the agenda. A diplomat briefed on the talks said Iran is carefully reviewing proposals put forward by Qatar on its atomic activities.

The report says any concession on the issue could prove a major breakthrough. It also notes that US President Donald Trump recently spoke by phone with the Amir of Qatar about reducing regional tensions. The call signalled that Trump wants to end the war after his Republican Party's defeat in this week's US midterm elections.
