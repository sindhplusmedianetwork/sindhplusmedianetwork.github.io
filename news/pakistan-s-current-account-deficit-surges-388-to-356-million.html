Pakistan's Current Account Deficit Surges 388% to $356 Million in September

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's current account deficit widened sharply to $356 million in September, data from the State Bank of Pakistan showed. The figure was up 388 per cent month-on-month from just $73 million in August. Analysts attributed the sharp widening to higher imports alongside no significant improvement in exports.

The cumulative current account deficit for the first quarter of the fiscal year, covering July to September, stood at $874 million. That was 16 per cent higher than the same period last year, raising questions about the sustainability of the external account amid regional energy market turbulence.
