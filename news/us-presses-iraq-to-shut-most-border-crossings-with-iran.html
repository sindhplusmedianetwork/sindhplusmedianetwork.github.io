US presses Iraq to shut most border crossings with Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has asked Iraq to close most of its border crossings with Iran, Iraqi Foreign Minister Fuad Hussein has said. Speaking to Al Jazeera, Hussein said the demand came from the US Treasury as part of Washington's policy of increasing economic pressure on Tehran. He said the United States wants Iraq to keep only two crossings with Iran open, one of them in the semi-autonomous Kurdistan region.

Hussein said Iraq and Iran share a large number of official and unofficial crossings, and closing all of them would not be easy. He warned that shutting the crossings could also hurt Iraq's economy, which depends on cross-border trade with Iran. He added that Baghdad remains in talks with Washington over the matter, while the United States continues to press its demand.
