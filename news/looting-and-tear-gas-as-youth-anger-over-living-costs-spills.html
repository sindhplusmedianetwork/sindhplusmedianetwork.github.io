Looting and tear gas as youth anger over living costs spills across Europe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Youth-led anger over the cost of living, housing shortages and strained public services spilled onto streets across Europe over the weekend. In Germany, tens of thousands of people marched in 34 cities under the banner 'Gen Z regelt', before hooded rioters looted stores in Frankfurt late on Saturday. In Portugal, police cleared a camp of more than 50 tents at Lisbon's Rossio Square.

In France, clashes broke out in several cities, and authorities opened an attempted-murder investigation after a petrol bomb was thrown at police officers in Paris. The unrest reflects a generation's frustration with rising prices and shrinking opportunities. Governments across the continent are under pressure to respond as the protests show little sign of fading.
