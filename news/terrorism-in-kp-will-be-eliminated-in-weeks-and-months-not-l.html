Terrorism in KP Will Be Eliminated in Weeks and Months, Not Long: Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Adviser to the Prime Minister on political affairs Rana Sanaullah has said that terrorism in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa will be eliminated in weeks and months, and that it will not take long. Speaking on Geo News' programme 'Naya Pakistan', he expressed the hope that the PTI would cooperate in the war on terror. He said intelligence agencies know how many terrorists there are and where they are — formations of three to 15 men, with 70 per cent of them in sensitive areas mixed in with the population.

He said residents of the affected areas were told to push the militants out of the settlements or step aside for operations, and were warned of the risk of collateral damage otherwise. Sanaullah added that the Sohail Afridi-led provincial government had stopped people from cooperating with law-enforcement agencies, while the governor-led Khyber Pakhtunkhwa setup would fully cooperate, which he said made the goal achievable.
