FBI-Wanted Drug Lord El Chapo Isidro Arrested in Mexico Operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mexican security forces arrested Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as El Chapo Isidro, an alleged major drug smuggler wanted by the US FBI, in a major operation in Guasave city of Sinaloa state. The operation involved special units of the Mexican army and National Guard, with help from US intelligence information. According to Mexico's defence ministry, two of his alleged associates were also taken into custody, including the organisation's deputy head and a key operative handling financial affairs.

After the arrest, tension spread in the area with reports of firing and explosives against security forces, roads were blocked and vehicles were set on fire, and a baseball match had to be stopped as frightened spectators and players left the field. El Chapo Isidro was included in the FBI's Ten Most Wanted list, and US authorities had announced a 5 million dollar reward for information leading to his arrest or conviction. He faces serious charges in the United States of smuggling cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine and other drugs, and Mexican officials said a legal order related to his extradition to the United States also exists.
