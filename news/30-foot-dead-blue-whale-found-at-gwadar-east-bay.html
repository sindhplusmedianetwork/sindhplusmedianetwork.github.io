30-foot dead blue whale found at Gwadar East Bay

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A large dead blue whale, about 30 feet long, was found at Gwadar East Bay, WWF-Pakistan technical adviser Moazzam Khan said. He said this is not the first stranding of a blue whale on Pakistan's coast, and the whale is an extremely rare species. The whale may have lost its way after entering shallow water, he said.

WWF-Pakistan is investigating the exact cause of the whale's death. The discovery has drawn attention to the rare marine life found along Pakistan's coastline.
