Lahore tops global pollution ranking as air quality turns 'very unhealthy'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

LAHORE: Lahore has once again topped the global pollution rankings, with the city's air quality falling into the 'very unhealthy' category on Friday. According to IQAir, Lahore recorded an Air Quality Index of 410, making it the most polluted city in the world ahead of New Delhi at 203 and Dakar at 196. The Punjab government's own monitoring platform had recorded an AQI of 339 at 11am on Thursday.

IQAir advised residents to avoid outdoor exercise, keep windows closed and wear masks to protect themselves from the hazardous air. The hazardous readings come as winter approaches, a season when smog typically worsens across Punjab.
