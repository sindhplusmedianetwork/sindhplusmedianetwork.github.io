Tractor-trolley crushes two sisters in Chiniot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two young sisters were killed when a brick-laden tractor-trolley rammed into an electric scooter near Islamia Ground on the Chiniot-Jhumra road on Saturday. Five-year-old Dua Zahra and two-year-old Fajr Fatima, residents of Chak 126 Nekokara, died in the accident, according to Rescue 1122. The sisters were travelling on the electric scooter with their mother when the speeding tractor-trolley hit them.

Their mother escaped unhurt in the accident. The bodies were shifted to the District Headquarters Hospital in Chiniot. Police and rescue teams reached the spot after the accident.
