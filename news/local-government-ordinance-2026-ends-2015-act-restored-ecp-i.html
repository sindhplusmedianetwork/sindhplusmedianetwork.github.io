Local Government Ordinance 2026 ends, 2015 Act restored, ECP issues detailed decision

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Election Commission has issued a detailed decision in the case of the delay in Islamabad's local government elections, after having issued a short decision on October 21. The commission ruled that the Islamabad Local Government Ordinance 2026 expired on September 6 after completing its term, and that all actions taken under the ordinance stand terminated. With the ordinance gone, the commission said, the Islamabad Local Government Act 2015 has been restored, and the election schedule for Islamabad must be issued under the 2015 Act, which is the law currently in force.

The commission said the constitutional requirement for establishing local governments must be implemented, and no administrative or legal obstacle should be allowed to stand in the way of local government elections. It said delimitations based on the census results were published on March 12, 2025 for 125 union councils, noting that it had carried out delimitation six times and announced the election schedule four times for the Islamabad polls. The commission said it had written to the Interior Ministry asking for early legislation on the Islamabad local government ordinance, and the ministry had said the law would be presented in the National Assembly after approval by the standing committee.

The commission held that repeated new legislation and proposed amendments by the government had caused the delay, and said such changes in the law cannot be allowed to obstruct the electoral process or postpone it indefinitely.
