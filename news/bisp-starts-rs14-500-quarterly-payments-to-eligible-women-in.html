BISP starts Rs14,500 quarterly payments to eligible women in Attock

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) has launched the disbursement of quarterly financial assistance to deserving women in Attock, with each eligible beneficiary set to receive Rs14,500 during the current quarter. According to an announcement issued by Deputy Director BISP Attock Muhammad Aashim on Saturday, payments are being distributed through the 1LINK Payment Interoperability System to ensure transparency, convenience and efficiency. Eligible beneficiaries can collect their payments through designated banking channels and authorised retailers and agents, including JazzCash, Easypaisa, HBL Connect, Bank of Punjab, Bank Alfalah and HBL Microfinance Bank.

The BISP administration has advised beneficiaries to count the full amount carefully at the time of collection and immediately report any unauthorised deductions or illegal demands through the programme's helpline at 0800-26477, warning that any deductions from the financial assistance would not be tolerated. Beneficiaries can also register complaints at their nearest BISP tehsil office or the district office in Attock, and the administration urged relevant stakeholders to widely publicise the payment arrangements so that all eligible women receive their full financial assistance without deductions.
