Lahore Once Again Declared World's Most Polluted City as AQI Hits 362

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore has once again been declared the world's most polluted city, topping the global air quality rankings on November 4, 2026. The city's overall Air Quality Index reached 362, well into the hazardous range. The highest reading of 722 was recorded in Iqbal Town.

Burki Road recorded 455, while Model Town recorded 362. The city regained the top spot after a one-day interval. Smog season has begun to grip Punjab's capital, with health experts advising residents to limit outdoor activity.

Authorities have yet to announce fresh emergency measures.
