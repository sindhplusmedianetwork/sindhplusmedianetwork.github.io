World Diabetes Prevention Day to be observed in Pakistan and around the world on November 14

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

World Diabetes Prevention Day will be observed in Pakistan and around the world on November 14. The day aims to raise awareness about the complications, symptoms and prevention of diabetes, with seminars, walks and other events planned across the country, including in Faisalabad, by the health department and social organisations. The day is observed on the birthday of the scientist who developed insulin, the medicine used to treat the disease.

According to a report, tens of millions of people around the world are affected by diabetes, and in Pakistan, by a cautious estimate, more than 10 million people suffer from the disease. Experts say obesity, smoking and lack of exercise are important causes of diabetes, and precautions and proper exercise can protect against the disease. They add that even after developing the disease, a normal life can be lived.
