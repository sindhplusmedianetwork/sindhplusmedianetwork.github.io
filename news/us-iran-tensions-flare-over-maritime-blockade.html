US-Iran tensions flare over maritime blockade

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States military has disabled a commercial vessel attempting to breach its naval blockade of Iranian ports in the Gulf of Oman, as Tehran vowed to challenge the embargo and keep the strategically vital Strait of Hormuz closed. US Central Command said military aircraft fired precision munitions at the stern of the Palau-flagged M/V Pacific Archer on Thursday after it departed from the Iranian port of Bandar Abbas, disabling the vessel's propulsion system while its civilian crew remained unharmed. Ship-tracking websites listed Singapore as the vessel's destination.

An adviser to Iran's Supreme Leader, Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei, said Tehran would neither reopen the Strait of Hormuz nor allow the American blockade to remain in place. The commander of the Revolutionary Guards' Aerospace Force, Brigadier General Majid Mousavi, spoke of Tehran's firm mission and unwavering determination, while the chief of staff of the Iranian Armed Forces, Major General Ali Abdollahi, warned that any threat or aggression would invite a decisive, effective and regret-inducing response. Iranian Navy commander Rear Admiral Shahram Irani also claimed that Iranian forces had struck the US aircraft carrier USS Abraham Lincoln with a missile on the first day of the US-Israeli war, a claim that was not independently verified.

The confrontation comes as the conflict, which began in late February, has settled into a prolonged stalemate, with Washington maintaining two aircraft carriers and several other warships in the region and Central Command saying it has redirected 158 merchant vessels since the blockade was reinstated.
