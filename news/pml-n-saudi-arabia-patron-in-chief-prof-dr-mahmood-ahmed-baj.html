PML-N Saudi Arabia Patron-in-Chief Prof Dr Mahmood Ahmed Bajwa Passes Away

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prof Dr Mahmood Ahmed Bajwa, the patron-in-chief of PML-N Saudi Arabia, has passed away in Riyadh. He was a senior leader of Majlis-e-Pakistan and a prominent social and political figure of the Pakistani community in Riyadh. News of his death has cast a pall of grief over Pakistanis living in the Saudi capital.

He was respected for his compassionate nature, sincere conduct and wise thinking, and for his social, literary and national services. Community members said the Pakistani community in Riyadh has lost a respected elder, an experienced leader and a sincere social worker. Condolences are being offered to his family, relatives and friends, with prayers for his forgiveness and patience for the bereaved family.
