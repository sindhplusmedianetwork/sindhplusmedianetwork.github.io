Even opponents accept PTI founder is a political prisoner, says Barrister Gohar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf chairman Barrister Gohar has said that even the party's political opponents now accept that the PTI founder is a political prisoner. Speaking to the media, he said the sentences in the cases against the founder were handed down behind closed doors in the darkness of the night. Gohar said the party had asked the court that its position be heard in open court in the light of day.

He maintained that the founder's detention was political in nature rather than criminal. The PTI founder is serving a 14-year prison sentence in the £190 million case, while Bushra Bibi was sentenced to seven years in the same case.
