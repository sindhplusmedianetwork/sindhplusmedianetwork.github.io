UHS Lahore raises public medical college fees by 233% after 15-year freeze

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The University of Health Sciences Lahore has announced a 233 per cent increase in annual fees for public-sector medical and dental colleges. According to the university's spokesperson, the hike comes under the new admission policy approved for the 2026–27 session. Fees had remained frozen at Rs15,000 since 2010, a gap of about fifteen years.

Students securing admission in the 2026–27 session will now pay Rs50,000 per year. The spokesperson said the revision was necessary to align fee structures with rising operational costs. The decision affects government medical and dental colleges across Punjab affiliated with UHS.

Parents and student groups have raised concerns about the sharp increase, while the university maintains the move was overdue.
