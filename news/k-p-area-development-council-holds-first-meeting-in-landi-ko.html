K-P Area Development Council holds first meeting in Landi Kotal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The first meeting of the Area Development Council set up for the development of 12 designated districts of Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa was held at the Landi Kotal Jirga Hall. More than 100 members attended the meeting. Council chairman and former K-P governor Shaukatullah Khan chaired the session.

He said the council's main aim was to establish sustainable peace in the 12 districts and advance development through broad consultation. Participants expressed a collective resolve to work together for peace, development projects and the resolution of public issues. The council is expected to play a central role in guiding development work across the designated districts.
