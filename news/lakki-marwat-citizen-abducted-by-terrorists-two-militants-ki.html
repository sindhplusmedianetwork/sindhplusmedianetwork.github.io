Lakki Marwat: citizen abducted by terrorists, two militants killed in police and CTD operation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In Khairu Khel Pakka in Lakki Marwat, terrorists abducted a shepherd and took him towards the hills. Police, the Counter Terrorism Department and local peace committees chased the abductors and killed two militants, Hewad and Sohail, in an exchange of fire. One civilian was injured in the firing and was taken to hospital.

Police took the bodies of the killed militants into custody. A search operation has been launched for the militants' hideouts and accomplices.
