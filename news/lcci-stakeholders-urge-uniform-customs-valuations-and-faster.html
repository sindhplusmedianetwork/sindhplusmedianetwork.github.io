LCCI stakeholders urge uniform customs valuations and faster import clearance

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Business leaders, industrialists and customs clearing agents have called for a transparent, uniform and market-based customs valuation system. The call came at a consultative session at the Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry. Stakeholders cited inconsistent assessments, unrealistic valuations, long appeal procedures and the detention of consignments as key problems.

They urged that valuations be based on international trade data and actual transaction values. LCCI President Ali Hussam Asghar stressed that stakeholders must be consulted before any changes are made to the valuation mechanism.
