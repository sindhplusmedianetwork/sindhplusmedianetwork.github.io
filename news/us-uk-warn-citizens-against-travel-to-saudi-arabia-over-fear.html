US, UK Warn Citizens Against Travel to Saudi Arabia Over Fears of More Airport Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States and Britain have issued fresh travel warnings for Saudi Arabia, citing security concerns and the threat of further attacks on the kingdom's airports. Britain's Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office advised its citizens against all travel within 10 kilometres of the Yemen border and against all but essential travel to the rest of Saudi Arabia, warning that defying official advice could invalidate travel insurance. The FCDO also told British nationals to stay away from Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport after what it described as a serious incident there on October 10, and warned that further attacks on Saudi airports could disrupt or cancel flights.

Britons wishing to leave were advised to consider travelling overland to a neighbouring country and flying out from there, after checking entry and exit rules. Separately, the US embassy in Riyadh issued a security alert for American citizens following recent missile and drone attacks on Saudi airports, urging extreme caution when using them and strict compliance with Saudi security directives. The embassy said operations at Riyadh's airport remained suspended, while Jeddah and Dammam airports continued operating despite some delays and cancellations.

US government personnel were told they now need special permission from the chief of mission to use any airport in Saudi Arabia. The embassy also advised Americans to monitor Saudi civil defence alerts and to check flight status with their airline and airport before travelling.
