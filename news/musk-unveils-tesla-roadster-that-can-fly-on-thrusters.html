Musk unveils Tesla Roadster that can 'fly' on thrusters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Tesla CEO Elon Musk has unveiled what he described as a 'flying' next-generation Roadster during a livestreamed event at SpaceX's test facility in Texas. The car is equipped with thrusters powered by ultra high-pressure air tanks. In the demonstration, the vehicle launched from a steep ramp and landed safely on its wheels.

Musk told viewers the car represented a major leap in automotive engineering. He announced that the vehicle would go into production in 2028. The reveal drew wide attention online, with the 'flying car' concept sparking debate over its real-world feasibility.
