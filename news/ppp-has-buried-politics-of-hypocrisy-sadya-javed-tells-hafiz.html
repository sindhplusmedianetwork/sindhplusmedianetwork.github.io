PPP Has Buried Politics of Hypocrisy, Sadya Javed Tells Hafiz Naeem

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh government spokesperson Sadya Javed responded to Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman's statement on Friday. She said the Pakistan Peoples Party has buried the politics of hypocrisy and lies, adding that Naeem was suffering from political frustration. Javed said Jamaat-e-Islami used to accuse the Sindh government of withholding funds from towns, yet projects completed with Sindh government resources are now being inaugurated.

She said the PPP never discriminated against any town on the basis of political affiliation. Funds were provided to all towns without distinction for the uniform development of the city, she added. The exchange marks a fresh war of words between the PPP and Jamaat-e-Islami over Karachi's towns.
