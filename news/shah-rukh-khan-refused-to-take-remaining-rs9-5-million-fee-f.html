Shah Rukh Khan Refused to Take Remaining Rs9.5 Million Fee for 2002 Film, Reveals Producer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Veteran Indian filmmaker K.C. Bokadia has revealed that Shah Rukh Khan never collected the remaining Rs9.5 million of his fee for the 2002 film Hum Tumhare Hain Sanam. Bokadia said he had agreed a fee of Rs20 million with Shah Rukh for the film.

Of that amount, Rs10.5 million was paid, leaving Rs9.5 million outstanding. According to Bokadia, the actor later told his son that he did not want the balance out of respect for him. Bokadia praised Shah Rukh, Amitabh Bachchan, Salman Khan, Jackie Shroff and Rajinikanth for keeping their demands modest.

He said the actors did not pass their personal staff costs on to producers.
