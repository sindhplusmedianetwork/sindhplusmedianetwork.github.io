NCCIA recovers Rs2.5 million a woman lost to fake Qurani wazaif page on Facebook

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) Karachi Zone recovered the full Rs2.5 million a woman lost to a Facebook page offering Qurani wazaif and spiritual servants. The victim, Syeda Arjumand, transferred the money in November 2025 to bank accounts later traced to a man named Aafaq. The agency recovered the entire amount after reaching the account holder.

The NCCIA warned the public to beware of fake spiritual healers on social media who demand money in the name of religious treatment.
