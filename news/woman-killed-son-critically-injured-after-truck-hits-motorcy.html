Woman Killed, Son Critically Injured After Truck Hits Motorcycle in Sialkot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman was killed and her son critically injured on Wednesday when a speeding truck hit their motorcycle near Aladdin Park on the Pasrur-Daska Road in Sialkot, rescue officials said. Following the initial collision, the mother and son fell onto the road and were then struck by an oncoming tractor-trolley coming from the opposite direction. The deceased was identified as Shamim Akhtar, 45, who died on the spot, while her 19-year-old son, Salman Qamar Din, sustained critical injuries.

Rescue 1122 personnel arrived at the scene and shifted the injured youth and the body of the deceased to the Tehsil Headquarters Hospital in Pasrur. Police have not yet released details of any arrests in connection with the accident.
