Fire at Lahore Foam Factory Brought Under Control, One Body Recovered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A massive fire at the Star Foam factory in Lahore's Sundar Industrial Estate has been brought under control, rescue officials said, but a search operation later recovered one body from the premises. The fire broke out at 10:21pm in raw material used for foam manufacturing and quickly engulfed the factory. Rescue 1122 dispatched six fire vehicles, four rescue vehicles, an ambulance and a specialised vehicle, with more than 35 rescuers taking part in the operation.

Once the flames were doused, rescuers carried out a search of the site and found the body of one person. Officials said the identity of the deceased could not yet be ascertained. The cause of the fire was not immediately stated; rescue authorities said further details were awaited.
