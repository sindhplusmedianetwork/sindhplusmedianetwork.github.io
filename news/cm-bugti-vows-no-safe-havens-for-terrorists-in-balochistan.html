CM Bugti vows no safe havens for terrorists in Balochistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan Chief Minister Mir Sarfraz Bugti has declared that terrorist elements seeking to destabilise the province will not be allowed to establish safe havens. He said the state would employ an effective strategy to defeat terrorism and protect the lives of citizens. Bugti was commenting on a security operation in the Kolpur area, in which eight terrorists affiliated with Fitna al-Hindustan were killed.

He said intelligence-based operations are continuing in Kolpur, Kirdgap and Nushki. The chief minister also paid tribute to Major Umar Minhas Nasir and Havaldar Muhammad Adnan Khan, who were martyred in a terrorist attack.
