WhatsApp Testing Feature to Let Users Hide Their Phone Number

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WhatsApp is developing a new privacy feature that will let users decide who can see their phone number. The feature was spotted in Android beta version 2.26.45.1, according to technology website WABetaInfo. Users will get four options for displaying their number: everyone, only people in their contacts, everyone except selected contacts, and nobody.

The setting will apply to calls and chats. The feature will be available only to users who have created a username on WhatsApp. It will hide the number by default from people who contact the user through a username.

No launch date has been announced.
