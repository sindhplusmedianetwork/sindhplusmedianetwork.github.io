Israel launches sweeping West Bank operation after two soldiers killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israel's military launched a major operation in the occupied West Bank on Friday, expected to last several days, after gunmen killed two soldiers at the Farsh al-Hawa checkpoint near Hebron. The operation includes searches, questioning of Palestinians, and the demolition of buildings, as forces seek to dismantle what they call terrorist infrastructure in the area from which the attackers emerged. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, who visited the scene, ordered a lockdown of the West Bank and said he was considering an operation more extensive than any since the 1967 war.

An AFP photographer witnessed the gun battle at the checkpoint, in which the two Palestinian attackers were eventually shot dead. Hamas, without claiming responsibility, praised the attack as a heroic operation and called for more. Israeli rights group B'Tselem called for urgent international pressure to prevent plans for collective punishment, while UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres voiced concern over closures affecting Palestinian livelihoods.

According to an AFP tally, Israeli forces and settlers have killed 1,117 Palestinians in the territory since the Gaza war began in October 2023.
