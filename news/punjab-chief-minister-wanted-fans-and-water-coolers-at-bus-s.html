Punjab chief minister wanted fans and water coolers at bus shelters — but feared they would be stolen

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has said she wanted to install fans and water coolers at bus shelters so that passengers waiting for transport would not have to stand in the sun, rain or cold. She was speaking at a Honhaar Scholarship ceremony in Rawalpindi, where she shared the idea with the audience. The chief minister said she was told that people would steal the fans and water coolers if they were installed.

She described the situation as shameful and said it was every Pakistani's duty to discourage such theft. She added that even manhole covers are stolen at night by people on donkey carts. The remarks highlighted the challenge of providing public facilities when theft remains a persistent problem.
