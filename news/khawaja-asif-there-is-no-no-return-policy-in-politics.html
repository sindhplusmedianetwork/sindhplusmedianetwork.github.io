Khawaja Asif: There Is No 'No Return' Policy in Politics

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defence Minister Khawaja Asif has said there is no 'no return' policy in politics. Yesterday's friends become today's enemies and yesterday's enemies become today's friends, he remarked while responding to Maulana Fazlur Rehman's harsh remarks at Parliament House. Asif said voices inside the PTI are calling for politics instead of resistance.

He advised the PTI's leadership to step out of social media's influence, adding that doing so would benefit their politics. His comments come amid shifting political alignments in the country.
