Fake Gabapin 300 mg Seized in Lahore's Lohari Market Raid

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab's Drug Control department has raided Lohari Market in Lahore's Data Ganj Bakhsh Town and seized a large quantity of counterfeit Gabapin 300 mg tablets falsely branded as Braille Pharma's product. Samples have been sent for laboratory analysis and permission has been sought to register a case against those involved. Investigators are tracing the origin and supply chain of the fake medicine.

The public has been advised to buy medicines only on a doctor's prescription from licensed pharmacies. Chief Drug Controller Dr Faisal Mahmood said the counterfeit-drug mafia's 'back has been broken' and operations against the remaining elements are continuing.
