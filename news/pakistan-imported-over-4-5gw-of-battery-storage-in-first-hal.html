Pakistan imported over 4.5GW of battery storage in first half of 2026

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan imported more than 4.5 gigawatts of battery storage equipment in the first half of 2026, nearly half of the country's cumulative imports of 9.5GW. The figures were disclosed at a parliamentary dialogue on energy and the economy organised by the Parliamentary Forum on Energy and Economy in Karachi. Participants discussed the future of battery storage in the power sector and the regulatory arrangements needed to govern it.

The rapid uptake shows households and industries increasingly turning to energy storage to avoid high electricity tariffs. The dialogue brought together discussion on how storage can reshape Pakistan's power mix and the policy framework around it.
