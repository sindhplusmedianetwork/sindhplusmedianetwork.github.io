Musk Ordered Limits on Starshield Services for Ukraine, WSJ Reports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Elon Musk ordered limits on Starshield satellite communication services for Ukraine, the Wall Street Journal reported, citing unnamed US officials and people familiar with the matter. According to the report, the restrictions slowed the connection speeds of Ukrainian devices, rendering some drones inoperable. Ukraine had earlier accused Washington of deliberately slowing the system.

President Donald Trump denied that he had requested any restrictions. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Washington had no prior knowledge of the move and described Starshield as a private service. The Pentagon also said it had issued no such directive.

The report has intensified the row over Musk's role in Ukraine's communications.
