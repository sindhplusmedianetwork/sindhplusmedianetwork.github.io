Fawad Chaudhry Says PPP Has Rejected Referendum on New Provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former federal minister Fawad Chaudhry said the Pakistan Peoples Party has rejected a referendum on the creation of new provinces. In a statement on X, he said he spoke with President Asif Ali Zardari and PPP leaders at a wedding in Lahore. He said their conversation left him with the impression that the party is not in favour of a referendum on new provinces.

Fawad Chaudhry said that allegations of rigging in the elections aside, there is no doubt that the current parliament was formed by changing the results. He said questions remain over the public mandate behind the formation of the current parliament. He said a referendum is the best way to learn the people's real opinion.

He said the people should get the chance to express their opinion directly.
