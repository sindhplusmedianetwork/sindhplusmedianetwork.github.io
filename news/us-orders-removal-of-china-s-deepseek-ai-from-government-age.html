US Orders Removal of China's DeepSeek AI From Government Agency Systems

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The US Department of Homeland Security has ordered DeepSeek products and third-party services built on the Chinese AI model to be removed from civilian agency IT systems. A notice published on Monday said the models present 'multiple threat vectors'. It warned that use by government employees or vendors 'may result in the transfer of sensitive data' to China.

The department also cautioned that the models could contain malware or security vulnerabilities. The order adds to earlier bans on the Chinese chatbot imposed by agencies including the Commerce Department and the Navy. Several US states have also moved to restrict DeepSeek on government devices.
