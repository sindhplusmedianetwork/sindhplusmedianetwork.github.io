Militants Killed While Trying to Escape Disguised as Women in North Waziristan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces foiled an attempt by Fitna al-Khawarij terrorists to escape disguised as women in the Mir Ali area of North Waziristan. According to security sources, the Khawarij terrorists who were trying to escape in a rickshaw disguised as women were killed. The report said the Khawarij use of women's burqas is not only contrary to Islamic and moral values but also causes exploitation of the sanctity of women.

After being pushed back by the security forces, the terrorists are making failed attempts to survive through cheap and low tactics. Evidence has also emerged of the Khawarij hiding in mosques and in civilian populations for terrorist activities and placing explosive material there. In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Fitna al-Khawarij terrorists also use innocent children and women as human shields for their nefarious purposes.

Security forces are continuing full operations under the Azm-e-Istehkam vision for the complete elimination of Fitna al-Khawarij terrorists from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
