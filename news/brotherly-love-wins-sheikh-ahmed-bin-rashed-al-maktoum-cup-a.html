Brotherly Love Wins Sheikh Ahmed Bin Rashed Al Maktoum Cup At Dubai Racing Carnival Opener

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brotherly Love, trained by British trainer Jamie Osborne and ridden by Danny Tudhope, won the Sheikh Ahmed Bin Rashed Al Maktoum Cup as the 2026-2027 Dubai Racing Carnival opened at Meydan Racecourse on Friday. The three-year-old beat Sirocco Winds by five and a quarter lengths on his handicap debut, having finished fourth in the G2 UAE Derby on his previous appearance. Colour Up claimed his first Meydan victory since 2023 in the Emirates Airline Conditions Stakes.

French jockey Jules Mobian secured his first Meydan win aboard Desert Horizon.
