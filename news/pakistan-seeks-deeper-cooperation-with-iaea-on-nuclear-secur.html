Pakistan seeks deeper cooperation with IAEA on nuclear security

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan looks forward to further deepening cooperation with the International Atomic Energy Agency and international partners in support of sustainable nuclear security, the Foreign Office said on Friday. The statement came as the 15th annual meeting of the International Network for Nuclear Security Training and Support Centres concluded in Islamabad. Pakistan hosted the meeting in collaboration with the IAEA.

The meeting was held from November 23 to 26. It brought together delegates from 49 member states to discuss nuclear security training and capacity building. The gathering focused on strengthening cooperation in nuclear security training and support.
