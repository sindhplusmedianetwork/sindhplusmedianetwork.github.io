Industrialists oppose separating income and sales tax systems, demand one-window facility

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The business community in Karachi has expressed concern over the Federal Board of Revenue's decision to separate the income tax and sales tax systems. Traders described the proposed separation as a heavy financial burden and a source of confusion. SITE Association of Industry President Abdul Hadi said traders should not be made to visit multiple offices in the name of reforms.

He demanded consultation with the business community on the proposed policy. He also called for a true one-window facility where all tax matters can be resolved in one place.
