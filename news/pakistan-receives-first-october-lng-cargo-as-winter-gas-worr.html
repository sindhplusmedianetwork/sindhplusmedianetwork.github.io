Pakistan Receives First October LNG Cargo as Winter Gas Worries Grow

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan received its first liquefied natural gas cargo of October from Qatar this week. The country will need around 24 LNG cargoes to meet gas requirements during the winter of 2026-27. Sui Northern's gas demand is projected to surge from 550 million to 1,550 million cubic feet per day in the cold months.

The widening demand-supply gap has raised fears of gas shortages in winter. Industrial units, the CNG sector and cement plants may face supply curtailments during peak winter days. Authorities are working on a load-management plan to prioritise domestic consumers.
