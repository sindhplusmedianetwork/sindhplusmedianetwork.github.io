Karachi Residents Say New Administrative Units and Smaller Provinces Inevitable

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Residents of Karachi have said new administrative units and smaller provinces have become inevitable. They linked the demand to the city's growing population and increasing administrative needs. Citizens said more provinces would strengthen local administration.

They added that smaller provinces would improve the performance of chief ministers. Residents said the number of provinces should grow with the population. They said this would help people benefit from budgets and basic facilities.
