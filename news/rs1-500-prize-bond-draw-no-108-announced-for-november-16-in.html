Rs1,500 prize bond draw No. 108 announced for November 16 in Hyderabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Central Directorate of National Savings has announced the date for the next Rs1,500 prize bond draw. Draw number 108 will be held on Monday, November 16, 2026, in Hyderabad. It will be the fourth and final draw of the year for this denomination.

The first prize carries Rs3 million, the second prize Rs1 million, and each third prize is worth Rs18,500. Results will be published on the National Savings website after the draw. Only bonds issued at least 60 days before the draw date qualify for the ballot.

Winnings are subject to withholding tax of 15 percent for filers and 30 percent for non-filers.
