Man Hid Friend's Body in Freezer for Three Years and Took Loans in Her Name

A man is accused of hiding his friend's body in a freezer for three years while taking out loans in her name. George Gordon, 65, is accused of murdering his friend Delia McEnerney in April 2023. The alleged murder took place just days before she was due to move to Portugal for what was described as a 'new life'.

Prosecutors say Gordon hid her body in a freezer for three years. During that time, he allegedly took out loans in her name.
