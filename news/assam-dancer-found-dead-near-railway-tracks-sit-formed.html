Assam Dancer Found Dead Near Railway Tracks; SIT Formed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The death of 20-year-old Assamese student Himashree Boro, whose body was found near railway tracks in Andhra Pradesh's Srikakulam district on October 7, is now being probed by a Special Investigation Team. Boro, a BBA student at Guwahati's B Borooah College, had travelled to Visakhapatnam on August 16 with a 10-member dance troupe for cultural programmes. After missing her train home on October 6, she boarded the late-running Vivek Express, where she reportedly called a friend complaining of misbehaviour by fellow passengers.

Her body was later recovered near Penubarthi village between Vizianagaram and Srikakulam Road. A preliminary post-mortem found no signs of physical or sexual violence, though investigators are still determining whether she fell accidentally or was pushed. Forensic experts, clues teams, dog squads and drone teams have inspected the scene, while police are analysing CCTV footage, call records and witness statements.

Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma has written to the Andhra Pradesh chief minister and the Union railway minister seeking a thorough, impartial and time-bound investigation.
