Man bites four people with his teeth in Sanghar and flees

In a startling incident in Sanghar, a man allegedly bit four people with his teeth and then fled the scene. The unusual episode took place in front of a private bank in Sanghar city, where people were standing when the man suddenly attacked them. Two of the injured were identified as Sanol Younis and Yaseen Anwar, while the identities of the other two have not yet been confirmed.

According to the victims, the man appeared out of nowhere, bit them, and ran away. Police have obtained the CCTV footage of the incident as part of the inquiry. The bizarre attack has astonished residents across the city.
