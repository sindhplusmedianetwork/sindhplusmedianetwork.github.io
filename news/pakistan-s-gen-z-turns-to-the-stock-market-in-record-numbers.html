Pakistan's 'Gen-Z' Turns to the Stock Market in Record Numbers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Young Pakistanis are entering the stock market in record numbers, according to new data from the National Clearing Company of Pakistan. Between August 2025 and September 2026, more than 254,000 new investor accounts were opened, of which 46 percent belonged to people aged 18 to 30. September 2026 alone brought 25,281 new investors — the highest number for any single month — and 54 percent of them were young people.

Market watchers attribute the surge to the ease of opening accounts, stability in the dollar's value, strong market returns of 85 percent in 2023 and over 55 percent in 2025, losses in cryptocurrency, and growing financial awareness through social media. The report highlights examples including 23-year-old Zainab Fatima from Hyderabad, Noor from Jhelum, and A-level students Muhammad Ali Madani and Talha Anas from Karachi. The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan aims to raise the total number of investors to 2.5 million.
