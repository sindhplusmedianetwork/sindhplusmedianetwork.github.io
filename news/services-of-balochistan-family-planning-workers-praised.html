Services of Balochistan Family Planning Workers Praised

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Community-based workers providing door-to-door family planning services were praised at a meeting of the Population Welfare Department and the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) in Quetta on Sunday. Officials of the Population Welfare Department, UNFPA, representatives and female workers from 15 districts of Balochistan presented their performance reports and the challenges and difficulties they were facing in their respective districts during their campaign. The meeting was informed that female community workers visiting households to provide married women with information about family planning have been playing a vital role in raising awareness and promoting responsible family planning practices.

Appreciating the efforts of female workers delivering these services at the household level, officials urged them to further improve their performance and reach out to a greater number of married couples with family planning information. They stressed that expanding awareness and access to family planning services was essential to addressing the rapid population growth in Pakistan, particularly in Balochistan.
