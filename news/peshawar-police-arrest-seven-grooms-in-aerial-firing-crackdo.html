Peshawar police arrest seven grooms in aerial-firing crackdown

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Peshawar have arrested seven grooms and 25 others over aerial firing during wedding celebrations. The crackdown was launched on the directives of the capital city police chief, Dr Mian Saeed. Police said the seven grooms were arrested within just two days, and weapons were recovered from them.

The arrests were made in different parts of the city during wedding functions. Cases have been registered under Sections 3 and 4 relating to aerial firing. Police have warned that action against celebratory firing will continue.
