Mustafa Kamal Backs Karachi as Part of Federation, Vows No Retreat on New Provinces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister Mustafa Kamal has said he will support any move to make Karachi part of the federation and will not retreat an inch from the new provinces movement. He described December as a crucial month and said whatever is to happen must happen before the next budget. He called new provinces a matter of survival for coming generations.

He also advised the PTI to support new provinces unconditionally. He said the prime minister could summon a joint session of parliament to approve a referendum on the issue.
