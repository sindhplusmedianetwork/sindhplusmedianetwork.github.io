1,419 Crime Cases Registered in Islamabad This Year, Police Document Reveals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad police have recorded 1,419 crime cases in the federal capital this year, according to a police document. Across five categories of crime, 2,428 accused were named, of whom 876 have been arrested while 1,550 remain absconders. Robbery and looting of homes and shops made up the largest share with 723 cases, followed by 491 kidnapping cases.

Sexual crimes accounted for 177 cases with 254 accused named, while 327 accused in kidnapping cases are still absconding. Murder cases numbered 74 with 192 accused named and 80 absconding, and 70 cases of vehicle theft were registered in which 52 accused have not been arrested so far. The document further states that 771 cases are under investigation, challans in 380 cases have been submitted in courts, 200 cases were cancelled and 68 remain untraced.

The figures paint a picture of persistent crime and a large backlog of wanted suspects in the federal capital.
