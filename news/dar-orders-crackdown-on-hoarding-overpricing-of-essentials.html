Dar Orders Crackdown on Hoarding, Overpricing of Essentials

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar on Friday ordered authorities to take strict action against hoarding, artificial shortages and overpricing of essential commodities, chairing a meeting of the Cabinet Committee on Essential Commodities in Islamabad. The committee reviewed stock positions, supply arrangements and prices, and Dar was informed that adequate stocks were available to meet domestic demand with no shortages anticipated and prices stabilising. The meeting was attended by the ministers for National Food Security and Research and Climate Change, Special Assistant to the Prime Minister Tariq Bajwa, the commerce secretary and senior federal and provincial officials.

The directive comes as September's year-on-year consumer inflation stood at 10.3 per cent, with the Sensitive Price Indicator rising nearly 12 per cent in the week ending October 8; onion prices more than doubled over the year while petrol climbed 47.52 per cent. Punjab's wheat troubles formed the backdrop: the province procured only 480,000 tonnes of its planned three million and could not trace about four million tonnes believed to have been hoarded. In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, restrictions on wheat supplies from Punjab disrupted mill deliveries and pushed a 20kg flour bag to Rs3,300 in Peshawar in early October.

The IMF has meanwhile raised its inflation forecast for Pakistan to 9.9 per cent for the current fiscal year, up from 8.4 per cent, while keeping the growth forecast at 3.5 per cent.
