Sindh Health Department Launches Digital System for Hospitals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Health Minister Dr Azra Pechuho inaugurated a digital health system at a ceremony held in a local Karachi hotel on Tuesday. Under the new system, patient records in hospitals across Sindh will be digitized, helping keep patient data secure and organized. The minister said digital data will prove vital for future health policy-making and will ensure transparency in service delivery and operational spending.

HMIS, portal tracking, DHIS2 and EMR data will be integrated under the unified platform. Officials said the rollout will begin in major public hospitals before expanding to facilities across the province.
