Khloé Kardashian says Lamar Odom is 'dead to me' after Netflix documentary

Khloé Kardashian has said she will never forgive her former husband Lamar Odom after taking issue with comments he made about their marriage in his Netflix documentary, Untold: The Death & Life of Lamar Odom. Speaking on the November 12 episode of Hulu's The Kardashians, she said she was unhappy with how their relationship was portrayed. She criticised his suggestion that he married her to gain popularity.

Kardashian said the bridge is burned and there is no more rope left to throw over. Her remarks show the pair's relationship remains broken after the documentary. The episode aired on November 12.
