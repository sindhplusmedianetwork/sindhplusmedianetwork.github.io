Bitcoin rebounds sharply after sliding to two-year low

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bitcoin staged a sharp recovery on Saturday, climbing more than 8 per cent to around $59,952 after a steep slide earlier. The cryptocurrency had fallen to $55,251.60, marking its lowest level in two years. The decline followed stretched valuations in artificial intelligence stocks, which rattled investors and weighed heavily on risk assets.

Market conditions also turned tougher for cryptocurrencies as the dollar strengthened. Higher real yields added further pressure on digital assets during the sell-off. Greater volatility across markets created additional headwinds for cryptocurrencies.

The rebound on Saturday lifted Bitcoin back from its two-year low.
