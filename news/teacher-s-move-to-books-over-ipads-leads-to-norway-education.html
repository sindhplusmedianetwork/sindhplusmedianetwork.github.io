Teacher's Move to Books Over iPads Leads to Norway Education Policy Review

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A step taken by a school teacher three years ago is now taking the shape of a change in Norway's education policy. After children's reading and writing skills were affected, Norway has started reviewing its digital education experiment, and the requirement of an iPad or laptop for every child in primary schools has ended. Three years ago, a teacher, seeing students' weak academic performance, removed iPads and started teaching with books, pen and paper, for which he was given a written warning.

Digital education had been promoted in Norway for about 20 years, but in the 2025 PISA survey, the performance of 15-year-old students in reading, mathematics and science remained below the OECD average. Proposals for a social media ban for children under 16 in Norway and limiting the use of smart glasses are also under consideration.
