Shaheen Afridi Takes Seven Wickets as FATA Beat Faisalabad by Seven Wickets

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

FATA recorded a seven-wicket victory over Faisalabad in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy, wrapping up the match inside two days. Shaheen Shah Afridi led the way in the first innings, taking seven wickets for 43 runs while representing FATA. In the second innings, Afaq Afridi claimed eight wickets for 70 runs as Faisalabad were bowled out for 154.

Chasing a target of 135, FATA reached the total for the loss of only three wickets. Mohammad Farooq played a central role with the bat, scoring 90 in the first innings and remaining unbeaten on 62 in the second. His two innings helped FATA stay in control throughout the match.

The win underlined a strong all-round performance from the FATA side in both departments.
