Israel Piles Pressure on Arab Parties as Netanyahu Seeks Government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israel on Thursday piled pressure on Arab parties as Benjamin Netanyahu sought to form a government following the inconclusive Israeli election. Netanyahu's right-wing alliance won a projected 53 seats while the opposition alliance took 48, neither reaching the 61-seat majority needed to form a government. Arab parties achieved a historic breakthrough with 19 seats, making them the decisive factor in government formation, with Netanyahu's Likud remaining the largest single party at 26 seats.

Qatar's foreign ministry rejected claims by Israeli officials that the Gulf country had interfered in the election. Netanyahu is set to remain caretaker prime minister while negotiations continue.
