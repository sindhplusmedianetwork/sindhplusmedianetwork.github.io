SECP notifies rules to define and prohibit Ponzi schemes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has notified the Companies (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Regulations, 2026. The regulations define and prohibit Ponzi schemes, pyramid schemes, deceptive multi-level marketing and unlawful referral marketing. They apply to all companies, including foreign firms operating in Pakistan, and to digital channels.

The rules bar companies from deriving income primarily through recruitment. They also prohibit paying old investors with the funds of new investors.
