Court Orders Arrest of Rakhi Sawant in Suicide Case

WhatsApp Facebook X

A court in Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh, has issued non-bailable warrants against Indian actress and dancer Rakhi Sawant in a 2010 suicide case. According to Indian media, the case relates to alleged defamatory remarks made against a man on her TV show 'Rakhi ka Insaaf', after which he allegedly took his own life. The additional district and sessions judge of a fast-track court issued the warrants on October 16 after the actress repeatedly failed to appear in court.

Police have been directed to arrest Rakhi Sawant and produce her in court. Prosecutor Mridul Kant Srivastava said the next hearing will be on December 1. The case concerns the death of Lakshman Ahirwar, a resident of Hansari Prem Nagar, Jhansi, who died on November 10, 2010.

After his death, his mother Savitri filed a case alleging that Rakhi Sawant had used derogatory and inappropriate words against her son in her October 2010 programme. According to the prosecution, Lakshman and his wife Anita were invited to the programme because of their differences.
