Two missiles fired towards Riyadh destroyed, Saudi-led coalition says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Saudi-led coalition said two missiles fired by the Houthi militia towards the capital Riyadh were destroyed. The missiles were intercepted before they could reach the city, according to the coalition. After the interception, the danger alert in Riyadh was withdrawn and life in the capital returned to normal.

Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki announced the interception on X, the social media platform. He gave no further details about where the missiles were launched from or how they were stopped. The Houthis have repeatedly fired missiles and drones towards Saudi Arabia during the conflict in Yemen.

Saudi air defences regularly intercept such projectiles before they reach populated areas.
