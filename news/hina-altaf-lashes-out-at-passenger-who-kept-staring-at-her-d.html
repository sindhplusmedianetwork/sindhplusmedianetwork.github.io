Hina Altaf lashes out at passenger who kept staring at her during flight

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actress Hina Altaf shared a video on social media. In the video, she expressed anger at a fellow passenger who she said kept staring at her during a flight. She said the staring left her very uncomfortable.

She said on Instagram that when the man realised she was recording him, he hid his face briefly and then resumed staring. She called his behaviour shameless. She also shared a photo of herself in simple clothes, glasses and a face mask, asking whether anything was wrong with her dress.
