Heavy snowfall disrupts life in Japan, highways closed and trains suspended

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A powerful winter storm and heavy snowfall disrupted transport in northern and eastern Japan, suspending rail services, closing highways and leaving about 1,400 people stranded. Fukushima was among the worst-hit prefectures as the storm brought daily life to a standstill in affected areas. In Kitashiobara village, residents and tourists were cut off by closed roads and power outages caused by fallen trees.

A train also derailed after hitting a tree on the Banetsu West Line, with no passengers aboard at the time.
