PSX closes on a positive note as KSE-100 gains 1,068 points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) saw a positive trading day on Tuesday, with the benchmark KSE-100 Index gaining 1,068 points. The index closed at 178,542 points at the end of trading. During the day, 640 million shares were traded, with the total value of the deals standing at Rs23 billion.

Market capitalisation rose by Rs131 billion to reach Rs19,385 billion. The positive close came after a day of trading in which investors remained active across the market.
