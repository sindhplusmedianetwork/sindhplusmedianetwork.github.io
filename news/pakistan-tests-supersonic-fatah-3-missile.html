Pakistan tests supersonic Fatah-3 missile

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan successfully test-fired the Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile on Wednesday, the military's media wing said. According to the Inter-Services Public Relations, the missile is equipped with advanced navigation and propulsion systems. It can engage targets at long ranges with high precision at supersonic speeds.

The launch was witnessed by the commander of the Army Rocket Force Command, senior officers and scientists. President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Field Marshal Asim Munir congratulated the armed forces. The test demonstrated the missile's advanced navigation and propulsion capabilities.

The successful launch marked another step in Pakistan's missile programme.
