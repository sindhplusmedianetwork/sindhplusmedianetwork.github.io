Over 23.4 Million Children Vaccinated in First Five Days of Polio Drive

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 23.4 million children have received polio drops in the first five days of the sub-national anti-polio campaign running in 97 selected districts across the country, the National Emergency Operations Centre (NEOC) said. The sixth day of the campaign is under way, and vaccination activities will continue without interruption until November 8. The campaign aims to vaccinate around 24 million children under the age of five, with more than 200,000 frontline polio workers on duty.

Provincial figures show that over 9.025 million children were vaccinated in 13 selected districts of Punjab, more than 6.856 million in 20 districts of Sindh, over 4.624 million in 27 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and 2.169 million in 31 districts of Balochistan. More than 450,000 children were vaccinated in Islamabad, 161,000 in three selected districts of Gilgit-Baltistan, and about 192,000 in two selected districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir. The NEOC warned that the risk of poliovirus remains across the country, stressing that polio is incurable and vaccination is the only effective protection, and urged parents to cooperate fully with polio teams and complete their children's routine immunisation on time.
