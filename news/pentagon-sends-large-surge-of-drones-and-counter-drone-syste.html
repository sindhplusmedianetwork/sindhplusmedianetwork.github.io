Pentagon Sends Large Surge of Drones and Counter-Drone Systems to Middle East Forces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pentagon said on Wednesday it delivered more than 25,000 unmanned systems and counter-drone capabilities to US Central Command this year. The deliveries followed a $782 million investment in unmanned and counter-drone technology. They include more than 1,300 one-way attack drones and over 100 unmanned surface and underwater vessels.

More than 24,000 counter-drone systems were also delivered to forces in the region. The effort is part of a wider push to expand the use of unmanned systems across the military. It also supports plans for a new Autonomous Warfare Command.

That command is expected to become operational by October 1, 2027.
