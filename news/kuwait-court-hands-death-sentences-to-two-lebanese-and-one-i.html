Kuwait Court Hands Death Sentences to Two Lebanese and One Indian in Drug Cases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Kuwaiti criminal court has sentenced two Lebanese nationals and one Indian national to death for drug smuggling offences, according to Kuwaiti media. The court issued the verdicts in two separate drug-related cases. In the first case, two Lebanese citizens were found guilty of buying, selling and using narcotics and banned medicines.

In the second case, an Indian national was sentenced to death for attempting to smuggle psychotropic substances into the country, allegedly hiding them in a consignment of shoes imported from abroad. In the same hearings, the court handed a woman life imprisonment plus a 50,000-dinar fine for dealing and using narcotic substances and driving under their influence, suspending her driving licence for a year. Separately, Kuwaiti authorities announced the arrest of a Bangladeshi security guard accused of murdering a fellow Yemeni guard at a construction site in Al-Mutlaa.
