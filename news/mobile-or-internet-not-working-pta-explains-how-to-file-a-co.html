Mobile or internet not working? PTA explains how to file a complaint

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority has explained how users can complain when their mobile network, internet or other telecom service is not working properly. Users should first contact their own telecom company through its call centre, website, app or service centre and obtain a complaint or reference number, giving the company reasonable time to fix the problem. If the issue is still not resolved, they can take the matter to the PTA through its Complaint Management System portal, the PTA CMS mobile app, the helpline 0800-55055, or by visiting a PTA office in person.

When complaining to the PTA, users should quote the reference number they received from their company. The authority said following this route helps complaints reach the right place and be tracked properly.
