Suspect arrested in Karachi murder case; cash and iPhones recovered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Karachi have arrested a suspect in the murder of 21-year-old Brian Robin. Defence police arrested the suspect, identified as Younus alias Alex. The police recovered Rs200,000 in cash and two expensive iPhones that had been taken from the victim.

According to police, the suspect strangled the victim on the night of November 6. He then hanged the body from a ceiling fan to make the killing look like a suicide. Police said the suspect confessed to the crime during interrogation..
