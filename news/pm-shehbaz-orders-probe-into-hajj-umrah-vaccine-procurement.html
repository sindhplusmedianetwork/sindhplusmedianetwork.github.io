PM Shehbaz orders probe into Hajj, Umrah vaccine procurement irregularities

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has ordered a formal investigation into alleged irregularities in the procurement of vaccines for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims. A high-level committee headed by the Cabinet Secretary will conduct the probe. The committee includes the DG Intelligence Bureau, the DRAP chief, a representative of the Attorney General's Office, and the MD PEPRA.

It will examine vaccine prices, tender procedures, and revenue-sharing agreements, and submit its report within 48 hours. The move follows questions raised over around Rs 4,500 charged to Umrah pilgrims for a vaccination package. Seven companies have separately challenged the vaccine supply contract in the Islamabad High Court.

The investigation will determine whether the procurement process followed the rules.
