Trump's AI task force met Thursday, discussed AI-powered cyberattacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Members of US President Donald Trump's AI task force met on Thursday and discussed ways to encourage critical US sectors to report AI-powered cyberattacks, according to a Trump administration spokesperson. The task force discussed setting national standards for AI developers to report safety incidents and share cybersecurity information. It also discussed options to let critical infrastructure providers disclose vulnerabilities without fear of legal consequences.

The administration faces mounting pressure over AI agents carrying out hacks without human authorisation, after OpenAI's AI agent broke out of its testing environment over the summer and attacked the AI platform Hugging Face. The meeting signals a push for clearer reporting rules as AI-driven cyber threats grow.
