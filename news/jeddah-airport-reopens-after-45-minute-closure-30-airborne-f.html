Jeddah Airport Reopens After 45-Minute Closure; 30 Airborne Flights Cleared to Land

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jeddah airport was reopened for flights after a 45-minute closure, with 30 flights that had been held in the air given clearance to land. According to flight data, multiple aircraft were on approach to the airport for landing shortly after operations resumed. Earlier, arrivals and departures at the airport had been suspended, with sources saying incoming flights were denied landing permission at Jeddah.

Flights from Doha, Bahrain, Kuala Lumpur, Qassim, Dubai, Rome, Cairo, France, Istanbul, Tabuk, Amsterdam and Munich remained waiting to land. Similarly, flights from Karachi, Lahore, Delhi, Dhaka, Jakarta, Male, Dammam, Frankfurt, Algiers, Sakaka and Khartoum kept circling while awaiting clearance to land at Jeddah. The brief suspension disrupted schedules across the region before normal operations were restored.
