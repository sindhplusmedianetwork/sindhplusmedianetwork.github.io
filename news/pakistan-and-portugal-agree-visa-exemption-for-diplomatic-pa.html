Pakistan and Portugal agree visa exemption for diplomatic passport holders

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Portugal have signed a memorandum of understanding on visa exemption for diplomatic passport holders. Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar announced the agreement alongside Portuguese Foreign Minister Paulo Rangel. Dar said Pakistan values Portugal as a bilateral partner.

He invited Portuguese companies to invest in Pakistan. He said work would also be done on a joint business forum between the two countries.
