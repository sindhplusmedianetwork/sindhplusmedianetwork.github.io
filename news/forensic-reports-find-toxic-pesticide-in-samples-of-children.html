Forensic reports find toxic pesticide in samples of children who died mysteriously in Khyber

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Forensic toxicology reports from Khyber Medical College Peshawar found the highly toxic pesticide carbofuran in the stomach samples of a seven-year-old girl who died in the Bazaar Zakha Khel area of Khyber district. The medicine metronidazole was also found in the samples. The department has completed reports for three of the children who died and sent them to the District Health Officer Khyber for further legal and medical action.

Two other children from Landi Kotal remain under treatment at Hayatabad Medical Complex and are out of danger. Doctors say the exact link between the chemical and the deaths can only be established after complete medical evidence and investigation.
