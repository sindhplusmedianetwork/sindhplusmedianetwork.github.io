Karachi Police Briefly Detain JI UC Chairmen, Councillors Before Releasing Them

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Karachi briefly detained more than 10 elected local government representatives of Jamaat-i-Islami at different police stations across the city before releasing them a few hours later. Jamaat-i-Islami Karachi chief Munim Zafar Khan said union committee chairmen and councillors from various parts of the city were summoned to police stations and detained without being given any reason. He alleged that police had launched an undeclared crackdown against Jamaat-i-Islami in Karachi, adding that reports had also emerged of raids at several locations, and he strongly condemned the detentions.

Mr Zafar alleged that the action was being taken at the behest of those who had come to power through what he described as 'Form 47', and claimed the move was aimed at obstructing development work being carried out by Jamaat-i-Islami's elected representatives, adding that the detentions would not prevent them from continuing their efforts for the city's development. According to a Jamaat-i-Islami spokesperson, those detained included Khursheed Iqbal, chairman of UC-7 in Zaman Town; Adil Muhammad, vice-chairman of UC-6 in Gulshan-e-Iqbal; Athar Imam, a councillor from UC-1 in Chaneser Town; Javed, a councillor from UC-1 in Gulshan; Sajid Jilani, a ward councillor from UC-1 in Jinnah Town; and Allah Gul, who was held at the Site Super Highway police station. Mr Zafar said police had taken Khursheed Iqbal to Zaman Town police station after visiting his residence and asking him to accompany them, and alleged that Iqbal was subsequently prevented from leaving the station, while councillors Athar Imam and Mujahid were summoned to Tipu Sultan police station and detained, and Sajid Jilani and a ward councillor from Soldier Bazaar were also reportedly held.

Later on Sunday night, Jamaat-i-Islami secretary Taufiquddin said all the elected representatives detained by police had been released.
