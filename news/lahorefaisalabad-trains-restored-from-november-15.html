Lahore–Faisalabad Trains Restored From November 15

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Railways has issued a notification restoring train operations on the Lahore-Faisalabad section from 15 November. A new railway bridge has been built between the flood-affected areas of Musa Kalar and Qila Sheikhupura, and a successful engine trial has been completed on the section. Karakoram Express and Shalimar Express will return to their original Lahore-Faisalabad route.

The restoration ends the long detour via Sahiwal that passengers had been using since the floods damaged the track. Railway officials said the resumption would ease travel for passengers between the two cities. The trains had been diverted for weeks while repair work continued.
