Rizwan, Imam probe nearing completion, says Naqvi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency probe into senior cricketers Mohammad Rizwan and Imam-ul-Haq is nearing completion, PCB chairman Mohsin Naqvi said in Lahore on Thursday. The two players are under scrutiny over alleged leaks of confidential information and dressing-room matters during Pakistan's 0-3 Test defeat in England. Both have been allowed to resume domestic cricket while the probe continues.

Naqvi was speaking to reporters in Lahore when he shared the update on the investigation. He also said the new PCB central contracts had been finalised. The contracts, which are around three months overdue, would be announced soon, he added.
