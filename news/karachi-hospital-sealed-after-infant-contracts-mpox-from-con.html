Karachi Hospital Sealed After Infant Contracts Mpox From Contaminated Scissors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The action followed a report from the District Health Officer Korangi about the laboratory-confirmed case at the hospital in Malir 15, within the Saudabad police station's jurisdiction. According to the commission, the inquiry revealed that instruments used for the newborn's circumcision were contaminated with the mpox virus. SHCC officials, supported by the DHO Korangi and the SSP, sealed the facility over poor hygiene, inadequate infection prevention and control measures, and reported malpractices that raised serious concerns about patient safety.

The statement said the hospital was being run illegally by non-qualified staff and quacks, and that officials faced resistance during the operation before Korangi police brought the situation under control. The infant is receiving treatment at the Sindh Infectious Diseases Hospital and Research Centre after developing fever and rashes.
