Drinking Coffee Daily May Lower Dementia Risk, 43-Year Study Finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Drinking coffee daily may lower the risk of dementia, according to a 43-year study. The study found that two to three cups of caffeinated coffee a day proved most beneficial. Regular coffee drinkers had a dementia rate of 141 cases per 100,000 people.

Decaffeinated coffee showed no such benefit, the study found. Tea was also linked to a lower dementia risk. The findings suggest the protective effect is tied to caffeinated coffee rather than coffee in general.
