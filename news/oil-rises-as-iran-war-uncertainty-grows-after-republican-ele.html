Oil rises as Iran war uncertainty grows after Republican election rout

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Oil prices climbed on Wednesday as investors weighed Washington's next move in the Iran war following the Democrats' capture of the US Congress. Brent crude rose 82 cents to $104.42 a barrel, while US West Texas Intermediate gained to $96.59. The United States carried out what it called 'defensive' strikes on Iran's Qeshm island on Tuesday, targeting surface-to-air missile sites.

President Trump told the Financial Times the US had no immediate plans to strike Iran further. US crude stocks fell by 5.27 million barrels last week, according to API data, adding support to prices. Qatar is stepping up mediation efforts between the parties.
