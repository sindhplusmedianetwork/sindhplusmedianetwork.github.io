Migrant Boat Capsizes Off Senegal, Killing at Least 35

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A boat carrying migrants trying to reach Europe capsized off the northwestern coast of the West African country of Senegal. At least 35 people were killed when the boat overturned in the sea. Only one person was rescued alive, according to the report.

The migrants were attempting the dangerous sea journey to Europe when the tragedy struck. The boat capsized near Senegal's northwestern coast, and the death toll of at least 35 was reported on Monday.
