Imran Khan will not bow down, says Salman Akram Raja

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja has said Imran Khan is not ready to bow down and the party stands firm to restore rights and the rule of law in the country. He was speaking at an all-parties conference at the Lahore High Court Bar Association, attended by Mahmood Khan Achakzai, PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar, Asad Qaiser, Raja Nasir Abbas, journalist Hamid Mir and Senator Barrister Ali Zafar. Raja said that at the event, Maulana Fazlur Rehman had been placed under house arrest and lawyers were challenged, and the state was not tolerating talk of the Constitution and the law.

He said trials of civilians through military courts had been upheld, and unconstitutional decisions had been made against Imran Khan, whose trial, along with that of Bushra Bibi, was held in closed rooms under the 26th and 27th constitutional amendments. He said the people had voted for Imran Khan, yet the party was denied its reserved seats, people were abducted to pass amendments, and there was now talk of a 28th amendment and a referendum. He added that Imran Khan remains in jail while others practise opportunistic politics, and that Bilawal Bhutto had said Imran Khan is a political prisoner.

Raja said the party would show the nation that it stands for rights, and would not support amendments brought in by others.
