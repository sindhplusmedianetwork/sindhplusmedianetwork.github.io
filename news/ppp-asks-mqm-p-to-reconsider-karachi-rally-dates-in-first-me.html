PPP asks MQM-P to reconsider Karachi rally dates in first meeting amid provinces row

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Delegations of the PPP and MQM-P held their first face-to-face meeting at the Sindh Assembly amid tensions over the proposed creation of new provinces. The PPP delegation, led by Nisar Khuhro, requested MQM-P to reconsider its planned rallies in Karachi. The rallies in question are scheduled for November 29 and December 6.

The PPP cited concerns about law and order in making the request. MQM-P leader Dr Farooq Sattar said the party would consult its leadership before responding to the request.
