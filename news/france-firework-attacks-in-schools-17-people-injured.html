France: Firework Attacks in Schools, 17 People Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Seventeen students and teachers were injured in firework attacks on schools in France. According to foreign media reports, incidents of throwing fireworks at educational institutions occurred in different cities including the suburbs of Paris. At least 21 incidents were reported yesterday, of which 12 incidents occurred inside schools.

According to the regional council, 17 people were injured or affected by trauma in these incidents. Eight people were injured in Lyon, 9 students in Amiens and 2 teachers in Argenteuil, while 5 people were reported detained in Lyon. The French government has banned the sale of fireworks until November 27.

According to media reports, an investigation has also begun into the leaking of secret information from the French Interior Ministry to a right-wing party; a former senior official of the Interior Ministry is accused of giving information to the National Rally, and searches were carried out at the Interior Ministry and the party's central office.
