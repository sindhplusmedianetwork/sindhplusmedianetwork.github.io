President Zardari to Visit Germany From Oct 22-24 for Bilateral Talks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari will undertake a visit to Germany from October 22 to 24 as part of continuing high-level exchanges between the two countries, the Foreign Office announced on Thursday. The visit takes place at a symbolic moment, marking the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Pakistan and Germany. During his stay, President Zardari will meet German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier, senior government leadership and leading business figures.

The talks will focus on strengthening economic and trade cooperation. Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah is expected to accompany the president on the visit. Officials say the trip reflects the growing partnership between the two countries across trade, investment and political dialogue.
