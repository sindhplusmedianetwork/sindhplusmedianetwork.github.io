Mir Raza Case: JIT Holds First Meeting, Records Father's Detailed Statement

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The joint investigation team (JIT) formed by the Sindh government to investigate the Mir Raza case held its first meeting, in which the deceased's family recorded their statement. The meeting was held at the DIG West office under the chairmanship of DIG Irfan Baloch, and representatives of sensitive institutions also attended. Mir Raza's family arrived with their lawyer, Advocate Jibran Nasir, and the JIT recorded the father's statement.

Speaking to the media after the meeting, Jibran Nasir said that on Monday they would write to the Establishment Division and the chief minister asking why the judicial commission's recommendations were not being implemented. He said that only the father's statement was recorded today, while the sister and mother may also record their statements, and the DIG has asked the family to appear again. He said Mir Raza's father has once again recorded his detailed statement, and expressed hope that the JIT would carry out important investigations in the next two weeks.

He demanded investigations into the roles of SHO Ferozabad, and of Mir Raza's business partners Ahmed and Abdullah Bharde, saying the commission's recommendations were not being implemented.
