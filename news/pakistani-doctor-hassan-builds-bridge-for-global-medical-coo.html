Pakistani doctor Hassan builds bridge for global medical cooperation in China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani national Hassan has become an important link in promoting global medical cooperation in China. Inspired by Chinese history and culture, he first visited Beijing in 2010, later learned Chinese in Liaoning province, and eventually settled in Hainan, where he earned his medical degree from Hainan Medical University. After nearly two years as a doctor at the university's first affiliated hospital, he left clinical practice to work in international medical cooperation.

He now serves as business development manager at the Boao Yiling Life Care Center in the Boao Lecheng International Medical Tourism Pilot Zone. Drawing on his medical education and command of multiple languages, he helps connect overseas patients, medical institutions and health-sector partners with China's modern medical facilities. In his current role over the past year and a half, he has hosted 10 international delegations, including a group from 32 countries linked to the Boao Forum for Asia.

Hassan says his goal is to connect the whole world with China's healthcare system, and he is keen to promote medical cooperation between Pakistan and China under the Belt and Road Initiative.
