Profit-taking drags PSX below 178,000

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange remained depressed on Friday as political tensions and profit-taking pushed the benchmark KSE-100 index below the 178,000 mark at the close of the weekend session. Topline Securities said the index declined 397 points, or 0.22 per cent, to close at 177,664 points amid a lack of positive triggers. Lucky Cement, Engro Holdings, Meezan Bank, Mari Energies and PSO were the major laggards.

Together, the five stocks contributed a negative impact of 237 points. The market stayed under pressure through the session as investors booked profits after recent gains.
