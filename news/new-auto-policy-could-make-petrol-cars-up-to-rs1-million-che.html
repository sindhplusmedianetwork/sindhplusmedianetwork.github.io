New auto policy could make petrol cars up to Rs1 million cheaper

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Petrol cars in Pakistan could become cheaper by up to Rs1 million under the new auto policy approved on the instructions of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. The reduction is expected to come through cuts in federal excise duty and customs duty, with details of the new prices for different categories now made public. According to the prime minister's aide Haroon Akhtar, a locally manufactured car of up to 658cc could become cheaper by Rs375,000, bringing its price down to Rs2.845 million.

A jeep of 1,598cc could see a reduction of Rs1 million. The policy also aims to raise the country's auto exports to $6 billion. The policy sets out new prices for different categories of vehicles.
