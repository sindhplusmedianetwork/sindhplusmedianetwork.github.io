Red Line Digging Damages Gas Pipeline, Halts Supply in Karachi Areas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gas supply to several Karachi neighbourhoods was suspended on Saturday after a high-pressure pipeline was damaged during excavation work for the BRT Red Line project. According to an SSGC spokesperson, the pipeline on University Road, near the Gulshan-i-Iqbal telephone exchange, was damaged around 1pm. The disruption affected Gulshan-i-Iqbal, areas around Mazar-i-Quaid, and parts of Garden, Saddar and Burns Road.

The spokesperson said repairs were completed by 5pm, after which supply was restored. The incident came during lunch hours, when many households rely on gas for cooking.
