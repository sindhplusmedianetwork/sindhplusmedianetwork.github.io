KP to Act Against 1,623 Absent Health Staff in 13 Districts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Action has been decided against absent health staff in specified districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. According to the Area Development Secretariat report, absence of 1,623 employees of the health department has been reported in 13 specified districts of the province. According to the report, instructions have been issued for action against absent medical staff and action against them will be taken in accordance with relevant service rules and regulations.

According to the Area Development Secretariat, 558 medical staff are absent in Lakki Marwat district, 410 in Mohmand, 208 in Bannu, 120 in Dera Ismail Khan and 101 in North Waziristan. According to the report, absence of 95 medical staff has been reported in Khyber district, 89 in South Waziristan Upper, 77 in Tank, 68 in Kurram Lower Central, 43 in Orakzai, 32 in Kurram Upper, 18 in South Waziristan Lower and 4 in Bajaur. According to the Area Development Secretariat, there is no room for incompetence and negligence of duty in the specified districts, and explanations have been sought from health department employees absent from duty in the relevant areas.
