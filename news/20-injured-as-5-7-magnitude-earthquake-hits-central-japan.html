20 Injured as 5.7-Magnitude Earthquake Hits Central Japan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 20 people were injured after a 5.7-magnitude earthquake struck central Japan early on Sunday. Injuries were reported in Aichi and Shizuoka prefectures. The quake originated in western Shizuoka at a depth of about 40 kilometres, and no tsunami warning was issued.

Authorities suspended some local train services in the affected areas as a precaution. No abnormalities were detected at the Hamaoka nuclear power plant. Emergency teams were assessing damage in the region after the tremor.
