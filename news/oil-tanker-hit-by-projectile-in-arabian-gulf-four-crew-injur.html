Oil Tanker Hit by Projectile in Arabian Gulf, Four Crew Injured

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Liberia-flagged oil tanker MT Fujairah Energy was hit by an unknown projectile in the Arabian Gulf, injuring four crew members. The incident occurred on November 10, with 24 crew on board. Indian authorities said one of the six Indian nationals on board suffered minor injuries and was moved ashore for medical examination.

The remaining 18 crew members, including five other Indians, are safe. UK Maritime Trade Operations confirmed receiving a report of an incident involving an oil tanker in the Strait of Hormuz on the morning of November 11. In a separate incident, UAE company ADNOC Logistics confirmed that one of its vessels was damaged in an attack in the Arabian Gulf on the afternoon of November 10.
