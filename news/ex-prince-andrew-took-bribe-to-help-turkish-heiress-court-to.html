Ex-Prince Andrew Took 'Bribe' to Help Turkish Heiress, Court Told

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Britain's former prince Andrew received 976,000 dollars as an alleged bribe to help a Turkish heiress, the High Court in London was told on Monday, with the disgraced British royal due to testify in the civil trial. The claim around the payment emerged at the start of the anticipated four-month trial, which pits Nebahat Isbilen, whose family founded the Evyap soap and cosmetics group, against her former adviser and businessman, Selman Turk. Isbilen, the wife of a jailed Turkish former MP, is suing Turk, alleging he misappropriated tens of millions of pounds of her funds and did not explain where the money went, which he denies.

Andrew, now known as Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and who is not accused of wrongdoing, returned the sum after Isbilen wrote to him at the start of the legal proceedings several years ago, according to her legal submissions. He is due to give evidence on December 14, after the judge earlier this year permitted Turk to issue him with a witness summons, compelling him to appear at the trial. Turk's lawyer told the court that Isbilen's own case was that she understood the payment to the Duke of York was a bribe, either for help with her passport or her UK residency, while also claiming the payment was really about discussing her husband's case with Andrew.

The former prince was stripped of his royal titles last year following revelations stemming from his friendship with the late US sex offender Jeffrey Epstein.
