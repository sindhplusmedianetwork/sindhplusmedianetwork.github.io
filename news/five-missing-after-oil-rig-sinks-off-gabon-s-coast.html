Five Missing After Oil Rig Sinks Off Gabon's Coast

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five people remain missing after the Prospector 5 drilling rig sank off Gabon's southern coast on Saturday night, operator Borr Drilling and BW Energy have said. The rig lost stability at 8:37pm local time on October 24 during jacking operations in preparation for a planned drilling campaign on the Dussafu Marine licence, and subsequently sank. Of the 115 people on board, 110 have been safely accounted for and are being brought ashore in Gabon, where care and support is being provided.

Borr Drilling said extensive marine and air search and rescue operations remain ongoing and that it is in contact with the families of the missing personnel. A government statement identified the five missing as three Gabonese nationals, one Indian and one Croatian. BW Energy said production at the nearby MaBoMo facility was shut down under standard procedure during drilling preparations, with no reported damage to that facility and no accidental discharge of harmful substances from it.

Gabon's navy is supporting the search, and an investigation into the cause of the incident is to be carried out.
