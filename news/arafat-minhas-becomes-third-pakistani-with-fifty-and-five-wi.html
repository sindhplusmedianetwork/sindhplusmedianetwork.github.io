Arafat Minhas Becomes Third Pakistani With Fifty and Five Wickets in One ODI

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Twenty-one-year-old all-rounder Arafat Minhas produced a dream performance in Rawalpindi, scoring a fifty under pressure and taking six wickets with his left-arm spin as Pakistan defeated England by 87 runs. It was his second ODI five-wicket haul, and he was named player of the match in only his fifth one-day international. With the feat, Minhas became only the third Pakistani after Shahid Afridi and Abdul Razzaq to score a fifty and take five wickets in the same ODI.

Captain Shaheen Shah Afridi praised him as a complete all-rounder, while England captain Harry Brook also acknowledged him as the standout performer of the match. The victory gave Pakistan a strong result in the tri-nation ODI series at home.
