Balochistan Division Imperative for Better Governance, Says CM Bugti

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti has called for a review of the country's administrative structure, citing the province's vast geographical expanse and governance challenges. Speaking in Islamabad at a national dialogue on administrative reorganisation and the creation of new provinces organised by the Sanober Foundation, Bugti said governing a vast province like Balochistan solely from Quetta posed significant administrative challenges. He said additional administrative units, stronger local governments and a revised resource-sharing framework under the National Finance Commission Award were needed to improve public service delivery.

Bugti welcomed the ongoing national debate on governance reforms but said such discussions should not remain confined to seminars and hotels and must be taken to parliament for broader political consensus. Illustrating the province's scale, he said a journey across Punjab from the Wagah border to Rajanpur takes six to seven hours, whereas travelling within a single district of Balochistan can take up to ten hours. He recalled that during his tenure as interior minister, a deputy commissioner needed nearly nine hours to reach the site of a suicide attack in the remote Sarona area of Khuzdar district.

Bugti also noted the provincial government had already increased the province's divisions from eight to 12 and its districts to 41.
