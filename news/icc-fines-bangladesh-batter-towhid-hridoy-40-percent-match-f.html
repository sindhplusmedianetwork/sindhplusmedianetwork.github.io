ICC Fines Bangladesh Batter Towhid Hridoy 40 Percent Match Fee for Dissent

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Cricket Council has fined Bangladesh batter Towhid Hridoy 40 percent of his match fee and handed him two demerit points for showing dissent during the first Test against South Africa. The incident took place on the fourth day of the Test in Johannesburg, when play had been stopped due to bad light. As on-field umpires Ahsan Raza and Alex Wharf were passing near the Bangladesh dressing room balcony, Hridoy was heard criticising the umpires' decision, saying play should have been stopped earlier.

According to an ICC statement, Hridoy spoke aggressively and inappropriately to the umpires. The ICC described the conduct as a Level 1 breach of its Code of Conduct and imposed the fine along with two demerit points.
