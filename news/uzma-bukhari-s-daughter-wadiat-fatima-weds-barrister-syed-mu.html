Uzma Bukhari's Daughter Wadiat Fatima Weds Barrister Syed Musa Raza

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Minister for Information and Culture Uzma Bukhari's daughter Wadiat Fatima has entered into matrimony with Barrister Syed Musa Raza. The nikkah ceremony was attended by a number of political and government personalities. During the ceremony, Uzma Bukhari introduced the groom to Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif.

Among those present were Health Minister Salman Rafique, Deputy Speaker of the Punjab Assembly Zaheer Iqbal Channar, and other officials of the Punjab government. The wedding of the minister's daughter drew attention in political circles, with well-wishers congratulating the family on the occasion. It was a largely family affair with prominent figures from the province's political landscape in attendance.
