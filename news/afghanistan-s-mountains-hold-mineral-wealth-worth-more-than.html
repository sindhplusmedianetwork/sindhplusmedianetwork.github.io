Afghanistan's mountains hold mineral wealth worth more than $3 trillion

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Afghanistan holds natural resources including gold, copper, iron, lithium, oil and gas valued at more than three trillion dollars, according to research into the country's known mineral reserves. Copper reserves are estimated at 60 million tonnes under new reports, up from earlier estimates of 30 million tonnes, and could be worth around 720 billion dollars at current world prices. The country also holds the region's largest iron reserves, estimated at nearly three billion tonnes and worth more than 300 billion dollars.

Gold reserves are estimated at around 682 tonnes, lithium reserves could be worth up to one trillion dollars, and oil reserves stand at about 1.6 billion barrels, with 500 billion cubic metres of natural gas. To put the scale in perspective, if the country held three trillion dollars and the government's annual budget is around 10 billion dollars, it would need no outside financial help for about 300 years. Yet most of the resources remain underground and extraction has not begun, the economy remains weak, and many of the estimates have yet to be verified.
