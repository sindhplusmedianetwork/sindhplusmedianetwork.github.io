Trump Urges Senate to Pass Sunshine Protection Act, Ending Time Changes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump on Wednesday renewed his call for Congress to end seasonal clock changes, criticising the Senate for not advancing the Sunshine Protection Act. Writing on his Truth Social platform, Trump said it was a pity the Senate had not yet approved the bill, which he called the "I Don't Want to Change My Clocks Anymore Act". He pointed to the early sunset, noting that the sun had set at 5:06 pm and darkness had fallen by 5:31 pm.

"Isn't that ridiculous?" he said, adding that the weather and daylight had been more favourable just days earlier. Trump said he had avoided making the issue a major focus before the midterm elections, claiming he did not want certain senators to face electoral consequences over the matter. He also criticised the Senate for not taking up the legislation despite what he called great bipartisan support in the House, where the measure passed by a 308-117 vote in July.

The proposal would make daylight saving time permanent while allowing states to opt instead for permanent standard time. Supporters favour later sunsets, while opponents warn the change would mean darker winter mornings, particularly for children travelling to school.
