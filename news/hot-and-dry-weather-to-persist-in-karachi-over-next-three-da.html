Hot and dry weather to persist in Karachi over next three days

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi’s maximum temperature was recorded at 38.5 degrees Celsius with humidity at 17 percent. The Met Department’s early warning center expects hot and dry weather to persist over the next three days. Because daytime humidity is low, no heatwave-like conditions are expected.

Rural Sindh also faced intense heat, with 41 degrees recorded in Mithi and 40 degrees in Shaheed Benazirabad. Residents have been advised to take precautions during the warm spell.
