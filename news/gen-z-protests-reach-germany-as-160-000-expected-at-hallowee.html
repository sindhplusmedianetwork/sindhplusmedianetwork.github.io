Gen Z Protests Reach Germany as 160,000 Expected at Halloween Rallies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Germany is bracing for large youth-led protests on Saturday as more than 160,000 people are expected to join demonstrations organised by the campaign group Gen Z regelt. Rallies are planned in 34 cities, including Berlin, Frankfurt and Munich. The protesters are demanding greater investment in education and opposing compulsory military service, with rising living costs also among their core grievances.

German authorities say the Halloween-day rallies should be largely peaceful, but they have warned that extremist groups may try to exploit the gatherings. Amid concerns over possible unrest, some businesses along Berlin's famous Kurfuerstendamm shopping street boarded up their windows. The demonstrations mark the arrival in Germany of the wider Gen Z protest wave seen in several countries.
