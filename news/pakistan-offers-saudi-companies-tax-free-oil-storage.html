Pakistan Offers Saudi Companies Tax-Free Oil Storage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has offered Saudi companies the opportunity to establish commercial oil reserves in the country under a tax-neutral bonded storage policy. The offer was extended during a meeting between Federal Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik and Saudi Arabia's new ambassador to Pakistan, Dr Rajah bin Tami al-Baqami. The two sides held detailed discussions on expanding bilateral cooperation and investment in minerals, petroleum and the energy sector.

Pakistan also presented a plan for a large petroleum storage and refining complex in the Hub area of Balochistan. Officials said the initiative could deepen energy ties between the two countries and attract major Saudi investment into Pakistan's petroleum infrastructure.
