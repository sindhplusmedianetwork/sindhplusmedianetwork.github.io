Pakistan, 14 other countries welcome UK, Australia sanctions on Israeli 'settlement activities'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan, along with 14 other countries, on Thursday welcomed sanctions on Israeli settlement activities. The United Kingdom and Australia imposed the sanctions on companies involved in abetting the activities. The activities include settlement construction in the West Bank.

The UK said it would sanction eight Israeli construction companies. Australia imposed targeted sanctions on three individuals and four organisations. The foreign ministers underscored that Gaza is an integral part of the Occupied Palestinian Territory and backed a two-state solution.
