Pakistan Hockey Revival: Equipment Distributed to 450 Schools in First Phase

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A grassroots mission to revive hockey in Pakistan has begun, with hockey kits being distributed to schools across the country. In the first phase, 450 schools have received equipment for boys and girls, including 20 hockey sticks and special goalkeeping kits per school. Officials said a further 350 schools will be covered within a month.

The programme aims to reach 1,250 schools nationwide. The initiative is intended to rebuild the sport from the school level, where young players first pick up a stick. Pakistan, once a dominant force in world hockey, has struggled in recent years, and officials hope the school programme will help find new talent.
