Trump calls Iranian negotiators tap dancers, warns of renewed attacks after New Year

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read

US President Donald Trump has threatened that if negotiations with Iran do not reach an agreement, the US could resume military operations after the New Year. In an exclusive interview with Fox News, Trump said indirect negotiations with Iran are continuing but Iranian officials are not showing seriousness regarding an agreement. Trump criticized Iranian negotiators, calling them professional tap dancers, and said they keep talking continuously but reach no conclusion.

The US president said Iran is adopting a more aggressive attitude than in the past and is increasing tension in the region alongside negotiations. Trump warned that if diplomatic efforts fail, the US is ready to adopt the path of renewed military attacks against Iran after the New Year. He also warned Iranian naval vessels challenging the US naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz that they would be sent to the bottom of the sea if they try to break the blockade.
