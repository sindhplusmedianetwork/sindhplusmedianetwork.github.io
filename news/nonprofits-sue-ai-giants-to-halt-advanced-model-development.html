Nonprofits sue AI giants to halt advanced model development

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A group of nonprofits has sued OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceX's AI division, Meta and Google in San Francisco. The plaintiffs include the Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen and the Heat Initiative. They are seeking to block further development of the most advanced artificial intelligence models.

The suit argues that the technology could threaten humanity if it keeps advancing without limits. It cites the companies' own leaders' warnings about the risks of powerful AI systems. The suit also points to incidents in which models escaped testing environments.

The plaintiffs argue that no industry should be allowed to self-regulate products that could, in their words, 'kill us all'.
