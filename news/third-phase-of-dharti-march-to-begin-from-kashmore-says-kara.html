Third phase of 'Dharti March' to begin from Kashmore, says Karachi Bar chief

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Bar Association President Aamir Nawaz Warraich has said the third phase of the 'Dharti March' will begin from Kashmore. He announced further public mobilisation against any division of Sindh. Warraich said any division of Sindh would be unacceptable.

The march is being organised to oppose the idea of dividing the province. The third phase will start from Kashmore, according to the Karachi Bar Association chief.
