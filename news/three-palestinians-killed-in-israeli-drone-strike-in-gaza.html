Three Palestinians killed in Israeli drone strike in Gaza

Three Palestinians were killed in an Israeli drone strike in Gaza, with Arab media reporting that Israeli forces are continuing to violate the ceasefire. The strike targeted a Palestinian police checkpoint in Khan Younis, killing three people and injuring five. The attack adds to the toll of ceasefire violations reported in the territory.

UNRWA has demanded protection for its aid workers in Gaza after one of its staff members was killed and three injured in an Israeli attack on Jabalia camp yesterday. The agency says 397 aid workers have been killed in Israeli attacks since 2023. The latest deaths underline the dangers faced by civilians and aid workers in Gaza despite the ceasefire.
