Short-term inflation climbs to 11.32pc in last week of October on food prices

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Short-term inflation rose to 11.32 per cent year-on-year in the week ending Oct 29, driven largely by perishable food products, according to official data released by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics on Friday. The index, comprising 51 items whose prices are collected from 50 markets in 17 cities, is calculated weekly to track changes in the prices of essential commodities and services. Short-term inflation has remained in double digits for several consecutive weeks, largely on the back of persistent increases in petroleum and diesel prices.

Of the 51 essential items monitored during the week, the prices of 15 commodities increased while those of 12 items declined, and 24 items remained unchanged. The Sensitive Price Index decreased slightly by 0.35 per cent week-on-week. The items recording the highest week-on-week price increases included tomatoes at 34.91 per cent, potatoes at 3.27 per cent, diesel at 2.53 per cent and onions at 0.62 per cent, while chicken fell 2.95 per cent and eggs 2.81 per cent.

On an annual basis, LPG recorded the highest increase at 106.86 per cent, followed by electricity charges for Q1 at 52.38 per cent, diesel at 45.13 per cent and petrol at 43.61 per cent.
