PTI founder directs party to keep talking to government, says Ali Zafar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI senior leader Barrister Ali Zafar has said the PTI founder has directed the party to continue negotiations with the government. He said the founder also supports the creation of new administrative units. Zafar said the PTI has neither rejected nor accepted the possible 28th constitutional amendment.

He added that provinces should be created on administrative grounds. The PTI parliamentary party will meet on Monday to discuss the founder's instructions and the party's political and parliamentary strategy in detail.
