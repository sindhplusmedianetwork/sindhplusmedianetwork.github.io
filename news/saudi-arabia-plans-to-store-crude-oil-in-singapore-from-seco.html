Saudi Arabia Plans to Store Crude Oil in Singapore From Second Half of 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia has planned to store crude oil in Singapore to deal with possible disruptions to global oil supply and to secure access to Asian markets, with implementation expected in the second half of 2027. According to Reuters, the arrangement aims to meet Singapore's energy needs if oil deliveries are disrupted and to make Saudi oil supply in Southeast Asia more reliable. The quantity of crude to be stored and the financial details of the deal were not disclosed, but the Saudi and Singaporean ministries said Saudi crude would be kept in underground rock caverns on Singapore's Jurong island.

Saudi Aramco and Singapore's relevant institutions have exchanged a memorandum of understanding, and the target is to complete commercial negotiations in the coming months. Singapore's underground storage facilities have a total capacity of about 9 million barrels of liquid hydrocarbons, although it is not clear how much of that space would be allocated to Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia has previously made similar crude storage arrangements in Japan and South Korea, through which it tries to keep oil available near Asian consumers and maintain supply in emergencies.
