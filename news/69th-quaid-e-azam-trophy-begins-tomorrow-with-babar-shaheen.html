69th Quaid-e-Azam Trophy begins tomorrow with Babar, Shaheen, Rizwan in action

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The 69th edition of Pakistan's premier first-class competition, the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy, begins on Tuesday with eight teams competing in the Gold tier across Islamabad, Abbottabad and Rawalpindi, The Express Tribune reported. The 12-team Silver tier will run simultaneously as a qualification pathway to the Gold competition for next season. Defending champions Karachi Blues, Faisalabad, FATA, Lahore Whites, Islamabad, Peshawar Blues, Multan and Sialkot Blues will compete in the Gold tier, which runs from November 3 to December 12, with the five-day final scheduled for December 15-19.

The Silver competition, featuring 12 teams in two groups, runs from November 3 to 30, with its final set for December 3-7. A total of 29 first-class matches will be played in the Gold tier and 31 in the Silver competition. Pakistan Test captain Babar Azam is set to represent Lahore Whites in the opening round against FATA, led by Shaheen Shah Afridi, at Diamond Cricket Ground in Islamabad.

Other prominent internationals in action include Saud Shakeel, Saim Ayub, Imam-ul-Haq, Mohammad Rizwan, Abdullah Shafique and Hasan Ali.
