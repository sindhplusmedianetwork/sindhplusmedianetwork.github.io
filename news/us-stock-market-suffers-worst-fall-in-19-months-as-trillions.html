US stock market suffers worst fall in 19 months as trillions wiped out

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Wall Street's sharp rally after the US midterm election results turned into a steep sell-off the very next day, with the American stock market suffering its worst fall in 19 months. Investors had initially welcomed the Democrats' success, expecting the shift in the balance of power in Congress to reduce political and economic uncertainty. But the mood changed the next day amid fears that the Democrats' growing strength in Congress and state governments could bring new curbs on the building of AI data centres, electricity use and technology investment.

The Nasdaq fell 5.52 percent, the S&P 500 dropped 4.36 percent, the Dow Jones fell 3.44 percent and the semiconductor index plunged about 12 percent. Nvidia's market value fell by around $450 billion, while the S&P 500 lost roughly $3 trillion in total value. The heaviest losses were in semiconductor and AI-linked companies, whose shares had been trading at high levels, prompting investors to sell on a large scale to cut risk.

Experts say investors are now weighing heavy AI spending against companies' real earnings rather than the sector's future promise. Concerns over a possible slowdown in AI data centre construction and new government curbs have made investors more cautious.
