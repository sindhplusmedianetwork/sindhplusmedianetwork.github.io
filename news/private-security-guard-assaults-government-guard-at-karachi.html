Private security guard assaults government guard at Karachi's Jinnah Hospital, video goes viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A private security guard assaulted a government security guard outside the emergency department at Jinnah Hospital in Karachi after a verbal exchange, and video of the incident went viral. The clash took place outside the hospital's emergency department. The hospital administration said the clash stemmed from a misunderstanding.

It took immediate notice of the incident, resolved the matter and reconciled the two guards. The viral video had drawn wide attention to the scuffle between the two guards on duty at the hospital.
