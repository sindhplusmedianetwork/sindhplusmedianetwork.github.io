PTI Lawmakers Oppose Further Talks After Heated Seven-Hour Meeting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A joint parliamentary party meeting of the PTI in Islamabad lasted more than seven hours and saw heated arguments, with most lawmakers opposing any further effort to negotiate with the powers that be. The majority argued that the other side had already secured what it wanted and that pursuing further talks would only show weakness. Several members also expressed dissatisfaction with the party leadership's handling of the prevailing challenges and questioned its overreliance on then-Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister Sohail Afridi, who they said held no position within the party hierarchy.

Around 15 lawmakers, on the other hand, favoured supporting the proposed 28th Amendment, arguing that it could help normalise relations with the establishment and pave the way for the release of Imran Khan. The meeting, chaired by PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan at Parliament House, also saw former National Assembly speaker Asad Qaiser present a pre-written declaration that had not been discussed, which annoyed several members. Faisal Amin Gandapur reacted by asking whether it was a joke and walked out following an exchange with Barrister Gohar, while Azam Swati urged Barrister Gohar and his colleagues to step down.

Party whip Amir Dogar said afterwards that several prevailing issues had been discussed but decisions would be taken after consultation with opposition leaders, and that another meeting would follow.
