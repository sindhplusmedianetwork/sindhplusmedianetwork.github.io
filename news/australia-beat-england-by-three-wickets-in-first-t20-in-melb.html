Australia Beat England by Three Wickets in First T20 in Melbourne

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hosts Australia defeated England by three wickets in the first T20 match of the series in Melbourne. Batting first, England scored 187 runs for the loss of nine wickets in 20 overs. Australia chased down the 188-run target on the fifth ball of the final over.

Nikhil Chaudhary top-scored for Australia with 66 runs, while Mitchell Marsh made 37 and Jack Edwards scored 25. Chaudhary was named player of the match for his innings.
