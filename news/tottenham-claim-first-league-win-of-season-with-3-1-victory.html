Tottenham Claim First League Win of Season With 3-1 Victory Over Coventry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tottenham beat Coventry 3-1 on Monday to claim their first Premier League win of the season. James Maddison marked his first start in 17 months with an early goal. Jack Rudoni levelled for Coventry before Omar Marmoush and substitute Ben Davies sealed the points for Spurs.

The victory lifted Tottenham to 16th in the table. Dejan Kulusevski returned to the matchday squad after missing all of last season. The win eased pressure on coach Roberto De Zerbi after a difficult start to the campaign.
