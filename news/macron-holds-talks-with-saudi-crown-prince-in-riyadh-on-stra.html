Macron Holds Talks With Saudi Crown Prince in Riyadh on Strategic Ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French President Emmanuel Macron held talks with Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in Riyadh, in a meeting centred on the future of French-Saudi relations. According to Saudi media, the two leaders reviewed what they called the historic and strategic ties binding their countries. They discussed ways to deepen bilateral cooperation across a range of sectors, from the economy to security.

Regional and global peace and stability also featured prominently in their exchange of views. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan joined the meeting alongside other senior Saudi officials. Macron was accompanied by the French foreign minister and senior members of his delegation.

The meeting comes as both countries continue to expand their diplomatic and economic engagement. No detailed joint statement was immediately released.
