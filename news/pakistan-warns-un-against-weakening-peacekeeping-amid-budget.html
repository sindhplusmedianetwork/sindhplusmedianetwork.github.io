Pakistan Warns UN Against Weakening Peacekeeping Amid Budget Cuts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has urged the United Nations not to allow financial constraints and cost-cutting to weaken peacekeeping operations. The warning was delivered by Pakistan's Permanent Representative, Ambassador Syed Haider Shah, to the UN General Assembly's Fourth Committee. He said resource cuts could undermine international peace and security.

The General Assembly approved a 5.1 billion dollar peacekeeping budget for 2026-27, about 10 percent lower than the previous year. Shah said efficiency measures must strengthen peacekeeping, not hollow out its capabilities. He also outlined six reform priorities for the operations.
