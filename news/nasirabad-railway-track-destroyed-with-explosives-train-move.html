Nasirabad railway track destroyed with explosives, train movement suspended

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Unknown persons have blown up a railway track in Nasirabad district. The blast left a one-foot-deep crater and suspended train movement. Railway officials said restoration would take several hours.

Police cordoned off the area and began an investigation. Security was also tightened after the incident.
