NCCIA Raids Two Illegal Call Centres in Lahore, Arrests 12 Including Two Women

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) Lahore Zone has raided two illegal call centres and arrested 12 suspects, including two women. The centres were Solution Hub in Batapur and Task Taker in Punjab Housing Society. During the raids, officials seized 28 digital devices, among them 13 laptops, two desktop computers and 13 mobile phones.

Investigators say the centres defrauded citizens in Canada, the United States and other countries with fake mobile upgrade, network package and SIM offers. The suspects allegedly impersonated staff of Rogers, Bell, Telus and AT&T to obtain financial information for unauthorised transactions and carding fraud. Cases have been registered against the arrested suspects.

The seized devices have been sent for forensic analysis as the investigation continues.
