UN Warns Military AI Is Crowding Out Human Decisions; Pakistan Flags Risks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A senior United Nations official has warned that military artificial intelligence is increasingly crowding out human decision-making. Robin Geiss, director of the UN Institute for Disarmament Research, raised the question of whether the world will allow machines to target and kill human beings. He cautioned that the opacity of AI systems heightens the risks of miscalculation, including in the nuclear domain.

Pakistan, participating in the discussion, flagged the emerging risks posed by autonomous weapons systems. Islamabad has consistently called for responsible international oversight of military AI. The debate reflects growing global unease over the unchecked militarisation of artificial intelligence.
