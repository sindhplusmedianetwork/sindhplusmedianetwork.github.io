SHC Sets Aside Most Censor Board Cuts in Film 'Ghost School'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court on Monday struck down most of the cuts imposed by the Sindh Board of Film Censors on the feature film Ghost School. A division bench comprising Justices Yousuf Ali Sayeed and Adnan Iqbal Chaudhry ruled that the board had failed to justify its decision to order the cuts. The court observed that 'the power to excise is not unbounded', underlining that censorship powers must be exercised within legal limits.

The bench directed the censor board to reconsider the film's certification within 10 days. The court also noted that the Central and Punjab censor boards had already cleared the same film for unrestricted exhibition without any cuts. The ruling is being seen as a significant statement on the limits of film censorship in the province.
