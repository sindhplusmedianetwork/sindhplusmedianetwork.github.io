150-bed heart hospital in Quetta nears completion, set to open this month

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti has said that the Balochistan Institute of Cardiovascular Diseases (BICVD), a modern 150-bed hospital, is nearing completion and is expected to be inaugurated later this month. He expressed these views while chairing the first meeting of the BICVD Board of Directors, which reviewed progress on the hospital's completion, provision of medical facilities, administrative structure and preparations for making the institution operational. He said the establishment of the institute would provide people in Balochistan with modern diagnostic and treatment facilities for heart diseases within the province, eliminating the need for patients to travel to other provinces for specialised cardiac care.

The meeting approved the appointment of renowned cardiologist Dr Jalal Khan as the institute's interim head. He will oversee preparations to make the institution operational in accordance with modern medical standards. The meeting was informed that three satellite centres linked to the hospital would also become operational during the initial phase to provide basic medical services and ensure timely diagnosis.

Provincial Health Minister Bakht Muhammad Kakar informed the meeting that, in the second phase, the network of satellite centres would be expanded to all divisional headquarters. Chest Pain Management Centres would also be established in every district to provide immediate medical assistance and ensure timely diagnosis for patients experiencing chest pain or symptoms of cardiovascular diseases. CM Bugti said establishing modern medical institutions and providing quality healthcare facilities closer to people's homes were among the provincial government's key priorities, and that investment in the health sector was aimed at providing relief to the public and addressing difficulties faced by patients in accessing medical treatment.
