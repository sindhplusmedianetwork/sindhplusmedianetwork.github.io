Ahsan Bhon Group's Syed Amjad Ali Shah Elected Supreme Court Bar President

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Syed Amjad Ali Shah of the Ahsan Bhon/Independent Group has been elected president of the Supreme Court Bar Association, according to unofficial results. He secured 644 votes from the Lahore seat, comfortably defeating the Professional Group's candidate Sardar Aman Khan, who managed 400 votes. Group leader Ahsan Bhon congratulated the lawyers on the victory and thanked Chaudhry Pervez Elahi for his support.

In his first remarks after the win, the newly elected president pledged that there would be no compromise on the supremacy of the Constitution and the rule of law. The annual Supreme Court Bar elections are closely watched as a barometer of the legal community's political leanings.
