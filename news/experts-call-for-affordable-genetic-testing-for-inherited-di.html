Experts call for affordable genetic testing for inherited diseases in Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Medical experts at the one-day 'Future Health 2035' summit in Islamabad have called for affordable genetic screening, early diagnosis and specialised treatment facilities in Pakistan. They warned that families affected by inherited disorders struggle to get proper healthcare. Specialists at the summit said the country lacked a national genetic institute and organised support systems for such patients.

They noted that around 60 percent of marriages in Pakistan take place within families, which increases the risk of inherited disorders. Strive chief executive Yasir Khan said genetic testing remained beyond the financial reach of about 99 percent of the population because of its high cost. The experts urged a national strategy to make genetic testing and care accessible and affordable.
