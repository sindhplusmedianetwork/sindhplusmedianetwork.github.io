Osaka vows to return stronger after shock Tokyo exit to 18-year-old Pohankova

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Naomi Osaka's season came to an end on Thursday as the four-time Grand Slam champion fell 6-3, 7-5 to 18-year-old Slovakian Mia Pohankova in the second round of the Pan Pacific Open in Tokyo. Osaka was playing at the event for the first time in four years. Pohankova, ranked 144th and the 2025 Wimbledon junior champion, advances to the quarter-finals.

Despite the defeat, Osaka said she was 'at peace' with her game and vowed to 'keep attempting' to get back to Grand Slam-winning form. Top-ranked Elena Rybakina also progressed on the same day.
