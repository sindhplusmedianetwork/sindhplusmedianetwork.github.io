Samar Haroon Bilour Alleges Irregularities in Billion-Rupee Gold Mining Contracts During PTI Rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa governor's information adviser, Samar Haroon Bilour, has alleged serious irregularities in multi-billion-rupee gold mining contracts awarded during the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf government's tenure in the province. Speaking on the issue, she cited an official report from October 2024, which she said documented the violations. According to Bilour, four mineral blocks for placer gold along the Indus and Kabul rivers were auctioned for about Rs4.93 billion.

She said the National Accountability Bureau had raised objections over the auction procedures and violations of the Mineral Auction Rules 2022. Bilour further claimed that mining activity continued in November 2024 despite a stay order issued by the Peshawar High Court. She added that NAB has sought the complete record of the disputed contracts from the KP minerals department.
