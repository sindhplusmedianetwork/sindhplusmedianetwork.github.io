Delivery Boy Dies by Suicide After Customer's Assault Over Torn Note in India

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 23-year-old Muslim delivery worker in India, Sameer Hussain, died by suicide after allegedly being slapped and humiliated by a customer over a request to exchange a torn 10-rupee note. According to Indian media, on the night of October 4, Sameer went to deliver a coffee order worth around 150 rupees to a man named Rampal Singh in Vinod Nagar, Delhi. In a video he recorded, Sameer alleged that the customer slapped him three times and misbehaved when he asked for the torn note to be replaced.

In the video recorded before his suicide, Sameer spoke of being mocked for his poverty and his fear of not getting justice, blaming the customer Rampal Singh and his grandson Rakesh for the incident. He consumed poison on October 5 and was shifted to a hospital, where he died during treatment on October 20. His mother Kaleema said her son ended his life just days before his 24th birthday, adding that the accused has political connections and mocked her son's poverty.

Indian media reports that police have added abetment-to-suicide sections to the registered case and begun investigating the matter.
