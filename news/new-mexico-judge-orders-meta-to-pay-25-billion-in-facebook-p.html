New Mexico Judge Orders Meta to Pay $25 Billion in Facebook Privacy Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A judge in New Mexico has ordered Meta to pay $25 billion after a jury found the company misled consumers about privacy on Facebook, in one of the largest penalties ever imposed on the social media giant. Judge Francis Mathew ruled that Facebook's deception was intentional and that the company had falsely promised users they were in control of their personal data. He wrote that a $219 billion penalty was warranted under New Mexico law, but reduced the amount to $25 billion.

The case traces back to the 2016 Cambridge Analytica scandal, in which data from up to 87 million Facebook users was harvested and used for political targeting. Meta has said it is confident it will prevail on appeal. The ruling adds to the mounting legal pressure on the company over its handling of user data.
