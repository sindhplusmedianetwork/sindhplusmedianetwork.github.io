Pakistan sets up 13 child courts, 45 juvenile justice committees active

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has set up 13 child courts to try cases of violence against children, and 45 juvenile justice committees are active. The figures were shared by Sara Ahmed, chairperson of Punjab's Child Protection and Welfare Bureau, at the second global ministerial conference on ending violence against children in Manila. A Pakistani delegation led by Federal Minister for Human Rights Azam Nazeer Tarar attended the conference, where Ahmed presented Pakistan's position on an effective justice system for children.

She said 661 children's cases were resolved through alternative procedures between 2019 and 2021. Under the Juvenile Justice System Rules 2023, attention is also being given to children's education, health, technical training and rehabilitation. She said cooperation with international organisations is necessary for the protection of children.

The Punjab bureau provides shelter, education, psychological and legal support to children, while the child helpline 1121 also provides help.
