Rabia Ahsan murder probe: accused says he fired at wife 'as a joke'; phone analysis complete

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police sources say the investigation into the murder of anchorperson Rabia Ahsan is continuing, and some details of the accused Ahsan Tahir's statement have come to light. The accused told investigators he had obtained an illegal 30-bore pistol from a friend about six months ago and knew very little about how to use the weapon. He said he kept the pistol and a magazine full of bullets in separate places, and investigators believe a bullet may have remained in the chamber when the magazine was removed six months ago.

According to his statement, the pistol came out of a cupboard while he was taking clothes out on the day of the incident, and because it had no magazine, he believed it contained no bullet. He then pointed the pistol at Rabia as a joke and pulled the trigger, but it fired, and the bullet struck her below the neck, in the hollow between the collarbones. Rabia fell to the ground bleeding, and the accused said he called Rescue 1122 in a state of great distress; rescuers reached the scene before police and tried to restore her breathing, but she could not be saved.

Police sources say initial analysis of the accused's mobile phone has been completed and the full chat data between him and Rabia has been obtained, with no sign of quarrels in the messages. Police say the investigation is being advanced in light of the accused's statement and the details of the incident, and his statement is being checked against evidence from the crime scene, other circumstances and further statements.
