'No Goonda Culture Will Be Tolerated in Punjab, No One Will Be Spared': Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has said that no 'goonda culture' will be tolerated in the province and no one will be spared. Chairing a law-and-order meeting attended by regional and district police officers via video link, she gave field officers four weeks to improve law and order, end crimes against women and children, and make police performance more effective — warning that negligent officers would be removed from their posts. She ordered a halt to wheat smuggling, especially in border districts, and directed a faster anti-corruption drive within the police force.

The chief minister also ordered strict action against over-speeding and illegal weapons, enforcement of the ban on camera-glasses and spy gadgets, and more effective patrolling including motorcycles and improved facilities for Lahore's Dolphin Force. She announced lifetime salaries plus a special package for the families of martyred police officers.
