'Sardaar Ji 3' finally reaches Indian audiences on OTT

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The cross-border Punjabi film 'Sardaar Ji 3', starring Pakistani actor Hania Aamir alongside Indian singer and actor Diljit Dosanjh and Neeru Bajwa, has finally reached Indian audiences through a digital release, more than a year after political tensions forced its makers to abandon a theatrical opening in the country. The horror-comedy premiered on Chaupal OTT on November 9, 2026, giving Indian viewers access to a film that had already enjoyed an international theatrical release, including in Pakistan, on June 27, 2025. The film was initially kept out of Indian cinemas following objections to Hania's casting amid deteriorating relations between India and Pakistan, and the controversy intensified after the April 22, 2025, Pahalgam attack.

The Federation of Western India Cine Employees urged the Central Board of Film Certification to withhold certification, and the makers opted for an overseas release rather than risk an Indian theatrical opening. The production team maintained that the film had been shot in February 2025, before the political situation changed, and that no Pakistani artist had been signed after the Pahalgam attack. Despite missing an Indian cinema release, the film reportedly performed strongly in Pakistan, with exhibitor Nadeem Mandviwalla estimating its local box-office earnings at approximately Rs600 million.

Directed by Amar Hundal, the film is the third instalment in the Punjabi horror-comedy franchise, with Diljit playing Jaggi, a ghost hunter who takes on a supernatural assignment at a mansion in the United Kingdom.
