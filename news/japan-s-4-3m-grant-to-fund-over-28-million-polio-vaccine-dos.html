Japan's $4.3m grant to fund over 28 million polio vaccine doses for Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Japan has announced a new grant of 4.3 million US dollars to help Pakistan buy oral polio vaccines. The Pakistan Polio Eradication Initiative will use the money to procure more than 28 million doses for the vaccination campaigns planned for 2027. Pakistan has reported eight polio cases so far in 2026, six of them in south Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

The grant is the latest contribution in a long-running partnership: since 1996, Japan has provided more than 248 million US dollars in grants and loans through UNICEF to support polio vaccination in Pakistan. The funding will help sustain the country's efforts to reach every child with the vaccine and move closer to ending polio.
