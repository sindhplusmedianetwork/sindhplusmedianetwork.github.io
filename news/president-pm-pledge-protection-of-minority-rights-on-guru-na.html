President, PM pledge protection of minority rights on Guru Nanak's birth anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif have reaffirmed the government's commitment to protecting the rights of all citizens, particularly minorities. The two leaders expressed these views on the birth anniversary of Baba Guru Nanak, which is being celebrated on November 24. The president extended greetings to the Sikh community in Pakistan and across the globe on the occasion.

He said the government remains committed to ensuring religious freedom and preserving the cultural traditions of all communities. The prime minister also reaffirmed the government's commitment to protecting minorities and safeguarding their religious freedoms. The anniversary is being observed by the Sikh community in Pakistan and around the world.
