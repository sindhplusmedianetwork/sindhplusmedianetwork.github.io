Pakistani YouTuber Imtan Ahmed Announces Quitting Social Media

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistani YouTuber and digital content creator Imtan Ahmed has announced that he is leaving social media. In his announcement, he cited health problems as the reason behind the decision. He said his health no longer allows him to continue making videos or to stay active as he was before.

Imtan Ahmed is known for his informative and research-based videos on wars, global affairs, science and social topics. The announcement has left his large viewer base concerned, with many expressing their support for him.
