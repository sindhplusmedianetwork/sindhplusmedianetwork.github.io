Sanaullah Calls for Empowering Local Governments

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Adviser to Prime Minister Rana Sanaullah on Friday said that parliament was the appropriate forum to discuss issues of national importance. Speaking to a private television channel, he said political parties should hold discussions on the establishment of new provinces at the appropriate forum. He said public opinion and consensus among political parties were essential for creating new provinces.

He also stressed the need to empower the local government system, saying collective efforts were required to address the challenges confronting the nation. Commenting on PTI, he said the party had promoted the politics of agitation in the country. Despite various challenges, Pakistan was making progress in different fields, he said.

He further said that the civil and military leadership had played a vital role in maintaining peace in the region. Meanwhile, Federal Minister for Climate Change Dr Musadik Malik has said that dialogue was the only way to resolve political differences and address the issues facing the country.
