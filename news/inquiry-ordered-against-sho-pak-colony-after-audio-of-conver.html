Inquiry Ordered Against SHO Pak Colony After Audio of Conversation With Drug Dealer

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

SSP District Keamari Singhar Malik has ordered an inquiry against SHO Pak Colony Inspector Faisal Gul after an alleged audio recording surfaced on social media on November 5, 2026. In the recording, the inspector and Constable Bilal were purportedly heard in an unauthorised and compromising conversation with drug dealer Kashif alias Dada. The order names the SDPO of the Baldia sub-division as the inquiry officer.

The alleged fraternisation with a criminal and the act of asking a subordinate to apologise to the dealer have been described as highly damaging to police discipline. The inquiry officer has been directed to submit a report with recommendations within seven days. The case has raised fresh questions about discipline and conduct within the police force.
