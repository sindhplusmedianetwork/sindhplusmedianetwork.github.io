US and UK Warn Citizens Against Travel to Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States and Britain have warned their citizens about travelling to Saudi Arabia amid security concerns and fears of further attacks on airports. According to Geo News, the British foreign ministry advised its nationals to avoid all travel within 10 kilometres of the border with Yemen. For the rest of the kingdom, it recommended only essential travel, warning that travel insurance could be invalidated for those who ignore the guidance.

London also told Britons to stay away from King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh after a serious incident there on October 10 at around 3pm local time. It advised passengers to follow Saudi authorities' instructions and to consider travelling overland to a neighbouring country before flying. The US embassy in Riyadh separately issued a security alert after recent missile and drone attacks on Saudi airports, urging American citizens to exercise extreme caution when using them.

It said operations at King Khalid airport remained suspended, while Jeddah and Dammam airports were operating with some delays and cancellations. US government employees were told they need special permission from the chief of mission to use any Saudi airport.
