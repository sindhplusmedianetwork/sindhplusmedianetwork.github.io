US Will Not Attack Iran Before November Midterms, Says White House

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The administration of US President Donald Trump has clarified that the United States will not attack Iran before the midterm elections in November. According to reports, White House deputy press secretary Anna Kelly was asked about alleged talks between President Trump and Netanyahu on targeting Iran's leadership, and said that in Trump's view Iran was in very bad shape both economically and militarily. She also claimed that Iran's naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz would continue at full intensity and that oil shipments through the strait were continuing at record levels despite the Iranian blockade.

Kelly said in clear words that the US would not attack Iran at any time before the November midterm elections. Earlier, President Trump had also hinted that he would avoid attacking Iran before the midterms but would then launch a full and final attack. Earlier, Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu had claimed that President Trump fully supported his plan to eliminate the Iranian leadership, but the White House's fresh statement rules out any immediate attack.

Notably, the US midterm elections will be held in November, and President Trump's popularity has dropped massively amid rising petrol prices from the Iran war and the lack of decisive success in the war.
