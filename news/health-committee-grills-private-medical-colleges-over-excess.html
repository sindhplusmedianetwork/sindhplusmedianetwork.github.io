Health Committee Grills Private Medical Colleges Over Excess Fees

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Assembly Standing Committee on Health on Wednesday took notice of the failure to implement a decision extending the validity of Medical and Dental College Admission Test (MDCAT) results to three years, while expressing concern over private medical colleges allegedly charging students fees beyond the prescribed limits. The committee sought inspection reports of private medical colleges for the past two years after being informed that 45 institutions were collecting additional amounts from students, with some demanding as much as Rs600,000 over and above the approved fee. Health Minister Mustafa Kamal informed the committee that his ministry had approved the proposal to extend the validity of MDCAT results.

The committee expressed concern that private medical colleges were charging students fees beyond the prescribed limits. The decision to extend MDCAT result validity to three years had not yet been implemented, the committee noted.
