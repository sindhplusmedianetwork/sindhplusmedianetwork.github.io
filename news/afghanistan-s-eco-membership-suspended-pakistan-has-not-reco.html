Afghanistan's ECO Membership Suspended, Pakistan Has Not Recognised Taliban Regime, Says Dar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar has said Afghanistan's ECO membership remains suspended and Pakistan has not recognised the Taliban regime. He was speaking after the 30th ECO Council of Ministers meeting in Islamabad. Dar said Pakistan wants a peaceful Afghanistan and remains committed to the ECO charter.

He said Pakistan will keep playing its role for regional development and stability. He said the ECO chairmanship will stay with Pakistan for two years. He added that the ECO Vision 2026-35 sets the future direction for regional cooperation.
