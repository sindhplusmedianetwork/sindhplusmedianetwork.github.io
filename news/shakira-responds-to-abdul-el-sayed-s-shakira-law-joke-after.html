Shakira responds to Abdul El-Sayed's 'Shakira Law' joke after election victory speech in Michigan

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read

Colombian singer Shakira has joined the conversation surrounding Michigan senator-elect Abdul El-Sayed after delivering a witty response to his joke about introducing "Shakira law". El-Sayed, who defeated Republican Mike Rogers in the Michigan Senate race, faced Islamophobic attacks during his campaign, including false claims that he intended to introduce Sharia law, and he will become the first Muslim senator in US history when he takes office in January. Addressing the allegations during his election victory speech, El-Sayed turned the criticism into a joke, saying: "No one is trying to push Sharia law on anyone, but there is Shakira law.

And Shakira law has one commandment: that the hips don't lie." The remark, accompanied by a playful hip movement, was shared in a video on his campaign team's official Instagram account. Shakira herself subsequently joined in, leaving a comment that quickly attracted attention: "Finally, a law my hips can agree with! Habibi, think you can get it through Congress?" Her response prompted an enthusiastic reaction from social media users, with many praising the exchange and El-Sayed's sense of humour, and one commenter describing the remark as "such a dad joke".

The exchange has turned El-Sayed's campaign punchline into an unexpected viral moment, with Shakira adding her own unmistakable stamp of approval.
