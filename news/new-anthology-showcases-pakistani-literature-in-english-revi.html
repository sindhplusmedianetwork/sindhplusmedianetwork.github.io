New Anthology Showcases Pakistani Literature in English, Review Notes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dawn's Books & Authors has reviewed 'An Anthology of Literature in English from Pakistan', edited by Yasmeen Hameed and published by the Pakistan Academy of Letters. The 544-page compilation is divided into four sections covering poetry, short fiction, long fiction and non-fiction. It features writers such as Zulfikar Ghose, Daud Kamal, Maki Kureishi, Zeb-un-Nissa Hamidullah and Sara Suleri Goodyear.

The review finds the poetry section the strongest, presenting both established and new voices, including two previously unpublished sonnets by M. Athar Tahir. It notes the anthology overlooks significant contributions of the last decade and favours the 'old guard', but concludes it remains a valuable guide for students and literary historians.
