ANP Balochistan chief Asghar Achakzai arrested as court cancels bail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Awami National Party Balochistan president Asghar Khan Achakzai was arrested from a Chaman courtroom on Wednesday after the District and Sessions Judge cancelled his interim bail. Police took him into custody on the court's orders. According to police, Achakzai was arrested in murder and kidnapping cases connected to the killing of a local man, Taimur Shah, during a tribal dispute in Chaman about a year ago.

The cases were registered under Section 302 (murder) and Section 365 (kidnapping) of the Pakistan Penal Code on the complaint of the victim's father, Zahir Shah. Following the cancellation of his bail, Achakzai was shifted to the Chaman Bypass police lockup. He has also served as a member of the Balochistan Assembly.
