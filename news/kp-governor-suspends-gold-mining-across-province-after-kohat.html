KP Governor Suspends Gold Mining Across Province After Kohat Attack

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi on Saturday announced a ban on gold mining across the province and the revocation of all mining leases. The measures are part of efforts to curb illegal mining in the province. Addressing a press conference in Peshawar, the governor condemned the attack in Kohat that claimed the lives of 10 police personnel.

The personnel were returning from an operation against illegal mining when they were attacked. Kundi said illegal mining was worth Rs4.92 billion a month. He said a task force and a judicial commission would be formed to investigate the Kohat attack and mining activities in the province.
