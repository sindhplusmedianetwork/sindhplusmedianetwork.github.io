Bus conductor dies after being pushed from moving bus over Rs10 ticket change

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A 26-year-old woman bus conductor in India's Telangana state has died after allegedly being pushed from a moving bus during an argument over change for a 10-rupee ticket. Pravalika, who worked for the Telangana State Road Transport Corporation at the Manuguru depot, was travelling towards Bhadrachalam on October 25 when the dispute began. According to her colleagues, a woman passenger named Monica had given a 100-rupee note for her son's 10-rupee ticket and demanded the change at once, while Pravalika said she had no loose change and would return it later.

When Monica threatened to get off the moving bus, Pravalika tried to stop her, and the passenger allegedly pushed her with both hands, sending her falling onto the road, where the bus wheels ran over her legs. She spent ten days in intensive care and died on November 3, her mother Vijayalakshmi told the BBC. Police arrested Monica and, after Pravalika's death, changed the earlier attempt-to-murder case into a murder case.

The Telangana government has announced one million rupees in compensation for the family, while the transport corporation's insurance scheme will provide ten million rupees and her mother will be given a job, and staff at the Manuguru depot held a one-day strike over her death.
