Thal Jeep Rally stock races conclude as three vehicles meet accidents

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The stock category races of the 11th Thal Jeep Rally concluded in Muzaffargarh, during which three jeep racers’ vehicles, including one woman racer, met with accidents, though the racers suffered only minor injuries. Racer Azmeer Sar Anjam’s vehicle caught fire but he remained safe, Zubair Hayat’s vehicle overturned due to overspeeding, and Karachi-based woman racer Ambreen Faraz’s vehicle also met with an accident. In Stock A, Asif Imam completed the 200-kilometre track in 2 hours 35 minutes; in Stock B, Rab Nawaz Joya finished in 2 hours 26 minutes; and in Stock C, Hassan Jhandeer finished in 2 hours 36 minutes.

The final results will be announced at the prize distribution ceremony on the last day of the rally. The prepared category races will be held in the final stage of the event.
