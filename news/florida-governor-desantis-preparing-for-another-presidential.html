Florida Governor DeSantis preparing for another presidential run, report claims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Florida's Republican Governor Ron DeSantis has begun preparing to run for president again. A US newspaper has claimed that DeSantis wants to become the Republican presidential candidate in 2028. The report notes that Florida managed to stop the Democrats' 'blue wave' and that DeSantis wants to take advantage of that.

DeSantis, however, has dismissed the report as completely fabricated, saying the newspaper's move is an attempt to draw people's attention towards itself. The claim and the denial have set off fresh speculation about the 2028 Republican race.
