Anthropic Circulates Draft AI Regulation Bill Among US Lawmakers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI assistant, has confirmed it is circulating a draft bill among US lawmakers aimed at regulating artificial intelligence. The draft, first reported by Politico, would allow Washington to block a model's release or use if it poses an imminent risk of mass casualties or more than $1 billion in damages, subject to judicial approval. Frontier AI developers would also be required to test their most powerful models, be transparent about the findings, and submit them to independent evaluation.

The proposal marks a significant step by a leading AI lab towards formal regulation of the technology. Lawmakers are expected to debate the bill in the coming months.
