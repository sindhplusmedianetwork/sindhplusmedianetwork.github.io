Weekly Inflation Eases Slightly but Stays in Double Digits at 10.41 Percent

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's Sensitive Price Indicator (SPI) eased by 0.65 percent week-on-week for the week ended November 19, 2026, but remained in double digits on a year-on-year basis at 10.41 percent, according to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics. The combined SPI stood at 370.47 points, compared with 372.90 points in the preceding week and 335.54 points in the corresponding week of 2025. The annual increase was led by energy-related items, with liquefied petroleum gas up 87.99 percent, electricity charges for the lowest consumption quintile up 36.30 percent, high-speed diesel up 24.84 percent and petrol up 21.35 percent.

On a weekly basis, prices fell sharply for tomatoes, onions, LPG, petrol and diesel, while eggs, potatoes, wheat flour and chicken became costlier. Of the 51 essential items monitored across 50 markets in 17 cities, prices of 23 items increased, eight decreased and 20 remained unchanged. The weekly easing was recorded across all consumption quintiles, with the lowest quintile down 0.46 percent and the highest down 0.81 percent.

The sustained double-digit annual reading comes as prolonged regional tensions have disrupted energy flows through the Strait of Hormuz, while the monthly Consumer Price Index rose 9.6 percent year-on-year in October 2026.
