Faulty vehicle in President Zardari's motorcade triggers Sindh police inquiry

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh police have launched a high-level inquiry after an unroadworthy government vehicle was included in President Asif Ali Zardari's motorcade during his visit to Thatta. The vehicle was a government-owned Toyota Revo double-cabin pickup bearing registration number SPF-961. Its presence in the presidential motorcade raised serious questions about how a faulty vehicle was cleared for duty.

Inspector General of Police Sindh has formed a three-member committee to investigate the matter. The committee is chaired by Additional IGP Karachi Azad Khan. It will examine how the vehicle was assigned to the motorcade and submit its report within seven days.
