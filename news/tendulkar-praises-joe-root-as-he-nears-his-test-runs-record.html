Tendulkar praises Joe Root as he nears his Test runs record

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Indian cricketer Sachin Tendulkar has praised England batter Joe Root, saying he has admired Root's ability since his first Test match in 2012. Speaking during a question-and-answer session on Reddit, Tendulkar said he has always considered Root an excellent batter and believed from his debut that he would achieve great success. Root made his Test debut against India in Nagpur in December 2012, scoring 73 runs off 229 balls in the first innings and remaining 20 not out in the second as the match was drawn.

Root has so far scored 14,278 runs in 309 innings across 169 Tests, and needs 1,643 more runs to pass Tendulkar's world record of 15,921 Test runs. Tendulkar scored his 15,921 runs in 329 innings across 200 Tests, which remains the world record for most runs in Test cricket. Asked about his favourite non-Indian batters, Tendulkar named England's Harry Brook and Joe Root and Australia's Steve Smith.

Tendulkar also repeated his suggestion that a 50-over match should be split into two innings of 25 overs each, warning that change is needed in one-day cricket to protect the role of bowlers.
