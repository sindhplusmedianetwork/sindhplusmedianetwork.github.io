Petrol, diesel prices cut by Rs3 a litre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government on Wednesday cut petrol and diesel prices by Rs3 per litre each. It is the twelfth consecutive decrease in petrol's price. Petrol will now retail at Rs322 per litre and high-speed diesel at Rs355 from 19 November, according to the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority.

The government continues to levy Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 on diesel. Officials say refineries will bear a cumulative Rs15 billion impact over 30 days to fund part of the relief. The latest cut brings further relief to consumers after a series of reductions in fuel prices.
