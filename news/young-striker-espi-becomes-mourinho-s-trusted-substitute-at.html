Young Striker Espi Becomes Mourinho's Trusted Substitute at Real Madrid

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Young striker Carlos Espi has become Jose Mourinho's go-to man off the bench at Real Madrid. He has scored four goals in just 62 minutes spread across 12 matches in all competitions. His 85th-minute winner on Saturday secured a 2-1 victory over Celta Vigo and kept the pressure on La Liga leaders Barcelona.

The 21-year-old joined from Levante in the summer and has made a strong impression despite limited playing time. However, he faces stiff competition for a starting place from Kylian Mbappe. His impact as a substitute has nevertheless made him an important part of the manager's plans.
