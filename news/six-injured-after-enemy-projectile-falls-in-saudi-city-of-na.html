Six Injured After Enemy Projectile Falls in Saudi City of Najran, Civil Defence Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's civil defence directorate said on Sunday that an enemy projectile fell in the city of Najran, injuring six citizens. The blast also damaged three houses and three vehicles in the area. The alert came just hours after the directorate had announced that the "danger had passed" in Najran city and the As Sulayyil governorate.

The incident occurred amid intensified Houthi attacks targeting the kingdom. Civil defence teams responded to the scene and assessed the damage caused by the strike. Authorities have not released further details about the origin of the projectile.

The episode underscores the continuing security risks along the kingdom's southern border.
