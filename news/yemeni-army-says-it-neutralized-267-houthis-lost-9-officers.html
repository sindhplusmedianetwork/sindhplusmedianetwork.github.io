Yemeni army says it 'neutralized' 267 Houthis, lost 9 officers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's army said 992 operations against Houthi forces had left around 267 fighters and commanders dead, wounded or fleeing. Nine of the army's own officers were killed in the operations, the army said. Military spokesman Majed al-Nuzaili said the operations included 19 airstrikes, 29 drone strikes and 944 artillery and rocket attacks.

He also reported advances by government forces in Al-Jawf province. The Saudi-led coalition, meanwhile, said it intercepted a Houthi drone and five missiles aimed at Saudi Arabia. Falling debris caused minor damage to an Aramco building in Riyadh, with no casualties reported.
