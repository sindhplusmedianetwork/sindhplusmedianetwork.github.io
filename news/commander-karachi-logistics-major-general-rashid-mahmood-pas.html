Commander Karachi Logistics Major General Rashid Mahmood passes away

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Major General Rashid Mahmood, serving as Commander Karachi Logistics Area, passed away on Tuesday after suffering a sudden cardiac arrest. He was admitted in critical condition to Tabba Heart Institute in Azizabad, Karachi, where he breathed his last despite medical efforts. A member of the Pakistan Military Academy's 84th Long Course, he had previously been associated with 29 Cavalry.

News of his death prompted an outpouring of tributes on social media, where users shared emotional messages remembering the deceased officer and his service.
