Two firing incidents in a day rock occupied Kashmir

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A fresh wave of violence continued in occupied Kashmir, with two firing incidents reported in a single day in Kulgam and Kishtwar districts. In Kulgam, three personnel of the Central Reserve Police Force were injured when gunmen opened fire at a security checkpoint near Mir Bazar on Wednesday afternoon. In Kishtwar, the bullet-riddled bodies of two Special Police Officers were found on Thursday, a day after they went missing in the remote Dachhan area.

Security forces launched massive search operations in both districts to track down the attackers. A general security alert was sounded across the region, with extra checkposts and barricades set up to search vehicles. Former chief minister Farooq Abdullah condemned the attacks and said terrorism would never succeed in Kashmir.
