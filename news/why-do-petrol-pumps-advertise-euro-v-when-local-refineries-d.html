Why do petrol pumps advertise Euro-V when local refineries don't make it, asks committee

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Assembly's Standing Committee on Petroleum has expressed anger over fuel quality, asking why petrol pumps advertise Euro-V fuel when local refineries do not produce it. The committee's meeting, chaired by Syed Mustafa Mahmood, reviewed the quality of fuel from local refineries and proposed petroleum and gas amendment bills. The chairman said Pakistani refineries are not producing Euro-V fuel and only imported petrol and diesel in the country meets the Euro-V standard, asking why Euro-V is written at petrol pumps when local refineries do not make it at all.

The managing director of PSO said local refineries add extra chemicals to fuel to make it Euro-V standard, but the chairman rejected the explanation, saying ordinary fuel cannot be declared Euro-V by adding extra chemicals. On a member's objection, discussion on the Gas Development Surcharge Amendment Bill 2026 and the Gas Infrastructure Development Cess Amendment Bill 2026 was deferred, with the chairman saying a major political party objects to the bills. Under the proposed bill, refineries will have to deposit the petroleum levy before their products leave the gate, and a fine will be imposed for late deposit of the petroleum and climate support levy.

Member Naveed Qamar expressed reservations, saying collecting the levy before products leave the gate will burden refineries financially and asking whether refineries should not have been consulted first. Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik defended the bill, saying refineries should deposit the levy amount on time so government money does not remain unnecessarily with refineries.
