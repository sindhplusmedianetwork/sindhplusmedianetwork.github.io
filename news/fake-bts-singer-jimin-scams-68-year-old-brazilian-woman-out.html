Fake BTS singer Jimin scams 68-year-old Brazilian woman out of $50,000

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 68-year-old retired woman in the Brazilian city of Sao Paulo was defrauded in an online romance scam by a fraudster posing as Jimin, the singer of the South Korean music group BTS. The scammer took about 300,000 Brazilian reals, equal to roughly 52,000 to 56,000 US dollars, from the woman. According to police records, the woman stayed in online contact with the fraudster for about four months, receiving affectionate messages that created the impression of a close relationship.

The fraudster later promised to send expensive gifts, but then claimed the gifts had been held by Brazilian customs and that duty had to be paid to release them. The woman paid the demanded sums several times, even taking a bank loan to make the payments. The case was first reported by the Recife newspaper Diario de Pernambuco, and Sao Paulo civil police are investigating the network behind the fraud.
