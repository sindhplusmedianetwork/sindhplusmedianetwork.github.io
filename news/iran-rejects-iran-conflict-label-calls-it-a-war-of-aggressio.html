Iran Rejects 'Iran Conflict' Label, Calls It a War of Aggression

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei criticised the use of the term 'Iran conflict' to describe the war involving Tehran, the United States and Israel. He said the phrase obscures responsibility and portrays the country under attack as the aggressor. Baqaei called the military campaign an unlawful war of aggression by Washington and Israel.

Meanwhile, Brent crude touched a more than five-month high of $113.96 a barrel as Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia stoked fears of further energy supply disruption. Etihad Airways also extended its suspension of flights to Riyadh and Dammam until November 5. The remarks underline Tehran's effort to shape the narrative around the ongoing conflict.
