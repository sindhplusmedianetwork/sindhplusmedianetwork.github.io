Death Toll in Baloch Colony Building Collapse Rises to 11

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The death toll from the collapse of a six-storey building near Karachi's Baloch Colony area has risen to 11 after rescue workers recovered three more bodies from the rubble. At least eight more people are still reported missing, and the search for those trapped under the debris continues. The under-construction building, reportedly built illegally on a residential plot, collapsed on Wednesday, initially killing three people and injuring 14.

The Karachi Commissioner's office told the chief minister that 516 buildings have been sealed so far in an ongoing crackdown on illegal construction across the city. An FIR has been registered at Tipu Sultan police station, naming the building's owners, builder, architect, structural engineer and contractors.
