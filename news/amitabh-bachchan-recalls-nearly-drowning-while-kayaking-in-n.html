Amitabh Bachchan recalls nearly drowning while kayaking in Nepal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood veteran Amitabh Bachchan revealed that he nearly drowned while kayaking during a film shoot in Nepal. His small kayak capsized and he was trapped in the water. He said it took him about 45 minutes just to sit in the narrow kayak.

After it overturned, no one came to his rescue for a long time despite his waving for help. A man standing near a waterfall eventually realised something was wrong and pulled him out of the water. Bachchan called it one of the most frightening experiences of his life.
