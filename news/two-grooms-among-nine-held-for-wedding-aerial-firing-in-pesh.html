Two Grooms Among Nine Held for Wedding Aerial Firing in Peshawar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Peshawar have arrested two grooms and seven of their friends for aerial firing during wedding ceremonies in different areas of the city. Police from five police stations raided the wedding functions and seized the weapons used in the firing. The action was taken on the instructions of the capital city police chief, Dr Mian Saeed Ahmad.

Cases have been registered against those arrested and further investigation is under way. Police said a zero-tolerance policy against aerial firing at weddings will continue, and action will be taken against anyone found firing into the air at celebrations.
