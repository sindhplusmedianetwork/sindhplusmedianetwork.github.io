Azma Bukhari Hits Back at Kamran Murtaza Over Remarks on Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bukhari has responded to JUI-F Senator Kamran Murtaza's statement about Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz. In a statement issued late on Friday, she said Maryam Nawaz's practical steps and performance for public welfare cause discomfort not only to the PTI but to many others as well. Bukhari said the PTI alone is enough for point-scoring against Maryam Nawaz, and the JUI-F should not attempt to become its B-team.

She added that the politics of 'a knife under the arm and Ram Ram on the tongue' will no longer work. The minister said the people of Punjab had given Maryam Nawaz a mandate, and living up to their trust is her foremost priority. She stressed that the protection of women and children tops the Punjab government's priorities and that violence against women and abuse of children are unacceptable in any form.

Bukhari said Maryam Nawaz will take every step needed to establish law and order in Punjab and protect the lives and property of the people, and that she cares little for criticism and will make no compromise on public safety.
