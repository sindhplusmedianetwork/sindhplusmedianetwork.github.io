Fichardt storms into Simmons Bank Championship lead

By Sindh Plus Media Network

South African Darren Fichardt tied the mark for the lowest round of the PGA Tour Champions season with an 11-under-par bogey-free 61 in the second round on Saturday. The 51-year-old tour rookie started with birdie-birdie-eagle-birdie from the second hole, shooting an 8-under 28 on the front nine. His total of 14-under 130 gives him a one-stroke lead heading into the final round of the Simmons Bank Championship in Little Rock, Arkansas.

It was a stunning display of ball-striking from the veteran, who is chasing his first title on the over-50 circuit.
