Bilawal Calls KP CM 'Coward', Defends Constitutional Emergency Powers at Karsaz Anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Addressing the 19th anniversary ceremony of the Karsaz tragedy in Karachi, Pakistan Peoples Party Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari launched a sharp attack on the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government. He described Chief Minister Suhail Afridi as a coward who was not ready to defend his own land. Bilawal said emergencies could only be imposed through constitutional means, adding that nothing could be done in KP without the signature of President Asif Ali Zardari.

The PPP chairman paid tribute to the martyrs of the Karsaz incident and recalled the return of former prime minister Benazir Bhutto to Pakistan. He also reminded the audience of the military operations carried out in Swat. Turning to Maulana Fazlur Rehman, Bilawal urged him to speak openly against terrorists instead of playing politics over terrorism.

He said the fight against militancy needed clear positions rather than political point-scoring.
