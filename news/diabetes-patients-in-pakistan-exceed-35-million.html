Diabetes patients in Pakistan exceed 35 million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The number of people with diabetes in Pakistan has exceeded 35 million. Experts have identified unhealthy lifestyles as a major reason for the rise in the disease. Medical experts say one in every five people in Hyderabad has diabetes.

In Pakistan, 33.5 percent of people aged over 18, approximately 35 million, are affected by the disease. Experts advise at least 30 minutes of walking daily, eating home-cooked food and avoiding meals from outside. They note that World Diabetes Prevention Day is observed every year on November 14.

The figures underline the scale of the health challenge facing the country.
