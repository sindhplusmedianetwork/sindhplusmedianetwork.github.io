Man accused of burning his mother and two sisters alive killed in alleged police encounter in Multan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Aman Abbas, 22, accused of killing his mother and two sisters in Multan on October 28 and setting their bodies on fire to make the murders look like an accident, has been killed in an alleged police encounter. Police say the bodies of his mother, 45-year-old Parveen Sarwar, and her two daughters, aged 17 and 14, were found after a fire at their home in Lutafabad on the evening of October 28. Investigators said the position of the bodies raised suspicion that the three had been killed before the fire was set, and CCTV from a petrol pump showed Abbas buying petrol in a bottle at 11:54am the same day.

The FIR says Abbas told his uncle he had struck his mother and sisters with a stick before setting the bodies on fire, and that he opposed his mother's wish to remarry. Abbas was taken into custody on November 3 and remanded into Crime Control Department custody the next day. Police say he was being taken to Kahror Pacca in Lodhran for recovery and identification of the crime scene when armed companions opened fire on the police party and freed him.

Police say that hours later, in a second encounter, Abbas was hit by his own companions' bullets and died, while the attackers fled. His body has been sent for post-mortem and a search operation is under way for the men who fled.
