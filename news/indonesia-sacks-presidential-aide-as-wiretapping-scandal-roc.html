Indonesia Sacks Presidential Aide as Wiretapping Scandal Rocks Government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indonesia's president dismissed his chief of staff on November 4, 2026, after the arrest of three senior police officers accused of illegally tapping the phones of President Prabowo Subianto and others. The government said the wiretapping, believed to have run since early 2025, could amount to treason. The military mobilised thousands of troops in Jakarta to safeguard security amid the unfolding scandal.

Officials have not yet detailed the full extent of the surveillance. The rift comes against widening public discontent over the president's spending policies. Investor confidence in the country has also been sliding in recent months.
