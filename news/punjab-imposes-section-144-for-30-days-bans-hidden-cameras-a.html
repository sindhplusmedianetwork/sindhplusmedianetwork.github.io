Punjab Imposes Section 144 for 30 Days, Bans Hidden Cameras and Spy Gadgets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has imposed Section 144 across the province for 30 days, placing a ban on hidden recording devices and equipment used for spying. According to a notification issued by the Punjab Home Department, the restriction covers smart glasses fitted with hidden cameras and micro-cameras concealed in everyday objects such as pens, keychains and other daily-use items. No shopkeeper, trader or distributor will be allowed to sell these concealed recording devices, and their online sale and purchase is also prohibited.

The ban applies to any device capable of secretly capturing and transmitting photographs, video or audio. Laptops, tablets, mobile phones and PTA-approved terminal devices are exempt from the prohibition. The Home Department said concealed recording devices threaten citizens' private lives, sensitive information and the security of installations, and that the ban aims to ensure the protection of the honour, dignity and privacy of citizens, particularly women and children.
