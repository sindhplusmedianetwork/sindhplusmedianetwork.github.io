Mir Raza Commission Suspects Woman Police Officer of Facilitation, Seeks Probe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The judicial commission on the Mir Raza case has recommended action against officers who showed negligence and carelessness in the investigation of the case. The one-member judicial commission of Justice Umar Sial, formed by the Sindh government, issued observations about the role of bureaucrats and police personnel. The commission presented observations on the role of the health secretary, two DIGs and 16 officers and officials, writing in the report that there was no doubt about the role of Additional IG Karachi Azad Khan but the investigation team had failed him.

The report said a serious lack of leadership and oversight was visible in Health Secretary Tahir Sangi, while it recommended starting an inquiry against Special Health Secretary Shahryar Memon. The commission observed that DIG East Farrukh Lanjar did not fulfil his responsibilities during the case and appeared non-serious, while DIG Aamer Farooqi displayed professional negligence and non-seriousness in the investigation, with a serious lack of leadership and oversight in both. It said the two officers' answers to the questionnaire provided to the commission were non-serious and unsatisfactory, while SSP Samiullah Soomro showed a lack of leadership and used unusual investigation methods without considering the case's legal aspects, needing training for high-profile cases.

The commission wrote that ASP Nida Junaid was an unprofessional officer, that there was suspicion of facilitation by her in the Mir Raza case, and therefore she should be investigated.
