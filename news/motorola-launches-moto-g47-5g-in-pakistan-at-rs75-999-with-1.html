Motorola launches Moto G47 5G in Pakistan at Rs75,999 with 108MP camera

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Motorola has launched its mid-range Moto G47 5G in Pakistan. The phone is priced at Rs75,999 for the single model with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. It is powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 6300 processor.

The phone has a 6.67-inch display with a 120Hz refresh rate and a 108-megapixel main camera. It carries a 5,200mAh battery and runs Android 16. The handset is IP64 rated for dust and splash resistance.
