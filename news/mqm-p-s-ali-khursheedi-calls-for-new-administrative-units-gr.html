MQM-P's Ali Khursheedi Calls for New Administrative Units, Greater Local Authority in Sindh

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Opposition Leader in the Sindh Assembly, Ali Khursheedi, has said the creation of new administrative units has become a need of the time because the existing provincial governments have failed to deliver. Addressing workers of Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan in Hyderabad on Saturday, the MPA said Hyderabad was Sindh's second largest city but had continuously suffered from the government's neglect of its development and civic issues. He maintained that MQM-P did not want the division of Sindh but was supporting the idea of transferring authority to the lowest level.

He said unless authority and funds are transferred to local government, the civic and development issues of the cities will keep eluding solutions. Khursheedi said the creation of more administrative units within the provinces was not a conspiracy against any province, and the move was meant to devolve authority to smaller administrations for better service delivery. Local leaders of the MQM-P Hyderabad chapter also addressed the workers in Phuleli, Pareetabad and Kohsar areas.
