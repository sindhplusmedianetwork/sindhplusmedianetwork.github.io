State Bank licenses two credit bureaus to boost SME lending

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The State Bank of Pakistan has issued licences to two credit bureaus, a step expected to strengthen lending in the country. The bureaus will help identify borrowers' needs and assess the correct loan amounts, boosting growth and lending in the SME sector. Deputy Governor Saleemullah spoke about the development at a Bank of the Future forum.

He said the use of AI in banking is a key need of the time. He added that the central bank has prepared guidelines for AI use in banking, which are awaiting internal approval. The licensing of the new bureaus is part of wider efforts to improve credit assessment and expand access to finance for small and medium enterprises.
