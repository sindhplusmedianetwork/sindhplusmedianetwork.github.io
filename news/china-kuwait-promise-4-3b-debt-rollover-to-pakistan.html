China, Kuwait promise $4.3b debt rollover to Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has secured assurances from China and Kuwait to roll over 4.3 billion dollars in loans, following a similar arrangement from Saudi Arabia, taking the total financial relief expected from the three countries to 7.3 billion dollars. According to Finance Ministry sources, China will roll over 4 billion dollars in loans, while Kuwait will extend a rollover of 300 million dollars, with the Chinese loan expected to be rolled over in March 2027 and all three arrangements extended for approximately one year. The commitments are expected to ease Pakistan's external financing pressures, with 7.3 billion dollars in rollovers accounting for a substantial portion of the 21 billion dollars in debt repayments due during the current fiscal year.

Sources also said Pakistan was expected to receive 1.21 billion dollars from the International Monetary Fund in December. The latest assurances follow Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb's statement a day earlier that Saudi Arabia had already rolled over its 3 billion dollar loan. Aurangzeb said the country had foreign exchange reserves sufficient to cover three months of imports and that the government was working to ensure there were no difficulties in meeting its obligations, including about 1.5 billion euros in Eurobond repayments annually.

Pakistan remains dependent on the rollover of existing debts by the bilateral and commercial creditors to whom it owes 10.5 billion dollars this fiscal year.
