WHO Warns of High Child Death Rate in DR Congo Ebola Outbreak

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The World Health Organization has warned of a high death rate among children in an outbreak of the rare Bundibugyo version of the Ebola virus in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. According to data from the Congolese Ministry of Health and the WHO, there have been 1,207 confirmed cases in children under the age of 5, of whom 844 have died, roughly 7 in 10. That rate is higher than among adults, for whom the fatality rate is around 50 percent.

Officials said early diagnosis is difficult because bleeding symptoms occur in only around 5 to 10 percent of confirmed Bundibugyo cases, so the disease can be mistaken for common illnesses such as malaria. Around 70 percent of patients are still dying in their communities rather than in healthcare centres. There are no licensed treatments or vaccines for this strain, and more than 11,000 cases and over 5,500 deaths have been reported overall.
