Key Arrest in Gadap Farmhouse Case of Phones Snatched From 50 Women

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Investigation police have made a significant arrest in the Gadap farmhouse case, in which more than 50 women were allegedly lured to a farmhouse with promises of jobs and training before their mobile phones were snatched and the accused fled. Officers picked up the main suspect Sahil Salman's neighbour from Baldia Town, and examination of the detained man's mobile phone data confirmed his contact with the prime accused. The women were called to the farmhouse in Gadap on the pretext of employment and vocational training, after which their phones were taken.

The main accused has secured pre-arrest bail from a court, and the bail hearing is scheduled for October 24. Police say the investigation is continuing to trace the stolen phones and any wider network behind the scheme.
