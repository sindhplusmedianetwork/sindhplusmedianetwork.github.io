Shazia Marri asks why 27th Amendment was passed by numbers, not consensus

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP central spokesperson Shazia Marri has asked Minister of State Talal Chaudhry to explain why the 27th Constitutional Amendment was passed on the strength of a numerical majority instead of political consensus. She said the PPP had not spoken of the system's failure, a political reset or dividing provinces, and asked whether expressing an opinion was now also banned. Marri added that Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari was demanding supremacy of the Constitution, not political favours.

She said new provinces could only be created through the constitutional procedure, and pointed out that the Sindh Assembly had repeatedly rejected the division of Sindh. Marri also noted that Sindh provides the federation about 585 billion rupees in additional financial support, yet the federal government questions the province's performance. Her remarks come amid the continuing political debate over the 27th Amendment and the issue of new provinces, on which the PPP and its critics remain sharply divided.
