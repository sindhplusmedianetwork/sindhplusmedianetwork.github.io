Five-Year Divorce Battle Ends in Delhi Court as Woman Tears Up Papers and Hugs Husband

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A five-year divorce battle in a Delhi court ended in reconciliation when a woman tore up the divorce papers and embraced her husband. The couple, who married in 2020, had been locked in litigation for five years. The turning point came when the woman's father suffered a heart attack and her husband helped during that time.

His help softened the rift between them. The long legal battle finally came to an end with the couple reuniting in court.
