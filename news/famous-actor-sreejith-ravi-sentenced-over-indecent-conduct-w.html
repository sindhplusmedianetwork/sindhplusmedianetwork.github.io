Famous actor Sreejith Ravi sentenced over indecent conduct with children

Indian Malayalam film actor Sreejith Ravi has been sentenced to one year of rigorous imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 Indian rupees for indecent conduct in front of minor children. A fast-track court in Thrissur announced the verdict on Monday in a case relating to an incident in 2022, finding the actor guilty under Section 509 of the Indian Penal Code and Section 11(i) of the POCSO law, which protects children from sexual offences. Section 509 concerns words, gestures or acts intended to insult the modesty of a woman, while Section 11 of the POCSO law protects children from sexual harassment, and because the incident took place before the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita came into force in July 2024, the case was tried under the previous law.

The court said that if the actor fails to pay the fine of 50,000 rupees, he will have to serve an additional one month of rigorous imprisonment. After the verdict, Sreejith Ravi told the court he wanted to file an appeal in the Kerala High Court, and the court suspended the sentence for one month and ordered his release on bail with two sureties and a bond of 50,000 rupees, directing him to appear in court again on 23 December 2026. According to the prosecution, the incident occurred on 4 July 2022, when children returning from school towards their residential apartment complex were allegedly subjected to an indecent act by the actor, who also threatened them.

The case passed through several stages: in 2022 Ravi sought discharge from the trial court, but the court rejected his plea, and the Kerala High Court later also rejected his petition, after which the trial began and all 21 prosecution witnesses supported the prosecution's case.
