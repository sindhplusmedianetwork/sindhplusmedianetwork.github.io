Khawaja Says He Made No One a Scapegoat in Book 'Sabr'

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Australian cricketer Usman Khawaja says his book 'Sabr' did not make any player a scapegoat. The book contains new revelations about the 2018 sandpaper scandal, which has drawn criticism from some quarters. Khawaja said his account of the ball-tampering episode did not single out any teammate for blame.

He said the book tells his own experience of the episode. Khawaja insisted that no player was blamed unfairly in his account.
