Punjab Senior Minister Maryam Aurangzeb's sister-in-law passes away

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Senior Minister Maryam Aurangzeb's sister-in-law passed away on Wednesday, her family confirmed. The news was shared by the family, who announced the funeral arrangements. According to the family, the funeral prayer of the deceased will be offered at 1:30 in the afternoon at the Event Complex, GOR One.

Political figures and colleagues are expected to attend the funeral to express condolences. The family has requested prayers for the departed soul. Further details about the burial will be shared by the family.
