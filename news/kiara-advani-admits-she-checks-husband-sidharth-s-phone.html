Kiara Advani Admits She Checks Husband Sidharth's Phone

Bollywood actress Kiara Advani has admitted that she checks her husband Sidharth Malhotra's phone. The couple appeared together in a recent episode of the ninth season of the chat show Koffee with Karan. During the conversation, host Karan Johar asked them whether they had ever checked each other's mobile phones.

Sidharth did not respond immediately, but Kiara admitted that she does check his phone. When Karan asked what she usually looks at in the phone, she said she sometimes enjoys reading random chats, just like that. Her admission drew a playful response from Sidharth, who said he would now have to check her phone as well.

The exchange was one of several light-hearted revelations the couple shared about their life together.
