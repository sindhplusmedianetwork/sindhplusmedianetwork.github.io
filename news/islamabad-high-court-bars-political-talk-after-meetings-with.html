Islamabad High Court bars political talk after meetings with Imran Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has issued a two-page order directing lawyers meeting PTI founder Imran Khan to avoid discussing political matters and to confine themselves to legal issues. A bench of Chief Justice Sarfraz Dogar and Justice Muhammad Asif said it expects Salman Safdar and Latif Khosa not to engage in political discussion during or after jail meetings with Khan. The court said both lawyers are highly professional and it expects them to avoid political discussion during the meetings and afterwards.

The order was passed during the hearing of appeals by Khan and Bushra Bibi in the 190 million pound case. The court directed the Advocate General to arrange the meeting for not less than one hour. During the hearing, Latif Khosa said he could not argue without meeting Khan, while Salman Safdar said he had received instructions to argue the appeal but the meeting duration had been short.

On the lawyers' request, the court gave them one week to prepare arguments and ordered the office to prepare paper books of the appeals within seven days.
