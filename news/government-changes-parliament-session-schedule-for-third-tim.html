Government changes Parliament session schedule for third time in a month

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has changed the schedule for calling a session of Parliament for the third time in a month. The change was made apparently in an effort to keep the situation under control. Senior political sources told The News and Jang on Thursday evening about the revised scheduling.

The sources said the government had planned to summon the 31st session of the National Assembly on the 7th of this month. The report was published in Jang on November 20, 2026.
