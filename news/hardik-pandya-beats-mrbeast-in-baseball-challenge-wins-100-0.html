Hardik Pandya Beats MrBeast in Baseball Challenge, Wins $100,000 for Charity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian all-rounder Hardik Pandya won $100,000 in a baseball challenge featured in a MrBeast video that included other sports stars. Pandya out-hit his opponent by smashing more home runs in the challenge. The prize amounts to roughly 94.9 lakh Indian rupees.

After his victory, Pandya decided to donate the entire prize money to charities working for stray dogs and underprivileged children. The video put a cricket star in an unusual sporting contest, drawing wide attention from fans. Pandya's decision to give away the winnings won praise from supporters.
