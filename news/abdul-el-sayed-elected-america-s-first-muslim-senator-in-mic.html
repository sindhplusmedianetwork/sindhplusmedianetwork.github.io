Abdul El-Sayed elected America's first Muslim senator in Michigan

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read

Democrat candidate Abdul El-Sayed defeated Republican rival and former congressman Mike Rogers in Michigan to win the Senate seat. With this victory, he will become the first Muslim senator in American history. According to the Detroit Free Press, Decision Desk HQ and Fox News announced his victory at around 2am on Wednesday, November 4, when with 70 percent of votes counted he was leading with 52 percent against Rogers' 46 percent.

The 42-year-old is from Ann Arbor, Michigan, has served as health director of Wayne County and Detroit, and comes from a family of Egyptian immigrants. He will replace outgoing Democratic senator Gary Peters, who decided to step down after completing two terms, and will join the Senate when the new Congress begins in January. The issue of Israel and Palestine was prominent in his campaign; he has been a strong critic of Israeli government policies and the treatment of Palestinians, and demanded an end to US military aid to Israel.

During the campaign he was targeted by advertisements trying to portray him as sympathetic to extremism and terrorism, which he rejected while repeatedly condemning antisemitism and all forms of political violence.
