Three Women Injured as Stray Bullets Keep Hitting Karachi Homes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The spate of bullets fired from unknown directions continued in Karachi, injuring three women in different areas of the city. In Korangi 100 Quarters, lane 16, a 25-year-old woman named Momna was injured when a bullet fired from an unknown direction hit her inside her home. Ibrahim Hyderi police said the incident was caused by a bullet coming from an unknown direction.

In Mianwali Colony in the Peerabad area, a 45-year-old woman was injured by a bullet that struck inside her home, while in Ittehad Town, near Ameer Muawiya Chowk, a 30-year-old woman named Saima was injured in a similar incident inside her house. All three women were given immediate medical aid at Abbasi Shaheed Hospital and Civil Hospital. Police have not yet determined who fired the shots or from where.
