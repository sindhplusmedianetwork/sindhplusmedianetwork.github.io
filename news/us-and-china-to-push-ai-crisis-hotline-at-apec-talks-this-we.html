US and China to push AI crisis hotline at APEC talks this week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping will attempt this week at the APEC meeting on November 18-19 to advance an AI crisis hotline agreed in September. Cooperation on artificial intelligence risks is seen as increasingly urgent despite the fierce tech rivalry between the two countries. The hotline is meant to help the two sides manage crises linked to artificial intelligence.

The talks are overshadowed by Jensen Huang's resignation as boss of US chip titan Nvidia after revelations of an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. The meeting will test whether the two leaders can make progress on AI safety even as competition in technology intensifies.
