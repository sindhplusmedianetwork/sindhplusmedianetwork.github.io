Faysal Quraishi, Hareem Farooq Flag 'Paid' Negative Reviews of Mango Jutt

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Actors Faysal Quraishi and Hareem Farooq have raised concerns over alleged paid negative publicity targeting their Punjabi-language film Mango Jutt, warning that such campaigns could damage both the film and Pakistan's struggling cinema industry. Quraishi said in an Instagram video that some negative reviews appear the day after a film's release, unlike serious critics who wait until the weekend has passed before reviewing a film. He questioned how many people are harmed by those pursuing their own gains through paid propaganda, and said a film's commercial fate should ultimately be decided by audiences.

Farooq, who produced and stars in Mango Jutt, shared screenshots on Instagram that appeared to raise questions about whether some negative posts about the film had been paid for. One exchange asked whether a negative post was a paid post, and the reply was affirmative, followed by a question about sharing the cost or budget. In a separate post, she highlighted reports that several cinemas had dropped the film, while others had reduced its showtimes or shifted screenings away from prime-time slots.

The post attributed the reported decline to competition from Hollywood releases and negative word of mouth. The actors argue that genuine criticism should be distinguished from deliberately generated negativity, which they say causes losses to an already small industry.
