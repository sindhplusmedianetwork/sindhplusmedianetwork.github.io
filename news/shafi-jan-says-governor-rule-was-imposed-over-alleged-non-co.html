Shafi Jan Says Governor Rule Was Imposed Over Alleged Non-Cooperation on Terrorism, Demands Details of Operations in a Month

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf leader Shafi Jan said four weeks have passed since governor rule was imposed in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, yet the province's elected chief minister, Sohail Afridi, and the provincial assembly speaker remain missing. In a statement on X, he said the stated basis for imposing governor rule was the provincial government's alleged non-cooperation against terrorism, but after a month the authorities should explain what operations were carried out against terrorism, where they took place and what results they produced. He alleged that operations in the province are only being carried out against PTI, targeting party flags, plaques of development projects, workers, elected representatives and their relatives.

He also alleged that anti-encroachment drives are being used against poor people and that appointments and transfers in government departments are being openly auctioned. Shafi Jan said those who levelled allegations of mismanagement and corruption in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have so far produced no solid evidence, while corruption and administrative problems in Punjab are being discussed by government circles themselves and similar problems exist in Sindh and Balochistan. He warned that the current measures could worsen law and order in the province and that the federal government's priority appears to be finishing PTI politically rather than ending terrorism.

He claimed Chief Minister Afridi had offered to restore peace in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa within 100 days through the provincial government's anti-terrorism policy, presented at the Apex Committee meeting, but it was ignored. He said the provincial government had invited all stakeholders to sit together for a permanent solution to terrorism to protect citizens, businesspeople and personnel of the army, police and other state institutions, but the federal government did not cooperate.
