Sindh High Court Adjourns E-Challan Petitions Indefinitely as Bus Owners Cite Missing Bus Stops

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court on Thursday heard petitions challenging the issuance of e-challans for traffic violations and adjourned the hearing for an indefinite period. Jamaat-e-Islami's lawyer Usman Farooq and the bus owners' lawyer, Munsif Jan Advocate, appeared before the court. The bus owners' lawyer argued that the Sindh government had still not marked bus stops, which meant a challan of Rs20,000 was issued even when a bus stopped only to drop off passengers.

He said private bus owners were finding it difficult to keep their buses running on the roads under these conditions. The court was told that the petitioners' lawyer, Tariq Mansoor Advocate, was unwell and could not appear, after which the court put off the hearing indefinitely.
