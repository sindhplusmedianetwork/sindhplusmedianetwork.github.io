PCB begins hunt for new head coach of Pakistan women's cricket team

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has begun searching for a new head coach of the Pakistan women's cricket team, according to a report published in Jang. Sources say the appointment is likely to be a foreign coach and will be finalised before the team's upcoming tour of the West Indies. Wahab Riaz is currently serving as the mentor of the women's side.

The move comes as the board reviews its plans for the women's team during an ongoing preparations cycle. No timeline has been officially announced for the appointment. The decision marks a fresh phase for the women's setup as it heads into an important international season.
