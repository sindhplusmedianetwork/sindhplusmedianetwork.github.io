Pentagon official says Iran war has cost US more than $48bn, excluding damage to bases

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The war against Iran has cost the US military more than $48 billion, a Pentagon official has told lawmakers. The United States and Israel launched the conflict in late February. Deputy comptroller Arthur Miller gave the figure to lawmakers.

He said the cost stood at $48.3 billion as of November 13. The figure covers munitions, equipment replacement and operational costs. The total excludes damage to US bases in the Middle East and harm to the global economy.
