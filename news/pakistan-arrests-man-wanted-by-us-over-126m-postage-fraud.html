Pakistan arrests man wanted by US over $126m postage fraud

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has arrested a man accused of running a counterfeit postage operation that allegedly cost the United States Postal Service $126 million. The suspect, 33-year-old Faheem Akram of Khanewal, is accused of selling more than 5.1 million fake postage labels through the website LabelsBank.com. US prosecutors say he sold the counterfeit labels at a fixed price of $2 each from August 2023 onwards.

A federal grand jury in Florida indicted him last month on charges of conspiracy, counterfeit postage and wire fraud. The arrest marks a significant step in the cross-border investigation into the large-scale fraud scheme.
