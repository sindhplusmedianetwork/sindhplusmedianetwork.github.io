Controlling Smog Will Take Years of Sustained Effort, Says Punjab Assembly Speaker

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Assembly Speaker Malik Muhammad Ahmad Khan has said it will take several years of continuous hard work to bring smog under control. He said the Punjab government is taking concrete steps to tackle the problem. The Speaker was speaking at the inauguration of an industrial expo in Lahore.

He said the path to development in the country has begun and will keep growing. He added that the economy cannot move forward in the presence of terrorism. According to him, a single attack shakes the entire economy.

He said those who invest today deserve to be called heroes.
