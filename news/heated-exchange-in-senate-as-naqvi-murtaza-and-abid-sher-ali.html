Heated Exchange in Senate as Naqvi, Murtaza and Abid Sher Ali Clash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Senate session turned disorderly on Thursday when JUI-F Senator Kamran Murtaza protested Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's speech. Naqvi told Murtaza that he would have to listen to the response, adding that critics should have the courage to hear replies. Murtaza shot back, telling the minister to "mind your language." PML-N Senator Abid Sher Ali then clashed with Murtaza, telling him "you should be ashamed," and Minister of State Talal Chaudhry had to intervene and ask Ali to sit down.

The exchange added to the day's tensions in the upper house.
