Houthis Say They Fired Ballistic Missiles at Riyadh Airport and Financial Centre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's Houthis said they targeted King Khalid International Airport and a financial centre in the Saudi capital Riyadh with two ballistic missiles, Reuters reported. The group said the missiles were fired toward the Saudi capital. Military and government sources also told AFP that Houthi forces had advanced to about 60 kilometres from Aden city.

The sources said the Houthis seized control of the Al-Maqatirah and Al-Madaribah districts in the advance. The sources said the Houthi advance had brought their forces closer to Aden city.
