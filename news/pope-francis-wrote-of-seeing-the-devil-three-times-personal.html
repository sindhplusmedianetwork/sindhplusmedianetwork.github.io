Pope Francis wrote of seeing the devil three times, personal diary reveals

The late Pope Francis wrote in his personal diary that he saw the devil three times and once saw the entrance to hell, according to excerpts published on Tuesday in a new book. The Private Diary of Pope Francis, compiled by Argentine Cardinal Victor Manuel Fernandez, draws on a journal the former Jorge Bergoglio kept between 1992 and 2024, revealing his worries, regrets and self-criticism as well as his faith. Francis wrote that while he was archbishop of Buenos Aires he saw the devil on three occasions, including from a car window and in a chapel choir loft during Mass, and once more in Rome after a visit to Chile.

In a journal entry dated January 24, 2021, he recalled the devil watching him with fiery eyes, and in a separate entry he described seeing the entrance to hell on the night of June 6, 2018, an experience that left him terrified and reaching for his rosary. Throughout the diary Francis does not spare himself, writing of feelings of disdain, envy and pride, and describing himself in December 2022 as proud, self-sufficient, cynical and self-deceptive. Mercy is a recurring theme, and he also worried about his ageing and the shortness of his remaining time, writing in 1996, at the age of 60, that he wanted to do good in the little time he had left.

The journal contains 86 pages written before his election as pope in 2013 and 55 written afterwards, and one of its briefest entries, dated July 21, 2013, records simply that on March 13 they elected him pope. Fernandez, who heads the Vatican's Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, said Francis had asked him to make the contents of the black plastic journal known to the world, and his successor, Leo XIV, allowed the passages to be published; Francis died on April 21, 2025.
