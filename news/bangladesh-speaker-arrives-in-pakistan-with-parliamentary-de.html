Bangladesh speaker arrives in Pakistan with parliamentary delegation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bangladesh Speaker Hafizuddin Ahmed Bir Bikram has arrived in Pakistan with a high-level parliamentary delegation. The visit is taking place at the special invitation of National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq. Sadiq said the visit marks a new era in parliamentary relations between the two countries.

He added that it is the first visit to Pakistan by a Bangladeshi speaker. The delegation's arrival is seen as an important step in strengthening ties between the parliaments of Pakistan and Bangladesh. Further meetings between the two sides are expected during the visit.
