Actor Imran Khan Challenges 'Quit India' Notice in Bombay High Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Imran Khan has moved the Bombay High Court against the 'Bharat Chhodo' (Quit India) notice issued to him by the Foreigners Regional Registration Office. The notice followed his participation in a protest organised by the Cockroach Party (CJP) in Mumbai on October 10, demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Police alleged that the actor violated the Foreigners Act because he is not an Indian citizen.

The Mumbai FRRO then asked him to leave the country by October 28. He was detained on October 10 during the CJP's Jantar Mantar protest in Mumbai. After the detention, the actor shared a photo from inside a police van on Instagram and wrote, 'obviously, I am going for a drive.' His girlfriend Lekha Washington shared a story with the words 'romance ho to aisa.'
