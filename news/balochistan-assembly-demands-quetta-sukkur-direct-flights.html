Balochistan Assembly demands Quetta-Sukkur direct flights

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Balochistan Assembly on Friday unanimously adopted three resolutions calling for the immediate start of direct flights between Quetta and Sukkur, a fair resolution of the long-pending cases of provincial officers serving on deputation in the Federal Secretariat, and the restoration of all closed private television channel bureaus in Quetta along with an end to forced layoffs of media workers. The session began 50 minutes late under Panel of Chairmen member Dr Ashok Kumar and deferred three other resolutions owing to the absence of their movers, relating to Balochistan's share in the United Nations Climate Justice Fund, a province-wide ban on sheesha, e-cigarettes, vapes, Sting and other harmful beverages, and enforcement of an Islamic system in the country. Moving a joint resolution, MPA Farah Azeem Shah along with Deputy Speaker Ghazala Gola and MPAs Safia Bibi, Umm-e-Kulsum and Salma Bibi highlighted hardships faced by patients, elderly citizens, students, government employees and traders due to the absence of direct air links, and the house recommended the provincial government approach the federal government to launch Quetta-Sukkur and Sukkur-Quetta flights, with BNP-A's Mir Asadullah Baloch also urging consideration of flights from Panjgur.

Awami National Party's Engineer Zmarak Khan Achakzai presented a resolution concerning 11 Section Officers of Grades 17 to 19 from Balochistan serving on deputation in federal ministries and divisions since 2008, noting successive prime ministers had approved their induction into the Office Management Group in 2008 and 2013 and a former chief minister had taken up the matter in 2015 without a final decision. The house unanimously urged the provincial government to seek a review of recent repatriation orders and an early final decision so Balochistan's administrative balance and the officers' experience could be preserved. Jamaat-e-Islami's Maulana Hidayatur Rehman Baloch, joined by Leader of the Opposition Mir Younus Aziz Zehri and several other members, moved a resolution on the media crisis, noting that 11 national television channel bureaus had been closed in Quetta in recent years.
