Was Rabia Shot Accidentally? Probe Continues as Polygraph Test Under Way

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rawalpindi CPO Hasan Mushtaq Sukhera says the probe has established that suspect Ahsan fired the fatal shot that killed anchorperson Rabia. However, investigators are still determining whether the firing was accidental or deliberate. Ahsan claims the gun went off accidentally.

A professional investigation team is probing the case, and evidence has gone to the Punjab Forensic Science Agency. Ahsan's polygraph test is under way. Rabia was shot on November 8 in Gulshanabad Sector 2, and her husband Ahsan was arrested after a murder case was registered.
