Two Killed, Two Hurt as Drone Downed Over Aramco Facility in Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's official news agency, citing an energy ministry official, reported that a drone was intercepted over the Shaybah oil field in the kingdom's east as it attempted to target a facility belonging to Saudi Aramco. The incident resulted in a fire in a vehicle belonging to a contractor working at the field, outside the facility's perimeter, without affecting operational activities, the agency said. The attempted attack killed two residents, identified as one Filipino and one Nepalese national, and injured two residents, one Pakistani and one Indian, according to the report.

The energy ministry confirmed the casualties in a separate statement. The interception marks the latest in a series of attempted strikes on Saudi energy infrastructure amid sharply escalating regional tensions.
