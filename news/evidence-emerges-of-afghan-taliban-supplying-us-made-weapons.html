Evidence Emerges of Afghan Taliban Supplying US-Made Weapons to Terrorists

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Evidence has come to light that the Afghan Taliban regime has been supplying American-manufactured weapons to Fitna al-Hindustan and Fitna al-Khawarij. Security forces in Balochistan recovered US-made arms from terrorists of the Indian proxy Fitna al-Hindustan killed in operations, including M16 and M4 rifles, aiming lasers and night-vision devices. A letter attributed to Taliban leader Mullah Hibatullah, directing the Taliban regime to supply weapons to Fitna al-Hindustan, is also cited as clear proof of the nexus between the two.

According to a secret report, the Indian intelligence agency RAW paid 7.8 million dollars for sabotage and terrorism in Balochistan. A retired Indian colonel has already acknowledged the Indian and Israeli role, through Afghan soil, in terrorist attacks in Pakistan via Fitna al-Hindustan and Fitna al-Khawarij. The American journal Associated Press has reported an intelligence disclosure about a meeting of Indian and Afghan intelligence officials, hosted by Afghan governor Mullah Shireen, aimed at terrorism in Pakistan.

According to the US watchdog SIGAR, 7.1 billion dollars' worth of American weaponry left in Afghanistan is being used by terrorist organisations under the patronage of the Afghan regime. The United Nations and international journals have also exposed the grave aspect of Taliban-supplied American weapons reaching terrorist groups.
