NATO Believes Russia Is Ready to Launch Nuclear-Armed Satellite

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

NATO members' intelligence services believe Russia is ready to launch a nuclear-armed satellite, German media have reported. Western services, including Germany's BND and armed forces, exchanged intelligence showing the satellite, named Sputnik-S, was assembled several years ago and is stationed in the Moscow region. The report was based on the intelligence exchanged among Western services.

The Kremlin denied the reports, saying they should not be taken seriously. The claims have raised concern among NATO members about space-based weapons. No launch date was given in the report.
