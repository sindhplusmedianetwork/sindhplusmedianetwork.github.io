Speeding car kills two girls in Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two girls were killed after being hit by a speeding car in Rawalpindi. The incident took place on Murree Road, where the girls were crossing the road. The speeding car ran over both of them, killing one girl on the spot.

The second girl was taken to hospital, where she later died. CCTV footage of the incident has emerged, showing the car striking the two girls as they crossed the road.
