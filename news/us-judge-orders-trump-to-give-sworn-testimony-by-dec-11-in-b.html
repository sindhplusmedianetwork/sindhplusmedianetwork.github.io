US Judge Orders Trump to Give Sworn Testimony by Dec 11 in BBC Defamation Suit

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A United States judge has directed President Donald Trump to record his sworn statement by December 11 in the defamation case he filed against the BBC. The court warned that Trump's $10 billion lawsuit could be dismissed if he fails to comply with the order. The federal magistrate judge also instructed Trump's lawyers to provide the required information and documents.

Trump filed the case in December 2025, claiming that a documentary broadcast before the 2024 election presented his January 6, 2021 speech in a misleading way. The broadcaster has not publicly commented on the latest court order. The case is one of several legal battles involving the US president and international media.
