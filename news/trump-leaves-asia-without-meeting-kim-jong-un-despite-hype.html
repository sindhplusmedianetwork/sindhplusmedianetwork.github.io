Trump Leaves Asia Without Meeting Kim Jong Un Despite Hype

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump left Asia late on Thursday without meeting North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, despite speculation that the two might meet during his trip. Trump departed after a trilateral meeting with the presidents of China and Russia, while Kim was shown during the week at a ski resort, the sacred Mount Paektu and a naval university, where he stressed the need to bolster maritime defence. Trump and Kim met three times during Trump's first term, but their 2019 Hanoi summit collapsed without a deal.

Trump had floated the prospect of another meeting and told a reporter while flying back that the relationship was there but the two did not meet because there were too many meetings, adding that they would meet at some point. Experts quoted in the report said concrete contacts appear unlikely, noting that Kim has concluded the United States is untrustworthy and that a proper summit would require envoys and preparatory work. Analysts also said Pyongyang is unlikely to respond unless Washington recognises it as a nuclear state, while the United States continues to demand denuclearisation.

Closer ties with Beijing and Moscow have also reduced Kim's incentive to engage, the experts said, though Kim's failure to explicitly reject a meeting leaves the door open.
