Mureed Abbas Case Fugitive Arrested on Return From Dubai After Six Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A suspect who had been a fugitive for six years in the murder case of media anchor Mureed Abbas has been arrested on his return from Dubai. Adil Zaman was taken into custody at Peshawar airport after arriving from Dubai and was later transferred to Karachi and handed over to Darakhshan police. An additional district and sessions judge in Karachi's South district sent the suspect to jail on judicial remand until November 9 and sought a supplementary challan from the investigating officer.

Mureed Abbas and Khizar Hayat were killed on July 9, 2019, in Karachi's Defence Housing Authority area of Khayaban-e-Bukhari. The main accused in the case, Atif Zaman, has already been sentenced to death.
