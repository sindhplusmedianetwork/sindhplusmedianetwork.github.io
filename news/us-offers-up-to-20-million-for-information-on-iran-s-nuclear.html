US Offers Up to $20 Million for Information on Iran's Nuclear Programme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States has offered rewards of up to $20 million for credible information on Iran's nuclear programme. The offer was made under the State Department's Rewards for Justice programme. It was announced by US Central Command on X on November 6.

The reward covers nuclear sites and their secret locations, as well as uranium enrichment equipment and technology. It also covers the identities of scientists and personnel linked to the programme. Relocation will be offered to informants if needed, according to the announcement.
