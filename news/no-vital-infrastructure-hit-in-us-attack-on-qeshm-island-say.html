No vital infrastructure hit in US attack on Qeshm island, says Iranian official

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

No vital infrastructure or services were affected by a US attack on Iran's Qeshm island late on Sunday, a provincial official told the semi-official Fars news agency on Monday. The official said the targeted sites were away from residential and commercial areas. Iranian state media had reported explosions late on Sunday in Qeshm, near the Strait of Hormuz.

Separately, an unnamed Revolutionary Guards Navy official told Iranian state media that navigational aid systems in the south of the Strait of Hormuz were destroyed and that no unauthorised vessels had transited the route overnight. Meanwhile, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Sunday that a diplomatic agreement with Iran may not be possible, describing Tehran's demands as unrealistic and the negotiations as not close to a breakthrough. Rubio was speaking before leaving for Paris to attend a meeting of G7 foreign ministers, where the Iran conflict is among the issues to be discussed.

The US and Israel launched a military offensive against Iran on February 28, and a ceasefire was reached in April, followed by a memorandum in June aimed at a peace agreement, but the talks have not produced a final settlement.
