Pakistani-Origin Man Dies Four Days After Street Attack in Halifax

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 62-year-old man of Pakistani origin has died in hospital four days after a street attack in Halifax, Britain. Haji Asghar Ali was seriously injured in the incident on Gibbet Street and was taken to hospital in a serious condition. According to West Yorkshire Police, officers arrested a 35-year-old man in connection with the case.

The man has been charged with offences including intentionally causing grievous bodily harm, racially or religiously aggravated assault on a police officer, threats to damage property, harassment and drug possession. He is due to appear at Bradford Crown Court on 17 December.
