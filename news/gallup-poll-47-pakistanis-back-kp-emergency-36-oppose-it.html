Gallup Poll: 47% Pakistanis Back KP Emergency, 36% Oppose It

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Forty-seven per cent of Pakistanis have supported the federal government's imposition of emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa while 36 per cent opposed it, according to a new Gallup Pakistan survey. Within Khyber Pakhtunkhwa itself, public opinion on the matter was split, with 40 per cent supporting the federal government's decision and 42 per cent opposing it. The survey also found that 36 per cent rejected the justification of the provincial government's non-cooperation in the war against terrorism, while 29 per cent appeared satisfied with it.

Asked whether the decision would improve the security situation in the province, 38 per cent were hopeful while 30 per cent feared further deterioration. Forty-one per cent of respondents considered the imposition of governor's rule and emergency to be constitutionally correct as well. Support for the emergency was highest in the Hazara division at 48 per cent, while opposition was strongest in South Khyber Pakhtunkhwa at 53 per cent.
