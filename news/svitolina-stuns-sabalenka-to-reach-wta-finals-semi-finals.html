Svitolina stuns Sabalenka to reach WTA Finals semi-finals

WhatsApp Facebook X

Ukrainian veteran Elina Svitolina has beaten world number two Aryna Sabalenka to reach the semi-finals of the WTA Finals. Svitolina won the three-set match 6-3, 3-6, 6-1 on Friday in a rollercoaster contest. She was broken at the start of the deciding set but then won six straight games to seal the victory.

It was only her second career win in eight meetings with the Belarusian. Svitolina will now face Wimbledon champion Linda Noskova in the semi-finals. In the other semi-final, French Open champion Mirra Andreeva takes on Jessica Pegula.
