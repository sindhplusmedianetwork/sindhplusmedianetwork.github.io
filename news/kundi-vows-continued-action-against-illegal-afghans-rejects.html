Kundi vows continued action against illegal Afghans, rejects encroachment pressure

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PESHAWAR: Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi on Monday declared that the provincial government's policy on Afghan nationals residing illegally in the province was fully aligned with the federal stance, and action against them would continue, though Afghans entering on valid visas would be welcomed. Speaking to the media in Peshawar, he said no country permits foreigners to stay without legal status. On the anti-encroachment campaign, he was categorical that the removal of illegal structures would continue and no pressure of any kind would be accepted.

On load-shedding, he said he would take up the matter with the relevant authorities, reminded the public that electricity must be paid for, and revealed that work was under way to introduce transformer-level load management instead of feeder-level cuts, allowing more precise and equitable power cuts. He welcomed the federal government's Rs160 billion development package for K-P, saying 70 per cent would be spent in historically deprived areas. On corruption allegations, he said the law applied equally to everyone, including Chief Minister Sohail Afridi, if credible charges existed.
