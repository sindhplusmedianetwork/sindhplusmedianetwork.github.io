Not Making Global Laws on Artificial Intelligence Is a Huge Risk: Canadian PM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney, expressing concern over the growing impacts of artificial intelligence, has warned that the absence of common global laws and an oversight system for this technology is a huge risk for the world. According to international media reports, Mark Carney, at a press conference on the occasion of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in the Chinese city of Shenzhen, said the lack of global oversight regarding artificial intelligence is a huge risk. He said this risk is not limited to economic loss but also includes concerns such as the possible use of biological weapons, harming democratic systems and affecting human dignity.

The Canadian prime minister said people running the world's big AI laboratories are themselves warning about the risks associated with their own industry, which is an extraordinary thing. He said he also discussed forming a global oversight system for artificial intelligence in his meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping. Mark Carney said there should at least be a system under global cooperation through which dangerous AI-related incidents can be monitored and different countries can share their experiences and ways of dealing with them.

He said the benefits of AI cannot be denied, but effective measures are also necessary to deal with risks alongside its rapid development, and no single country alone will be enough to confront these risks.
