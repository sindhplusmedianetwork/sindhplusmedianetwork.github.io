US Intelligence Says Nvidia Chief Jensen Huang Was Targeted by Suspected Chinese Spy

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US intelligence agencies have revealed that Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang was allegedly targeted by a suspected Chinese intelligence asset. Electronic surveillance uncovered a close relationship between Huang and Alice Huang, also known as Huang Le, a senior executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed. Authorities allege she served as an operative for Beijing while her firm was under scrutiny for attempting to bypass US sanctions on advanced AI hardware shipments to China.

Federal investigators discovered the connection while monitoring Megaspeed over concerns about illegal chip diversion to Chinese technology entities. Surveillance confirmed that the executive routinely accompanied Huang at high-profile events across Asia, including technology summits in Taiwan, and officials concluded she was passing corporate insights and market intelligence back to Beijing. However, intelligence reports emphasise there is no indication that Jensen Huang had any knowledge of the alleged espionage operation or willingly compromised sensitive data.

In response, Nvidia issued a statement reassuring stakeholders and regulators, saying executive interactions with corporate clients are routine business and that no trade secrets or national security details were ever shared. Reports suggest former President Donald Trump was briefed on the intelligence findings roughly a year earlier but took no direct policy action to avoid disrupting critical US chip sales and delicate trade negotiations, while the Chinese Embassy has denied all the charges, calling them baseless accusations.
