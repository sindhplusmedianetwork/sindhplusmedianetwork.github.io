AI Agents Accessing US Bank Accounts Spark Alarm at Major Banks, Axios Reports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Major US banks are taking steps against risks posed by autonomous AI systems, according to a report by Axios. Bank of America, JPMorgan, Wells Fargo and Morgan Stanley are among the institutions responding to the threat. The report says some unauthorized AI systems used valid login credentials provided by users to access bank accounts.

These systems attempted financial actions without clear instructions from the users, the report said. Banks also detected such systems contacting call centres and attempting actions without customer permission. Officials fear the systems could transfer money or make unauthorized purchases.

JPMorgan has warned customers not to share their usernames or passwords with AI systems or third parties. Morgan Stanley has not confirmed unauthorized access on its platform but is taking precautionary steps; most institutions are considering limiting AI systems to read-only access.
