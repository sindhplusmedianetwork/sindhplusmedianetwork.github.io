Illegal Trade Causing Trillions in Losses, Says Information Minister Tarar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Information Minister Attaullah Tarar has said illegal trade is causing trillions of rupees in losses to the national economy. Speaking at a recent event, he said many large manufacturers collect sales tax from consumers but under-invoice and hide their production capacity to evade taxes, creating a parallel economy that harms legitimate businesses and limits fiscal space. He cited the sugar sector as an example, saying cameras were installed on every production unit and QR codes placed on sugar bags, and with the prime minister's intervention an additional Rs60 billion in sales tax was collected in one year, with the monitoring system now being expanded to cement, textiles and beverages.

Tarar said the formal tobacco sector pays Rs150 to 200 billion in taxes every year, but the undocumented sector, including hidden factories that grow, manufacture and sell cigarettes, has now been brought into the tax net and operations have been conducted against it. According to industry circles, undocumented tobacco alone causes an annual revenue loss of around Rs300 billion. He said this illegal trade is present across cement, textiles, sugar, beverages and tobacco sectors.
