Rule of law and equal opportunities must be ensured in the country: Ishaq Dar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar said Pakistan must ensure the rule of law and equal opportunities for all citizens. He was paying tribute to Allama Iqbal on the poet-philosopher's birth anniversary. Dar said Iqbal gave the Muslims of the subcontinent awareness of their identity, their rights and a collective future through the Allahabad address and his poetry.

He said Iqbal's philosophy of selfhood teaches people to believe in their own abilities. Dar stressed that the youth must be given quality education, skills and opportunities. He said the nation should carry forward Iqbal's message by empowering its young people.
