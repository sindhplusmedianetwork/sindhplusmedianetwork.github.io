Netanyahu Alliance Wins 53 Seats in Israel as Official Results Handed Over

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The chairman of Israel's Central Elections Committee, Justice Noam Solberg, has handed the official results of the parliamentary elections to President Isaac Herzog. The Netanyahu alliance won 53 seats, opposition parties secured 67, and Arab parties won 19 in the October 27 vote. President Herzog condemned racist statements directed at the Arab community in the wake of the results.

He said allegations of vote rigging should be fully investigated. The outcome sets the stage for difficult coalition talks. Opposition demands for Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's resignation have continued to grow.
