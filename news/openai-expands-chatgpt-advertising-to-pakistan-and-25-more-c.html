OpenAI Expands ChatGPT Advertising to Pakistan and 25 More Countries

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

OpenAI announced on Wednesday that it is expanding advertising on ChatGPT to Pakistan and 25 additional countries. The move brings the total number of markets where ChatGPT ads are available to 89, as the artificial intelligence company seeks to increase revenue ahead of a possible initial public offering. The latest expansion covers 14 more Latin American countries alongside markets across Europe, the Middle East, Africa and Asia, including Nigeria, South Africa, Serbia, Ukraine, Cambodia and Bangladesh.

Advertisers will be able to purchase placements through technology partners or through a self-service Ads Manager. The company said advertisements will appear only for users on its Free and Go plans. Subscribers on Plus, Pro and Enterprise plans will continue to see an ad-free experience.
