Senate Approves Bill Letting Ex-Lawmakers Keep Blue Passports

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Senate on Monday passed a bill allowing former lawmakers to retain blue passports even if they did not complete a full term in parliament. The bill, moved by Senator Abdul Qadir, inserts a new Section 3-A into the Passports Act of 1974 to formalise the entitlement. Supporters argued the measure recognises the service of elected representatives, while critics questioned the need for special privileges for former legislators.

A companion bill that would have extended blue passports to the dependent children of former MPs was withdrawn after facing opposition from the treasury benches. The bill will now move forward in the legislative process as the government looks to settle the long-debated question of official travel documents for ex-lawmakers.
