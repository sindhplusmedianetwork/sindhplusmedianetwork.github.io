Sindh Approves Pricing Overhaul for Thar Coal Sales to Wider Market

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has approved a revised pricing mechanism for Thar coal sales to buyers other than independent power producers. The move is aimed at expanding the market for the indigenous fuel. The decisions were taken at the 31st meeting of the Thar Coal and Energy Board.

The meeting also endorsed Rs9.66 billion in verified financial claims related to duties, taxes and workers' profit participation. The revised framework sets a transparent mechanism for non-IPP buyers. It also ensures coal-sale costs are not passed on through IPP tariffs.

The arrangement is meant to protect electricity consumers.
