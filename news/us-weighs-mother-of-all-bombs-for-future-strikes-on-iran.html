US Weighs 'Mother of All Bombs' for Future Strikes on Iran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States is weighing the use of the 21,600-pound GBU-43/B 'Mother of All Bombs' in future strikes on Iran, CNN reported. The plans could see the massive ordnance used against Iranian energy and nuclear sites, including the Pickaxe Mountain facility, if President Donald Trump orders a resumption of strikes. Trump told Newsmax that the MOAB was not a nuclear weapon and ruled out both nuclear weapons and ground troops in any Iran operation.

Analysts have raised doubts about whether the bomb can penetrate deeply buried nuclear sites fortified by layers of granite. CNN reported the military was preparing for the possibility of additional strikes around the November midterm elections. The Iran war, which began in February 2026, has continued despite earlier US strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities.
