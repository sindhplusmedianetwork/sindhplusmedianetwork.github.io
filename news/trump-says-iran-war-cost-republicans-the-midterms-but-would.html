Trump Says Iran War Cost Republicans the Midterms — but 'Would Do It Again'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump has acknowledged that the war with Iran was the main reason his Republican party suffered heavy losses in the midterm elections. Speaking to reporters, he said the conflict 'drove the gasoline up a little bit short term,' hurting the party at the polls. Despite the defeat, Trump insisted he would do it again 'if he had to,' arguing that Iran cannot be allowed to obtain a nuclear weapon.

Vice President JD Vance had earlier urged fellow Republicans to be 'honest' that the unpopular war was the reason they lost. Washington has meanwhile issued fresh Iran-related sanctions, while global oil prices fell below $100 a barrel. Democrats swept the midterms, capturing both the Senate and the House in what was described as a nationwide rejection of Trump's policies.
