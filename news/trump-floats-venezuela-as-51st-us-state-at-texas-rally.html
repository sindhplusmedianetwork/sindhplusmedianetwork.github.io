Trump Floats Venezuela as 51st US State at Texas Rally

US President Donald Trump has suggested that Venezuela could eventually join the Union as the 51st US state, speaking at a midterm election rally in Corpus Christi, Texas. Pointing to a tanker carrying one million barrels of Venezuelan crude oil, Trump told supporters that deeper energy ties between the two nations could reshape their political landscape. He proposed that instead of electing a national president, the South American country could one day elect a governor.

Trump framed the cargo as a major geopolitical win, saying recent agreements had given the United States greater influence over global energy supplies. He confirmed the incoming shipment of heavy crude would be refined directly in Texas refineries at no extra cost to American taxpayers. The president also highlighted improving bilateral relations following significant political shifts in Caracas earlier this year.

The remarks come as Republicans face pressure over rising domestic fuel prices ahead of the midterm elections. International observers note that Venezuelan officials have consistently rejected proposals compromising national sovereignty.
