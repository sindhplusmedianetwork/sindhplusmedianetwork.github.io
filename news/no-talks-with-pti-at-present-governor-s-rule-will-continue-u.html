No talks with PTI at present, governor's rule will continue until objectives met: Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's adviser on political affairs Rana Sanaullah has said no formal negotiations are under way with PTI at present, though backdoor contacts continue. Speaking on Geo News, he said governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa will continue until its objectives are achieved. He said terrorism fuelled with help from India and Afghanistan is spreading in the province, and stopping it is the priority.

Sanaullah added that everyone will be taken on board as the government works to restore order. He said Maulana Fazlur Rehman's disagreement over governor's rule is a difference of opinion that should stay within democratic norms. The adviser also said allegations should not be made without evidence, responding to remarks by Chaudhry Salik, who said he has no evidence.

His comments come as the political situation in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa remains tense following the imposition of governor's rule.
