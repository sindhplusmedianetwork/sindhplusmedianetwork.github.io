PSX Briefly Crosses 180,000 as Profit-Taking Eases Index Lower

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange witnessed a range-bound session on Wednesday as investors booked profits after the previous day's sharp rally, with the benchmark KSE-100 index briefly crossing 180,000 points before closing 251.44 points lower at 179,083.80. The stock market saw a day of consolidation after a strong earlier advance, with the KSE-100 index touching the intra-day high near 180,800 before easing, according to Arif Habib Limited. Market breadth was mildly positive, as 52 shares advanced against 47 decliners.

The index had risen sharply in the previous session, and investors used the strength to book profits during Wednesday's trading. Trading remained range-bound through the day as the market consolidated near the 180,000 level.
