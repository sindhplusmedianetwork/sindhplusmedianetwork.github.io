Arrest of former Afghan police chief and alleged confession video: who is Allah Dad Fidayee?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Media outlets regarded as close to the Taliban's interim government in Afghanistan have broadcast a video of former police chief Allah Dad Fidayee. The video is said to be based on his alleged confession statement. Fidayee was a well-known police chief under the government of former president Ashraf Ghani.

He served as police commander in Maidan Wardak, Baghlan, Nimroz and Laghman provinces. Taliban-linked media say he was arrested some time ago. The circumstances of the video and his detention remain disputed.
