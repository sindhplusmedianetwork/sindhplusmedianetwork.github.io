Dubai closes E611 lanes and airport tunnel for six days for autonomous-taxi trials

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dubai will close three lanes of the key Emirates Road (E611) and the Al Maktoum International Airport tunnel in both directions for six days for autonomous-taxi trials. According to Khaleej Times, Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority announced the traffic closures on Sunday, October 25. The three lanes on Emirates Road in front of Al Maktoum International Airport will remain closed, and the airport tunnel will be fully closed to traffic in both directions.

The closures will apply from October 26 to 31 and will affect traffic daily from 9am to 4pm. The authority advised drivers to plan their routes before travelling and to follow the guide signs on the roads to keep traffic flowing. The closures are part of trials of driverless taxis as the United Arab Emirates prepares to expand the use of vehicles that run without a driver.

A senior UAE official said this month that the country plans to operate more than 10,000 autonomous taxis and delivery vans next year, and that orders for the vehicles have already been placed. Commercial autonomous-taxi services have already begun in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, and Abu Dhabi Mobility says Abu Dhabi has become the first city in the Middle East and North Africa where fully driverless Level 4 commercial services have started.
