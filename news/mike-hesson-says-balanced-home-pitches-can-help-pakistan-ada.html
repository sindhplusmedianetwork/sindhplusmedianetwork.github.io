Mike Hesson says balanced home pitches can help Pakistan adapt to overseas conditions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan head coach Mike Hesson has defended the team's shift toward more balanced, grass-covered home pitches, saying it will prepare players for conditions around the world. He was speaking after day two of the second Test against Sri Lanka. Hesson said Pakistan wanted to move away from turning tracks where matches could become a matter of luck.

He said balanced pitches reward good cricket rather than letting the surface decide the result. He said playing on such pitches at home will help the players adapt when they tour abroad, where conditions are very different.
