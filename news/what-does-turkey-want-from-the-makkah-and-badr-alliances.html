What Does Turkey Want From the 'Makkah' and 'Badr' Alliances?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkey's recent foreign and security policy has seen a notable shift with the establishment of alliances and cooperation frameworks with key Middle Eastern countries. The Makkah joint defence agreement and the Badr defence cooperation accord are the most prominent examples of this trend. Ankara describes its approach as 'regional ownership' of security matters.

Analysts are examining what Turkey actually seeks to gain from these partnerships — whether deterrence, diplomatic leverage or a larger role in regional security architecture. The agreements come amid heightened tensions in the Gulf and the wider Middle East. Observers say the coming months will test how these paper alliances translate into practical cooperation.
