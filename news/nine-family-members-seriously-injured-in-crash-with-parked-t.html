Nine Family Members Seriously Injured in Crash With Parked Truck Near Fatehpur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nine members of a family, including women and children, were seriously injured when their car rammed into the rear of a parked truck near 217 TDA, Fatehpur, on Friday. The family was returning from a wedding in Mankera at the time of the crash, according to Rescue 1122. The injured are residents of Chak No.

341 TDA, Chowk Azam. Rescue teams reached the scene and shifted the injured to Tehsil Headquarters Hospital for treatment. Four of the injured were reported to be in critical condition at the hospital.

Rescue officials said the car struck the parked truck from behind, causing serious injuries to the family members travelling in it.
