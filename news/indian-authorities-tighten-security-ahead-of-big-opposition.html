Indian authorities tighten security ahead of big opposition march in Delhi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indian authorities have tightened security ahead of a big opposition march in Delhi on Sunday. Security personnel carrying batons and shields, accompanied by riot control vehicles, surrounded the site of the planned protest, which is demanding the resignation of the country's election chief over a controversial revision of voter lists. Authorities warned of traffic diversions ahead of the protest march and rally, which is to be led by Congress leader Rahul Gandhi.

The opposition expects a large turnout as it accuses the election commission of manipulating the voter rolls.
