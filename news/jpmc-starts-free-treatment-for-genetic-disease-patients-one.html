JPMC Starts Free Treatment for Genetic Disease Patients, One Syrup Costs Over Rs1.3 Million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre (JPMC) in Karachi has started free treatment for children and adults suffering from spinal muscular atrophy (SMA), a genetic disease, with the support of the Sindh health department. Under the Anmol programme, all 25 registered patients have been given the first bottle of risdiplam syrup, which costs more than Rs1.3 million per bottle. Under the programme, each patient will receive 24 bottles a year, which means 96 bottles free of cost over four years.

The hospital administration said the treatment was beyond the reach of ordinary families because of the medicine's high price. With the support of Roche Pakistan, the purchase agreement provides three more bottles free with every bottle bought, which will help ensure a steady supply of the medicine. SMA is a hereditary genetic disease that affects the nerve cells controlling the muscles, according to medical experts.
