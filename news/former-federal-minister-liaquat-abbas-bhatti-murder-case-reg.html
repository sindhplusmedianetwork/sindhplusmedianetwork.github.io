Former federal minister Liaquat Abbas Bhatti murder: case registered against 11 including guards

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police registered a murder and robbery case against 11 people, including four nominated accused, in the murder of former federal minister Liaquat Abbas Bhatti in Hafizabad, with the victim's own guards said to be the alleged killers. The case was registered on the complaint of the victim's nephew and Punjab Assembly member Zameer-ul-Hassan Bhatti. The FIR alleges that the accused tied Liaquat Abbas Bhatti with ropes, strangled him, and fled after taking Rs2 million in cash, 50 tola of gold jewellery, an ATM card and valuable documents from the house.

The nominated accused include Zahid Ali and Farhan Ali, who are said to belong to Swabi district, and the accused allegedly also took the victim's Prado, Land Cruiser and Vigo Dala vehicles with them. DPO Hafizabad Fawad Ahmed said raids are being conducted at various places to arrest the accused, while investigations are continuing with the help of evidence found at the scene and modern technology. Liaquat Abbas Bhatti had served as a federal minister during the PML-Q government and was elected twice as a member of the National Assembly and once as a member of the Punjab Assembly.

His funeral prayers were to be offered at 3pm today in his ancestral village of Burjdara.
