'SME financing targeted at Rs2tr by 2028'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Finance and Revenue Senator Muhammad Aurangzeb has highlighted efforts to expand access to finance for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) while addressing the Ministerial Dialogue on SME Growth at the Global SME Finance Forum 2026 in Singapore. The minister underscored the vital role SMEs played in Pakistan's economy, accounting for 90 per cent of businesses, 40 per cent of GDP, around 20 per cent of exports and approximately 78 per cent of non-farm employment, observing that the sector could contribute more with improved access to finance and a better business environment. He outlined the target of increasing SME financing to Rs2 trillion by June 2028, almost double the current level, and emphasised the need to move beyond conventional, collateral-based lending towards technology-enabled, data-driven and inclusive financing solutions.

He highlighted the SME Connect Portal, launched by the State Bank of Pakistan on November 1, 2026, as an important step towards facilitating SME registration, improving access to financing, tracking applications and enhancing transparency in banks' processing times. He stressed the importance of accessible working capital and long-term financing, the role of the Small and Medium Enterprises Development Authority (SMEDA) in building business capacity, and the use of fintech solutions, digital payments, alternative credit assessment and digital lending to widen access, particularly for businesses lacking conventional collateral. The minister also visited the Singapore FinTech Festival, inaugurating Ignite's Pakistan Pavilion and interacting with companies including Tax Nerd Pakistan, Ping Up, Bagh-e, Fortanixor Technologies and Lendverse/Tawha Technologies, and visited the Ant International pavilion.
