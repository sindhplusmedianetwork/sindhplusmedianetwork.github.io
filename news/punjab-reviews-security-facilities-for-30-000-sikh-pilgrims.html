Punjab reviews security, facilities for 30,000 Sikh pilgrims expected for Guru Nanak anniversary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government on Wednesday reviewed security and facilitation arrangements for the 557th birth anniversary celebrations of Baba Guru Nanak Dev Ji. Around 30,000 Sikh pilgrims, including 3,000 from India, are expected to arrive between November 22 and December 1. A high-level meeting chaired by provincial ministers Khawaja Salman Rafique and Sardar Ramesh Singh Arora was told the pilgrims would visit Nankana Sahib, Farooqabad, Hasan Abdal, Kartarpur and Lahore.

Officials were directed to ensure foolproof security, transport, medical facilities and food for the visitors. The Federal Investigation Agency was asked to set up maximum immigration counters for the pilgrims. The arrangements are being made ahead of the anniversary celebrations later this month.
