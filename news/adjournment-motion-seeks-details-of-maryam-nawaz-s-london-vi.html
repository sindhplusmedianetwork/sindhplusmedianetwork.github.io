Adjournment Motion Seeks Details of Maryam Nawaz's London Visit Expenses

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An adjournment motion has been filed in the Punjab Assembly seeking details of the heavy expenses incurred during Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif's visit to London in September 2026. Opposition MPA Waqas Mahmood formally submitted the motion to the Assembly Secretariat, demanding that the full and transparent details of the official aircraft used for the visit, all travel expenses and the relevant amounts be presented in the House. The text of the motion says that on September 17, 2026, an amount of Rs27,194,004 was deposited towards travel expenses through a Bank of Punjab cheque, handed over through a man named Umar Abbas.

However, no transparent details were provided by the government or the relevant institutions about the account from which the money was deposited or its holder. The motion also questions whether the chief minister can afford such expensive personal travel on her officially declared income, and says the Punjab government has given contradictory and vague answers about the use, ownership and expenses of the official aircraft. It further claims that two foreign pilots have been hired to fly the aircraft.
