Lahore ranked world's most polluted city as AQI hits 448

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore has been ranked the world's most polluted city, with its Air Quality Index soaring to 448, according to air quality data reported on November 1. An AQI of 448 is rated as extremely hazardous to health. The provincial capital topped the global list of major cities measured for air pollution.

Karachi also figured in the ranking, placed third among the world's most polluted cities with an AQI of 173. The latest readings show pollution levels in Pakistan's two largest cities reaching dangerous highs. Air quality in Lahore has now crossed deep into the hazardous zone, underscoring the severity of the city's smog crisis.
