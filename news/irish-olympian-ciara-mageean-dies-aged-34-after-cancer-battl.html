Irish Olympian Ciara Mageean Dies Aged 34 After Cancer Battle

Former European 1500m champion and two-time Olympian Ciara Mageean has died at the age of 34 after a battle with cancer. The runner from Portaferry in Northern Ireland had been diagnosed with stage four bowel cancer in May 2025, according to the BBC. A statement posted on her social media on Sunday said she died at 6.30am surrounded by her loved ones, calling her a beloved daughter, sister, wife, friend and inspiration to so many.

Athletics Ireland said it was heartbroken, remembering her courage, kindness and incredible spirit. Mageean represented Ireland at the 2016 and 2020 Olympics and claimed the first major title of her career with 1500m gold at the 2024 European Championships in Rome, along with Commonwealth silver at Birmingham 2022. She had been forced to withdraw from the Paris 2024 Games with an Achilles injury, and only last month she married her long-term partner Thomas Moran in a small civil ceremony in Belfast.

Tributes have poured in for an athlete whose junior career brought 1500m silvers at the 2010 World Junior and 2011 European Junior Championships.
