AI Godfather Geoffrey Hinton Warns of Chernobyl-Scale Crisis

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read

Nobel laureate scientist Geoffrey Hinton has warned that artificial intelligence could cause a major crisis on the scale of Chernobyl this year or next, temporarily paralysing electricity or financial systems. In an interview with the British newspaper The Guardian, Hinton said he was not pessimistic about artificial intelligence, but believed humanity would have been better off not inventing the technology. He said a Chernobyl-like event linked to artificial intelligence could occur this year or next, forcing world leaders to take the risks of the technology seriously.

The scientist clarified that he meant world leaders other than United States President Donald Trump. He also criticised business figures Mark Zuckerberg, Elon Musk and Sam Altman, who are part of the race to develop artificial intelligence, saying they are aware of the possible dangers but financial gain matters more to them. Hinton, who laid the foundations of the current development of artificial intelligence, was formerly associated with Google but left the company so he could speak openly about the growing risks of the technology.

He has previously warned that if artificial intelligence systems start developing their own language and ways of thinking, the situation could go beyond human control, and he says humans still retain control over artificial intelligence and a major disaster can be prevented through proper measures and strong efforts.
