Cabinet committee approves board changes at NESPAK, HESCO and SEPCO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Cabinet Committee on State-Owned Enterprises on Tuesday approved proposals for the appointment of a chairman and independent director at National Engineering Services Pakistan (NESPAK), the reconstitution of the board of Hyderabad Electric Supply Company (HESCO) and the appointment of an independent director to the board of Sukkur Electric Power Company (SEPCO). The decisions were taken at a meeting chaired by Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb at the Finance Division, according to a statement from the Ministry of Finance. Science and Technology Minister Khalid Hussain Magsi attended the meeting along with federal secretaries and senior representatives of ministries, divisions and regulatory bodies.

The statement said the committee considered two agenda items and approved both proposals after deliberations. The finance minister emphasised that government representatives serving as ex-officio directors on these boards should be appropriately placed, competent and experienced, with the institutional understanding needed to represent their divisions. The appointments come amid the government's broader effort to improve governance and performance at state-owned enterprises, particularly power distribution companies, whose boards were mostly reconstituted in May 2025 while the process for HESCO and SEPCO was still being finalised.
