Ukraine and NATO pushed Russia to possibility of nuclear attack, says former Russian president

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Russian president Dmitry Medvedev has said Ukraine and NATO have pushed Russia to the possibility of a nuclear attack. Medvedev is deputy head of Russia's Security Council. He said President Putin considers all factors and possible outcomes in every decision.

He said Russia compares the damage and the possible response before any retaliatory action. The remarks were made amid the ongoing conflict between Russia and Ukraine.
