Kadeena Cox Wins Second Gold With Record Ride at Para Cycling Worlds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kadeena Cox has won her second gold medal of the Para-cycling Track World Championships in the Netherlands, adding a record-breaking sprint title to her collection. The 35-year-old broke a world record in qualifying, crossing the line in 11.409 seconds in the first round of the C4 sprint. She then led through the semi-finals before edging past Australia's Tara Neyland on the line in a close final to win her first individual sprint world title.

It followed her C4 1km victory on Friday, October 30, when the five-time Paralympic gold medallist set another world record of 1 minute 9.545 seconds to claim her seventh world title. Great Britain enjoyed a strong championships overall, with James Ball and Aaron Pope adding a second gold in the men's race and Sophie Unwin and Jenny Holl taking their sixth world title in the women's event. The British medal tally at the championships reached six golds, eight silvers and five bronze.
