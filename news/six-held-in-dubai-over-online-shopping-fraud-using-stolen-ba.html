Six Held in Dubai Over Online Shopping Fraud Using Stolen Bank Cards

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dubai authorities have arrested six suspects accused of online shopping fraud through fake shopping websites. The suspects allegedly stole victims' bank card information through the bogus sites, according to officials. They reportedly transferred the stolen money into digital wallets and also sold the stolen card details to others.

Victims' bank cards and cheque books were recovered from the arrested suspects. Among the victims was a woman whose savings of several years were looted in the fraud. The arrests were reported on Friday as authorities continue their crackdown on cyber-enabled financial crime.
