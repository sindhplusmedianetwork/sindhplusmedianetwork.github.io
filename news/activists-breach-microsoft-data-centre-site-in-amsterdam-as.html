Activists breach Microsoft data centre site in Amsterdam as protests spread in Europe

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Protesters broke into the construction site of a Microsoft data centre in Amsterdam on Friday at the start of two days of rallies. The rallies are part of a growing global backlash over the rapid build-out of computing power needed for artificial intelligence, with activist groups announcing actions in 16 countries. More than 100 activists gathered at the site, marching arm in arm before breaking in, climbing cranes, hanging balloons and dancing while police stood by.

Site owner Pure Data Centres said it was committed to engagement with local stakeholders. Microsoft said it respected people's right to express their feelings peacefully but did not condone any unlawful actions.
