Steel plant explosion in Turkiye kills 16, injures 13

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 16 people were killed and 13 injured in an explosion at the HABAS iron and steel plant in the Aliaga district of Izmir in western Turkiye. The blast happened during furnace replacement work at the plant. President Erdogan said judicial and administrative investigations had been launched into the incident.

According to initial reports, a ceiling crane collapsed, damaging a gas line. The damaged gas line triggered the explosion.
