Pakistan's Got Latent Ready for Release; First Episode on 28 November

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Pakistani version of the comedy show India's Got Latent, titled 'Pakistan's Got Latent', is ready for release, with its first episode due on YouTube on 28 November. The show was announced on 9 November. Its judges are Sharabeel Siddiqui, Usman Mazhar, Muzammil Mesia and Mathira.

Its promotional videos feature Feroze Khan, Humaima Malick, Adnan Shah Tipu, Shakeel Siddiqui, Rauf Lala and Indian actress Rakhi Sawant. With the format closely resembling the Indian show, comparisons have already begun on social media. Questions have also been raised about the show's originality ahead of its release.
