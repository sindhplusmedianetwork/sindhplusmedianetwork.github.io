PM Shehbaz urges grant-based climate finance for vulnerable nations at COP31

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Thursday urged world leaders at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkiye, to provide grant-based finance to countries most exposed to climate change, saying vulnerable nations needed timely help that did not deepen their debt. He called for public-private partnerships and a significant increase in resilience and adaptation funds, describing them as survival funds for struggling economies. The prime minister said clean energy sources accounted for nearly 55 per cent of Pakistan's energy needs, and the country aimed to cut its projected emissions by about 50 per cent by 2035.

Recalling the flash floods that struck Buner in August 2025, he told delegates of a young man who was two days away from his wedding when floodwaters swept through his village, turning a house preparing for celebrations into a house of mourning. He also recalled the 2022 floods, in which more than 1,700 people lost their lives and economic losses exceeded 30 billion dollars. Warning against the weaponisation of water, he said no nation should be allowed to put the lives and livelihoods of people downstream at risk, and water-sharing agreements must be honoured in letter and spirit.

Nearly 200 countries are attending the summit in Antalya, while the United States has stayed away from the talks for a second year running.
