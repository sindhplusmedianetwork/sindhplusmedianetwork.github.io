Only Child Born After 21 Years Among Victims of Peshawar School Tragedy

The Peshawar school roof collapse tragedy has left behind heartbreaking stories, among them that of ten-year-old Abdul Baseer. Baseer was the only child of his parents, born after 21 years of marriage, and the apple of his family's eye. Known as a loving and affectionate boy, he had gone to school like any other day, never to return home.

His death has shattered his parents and relatives, who are struggling to come to terms with the loss. Several of his school friends also lost their lives in the same incident. The tragedy has sent a wave of grief through the local community, where residents have expressed sorrow and demanded accountability for the incident.
