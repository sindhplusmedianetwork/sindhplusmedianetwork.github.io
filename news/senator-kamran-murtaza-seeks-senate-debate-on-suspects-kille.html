Senator Kamran Murtaza Seeks Senate Debate on Suspects Killed in Police Encounters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam (F) Senator Kamran Murtaza submitted a calling-attention notice in the Senate on October 22, seeking a debate on the killing of arrested suspects in police encounters. In the notice, Murtaza cited the Laiba Ejaz case, in which the main accused was killed in a police encounter in Islamabad, and the case of a 15-year-old orphan boy murdered in Multan, whose accused was also killed in a police encounter in Bahawalpur. He said the deaths of suspects in police custody during encounters were clear evidence of administrative weakness.

The notice also highlighted alleged corruption and mismanagement within the police force, as well as a lack of public trust in the department. Murtaza said Pakistan's police department faces deep structural, political and administrative problems that are weakening the rule of law and public trust. The notice called on the Senate to debate police reforms and the killing of suspects in police encounters.
