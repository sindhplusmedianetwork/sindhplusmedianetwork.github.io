97-year-old man dies while dancing at his birthday party as video goes viral

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

A 97-year-old man in California, named Vito, died moments after dancing at his birthday party, and a video of the moment has gone viral on social media. He had celebrated his 97th birthday last week with a Halloween theme, and his 24-year-old neighbour Baker Eden Millenov, who made the cake with red wine and chocolate, shared the video. In it, Vito is seen clapping, dancing and spending time with guests as the Bee Gees' song 'Stayin' Alive' plays in the background, before he suddenly collapsed shortly after the cake was cut.

His neighbour said he was not intoxicated and that it was an unexpected incident. The video has drawn wide attention online as people shared the clip of his final celebration.
