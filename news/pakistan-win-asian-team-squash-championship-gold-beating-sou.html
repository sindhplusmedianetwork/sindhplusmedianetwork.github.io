Pakistan win Asian Team Squash Championship gold, beating South Korea 2-0

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's men's squash team has won gold at the 23rd Asian Team Squash Championship 2026 in Islamabad, beating South Korea 2-0 in Saturday night's final. Noor Zaman and Muhammad Asim Khan won their matches to seal the victory for Pakistan. Federal Minister Rana Sanaullah, the prime minister's coordinator Mehreen Sajid and IPC Secretary Mohyuddin Ahmad Wani congratulated the team on the win.

Pakistan Sports Board officials also congratulated the team. Hong Kong China won the women's gold, beating Malaysia 2-1, while South Korea took the men's silver and Malaysia the women's silver.
