CDA Orders Removal of 'Unauthorised' Construction at JUI-F Chief's Islamabad Home

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

ISLAMABAD: The Directorate of Building Control (City) of the Capital Development Authority on Friday issued a warning notice to remove within 15 days a guards' room and 'unauthorised and illegal' construction at the residence of JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman in the federal capital's posh G-6 sector. The notice, affixed outside the residence and dated Oct 30, highlighted three violations under the Islamabad Capital Territory Building Control Regulations 2020: illegal construction in building setbacks, a guard post built on CDA land, and a generator on CDA land. It noted that according to records, the owner had submitted an affidavit on March 18, 2019 at the time of issuance of a no-objection certificate, stating setback violations would be removed within three months — but 'no compliance is made'.

The city manager warned that failure to remove the violations could lead to the sealing or demolishing of the structures and initiation of a case for cancellation of the allotment. After the notice was issued, Maulana Asjad Mahmood, the son of the JUI-F chief, claimed it was retaliation for pointing out alleged encroachments by a senior military official, calling the move underhanded tactics.
