Pregnant woman killed, husband injured as trailer hits motorcycle in Karachi's Liaquatabad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

In Karachi's Liaquatabad area, a speeding trailer crushed a motorcycle-riding couple near Liaquatabad No. 10, killing a pregnant woman and injuring her husband, Express News reported. According to details, the trailer crushed the woman riding the motorcycle with her husband; after the accident the trailer driver fled while enraged citizens pelted the trailer with stones, broke its windows and then set it on fire.

The trailer was loaded with rolls of cardboard, which burned to ashes in the fire. Police and rescue teams reached the spot as soon as they were informed, and the fire brigade controlled the blaze with one fire tender. Police shifted the deceased woman and the injured husband to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital with the help of a Chhipa ambulance, where the deceased was identified as Faiza and the injured husband Fahim was moved to Jinnah Hospital after first aid.

The affected couple's family rushed to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital, where emotional scenes were witnessed. The deceased woman's elder brother Mazhar told the media that Faiza was pregnant and had come to the Sindh Government Hospital for a checkup with her husband. After the checkup they were returning to their home in Ahsanabad when they met the accident a short distance from the hospital.

He added that the deceased had two children — a daughter and a son — and was the youngest of ten siblings, including seven brothers and three sisters. Witnesses said the driver abandoned the vehicle and fled immediately after the accident; police have taken the truck and the motorcycle into custody and started a search for the fleeing driver. According to statistics, the number of people killed in various traffic accidents in Karachi this year has crossed 745.
