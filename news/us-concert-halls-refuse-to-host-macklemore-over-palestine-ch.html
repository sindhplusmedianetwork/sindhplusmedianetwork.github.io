US Concert Halls Refuse to Host Macklemore Over Palestine Charity Shows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US rapper Macklemore says several major American concert venues have refused to host his charity concerts. The concerts were planned in support of the Palestinian people. Macklemore said venues including Madison Square Garden and Barclays Center initially offered him dates.

He said they declined once they learned the concerts were fundraisers for Palestine. The rapper said he would set up his own stages if needed. He added that he would keep raising funds for Palestinians.
