Ronaldo Handed 30-Day Suspension After Walking Out on Portugal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cristiano Ronaldo has been handed a 30-day suspension by the Portuguese football federation for leaving the national team camp, the minimum penalty under its regulations for such departures. The 41-year-old must also pay a €255 fine. He walked out on September 30 during the UEFA Nations League after accusing coach Jorge Jesus of breaking a promise over playing time.

Ronaldo apologised in October, saying he would accept any punishment imposed on him. The ban runs from the Denmark match, meaning he has already served 19 days and will complete it by the end of October. Portugal won all four of its Nations League matches in his absence, and the federation's disciplinary committee confirmed the decision this week.
