Olivia Rodrigo Releases First Christmas Song 'Take Me Home (4 Christmas)'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Olivia Rodrigo has released her first original Christmas song, 'take me home (4 christmas)', on the evening of Thursday, November 5, alongside a festive music video. The upbeat single, about wanting a serious commitment and spending the holidays with someone you love, was written by Rodrigo with Dan Nigro and Amy Allen and produced by Nigro. The music video stars actor Cynthia Nixon and comedian Ben Marshall, who play the mother and boyfriend in a family Christmas dinner scene with paper crowns, gingerbread houses and guitar-playing under strings of lights.

Rodrigo had announced the song on Monday, November 2, sharing its cover photo of herself in a Santa hat, and told fans she was excited about her first Christmas release. Physical copies on 7-inch vinyl and cassette include a B-side called 'miss this christmas'. The release follows her October remix of Chappell Roan's 'expectations', which reached No.

3 on the Billboard Hot 100, and arrives while she is on her Unraveled Tour, which runs through December 20.
