WTA takes blame after Pegula comments on Andreeva get livestreamed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The WTA has taken full responsibility after private comments by Jessica Pegula about Mirra Andreeva were livestreamed during a practice session. The WTA had streamed practice sessions on its YouTube channel this week, with microphones capturing a conversation between world number five Pegula and her coach, Mark Knowles. In remarks that later circulated on social media, the American described French Open champion Andreeva as a diva and said she needed to be humbled, and suggested coach Conchita Martinez encouraged the 19-year-old's behaviour.

The WTA said it took full responsibility for failing to inform players and their teams that the microphones, in place to give fans a livestream of the practice, were active on the courts. The governing body subsequently removed recordings of the practice sessions from YouTube, although clips of the exchange had already been widely shared online. A representative for Pegula said the American had absolutely no idea her conversation was being livestreamed and never expected a private discussion during practice to be made public.
