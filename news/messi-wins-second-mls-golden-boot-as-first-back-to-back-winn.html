Messi Wins Second MLS Golden Boot as First Back-to-Back Winner

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Inter Miami's Lionel Messi won his second consecutive MLS Golden Boot award on Saturday, becoming the first player to win the award in back-to-back seasons. Messi scored 26 goals this season, finishing ahead of Dallas forward Petar Musa, who was second on 22 goals. The Argentine did not score in Miami's final regular-season match, a 3-3 draw against Charlotte, but set up the first two goals from corners to help his side secure home-field advantage for the first two rounds of the playoffs.

Ian Fray, Luis Suarez and Rodrigo De Paul scored for Miami before Liel Abada's brace and Rodolfo Aloko's late strike completed Charlotte's comeback. Miami finished second in the Eastern Conference on 60 points, while runaway leaders Nashville broke the all-time MLS points record with 77 after beating Cincinnati 2-0. Messi's side will face Columbus in the first round of the MLS playoffs.
