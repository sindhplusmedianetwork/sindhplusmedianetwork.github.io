Suspect Held After Men in Fake Police Uniforms Rob Turkish Scholars and Bangladeshi Visitors in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi police say they have arrested a man accused of posing as a policeman to rob foreign visitors in the city. The suspect, identified as Muslim Shan, was traced after he stopped three Turkish academics of Gaziantep University near Saddar's Passport Office on Sunday, October 11, on the pretext of checking their travel documents. After searching them, he allegedly took $2,000 from their pockets and fled in a private car.

The three-member delegation was in Karachi to attend the International Summit of Islamic World University Chancellors at Jamia-tur-Rasheed's Al-Ghazali Campus. South DIG Syed Asad Raza said police traced the vehicle, identified those involved and recovered the looted amount. He said the suspect had used a car bearing a fake number plate and was wearing a fake police uniform.

According to the officer, the same man and his accomplices had intercepted Bangladeshi Tableeghi Jamaat visitors in Garden three days earlier, taking them to a spot where they snatched Tk500,000 and other valuables. The Bangladeshi visitors had travelled to Karachi to attend a gathering at a mosque in Garden when they were robbed.
