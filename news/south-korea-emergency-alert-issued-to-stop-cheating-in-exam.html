South Korea: Emergency Alert Issued to Stop Cheating in Exam, 22 Students Caught Red-Handed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

During South Korea's annual university entrance test, the Suneung, the government took a unique step to prevent cheating, and 22 students were caught red-handed cheating. According to foreign media reports, during the entrance test held yesterday, the South Korean government used for the first time the country's highest emergency alert system, which is generally used at the time of a disaster, to prevent cheating. In this test, counted among the world's most difficult examinations, this extraordinary step was taken to prevent cheating, especially in view of the increasing use of artificial intelligence (AI).

At about 8:30 in the morning, an emergency siren lasting ten and a half seconds was sounded at an extremely loud volume, after which two more unannounced alerts were issued at 2:21 pm and 2:57 pm. According to the media report, this strategy proved effective and during the examination, 22 students were caught from 9 cities and provinces across the country for having mobile phones in their possession. The caught students have been handed over to the examination administrators so that it can be determined whether they also had other electronic devices, including small hidden cameras, or not.

If cheating through such devices is confirmed, the Ministry of Education has decided on strict action, which also includes filing criminal complaints on charges of obstructing the performance of official duties through deception. It should be noted that in South Korea, on November 15, the candidate who obtained the highest marks in the tax accountant's national examination admitted that he had cheated with the help of a small hidden camera, after which the government declared the said step inevitable.
