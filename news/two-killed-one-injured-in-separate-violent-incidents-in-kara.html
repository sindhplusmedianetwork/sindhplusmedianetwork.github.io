Two killed, one injured in separate violent incidents in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people, including a woman, were killed and another person was injured in separate violent incidents in different parts of Karachi on Tuesday. According to Chhipa, 32-year-old Saima died in Safoora Goth's Ghazi Goth area near the G-17 bus stop after eating a poisonous substance at her home. Her body was shifted to hospital.

In a separate incident in Mahmoodabad's Junejo Town, a man was stabbed to death near the Khulafa-e-Rashideen Mosque. Another man was seriously injured in the same attack. The body and the injured man were shifted to Jinnah Hospital.
