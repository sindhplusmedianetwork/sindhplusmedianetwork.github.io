Court Sets November 26 for Prosecution's Final Arguments in Sabtain Khan Contracts Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An anti-corruption court in Lahore on Wednesday sought final arguments from the prosecution on November 26 after defence lawyers completed their arguments on acquittal applications filed by former Punjab Assembly speaker Sabtain Khan and other accused in a mining and minerals contracts reference. The court, presided over by Judge Muhammad Jamil, adjourned further proceedings until November 26. Sabtain Khan and the other accused appeared before the court and marked their attendance.

The co-accused include former Secretary Mines and Minerals Imtiaz Ahmed Cheema, former Chairman Planning and Development Department Salman Ghani and Chief Inspector of Mines Punjab Abdul Sattar. The reference was initially filed by the National Accountability Bureau in 2020 and was subsequently transferred from the accountability court to the anti-corruption court. In their acquittal applications, the accused maintained that there was no evidence to substantiate the allegations against them and sought their acquittal.

Sabtain Khan and the other accused are facing allegations of awarding mining and minerals contracts to their favoured individuals.
