Surveillance Drone Crashes Into CTD Headquarters in Lahore

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A surveillance drone struck the Counter Terrorism Department headquarters building in Lahore shortly after midnight and was seized by the department's security team. The drone came from the direction of Jalo Mor, hit the headquarters' outer wall and fell to the ground, spreading panic inside the building. Security staff found its cameras and battery lights still operational when they took it into custody.

The bomb disposal squad examined the device and later gave clearance. Police say unidentified suspects flew the drone toward the CTD headquarters with terrorist intent, and the CTD security team has submitted an application for legal action to the Harbanspura police station. An investigation into the incident is underway from all angles.

The report was attributed to Dunya News.
