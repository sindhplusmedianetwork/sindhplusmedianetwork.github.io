Five Palestinians, Including a Seven-Year-Old Girl, Killed in Gaza Strikes

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five Palestinians, including a seven-year-old girl, were killed in Israeli attacks across the Gaza Strip on Monday, according to medical sources and first responders. Three of the deaths were reported in the al-Mawasi area of Khan Younis in southern Gaza, where Nasser Hospital identified the dead as Hossam al-Zamli, Tamer Abu Sneima and Jouri al-Soufi, with Gaza's education ministry giving the girl's age as seven. Gaza's Civil Defence agency said a fourth person was killed in an airstrike on a tent housing displaced Palestinians near Khan Younis, and a fifth person was killed with three injured in Gaza City's Rimal neighbourhood.

Civil Defence spokesman Mahmoud Bassal told reporters that over the past 24 hours, 13 people had been killed and about 60 wounded by Israeli fire and airstrikes in Gaza. The Israeli military said it was unaware of any military activity in al-Mawasi on Monday, while also saying it had killed six Hamas and Islamic Jihad operatives in earlier strikes and targeted three operatives who emerged from a tunnel in Khan Younis. A security source in Gaza also said tanks and military vehicles advanced on Monday morning into the area west of the Yellow Line in the eastern part of Deir al-Balah, in central Gaza.
