Vasectomy cases in Sindh jump from 24 in 2021 to over 4,000 in 2025

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Male sterilisation, known as vasectomy, remains a little-discussed subject in Pakistan, but the trend is growing sharply in Sindh. According to the Sindh Population Welfare Department, vasectomy cases in the province stood at just 24 in 2021 and rose to more than 4,000 in 2025. The facility is available in all districts of Sindh, but around 80 per cent of the cases were reported from the seven districts of Karachi, while 16 per cent came from Larkana.

A senior official of the department, Dr Talib Lashari, said vasectomy is considered a safe and effective method of permanent family planning, meant to prevent further children once a family is complete. He said the procedure closes the male reproductive passage through a minor surgical procedure, after which pregnancy is not possible. The department says the rise is the result of sustained awareness campaigns, not of any pressure, and that growing awareness has convinced more men that sterilisation is not a harm but a guarantee of a better future.

The services are available at major government hospitals, district headquarters hospitals and tertiary care hospitals across the province.
