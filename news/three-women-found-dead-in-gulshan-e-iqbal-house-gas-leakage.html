Three women found dead in Gulshan-e-Iqbal house, gas leakage suspected

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three bodies have been found in a house in Block 1, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi. Police said all three bodies were women and that initial indications were that they died of suffocation caused by a gas leakage. The Sindh home minister has sought a report on the incident from the SSP East.

Police said the bodies were shifted for medico-legal formalities to confirm the cause of death. The incident came to light when the women were found unresponsive inside the house. Police said further investigation is under way to establish how the gas leaked.
