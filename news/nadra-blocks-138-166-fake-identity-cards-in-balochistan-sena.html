NADRA Blocks 138,166 Fake Identity Cards in Balochistan, Senate Told

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Senate was informed on Tuesday that the National Database and Registration Authority has blocked 138,166 identity cards in Balochistan. Minister of State for Interior Senator Talal Chaudhry, responding to questions in the upper house, said 8,875 of these cards were blocked in the Makran and Rakhshan divisions alone. He explained that NADRA blocks identity cards across the country when the holders are found ineligible or are discovered to have obtained them using forged documents.

Such cases are reviewed by boards at the regional level before a final decision, he said. The minister added that some foreign nationals had managed to obtain Pakistani CNICs by submitting false documents, while others were wrongly added to the family trees of Pakistani citizens. The government is continuing its campaign to identify and block fraudulently obtained identity cards, he said.
