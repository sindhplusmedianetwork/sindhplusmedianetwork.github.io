Rawalpindi tenants accused of extorting landlord's daughter with 'black magic' threats

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

RAWALPINDI: Tenants living in the RA Bazaar police jurisdiction allegedly extorted gold jewellery and millions of rupees in cash from their landlord's daughter by threatening her with black magic, police said on Thursday. Police have registered a case and initiated an investigation into the matter. The complainant, Sardar Hussain, told police he had rented out the upper portion of his house to a man named Nazir in January 2026, where Nazir lived with his son Ali Shan and daughter-in-law Iram.

Hussain stated that Ali Shan and his wife invited his daughter Aaliya Rubab to their portion, telling her they had found a spiritual healer who could resolve all her family's problems, provided she paid a fee. The woman was then allegedly made to hand over jewellery and cash through extortion and threats. Police said the accused are under investigation, though no arrests were immediately reported.
