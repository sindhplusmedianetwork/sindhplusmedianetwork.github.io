No Immediate Iranian Threat Needs Military Action, Says Israel's Likely PM Eisenkot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Israeli army chief Gadi Eisenkot said Iran remains Israel's most serious threat but there is no immediate danger requiring military action before a new government is formed. Eisenkot, who is seen as the country's expected next prime minister, spoke at a press briefing in Jerusalem. He said Israel should focus on about 440 kilograms of enriched uranium that has neither been destroyed nor removed from the country.

His remarks show differing views among Israel's political leadership on possible military action against Iran. Eisenkot's position contrasts with harder voices calling for earlier action. A new government is yet to be formed in Israel.
