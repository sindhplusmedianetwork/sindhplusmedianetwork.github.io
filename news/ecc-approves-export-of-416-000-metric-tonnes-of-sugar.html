ECC Approves Export of 416,000 Metric Tonnes of Sugar

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Economic Coordination Committee (ECC) of the Cabinet on Friday approved the export of 416,000 metric tonnes of sugar, the third such permission in four months, taking the total export quantity for this season to 724,000 metric tonnes. Minister of National Food Security Rana Tanveer Hussain said the crushing season was about to start and there was no shortage of the commodity. The Cabinet Committee on Export of Sugar had recommended allowing 400,000 metric tonnes and sought an additional quota of 16,000 metric tonnes for Sindh, raising the total to 416,000 metric tonnes.

The ECC had earlier allowed the export of 108,000 metric tonnes of imported sugar in August and another 200,000 metric tonnes of locally produced sugar in mid-September. The sugar export summary was presented as an additional agenda item and was not circulated to the relevant ministries, denying them the opportunity to discuss the matter and comment on it. The ECC chairman, Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, emphasised the need for deregulating the sector but again permitted the export, while the IMF condition that Pakistan completely deregulate the sugar sector and end the government role in export and import was not met, which Secretary Finance Imdadullah Bosal attributed to the refusal of one province.

According to a draft policy paper, sugar millers have earned $1.6 billion through exports during the past 10 years, when Pakistan exported 3.92 million tonnes of sugar and imported about 565,000 metric tonnes.
