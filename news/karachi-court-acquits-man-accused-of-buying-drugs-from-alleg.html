Karachi court acquits man accused of buying drugs from alleged dealer Anmol alias Pinky

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Karachi court has acquitted Muhammad Yaqub, accused of buying hashish from alleged drug dealer Anmol alias Pinky, giving him the benefit of the doubt. Additional District and Sessions Judge South noted that police never made Pinky an accused, even though the prosecution said Yaqub had confessed during investigation that he bought hashish from her. Darakhshan police had arrested Yaqub in 2018 and recovered 600 grams of hashish from him.

The judge asked why Pinky was not nominated as an accused in the case and told police to show her arrest. Yaqub, who had absconded after getting bail, was acquitted after his second bail. The court's decision highlights the gaps in the prosecution's case, in which the alleged supplier was never formally charged despite being named in the accused's statement.

The acquittal brings an end to a case that had been pending since the arrest eight years ago.
