Bournemouth stay perfect in Europa League as AC Milan lose again

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bournemouth extended their perfect Europa League record on Thursday, coming from behind to win 3-1 at 10-man Sparta Prague for a fourth successive victory in Europe. Tyler Adams equalised on 57 minutes, Evanilson put Bournemouth ahead nine minutes from time, and Rayan added a late third goal. The win keeps Bournemouth unbeaten in the competition, with Benfica ahead of them at the top of the standings only on goal difference.

AC Milan lost 2-0 at home to Ferencvaros and are outside the top 24 with just three points at the halfway stage of the league phase. Milan's defeat leaves them struggling for a place in the knockout rounds as the competition reaches its midpoint. Bournemouth, by contrast, continue to impress in their European campaign with four wins from four matches.
