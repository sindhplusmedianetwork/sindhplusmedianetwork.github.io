Indian Rickshaw Driver Puts Hania Aamir's Photo on His Rickshaw; Video Goes Viral

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An auto-rickshaw driver in India has displayed a photo of Pakistani actress Hania Aamir on the back of his rickshaw, and a video of the rickshaw has gone viral on social media. The video shows the actress's photo fixed prominently on the rear of the vehicle as it moves through traffic. Social media users praised the gesture, calling it an example of the popularity of Pakistani artists across the border.

Hania Aamir is among Pakistan's most popular television actresses. Her fame grew further after her role in the drama 'Mere Humsafar', which was widely watched on both sides of the border.
