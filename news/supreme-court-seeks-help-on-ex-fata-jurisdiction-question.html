Supreme Court seeks help on ex-FATA jurisdiction question

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Supreme Court has directed Attorney General for Pakistan Mansoor Awan to assist it on the question of jurisdiction in cases originating in the former Federally Administered Tribal Areas before their merger with Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa. A division bench headed by Justice Malik Shehzad Ahmed Khan and comprising Justice Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi is hearing the matter. The case involves a 14-year prison sentence imposed on an accused charged with providing shelter to terrorists.

The court also directed Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa state counsel Arshad Hussain Yousafzai and the petitioner's counsel, Mehnaz Advocate, to assist on the legal questions. During the hearing, Justice Khan observed that the case related to a period before the merger of the tribal areas. The Peshawar High Court had previously held that it lacked jurisdiction and had referred the matter back.

The state counsel informed the bench that the matter involved a 2016 decision, while a three-member high court bench had delivered another relevant judgment in 2024.
