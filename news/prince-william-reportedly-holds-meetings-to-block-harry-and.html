Prince William Reportedly Holds Meetings to Block Harry and Meghan's Royal-Style Events

Prince William is said to be furious over Prince Harry and Meghan Markle's royal-style public engagement in Nottingham on October 15. According to sources quoted by New Idea magazine, the Prince of Wales believes the appearance defied the King's letter setting out the couple's non-working royal status and upstaged the monarch. William has reportedly held intense meetings with the King's advisers to discuss ways of preventing the couple from gatecrashing or clashing with events attended by senior royals.

The reports also claim he has been declining King Charles's demand for a private one-on-one meeting with his estranged brother. The latest reports underline the continuing rift within the British royal family. Neither palace has publicly commented on the claims.
