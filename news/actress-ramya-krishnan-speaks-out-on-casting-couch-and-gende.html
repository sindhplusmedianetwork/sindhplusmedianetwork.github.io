Actress Ramya Krishnan speaks out on casting couch and gender exploitation

Veteran actress Ramya Krishnan has spoken openly about the casting couch and gender exploitation in the film industry, the report said. The actress shared her views on sensitive issues facing women in cinema. Her remarks touch on a subject that has long been debated within the industry, where women have spoken about the pressures they face.

Krishnan is a well-known figure in Indian cinema with a long career behind her. Her decision to speak publicly adds her voice to the wider conversation about safety and fairness for women in film. The report said she addressed the issues directly in her remarks.

Her comments are likely to draw further attention to the treatment of women in the industry.
