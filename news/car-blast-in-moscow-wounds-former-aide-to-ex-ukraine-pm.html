Car blast in Moscow wounds former aide to ex-Ukraine PM

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A car blast in Moscow on Friday wounded Sergei Zavorotny, a former adviser to Ukraine's fugitive ex-prime minister Mykola Azarov, Russian officials said, as they launched a criminal probe into the explosion. Zavorotny was behind the wheel when the car exploded on Friday morning, leaving him wounded. He has been taken to one of the city's hospitals, where he is receiving medical treatment.

Images from the site show the burned out and mangled chassis of a car purported to be that of Zavorotny, with investigators inspecting the scene, sealed off with red-and-white tape. The apparent attack came a day after a Russian colonel was killed in a car explosion in Saint Petersburg, the latest in a string of killings of Russian military figures.
