India to Overtake Japan as World's Fourth-Largest Economy in 2027: IMF

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India is set to become the world's fourth-largest economy in 2027, overtaking Japan, according to new projections from the International Monetary Fund. The IMF said India's gross domestic product would reach 4.52 trillion dollars that year, while Japan would slip to sixth place behind both India and the United Kingdom. Japan will remain the fourth-largest economy this year when measured by nominal GDP, the Fund added.

The IMF this week raised its 2026 growth forecast for India to 7 percent, praising the country's strong fundamentals despite global economic headwinds. Japan's growth, by contrast, is projected to slow to 0.7 percent in 2026, as higher energy prices and persistent inflation have pushed the Bank of Japan to raise interest rates to their highest level in three decades. The yen fell to its weakest level against the dollar in 40 years this summer, driving up import costs, while an ageing population continues to weigh on economic activity.

The United States remains the world's largest economy, followed by China and Germany, the projections showed. Tokyo's benchmark Nikkei index fell nearly 5 percent on Thursday as investors worried about the profitability of the artificial intelligence sector.
