Taimur Jhagra Goes Missing From Peshawar Police Custody as PTI Decries Kidnapping

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf leaders alleged that former provincial finance minister Taimur Saleem Jhagra was forcibly taken away by unidentified masked men from a Peshawar police station in the early hours of Friday, leaving his family and legal team struggling to establish his whereabouts. PTI leader Zulfi Bukhari shared the update, triggering serious questions about the security of individuals in police custody, particularly as Jhagra had been detained under the Maintenance of Public Order law despite securing protective bail from the Peshawar High Court. According to Bukhari, unidentified individuals entered the premises of Sharki Police Station, also known as East Cantt Police Station, during the early hours of Friday and forcibly removed Jhagra, and no information about the former minister's location had been provided to his family or lawyers since the reported incident.

The allegation has not been independently verified, and authorities have yet to publicly clarify whether Jhagra was transferred to another detention facility or taken elsewhere. Zulfi Bukhari claimed that Jhagra had effectively vanished from police custody after unidentified masked individuals took him away around 3 AM and demanded that the authorities immediately reveal Jhagra's location, guarantee his protection and produce him before a competent court. Earlier this week, the Anti-Terrorism Court rejected the bail applications of Taimur Saleem Jhagra, Kamran Bangash and Irfan Saleem in the Radio Pakistan attack case, after which the leaders approached the Peshawar High Court and secured interim protective bail until November 23, but police later detained all three leaders near the main entrance of the Peshawar High Court under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order.

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan visited Taimur Saleem Jhagra's residence and met his mother, expressing solidarity with the family and demanding the former minister's release.
