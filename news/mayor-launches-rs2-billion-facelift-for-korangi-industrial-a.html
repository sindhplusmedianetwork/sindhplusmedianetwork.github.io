Mayor Launches Rs2 Billion Facelift for Korangi Industrial Area

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Mayor Murtaza Wahab on Tuesday launched Rs2 billion worth of infrastructure rehabilitation works in the Korangi Industrial Area. Speaking at a ceremony of the Korangi Association of Trade and Industry, the mayor pledged improved facilities for businesses and faster execution of development projects. He said Rs68 billion was being spent on Karachi's overall infrastructure and 149 new roads would be built across the city.

According to the mayor, tenders for several projects have already been completed, and the remaining tenders are expected to be finalised within three to four days. Business representatives at the ceremony welcomed the announcement.
