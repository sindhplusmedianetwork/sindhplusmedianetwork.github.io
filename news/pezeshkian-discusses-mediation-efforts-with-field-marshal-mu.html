Pezeshkian Discusses Mediation Efforts With Field Marshal Munir in Tehran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian held talks with Pakistan's Chief of Defence Forces and Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir in Tehran on Tuesday, with mediation efforts and regional developments on the agenda. The meeting was reported by Reuters, citing Iran's Nour News. Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei separately told Munir that Tehran would not allow nuclear material to leave the country.

Rezaei added that there would be no negotiations with the United States until Washington takes steps to build trust and fulfils Iran's conditions. Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said Iran has 'no new message' for the US.
