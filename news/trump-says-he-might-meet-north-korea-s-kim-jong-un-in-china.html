Trump Says He Might Meet North Korea's Kim Jong Un in China This Week

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States President Donald Trump has said he might meet North Korean leader Kim Jong Un in China this week during his trip through East Asia. Speaking on Monday, Trump said there was a chance to accomplish a lot if the two leaders met. Trump, who met Kim several times during his first term from 2017 to 2021, said any meeting would take place in China and that Kim's presence was ultimately up to Chinese President Xi Jinping.

North Korea has publicly indicated that it has no intention of meeting the US president at this time. Trump has previously previewed meetings with Kim that never took place. The remarks came as Trump travels through the region for a series of meetings with Asian leaders.
