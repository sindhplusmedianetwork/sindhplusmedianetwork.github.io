Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov to Visit Tehran Today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov will visit Tehran today, Iranian media reported on Sunday. According to Iran's foreign ministry, the visit will include a review of the implementation of the agreements reached between the two countries. Lavrov is also expected to hold consultations with Iranian officials on bilateral matters.

The trip comes a day after the ninth joint ministerial meeting of the strategic dialogue between the Gulf Cooperation Council and Russia in Manama. At that meeting, Gulf states urged Iran to implement UN Security Council Resolution 2817.
