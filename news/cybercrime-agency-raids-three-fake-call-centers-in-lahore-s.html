Cybercrime Agency Raids Three Fake Call Centers in Lahore's Gulberg, 59 Held

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency has raided three fake call centers in Gulberg, Lahore, arresting 59 suspects, including 52 men and seven women. The operation recovered 61 laptops, 21 computers and 52 mobile phones from the premises. According to reports, the gang targeted restaurant credit-card data, while another group of the arrested suspects specialized in defrauding foreign nationals.

The accused allegedly lured victims abroad with promises of fake mobile upgrades and cheap packages, and hacked restaurant data to transfer money into their own accounts. The agency said the suspects had damaged the country's digital reputation, noting that the money was moved through trading and money-transfer apps. Investigators say the network used call centers as a front to contact foreigners and siphon off their funds through manipulated payment channels.
