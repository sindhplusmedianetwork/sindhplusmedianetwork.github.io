IHC Seeks Replies in Iman Mazari, Hadi Ali Chatha Trial Case

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Islamabad High Court has sought replies from the parties in the trial case against Iman Mazari and Hadi Ali Chatha. A division bench heard appeals against the rejection of pleas to quash the charge and remove terrorism clauses in the barrier-removal and interference case. Defence counsel Riasat Ali Azad argued that copies of the challan were not provided to the accused and the charge was not read out in their presence, making the foundation of the trial unlawful.

The court issued notices to the parties on the appeals. Separately, the trial hearing at Adiala Jail was adjourned to October 20. ATC judge Tahir Abbas Supra put off the proceedings after no prosecution witness could be recorded and the defence counsel withdrew her vakalatnama.

The case will now resume at the next hearing on October 20.
