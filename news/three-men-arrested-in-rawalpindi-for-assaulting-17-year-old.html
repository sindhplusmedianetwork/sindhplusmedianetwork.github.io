Three men arrested in Rawalpindi for assaulting 17-year-old boy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mandrah police in Rawalpindi have arrested three men accused of assaulting a 17-year-old boy. Police acted on a complaint lodged by the boy's father, after which a case was registered and the suspects were taken into custody. According to the first information report, the accused held the boy in wrongful confinement, assaulted him, filmed a video of him to blackmail him and threatened to kill him.

Superintendent of Police (Saddar) said the accused would be challaned in court with solid evidence. He added that harassment or abuse of women and children is intolerable and would not be allowed. Police said further legal proceedings against the arrested men were under way.
