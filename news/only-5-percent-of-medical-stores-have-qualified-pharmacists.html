Only 5 Percent of Medical Stores Have Qualified Pharmacists: Report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Only 5 percent of medical stores and pharmacies across Pakistan have qualified pharmacists present, according to reports by the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) and health experts. The reports state that 95 percent of medical stores involved in the purchase and sale of medicines are being operated by unrelated persons. The legal status of medical stores is divided into two categories.

Category A requires the mandatory presence of a certified pharmacist and allows the sale of all types of medicines. Category B stores operate on a pharmacy technician licence and are not legally permitted to sell specific and sensitive medicines.
