BJP Accuses Sonia Gandhi of Whitewashing Congress History in Memoir

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India's ruling Bharatiya Janata Party has accused opposition leader Sonia Gandhi of whitewashing the Congress party's historical wrongs in her new memoir 'Belonging', which was released on Tuesday. BJP spokesperson C.R. Kesavan cited the 1984 anti-Sikh riots and the 1975 Emergency, saying the book sought to 'camouflage the cardinal sins' committed by the Congress party.

Congress leaders hit back, accusing the BJP of trying to create a spectacle and give the book a 'political spin' ahead of key state elections. The exchange has turned the memoir's release into a fresh political flashpoint between the two parties. Gandhi's book, titled 'Belonging', is her account of her life and years in politics.

The BJP's criticism focused on what it said were the party's historical mistakes that the book did not confront.
