SRSO Trains SPHF and WASH Teams on Disability Inclusion in Khairpur Mirs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A one-day Disability Equality Training was organised for the SPHF and WASH teams by the Sindh Rural Support Organization (SRSO) at its district office in Khairpur Mirs, with the aim of making development interventions more inclusive and accessible for all. The session opened with remarks by the Regional General Manager, Khairpur Mirs Region, and the District Manager, who stressed the need to ensure that no community member is left behind in development work. The training was conducted through interactive and plenary sessions by lead trainer Zubair Soomro and co-trainer Hafeeza Bozdar.

Participants were briefed on the different types of disability and how they affect access to services, with the main focus on how SPHF and WASH interventions can be made more supportive, inclusive and accessible for persons with disabilities. The trainers encouraged participants to reflect on their field practices and identify practical steps to remove physical, communication and attitudinal barriers in their daily work. At the conclusion of the session, certificates were distributed among all participants.

Officials said the training was informative and would help field teams design and deliver interventions that are more inclusive, dignified and accessible for persons with disabilities and other vulnerable groups.
