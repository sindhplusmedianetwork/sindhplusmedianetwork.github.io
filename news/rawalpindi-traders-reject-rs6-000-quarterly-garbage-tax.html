Rawalpindi Traders Reject Rs6,000 Quarterly Garbage Tax

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Shopkeepers across Rawalpindi have been issued bills demanding Rs6,000 in garbage tax for three months, with notices directing them to pay within seven days and warning that shops will be sealed in case of non-payment. The tax has triggered strong protests from traders' organisations across the city. Former District Bar Association president Advocate Syed Tanvir Sohail has announced that he will challenge the Rs6,000 bills before the Lahore High Court's Rawalpindi Bench on Monday, November 2.

Traders have decided not to pay the tax, raising the possibility of a confrontation between the district administration and the business community. Traders' leaders said no sanitation worker from the Suthra Punjab Company had visited shops or plazas to provide cleaning services, noting that every market and commercial plaza already had its own paid sanitation workers. Meanwhile, households in residential areas have also started receiving garbage tax bills, demanding Rs3,000 per house for three months.
