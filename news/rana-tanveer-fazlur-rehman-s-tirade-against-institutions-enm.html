Rana Tanveer: Fazlur Rehman's Tirade Against Institutions 'Enmity With Country'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for National Food Security Rana Tanveer Hussain has said that Maulana Fazlur Rehman's tirade against his own institutions amounts to enmity with the country. Speaking to the media in Sharqpur Sharif on Saturday, he called Fazlur Rehman an astute politician and expressed hope that matters would improve soon. He said the governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa could last from two to six months.

He warned that attacking the state and its institutions would only worsen matters, adding that the politics of sit-ins would no longer be allowed. He said terrorism was highest in 12 districts of the province, where special measures are being taken, and no timeframe can be given for lifting the emergency there.
