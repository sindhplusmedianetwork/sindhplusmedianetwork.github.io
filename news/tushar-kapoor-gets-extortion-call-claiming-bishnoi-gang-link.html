Tushar Kapoor Gets Extortion Call Claiming Bishnoi Gang Link, Demands Rs50 Million

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actor Tushar Kapoor has received a phone call demanding an extortion payment of 50 million Indian rupees. The caller introduced himself as Harry Boxer, claiming to be a member of the Lawrence Bishnoi gang. Mumbai Crime Branch took up the investigation after the complaint and compared the audio recording with official voice samples.

The samples did not match, which has raised suspicion that the threat may be a hoax. The case has been handed to the Anti-Extortion Cell and the Crime Branch for further investigation. Police say the investigation is continuing and no arrests have been made so far.
