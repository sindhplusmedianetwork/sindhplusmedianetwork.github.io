Pakistani Forces Repel Unprovoked Afghan Taliban Firing Along Border; Several Taliban Dead, Base Destroyed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security sources said Afghan Taliban forces opened unprovoked fire in the Taida sector, Upper Kurram and Ghoozgarhi along the Pakistan-Afghanistan border in an attempt to push militants across into Pakistan. Pakistani security forces responded immediately and effectively to the firing, according to the sources. The retaliatory action killed several Afghan Taliban personnel and destroyed the Shapola base camp in the Upper Kurram sector.

The Taliban were forced to vacate a border post following the response, the sources added. The incident marks another flare-up along the sensitive frontier, where cross-border militancy remains a persistent flashpoint.
