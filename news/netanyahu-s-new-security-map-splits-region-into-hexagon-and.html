Netanyahu's New Security Map Splits Region Into 'Hexagon' and 'Diamond' Blocs

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Just days before Israel's general election, Benjamin Netanyahu has released a new national security doctrine accompanied by a map that splits the region into two blocs: a 'Hexagon alliance' comprising Israel, the UAE, Greece, Ethiopia and parts of India, and a 'Sunni Diamond alliance' of Pakistan, Turkey and Saudi Arabia. The map controversially places Egypt — which has a peace treaty with Israel — in the Sunni Diamond camp rather than among Israel's allies. The doctrine replaces the policy of containment with preventive strikes and formalises border buffer zones as official policy.

However, rivals Gadi Eisenkot and Yair Lapid dismissed the doctrine as hollow electioneering that avoids reckoning with the failures of 7 October. The coverage also notes Pakistan's military spokesperson warning the Houthis that any attack on Saudi Arabia would draw a collective response from the members of the Mecca defence pact.
