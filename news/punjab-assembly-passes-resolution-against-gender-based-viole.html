Punjab Assembly passes resolution against gender-based violence, seeks tougher penalties for insulting sacred personalities

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Assembly has unanimously passed a resolution against gender-based violence, while also demanding tougher punishment for insulting sacred personalities. The resolution on gender-based violence was moved in the House by Hina Pervaiz Butt of the ruling Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz, and it was passed unanimously. The resolution strongly condemned domestic, psychological, emotional and online violence against women on the occasion of the 16-day campaign against gender-based violence, observed from November 25 to December 10.

It praised the Punjab government for enacting legislation for the protection of women and access to justice, and demanded a strong awareness campaign across the province during the campaign, along with strict legal action against those involved in violence against women. The resolution also called for greater awareness of the Punjab Women Protection Authority helpline 080001737, and declared that protecting women is the responsibility of the state for all 365 days of the year, not just 16 days. A separate resolution seeking tougher punishment for insulting sacred personalities was moved by opposition member Rana Aurangzeb and was also passed unanimously.

He said the current punishment under Section 298-A of the Pakistan Penal Code is insufficient, as such insults hurt religious sentiments and damage social peace and harmony. The resolution demanded that the federal government legislate on a priority basis to increase the punishment under Section 298-A and ensure its effective implementation.
