Pakistan received $5.9 billion in foreign loans in first four months of fiscal year

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan received $5.9 billion in foreign loans and grants in the first four months of the current fiscal year, while foreign loan receipts rose by $2.88 billion compared with the same period last year. From July to October 2026, the government obtained Rs1,637 billion in foreign loans, Rs988 billion more than the same period last year. In October 2026, the government obtained Rs122 billion in foreign loans, of which Rs90 billion came as budget support and short-term loans and Rs24.5 billion as project financing, while loan receipts in October 2026 were Rs10 billion lower than in October 2025.

Pakistan also issued $3 billion in new bonds in the first four months of the fiscal year, including $1.75 billion of bonds at a 7.5 per cent rate maturing in 2032 and $1.25 billion of bonds at 7.9 per cent maturing in 2036, with the proceeds usable for budget support. Through Naya Pakistan Certificates, $440 million was received in October 2026, compared with $310 million in September 2026. From July to October, China provided $305.5 million in guaranteed loans, while Saudi Arabia provided $14.1 million in October, France $20 million and Germany $15 million.

The World Bank's IDA programme provided $427.5 million in the four months, the Asian Development Bank $170 million and the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank $27.6 million, while the Islamic Development Bank provided $31 million and a further $238.4 million in short-term financing.
