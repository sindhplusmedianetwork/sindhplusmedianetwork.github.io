Polluted winds from India make Punjab's air extremely unhealthy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Polluted air blowing in from Indian Punjab, following Diwali fireworks, has pushed Lahore to the top of the world's most polluted cities, with the city's average smog level recorded at 303. Gulberg II recorded 639 particulate metres, Syed Maratab Ali Road 475 and Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate 470, while Multan recorded 971 and Bahawalpur 796. Senior provincial minister Maryam Aurangzeb said action is under way against every local source of pollution, with smog guns and water sprinkling in high-AQI areas.

She said 28 industrial units were inspected, three sealed and 11 kilns closed across the province. Authorities have urged residents to limit outdoor activity and take precautions as the smog persists.
