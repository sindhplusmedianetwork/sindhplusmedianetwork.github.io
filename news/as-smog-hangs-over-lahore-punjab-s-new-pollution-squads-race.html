As smog hangs over Lahore, Punjab's new pollution squads race to douse stubble fires

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

With smog again settling over Lahore, Punjab's new quick-response pollution squads are racing to extinguish illegal stubble fires in rice-growing districts around the city. The province has formed a 750-member force in 67 rapid-response units to tackle the fires. The units aim to reach reported field fires within 10 to 15 minutes of a report.

Officials say 650 of the 679 fires reported this week were put out in a single day. Farmers caught burning stubble face fines of Rs15,000 per acre. The government has subsidised more than 10,000 super seeders to help farmers clear their fields without burning.

The squads are part of Punjab's effort to control the smog that returns to Lahore every winter.
