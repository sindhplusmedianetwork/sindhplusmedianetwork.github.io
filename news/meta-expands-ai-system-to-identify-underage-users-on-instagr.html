Meta expands AI system to identify underage users on Instagram worldwide

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Meta has announced it is expanding its artificial intelligence-based age-identification system worldwide. The system is meant to detect underage Instagram users who hide their real age and pose as adults. The AI estimates a user's true age from information beyond the date of birth they entered when creating the account.

If the system judges a user to be underage, the user can be moved automatically into Instagram's special Teen Accounts. Meta first introduced the technology in April 2025, and says the aim is to give children and teenagers age-appropriate content and improve their safety on Instagram. The expansion will make it harder for young users to present themselves as adults by entering an age of 18 or older in their accounts.
