Islamabad Residents Can Now Get Learner Permits and Licence Renewals From Home

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad Traffic Police has introduced an online facility allowing citizens to apply for learner permits, driving licence renewals and duplicate licences from home. Chief Traffic Officer Kainat Azhar Khan said the one-click service is available on the ITP web portal from Monday, October 19. The service can also be accessed at Traffic Headquarters Faizabad and police service centres.

Renewed and duplicate licences will be delivered to citizens' doorsteps through TCS. Applicants must enter their full name, correct address and an active mobile number. The initiative aims to reduce long queues and make licensing more convenient for Islamabad residents.
