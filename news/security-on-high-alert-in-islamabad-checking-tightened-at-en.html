Security on High Alert in Islamabad, Checking Tightened at Entry and Exit Routes

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad Police said security has been put on high alert in the federal capital. The police said checking has been tightened at the entry and exit routes of the city. The spokesperson of Islamabad Police said security in Islamabad has been put on high alert and checking at the city's entry and exit routes has been tightened.

He said measures are being taken to protect the lives and property of citizens. He said measures are also being taken to suppress miscreant elements. The spokesperson said citizens are requested to cooperate with police officers during checking.

He said the checking process is being monitored through Safe City.
