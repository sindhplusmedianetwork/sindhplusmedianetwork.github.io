Terrorist Attacks Fall 37% in October, but Kidnappings Rise Sharply

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan witnessed a marked improvement in key security indicators during October, as terrorist attacks declined 37 per cent compared with September, according to statistics released by the Islamabad-based Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies. The country recorded 143 attacks in October against 226 in September, reversing two consecutive months of rising violence. Deaths from attacks fell 57 per cent from 260 to 111, while injuries dropped 71 per cent from 242 to 69.

Balochistan posted one of the sharpest improvements, with attacks falling 46 per cent and fatalities dropping from 136 to 44, while mainland Khyber Pakhtunkhwa saw attacks decline 31 per cent. Suicide attacks also fell sharply, with only one incident in October — which caused no casualties — compared with three in September that killed 45 people. Security forces killed 156 terrorists in counterterrorism operations during the month and arrested seven suspects.

The one dark spot was kidnappings, which rose 22 per cent to 88 nationwide — the highest monthly figure in 12 months — with Balochistan accounting for 70 of them. Over the first ten months of 2026, Pakistan recorded 1,376 terrorist attacks that, alongside security operations, caused 3,902 deaths.
