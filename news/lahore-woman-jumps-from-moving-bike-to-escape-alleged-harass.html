Lahore Woman Jumps From Moving Bike to Escape Alleged Harassment by Rider

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman jumped from a moving motorcycle in Lahore's Johar Town to escape alleged harassment by an online bike-service rider, according to the case text. She had booked the bike on October 17 from F-2 Johar Town to Wattoo Palace, and the rider allegedly kept harassing her during the journey. She jumped from the motorcycle in the G-4 area, injuring her face, nose, throat and head.

Police registered a case on her brother's complaint, and the rider allegedly fled after she fell.
