Punjab CM Sets Target to Train One Million Youth Over Three Years

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has set a target of providing skill development training to one million young people over the next three years, as decided at a special meeting of the provincial government. The meeting also approved Punjab's first modern "weld working centre of excellence" along with a digitised examination system that will allow between 200,000 and 600,000 candidates to sit exams. Briefing officials said training has so far been completed for 400,000 youth, of whom more than 45 percent have found work, while others continued their education.

Programmes announced include certification for agriculture and heavy machinery operators, centres of excellence for electrical work, vehicles and digital marketing, a CM Language Learning Programme in 13 languages, and Punjab's first cleaning and cleaner academy. According to the briefing, 175,000 youth trained by the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Department have secured respectable jobs, with over 38,000 finding employment abroad. Further plans cover 25,000 BPO trainees under "Global Rozgar", 7,500 international technology certifications under "Advance Tech", a 2.6 billion rupee "Haryali" programme for 20,000 youth in agriculture and the green environment, training for 2,474 transgender persons, and 10,000 special persons under "Umeed".
