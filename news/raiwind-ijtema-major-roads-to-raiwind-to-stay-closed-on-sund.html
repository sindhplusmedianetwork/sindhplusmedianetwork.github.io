Raiwind Ijtema: Major Roads to Raiwind to Stay Closed on Sunday From 6am to 1pm

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Traffic arrangements for the Raiwind Tablighi Ijtema have been completed, and all major roads leading to Raiwind will remain closed to traffic on Sunday, November 8, from 6am to 1pm. Chief Traffic Officer Abdul Rahim Sherazi said traffic towards Raiwind city and the congregation site will also remain suspended during those hours. Under the traffic plan, one-way traffic will operate on both sides of the roads between Pajjian Mor, Qazalbash Chowk and Thokar Niaz Baig, and the same restriction will apply from Bhubtian Chowk to Mohlanwal.

Sundar Road, Manga Road, Kot Radha Kishan Road and Sui Asal Road will also carry one-way traffic to ease the movement of participants. During the return of participants, vehicles will not be allowed to exit towards Raiwind from the motorway and the ring road, and road crossings will be banned at various points on Raiwind, Manga, Sundar and Mohlanwal roads. Police have advised citizens to avoid travelling on Raiwind Road between 6am and 1pm on Sunday and to use alternative routes.
