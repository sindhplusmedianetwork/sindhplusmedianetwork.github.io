Time for Decision on Terrorism, Says PM Shehbaz in Federal Cabinet Address

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has said that civilians and security forces are being targeted in terrorist incidents while the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government does not utter a word against the terrorists. Addressing the federal cabinet, the prime minister said the water had gone above the head and the time for decision had come. He said the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government had been given Rs800 billion over the past ten years for the war against terrorism.

Despite the funds, he said, the province's Counter Terrorism Department was never strengthened to eliminate terrorism. The prime minister's remarks came amid a fresh wave of militant attacks in the province.
