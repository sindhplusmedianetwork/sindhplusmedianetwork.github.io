Man Accused of Killing Sister-in-Law and Fleeing Abroad Arrested in Pind Dadan Khan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police have arrested the accused who killed his sister-in-law in Pind Dadan Khan and fled the country. The accused had left Pakistan after the killing, according to the report. The arrest was made through Interpol.

No further details about the accused, including his name, or about the procedure of the arrest were given in the available report. The case relates to the killing of the accused's sister-in-law in Pind Dadan Khan.
