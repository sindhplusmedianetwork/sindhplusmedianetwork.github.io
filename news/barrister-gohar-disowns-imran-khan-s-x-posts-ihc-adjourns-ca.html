Barrister Gohar Disowns Imran Khan's X Posts, IHC Adjourns Case to Nov 23

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan on Thursday distanced the party from the posts on the X account in the name of PTI founder Imran Khan, telling the Islamabad High Court that the party does not claim ownership of them. Gohar appeared in person before Justice Arbab Muhammad Tahir in a petition filed by citizen Ghulam Murtaza seeking closure of Imran Khan's X account. Imran Khan's lawyers Khalid Yousaf Chaudhry and Shamsa Kiyani, along with National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency officials, were also present.

The court observed that Gohar had not been summoned in person and could have submitted a written response. Gohar maintained the party's position and sought more time to file a detailed reply, saying circumstances had changed. The judge accepted the request and adjourned the hearing to November 23, directing the party to submit its position in writing.
