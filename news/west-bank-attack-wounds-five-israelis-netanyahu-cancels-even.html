West Bank Attack Wounds Five Israelis; Netanyahu Cancels Events, Orders Hebron Cordon

WhatsApp Facebook X

At least five Israelis were wounded in an attack in the occupied West Bank on Friday, in a shootout that also left two Palestinians dead. An AFP journalist witnessed two Palestinian men in casual clothing, armed with a handgun and an automatic rifle, exchanging fire with Israeli forces at a checkpoint near Hebron. The Israeli military said its soldiers killed the two attackers at the Farsh al-Hawa checkpoint, also known as Judea Junction.

Israel's Magen David Adom emergency service said two of the five wounded Israelis were in critical condition. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, who is seeking a new term in elections on Tuesday, cancelled all of his political meetings on Friday. Netanyahu instructed the military to impose a cordon on the city of Hebron and to prepare for a full closure of the entire West Bank starting Friday.

Far-right minister Itamar Ben Gvir called for dismantling the Ramallah-based Palestinian Authority in response to the attack.
