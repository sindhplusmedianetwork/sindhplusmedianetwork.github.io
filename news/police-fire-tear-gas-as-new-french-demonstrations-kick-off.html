Police fire tear gas as new French demonstrations kick off

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French police started firing tear gas at protesters on Saturday as they stepped up security in major cities, aiming to prevent a repeat of rioting a week ago, on a day of fresh nationwide demonstrations over the high cost of living. Police in the southeast city of Lyon fired tear gas to disperse some 150 protesters near the city centre, where demonstrations had been banned, when they briefly tried to block a road. Elsewhere, authorities and rattled shopkeepers geared up for potential new street violence, and the Louvre and the Musee d’Orsay museums were closed.

Police were among 70,000 security personnel the interior ministry said were mobilised to counter the high threat of violence. “We will not tolerate any unrest,” Interior Minister Laurent Nunez said on Friday. Adding to tensions, far-right leader Marine Le Pen’s National Rally party was holding a weekend congress in the central city of Orleans, and thousands of people marched against Le Pen and against President Emmanuel Macron, yelling “Macron resign.

Le Pen in prison.” Last weekend saw rioters on the margins of the nationwide demonstrations clash with police, vandalise public buildings and loot hundreds of stores, with some 2,700 people arrested. Groups organising Saturday’s renewed demonstrations have accused Macron and his government of ignoring widespread public anger over rising costs and over classroom conditions for students.
