Azma Bukhari Hails Maryam Nawaz's Punjab as Changing, Developing Province

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bukhari has credited Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's policies with bringing visible change to the province's agriculture sector. Speaking to the media, she said the government has delivered on long-standing farmer demands, including the establishment of the Kissan Bank and a wheat support price of Rs4,500 per maund. She highlighted that 50,000 tractors are being provided to farmers for the first time in Punjab's history, calling it an unprecedented step.

According to her, one million farmers are benefiting directly through the Kisan Card scheme. Bukhari said the difference this time is that projects and relief packages are actually reaching the ground rather than remaining confined to files and social media announcements. She added that the province's direction under Maryam Nawaz reflects a focus on practical, people-centred governance.
