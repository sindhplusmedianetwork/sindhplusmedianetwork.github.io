Gold and Silver Prices Rebound After Seven Days of Decline

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 26, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

After seven days of falling prices, gold and silver prices rose on Monday, according to market figures reported in the press. Gold rose by US$14 per ounce internationally to US$4,009, pushing the local price up by Rs1,400 per tola to Rs423,436, while ten grams of gold rose by Rs1,201 to Rs363,028. Silver rose by 20 cents per ounce internationally to US$54, taking the local per-tola price up by Rs20 to Rs5,878.

The rebound followed a Rs2,500 fall in the local gold price on Saturday, October 24, and prices remained below their pre-weekend level despite Monday's recovery. Traders linked the local movement to the international market, where gold's rise lifted domestic rates in step with the global price. The figures were reported from Sarafa market rates, which set the daily benchmark for gold and silver trading across Pakistan.
