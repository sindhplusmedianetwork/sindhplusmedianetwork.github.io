SSGC explains surge in Quetta gas bills, sets up complaint help desk

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sui Southern Gas Company has issued a clarification over the recent increase in gas bills in Quetta. According to ARY News, the SSGC spokesperson said bills rose because winter has set in and gas consumption has gone up in the city, along with the tariff fixed by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority. The company said some consumers tamper with their meters to avoid large bills, and warned that tampered meters are billed on the basis of connected load.

Consumers may apply for a meter change and a laboratory test, and any amount collected in excess is refunded if the test does not prove additional charges. To address consumer complaints, SSGC said it has set up a help desk at its Balochistan regional office, which will work under the supervision of the Quetta regional manager. The company added that despite severe financial losses, gas supply to Quetta continues uninterrupted.
