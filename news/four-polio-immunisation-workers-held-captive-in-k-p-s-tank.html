Four polio immunisation workers held captive in K-P's Tank

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Four polio immunisation workers were abducted by unidentified armed men in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa's Tank district after their team came under fire, police said. The abduction took place in a village in the Sheikh Attar union council, where the gunmen seized the four vaccination workers and took them to an undisclosed location. Police said an investigation had been launched, though details about the perpetrators and the workers' whereabouts were not immediately available.

In a separate incident, two members of a polio vaccination team were kidnapped by armed men in the Azam Warsak area of Lower South Waziristan, but both vaccinators were later released; sources said the suspects were affiliated with the banned Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan. The incidents have renewed concerns over the safety of polio teams working in remote areas while carrying out immunisation campaigns. The abductions came a day after the National Emergency Operations Centre confirmed Pakistan's seventh polio case of the year, a 13-month-old girl from the Jan Baz Union Council in Bara tehsil, Khyber district.

Of the seven cases reported this year, six have emerged from Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa and one from Sindh, according to the NEOC.
