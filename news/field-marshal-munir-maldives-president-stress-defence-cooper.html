Field Marshal Munir, Maldives President Stress Defence Cooperation; 20 MoUs Signed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir, the Chief of Defence Forces, met Maldivian President Dr Mohamed Muizzu in Islamabad, with both sides stressing stronger defence cooperation. According to the military's media wing, the ISPR, the two discussed matters of mutual interest and the regional security situation. They underlined the need for deeper institutional linkages and wider professional exchanges between the two countries' armed forces.

President Muizzu praised the professionalism of Pakistan's armed forces and acknowledged their role in regional peace. Earlier in the day, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and President Muizzu oversaw the signing of 20 memorandums of understanding spanning defence, information technology, cybersecurity, health, education, tourism and trade. The two leaders also agreed to lift bilateral trade from $13.9 million to $200 million within two years.

The visit is the first by a Maldivian president to Pakistan in 11 years.
