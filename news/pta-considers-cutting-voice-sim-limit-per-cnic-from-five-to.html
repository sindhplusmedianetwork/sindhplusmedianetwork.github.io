PTA considers cutting voice SIM limit per CNIC from five to three

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority has begun considering a reduction in the number of voice SIMs allowed on one national identity card from five to three, while raising the limit for data SIMs. The move is aimed at curbing cybercrime and meeting the requirements of 5G services. PTA officials said that under a court decision, eight SIMs are currently allowed on one CNIC, including five voice SIMs and three data SIMs.

They said issuing a large number of voice SIMs increases the risk of misuse and involvement in cybercrime, making a lower limit necessary. Officials added that most users use only two or three voice SIMs at a time, so ordinary users would not be affected by the change. They also said the proposal to raise the data SIM limit from three is under serious consideration, as more data SIMs will be needed for the Internet of Things and connected devices once 5G is introduced.

The review will be completed after consultation with all mobile operators and telecom companies, after which the final policy will be announced.
