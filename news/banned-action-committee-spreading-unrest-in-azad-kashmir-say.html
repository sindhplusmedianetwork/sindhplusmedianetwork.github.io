Banned action committee spreading unrest in Azad Kashmir, says Talal Chaudhri

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Minister of State for Interior Talal Chaudhri has said the banned Joint Awami Action Committee is spreading unrest in Azad Kashmir. He said investigations of arrested miscreants led to further arrests, and that Imtiaz Aslam, a member of the committee's core committee, coordinates between its leadership. He said people were arrested who were taking weapons to Kashmir, with Asghar moving arms from border areas, Majid supplying arms through contact with Zia, and Nazim in contact with Ammar Sohail.

Chaudhri alleged that India is funding the banned committee. He said 37 of the committee's 38 demands have been accepted, and a man named Shehzad is actually a resident of Kashmir. He said audio recordings of the core committee's miscreants had been presented before the media, and the committee's movement is not a rights movement at all.
