Sindh PA unanimously tweaks anti-terrorism law

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Assembly has unanimously passed an amendment bill to the Anti-Terrorism Act, 1997, in a session that also took up other legislative business. The Sindh Farmers' Agriculture Bill 2026 was introduced in the house and referred to the relevant standing committee for detailed consideration. The house was informed that the Sindh governor had given assent to 10 bills passed by the assembly.

During question hour, Health Minister Dr Azra Fazal Pechuho said the Sindh Healthcare Commission had taken over 11,000 actions against illegal medical facilities. She said the commission had sealed more than 11,560 quack clinics across the province. The minister said the crackdown on unregistered and illegal medical practices would continue.

Members discussed the measures and the progress of the legislation before the house.
