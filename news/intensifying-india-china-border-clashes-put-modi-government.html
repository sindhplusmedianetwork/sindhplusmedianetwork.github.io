Intensifying India-China border clashes put Modi government's defence policy under global criticism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Express News reported, citing Al Jazeera, that border setbacks at the hands of China have become damning evidence of the Modi government's weak defence strategy. The report said the professional capability of the Chinese army has exposed security flaws of the Indian army in border areas. An earlier clash between China and India in which 20 Indian personnel were killed had already raised serious questions about Modi's war-fighting capability.

Indian satellite imagery has confirmed China's superior strategic control and India's failure in disputed areas, according to the report. A British news agency was cited as saying that Chinese forces advanced several kilometres into areas claimed by India and set up tents, exposing the incompetence of Indian border security arrangements. Security analysts cited in the report believe India's growing disputes with neighbouring countries have also put its regional policy and defence-related claims under pressure.

The developments have renewed debate over India's military preparedness, border management and the effectiveness of its regional security strategy.
