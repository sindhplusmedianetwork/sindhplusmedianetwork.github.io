Father Alleges Police Officer Took Bribe in Son's Kidnap-Murder Case; Officer Suspended

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi — The father of a young man kidnapped and brutally murdered in Karachi's Steel Town area has accused the police investigation officer of taking bribes from him, prompting the officer's suspension. Muhammad Khan, father of victim Zainul Abideen, told Express that Anti-Violent Crime Cell investigation officer Rizwan took a total of Rs22,000 from him, including Rs2,000 taken in the name of food. He said that while his son was kidnapped and cruelly killed, the investigation officer's attitude caused him immense pain, meeting him only three times and showing little interest in the case.

On his complaint of negligence filed on Thursday, DIG CIA Muqaddas Haider suspended investigation officer Rizwan and appointed a new investigation officer, though the new officer has not yet contacted the family. The grieving father demanded a transparent and impartial investigation, saying that despite paying Rs500,000 in ransom for his son's release, the boy was never recovered, and the kidnappers even sent the palm of his son's severed hand to frighten the family. Saudi-returned Zainul Abideen mysteriously went missing from Steel Town on October 14; a kidnapping-for-ransom case was registered on October 15 and the probe was handed to the AVCC, which recovered his body on October 16 from Chhatri Ground in Bin Qasim on the identification of the arrested suspects.

Police have arrested Faisal, a close relative of the victim, along with his accomplice Ansar.
