KP Food Authority seizes 1,500kg unsafe meat, arrests suspects over fake honey production

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Food Safety and Halal Food Authority seized 1,500kg of unsafe meat from a passenger bus in Peshawar after laboratory tests found it failed food safety standards, and destroyed the consignment. In Buner, officials uncovered a fake honey unit, recovering 2,550kg of fake honey and 1,300kg of sugar, sealed the unit and sent the suspects to jail. The meat was intercepted during a routine check before it could reach consumers in the city.

Laboratory testing confirmed the consignment did not meet food safety standards, leading to its destruction. In the separate Buner operation, officials found large quantities of fake honey and sugar at the unit, which was sealed on the spot. The suspects were taken into custody and sent to jail.
