Punishment Without Judicial Process Becoming the Norm, Warns Saad Rafique

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former federal minister and PML-N leader Khawaja Saad Rafique has said the judiciary and the government should jointly devise a strategy for swift justice in serious cases. He warned that punishment without judicial process is becoming the norm in the country. Rafique said there is a breakdown-like state in the justice system, with unusual delays and obstacles for complainants and victims, while law-enforcement agencies have themselves taken on a judicial role.

He warned that if this continues, no one will be safe, justice will pass into the hands of every powerful person, and people will set up their own courts in streets and markets.
