Trump calls Obama 'grossly incompetent' after ex-president's midterm rallies

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump hit back at his Democrat predecessor, Barack Obama, on Sunday, calling him incompetent after the former president delivered scathing criticisms of the current leader at rallies in Ohio and Michigan ahead of the November 3 midterm elections. In a post on his Truth Social platform, Trump wrote that Obama was a grossly incompetent president and criticised his record on Iran and his campaigning for Hillary Clinton and Kamala Harris. Trump also slammed Obama for supporting US Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed, calling him a fake doctor who runs what he described as the worst clinic in America, and urged voters to vote for Republican Mike Rogers.

Ten days out from the elections that will determine control of the closely divided Congress, Obama had taken to the campaign trail to accuse Trump of corruption, deride his strategy for Iran and skewer the aesthetic applied to White House renovations. Campaigning in Michigan for El-Sayed, Obama framed the November 3 vote as a circuit breaker to bring accountability to the government. A Reuters/Ipsos poll in August found that 63 per cent of Americans think the president and his family have inappropriately profited from cryptocurrency since he returned to the White House.
