Lahore DC Reviews Final Arrangements for Raiwind Ijtema’s Concluding Prayers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore Deputy Commissioner Muhammad Ali Ejaz has visited the Raiwind Ijtema site to review the final arrangements for the concluding prayers of the annual Tableeghi congregation. The gathering is being attended by participants from 109 countries, making it one of the largest religious congregations in the country. During the visit, the deputy commissioner directed the departments concerned to ensure medical camps, the availability of medicines, parking, sanitation and smooth traffic flow for the concluding prayers.

He said security would remain on high alert during the gathering, and arrangements were being finalised to manage the large crowds safely. Officials were also told to keep the facilities for participants in working order throughout the event. The deputy commissioner said the second phase of the congregation will begin on November 12.
