Petrol Price Up Rs1.20 to Rs393.86 as Diesel Sees Minor Cut

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Wednesday raised the price of petrol by Rs1.20 per litre while trimming the price of high-speed diesel by 17 paisas. Under the notification issued by the Petroleum Division, petrol will retail at Rs393.86 per litre and diesel at Rs396.65 per litre, effective Thursday, October 15. Petrol was previously priced at Rs392.66 per litre and diesel at Rs396.82 per litre.

The government continues to levy Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel. Kerosene oil and light diesel oil prices were left unchanged at Rs330.14 and Rs266.41 per litre respectively. The adjustment was made under the daily price-fixation mechanism introduced in July, with Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik saying prices are now fixed daily to pass on international market fluctuations to consumers immediately.

Officials linked the latest revision to movements in Platts benchmark prices, premiums and the volatile global oil market amid the ongoing Middle East conflict.
