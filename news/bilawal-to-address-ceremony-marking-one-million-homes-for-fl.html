Bilawal to address ceremony marking one million homes for flood victims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari will attend and address a ceremony on Tuesday, November 10, at the Chief Minister's House banquet hall in Karachi, marking the milestone of one million homes built under the Sindh People's Housing for Flood Affectees project. The project provides new homes to families in Sindh whose houses were destroyed in the floods. Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah, Chief Secretary Asif Hyder Shah and SPHF chief executive Khalid Mahmood Sheikh will also speak at the ceremony, along with the European Union's cooperation chief and the World Bank's country director for Pakistan.

Beneficiaries of the housing project will share how the new homes have changed their lives. The ceremony begins at 3pm.
