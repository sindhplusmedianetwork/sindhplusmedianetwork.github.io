Iran Lifts Self-Imposed 2,000km Limit on Missile Range

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has announced that it has lifted its self-imposed 2,000-kilometre limit on the range of ballistic missiles. IRGC spokesman General Hossein Mohebbi told a press conference in Tehran that the restriction had been lifted, saying Iran would demonstrate its capabilities in practice rather than make statements about them. He did not say whether Iran currently has missiles capable of travelling beyond that distance.

Iran is not known to have operational ballistic missiles exceeding the previous limit, a range that already puts parts of Greece and the Balkans, the entire Arabian Peninsula, the Levant and most of Egypt within reach. The announcement came days after Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei warned that any kind and level of threat from the United States or Israel would face a response without geographical limits. The move comes amid heightened tensions in the Gulf, with fresh United States strikes on Iranian air defences reported earlier in the week.
