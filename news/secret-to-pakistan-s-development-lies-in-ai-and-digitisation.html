Secret to Pakistan's development lies in AI and digitisation, PM Shehbaz stresses in Alibaba cooperation review

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has said that the secret to Pakistan's development lies in artificial intelligence and digitisation, while chairing a meeting in Islamabad reviewing progress on AI and digitisation in the economy and governance and cooperation with Alibaba. He said the effective use of digitalisation, AI and modern technology could improve trade, exports, agricultural productivity, ease of doing business and the quality of public services. The prime minister welcomed Alibaba's cooperation in facilitating Pakistani agricultural exports to Chinese markets and in automation in agriculture, and directed that an AI-assisted cancer early-detection system be implemented at the earliest in collaboration with Alibaba.

He also directed that a 15-day AI training programme for IT graduates be launched soon under the IT ministry's Ignite programme in collaboration with Alibaba, and instructed the relevant ministries to complete all necessary measures by November and submit a progress report.
