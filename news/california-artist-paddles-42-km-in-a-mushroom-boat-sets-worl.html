California artist paddles 42 km in a mushroom boat, sets world record

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An artist from California has set a Guinness World Record by paddling 42.43 kilometres in a boat made entirely of giant mushroom mycelium. Los Angeles resident Sam Shoemaker made the solo journey in August 2025, travelling from Catalina Island to Cabrillo Beach on the American mainland. For the trip he used a 12-foot-long kayak made completely of a particular type of mycelium.

Shoemaker completed the journey in about 12 hours, earning the Guinness World Record for the longest solo journey in a mycelium boat. He has been researching and experimenting with different types of fungi for more than a decade, and also shapes fungi into sculptures and works of art. An important aim of his research is to explore the possibilities of introducing mycelium from fungi as an eco-friendly alternative to plastic and non-renewable materials used in the construction industry.

His unusual experiment is not only an example of the combination of art and science, but also highlights new possibilities for the use of eco-friendly building materials.
