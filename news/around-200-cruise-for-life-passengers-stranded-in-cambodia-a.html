Around 200 'cruise for life' passengers stranded in Cambodia as operator runs out of money

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Around 200 passengers who paid to live aboard a cruise ship while travelling the world were abruptly left in the Cambodian port of Sihanoukville after the operator ran out of money. United States-based Villa Vie Residences announced it had suspended operations in Cambodia, citing extraordinary increases in fuel costs following the Iran war. The 200-metre ship Odyssey had left Northern Ireland in October 2024 for a continuous voyage on which residents could buy or rent rooms for a holiday or for life, with some passengers selling everything they had to pay for it.

Passengers were given just seven hours to pack their bags and leave during a scheduled stop in Sihanoukville, according to Long Dimanche, deputy governor of Preah Sihanouk province, who said 203 guests and 156 crew had arrived from Thailand on October 23 and had been due to go on to Vietnam. He said the sudden order to disembark had left many retirees and remote workers homeless thousands of miles from their home countries, that some passengers had taken flights out while others remained in the city, and that the ship itself was due to refuel and leave on October 31. Villa Vie said monthly fuel expenses per cabin had risen from an average of 1,800 dollars to an estimated 6,000 to 8,000 dollars in parts of Asia, and that slowing the ship and spending more time in port had failed to offset the increase.

The company said it could not simply pass increases of that scale on to residents, that the resulting shortfall had placed its operating model under unsustainable financial pressure, and that it was working with residents to coordinate disembarkation and identify temporary living arrangements. One passenger, Randy Cassingham, who had been aboard with his wife Kit since the ship left Belfast in October 2024 and spent around one million dollars on a voyage they had hoped would last at least 15 years, said Cambodia was their 65th country in two years and that it had been brilliant for a while.
