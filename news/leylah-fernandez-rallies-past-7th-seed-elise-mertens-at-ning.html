Leylah Fernandez Rallies Past 7th Seed Elise Mertens at Ningbo Open

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Canada's Leylah Fernandez staged a thrilling comeback on Centre Court at the Ningbo Open, beating seventh seed Elise Mertens 2-6, 6-3, 7-5 in the first round. The Belgian world No. 15 dominated the opening set, but Fernandez fought back, breaking serve to take the second set.

The decider was a grueling contest that lasted over two and a half hours before Fernandez sealed the win. The victory earns her a spot in the round of 16 and continues her strong run during the Asian swing of the season.
