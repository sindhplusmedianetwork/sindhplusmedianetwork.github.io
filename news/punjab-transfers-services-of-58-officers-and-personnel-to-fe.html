Punjab transfers services of 58 officers and personnel to federal government for new model jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government placed the services of 58 officers and personnel at the disposal of the federal government for the new model jail in Islamabad, according to the report. The transferred staff include 51 wardens among the 58 officers and personnel. A notification was issued in this regard by the Punjab government.

The report said a total of 500 staff are required for the new model jail. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa will also provide staff for the jail, the report added.
