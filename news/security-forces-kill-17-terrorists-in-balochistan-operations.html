Security forces kill 17 terrorists in Balochistan operations; soldier martyred, says ISPR

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces killed 17 terrorists in two intelligence-based operations carried out on November 1 in Balochistan's Kachhi and Kharan districts, the military's media wing ISPR said on Tuesday. According to the statement, the operations targeted the 'Indian proxy Fitna al Hindustan', and troops recovered weapons and ammunition from the terrorists' hideouts. During the intense exchange of fire, 25-year-old Sepoy Hikmatullah of Karak district embraced martyrdom while fighting bravely.

President Asif Ali Zardari and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi paid tributes to the fallen soldier and praised the security forces for their successful action. The ISPR said clearance operations were continuing in the area to eliminate any remaining threats.
