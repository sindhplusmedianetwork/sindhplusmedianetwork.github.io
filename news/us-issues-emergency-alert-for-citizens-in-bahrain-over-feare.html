US Issues Emergency Alert for Citizens in Bahrain Over Feared Iran and Houthi Attacks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The US Embassy in Bahrain warned American citizens to exercise unusual caution amid rising threats of armed attacks. The warning cited alleged Iranian efforts and Iran-backed Houthi groups targeting US officials and installations. Citizens were advised to prepare for possible security alerts.

They were also told to prepare for flight cancellations, airspace closures and travel disruptions. The embassy advised them to monitor airport and airline updates. The alert reflects heightened tensions in the region amid fears of attacks.
