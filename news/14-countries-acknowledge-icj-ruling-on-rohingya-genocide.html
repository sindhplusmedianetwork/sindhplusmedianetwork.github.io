14 Countries 'Acknowledge' ICJ Ruling on Rohingya 'Genocide'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United Kingdom and 13 other countries on Monday acknowledged the International Court of Justice ruling that Myanmar perpetrated genocide against its Rohingya minority, as London announced new sanctions. The foreign ministers of Australia, Belgium, Canada, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Estonia, Iceland, Latvia, Luxembourg, New Zealand, Portugal, Spain, The Gambia and the UK said Myanmar had violated its obligations under the Genocide Convention and called on it to comply fully. The UK separately said it formally recognises the genocide and sanctioned five senior military figures with asset freezes and travel bans, plus five million pounds in new funding for Rakhine state.

The ICJ ruling was a landmark finding against Myanmar over its treatment of the Rohingya, hundreds of thousands of whom fled to Bangladesh. Myanmar has rejected the ruling, and the international community continues to press it to comply with the court's orders.
