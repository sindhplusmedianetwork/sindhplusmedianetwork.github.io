Pakistan and Oman Push Diplomacy for Middle East Peace, Sign Six Agreements

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Oman urged diplomacy to bring peace to the Middle East during the eighth session of the Pakistan-Oman Joint Ministerial Commission held in Islamabad. Omani Foreign Minister Sayyid Badr bin Hamad bin Hamood Albusaidi undertook a two-day visit to Islamabad from October 13 to 14 to attend the session with Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar. The two sides exchanged views on regional and international developments, with the ministers condemning the recent Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and reaffirming their commitment to regional security.

They also underscored the importance of ensuring the safety and freedom of navigation through the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab. The Omani foreign minister appreciated Pakistan's sustained diplomatic efforts to de-escalate regional tensions. Later, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif witnessed the exchange of six agreements and memorandums of understanding.

These included the protocol of the eighth JMC session, a mutual visa exemption agreement for holders of diplomatic, special, official and service passports, and MoUs on records and archives, information and broadcasting, information technology and telecommunications, and news exchange and media cooperation. The two sides agreed to hold the ninth JMC session in Muscat next year.
