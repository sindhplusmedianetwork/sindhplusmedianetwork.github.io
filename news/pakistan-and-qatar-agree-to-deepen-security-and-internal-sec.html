Pakistan and Qatar Agree to Deepen Security and Internal-Security Cooperation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and Qatar on Tuesday agreed to expand their security partnership and strengthen cooperation in internal security during a meeting between Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi and his Qatari counterpart, Sheikh Khalifa bin Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, in Doha. The meeting, also attended by the commander of Qatar's Internal Security Force (Lekhwiya), focused on enhancing cooperation between the two interior ministries as well as the prevailing regional situation. 'Pakistan and Qatar are working together for regional peace and stability,' Naqvi said, while the Qatari interior minister said his country attached great importance to its relations with Pakistan.

Naqvi is in Doha attending the eighth Global Security Forum, where he highlighted Qatar's role in diplomatic efforts to ease tensions between Iran and the United States. He said the two countries had worked to prevent further escalation and save lives, citing the involvement of Qatar's Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani and Pakistan's Chief of Defence Forces and Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir. He recalled the Lake Lucerne summit in Switzerland, where Qatar's prime minister and the Pakistani field marshal worked for more than 16 hours to keep negotiations on track.

'The aim of both countries was never to take credit; the only aim was to stop further conflict and to save innocent lives,' Naqvi said, adding that Pakistan and Qatar should keep diplomatic channels open and build trust. He also called for stronger international cooperation, intelligence-sharing and joint efforts to combat terrorism, describing it as a common enemy.
