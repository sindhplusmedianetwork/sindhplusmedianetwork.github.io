Danish Taimoor Explains Why He and Ayeza Khan Are Rarely Seen Together

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Popular actor Danish Taimoor has explained why he and his wife, Ayeza Khan, are rarely seen together at public events. Speaking at a fundraising event in Houston, he said Ayeza is currently busy shooting her upcoming film. He said the couple are waiting for a good script before working together in a drama again.

Being husband and wife in real life, he added, is not reason enough to share a drama, as the story must also be strong. He confirmed that the couple are still together, even though social media users continue to question why they do not appear together at functions. Many fans expressed relief at his clarification.

The remarks put to rest speculation about the couple's relationship that had been circulating online.
