Moonis Elahi Says New Provinces Issue May Be Deferred Until After 2029 Elections

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Moonis Elahi has reacted to reports suggesting that the matter of creating new provinces has been deferred until after the 2029 elections. Commenting on the reports in a post on X, he remarked sarcastically that 'the grapes are sour'. In the same post, he turned to the internal divisions within the Chaudhry family.

He said Chaudhry Wajahat's children are making noise because, in his words, after rigging, Chaudhry Shujaat Hussain's children kept everything for themselves and gave them no ministries. Elahi added that had the PML-N also given them ministries, they would be happy today. His comments linked the debate over new provinces to the long-running family dispute over power and ministries.
