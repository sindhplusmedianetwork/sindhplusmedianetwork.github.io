'A book for children, devotion for us': Religious education for Hindu students begins for first time in Sindh government schools

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Religious education for Hindu students has begun for the first time in Sindh's government schools. The Sanatan Dharma books introduce the Ramayana, the Bhagavad Gita, Hindu deities, religious festivals and rituals. The Karachi-based Hindu welfare organisation Prem Sagar Sanstha prepared the books together with Hindu religious leaders.

The books were published with the Sindh government's permission. After publication, philanthropist Hindus distributed the books in schools in Umerkot and Tharparkar. The report describes the books as a book for children and devotion for the community.
