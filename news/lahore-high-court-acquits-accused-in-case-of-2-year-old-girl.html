Lahore High Court Acquits Accused in Case of 2-Year-Old Girl's Death

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Lahore High Court has acquitted Shah Nawaz in the case of a two-year-old girl who died after being crushed under a tractor, setting aside his sentence. Justice Tariq Saleem Sheikh issued a 14-page judgment on Shah Nawaz's jail appeal, which has been declared a judicial precedent. The trial court had sentenced the accused to pay Rs2,055,000 in diyat, and the sessions court had upheld that sentence, but the High Court set aside both decisions.

The court held that the prosecution had failed to prove the charge against the accused and ordered his immediate release. According to the judgment, the complainant Sakina Bibi admitted during cross-examination that she had not seen the accident herself, and the girl's father's statement showed improvement and contradiction with his statement under Section 161, while the court drew an adverse inference against the prosecution for not producing the girl's mother, Kulsoom Bibi, as a witness. The court also noted that the FIR was registered six months and 26 days late without any reasonable explanation, the investigating officer admitted that no driver had deliberately driven the tractor over the girl, and the recovered tractor was never produced in court, with no forensic evidence linking it to the incident.

The identity of the skeleton found during exhumation could not be established: the lady doctor said she could not say with certainty that it was the skeleton of the girl, no DNA test or X-ray was carried out, and no fractures of the bones could be confirmed. The court found that the Punjab Forensic Science Agency's report neither established the skeleton's identity nor the cause of death, and that there were also flaws in the record of the samples' custody and transfer, while the Lahore High Court also issued guidelines on the exhumation process in its judgment and ordered Shah Nawaz to be released immediately, subject to his not being wanted in any other case.
