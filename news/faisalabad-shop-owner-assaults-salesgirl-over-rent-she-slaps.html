Faisalabad shop owner assaults salesgirl over rent; she slaps him in police custody

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A shop owner in Faisalabad assaulted a salesgirl at a local shopping mall over unpaid shop rent, prompting the salesgirl to slap the suspect repeatedly while he was in police custody, according to UrduPoint. Shop owner Hamid had come to the shop to collect the rent and, finding the shopkeeper absent, subjected the salesgirl there to violence and threats. The video of the incident shows that when the girl tried to stop him, Hamid severely assaulted her.

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif warned in a post on X that anyone who raises a hand against the poor or vulnerable must be prepared to face a response. She said no one, regardless of how rich, influential or powerful they may be, can threaten or harm an ordinary citizen, and that she would stand with the vulnerable and oppressed. The chief minister added that the rich will no longer be given a free hand to oppress the poor, nor the powerful to mistreat the weak, and that the government will respond.
