JUI-F's Kamran Murtaza Hits Back at Khawaja Asif Over Army Remarks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

JUI-F Senator Kamran Murtaza has hit back at Defence Minister Khawaja Asif, saying that 'people like him come and go'. Speaking to journalists outside Parliament House, the JUI-F leader said those now targeting his party had themselves used inappropriate language about the army in the past. He insisted that Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam respects the military.

On the occasion, JUI leader Maulana Ziaur Rehman also criticised Khawaja Asif, asking why his party had failed to act during its four governments in the last 30 years on the very allegations against Maulana Fazlur Rehman's family that Asif himself had raised regarding plazas and factories. The exchange reflects the ongoing war of words between the ruling PML-N and the JUI-F.
