Indian players' gestures after U-19 Asia Cup win spark criticism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

India beat Pakistan by one wicket to win the Under-19 Asia Cup title. A video circulating on social media has sparked controversy over the Indian players' behaviour after the match. The video appears to show Indian player Manal Chauhan making an inappropriate gesture towards Pakistan's Hamza Zahoor.

Cricket fans criticised the players online after watching the video. They questioned whether it was an appropriate way to celebrate a win. Fans also asked whether any action would be taken over the players' behaviour.

One user said the team's support staff tried to stop the players during the celebrations.
