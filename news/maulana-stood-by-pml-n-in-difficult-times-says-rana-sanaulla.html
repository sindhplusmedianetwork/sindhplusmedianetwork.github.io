Maulana stood by PML-N in difficult times, says Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's adviser on political affairs Rana Sanaullah has praised JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, saying he stood by the PML-N in difficult times. Speaking on Geo Pakistan, Sanaullah said such situations arise between political parties and there is nothing alarming about it, adding that his party would try to stop the harsh exchanges. He said the draft of the 28th Amendment has not been finalized yet, and it will be debated once it comes before parliament.

A day earlier, speaking to the same channel, Sanaullah had described the JUI-F chief as a seasoned and senior politician, recalling the good memories of working with him during the PDM era. He said harsh remarks had been made about Maulana regarding the state and martyrs, and that similar responses had come from his own side. Sanaullah said the effort would still be to sit with the Maulana and resolve these issues.
