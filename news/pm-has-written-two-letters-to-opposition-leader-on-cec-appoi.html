PM Has Written Two Letters to Opposition Leader on CEC Appointment, Says Rana Sanaullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister's adviser Rana Sanaullah said on Sunday that the Prime Minister has written two letters to the opposition leader regarding the appointment of the Chief Election Commissioner and members of the Election Commission of Pakistan. He said the government is still awaiting a response to those letters. Sanaullah rejected any suggestion of a third-party venue for the proposed meeting between the Prime Minister and the opposition leader, insisting the meeting must take place at the PM House.

He described the PM House as state property and not anyone's private residence. On dialogue with the PTI, he said talks could not be made conditional on lifting the emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. However, he added that the PTI's cooperation in the fight against terrorism could open room for negotiation.
