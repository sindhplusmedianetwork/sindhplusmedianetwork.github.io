Kim Kardashian faces scrutiny over SKIMS Israel ties after Lewis Hamilton's Gaza visit

Kim Kardashian is facing renewed criticism over her business interests in Israel and political connections after her boyfriend Lewis Hamilton publicly condemned the genocide in Gaza following a visit to Egypt's Rafah border crossing. Hamilton travelled to Egypt on November 10 and wrote on Instagram on November 11 that the past three years of Israel's genocide in Gaza is appalling and demanded aid trucks be allowed in. The scrutiny centres on her shapewear brand SKIMS, which entered the Israeli market in November 2025, and a reported private SKIMS event in Los Angeles involving daughters of Israeli soldiers.

The criticism has linked her business ties to Hamilton's public remarks on Gaza. The episode has drawn attention to the contrast between the couple's public positions on the conflict.
