Armed men rob entire night-cricket team in Karachi's Manzoor Colony; CCTV footage surfaces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi — An entire team of young men playing a night cricket match was robbed by three armed dacoits in the Manzoor Colony area of Karachi late last night. The attackers held the players at gunpoint in Sector F of the neighbourhood and snatched mobile phones and cash from every member of the team before fleeing. CCTV footage of the incident has surfaced, showing the accused also assaulting the young cricketers during the robbery.

Despite the violence, the armed men managed to escape easily after the crime. SHO Baloch Colony Shahzada Saleem said legal proceedings are underway and the culprits would soon be arrested with the help of the CCTV footage. Police have begun tracing the suspects through the video evidence.
