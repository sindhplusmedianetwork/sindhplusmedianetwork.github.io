Resolution tabled in Balochistan Assembly to make Arabic compulsory in government primary schools

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A resolution has been tabled in the Balochistan Assembly to introduce Arabic as a compulsory subject in government primary schools. MPA Shahida Rauf presented the resolution in the house. The resolution recommends basic Arabic education in all government primary schools across the province.

It says children should get equal opportunities to learn the language from the early stages of their schooling. The resolution notes that Arabic is the language of the Holy Quran. It says basic Arabic would help children understand Quranic verses.

The house will now take up the resolution for further consideration.
