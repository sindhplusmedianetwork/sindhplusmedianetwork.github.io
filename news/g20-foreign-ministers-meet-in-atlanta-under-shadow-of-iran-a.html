G20 Foreign Ministers Meet in Atlanta Under Shadow of Iran and Ukraine Wars

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foreign ministers of the world's 20 largest economies have begun a two-day meeting in the US city of Atlanta on October 30–31, chaired by US Secretary of State Marco Rubio. The gathering is taking place under the shadow of the Iran and Ukraine wars, which dominate the global agenda. The participation of Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov has divided Western countries, reflecting deep disagreements over Moscow's role in international forums.

Organisers cancelled the traditional group photograph amid the tensions. Diplomats say the ministers will discuss conflict resolution, trade and climate issues, though expectations of breakthroughs remain low. The meeting comes at a time of heightened geopolitical friction, with several member states at odds over sanctions and security policy.
