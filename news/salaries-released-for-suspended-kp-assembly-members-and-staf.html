Salaries Released for Suspended KP Assembly Members and Staff After Governor's Rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Salaries have been released to the suspended members of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Assembly and the assembly secretariat employees. The payments were released after the imposition of governor's rule and emergency in the province, and the amounts have been transferred to the accounts of the members and employees. The provincial assembly was suspended and its secretariat has remained closed since October 11.

President Asif Ali Zardari signed the summary for emergency and governor's rule in the province on October 11, 2026. The summary was signed on the advice of the prime minister under Articles 232 and 234 of the Constitution. The move cited rising terrorism and a worsening law and order situation in the province.
