Case registered against eight over alleged assault of 10-year-old girl in Sukkur

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Sukkur have registered a case against eight people over the alleged sexual assault of a 10-year-old girl. Five suspects, including two women, have been arrested in the case. Police said the girl was found unconscious near Labour Colony.

Initial investigation indicates that she was taken to a hotel and assaulted there. According to police, the arrested neighbouring women used to take the girl along for begging, and they had taken her with them on the day of the incident. The nominated accused include the hotel owner and its employees.

Police said the investigation is continuing.
