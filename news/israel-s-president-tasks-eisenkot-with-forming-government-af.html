Israel's President Tasks Eisenkot With Forming Government After Deadlocked Vote

By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Israel's President Isaac Herzog has chosen Gadi Eisenkot, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's strongest rival, to try to form a new government after a deadlocked election left neither man with a clear path to power. Factions comprising 67 Knesset members recommended Eisenkot, while 46 recommended Netanyahu and seven chose no one. Eisenkot, a former military chief, leads the Yashar party, which won 21 seats in the October 27 election.

He now has 28 days to forge a coalition, with a possible 14-day extension if he needs more time. Netanyahu's Likud won the most seats of any single party with 27, but his wider religious-nationalist coalition won only 53 seats in total. Herzog had consulted the parties on government formation and said he would hand out the mandate on Sunday after further talks with party leaders.
