Iranian Armed Forces 'Stronger' Than at Start of War, Army Spokesman Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

Iranian army spokesman Mohammad Akraminia has claimed that Iran's armed forces are now stronger, more alert and more motivated than at the start of the war with the United States and Israel. He said direct combat experience had increased the forces' capability and self-confidence, according to state news agency IRNA. The claim comes as military tensions in the region remain in focus since the war began in February.

The report notes that the statement is an official position without independent verification. The spokesman's remarks present the armed forces as having grown more capable through the fighting.
