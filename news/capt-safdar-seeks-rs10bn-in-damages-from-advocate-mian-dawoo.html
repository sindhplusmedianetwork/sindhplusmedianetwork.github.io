Capt Safdar Seeks Rs10bn in Damages from Advocate Mian Dawood over Defamatory Remarks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz's spouse and former MNA, retired Capt Muhammad Safdar, has served a legal notice on Advocate Mian Dawood demanding an unconditional public apology, retraction of allegedly defamatory remarks, removal of videos and Rs10 billion in damages. The notice, dated November 20, was issued through a Rawalpindi-based lawyer, Majad Ali Butt, under Section 10 of the Punjab Defamation Act 2024, and Advocate Dawood confirmed he had received it, saying an appropriate response would be sent after examining it. According to the notice, the dispute arose from Safdar's public demand that former president of the Supreme Court Bar Association, Ahsan Bhoon, produce his LLB degree, a matter it says is already before the Supreme Court and the Pakistan Bar Council.

The notice cited three pieces of content, including a two-minute video uploaded on September 24 on YouTube, a news channel interview aired on October 29 and a video statement made after the SCBA elections held last month. It demanded that the lawyer publish, within 14 days, an apology on the same platforms and in leading English and Urdu newspapers, retract the statements, have the videos removed, pay Rs10 billion and refrain from repeating the remarks, failing which Safdar would approach the Special Tribunal under the Defamation Act. Bhoon had earlier filed a Rs5 billion defamation suit against Safdar over remarks questioning the authenticity of his LLB degree, which the Punjab Defamation Tribunal admitted for hearing.
