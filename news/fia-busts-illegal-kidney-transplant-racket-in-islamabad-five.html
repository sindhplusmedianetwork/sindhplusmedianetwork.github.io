FIA Busts Illegal Kidney Transplant Racket in Islamabad; Five Held

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency has busted an organised gang involved in illegal commercial kidney transplants in Islamabad, arresting five persons, seizing Rs1 million in cash, freezing properties worth Rs87.5 million and impounding three vehicles. Acting with the Human Organ Transplantation Authority, the FIA raided a premises in PWD Society, Islamabad, where recipient Mohammad Qasim had received the kidney of donor Ramzan Arshad in violation of the law. Three suspects were arrested on the spot and two others rounded up later, and a court granted seven-day physical remand of the suspects.

An FIR was registered under the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act, 2010, along with sections of the Pakistan Penal Code. Investigations revealed the recipient paid Rs3 million in cash for the transplant while the donor was promised Rs400,000, of which Rs340,000 was to go to the donor and Rs60,000 distributed among three agents as commission. The suspects performed transplants in residential houses without any medical degree or certificate, conducting tissue-typing tests illegally through a hospital and submitting donor samples under relatives' names with fake referral letters.

The network included agents, a doctor, a nurse, an anaesthesia provider, a medicine supplier, a laboratory test facilitator and a dialysis provider, operating in PWD Society, Bahria Phase-4 and Peshawar. The suspects had bought properties in Islamabad from illicit proceeds, including a 10-marla house in Media Town for Rs62.5 million and a 5-marla house in Jinnah Garden Phase-I for Rs25 million, whose transfers have been frozen. Teams from HOTA, the Islamabad Healthcare Regulatory Authority and DRAP inspected and sealed the illegal set-up, and raids are continuing for the remaining suspects.
