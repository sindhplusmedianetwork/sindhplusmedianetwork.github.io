Karachi's Hindu Community Concludes Nine-Day Navratri Festival

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Hindu religious festival of Navratri has concluded in Karachi after nine nights of worship, ending with the Vijayadashami and Dussehra celebrations on the tenth day. The Hindu community in the city worshipped Goddess Durga with religious devotion and respect, with special prayers and aarti arranged in temples. Devotees offered prasad at the temples of the goddess and prayed for peace, security and prosperity in the country, and the Pakistani flag was also hoisted on the occasion.

In the Hindu faith, Vijayadashami is regarded as a symbol of the victory of good over evil; according to religious tradition, the day recalls Goddess Durga's victory over Mahishasura and Lord Ram's victory over Ravan, which is why it is also called Dussehra. Ram Nath Maharaj said on the occasion that the festival gives the message of the triumph of good over evil. After the religious rituals of the tenth day, the festival came to an end; on Dussehra, processions were taken out at some places and immersions were carried out in the sea.

During Navratri, garba and dandiya dances also lent the festival a cultural colour, as the Hindu community expressed its religious and cultural traditions through traditional dress, music and collective dance.
