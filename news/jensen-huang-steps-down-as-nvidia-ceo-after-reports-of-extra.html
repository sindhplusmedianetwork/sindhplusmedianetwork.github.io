Jensen Huang steps down as Nvidia CEO after reports of extramarital affairs

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read

Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive of Nvidia on Thursday, ending his 33-year run at the helm of the AI giant he co-founded after revelations that he had two extramarital affairs, including with a woman accused of spying for China. The company's board said Huang had stepped down as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. Chief financial officer Colette Kress will serve as interim chief executive while keeping her finance role, and the board will consider internal and external candidates for a permanent replacement.

The board also named former Synopsys chief executive Harvey Jones as its first-ever board chairman, while Huang, who is the company's fourth-largest investor with a stake of about 3.61 per cent, will exit the board as well. Nvidia shares fell more than 10 per cent in premarket trading after the announcement, weighing on the broader market. Pressure had been mounting on Huang after reports said the 63-year-old, who is married, had an affair with a Chinese businesswoman whom US authorities suspect of spying for Beijing, and that the board had met on Wednesday to consider his ouster.

The controversy escalated further when the New York Times reported that Huang also had an affair with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's vice president of healthcare, who reported to him.
