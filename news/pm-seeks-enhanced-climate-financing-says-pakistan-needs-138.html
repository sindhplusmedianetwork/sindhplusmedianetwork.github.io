PM Seeks Enhanced Climate Financing, Says Pakistan Needs $138 Billion by 2035

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 12, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has told a COP31 session in Antalya that Pakistan needs about $138 billion by 2035 for low-carbon power generation. He said the country needs another $50 billion to replace coal-based power with solar energy. The prime minister called for enhanced international financing and affordable technology to support the shift.

He said clean energy now accounts for nearly 55 percent of Pakistan's energy mix, and outlined targets including making 30 percent of new vehicles electric by 2030. On the sidelines of the session, he met Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Kuwait's prime minister. The meetings reaffirmed bilateral ties and cooperation between Pakistan and the two countries.
