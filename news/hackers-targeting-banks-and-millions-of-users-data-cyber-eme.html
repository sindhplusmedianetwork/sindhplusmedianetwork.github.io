Hackers Targeting Banks and Millions of Users' Data, Cyber Emergency Team Warns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Cyber Emergency Response Team has issued an urgent cybersecurity advisory warning that hackers are increasingly using artificial intelligence and automated bots to target Pakistan's financial institutions, banks and key national installations. The advisory says banks' online systems, digital banking platforms and the private data of millions of users are the main targets. Hackers can use AI to show fake balances or chaos in accounts to spread panic.

Oil and gas installations, power grids, industrial systems, water supply and transport are also at risk. All public and private institutions have been told to immediately tighten their cybersecurity arrangements and firewalls.
