PM Announces Feasibility Study for Direct Pakistan-Maldives Flights

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has announced that he has instructed the civil aviation ministry to prepare a feasibility study for launching direct flights between Pakistan and the Maldives. Addressing the Pakistan-Maldives Business Forum alongside visiting Maldivian President Mohamed Muizzu on Thursday, the prime minister noted that no direct air link currently exists between Malé and either Islamabad or Karachi. He stressed that direct connectivity would boost tourism, trade and people-to-people contact between the two countries.

President Muizzu reportedly agreed to raise bilateral trade from the current $13 million to $200 million over the next two years. The announcement came during the Maldivian president's three-day visit to Pakistan. Officials say improved air connectivity is seen as a key step in deepening economic ties between the two nations.
