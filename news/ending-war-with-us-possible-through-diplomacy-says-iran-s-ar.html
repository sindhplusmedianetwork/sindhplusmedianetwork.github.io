Ending War With US Possible Through Diplomacy, Says Iran's Araghchi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said that ending the war with the United States is possible through diplomacy. In a statement, Araghchi said the exchange of messages with the US through intermediaries is still continuing. He said the diplomatic path to end the war and resolve the conflict is still possible and that Iran wants negotiations.

Araghchi said Iran wants an agreement that blocks the path to war in the future, but enriched uranium will not be transferred outside Iran. He said the Strait of Hormuz will remain closed until Iran's conditions are met and that a guarantee of US seriousness is required before negotiations are taken forward. The Iranian foreign minister further said there has been no change in Iran's position on the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) or in its nuclear doctrine.
