Why Are Mothers and Sisters Targeted with Abuse During Arguments?

When two people argue, the abuse often drags in a woman from the other person's family who has nothing to do with the dispute. Sexually abusive words about mothers and sisters are used to humiliate or silence others, in personal disputes as well as in debates on social media. This language is used not only by men; women use it too.

Recently, Bangladeshi actress Pori Moni made headlines after she abused a journalist's mother in foul language in public and threatened murder while on her way to court on November 5. The incident has raised an old question: why do the women of a family become targets when two people argue? Experts say such abuse is not just foul language; it is linked to how sexuality, power and family honour are tied to women.

A 2024 study published in the journal Behavioral Research Methods, covering 13 languages in 17 countries, found that words about sexuality, sexual organs and sexual identity feature prominently in common swear words.
