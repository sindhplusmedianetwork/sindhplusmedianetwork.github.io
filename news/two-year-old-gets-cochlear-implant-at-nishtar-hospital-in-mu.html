Two-Year-Old Gets Cochlear Implant at Nishtar Hospital in Multan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A two-year-old boy has undergone cochlear implant surgery at Nishtar Medical University and Hospital in Multan, marking a significant step towards providing advanced hearing treatment to children with hearing impairment in South Punjab. The procedure was performed on Turab Abbas at the university hospital's Department of Ear, Nose and Throat, with the cochlear implant donated through the support of NSUK, NANA and EMRA. The child's father, Dr Fazal Abbas, who works at Ward 7 of Nishtar Hospital, said he first became concerned when Turab was six to eight months old and did not respond when called.

After consultations with several doctors, the family learnt that the child had congenital hearing loss. He said he remained in contact with ENT surgeons at Nishtar Hospital and was delighted when he learnt that the hospital, in collaboration with charitable organisations, was planning its first cochlear implant procedure. The successful surgery had given the family renewed hope, he added.

Nishtar Medical University spokesperson Dr Jam Zubair said the implant had been donated with the support of NSUK, NANA and EMRA, and credited Dr Ehtesham Qureshi for his efforts in arranging the support.
