Asad Qaiser demands full medical reports of Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi in Adiala Jail be made public

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former National Assembly speaker Asad Qaiser has demanded that the complete medical test reports of Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi in Adiala Jail be made public immediately. In a joint statement with Tehreek Tahafuz Aeen-e-Pakistan chairman Mahmood Khan Achakzai and vice-chairman Raja Nasir Abbas, he said no negligence or delay in the PTI founder's treatment is acceptable. The statement demanded immediate implementation of the Supreme Court's decision on shifting Imran Khan to Shifa International Hospital for treatment in the presence of his personal doctors.

It also condemned the character-assassination campaign against JUI chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, saying his constitutional questions on governor's rule and emergency should be answered with argument and law rather than personal attacks. The leaders expressed concern over the suspension of hearings on Article 199 petitions in the Peshawar High Court following a Federal Constitutional Court decision, and over detentions under the 3MPO. They also demanded the release of detained elected representatives and political workers, including Imran Khan's sisters Aleema Khan, Uzma Khan and Noreen Niazi.

The statement reaffirmed that there would be no compromise on the supremacy of the Constitution, the rule of law, the independence of the judiciary and the fundamental rights of the people.
