Nvidia chief Jensen Huang faces probe over alleged link to suspected Chinese spy; admits infidelity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Nvidia has launched an investigation into its chief executive Jensen Huang over an alleged relationship with a Chinese businesswoman, Alice Huang, whom US intelligence reportedly believes spied for Beijing. The company's board is examining whether Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, after reports alleged a close relationship between him and the businesswoman. Huang has admitted that he was unfaithful to his wife, apologised to his family and said he takes full responsibility for his actions.

Nvidia's board said it found no reason to believe that company secrets or national security were affected. Huang remains committed to leading the company, according to the board.
