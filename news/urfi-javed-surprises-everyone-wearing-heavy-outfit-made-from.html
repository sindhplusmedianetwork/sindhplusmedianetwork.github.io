Urfi Javed Surprises Everyone Wearing Heavy Outfit Made From Bangles

Urfi Javed, famous for surprising people with her unconventional outfits, has done it again with a dress made entirely of bangles. The social media star's unique outfit is reportedly made from around 13,000 bangles and weighs about 20 kilograms. Carrying and managing such a heavy dress is certainly no easy task, yet Urfi once again succeeded in grabbing attention with her distinctive style.

Pictures and videos of the bangle outfit have been circulating on social media, drawing reactions from fans and fashion watchers alike.
