BISP audit reveals Rs24bn irregularities, 2.25m people removed from programme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A major scandal has surfaced in the Benazir Income Support Programme, with an audit report revealing financial irregularities of Rs24 billion. More than 2.25 million people were removed from the programme following the audit. The report identified 140,488 government employees and their family members among the beneficiaries.

Authorities said over Rs712 million had so far been recovered from those who had fraudulently drawn benefits. In light of a 2019 federal cabinet decision, payments to more than 800,000 fake beneficiaries were also stopped. Officials said action was being taken against those responsible for the irregularities.
