LPG availability, fair pricing among government priorities, says petroleum minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik said on Saturday that addressing public concerns over the availability and unfair pricing of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) was a government priority. Earlier this week, the government unveiled a new LPG policy under which locally produced gas will be sold through open auctions to cut out intermediaries. The minister said the new auction system would help ensure fair distribution of LPG, stability in the supply chain and energy security.

He said the government is aware of the difficulties faced by consumers because of high prices and shortages. The minister added that the new policy will bring transparency to the LPG market and protect consumers from overcharging.
