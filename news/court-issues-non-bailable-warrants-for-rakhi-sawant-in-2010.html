Court Issues Non-Bailable Warrants for Rakhi Sawant in 2010 Suicide Case

A court in Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh, has issued non-bailable warrants against Indian actress Rakhi Sawant in a 2010 suicide case. The case concerns alleged insulting remarks made against a man on her television show 'Rakhi ka Insaaf', after which he allegedly died by suicide. The additional district and sessions judge of the fast-track court issued the warrants on October 16 after her repeated non-appearance.

Police were directed to arrest her and produce her in court. Government counsel Mridul Kant Srivastava said the next hearing is on December 1. The case relates to the death of Lakshman Ahirwar of Hansari Prem Nagar, Jhansi, who died on November 10, 2010.

His mother Savitri registered the case, alleging that Rakhi Sawant used insulting and inappropriate words for her son in the October 2010 broadcast. The prosecution says Lakshman and his wife Anita had been invited to the programme over marital differences.
