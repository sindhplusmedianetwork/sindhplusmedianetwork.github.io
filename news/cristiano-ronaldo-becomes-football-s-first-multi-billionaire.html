Cristiano Ronaldo becomes football's first multi-billionaire, Forbes says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Portugal's world-famous footballer Cristiano Ronaldo has made history in the world of wealth as well, becoming the first footballer in history whose assets and income have crossed the multi-billionaire level, according to American magazine Forbes. The 41-year-old earned about £225 million over the past year. Saudi club Al-Nassr pays him about £488,000 a day, while he earned a further £42 million from advertisements and business activities.

Argentina's Lionel Messi is second on Forbes' list of richest footballers, with annual earnings of about £109 million. French star Kylian Mbappe is third with £75 million. Manchester City's Erling Haaland and Real Madrid's Vinicius Junior are fourth and fifth respectively, while young Spanish star Lamine Yamal is sixth.

Ronaldo and Messi have occupied the top two places on the list 12 times in the past 13 years, a sign of their sustained financial success.
