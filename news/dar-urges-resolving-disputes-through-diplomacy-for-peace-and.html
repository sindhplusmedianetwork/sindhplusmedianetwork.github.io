Dar urges resolving disputes through diplomacy for peace and prosperity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar said on Tuesday that disputes should be resolved through dialogue and diplomacy to achieve peace and prosperity. He was addressing the Margalla Dialogue in Islamabad. Dar said multilateral diplomacy and mutual cooperation are needed to tackle shared challenges facing the world.

He said the United Nations and other global institutions must play a more effective role in resolving conflicts. He added that the uneven application of international law fuels division and mistrust among nations. Dar said Pakistan strongly supports the peaceful resolution of all disputes.

He said internal strength and effective diplomacy are vital for national security and the prosperity of the people.
