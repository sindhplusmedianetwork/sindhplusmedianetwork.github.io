Woman Assaulted on Pretext of Job in Lahore; One Suspect Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kot Lakhpat police in Lahore have arrested one of two men accused of sexually assaulting a married woman after luring her with the offer of a factory job. The second suspect is still at large and police say raids are under way to arrest him. According to SP Model Town, the arrested suspect, Zeeshan, called the woman to Chungi stop on the pretext of arranging factory work for her.

He then took her to different places before luring her to the house of his accomplice, Waseem, in Ghulam Bhatti Colony, where the two men assaulted her together. The suspects later abandoned the woman at Thokar Niaz Beg, police said.
