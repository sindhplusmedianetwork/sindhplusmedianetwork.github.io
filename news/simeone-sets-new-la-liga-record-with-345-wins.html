Simeone sets new La Liga record with 345 wins

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Atletico Madrid's Argentine coach Diego Simeone has made history in Spain's La Liga, becoming the manager with the most wins in the league. After Atletico Madrid's 3-0 victory over Getafe on Sunday, Simeone's tally of La Liga wins as a coach reached 345. With that, he broke the record of 344 wins held by former Atletico coach Luis Aragones.

Simeone took charge of Atletico in December 2011 and has led the team to many major successes during his time in charge. His long tenure at the club has made him one of the most successful managers in Spanish football. The new record adds another milestone to a coaching career built over more than a decade at Atletico Madrid.
