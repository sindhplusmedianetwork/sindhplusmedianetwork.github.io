Cricket Australia names Michael Hussey coaching consultant for global events

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cricket Australia has appointed former Test cricketer Michael Hussey as a coaching consultant to help secure victories in global tournaments. According to Cricinfo, Hussey will support the team for the 2027 Ashes and ODI World Cup. He will also support the team for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics and T20 World Cup.

He will take up the role from the T20 series against England. Hussey has previously worked with Australia as a short-term batting consultant.
