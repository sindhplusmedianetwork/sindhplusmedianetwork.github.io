France unrest spills into second day as police battle protesters and looters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

French police battled protesters and looters for a second day on Sunday as the country reeled from a wave of cost-of-living demonstrations that led to about 2,000 arrests nationwide. Several hundred people gathered at Republique square in Paris following social media calls for a protest that authorities said had not been approved. Television footage showed the looting of a clothing store while bicycles were set alight nearby, and AFP journalists saw police firing tear gas and stun grenades as bottles and rocks were hurled at officers.

Some 550 of the 855 people arrested in Paris on Saturday remained in detention as police stations struggled to cope. The unrest followed Saturday's nationwide day of action, which drew between 550,000 and 800,000 people and left around 1,500 businesses damaged with losses estimated at hundreds of millions of dollars. One person died during the main march in Paris in what officials called a health-related case, while around 500 police, firefighters and paramedics were injured.

President Emmanuel Macron, who vowed that vandals and looters would be prosecuted, was set to address the nation on Sunday evening as far-right candidate Marine Le Pen said his administration was 'running on fumes, exhausted and disoriented.' US President Donald Trump also weighed in on social media, blaming immigration for the unrest.
