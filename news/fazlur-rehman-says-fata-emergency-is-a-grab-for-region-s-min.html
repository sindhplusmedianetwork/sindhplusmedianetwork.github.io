Fazlur Rehman says FATA emergency is a grab for region's mineral resources

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman has strongly criticized the emergency imposed in the former tribal areas. He said the emergency is a move to seize the region's mineral resources. He accused the government of making deals with the Americans, making them owners of national resources, and opening doors for their investment.

Fazlur Rehman said China and Russia also have reservations about the development. He accused the government of dragging foreign powers into the region. His remarks reflect growing political opposition to the emergency measures in the tribal belt.
