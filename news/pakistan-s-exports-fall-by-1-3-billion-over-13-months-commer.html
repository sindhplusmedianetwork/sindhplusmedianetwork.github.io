Pakistan's Exports Fall by $1.3 Billion Over 13 Months, Commerce Ministry Document Shows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's exports fell by $1.3 billion over 13 months, according to a Ministry of Commerce document. Afghan transit income dropped by $200 million in the same period. The document said exports to Central Asian states via the Iranian corridor rose slightly to $392 million.

Meat and pharmaceutical sectors were hit by higher transit costs and times. It said Afghanistan has shifted most of its trade and transit away from Pakistan towards Iran, Central Asia, India, China and the UAE.
