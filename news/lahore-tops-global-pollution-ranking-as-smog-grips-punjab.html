Lahore Tops Global Pollution Ranking as Smog Grips Punjab

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lahore has topped IQAir's live ranking of the world's most polluted major cities, recording an Air Quality Index (AQI) of 189 on Thursday. The reading falls in the 'unhealthy' category on the US AQI scale used by the global tracker. Delhi followed in second place with an AQI of 183, while Kolkata recorded 172, Kuala Lumpur 169 and Mumbai 166.

The ranking compares air quality across more than 100 major cities worldwide and shifts as monitoring data is updated. Within Punjab, pollution levels ran even higher: the province's official dashboard placed Rajanpur at the top with a hazardous AQI of 339 at around 7pm. Kasur followed at 266, Faisalabad at 251, Pakpattan at 245 and Vehari at 233, while Lahore itself stood at 198, ranking 12th on the provincial dashboard at the time of the update.
