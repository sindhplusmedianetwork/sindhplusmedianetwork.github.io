Governor rule in KP will end constitutionally after six months, provincial government to be restored: Tariq Fazal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Tariq Fazal Chaudhry has said that governor rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa will end constitutionally after six months and cannot be extended, after which the provincial government will be restored. Speaking on a private television programme, he said governor rule and emergency are in force across the province, but after six months the provincial assembly will be restored while the emergency will remain until terrorism is eliminated. He invited the opposition to call an all-parties conference against the emergency and invite the government so that the facts can be explained, adding that the government is still ready to settle all matters with the opposition through talks.

He said PTI leaders speak of negotiations in the media but evade when invited, and that no single PTI leader commands the agreement of all.
