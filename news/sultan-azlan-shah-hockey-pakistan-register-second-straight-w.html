Sultan Azlan Shah Hockey: Pakistan Register Second Straight Win, Beat USA 5-1

Pakistan registered another victory in the Sultan Azlan Shah Hockey Tournament, defeating the USA by five goals to one in their second match of the event. In the event under way in Malaysia's city of Ipoh, Pakistan played their second match against the USA, in which Pakistani players displayed excellent hockey and overcame the rival team by five goals to one. Neither team could score in the first quarter, and Pakistan's scoring began in the second quarter when Rana Waheed Ashraf scored in the 18th minute and Sufyan Khan doubled the lead by scoring on a penalty corner in the 21st minute.

In the third quarter, the USA's Sekai scored to make the contest 2-1, but Muhammad Imad immediately scored to make the score 3-1. In the final quarter, the Pakistani team succeeded in scoring two more goals, scored by Abu Muhammad in the 46th minute and Hanan Shahid in the 58th minute, and thus Pakistan won the match 5-1.
