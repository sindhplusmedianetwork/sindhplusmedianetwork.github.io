Metagenomic Testing: The New Test That Can Trace Almost Every Disease

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The genetic technology that played a key role in diagnosing COVID-19 is now becoming a breakthrough in medicine. Known as metagenomic testing, it allows doctors to detect diseases quickly and, in some cases, make timely treatment decisions. Scientists used the technique to identify the virus in patient samples as belonging to the coronavirus family.

Some members of that family had previously caused pneumonia and severe respiratory illness in humans, including MERS. It has also identified two cases of respiratory illness caused by parvovirus B19.
