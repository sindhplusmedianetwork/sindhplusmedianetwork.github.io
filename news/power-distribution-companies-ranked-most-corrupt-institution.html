Power distribution companies ranked most corrupt institutions, FBR second, says report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government has released a national assessment report on corruption risks, in which power distribution companies have been ranked the most corrupt institutions, with the Federal Board of Revenue second and the Capital Development Authority third. The report says that no institution fell into the very high or extremely high risk band. It identified 56 institutional typologies, 10 cross-cutting governance typologies and 18 regulatory typologies, but no institution reached the very high or extremely high risk band.

The distribution companies, with a score of 61.1, were declared the highest-risk institutions and were the only ones in the high risk band. Among the ten highest-risk institutions, the FBR stood second with a score of 54.4, the CDA third with 49.5, PASSCO fourth with 47.1 and the National Highway Authority fifth with 43.4. Pakistan Railways was sixth with 41.1, the Benazir Income Support Programme seventh with 40.1, Pakistan Post eighth with 39.9, Sui Northern Gas Pipelines ninth with 39.3 and the Evacuee Trust Property Board tenth, also with 39.3.

The findings present a ranked picture of corruption risk across major public institutions, with the power sector at the top of the list.
