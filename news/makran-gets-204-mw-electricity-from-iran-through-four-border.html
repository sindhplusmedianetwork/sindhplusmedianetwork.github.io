Makran Gets 204 MW Electricity From Iran Through Four Border Lines

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The energy minister has told the National Assembly that the Makran region is receiving 204 megawatts of electricity from Iran through four border transmission lines. The areas covered include Gwadar, Turbat, Panjgur, Tump, Jiwani and Pasni, with $12 million payable to Iran. The minister said the rates are approved by NEPRA, the CPPA is responsible for payments and Quetta Electric collects the bills.

He said load-shedding in the area is linked to local losses and the Makran region is now fully connected to the national grid. The written reply also disclosed that circular debt stood at Rs 1,675 billion on June 30, 2026, driven by line losses, low recovery and financial costs.
