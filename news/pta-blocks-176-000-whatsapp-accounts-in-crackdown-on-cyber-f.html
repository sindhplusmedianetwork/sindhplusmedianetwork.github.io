PTA Blocks 176,000 WhatsApp Accounts in Crackdown on Cyber Fraud

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority has blocked 176,000 WhatsApp accounts that were active on reissued mobile numbers, working with Meta to prevent cyber fraud. The action followed complaints that data from inactive or cancelled SIMs was being used for fraudulent activity on WhatsApp. The PTA has shared a list of about 10 million recycled numbers with Meta so that accounts linked to them can be checked and suspicious or unrelated accounts disabled.

The two sides have also agreed to share a list of 5,000 reissued numbers every month to prevent cyber crime. Telecom operators in the country currently hold about 16 million recycled numbers. The PTA said that if a WhatsApp account remains active even after the original user's SIM is cancelled, the person who receives the number, or a cyber criminal, can misuse it.

Officials said that during the past year Meta acted promptly on 82 percent of the complaints sent by the regulator and resolved the issues. The PTA said the step aims to improve digital security, protect citizens from financial and cyber fraud, and stop the misuse of reissued numbers.
