Israeli president asks Eisenkot to form new government

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Israeli President Isaac Herzog has formally authorised Yesh Atid leader and former military chief Gadi Eisenkot to form a new government. Eisenkot said Israel needs new leadership as much as it needs air to breathe. He said a broad national emergency government with wide parliamentary support, built on four basic principles, could be formed quickly and was necessary.

In the 120-member Knesset, 67 members recommended Eisenkot for the task, while 46 backed Benjamin Netanyahu. Eisenkot will have an initial 28 days to form a government, and Netanyahu will continue in a caretaker capacity until a new government is in place.
