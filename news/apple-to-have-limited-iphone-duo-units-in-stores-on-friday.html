Apple to have limited iPhone Duo units in stores on Friday

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apple has said it will have only limited walk-in units of the iPhone Duo, its first folding phone, available in stores on Friday. Online orders for the device sold out within minutes of launch, with delivery dates pushed back to January. Limited in-store stock will be available in the United States, Australia, Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom and Europe.

Buyers in China, India, Japan, France, the UAE and several other countries will only be able to order online. The constrained supply points to strong early demand for Apple's entry into the foldable phone market.
