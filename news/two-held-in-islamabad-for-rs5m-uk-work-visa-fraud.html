Two held in Islamabad for Rs5m UK work visa fraud

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Investigation Agency's Anti-Human Trafficking Circle in Islamabad has arrested two men for allegedly extorting more than five million rupees from a citizen on the pretext of arranging a work visa for the United Kingdom. The suspects have been identified as Mohammad Usman and Babar Hussain. According to the FIA, the suspects, in alleged connivance with a third accomplice, Tariq Bashir, lured the citizen with the promise of arranging a UK work visa for his wife and received a total of Rs5,042,000 and 200 British pounds.

Initial investigation revealed that the suspects allegedly also prepared a fake experience certificate for visa processing. Despite the rejection of the visa application by the UK Home Office, the suspects neither provided the visa nor returned the money. A case has been registered against the suspects under Sections 18 and 22 of the Emigration Ordinance, 1979, read with Section 109 of the Pakistan Penal Code.

The role of the third person and other aspects of the case will be determined during the investigation.
