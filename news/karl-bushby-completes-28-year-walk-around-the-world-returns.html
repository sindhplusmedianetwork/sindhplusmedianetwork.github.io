Karl Bushby completes 28-year walk around the world, returns home to Hull

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fifty-six-year-old British former paratrooper Karl Bushby has completed an extraordinary walk around the globe, arriving home in Hull 28 years after setting out from the southern tip of Chile in November 1998. His original plan was to cover roughly 57,936 kilometres through 29 countries across four continents in 12 years. Instead, the journey was prolonged by serious setbacks, including arrest on espionage charges in Russia, an encounter with a polar bear, and being held at gunpoint.

He also had to replace the trolley he used to carry his supplies after it broke down. According to the BBC, Bushby walked the final few kilometres from Hull City Hall to his mother's house, where she greeted him emotionally after 28 years apart. His mother Angela reportedly welcomed him with humour, asking where he had been all this time.
