The story has not ended, it is just the beginning, says slain anchor's co-host on husband's statement

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The co-host of slain anchorperson Rabia Ahsan, Amir Mateen, has reacted to the statement of her husband, saying the story has not ended and is perhaps only just beginning. He said the husband's account is that a bullet struck Rabia in the neck while he was playing with a pistol, and how Rabia lost her life awaits the police investigation. In a post on X, he asked whether our responsibility is only to tell the time and place of the funeral, and whether it is not necessary to say that the funeral was of Rabia, who was only 41.

He said Rabia's story is that of a hardworking woman who made her place despite family pressure, and that she had command over Pashto, English and Urdu, adding that he used to correct his own pronunciation from her every day. He said Rabia was a banker, a television anchor and a model, ran her own salon in Islamabad, and was also a communication consultant who could speak on any subject from politics to world affairs, and that above all she was a beautiful human being, a good friend and a colleague. Rawalpindi police have formally arrested her husband, Ahsan Tahir, in the murder case.
