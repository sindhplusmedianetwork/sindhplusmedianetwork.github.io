Muslim Americans seek greater voice as US midterms approach

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

WASHINGTON: Muslim and Arab Americans are seeking a greater political voice in the November 3 midterm elections, with candidates contesting congressional, state legislative and local offices across the US. Community organisers say the community is more politically mobilised than ever amid growing debate over American policy in the Middle East. The wars in Gaza and Iran have intensified questions about US foreign policy among Muslim and Arab American voters.

Candidates from the community are running at multiple levels, from city councils to Congress. Analysts say their participation could influence tight races in several states. The midterms are being closely watched as a measure of shifting political alignments in the country.
