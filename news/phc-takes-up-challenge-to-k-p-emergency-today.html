PHC Takes Up Challenge to K-P Emergency Today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Peshawar High Court is scheduled to take up petitions challenging the imposition of emergency and governor's rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa on Tuesday. A division bench comprising Justice Ijaz Anwar and Justice Muhammad Faheem Wali will hear the petitions filed by PTI Chairman Gohar Ali Khan and National Democratic Movement leaders including Mohsin Dawar. On Monday, Gohar submitted an application seeking to amend his earlier petition so it also challenges the resolutions passed by the National Assembly and Senate on October 14 and by a joint sitting of parliament on October 15 endorsing the emergency.

Filed through Shah Faisal Ilyas, the application argues that the presidential action under Article 234 is unconstitutional and unlawful, and that parliament's subsequent endorsements cannot validate a measure taken without fulfilling constitutional requirements. The petitioner maintains that Article 232 requires a resolution from the relevant provincial assembly for an emergency on grounds of internal disturbance, which the K-P Assembly never passed. He has asked the court to declare the parliamentary resolutions and the emergency notification null and void, and sought suspension of the notification until the case is finally decided.
