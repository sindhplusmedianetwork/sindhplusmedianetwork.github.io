Smog shuts schools and colleges across Punjab until Nov 17; private offices told to shift half their staff online

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has closed all public and private schools, colleges, academies and tuition centres across the province because of the dangerous smog situation. Under the notification, all educational institutions from nursery to class 12 will remain closed from November 13 to November 17, with online classes ordered in their place. Education Minister Rana Sikandar Hayat said the decision was taken to protect children from the harmful effects of smog.

Lahore remained first in air pollution, with its air quality index recorded at 1,045 and particulate matter at 910 in the morning, while Multan stood second with 806 particulate matter recorded. Dense fog also affected more than 10 flights and led to the closure of motorways. The provincial environment department directed private offices in four divisions to move 50 percent of their staff to work from home, with only half the staff to be called to offices and meetings between departments to be held online.

Health officials said smog has increased cases of dry cough, breathing difficulty, pneumonia in children and chest infections, with more than 35,000 patients reported at five major government hospitals in Lahore in a week.
