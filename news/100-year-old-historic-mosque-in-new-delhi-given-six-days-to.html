100-Year-Old Historic Mosque in New Delhi Given Six Days to Vacate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 100-year-old historic mosque at New Delhi's Race Course in the Indian capital has been issued a notice to vacate within six days. According to media reports, the mosque is located near Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi's official residence. Hurriyat leader Mirwaiz Umar Farooq said in a statement that if even a legally recognized mosque could not be protected, what guarantee remained for other mosques.

The Race Course mosque has been listed as waqf property in the official gazette since 1977 and is included in the heritage list, also featuring on the New Delhi Municipal Council's heritage list. The mosque's imam, Maulana Haroon, said the central government's Land and Development Office had indicated its intention to demolish the mosque next week.
