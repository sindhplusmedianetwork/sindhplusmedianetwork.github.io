At least 14 missing after Russian drone hits building in Kyiv region

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 14 people were reported missing after a Russian drone hit a building in the Kyiv region. President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said the people were missing after the strike. Rescue teams were searching the damaged building.

The strike was part of continued drone attacks on the region. The report gave no further detail about the condition of the missing people.
