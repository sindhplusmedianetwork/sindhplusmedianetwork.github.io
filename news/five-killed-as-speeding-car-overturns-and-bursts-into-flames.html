Five Killed as Speeding Car Overturns and Bursts Into Flames in Multan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Five young men lost their lives early on Monday when a speeding car went out of control, overturned and caught fire on Bosan Road in Multan. According to Rescue 1122, the car spun out of control near the Sabzazar Metro Station, struck a road divider, overturned and crashed into a wall before bursting into flames. The bodies were shifted to Nishtar Hospital, where they were identified as Muhammad Yasir, 22, Muhammad Muzammil, 23, Ramazan, 28, Wahab and Kasho Khakhi.

All five belonged to Shujaabad tehsil. Multan's City Police Officer Akhtar Farooq reached the scene along with a heavy police contingent. Rescue officials noted that similar overspeeding crashes had previously occurred on Bosan Road, Multan Avenue and Multan Public School Road.
