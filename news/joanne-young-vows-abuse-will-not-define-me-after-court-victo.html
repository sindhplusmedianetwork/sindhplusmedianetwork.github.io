Joanne Young vows abuse 'will not define me' after court victory

Joanne Young has said she is determined that horrific abuse suffered at the hands of her husband and other abusers will not define her, and that she will use her experience to try to help others following the convictions of four men against her. Four British men were convicted on Thursday, November 19, of raping or sexually abusing a woman who was drugged by her then-husband, in a case with echoes of that of Gisele Pelicot, the French woman who became a global symbol in the fight against sexual violence. Joanne, like Pelicot, decided to waive her legal right to anonymity after discovering that her husband at the time had repeatedly drugged her and advertised her for sex online to strangers over more than a decade.

Philip Young, a 50-year-old former local councillor, pleaded guilty in January to more than 50 charges against his ex-wife, including 11 counts of rape and charges relating to recording the abuse and publishing it online hundreds of times. Joanne said outside court that the abuse had cost her her life as she knew it and that the person she trusted most in the world had betrayed her in the most horrific way. She added that she was determined their choices would not define her, that she would now use her experience to try to help others, and that she owed it to herself to live the best life she could with her children and loved ones.

Police discovered the abuse only after an account linked to Philip Young posted bestiality images online in 2024, after which they seized his devices containing videos and images of the abuse.
