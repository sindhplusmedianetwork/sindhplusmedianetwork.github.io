Punjab Safe Cities Authority Intensifies Crackdown on Concealed Number Plates

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab Safe Cities Authority has stepped up its crackdown on motorists who hide or alter their vehicle number plates to avoid traffic enforcement. A spokesperson said surveillance cameras had recorded several cases in which drivers used their hands, clothes, tape and other objects to cover their plates or change how they looked. The authority said its artificial intelligence cameras can still identify vehicles and motorcycles despite such attempts, and alerts are passed promptly to the relevant police for legal action.

The spokesperson warned that no leniency would be shown to offenders, saying Safe City cameras are continuously monitoring traffic violations across the province. He said hiding a number plate is not only a traffic offence but a deliberate attempt to conceal identity and evade the law, and such practices can hinder crime prevention and the identification of offenders. Citizens have been urged to follow traffic regulations and keep their plates clearly visible and in line with the prescribed standards.

The spokesperson added that ignoring serious traffic violations can lead to accidents and the loss of precious lives, and the authority is using modern technology and surveillance systems to ensure compliance and protect citizens' lives and property.
