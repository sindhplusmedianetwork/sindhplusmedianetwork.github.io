US-Iran Talks to Continue in Doha Today to Pave Way for Agreement

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 31, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Diplomatic sources say talks aimed at paving the way for a US-Iran agreement will continue in Doha today. Qatari mediators held talks in Tehran on Friday that lasted more than 12 hours, and media reports described positive progress. A US official said two conflicting demands are blocking the negotiations.

The Trump administration wants Iranian concessions on the nuclear issue in return for reviving the negotiations. Iran, on the other hand, is demanding an end to the blockade, the release of its frozen assets, and permission to sell its oil. The Doha round is expected to test whether the two sides can narrow these gaps.
