Iran says strikes on US military assets were in self-defence

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's Foreign Ministry said late on Wednesday that Iranian forces struck US Patriot air defence systems and drone and missile launch platforms in self-defence. In a statement, the ministry said the strikes aimed to counter the source of attacks on Iran and the infrastructure supporting them. It reaffirmed Tehran's respect for the sovereignty and territorial integrity of countries in the region, urging their governments to prevent their territory from being used for attacks against Iran.

Earlier on Wednesday, Iran's army said its drones had targeted US Patriot systems and drone and missile launch platforms at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait. Kuwait condemned the Iranian attacks as a violation of its sovereignty, and Bahrain also said its air defences had intercepted Iranian aerial attacks. Tensions between Iran and the US have persisted since a US and Israeli offensive against Iran began on February 28.

Separately, an unknown projectile struck a tanker on Wednesday as it sailed out through the Strait of Hormuz, according to the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency, which said the crew were safe and the damage was not yet known. Three oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire in the eastern Strait of Hormuz earlier on Wednesday, leaving them unable to sail, Iran's army said. The United Arab Emirates' national oil company, ADNOC, also said one of its vessels had come under attack while transiting the strait on Tuesday evening, injuring 11 crew members and leaving another missing.
