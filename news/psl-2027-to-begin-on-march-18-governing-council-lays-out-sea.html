PSL 2027 to Begin on March 18: Governing Council Lays Out Season Plan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Super League Governing Council met virtually under the chairmanship of PCB Chairman Mohsin Naqvi to discuss preparations for the 2027 season. According to Express News, the council proposed starting the season on March 18, with the schedule to be announced by the PSL Cricket Committee after mutual consultation. During the South Asian Games, to be held in Pakistan from April 3 to 11, no PSL matches will be played in Lahore and Rawalpindi.

The final and the closing ceremony are likely to be held in Lahore. The meeting also reviewed proposals on players' retention and direct signing, with a players' auction proposed for January 23. No final decision was reached on holding the PSL 12 auction abroad.

The deadline for non-recoverable payments was set at October 31, and it was decided that the extra surcharge on late payments by team owners will end from the next season. The PSL financial model was also discussed in detail. Meanwhile, Islamabad United owner Ali Naqvi described the meeting as very positive on X, saying important financial and operational decisions were taken.
