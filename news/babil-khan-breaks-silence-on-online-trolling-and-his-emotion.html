Babil Khan Breaks Silence on Online Trolling and His Emotional Nature

Young Indian actor Babil Khan has broken his silence on the online trolling he faces and his emotional nature. Babil, the son of the late actor Irrfan Khan, has often been criticised on social media for his emotional behaviour and humility, which some users describe as overacting or pretence. In a recent conversation, he spoke openly about those comments for the first time.

He recalled the trolling that followed a video from a 2024 event, in which he repeatedly apologised after accidentally stepping in front of a woman's photograph. Babil said his reaction was completely natural, although he did feel some hesitation at the time, fearing people might give it the wrong meaning. Speaking about his six years in the industry, he said he will remain exactly as he is and has stopped caring what critics say.

He added that the pain still hurts, but its effect is now much less, and he has learned to separate himself from the negative experience and move forward.
