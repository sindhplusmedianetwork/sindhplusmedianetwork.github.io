PM warns banks: no leniency for slow disbursement of 'Apna Ghar' loans

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has warned that banks dragging their feet on disbursing loans under the 'Apna Ghar' housing scheme and other priority sectors will face strict action with no leniency. He was chairing a review meeting on easy financing for priority sectors. The prime minister said farmers, small and medium enterprises and the Apna Ghar scheme are vital for national development, and their financing must be ensured without delay.

He directed the concerned authorities to clear the bottlenecks holding up loan approvals. He also instructed that tax-related problems faced by SMEs and exporters be resolved on a priority basis. Officials at the meeting briefed him on the pace of disbursements so far.
