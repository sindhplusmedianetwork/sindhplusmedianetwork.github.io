Punjab to sue Salik over graft claims against Maryam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Punjab government has announced plans to take Federal Minister for Overseas Pakistanis Salik Hussain to court after he refused to retract his remarks against Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz. The chief minister had served him a Rs5 billion defamation notice, demanding an apology, after he alleged in an interview that he regularly received complaints of corruption in Punjab and criticised lavish government spending. In his reply, Salik said he would defend his position before any legal forum and would neither withdraw his remarks nor apologise.

He also said he could seek damages of up to ten times the Rs5 billion claimed by the chief minister, taking his counterclaim to Rs50 billion. In a parallel development, Salik served a defamation notice on Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari, accusing her of making false and damaging statements that portrayed him as seeking to influence the appointment of the deputy commissioner of Gujrat. Information Minister Bokhari said that since Salik is not withdrawing his allegations of corruption against the Punjab government as he has no evidence, the chief minister will take him to court for defaming her government.

She said she would respond to his defamation notice after receiving it.
