Senate Passes Rahmat-ul-Alamin Authority Amendment Bill Amid Opposition Protest

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Senate on Thursday passed the National Rahmat-ul-Alamin wa Khatam-un-Nabiyyin (peace be upon him) Authority Amendment Bill, officials said. The upper house also approved the National Textile University Amendment Bill, the Life Insurance Nationalisation Amendment Bill, the Protected Documentary Material Amendment Bill and the Centres of Excellence Amendment Bill. After the passage of the bills, opposition senators protested in the house, saying they had not been given a chance to speak.

The Rahmat-ul-Alamin Authority bill concerns the national body set up to promote the Prophet Muhammad's (PBUH) teachings of mercy and tolerance.
