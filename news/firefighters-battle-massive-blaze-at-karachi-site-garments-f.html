Firefighters Battle Massive Blaze at Karachi SITE Garments Factory

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A massive fire engulfed a garments factory near Metroville in Karachi's SITE area on Monday, prompting a large-scale firefighting operation. KMC Chief Fire Officer Humayun Khan said 12 fire tenders, two water bowsers and two snorkels were battling the blaze, with a Pakistan Navy fire tender also assisting. Plastic rolls stored on all three floors of the factory and heavy smoke inside made the operation extremely challenging.

The intensity of the fire weakened the building's structure, leaving it in a precarious condition, officials said. The Karachi Water and Sewerage Corporation declared an emergency at hydrants to ensure uninterrupted water supply, with around 300,000 gallons supplied to fire brigades. Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah took notice of the incident and directed the administration to mobilise additional resources immediately.

He also sought a detailed report on the incident and ordered a review of fire safety arrangements in industrial areas. The cause of the fire had not been established.
