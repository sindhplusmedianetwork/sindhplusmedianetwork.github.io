COVID-19 and flu cases surge as winter sets in; hospital admissions up 40% in Britain

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Health authorities are warning of a fresh surge in COVID-19 and seasonal influenza as winter begins. British media reports cited in the story say flu-related hospital admissions in Britain have climbed 40 percent. Experts say hospitalizations are rising, with the elderly and people suffering from chronic illnesses most at risk.

They have urged people to get vaccinated in time, wear masks in crowded places, and wash hands frequently. The resurgence has renewed concerns about pressure on hospitals during the winter months. Officials say simple precautions can significantly reduce the spread of both viruses.
