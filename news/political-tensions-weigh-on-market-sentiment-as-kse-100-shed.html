Political tensions weigh on market sentiment as KSE-100 sheds 1,223 points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange remained under selling pressure on Wednesday as investors continued to offload shares amid rising political tensions among the ruling allies over a proposal to create new administrative units across the country. The benchmark KSE-100 index settled at 176,294.33 points, shedding 1,223.41 points, or 0.69 per cent, on the day, after touching an intraday high of 177,719.05 and a low of 176,053.64. Topline Securities said the bourse extended its losing streak as persistent profit-taking weighed on sentiment, while PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari reiterated that the division of Sindh was unacceptable.

Engro Fertiliser, MCB Bank, Cnergyico PK and Fauji Fertiliser were the leading positive contributors, together adding 199 points, while Engro Holdings, UBL, Lucky Cement and Meezan Bank eroded 608 points. Market participation moderated, with total traded volume down 11.57 per cent to 742 million shares, though turnover rose 28.85 per cent to Rs25.97 billion. Meanwhile, oil prices edged lower in the international market, and US warplanes struck an Iranian missile launch site on Qeshm Island, further heightening regional geopolitical uncertainty.
