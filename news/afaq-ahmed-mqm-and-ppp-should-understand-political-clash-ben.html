Afaq Ahmed: MQM and PPP should understand, political clash benefits no one

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mohajir Qaumi Movement chairman Afaq Ahmed has said that MQM and the Pakistan Peoples Party should understand that a political clash benefits no one. Speaking at a press conference in Hyderabad, he said a third party would gain from conflict in Sindh. He said those who loot Sindh's resources are the ones who use the Sindh card.

He described the fair distribution of NFC funds as Sindh's key issue. He added that the demand for a Karachi province is the federation's slogan, not the Mohajirs'. He also said the people of Sindh are deprived of health and education.
