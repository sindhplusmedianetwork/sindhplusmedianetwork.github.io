Jan Rambo overjoyed as son Ahsan graduates from LUMS

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior Pakistani actor Jan Rambo has celebrated his son Ahsan Khan's graduation, calling it the biggest honour of his life. The actor, whose real name is Afzal Khan, attended the graduation ceremony at the Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS) along with his wife Sahiba Afzal. On the occasion, he shared a video on social media in which he appeared emotional and overjoyed at his son's achievement.

Jan Rambo said he feels like the luckiest father in the world today. He added that although he received many awards during his acting career, his son graduating from LUMS is the biggest award of his life.
