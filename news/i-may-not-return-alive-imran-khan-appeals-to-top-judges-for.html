'I May Not Return Alive': Imran Khan Appeals to Top Judges for Medical Care in Letter

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI founder Imran Khan has written an open letter to the chief justices of the Supreme Court, the Federal Constitutional Court and the Islamabad High Court. In the letter, he appeals for the implementation of court orders on his medical treatment and prison conditions. He says doctors have warned that he is highly vulnerable to a brain haemorrhage and cardiac arrest.

He says his heart has already suffered damage and that his blood pressure rose to 192-193 on November 10 during solitary confinement. In the letter, he warns that he may not return alive if court orders on his treatment are not carried out. He also raised his wife Bushra Bibi's deteriorating eyesight.

He said a Supreme Court order of August 18 to shift him to Shifa International Hospital within two days has not been implemented.
