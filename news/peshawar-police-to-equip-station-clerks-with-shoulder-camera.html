Peshawar police to equip station clerks with shoulder cameras

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Peshawar police have decided to equip muharrar (clerk) staff at police stations with shoulder-mounted cameras to improve public dealing and ensure transparency. The cameras will allow online monitoring of conversations between visitors and staff. They will be linked to the Safe City system, with monitoring also at the CCPO office.

The move is aimed at making dealings at police stations more open and accountable. Recorded footage can be used as evidence in case of staff misconduct. The step is part of efforts to strengthen oversight of day-to-day work at police stations.
