Kidnapped Youth Recovered in Karachi, Two Suspects Arrested

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police have safely recovered a 24-year-old man who was kidnapped at gunpoint after his car was intercepted near Water Pump Chowrangi in Federal B Area, Block 16, and arrested two suspects from the Malir Cantt area. Weapons and a vehicle were also seized from their possession, while two accomplices managed to escape. The incident occurred on the intervening night of Monday and Tuesday within the jurisdiction of Yousuf Plaza police station.

Four unidentified men travelling in a white Toyota Grande intercepted the car of 24-year-old Muhammad Ubaid, son of Muhammad Abid, and abducted him at gunpoint. SSP Central Dr Imran Khan told reporters that the victim's father contacted the police and a case was registered. Police launched a search operation by setting up checkpoints across the area, which led to the recovery of the young man and the arrest of two of the suspects.
