Pink rehri bazaar set up in Rawalpindi so women can sell their handmade goods

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A pink rehri bazaar for women has been set up on Liaquat Road in Rawalpindi. The bazaar was established on the directions of Deputy Commissioner Captain (retired) Nadeem Nasir. It has been set up under the Punjab chief minister's vision of giving women respectable employment and business opportunities.

Women will be able to sell their handmade products and other goods in the bazaar in a safe environment. The initiative is aimed at helping women become financially independent by giving them a dedicated place to run their businesses.
