Hajj 2026 Organisers Demand Immediate Return of Performance Guarantees

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Private Hajj scheme organisers across the country have raised their voice for the return of their performance guarantees, with 904 organisers jointly demanding the Ministry of Religious Affairs release the amounts without further delay. The organisers said the ministry had earlier set July 20 and later October 20 as deadlines for the return, yet the guarantees remain withheld. They pointed out that Hajj 2027 operations and bookings are already underway, and the delays are complicating their planning.

A key concern, they said, is the severe difficulty in transferring funds to Saudi Arabia, which has put many operators under financial pressure. Returning the five percent bank and cash guarantees, they argued, would provide them significant relief. The organisers urged the ministry to act on its own announced schedule and return the money at once.
