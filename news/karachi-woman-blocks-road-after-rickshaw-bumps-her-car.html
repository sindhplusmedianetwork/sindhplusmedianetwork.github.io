Karachi: Woman Blocks Road After Rickshaw Bumps Her Car

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At Gulistan-e-Johar Mor in Karachi on Thursday, a minor collision between a rickshaw and a car sent the female occupant of the car into a rage. The woman knocked down several motorcycles, slapped a man, sat in the rickshaw driver's seat demanding her own car's keys, and at one point climbed onto the bonnet of a moving car. Her commotion completely blocked traffic on the Rashid Minhas Road-Jauhar Mor route, leaving commuters stranded for hours.

Traffic began to move only after police arrived at the scene and brought the situation under control.
