Maldives President Muizzu to Visit Pakistan From Oct 20-22: Foreign Office

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Maldives President Dr Mohamed Muizzu will make a three-day state visit to Pakistan from October 20 to 22 at the invitation of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, the Foreign Office announced on Sunday. He will be accompanied by First Lady Sajidha Mohamed and a high-level delegation. During the visit, Muizzu will meet President Asif Ali Zardari and hold talks with the prime minister.

The discussions will cover trade, investment, health, defence, climate change and other areas of cooperation. It will be the first visit by a Maldivian president to Pakistan in 11 years. The two countries are also marking 60 years of diplomatic relations this year.

Officials said the visit would strengthen bilateral ties and open new avenues of partnership.
