Pakistan condemns Houthi attacks at UN Security Council, backs Saudi sovereignty

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has condemned attacks by the Houthi militia at the United Nations Security Council, reaffirming its support for Saudi Arabia's sovereignty and for peace and stability in Yemen. Pakistan's envoy told the Council that Saudi Arabia has the full right to defend its territory and its citizens against such attacks. He reaffirmed Pakistan's solidarity with Saudi Arabia and its support for efforts to restore calm in Yemen.

He added that lasting peace in Yemen could only come through a comprehensive political process. The statement was made during the Security Council's discussion of the situation in Yemen.
