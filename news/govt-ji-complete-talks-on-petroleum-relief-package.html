Govt, JI complete talks on petroleum relief package

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-i-Islami and the federal government have completed negotiations at the technical committee level, with both sides finalising draft proposals on public relief measures, JI Naib Emir Liaqat Baloch said on Thursday. Baloch, who heads the JI negotiating committee, said the government had prepared a plan to gradually reduce the petroleum levy before the next budget to the level from which it had originally been increased. He added that the final relief package would be announced after a meeting between Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and JI Emir Hafiz Naeemur Rehman scheduled for November 24 or 25.

The development followed more than two months of negotiations between the government and JI over the party's demand for abolition of the petroleum levy and relief for consumers affected by rising fuel prices, with committees constituted in early September and broad outlines agreed in the 10th round of talks on November 10. Baloch said the government had cited its commitments to the International Monetary Fund as constraints during the negotiations, while the petroleum levy remains a major source of federal revenue with approximately Rs1.677 trillion budgeted in receipts during the current fiscal year. He claimed Jamaat-i-Islami's campaign had secured relief of Rs146 per litre on petrol and more than Rs200 per litre on diesel, with Rs16.36 per litre in relief also provided through petroleum levy arrangements.

Baloch said the government had agreed to expand the prime minister's fuel-relief programme to include owners of vehicles with engine capacities of up to 1,000cc, while farmers owning up to 12.5 acres would receive relief on fuel used in tractors.
