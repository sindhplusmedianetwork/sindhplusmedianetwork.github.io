BBC Announces Over 100 Job Cuts and Closure of Two Broadcast Channels

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The BBC has announced more than 100 job cuts and the closure of two broadcast channels as it races to save £500 million ($668 million) over two years. BBC News director Rachel Jupp told staff on Tuesday of the 'net closure of just over 100 roles', worth £5.5 million in savings, as part of around 2,000 cuts planned across the corporation. Under the changes, the BBC will end broadcast transmission of BBC Scotland and the youth channel BBC Three, with both moving to the iPlayer streaming platform.

Radio 4 Extra will also close, with its archive programmes shifting to BBC Sounds. The changes are set to take effect in spring 2027. Director-general Matt Brittin said 'we need to make every pound work harder', pointing to licence-fee income that has fallen by roughly a quarter in real terms since 2017.

The announcement follows a June plan for 550 more role cuts to deliver £160 million in savings, though Scottish culture minister Mairi McAllan criticised the plans, calling the move to take BBC Scotland off air 'wholly unacceptable'.
