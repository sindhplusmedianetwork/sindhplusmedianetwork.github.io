Sindh CM Approves Thar Drought Relief Package Implementation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah has approved the implementation of the Thar drought relief package. During a briefing at the meeting, it was stated that 25 villages in the desert areas of Umerkot are included among the proposed disaster-hit areas. The chief minister directed implementation of a relief package worth Rs5.3 billion for drought victims.

A subsidy of Rs4,000 per acre on DAP fertiliser for farmers was also announced. The measures are aimed at supporting affected communities and farmers in the drought-hit areas.
