Two killed, one abducted by armed men in Balochistan's Qila Abdullah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people were killed and another was abducted by armed men in the Gulistan tehsil of Balochistan's Qila Abdullah district on Sunday, officials said. Unidentified armed men abducted Haider, son of Bismillah, from the area. Local residents confronted the abductors as they tried to leave, and an exchange of fire broke out.

Abdul Wali and Hanifullah were killed in the shooting, officials said. The armed men managed to flee with the abducted man. Police and other law-enforcement agencies have launched a search operation to recover the captive and track down the attackers.
