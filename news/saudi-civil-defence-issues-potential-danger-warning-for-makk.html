Saudi civil defence issues 'potential danger' warning for Makkah, Jeddah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's civil defence agency has broadcast warnings of 'potential danger' for four of its western provinces, including Makkah, home to Islam's holiest city. The alerts also covered the major Red Sea city of Jeddah, nearby Taif and the coastal oil hub of Yanbu. Residents were instructed to proceed to the nearest safe place.

The agency also repeated a legal warning against sharing images or information related to countering missiles and drones. The alerts were all rescinded minutes later. Later, the Saudi-led coalition fighting in Yemen announced that it had intercepted two missiles fired by the Houthis towards its western provinces of Taiz and Yanbu.

Yanbu is a major oil export hub on the Red Sea coast that has allowed Riyadh to bypass Iran's blockade of the Strait of Hormuz.
