Babar Azam to lead Pakistan as three newcomers join Sri Lanka Test squad

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 25, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has announced a 14-member initial squad for the two-match ICC World Test Championship series against Sri Lanka, with Babar Azam retaining the captaincy. Muhammad Taha, Sameer Minhas and Muhammad Suleman have been included in a Test squad for the first time. The squad also includes Abdullah Fazal, Ali Usman, Arafat Minhas, Azan Awais, Muhammad Abbas, Muhammad Ghazi Ghori, Rizwanullah, Saad Baig, Salman Ali Agha and Saud Shakeel.

A three-day warm-up match will be played at the Islamabad Club from November 4 to 6, with Saad Baig set to lead a PCB Patrons XI that includes six squad players and five fast bowlers. Two fast bowlers from the Patrons side will be added to the Test team after the warm-up game. The 26-year-old left-handed batter Muhammad Taha scored 730 runs in seven matches in the President's Trophy first-class tournament, including three centuries and a best of 225.

Muhammad Suleman was among the tournament's leading batters with 652 runs, while Sameer Minhas scored 405 runs in his first first-class season. The first Test will be played at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium from November 9 to 13, and the second at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium from November 17 to 21.
