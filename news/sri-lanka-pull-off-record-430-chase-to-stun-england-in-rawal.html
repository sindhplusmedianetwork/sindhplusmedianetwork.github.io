Sri Lanka pull off record 430 chase to stun England in Rawalpindi thriller

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sri Lanka produced one of the greatest run chases in one-day international history, hunting down England's massive 430-run target with two wickets in hand in the third match of the tri-nation ODI series in Rawalpindi. England had posted 429 for 4, built around captain Harry Brook's unbeaten 202 off 116 balls, studded with 21 fours and 10 sixes. Pawan Rathnayake led Sri Lanka's stunning reply with 130 and was named player of the match, while Dasun Shanaka smashed 97 off 50 balls and Janith Liyanage added a blistering 61 off 27.

Sri Lanka reached the target in 49.5 overs with eight wickets down, completing the second-highest successful chase in ODI history. The defeat pushed England to the bottom of the tri-series table with two losses from two matches. It was a famous night for the visitors, who never let the required rate slip away despite losing wickets late in the chase.

The Pindi Cricket Stadium crowd witnessed a modern classic that will be remembered among the greatest ODI contests ever played on Pakistani soil.
