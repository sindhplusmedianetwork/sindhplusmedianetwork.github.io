Arafat's Performance a Very Good Sign for Pakistan, Says Hasan Nawaz

Pakistan middle-order batter Hasan Nawaz has said that the way Arafat Minhas played was a very good sign for Pakistan, and the credit went to him. Arafat Minhas scored 59 runs and took six wickets against England, playing a key role in Pakistan's victory. Speaking at a press conference in Rawalpindi after the win against England, Nawaz said England was counted among the world's top teams and Pakistan would also join the top-level teams.

He said he had in mind that scoring 320 could win the match; he himself was set and felt 330 was possible, but unfortunately got out. Talking about himself, he said he felt he would have to improve his fitness because his reflexes seemed to have slowed down, and that needed fixing. He added that he had to improve his game and perform against big teams, and he was trying to meet that challenge.
