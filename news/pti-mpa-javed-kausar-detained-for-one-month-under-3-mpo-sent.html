PTI MPA Javed Kausar detained for one month under 3-MPO, sent to Adiala Jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf member of the Punjab Assembly Javed Kausar has been detained for one month under Section 3 of the Maintenance of Public Order (3-MPO), according to a notification issued by the deputy commissioner. The detention was ordered on the recommendation of the Rawalpindi city police officer and the District Intelligence Committee, and Kausar was sent to Adiala Jail. A heavy police contingent arrested him outside the Anti-Terrorism Court on Thursday as he arrived for a routine hearing in the GHQ attack case.

Kausar, who represents Gujar Khan, was taken into custody at the court gate and shifted to Civil Lines police station, with a large number of police personnel deployed outside the court ahead of his arrival. Police sources said his bail in cases related to the October 24 and 26 protests had been cancelled about a year ago, leaving him without protective bail, and that he was arrested on that basis. However, no formal arrest had been recorded against him in any case by late Thursday night, and PTI leaders continued efforts to secure his release.
