Al Ahly Sack Coach Ammouta After Home Defeat to Pyramids

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Al Ahly sacked coach Hussein Ammouta on Tuesday, hours after a 2-0 home defeat by Egyptian league leaders Pyramids left them seven points adrift in the title race. The decision ended the Moroccan's five-month spell in charge. During his time at the club, he won 11 of 14 matches in all competitions.

Al Ahly have won a record 45 Egyptian league titles. They are currently third in the standings behind Pyramids and Zamalek. The club will now look for a new coach to revive its title challenge.
