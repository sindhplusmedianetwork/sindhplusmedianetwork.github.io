At least 23 killed as truck collides with bus in India's Assam

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 23 people were killed and several others injured after a container truck collided with a bus in India's northeastern state of Assam on Friday, police said. The accident took place on a highway near Kaziranga National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site famous for its one-horned rhinoceroses. Police said the death toll was likely to rise as rescue work continued.

Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma said he was deeply distressed by the accident. He said he had sent a senior minister to the site to oversee relief efforts. Authorities were working to identify the victims and provide medical care to the injured.
