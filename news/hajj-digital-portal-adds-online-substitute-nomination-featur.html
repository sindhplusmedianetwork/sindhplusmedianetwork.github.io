Hajj digital portal adds online substitute-nomination feature

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Ministry of Religious Affairs has added a new feature to the Hajj digital portal to make pilgrimage arrangements easier and more fully digital. Under the new option, a successful applicant can nominate a close relative as a substitute online in case of an emergency. If an applicant passes away, a close relative may also submit an online substitute application on their behalf.

The substitute must already be registered on the portal, and supporting documents — such as medical reports or a death certificate — have to be uploaded online. Importantly, any Hajj dues already paid will be transferred to the substitute, so the payment does not need to be made again. Officials say the step is aimed at sparing families the hassle of manual paperwork during difficult circumstances.
