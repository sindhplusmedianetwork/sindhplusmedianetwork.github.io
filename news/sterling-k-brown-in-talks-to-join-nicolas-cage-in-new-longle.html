Sterling K. Brown in talks to join Nicolas Cage in new Longlegs film

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sterling K. Brown is reportedly in negotiations to star alongside Nicolas Cage in Paramount Pictures' upcoming Longlegs movie, expanding the universe of the 2024 horror hit. According to Deadline, Brown is being considered for a leading role in the film, which will see Cage return as the titular serial killer, while director Osgood Perkins is also returning to helm the project after writing the screenplay and signing on as a producer.

Cage will produce the film alongside Brian Kavanaugh Jones through Range Media Partners, Perkins and Chris Ferguson through Phobo, and Dave Caplan under his C2 banner, with the project scheduled to arrive in cinemas on January 14, 2028. The announcement follows confirmation earlier this year that Perkins and Cage would reunite for another instalment in the Longlegs universe, to be released by Paramount in a change from the original film's distributor Neon. Released in 2024, Longlegs starred Cage and Maika Monroe and followed an FBI agent investigating a series of murder-suicides in Oregon connected to a mysterious serial killer known as Longlegs, becoming one of Neon's biggest box office successes with approximately 128 million dollars earned worldwide.

Details about the new film's storyline and Brown's potential character have not been revealed, though the project is reportedly not a direct sequel and Monroe is not expected to reprise her role. Brown, a three-time Emmy winner and Oscar nominee, is also set to return for the third season of Hulu's Paradise, with upcoming film projects including The Gallerist opposite Natalie Portman, The Comebacker with Tom Hanks and Amazon MGM's Voltron expected in spring 2027.
