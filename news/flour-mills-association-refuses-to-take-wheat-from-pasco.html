Flour Mills Association refuses to take wheat from PASCO

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Flour Mills Association has refused to take wheat supplied by PASSCO, saying the wheat provided is of poor quality. At a press conference in Lahore, association chairman Asim Raza and Punjab president Shahid Ishaq said that after the Punjab government gave mills wheat at 4,500 rupees per maund, flour prices rose further. The rate of 10kg flour increased to 1,275 rupees, while chakki flour rose by 20 rupees to 200 rupees per kg.

The association's leaders said the country faces a wheat shortfall of 4.5 million tonnes, blaming PASSCO for it. They warned that if the shortfall is not ended, flour and roti prices could rise several-fold in the coming days. They also said that if the policy is not improved, a wheat crisis could emerge.
