Sindh Raises SPSC Exam Age Limit to 33, Increases Attempts to Five

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah, has approved major relaxations for candidates of the Sindh Public Service Commission's Combined Competitive Examination. The upper age limit has been raised from 30 to 33 years, and the number of permitted attempts increased from three to five. The cabinet also reduced the CCE 2026 screening-test qualifying marks from 40 per cent to 33 per cent.

It approved computer-based testing for single-paper examinations with immediate results. The cabinet further approved making the Mir Raza judicial inquiry commission's report public, with a subcommittee formed for its implementation. The decisions are expected to benefit thousands of aspirants across the province.
