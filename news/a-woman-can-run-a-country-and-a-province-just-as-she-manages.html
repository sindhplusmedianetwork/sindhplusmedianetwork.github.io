A woman can run a country and a province just as she manages a household, says Maryam Nawaz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif attended the eighth convocation of the Women University Multan and addressed the students at the ceremony. She said it was a matter of pride to see such a large number of educated daughters at a university in South Punjab, and described the students' parents as superheroes whose sacrifices deserved praise. She told the students that future commissioners, deputy commissioners and chief ministers would also come from among them, and urged them to keep their minds open, not to consider themselves inferior, and to use their abilities for the welfare and progress of the country rather than only staying at home.

Speaking about educational and financial independence, she announced that female students would start receiving scooties from December, that more applications had been received from girls than boys, and that more than 100,000 scooties would be given from funds of over Rs22 billion. She said 1,600 girls were receiving scholarships, the Punjab government was giving 100,000 free Google certificates, and interest-free loans were being provided to women who wanted to start businesses. She said appointments of vice-chancellors and heads of educational institutions were now being made on merit, that a woman vice-chancellor had been appointed at Fatima Jinnah Medical College for the first time, and that awards would be given to the deputy commissioner and district police officer of Nankana Sahib for their performance.

She said Punjab had been made safe for daughters, mothers and sisters, that injustice against any woman was intolerable, and she urged all women to download the Women Safety App and to call the panic button or 15 immediately in case of danger or harassment. She said as much development had taken place in South Punjab in three years as had not taken place in 80 years, and that the same development under way in Lahore was also taking place in Multan, Layyah, Khanewal and other areas of South Punjab. She said a woman can run a country and a province just as she manages a household, and recalled that she had faced harassment in jail during the previous government, while Punjab was now being praised at the international level.
