Twin Cities Brace as Smog Season Begins With Hazardous Air

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The smog season, which runs from October 15 to January 15, has begun with air quality reaching hazardous levels across Punjab and the twin cities. Lahore recorded an air quality index of 251, classified as extremely hazardous, while Gujranwala stood at 213, Faisalabad 155, Islamabad 131, Rawalpindi 176 and Multan 126. Officials in Rawalpindi Division announced that smog and smoke emissions would be monitored through drones and CCTV cameras.

Since October 1, authorities have shut down 20 stone-crushing machines, demolished 14 brick kilns, sealed 119 kilns, issued notices to 1,403 farmers for crop burning, and challaned 11,953 vehicles, imposing fines totalling Rs35.86 million.
