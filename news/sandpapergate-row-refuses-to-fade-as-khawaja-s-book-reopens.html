Sandpapergate row refuses to fade as Khawaja's book reopens old wounds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Australian captain Pat Cummins wants to draw a line under the 2018 ball-tampering scandal, but the row refuses to fade after former teammate Usman Khawaja reopened old wounds in his autobiography, claiming the whole team knew about the sandpaper plot. Cummins held a 70-minute press conference on Friday insisting Khawaja's version was 100 percent wrong. David Warner backed Khawaja and accused Cricket Australia's investigation of a cover-up.

Cricket Australia says it will not reopen its investigation into the scandal. Several key players, including Steve Smith and Cameron Bancroft, have yet to comment on the fresh claims. The scandal has haunted Australian cricket since 2018, and the latest exchange shows it still divides the team years later.
