27 Dow University Employees Found With Fake Degrees

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An audit of the Dow University of Health Sciences has found that 27 of its employees were working on fake degrees. The Sindh Assembly's Public Accounts Committee, chaired by Nisar Khuhro, took notice of the finding and ordered the recovery of all salaries paid to the dismissed staff. The committee also directed all public universities in the province to submit degree-verification reports.

According to the audit, of the university's 5,350 employees, only 1,945 have been verified so far, leaving more than 3,400 cases pending. The verification exercise was outsourced to the firm HRSG under a Rs7.36 million contract.
