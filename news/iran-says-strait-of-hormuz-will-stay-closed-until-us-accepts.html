Iran says Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until US accepts Tehran's terms, army says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has said the Strait of Hormuz will remain closed until the United States accepts Tehran's terms. Armed forces spokesperson Brigadier General Muhammad Akram Nia said Iran's forces have full control over the vital sea route. Speaking at a ceremony in Pishva, Tehran province, he said opening the strait depends on the US accepting Iran's conditions.

He said around 130 ships passed through the strait daily before the war, carrying about 20 percent of the world's oil as well as aircraft fuel and chemical fertilisers. The strait's future is tied to Iran's terms in the ongoing dispute and negotiations with the US, he said.
