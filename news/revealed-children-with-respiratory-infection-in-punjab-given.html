Revealed: children with respiratory infection in Punjab given antibiotics

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A survey has revealed that most children in Punjab suffering from respiratory infections were given antibiotics despite having viral symptoms. According to Gallup Pakistan, the Punjab Health and Population Survey found symptoms of respiratory infection in 6.5 percent of children up to the age of five. Gallup said most of these children, 66 percent, had high fever, while 48 percent had a cough, 40 percent had a sore throat and 38 percent had a runny nose.

Signs of pneumonia such as rapid breathing were seen in 26 percent of the children, while the chest sinking inward affected about 7 percent. Despite this, between 43 and 88 percent of the children were prescribed different antibiotic syrups, medicines and injections. Gallup also found that 2 percent of the children were given aspirin, a blood-thinning medicine that is not recommended for young children and infants.
