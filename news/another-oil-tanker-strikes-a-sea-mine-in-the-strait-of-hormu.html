Another Oil Tanker Strikes a Sea Mine in the Strait of Hormuz, Iran's Navy Says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran's navy says another oil tanker has struck a sea mine in the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire, leaving the vessel unable to move. The navy said the incident took place on Monday morning in a restricted maritime area east of the strait and described the vessel as a violator. The tanker was left stranded after the blast and the fire, according to the statement.

Iran's navy said it was continuously monitoring and controlling shipping east of the strait. The Revolutionary Guards had earlier claimed that two tankers had hit mines in the same waterway. The Guards also said six tankers had caught fire in the strait since Sunday.
