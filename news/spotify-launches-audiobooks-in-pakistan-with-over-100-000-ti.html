Spotify Launches Audiobooks in Pakistan With Over 100,000 Titles

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Spotify has introduced audiobooks in Pakistan, allowing users to listen to more than 100,000 English-language titles. The service, called Audiobooks+, became available from November 4 and offers both Free and Premium subscribers 15 hours of audiobook listening a month for Rs489. The subscription renews monthly, with unused hours resetting at the start of each billing cycle.

Listeners who use up their allowance can buy 10 extra hours for Rs579, or purchase individual audiobook titles without a subscription. The company has added a dedicated Audiobooks section in its app, where users can browse, search and discover books. Features include automatic bookmarking, a sleep timer, offline downloads and Page Match, which lets listeners switch between a physical or digital book and its audiobook version.

The launch in Pakistan is part of a wider rollout announced at the Frankfurt Book Fair on October 7, with Spotify planning to offer audiobooks in more than 180 markets by the end of 2026. Spotify's vice-president of Audiobooks, Owen Smith, said audiobooks help many people find their way back to reading on the commute, on a walk, or at the end of a long day.
