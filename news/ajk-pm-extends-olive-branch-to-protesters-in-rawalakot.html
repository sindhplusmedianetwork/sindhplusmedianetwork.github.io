AJK PM extends olive branch to protesters in Rawalakot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Azad Jammu and Kashmir Prime Minister Iftikhar Gillani on Friday offered to discuss the demands of protesters in Rawalakot within the constitutional and legal framework, urging them to dissociate themselves from armed groups. Addressing a public meeting, the prime minister said the government was prepared to take every possible measure, in the light of a recent resolution of the assembly, to restore peace and stability in the region. He said some people publicly distanced themselves from armed groups but continued to provide them space, which was detrimental to efforts to restore peace.

Referring to some of the protesters' demands, he said the government was planning to establish catheterisation laboratories and stroke centres in different parts of AJK, alongside road improvement projects, the Kashmir Express electric bus service and employment and self-employment opportunities for young people. Mr Gillani also outlined a 100-day action plan forming part of a five-year strategy covering healthcare, education, employment, tourism, transport, digital governance and urban development, with recruitment in government departments to be carried out through third-party agencies on merit and interest-free loans for young people to encourage entrepreneurship. Legislative Assembly Speaker Chaudhry Tariq Farooq said the people of Poonch should regain their right to representation through elections on the seven Assembly seats that remained unfilled, while Energy and Water Resources Minister Saqib Majeed Raja said unrest and uncertainty in AJK since 2023 had affected the region's administrative system, economy and everyday life.

He said lasting solutions to public grievances lay in dialogue, democratic processes and effective performance by elected representatives rather than protests, confrontation and vandalism.
