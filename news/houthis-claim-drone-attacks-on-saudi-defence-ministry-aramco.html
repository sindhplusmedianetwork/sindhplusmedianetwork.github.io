Houthis Claim Drone Attacks on Saudi Defence Ministry, Aramco Sites

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Yemen's Houthi movement said on Thursday that it had launched a series of attacks on targets inside Saudi Arabia. The group claimed strikes on the Saudi defence ministry in Riyadh, Saudi Aramco facilities in the Red Sea city of Yanbu, and King Fahd International Airport in Dammam. Saudi authorities said two people were killed in the attack on the Dammam airport.

The Saudi-led coalition said its forces intercepted and destroyed four drones over the Riyadh and Madina regions. The escalation rattled energy markets, with Brent crude climbing to its highest level since May on fears that the conflict could spread further across the Middle East.
