No flood risk in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa from Tarbela incident, says irrigation adviser

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa irrigation adviser Dr Arshad Khan has said there is no flood risk in the province from the Tarbela incident. He gave the assurance according to WAPDA data. He said the situation is fully under control.

Water release from Tarbela was recorded at 144,200 cusecs at 7pm on November 9. The dam's water level is being lowered to 1,490 feet. The process is expected to complete by noon the next day.
