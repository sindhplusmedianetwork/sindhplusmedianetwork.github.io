Mirpur police recover over Rs2 billion for loan, visa fraud victims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mirpur Police recovered more than Rs2 billion and returned it to victims in a crackdown on illegal moneylenders and agents involved in visa fraud. Senior Superintendent of Police Khurram Iqbal said an extensive campaign had been launched against people accused of defrauding citizens on the pretext of arranging overseas visas. He said operations against visa fraudsters were being expanded.

He urged victims of illegal lending to approach police rather than pay further interest to loan sharks. The recovered money, worth more than Rs2 billion, was handed back to the victims. The crackdown targets illegal moneylenders and agents running visa fraud in the area.
