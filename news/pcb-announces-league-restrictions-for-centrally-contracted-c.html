PCB Announces League Restrictions for Centrally Contracted Cricketers

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Cricket Board has announced major league restrictions for centrally contracted cricketers. Director of High Performance Aaqib Javed said players who play Test cricket will not be allowed to participate in any T20 league other than the Pakistan Super League. Test specialists will, however, be allowed to play in the County Championship.

ODI-format players can take part in one league in addition to the PSL. Players in the ODI and T20 categories can play in two overseas leagues, while T20 specialists can feature in three overseas leagues alongside the PSL. Domestic match requirements were also set by category, with contracted players to receive payments after meeting them.
