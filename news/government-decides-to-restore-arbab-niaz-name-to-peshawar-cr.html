Government decides to restore Arbab Niaz name to Peshawar cricket stadium

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government has decided to rename Peshawar's Imran Khan Cricket Stadium. The stadium's old name, Arbab Niaz Cricket Stadium, will be restored. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi said the stadium will not be named after a party personality.

Sources said the name will be changed before the PSL. The decision restores the venue's former name. No date for the formal renaming was given in the report.
