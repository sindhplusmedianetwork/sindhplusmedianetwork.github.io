LPG Hits Rs500/kg in Punjab and KP, Far Above Official Rs279 Rate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Liquefied petroleum gas prices have surged to record levels across Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with the fuel selling at Rs500 per kilogram in both provinces. In hilly and mountainous areas, the price has climbed to Rs600 per kilogram, according to LPG Distributors Association chairman Irfan Khokhar. A domestic LPG cylinder now costs Rs6,000 in Punjab and KP and Rs7,200 in hilly regions, nearly double the official rates.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority had fixed the LPG price at Rs279 per kilogram for October, with the domestic cylinder priced at Rs3,350. Khokhar criticised the regulator for remaining a silent spectator while consumers paid far above the notified rates. He urged the government to take immediate steps to control prices, including using the Sui Southern Gas Company terminal and arranging urgent LPG imports to improve supply.

The price spike has added to the burden on households that depend on LPG for cooking, especially in areas where piped gas is unavailable.
