SICVD Provided Free Emergency Heart Treatment to Over 22,000 Patients in 10 Months

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Institute of Cardiovascular Diseases has released its performance report for the first 10 months of 2026. According to the report, the institute provided emergency primary angioplasty and angiography to 22,639 heart-attack patients between January and October 2026. It also performed 1,626 free open-heart operations during the same period.

The institute's 30 chest pain units handled about 464,000 emergency cases. In total, the institute provided 3.77 million free medical services during the 10 months.
