Arrested suspect says voices told him to kill Trump

By Sindh Plus Media Network

US Secret Service officers arrested a man acting suspiciously in a parked car near the White House on Thursday night. A case was registered against him after officers found several weapons in the vehicle. US officials identified the suspect as Ronny Yordrick Guanipa Chirinos, a Venezuelan national who had been living illegally in the United States since 2024.

He allegedly told investigators that voices in his head told him to come to Washington and kill President Donald Trump. A search of the stolen car recovered three firearms and dozens of rounds. The suspect remains in custody as authorities continue their investigation into the incident.
