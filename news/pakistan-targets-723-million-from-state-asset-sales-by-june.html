Pakistan Targets $723 Million From State Asset Sales by June 2027

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan expects to raise up to $723 million by June 2027 through the privatisation of state-owned enterprises and the management of airports, Privatisation Adviser Muhammad Ali said in an interview with Arab News on Friday. He said the government is working on a major privatisation plan to attract private investment and reduce the financial burden on the national exchequer. The move is part of reforms under Pakistan's $7 billion International Monetary Fund programme, which aims to cut the losses of state enterprises, improve public services and revive investment in an economy hit by high energy prices and long-standing economic and structural problems.

The government aims to complete at least six privatisation deals, including the sale of three power distribution companies and two financial institutions, besides a long-term agreement to hand over the management of Islamabad International Airport to the private sector. The power companies supply electricity to Faisalabad, Gujranwala and Islamabad, while the financial institutions are Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited and House Building Finance Corporation. Turkish and Saudi investors have shown interest in the power companies, and Muhammad Ali said the government hopes to raise about Rs150 to 200 billion, or $542 to 723 million, during the current fiscal year.
