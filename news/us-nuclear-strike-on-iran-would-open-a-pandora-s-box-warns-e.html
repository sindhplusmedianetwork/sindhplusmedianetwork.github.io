US Nuclear Strike on Iran Would Open a Pandora's Box, Warns Ex-Ambassador

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former ambassador Jameel Ahmad Khan has warned that a United States nuclear strike on Iran would open a Pandora's box, after which any country could attack another with nuclear weapons. Speaking on a Samaa News programme, he argued that under the US constitution, Trump's authority as commander-in-chief and to issue executive orders would not diminish even if he lost the election. He said the seven-point deal includes a proposal to begin reducing American forces from the region, but that Washington would not agree easily.

According to him, Trump keeps demanding the elimination of enriched material — a controversial point — and there is a big Israeli game behind it. He described Iran as having stood firm against Israel for seven months and claimed the country is now militarily stronger than it was on February 28, having expanded its missile capability and range over eight months. He predicted Trump would lose the House in the midterm elections while facing a tough contest for three seats in the upper house.
