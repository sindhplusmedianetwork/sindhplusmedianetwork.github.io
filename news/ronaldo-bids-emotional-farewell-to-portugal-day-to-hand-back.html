Ronaldo bids emotional farewell to Portugal: 'Day to hand back No. 7 shirt'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cristiano Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football on Thursday 5 November 2026, ending a 23-year Portugal career in which he scored a record 146 goals in 234 matches. Taking to social media, the 41-year-old said the day had come to hand back the number seven shirt of the national team. He said he never felt the shirt belonged to him alone, but for 23 years he had the privilege and responsibility of wearing it and carrying it into stadiums around the world.

Ronaldo said the decision was not easy, because some decisions are made with the heart as well as the mind, and a journey that began in August 2003 has now come to an end. The five-time Ballon d'Or winner won Euro 2016 with Portugal and two Nations League titles in 2019 and 2025. He also became the oldest player to score in a FIFA World Cup after representing Portugal for a sixth time this year.

Portugal exited the 2026 World Cup in the round of 16 after a loss to Spain, who later became world champions. Ronaldo will continue playing club football with the Saudi club Al-Nassr until June 2027.
