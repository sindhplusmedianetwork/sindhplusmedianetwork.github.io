Hangu: Remote-Controlled Bomb Near High School Defused, Major Attack Foiled

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Hangu foiled a planned major attack on Tuesday by defusing a remote-controlled bomb planted near Darsamand High School. The Bomb Disposal Squad team, led by SHO Doaba Waheed Noor Khan, safely neutralized the device. The explosive device contained approximately five to six kilograms of explosives, officials said.

The area was cordoned off before the disposal operation was carried out to keep residents safe. DPO Hangu Salahuddin Kundi has ordered clearance operations to continue in the area. The operations aim to eliminate any possible remaining threats and ensure the safety of the public.
