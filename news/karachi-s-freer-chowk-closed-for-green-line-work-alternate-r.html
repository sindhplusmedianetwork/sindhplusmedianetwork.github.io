Karachi's Freer Chowk Closed for Green Line Work, Alternate Routes Announced

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi's traffic police have announced alternate routes after Freer Chowk (Naz Plaza) on MA Jinnah Road was closed to traffic because of Green Line project development work. According to the traffic police spokesperson, traffic heading from Numaish Chowk towards the Coast Guard via Freer Chowk can reach its destination by taking the Garden Chowk (Mobile Market) route. Similarly, citizens travelling from Empress Market towards the Coast Guard or Numaish Chowk via Freer Chowk can also use the Garden Chowk alternate route.

Commercial traffic going from Numaish towards the Coast Guard and Garden will turn right at the Capri light signal, pass Saddar Hospital, Empress Market and Freer Chowk on the left, and take MA Jinnah Road to Garden Chowk, where a U-turn will lead them onward. Commercial vehicles from Saddar heading to Numaish, the Coast Guard, Soldier Bazaar and Garden will take MA Jinnah Road from the left of Freer Chowk and make a U-turn at Garden Chowk. The spokesperson said personnel will be deployed on the ground to guide citizens and keep traffic moving, urging people to use the alternates to avoid delays and to call the traffic police helpline 1915 or listen to FM 88.6 for the latest traffic situation.
