Motorola Releases Expanded List of Smartphones Eligible for Android 17 Update

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Motorola has expanded its list of smartphones eligible for the Android 17 update. The company published a more comprehensive list through its Brazil website after an initial list in August omitted many global and US models. The new list includes numerous devices from the Razr, Edge and Moto series.

Motorola confirmed that the update is being rolled out in phases. Availability of the update will vary by region and device, the company said.
