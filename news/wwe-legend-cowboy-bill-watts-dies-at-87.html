WWE legend 'Cowboy' Bill Watts dies at 87

WhatsApp Facebook X

Legendary WWE wrestler 'Cowboy' Bill Watts has died at the age of 87. Born in the American state of Oklahoma, Watts began his wrestling career in the 1960s and later became a successful promoter. He led Mid-South Wrestling in the 1970s and 1980s, where famous wrestlers such as Shawn Michaels and Ted DiBiase also worked.

Watts is counted among the founders and innovators of the wrestling industry, known for presenting weekly dramatic storylines and giving wrestlers distinct identities. His promotion helped shape the careers of several stars who went on to wider fame. In recognition of his services to wrestling, he was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2009.
