Oil Prices Fall Sharply as US Crude Drops Below $80 a Barrel

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Global crude oil prices fell sharply on Monday, with US benchmark West Texas Intermediate dropping more than 4 percent to $79.95 a barrel, below $80 for the first time since August. Brent crude, the international benchmark, fell 4.1 percent to $88.75 a barrel. News agencies attributed the drop to investor concerns about rising global supply and excess stockpiles.

Increased supply from the Middle East and the restart of a key Libyan oil pipeline added to the downward pressure on prices. The report noted that the Strait of Hormuz has been closed since the US and Israeli attacks on Iran, but shipping is gradually recovering as attacks have eased.
