Division of Sindh Unacceptable Under Any Circumstances, Says Nasir Hussain Shah

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Local Government Minister Nasir Hussain Shah has said the division of Sindh is unacceptable under any circumstances. Speaking to journalists in Hyderabad, he said no compromise can be made on Sindh's unity and the province is one and will remain one. He said the Pakistan Peoples Party is not afraid of a referendum on the issue.

However, he warned that if any decision is taken without taking the party into confidence, the PPP will stand with the people of Sindh. The minister's remarks came amid continuing debate over proposals for new provinces and a possible referendum.
