No threat to Tarbela Dam's integrity after tunnel accident, WAPDA says

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

WAPDA has said there is no threat to the integrity of the main Tarbela Dam or any of its critical structures after a rock collapse flooded a tunnel at the 1,530-megawatt Tarbela Fifth Extension project. WAPDA Chairman Lt Gen (retd) Muhammad Saeed visited the site on November 9 and 10 and ordered a departmental inquiry along with immediate rehabilitation measures. Construction work on the project has been suspended indefinitely since the breach on Monday, which also raised flows in the River Indus.

The Ministry of Water Resources has also sought a comprehensive report from WAPDA on the incident.
