England Topple Spain as Belgium Smash Italy to Reach Nations League Quarters

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

England ended Spain's 44-game unbeaten run in competitive matches with a 3-2 win in Madrid on Sunday. Harry Kane scored from the penalty spot, Ezri Konsa struck after the break and Ben White made it 3-0 before England held on with 10 men. Belgium thumped Italy 4-1 in Brussels to join France in the Nations League quarter-finals, with Jeremy Doku among the scorers.

France beat Turkiye 2-1 to top their group ahead of Friday's quarter-final draw in Nyon. The results set up an open race for the Nations League title as the quarter-final line-up took shape.
