US Costs of Iran War Rise Past $47 Billion, Pentagon Estimate Shows

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States military has spent more than $47 billion so far on the war in Iran, according to Pentagon data obtained by Reuters, a figure more than 25 per cent higher than the Defence Department's July estimate of $37.5 billion. The latest estimate covers the operational costs of the war, including a projected cost of more than $29 billion to replace munitions used in the conflict and more than $6 billion to replace military equipment and so-called platform losses, including aircraft and many drones lost during the fighting. The Pentagon has not shared the estimate publicly and did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

President Donald Trump launched the war jointly with Israel in February, predicting it would last a short time, but an April ceasefire gave Iran time to restore underground storage facilities and bring some defences, missiles and drones back online. Trump has since imposed a naval blockade on Iran to cut off its oil export revenue and has held out the possibility of restarting combat operations after the midterm elections, while US officials say the Pentagon has been planning to resume heavy fighting if ordered to do so. The figures do not clearly account for the expected cost of repairing damage to US bases from Iranian missile and drone attacks.

The disclosure comes as Republicans face the political fallout of the unpopular conflict ahead of the November 3 election that will decide control of Congress, with the war's impact on global energy costs weighing on support for Republican candidates.
