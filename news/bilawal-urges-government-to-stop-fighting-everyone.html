Bilawal urges government to stop 'fighting everyone'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has urged the federal government and political parties to set aside differences and engage all political, social and provincial stakeholders to tackle the economic crisis. He was speaking at the groundbreaking of a 600-bed hospital in Larkana on Friday. Bilawal warned that the fallout from the Iran war was placing an unprecedented burden on ordinary Pakistanis.

He also urged parties to join the proposed Parliamentary Committee on Electoral Reforms. He rejected claims that counterterrorism concerns were being used as a pretext to remove the elected PTI government in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He said all sides should work together to face the economic crisis.
