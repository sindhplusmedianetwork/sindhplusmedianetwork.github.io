UK man drugged wife and had strangers rape her; trial under way in Manchester

A 60-year-old man in the United Kingdom is standing trial at Manchester Crown Court accused of drugging his wife with sedatives and inviting strangers to rape her while she was unconscious. Prosecutors told the court the woman had no idea for a long time what was happening to her, believing the couple had not been intimate for nine years. They said the husband was living a double life: outwardly a contented family man, while secretly drugging his wife, inviting men to their home and filming the assaults.

Police seized his mobile phone and recovered more than a million photos and videos, which exposed the alleged crimes and his contacts with other men. The investigation began in October 2025 after undercover journalists spotted images and videos of attacks on unconscious women in a private Telegram group, and a user was traced through a phone number linked to the husband. The husband has admitted 62 offences and four other men have admitted various offences, while nine defendants, including a paramedic, a taxi driver and an amateur football coach, are now on trial denying the charges.

The prosecution said the incidents were clear sexual crimes, not consensual activity. The case echoes a similar 2024 trial in France; unlike that case, the Manchester victim has chosen to remain anonymous and her identity is protected by law.
