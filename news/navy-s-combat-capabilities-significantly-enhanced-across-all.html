Navy's combat capabilities significantly enhanced across all operational domains, says Admiral Ashraf

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief of the Naval Staff Admiral Naveed Ashraf said the Pakistan Navy has significantly enhanced its combat capabilities. He said the enhancement covers all operational domains. Admiral Ashraf was addressing the graduation parade of the Pakistan Air Force Academy Asghar Khan in Risalpur.

He said the navy is modernising its fleet with state-of-the-art warships. The modernisation also includes Hangor-class submarines and indigenously developed long-range weapons. Unmanned systems are also part of the fleet modernisation.
