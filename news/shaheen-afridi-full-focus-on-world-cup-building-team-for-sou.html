Shaheen Afridi: Full Focus on World Cup, Building Team for South African Conditions

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's white-ball captain Shaheen Shah Afridi said on Saturday that the national team's full focus was on the World Cup, with the squad being shaped around South Africa's playing conditions. Addressing a press conference in Rawalpindi ahead of the tri-nation series, Afridi said Pakistan had played the recent T20 series well and that the management was trying to build the team on the back of the last series. He declined to discuss Test cricket, keeping his remarks to the white-ball formats.

According to Samaa TV, Afridi praised Salman Agha as a fine cricketer coming off a first-class triple century, saying the batter understood his role and that the team expected strong performances from him. The captain called Babar Azam the backbone of the side, insisting there was no pressure on the senior batter, who had always scored runs for Pakistan, and noted that Babar's captaincy experience was a major benefit to the dressing room. Afridi said the squad enjoyed complete harmony with everyone focused on winning the World Cup, adding that Pakistan possessed a strong batting order capable of chasing down targets of 350 or 400 depending on conditions.

He also stressed that young players were being given opportunities gradually and underlined that Saim Ayub's bowling would be badly needed in one-day internationals.
