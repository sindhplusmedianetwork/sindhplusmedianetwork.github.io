Kohli equals Tendulkar's record for most ducks in ODI cricket

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Virat Kohli was dismissed for a four-ball duck in the fourth ODI against New Zealand at Mount Maunganui, equalling Sachin Tendulkar's record of 20 ducks in ODI cricket. Kohli, one of the most prolific batters in the format, was sent back without scoring after facing just four deliveries. The dismissal drew attention because it put him level with Tendulkar, whose 20 ducks had long stood as the mark for an Indian batter in one-day internationals.

Tendulkar played 463 ODIs during his career, while Kohli reached the same number of ducks in far fewer matches. The record is an unusual one for a batter of Kohli's stature, known for his consistency at the top of the order. India were playing the fourth match of the series against New Zealand when the dismissal took place.

Kohli will now hope to return to form in the remaining matches of the series.
