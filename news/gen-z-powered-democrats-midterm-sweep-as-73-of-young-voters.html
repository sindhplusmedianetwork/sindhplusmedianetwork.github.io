Gen Z Powered Democrats' Midterm Sweep as 73% of Young Voters Backed Them

Young voters played a decisive role in the Democrats' victories in the United States midterm elections, according to a survey by American television cited by Jang. The survey found that 73 per cent of Gen Z voters backed Democratic candidates, while 25 per cent voted for Republicans. Among Millennials aged 30 to 45, 60 per cent voted for Democrats and 36 per cent for Republicans.

Among Generation X voters aged 46 to 61, 52 per cent backed Democrats and 47 per cent backed Republicans. The youth swing helped the Democrats take control of Congress in the midterm vote. US President Donald Trump said he hoped the new governor of Texas would continue the border security policy.
