Houthis Yet to Respond to Iran's Mediation Offer, Says Tehran

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iran has said that Saudi Arabia and Yemen's Houthi rebels have still not responded to Tehran's offer of mediation to end the fighting in Yemen. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi told Iraqi television that Iran is ready to facilitate talks between Saudi Arabia and the Houthis. He said Tehran's offer remains on the table and awaits a response from both sides.

The foreign minister also warned that any external military intervention in Yemen would only complicate the situation further. The fighting in Yemen between the Saudi-led coalition and the Houthis has continued for years.
