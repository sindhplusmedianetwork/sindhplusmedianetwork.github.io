Unified crypto industry body proposed for Pakistan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Asia Web3 Alliance Japan President Hinza Asif has called for a unified industry representative body to safeguard digital asset investors in Pakistan. The proposal follows a recent multibillion-dollar cybersecurity breach involving the crypto exchange Binance. Asif said Pakistan's growing cryptocurrency market faces serious risks without verified investor data and an organised mechanism to represent stakeholders.

Pakistan ranks third in the 2025 Global Crypto Adoption Index, underlining the scale of public participation. She welcomed the government's recent regulatory initiatives but said industry representation remains missing. A single representative body, she argued, would give investors a voice and help shape safer rules.
