Several Arrested During Gen Z Demonstration in Berlin

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

At least 20 people were arrested during a Gen Z protest march in Berlin, where police deployed 700 officers and filed 28 criminal complaints over assaults, unrest and explosives violations. Riot officers clashed with demonstrators at a 'Gen Z regelt' march joined by pro-Palestine activists, and at least three police officers were injured. The march was part of a wave of youth-led demonstrations across Europe.

Larger Europe-wide youth demonstrations are expected on November 20, with organisers in Berlin expecting around 15,000 protesters. Police said they were prepared for the larger gathering.
