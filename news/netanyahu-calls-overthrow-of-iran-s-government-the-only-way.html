Netanyahu calls overthrow of Iran's government the only way to end the threat

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has declared that the only way to end the Iranian threat is to overthrow Iran's government. He announced that he has issued instructions to the Israel Defense Forces, the Mossad intelligence agency and all of Israel's security institutions in this regard. Netanyahu was speaking at an election rally in Dimona, in southern Israel.

He said Iran had wanted to destroy Israel but was stopped 'with full force'. His remarks come as the Middle East situation continues to develop, and the story is being tracked on a live blog by the BBC.
