Gamer With Just 28 Viewers Lands $100,000 Job Playing GTA 6

By Sindh Plus Media Network

A little-known gamer in the United States has seen his fortunes change after landing a job paying more than $100,000 a year to play the popular video game GTA 6. The American company Kalshi selected Cliff Savage, a 36-year-old gamer from California, from more than 35,000 applicants for the unusual role. When he was told he had got the job, only 28 people were watching his livestream — and he fell to the ground in excitement on hearing the news.

Before this, Cliff had been working temporary jobs alongside livestreaming video games. His new duties include playing GTA 6, completing the game's story and preparing research reports on its virtual economy. After landing the job, his first livestream drew nearly 2,000 views, while his Instagram followers rose to around 21,000.
