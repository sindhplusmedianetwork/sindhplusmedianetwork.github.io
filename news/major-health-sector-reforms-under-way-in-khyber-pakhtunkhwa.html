Major Health-Sector Reforms Under Way in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa After Governor's Rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Practical steps are under way to improve the health sector in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa after the imposition of governor's rule. Under the vision of a changing, improving and stronger Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, a comprehensive review of the health department was carried out over the past month under the leadership of health adviser Lt Gen (retd) Nighat Johar and the health secretary. According to government sources, the performance of all health sectors, the administrative structure and the problems faced were reviewed in detail, and a future reform strategy was also prepared.

A target has been set to ensure 90 percent availability of contract doctors in remote areas and to make 250 basic emergency maternity and newborn centres functional 24 hours a day. A total of 114 rural health centres in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa will be shifted to solar power, while 100 nursing students will be provided scholarships. Mobile drug-testing laboratories that had been inactive for a long time have been reactivated to prevent fake and substandard medicines in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

The total estimated cost of the health department's 12 priority reform projects has been set at Rs7.10 billion.
