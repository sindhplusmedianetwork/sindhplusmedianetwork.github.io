Former Minister Liaqat Abbas Bhatti Killed for Cash and Jewellery, Four Suspects Arrested: Police

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hafizabad police arrested suspects in the murder case of former minister of state Liaqat Abbas Bhatti. Officials said the accused were arrested from Malakand in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Police said the suspects allegedly acted to loot cash and jewellery present in the house.

Vehicles and weapons taken from the scene were recovered, police said. An FIR was registered against 11 accused, including four personal guards. The FIR was registered under sections 302 and 395 of the Pakistan Penal Code.
