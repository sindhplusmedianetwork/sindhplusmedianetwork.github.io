Faisalabad's deputy commissioner seeks explanation from EPA as AQI hits 220

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Faisalabad's deputy commissioner has sought a written explanation within three days from the deputy director of the Environmental Protection Agency. The move follows delays in submitting reports, lack of coordination and failure to act against air-pollution sources. These sources include brick kilns and industrial units emitting black smoke.

The city's Air Quality Index was recorded at 220 on Saturday, placing it in the very unhealthy category. PM2.5 levels were measured at 145 micrograms per cubic metre. Monitoring stations near Government College University and Gulistan Colony reportedly crossed 300.

The EPA issued a health advisory asking children, the elderly and people with heart or lung conditions to avoid outdoor physical activity. It also advised the public to wear N95 masks and keep windows closed.
