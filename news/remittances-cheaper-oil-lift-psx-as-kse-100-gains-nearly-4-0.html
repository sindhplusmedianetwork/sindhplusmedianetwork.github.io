Remittances, cheaper oil lift PSX as KSE-100 gains nearly 4,000 points

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange closed the outgoing week higher, with the benchmark KSE-100 index gaining 3,987 points, or 2.29 per cent, to settle at 177,874. Market sentiment was supported by falling international oil prices, strong remittances from overseas Pakistanis and easing concerns about inflation. Remittances rose 14 per cent year on year to 3.9 billion dollars in October 2026.

Total liquid foreign exchange reserves stood at 26.8 billion dollars as of November 6, 2026. The rupee closed marginally firmer at 276.60 rupees per US dollar. Petrol and diesel prices were also cut, to 332 rupees and 360 rupees per litre respectively.
