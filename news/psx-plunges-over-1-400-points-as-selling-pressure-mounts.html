PSX Plunges Over 1,400 Points as Selling Pressure Mounts

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Pakistan Stock Exchange came under heavy selling pressure on Thursday, with the benchmark KSE-100 Index falling 1,421.85 points, or 0.86 percent, to 164,509.13 by midday. The slide wiped out the previous session's gains of 779 points. Trading volume stood at 60.55 million shares, with shares worth Rs5.11 billion changing hands.

Selling was broad-based, hitting heavyweights including OGDC, PPL, MARI, HUBCO, PSO, HBL and UBL. The pressure spread across the oil, power, cement, auto and refinery sectors. Market watchers attributed the decline to profit-taking and cautious sentiment among investors.
