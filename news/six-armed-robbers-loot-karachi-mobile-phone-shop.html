Six Armed Robbers Loot Karachi Mobile Phone Shop

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Six armed robbers looted a mobile phone shop in Karachi's Gulshan-e-Hadeed Phase-I, fleeing with phones worth over Rs7 million. Police said five to six suspects arrived on two motorcycles, and three of them broke the shop's locks to get inside. The shopkeeper said the looted stock represented his life's savings and appealed to the Sindh Inspector General of Police for help.

CCTV footage of the incident has been obtained by investigators. The president of the mobile dealers' association, Minhaj Gulfam, condemned the incident and demanded the culprits be arrested. Police have registered a case and started raids to trace the gang.
