JI Announces 150 Sit-Ins Across Pakistan on October 23 Against Petroleum Levy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-i-Islami chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman has announced that the party will hold 150 sit-ins across Pakistan on October 23 as part of the third phase of its movement against the petroleum levy. He made the announcement in Karachi while inaugurating the Abu Turab School and the Dr Abdul Qadeer Khan Tech Space, along with 70 roads and 300 streets in Gulberg Town. He said the party had effectively highlighted the problems faced by ordinary people and would resume its protests against the government over the petroleum levy from Friday.

He also announced Education Cards for children in Karachi towns under the party's municipal administration, saying quality education was a basic right of every child and a responsibility of the state. Party Karachi chief Monem Zafar said providing quality education to every child was a fundamental responsibility of the state, noting that 7.8 million children in Sindh were out of school, which he called alarming. He said 63 percent of schools in the province lacked water facilities, 51 percent had no boundary walls and 25 percent lacked proper washrooms for boys and girls, adding that the number of schools without boundary walls had risen from 4,500 to 5,500 during the incumbent provincial government's tenure.

He said the nine towns under the party's administration had restored 60 schools over the past three years. Hafiz Naeem said the reported 52 percent failure rate among ninth-grade students in Punjab raised serious questions about the performance of the education system.
