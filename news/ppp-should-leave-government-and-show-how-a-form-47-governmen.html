PPP should leave government and show how a Form 47 government survives, says Zaidi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI leader Ali Haider Zaidi said the PPP should separate from the government and show how a Form 47 government survives. He was reacting to Bilawal Bhutto Zardari's statement on constitutional supremacy and provincial autonomy. Zaidi asked why the PPP still supports the very government that Bilawal criticises.

He said that if Bilawal is serious, the PPP should withdraw its support and sit on the opposition benches. He also questioned whether the sudden talk of constitutional supremacy is genuine or another staged political drama.
