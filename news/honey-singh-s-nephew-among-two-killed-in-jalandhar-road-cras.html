Honey Singh's nephew among two killed in Jalandhar road crash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two young men, including singer Honey Singh's nephew Vidit Olak, were killed in a road accident in Jalandhar, Punjab, in the early hours of Sunday. Vidit, the son of Speed Records owner Dinesh, and his friend Aarav Talwar, son of the owner of Pappy Sweets, were returning from a birthday party when their Volkswagen rammed into a parked municipal tractor-trolley on a flyover near the Police Lines area. The car was travelling from BMC Chowk towards BSF Chowk when it crashed at around 3am, and both died on the spot.

Five people had set out in the car after the party, but three had already been dropped home before the crash. Baradari police station SHO Sahil Chaudhary said sanitation workers had parked the trolley on the flyover for cleaning work. The families decided not to pursue legal action, and the bodies were handed over to them.

Honey Singh shared his grief on Instagram, writing that losing his nephew was devastating.
