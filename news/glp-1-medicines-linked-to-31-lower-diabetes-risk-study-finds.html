GLP-1 Medicines Linked to 31% Lower Diabetes Risk, Study Finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new medical study has found that GLP-1 medicines may cut the risk of type 2 diabetes by up to 31 percent in people with obesity and pre-diabetes, compared with metformin, over about three years. The findings draw on electronic health records from 104 institutions covering 2010 to 2023. The study also recorded fewer heart attacks, strokes and heart-failure events among GLP-1 users, and a 41 percent lower risk of death from any cause.

The results are to be presented at the American Heart Association's Scientific Sessions in Chicago from 6 to 9 November 2026. The research was led by Dr Vinicius de Sousa Barzon Seira, and covered medicines including semaglutide and tirzepatide. Experts stress the findings are observational and preliminary, showing an association rather than proof that the drugs alone caused the benefit, and note the medicines are far more expensive than metformin with insurance approval sometimes difficult.
