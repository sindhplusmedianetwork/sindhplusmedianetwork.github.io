Registration opens for online auction of attractive vehicle registration numbers; citizens can apply until November 30

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Registration has opened for the online auction of attractive vehicle registration numbers in Lahore, with November 30 set as the last date to apply. PITB Chairman Faisal Yousuf said citizens can take part in the auction from home through the e-auction app and web portal. The auction covers attractive registration numbers for motorcycles, cars and commercial vehicles.

Citizens will not need to visit excise offices to take part in the process. The online system is aimed at making the auction easier and more transparent for applicants. Those interested in securing a distinctive registration number for their vehicle have until November 30 to complete their registration.
