Speeding Car Hits Green Line Stop Railings in Karachi's North Nazimabad, Youth Killed

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A speeding car crashed into the protective railings near a Green Line bus stop in Karachi's North Nazimabad, killing a 24-year-old man and injuring a 17-year-old boy. The accident took place in the Shadman No. 2 area, where the car struck the railings of the Green Line stop.

According to Chhipa officials, the crash happened because of speeding, and both people travelling in the car were injured. They were taken immediately to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital, where 24-year-old Akram died, while 17-year-old Abdullah remains injured. Rescue officials said the identities of the deceased young man and the injured boy have been confirmed.
