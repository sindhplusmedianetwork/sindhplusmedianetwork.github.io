Turkiye Hands Over First Kizilelma Unmanned Fighter Jets to Armed Forces

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Turkiye has formally handed over its first unmanned fighter jet, Bayraktar Kizilelma, to the security forces. In the initial phase, three aircraft have been inducted into the inventory of the Turkish armed forces, making Turkiye the first country in the world to induct such an unmanned fighter jet into military service. Turkish defence company Baykar said all three aircraft successfully completed the necessary acceptance tests, after which they were handed over to the Turkish security forces on November 19.

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan termed the induction of Kizilelma into the military inventory a historic achievement, saying it was a day when a great dream came true. Kizilelma is a jet-engine-powered unmanned combat aircraft designed for aerial combat and striking ground targets, developed by Baykar with its own resources. According to the company, the aircraft can reach a maximum speed of Mach 0.9, about 90 percent of the speed of sound, and can fly for more than three hours.

Its length is about 14.5 metres, wingspan 10 metres and maximum take-off weight 8.5 tons, and a total of 600 test and training flights have been completed under the programme since its first successful flight on December 14, 2022.
