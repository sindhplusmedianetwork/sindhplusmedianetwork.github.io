Haleem Adil Sheikh voices concern over corruption, Sindh's 18-year rule

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Sindh president Haleem Adil Sheikh met Iqbal Niazi in Hyderabad, recalling Niazi's role in organising PTI in the city in 1996-97. At that time few people knew the party, and workers took Imran Khan to shops and hotels to introduce him. Sheikh said he saluted ideological workers like Niazi who stood by Khan since 1996-97 despite the hardest times.

He credited their sacrifices for PTI's growth from a handful of workers into a large tree. He added that he had serious reservations over the state of the judiciary after the 26th and 27th constitutional amendments. He also cited rising corruption in the country and what he called 18 years of destruction in Sindh, saying the judiciary must be fixed for the country to move forward.
