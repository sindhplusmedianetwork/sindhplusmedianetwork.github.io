Aurangzeb: $3 Billion Saudi Loan Rolled Over, No Further Saudi Support Needed for Now

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Federal Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb has confirmed that Pakistan's $3 billion loan from Saudi Arabia has been rolled over, and that the country does not need any further Saudi support for the time being. Speaking to the media at Parliament House after a meeting of the Senate Standing Committee on Finance, the minister said he had a very good meeting with the Saudi finance minister and that the loan matter had been deferred for an appropriate period. He added that Pakistan has three months of import cover and, given the nature of relations with Saudi Arabia, there was no cause for worry.

Aurangzeb also said Pakistan pays roughly 1.5 billion euros in eurobond payments each year and that the government wants to ensure no administration faces difficulty in making them. He said approval of the next IMF tranche by the Fund's board is expected in December.
