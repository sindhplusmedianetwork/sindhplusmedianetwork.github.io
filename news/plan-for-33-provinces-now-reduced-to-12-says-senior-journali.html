Plan for 33 Provinces Now Reduced to 12, Says Senior Journalist

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A plan to create 33 provinces has now been limited to 12, senior journalist Aamir Ilyas Rana has said. Speaking in a television appearance, he said the initial plan envisaged 33 provinces, which has now been reduced to 12. He added that those seeking new provinces say Pakistan cannot be run this way.

Rana said the conflict appeared to have intensified. He added that Maulana Fazlur Rehman's statements had become increasingly heated. Those statements, he said, were directly targeting the military.
