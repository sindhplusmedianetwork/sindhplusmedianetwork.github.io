Aramco Nears $10 Billion Loan Deal With Global Banks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi Arabia's state-owned oil company Saudi Aramco is close to securing a $10 billion loan from a large group of international banks, according to a report published by Bloomberg on Monday. The company is preparing to obtain the financing to strengthen its financial resources amid ongoing military tensions in the Middle East, risks to oil exports and an uncertain economic outlook. The report said Aramco could sign an agreement for the new loan in the coming weeks, adding that talks with banks from the United States, Britain, China and Japan had reached their final stages.

Sources familiar with the development said the company is seeking a revolving credit facility, meaning it can draw funds up to a specified limit as needed and draw again after repayment. Major banks expected to participate include JPMorgan Chase and Citigroup of the United States, HSBC of the United Kingdom, Bank of China and Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, and Japan's Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group. The proposed interest rate could be 60 basis points above SOFR, the benchmark rate used for US dollar financing.

Aramco previously arranged a $10 billion revolving credit facility in 2024, of which $7 billion came from international banks and $3 billion from regional banks under Islamic murabaha financing. It is unclear whether the new arrangement will replace or renew the existing facility or serve as additional capacity, and the company and some banks have declined to comment.
