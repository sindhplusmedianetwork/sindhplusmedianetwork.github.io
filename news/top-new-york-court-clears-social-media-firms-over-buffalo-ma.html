Top New York Court Clears Social Media Firms Over Buffalo Mass Shooting

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Several major social media companies should not be held liable over accusations that they helped an avowed white supremacist who in 2022 killed 10 Black people at a grocery store in Buffalo, New York, the state's highest court ruled on Monday. In a 4-3 decision, New York's Court of Appeals let stand a ruling that Google and YouTube, Meta's Instagram, Reddit and Snapchat were immune under a federal law protecting online platforms from liability over user content. The majority said the shooter's actions were so extraordinary as to sever the causal chain as a matter of law.

The ruling brings a major legal dispute over the platforms' responsibility to an end at the state level. The 2022 attack had raised serious questions about the role of online platforms in such violence.
