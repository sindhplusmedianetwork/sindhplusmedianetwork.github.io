Vast Scope to Expand Pakistan-Britain Trade, Says High Commissioner

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's High Commissioner to Britain, Tipu Usman, has said there are vast opportunities to increase trade and investment between Pakistan and Britain in fintech, textiles and other sectors. He was addressing the West Yorkshire Pakistan Trade, Investment and Innovation Summit 2026 in Bradford. The high commissioner said the Pakistani textile industry could produce modern yarn, fibres and new products in partnership with British institutions, and that companies from both countries should launch joint production ventures.

He said around 250,000 people of Pakistani origin lived in West Yorkshire and played a prominent role in various sectors. He added that the annual volume of trade between Pakistan and Britain had already reached six billion pounds. He also said there was room to expand bilateral cooperation further in textiles, environment-friendly production and halal food.
