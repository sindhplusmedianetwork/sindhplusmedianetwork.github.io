Young authors launch 110 books at Sharjah Book Fair

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A total of 110 books by authors aged six to 18 were launched at the 45th Sharjah International Book Fair on 14 November. The books, including short stories, poetry and comics, were published through Learner Circle, a platform that pairs young writers with mentors. The fair ran from 4 to 15 November under the theme 'Made of Stories'.

It brought together 3,212 publishers from 126 countries and showcased more than 1.7 million titles. The books were launched on 14 November, a day before the fair closed on 15 November.
