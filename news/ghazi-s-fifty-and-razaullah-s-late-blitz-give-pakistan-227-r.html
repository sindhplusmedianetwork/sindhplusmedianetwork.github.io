Ghazi's fifty and Razaullah's late blitz give Pakistan 227-run lead over Sri Lanka in second Test

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Wicketkeeper-batter Ghazi Ghori struck a resilient half-century to lift Pakistan to 325 all out on the second day of the second Test at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Wednesday. Razaullah provided late fireworks as Pakistan built a commanding 227-run first-innings lead over Sri Lanka. Pakistan had resumed the day at 121 for three.

Sri Lanka had fought back through Lahiru Kumara's five-wicket haul before Ghazi and the lower order wrested back the initiative. Ghazi's fifty anchored the innings after the early wickets had left the hosts under pressure. Razaullah's late hitting then pushed the total past 300 and stretched the lead beyond 200.
