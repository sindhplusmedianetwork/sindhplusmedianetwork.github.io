Petrol Down Rs3.19, Diesel Up Rs2.25 for October 28

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Tuesday cut the price of petrol by Rs3.19 per litre and raised the price of high-speed diesel by Rs2.25 per litre for October 28. According to a notification issued by the Petroleum Division, petrol will now cost Rs376.06 per litre, while high-speed diesel will be sold at Rs398.07 per litre for Wednesday. The revision comes a day after petrol and diesel prices were increased by Rs1.54 and Rs2.16 per litre respectively for October 27.

Since July 17, fuel prices have been reviewed and notified daily under a new pricing mechanism, replacing the earlier weekly system, as renewed tensions in the Middle East keep global oil markets volatile. The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority revises the prices in line with the mechanism, taking into account international prices, Platts assessments, premiums and other costs. The government has also continued a relief scheme announced on September 13, offering Rs100 per litre off petrol for motorcycles, three-wheeler rickshaws and cars with engines up to 800cc.
