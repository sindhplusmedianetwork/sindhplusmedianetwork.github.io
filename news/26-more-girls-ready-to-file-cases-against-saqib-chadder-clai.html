26 More Girls Ready to File Cases Against Saqib Chadder, Claims Ramsha Iqbal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Advocate Ramsha Iqbal, the sister of actress Momena Iqbal, has claimed that 26 more girls, including actresses, are ready to file legal cases against Saqib Chadder. She made the claim at a press conference where, she said, she presented certified copies of the evidence. Ramsha said she was grateful to those who supported her through the ordeal.

She also said that decisions on applications filed against her had gone in her favour. She added that the institutions would determine the truth in the matter. The case has drawn wide attention in the country's entertainment circles.
