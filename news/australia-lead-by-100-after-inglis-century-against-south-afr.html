Australia lead by 100 after Inglis century against South Africa

Josh Inglis scored his second Test century as Australia built a first-innings lead of 100 against South Africa on day three of the third Test at Newlands on Thursday. Inglis made 102 before Australia were bowled out for 429 in reply to South Africa's total. He shared a 185-run fifth-wicket partnership with Alex Carey, who contributed 80, after Australia had resumed the day on 330 for four.

Inglis reached his century off 132 balls with a reverse sweep off Simon Harmer, but was caught behind off the same bowler just three balls later. South Africa will begin their second innings trailing by 100 runs.
