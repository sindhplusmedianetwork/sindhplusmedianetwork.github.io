Russian bombs kill 26 at Ukraine aid centre and shelter in Komyshuvakha

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russian aerial bombs struck a school and a neighbouring shelter in the village of Komyshuvakha in southern Ukraine's Zaporizhzhia region on Monday, killing 26 people, Ukrainian officials said. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the school was being used as a shelter and a weekly humanitarian-aid distribution point, and that a second wave of nine bombs hit the shelter where people had taken cover. Ukraine's emergency services said rescuers and police officers were among the dead, while about 20 people were still missing.

Rescue work was hampered by Russian drones hovering over the area, officials said. The attack came as a limited ceasefire in the Kherson region expired with no extension announced. Russia said its air defences had shot down 212 Ukrainian drones overnight.
