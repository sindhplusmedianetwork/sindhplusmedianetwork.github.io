PSX slides 1,223 points over political noise

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Selling pressure intensified at the Pakistan Stock Exchange on Wednesday, where the benchmark KSE-100 index fell 1,223.41 points, or 0.69 percent, to settle at 176,294.33. Investor sentiment was dampened by political clamour at home, adding to concerns about the Middle East conflict and oil market volatility. Heavyweight stocks across cement, banks, oil and gas and power came under pressure during the session.

Trading volume decreased to 742 million shares worth Rs26 billion. A total of 285 stocks fell while 179 closed higher. The decline reflected cautious trading as investors weighed domestic political noise against external pressures.

The market closed well below its opening level after a day of broad-based selling.
