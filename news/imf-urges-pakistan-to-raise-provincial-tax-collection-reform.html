IMF urges Pakistan to raise provincial tax collection, reform energy sector

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has called on Pakistan to increase provincial tax collection and carry out reforms in the energy sector. The Fund says Pakistan must move beyond economic stability, raise tax collection and take decisions that increase investment. To pull the economy out of repeated crises, Pakistan cannot rely only on loans and economic stability, according to the Fund.

Instead, fundamental changes will be needed in the tax system, energy, education, government institutions and the business environment. The Fund's position is that lasting recovery depends on deeper structural reform rather than short-term stabilisation alone.
