White House Now Looks Like a Restaurant and a Bathroom, Ex-US Presidents Mock Trump

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Former US presidents Barack Obama and Bill Clinton have mocked President Donald Trump's lavish renovation of the White House, comparing it to a gaudy restaurant and a glittering bathroom. Speaking at an election rally in Georgia, Obama joked that Trump had turned the White House into something resembling a Cheesecake Factory restaurant opened by Saddam Hussein. Obama said the Trump administration was entangled in unnecessary controversies instead of addressing serious issues like inflation and economic hardship.

He accused the government of diverting public attention from real problems through manufactured anger and rhetoric. Former president Bill Clinton, meanwhile, compared the decoration of the White House Rose Garden to the glittering bathroom of famous musician Liberace. Hillary Clinton has previously described the excessive use of gold in the Oval Office as an act of Trump's self-display.
