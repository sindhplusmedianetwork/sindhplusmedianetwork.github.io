IMC flags misuse of used car import scheme

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Indus Motor Company (IMC), the assembler of Toyota vehicles in Pakistan, has said the misuse of the gift scheme for importing used vehicles and the absence of a clear, long-term automotive policy are hurting local manufacturing and discouraging investment, The Express Tribune reported. CEO Ali Asghar Jamali said the misuse was undermining local production and diverting economic activity away from domestic manufacturers, with negative implications for employment, localisation and government revenues. He called for stricter enforcement of import rules, a consistent automotive policy, rationalisation of duties and taxes and better access to auto financing.

Despite these concerns, IMC's performance improved: it sold 13,380 vehicles in the July–September quarter, up 34% year-on-year, with net sales of Rs74.015 billion and profit after tax of Rs7.384 billion. The board declared an interim cash dividend of Rs56 per share.
