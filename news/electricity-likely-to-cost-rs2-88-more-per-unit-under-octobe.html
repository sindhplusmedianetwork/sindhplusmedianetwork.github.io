Electricity likely to cost Rs2.88 more per unit under October fuel adjustment

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Electricity is likely to become Rs2.88 per unit costlier under the October monthly fuel price adjustment. The Central Power Purchasing Agency has filed a petition with NEPRA seeking the increase. Distribution companies sold 10.35 billion units of electricity in October.

NEPRA will hear the petition on November 26. If the petition is approved, consumers will face an extra burden of about Rs29.8 billion. The final decision will follow the hearing.
