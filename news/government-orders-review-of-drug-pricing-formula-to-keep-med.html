Government Orders Review of Drug Pricing Formula to Keep Medicines Fairly Priced

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government warned on Monday that drug prices must not be increased without valid justification and ordered a review of the existing pricing formula so that medicines are priced fairly. Economic Affairs Minister Ahad Cheema, who chaired a meeting of the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan, directed officials to expedite the processing of pending drug-pricing cases after being briefed that their case-by-case evaluation was almost complete. Officials told the meeting that the authority was examining provisions in the current formula that could hinder the determination of fair and accurate prices.

Cheema suggested that the authority consider subscribing to specialised international databases on drug prices to strengthen independent assessment and provide more reliable benchmarks. He stressed that public facilitation should remain the government's top priority and that any increase in medicine prices without valid justification was unacceptable. The meeting was also informed that sufficient quantities of the World Health Organisation-recommended trivalent influenza vaccine were available in the market.

The pharmaceutical industry, meanwhile, urged the government to engage it in the formula review so that the outcome is workable and sustainable, while agreeing that medicines must remain affordable and accessible.
