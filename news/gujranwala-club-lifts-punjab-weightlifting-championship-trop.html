Gujranwala Club Lifts Punjab Weightlifting Championship Trophy

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Chaudhry Muhammad Amin Weightlifting Club of Gujranwala has won the Punjab Senior Men's and Women's Weightlifting Championship. According to Punjab Weightlifting Association secretary Aqeel Javed Butt, 94 athletes from across Punjab took part in the three-day championship held at the Old Campus Ground. The Gujranwala club lifted the trophy with 34 points.

The Samanabad Weightlifting Club of Faisalabad finished second with 24 points, while the Muslim Health Club of Shad Bagh secured third position with 23 points.
