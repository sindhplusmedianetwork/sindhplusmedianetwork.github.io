Iran-US War Will End, Trump Will Seek a Negotiated Path: Maleeha Lodhi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former ambassador Dr Maleeha Lodhi has said the Iran-US war will come to an end and that US President Donald Trump will look for a route to negotiations. Speaking on Geo News programme Capital Talk, Lodhi said that if Congress passed a resolution to end the war, it would have to be implemented. Former foreign secretary Aizaz Chaudhry, also on the programme, said Trump would now diplomatically declare that he was not getting into a war.

Chaudhry, however, warned that after some time Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu could carry out a false-flag operation against Iran to push matters toward war. Defence analyst Major General (retd) Zahid Mahmood said that in the Iran-US war, one could now see a strategy for exiting the war. The comments come amid ongoing tensions between Washington and Tehran.
