Five Pakistanis and Bangladeshis Arrested in Saudi Arabia for Illegal Soil Excavation

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saudi authorities have detained five foreign nationals in the Eastern Region on charges of violating environmental regulations. According to the Saudi newspaper Al-Sabq, the Special Forces for Environmental Security arrested five foreigners, from Pakistan and Bangladesh, in the Eastern Region. The suspects were arrested on charges of the unauthorised extraction of natural resources and the illegal excavation of soil.

Authorities said four soil-digging machines were also seized from the suspects. The Special Forces appealed to citizens and residents to immediately report any incident that harms the environment or wildlife so that timely action can be taken against offenders. The forces stressed that all information will be kept completely confidential and that those who report will face no legal liability or questioning.
