UAE Royal Offers Condolences to Families of Fujairah Crash Victims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sheikh Theyab bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan has offered condolences to the families of the 14 people killed in the Fujairah traffic accident of November 12. Sheikh Theyab, Deputy Chairman of the Presidential Court for Development and Fallen Heroes' Affairs, named each of the victims as he extended sympathies to their families. He visited the injured in hospitals in Fujairah, enquired about their health and wished them a speedy recovery.

He also visited the mourning gatherings held for the bereaved families. The accident, a pile-up on November 12, left 14 people dead and 30 injured.
