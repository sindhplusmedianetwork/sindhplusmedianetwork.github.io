I Will Make Such a Deal with Iran That It Survives Even an Incompetent President After 15 or 20 Years: Trump

SP

US President Donald Trump has said he will make such a deal with Iran that it will remain intact even if an incompetent president comes to power after 15 or 20 years. In an interview with Fox News, Trump said his aim is to conclude a lasting and permanent agreement with Iran under which Iran is prevented from developing nuclear weapons. He said he will make a deal that will be permanent, so that even if an incompetent president comes to power in four, 15 or 20 years, the agreement will still hold.

Trump said he will not allow anyone to let Iran obtain nuclear weapons. He also said he has never had much interest in Iran's current system of government.
