Canada-Returned Elderly Couple Found Dead at North Karachi Home; Murder Probe Under Way

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The bodies of an elderly couple who had recently returned from Canada were found at their home in North Karachi on Wednesday, in what police are treating as a double murder. The deceased were identified as Muhammad Yameen and his wife Nafees Akhtar, both aged between 65 and 70. Their bodies were recovered from a house near Amma Park in Sector 11-C within the jurisdiction of the Sir Syed police station.

Police said the couple appeared to have been killed with hard and blunt instruments, and initial post-mortem findings pointed to severe head injuries. The bodies were shifted to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital for medico-legal formalities. A murder case was registered at the Sir Syed police station on the complaint of the deceased man's brother, Muhammad Anwar.

Investigators said the house was undergoing renovation work, and labourers who had been working there are being traced as part of the inquiry. No signs of robbery were found at the house, while mobile phones were recovered from the premises. Sindh Home Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar took notice of the incident and sought details from the SSP Central, as police continue to examine CCTV footage from the area.
