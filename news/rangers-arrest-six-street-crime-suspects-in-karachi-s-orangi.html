Rangers Arrest Six Street Crime Suspects in Karachi's Orangi Town

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 07, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rangers arrested six suspects, including the gang leader, in an intelligence-based operation in Karachi's Orangi Town. The operation was carried out in Khairaabad and Altaf Nagar. The suspects are allegedly involved in robbery and street crime.

During interrogation, they confessed to more than 15 crimes. Four illegal pistols, ammunition, three mobile phones and cash were recovered from them.
