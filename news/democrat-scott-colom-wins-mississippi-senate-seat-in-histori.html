Democrat Scott Colom Wins Mississippi Senate Seat in Historic Upset

Democrat candidate Scott Colom defeated Republican Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith in the US state of Mississippi, creating a major political upset in the 2026 midterm elections. According to American media, Colom's victory in a state traditionally considered a Republican stronghold is being described as a historic advance for the Democrats. Scott Colom has become the first Democrat to win a US Senate seat from Mississippi since 1982.

He is also the first Black senator elected from the state since the Reconstruction era, and his victory has ended decades of Republican dominance in Mississippi's Senate representation. According to American media reports, the contest remained extremely close, and Colom secured victory after the vote count was completed. Republican candidate Cindy Hyde-Smith conceded defeat and congratulated Scott Colom on his victory.
