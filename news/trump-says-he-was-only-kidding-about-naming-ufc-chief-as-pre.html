Trump Says He Was 'Only Kidding' About Naming UFC Chief as Press Secretary

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump said on Sunday he was only kidding when he suggested Ultimate Fighting Championship chief executive Dana White could become his next White House press secretary. The remark came after a social media post a day earlier stirred speculation about recruiting his feisty ally for the role. Trump said White, a prominent supporter, loves the idea of taking the job but insisted he had not been serious about the prospect.

He described the suggestion as just a little fun. In Saturday's post, Trump described White as a violent fighter and warrior and suggested he bring several current or former UFC champions to serve as assistants. The walk-back ended a day of speculation over whether the UFC chief would take up a role in the White House press team.
