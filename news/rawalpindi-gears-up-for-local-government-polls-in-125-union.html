Rawalpindi gears up for local government polls in 125 union councils

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Preparations for local government elections in 125 union councils of Rawalpindi district have formally begun under the new Local Government Act 2025. The first phase of local government elections in Punjab will begin on February 7, 2027, although uncertainty continues to surround whether the elections will actually be held, with serious doubts persisting over the exercise. Under the new system, each union council will comprise 13 members, of whom the public will directly elect nine general members, while the remaining four reserved seats, for women, farmers and labourers, youth and minorities, will be filled through a show of hands by the elected general members.

The 13 members of each union council will jointly elect its chairman and vice chairman, and the public will not directly vote for either position. Under the new Act, Rawalpindi city has been divided into four administrative units comprising two municipal corporation towns and two tehsil councils, giving the city two mayors and four deputy mayors. The city has been split into Rawal Municipal Town and Potohar Municipal Town, with union councils reduced from 46 to 41 in Rawal and from 36 to 33 in Potohar, while the new Tehsil Council Saddar will have 22 union councils and Tehsil Council Rawalpindi Cantt will have 29.

Overall, the number of union councils in Rawalpindi district has risen from 82 to 125, and candidates from the PML-N, PPP, Pakistan Tehreek-i-Insaf, Jamaat-i-Islami, Istehkam-i-Pakistan Party and Pakistan Awami Tehreek have begun entering the electoral arena, with the schedule for submitting nomination papers already prepared.
