PM Shehbaz meets ASEAN Secretary General, pledges stronger partnership

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif met ASEAN Secretary General Dr Kao Kim Hourn at the Prime Minister House in Islamabad. The prime minister reiterated Pakistan's commitment to further strengthen its longstanding and growing partnership with ASEAN and the Southeast Asian region. The two sides discussed ways to promote trade, investment and economic cooperation and to expand collaboration in food security, climate change, information technology and education.

The prime minister stressed that existing goodwill should be turned into concrete economic and development partnership. He also highlighted Pakistan's strategic location at the junction of Central Asia, the Middle East and West Asia and its potential as a gateway for the wider region, saying this would promote regional connectivity, trade and economic integration. The secretary general thanked the prime minister for the warm hospitality and arrangements for his visit, reaffirmed ASEAN's commitment to strengthening ties with Pakistan, and expressed confidence that continued cooperation and the visit would help advance the partnership further.
