Soni Razdan Reveals She Smoked Nearly 100 Cigarettes in a Day Before Alia Bhatt's Birth

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bollywood actress Soni Razdan has revealed that during the shooting of a film while pregnant with her younger daughter Alia Bhatt, she smoked almost 100 cigarettes in a single day. Speaking in an interview about her personal life, she said she became pregnant during work on various film and television projects and received strong support from fellow artists and production teams. She said she first became pregnant during the shooting of the TV drama 'Buniyaad', which aired in 1986.

Later, during the shooting of the 1993 film 'Gumrah', a scene required her to smoke and she smoked around 100 cigarettes in one day, unaware at the time that she was carrying Alia Bhatt. She added that she did not normally smoke, but after the incident she began to feel the urge to smoke.
