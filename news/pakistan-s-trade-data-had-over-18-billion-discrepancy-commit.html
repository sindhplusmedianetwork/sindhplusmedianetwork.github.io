Pakistan's Trade Data Had Over $18 Billion Discrepancy, Committee Tells IMF

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A high-powered committee has informed the International Monetary Fund that Pakistan's trade data carried a discrepancy of over $18 billion across five fiscal years, from 2020-21 to 2024-25. Officials said the gap has since been identified and removed. The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics prepared a detailed report on shifting the source of trade data from PRAL to the Pakistan Single Window and shared it with the planning and finance ministries as well as the IMF.

The FBR formed the committee on the direction of the Prime Minister's Office after large discrepancies surfaced in bilateral trade figures with China. The development raises questions about the accuracy of trade statistics used for economic planning.
