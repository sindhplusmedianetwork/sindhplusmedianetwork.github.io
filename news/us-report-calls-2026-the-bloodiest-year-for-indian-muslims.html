US report calls 2026 the bloodiest year for Indian Muslims

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A review report by an international human rights organisation has laid bare hate crimes by Hindu extremists against Muslims in India. According to the quarterly report of the American human rights organisation, the South Asia Justice Campaign, 16 Muslims were killed in religion-based violence in India over the past three months. The report says 118 fake police encounters against Indian Muslims took place from August to October, of which 105 occurred in Uttar Pradesh.

It also says the names of 61.6 million Muslims were removed from voter lists in 18 states and union territories of India. Dozens of incidents were reported in Indian states in which Muslims were forced to chant Hindutva slogans and were humiliated. According to the organisation's record of the past four years, 2026 has proved to be the bloodiest year for Indian Muslims.
