US must immediately stop illegal actions against Iran and compensate war damages, says Araghchi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has said the United States must stop its illegal actions against Iran and pay compensation for the damages caused by the war. He said US attacks on Iran violate the United Nations Charter and international law, and that Iran has the right to defend itself under Article 51 of the UN Charter. Araghchi added that the US did not have Security Council authorisation for its actions.

He said the use of force on the basis of possible threats is not justified. His remarks restate Iran's position that the American attacks lacked legal grounding and that Tehran is entitled to compensation for the destruction caused.
