Buddhist leaders explore Pakistan's ancient Gandhara heritage

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

An international delegation of Buddhist monks and religious scholars from eight Asian countries visited the Islamabad Museum and the historic Shah Allah Ditta Caves on Tuesday. The visit was part of the three-day Gandhara Strategic Dialogue 2026. The delegation came from Thailand, Laos, Vietnam, Japan, Cambodia, Sri Lanka, South Korea and Bangladesh.

The visitors explored Gandharan antiquities and an immersive digital presentation on the region's Buddhist legacy. The dialogue, themed 'Towards a Shared Future', aims to strengthen cooperation on the preservation, promotion and global recognition of Gandhara's shared Buddhist heritage.
