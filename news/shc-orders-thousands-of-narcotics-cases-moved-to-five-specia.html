SHC orders thousands of narcotics cases moved to five special courts in Karachi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh High Court has directed the transfer of thousands of pending cases registered under the Sindh Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 2024, to the five newly constituted special courts in Karachi. According to an official communication issued by SHC Member Inspection Team-II, Muhammad Jahangir Khan, on the directions of the chief justice, all district and sessions judges in Karachi South, East, West, Central and Malir have been instructed to ensure the transfer of the pending drug cases to the special courts constituted under Article 30 of the Act. The cases have been transferred to CNS Courts-I, II, IV, V and VI in Karachi in accordance with territorial jurisdiction, and the district judges have been directed to ensure compliance and submit a report to Khan.

The development follows changes to the provincial narcotics law after the Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 1997 was repealed on October 15, 2024, and replaced by the Sindh CNS Act, 2024. According to authorities, all cases registered under the latest act have been transferred to the new courts, including four cases registered against alleged Cocaine Queen Anmol alias Pinky. Under the new arrangement, remand proceedings in newly registered cases will also be conducted before the CNS courts, and interim and final charge sheets in both new and pending cases will also be submitted to these courts.

The new law also allows the government to establish narcotics control police stations, check posts and check points to eliminate drug trafficking, and provides stricter punishments alongside safeguards such as video evidence in cases to protect the rights of citizens.
