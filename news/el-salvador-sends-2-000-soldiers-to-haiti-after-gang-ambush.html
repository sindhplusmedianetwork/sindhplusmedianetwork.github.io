El Salvador Sends 2,000 Soldiers to Haiti After Gang Ambush

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 30, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

El Salvador is sending more than 2,000 soldiers to Haiti to reinforce international security operations and track down four Salvadoran military aviation technicians kidnapped by gangs. President Nayib Bukele authorised the deployment following a telephone conference with Haitian Prime Minister Alix Didier Fils-Aime. The crisis erupted after heavy gang fire targeted a military armoured vehicle in gang-controlled territory east of Port-au-Prince, and the four technicians went missing during the attack.

Armed groups also fired on responding rescue helicopters, and Salvadoran forces are now conducting targeted sweep operations alongside Haitian police in high-risk districts such as Bon Repos. Haiti faces severe political collapse and widespread lawlessness, with violent gangs controlling major transit corridors and large parts of the capital, while millions urgently need humanitarian assistance. Bukele, known for his hardline domestic crackdown on gangs, reaffirmed his commitment to helping stabilise Haiti, and US defence aircraft helped transport the Salvadoran contingents to speed up deployment.
