Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir visits Miranshah in North Waziristan

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir visited Miranshah in North Waziristan. The visit was reported from Rawalpindi on Friday, November 20, 2026. According to the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), the Pakistan Army's public relations wing, the Field Marshal visited the martyrs' memorial in Miranshah.

He laid flowers at the memorial during the visit. The Field Marshal was also briefed on the security situation in the area.
