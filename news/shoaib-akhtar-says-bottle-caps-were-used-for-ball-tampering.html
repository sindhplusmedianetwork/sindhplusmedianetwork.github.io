Shoaib Akhtar says bottle caps were used for ball tampering in England in his era

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Pakistan fast bowler Shoaib Akhtar revealed that during his playing days, bottle caps — not sandpaper — were used for ball tampering in England. Speaking on a cricket programme, he said he stands with Usman Khawaja on the recent controversy. He added, however, that the timing of raising it could have been better and that the matter should be let go.

Akhtar also criticised changes to cricket's laws, saying two new balls have tilted the game towards batters. He further lamented the lack of new superstars in Pakistan cricket.
