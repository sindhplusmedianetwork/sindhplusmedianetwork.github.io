Nvidia fallout throws spotlight on Southeast Asia's AI boom

By Sindh Plus Media Network

Southeast Asia's chip and data centre industries expect greater scrutiny after news of former Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang's affair with a suspected Chinese spy put the spotlight on Singapore and Malaysia, executives and analysts said. US lawmakers from both parties are pushing for tougher restrictions on China's access to advanced artificial intelligence chips. Tougher regulations could include new export rules and licensing requirements to Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Singapore.

Malaysia said on Thursday it was weighing a law to monitor data centre operations and ensure any chips subject to US restrictions were imported for domestic use rather than for re-export. The developments have brought renewed attention to the region's fast-growing role in the global artificial intelligence supply chain.
