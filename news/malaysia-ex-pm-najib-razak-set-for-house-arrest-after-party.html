Malaysia ex-PM Najib Razak set for house arrest after party pays fine to leave jail

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Malaysian court has issued an order allowing jailed former prime minister Najib Razak's transfer to house arrest. The order came after his party UMNO paid a 50 million ringgit ($12.32 million) fine under the terms of a royal pardon. Najib's lawyer said the order was being delivered to Kajang prison and the 73-year-old could go home on Friday if the process goes to plan.

King Sultan Ibrahim agreed in September to let Najib serve the remainder of his sentence under house arrest. Najib was convicted of corruption linked to the 1MDB state fund scandal.
