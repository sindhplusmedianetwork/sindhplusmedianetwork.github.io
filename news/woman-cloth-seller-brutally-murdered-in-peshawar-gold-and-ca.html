Woman Cloth-Seller Brutally Murdered in Peshawar; Gold and Cash Stolen

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A woman who earned her living by selling clothes in Peshawar was brutally murdered after severe torture in the Musa Zai Sorizai area of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa capital, while unidentified killers also snatched gold earrings, a chain and cash from her. According to Inqilab police, the body of Samina Bibi, wife of Syed Faraz and a resident of Hashtnagri, was recovered from the Sorizai lands on Monday night. Her husband and other heirs said a former local nazim informed them of the murder after seeing her identity card on social media.

They claimed her hands bore knife wounds and her head had injuries, and that she was killed after being brutally tortured. The deceased had been associated with the buying and selling of clothes for 10 to 12 years and sold clothes from house to house. On the day of the incident, she had received calls asking her to bring 10 men's suits and 4 women's suits for a wedding event.

The family said Samina sent her young son to the madrassa and went first to the market to buy clothes and then to Musa Zai. The heirs said unidentified persons also took gold and cash from her; her husband is a painter, and she was the support of her poor husband as well as her family members — a support now snatched away. The deceased left behind four children.

They demanded that the CCPO expose those involved in the incident. Police have started work on the victim's mobile phone records and other evidence to trace the murder case.
