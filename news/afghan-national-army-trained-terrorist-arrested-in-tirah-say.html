Afghan National Army-Trained Terrorist Arrested in Tirah, Says Additional IG KP

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 11, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Security forces have arrested an Afghan National Army-trained terrorist during an operation in Tirah, Khyber district. Additional Inspector General of Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Abbas Ahsan announced the arrest at a press conference. The arrested man was identified as Hikmatullah alias Hamza, from Mangryal village in Afghanistan's Paktia province.

He was arrested on November 7. Police say he was associated with the Afghan National Army and had received training from it. Police also say he had worked with the Gul Bahadur group and had contacts with terrorists across the border and inside Pakistan.
