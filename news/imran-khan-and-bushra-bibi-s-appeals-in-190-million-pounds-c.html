Imran Khan and Bushra Bibi's Appeals in 190 Million Pounds Case Fixed for Hearing

By Sindh Plus Media Network

The Islamabad High Court has formally fixed the appeals of PTI founder Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi against their sentence in the 190 million pounds case for November 25. The Registrar of the High Court has issued the cause list regarding the appeals, according to which Chief Justice Sardar Muhammad Sarfraz Dogar and Justice Muhammad Asif will hear the appeals. At the previous hearing, the lawyer of the PTI founder had sought time to prepare the case, while Barrister Salman Safdar had requested the court to grant two weeks' time.

The court had given one week's time for preparation of the case at the previous hearing. The Islamabad High Court has now fixed the appeals of the PTI founder and Bushra Bibi against their sentence for hearing on November 25.
