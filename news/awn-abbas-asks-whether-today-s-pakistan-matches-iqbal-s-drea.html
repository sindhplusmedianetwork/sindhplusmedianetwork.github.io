Awn Abbas Asks Whether Today's Pakistan Matches Iqbal's Dream

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI Senator Awn Abbas Buppi marked Iqbal Day with a political statement questioning whether present-day Pakistan reflects the vision of Allama Iqbal. He said the question echoing in every Pakistani's mind today is whether this is the Pakistan Iqbal dreamed of. In a post on X, he said that where freedom is taken away, political restrictions exist, the law is meaningless and people are trapped in difficulties, that negates Iqbal's vision rather than fulfilling it.

He added that it is regrettable that those lecturing on Iqbal's philosophy today are people whose politics stands on personal interest rather than selfhood. According to him, such politics exists amid corruption and lies. His remarks framed Iqbal Day as a moment to measure the country's present condition against the poet-philosopher's ideals.
