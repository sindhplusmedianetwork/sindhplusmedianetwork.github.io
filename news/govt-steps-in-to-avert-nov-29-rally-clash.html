Govt steps in to avert Nov 29 rally clash

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh government has formed a four-member committee to approach the Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan and a nationalist party over their planned rallies on November 29, urging both sides to avoid actions that could escalate political tensions in the province. Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon announced that the committee would request the two parties to refrain from creating an atmosphere of confrontation. The panel comprises Nisar Khuhro, Waqar Mehdi, Nasir Hussain Shah and Ziaul Hassan Lanjar.

Memon rejected allegations of violence against the PPP, saying the party had never believed in violent politics or taken up arms, and that the provincial government wanted to ensure peace and prevent any untoward situation arising from the simultaneous rally announcements. Defending PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari's remarks about the possibility of bloodshed over new provinces, Memon said the chairman had neither issued a threat nor advocated violence but had merely referred to Karachi's ground realities and its troubled past.
