Rs10.44 Billion Hajj Vaccine Scandal: Agency Seeks Permission to Arrest Health Minister Mustafa Kamal

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Alleged embezzlement in the Rs10.44 billion purchase of vaccines for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims has taken a new turn, with a federal investigation agency seeking permission to arrest Health Minister Mustafa Kamal. Sources say the FIA found an extra cost of Rs2.44 billion in a five-year contract awarded to M/s Pharma Connect, allegedly at prices above the Drug Regulatory Authority's rates. Three officers, including the Director of Border Health Services, have already been arrested in the case.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has formed a five-member inquiry committee to look into the matter. The minister's coordinator and a former secretary are also under investigation. The scandal relates to the procurement of meningitis vaccines for pilgrims travelling for Hajj and Umrah.

Investigators say the inquiry is continuing and further action will follow its findings.
