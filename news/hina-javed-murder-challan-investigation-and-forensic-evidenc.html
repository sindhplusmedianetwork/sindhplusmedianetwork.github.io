Hina Javed Murder Challan, Investigation and Forensic Evidence Sent to Court: CPO Rawalpindi

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

CPO Rawalpindi Sardar Ghias Gul has said the challan in the Hina Javed murder case has been sent to court, along with the police investigation and forensic evidence. He said the court will decide who is guilty and who is innocent in the case. Hina Javed was tortured and murdered at her home on 20 August this year.

The family had expressed no confidence in the police investigation and demanded a judicial commission, while police had not submitted the challan even after three months had passed. The District Public Prosecutor had also written to the CPO Rawalpindi over the continuous delay, and the Punjab government formed a high-powered committee headed by Additional IG CCD Sohail Zafar Chattha to investigate the case. The committee declared the victim's domestic servant the main accused, and police arrested her husband Faisal Asfar and the servant, both of whom are in Adiala Jail on judicial remand.
