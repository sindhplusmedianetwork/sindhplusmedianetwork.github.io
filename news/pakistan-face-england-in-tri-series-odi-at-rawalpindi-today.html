Pakistan Face England in Tri-Series ODI at Rawalpindi Today

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan will take on England in the second one-day international of the tri-series at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Tuesday, with the match starting at 1pm. Pakistan opened the series in style on Sunday, beating Sri Lanka by 37 runs after Salman Ali Agha's brilliant 107 carried the hosts to 305, and the pace attack then bowled the visitors out for 268. England, fresh off their own preparations, welcome back Adil Rashid and Sam Curran from injury for the encounter.

However, the tourists will be without Jos Buttler, who is sidelined with a hamstring injury, while Jofra Archer has been rested. A win for Pakistan would put them in a commanding position in the series, with the final scheduled in Lahore on October 31.
