Government extends security guarantees to foreign investors

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Political leaders of Pakistan's mineral-rich provinces extended security guarantees to foreign investors on the opening day of the third Pakistan International Mineral Forum. Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz offered concessional loans, cheaper leased land and waived customs duties on mining imports. Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti announced a new Frontier Constabulary security division to protect investors.

The assurances were aimed at encouraging foreign investment in the country's mineral sector. The Reko Diq project remains paused until June next year. It is expected to become operational by 2029.
