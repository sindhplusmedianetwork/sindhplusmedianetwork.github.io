Man Utd 'Reach Verbal Agreement' With Pochettino, Reports Say

Manchester United have reportedly reached a verbal agreement in principle with Mauricio Pochettino to replace Michael Carrick as manager, in a bid to revive the fortunes of the struggling English giants. Carrick's position has been under intense speculation following United's worst start to a season in 40 years, leaving them in the Premier League relegation zone after an eight-match winless run. His future is expected to be decided at a meeting in Monaco on Wednesday.

Pochettino, the United States head coach, signed a contract extension only three months ago through to the 2030 World Cup, and US Soccer would be due compensation. The 54-year-old is understood to have a $10 million release clause.
