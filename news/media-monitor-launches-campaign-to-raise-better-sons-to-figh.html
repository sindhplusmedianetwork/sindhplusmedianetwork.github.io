Media monitor launches campaign to 'raise better sons' to fight gender-based violence

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Uks Research Centre organised a panel discussion on 'Baita sudharo, baiti bachao' (raise better sons, protect our daughters) at the Karachi Press Club on Tuesday, as the curtain raiser to the global 16 Days of Activism Against Gender-Based Violence 2026 campaign, which runs from November 25 to December 10. The campaign, in line with the United Nations 2026 theme 'UNiTE to End Violence Against Women and Girls: Ensure Justice, Protect Survivors, End Impunity', will highlight the need to challenge misogyny and rethink how boys are raised, with families, teachers and communities playing a vital role through panel discussions, podcasts and messages from prominent voices. Uks director Tasneem Ahmar said eight podcasts had been recorded with notable personalities covering many aspects, adding: 'We want to bring change through the media.' Senior educator Dr Tauseef Ahmed Khan said women were still viewed as inferior to men in society despite their hard work, while senior journalist Lubna Jerar said education was very important for women, though the education infrastructure had been designed by men.

Women's rights advocate Mahnaz Rahman said the campaign was about changing attitudes, noting that behaviour such as a child demanding freshly cooked food from his grandmother needed to be corrected early, or he would grow up making similar demands from his wife. Ahmar also pointed out that boys were taught from childhood that they were not supposed to cry, while senior journalist Sohail Sangi said attitudes towards women had changed with religions, regions and economic inequality.
