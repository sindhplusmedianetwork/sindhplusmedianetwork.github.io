BISP Launches 'Social Protection Wallet' for Deserving Women

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Benazir Income Support Programme has introduced a 'Social Protection Wallet', a modern, transparent and secure digital payment system for deserving women. Registered women will receive a free mobile SIM linked to a digital wallet. The wallet works on ordinary feature phones, so no smartphone is required.

It removes the old limited-mandate account rule under which funds lapsed if not withdrawn within a fixed period. It also adds inter-bank interoperability so women are not tied to one bank or agent. An SMS notification will be sent on each transfer, giving beneficiaries direct visibility of their payments.
