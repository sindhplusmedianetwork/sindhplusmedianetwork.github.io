US Homeland Security chief dismisses Iran 'dirty bomb' report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

United States Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin has said he does not believe there is an immediate threat of Iran attacking the US with a "dirty bomb", as claimed by a CNN report. The statement echoes a similar rejection by Secretary of State Marco Rubio, who had dismissed the report a day earlier. Asked by Democratic Representative Al Green whether he agreed that Iran is not an imminent threat in terms of a dirty bomb plot, Mullin responded: "Yes." Rubio had rejected a CNN report citing US intelligence that Iran was developing plans to target senior Trump administration officials with a chemical weapon, saying he did not think there was any imminent threat.

The exchanges came as hostilities flare between Washington and Tehran, with US Central Command chief Admiral Brad Cooper meeting Israeli military officials during a visit to Israel. The US military has also warned that ships receiving cargo at sea from vessels loaded in Iran could be treated as evading the US blockade of Iranian ports.
