Bilawal Lays Foundation of 600-Bed Hospital in Larkana

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has laid the foundation stone of a 600-bed hospital in Larkana, saying the free treatment facility will be completed by 2028. Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah joined him at the ceremony. The hospital will give free treatment to the people of Larkana and modern healthcare to people from underserved areas.

Speaking at the ceremony, Mr Bhutto-Zardari said that after being elected MNA from Larkana, he met doctors at Chandka Hospital who were doing everything they could for patients. Chandka Hospital was built in the 1970s for the population of that time, he said, and also serves people from surrounding areas and other cities. He said he had thought that if he got the opportunity, he would build a new hospital for the people of Larkana, and that dream has come true today.

He added that Larkana residents would also be offered treatment through SIUT. Turning to civic services, the PPP leader said work had begun to revive and expand the Ratodero water supply scheme, a project his mother had set up that was later shut down.
