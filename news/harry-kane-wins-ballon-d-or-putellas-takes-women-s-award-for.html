Harry Kane Wins Ballon d'Or, Putellas Takes Women's Award for Third Time

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 27, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harry Kane completed his long quest for football's highest individual honour on Monday, winning the Ballon d'Or after a record-breaking season that cemented his status as one of the game's greatest goalscorers. Kane is the first Englishman to win the prestigious award in 25 years, after Michael Owen won it in 2001. Accepting the award in London, he said he was almost speechless and that being in London made the night extra special.

In 2025-26, Kane produced the most prolific campaign of his life, scoring 61 goals in 51 games for Bayern as the German club completed a domestic league and cup double. He finished the campaign with 73 goals for club and country, and scored six goals during the tournament in which he led England to the semi-finals. At 33, Kane has claimed the game's highest individual honour as England's all-time leading marksman and their record World Cup scorer.

Spain midfielder Alexia Putellas won the Ballon d'Or Feminin for the third time, after winning the treble with Barcelona in the 2025-26 campaign in addition to the Spanish Super Cup, and she moved to Women's Super League side London City Lionesses in July after 14 years at Barcelona.
