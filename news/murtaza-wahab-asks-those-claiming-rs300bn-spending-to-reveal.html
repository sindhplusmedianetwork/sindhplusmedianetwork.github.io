Murtaza Wahab Asks Those Claiming Rs300bn Spending to Reveal Source of Funds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 03, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Karachi Mayor Murtaza Wahab has challenged those who claim to have spent Rs300 billion on development projects to disclose where the money came from and who approved the budget. Addressing a press conference at the Karachi Metropolitan Corporation, Wahab said development work was visibly underway in towns whose representatives claimed they lacked powers and resources. He argued that the local government system had no flaws and that what mattered was the will to work.

Those claiming massive spending, he said, should explain the source of the funds and the budget approval process. The mayor clarified that his purpose was not to demand accountability from anyone but to understand the details of how development works were funded and carried out.
