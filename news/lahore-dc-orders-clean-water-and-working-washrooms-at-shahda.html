Lahore DC Orders Clean Water and Working Washrooms at Shahdara School

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Deputy Commissioner Muhammad Ali Ejaz visited Ravi Tehsil and inspected cleanliness arrangements and educational facilities at a government school in Shahdara Town. During the visit, he reviewed the facilities available to students at the school. He directed the school administration to ensure the immediate provision of clean drinking water for the students.

He also directed that functional washrooms be made available to them. The deputy commissioner further planted a sapling on the school premises as part of the plantation campaign. The visit focused on improving the educational environment and basic facilities for students at the school.
