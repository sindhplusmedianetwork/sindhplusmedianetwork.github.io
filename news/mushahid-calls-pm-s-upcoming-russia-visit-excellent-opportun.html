Mushahid calls PM's upcoming Russia visit 'excellent opportunity' to reset bilateral ties

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed has described Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif's upcoming visit to Russia as an excellent opportunity to reset bilateral relations, saying the engagement could open a new chapter in ties between the two countries. Addressing the Global South political conference hosted by Russia's ruling United Russia Party in Moscow as a keynote speaker, Mushahid called Russia a good friend of Pakistan, saying the two countries shared a common worldview and converging interests as integral partners in Eurasia. He said Pakistan looked forward to warmly welcoming Russian President Vladimir Putin to Islamabad for the 2027 summit of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation.

The former senator was invited to chair both the opening plenary and concluding sessions of the conference, which brought together around 300 representatives from 72 countries and was presided over by former Russian president and United Russia Party chairman Dmitry Medvedev. On the sidelines, Mushahid also met Medvedev, who conveyed his good wishes to the people of Pakistan. In his keynote address, he said the changing international order was marked by transformation, turbulence and transition, pointing to the relative retrenchment of the West, the rise of China and the growing influence of middle powers, including Pakistan.

Mushahid also welcomed the proposed November 19 meeting in Shenzhen between Chinese President Xi Jinping, Russian President Vladimir Putin and US President Donald Trump, describing it as a significant moment in the evolution of the international order, and he concluded with a call for building a better, peaceful and prosperous tomorrow with no overlords and no underdogs.
