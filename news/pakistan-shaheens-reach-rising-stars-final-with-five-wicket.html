Pakistan Shaheens Reach Rising Stars Final With Five-Wicket Win Over Bangladesh A

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan Shaheens produced a composed five-wicket victory over Bangladesh A in the second semi-final of the Asia Cup Rising Stars on Friday. Chasing a target of 233, the defending champions reached 233 for five in 45.2 overs to book their place in Sunday's final against Sri Lanka A. The chase was shaped by a measured third-wicket partnership between Hasan Nawaz and Shahzaib Khan after Pakistan had lost both openers inside the first five overs.

Shahzaib made 50 from 85 deliveries, while Hasan's 91-ball 62 included three fours and three sixes. Their stand took the total to 128 before Hasan was dismissed in the 31st over, and Shahzaib fell in the 35th over after reaching his half-century. Captain Abdul Samad then struck 44 from 33 balls, and Farhan Yousaf remained unbeaten on 46 from 39 deliveries to see Pakistan home.

Earlier, Bangladesh A had posted 232 for eight, lifted by a superb unbeaten 103 from Shahadat Hossain, who faced 118 balls and hit seven fours and three sixes. Sufyan Moqim was central to Pakistan's recovery with three wickets for 29 runs, while Hasan took two wickets for 30 and Ali Raza also claimed two wickets, and Hasan Nawaz was named player of the match for his all-round performance.
