Karachi: sewer cover flips water tanker, CCTV video emerges

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 01, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A water-filled heavy tanker overturned on a Karachi road after a sewer line cover near Nipa Chowrangi failed to bear its weight. CCTV footage of the incident has emerged, showing the tanker coming out of a side street onto the main road. The footage shows the tanker's front wheel climbing onto the sewer-line hole, which dislodged the heavy cover from its place.

As soon as the tanker's rear wheel reached the hole, the vehicle lost balance and toppled over. The driver suffered minor injuries in the accident, while the water spilling from the tanker turned the road into a pond-like scene. The incident once again highlighted the poor condition of the city's sewer infrastructure.
