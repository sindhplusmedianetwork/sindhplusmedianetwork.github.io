Democrats Will Work in January to Stop 'Illegal War' With Iran, Says Wasserman Schultz

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US Congresswoman Debbie Wasserman Schultz has announced that the Democrats will work to stop the 'illegal war' with Iran. In a statement posted on social media, she said the Democrats, who won majorities in both houses of Congress in the midterm elections, will work in January to stop the war with Iran. She referred to the slogan of President Trump's supporters, 'Make America Great Again', and said the extremists of the movement had tried to break the country but failed.

Wasserman Schultz said the Democrats would bring back American values and put an end to extremism. Her statement comes as debate continues in Washington over the US military action against Iran and whether it had proper authorisation. The Democrats are preparing to take control of Congress in January after their midterm victories.
