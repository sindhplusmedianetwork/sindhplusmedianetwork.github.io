Oil slumps 9%, Wall Street rallies after Democrats sweep US midterms

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Oil prices tumbled nearly 9% on Wednesday in volatile trading as markets reacted to the Democrats' sweep of the US Congress and awaited Washington's next move in the US-Israeli war with Iran. Brent crude dropped $9.27, or 8.9%, to $94.29 a barrel, its lowest since September 4, while US WTI crude fell $7.44, or 7.8%, to $88.54. US stocks climbed broadly on Wednesday as the midterm result eased political uncertainty and boosted risk appetite, although shares of data centre companies slipped on fears of tighter AI regulation.

The prospect of fewer sweeping legislative changes lifted some large technology stocks, but companies tied to the AI infrastructure buildout fell. The dollar weakened against most currencies and yields on long-dated US Treasuries moved lower. President Trump acknowledged that the US-Israeli war on Iran had played a role in his party's defeat in the elections, while Iran's supreme leader said in a written statement that US hostility would persist regardless of which party held power.

Defence stocks including Northrop Grumman, RTX and Lockheed Martin fell sharply, as analysts warned that Trump's $1.5 trillion defence budget request could now face a much tougher road through Congress. One analyst said the elections were giving market bears a reason to cast doubt on the continuation of the Iran campaign.
