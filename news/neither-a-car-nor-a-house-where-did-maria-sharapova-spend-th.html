Neither a Car nor a House: Where Did Maria Sharapova Spend Thousands of Dollars After Winning Wimbledon?

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Russian tennis star Maria Sharapova has revealed that with the prize money she received after winning the Wimbledon title, she bought neither a car nor a house but an expensive mattress. Sharapova defeated American star Serena Williams in the 2004 Wimbledon women's singles final to win a Grand Slam title at just 17 years of age, after which she received prize money of 560,500 pounds. Speaking on the Pretty Tough podcast, she said that after receiving her first big prize money she went with her mother to a mattress shop and bought a mattress worth 10,000 dollars.

She said she still feels embarrassed about the purchase, but it was the first big purchase of her life. Sharapova recounted that when she returned home with her mother, her father asked where they had gone, and she replied that they had gone shopping. Her father asked which bag she had bought, to which Sharapova replied that she had not bought any bag but a mattress costing 10,000 dollars.

According to Sharapova, her father was surprised at the unusual purchase, and the mattress is still with her today.
