150+ Writers and Artists Back Rooney, Call UK Palestine Action Ban 'Draconian'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 150 writers and artists have signed an open letter urging the UK government to amend its ban on Palestine Action, which the letter calls draconian. The move follows Irish author Sally Rooney's announcement that she was halting book sales in Britain because of the ban. The letter carries the signatures of Nobel laureates Annie Ernaux and Anne Carson, along with actors Paul Mescal and Tilda Swinton.

It says the withdrawal of Rooney's books is an affront to basic principles of free speech. Rooney and her publisher Faber ended UK sales last week because of her support for the pro-Palestinian group. The group was proscribed as a terrorist group in July 2025, which means Faber cannot legally pass UK royalties to her.
