Houthi Missile and Drone Attacks on Saudi Arabia Intensify, Oil Sites Claimed as Targets

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Missile and drone attacks by Yemen's Houthi group on Saudi Arabia have intensified, with the Houthis claiming strikes on key oil installations. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said on X that a drone hit pumping station 7 of the East-West oil pipeline and a ballistic missile targeted an oil facility at Yanbu on the Red Sea coast. He also claimed strikes on an airport and an airbase near the Yemeni border.

The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted and destroyed more drones over the Eastern Province and Riyadh. The Houthi claims have not been independently verified, and Saudi authorities have not yet confirmed or denied them in detail.
