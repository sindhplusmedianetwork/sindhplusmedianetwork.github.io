MQM-P seeks court action over Bilawal's bloodshed threat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan Chairman and Federal Minister Dr Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui on Thursday demanded that the federal government and courts take notice of what he described as a bloodshed threat by PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari. He said MQM-P was ready for dialogue but would continue its struggle through legal, judicial and parliamentary forums. Siddiqui said the party respected Bilawal's mandate and did not want confrontation, adding that it would review its November 29 rally following requests concerning law and order and consult its supporters before making a final decision.

He reiterated that new provinces should be created across the country in proportion to population growth, insisting the matter be pursued through political, democratic and constitutional means. Addressing a press conference at the party's Bahadurabad headquarters, he said MQM-P was not demanding a separate Muhajir province but advocating new provinces throughout the country, while urban Sindh had the strongest case for administrative reorganisation. He added that threats would not deter MQM-P and urged the PPP leadership to respond to its arguments in parliament rather than through confrontational statements.
