Two wanted suspects killed in police operation in Kot Momin

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two proclaimed offenders wanted in dozens of serious criminal cases were killed in an alleged exchange of fire during a joint operation by Sargodha Police and the Counter Terrorism Department in Kot Momin on Saturday night. Police said Inam Wains, wanted in 32 serious cases including contract killings, kidnapping for ransom and terrorism, and Faisal Budheka died in the exchange of fire. Officials said the two men allegedly opened fire on a police party at a checkpoint near Chak 12 SB.

Police said weapons and ammunition were recovered from the slain suspects. The recovered weapons included a rocket launcher, a drone camera and hand grenades, officials said. Hundreds of rounds of ammunition were also recovered, according to officials.
