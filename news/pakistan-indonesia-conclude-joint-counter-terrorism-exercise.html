Pakistan, Indonesia Conclude Joint Counter-Terrorism Exercise Elang Strike Three

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The joint counter-terrorism exercise Elang Strike Three between the armed forces of Pakistan and Indonesia has concluded. According to the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), the exercise held under military cooperation between the two countries had started on November 9. The joint military training activities were organised at the National Counter Terrorism Centre (NCTC) in Pabbi, where personnel of the Pakistan Army and the Indonesian Army participated to improve their skills in various fields of counter-terrorism.

The basic purpose of the exercise was the exchange of experiences gained in counter-terrorism operations, improvement in operational procedures and making practical exercises more effective. During the exercise, the forces of both countries benefited from each other's experiences, with special focus on mutual cooperation, exchange of best practices and enhancement of combat skills. The ISPR said the joint exercise reflects the longstanding military cooperation between the two countries and reiterated the shared resolve to further strengthen defence relations between Pakistan and Indonesia.
