Hafiz Naeem says PPP winning 7 Karachi seats would be 'near doomsday'

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamaat-e-Islami chief Hafiz Naeem-ur-Rehman said it would be akin to the Day of Judgment if the Pakistan Peoples Party won seven National Assembly seats from Karachi. Speaking at an event, he said the MQM and the PPP have practically become history, and claimed his party's average vote is 45,000 against the PPP's 15,000. He said Karachi's seats would have increased if the population had been counted correctly.

Hafiz Naeem criticised incomplete projects in the city, including the K-IV water scheme, the S-III sewerage project, and the Green Line and Red Line. He also said Karachi was owed Rs3,360 billion over 15 years and that its share of provincial funds fell from 76 percent to 18 percent.
