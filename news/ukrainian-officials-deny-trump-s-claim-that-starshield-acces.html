Ukrainian officials deny Trump's claim that Starshield access was restored

By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Donald Trump said on Thursday that Elon Musk had restored Ukraine's access to the military-grade Starshield satellite service following a phone call between the two. However, Ukrainian officials said on Friday morning that the restrictions were still in place, hampering interceptor drones and military operations. Serhiy Beskrestnov, an adviser to President Zelensky, said: 'At this time, we are not observing any lifting of restrictions on Starlink.' A Ukrainian officer told CNN that connection drops of ten to fifteen seconds were complicating battlefield coordination.

Musk responded that the limits 'are decided by the US military and ultimately POTUS.' The dispute came as Russia launched 176 attack drones overnight on Thursday, killing nine civilians and injuring 76, while over 500,000 people lost power across six regions.
