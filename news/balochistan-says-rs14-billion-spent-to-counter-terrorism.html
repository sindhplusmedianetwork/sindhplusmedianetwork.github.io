Balochistan Says Rs14 Billion Spent to Counter Terrorism

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Balochistan government officials have said that more than Rs14 billion has been spent in the province to counter terrorism under the National Action Plan. At a joint press conference, Home Minister Ziaullah Langove, Adviser Shahid Rind and Additional Chief Secretary Home Hamza Shafqaat said 18 laws were enacted and 17 institutions established. They claimed that over 60,000 counterterrorism operations had been carried out up to October, in which more than 1,100 terrorists were killed.

The officials said 101 policemen, around 200 law-enforcement personnel and 551 civilians lost their lives in the province. The briefing was aimed at presenting the province's account of its fight against militancy.
