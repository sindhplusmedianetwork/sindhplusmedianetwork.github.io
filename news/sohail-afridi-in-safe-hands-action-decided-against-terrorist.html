Sohail Afridi in Safe Hands; Action Decided Against Terrorists and Facilitators, Says KP Governor

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has said that former chief minister Sohail Afridi is in safe hands. He was speaking during a visit to Charsadda, where he offered condolences to the families of martyred DSP Imranuddin and police constable Kazim Jan. The governor said the apex committee has decided that action will be taken against terrorists as well as their facilitators.

He added that the prime minister and the field marshal have assured the province of all required resources. Kundi claimed that terrorists are using weapons abandoned by the United States in Afghanistan against Pakistan. He warned that those who refuse to accept Pakistan's constitution and law will face the consequences.
