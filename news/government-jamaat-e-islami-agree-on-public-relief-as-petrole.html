Government, Jamaat-e-Islami agree on public relief as petroleum levy talks conclude

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Talks between Jamaat-e-Islami and government committees on ending the petroleum levy and reducing prices have concluded, Jamaat-e-Islami leader Liaquat Baloch said after the meeting. He said ten rounds of negotiations had been completed and that the two sides agreed in Tuesday's session that the public would be given relief. Baloch said the government had pointed to war conditions around the world, but it was agreed that the scope and duration of the relief would be expanded.

He said Jamaat-e-Islami proposed including 1,000cc cars, tractors and public transport in the relief, and the government was largely willing to include 1,000cc cars and tractors. He added that the government recognised that ending the petroleum levy could bring relief to the public. Baloch said the government had begun work on the matter and would present its own plan for ending the levy, along with a programme running up to the budget.
