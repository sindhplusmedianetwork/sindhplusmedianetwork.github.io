Pakistan reach 281-5 as debutants shine against Sri Lanka

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Captain Babar Azam and two debutants were among four batters to hit half-centuries as Pakistan reached 281-5 on day one of the first Test against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi on Monday. Opener Tayyab Arif scored an impressive 57 and Mohammad Taha a patient 51, both playing their first Test, while Azam hit 50 and Saud Shakeel was unbeaten on 57 when bad light stopped play 15 minutes before schedule, with Ghazi Ghori seven not out. Sri Lanka's decision to bowl on what appeared to be a green pitch seemed to backfire, and their choice to go with a pace quartet and without a frontline spinner could also prove costly, with the pitch not offering much to the fast bowlers.

Asitha Fernando was the pick of the bowlers with 2-64, while Azam looked in good touch but was dismissed by Dilshan Madushanka after the lunch break, having hit seven fours in his 58-ball innings and now gone 41 innings without a Test century, his last coming against New Zealand in Karachi in December 2022. Arif put on a confident 86-run opening stand with Azan Awais, who made 30, and Sri Lanka had to wait until the 16th over for a breakthrough, when Milan Rathnayaka had Awais caught behind by Kusal Mendis. All-rounder Arafat Minhas and pace bowler Saqib Khan are also making Test debuts for Pakistan, and the second and final Test is in Lahore from November 17.
