Pakistan successfully tests Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has successfully test-fired the Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile, the Inter-Services Public Relations said. The military's media wing described the test as another significant milestone in strengthening the country's national defence capabilities. The missile is equipped with advanced navigation and propulsion systems, allowing it to engage targets at long ranges.

It can strike with high precision and lethality while travelling at supersonic speeds. The President, the prime minister and the chief of defence forces congratulated the armed forces on the successful test. They also praised the scientists and engineers who worked on the missile system.

The test marks the latest addition to Pakistan's growing missile programme.
