Chinese Scientists Discover Two Huge Tears in Tectonic Plate Beneath Iran

By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chinese scientists have discovered two enormous tears in a large tectonic plate beneath south-eastern Iran, in the Arabian Plate. The research, led by scientists at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, says the tears formed because of differences in the density and structure of the rock types in the plate. One part of the plate was once part of the ancient Neo-Tethys ocean floor, where heavy basaltic rocks are sinking beneath another plate, while the other part consists of lighter and thicker continental rocks.

Scientists say the difference between oceanic and continental rocks puts pressure on different parts of the plate, producing the large tears, while the continental rocks resist sinking and instead help form mountains. The study has been published in the journal Nature Geoscience. Researchers say the discovery will help scientists understand how oceans close over millions of years, how continents collide and how major geographical changes take place on the Earth's surface.

They describe it as an important step in understanding the Earth's geological history and the complex movement of tectonic plates.
