27 Dow University Employees Found With Fake Degrees; Salaries Ordered Recovered

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 21, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Sindh Assembly's Public Accounts Committee has uncovered that 27 employees of the Dow University of Health Sciences were working on fake educational degrees. The revelation came during a meeting of the committee chaired by Nisar Khuhro, where officials confirmed the degrees had been declared bogus. The committee ordered that the entire salaries drawn by the 27 employees be recovered from them.

It also directed that a report on the verification of staff degrees be submitted for all government universities of Sindh. Officials told the committee that out of 5,350 employees of Dow University, degrees of only 1,945 have been verified so far. The committee expressed concern over the slow pace of verification and asked for the process to be completed on priority.
