£190m Case Hearing Set for November 25

The Islamabad High Court has scheduled the hearing for the appeals filed by former prime minister Imran Khan and his spouse Bushra Bibi in the £190m case. A division bench will resume hearing the appeal on November 25. The bench consists of Chief Justice Sardar Muhammad Safraz Dogar and Justice Muhammad Asif.

At the last hearing, the court rejected Bushra Bibi's request for immediate transfer to a specialized hospital. The court ordered authorities to ensure she receives proper treatment at Adiala Jail.
