Imran voices concern over Bushra Bibi's eye ailment, directs lawyer to seek court relief

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

PTI founder Imran Khan has expressed concern over his wife Bushra Bibi's health and directed his lawyer to seek relief for her through the courts. During Monday's meeting at Adiala Jail, Khan told his counsel that the legal route should be pursued, especially in Bushra Bibi's case, and the matter raised at the appropriate judicial forums. Sources told Geo News that Bushra Bibi is facing an eye-related ailment, which has become a source of worry for the former prime minister.

The meeting, held after a long gap, was kept strictly professional and limited to legal matters by mutual agreement, with no political discussion taking place. Sources said Khan was already aware of recent political developments, including the imposition of governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and talk of a federal emergency, even though his lawyer did not brief him on these issues. His own health was described as satisfactory during the conversation.

The meeting took place amid intense political tension following the federal takeover in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, where the PTI had been in power.
