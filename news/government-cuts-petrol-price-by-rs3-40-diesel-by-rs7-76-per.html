Government cuts petrol price by Rs3.40, diesel by Rs7.76 per litre

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The federal government on Monday slashed the price of petrol by Rs3.40 per litre and high-speed diesel by Rs7.76 per litre. Following the revision, petrol will retail at Rs374.51 per litre while high-speed diesel will cost Rs395.20, with the new prices applicable from Tuesday, November 3, according to a notification by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority. The government will continue to levy Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel.

The move follows the government's announcement that oil refineries would absorb a burden of about Rs15 billion a month so that petrol could be sold Rs15 cheaper than the global market amid the US-Israeli war on Iran. At a press conference in Islamabad, Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik thanked the refineries for raising their throughput and said their cooperation had helped avert the Rs15 increase that was due on Friday. The minister added that the cabinet and the prime minister had decided to give Ogra the responsibility of fixing fuel prices daily in line with international market trends.

Separately, Jamaat-i-Islami chief Hafiz Naeemur Rehman, who met Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on October 27 over the party's demand for levy relief, agreed with the government on forming a joint committee to consider expanding the fuel relief scheme for motorcycles, rickshaws and vehicles of up to 800cc. The government has also reintroduced austerity measures in response to rising fuel prices, requiring markets to close by 9pm and cutting fuel allocations for official vehicles by 50 per cent for three months.
