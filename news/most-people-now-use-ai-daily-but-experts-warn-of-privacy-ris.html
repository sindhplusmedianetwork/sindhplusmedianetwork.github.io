Most people now use AI daily, but experts warn of privacy risks

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Kaspersky survey across the Middle East, Turkiye and Africa has found that 79 per cent of respondents use artificial intelligence services in their daily lives. About 74 per cent said they use AI to search for and summarise information, while many also turn to it for health questions and personal advice. The findings show how quickly AI tools have become part of everyday routines across the region.

Experts warned that the trend raises privacy and cybersecurity risks, because people are sharing personal information with AI tools. They urged users to be careful about what they disclose when seeking help or advice from such services. The survey underlines the need for safer use of AI as its popularity keeps growing.
