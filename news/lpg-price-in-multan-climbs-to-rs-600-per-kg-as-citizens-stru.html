LPG price in Multan climbs to Rs 600 per kg as citizens struggle with costly gas

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

LPG prices in Multan have continued to climb sharply, adding to citizens' difficulties as gas is now being sold at between Rs 560 and Rs 600 per kilogram in different areas of the city, up from Rs 530. Shopkeepers say commercial cylinder prices have also risen steeply, with a 43-kilogram commercial cylinder now selling for Rs 23,700. Citizens have complained about the persistent price increases with the change of weather and demanded that the authorities take immediate notice.

The rising prices are hitting household budgets as winter approaches and demand for gas increases. Residents say the repeated hikes have made cooking gas unaffordable for many families. They are urging the administration to check the prices and provide relief.
