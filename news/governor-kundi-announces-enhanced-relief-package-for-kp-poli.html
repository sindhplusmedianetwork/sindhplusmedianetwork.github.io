Governor Kundi Announces Enhanced Relief Package for KP Police

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 15, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has announced a major revised welfare package for the provincial police, sharply raising compensation for martyrs, injured personnel and their families. Speaking at a police martyrs' ceremony at the Governor House in Peshawar, he said compensation for martyred constables, head constables, assistant sub-inspectors, sub-inspectors and inspectors would rise from Rs11 million to Rs17 million. The package for martyred DSPs and SPs has been set at Rs25 million, Rs30 million for SSPs, AIGs and officers in grades 18 and 19, and Rs35 million for DIGs, the provincial police chief and officers in grade 20 and above.

Cash payments will replace residential plots for bereaved families, ranging from Rs18 million for families of constables and head constables up to Rs70 million for the most senior officers. Compensation for personnel left permanently disabled has increased from Rs500,000 to Rs5 million, and for the seriously injured from Rs100,000 to Rs1.5 million. The governor also pledged Rs1 million each for the marriage of martyrs' daughters, financial help for children's education, and a raise in police salaries modeled on Punjab's welfare package.

The announcement comes as the province faces continued security challenges, including a recent attack on a police post in Peshawar's Hassan Khel area that claimed the life of police official Muhammad Jalal.
