JUI-F Deputy Chief Qari Iftikhar taken into custody, says party spokesperson

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) Deputy Chief Qari Iftikhar has allegedly been taken into custody by unidentified individuals, the party's spokesperson said on Monday. JUI-F spokesperson Aslam Ghauri said he and his colleagues were intercepted by men dressed in plain clothes near E-11 in Islamabad, and four to five individuals forced them into a vehicle. He said the men also confiscated his mobile phone and other identification documents.

"We were not told which institution these individuals belonged to or why we were being taken into custody," Ghauri said. He said he and one of his colleagues were driven around the city for approximately an hour before being released, while the unidentified men took Qari Iftikhar away and gave no information about his whereabouts. Iftikhar serves as the deputy chief of JUI-F and is also in charge of the party's Islamabad office.

There was no immediate official statement from the relevant authorities regarding the alleged incident, which comes amid growing tensions between the government and the JUI-F following the imposition of governor's rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa.
