'Imran Khan was also from Aitchison': debutants' fifties win praise on difficult Rawalpindi pitch

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 10, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Debutant Tayyab Arif won praise for his half-century on a difficult Rawalpindi pitch on the opening day of the first Test against Sri Lanka, in a column tracing the long line of Aitchison College alumni in Pakistan cricket. Columnist Dr Nauman Niaz pointed out that Majid Khan, Imran Khan, Aamer Hameed, Ramiz Raja, Bazid Khan and Wahab Riaz were all educated at Aitchison College in Lahore. Tayyab Arif, 20, struck 57 from 51 balls in his first Test innings, reaching his fifty in 46 balls and sharing an 86-run opening stand with Azan Awais.

Fellow debutant Mohammad Taha made 51, while captain Babar Azam scored 50 and Saud Shakeel remained unbeaten on 57. Pakistan finished the day on 281 for 5 after 67.2 overs, with bad light ending play early, as Sri Lanka's decision to bowl first on a green-looking pitch backfired.
