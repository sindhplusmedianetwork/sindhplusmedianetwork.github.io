Making youth financially stable is Maryam Nawaz's top priority, says Uzma Bukhari

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Information and Culture Minister Uzma Bukhari has said that making young people financially stable is among the top priorities of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz. She said the CM IT Internship Programme is practical proof of that vision, as thousands of young people were given opportunities to receive training and practical experience in the IT sector. In her statement, Bukhari said that during the four-month internship programme, each intern was also given a monthly stipend of Rs50,000.

She said the aim was to equip young graduates with modern IT skills and to make them financially self-reliant. Registration for the third phase of the programme is now open, through which more graduates will be given opportunities to receive training in the IT sector and earn a livelihood. Bukhari said the Punjab government was taking practical steps, not just making promises, to improve the future of young people, and that giving young people skills, training and financial opportunities was the practical expression of Maryam Nawaz's developing Punjab.
