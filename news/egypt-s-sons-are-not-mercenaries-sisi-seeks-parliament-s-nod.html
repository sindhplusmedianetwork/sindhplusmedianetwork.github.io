'Egypt's Sons Are Not Mercenaries': Sisi Seeks Parliament's Nod to Defend Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi has appealed to parliament to authorise 'necessary measures' to defend Saudi Arabia should Riyadh request it. He said Egypt would not merely be a spectator if Saudi Arabia's security was harmed. Any military intervention, he said, would be defensive — not aimed at attack or waging war.

Supporters described the move as backing for 'brotherly countries'. Critics opposed what they called a blank check for unclear military intervention, saying 'Egypt's sons are not mercenaries'. The appeal came as Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman visited Cairo.

In his Saturday speech, Sisi also recalled Gulf support for Egypt after 2013, saying it played an important role in restoring the Egyptian state's capabilities.
