Armed Men Abduct 20 Under-Training Police Personnel in Mastung

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Unidentified armed men abducted 20 under-training police personnel in Balochistan's Mastung district on Saturday, officials confirmed. The recruits, belonging to the Levies Force of the Rakhshan division, were undergoing training at the Kalat Police Training Centre and were travelling in a passenger van along the Quetta-Karachi Highway when they were intercepted. According to officials, the personnel were heading home for the weekend on leave when the armed men stopped their van, took the recruits to an undisclosed location, and released the driver along with three other passengers.

A senior Balochistan government official confirmed the abduction, while police said efforts for the safe recovery of the personnel were underway. Security forces have launched a search operation in the nearby mountains. No group has claimed responsibility for the abduction so far.
