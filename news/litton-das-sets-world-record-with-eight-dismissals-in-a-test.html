Litton Das sets world record with eight dismissals in a Test innings

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Bangladesh wicketkeeper-batter Litton Das has set a new world record in Test cricket, taking part in eight dismissals in a single innings against South Africa in the Johannesburg Test. He reached the mark on day four of the match by stumping South African fast bowler Kagiso Rabada, the dismissal that took him past the previous record. The old record of seven dismissals in a Test innings was shared by five wicketkeepers, and no keeper had ever managed eight before Litton.

His record-breaking performance came behind the stumps in a match in which he was involved in every dismissal of the innings. Litton also became the first wicketkeeper in Test history to score a century and claim eight dismissals in the same match, a double that underlines the scale of his achievement. The performance places the Bangladesh keeper alone at the top of a record list that had stood shared for years.

For Bangladesh cricket, it is a landmark individual feat on one of the biggest stages of the game.
