Nepra notifies costlier Rs25.30 power rate for industry despite dissent within regulator

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The National Electric Power Regulatory Authority on Thursday notified a revised incremental consumption package of Rs25.30 per unit for industrial and agricultural consumers to encourage higher electricity use. The revised rate replaces the previous Rs22.98 per unit, introduced in December last year for six months, while the normal rate stood at Rs34.50 per unit last month. Nepra issued the decision, dated November 6, through a statutory regulatory order in the Gazette of Pakistan, along with dissent notes from members Amina Ahmed and Maqsood Anwar Khan and the casting vote of Chairman Waseem Mukhtar.

The authority was divided 2-2 on the matter, and the chairman fully endorsed the dissenting members' arguments before using his casting vote to approve the rate. The two dissenting members maintained that the Rs25.30 rate was a 'random number' unsupported by evidence, noting that Nepra's own calculations put the marginal cost at Rs31.83 per unit, and they called for the package to be suspended until authentic data became available. Industrial consumers complained that the revised rate would result in higher monthly fuel cost adjustments and burden all consumer categories across the country, contrary to the earlier commitment that additional costs would remain ring-fenced.

In his casting vote, the chairman observed that the dissenting members' conclusions needed serious consideration, but said suspending the package at this stage would not be appropriate and the Ministry of Energy should be given another opportunity to provide the information repeatedly sought by the regulator.
