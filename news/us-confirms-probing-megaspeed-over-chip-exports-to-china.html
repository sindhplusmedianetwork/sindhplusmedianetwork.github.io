US confirms probing Megaspeed over chip exports to China

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The United States government confirmed on Monday that it is investigating Singapore-based firm Megaspeed over alleged exports of Nvidia chips to China. Data centre company Megaspeed is suspected of helping to shift Nvidia's most powerful technology to China in circumvention of US rules. The Commerce Department began a probe into Megaspeed in 2024 for potential export control violations, and in May 2025 it requested records from Nvidia concerning direct or indirect sales to the firm.

The following month, Nvidia placed Megaspeed on a no-sell list, while US federal agents conducted an on-site check at the Singapore-based firm and interviewed a former Megaspeed executive, Alice Huang. The Commerce and Justice departments said in a joint statement they were working together to bring the full extent of the law against Megaspeed for any criminal conduct that may have occurred. The scandal has exposed a rift within the administration, with the Treasury Secretary accusing the Commerce Department of ignoring reports that Megaspeed was shifting Nvidia technology to China.

The Commerce Secretary has agreed to testify before the Senate on the matter, while Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang has agreed to testify behind closed doors in the House of Representatives before Thanksgiving, which falls on November 26.
