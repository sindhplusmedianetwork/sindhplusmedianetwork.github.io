UN extends Yemen sanctions by one year, condemns Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The UN Security Council has unanimously adopted a resolution extending the Yemen-related sanctions for one year, keeping asset freezes and travel bans on listed individuals and entities in place until 14 November 2027. The Council also extended the mandate of its Yemen experts panel until December and condemned Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, urging the group to halt military operations against civilians, airports and shipping lanes. Britain's UN envoy Sarah MacIntosh said the move would make the arms embargo more effective, with tighter checks on chemicals and materials used in weapons production.

Yemen's internationally recognised government welcomed the decision but regretted that the Houthis were not formally designated a terrorist group. The resolution keeps the sanctions framework in place while the war in Yemen continues.
