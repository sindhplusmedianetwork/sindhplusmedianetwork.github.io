Rabia Ahsan murder case: husband arrested after telling police he fired the shot

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rawalpindi police have formally arrested Ahsan Tahir, the husband of television anchor Rabia Ahsan, in connection with her murder. The case was registered at Sadar Bairuni police station on the complaint of her brother, Khurram Ahmed, who said the shooting took place on November 8 while her mother and sister were in the lawn of the house. According to the FIR, a gunshot was heard from the bedroom, and when her sister entered the room she found Rabia lying on the floor in a pool of blood with a pistol in her husband's hand.

Police said Ahsan Tahir told them he had fired at his wife with the same pistol, and he could not produce a licence for the 30-bore weapon. A murder charge under Section 302 has been added to the case, along with a section of the Punjab Arms Amendment Ordinance. Police said initial reports suggested the bullet may have gone off accidentally while the pistol was being taken out of clothes during a change of seasonal clothing, but they are investigating all angles, including murder.

The initial post-mortem report found that a bullet struck the lower part of the front of her neck and caused her death, and forensic samples from the scene have been sent to the laboratory.
