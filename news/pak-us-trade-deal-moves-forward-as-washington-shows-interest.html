Pak-US Trade Deal Moves Forward as Washington Shows Interest in Farm Goods and Minerals

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan and the United States have made significant progress in negotiations on a bilateral trade agreement, Commerce Minister Jam Kamal Khan said after meeting US Chargé d'Affaires Natalie A. Baker in Islamabad. A few technical clauses still require further discussions with the office of the US trade representative before the deal can be finalised.

The two sides reviewed prospects for Pakistani mangoes and dates in the American market, including speeding up visits by US inspection teams for mango exports. They also discussed raising Pakistan's imports of American cotton and soybeans. The US side showed strong interest in Pakistan's minerals and mining sector, which Islamabad sees as a major avenue for investment.

Khan invited American businesses to take part in the FoodAg 2026 exhibition, scheduled in Karachi from November 23 to 26.
