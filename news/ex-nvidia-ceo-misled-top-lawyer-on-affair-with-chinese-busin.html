Ex-Nvidia CEO misled top lawyer on affair with Chinese businesswoman, sources say

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network

WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang misled several of the company's leaders about the nature of his relationship with Chinese businesswoman Alice Huang, with whom he had an affair. Those misled included general counsel Tim Teter and chief marketing officer Alison Wagonfeld, according to two people familiar with the matter and internal correspondence reviewed by Reuters. Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO on Thursday.

Nvidia's stock has fallen more than 30% since its October 6 closing high. The fall has erased more than $1.8 trillion from the company's market capitalisation. The disclosures add to the scrutiny surrounding Huang's departure from the company he led.
