Sheikh Hasina says she would rather die in her homeland, plans return in December

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 06, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Former Bangladesh prime minister Sheikh Hasina has said she intends to return home in December this year. In an email interview with the Associated Press, she said that instead of living in exile she is ready to go to jail or face death, adding that if death is her fate she would prefer to die in her own country among her people. Hasina went to India in 2024 after being removed from power by public protests.

Before her removal, she had ordered a violent crackdown on student protesters, in which hundreds were killed and thousands injured, with the United Nations saying around 1,400 people died during the protest movement. In 2025, Dhaka's International Crimes Tribunal sentenced her to death in absentia over the crackdown and crimes against humanity; she has rejected the verdict, questioned the trial's legality and denied involvement in any wrongdoing. Bangladesh has repeatedly sought her extradition, but India has not handed her over.

Her planned return comes at a time when her party, the Awami League, is banned from taking part in political activities and was barred from the general elections held in February, which brought Bangladesh Nationalist Party leader Tariq Rahman to power.
