Trump Predicts Swift End to Iran War, Vows Oil Prices Will Drop

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 16, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

US President Donald Trump once again predicted a swift end to the war with Iran and a sharp drop in oil prices once the conflict concludes. Speaking to reporters in Montana, Trump said the Iran war would end very soon, adding that Iran would never have a nuclear weapon and oil prices would drop like a rock, while expressing uncertainty over which Iranian officials the US was negotiating with. He claimed that US strikes had destroyed Iran's naval fleet, asserting that of the country's 159 ships none remained operational, and that its fighter jets had been destroyed on their runways.

Meanwhile in Tehran, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian warned that military action could not force the country into submission, stressing the need to strengthen national cohesion and unity. He said Iran was facing its most intense blockade since the 1979 Revolution, accusing the United States of trying to make life difficult for Iranians to fuel public anger and trigger unrest. Separately, CNN reported on Thursday that the US was considering using the GBU-43/B Massive Ordnance Air Blast bomb, known as the Mother Of All Bombs, against Iranian energy infrastructure and nuclear sites if Trump decided to renew combat, though officials raised doubts about the bomb's ability to destroy suspected underground nuclear sites and voiced concerns over possible civilian casualties.

Trump is trying to boost his low approval ratings ahead of November's midterm elections, with polls showing the unpopular war in Iran and the resulting fuel and price rises are hurting his Republican Party. Diplomatic efforts to end the conflict have been at an impasse since the summer, and Washington has in recent weeks dialled back military operations in favour of economic pressure.
