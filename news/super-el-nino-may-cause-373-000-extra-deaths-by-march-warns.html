Super El Niño may cause 373,000 extra deaths by March, warns new estimate

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 373,000 excess deaths worldwide are feared in the next six months due to extreme heat linked to the super El Niño, according to new estimates by the Climate Impact Lab. The projection covers the period through next March, with India facing a feared toll of 55,000 deaths and Nigeria 56,000. The World Meteorological Organization says this year's El Niño is the strongest since 1950 and is expected to peak in December.

Scientists warn that prolonged heat waves will hit the most vulnerable populations hardest, including the elderly and outdoor workers. Health experts are urging governments to expand heat-action plans and early-warning systems. The estimate adds to growing concern over climate-driven extreme weather events worldwide.
