Karachi singer Tufail Sanjrani's car stolen outside hospital in Baldia Town

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Singer Tufail Sanjrani's car was stolen from outside a hospital in Karachi's Baldia Town area. Sanjrani lodged a case at Madina Colony police station, saying the car was gone when he came out after a medical check-up. Police have begun searching for the stolen vehicle.

Sindh Inspector General Javed Alam Odho took notice of the incident, directing the SSP Kemari and AVLC officials to recover the car quickly using modern technical and investigative means. He also directed them to arrest those involved in the theft.
