Pakistan Beat Uzbekistan 3-0 in 23rd Asian Team Squash Championship Opener

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan's men's squash team made a winning start to the 23rd Asian Team Squash Championship 2026 at the Mushaf Squash Complex in Islamabad on November 3, beating Uzbekistan 3-0. Noor Zaman led from the front, defeating Muhammad Mustafa Abdurrahmanov 11-2, 11-2, 11-4 in the opening tie. Muhammad Asim Khan doubled the lead with an 11-3, 11-4, 11-2 win over Yekhyokhon Nazirov.

Nasir Iqbal completed the clean sweep, beating Botirjon Anvarjonov 11-6, 11-5, 11-6 in the third rubber. Hosts Pakistan entered the championship as the top seeds in Pool A and the firm favourites to reclaim the Asian team title. The action continues on day two on November 4, with Pakistan's women's team facing Hong Kong China at 2pm while the men take on Korea at 4pm.

The five-day championship brings together 44 players from six nations, including Hong Kong China, Malaysia, Korea, Iran and Uzbekistan.
