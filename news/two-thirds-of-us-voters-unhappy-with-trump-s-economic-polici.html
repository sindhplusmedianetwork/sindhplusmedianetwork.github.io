Two-Thirds of US Voters Unhappy With Trump's Economic Policies, BBC Poll Finds

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 04, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A BBC survey report has found that nearly two-thirds of American voters are unhappy with President Donald Trump's economic policies as the US midterm elections unfolded. According to the survey, 65 percent of voters believe Trump's policies have made the economy worse, while only 28 percent said they improved it. The report says 43 percent of voters named opposition to Trump as a major reason for casting their vote, compared with 16 percent who said support for him motivated them.

On inflation, 79 percent of voters said they had been badly affected by rising prices over the past year, 62 percent blamed Trump's policies for it, 59 percent were deeply concerned about petrol and diesel price increases, and 58 percent worried about rising food and essential goods prices. Early midterm results cited in the report showed Democrats regaining control of the House of Representatives with 174 seats to the Republicans' 104, while in the Senate the Democrats reached 51 seats against 39 for the Republicans. A party needs 218 of the 435 House seats and 51 of 100 Senate seats for a majority, and vote counting was still underway in several states including Vermont, Virginia, South Carolina, Georgia and Florida.
