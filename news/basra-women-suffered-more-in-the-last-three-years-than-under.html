Basra: Women Suffered More in the Last Three Years Than Under the Worst Martial Laws

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Shaukat Basra said in a post on X that those who have their own mothers, sisters and daughters would not inflict such cruelty on other people's mothers, sisters and daughters. He said the worst martial laws did not commit as much oppression against women as has been seen in the last three years. Basra also took aim at the Punjab police over the reported recovery of a stick and a petrol bomb from Hammad Azhar after three years.

He mocked the investigating officer, Inspector Mansab Khan, saying the 'scientist, genius and artistic' officer would surely now receive a Sitara-e-Imtiaz. He said Azhar had carefully preserved the stick and petrol bomb he allegedly had on May 9 so that the officer could recover them after three years. He described Maryam Safdar as a fake chief minister of Form 47 and ridiculed her police force.
