Field Marshal Munir Vows to Decisively Eliminate Terrorism During North Waziristan Visit

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir reaffirmed the security forces' resolve to decisively eliminate terrorism and preserve the hard-earned peace and stability in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa during a visit to Miran Shah in North Waziristan, the military's media wing said on Friday. According to the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), he was given a comprehensive briefing on the prevailing security situation, ongoing intelligence-based operations and measures being undertaken to consolidate the gains achieved through sustained counter-terrorism efforts and ensure lasting peace and stability in the area. The visit to North Waziristan comes a day after Field Marshal Munir visited Turbat in Balochistan, where he was briefed on the security environment, operational preparedness and ongoing operations.

He also interacted with officers and troops and appreciated their high level of preparedness, professionalism and unwavering commitment to the defence of the motherland. The field marshal described the K-P Police as a brave frontline force and expressed pride in their resilience and sacrifices against terrorism and Fitna al-Khawarij. Upon his arrival in Miran Shah, he was received by the Commander Peshawar Corps, and he laid a floral wreath at Yadgar-e-Shuhada to pay homage to the sacrifices of the martyrs of the Pakistan Army.

The latest visit is his third reported visit to North Waziristan during his tenure as army chief.
