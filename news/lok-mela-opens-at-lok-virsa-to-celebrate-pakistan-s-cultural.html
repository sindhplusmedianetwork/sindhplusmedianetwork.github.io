Lok Mela opens at Lok Virsa to celebrate Pakistan's cultural diversity

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The annual Lok Mela opened at Lok Virsa in Islamabad on Friday, bringing together Pakistan's cultural traditions, folk music, handicrafts and regional cuisines. The 10-day festival is being held under the theme 'Naqoosh-i-Watan: Hamara Wirsa, Hamara Mustaqbil' and will run until December 6. Federal Minister for National Heritage and Culture Aurangzeb Khan Khichi inaugurated the festival.

More than 250 artists and artisans are taking part, with 522 stalls set up at the venue. Artisans from all provinces, Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan are showcasing their crafts. Visitors can enjoy folk performances, traditional food and handmade goods from every region of the country.
