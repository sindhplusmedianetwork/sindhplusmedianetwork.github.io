Musadik Malik to visit New Delhi for SCO dialogue, first ministerial visit since 2023

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 19, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik is set to visit New Delhi. He met Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar ahead of the visit. The visit is to attend a Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Civilisational Dialogue.

It is scheduled for November 20 to 22. The trip will be the first by a Pakistani federal minister to India since May 2023. India's opposition Congress party has questioned the decision to invite a Pakistani minister amid strained bilateral relations.
