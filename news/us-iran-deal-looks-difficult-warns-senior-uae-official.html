US-Iran deal looks 'difficult', warns senior UAE official

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 09, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A senior adviser to the prime minister of the United Arab Emirates has said that reaching a political deal between the United States and Iran to end the eight-month war looks difficult. Anwar Gargash was speaking at a conference in Abu Dhabi on Monday, according to the news agency AFP. He warned that the region could face a long period of instability, uncertainty and an unclear political and security environment.

Gargash said the UAE must prepare for a long period of uncertainty in which neither a decisive resolution nor a stable regional order can be taken for granted. He added that the consequences of the American war on Iran may persist for a long time. Gargash said the UAE, which opposed the US attack on Iran from the outset, would need to invest in its defensive capabilities, maintain a credible deterrent and strengthen its international security partners.
