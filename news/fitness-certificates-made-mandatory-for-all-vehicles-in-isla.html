Fitness certificates made mandatory for all vehicles in Islamabad; city to be smog-free by 30 November

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Islamabad police have made fitness certificates mandatory for all vehicles in the capital. The goods transport union has been directed to make vehicles smog-free by 30 November. CTO Kainat Azhar Khan said strict legal action will follow against smoke-emitting vehicles after the deadline.

Vehicles carrying sand, gravel and cement must be covered with tarpaulin, she said. She added that protecting human lives is the priority of the Islamabad Traffic Police. A crackdown on overloading will continue without discrimination, she said.

The measures are part of efforts to cut vehicle smoke and improve air quality in the capital.
