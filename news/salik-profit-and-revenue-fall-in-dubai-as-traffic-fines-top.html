Salik Profit and Revenue Fall in Dubai as Traffic Fines Top AED 220 Million

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 13, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Dubai toll-gate operator Salik has reported lower profit and revenue for the first nine months of 2026, according to results announced on November 13. Net profit stood at AED 1.045 billion, down 8.6 per cent from the same period last year, while total revenue fell 6.9 per cent to AED 2.117 billion. Profit in the third quarter alone was AED 341.1 million.

The number of trips through Salik's toll gates dropped 8.1 per cent to 577.8 million, weighing on overall revenue. In contrast, revenue from traffic fines rose 6.5 per cent to AED 220.1 million. Despite the weaker nine-month performance, the company guided revenue growth of 6 to 8 per cent for 2027.
