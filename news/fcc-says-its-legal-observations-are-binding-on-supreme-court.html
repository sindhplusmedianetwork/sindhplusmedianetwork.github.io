FCC Says Its Legal Observations Are Binding on Supreme Court

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 05, 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Federal Constitutional Court has held that any observation made by it in a judgment or order which decides a question of law or enunciates a legal principle will have binding effect on all courts, including the Supreme Court, under Article 189 of the Constitution. The 16-page judgment was authored by Justice Aamer Farooq, and was issued by a three-member bench headed by Chief Justice Amin-ud-Din Khan while deciding a matter concerning two permanent appointments in the Islamabad Capital Territory Private Educational Institutions Regulatory Authority. The court observed that Article 189(1), as amended by the 27th Constitutional Amendment, clearly stipulates that its judgments, to the extent that they decide questions of law or enunciate principles of law, are binding on all other courts in Pakistan, including the Supreme Court.

The judgment states that the binding effect of the court's judgments is not confined only to the ratio decidendi but also covers obiter dicta. The ruling comes amid an apparent lack of clarity over the relationship between the Supreme Court and the constitutional court following the 27th Constitutional Amendment, and the constitutional court has repeatedly asserted that its judgments are binding on the Supreme Court, while maintaining that Supreme Court judgments are not binding on it, though they may carry persuasive value. The judgment also examined the legal status of appointments made under the Islamabad Capital Territory Private Educational Institutions Ordinance, 2009, after its repeal and the enactment of the 2013 Act, which did not expressly save previous actions taken under the ordinance.

Referring to Article 89 of the Constitution, the judgment explained that a presidential ordinance remains in force for 120 days unless disapproved, may be extended once for another 120 days, and stands repealed on expiry of the period, while the president may also withdraw it at any time.
