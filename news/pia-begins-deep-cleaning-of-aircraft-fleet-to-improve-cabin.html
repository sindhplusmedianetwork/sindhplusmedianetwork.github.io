PIA Begins Deep Cleaning of Aircraft Fleet to Improve Cabin Standards

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 08, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan International Airlines (PIA) has begun a deep-cleaning campaign for the aircraft in its fleet, focusing on improving cabin cleanliness, cabin maintenance and overall service standards. The airline has decided to pay special attention to the quality of aircraft cleaning and cabin upkeep, and has started measures to raise standards. According to the PIA spokesperson, special cabin-cleaning arrangements for PIA aircraft were inspected in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad.

PIA Chief Executive Officer Adrian Hamilton-Manns said aircraft cleanliness is extremely important and it is essential to keep the brand at the highest standard, adding that keeping aircraft in the best condition is the airline's priority. He said this marks the beginning of a process and pace through which aircraft will be kept in the best condition. The chief executive also pledged to continue measures to promote high standards of cleanliness, quality and service at PIA.

Employees who played a prominent role in the cleaning process were given shields and certificates, while staff showing the best performance were also given cash rewards.
