Italy Edge Past Czechs to Reach Davis Cup Final Again

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 28, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Defending champions Italy reached the Davis Cup final again after beating the Czech Republic 2-1 in a hard-fought semi-final in Bologna on Friday. Lorenzo Musetti put Italy ahead with a 6-3, 6-4 win over Jakub Mensik in the opening singles, but Jiri Lehecka levelled the tie by beating Flavio Cobolli 6-2, 1-6, 6-3. The tie was decided in the doubles, where Musetti and Andrea Vavassori came from a set down to beat Lehecka and Tomas Machac 3-6, 6-1, 7-6(4).

Italy, playing without world number two Jannik Sinner, have now won 16 successive Davis Cup ties dating back to 2023. They will play Sunday's final against the winner of Saturday's semi-final between Spain and Britain, aiming for a fourth consecutive title. Should Italy lift the trophy again, they would become the first nation since the United States in the early 1970s to win the men's team event four times in a row.
