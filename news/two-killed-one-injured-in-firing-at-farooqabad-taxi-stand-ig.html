Two Killed, One Injured in Firing at Farooqabad Taxi Stand; IG Punjab Seeks Report

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people were killed and one injured in a firing incident at a taxi stand in Farooqabad, Sheikhupura, police said. The firing took place during a quarrel at the lorry adda, claiming the lives of Zahid Lodhi and Khurram Shahzad, while Fahad Lodhi was injured. IG Punjab Abdul Karim took notice of the incident and sought a report from the RPO Sheikhupura.

He directed the DPO Sheikhupura to arrest the accused immediately using all available resources. Senior police officers and forensic teams collected evidence from the crime scene. Investigation teams have been formed under the supervision of the SP Investigation to probe the incident.
