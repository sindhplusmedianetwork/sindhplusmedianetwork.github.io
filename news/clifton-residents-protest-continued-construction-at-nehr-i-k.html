Clifton Residents Protest Continued Construction at Nehr-i-Khayyam Despite Court Order

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Residents of Clifton held their weekly protest on Sunday against continued construction at Nehr-i-Khayyam in Karachi. The protesters marched from Do Talwar to the Nehr-i-Khayyam site and a 46-storey under-construction building under the banners of Save Clifton and Liveable Clifton. They said construction at Nehr-i-Khayyam was continuing round the clock despite Sindh High Court directions to halt it.

The residents demanded enforcement of building and zoning regulations in the area. They also called for the protection of parks and amenity plots and an end to unauthorised commercial conversions in residential neighbourhoods.
