Journalism as Institution in Serious Decline, Hundreds of Journalists Jobless, Says Nasir Baig Chughtai

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 20, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Senior journalist and well-known anchorperson Nasir Baig Chughtai, expressing concern over the falling standard of journalism, said this decline is not limited to the quality of news but journalists and journalism as an institution are also in serious decline. The former editor news of Saudi Arabia's first Urdu newspaper Urdu News, expressing insightful views on the present situation of journalism in Pakistan, the changing professional landscape, the role of journalistic trade unions and the historic struggle of the Pakistan Federal Union of Journalists, said the traditional spirit of collective struggle for journalists' rights, professional freedom and ethical values has gradually weakened. He said hundreds of professional journalists are being made unemployed but no one is raising a voice, speaking at a special dinner hosted in his honour by Pakistan Journalists Forum Jeddah, while he is currently visiting the Kingdom to perform Umrah.

Members of the forum and senior journalists participated in the event, and the sitting became a means of exchange on the evolution of journalism in Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, professional experiences and memories. Participants congratulated Nasir Chughtai on performing Umrah and visiting the Prophet's Mosque and stressed that for a strong and free journalistic community, not only professional skill but also unity, courage, institutional integrity and commitment to principles of responsible journalism are necessary. Describing his experiences in Saudi Arabia, Nasir Baig Chughtai highlighted the important and historic role of Urdu News in promoting Urdu journalism in the Kingdom and presented several examples of the newspaper's commitment to highlighting public interest issues and its journalistic journey.

He thanked the visionary leadership of Saudi Arabia for efforts for peace and stability in the region and said Saudi Arabia and Pakistan have played a wide positive and effective role for peace and prosperity in the past few months.
