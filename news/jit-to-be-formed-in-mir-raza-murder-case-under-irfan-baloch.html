JIT to be formed in Mir Raza murder case under Irfan Baloch, says Sindh home minister

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sindh Home Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar has announced that a joint investigation team will be formed in the Mir Raza murder case under the supervision of Irfan Baloch. In his statement, the provincial home minister said a summary for constituting the JIT is being sent to the chief minister. He said a separate committee under the supervision of DIG Zulfiqar Larik has been formed to take action against police officers, and that the committee will act on the recommendations of the commission on police matters.

The announcement came a day after a cabinet sub-committee met to implement the commission's report on the Mir Raza killing, convened by sub-committee convener and home minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar. The meeting reviewed the report's findings and the procedure for their implementation. Mir Raza Ali Khan had gone missing from Karachi's PECHS on July 28, and his body was found two days later in Gulistan-e-Johar.

Police said he had been shot in the chest and his body was found in a badly damaged state, while his social media accounts were deactivated a day before the body was discovered.
