Pakistan links regenerative farming to global carbon markets in new NARC project

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 02, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pakistan has launched a regenerative-organic agriculture project at the National Agricultural Research Centre (NARC) in Islamabad. The project aims to link soil-restoring farming practices with global carbon markets. Federal Minister for National Food Security Rana Tanveer Hussain inaugurated the 10-acre demonstration block for the project.

The block will be used to test biofertilizers and reduced-tillage machinery on a working scale. The project will also develop a measurement, reporting and verification system to enable farmers to earn revenues from carbon credits.
