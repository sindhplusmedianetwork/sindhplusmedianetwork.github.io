Punjab CM Maryam Nawaz Scraps 'Misaali Gaon' Project Over Corruption Complaints

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 23, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has shut down the Rs40 billion 'Misaali Gaon' project following complaints of corruption and fraud. She also ordered the Punjab Anti-Corruption Establishment to investigate the alleged irregularities. According to Express News, the project was meant to turn 800 villages in Punjab into model villages, with 400 villages included in the first phase and over Rs4 billion released for development work.

Work was initiated in villages of Sargodha division through formal tenders, but in Toba Tek Singh, influential officers and political networks allegedly registered fake companies on a partnership basis and secured contracts through collusion. Contractors were paid more than Rs3.5 billion for development work, while a report sought by the chief minister in the Punjab Assembly revealed that over 65 percent of the work in Sargodha division was never carried out on the ground. An anti-corruption case has been registered over the Toba Tek Singh corruption.

Local Government Minister Zeeshan Rafique said the project has been ended on these complaints and a better new project involving the community will be launched. He added that payments will be made where genuine work was done, while cases will be registered and recoveries made where corruption occurred.
