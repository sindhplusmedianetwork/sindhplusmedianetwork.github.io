Shehbaz orders port upgrade, PNSC reform

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 18, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Tuesday directed all port-related institutions to work in close coordination and adopt an integrated strategy to resolve the problems facing the business community. He was chairing a high-level meeting on the country's ports and the Pakistan National Shipping Corporation. He stressed that increasing port capacity is essential if Pakistan is to become a key trade and transshipment hub.

He directed that cargo movement and clearance be modernised. He also called for comprehensive reforms to transform PNSC into a modern, stable and competitive organisation, along with a plan to expand its fleet.
