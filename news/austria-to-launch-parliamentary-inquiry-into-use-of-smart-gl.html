Austria to launch parliamentary inquiry into use of smart glasses amid privacy concerns

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
October 29, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Austria is set to launch a parliamentary inquiry into the use of smart glasses as privacy concerns grow around the devices, digitalisation secretary Alexander Proll said on Wednesday. Proll proposed a three-stage model that would ban recordings in sensitive areas such as schools, courts, prisons and military facilities. He ruled out a total ban on the glasses.

Nearly 25,000 people have signed a petition calling for restrictions, and Vienna's city government has urged federal authorities to examine limits on public transport and in schools. State broadcaster ORF reported the development amid a wider European debate on wearable cameras.
