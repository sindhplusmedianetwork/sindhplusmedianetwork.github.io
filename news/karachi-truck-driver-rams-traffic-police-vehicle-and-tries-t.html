Karachi truck driver rams traffic police vehicle and tries to flee

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 17, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

A truck driver in Karachi's Defence Library Road area rammed a traffic police vehicle and then tried to flee, police said. Officers had tried to stop the truck because it was overloaded and swaying on the road. The driver instead drove into the police mobile, pushing it onto the footpath.

The officer then chased the truck in the damaged vehicle and caught the driver. Traffic police said legal action has been taken against the driver under the Motor Vehicle Ordinance. The truck owner has assured the police that compensation will be paid for the damage to the vehicle.
