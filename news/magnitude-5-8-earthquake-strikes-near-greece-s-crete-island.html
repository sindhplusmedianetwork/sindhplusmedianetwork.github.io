Magnitude 5.8 earthquake strikes near Greece's Crete island; no tsunami threat

SP
By Sindh Plus Media Network
November 14, 2026 · 1 min read
WhatsApp Facebook X

Strong earthquake tremors were felt near the Greek tourist island of Crete in the Mediterranean, spreading fear in coastal towns and villages as people came out of their homes. According to Greek meteorological and seismological authorities, the quake measured 5.8 on the Richter scale, with its epicentre 30 kilometres deep under the sea. Officials said the tremors were so strong that buildings shook on nearby islands as well, and people staying in tourist hotels gathered in open fields.

Rescue agencies immediately assessed the affected areas, but no major loss of life or property was reported. Maritime authorities ruled out any tsunami threat for coastal populations and described the situation as under control.
