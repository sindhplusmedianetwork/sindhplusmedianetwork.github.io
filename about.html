Biography

Tahir Adnan is a 27-year-old journalist from Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan. He earned his Master of Arts in Journalism from Shah Abdul Latif University and a Bachelor of Science in Natural Sciences from the same university. From 2020 to 2024 he worked in media and public relations at PTCL, Pakistan's national telecom company, before teaching English as a Primary School Teacher (PST) for two years (2023–2025).

In 2025 he earned a 120-hour TEFL certificate with Distinction. As a freelance journalist he reports on the issues mainstream outlets overlook — the water crisis in rural Ghotki, the struggles of working journalists in Pakistan, and the untold stories of rural Sindh. He founded Sindh Plus Media Network to give those stories a home.
